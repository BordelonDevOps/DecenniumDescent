# Canon Boundary — Known Descent and Sealed Depths

**Aligned with owner lock 2026-08-05 (§2 Floor 97 / Old God dual-layer).**

## Publicly recognized achievement

- A moderately successful wealth expedition reaches approximately Tier 2.5 and the Haven cities. This is career-defining.
- Some companies operate into Tier III.
- Most professional expeditions turn back around Tier IV because of darker rumors, losses, and unexplained phenomena.
- Tier IV introduces the first slow, ambiguous evidence of the entities later identified as the Old Ones. Public text must show contradictions, not explain the cosmology.
- Returning from Tier V makes an expedition legendary.
- The most famous recognized expedition reached the Tier VI Gate Guardian and failed.
- No reliable public record confirms passage beyond Gate VI.
- **Public knowledge ends at the failed Sixth Gate.** Do not publish Floor 97, Floor 100, the focusing crystal, or the Old God’s actual vulnerability in this volume or any player-facing book.
- Binding / Interstice language must **not** appear as publicly reliable mechanics. At most: vague contradictory rumors, false scholarship, corrupted translations, or discarded design history.
- Old Ones / deep endgame: contradictory rumors only — never the crystal conquest path.

## Director truth

- Tiers VII through X exist but are unknown to the world.
- The Marquis of Silence was the sole survivor of the truly most successful expedition.
- That survivor inherited the Marquis office and became bound to the dungeon.
- The office persists until the Marquis is defeated.
- Hidden claimant and succession mechanics must never appear in player-facing material.
- **Crystal path (Director-only):** final known sequence → Floor 100 confrontation produces/exposes the focusing crystal → crystal weakens / makes the Old God vulnerable → confront the Old God under those conditions. Floor 97 is a regular zone containing a hidden chamber where the Old Ones sleep. See `docs/mechanics/LEVEL_97_OLD_GOD_Q6_OPEN.md`.
- Full Sixth Gate encounter, Tiers VII–X guardians, Marquis origin, and crystal-path endgame live in the **Director’s Sealed Bestiary** / sealed continuity — never in Known Descent.
