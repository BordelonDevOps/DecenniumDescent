## What It Is

Umbrin Warborn is a heavy retainer bred and trained for house war. Even when encountered alone, it is connected to a house objective, and surviving witnesses are likely to affect later negotiations.

## What Experienced Expeditions Notice

Silk laid in deliberate geometric intervals; lamps hooded from the viewer rather than the corridor; cut gemstones left where a scout must choose whether to touch them; paired footprints that abruptly become one set.

## How It Fights

It opens with **Weapon Strike** against the target most capable of controlling its movement. It uses **Off-hand Strike** to punish anyone who tries to stabilize that target. It fights from darkness and vertical routes, spending lives only when a house objective justifies the cost.

## How to Defeat It

**Prepare Before Contact**

- Prepare resistance or mitigation for slashing, piercing damage.
- Use broad illumination and cut silk routes.

**Combat Priority**

Remove the shadowblade or arachnomancer route network, then isolate command.

**Exploit**

The defining weakness is **bright-light disruption (tactical)**. Treat this as an encounter solution rather than an automatic damage vulnerability. Successful Research, Recon, or Resource actions should make the exploit easier or safer.

**Withdraw When**

Withdraw or change the objective when its full multiattack reaches the support line. A failed retreat against this creature usually costs more than abandoning the immediate treasure.

## Habitat, Goals, and Lair

Umbrin sites emphasize verticality, narrow sight lines, web bridges, false balconies, and concealed side galleries. Their fighters know every line that can be crossed without entering bright light.

The Umbrin are a subterranean people whose houses treat darkness, silk, memory, and debt as instruments of government. Their patrols are not random dungeon encounters; each is an extension of a house policy, vendetta, or negotiation.

They respect accurate names, witnessed bargains, and demonstrated restraint. Threats against children, eggs, or house memory usually end negotiation immediately.
