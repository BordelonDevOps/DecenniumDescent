## What It Is

Choralith, Voice Below is the Gate Guardian for motion, rhythm, and the refusal to become static. It is an arena-scale adversary whose body, Seal, and terrain operate as one examination. Reducing it to zero hit points is necessary, but an Expedition that ignores its Gate principle can still lose the passage, the reward, or members of the team.

## What Experienced Expeditions Notice

Architecture becomes ceremonial; lesser monsters stop crossing a boundary; previous Expeditions leave preparation caches rather than camps; repeated symbols appear in terrain and wounds. Different witnesses remember the same sound differently.

### Traits

**Gate-Bound.** The Guardian cannot be permanently displaced from its arena while the Seal remains active.

**Aerial Hunter.** It ignores ground difficult terrain and seeks angles that bypass front-line cover.

**Escalation Pulse.** **Subsonic Chord** recharges on 5-6; visible signs appear one round before the first use.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The creature makes 2 attacks using the actions below.

**Resonant Lash.** *Attack:* +11 to hit, one target. *Hit:* 3d12+8 thunder damage (average 28).

**Echo Needle.** *Attack:* +11 to hit, one target. *Hit:* 2d10+6 psychic damage (average 17).

**Subsonic Chord (Recharge 5-6).** Creatures in the affected area make a DC 20 Constitution save, taking 6d10 thunder damage (average 33) on a failure and half on a success. The Director should describe the charge-up before resolving it.

## How It Fights

It opens with **Resonant Lash** against the target most capable of controlling its movement. It uses **Echo Needle** to punish anyone who tries to stabilize that target. When **Subsonic Chord** recharges, it delays only if it can catch a more important cluster or interrupt a decisive ritual. It escalates by phase and uses the Seal Pulse to punish parties that solve damage but ignore the test.

## How to Defeat It

**Prepare Before Contact**

- Prepare resistance or mitigation for thunder, psychic damage.
- Carry ranged attacks, grounding tools, or vertical cover.
- Spread before **Subsonic Chord** becomes available.

**Combat Priority**

Complete the arena objective while maintaining damage pressure.

**Exploit**

The defining weakness is **None identified**. Treat this as an encounter solution rather than an automatic damage vulnerability. Successful Research, Recon, or Resource actions should make the exploit easier or safer.

**Withdraw When**

Withdraw or change the objective when Subsonic Chord catches three or more Expedition members; resources fall below half before the arena mechanic is solved. A failed retreat against this creature usually costs more than abandoning the immediate treasure.

## Habitat, Goals, and Lair

A Gate arena is part of the creature. Terrain, Seal Pulses, objectives, and failure conditions matter as much as hit points.

Gate Guardians are not random apex monsters. Each is a living examination tied to a Division of the dungeon, a Gate Seal, and a principle the Expedition must demonstrate under pressure.

Some Guardians speak, bargain, or test. None are safely reduced to ordinary patrons or quest-givers.
