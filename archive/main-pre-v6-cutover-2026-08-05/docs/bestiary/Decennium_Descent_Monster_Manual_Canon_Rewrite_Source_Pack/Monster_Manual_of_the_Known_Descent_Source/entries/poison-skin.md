## What It Is

Poison-Skin is part of Bloomdeep’s economy of rot, medicine, hunger, and propagation. Its body and lair exchange material constantly, so killing the visible creature does not always end the encounter.

## What Experienced Expeditions Notice

Bubbles moving against the current; mushrooms opening toward wounded creatures; bones polished clean without tooth marks; bright amphibian prints ending beneath stagnant water.

## How It Fights

It opens with **Heavy Blow** against the target most capable of controlling its movement. It turns corpses, spilled reagents, open wounds, and standing water into additional pressure.

## How to Defeat It

**Prepare Before Contact**

- Prepare resistance or mitigation for bludgeoning damage.
- Seal skin and breathing; separate fire from concentrated spores.

**Combat Priority**

Remove brood pools, root nodes, corpses, or chemical trails that sustain the creature.

**Exploit**

The defining weakness is **airflow control and source-node destruction**. Treat this as an encounter solution rather than an automatic damage vulnerability. Successful Research, Recon, or Resource actions should make the exploit easier or safer.

**Withdraw When**

Withdraw or change the objective when the creature reaches favorable terrain or isolates a target. A failed retreat against this creature usually costs more than abandoning the immediate treasure.

## Habitat, Goals, and Lair

Marsh lairs use water depth, root tunnels, spore pockets, floating ground, and corrosive pools. Fire solves some problems but can turn enclosed spores into explosions.

Bloomdeep turns hunger, rot, medicine, and reproduction into the same ecological process. Its creatures are dangerous because every wound, corpse, and abandoned ration can become part of the next encounter.

Sporefolk and Poison-Skins can negotiate. Grazers and crawlers are driven by chemical cues; changing scent may redirect them.
