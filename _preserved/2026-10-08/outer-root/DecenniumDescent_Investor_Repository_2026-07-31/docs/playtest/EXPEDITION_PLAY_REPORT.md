## Session zero — Resonance briefing

**Scene:** Reach watchpost (Commander Rhal-Voss), contract sigil, two-hour march to Glassbite Hollow.

**DM explains to Vesper (novice):**

- One **Resonance pool** — not “four spell slots.” Cantrips = **0 RP**. Leveled spells ≈ **2 RP per circle**.

## Play-by-play

### Encounter 1 — Glass thicket scouts (2 × **Chitine**, CR 1)

**Initiative (d20 + Dex):** Kethren 19, Chitine A 17, Kael 14, Chitine B 12, Helga 8, Vesper 5.

**Round 1**

- **Kethren:** Tiger stance (free **Stance Shift**). Move to flank Chitine A. **Flurry of Blows** — hit/hit, **9** bludgeoning total. Chitine A **43** HP. (+1 Ki from Flowing Form narrative.)
- **Chitine A:** *Freak Bite* vs Kael — miss (AC 17).
- **Kael:** Greataxe vs Chitine A — hit, **14** slashing. Chitine A **29** HP. P4 logs DPR tick.
- **Chitine B:** Drops on **Vesper** (novice forward) — hit, **8** piercing. Vesper **10** HP.
- **Helga:** **Sacred Flame** vs Chitine B — fail save, **5** radiant. Chitine B **47** HP.
- **Vesper:** Burns **2 RP** on *Dissonant Whispers* vs Chitine B — **fail** save, **8** psychic + flee (narrative). “That was two of my four slots.” Still has **2 RP**.

**Round 2–3:** Focus fire on B then A. Kethren **Combo Step** setup (third distinct attack) — stun applied (table: WIS save DC 13, fail). Kael finishes A. Vesper **Vicious Mockery** (0 RP) on B; **miss** on rapier.

**Outcome:** Win in **3 rounds**. Party loss **~18 HP** (Vesper 10, Kael 8 from one bite). EV model: **~35% party HP** over 6 rounds if spread; focus fire + AC 17 frontline reduced actual loss.

**Persona beats:** P1 forgot Bardic Inspiration was on herself (unused). P3 called flank positions; table confirmed **no flanking rule** — used Help once. P4 called target priority on Chitine B after Vesper dropped.

**Draft XP:** `[Draft XP] Chitine (CR 1): 200 × 2 = 400 total → 100 each (4 participants)`

**Short rest (Retune):** Vesper +1 RP (retune 1 on 4 max); Helga +3 RP (¼×7 + WIS 2); Kethren +0–1 RP (minor pool, table rounded **+1**). Kael: **Second Wind** not spent. Horn **+1 h** → **3 h**.

### Encounter 2 — Consort on the rope bridge (**Umbrin Favored Consort**, CR 2)

**Scene:** Blackglass slats, steam vent. P3 spends 8 minutes on **Aid** prep (PF reflex) — ruled narrative, no stat block. P1 nearly slips looking at reflections; **Acrobatics** pass.

**Initiative:** Consort 16, Kael 15, Kethren 13, Helga 11, Vesper 7.

**Round 1**

- **Consort:** *Monstrous Claw* vs Kael — hit, **11** slashing. Kael **24** HP.
- **Kael:** Greataxe — hit, **13**. Consort **55** HP. Regen tick pending (5%/2 turns ≈ **2 HP** later).
- **Kethren:** Tortoise stance; moves adjacent to Vesper on narrow bridge. **Unarmed strike** — hit, **5**. Consort **50** HP.
- **Helga:** **Bless** on Kael + Kethren + self (2 RP) — **5 RP** left. Concentration begins.
- **Vesper:** Holds bridge rope; **no attack** (good instinct, late). “Can I inspire Kael?” — already gave herself BI earlier.

**Round 2**

- **Consort:** *Monstrous Claw* vs Kael — hit, **9**. Kael **15** HP.
- **Kael:** **Action Surge** nova — greataxe ×2 + **1 mutation activation** (Juggernaut cap: one activation/round). Hits: **14** + **11** = **25**. Consort **25** HP. P4 logs cap compliance.
- **Kethren:** Flurry — hit/miss, **4**. Consort **21** HP.
- **Helga:** **Sacred Flame** — hit, **6**. Consort **15** HP.
- **Vesper:** Rapier — **miss** (Sunlight Sensitivity not invoked — torch shadows).

**Round 3:** Kael **Second Wind** (+**8** HP → **23**). Kael kills Consort with greataxe **12**. Regen recovered **~4 HP** over fight.

**Outcome:** Win in **3 rounds**. Kael lowest **15 HP** before Second Wind. **No deaths.**

**Draft XP:** `[Draft XP] Umbrin Favored Consort (CR 2): 450 total → 112 each (4 participants)`

**Short rest:** Helga **Retune +3 RP** → **6 RP**. Vesper +1 → **3 RP**. Kael: Second Wind spent; Action Surge back. P2 asked for **Hit Dice** healing — **not in Session UI**; Kael stays at 23 HP. Horn **+1 h** → **5 h**.

### Encounter 3 — Vault weaver (**Gloom Weaver**, CR 3)

**Stakes:** Ritual completes if Weaver survives **3 rounds while holding shard** (fail condition). Shard on plinth.

**Pre-combat:** P4 **readies** greataxe if Weaver grabs shard. P2 casts **Shield of Faith** on Kael (2 RP) instead of re-Blessing — 5e habit “buff the tank.” P1 tries **Suggestion** before initiative — DM calls for initiative instead.

**Initiative:** Kethren 18, Gloom Weaver 16, Kael 12, Helga 10, Vesper 4.

**Round 1**

- **Kethren:** Tiger stance; rush Weaver. **Flurry** — hit/hit, **10**. Weaver **78** HP.
- **Weaver:** *Freak Bite* vs Kethren — hit, **9**. Kethren **12** HP.
- **Kael:** Move + greataxe — hit, **15**. Weaver **63** HP.
- **Helga:** Maintains **Shield of Faith**; **Sacred Flame** — fail, **6**. Weaver **57** HP.
- **Vesper:** *Healing Word* on Kethren (2 RP) — **6** healed. Kethren **18** HP. “I thought he was fine?” — P2 groans: wrong priority.

**Round 2**

- **Kethren:** **Webstep** to ceiling (1/rest) — vertical flank. Unarmed — hit, **5**. Weaver **52** HP.
- **Weaver:** Recharge **Shadow Weave** (rolled **6**) — DC 13 WIS. **Vesper fail** (**14** necrotic → **0 HP**, unconscious). **Kael success** (**7**). **Helga success** (**7**). **Kethren fail** (**14** → **4** HP).
- **Kael:** Greataxe — hit, **13**. Weaver **39** HP.
- **Helga:** **Healing Word** bonus on Vesper — **5** HP, conscious. **3 RP** left.
- **Vesper:** Death saves not needed; **Vicious Mockery** — **3** psychic.

**Death saves (Vesper):** Briefly at 0 before Helga's bonus — tracked manually.

**Round 3**

- **Kethren:** Flurry — hit/miss, **4**. Weaver **35** HP. Ki spent on stun attempt — **save success**.
- **Weaver:** *Freak Bite* vs **Kael** — hit, **10**. Kael **13** HP (Shield of Faith ended — concentration broken by damage? Table: **yes**, 10 ≥ CON save fail). 
- **Kael:** **Action Surge** + greataxe ×2 — **14** + **9** = **23**. Weaver **12** HP.
- **Helga:** Warhammer — **miss**.
- **Vesper:** Rapier — **miss**.

**Round 4**

- **Kethren:** Unarmed — hit, **6**. Weaver **6** HP.
- **Weaver:** *Freak Bite* vs Kethren — hit, **11**. Kethren **0 HP**, unconscious.
- **Kael:** Greataxe — **7**. **Gloom Weaver defeated.** Ritual broken; shard secured.

**Round 5:** Helga **Healing Word** on Kethren (**4** HP). Vesper stabilizes narrative.

**Outcome:** Win in **4 combat rounds** (5 calendar). **Two PCs touched 0 HP** (Vesper, Kethren); **no permanent deaths**. **Primary success** — shard recovered.

**Draft XP:** `[Draft XP] Gloom Weaver (CR 3): 700 total → 175 each (4 participants)`

**Extraction:** +2 h march. Final horn **7 h / 16 h** budget.
