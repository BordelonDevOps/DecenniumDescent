## Shared deep clocks (all T6–10)

### Horn pressure after day 50

Most parties that reach Floor 51+ are already in the **reckoning** (hours 1200–2400) or racing the Horn. Director guidance:

| Clock fact | Deep implication |
|------------|------------------|
| Horn sounded | Extraction contracts thin; NPC allies make final push/turn decisions |
| Hours left | Every Thread rewrite, Arena bout, or Spiral coil **costs calendar** as hard as hit points |
| Seal at 2400 | Trapped-until-next-Cycle is real; telegraph repeatedly (DM-01 Ch 3) |
| Soft quit already passed | Bar of Last Refuge is **behind** them — continuing is a campaign promise, not a default |

Do not invent extra Opening sub-clocks beyond day-50 Horn ([`DESCENT_WINDOW_CANON.md`](../mechanics/DESCENT_WINDOW_CANON.md)).

### Public vs secret (do not collapse)

| Layer | What deep parties may learn | What stays Director-only until earned |
|-------|----------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Mortal / explorer | Zone names, Guardian titles, rumor havens, Weave/oath/dusk hazards | — |
| Public myth | Crucible = Calan'thir + Asmodeus joint work; Divine Pact as cooperation monument | — |
| Rung 1–2 | Architecture older than script; Custodian / Bearer / Signatory offices | Silent Ones name |
| Rung 3 (T6–7 ceiling) | Pact **governs** mechanisms it did not originate | Heartcell full purpose |
| Rung 4 (Interstice+) | Operating cycle perceptible (resonance, bait, containment) | Peace-trigger threshold; Q6 Level 97 identity |
| Full endgame (T10 if earned) | Heartcell / Matrix consequences through **operation**, not monologue | Invented Level 97 room without owner answer |

Silent Ones = Old Ones (owner lock). Loot is bait; resonance **is** Heartcell operation. Do not write Silent Ones into Player Rulebook.

## Tier 6 — Crossroads of the Shattered Thread (F51–60)

**Guardian:** Lumen of the Shattered Thread · **Gate:** Lumen Gate (end F60) · **Hazard landmark:** Shattered Crossroads (F51)  
**Rumor haven:** Threadbare Camp (F53) — warded Weave-thread knot  
**Sources:** DM-01 Ch 31, 63 · Chronicle T6 · Instruments T6 d8

### Tone

Broken Weave-paths make **route knowledge political power**. Choices create temporary reality; maps need anchors; neighboring groups may inhabit incompatible versions of the same node. This is the last band that still feels like **civilization under pressure**, not pure myth-space.

### Factions & peoples

Build **at least two** settlements with different stabilization methods (DM-01):

| Method family | Example behavior | Expedition leverage |
|---------------|------------------|---------------------|
| Record & bell | Synchronized public clocks; copied maps fail without shared conditions | Charter recognition; Research translation of map *conditions* |
| Living guide | Memorized routes; guides are scarce political assets | Hire / kidnap / protect a guide |
| Lineage / legal | Crossing rights by family or civic recognition | Witness NPCs (e.g. Aru of Two Afternoons gallery) |

Surface hooks (Chronicle): Academic Factions pay premium for Weave-thread samples **especially post-Horn**; Quiet Deep thread-well rumors draw Cleansing Orders.

**Do not** use T6 societies as Silent Ones exposition mouths. They may know words like Signatory, Bearer, Custodian, Thread, First Architecture — and still be **wrong in sophisticated ways**.

### Travel

- Paths exist by timing, witnesses, carried anchors, or prior choices.  
- Footprints fade; lantern-light may pick the wrong thread.  
- Reconnaissance / Research translate **conditions**, not mere lines.  
- Separation risk: teams in non-identical versions of one node.

### Seal stakes (Lumen)

Test: can the Expedition **choose one path** without denying the reality of paths not taken? Possible beats: outcome-echoes, sacrificed futures, multiple civilizations recognizing one shared crossing. Defeat unlocks Tier 7 in fiction.

### Horn pressure

Post-Horn parties treat Threadbare Camp as a **final deep logistics hub**. Losing a map-anchor after day 50 is often worse than losing a fight. Academic extraction orders become desperate (Instruments d8).

### Public vs secret

| Public | Secret ceiling (rung 3) |
|--------|-------------------------|
| Weave labyrinth; last known floor cities; Lumen as Gate warden | Civic archive / treaty showing a Pact Signatory **assuming** preexisting crossings — Pact governs, did not originate |

**Sample seed (DM-01):** City of Two Afternoons — half the city remembers a morning treaty; half is still negotiating it.

## Tier 7 — Pactbound Arena (F61–70)

**Guardian:** Xarog, the Pactbound Furnace · **Gate:** Furnace Gate (end F70) · **Hazard landmark:** Pactbound Coliseum (F61)  
**Rumor haven:** Oathbreaker's Nook (F64) — broken pact-rune alcove  
**Sources:** DM-01 Ch 64 · Chronicle T7 · Instruments T7 d8

### Tone

Exact words, public promises, and spectatorship become **terrain**. Not merely gladiatorial sequence — a jurisdiction where conflict becomes contract. Blackglass / Narthon bloodsport companies color survivor slang.

### Factions & peoples

| Actor | Want | Deep use |
|-------|------|----------|
| Arena jurisdiction / heralds | Enforce stated terms | Contests with **legal** win conditions |
| Empty Advocate (gallery) | Someone to inherit a forgotten claim | Dangerous recognition |
| Surface warlords (Narthon) | Blood-tally fame | Rival Expeditions, gladiator recruits |
| Contract observers | Exact language | Devil / Pact-flavored witnesses without naming Silent Ones |

Durable "cities" here are **exceptional** — oath-locked compounds, repeating bout circuits, trapped offices.

### Travel

- Every promise can become leverage, restriction, protection, or fuel.  
- Write important terms down; state what each side believes the wording means.  
- Win the fight / lose the legal result if the stated objective was ignored.  
- Supply lines fail; maps decay into contradictory tallies.

### Seal stakes (Xarog)

Force which promises the Expedition will make **permanent**. Furnace may burn false/impossible/contradictory obligations while empowering accepted ones. Passage should leave **at least one binding term** that matters in Tier 8.

### Horn pressure

Arena victories attract stronger challengers — each bout burns Opening hours. Post-Horn, "one more match" is how Expeditions seal themselves.

### Public vs secret

| Public | Secret ceiling |
|--------|----------------|
| Bloodsport + oath-law; Xarog speaks Pact clauses no mortal court knows | Signatures/offices older than Divine Pact; Calan'thir & Asmodeus **assuming** jurisdiction rather than creating it |

**Sample seed:** Empty Grandstand — applause from prior Expeditions whose names were erased; accepting acclaim transfers unfinished claims.

### Concealment threshold (Director)

Beneath Tier 7, true purpose becomes harder to hide: Custodian sigils, pre-First-Cycle masonry, treasure inscriptions predating Calan'thir script (Chronicle + Matrix). Plant these **before** Interstice.

## Interstice — Old God (between T7 and T8)

**Not a tier. Not Floor 70 or 71 on public maps. Not a Gate Guardian.**  
**Sources:** DM-01 Ch 34, 65 · Chronicle Director threshold note · SILENT_ONES Q6 / WDL-4

### Tone

Genre and certainty break. The party has been surviving a dungeon; the Interstice suggests the dungeon is surviving **something else**. Ordinary tier assumptions stop being enough.

### Staging (safe — no Q6 invention)

Furnace Gate **can expose** the route; defeating Xarog does **not** automatically force confrontation. Treat as campaign-scale optional/threshold encounter.

**Must accomplish (DM-01):** break confidence · change scale · preserve mystery · demand a lasting cost · create before/after.

**Five forms:** Audience · Containment failure · Pilgrimage · Judgment · Predation.

**Build three layers:** perceptible · operational · interpretive (keep ≥2 plausible readings).

### What it may confirm (information limits)

- Something predates the public Crucible.  
- Pact sovereigns did not originate every mechanism.  
- Names have been deliberately buried.  
- Images that rhyme with Matrix: collapsing choices, merging histories, interchangeable names, sound losing distinction.

### What it must not do

- Lecture Silent Ones / Heartcell / full Matrix.  
- Answer **Level 97 Old God (Q6)** — folk "eleventh shard" / Binding myth is **not** this gate; echoes may confuse players intentionally.  
- Infer relationship between Interstice being and Silent Ones/Heartcell without owner ruling.

### Director-secret interpretation menu (choose one campaign truth; keep others as contradictory evidence)

Captive · wardened · feeding · dreaming · partially integrated into the mechanism. **Do not** close Q6 by equating this being with Level 97.

### Crossing consequences

Each character leaves with one personal, playable consequence (memory shared, unspeakable name, deep-architecture scar, lost deity certainty, promise witnessed outside the Pact). Tier 8 must echo those answers immediately.

**Sample:** Room With One History — escape requires preserving contradictory truths.

### Horn pressure

Interstice scenes are expensive in Opening hours by design. Telegraph seal risk if the party lingers for cosmology tourism.

## Tier 8 — Duskshard Pools (F71–80)

**Guardian:** Eirfax, the Dusk Crowned · **Gate:** Crown Gate (end F80) · **Hazard landmark:** Duskshard Basins (F71)  
**Rumor haven:** Twilight Shelf (F74) — dry ledge above basins  
**Sources:** DM-01 Ch 66 · Chronicle T8 · Instruments T8 d8

### Tone

Delayed identity. Reflections lag, retain discarded possibilities, sometimes act after the character moved on. Cooling light — radiance as residue, metal, prayer, crystallized dusk. Obsidian / Veyr-Obsid poetry colors banks without requiring surface cult membership.

### Factions & peoples

Durable settlements exceptional: oath-protected monasteries, reflection enclaves, survivors who rotate identities, camps at stable dusk wells. Existence depends on a bargain or procedure the party can learn.

Surface hooks: Vaskurhollow name-recovery contracts (Archpriest Gorthan); Twilight Week bleed into the tier.

Gallery NPC: Veyra Dusk-Returned — prove she is not merely a reflection.

### Travel

- Establish what each surface reflects **before** breaking the rule (intention / last action / rejected self).  
- Names, oaths, injuries may differ across banks.  
- Helping "earlier" versions of the party may create the future they warn against.

### Seal stakes (Eirfax)

Which version of the Expedition deserves continuity? Crown may offer to remove regret, duplicate a successful self, or abandon Interstice burden. Passage = choose identity **without erasing alternatives**.

### Horn pressure

Identity crises burn Research/Support periods. Post-Horn name-recovery deals become extortionate; failing them can strand injured members until Cycle seal.

### Public vs secret

| Public | Secret ceiling |
|--------|----------------|
| Reflections, dusk glass, name rites, Eirfax crown in every pool | Heartcell treating identity as **pattern** with operational value — reward echoes, discarded Gate outcomes |

**Sample seed:** Camp That Returned First — exhausted future-selves already camped ahead.

## Tier 9 — Veiled Iron Spiral (F81–90)

**Guardian:** Threxus, the Iron Spiral · **Gate:** Spiral Gate (end F90) · **Hazard landmark:** Veiled Helix (F81)  
**Rumor haven:** Iron Rest (F84) — brief Weave-pressure ease  
**Sources:** DM-01 Ch 67 · Chronicle T9 · Instruments T9 d8

### Tone

Compression of distance, memory, and strategic certainty. Progress feels repetitive because the tier **hides how much changed**. Clarify at table: **not** the Turning Spiral patron — name collision is intentional confusion, not identity.

### Factions & peoples

No ordinary cities. Possible: compressed siege havens, oracle paradox cells, Murkish-rivet side tunnels (political fallout if maps leak to surface). Guild monopoly rumors on spiral wire (Instruments).

### Travel

- Ordinary distance marks unreliable — anchor on material change, resonance, wounds, oaths, temperature, **external Opening clock**.  
- Maps record transformations, not turns.  
- Identical corridors at different depths if they change objects/memories differently.  
- Foes exploit approach/retreat and repeated routes.

### Seal stakes (Threxus)

Define progress without relying on distance: demonstrate caused changes, preserved people, carried truths, unrepeatable choices. Purely circular strategy strengthens the Guardian.

### Horn pressure

False progress is a calendar trap. Iron Rest is precious because every wasted coil burns hours toward seal. External 2400 h clock is often the only honest map.

### Public vs secret

| Public | Secret ceiling |
|--------|----------------|
| Iron helix, time-oracle gear rumors, Threxus as will-test | Heartcell measures difference across repeated states; Matrix inferable as reverse chain when distinctions collapse |

**Sample seed:** Siege on Every Turn — same attackers at different preparation moments; haven remembers only latest version.

## Tier 10 — Silencebound Void (F91–100)

**Guardian:** The Marquis of Silence · **Gate:** Marquis Gate (end F100) · **Hazard landmark:** Hush Threshold (F91)  
**Rumor haven:** Last Whisper (F93) — final echo before hush  
**Sources:** DM-01 Ch 68 · Chronicle T10 · Instruments T10 d8 · SILENT_ONES guardrails

### Tone

Not mere quiet — removal of distinctions by which characters know they are present, connected, and capable of choosing. Stillness offers attractive relief from conflict and responsibility. **Quiet Deep** myths rhyme with hush without equating to Q6.

### Factions & peoples

Any population is final remnant, protected enclave, transformed chorus, trapped office, or deliberate exception. Ask what it **surrendered** to remain. Gallery: Oris Last-Voice — which one sentence survives the Cycle.

### Travel / communication

- Do **not** enforce long OOC silence. Translate fiction: incomplete written plans, one word across a boundary, emotion without detail, pre-agreed signals.  
- Speech, gesture, magic, tracks, memory may fail to reach others.  
- Tier may react to unspoken intent.

### Seal stakes (Marquis)

Offer an end that is genuinely attractive: no more loss, contradiction, contested history. Victory may be defeat, replace, persuade, bind, or transform the **role**. Floor 100 must force an answer about difference, stillness, and what characters will preserve — not a treasure chest and silence (DM-01).

### Horn pressure

If the party reaches T10 with little Opening left, the Marquis temptation and the seal clock **rhyme**. Telegraph Cycle entrapment. Whoever holds Marquis Gate when the seal falls **names the Cycle** in survivor myth (Chronicle) — surface kingdoms send assassins and ambassadors upward.

### Public vs secret

| Public | Secret ceiling (if earned) |
|--------|----------------------------|
| Hush, anti-resonance gear rumors, Marquis without title | Heartcell / Ul'nak / Matrix through **operation and consequence** — what conflict preserves, what prison contains, what perfect stillness erases. Never reduce to one villain monologue. |

**Instruments d8 note:** "Silent Ones" line is **Director note only** — never spoken aloud as known fact.

**Sample seed:** Last Conversation — each character may speak one final remembered sentence; route opens when identity survives incomplete communication.

### Beyond the Marquis (Director)

Final seal / path toward Gate of Oblivion; First Completion survivor lore (Old Gods' Unity awareness; dungeon rewrite; Warden activation; machine learns). Use as campaign aftermath language — do not invent Level 97 room contents.
