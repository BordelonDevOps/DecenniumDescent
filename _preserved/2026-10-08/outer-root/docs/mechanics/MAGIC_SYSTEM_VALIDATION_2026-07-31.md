## Protected design conclusion

Spell overlap is intentional. The spell is a shared pattern in the Weave; the class determines how and when the character learns to sound it. Resonance Points limit total magical output, while Dissonance and Refrain prevent repetitive spam. Lich and Necromancer are the only current Hero Paths allowed to force ranked casting through Fractured Dissonance, and they pay direct magical trauma for doing so.
