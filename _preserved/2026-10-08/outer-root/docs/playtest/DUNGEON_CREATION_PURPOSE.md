## 1. Best-supported canon answer

The repo supports **two linked layers**: a **cosmological origin** (why *a* dungeon exists in the Weave) and a **mortal play layer** (why expeditions enter the **Great Descent** beneath the **Shardspire** each Cycle). Sources strongly agree on the first; the second is clear on *function* but thin on *explicit construction myth*.

### A. Primordial creation — the Crucible (First Seal)

At the dawn of the Spiral, **Calan'thir** (Architect of the Spiral; melodic warden) and **Asmodeus** (architect of law and consequence) seal the **Old Ones'** dreaming hush. Their seal requires **ongoing tension** — if the living perimeter goes silent, the fold loosens and silence seeps outward.

They build a venue where **motion and boundary meet in endless contest**:

- Calan'thir folds strands into cavities and corridors of pure resonance.
- Asmodeus traces a maze of decisions — each turn an edge, each chamber a rhythm.
- The construction **does not imprison bodies** (none yet walk); it **cages potential**, steering it through challenge after challenge.
- **Later scholars call this labyrinth the Dungeon**; at creation it is known only as **the Crucible**.
- Purpose: reinforce the Spiral seal; produce harmonics that keep the ring alive; serve as a **living whetstone** — **not a tomb**.
- Asmodeus: *"Let this crucible stand, proof that rest comes only at the end of striving."*
- Calan'thir: *"Let striving remember its melody."*

The Spiral itself becomes an **engine of perpetual motion** — conflict and melody must remain equal partners or the seal fails.

### B. Cosmic function after creation — mirror, trial, attunement

As planes mature, **the Dungeon mirrors cosmic tide**: new chambers reflect exchanges among fire, shadow, dream, and thought. Mortals are later granted subtle attunement; **Transition opens channels guiding adventurers into Crucible chambers**.

Calan'thir plants a final seed in the Dungeon heart: a challenge teaching that **unity of purpose emerges from conversation among powers that refuse to yield identity** — integrating opposites without dissipating purpose.

### C. Mortal era — Shardspire, Great Descent, Tenfold Cycles

**World myth (Aethel history)** describes the **playable** dungeon loop:

- **Shardspire activates** in the tenth year of a **10-year rotation**.
- **Mortals enter** the **Great Descent** (100 floors, 10 tiers in companion data).
- **Cycles are named after their victor**; deepest gate cleared colors the next Cycle's laws, weather, and rulers.
- **First Cycle (87 AE):** Shardspire glowed; a farmer emerged with a crown of flame, founding Tyrnathar — **"the advent of divine war through mortals."**
- The **Decennium reshapes each Cycle** when the Shardspire activates; horn rule and expedition structure remain (`DungeonGuide.tsx` overview copy).

**Separate later events** (not the original creation, but why the depths matter):

- **Binding of the Old God (666 AE):** An eleventh shard tried to consume the Pantheon; gods imprisoned it at **Level 97** of the Great Descent and forbade contact.
- **Betrayal of the Veil (301 AE):** A relic vanished into the dungeon, **altering its structure permanently**.

### D. Mortal Crucible (post-Shardfall)

After **Shardfall**, Calan'thir observes: **"The Spiral is now tuned to mortals. Our songs alone cannot guide it."** Mortals become **principal authors** of resonance; public choices ripple through the Weave. The **Mortal Crucible** heats — class traditions, concord steel, Matrix of Oblivion counter-proofs. This is the **moral/engine layer** atop the primordial Crucible: mortal choice as cosmic input, not merely looting a cave.

### E. Plain synthesis (one paragraph)

**In canon, the dungeon begins as the Crucible — a First Seal work of Calan'thir and Asmodeus to keep the Old Ones' hush sealed by endless striving (law + song), caging potential through trial rather than imprisoning bodies. Over ages it mirrors the cosmos and receives mortal explorers as the Weave tunes to Aethel. The Shardspire Cycle turns that into a recurring mortal contest: every ten years parties descend the Great Descent; the victor's depth reshapes the world, while deeper myths (Old God at 97, Veil relic) add prison and corruption layers to an structure that was built as seal, engine, and whetstone.**

## 6. Plain answer for the product owner (2–4 sentences)

The dungeon was first created as **the Crucible** — a First Seal work of **Calan'thir** and **Asmodeus** to keep the Old Ones' silence from unravelling the Spiral, by channelling endless striving (law and song) through corridors of trial rather than a prison for the living. On Aethel, the **Shardspire** opens that pattern to mortals every ten years: the **Great Descent** is where expeditions prove themselves, the victor reshapes the next Cycle, and deeper history (the **Old God** at level 97, the **Veil** relic) added prison and corruption to what began as seal and whetstone. After **Shardfall**, the Spiral is explicitly **tuned to mortals** — the Descent is how mortal choice feeds the Weave, not just a loot run. **Owner lock (2026-07-30, GM-secret, refined round 2 same date):** beneath public myth, **Silent Ones — confirmed the same entity as the Old Ones (round 2)** — built the actual machine (the **Heartcell**) with **loot as bait** to be discovered and released; Calan'thir and Asmodeus genuinely, publicly built the Crucible on top of it, discovering/claiming/activating it rather than inventing it ("the Silent Ones built the prison; Calan'thir and Asmodeus taught the world to call it a dungeon"); **The PACT (= the Divine Pact, confirmed round 2)** keeps them silent via **war as dissonance** (Matrix centralized); **peace** risks **Old Ones** awakening to silence the universe — see Owner lock § and [`SILENT_ONES_CANON.md`](../mechanics/SILENT_ONES_CANON.md). **The horn is locked at 1,200 hours/day 50 as the point of no return inside the 100-day opening, and the companion/data layer is aligned to that value** — see [`DESCENT_WINDOW_CANON.md`](../mechanics/DESCENT_WINDOW_CANON.md). **The Matrix/hidden-equation framework is now documented; who built the Shardspire, the Level 97 Old God's relationship to the Silent Ones, and the precise peace-trigger threshold remain protected open questions.**
