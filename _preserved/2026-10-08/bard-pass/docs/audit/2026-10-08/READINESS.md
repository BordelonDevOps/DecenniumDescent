## Decision discipline

Preserve every original unless both the notebook and file audit establish it is obsolete and deletion is useful. Distinguish recovered decisions, consistency repairs and new design. Stage a concrete reviewable result before any final approval request. Do not publish provisional numerical balance as tested fact.
