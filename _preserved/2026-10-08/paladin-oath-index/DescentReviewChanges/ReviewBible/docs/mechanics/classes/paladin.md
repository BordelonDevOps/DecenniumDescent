## Class engine and setup

**Book setup (provisional engine resolves any explicit conflicts below):**

- Main Attribute: Charisma (for auras, Smite, saves, spell DCs)
- Secondary Attributes: Strength or Constitution (based on build)
- Hit Points: High (10 + Constitution modifier at 1st level, 1d10 per level thereafter)
- Armor Proficiency: All armor types and shields
- Weapon Proficiency: All simple and martial weapons
- Tool Proficiency: None

Charisma governs Oath DC/M and divine technique attacks. Weapon attacks use their ordinary Strength/Dexterity ability. Hit Die d10; all armor, shields, simple and martial weapons. Paladin has no Conviction or Oracle Revelation Charges. Lay on Hands is its own pool, 5×Paladin level, fully restored only by long rest. Ordinary spells and printed Smite fuel use the same Paladin Core V 2 RP pool; healing points never convert to RP.

Base progression is explicit rather than the ordinary 25-card template: at 1 automatically gain Lay on Hands, Divine Sense, Martial Proficiency, Divine Strike, Protective Stance and Sacred Touch. At 2 gain Righteous Challenge, Bolstering Presence, Fighting Style and ordinary Paladin spell access. At 3 swear ONE of ten Oaths; gain only its Smite modification, Aura and first Revelation spell. This Oath is the Arch specialization. At 10 enter Hero Oath Mastery of that SAME Oath; no second Oath or additional generic Hero tree. Signature Power unlocks 11, Final Gift 20. Oath Revelations automatically unlock 3/5/9/13/17, always known in addition to the shared spell repertoire, rank 1/2/3/4/5 respectively. Exact printed RP costs replace generic cost only if the card explicitly supplies them.

At 2 choose one Fighting Style: Defense +1 AC while armored; Dueling +2 damage to first one-handed melee hit on your own turn; Protection Reaction impose disadvantage on one attack against an adjacent ally when using a shield. At 3 Divine Health gives advantage on disease saves, not universal immunity. At 5 Extra Attack makes two attacks when taking the ordinary Attack Action; named attack techniques use their own counts. These three features reconcile preserved main-registry entries; other old level-table extras, including extra level 14 ASI and undefined free slot riders, are not stacked into this draft. ASI/feat at 4/8/12/16/19 follows general advancement once.

Divine Strike at 1: after one melee weapon hit on your regular turn, spend 1 RP to add S d6 radiant. At 2 this becomes Divine Smite: choose 1 RP for S d6 or 2 RP for(S+1)d8 radiant, once/own turn total. Oath 3 changes type/rider as its card states, never adds another copy of the base damage. Smite is not an ordinary spell, cannot be counterspelled or generically Overcast, and causes no Dissonance. A critical hit doubles Smite damage dice under ordinary rules, but never healing, resources or percentage copies.

One Oath aura, requires consciousness and no concentration. Radius 10 ft at 3, 15 ft at 6, 20 ft at 10, 30 ft at 15. Benefits affect up to 3 allies or 3 enemies as the Oath card states, chosen at start of your turn and changed next turn; they must stay within aura. At 6 choose one additional aura benefit for that day: allies+1 saves against fear/charm, or 1 flat DR against weapon damage. At 7 once/short rest when enemy damages an ally in aura, Reaction make one melee attack if in reach; may pay normal Smite cost even off turn, but total Smite maximum one per round from this reactive feature. At 10 aura persists until start of your next regular turn after incapacitation; then ends if still incapacitated. At 15 also grant selected allies+1 initiative while in aura when initiative is rolled; no retroactive turn changes.

At 17 Aura Surge once/short rest Bonus Action: until start next turn choose up to 3 allies in aura to gain S+M temporary HP and+1 saves. This bounded surge replaces vague doubling of every aura number. At 20 Divine Manifestation makes the aura active upon initiative without an action, radius 30 ft; it ignores ordinary Silence and terrain obstruction between allies but not antimagic explicitly suppressing divine powers, planar barriers or the consciousness rule. Source cannot-be-suppressed language is bounded explicitly. It grants no free Signature, Smite or Revelation spell.

Protected interface: Smite, Aura and Lay on Hands generate no Weave Dissonance. After a successful direct melee hit, your next Oath spell before end next turn generates 1 less Dissonance, minimum 0, consuming the benefit. No stacking, RP discount or Refrain reset. Oath Revelation spells and the five replacement Forbidden spells below are ordinary spells for strain, countering and concentration; signature powers and final gifts are Oath techniques unless expressly tagged otherwise.

Oath conduct clauses remain intact in source and Oath cards. The Director identifies a relevant clause and a knowingly chosen serious breach before applying a fall. A disagreement, failed roll, coerced act or unknown fact does not silently remove a character. A fall is an explicit story transition recorded with the player: choose the corresponding Oathbreaker inversion or pursue an agreed restoration scene. Preserve class level and already spent resources. Oathbreaker replaces its Oath Aura, Smite rider, Signature and Revelation list, rather than stacking both sets. Base martial abilities remain; Lay on Hands becomes Death Touch using the same current 5 L pool, with no refill on switching.

Death Touch baseline: Action melee divine attack PB+CHA; declare and spend 1 through 2 S+M points before the attack from remaining Lay on Hands pool to deal that much necrotic damage (choose amount before roll; miss still spends declared points). Each Oath inversion may alter type or add its bounded rider; never copy innate traits, confiscate class engines or automatically execute a target. A listed inverted Signature is available only at 11 and replaces the ordinary Signature use, one/short rest. An inverted aura follows the same radius, target cap and timing. Oathbreaker 20 uses the new Black Manifestation common capstone instead of its original Final Gift.

The source names a Forbidden Spell List but does not supply five complete entries for each inversion. This pilot explicitly adds one shared five-spell replacement list (below), with each Oathbreaker using its inversion name as flavor. These are new test rules, not recovered historical spells. Choose the normal Oath package OR inverted package at a time. All later Oath prerequisites still use Paladin class level; the Oathbreaker label does not bypass 3/11/20 gates.

Oath integration limits: one paid Smite total per round, including the level-7 Reaction; one Oath-aura reactive event per round. Multiple damage-redirection options share one event per round unless Crown Undying expressly allows two. Apply original-target defenses once, then split actual damage; transferred damage cannot be mitigated again, reflected, redirected again or generate resources. This explicit rule supersedes wording that permits recipient defenses again.

Sustained Oath spell damage/healing zones use concentration, at most three pulses, and 1 RP upkeep after the first pulse unless a priced card expressly supplies a different finite cadence. Native Oath auras retain their printed passive cadence and target cap. Final auras last their stated finite duration and include their pulses. All hard-denial effects, including compelled Actions, share the common once/source/target/encounter guard; later failures only remove Reactions and reduce Speed by half for one turn.

An emergency Reaction whose trigger itself incapacitates you, such as Grace Reversal or Thorn Crown, may resolve before that incapacitation only if you had a Reaction available before the triggering event. This grants no other actions while incapacitated. Narrative labels such as oppressor require a specific act of captivity/coercion established in the scene, not an alignment or identity assumption.

The source ceiling’s half-pool-per-round Lay on Hands cap is a preserved alternative. This pilot instead uses maximum class level per ordinary Lay on Hands Action and the explicitly once-per-long-rest level-17 Wave that distributes the current remaining pool. No normal heal gains the Wave’s exception.

For any one damage event choose exactly one Paladin redirection feature. Using it consumes the shared redirection allowance for that round. Crown Undying permits two DIFFERENT ally-damage events per round, but still only one redirection feature may modify each event.

**Protected source interface:** Smite, Aura, and Lay on Hands are Oath mechanics and do not generate Weave Dissonance. After a successful melee hit, the next Oath spell before the end of the next turn generates 1 less Dissonance.

## Worked encounter

1. Paladin 5 Mercy, STR 16 CHA 16 PB 3 S 2 M 3: RP 6, Lay on Hands 25, two ordinary Attack Action attacks. Aura radius 10 ft until Paladin 6.
2. Turn 1 Attack Action: first hit permits 2 RP Smite, RP 6→4, adding 3d8 radiant. Second weapon hit receives no second Smite from this activation. Mercy healing uses only its printed bounded rider.
3. Turn 2 Action Lay on Hands spends 5 points to heal 5 HP, pool 25→20; it does not cost RP or add Dissonance. Bonus Action Protective Stance uses its own short-rest allowance; no free attack.
4. Turn 3 ordinary Attack Action hits. This primes the protected 1 D reduction for an Oath spell before end next turn; it does not refund 2 RP already spent or remove existing strain.
5. Turn 4 cast a rank 1 Oath Revelation for its printed 2 RP: RP 4→2 before strain surcharge, base 1 D becomes 0 from the primed hit. If immediately repeating the same spell, its Refrain still contributes after the single 1 D reduction.
6. At encounter end Lay on Hands remains 20 and RP 2. Short rest Retune restores RP by the shared formula up to 6, but never refills healing pool; long rest restores 25.

A complete card specification is not balance approval. Record actual play results, particularly resource exhaustion, condition locks, survival and how often choices become unusable.
