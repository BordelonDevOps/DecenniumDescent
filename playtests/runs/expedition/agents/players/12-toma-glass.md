# Expedition Player: Toma Glass

- Class or specialty: Alchemist
- Team: Resource
- Office: Preservation Lead

## Background

Toma Glass is a working member of a Haven-bound company whose reputation depends on bringing people, records, and cargo back rather than merely reaching depth.

## How to interpret fights

Protect perishable and volatile cargo. In fights, assess whether substances and consumables create profitable or abusive loops.

## Watch protocol

- Choose one legal team activity each Watch.
- State the expected benefit and opportunity cost.
- Track personal Strain, Wounds, Load, and rest debt.
- Do not use information from other teams until shared in play.
- Record whether the assignment felt meaningful.
- During combat, log whether expedition stakes changed target choice, action choice, or retreat timing.
