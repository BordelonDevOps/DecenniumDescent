# Soldier Level 1 build and initial selections

**Status: TEST_ONLY prototype fixture.** This increment combines source-backed character-build and equipment facts with explicit fixture choices. The Soldier card corpus is provisional and not balance-approved. Nothing here promotes content to release.

## Sources and chosen fixture

- Pinned source revision: `46a4d52728ee6956f0bebcb5d2103ffd5bc67ba4`.
- `docs/mechanics/CLASS_PLAYTEST_BUILD_EXAMPLES.md` line 649 gives the Champion level 1 choices: Warning Shot, Tactical Reposition, Shield Trained and Covering Fire. The four satisfy the Soldier schedule's four selections and minimum two active cards. Overwatch is a separate automatic level 1 engine grant.
- The character foundation records a d10 Soldier Hit Die, STR/CON save proficiency, two skill choices from Athletics, Intimidation, Perception and Survival, and the full starting-training profile. This fixture selects Athletics and Perception.
- The optional 27-point-buy method in `docs/mechanics/CHARACTER_CREATION_PLAYTEST.md` assigns a cost of 2 to each score of 10. The six-score-10 fixture therefore records 12 points spent. Point buy is an optional playtest method; ancestry, background, origin and a general character-creation flow are not selected here.
- The reviewed Dungeoneer's Pack candidate contains a 5 lb backpack, crowbar, hammer, ten pitons, ten torches, tinderbox, ten one-day rations, a full waterskin and 50 ft hemp rope. Its authored gross mass is 61.5 lb. Chain mail is 55 lb, AC 16, STR 13 minimum; shield is 6 lb and grants +2 AC while worn; dagger is 1 lb. The Repeating heavy crossbow and bolt ledger reuse the schema22 source-bound records.
- The `Shield Trained` card permits the repeating crossbow with a strapped shield, while reloading still requires an available hand or the repeating mechanism. Its stationary benefit is 1 flat damage reduction if the Soldier moved 0 ft since the start of its last turn; forced movement ends the benefit.
- The equipment procedure assigns distinct don/doff durations to armor and a tactical Action to don/doff a shield. Those procedures are not the generic one-second portable-item handling operation.

## Persisted policy

Schema 27 adds an immutable `CharacterInitialClassSelectionRecord`. It stores the actor, class level, pinned source revision and four selected card identities separately from earned-level milestones. Initial grants use `InitialClassSelection`; automatic Overwatch remains `AutomaticClassFeature`. Schemas 21–26 retain their previous byte layouts.

Schema 28 adds immutable `CharacterInitialLoadoutRecord` provenance and `ShieldStationarityRecord` state. The record ties the selected build to the actual Soldier character, life, skill, save, equipment, crossbow, ammunition, food, water and pack records rather than duplicating runtime inventory. Schema 27 remains byte-compatible; build and stationarity fields are appended only in schema 28.

The fixed fixture chooses six scores of 10, 12 optional point-buy points, current/max HP 10, Athletics and Perception, STR/CON saves, and the level 1 Soldier card set above. The authored kit includes chain mail, shield, dagger, the source-bound Repeating heavy crossbow and twenty bolts, and the complete Dungeoneer's Pack. The pack starts on the ground at `(0, 0)` with its contents and attachments represented as separate physical records: rations, waterskin, water, pitons and tinderbox inside; crowbar, hammer, torches and rope attached. The pack profile keeps the authored 30 lb / 1 cu ft limits and an explicit fit rule; there is no capacity waiver. The inherited two-pound ration and four-pint water fixture stores and actor waterskin are replaced so they do not remain as unmodeled duplicate stock; the separate companion waterskin stays in the physical ledger. Because the pack is not carried at the fixture start, the current Soldier body load is 83.5 lb: 55 chain mail + 6 shield + 1 dagger + 20 crossbow + 1.5 bolt bundle. The fixture's current speed projection applies both the chain-mail STR requirement penalty and the existing burden penalty. Rations use the inherited 30-day freshness estimate; this is a fixture assumption, not a freshness rule in the pack source.

## Connected behavior and limits

- Chain mail and a worn shield feed the shared AC projection used by the admitted melee, ranged and Overwatch attack paths. Chain mail's STR 13 minimum reduces speed by 10 ft when unmet; carrying burden is still calculated separately.
- Shield Trained's strapped-shield exception applies only to the repeating-crossbow attack hand check; generic hand availability continues to count the worn shield. Reloading still requires an actually available loading hand. The magazine starts empty, and the tactical shield-doff completion below is not a reload or attack-hand waiver. A hands-free refill mechanism is not inferred from the Repeating 5 profile. The stationary 1 DR is tracked across positive exploration, tactical, reposition, Press and admitted forced movement. It resets at that actor's next regular start. The damage reduction is applied before the fixture's resistance procedure and before life-state processing on the currently admitted incoming-damage paths.
- Soldier STR/CON save proficiency is added to the admitted STR Push and CON Wound saves. It does not affect death saves.
- Physical item records establish identity, custody, mass and pack placement. They do not make the Dagger, pitons, torches, rope, crowbar, hammer or tinderbox usable/consumable through a general item catalog. The portable layer still aggregates some multi-unit pack contents as a single mass record.
- The selected cards and provenance survive canonical save/restore. Tactical Reposition reuses its existing handler and shared Bonus Action ledger. Shield Trained's passive equipment, hand, movement, AC, damage-reduction and save interactions are connected in the bounded fixture. Warning Shot and Covering Fire remain selected-but-nonexecutable; the Soldier Dagger has no ordinary attack handler here.
- **Codex/GPT consensus completion (prototype rule):** during the Soldier's own tactical turn, a dedicated shield-doff command releases the repeating crossbow's supporting grip as part of the same operation, spends one Action, and changes the shield from worn to carried in its currently occupied hand. The crossbow is then held one-handed in the other hand. The shield's AC and stationary Shield Trained benefit end immediately; its owner, custodian and mass stay unchanged. The command does not stow, drop, reload or fire anything. Both hands remain occupied, so reload and two-handed crossbow attacks remain unavailable until a separate lawful handling step creates an actual free hand. The schema28 portable record now persists `HeldHands` for this shield state, and a tactical history record captures the doff.
- Dedicated tactical shield doff is enabled for this authored Soldier/crossbow fixture. Armor don/doff and generic equipment donning/removal remain `EquipmentDonDoffProcedureUnsupported` for this build rather than charging ordinary one-second handling. An already-doffed shield can be dropped using the existing portable handling operation, which frees its recorded hand; tactical object-interaction limits still apply. No armor-job pause/resume or general shield procedure system is claimed.
- No ancestry, background, general point-buy UI, general skill/save progression, source-driven level-up schedule, Champion level 2 continuation, or complete Soldier class is implemented. A source-pinned loadout does not certify balance, release status, full rules coverage or a playable game.
