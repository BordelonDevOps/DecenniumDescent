# Codex/GPT consensus: Recipe 14 divisibility and recovery

## Question resolved

The pinned Recipe 14 source defines three component occurrences and their quantities, but its recipe role and ingredient names do not establish which inputs may be subdivided or what an ordinary failure recovers. Those outcomes affect stored lot quantities and therefore needed an explicit bounded decision.

## Consensus

- Do not infer divisibility from a material category, role, or recipe field.
- For this prototype only, Recipe 14's five stabilizing-salts units and two distilled-spirits units are measurable loose bulk and divisible to 0.01 of their source quantity unit.
- Treat Recipe 14's black-blood sample as an indivisible catalyst.
- An ordinary `Failed` batch recovers exactly 1.25 salts units and 0.50 spirits units; it recovers no black blood. A mishap still consumes inputs through the existing mishap settlement path.
- Keep this rule attached to the exact Recipe 14 occurrences. It creates no category-wide default and does not alter recipe 4.

The review also confirmed that the recipe uses no source vessels and requires supplementary packaging for its chosen output: four vials for standard yield, five for exceptional yield, and six for masterwork yield. Five is derived from the already used exceptional 25% yield rule; the card directly specifies four standard and six masterwork vials.

These points were accepted as prototype completion rules, not represented as recovered source text. No unresolved decision in this bounded increment required escalation to the owner.
