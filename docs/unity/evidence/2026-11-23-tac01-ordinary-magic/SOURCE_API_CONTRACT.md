## CAL-01: allowance identity, segments and source calendar

One authoritative allowance identity follows the actual budget. Its origin is the actor plus the epoch/local/global owner Start that actually supplied that allowance. Carry into another epoch preserves that origin. A genuine initial carried/bootstrap origin is represented explicitly as carry provenance, never as a fictional local Start or `epoch.Start(-1)`. The admitted casting contexts remain reinforcement, post-recovery and Ferry; pre-reinforcement/bootstrap casting remains unsupported. Recording a bootstrap allowance does not authorize its use as a spellcasting turn. The initial reinforcement companion's off-calendar `LastStart=-1` carry has no invented previous Start, captured six-second deadline or automatic idle entitlement. The six producers remain unavailable on that bootstrap origin; its first actual supported owner Start creates the first eligible accounting opportunity. This does not restrict later genuine origin carry across post-recovery/Ferry admission.

The current epoch, current mode and current activity segment are distinct from allowance origin. A segment records an actual mode/admission boundary, not another Action. Every admitted one of the six operations binds its real command, revision, RuleTime, classification and paid producer record to this context. All costs continue to use the existing WorldInterval/Resonance/Conviction records and transaction rollback. There is no second clock, client interval argument, client activity classification or payment command.

In initiative, admission still requires the actual actor's processed own regular turn. Its one settlement opportunity is consumed at its actual regular End. Outside initiative, an otherwise lawful Action is admitted whenever that same real ordinary allowance is available, including after the short virtual slot End. An uninterrupted outside interval begins at the actual owner Start `s` and ends at the next actual scheduled owner Start `s+6`. It settles at that endpoint before voluntary use of the refreshed allowance. The short `s+2` End in a three-seat calendar remains available for its other established consumers, but does not settle outside magical activity in116.

The current allowance has an explicit settlement status. No-End-row and Dissonance 0 are not evidence of an open opportunity. A completed opportunity remains consumed through all mode changes, carry, saves and restores until a genuinely new allowance is supplied. A remainder segment after settlement can record later legal magic without rewriting completed flags, canceling a reduction or attributing the action to a future turn.

## CAL-03: transitions, carried deadlines and actual Starts

Mode change or epoch admission grants no Action, Bonus Action, Reaction, RP, Conviction, recharge tick or new settlement opportunity. Existing transition records continue to prove actual physical contact, source encounter closure, source ownership, initiative/RNG, mandates, positions and carried spending. Those checks are not relaxed.

Closure after the actor's End preserves consumed status. Closure before its End transfers pending covered activity into outside accounting; another actor's End/closure is not this actor's settlement. Capture the original allowance's next-owner-Start deadline. In same-epoch re-entry, retain the same origin, spending and status. An actual still-upcoming own End can settle the pending opportunity in initiative. Re-entry after the old End has passed does not manufacture an End.

New-epoch admission retains the old origin and original deadline, while recording the new context epoch and admission cause. `LocalStart=-1` in a carried WorldInterval is not a new Start. A pending carried opportunity closes at the earlier of its captured original deadline or an actual first new owner Start that replaces the carried allowance. At an early replacement, no automatic idle reduction; actual paid Grounding still settles PB if no magic; magic settles0. A full captured interval earns the ordinary idle outcome. Consumed opportunities never settle again.

Existing retreat/skipped-Start dispositions govern which Starts actually occur; arithmetic `LatestStart` alone is insufficient to prove refresh.

`PostRecoveryOldEndsSettled`, `FerryOldEndsSettled` and their restore validators branch for116: prove the old accounting disposition is either truthfully consumed or carried with exact deadline/activity/cause, rather than demanding an old short-slot End that116 deliberately does not produce outside.
