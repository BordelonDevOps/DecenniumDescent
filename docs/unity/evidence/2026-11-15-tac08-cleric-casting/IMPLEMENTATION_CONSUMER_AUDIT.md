## History capacity and rollback

The selected inventory-era simulation admits at most512 committed receipts. New cast/effect/Guidance/focus/admission limits are each512; activities allow1024. Each of those records requires an admitted command and cannot outgrow the receipt bound in a supported selected-profile history. Melee history separately limits actual attacks to128. A direct attack can require a WIS admission and a CON save; both are reserved by pure preflight before the first die. The CON history allows2048. Detached capacity checks exercise the boundary without claiming that repeated synthetic save records form a supported snapshot.

With initial3RP, no recovery and ranked base cost2, this cohort can fund at most one ranked spell. A cast adds at most3 Refrain and that single ranked base point. For at most512 cast commands, a conservative bound on recorded magical Ends plus subsequent one-point idle Ends is512 + 3×511 + 1 =2046, below the2048 End-history limit. Grounding removes at least as much per qualifying End; other spent commands reduce the number of possible casts. Spell/Rite/focus activity records can suppress decay but cannot fund a second ranked spell or increase Dissonance. This argument is specific to this admitted profile, not a generic capacity promise for future classes or recovery sources.
