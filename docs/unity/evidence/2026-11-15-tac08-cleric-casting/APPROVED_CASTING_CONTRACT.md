## 4. Shared cast transaction, components and strain

The typed payload `SpellCastRequest` contains source spell ID, exact target/batch ID, optional actual focus item ID, willing-target choice and explicit election of an eligible Focused benefit for this cast. The election selects a source-granted option, never a caller-provided DC. The existing `CastKnownSpell` command routes it; unknown IDs or unrelated payload fields are denied. Caller payloads cannot supply rank, cost, DC, modifiers, duration, dice, source hashes, contamination facts or aura secrets.

Preflight is pure: selected profile, current authority, exact known origin and class/rank access, actual own Action, usable V/S/F, target/perception/range/line of effect, source interaction support, RP cost, pending-state restrictions and history capacity. All six are Action and 30 feet; Detect targets self and separately limits aura perception to 30 feet. Arcane Ward requires sight; the other creature cards say perceive. This profile admits actual sight/self as the supported perception channel and reports other sensory casting unsupported, rather than treating a remembered location as perception. V requires actual ability to speak and no explicit verbal prohibition; S requires a usable hand or the exact focus-only hand allowance. Unmodeled anatomy/silence permissions cannot be invented as overrides.

Preserve actual stance selection before the relevant tactical spell/event. Focused permits an explicit election of +1 save DC for one spell; for Sanctuary consume that election once at its generating cast and retain the resulting DC for all later ward saves. Do not reapply or consume it per attacker. Preserve Focused's Reaction prohibition, including after its benefit is spent, and grant no post-closure stance-selection allowance. No current cohort spell uses a spell attack, so Focused's separate spell-attack option is not an invented attack producer.

Rank/base RP derive from the actor's actual Cleric access: rank0/0 for the three cantrips, rank1/2 for Sanctuary, Purify and earned Detect. Compute spell DC `8 + actual PB + actual Wisdom modifier`; no `max(1, modifier)` copied from Healing Rite. Cast base RP and strain surcharge are distinct from all prior Rite costs. Conviction capacity with unavailable current value cannot fund anything.

On a legal cast, in one transaction candidate: spend the shared Action and actual RP; perform components; displace prior concentration if required; record cast-beginning Dissonance/Refrain once; resolve any actually admitted interruption; apply the exact surviving effect; append chronological provenance and commit the receipt. Illegal preflight returns unchanged bytes, revision, RNG, resources, effects and journals. A legal resisted or negated cast never refunds costs or restores old concentration. No ordinary Counterspell is owned by the admitted roster: the admitted interruption set is empty. Explicitly reject Ready-spell/counterspell requests; do not simulate fake interruption success or claim nested counters implemented.

Cast history records command/revision, actor, known-spell origin, source/procedure hash, actual class level/rank/modifier/PB/DC, owning epoch/local start/global marker, clock, component/focus facts, targets, Action/RP before/paid/after, strain before/base/repeat/after and RNG endpoints. These records derive from execution and replay; they are not caller-authored authority.

Dissonance uses the pinned shared integration rule: limit `max(4, 2 + PB + max(0, Wisdom modifier))`; pre-cast ranked surcharge +1 at limit, +2 at limit+3, +3 at limit+6, with ordinary ranked casting blocked at limit+6. Rank0 has no RP surcharge and remains subject to Refrain. Base additions are0 at rank0 and1 at the admitted rank1. Consecutive same global spell identity adds0/1/2/3, capped at3; another named spell resets the repeat counter, not accumulated strain. Idle/weapon turns do not reset the name/counter. Alias placements do not create new identities.

**P4 — regular-turn accounting.** Persist whether a spell or magical class ability was actually used in the actor's current owning regular interval. Existing Healing Rite/Radiance count as magical class activity but add no automatic spell strain and do not reset Refrain. Derive skipped idle intervals from the shared calendar; prove the arithmetic equivalent without replaying unearned events. Preserve history across closure and calendar transitions. No Retune, long-rest RP recovery, meditation or generic strain-reset command is added here. Source text for those is retained as unsupported capability. ## 5. Shared effect and concentration lifecycle

Use typed persisted effect instances tied to a real generating cast, source/rank, caster/target, started time, actual expiry rule, remaining/present state and terminal reason. Effects are not generic condition names added to the condition journal. They reuse its real permission/consequence outputs and shared clock. One concentration slot per actor is shared across all admitted concentration sources; Sanctuary and Detect are its initial real producers. A new legal concentration cast ends the old at cast start before the new effect resolves. Ending an effect does not refund its cast or recreate another.

Absolute60/600-second deadlines use `RuleTime` and split the existing advance loop. Arcane Ward stores the actual caster's next regular Start in its owning epoch; do not approximate it by a fixed duration. At a boundary process already-due source endings, existing mandatory condition/life events and real regular markers in a deterministic documented order before allowing a new Action. A timed-out effect cannot influence a roll at that endpoint. Preserve existing repeat-before-expiry behavior for Frightened. Calendar replacement must map remaining caster-relative endpoints through the actual admitted transition; no restart or duplicate expiry.

**P5 — concentration damage ordering.** For each actual positive resolved damage packet after ordinary mitigation (including damage absorbed by temporary HP), complete that packet's existing life/Wound/knockout settlement first. If it immediately kills or incapacitates the caster, end concentration with that cause and do not draw an unnecessary concentration die. Otherwise draw one actual CON save, DC `max(10, floor(resolved damage / 2))`, using current shared save facts and condition clauses, after that packet's existing dice. Zero resolved damage prompts no save. One packet is not divided into an HP-loss save and a temporary-HP save. Pending knockout resolution retains the same damage identity and cannot cause a duplicate save. Save failure ends concentration; success retains its original expiry. This damage basis/order is the explicit approved integration agreement, not an inference from current HP differences.

Actual `EndConcentration` consequences, zero-HP Unconscious/Dead and any admitted loss of concentration permission end the slot immediately. Suppressing or later curing incapacity never resurrects ended concentration. Add an owner-authorized `EndConcentration` command with no resource gain; no target can end someone else's concentration by submitting its ID. All dependent effects end once and retain their cause/provenance. Querying effects never consumes RNG or creates saves.
