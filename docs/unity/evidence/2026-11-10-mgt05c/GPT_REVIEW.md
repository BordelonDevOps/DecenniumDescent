## Decisions adopted from GPT’s review

- **Strict opening window:** Let `O` be the qualifying `Open` handling completion and `D = O + 60 seconds`. A `Close` preserves protection only if its handling completion `K < D`. The open and close commands each take their existing one-second exploration interval; opening becomes effective at completion and the closing second counts as open. If closure has not completed strictly before `D`, protection ends prospectively at `D`; protection already experienced in `[O,D)` remains earned. No retroactive loss or later restoration.
- **Natural expiry and events:** Treatment coverage is `[C, E)`, with `E = CompletedAt + 86,400 seconds`. An actual breach, contamination, or incompatible-heat event ends coverage for then-covered vessel contents at its actual effective `RuleClock` time with no grace period. It is distinct from ordinary opening. Exact `RuleTime` values and existing committed command order apply.
- **Portions and physical removal:** One available, unreserved portion may be made in-place inside the vessel during a qualifying opening, using `OrdinaryInventory.Descendant` with its existing zero-duration split semantics. A new immutable association binds the opening evidence to the actual child and causal portion command/receipt. The child continues receiving the original treatment while physically covered in the vessel; successful retrieval permanently ends its future protection at `Retrieve.EndedAt`. Retain earlier earned protection and origin. Combine inherited and evidence-backed continuation intervals by union. Re-insertion cannot resume the treatment.
- **Clean-tool authority:** The existing physical Cook’s-utensils item may qualify through an explicit trusted TEST_ONLY cleanliness fact for this scenario. It is not inferred from name, owner, skill, or presence; the player cannot supply a free `clean=true`. No cleaning action, check, cost, or work interval is added.
- **Exposure authority and persistence:** Record actual exposure through a trusted TEST_ONLY occurrence adapter with stable semantic occurrence identity, typed cause, treatment/vessel scope, current shared-clock time, and causal order. No arbitrary caller backdating, unknown occurrence, free environmental assertion, duplicate event, mutable age, stored protection Boolean, or second timer. Saves preserve opening, portion, and exposure evidence; retries must be once-only and deterministic.
- **Version and mode:** Use an explicit schema76 TEST_ONLY profile with an appended evidence section. Preserve schemas74 and75 bytes, fingerprints, validation, profiles, and runtime behavior. New controlled opening, portion/retrieval, and fixture exposure are exploration-only; preserve unrelated tactical handling and reject unsupported tactical paths/mode transitions.
- **Acceptance:** GPT requires the existing fish/vessel fixture, real conserved split, save between split and retrieval, exact timeout/expiry boundaries, actual exposure, deterministic save/restore/replay, whole versus partitioned advances, duplicate-event checks, and unchanged schema74/75 behavior. Optional randomized stress coverage is not a gate. Re-treatment, recursive protected splitting, repackaging, production recipes, other materials, general environmental simulation, and Unity are deferred.
