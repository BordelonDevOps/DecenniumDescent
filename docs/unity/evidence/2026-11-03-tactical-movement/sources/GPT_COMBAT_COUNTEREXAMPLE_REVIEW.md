The periodic-family package can remain closed at its bounded scope. For this combat pass, the counterexamples expose several places where the first proposal needs refinement before implementation. The following is the settled candidate I would use.

## 1. Movement: use spent movement cost, not distance

**ACCEPT.** The V6 “subtract distance already moved” wording breaks once difficult terrain, standing, squeezing, crawling, and multiple movement modes interact. Pasted text

### NEW — normal-turn movement ledger

Track:

`spentMovementCost`

rather than only physical distance.

For each movement mode:

`availableModeBudget = (1 + legal Dash actions taken this turn) × current Speed for that mode`

`remainingModeBudget = max(0, availableModeBudget - spentMovementCost)`

Every voluntary movement expenditure adds its actual movement cost to the single ledger.

Examples:

- Walk 10 feet through difficult terrain → spend 20.
- Switch to Fly 30 → 10 feet of fly budget remain, **not 20**.
- Walk Speed 30, stand from Prone → spend 15.
- Then switch to Fly 30 → 15 remain.
- Dash once with Fly 30 → cap becomes 60; if 20 already spent, 40 remains.
- A second legal Dash, such as through the one-extra-Action allowance, raises that mode's cap to 90.

### Mid-turn Speed changes

Recalculate each mode's cap from its **current Speed**.

Already spent cost is never refunded.

Example:

- Speed 30, spend 20.
- Speed rises to 40 → 20 remains.
- Speed falls to 15 → remaining becomes 0.
- Later restoring Speed to 30 restores 10 remaining, not the already spent 20.

This is not retroactive movement reimbursement; it is recalculating the current cap against permanent expenditure.

### Special movement grants

Ready movement, Withdraw, Interpose and Press use **separate granted movement allowances**, but terrain/movement costs still apply to those allowances.

They do **not** consume or replenish normal-turn `spentMovementCost` unless their source expressly says they use normal movement.

Each has its own temporary:

`specialMovementBudget`

Examples:

- Interpose grants **5 feet of movement budget**.
- In ordinary terrain, that moves one 5-foot space.
- In difficult terrain, entering a 5-foot space costs 10, so a 5-foot Interpose budget cannot traverse it.
- Press behaves the same.
- Ready movement grants a budget equal to current Speed at release.
- Withdraw grants half Speed as its own budget.

Speed 0 prevents these voluntary movement grants from producing movement.

Teleportation and forced movement do not spend these voluntary movement ledgers.

## 2. Pin / Silence the Caster need a real tradeoff

**AGREE. Use `-2 attack roll`, not reduced damage.**

A damage reduction is awkward because:

- it scales inconsistently by weapon;
- it still makes the maneuver free on low-damage attacks;
- it interacts badly with crits;
- it can become irrelevant on riders.

A flat **-2 accuracy cost** is transparent, cannot be erased merely because disadvantage already exists, and creates a meaningful cost without importing a new resource system.

### NEW — optional maneuver declaration

For **Pin** or **Silence the Caster**:

- declare before the attack roll;
- attack roll takes `-2`;
- one attempt of that maneuver per **regular turn**;
- an attack can carry only one optional tactical maneuver unless a feature explicitly says otherwise;
- extra turns do not replenish these attempt limits.

### Pin

On a successful qualifying ranged attack:

- apply one ordinary Pin state;
- Speed `-10 ft`, minimum 0;
- multiple Pin source records do **not** stack Speed penalties.

Keep individual source records only for ownership/DC/ending metadata.

Effective Pin DC = **highest active ordinary Pin DC**.

At the start of each regular target turn, target may make one STR (Athletics) check against that effective DC. Success ends **all ordinary Pin records**.

Alternatively, spending one Action ends all ordinary Pin records automatically.

A specific card Pin may replace these generic endpoints.

This avoids `-10/-20/-30` stacking and multiple removal Actions.

### Silence the Caster

Declare before one melee attack; `-2` attack.

On hit:

- verbal-component Reactions are unavailable until start of target's next regular turn;
- if target is concentrating, resolve **exactly one** concentration save for that hit.

Concentration DC:

`max(10, floor(actual post-mitigation damage / 2))`

If actual damage is **0**, this maneuver still forces one DC 10 CON concentration save.

If damage is positive, this maneuver **replaces/fulfills** the ordinary concentration check generated by that damage. Never roll both.

That DC-10-at-zero case is the maneuver's substantive advantage beyond an ordinary melee hit.

## 3. Start-of-turn death / recovery ordering

This needs a fixed priority layer.

### NEW — regular-turn start sequence

At the start of every regular initiative slot:

1. **Expire effects whose endpoint is “start of this turn.”**
2. Apply immediate state changes caused by those expirations.
3. Refresh the creature's Reaction.
4. Tick regular-turn recharge counters and other non-action clocks explicitly keyed to start of regular turn.
5. Resolve any **already-due absolute state-recovery event**, such as Stable recovery, if it is now legally able to resolve.
6. If alive, dying, and at 0 HP, make the required **death save**.
7. Resolve other start-of-turn effects and periodic events in deterministic application order.
8. Begin voluntary turn decisions.

### Simultaneous start-turn effects

For step 7, no player chooses a favorable order.

Use:

1. earlier application timestamp first;
2. if created simultaneously by one source, use that source's printed internal order;
3. otherwise use stable application sequence;
4. mechanical/source ID only as final technical tie-break.

Do not sort beneficial before harmful or vice versa.

### Examples

**Dying at 0 + start-turn healing:**  
Death save occurs first. The ordinary start-turn healing then resolves.

**Stable at 0 + start-turn damage:**  
No death save because Stable. Damage then resolves; if it reaches HP normally, Stable ends and the closed at-zero damage/death rules apply.

**Natural 20 death save + Bleed:**  
Natural 20 restores 1 HP and clears counters. Then start-turn Bleed hits. If it drops the creature from 1 to 0, it is a newly-to-zero event, not “damage while already at zero.”

**Decay expires at start + Stable recovery already due:**  
Decay expires in step 1. At step 5 the previously blocked Stable recovery is now legal and restores its one HP. No new timer is rolled.

If Stable recovery is due but still legally blocked, it remains a single `dueBlocked` recovery event. It does not reschedule itself.

## 4. Hide / Search / passive Perception

You caught the contradiction. Use the closed four-degree task rules consistently.

### Passive Perception

For this pilot:

`Passive Perception DC = 10 + Wisdom (Perception) modifier`

including proficiency if applicable.

Do **not** convert advantage/disadvantage into passive ±5 or any other passive adjustment unless a reviewed DD source later defines that.

Advantage/disadvantage applies to an actual Search roll normally.

### Hide

Roll DEX (Stealth) **once**.

Record:

- natural die;
- final total.

Against each observer separately, treat that observer's Passive Perception as a **fixed DC**.

Use the closed task-degree system, including natural shifts.

So:

- total = DC → Success before natural adjustment;
- natural 1 worsens one degree;
- natural 20 improves one degree.

A single Hide roll can therefore produce different outcome degrees against different observers.

Only **Success or Critical Success** establishes Hidden from that observer.

### Hidden definition

For this integration, Hidden from an observer means:

- that observer cannot currently see you; and
- that observer does not currently know your location.

If the observer later learns your location but still cannot see you, **Hidden ends for that observer**, but you remain unseen.

That distinction matters for the already-reviewed Invisible rule.

### Search

Search against a known Hidden creature's recorded Stealth result uses that recorded final Stealth result as a **static DC**.

Roll Perception/Investigation as appropriate under the four-degree task system.

Meeting the DC is Success before natural adjustment.

Success reveals **location only** unless another sense/rule also grants sight.

It ends Hidden **only for that searcher**.

### Anti-fishing — NEW

A creature that failed to Hide against a particular observer cannot simply repeat Hide from the same position/circumstances to reroll the same task.

A new Hide attempt against that observer requires a materially changed situation, such as:

- movement to another hiding position;
- newly created concealment;
- the observer becoming distracted;
- broken sight by a new intervening event;
- an explicit feature allowing a reroll.

Search itself already costs an Action and may be attempted again on later turns; no additional generic prohibition is required.

## 5. Duration anchors

Your virtual-anchor proposal is the cleaner model.

### NEW — marker preservation

When an initiative-based effect is created, record its relevant **virtual initiative marker**.

Existing effects keep that marker until they end.

Initiative reorder, reroll, delayed entry, extra turns, or leaving/re-entering initiative **do not move that marker**.

The marker continues virtually every six seconds if the creature is no longer acting at that slot.

New effects created after an initiative change use the then-current slot.

### Hostile target-bound bare `N rounds`

If no other endpoint exists:

- record target's current regular-turn **end marker**;
- count the next `N` future occurrences strictly after application;
- effect ends at the Nth such virtual marker.

If applied during target's current turn, that current turn end **does not count**.

### Self/source bare `N rounds`

Use source's regular-turn **start marker**.

Ends at the Nth future start marker strictly after application.

Thus source activates a 3-round self effect during turn 1:

- next source-start = 1;
- next = 2;
- next = 3 → expires, corresponding to start of turn 4.

### Explicit next-turn endpoints

- `until end of target's next turn` → next target-end marker strictly after application.
- `until start of target's next turn` → next target-start marker strictly after application.
- unqualified `next turn` retains the existing shared-rule interpretation of source/user unless the card identifies target.

### Skipped turns

A regular marker still occurs even if the creature is:

- surprised;
- Stunned;
- Unconscious;
- Action-denied;
- otherwise unable to act.

The duration advances.

### Source death/removal

A timed effect does **not** automatically end because its source dies or leaves unless it is:

- concentration;
- an aura requiring its owner;
- a tether requiring range;
- or the source explicitly says so.

### Leaving initiative

Do not convert remaining markers using “time since last turn.”

Keep the virtual marker repeating every six elapsed seconds from its original schedule until all associated effects resolve.

### Outside-initiative application

An effect applied outside initiative for `N rounds` gets an absolute:

`applicationTime + N × 6 seconds`

deadline unless its source explicitly requires turns/pulses.

If initiative begins before that deadline, **keep the absolute deadline**. Do not re-anchor.

### Periodic profiles

Yes: the already-closed explicit periodic definitions retain their registered target-marker clocks and are not superseded by this generic duration model.

## 6. Ready: spell holding and pending-state limits

**AGREE.**

### One Ready state

A creature can maintain only **one pending Ready**.

Starting a new Ready cancels the earlier one.

If the prior Ready held a spell, its paid costs remain spent.

### What can be Readied

Only an action that can legally be taken as an **Action** may be Readied.

Therefore generic Ready cannot prepare:

- Bonus Action abilities;
- Reaction abilities;
- multi-minute rituals;
- long activities.

### Readied spell preparation

At preparation:

- choose the spell;
- verify it is currently known/prepared/legal;
- pay RP/material/resource costs;
- provide components;
- apply Dissonance;
- apply Refrain;
- trigger “when you cast” effects;
- create one held-spell concentration state.

Counterspell and similar cast-interruption opportunities occur **now**, because this is the actual casting event.

There is no second cast at release.

### All readied spells require holding concentration

Even if the spell normally does not require concentration.

Starting the held spell ends any incompatible existing concentration.

If holding concentration is lost, the Ready spell is lost.

### Target selection

**NEW:** spell/mode is chosen at preparation; legal target/area point is selected at release.

At release recheck:

- target existence/type;
- range;
- line of effect;
- perception/sight requirement;
- current target immunity/legality where relevant.

Do not repay casting costs.

If no legal target exists, the spell simply remains held until a later matching trigger or Ready expiry.

### On release

If the spell normally requires concentration, the held concentration continues seamlessly as the spell's normal concentration.

If it normally does **not** require concentration, the holding concentration ends immediately after the spell resolves.

### Expiry

Ready expires at the start of the creature's next regular turn.

A held spell that expires is lost. No refund.

### Attack Action

Previously agreed:

A readied Attack Action uses the creature's legal ordinary Attack Action attack count.

Own-turn-only riders remain unavailable off-turn.

## 7. Press / Interpose / Brace / Rally

### Press

Use one arming allowance per **regular-turn interval**.

On a qualifying melee hit during your regular turn, you may arm Press against that target.

If unconsumed, a later qualifying melee hit during that same regular turn may replace the target.

Once Press has triggered/been consumed, it **cannot be armed again until your next regular turn**, even if you gain an extra turn.

Extra-turn attacks do not refresh or create another Press allowance.

### Interpose

Core says “move 5 ft.” I recommend the explicit **NEW interpretation “move up to 5 ft.”** because otherwise a creature already correctly positioned would bizarrely need to leave its square to protect an adjacent ally. Pasted text

Thus:

- 0–5 feet permitted;
- if already in a legal protecting position, movement may be 0;
- Speed 0 does not prevent Interpose if no movement is required;
- if repositioning is required, Speed 0 prevents that voluntary movement;
- any movement uses the separate 5-foot special budget and pays terrain costs.

### Brace: Hold Ground

Narrow it.

It applies only to a **discrete forced displacement event** such as:

- shove;
- push;
- pull;
- knockback.

It does not apply to:

- gravity;
- Falling Steps;
- continuous current;
- conveyor movement;
- collapsing terrain carrying the creature;
- teleportation.

It reduces only displacement distance.

It never reduces damage or grants hovering/support.

### Brace: Intercept

Still requires:

- valid Reaction;
- legal melee weapon attack;
- target actually entering the selected breach/reach;
- legal reach/line.

No free attack outside those conditions.

### Rally

Choose **one specific active Frightened source record** before rolling.

Use that source DC if present, otherwise DC 10.

Success ends only that selected Frightened record.

It does not wipe all simultaneous fear sources.

## 8. Stances and extra turns

**AGREE. NEW regular-turn clarification.**

Standard stance choice occurs only during the creature's **regular turn**.

An extra turn:

- preserves the currently selected stance;
- does not allow choosing a new stance;
- does not reset a spent Mobile first-5-foot allowance;
- does not reset Focused's one-spell benefit;
- does not reset any other once-per-stance allowance.

The stance still ends at the original next regular-start marker.

Examples:

- Focused bonus already spent → extra turn retains reaction restriction but receives no new +1 spell bonus.
- Mobile first 5 feet already used → extra turn does not gain another protected 5 feet.
- Aggressive/Guarded/Carrying remain continuously active until normal expiry.

### Extra full Action

Keep the existing separate rule:

> maximum one extra full Action per creature per **numbered round**.

That cap is global-round based and is intentionally separate from Reaction's own-turn interval.

An extra Action:

- gives no extra BA;
- gives no Reaction;
- cannot grant/refresh another extra Action.

Paid movement or action effects likewise do not recursively manufacture another grant unless expressly permitted.
