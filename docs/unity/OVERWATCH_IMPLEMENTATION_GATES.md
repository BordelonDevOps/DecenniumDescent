## Required implementation boundary

The class profile states that the Soldier spends a Bonus Action to designate a visible 15-foot square within weapon range until the start of the next regular turn. Once during that interval, the Soldier may spend its Reaction when a perceived enemy enters or moves inside the zone, attacks an ally from inside it, or reveals itself inside it. The response is one legal ranged attack with ammunition and line of fire. The profile specifies concealment still applies, then permits a hit rider: Snare (Speed −10 feet through the end of the target's next turn), Bleed (1d4 at the start of its next turn), or Push (5 feet, STR save negates the push). The profile limits the Soldier to one zone and grants no extra Reaction. At level 17 it adds a separate once-per-round 5-foot post-shot movement benefit.

This feature cannot be wired safely by adding a trigger flag to the existing Club attacker. Schema22 now provides a source-bound ordinary crossbow transaction ([integration record](REPEATING_CROSSBOW_COMBAT_RULE_DECISIONS.md)): it validates its bounded fixture's actual weapon custody/hands and bolt lots, charges the ordinary Action, applies damage/life consequences and persists/replays the result. It deliberately does not spend Reactions or connect attacks to movement events. Its authored lane supplies visibility and line-of-fire facts; it does not derive terrain sight or calculate a zone. `PlanarCombatGeometry` provides range and cover only, while `ConditionCombatPolicy.CanSee` accounts for condition awareness/invisibility but not spatial visibility or line of fire.

## Existing implementation limits

- `PlanarCombatGeometry` handles planar distance, ranged normal/maximum range, best listed cover and one corner-step rule. It does not perform visibility, line-of-effect, occupied-volume or weapon-legality checks.
- `TacticalMovementPolicy` implements bounded voluntary movement, opportunity-attack offers and Press. Schema26 adds only the scoped post-step Overwatch/Press order and completed-step trigger. Other reaction types retain their own policies.
- `TestOnlyClubAttackPolicy` resolves bounded Club and ordinary unarmed melee attacks. Schema22 supplies the ordinary ranged transaction; schema26 composes one Reaction-costed Overwatch shot using the source-specific d8 and shared ammunition. General weapons, geometry and reactions remain incomplete.
- Schema23 `PeriodicRiderRecord` composes the registered `soldier.overwatch.bleed` profile with the shared world calendar; schema26 routes an eligible Overwatch hit into that profile. The profile's one-per-group arbitration is scoped to one target marker; distinct target applications are separate records. It is not generic Bleeding. Ordinary-remover invocation remains unimplemented.
- `ConditionCombatPolicy` uses condition-based awareness and invisibility for its current melee fixture. It does not model terrain sight or general concealment geometry. Schema26 supplies fixed visibility and firing-lane facts and excludes Staggered, automatic reveal transitions and non-cardinal Push geometry.

## Intended implementation sequence

1. **Implemented in schema24: shared Bonus Action budget and feature-grant origin.** One actor has one available Bonus Action per current regular interval. Tactical Reposition consumes the shared record while its Recharge and movement budget remain separate. The automatic Soldier 1 Overwatch grant is distinguished from selected cards and does not satisfy the four Base selections / two-active minimum. Schema21-23 validation, persistence and replay remain versioned unchanged. See [BONUS_ACTION_RULE_DECISIONS.md](BONUS_ACTION_RULE_DECISIONS.md).
2. **Implemented in schema25-26: bounded Overwatch execution.** Designation/expiry, interval-owned entitlement, completed-movement trigger, saved response, shared Reaction and ammunition spending, d8 shot, rider continuation, all three riders, OA and Press ordering, movement interruption and versioned replay are covered by [OVERWATCH_RULE_DECISIONS.md](OVERWATCH_RULE_DECISIONS.md) and the schema26 evidence.
3. **Next character dependency:** integrate automatic Overwatch into a source-valid Soldier 1 loadout only after all scheduled card choices are represented; then generalize advancement from explicit schedules. Ordinary condition removal needs its own source-connected remover before adding commands.
4. Expand only from new source-backed actor, weapon, visibility, trigger and geometry profiles. Do not infer general spatial queries, attack-from-zone effects, reveal transitions or reaction behavior from this single fixed fixture.

Each stage remains TEST_ONLY until its scope is source-bound, replayable, and independently reviewed. It does not complete all classes, all ranged weapons, the tactical system, or the full three-layer engine.
