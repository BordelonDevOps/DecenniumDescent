## Required implementation boundary

The class profile states that the Soldier spends a Bonus Action to designate a visible 15-foot square within weapon range until the start of the next regular turn. Once during that interval, the Soldier may spend its Reaction when a perceived enemy enters or moves inside the zone, attacks an ally from inside it, or reveals itself inside it. The response is one legal ranged attack with ammunition and line of fire. The profile specifies concealment still applies, then permits a hit rider: Snare (Speed −10 feet through the end of the target's next turn), Bleed (1d4 at the start of its next turn), or Push (5 feet, STR save negates the push). The profile limits the Soldier to one zone and grants no extra Reaction. At level 17 it adds a separate once-per-round 5-foot post-shot movement benefit.

This feature cannot be wired safely by adding a trigger flag to the existing Club attacker. Schema22 now provides a source-bound ordinary crossbow transaction ([integration record](REPEATING_CROSSBOW_COMBAT_RULE_DECISIONS.md)): it validates its bounded fixture's actual weapon custody/hands and bolt lots, charges the ordinary Action, applies damage/life consequences and persists/replays the result. It deliberately does not spend Reactions or connect attacks to movement events. Its authored lane supplies visibility and line-of-fire facts; it does not derive terrain sight or calculate a zone. `PlanarCombatGeometry` provides range and cover only, while `ConditionCombatPolicy.CanSee` accounts for condition awareness/invisibility but not spatial visibility or line of fire.

## Existing implementation limits

- `PlanarCombatGeometry` handles planar distance, ranged normal/maximum range, best listed cover and one corner-step rule. It does not perform visibility, line-of-effect, occupied-volume or weapon-legality checks.
- `TacticalMovementPolicy` implements bounded voluntary movement, opportunity-attack offers and Press. Cross-type ordering is settled only for the scoped post-step Overwatch/Press window in [OVERWATCH_RULE_DECISIONS.md](OVERWATCH_RULE_DECISIONS.md); attack and reveal triggers remain unconnected.
- `TestOnlyClubAttackPolicy` resolves bounded Club and ordinary unarmed melee attacks. `RangedAttackRules`, `RepeatingFiveMagazine` and `RepeatingCrossbowShotRules` are composed by schema22 into one ordinary ranged command path with Action charge, fixture-scoped custody/hands, actual bolt lot spending, damage/life resolution and persistence. The general weapon, geometry and reaction systems remain incomplete.
- Schema23 `PeriodicRiderRecord` composes the registered `soldier.overwatch.bleed` profile with the shared world calendar and simulation snapshot. The profile's one-per-group arbitration is scoped to one target marker; distinct target applications are separate records. It is not generic Bleeding or an Overwatch command. The fixed event fixture explicitly supplies an upstream event; ordinary-remover invocation and Overwatch attack/trigger execution remain unimplemented.
- `ConditionCombatPolicy` uses condition-based awareness and invisibility for its current melee fixture. It does not model terrain sight, concealment geometry, scheduled Bleed damage or an Overwatch Push save. The first Overwatch fixture must supply known zone visibility and firing-lane facts and exclude Staggered and reachable reveal transitions.

## Intended implementation sequence

1. **Implemented in schema24: shared Bonus Action budget and feature-grant origin.** One actor has one available Bonus Action per current regular interval. Tactical Reposition consumes the shared record while its Recharge and movement budget remain separate. The automatic Soldier 1 Overwatch grant is distinguished from selected cards and does not satisfy the four Base selections / two-active minimum. Schema21-23 validation, persistence and replay remain versioned unchanged. See [BONUS_ACTION_RULE_DECISIONS.md](BONUS_ACTION_RULE_DECISIONS.md).
2. **Next schema25 boundary:** Persist Overwatch designation, the fixed zone, its regular-turn interval, trigger identity, pending response and post-step occurrence order; connect only completed movement steps to the existing tactical flow and Reaction ledger.
3. Extend the schema22 ranged transaction with a Reaction-costed Overwatch response using the settled `1d8 + DEX` expression, preserving ordinary own-turn `1d10 + DEX` behavior. Keep actual weapon, hands, range, perception, line of fire and ammunition prerequisites.
4. Persist the exactly-one rider continuation and complete the set: source-qualified Snare timing/speed, the implemented registered Bleed tick/MEDICINE/healing subset, and Push save/displacement. Connect ordinary condition removal only with a source-qualified remover. Enforce unsupported attack/reveal triggers before payment and reject unsupported geometry before payment.
5. Add trigger-identity duplication, competing Press, OA-prevented movement, save/restore at each pending state, stale response, rider life/cure/expiry, Push success/failure/blocker and no-refund regressions. Later integrate the automatic level-1 grant into a source-valid Soldier 1 build only when all scheduled card choices are represented.

Each stage remains TEST_ONLY until its scope is source-bound, replayable, and independently reviewed. It does not complete all classes, all ranged weapons, the tactical system, or the full three-layer engine.
