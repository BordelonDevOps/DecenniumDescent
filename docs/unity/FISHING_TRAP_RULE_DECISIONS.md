# Unattended Trap implementation decisions

**Status:** schema38 TEST_ONLY C# fixture; source behavior is reviewed PLAYTEST material. This record distinguishes sourced procedure from fixture facts and implementation completion rules.

## Source-supported procedure

The pinned [Fishing operational playtest](evidence/2026-11-05-fishing-trap/sources/FISHING_RECIPE_PLAYTEST.md) requires a real finite site opportunity, candidate creature, complete physical capture assembly, recorded support and receiving arrangement. Setup, exposure, opportunity commitment, bait spending, passive soak, check, catch retention and recovery remain separate checkpoints. An unattended Trap uses five active minutes of setup, fifty continuous deployed minutes, one check at minute 55, then five active retrieval minutes. Retrieval requires a capable worker, both hands, legal access/path and enough capacity for the actual Trap and contents. Retrieval may be performed by someone other than the setter. Early retrieval cancels the pending check without catching; abandonment spends neither committed opportunity nor exposed bait back into stock and preserves actual physical dispositions.

The source supplies a 24 × 12 × 12 inch Trap body, 22 × 10 × 10 inch usable interior, 4 × 4 inch admission funnel, 8 × 8 inch service opening, one 10 pound / 1 cubic foot catch compartment, a separate 0.10 pound bait holder and a 20 foot recovery cord. It requires the actual prey to enter and fit. It does not supply a general target-selection, stock-renewal or Unity implementation.

## Deterministic implementation contract

- Schema35 remains the old no-bait attempt projection; schema36 remains the bait and timing projection; schema37 adds the physical stock member, Trap body, supported receiving location and retrieval records. Schema38 appends versioned finite stock manifests and opportunity allocation membership without changing older wire layouts.
- Schema37 can initialize multiple independently supplied profiles in one TEST_ONLY snapshot. The current witness covers two distinct sites, setters, opportunities, creatures, bait lots, Traps, supports, receivers and stock-version identities; each profile is resolved sequentially and survives save/restore. Profile order does not affect the content fingerprint.
- A separate contention witness points two distinct opportunities and stock-version labels at the same physical creature identity at one supplied site. At exposure, accepted command sequence determines which response reserves that body; the other committed opportunity spends its bait and resolves as `NoResponse`, without a replacement draw. Its empty Trap remains deployed and must still be physically retrieved. This is an explicit result of allocating at most one response from finite actual stock, not an additional catch check.
- Schema38 stores the shared stock identity, version identity, effective interval, canonical physical member IDs, and each opportunity's explicit member allocation. A whole-pool allocation must enumerate the complete manifest; candidates outside the allocation, mismatched versions, and versions not active at the exposure checkpoint are rejected. Duplicate labels do not hide shared IDs: the response lock still applies to the one actual creature body.
- The schema38 fixtures cover two distinct physical members partitioned across separate opportunities and two overlapping labels naming one shared member. This establishes explicit singleton allocations and same-site overlap detection only; multi-candidate weighting and general cross-site access remain outside this increment.
- The fixture has one opportunity and one 1 pound, 0.1 cubic foot ordinary fish; one unenchanted 4 pound Trap; one 0.10 pound compatible ordinary bait portion; one receiving platform; and no equipment bonus. These are fixture facts, not whole-game defaults. The supplying setter identity, site/Trap/receiver IDs, check DC and proficiency profile are fixture inputs; the setter's own WIS/proficiency is snapshotted and another capable actor may retrieve.
- Setup reserves the existing opportunity, candidate body, Trap and bait before work. At completed setup / first exposure the fixture spends bait and opportunity, records the setter's WIS/proficiency, reserves the same creature response, and deploys the physical Trap. A check remains anchored to attempt start + 55 minutes. Late or interrupted setup cannot restart that deadline.
- A successful check captures the same `FishingCreatureBodyRecord` in the Trap and records its recovery evaluation at resolution. It does not mint an inventory lot. A failed check releases the candidate response without refunding the spent opportunity or bait.
- Retrieval reserves one supported receiver before work. The receiver must be at the same site, have an intact support, enough authored mass/volume capacity, and an authored route no longer than the Trap's recovery cord. The worker must be capable, have two free hands, be idle and adjacent to the Trap. The bounded job needs 300 active seconds; pausing, save/load and worker handoff retain completed labor and the destination reservation. Completion moves the existing Trap and, if still captured, the same creature record into the receiving location and updates custody.
- At an exact due-check / retrieval-completion timestamp, resolve the fishing check first, then retrieval. Retrieval completed before the due check ends the attempt as `RetrievedBeforeCheck`; the committed opportunity and exposed bait remain spent, and no check consumes RNG.
- If the setter abandons a Trap while its retrieval is actively working, pause the retrieval and keep its receiver reservation. Its existing labor may resume later. The source says to preserve actual physical state but does not define this simultaneous-operation case; this is a TEST_ONLY completion policy.

## MGT-06 responsibility handover profile

**Status:** GPT accepted the bounded schema38 TEST_ONLY responsibility-handover implementation at code commit `9f8478b88399fae8fc8f4818f7518718d16afa20`, with retained evidence/docs at `dc89e73ed3acb211951e311988e12c7be88cab80`. No concrete blocker remains within this increment. It remains a shared-simulation behavior profile, not a new save schema or a general transfer/ownership system. The source/API contract is in [the MGT-06 source/API review](evidence/2026-11-10-mgt06/SOURCE_API_REVIEW.md); GPT's actual implementation verdict is in [the implementation review record](evidence/2026-11-10-mgt06/GPT_IMPLEMENTATION_REVIEW.md).

- The pinned operational playtest says one lead may have only one unresolved Fishing deployment, but does not define transferring that obligation between actors. The bounded TEST_ONLY profile treats the existing `FishingTrapAttemptRecord.ResponsibleHolderId` as the sole current-holder fact. It adds no allowance Boolean, owner cache, handover event collection or schema wire field.
- The new append-only `CommandKind.HandoverFishingTrapResponsibility` uses the actor as current holder and `Target` as recipient; other command fields/payloads are empty. A holder can transfer only its unique obligation. Discovery includes that attempt identity in the option ID, and discovery/query/execution share the same eligibility assessment.
- The trusted `Authority` must control the sender and declare that the existing recipient consents. The recipient must exist, differ from the sender and have no active deployment obligation. This operation does not require `CanModifyWorld`, inventory access or a separate inventory handover, and it grants no inventory or physical custody.
- Transfer is instantaneous at the current `RuleClock` in exploration. It books no work, Action or Reaction, advances no time, and consumes no RNG. It is available only while the same physical Trap remains deployed after committed setup, including a pending soak or an already resolved check. It is unavailable before commitment, after physical retrieval, during tactical mode, or when the sender is not the current holder.
- The transfer changes only `ResponsibleHolderId`, ordinary command receipt and revision. It leaves the original setter, ability/check profile, deadline, result, bait, opportunity, physical Trap, retrieval job, route, inventory and clock untouched. The existing capable-worker retrieval path remains authoritative; a worker already retrieving a Trap keeps that work through an active or paused transfer.
- The current responsible holder may use the existing abandonment route for its deployed obligation; a former holder cannot abandon merely because it originally set the Trap. Returning or canceling an independent setup follows its existing setup rules. A holder becomes free of that obligation after the physical Trap is retrieved or its existing abandonment/cancellation disposition closes it.
- The pinned source requires `d20 + WIS + one applicable proficiency contribution` for this Trap check. The existing schema37/38 profile historically derives Intelligence; this new TEST_ONLY profile derives and captures Wisdom from the shared `ActorCharacterRecord` at setup. Its explicit initial character facts seed that same authoritative record for the fixture. Handover never recalculates the captured check. Existing profiles retain their historical fingerprints, initialization and calculation for save replay.
- The source does not specify consent administration, transfer authority, contested custody, transfer reach, attendant negotiation or transfer during combat. Those are not generalized here. Trusted consent and the instantaneous exploration-only reconciliation are bounded prototype policy.

## Not implemented beyond schema38

This is not the full MGT-06 system. The manifests, allocations, actors and initial character facts are explicit fixture-supplied TEST_ONLY data. The current fixture selects one candidate per opportunity and keeps shared candidates at one supplied site; general cross-site route/access validation and arbitrary allocation-set audits remain open. It does not implement multi-creature weighted selection, documented stock renewal, attraction or prey pathing/anatomy admission. The fixture also does not author complete Trap subcomponent identities or implement bait receipts and suppression, equipment bonuses, rods, nets, attended fishing, assistants, escape/release, additional recovery categories, general deployment-allowance policy beyond one unique obligation per holder, production content authority, or Unity presentation. The code does not claim a complete exploration/management loop or a complete game engine.
