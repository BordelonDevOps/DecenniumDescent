## Preserved source interpretations

- Acquired Prone immunity suppresses effective penalties and crawling restrictions without standing the body. Legal standing permanently ends eligible Prone records, including suppressed records. Existing Unconscious cannot repeat its already-resolved fall. Voluntary DropProne while immune rejects without mutation.
- Positive-HP Unconscious is independent of dying. Recovery above zero removes the zero-HP cause, not an independent Unconscious application. External condition immunity/cures do not override zero-HP/death restrictions.
- Prone immunity can prevent a new zero-HP fall, while equipment dropping and incapacity still occur. It does not lift a body already down.
- Known location does not supply actual sight of an Invisible target for ordinary opportunity attacks.
- Incapacitated restricts Actions, Bonus Actions and Reactions; it does not itself prohibit locomotion or the mandatory stance choice of an aware creature.

## Explicit test-host event scheduling

The selected host admits fixed, fingerprinted condition events at their declared absolute phase-zero timestamp. It admits no concentration, general source acquisition, player condition payloads or arbitrary spell casting. Other phased endpoints require their own integration.

Events may run between fully resolved operations at unpaid OA/Press controller choices, at the same paused timestamp. They cannot run during an unresolved attack/knockout completion. The event, journal, physical consequences, invalidations and receipt commit atomically. Previous movement, rolls, resource spending and accepted Press entitlements remain.

Revalidate current and remaining captured OA offers in their existing order; add no new reactors or revived offers. An ineligible unpaid reactor spends nothing. An incapable mover interrupts the unpaid step. An unaccepted invalidated Press spends no entitlement; an already accepted follow retains its spent entitlement but spends no distance or movement cost without travel. Continue automatic resolution to the next real choice or completion. Stale controller responses reject.

This is a bounded fixture interleaving convention, not a general gameplay interrupt window. History capacity must remain sufficient to resolve any outstanding controller choices; capacity exhaustion rejects event injection before mutation.
