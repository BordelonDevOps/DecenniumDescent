# Manufacturing prototype scope

This records the bounded schema18 laboratory profile, not a new production rules source or a release decision. All recipe, trade, crafting-quality, ingredient, mishap, XP and potion procedures remain qualified by the pinned authored sources in the [manufacturing evidence](evidence/2026-11-03-manufacturing/SOURCE_BINDINGS.json).

The fixture exercises one exact reviewed public Alchemy recipe (`SharedResearchFixture.Recipe`) and its existing formula. It supplies two finite sets of the recipe's four identified materials, four supplemental glass vials, a qualified Alchemy worker and a fortified camp bench with its stated heat, measuring and sealing apparatus. Those stock and station facts select a bounded test scenario. They do not give players general ingredients, any recipe by name, arbitrary substitutions or an assumed workshop everywhere.

One manufacturing order reserves all required ingredients and selected packaging before work. It retains completed active phases, loses only uncompleted partial phase work when stopped, and resumes only after eligibility, material freshness, station and target claims are rechecked. The recorded actual work and its authored legal window use the shared expedition timeline. Fully completed work can wait for a later eligible check. A pending outcome choice freezes unrelated advancement; it survives save/load and settles its exact ingredient, packaging, dose, XP and incident changes once.

The implementation connects the recipe's source-defined task-quality bands, yield and durability choice, returned catalyst selection, divisible versus indivisible failure recovery, trade XP and all six native mishaps. It tracks Black Blood's original age when material is returned, stabilizes it at the source-defined work boundary and processes spoilage at the exact endpoint. The source's level10 specialty has no implementation in this profile, so an actor who reaches it cannot start another order.

The resulting fixture doses retain recipe and maker identity, ownership, custody, location, freshness-independent manufacturing time, HP and physical vial mass. One bounded command consumes an actual dose during its user's tactical turn at the supplied nearby bench, pays the Action and healing dice once, restores HP under current condition rules, and retains the exact empty vial. This is the single supplied alchemical product; the profile does not grant universal ingestion or create other effects.

The shared result verifies schema18 command replay, exact resource claims, interrupted and awaiting-choice saves, a suspended tactical slot, real crafting incident dispatch, dose use and overlapping schema1–17 save-byte parity. The source review and evidence state what the tests exercise. Other recipes, professions, actual material acquisition, proficiency training, general station access, broader quality profiles, arbitrary alchemy effects, companion assignment and release selection remain separate work.
