## Runnable acceptance for route mapping

The schema54 compatibility acceptance uses a fingerprinted Unknown route to persist a synthetic Risk-2 success, save/restore it, reject duplicate, unknown, future and wrong-actor results, apply a second distinct success to Sketched, and verify canonical replay. A Risk-1 critical result promotes once and retains surplus; failure cannot consume or activate that surplus. Its Risk-0 acceptance proves synthetic knowledge leaves authored Mapping Unknown and ordinary travel rejects. This command changes no elapsed time, RNG, inventory, quality, Risk or geometry.

Schema59's `route_mapping_work_uses_reserved_team_time_rng_and_persists` checks pure query/cost, trained assistance, invalid teams, incompatible labor, pause, save/restore, exact resume, deterministic completion, Mapping accounting, continued Unknown-route rejection and replay. `failed_route_mapping_retry_requires_a_fresh_480_minutes` verifies a failed result is immutable and retry work starts from zero. Condition cases cover Poisoned disadvantage, exact-expiry ordering, both-die persistence, and `route_mapping_work_check_uses_condition_history_at_its_causal_point`, where a later Poisoned event at the same timestamp must not rewrite the earlier check. `route_mapping_work_check_rejects_negative_condition_prefix` verifies malformed causal evidence fails closed. `route_mapping_work_synchronous_handling_cannot_cross_fractional_completion` protects the shared RuleClock completion boundary from synchronous one-second handling. `route_mapping_work_does_not_stop_an_unrelated_route_traveler` proves task binding does not close the route to unrelated movement. All work/check cases use the shared simulation. These are finite TEST_ONLY profiles, not complete route discovery.

Schema60 acceptance is `unknown_route_requires_scoped_reconnaissance_then_shared_travel`: an Unknown route rejects travel before recon; one approached lead reserves 28,800 active seconds, pauses and restores, completes a real WIS (Survival) check with condition history/RNG evidence, opens only the linked segment, and then advances through the shared route-travel resolver.
