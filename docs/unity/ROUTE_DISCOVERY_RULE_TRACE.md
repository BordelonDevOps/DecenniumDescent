## Runnable acceptance for the current synthetic accounting slice

Using a fingerprinted Unknown route, the runnable schema54 acceptance persists a synthetic Risk-2 success, saves/restores it, rejects duplicate, unknown, future and wrong-actor results, applies a second distinct success to Sketched, and verifies canonical replay. A Risk-1 critical success promotes once and retains surplus; failure cannot consume or activate that surplus, while a later success promotes once and leaves excess. A separate Risk-0 fixture proves that synthetic knowledge leaves authored Mapping at Unknown and ordinary travel still rejects with `RouteRequiresReconnaissance`. The command changes no elapsed time, RNG, inventory, quality, Risk or geometry. This validates TEST_ONLY persistence/accounting only; it does not implement the complete discovery slice or a real Map Route task.

The inspected sources provide a generic task time, team-check shape, assistant limit and four outcome degrees, but do not fully bind those generic rules to Map Route. The Map Route method/approach, route-versus-tier DC selection, failure/retry consequences and composition of 480 active work minutes with 480 nominal travel minutes remain open. Those values cannot be borrowed from FL-11 Forced March. FL-13 extraction checkpoint cadence and tests are separate from geometric route-motion checkpoints and are not wired into this executor. No executable ordinary Map Route check or reconnaissance procedure is claimed.
