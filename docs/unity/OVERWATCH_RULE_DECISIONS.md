# Soldier Overwatch implementation decisions

**Status: Codex/GPT consensus under the owner's standing delegation; TEST_ONLY implementation policy.** This record separates pinned source text from the scoped consensus completions. It does not promote the class draft to release-approved content.

## Pinned source facts

The source revision is `46a4d52728ee6956f0bebcb5d2103ffd5bc67ba4`. The source file hashes are listed in [OVERWATCH_IMPLEMENTATION_GATES.md](OVERWATCH_IMPLEMENTATION_GATES.md).

It designates a visible 15-ft square within weapon range until the Soldier's next regular start; it can trigger once during that interval on a perceived enemy entering/moving inside the zone, attacking an ally from inside it, or revealing itself inside it. It spends the Soldier's Reaction for one legal ranged attack with ammunition and line of fire. The profile says “normally” `1d8 + DEX` piercing and lists Snare, Bleed and Push hit outcomes.
- `soldier.json` `/workedExample/2` resolves a repeating-crossbow Overwatch hit as `1d8 + 3`; it does not add the crossbow's weapon die.
- `soldier.json` `/engine/0` identifies Constitution as the Soldier class-DC ability. `CLASS_PLAYTEST_RULES.md` “Reading a class card” defines class save DC as `8 + proficiency bonus + actual class ability modifier`. Together they source the Push save DC as `8 + PB + CON modifier`.
- `soldier.json` `/cards/0/effect` is a separate optional upgrade: it increases the zone and explicitly allows declining a triggered shot while continuing to wait.
- `EQUIPMENT_ACQUISITION_PLAYTEST.md` “New class equipment profiles” gives the Repeating heavy crossbow's ordinary `1d10` weapon profile, range, hands and magazine. `CLASS_PLAYTEST_RULES.md` “Reading a class card” states that specific technique wording governs its effect and that criticals double damage dice while flat modifiers do not double.
- `COMBAT_ACTION_PLAYTEST.md` places an ordinary opportunity attack immediately before a voluntary departure from reach. The adopted [movement policy](MOVEMENT_AND_UNARMED_RULE_DECISIONS.md) orders captured ordinary OA offers by initiative and charges movement only when the step completes. `COMBAT_ACTION_PLAYTEST.md` “Time and event ordering” orders simultaneous effects by application time, recorded stable sequence, then source/mechanical ID.

## Delegated consensus completions

On 2026-11-04 Codex independently checked the pinned sources and agreed with [Unity Rules Engine Development](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-p-67f17555a8dc8191a2b5b81e6d8adc12-dungeon-crawler/c/6ae59513-3008-83e9-a5c4-8b6c5c5b4206) on these bounded interpretations:

1. **Damage expression.** For the authored Repeating-crossbow Overwatch example, `1d8 + DEX` replaces the ordinary `1d10 + DEX` weapon damage; it is not an additional die/rider and is not compared against the larger weapon die. A critical doubles the feature's damage dice only: `2d8 + DEX`. Other flat modifiers apply once under their own rules. The separate Bleed tick is not doubled by a critical initial shot.
2. **Movement trigger checkpoint.** An Overwatch movement trigger is evaluated after a qualifying step actually completes. On a five-foot tactical grid, outside-to-inside is entry; inside-to-inside is movement inside; inside-to-outside alone is not a trigger. An announced but prevented step creates no movement trigger.
3. **OA before departure.** Capture and resolve pre-departure OA offers using the existing initiative-order policy. If an OA or other state change prevents the step, discard remaining departure offers and create no post-step Overwatch movement event. Preserve attacks, consequences and spent Reactions already committed; no movement cost is charged for a step that did not complete.
4. **Post-step occurrences.** Once a step completes, record resulting Overwatch and Press occurrences and process the post-step checkpoint by the existing effect order: application timestamp, stable recorded application sequence, then source/mechanical ID. This is a narrow movement-window policy; it does not create a universal priority order for every reaction type.
5. **Shared Reaction and stale offers.** Revalidate life/capability, Reaction availability, feature interval, weapon, ammunition and shot legality before each offer and accepted commitment. Accepting an OA consumes the same Reaction and makes a later Overwatch offer ineligible until legitimate refresh; declining the OA keeps the Reaction. Skip dead/incapacitated/ineligible reactors without cost. A stale/invalidated occurrence is not reopened by later recovery or revalidation, and cannot generate a duplicate trigger.
6. **Decline.** Declining a legal automatic Base Overwatch offer forfeits that designation's one shot for the interval without spending Reaction or ammunition. An invalidated offer is not a voluntary decline and costs nothing. The selected Overwatch upgrade's explicit wait-after-decline behavior remains separate and is not granted to Base Overwatch.

These completions are the narrow implementation policy authorized by the owner's consensus delegation. Preserve them as provisional TEST_ONLY policy, separate from the source and release status.

## Additional delegated consensus completions

On 2026-11-04 Codex and Unity Rules Engine Development also agreed on the following bounded implementation contract. These are provisional TEST_ONLY completions, not recovered source text:

1. **Zone footprint.** Represent a 15-foot square by a fixed 3-by-3 group of five-foot cells, with the command's `(x0,y0)` as the minimum coordinate and included cells `x0..x0+2`, `y0..y0+2`. The zone stays at that world location for its interval. This axis-aligned representation is a first-fixture restriction, not a universal template rule. Occupied cells are allowed. Require the whole zone to be visible and comfortably inside the weapon's range at designation; independently recheck the target's range, perception and line of fire for each shot.
2. **Push save.** Use the source-derived class save DC `8 + PB + CON modifier`, including a negative Constitution modifier. The target makes its actual Strength save with applicable modifiers and automatic outcomes; it is not a Shove contest.
3. **Snare.** Reduce Speed by 10 feet to a minimum of zero, without a second save, Restrained, or generic Pin removal. Preserve movement already spent and recalculate remaining movement from the reduced Speed. The source example's expiry “through the end of the target's next turn” means the next future target turn, not the current turn's already-upcoming end when hit during that turn.
4. **Bleed.** Apply registered `soldier.overwatch.bleed` (`custom_condition_bindings.json` `/definitions/38`), not generic Core Bleeding. Deal one piercing d4 at the next regular Start. Preserve its registry's suppression, immunity/family arbitration, Medicine Action cure and healing removal. The source family arbitration is scoped to one target's identical timing marker, so the one-damaging-instance group cap does not prevent the same owner/source from maintaining separate applications on different targets. The ordinary fallback cure DC is the Soldier class DC. A Medicine check resolves through the shared adjusted task degree; Success and Critical Success cure. Do not substitute an incurable one-tick timer.
5. **Rider choice and persistence.** Resolve damage and life first. If the target survives and is eligible, persist a controller-owned choice among exactly one of Snare, Bleed or Push. Freeze unrelated simulation mutations until it is answered; save/load remains allowed. No extra Action, Reaction, labor or reroll is charged. A miss has no prompt; fatal damage closes the continuation without refund.
6. **Push geometry.** The first fixture supports only cardinal firing lanes with known ground and blockers. Push is five feet directly away from the shooter, costs no voluntary movement, stops at obstacles, cannot provoke ordinary OA or Press, and can produce another zone entry/movement event under that zone's predicates. A blocked push may move zero feet and causes no collision damage or refund. Reject unsupported geometry before payment. Exclude Staggered from the first fixture rather than silently omitting its extra forced movement.
7. **Trigger enforcement and fixture limits.** Until connected, reject an ordinary attack from inside an active zone before payment or wire that attack event explicitly. Exclude reachable automatic reveal transitions from the initial fixture. Use one operative Overwatch owner initially to avoid chains. Acceptance must cover designation, completed movement, saved response, d8 shot, saved rider choice, each rider, future-turn markers, Bleed cure, Push save success/failure, blocked push, OA-prevented departure and mixed Press ordering.

On 2026-11-04 Codex and Unity Rules Engine Development also agreed on one registered-Bleed refresh detail. `custom_condition_bindings.json` `/definitions/38` specifies same-owner/source refresh without additional dice and Medicine against the recorded applying DC, but it does not explicitly say that refresh replaces that DC. Preserve the original record's applying DC, identity and arbitration order; refresh only its permitted due-marker state. Record the reapplication's contemporary class DC in the refresh audit event, but do not overwrite the active cure DC or recalculate it when Medicine is attempted. Once the old record ends, a lawful new application gets a new identity/order and captures the current DC. This is a **new provisional consensus completion**, not a verbatim source rule.

The 2026-11-04 review also confirmed that a different target is a separate application, not a refresh, rejection or retargeting of the first record. `custom_condition_bindings.json` `/familyRules/2` arbitrates within one family at one target's timing marker, and the same-marker rule further says different target markers resolve separately. Applying the same owner/source to target B while target A's record is present leaves A unchanged and creates a B-specific record with the current applying DC, new identity/order and B's next regular-start marker. This is a **source-backed scope interpretation**, not a new global multi-target grant; each application still requires its own lawful originating event.

The same review found that `cure.ordinaryConditionRemoval=true` makes the record eligible for applicable ordinary removers, but does not grant a remover, action cost, target rules or one/all/owner scope. Schema23 exposes no generic removal command. Ordinary-remover invocation remains deferred until a concrete source adapter is connected. The shared internal record-ending operation currently serves only the implemented Medicine and source-qualified healing paths. This implementation boundary is not a gameplay-rule decision.

On 2026-11-04 Codex and Unity Rules Engine Development agreed on the active-zone redeployment case. The pinned Soldier profile supplies one zone, Bonus Action designation, expiry at the next regular Start and one response during that interval, but it does not explicitly state what happens when a new zone is designated before expiry. The new provisional completion is: a legal new designation spends an actually available shared Bonus Action and atomically replaces the current zone. The actor's once-per-regular-interval Overwatch shot status belongs to the interval, not the zone, and is preserved through replacement: unused remains unused, spent remains spent, and forfeited after a legal Base decline remains forfeited. Replacement retains the interval identity and its existing next-regular-Start endpoint; it extends neither duration nor resources. The new designation receives its own identity and application-order sequence. Captured offers from the old zone do not transfer. A pending reaction/attack/rider continuation blocks redeployment; all validation occurs before payment so a rejected replacement leaves the old zone and budgets unchanged. This is a delegated consensus completion, not recovered source wording. No owner escalation was needed.

These completions are implemented as a bounded schema26 TEST_ONLY execution slice. Eleven focused regressions cover completed-movement triggers, saved responses and rider choices, source-specific shots and all three riders, shared Reaction ordering with OA and Press, prevented movement, and canonical save/replay. Ordinary attack-from-zone is rejected for the admitted hostile attack against the zone owner; reveal triggers remain excluded. The implementation does not provide general spatial visibility, arbitrary geometry, or a complete Soldier.
