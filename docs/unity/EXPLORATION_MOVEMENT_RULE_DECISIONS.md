# Exploration movement integration decisions

**Status:** TEST_ONLY C# integration policy, recorded and extended 2026-11-05. Schema40 implements the one-companion off-calendar activity/Dash ledger, ActiveLabor accounting and automatic legal Dash renewal for a still-running route. This remains provisional prototype policy, not release approval or a complete companion travel system.

## Source and design boundary

The pinned Expedition Player Book source (`EXPEDITION_PLAYER_BOOK.md`, blob `466871fc128dc5f06043a874da2b9c1d94ed80a5`) says to record each member's activity, location, intended result and actual start/end times, forbids one person from doing incompatible activities during the same seconds, and puts travel, work and combat on one elapsed clock (FL-01–03). Those requirements support using the existing shared clock and excluding overlapping work.

The source does not define a general runtime calendar for each companion or establish that a companion may move freely outside its assigned activity. Codex and **Unity Rules Engine Development** agreed to a narrow integration rule for the existing TEST_ONLY companion fixture:

- The one authored companion can walk toward a site while outside tactical combat. It moves at its current walking Speed divided by six per real-time second; the existing shared `RuleClock` advances both its travel and world work.
- Its actual `ActorState` position and partial `WorldTraversalRecord` remain authoritative. Snapshot save/restore resumes the same partial edge.
- The companion cannot travel during its incompatible active work. Once travel starts, the same companion cannot begin incompatible work until the route completes or is otherwise resolved.
- The companion stays outside `WorldSchedule.Order`; this path grants no tactical turn, Action, Reaction or scheduled movement budget.
- Schema40 gives this off-calendar actor one exact six-second activity interval anchored to `WorldSchedule.Origin`. Movement start, pause/resume, save/load and command timing do not reset the interval. The persisted record stores interval index, movement spent, Action commitment and its exact work-history or command-receipt reference.
- Ordinary walking spends the actor's current Speed per interval. Paying Dash commits the interval's Action and doubles that interval's movement allowance. A WorkerInterval overlapping any part of the current interval blocks Dash, including labor completed earlier in the interval and future labor already scheduled. Procedure `Working` records are normalized with that ledger; linked claims count once, while an unlinked active procedure still blocks Dash. Paused reservations without active work do not.
- Any positive credited active labor commits `ActiveLabor` for the interval. Consecutive, adjacent work segments may share it while preserving separate job and interval histories. Dash and ActiveLabor cannot share the ordinary Action. The scheduler also rejects active-labor credit after Dash. After Dash, supported work starts/resumes are rejected; pausing or canceling does not refund spending. Portioning remains outside the labor/action claim under its separately approved zero-time policy.
- Movement spent is preserved if Speed changes; remaining capacity is recalculated from the new Speed. The fixed interval boundary resets movement and Action together. After the outgoing interval and due shared-clock effects settle, a still-running route attempts and records one fresh legal Dash, then continues if paid. A failed attempt spends nothing and leaves the partial route suspended for explicit resume; a voluntarily suspended route never renews automatically. Pause/resume and save/restore within one interval do not refill either budget. Walking remains available if movement remains, including after Dash.
- Only one world traversal is admitted at a time. The adapter keeps the existing occupancy, route, condition and combat-transition gates.

The original schema38 travel integration reused actor position, traversal, time and persistence records without adding a schema. Schema39 later added persisted assignment records. Schema40 appends the activity ledger after schema39 in its versioned codec path; schemas1–39 do not write the new activity fields. The automatic renewal uses `WorldTraversalRecord.StartedRevision` to resolve the retained originating run receipt. The actor, calendar and interval ordinal distinguish each payment while the exact authorization receipt stays linked. A failed renewal preserves a suspended route without storing an explanatory reason; after restore, the UI can ask for resume and show current blockers without misreporting their historical cause. See the [Codex/GPT consensus receipt](evidence/2026-11-05-off-calendar-activity/GPT_CONSENSUS.md). This checkpoint has no frozen historical-byte comparison.

## Verification

The schema38 shared-world regression rejects running without changing state, checks work/travel exclusion, advances a partial walk, saves and restores the exact position, completes travel on the shared clock, and verifies that the companion never enters tactical initiative. Schema40 regressions cover fixed six-second boundaries, fractional origins, automatically renewed Dash across multiple boundaries, voluntary suspension, missing route-receipt rejection, paid Dash movement, save/restore and pause/resume, ActiveLabor persistence, sequential work segments, unlinked `Working` procedure claims, scheduler-crossed work, paused reservations, walking after Dash, work/action exclusion and receipt-ID reuse across principals. The schema39 compatibility case roundtrips the current schema39 codec path with no activity records; it is not a comparison to frozen pre-schema40 bytes. The Trap integration regression moves a second capable worker to the existing physical site during the soak, saves mid-route, resumes and retrieves the same catch.

The later [site-aware repair integration](SITE_AWARE_WORK_RULE_DECISIONS.md) reuses the same travel path for a second procedure: a remote repair request is rejected, the companion travels to a non-default authored site, saves and resumes mid-edge, and starts the existing repair only after arrival. This extends fixture coverage, not the off-calendar roster or assignment model.

See the [schema38 travel evidence](evidence/2026-11-05-companion-travel/README.md) and [schema40 activity evidence](evidence/2026-11-05-off-calendar-activity/README.md) for exact compile, headless, package and source-binding results. Schema39 kept running rejected until the activity ledger existed; schema40 enables paid running and automatic legal renewal at each fixed boundary.

## Not implemented

This does not add a general expedition roster or assignment calendar, autonomous route selection, simultaneous off-calendar movement, arbitrary companion access between sites, new combat participation, Unity integration, a universal Action economy for every future exploration operation, or a release-qualified exploration system. These limits remain explicit while broader engine work continues.
