# Instruments of the Weave
## TB-01 — Director Toolbox for Expedition Periods, Downtime, and Salvage

*Official Director supplement. Aligned to the Player Rulebook and The Director's Crucible. 2026-07-30.*

**Book code:** TB-01  
**Audience:** Directors adjudicating non-combat loops, expedition logistics, craft queues, research clocks, camp upgrades, reputation fallout, rival companies, and multi-expedition coalitions.  
**Does not replace:** Player Rulebook procedures for character creation, combat, or Resonance. When in doubt, rulebook wins until the owner says otherwise.

---

## How to Use This Book

This volume is structured like a classic **options and downtime supplement**: each chapter gives a procedure, a worked example set in Aethel, and tables you can lift to the table. Nothing here is player-facing canon unless you choose to share it.

**Three clocks to keep separate:**

| Clock | Duration | Use |
|-------|----------|-----|
| **Opening window** | 100 days / 2400 hours | Cycle macro — when the Shardspire accepts entry |
| **Horn / point of no return** | Day 50 / 1200 hours | Retreat with what you have, or accept sealed-depth risk |
| **Expedition day periods** | Morning · afternoon · evening | Team assignments inside a single delve day |

Opening **2400 h / 100 days**; Horn PoNR **1200 h / day 50**. Soft quit at Floor 50 Canticle ≠ Horn.

**Organizational cap:** one Expedition holds **up to twelve characters** working toward common goals — not merely who rolls initiative. Forces larger than twelve are **multiple Expeditions coordinating**, never one illegal roster.

# Part I — The Twelve-Seat Expedition

## Five Teams

| Team | Optimal size | Primary outputs |
|------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Combat** | 4–6 | Advancement, treasure, reputation, monster parts |
| **Reconnaissance** | 1–3 | Maps, intelligence, future encounter advantage |
| **Research** | 1–3 | Identification, lore, spell discoveries, historical context |
| **Resource** | 2–4 | Food, materials, craft components, salvage |
| **Support** | 1–3 | Camp duration, equipment bonuses, recovery |

Characters may shift teams each period. Multi-period tasks (deep research, long scouting, major craft chains) consume **consecutive blocks**. Injured PCs can contribute from Support or Research without entering combat scenes.

**Alignment note:** the Combat team should maintain compatibility per Player Rulebook guidance. Mixed Good/Evil delves invite narrative fracture — use it, do not ignore it.

## Activity Resolution Methods

The Player Rulebook names four methods. Pick one per activity block.

| Method | When to use | Time at table |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| **Quick Resolution** | Routine forage, maintenance, known routes | One roll + outcome sentence |
| **Scene-Based** | Pivotal discovery, negotiation, moral choice | 5–20 minutes focused play |
| **Parallel Play** | Teams split in different locations same period | Rotate spotlight each round |
| **Between-Session** | Long research, batch craft, multi-day surface jobs | Resolve before next session |

**Spotlight rule:** rotate detail. Montage the rest. One player may control multiple characters across teams if the table agrees.

### Quick Resolution — examples

| Activity | Skill | DC | Success | Failure |
|----------|-------|-----|---------|---------|
| Harvest luminous ore (F1–2) | Mining or Salvaging | 12 | 1d4 ore units | Noise attracts patrol |
| Cook week rations | Cooking | 10 | +1 morale next day | Spoil 1 day food |
| Patch armor | Tinkering | 13 | +1 AC one encounter | Tool break |
| Translate shop sign | Scribing | 11 | Merchant discount | Misread contract clause |

### Scene-Based — examples

**Warded Gate brazier (F3):** Mira (Arcana), Saffron (Investigation), Vesper (Performance harmonics). Three beats — activate Sun, lock Binding sequence, hold Silence protocol. Failure on any beat triggers backlash (Scene consequence, not surprise combat unless table wants both).

**Reedcaller signal doctrine:** Party invents hand signals. One Scene to establish; future Gallery transits downgrade to Quick with advantage.

### Parallel Play — examples

**Split delves:** Combat holds a choke while Resource harvests behind them — run Combat in initiative briefly, then cut to Resource Quick rolls, then back. Recon on another floor runs a **chase complication** (Part VIII) while Combat fights — only run both if players can follow; otherwise Between-session the lagging team.

**Coalition day:** Dawn Concord breaches bridge (Parallel spotlight A) while Mirror Guild scries obelisk (Parallel spotlight B). Choir Covenant runs Support for both — one player tracks triage queue.

### Between-Session — examples

**Five-recipe craft queue** at Hearth (Sim #2 pattern): Brin completes iron sword, dwarven hammer, scavenged refiner, Helga oils and salves, Joss rope — Director assigns inputs from salvage ledger between sessions; returns completed items at next open.

**Academic filing:** Saffron's acoustics appendix → **+1 Academic Factions** rep when filed (Part VI).

**Gate rune deep study:** Three sessions of Research clocks (Part IV) — player sees "2/6 facets"; Director tracks secret layer separately.

# Part III — Craft, Salvage Economy, and Tradeskills

## The recipe corpus

The companion holds **623 recipes** in `crafting-recipes.json`. As of **2026-07-31**, every recipe ships with a filled `components[]` array (base ingredients + remapped salvage parts). See [`T1_5_CRAFTING_COMPONENTS_2026-07-31.md`](../mechanics/T1_5_CRAFTING_COMPONENTS_2026-07-31.md). The engine still does **not** auto-deduct materials from character inventory.

**Director rule:** use the recipe's listed components as the default cost. You may substitute equivalent salvage from the tier tables below (or scene loot) on a 1:1 name or category swap — log deductions on a salvage ledger. Recipes with `needsReview: true` (Legendary/Mythic or skill 11+) are provisional for deep tiers — **components stay filled**; see [`../mechanics/DEEP_TIER_CRAFTING_DIRECTOR_GUIDANCE_2026-07-31.md`](../mechanics/DEEP_TIER_CRAFTING_DIRECTOR_GUIDANCE_2026-07-31.md) and each recipe's `directorGuidance` field.

## Tradeskills in the corpus

Alchemy · Blacksmithing · Cooking · Enchanting · Fishing · Herbalism · Jewelcrafting · Leatherworking · Mining · Poisoncrafting · Rune Carving · Salvaging · Scribing · Skinning · Tailoring · Tinkering

## Craft adjudication procedure

1. **Select recipe** — note skill, difficulty, crafting time, and listed `components[]` from corpus.
2. **Spend / substitute components** — default to recipe list; optional 1:1 swap from tier salvage table below or scene loot.
3. **Assign time** — map `craftingTime` to periods (1 hour ≈ one evening block; 2 hours ≈ half day) or horn hours if simming (2 h significant craft is playtest convention).
4. **Roll** — skill check vs difficulty; proficiency grants advantage (Player Rulebook).
5. **Log output** — result item enters party inventory; cross off inputs.

**Batch craft:** Resource team may run parallel Quick rolls for duplicate low-tier items (torches, salves) — cap **three identical items per period** to avoid montage abuse.

**Failure:** lose half materials (Director choice); botch on natural 1 may cause hazard (poison smoke, brittle blade).

## Salvage economy — buying and selling

Dungeon-touched goods **seed surface wars**. Price them like bait, not like generic loot.

### Selling dungeon salvage (surface)

| Buyer | Finds | Multiplier | Complication |
|-------|-------|------------|--------------|
| Crystalfront fence | Common T1 parts | ×0.4 book value | Authorities check |
| Guild licensed buyer | Documented parts | ×0.7 | Requires Scribing log |
| Academic Factions | Maps, rubbings, seals | ×1.0 + rep | They copy and publish |
| Religious Orders | Relics, choir items | donation | Moral scrutiny |
| Rival expedition | Anything | ×1.2 | They remember what you sold |

# Part IV — Research Clocks

Research teams translate, identify, and map. Long discoveries use **clocks** — six ticks by default. One meaningful period of focused Research advances **one tick** on success (DC set by tier, typically 12 + tier).

## Player-facing clocks (safe to show)

| Clock | Facets (example) | Player-visible reward |
|-------|------------------|------------------------|
| **Warded Gate runes** | Sun · Binding · Silence · Threshold | Opens F4 without backlash |
| **Spore-Veil timing** | Read · Breathe · Cross | Safe Falls transit |
| **Sunforged Obelisk rings** | Dawn · Zenith · Prism | Hidden grotto |
| **Canticle Gate notes** | Four precise tones | Tier 5 gate |
| **Heartroot Surveyor's Seal** | Identify · Authenticate · File | Academic rep + legal claim |

Show players a progress bar or checklist. **Do not** label secret layers.

## Director-only stratum (never player-facing)

Some research touches machinery older than public myth. Use a **hidden track** parallel to player clocks.

| Player tick | What they think they learned | Director secret (examples) |
|-------------|------------------------------|----------------------------|
| Gate rune 3/6 | "Silence ward reduces echo" | Pattern matches pre-Pact masonry |
| Seal authenticated | "Surveyor had legal authority" | Seal geometry recurs on deeper floors |
| Obelisk aligned | "Light opens grotto" | Refraction angle implies **ten** divisions, not nine |
| Choir reliquary | "Sound calms wardens" | Resonance frequency matches bait architecture |

**Heartcell clues without spoiling:** reward Researchers with **actionable delver benefits** (advantage on next gate, identify loot) while you privately note **custodian vs architect** tension. Never say "Heartcell" or "Silent Ones" at the table unless players earn that name through deep-tier play.

**Runes and seals:** Rune Carving can assist Research — advantage if carver participates. Failed seal authentication may still advance the clock with a **complication** (forged copy, rival claim).

## Research clock complications (1d6 on failure or natural 1)

| d6 | Complication |
|----|--------------|
| 1 | Rival publishes first — lose rep race |
| 2 | Cursed text — Sorrow save or nightmares |
| 3 | False lead — next tick needs Scene to undo |
| 4 | Attracts warden attention |
| 5 | Academic patron demands exclusive rights |
| 6 | Breakthrough — free tick but collateral damage |

- Recon finds escape vector (Quick)
- Failure: downgrade camp one tier; lose 1d6 days supplies

## Siege of camps

Trigger from pressure tables, rivals, or tier escalation.

**Siege rounds (abstract):**

1. **Assault vector** — roll tier pressure or pick (beasts, cult, environment)
2. **Combat team** — hold (Quick or tactical combat)
3. **Support** — triage three injuries or lose supply
4. **Recon** — spot relief or flanking (Scene)
5. **Resolution** — success holds camp; fail downgrade or retreat

# Part IX — Coalitions and Mass Combat

Forces larger than twelve are **multiple Expeditions coordinating** — each respects the twelve-seat cap. Playtest coalitions (e.g. 24 PCs as 3×8 companies) are **charter stress tests**, not RAW roster changes.

## Coalition structure

| Role | Who | Duty |
|------|-----|------|
| **Expedition Marshal** | Rotating PC or NPC | Day plan, dispute calls |
| **Company commander** | One per Expedition | Team assignments |
| **Medical authority** | Support lead (often choir) | Triage order |
| **Scry coordinator** | Research lead | Shared clocks |
| **Quartermaster** | Resource lead | Salvage ledger |

**Shared base camp:** one camp track; all companies pay into upgrades. **Separate combat teams:** max ~8 detailed initiative combatants per Engagement; abstract the rest.

## Mass combat procedure

1. **Stake the objective** — gate, bridge, ritual, extraction.
2. **Split companies** — assign A/B/C to vectors (assault, flank, reserve).
3. **Run primary Engagement** — up to 8 PCs + relevant foes in initiative.
4. **Abstract secondary fronts** — each round off-map: roll commander Leadership vs foe morale DC.
5. **Reinforcement clock** — 4 ticks; fill on delays; empty triggers fresh foes or rival advantage.
6. **Casualty ledger** — named PCs in detail; red shirts as HP pools.

**Example — Radiance Gate combined assault (24 seats):** Dawn Concord holds bridge ogres (Encounter in detail). Mirror Guild runs obelisk rings (Parallel Scene). Choir Covenant completes vial install (3-success skill challenge). If reinforcement clock fills before install, Architect cult floods chamber — siege rules apply.

## Coalition charter (Between-session)

Document: profit split, map rights, horn retreat vote threshold (majority or unanimous), casualty recovery policy, rep who gets credit. **Broken charter** → −1 all allied factions + rival propaganda.

## Shared campaign options

Tables running multiple player groups in one Opening may share:

| Option | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Shared Opening calendar** | All groups advance same day counter |
| **Shared faction rep** | Actions of Group A affect Group B prices |
| **Shared rival clock** | One rival progresses globally |
| **Split tier responsibility** | Group A holds T1 supply; Group B pushes T4 |
| **Common camp tier** | Joint upgrades benefit all |
| **Asynchronous Between-session** | Groups submit period plans; Director resolves |

**Vote to retreat past horn PoNR:** require coalition supermajority or suffer narrative fracture.
