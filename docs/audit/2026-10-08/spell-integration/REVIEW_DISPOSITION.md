## Falling review

Codex rejected two defects in GPT's first proposal: the victim's next turn did not guarantee a caster's Action opportunity, and final-step-only damage allowed a 310-foot fall to deal zero damage after a 300-foot first step. GPT agreed with both counterexamples and the replacement.

The saved procedure uses a fixed marker in the following round, cumulative uncontrolled distance, and a separate bounded impact formula while Feather Fall is active. No landing is retroactively undone. All newly chosen timing, impact and momentum values are labelled provisional. Focused source searches did not locate an executable falling rule; that is a search limitation, not proof that none exists in historical archives. Wider hazard-source review remains required before canon promotion.

GPT verified the exact saved text and accepted the reaction/extra-turn anchor and expiry ordering. Its last clarification, treating the virtual pre-round slot as the anchor before any regular slot has completed, was added verbatim. **Falling interface closed for this provisional packet.**
