Independent file-side corrections in the closing pass:

- Core V2 explicitly defines Poisoned as disadvantage on attacks and ability checks. Thief’s conflicting flat penalty was replaced; a source-linked condition reference was added to shared rules.
- Mage, Ranger, Shaman and Soldier’s shared level-20 completions are now explicitly automatic in data and advancement tables, matching their engines.
- Oracle Curse Boons have their own activation, payment, range and duration fields rather than inheriting a misleading passive/zero-cost header from the parent Curse.
- Worked examples and supplemental acquisition rows were reconciled with the printed costs and gates.

The final library remains 1,223 cards: splitting one Thief finale adds a card, while restoring Oracle’s four-milestone ASI schedule removes one operational card. No historical information was discarded. The class validator passes 151 checks; this is not numerical balance certification.
