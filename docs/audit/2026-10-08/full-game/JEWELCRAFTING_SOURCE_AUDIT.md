## Questions for the source conference

- All43 effects say “while worn” and “+1/+2/+3 to one [tag]-related check or save.” Define what “one” selects or limits, the eligible actor, exact check/save scopes for all14 tags, and timing. Do not infer an unlimited universal bonus, an attack bonus, resistance or a proficiency.
- Each item grants a finite source maximum of1–6 charges; spending one gives advantage on such a roll. Define declaration timing relative to dice/results, Action/Reaction costs if any, already-present advantage/disadvantage, copied/repeated rolls, interruption and legal refunds. Bonus eligibility and charge spending must remain distinct.
- Define dawn recharge using the already reviewed campaign time procedure, physical item identity, the actually recorded maximum and one legitimate recharge event. Address underground use, rest, travel, transfer, suppression, breakage, rebuilding and unspent charges without inventing cosmology, planar travel or repeated dawn farming.
- Resolve active wearing slots and complete bodies for Bracelet, Earring, Crown, Amulet, Ring, Tiara, Necklace, Brooch, Gem and Pendant. Define whether an Earring output is one piece or one matched pair. A loose Gem needs a legal mounted/worn state; carrying it or setting it in a weapon is not automatically “worn.”
- Account for actual anatomical compatibility, hands, fitting, don/doff and the existing Helm/Gauntlets slots. Wearing numerous rings, bracelets, amulets or brooches must not silently establish unlimited simultaneous bonuses or charge pools.
- Define combination on one roll with another jewel and existing class/spell/equipment bonuses, including Leatherworking and Tailoring only after their actual decisions. A save can genuinely involve more than one tag; do not grant multiple independent roll events merely to use more charges.
- Resolve physical identity, required supplemental bodies or stock, reserved order, damaged inputs, latest completion HP, return/recovery, mass, ordinary value and finished-item reference. Source materials and tiny bills do not automatically fund unlimited complete jewelry bodies.
- The source contains200 consumed component occurrences, including11 actual catalyst rows. Preserve every occurrence and quantity; Masterwork returns at most the framework’s one eligible actual unit. A component’s name, gem category or donor identity cannot relabel its role.
- Give an explicit quality menu for this charged item family. The source quality labels mention charges, but the reviewed framework defaults only to structural HP and explicitly excludes charge increases. Any replacement charge/property option needs its own precise review; decide the permitted improvement, persistent flaw, maximum/current charge state, recharge and repair interaction. Numerical balance remains separately unapproved.
- All43 fieldCraftable flags are false despite ten field timeMode labels. All source work-hour values exist. Preserve actual DEX/INT choices, Jeweler’s-tools proficiency, Jeweler’s Bench, Fine files, Magnifying lens, assistants and phase/integration rules.
- Define trained repair/reconstruction, recoverable-body conditions, DCs, active time, materials and failed work. Repair must not teach a formula, duplicate jewels, refresh spent charges or erase a persistent flaw without explicit reviewed authority.
- Source charges, bonus and operative tag agree with exact effect prose. Current/legacy names, secondary tags and whole-creature metadata remain preserved evidence; they grant no donor powers, sentience, origin, deity, resistance or other unprinted effect. In particular, Unholy, Vitality, Arcane and donor-name wording require bounded mechanical eligibility without undertaking the deferred cosmology review.
