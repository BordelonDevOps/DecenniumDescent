## What It Is

Threxus, the Iron Spiral is the Gate Guardian for will against adaptive pressure. It is an arena-scale adversary whose body, Seal, and terrain operate as one examination. Reducing it to zero hit points is necessary, but an Expedition that ignores its Gate principle can still lose the passage, the reward, or members of the team.

## What Experienced Expeditions Notice

Architecture becomes ceremonial; lesser monsters stop crossing a boundary; previous Expeditions leave preparation caches rather than camps; repeated symbols appear in terrain and wounds. Loose metal turns to face the same direction.

### Traits

**Gate-Bound.** The Guardian cannot be permanently displaced from its arena while the Seal remains active.

**Escalation Pulse.** **Iron Vortex** recharges on 5-6; visible signs appear one round before the first use.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The creature makes 3 attacks using the actions below.

**Spiral Strike.** *Attack:* +13 to hit, one target. *Hit:* 4d12+18 slashing damage (average 44).

**Coil Crush.** *Attack:* +13 to hit, one target. *Hit:* 3d12+14 bludgeoning damage (average 34).

**Iron Vortex (Recharge 5-6).** Creatures in the affected area make a DC 24 Strength save, taking 10d10 bludgeoning damage (average 55) on a failure and half on a success. The Director should describe the charge-up before resolving it.

## How It Fights

It opens with **Spiral Strike** against the target most capable of controlling its movement. It uses **Coil Crush** to punish anyone who tries to stabilize that target. When **Iron Vortex** recharges, it delays only if it can catch a more important cluster or interrupt a decisive ritual. It escalates by phase and uses the Seal Pulse to punish parties that solve damage but ignore the test.

## How to Defeat It

**Prepare Before Contact**

- Prepare resistance or mitigation for slashing, bludgeoning damage.
- Spread before **Iron Vortex** becomes available.

**Combat Priority**

Complete the arena objective while maintaining damage pressure.

**Exploit**

The defining weakness is **None identified**. Treat this as an encounter solution rather than an automatic damage vulnerability. Successful Research, Recon, or Resource actions should make the exploit easier or safer.

**Withdraw When**

Withdraw or change the objective when its full multiattack reaches the support line; Iron Vortex catches three or more Expedition members; resources fall below half before the arena mechanic is solved. A failed retreat against this creature usually costs more than abandoning the immediate treasure.

## Habitat, Goals, and Lair

A Gate arena is part of the creature. Terrain, Seal Pulses, objectives, and failure conditions matter as much as hit points.

Gate Guardians are not random apex monsters. Each is a living examination tied to a Division of the dungeon, a Gate Seal, and a principle the Expedition must demonstrate under pressure.

Some Guardians speak, bargain, or test. None are safely reduced to ordinary patrons or quest-givers.
