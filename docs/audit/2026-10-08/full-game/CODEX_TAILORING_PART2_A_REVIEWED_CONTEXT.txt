# Bodies equipment and carrying

**REVIEWED — authored tabletop body, equipment, carrying and continuous meld procedures.** New integration mechanics remain explicitly provisional; runtime implementation is pending. These procedures connect the [Druid form rules](FORM_TEMP_HP_PLAYTEST.md) and [transformation spells](TRANSFORMATION_SPELL_PLAYTEST.md). They apply to the same character, HP record and physical belongings throughout a change. They grant no additional forms, spell access, actions or resources. The approved books remain preserved.

## Entering and changing forms

Before paying for a voluntary change, choose a legal form and size, check its space at your current position, and identify any equipment that must be dropped. If the destination or required drops are illegal, the change does not begin and spends nothing. Otherwise pay the source's normal costs and resolve its actual entry effects once. A successful hostile or forced transformation cannot be prevented merely by excess gear: resolve it under its source rules, then reconcile excess equipment by keeping it external, dropping it or recording pending placement. Other source requirements still apply.

Your Druid form uses your own HP, ability scores, saves, proficiency and conditions. It supplies only its printed anatomy and features. Speech does not imply hands: none of the 26 current Druid profiles grants ordinary hand-based equipment use. Of the 12 spell profiles, only Humanoid grants hands. Each profile retains its exact size and movement permissions.

| Change | Equipment procedure |
|---|---|
| Root body to first Druid form | Record and meld the equipment physically worn, held or carried by your body, including carried containers and their contents. |
| Druid form to another Druid form | Keep the same original melded equipment. End the former form's dependent effects and start the new form under its normal rules. Reconcile newly acquired external items with the new anatomy. |
| A successful spell replaces a Druid or spell form | Close the former transformation and capture the legitimate resulting gear under the new spell's meld rules in one continuous change. There is no moment when you can use the returning equipment. |
| Shapechange changes its current spell profile | Keep compatible external items. Drop incompatible external items; they do not join the original melded equipment. |
| Suppression | Follow the spell's suppression procedure. It is not an ending or a fresh capture of equipment. Original melded gear stays inaccessible. |
| Final reversion, expiry or forced ending | Return to root anatomy, end the source's dependent effects and reconcile surviving equipment as described below. |

The continuing meld record across Druid-to-Druid changes is a **new provisional integration rule**. It prevents shifting from opening an equipment-use window between forms. A spell replacement retains the already-reviewed close-and-capture procedure.

## What melds

Record where each item was before the first change: worn in a particular slot, held in a particular hand, or carried in a particular container or on the body. Ownership alone is insufficient. A sword stored in a wagon stays in the wagon when its owner becomes a Hawk. A legitimately borrowed sword in your actual custody can meld and return normally; the lender remains its owner. Changing ownership metadata does not itself move an inaccessible physical item.

A backpack and each of its contents remain distinct items. Count each item's own weight or Load once; a container does not erase its contents. Melded items remain in your custody but are inaccessible. You cannot withdraw or add contents, use components, consume supplies, fire ammunition or activate equipment through the meld.

Melding never repairs damage, reloads a weapon, restores charges, replenishes supplies or creates a replacement item. Destruction, consumption and legitimate transfers remain real. Keep the current condition and quantity of each actual item.

## Using equipment in a form

| Item or effect | Rule |
|---|---|
| Original armor and shield | Melded and unusable. Exclude their worn or held AC benefits from the form's normal AC. |
| Held weapon, tool, focus or material component | Requires actual access and the appropriate anatomy. A form without hands receives no general permission to use it. |
| Effect requiring an item to be worn, held or equipped | Suspended while the item is melded. |
| Effect requiring only possession or attunement | May continue only when its exact rule is independent of the lost anatomy and physical use. An unspecified magic bonus is not automatically operative. |
| Newly acquired armor, harness, collar or container | Must fit the actual size and anatomy and have a legal way to remain attached or carried. Hands are required for held items, not automatically for every wearable item. |
| Newly acquired external gear when the form changes | Remains external if compatible. Otherwise use the normal drop procedure. It does not silently join an existing meld record. |

The possession-versus-use distinction and individual compatibility checks are **new provisional defaults**. A specific item or form can supply an explicit exception. Do not infer one from a creature's name, appearance or ability to speak. Item-specific compatibility across the full equipment catalog remains to be authored.

## Returning equipment

Resolve a final reversion in this order:

1. Keep current external items that still exist and remain legally equipped or carried by the root body.
2. Return each surviving melded item that remains in the character's legitimate custody to its recorded former slot or container, if available and legal.
3. If an external item now occupies that slot, leave it there. Return the former item as carried inventory if there is a legal place and sufficient capacity.
4. Drop an item that cannot be carried or equipped at the current position or the nearest legal adjacent item position.
5. If no legal drop position exists, record that item's placement as pending. It continues to exist; it is not duplicated, destroyed or used to displace another creature.

Current external equipment taking priority over a former slot is a **new provisional integration rule**. A returned sword may go into available carried storage instead of replacing a sword you actually acquired and equipped during the transformation. Both retain their current condition and ownership.

Destroyed, consumed, physically transferred, lost or otherwise unavailable items never reappear. An item in another custodian's possession cannot return merely because you still claim ownership of it. Someone else's legal ownership does not prevent an intact borrowed item from returning to your continuing legitimate custody.

## Placement and involuntary endings

A transformation does not grant movement. A voluntary size change keeps the same spatial anchor and must fit before payment. Preserve the existing footprint on a same-size change when possible.

A mandatory ending still occurs if the root anatomy does not fit. First try its normal space at the same position, then a legal squeeze under the [movement rules](COMBAT_ACTION_PLAYTEST.md). If neither fits, record a placement conflict. The **new provisional placement procedure** permits only a nearest legal adjacent placement in the same unobstructed region. It cannot pass through a wall, closed barrier or occupied space. Choose the minimum displacement that fits the whole body; among ties, preserve the greatest overlap with its previous footprint. If still tied, the Director uses a fixed neutral board order, not a tactical choice by the affected player. If no adjacent candidate fits, the conflict remains until the obstruction is removed or a legal relocation effect resolves it. Do not search farther afield.

This mandatory normalization spends no movement and triggers no opportunity attack, charge distance, movement benefit or other movement rider. It changes no remaining movement allowance and grants no invented crushing damage. It does not permit a voluntary form change to bypass its preflight.

Track actual height and terrain. Losing flight while unsupported starts ordinary falling from the current location; reversion never moves the creature directly to the ground. Ending a burrowing form underground does not move the creature to the surface. Master of Vermin's permission to pass through Tiny gaps changes clearance, not its printed size.

Use the actual space of the expressed anatomy:

| Size | Space | On a grid of 2.5-foot subcells |
|---|---|---|
| Tiny | 2.5 by 2.5 feet | 1 subcell |
| Small | 5 by 5 feet | 2 by 2 subcells |
| Medium | 5 by 5 feet | 2 by 2 subcells |
| Large | 10 by 10 feet | 4 by 4 subcells |

V6 explicitly supplies Tiny and Medium space. Small and Large complete this bounded table provisionally; the supplied V6 passage does not separately list them. Size category still matters even when two sizes occupy the same space. Squeezing changes clearance and its penalties; it does not change size, carrying capacity or weight. Exact Tiny occupancy requires subcells or equivalent measurement, not treating Tiny as Medium.

## Body weight and conditions

**New provisional rule:** a creature-form transformation preserves the body's existing base physical weight unless an exact rule changes it. Size alone does not change weight. A 180-pound character therefore remains 180 pounds as a Tiny Hawk or Large Bear. This is a magical transformation rule, not a claim about the weight of ordinary animals. It applies equally to the current 26 Druid and 12 creature-form spell profiles.

Record base body weight separately from equipment and condition effects. Petrified multiplies current body weight by ten once; it does not rewrite the base weight or multiply repeatedly for multiple applications. Ending or changing a form does not cure that condition. Temporary Druid form ends under its existing Incapacitated rule, then Petrified continues to apply to the reverted body. An anatomy permitted to persist keeps its own base weight and the same condition overlay.

True Polymorph object anatomy uses its actual documented output weight. Its output ceiling remains the lower of current effective input weight and earliest recorded origin weight. Include a current applicable weight-changing condition in the input, but never rebase the origin ceiling upward. Equipment, casualty Load and estimated pounds do not establish material available for a transformation. Creature-origin object anatomy has no independent sale, salvage or harvest value.

For another creature, mount, floor or platform supporting you, count your effective body weight plus all actual equipment in your custody, including melded items, once. For your own inventory capacity, count what you carry, not your own body weight. Conditions change only the items or body their exact rules affect.

## Carrying and Load

Use pounds for actual weight and Load for inventory burden. Extraction Class describes cargo danger and attention. A casualty's Load describes the difficulty of transporting a body; it is not the body's weight. These quantities answer different questions and never convert automatically into each other.

The following is a **new provisional reconciliation** of V6 physical handling with the staged Gameplay Loop inventory rules. It does not promote the historical Gameplay Loop file into the approved books. V6's optional encumbrance variant remains preserved as an alternative; do not add its penalties to this Load procedure.

| Carrier size | Physical carry limit in pounds | Push, drag or lift limit in pounds | Inventory capacity in Load | Casualty Load for a body of this size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tiny | Strength score × 7.5 | Strength score × 15 | Strength score ÷ 2 | 1.5 |
| Small | Strength score × 15 | Strength score × 30 | Maximum of 0 and Strength score − 2 | 3 |
| Medium | Strength score × 15 | Strength score × 30 | Strength score | 6 |
| Large | Strength score × 30 | Strength score × 60 | Strength score × 2 | 12 |

Use the character's current Strength score, not the modifier or an imported monster score. Tiny's Inventory capacity and casualty Load are new provisional additions. The pound limits come from V6; Small, Medium and Large Load values come from the Gameplay Loop. Larger unsupported transport sizes require their own completed rule before this table can handle them.

Keep fractional Loads without rounding. An item with a printed Load uses that value. If it has only a known weight, its **provisional Load is pounds divided by ten**. A Load-only item may use ten pounds per Load as a clearly labeled estimate for ordinary hauling; this is insufficient for effects requiring documented physical mass.

Count a container's own empty Load plus the contents' Loads, unless an explicitly inclusive Load already covers those exact contents. Handle pounds separately: sum the empty container weight and contents' weights, or use an explicitly inclusive gross weight for the contents it covers. An inclusive Load does not make a printed weight inclusive, or vice versa. Apply this rule through nested containers and meld budgets so every item counts exactly once in each quantity.

With positive carried Load at or above half capacity and no greater than capacity, you are **Burdened**: reduce Speed by 10 feet and apply disadvantage to checks to climb, swim or move silently. An empty inventory is not Burdened, including at zero capacity. Above capacity, that inventory cannot be carried routinely; drop or transfer something, or use a legal alternative such as dragging or a vehicle. These threshold and zero-Load details are provisional clarifications.

Apply actual physical limits when handling a known weight. Between physical carry capacity and twice that capacity, pushing or dragging uses the lower of otherwise-current Speed and 5 feet. A Speed of 0 stays 0; a Speed of 3 stays 3. Lifting does not independently permit movement: moving while supporting a weight must satisfy carrying, pushing, dragging or another exact rule. An anatomy restriction, such as Hawk's prohibition on carrying a Medium ally, still applies even if numbers would fit. Burden penalties do not create hands, harnesses, lift or access to melded gear.

Transporting a creature must pass both checks: the carrier can physically handle the body's effective weight and associated equipment, and can accommodate the casualty Load plus the equipment's Load. Petrified multiplies the body weight by ten, not its casualty Load. Removing gear can reduce equipment weight and Load; it cannot reduce the body's weight or erase its condition.

## Meld limits across repeated transformations

**New provisional meld exception:** legally melded gear is excluded from both your transformed body's active Inventory Load and its own active pound-carrying limit. Record its full Load and weight separately while it remains inaccessible. New external equipment uses the expressed form's current capacities and still needs compatible anatomy and custody.

A **continuous meld chain** begins when root anatomy first enters a transformation that melds gear. At that instant, record two fixed ceilings: the pre-transformation body's ordinary active Load capacity and its ordinary physical carrying capacity in pounds. Legally melded gear must fit both. Keep the actual total Load and pounds of the hidden gear, counting containers and contents once in each quantity.

Druid-to-Druid changes, internal Shapechange switches, suppression, permanent True Polymorph and continuous replacement by another spell do not end the chain or increase its ceilings. Later changes to size or Strength neither increase nor retroactively shrink these historical ceilings. An existing item is not expelled merely to make room for a new one.

A later spell replacement may capture an external item only if the whole item fits the remaining room under **both** ceilings. Do not partially meld an item unless its source defines a divisible cargo quantity. A new item that will not fit remains external if compatible with the destination and within its current carrying limits; otherwise drop it or, for a forced resolution without legal placement, record its placement as pending. Excess cargo never grants immunity to a hostile or forced transformation. Druid-to-Druid changes continue to preserve their existing meld and never recapture external cargo.

For a voluntary change, declare and preflight the full item disposition before spending costs. A successful hostile or forced change resolves its mandatory custody consequences without deleting items or refunding its costs. This same overflow procedure applies at the first forced transformation if the target began with excess cargo; only gear within both starting ceilings can become hidden.

When someone else carries the transformed character, count its casualty Load plus both its melded and external equipment Load. Count the effective physical body weight plus all that equipment for actual weight handling. Melding therefore provides no weightless cargo for another carrier.

Only an actual final return to root anatomy, with the hidden equipment released and reconciled, ends the chain. Returned gear rejoins active inventory under the root body's **current** capacities. Resolve excess with the return-and-drop procedure; overload cannot prevent a mandatory reversion. A later new transformation can then establish a new pair of ceilings from its actual legal starting state.

## Extraction Class and Secure Cargo

Extraction Class is a danger and logistics rating, not a carrying allowance:

| Class | Cargo |
|---|---|
| 0 | Ordinary trade goods |
| 1 | Common monster parts or durable salvage |
| 2 | Uncommon, fragile or regulated cargo |
| 3 | Rare artifacts, volatile substances or living captives |
| 4 | Very rare strategic cargo or dangerous curses |
| 5 | Epic cargo likely to attract sovereign or faction intervention |
| 6 | Legendary cargo requiring a dedicated extraction operation |

Retain relevant Fragile, Volatile, Cursed, Living, Contraband, Perishable and Attention tags. A light object can carry a high Extraction Class, and a heavy ordinary crate can remain Class 0.

**New provisional wording for Secure Cargo:** spend an Action on one legal attach, lift, transfer or protective operation involving one discrete cargo unit or bundle with an assigned Extraction Class. The unit must be physically handleable as declared; naming an entire convoy a bundle does not make it one operation. The action grants no extra capacity, hands, equipment, automatic success, invulnerability or reduction in Extraction Class. Apply actual restraints, harnesses, checks, hazards and source-specific handling requirements.

## Examples

**The remote sword.** A Druid wears armor, holds a staff and carries a pack containing two potions. A spare sword is in a wagon. The armor, staff, pack and both potions meld when the Druid changes. The sword remains in the wagon. Neither potion can be consumed from the melded pack.

**A compatible harness.** A transformed creature acquires a harness made for its present anatomy. A later change checks the harness against the destination form. If it does not fit, the voluntary change requires a legal drop. The harness is neither erased nor added to the old melded equipment.

**The crowded hand.** A spell Humanoid acquires and holds a new weapon. When the spell ends, root anatomy can keep that weapon if legal. The original melded weapon returns to an available legal carrying position, or is dropped if necessary. It does not replace or duplicate the new weapon.

**The loaded Hawk.** A Strength 9 Druid becomes Tiny with 3 Load of legally melded gear. It stays inaccessible and uses neither of the Hawk's active carrying capacities. Tiny capacity is 4.5 Load and 67.5 pounds. An additional package of 3 Load and 30 pounds in a genuinely compatible external carrying arrangement fits both capacities, but exceeds half the Load capacity of 2.25 and makes the Druid Burdened. A rescuer carrying that Hawk counts 1.5 casualty Load plus all 6 Load of equipment, totaling 7.5 Load, and separately satisfies the full body and equipment weight.

**The repeated replacement.** A Strength 9 Medium character starts a chain with 8 Load and 80 pounds of legal gear. The fixed ceilings are 9 Load and 135 pounds. The remaining hidden capacity is therefore 1 Load and 55 pounds. A later spell cannot hide a new 3-Load, 30-pound package even if the current form can carry it externally. A whole 0.5-Load, 50-pound item fits both remaining ceilings; a 0.5-Load, 60-pound item fails the pound limit. Changing form or casting again does not refresh either ceiling.

**The stone casualty.** A Medium ally has a documented base weight of 180 pounds and becomes Petrified. The body now weighs 1,800 pounds but still contributes 6 casualty Load. A Strength 18 Medium rescuer can normally carry 270 pounds or push, drag or lift 540 pounds, so cannot move that body alone through these ordinary procedures, even with enough spare Load capacity. Additional gear is counted separately.

## Trade XP and specialties

**Source:** first successful craft 4 XP; nontrivial repeat 2; difficult failed attempt with usable research 1; trivial mass production 0. Advancing from level L to L+1 costs 10+L trade XP. Maximum level 20. The source per-recipe 4-XP field is not an extra award on top of this system.

**Completion:** a successful craft means Standard, Exceptional or Masterwork. Award 4 for the first success of a recipe ID in that trade if not trivial; subsequent nontrivial successes award 2. A recipe is trivial when current trade level is at least `trivialAtSkillLevel` (all source thresholds are recipe skill level +5). Triviality wins even for a previously unmade recipe. Changing ingredients, quality, batch size, location or a source alias does not reset first-success history.

A Flawed/Failed/Mishap order may give 1 XP only when its recipe is at least the crafter's current trade level, it consumed actual inputs and hours, and the lead records a concrete usable research finding. This is once per recipe ID per trade level, not repeat failure farming; mishap 6 gives none. No assistant or uninvolved observer receives this work-order award. Untrained attempts record experience narratively but bank no numerical trade XP and grant no proficiency. Training initializes zero XP.

Spend XP immediately when a threshold is reached; carry excess toward the next level, repeat if enough, and stop at 20. At level 10 select one concrete specialty such as antidotes, metal shields or textile containers. Record its scope before the next check; it cannot be renamed for each work order. Its +1 applies once. Further specialties, retraining and unique substitutions require another explicit feature or agreed research result; the rank alone grants none.

## Material identity, consumption and substitution

Keep the exact recipe ID and component occurrence. Global material names alone are insufficient: 143 embedded names have different tier/price/weight variants. Record identity as the recipe component's name, tier, category, properties, unit weight, unit value and source occurrence. Quantity belongs to the inventory lot, not the identity. Preserve component IDs where supplied; none are silently merged or normalized to a cheaper same-name material. The 2-gp crafting Crystal Vial and 5-gp equipment Vial remain distinct records unless an explicit compatibility/substitution ruling is recorded.

An exact recipe component's stored numeric `tier` remains part of that component's identity and may be compared for that recipe's explicit substitution requirements even when the stored number exceeds 6. This does not extend the generic V4 material-tier ladder or generic harvesting entitlement beyond Tier VI. A Tier 7–10 stored component therefore receives no generic harvest DC, public availability, rarity right or substitution source merely from that number; it requires its actual recipe/source acquisition procedure or another separately reviewed rule.

**Source:** substitutions require the relevant properties and tier unless the recipe names an irreplaceable ingredient. **Completion:** before spending materials, a substitute must satisfy each replaced unit's tier (equal or higher), all required functional properties and role, compatible physical form, and at least its required amount. The Director records the mapping and its actual unit quantities/weights/value. Higher price alone is not compatibility; a higher-tier component does not increase the result tier, quantity or effect. A unique named core cannot be replaced under this general permission. Do not repeatedly change the substitution after observing the outcome.

Reserve consumed quantities at the start and deduct them once at resolution or deliberate abandonment. Abandonment after work starts loses consumed inputs with no product/recovery roll; actual nonconsumed tools and intact independently stored unused supplies remain. Ingredients left outside the reservation remain usable but cannot also fund another order. Return only the exact quantities authorized by Masterwork or ordinary failure, never both for one result. No recovery from a Mishap. Component `consumed=false` returns its full reserved quantity; role=tool is not itself a consumed flag.

Nineteen source component rows have role=tool and consumed=true. Treat these as incorporated parts in those exact recipes, not the separately required reusable toolkit; retain their flags until individual recipe review. A focusing lens incorporated into a device is not a reusable station lens. The work order must supply both if it needs both. Never 'repair' all role=tool rows to nonconsumed in bulk.

Ordinary failure recovery uses each identity's physical units: an indivisible organ, lens, bottle or crystal rounds down to whole units; explicitly divisible bulk reagent can be recorded to 0.01 source units. Recovery never creates money. For example, four ordinary indivisible non-catalyst vials return one; one irreplaceable catalyst returns zero. These recovered lots retain their age and identity. A failed batch cannot be salvaged again for another automatic 25%.

## Identify and harvest

**Source:** identify with the creature type's knowledge skill, then extract using a relevant gathering trade. The type mapping and size times/yields are in the trade reference. This procedure concerns actual remains and environmental nodes already present; it does not create a creature, corpse, mineral vein, legal entitlement or formula.

**Completion:** the Director records a finite harvest manifest before checks: actual accessible categories, unit identities/tier/weight/value, quantities or yield dice, damaged/unavailable parts, preservation deadlines, and any exceptional drop. Normally expose four to six plausible categories, but a creature with fewer real categories has fewer. These are choices within one finite yield pool, not four to six copies of the yield roll. A Tiny creature cannot produce a Large creature's organ or an unlimited number of crystals.

Use the exact component's explicit harvest DC when present; otherwise the highest desired material tier sets DC: I 12, II 13, III 15, IV 16, V 18, VI 19. These are the existing expedition tier DCs, not recipe crafting DCs. The generic table has no public Tier VII–X harvest entitlement. A specially restricted ingredient beyond this table needs a separately stated DC and access procedure before rolling.

Identification takes 1 minute of close examination. Use INT and the source type skill except Medicine uses WIS; PB requires actual relevant training, with existing task-scoped expertise replacing it. One lead and at most one trained assistant (+1) can participate. A success reveals the manifest's usable categories, hazards and preservation needs; failure identifies no hidden qualities. There is no invented false fact on failure. The party may still extract a plainly recognizable category such as a hide, using the declared DC with disadvantage while unidentified. Further identification attempts require new evidence, a different method or consultation, not repeated rolls by every party member on unchanged evidence. Identification does not itself extract anything.

The extraction lead selects a compatible gathering trade: Skinning for animal parts, Herbalism for plants, Mining for mineral deposits, Fishing for aquatic gathering, Salvaging for constructs and dismantled devices; a specific listed source may authorize another. Use that trade's ability and primary-tool rules. Recipe-level access gates do not apply to an unranked generic harvest; actual restricted material access still does. Up to one eligible assistant gives +1 and spends the same extraction time. Tools and access are required; no station is required for ordinary physical field removal beyond a stable accessible work surface and appropriate containers. A kit cannot contain an unsafe magical essence without a suitable actual vessel.

|---|---|---|
| Tiny | 10 minutes | 1 |

| Medium | 30 minutes | 1d4 |
| Large | 1 hour | 2d4 |
| Huge | 2 hours | 4d4 |

Roll base yield once for the remains when the manifest is made. It is the shared total of ordinary units across categories, constrained by actual anatomy and mass. An explicit bestiary drop uses its own finite chance/quantity, once, in addition only when the source calls it an exceptional separate drop. A global material's dropRate does not add another independent roll for every identical name in the material catalog. State which exceptional drops depend on a successful extraction before rolling.

Full extraction uses the listed time. For a partial harvest, select which manifest categories to attempt first. Time is full time × chosen categories / available categories, rounded up to whole minutes, minimum 1 minute. Allocation of the finite units across categories is fixed before selection, so selecting one category cannot redirect the entire pool into its most valuable part. Once partial harvesting begins, unselected ordinary categories are forfeited for this generic procedure; another worker cannot harvest the same remainder again. An explicit unique drop can retain its separately stated procedure.

Use the core four-degree check for extraction: total ≥DC+10 critical success, ≥DC success, >DC−10 failure, ≤DC−10 critical failure; natural 20 improves one degree and natural 1 worsens one. Success recovers the selected allocation. Critical success additionally preserves one otherwise damaged ordinary unit within the selected attempted allocation that the manifest already recorded as damaged but potentially recoverable. If the selected allocation contains no such unit, Critical Success grants no additional unit. It cannot recover an unselected or already-forfeited category, duplicate an exceptional drop or exceed the manifest's finite anatomy. Failure ruins one selected unit (highest-value selected unit, tied at player's choice), recovers no selected units, and ends that attempt. Critical failure ruins all selected ordinary units, recovers none, and invokes only a hazard explicitly declared in the manifest; absent one, apply the gathering trade's minor accident (1d4 damage) to the lead. No reroll or new generic attempt on the same exhausted selection; named exceptional-drop procedures remain as stated.

A hurried extraction takes half the listed/partial time, rounded up to whole minutes, minimum 1 minute, with disadvantage. This is a new reconciliation of the detailed size table with the older one-hour rushed Watch summary; it avoids making a hurried Tiny harvest slower than normal. Normal and hurried harvesting consume actual Watch time, not a second automatic eight-hour charge. Yield, limits and spoilage are unchanged. Resource Team membership grants no duplicate attempt or extra yield.

Harvested lots become actual cargo immediately, including containers and weight. Food, water or fuel gathered separately does not magically become the complete expedition Supply bundle. An ancestry's explicit forage or Natural Remedy grant follows its own finite procedure; this generic harvest does not refresh those uses. Humanoid and celestial remains keep the source's consent, lawful-authority or explicit-dark-path restriction; the procedure supplies no blanket permission and makes no cosmological conclusion.

## Preservation and spoilage

**Source:** blood/organs 1–4 hours, essence 30 minutes–2 hours unless contained, meat 8–24 hours, hides 12–24 hours before curing; dry bone/horn/teeth/mineral and ordinary construct parts can last indefinitely. Active cores are exceptions. **Completion:** the manifest fixes the interval before harvesting. Where no more specific interval is stated, use the conservative low end: organs/blood 1 hour, essence 30 minutes, meat 8 hours, hide 12 hours. The clock starts when the creature dies or the material leaves its sustaining environment, not when the player finally harvests it.

Track condition separately from material tier and rarity: Fresh → Degraded → Spoiled. At one unprotected interval the lot becomes Degraded: its unit value halves and it cannot meet a recipe calling for Fresh ingredients, but otherwise retains its exact tier/properties. At two unprotected intervals it is Spoiled, value zero and unusable as an ordinary recipe ingredient or edible food. A specific recipe may explicitly require spoiled material. No new poison, curse or disease is inferred. Dividing, trading, repackaging, recovering or reharvesting a lot cannot reset its elapsed age. The label quality here is preservation condition, never an equipment grade or crafting outcome.

Work on a perishable ingredient must begin while it is eligible; ordinary reservation does not suspend aging. An exact recipe card may say its first processing phase stabilizes the input; otherwise it must remain eligible through completion. A generic engine cannot promise a 160-hour potion made from an untreated one-hour organ will succeed. This makes the card's storage/processing details mandatory.

If a required reserved component ceases to satisfy its recipe requirement before completion and no completed recipe phase expressly stabilized it, pause the order before further active work. Before resuming, the lead may reserve a fresh legal component satisfying that same requirement, including an already-legal substitution. The replacement lot keeps its own identity and age. Completed active-work phases remain completed, but any component already physically consumed or irreversibly processed before becoming unusable remains spent. If no legal replacement is supplied, abandon the order under the normal abandonment rule; do not make a completion or quality roll.

The following new mundane preservation procedure supplies a usable default without inventing magical stasis. For each lot of at most 10 lb of eligible blood/organs, meat or hide, spend 30 active minutes, a suitable trade kit, a sealable appropriately sized container, and 1 gp/0.5 lb of preservative supplies (salt, clean packing and the appropriate nonmagical stabilizer). These supplies have no independent combat or magical effect. Alchemy handles specimens, Cooking handles food, and Skinning handles hide. Make one DC 10 check using the trade's normal ability/proficiency, no assistants or quality-yield table. Success pauses spoilage for 24 elapsed hours; failure uses the supplies and time but gives no extension. This treatment must finish before the lot becomes Spoiled. It does not restore Fresh condition or remove existing age.

On a successful treatment, freeze accumulated unprotected age and resume it after the 24 hours. A replacement treatment takes the same cost/time and extends protection from its completion to 24 hours later, never adding stacked days. Any actual container breach, contamination or incompatible heat ends protection; merely opening to portion with clean tools for less than a minute does not. Preserved supplies and container mass count as cargo until discarded or used. Processing into food is not automatically safe if the source explicitly says otherwise.

Mundane specimen jars alone contain fluids but do not pause decay. They do not stabilize magical essence or active cores. Those require a separately defined compatible containment method with its own lifetime, capacity and costs; without one, the printed/default clock continues. Dry durable materials remain indefinite only while kept dry and physically intact. No generic refrigeration, freezing or stasis benefit is inferred from a spell name; its actual reviewed effect must support the storage method.

## Salvage, research and sale

Do not confuse harvesting remains, manufacturing a Salvaging-tool recipe, and dismantling an existing item. A salvage tool's particular bonus requires its reviewed card. Ordinary failure recovery is already part of the batch check and cannot be claimed again as dismantling that failed batch.

**New basic dismantling procedure:** spend 1 active hour per item of at most 50 lb with actual Salvager's tools, a stable work surface, and applicable proficiency. Larger or magical/unique items require an explicit salvage profile first. Before work begins, record each recoverable ordinary component and the maximum quantity physically remaining in the item after known prior damage, loss or consumption. On Success, recover 25% of each recorded recoverable quantity, rounded down under that component's normal unit rule. Explicitly divisible bulk material may use the framework's 0.01-source-unit precision. Failure recovers none. No later appraisal may increase the predeclared recoverable cap. An unknown bill of materials gives no invented valuable component. Roll INT or WIS plus the one applicable PB contribution against DC 10. The item is irreversibly dismantled on either result. No general recovery of catalysts, expended ammunition/consumables, enchanting power, RP, souls, formulas or previously missing parts. No sale-value-to-free-material conversion. This check uses binary success; no natural-die degree bonus or extra recovery is introduced. If there are no known recoverable materials, ordinary scrap may be described but has no assigned recipe identity or automatic resale value.

**Source:** experiment with a stated goal, compatible components, station and research check; failed research can create notes. **Completion:** researching an existing available formula uses its `minimumResearchHours`, minimum 8 active hours, and a copy/sample or credible source sufficient for that knowledge. It uses the trade ability with proficiency against that recipe's DC, plus explicit applicable research bonuses. Success yields a usable transcription of the exact formula if the source actually contains enough information; failure yields notes, no formula, and consumes those research hours. No automatic ingredient consumption unless a destructive sample test is declared with actual quantities before research. Do not reroll unchanged evidence; a fresh attempt needs a new source/sample or eight additional hours of a concretely different method. Copying an already legible legitimately accessible formula requires 1 hour and 1 sp of writing supplies per recipe and no check, and grants no trade XP. These routes grant knowledge, not item effects or access through a faction's restrictions.

Designing a new recipe, a unique substitution, or reverse-engineering an unknown magical item yields a proposal for a new reviewed card. It cannot automatically invent a numerical power from a high roll. State the desired result, consumed test materials, facility, hours, DC and what information success can actually reveal before experimentation. This is an authoring boundary, not cosmology research or permission to expose Director lore.

**Batch pricing completion:** `recommendedMarketValue` is the recommendation for the Standard batch. A nominal unit reference value is batch value divided by Standard batch quantity; keep exact precision until the final transaction. Market recommendation is not a guaranteed buyer or profit. Actual quotes specify units, finished quality, condition, availability, legal claims and demand. Ordinary crafted mundane equipment uses the already reviewed mundane resale standing percentages. Actual artifacts use the Core artifact market procedure only when their reviewed profiles classify them as artifacts. The frameworks are not multiplied together indiscriminately. Crafting quality alone supplies no extra sale multiplier.

The source commission default is a 50% deposit on the agreed complete quote, plus delivery of all agreed irreplaceable monster parts before work. The quote explicitly states who supplies other ingredients, whether those costs are included, delivery schedule, acceptable quality and failure/replacement terms. There is no universal refund or loss of all funds inferred from a failed roll. Record these terms before accepting a commission in the fiction. Do not charge the same supplied ingredient twice or turn returned unused ingredients into a second payout. Use reviewed equipment rounding: final purchase cost up to a copper; final sale proceeds down to a copper, once.

A buyer commits to a finite quoted quantity and price before mass-production plans rely on that sale. Once it is filled, more production needs another actual buyer/quote; a reference price does not generate infinite demand. Existing Core/local market procedures can change later quotes. Generic crafting does not manufacture gold, create a guaranteed arbitrage floor, or silently apply the artifact market to every mundane crafted object.

The older expedition 0–3 Integrity scale does not replace reviewed structural HP for personal equipment or this chapter's kits. Use the equipment repair procedure for those actual items. Convoy/vehicle Integrity, wider Haven services, Supply conversion and the full expedition loop remain separate integration work; this supplement does not claim they are completed.

## Hands and focuses

A buckler straps to one forearm. Its partially available hand can carry a small nonweapon object, but supplies no free S hand, loading hand, second-weapon attack, two-handed weapon support or active focus manipulation. An ordinary shield or tower shield occupies one hand. Soldier Shield Trained is a specific selected-feature exception for its repeating-crossbow/strapped-shield use, with that feature's actual reload requirements.

A two-handed weapon requires both hands when attacking with it. Releasing one grip costs no Action; regripping is part of its next legal use. This does not free a hand already occupied by another object, restraint or incompatible anatomy.

F requires an actual focus legal for the casting source. A hand holding only that focus may manipulate it and supply S for the same cast; the focus itself is not a conflicting object. A weapon, shield, second object, loading requirement or other hand restriction can make that hand unavailable. An item independently serving as both weapon and focus can use this rule only when an actual source authorizes that item as the caster's focus.

A holy symbol may supply F while held, visibly worn or borne on a shield where that casting source permits holy symbols. A worn or shield-borne symbol does not supply a free S hand. V still requires actual audible speech, and neither a focus nor component pouch waives explicitly priced or consumed materials. The component pouch supplies its ordinary storage function; this guide does not give it a new universal F substitution.

The following focus assignments are **new class integration** for actual legal class spells requiring F. They grant no spells or resource pools. Exact class features may override their own focus rules.

| Casting class | Ordinary focus |
|---|---|
| Bard | One actual instrument |
| Cleric, Paladin | Holy symbol |
| Druid, Shaman, Ranger | Druidic focus |
| Mage, Wizard | Arcane focus |
| Mirrorsage | Steel mirror |
| Oracle | Divination token |
| Crimson Agent, Dragoon, Monk, Thief, Tinker | Token for that class |

Soldier and Warrior gain no spellcasting from focus ownership. An ancestry casting token remains ancestry-grant scoped and cannot substitute for a class focus. An explicitly granted ordinary background arcane focus or holy symbol retains its normal equipment function for a casting source already entitled to that focus category.

## Weapons and ammunition

Use the source table's exact weapon damage, type, properties, cost and weight. Proficiency follows the reviewed [character foundation](CHARACTER_FOUNDATION_TABLES.md), not the source introduction's general statement about most people using simple weapons. A normal proficient attack adds PB once. Normal ability damage follows the attack's actual rules; an item procedure saying no ability damage overrides it for that item.

- **Finesse:** choose STR or DEX for both attack and damage.
- **Thrown:** a thrown melee weapon uses its ordinary melee ability; Finesse can still supply its choice.
- **Heavy:** Small creatures attack with disadvantage. Extending this to Tiny is a new completion.
- **Light:** enables only the actual reviewed Two-Weapon Fighting procedure, not an independent free attack.
- **Reach:** add 5 feet for that weapon's attacks and its actual opportunity-attack reach.
- **Two-handed:** both hands are required for an attack, subject to an explicit feature exception.
- **Versatile:** use the printed alternate damage for an actual two-handed melee attack.
- **Ammunition:** spend one real compatible ammunition unit per attack. Drawing it is part of the attack; non-repeating loading needs an available hand. Using an ammunition weapon in melee is improvised; a sling must be loaded to deal its improvised damage.
- **Loading:** only one ammunition attack per Action, Bonus Action or Reaction, regardless of Extra Attack. This is not one attack per entire turn or round.
- **Range:** the first number is normal range, the second maximum. Beyond normal range gives disadvantage; beyond maximum is illegal. Ordinary close-range ranged-attack rules still apply.

After an encounter, one uninterrupted minute searching accessible locations recovers `floor(eligible ordinary ammunition expended in that encounter / 2)`. Resolve recovery once for that encounter's expended ammunition; another search cannot recover another half. Only physically accessible, intact ordinary ammunition qualifies. Destroyed, consumed, irretrievable or specifically nonrecoverable ammunition does not. Recovered units remain the same physical items and cannot exist in two inventories. Nets and ordinary thrown weapons do not use this half-ammunition rule.

**Ammunition retail units.** A source ammunition bundle remains the ordinary market purchase unit. `unitPriceCp` and `unitWeightLb` are derived inventory/accounting values and do not by themselves guarantee that a merchant sells fractional bundles. If an actual buyer or seller agrees to split a bundle, price the agreed quantity proportionally and apply transaction rounding once to the final transaction. Starting-kit references record one retail bundle and its twenty `grantedUnits`, not twenty bundles.

**Lance.** Attacks against a target within 5 feet have disadvantage. It requires two hands unless mounted; the item supplies no mount or general mounted-combat procedure.

**Net.** A hit restrains a Large-or-smaller creature unless formless; it deals no damage. Its range remains 5/15 feet. The target or a helper within reach can spend an Action on DC 10 STR to free it. Dealing 5 slashing damage to the net against AC 10 destroys it and frees the victim without hurting that victim. When using a net with an Action, Bonus Action or Reaction, make only one attack for that Action/BA/Reaction regardless of normal attack count. Use the reviewed Restrained condition. A missed net remains an actual object at the target location if physically accessible, not an automatically returned weapon.

**Improvised weapon.** Before rolling, the Director may agree that an actual similar object uses an existing weapon's full profile and relevant proficiency. Otherwise it deals 1d4 appropriate physical damage, with no PB absent actual improvised-weapon proficiency. Throwing an object without Thrown, or using a ranged weapon in melee, uses the improvised rule; improvised throwing range is 20/60 feet.

**Silvering.** Silver one weapon or ten ammunition units for 100 gp. This supplies actual silver eligibility only, with no automatic magic tag, attack bonus or damage bonus.

## Equipment grade

An explicit V6 equipment grade changes the item's statistics and mundane baseline price as below. Structural damage does not automatically change grade. Ordinary grade does not create magical eligibility. Only one equipment grade applies.

| Grade | Worn armor | Weapon | Baseline price |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Crude | −1 AC; half normal structural HP, rounded down, minimum 1 | −1 attack; natural-1 break check | ×0.5, new price completion |
| Standard | Printed profile | Printed profile | ×1 |
| Fine | +1 AC | +1 attack | ×3 |
| Superior | +2 AC; 80% ordinary weight | +2 attack; one extra single base weapon die on a critical | ×10 |
| Exceptional | +3 AC; 70% ordinary weight | +3 attack; two extra single base weapon dice on a critical | ×25 |

Armor grade applies to worn suits, not shield AC. A future explicit crafted output may define its own shield improvements. Weapons gain no generic flat damage bonus. On a critical, double the ordinary damage dice, then add the quality dice once; a greatsword's extra quality die is one d6, not its entire 2d6 profile. A Crude weapon's natural-1 attack rolls d100: 5 or less sets structural HP to zero and breaks it. Its material remains recoverable.

Exceptional's source “minor special property” provides no additional effect until an exact property is authored. No free random enchantment is implied. Repair preserves the surviving item's recorded grade unless an exact procedure changes it.

Keep three distinct records:

| Record | What it affects |
|---|---|
| V6 equipment grade | The above combat statistics and mundane baseline price |
| Crafting outcome such as Exceptional or Masterwork | That recipe's actual yield, charges, catalyst or result procedure |
| Core artifact-market Quality | One factor inside the actual artifact valuation formula |

A successful craft does not automatically acquire a V6 grade. A recipe must explicitly specify its resulting equipment grade. Forge Dwarf's retained “exceptional quality” label remains nonnumeric until the crafting integration defines its actual result interaction. Do not multiply all three systems merely because they use similar words.

## Carrying and containers

Use the reviewed [body and equipment guide](BODY_EQUIPMENT_PLAYTEST.md) for actual pounds, Load, size, casualty transport and melded equipment. Keep known physical mass distinct from Load and Extraction Class. Known-weight-only items use pounds divided by ten as provisional Load. Every item's weight and Load count once, including containers and contents. An inclusive gross value covers only the contents it names; it does not erase unrelated quantities or supplies.

An ink bottle also uses the small-fluid-container 0.1-lb profile. Narrative background small tokens use the separately stated 0.1-lb accessory profile. Record these as operational estimates, keeping the original source weight null. Larger unweighed items, mounts, vehicles and services do not use this fallback. Do not round away accumulated small items. Coins of every ordinary denomination use fifty coins per pound as a new game-weight convention.

## Fit and size

Equipment must fit its intended user. Small/Medium and Tiny fitted ordinary items retain the listed price, mass and profile in this candidate; fit creates no unprinted benefit. **New Large sizing:** ordinary armor, shields and weapons use twice the baseline price, twice the weight and twice generic structural HP, with the same AC, damage, reach and properties unless an exact source says otherwise.

A fixed creation package includes the necessary fitting without an added creation charge. Its sized baseline matters for replacement, resale and repair. On the wealth route, buy the fitted item at its actual sized price. Powerful Build changes only its expressly permitted carrying calculations, not item size or weapon damage. It does not resize equipment on transformation; the reviewed body/equipment rules govern that transition.

## Object damage and Sunder

These are **new mundane object procedures** completing the Core Sunder interface. A target must be one actual separately tracked object or explicitly modeled separable component. Naming an armor strap, pin, lace, hinge or seam does not create a cheaper independent target. Construct joints remain subject to the creature's actual stat block.

Sunder uses an actual legal weapon attack against one perceived reachable object/component, consuming one attack when taking the Attack Action. It grants no extra attack or free damage to a wearer. The attacker cannot target a hidden component through a creature or solid obstruction. Against worn/held gear, target AC is the higher of the material AC and wearer's current AC. Unattended gear uses material AC. Ordinary attack eligibility and printed object-target exclusions still apply.

Worn/carried inventory is never damaged merely because its creature is hit. An attack, spell or hazard damages equipment only if its actual rule expressly targets/includes objects, structures, carried gear or that item. Sunder never also damages the wearer unless another source expressly says so.

| Load-bearing material | AC |
|---|---:|
| Cloth, paper, rope | 11 |
| Glass, crystal | 13 |
| Wood, leather | 15 |
| Stone | 17 |
| Iron, steel | 19 |

For a mixed object, establish its dominant load-bearing material before damage. Do not retroactively select a material after the attack roll.

| Mundane object | Maximum structural HP |
|---|---:|
| Tiny vial or letter | 1 |
| Tool, weapon or focus | 5 |
| Ordinary shield | 10 |
| Tower shield | 15 |
| Light armor | 10 |

| Heavy armor | 20 |
| Ordinary chest or door | 20 |
| Cart | 30 |
| Targeted rope segment | 5 |
| Targeted chain segment | 10 |

Large-sized wearable/item generic HP doubles; Crude armor's half-HP rule then applies. Specific source/card durability wins, including a net's AC 10/5 slashing destruction rule and Tinker gnome devices' AC 5/HP 1. The ordinary buckler is a shield using 10 generic HP. Objects outside a listed or genuinely comparable category need an actual assigned profile before targeted durability resolution; no automatic structure or artifact certification is implied.

Objects are immune to poison and psychic damage unless an exact rule says otherwise. Establish physical damage compatibility before rolling: a sword cannot chop a liquid. Do not invent retroactive resistances. Use ordinary attack damage and applicable weapon properties; creature-only conditions and resource triggers do not become object-eligible.

At zero structural HP, the object is **Broken** and loses usable AC, weapon, focus, tool or other functional benefits. It still exists, occupies its hand/slot/container and retains mass until dropped, removed or repaired. Broken armor does not disappear. No negative HP, duplicated loot, automatic explosion, passive regeneration or repair from ordinary rest. Magic items/artifacts need their own durability; no mundane default is assigned to unresolved magical gear. True Polymorph's independently excluded-object rules still apply even when an object has HP.

A Broken item grants none of its positive usable benefits, but remains physically worn, held or strapped until removed. It continues to occupy its hand/slot and contribute weight/Load, and retains physical drawbacks from still wearing or carrying it: applicable don/doff requirements, armor Stealth disadvantage, STR-minimum movement penalty and nonproficiency consequences. A Broken shield grants no AC bonus but still occupies its actual hand/forearm until doffed. A specific source may state otherwise.

## Repair

**Self-repair — new procedure.** Use a relevant trained toolkit and workable materials. Materials cost 25% of the item's current mundane equipment-grade baseline purchase price before temporary market/standing adjustments. Include its actual size. Superior uses Standard price ×10, then 25%; Crude uses ×0.5. An item without an equipment grade uses its ordinary listed baseline.

Spend `max(1 hour, maximum structural HP / 10 hours)` active work and make the appropriate DC 10 tool task check. Success restores structural HP to maximum. Failure consumes the time and materials; another attempt requires fresh payment. No unprinted extra effect arises from a task critical result. Repair at zero HP needs the recoverable main body. It cannot recreate consumed potions, expended ammunition, burnt pages or a missing item. Preserve recorded grade; magical/artifact reconstruction requires its own rule.

**Core dungeon repair service.** Where an appropriate service is actually available, Tier I–III costs 10% and Tier IV–VI costs 15% of the item's applicable undamaged valuation immediately before structural-damage discounting. For an ordinary mundane item, use its legitimate current size and equipment-grade baseline value before damage; for an actual artifact or magic item, use its separately established applicable valuation. Do not apply a damaged-resale discount, buyer-standing sale fraction or transaction-sale penalty to the repair-price base. This percentage is the complete quoted repair service unless that service expressly lists additional costs. Do not automatically add the self-repair material price. The service cannot recreate a consumed item, missing main body, expended ammunition, burnt pages or an unresolved destroyed magical/artifact item. These source service prices do not establish full transport or Tier VI content readiness.

## Purchase and sale

Preserve the source exchange rates: 1 sp = 10 cp, 1 gp = 100 cp, 1 pp = 1,000 cp, and 1 Mithril Mark = 500 cp = 5 gp. Regional Embermark is 2 sp, Soulshard 1 gp and Tidesilver 1 sp where accepted; a Verdant Leaf has no universal GP price. Special soul-linked currency and its origin claims are outside this mundane integration.

An actual available seller/buyer and a legitimate agreed transaction are required. No infinite catalog inventory, guaranteed buyback or universal acceptance. Source trade goods retain their actual units. Ordinary undamaged mundane equipment sells at the following fraction of its actual equipment-grade baseline price, including legitimate item sizing, to that buyer:

| Qualitative standing | Sale fraction |
|---|---:|
| Honored | 75% |
| Liked | 60% |
| Neutral | 50% |
| Disliked | 40% |
| Hated | 25%, or refusal |

Use the actual qualitative relationship to that buyer. This does not choose between the competing historical numeric reputation scales. Damaged equipment needs an explicit quoted valuation; no percentage creates a minimum sale. Gems, jewelry and art can sell at full assessed value only with an actual suitable buyer. Monster-used gear may have no saleable market under the source rule. Trade goods use the source barter context and actual deal; relabeling an assembled kit or quality cannot create a guaranteed arbitrage transaction.

**New rounding:** convert the final transaction price to cp. A purchase price paid by the character rounds up to the nearest cp. Sale proceeds paid to the character round down to the nearest cp, minimum zero. Round only after all applicable modifiers, not at intermediate multiplication steps.

Recipe material/component units retain their own explicit identities and costs until reconciled. A Crafting crystal vial priced at 2 gp is not silently rewritten to the Player catalog's 5 gp retail vial, or vice versa. Existing recipe totals remain intact.

Core's actual artifact formula is `base function value × Gate tier multiplier × market Quality × provenance × demand × legality − actual stabilization, transport, tax and liability costs`. It wins conflicting historical artifact-sale formulas. It is not applied to an ordinary sword simply because recovered in the dungeon. This guide records that boundary without certifying unfinished artifact valuation, factions, tax, wages, demand, commissions or all crafting outputs.

### Outside initiative and transitions — NEW exact clarification

Use the existing game timeline, measured in elapsed game seconds. Do not use real-world table time or advance the game clock by six seconds merely because another creature takes a turn.

**Outside-window origin.** For each physical item with a printed elemental rider, record `outsideWindowOriginTime` when its first relevant lawful, non-copied weapon attack is resolved genuinely outside initiative. Establish this origin whether that attack hits or misses and whether its rider is used or declined. An attack routed into an encounter before resolution does not establish an outside origin.

Once established, preserve that origin for the same physical item. At game time `t`, its outside-window index is:

`floor((t − outsideWindowOriginTime) / 6)`

Window `k` includes its starting boundary and excludes its ending boundary:

`[outsideWindowOriginTime + 6k, outsideWindowOriginTime + 6(k + 1))`

The item can use its rider at most once in each such window. Skipped windows supply no accumulated uses. Another attack, ownership transfer, suppression, breakage, repair, melding or initiative-mode change does not reanchor the windows or clear a spent window.

This is a fixed-window allowance, not an ordinary rolling six-second cooldown measured from every outside trigger. Separate transition restrictions below still apply. The window procedure grants no attack, action, acceleration or permission to strike a target.

**Entering initiative after an outside trigger.** When initiative begins governing the item, examine its most recent actual rider trigger. If that trigger occurred outside initiative at time `L`, and entry occurs before `L + 6`, establish a transition guard associated with that specific trigger event. Its assignment deadline is `L + 6`.

If an actual turn is already running at entry, immediately assign the guard to that host turn. Otherwise assign it when the first genuine regular or extra turn begins before the deadline. Mark the item’s rider budget for that actual turn as spent immediately, whether or not anyone attacks with, holds, transfers or attempts to activate the item during that turn.

The guard does not wait for an attack, hit, attempted activation, the wielder’s own turn or an eligible wielder. Assignment itself does not constitute a rider trigger and does not change `lastElementalRiderTriggerTime`.

Once assigned, that turn’s budget remains spent for the entire actual turn, including any portion occurring after the assignment deadline. The guard does not carry forward to another turn merely because the item was unused. Subsequent genuine turns have their normal independent budgets, even if they occur before the former assignment deadline.

If no actual turn has received the guard when the deadline is reached, the unassigned guard expires. A turn first becoming applicable exactly at or after the deadline is not blocked by that expired guard.

**Actual turn identity.** Use the identity of the actual host turn, not merely the acting creature, a numbered round, or the most recently observed turn. Reactions and readied attacks use their host turn. A genuine extra turn has its own identity. Resuming an interrupted parent turn resumes that parent’s existing budget; it does not reopen it.

Virtual duration markers, empty initiative slots and bookkeeping-only mode changes are not additional actual turns. While initiative governs but no actual turn is running, an otherwise lawful attack receives no elemental-rider allowance from this procedure. It cannot borrow an outside window. Its ordinary weapon effects still follow their own rules.

**Leaving initiative.** If the item’s most recent actual rider trigger occurred in initiative at time `L`, another outside rider use is prohibited before `L + 6`. At or after that deadline, the relevant anchored outside window must also be unspent. Ending initiative does not itself establish a new origin or provide a rider use.

Preserve the item’s outside-window record, actual-turn expenditure records needed for continuing or resumable turns, and transition-guard history across mode changes. A bookkeeping toggle must not manufacture another turn identity, erase expenditure, or recreate an already assigned guard as a fresh resource. Associate a guard with its originating trigger event so repeated entry does not move or duplicate that guard.

**Actual expenditure.** On a qualifying hit, the attacking creature chooses whether to use the rider before finalizing damage. An accepted legal use spends the applicable item budget and records the actual trigger time, trigger context and trigger-event identity before damage mitigation. Immunity or mitigation reducing that component to zero does not refund the use.

A miss, declined rider, ineligible attack, or suppressed/unavailable rider does not spend an actual rider use. None resets an established window or removes a transition guard. Suppression ending can make an otherwise unspent, unblocked budget usable; it cannot reopen a spent or guarded budget.
