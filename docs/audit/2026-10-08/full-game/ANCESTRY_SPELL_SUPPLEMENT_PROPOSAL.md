# Proposed ancestry spell dependency supplement

UNREVIEWED candidates. Ten names are granted in Player ancestry traits but have no ordinary catalog record. Seven more have preserved Core records without an approved operational procedure. The latter keep existing IDs and original class access; additions do not acquire ordinary class lists merely from their spell names. Do not merge names with vaguely similar existing spells. These are authored Decennium playtest proposals, not claims about external games.

Unless a card says otherwise: no automatic level scaling; one actual cast; normal attack d20+PB+the selected source's casting modifier, DC8+PB+that modifier, existing concentration and condition rules, no effect on a countered cast. Caster needs legal V/S/F components as listed. Unwilling targets require the printed attack/save. Damaging cards specify success results. Cannot target behind total cover unless specifically allowed. Actual life-state healing rules apply. A limited innate source pays its use instead of an RP price; an ordinary existing catalog source pays its actual rank cost. Additions below are ancestry-grant-only pending any separately reviewed other access. Counter/dispelling rank and school are recorded here.

## Seven existing ordinary spell identities

**Shape Water — Transmutation rank 0.** Core paragraph 9897: Action, 5 ft, Instantaneous, V/S, manipulate water in 5 ft cube. NEW exact mode: choose one contiguous quantity of unattended nonmagical liquid water fitting a 5 ft cube within 5 ft. Move it up to 5 ft along an unobstructed path into available space or reshape its surface harmlessly; gravity immediately resumes after resolution. Cannot freeze, create, purify, split an occupied body, lift a creature, deal damage, grant a swimming speed or maintain unsupported shapes. It moves actual water, including actual potable water, without fabricating abstract Supply. No save, no concentration, no lasting supernatural effect, no targeting worn/carried containers without owner's willingness.

**Mold Earth — Transmutation rank 0.** Core 9898: Action, 5 ft, Instantaneous, V/S, reshape earth in 5 ft cube. Select unattended loose earth/sand fitting 5 ft cube; excavate, mound or smooth it into vacant contiguous space up to 5 ft away, subject to gravity/support afterward. Cannot manipulate worked stone, solid rock, foundations, a creature's occupied space, or bury/crush a creature; cannot move valuables separately or turn earth into materials. No direct damage, no concentration. Ordinary earthworks remain and may have ordinary terrain consequences. Structural collapse cannot be manufactured in the same cast by ignoring the foundation exclusion.

**Gust — Transmutation rank 0.** Core 9900: Action, 30 ft, Instantaneous, V/S, small burst of wind. Choose one visible Medium-or-smaller creature, one unattended object<=5 lb, or a harmless air effect in 5 ft cube within 30 ft. Creature makes STR save; failure pushes 5 ft directly away from caster into legal space, success no movement. No prone or damage; hazard consequences and forced-movement opportunity rules apply normally. Object moves 5 ft away along clear path, no attack damage. Harmless mode briefly disperses ordinary smoke in 5 ft cube or extinguishes one candle; does not end a magical cloud, defeat weather, provide ongoing air, or change a sound spell. No concentration.

**Minor Illusion — Illusion rank 0.** Core 9636: Action, 30 ft, concentration<=1 minute, V/S, sound or object image. Choose either a stationary image of one object fitting 5 ft cube in range or a sound originating at one point in range. Image has no light, weight, smell, touch, movement or actual cover; sound may be a simple noise or short repeated phrase at volume no louder than ordinary speaking. It does not impersonate a voice not actually heard. Physical interaction reveals image to that observer; deliberate Action INT(Investigation) vs spell DC discerns either mode. An informed observer perceives through it. No Charmed/Frightened, automatic disadvantage or false control of another creature. It may create a believable distraction under ordinary checks. One concentration, no automatic damage/effect from apparent objects.

**Disguise Self — Illusion rank 1.** Core 9654: Action, Self, 1 h, V/S/F. Change visible appearance of your body and worn/carried gear; same basic limb arrangement and size category, apparent height up to 1 ft different. No physical change, voice change, reach/AC/proficiency grant, identity records or illusion on items after they leave your possession. Touch detects a mismatch; observer may use Action INT(Investigation) vs spell DC to recognize disguise. No concentration. Appearance can include an actual studied individual but does not supply unknown mannerisms/passwords. Recast replaces previous disguise without stacking. Ends on expiry, dispel or voluntary dismissal as Action.

**Water Breathing — Transmutation rank 3.** Core 9939: Action, 60 ft, V/S/F, up to 10 creatures; source Instantaneous lacks benefit lifetime. NEW select up to 10 willing visible living creatures in range; each retains normal respiration and can breathe water for 24 elapsed hours. No concentration, no swimming speed, temperature/depth immunity, freedom of movement or immunity to suffocation outside breathable air/water. Effect does not create water or remove poison. Casting duration instantaneous; persistent benefit can be dispelled on an affected creature individually and carries original rank/school. Recasting refreshes duration on selected targets only, no doubled benefits.

**Vampiric Touch — Necromancy rank 3.** Core 9808: Action, Touch, Instantaneous, V/S/F, 3d6 necrotic and heal half damage. One melee spell attack against a living creature in actual touch reach. Hit 3d6 necrotic, miss 0 and no heal; after damage resolution heal yourself floor(actualDamageDealt/2), using legal terminal damage branches without double count/overkill. No ongoing repeat attack permission or concentration is invented; source is one Action, instantaneous. Ordinary healing modifiers/prohibitions/caps apply. Healing is one secondary result and cannot reproduce this attack or generate a second Vampiric Touch healing result for the same cast. Ordinary unrelated eligible triggers remain subject to their own restrictions.

## Ten newly authored missing named spell procedures

**Create or Destroy Water — Transmutation rank 1; new ID create-or-destroy-water.** Action, 30 ft, V/S, instantaneous, no concentration. Choose creation or destruction. Create up to 10 US gallons of clean drinkable water in one or more open unattended/willingly held containers wholly within a 5 ft cube you see, limited by their actual capacity; or destroy up to 10 gallons of unattended nonmagical exposed water within such a cube. No water inside creatures, blood, potions, acid, seawater desalination by selective fractions, hostile held containers or magical fluid. No damage/flood attack/drowning resolution. Created water persists normally; it meets actual drinking requirements at real volume but never also creates food/fuel/general Supply. No save. If containers lack capacity create only the amount that fits.

**Faerie Fire — Evocation rank 1; new ID faerie-fire.** Action, 60 ft, V/S, concentration<=1 minute. Select 20 ft cube with visible center in range; every creature in area DEX save, failures are outlined in dim light 10 ft; unattended objects in area are outlined automatically. Attacks against an outlined creature have advantage if attacker can actually see it; its Invisibility cannot conceal it visually while outlined. Does not remove the condition, reveal through walls, eliminate total cover or grant magic tracking after effect ends. Successful save has no effect. No repeated save, no damage, no safe allies exemption. Cast once records affected objects/creatures; later entry not affected. Outline ends with concentration/expiry/dispel.

**Darkness — Evocation rank 2; new ID darkness.** Action, 60 ft, V/S/F, concentration<=10 minutes. Stationary 15 ft radius sphere centered on a point you can see in range, blocked by total cover. Opaque magical darkness prevents sight through or within the area for ordinary vision and darkvision; an explicit see-through-magical-darkness feature still works. Extinguishes neither physical fires nor permanent light; overlapping ordinary/nonmagical light cannot illuminate it. Against magical illumination, highest actual generating spell rank governs overlap (light wins tie); a rankless feature must explicitly illuminate magical darkness to override. This is a NEW explicit light interaction requiring review, not automatic dispelling. It doesn't create silence, Blind condition, damage, cover against nonvisual targeting or a new plane. Creatures leave normally. No save; ends concentration/expiry/dispel.

**Speak with Plants — Divination rank 3; new ID speak-with-plants.** Action, Self(30 ft radius communication), V/S, 10 minutes, no concentration. Exchange simple spoken questions/responses with actual plants within 30 ft; innate Cavern Mystic limits this to fungi. They can relay limited impressions of events they could locally sense in the preceding 24 h, relative movement and nearby growing conditions. No remote memory network, automatic identification of names/species/magic, compelled truth from intelligent plant creatures, movement control or new plant souls. Plant creatures remain autonomous participants; noncreature plants convey sensory impressions as the spell's information procedure without adjudicating ontology. No save for communication, no guaranteed answers to unknowable questions.

**Produce Flame — Evocation rank 0; new ID produce-flame.** Action, Self or 30 ft attack, V/S, up to 10 minutes, no concentration. Either create one harmless flame in your hand illuminating bright 10 ft/dim 10 ft further, or make one ranged spell attack at a visible creature within 30 ft for 1d8 fire on hit/0 miss. Choosing attack extinguishes any flame from this spell; may directly attack without preparing one. Casting another light mode replaces your previous light. Light does not damage held gear/ignite automatically; an Action can ignite one unattended mundane combustible touch object, ending flame; never hostile worn/carried gear. Every new casting updates casting history; using an existing flame to light kindling is not another cast. No automatic level scaling.

**Misty Step — Conjuration rank 2; new ID misty-step.** Bonus Action, Self, V, instantaneous, no concentration. Teleport yourself and normally carried gear up to 30 ft to an unoccupied space you can see and fit within. No passenger, no traversal of intervening hazards, no exit from an explicit teleport-blocking effect. Normal destination hazards apply. Does not clear conditions whose actual attachments continue; may end restraint solely from physical position if you leave its actual grip/fixture. Does not grant ethereal travel or plane access. No attack/save.

**Thorn Whip — Transmutation rank 0; new ID thorn-whip.** Action, 30 ft, V/S, instantaneous, no concentration. One spell-local 30 ft melee spell attack against a visible creature; hit 1d6 piercing, miss 0. On hit may pull a Large-or-smaller target up to 10 ft straight toward you to a fitting unoccupied space; stop at obstruction, no pass-through creatures/walls, use ordinary forced movement/hazard rules, no invented prone. The whip exists only during resolution, not persistent rope/grapple/material. No level scaling. Cannot target gear or select multiple creatures.

**Goodberry — Transmutation rank 1; new ID goodberry.** Action, Touch, V/S, instantaneous creation; magical potency 24 h, no concentration. Create 10 berries in your empty hand or an open willingly held container; each berry consumed as Action by living creature or administered as Action to a willing/helpless living creature within reach heals 1 HP under ordinary healing rules and provides one day's ordinary food for one recipient. No water/fuel/shelter, no additional nutrition from eating multiple. Track each berry, consume exactly once, potency expires 24 h from cast; no stockpile past that deadline, no product duplication/copying. Source healing flat 1 is not a die. Ordinary blocked healing does not return berry, no dead revival. Feeding an unconscious creature requires safe administration/not blocked by sealed mouth or aspirating conditions; does not grant generic remote healing. Creating food grants actual specified food; convert to numbered recipients/day, never double credit abstract Supply.

**Speak with Animals — Divination rank 1; new ID speak-with-animals.** Action, Self, V/S, 10 minutes, no concentration. Understand and communicate basic spoken ideas with Beasts within ordinary auditory range which can hear you. Knowledge/language complexity limited by actual creature experience/intelligence. No Charmed, domination, forced truth, automatic favor or summon. Ordinary social checks may apply to requests. Does not add language proficiency or expand to other creature types. No save for communication.

**Blood Manipulation — Transmutation rank 0; new ID blood-manipulation.** Action, 5 ft, V/S, instantaneous, no concentration. Move or shape up to one pint of exposed unattended blood outside a living creature up to 5 ft into an open container or harmless pattern. Cannot target blood inside creatures, a worn bandage, hostile held blood, magic items, create blood, diagnose identity/disease, transmit damage, heal, stabilize, stop Bleed, replenish RP/RC, create viable transfusion/reagents or animate a creature. Actual existing blood remains and decays normally; gravity applies after resolution. No attack/save. This supplies a deliberately minor utility role for the named cantrip, without silently creating free hemomancy combat.
