# Actual GPT source conference

The twelve are settled for **authored tabletop card drafting** as follows. Conflicting source placements remain preserved as evidence; nothing here deletes them or grants cross-placement powers.

1. **Protective Sphere** — **Action; 10 ft; V/S/F; concentration, up to 1 minute.** Choose a fixed point you can perceive within range; create a **10-foot-radius stationary sphere**. Its boundary blocks physical projectiles and Tiny/Small creatures from crossing **either direction**. A projectile is a discrete physical object fired, thrown, launched, or explicitly propelled as a projectile; not rays, beams, gas, sound, spell effects without a projectile, or ordinary falling debris. Medium+ creatures cross normally. Worn/held/carried objects travel with their bearer and do not make a Medium+ creature ineligible; carrying a Tiny/Small **creature** does not bypass that creature restriction. The boundary cannot initially intersect a Tiny/Small creature’s occupied space; such a creature may begin wholly inside or wholly outside. Creation causes no damage/displacement. Stopped projectiles simply stop at the boundary, then ordinary gravity applies. The sphere is not ground, floor, platform, cover, or structural support and gives no one-way firing privilege. **NEW geometry/boundary ruling.**

2. **Dispel Resonance** — **Action; 30 ft; V/S/F; instantaneous.** Target one perceived ongoing magical effect generated by an actual **rank-0 or rank-1 spell cast**. No save/check. End that selected effect. Eligibility follows the **generating cast’s actual rank**, not the dispeller and not a broad “Tier 1” bucket. Tier 1 does not mean ranks 1–4. Cannot remove class engines, creature traits, permanent magic items/enchantments, curses unless themselves an eligible ongoing spell effect, or repair damage/consequences already resolved. No retroactive undo. Dispel Minor retains its own harmless-cosmetic exception. **Operational reconciliation: “1st level or lower” controls; ambiguous Tier wording is preserved but not broadened.**

3. **Binding Circle** — **Action; 5 ft; V/S/F; concentration, up to 1 minute.** Choose a fixed point on a solid surface within range; create a **5-foot-radius circle** whose boundary extends **10 feet vertically** above that surface. Only creatures carrying an explicit current source-authorized **extraplanar** record/tag are affected. No inference from appearance, faction, species name, or lore. Such creatures cannot physically cross the boundary. No save is added because the source supplies none. The circle does not cost Actions, suppress magic, prevent attacks, or hinder movement wholly inside/outside. Teleportation or planar relocation does not physically cross the boundary and is not stopped absent another exact rule; likewise passing below/above the bounded wall is outside this spell. Boundary cannot initially intersect an affected creature. **NEW conflict ruling:** ongoing 1-minute concentration replaces unusable Instantaneous metadata; “5-ft circle” means 5-ft radius.

4. **Armor of Faith** — **Action; 30 ft; one willing creature you perceive, including self; V/S/F; 1 hour; no concentration.** Target gains **+3 AC**. Spectral armor is only a magical AC bonus: no item, armor proficiency, slot, hand, Strength requirement, weight, don/doff, price, or resale. Same-stat strongest-flat-bonus rule applies, so it does not add to Shield, Warding Bond AC, Alignment Shield, etc.; each effect otherwise keeps its own duration.

5. **Purify Food and Drink** — **Action; 30 ft; V/S/F; instantaneous.** Target one discrete batch/container containing up to **5 lb of food or 1 gallon of drink**. Remove ordinary nonmagical poison and disease-causing contamination/pathogens from that consumable. A mundane alchemical poison deliberately mixed into valid food/drink qualifies; magical poison, curses, Corruption, supernatural contamination, or enchantments do not. Does not cure a creature already poisoned/diseased, turn poison stock into food, create supplies, restore abstract ration/expedition resources, manufacture medicine, or yield recovered poison/pathogens as crafting resources. **NEW finite capacity.**

6. **Detect Poison** — keep **one ID with two placement-specific modes**; never give rank-0 casters rank-1 information.

   **Divination rank 0:** Action; 30 ft; one creature or object you perceive; V/S; instantaneous. Learn only whether it currently contains or is affected by an explicit poison/Poison record. No identity, type, dose, DC, severity, source, cure, onset, or duration.

   **Abjuration rank 1:** Action; self; 30-ft sensing radius with LoE; V/S/F; instantaneous. Learn whether qualifying poison is present and the **actual source-authorized poison type/name/category records present**. Does not identify which creature/object carries which poison, exact location, dose, DC, severity, cure, or unrelated medical conditions. Total cover blocks sensing. A generic Poisoned condition counts only when its provenance is actually poison-linked.

7. **Repulsion Field** — only the **rank-1 placement becomes operational**. **Action; self; 5-ft radius; V/S/F; instantaneous.** Push each unattended, unsecured **Small-or-smaller object ≤10 lb** currently in the area up to 5 ft directly away, stopping before solid terrain/illegal placement. Worn, held, attached, restrained, locked, anchored, or secured objects are ineligible; floor/table support alone is not “secured.” No creatures, disarm, projectile Reaction, attack interception, collision damage, or chain push. Ordinary Falling may begin if support is lost. The separate **rank-6 CHA-save creature version stays preserved but blocked/not selectable**; higher level or upcasting never grants it.

8. **Warding Bond** — current operational version is **rank 1 / 2 RP**; rank-2 duplicate remains evidence only. **Action; 30 ft; one willing living ally other than self; V/S/F; concentration up to 1 minute.** Ally gains **+1 AC and +1 saves**, subject to strongest-flat-bonus rules.

   Damage is a **mandatory post-defense conserved split**. After the ally’s ordinary defenses and legal prevention, before temp HP, if positive damage remains:
   - caster receives `floor(remaining/2)`;
   - ally keeps the remainder.

   A 1-damage packet transfers 0. Transferred damage keeps type, receives **no second mitigation/prevention**, cannot transfer again, then uses caster temp HP/HP and normal life consequences. Ally retains original hit/save/critical identity; caster was not hit and did not fail the save.

   Use the existing **one-transfer-operation-per-root** rule. A prior pre-mitigation split preempts it; same-stage mandatory conflicts follow the existing original-target arbitration. Only one Warding Bond may involve a creature at once, whether caster or protected ally. Initial 30-ft range/LoE is required; **no ongoing distance or LoE tether is invented afterward**. Reaching 0 HP alone does not end it; death or concentration ending does. Finish the current damage split before any concentration failure caused by that packet ends the bond.

   This ordinary spell **does not use or enlarge the Paladin redirection round ledger**, does not authorize Paladin healing/resource riders, and imports none of the named Paladin transfer-source proc permissions.

9. **Alignment Shield** — retain **two placement modes under one ID**. “Opposed alignment” uses only explicit canonical alignment records on the Lawful–Neutral–Chaotic and Good–Neutral–Evil axes. Opposition exists when at least one axis is exact opposite: Lawful↔Chaotic or Good↔Evil. Neutral is not opposed; missing/unknown gives no bonus. Never infer alignment from ancestry, faction, reputation, faith, appearance, or behavior.

   **Abjuration rank 1:** Action; 30 ft; one willing creature; V/S/F; **concentration up to 1 minute**; +2 AC against attack rolls from explicitly opposed-alignment creatures.

   **Conjuration rank 3:** Action; 60 ft; one willing creature; V/S/F; concentration up to 1 minute; same bounded **+2 AC** against those attacks. “Summon a shield” is spectral only: no equipment, item HP, hand, cover, projectile blocking, resale, or immunity.

   Same-stat strongest-flat-bonus rule applies. **NEW conflict rulings:** rank-1 Instantaneous becomes finite 1-minute concentration; rank-3 “blocks attacks” is bounded to +2 AC rather than immunity. Each class gets only its listed placement.

10. **Corruption Ward** — **Action; 30 ft; one willing creature including self; V/S/F; 1 hour; no concentration.** Target has advantage on **actual saving throws explicitly caused by an effect carrying a canonical Corruption tag/state/source**. It creates no save where none exists; does not remove Corruption, reduce values, cure consequences, alter thresholds, or grant immunity. Advantage does not stack. One active Corruption Ward per target across all casters; later application replaces the prior one and owns its own 3600-second endpoint.

11. **Resonance Lock** — **Action; 30 ft; one perceived creature; V/S/F; WIS save; 1 hostile round = first future target regular-turn end.** Pay before save. On failure, target cannot make a payment that **spends RP** until that deadline. It does not remove existing RP, block RC/BT/Harmony/charges/ammo/other resources, undo completed costs, cancel already-resolved effects, prohibit weapons/non-RP actions, or stop genuine 0-RP abilities/cantrips. If an ongoing effect reaches a required RP upkeep during the lock, the payment cannot be made; that effect then follows its own printed missed-upkeep/end rule.

   This is **not whole-Action hard denial**, so it does not consume the shared hard-denial ledger. Instead add a narrow **source-character/target/encounter Resonance Lock guard**: once that target actually **fails** this character’s Resonance Lock save, that same character cannot successfully impose Resonance Lock on that target again that encounter. Successful saves do not spend the guard. Normal boss/save defenses remain.

12. **Faction Seal** — **Action; 30 ft; V/S/F; fixed 10-ft-radius, 10-ft-high area; 1 hour; no concentration.** Choose one exact canonical `factionId` that an actual campaign/source rule authorizes the caster to invoke. Naming a faction does not create membership, standing, credentials, authority, or permission.

   While active, the visible seal **flares** whenever at least one creature occupying the area with LoE to the seal has an actual reputation record of **−3, −2, or −1** for that faction. Score 0+ does not trigger; missing reputation is **unknown, not negative**. The flare reports only that at least one qualifying negative-standing creature is present. It does not identify creature, square, disguise, true identity, alignment, intent, statistics, or exact score. Invisible/hidden creatures can trigger it, but it does not reveal their position or cancel invisibility. Teleportation counts once the creature actually occupies the area. It never changes faction standing. **NEW finite area/duration/reveal-cue ruling replacing unusable Instantaneous area metadata.**

**Disposition:** all twelve are **SOURCE-RECONCILED / DRAFTABLE for exact card authoring**. The catalog remains **95 reviewed operational drafts** until the authored cards receive final manuscript review. The four placement conflicts remain preserved explicitly: Detect Poison dual modes, Repulsion Field rank-6 blocked variant, Warding Bond rank-2 duplicate, and Alignment Shield dual placement modes.
