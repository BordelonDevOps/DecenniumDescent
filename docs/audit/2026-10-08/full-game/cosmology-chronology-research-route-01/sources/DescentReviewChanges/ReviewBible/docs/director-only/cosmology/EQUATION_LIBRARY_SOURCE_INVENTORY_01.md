Citadel and Academy are founded in later narrated events. Current campaign placement cannot be inferred from the mere existence of these passages.

- **LR06:** Physical preservation, translation, functional understanding, knowledge taint, proximity taint and initiation of a Hunt are distinct questions. Silent reading, tone-stones, copied maps or inherited books do not answer the other questions automatically.
- **LR07:** Secondary Director civic-archive and Custodian hooks are suggestions and remain subordinate to original cosmology plus explicit owner corrections. They cannot settle the builder or allow guaranteed public knowledge beyond the agreed revelation ceiling.
