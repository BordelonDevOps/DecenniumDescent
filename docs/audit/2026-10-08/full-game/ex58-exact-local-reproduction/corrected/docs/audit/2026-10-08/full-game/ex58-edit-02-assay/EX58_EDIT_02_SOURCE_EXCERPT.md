## Original §17, Coin assay and reminting

The following source text is unchanged. Line numbers refer to the complete frozen Markdown member.

L613: ### Coin assay and reminting
L614: 
L615: Iron Ledger assay houses use Investigation, relevant tools, or mercantile proficiency. Standard DCs are 10 for obvious shaving, 13 for professional clipping or mixed weight, 16 for plated or sophisticated counterfeit coins, and 19 for a masterwork false die or mint. Assay costs 1% of the lot's face value, minimum 1 gp. Reminting costs 2% of recovered metal value. The holder receives the actual legitimate metal value minus fees.
L616: 
L617: Confirmed counterfeit coin or plated false currency is seized in full. Genuine coins or legitimate metal in the same documented lot are returned after fees. An innocent holder with documented provenance pays no criminal fine. A knowing holder pays the greater of 10% of the false lot's face value or the assay and remint fees.
