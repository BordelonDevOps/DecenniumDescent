# Social scenes and faction turns proposal

**Draft for the next GPT conference. Unsent and unadopted.** This proposal answers SO-01 through SO-12 and recommends one resolution for the shared FL-23 reputation conflict. It leaves the current rules and all original sources intact. The new decisions below require joint review before they enter the playable candidate.

The intended result is one ordinary social procedure with visible stakes, meaningful institutional relationships and rivals that spend real time and resources. Use the [complete source audit](SOCIAL_FACTION_SOURCE_AUDIT.md), [original paragraphs and tables](SOCIAL_FACTION_SOURCE_PREPARATION.json) and [source-link map](SOCIAL_FACTION_SOURCE_LINK_MAP.json) alongside this proposal. The numerical reputation recommendation is deliberately explicit because the three source scales cannot all govern one score.

## Establish what the scene can change

**SO-01 and SO-09. Preserve the Director frame; new completion of its declaration procedure.**

Before a check, record the five source fields: Want, Leverage, Limit, Clock and Witness. The acting player declares what their character does and the concrete concession or information sought. The Director identifies the person or institution able to supply it. State the communication channel, the relevant uncertainty, the fictional duration and the consequences of success and failure that the character can reasonably foresee. The Director may keep unknown facts private, but must not conceal the fact that the proposed approach cannot obtain the requested result.

A willing person with the necessary authority can agree without a roll. An impossible request, absent decision-maker, unavailable resource or established non-negotiable limit does not become possible through a high total. The Director explains the observable obstacle and allows the player to revise the approach before committing resources. A change in leverage can change a limit; a different speaker alone cannot.

Once a legal check with declared stakes is committed, honor its result. Do not retroactively invent a secret limit merely to cancel a success. A successful request obtains exactly the stated agreement or information within the other party's actual authority. It creates no unseen property, retroactive permission or obligation for a person who was never represented.

Players may describe their approach plainly, in writing or through roleplay. Performance by the human at the table is not an additional prerequisite for the character's skill. Keep the reviewed [Session Zero agreement](../../../../DescentReviewChanges/ReviewBible/docs/playtest/SESSION_ZERO_PLAYTEST.md) in effect. A fictional social result does not provide another player's table agreement or the explicit willingness required by a different effect.

## Resolve the declared uncertainty

**SO-02. Proposed reconciliation of the manual's three narrative results with the four reviewed task degrees. This mapping is NEW.**

Choose the roll type before rolling. Use a fixed-DC task when resolving uncertain performance against circumstances. Use an opposed check when a specific participant is actively contesting that performance and the scene identifies both efforts. Do not roll both procedures for the same uncertainty. Use the actual ability, applicable skill proficiency and separately eligible modifiers once.

For a fixed DC, retain the complete [Core task rule](../../../../DescentReviewChanges/ReviewBible/docs/mechanics/CORE_RESOLUTION_PLAYTEST.md#rolls), including natural-result shifts. Meeting the DC means the declared success is achieved; do not secretly require DC + 10 to obtain the ordinary success that was promised.

| Native task degree | Proposed social result |
|---|---|
| Critical Success | The declared success, plus only an additional benefit stated before the roll. With no such benefit, resolve ordinary success. |
| Success | The declared success on its stated terms. Terms already included in the request remain part of it. |
| Failure | The declared refusal or failure consequence. The other party may offer the separately prepared costly agreement below; the player may accept or decline it. |
| Critical Failure | The declared failure, plus only an additional consequence stated before the roll. With none, resolve ordinary failure. The Director may offer the prepared alternative only if this branch allowed it before the roll. |

The Director prepares the source's full success, costly success and refusal/reversal as possible scene outcomes. **A costly agreement is a real alternative offer, not a retroactive downgrade of a successful check.** Record its exact price, obligation, supervision or disclosure before the roll, or openly negotiate a new offer after failure without claiming it was the roll's automatic effect. Acceptance obtains the stated concession; declining preserves the actual failure and time already spent. No payment, future debt or transfer occurs merely because a player heard an offer. The character must own the committed resource or have actual authority to promise it.

For an opposed check, compare final totals. Higher wins. An exact tie leaves the existing state unchanged, so the initiating creature does not change it. Natural task shifts do not become hidden opposed modifiers. Do not add critical opposed outcomes unless the particular reviewed effect supplies them. A losing initiator may still receive an openly offered, legal bargain; that does not change which check won.

This recommendation avoids turning every routine social success into a taxed result or importing a second critical system. GPT must expressly accept or replace the mapping; the original three-result paragraph remains preserved either way.

## Choose a skill by the approach

**SO-03. Preserve the five Player Charisma skills and the Director's Insight role. The limits below complete their ordinary use.**

| Approach | What a successful check can establish | What it cannot establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Persuasion | A declared, plausible agreement through a good-faith argument or offer. | Authority the listener lacks, obedience beyond the agreement, or consent to unrelated effects. |
| Deception | The declared success of hiding or presenting information under the scene's actual scrutiny. | A forged document becoming genuine, a false fact becoming true, or universal belief by absent observers. |
| Intimidation | The declared response to a credible perceived risk. | The Frightened condition, unprinted compelled behavior or a threat the character cannot plausibly convey. |
| Performance | The declared audience response to the entertainment or presentation. | A second free Persuasion check, institutional recognition or an unrelated concession. |
| Reputation | Recognition, a hearing or a specific favor based on actual known standing, deeds or contacts. | A new standing score, a fabricated contact, a nonexistent license or automatic payment of a favor. |
| Insight | A stated inference from observable behavior, context or evidence available to the character. | Telepathy, a generic certainty that every lie is false, hidden alignment or facts no available evidence could support. |

Use the character's actual governing ability and proficiency records. When a skill's plausible approach is already resolved by another check, describe the relevant information without demanding an extra roll for the same uncertainty. A new check needs a distinct consequence and a new unresolved question.

For an active deception-versus-scrutiny contest, name the deceiver's presentation and the scrutinizer's actual method before comparing totals. For an Insight task without an active opposing effort, set a DC for the specific obtainable inference. A failed Insight check means that inference is not established; it does not automatically compel the player to believe a fabricated statement. Any source-specific misleading-information effect must carry its own reviewed rule.

## Use one institutional standing policy

**SO-04, SO-10 and FL-23. This entire reconciliation is a NEW recommendation, not a finding that the sources already agree.**

Recommended primary standing is the existing [Reputation guide](../../../../DescentReviewChanges/ReviewBible/docs/mechanics/REPUTATION.md) and companion's integer range **−3 through +3**. Keep the existing companion's five qualitative bands: −3 or −2 Hated; −1 Disliked; 0 Neutral; +1 Liked; +2 or +3 Honored. The endpoint values remain distinct numerical records even where their displayed band matches.

Recommend retiring the older Loop's eleven-step numeric track and the Player book's separate seven-band automatic Charisma-modifier table from the active overlay, while preserving both complete original tables. This is a consequential change that GPT must explicitly adjudicate. **Do not apply it merely because this proposal exists.**

Under the recommendation, standing changes the institutional context: who will meet the character, which requests are plausible and which named offers can be made. It gives no automatic numerical check modifier. Charisma (Reputation) still uses the actual ability and applicable proficiency. A separately earned, explicitly written benefit can add its own modifier within its stated scope; list that source once. This avoids adding standing again simply because the chosen skill is named Reputation. All existing prohibitions on automatic RP, rank, Dissonance or moral-alignment changes remain in force.

Standing alone does not issue a license, transfer authority, waive a tax or make an institution solvent. Record actual licenses, claims, exemptions, credit agreements and other entitlements as named grants with scope, issuer and any expiry or conditions. A change of score does not silently erase an already recorded grant or cancel its written conditions. The larger expedition conference must replace each surviving Loop access threshold with an explicit entitlement or an agreed access rule; this proposal does not invent those missing licenses.

Keep personal relationships, standing with each institution and Expedition renown as separate records. A statement about one guard does not change every member of that guard's faction. One significant attributable event can affect several institutions only when each has its own plausible information and reason to react.

For a confirmed standing event, recommend the Loop's existing movement sizes within the selected range: ordinary significant consequence ±1; major public success or betrayal ±2; at most a net change of ±2 for one scene unless a specifically declared campaign-defining event authorizes more. Use one signed disposition per scene and institution, record the contributing evidence, then bound the resulting score to −3 through +3. Preserve attempted change, applied change and overflow in the event history, so reaching an endpoint does not destroy the record of what happened. Replaying a scene ID does not apply its change again.

A social roll alone awards no standing merely for being rolled or succeeding. Any standing consequence belongs to the attributable fictional event and its predeclared stakes. The same promise, public deed and later report of that deed remain one event unless a distinct subsequent act has its own consequences. A private kindness can change the recipient's relationship immediately; institutional standing waits for actual communication or evidence. False accusations may change what an institution believes, but record the allegation, reporter and uncertainty separately from established fact.

### Preserve existing character records

Use the [preservation findings](REPUTATION_PRESERVATION_SOURCE_ADDENDUM.md) as required input. The current normalizer demonstrably loses unknown keys and out-of-range or malformed values; it must not be used as a migration converter.

Before reconciliation, retain the exact original reputation object, whether the field was absent, its source format if known, its faction keys, numeric or textual values and any available history. Keep unknown institutions as unresolved keys; never invent a known faction because its display name looks similar. Keep malformed values visible as unresolved source data, rather than replacing them with zero. Preserve the previous saved file or export as a recoverable original before any eventual live migration.

For a record positively identified as using the existing −3..+3 numeric schema, carry valid integers and explicit zeroes forward unchanged. Absence may display as no recorded standing, but must remain distinct from a written zero. A five-label-only legacy record, an eleven-step Loop value, a Player modifier label, an unknown-format number or a malformed value requires an explicit recorded interpretation before it affects mechanics. Do not infer the source schema from whether the number fits within three. In particular, a Loop +2 and a companion +2 have different stated meanings.

The Director and affected player choose the new standing and carry over actual named entitlements when a record cannot be converted unambiguously. Record the original value, chosen result, rule version and reason; retain the original rather than overwriting it. This is a character migration procedure, not an additional approval gate for the already authorized eventual GitHub push.

Background standing is an initialization event for a newly created character. Applying the same background again cannot reset earned history. A later background correction records a specific correction with its reason; it must not replace the whole map. This recommendation defines a preservation contract only. No saves are read, migrated or changed by this draft.

## Account for communication and assistance

**SO-06 and SO-07. Preserve LAX and combat Help; new scene accounting and retry limits.**

Use the full [language and communication procedure](../../../../DescentReviewChanges/ReviewBible/docs/mechanics/LANGUAGE_COMMUNICATION_PLAYTEST.md). Record who can perceive and understand the chosen speech, signs or text, and any interpreter's actual contribution. Translation, hearing or a written medium does not silently satisfy an unrelated spell component. Remote participants need an actual channel and the time required to use it.

Declare the fictional duration of an exchange before resolving it. Count that duration once on the campaign clock for everyone participating; simultaneous speech does not multiply elapsed time. Track travel, interpretation and preparation when they add real duration. In initiative, keep six-second rounds, existing turn permissions and actual Help costs and deadlines. A minute-long negotiation cannot be compressed into a free six-second utterance. Move to a longer social interval only if the actual threats permit that transition; preserve current effects, deadlines and paid resources.

Assistance needs a concrete contribution the helper can actually make. In initiative, use the existing Help Action and next-regular-start expiry exactly. Outside initiative, the proposed completion is that useful assistance accompanies the relevant work through its resolution and supplies advantage on that one check; the helper spends the same actual work interval and cannot also perform incompatible work. Require any language, access, tools or information needed for that contribution, without inventing a blanket proficiency requirement where the governing action has none. Multiple helpers do not stack advantage, and advantage/disadvantage cancels under Core.

One committed approach receives one resolution. Changing the face at the front of the group, repeating the same sentence or making a separate die roll for every ally does not reopen it. A different offer, newly obtained evidence, a changed audience or a genuinely changed deadline can create a new uncertainty. The Director states what changed and new stakes before allowing another attempt. Preserve the previous decision, spent time and resources. A delayed opportunity is not a resource refresh.

## Apply social magic within its own limits

**SO-08. Carry reviewed effects without a new generic control power.**

Use only an actually acquired, operative effect and its complete target, cost, component, save, awareness, duration and ending procedure. Charmed restricts knowingly harming the identified charmer and gives that charmer advantage on social ability checks against the target; it does not itself supply obedience, affection or consent. Frightened retains its own visibility-dependent check and attack disadvantage and restriction on willing approach. A successful ordinary Intimidation roll does not apply that condition.

Do not turn a social check into a substitute save against a spell, or turn a spell's successful save into a new free social roll that reproduces its failed payload. An explicit effect can grant a narrowly defined influence or control result; it does not grant a second, broader result through these rules. Track each actual effect independently. Pending spell entries and class comparison records gain no new approval from this chapter.

## Run faction updates on real events

**SO-11. Preserve up to three watched factions and one action each after a genuine return. The accounting below is NEW.**

Give each expedition departure, return and faction action a stable record ID. A return checkpoint closes a real expedition episode with an objective and recorded outcome, including an actual abandonment or extraction. Crossing a threshold repeatedly within the same episode does not create another checkpoint. An earlier unresolved action continues under its original ID.

At a valid return, review up to three watched factions. Each can resolve at most one eligible planned action: recruit, denounce, buy, raid, research, fortify, bargain, conceal or wait. The action record names its responsible actors, location, information source, required access, actual resources, start, duration, completion requirements and observable consequence. Choose these facts before resolving the uncertainty they govern; do not backdate a new project to give a rival knowledge it could not have acquired.

A checkpoint grants no elapsed hours, travel, funds, crew or free roll. Resolve an action only if its requirements and duration actually occurred. An action that is not ready remains in progress; the faction may wait or plan its next action rather than gaining an immediate substitute completion. An already completed action is recorded once even if several later returns reveal it. A newly planned action begins no earlier than its real preparation and takes its declared positive duration, unless a separate operative effect explicitly completes it immediately.

One actor cannot spend the same interval on incompatible faction actions, player assistance, travel or recovery. Independent staff can act in parallel only when their actual assignment and resources support it. Uncertain dangerous actions use the appropriate already-governed task, encounter, travel or project procedure; a faction-action label is not an automatic success. For a routine uncontested action, record its actual completion without inventing a roll.

The next session should expose a plausible consequence through a rumor, messenger, changed offer, visible guard presence or other information the party can actually receive. A rumor is evidence of a report, not automatic proof of the underlying claim. Respect Director-only information and the source's requirement to track rival routes, information and time.

**Connection still requiring the expedition conference:** long Haven stays also need world updates. Recommend one review checkpoint at the end of each completed 24-hour Haven interval, processed on the same clock as a return. If both are due together, process one checkpoint. This new cadence must be reconciled explicitly with FL-03 and FL-24; it is not currently operative. Checkpoints never repeat an action or duplicate elapsed hours.

## Give rival clocks finite consequences

**SO-12. Preserve the four source clocks and rival sheet; new trigger and completion rules.**

Retain all nine original rival fields: public identity, backer, current depth, objective, preferred method, professed limit, need from the party, feared disclosure and current relationship. Add actual actors, assets, route, known information, action history and links to standing/relationship records. A runtime faction ID is not a complete institution or Expedition; the 44 existing IDs remain a registry awaiting the relevant setting work.

| Clock | Source length | Eligible source event |
|---|---|---|
| Depth | Choose and record 4 to 8 segments when the objective begins. | Actual travel or support that materially advances the named route objective. |
| Claim | 4 segments. | Acquired proof, a witness or legal support relevant to the named claim. |
| Grudge | 4 segments. | An attributable humiliation, loss, betrayal or public accusation that the rival learns of. |
| Collapse | Choose and record 4 to 6 segments when the crisis begins. | Actual casualties, debt, internal conflict or exposure advancing that crisis. |

Recommend **one segment per distinct qualifying event per clock**, regardless of how many triggers describe that same event. A report of an already recorded event does not earn another segment; for an information-dependent clock, its first credible receipt is the one eligible checkpoint. One event may affect different clocks only if its actual consequences independently satisfy each trigger. Record the event IDs, not just a running total.

Choose the exact completion consequence and necessary prerequisites when opening a clock. At the last segment, resolve that consequence once if its real requirements are satisfied. If an essential requirement is absent, leave the project at its threshold with a named unmet requirement; the clock grants no teleportation, ownership, new legal authority or ability to act from an impossible location. A Depth clock cannot erase travel, and a Claim clock cannot authenticate forged evidence. Filling Grudge schedules the stated hostile response through an actual action; it does not grant an immediate extra attack.

Once resolved, close that objective's clock and retain its events and consequence. A new clock needs a distinct new objective or crisis and its own trigger/length, not merely reopening the same record. The Director can resolve an already completed objective immediately when all its actual requirements are met; segments must not force meaningless additional repetition.

### Worked ordinary scene

**Synthetic example under the proposal, not a new setting fact or an approved character build.** At 09:00 a speaker with Charisma modifier +2 and applicable Persuasion proficiency +2 requests a supervised look at an existing route ledger. The clerk has authority to permit that viewing, but cannot transfer the ledger or waive a fee charged by a different office. Both parties understand the spoken language. The Director sets a ten-minute exchange and DC 12. Success grants the viewing; failure refuses the unpaid request but permits an offer of the same viewing for 5 gp paid to the authorized desk. No extra critical benefit or penalty is declared. A deadline closes the desk at 09:20.

The speaker rolls 7, for total 11: Failure. The clock is now 09:10. The clerk offers the recorded 5 gp alternative. The player accepts and spends 5 gp they actually possess; the receipt and supervised access are recorded. The failed roll remains a failure, while the separate accepted bargain supplies the access. The Director does not also charge the time a second time or call for another persuasion roll. The view itself takes the declared five minutes, ending at 09:15. A companion arriving at 09:16 cannot replay the original request to refund the paid fee. Any obtained map information is limited to what the real ledger contains.

In the alternate branch where the player declines, keep the 09:10 refusal and the 5 gp. Simply asking a second character to repeat the same unpaid request does not create a new check. Producing an actual authorization letter could create a distinct approach if the clerk can honor it, with new declared time and stakes before 09:20.

### Worked rival follow-up

**Synthetic example; names, actions and times here are illustrative.** A rival's Claim clock has four segments and currently holds two distinct evidence events. Its recorded objective is recognition of a particular salvage claim. A named hearing officer will decide the claim only after a genuine witness and specified documents are available. The rival schedules a two-hour interview with that witness from 10:00 to 12:00, with one assigned representative and an actual communication route.

The party returns at 11:00. That return is a valid report checkpoint, but the interview is incomplete. Record it as in progress; the return grants no tick or instant hearing. Another crossing of the Haven boundary at 11:05 belongs to the same return and changes nothing.

At a later valid checkpoint after 12:00, the completed interview supplies one distinct qualifying event if its actual resolution obtained relevant testimony. Claim rises from two to three. Recording a rumor about that same interview does not add a fourth segment. A later distinct document event can take the clock to four, but final recognition still requires the recorded hearing and the officer's authority. The clock cannot forge the missing document or make the party's contrary evidence disappear. Resolve the hearing under its actual stakes, record the resulting claim and close this objective once. Public notice or a plausible messenger can reveal the consequence without giving the party the Director's entire rival record.
