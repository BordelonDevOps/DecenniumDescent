# Character record - playtest sheet

**Reviewed authored teaching/presentation — TEX-EXACT-2026-10-11.** [Open the five-page fillable PDF](../../output/pdf/Decennium_Descent_Character_Sheet_Playtest.pdf). It can also be printed and completed by hand. The fields record actual decisions and events; they do not calculate values, grant abilities, resolve missing rules or synchronize with the companion application.

Use the [character foundation](../mechanics/CHARACTER_CREATION_PLAYTEST.md) and [17-class tables](../mechanics/CHARACTER_FOUNDATION_TABLES.md) to create or advance the character. The current [ancestry/background rules](../mechanics/ANCESTRY_BACKGROUND_PLAYTEST.md), [equipment rules](../mechanics/EQUIPMENT_ACQUISITION_PLAYTEST.md) and their actual review-status files carry their later completed scopes; older pending-pass labels in the preserved foundation do not revoke those decisions. Use only each option's actual current permissions.

Keep original rolls, choices and sources. A blank means unfinished information, not zero, no restriction or an extra grant. Write an explicit zero when zero is the actual value and an explicit not-applicable note where appropriate. Attach additional records using the same headings when a character needs more rows; the number of rows printed is never a mechanical capacity limit. Keep the character reference on continuation records.

## Page 1 - character, abilities and training

Record the actual ancestry and variant, one background, first Base class, chronological class-entry order, levels in each class, total character level and total-level PB. Arch/Hero are later stages of the relevant class. Record only acquired path choices, including a Paladin's actual Oath or inversion, an Oracle's chosen Mystery and Curse, or a Druid's Circle.

For each ability, keep the assigned score, ancestry adjustment, other actual adjustments, current score and its modifier. Record the source and ending of a temporary adjustment on page 2; do not silently turn it into permanent advancement. The modifier is `floor((score - 10) / 2)`. Keep the original generation record with page 5 rather than replacing it when an ability changes.

The sheet lists all 20 foundation skills under their source ability. Record actual training and final modifiers; a printed skill row is not proficiency. The same applies to saving throws, armor, weapons and tools. Apply PB once when applicable; an explicit Expertise multiplier changes that contribution rather than adding another independent PB. Resolve duplicate or exhausted grants through the foundation's own procedure.

Attach the [language and communication worksheet](CHARACTER_LANGUAGE_WORKSHEET.md). Its eight source-qualified forms preserve actual modes, grants, choices, temporary effects, courses and language projects. A bare language name on this general sheet does not replace those records or imply every mode.

## Page 2 - combat, health and active effects

Record the actual encounter and numbered round, regular-turn marker, initiative, root body, current form, size, space and movement. Use one legal AC calculation and its eligible bonuses, keeping suppressed or inaccessible equipment separate. The [combat procedures](../mechanics/COMBAT_ACTION_PLAYTEST.md), [body/equipment rules](../mechanics/BODY_EQUIPMENT_PLAYTEST.md) and exact source effects govern those values.

Record the required **Core combat stance** in an existing page-2 active-effects row: its name and source, the character who chose it, its restrictions and spent allowances, and its ending at that character's next regular-turn start. Choose one during each regular turn before the first event to which a stance benefit or restriction applies; if no such event occurs, choose it before that turn ends. An extra turn grants neither another choice nor refreshed stance allowances. There is no implicit neutral stance. Keep this record distinct from a Monk's separate class stance. The existing effects rows or a linked continuation record suffice; this adds no PDF field or new stance rule. Follow the [five actual combat stances](../mechanics/COMBAT_ACTION_PLAYTEST.md#stances).

Keep **base maximum HP, effective maximum HP, current HP and healing ceiling** distinct. The [maximum-HP rules](../mechanics/MAXIMUM_HP_PLAYTEST.md) apply actual adjustments to the recorded base. A capacity increase is not healing. Temporary HP has its own grant, source, current amount, endpoint and dependencies on the active-effects table; it is not a second normal-HP pool. Use the [forms and temporary-HP rules](../mechanics/FORM_TEMP_HP_PLAYTEST.md).

Track life state, death-save counters and the absolute Stable recovery deadline under [Core resolution](../mechanics/CORE_RESOLUTION_PLAYTEST.md), with the actual exceptions in the [healing interface](../mechanics/HEALING_DEATH_PLAYTEST.md). The text field's suggested states are reminders, not an alternate state machine. Independent Unconscious, Prone or other conditions remain separate even after positive HP is restored.

Each of the three Wound rows records one actual Wound and its linked Injury, if any. The third Wound is the Critical state; the next failed Wound save is fatal, not a fourth row. Removal ends that Wound's linked Injury; identical Injury penalties do not stack. A duplicate Injury may remain attached to a different Wound. Use the complete [Wound rules](../mechanics/CORE_RESOLUTION_PLAYTEST.md#wounds-and-critical-state), not a single combined injury checkbox.

For each condition or effect, record its actual source, owner, restrictions, endpoint and any blocked recovery. Record concentration, paid maintenance and pending effects separately. For actions, mark what was actually used; the printed fields create no extra Action, Bonus Action or Reaction. Use the source's real regular-turn, numbered-round, encounter or elapsed-time scope.

## Page 3 - equipment, carrying and attacks

Give each possession a physical identity, actual source profile, grade/fit, quantity, remaining contents/charges, owner, custodian, location and condition. Keep unknown source values distinct from explicit operational estimates. Containers and their contents remain individual records. Count their mass once; a full-container gross value already includes the contents it names. Keep pounds, Load and any other source-specific transport measure distinct.

Record whether character creation used starting packages **or** rolled wealth. Preserve purchases and remaining coins; training does not create equipment. Use [equipment acquisition](../mechanics/EQUIPMENT_ACQUISITION_PLAYTEST.md), [item procedures](../mechanics/EQUIPMENT_ITEM_PROCEDURES.md), [starting packages](../mechanics/EQUIPMENT_STARTING_PACKAGES.md) and actual [carrying rules](../mechanics/BODY_EQUIPMENT_PLAYTEST.md). Rations and water do not automatically become complete abstract Supply.

Each attack row identifies the actual weapon or acquired technique, attack bonus or save DC, damage/type, range/reach and relevant hands, ammunition and costs. Keep a technique's action and attack count from its own card. A row is not a free attack or permission to use a same-named spell. Record held, worn, melded, inaccessible and pending-placement items explicitly; a form change never reloads or repairs them.

## Page 4 - magic, class resources and companions

Identify the actual primary and, if any, secondary RP contributor under the foundation's fixed ordering. Keep one merged RP amount and maximum, plus character-wide Dissonance and the remembered ordinary-spell identity/Refrain count. A cast still chooses an eligible casting class and uses that class's actual ability, access, repertoire and Limit. Changing class does not clear the shared history. Use the [Resonance reference](RESONANCE_CHEATSHEET.md) and [spell integration](../mechanics/SPELL_INTEGRATION_RULES.md).

Keep spell identity/version, actual source permission, casting class, rank, components and known/prepared/book state. Ordinary spells, ancestry uses, class matrices, copied Echoes and crafted activations retain their own records and permissions even when names match. Wizard duplicate matrices keep one underlying family for history. Pending catalog entries remain unavailable regardless of space on the form.

Use a separate resource row for every acquired class pool, gauge, prepared stock, recharge counter or limited feature. Record current and maximum values, the actual used allowance and its scope, and the actual recovery or expiry. Refer to the [all-class resource sheet](CLASS_RESOURCE_REFERENCE.md) and complete class cards. Attach additional source records for complex histories such as stored Echoes, performance sources, form instances or Brutality; a short summary cell cannot replace that history.

Companions and devices keep their actual identity, owner, profile, HP, contents/service state, command/channel, task, expiry and source limits. Shared command allowances belong to the controller's real record. HP repair, service, RP and commands do not refill one another. The [companion walkthrough](COMPANION_COMMAND_WALKTHROUGH.md) illustrates the current distinction; full [creature](../mechanics/CREATURE_LIFECYCLE_PLAYTEST.md), [device](../mechanics/DEVICE_LIFECYCLE_PLAYTEST.md) and [language/channel](../mechanics/LANGUAGE_COMMUNICATION_PLAYTEST.md) procedures retain their stated scopes.

## Page 5 - advancement, recovery and continuing records

Add one entry per attained character level, retaining the gained class level, class-entry order, Hit Die size and original HP die result or fixed base **before CON**. Record automatic grants, actual selections and ASI/feat decisions separately. The six printed rows are not a level cap; attach continuation rows through the game's actual combined level-20 limit. Recompute each attained level's HP contribution under the [foundation rules](../mechanics/CHARACTER_CREATION_PLAYTEST.md#gain-a-level); do not replace earlier rolls or refresh already spent Hit Dice.

Keep owned, spent and available Hit Dice by die size. Record each maximum-HP adjustment with its actual source rather than feeding an already modified maximum back into the base. An ordinary level-up changes capacity and acquisition; it does not heal, resurrect, clear Wounds or restore spent resources.

For rests, record real start/completion times, eligibility, interruptions, benefits actually applied and remaining limits. Apply the [reviewed rest limits](../mechanics/STRAIN_PLAYTEST.md#recovery-and-actual-elapsed-time) and each source's own recovery. Keep Warrior serum Strain, expedition Strain, Exhaustion and other ongoing conditions separate. The table does not make work and rest simultaneous or replay a completed benefit.

For work, courses and treatment, record source, target, paid inputs, active practitioner time, elapsed endpoint and result. Use actual work orders and the [language worksheet](CHARACTER_LANGUAGE_WORKSHEET.md), [crafting procedure](../mechanics/CRAFTING_HARVEST_PLAYTEST.md) or [injury/aid walkthrough](INJURY_AND_AID_WALKTHROUGH.md) as appropriate. Preserve original choices, actual transactions and unresolved fields for the next review; do not fill gaps by inventing an item, grant or source decision.

## Scope and verification

The editable PDF has five pages and 364 named fields. Its [field inventory](../../data/playtest/character_sheet_form_fields.json) maps labels and page positions for form verification. It is **not** a companion save schema, character-builder implementation or exhaustive representation of every source-specific effect. Longer and specialized records remain attached rather than compressed into a generic checkbox.

The builder uses the current foundation's exact 20-skill list. File, text, form-tree and rendering checks are recorded in the audit evidence; they do not establish gameplay legality, human usability or approval of every rule in the game. This complete blank sheet and its sources are prepared for the next bounded GPT presentation review.
