# Advanced crafting — player candidate guide

**REVIEWED — authored operational PLAYTEST.** Use this guide with the Enchanting, Rune Carving, Scribing and Tinkering candidate cards and their body catalogue. Each card supplies its actual numbers, ingredients, formula access, station and work time. This guide supplies the common procedures. A recipe name, price or ingredient mention grants no unprinted power or automatic access to stock.

## Make one recorded work order

Choose the recipe and one permitted complete body model and size before starting. Reserve `floor(1.5 × printed quantity)` actual complete ordinary bodies in a fixed order. These bodies are additional to all printed process components and required working implements. They must never have been magically altered, must have positive HP and remain complete, and cannot simultaneously serve another order, a consumed ingredient or the working toolkit. An ordinary weapon or armor suit retains its genuinely established grade and permitted physical history. Fixed carriers use Standard grade. Automaton chassis must be unmodified, unflawed ordinary B5 chassis, with Tiny or Small chosen before work.

All maximum-yield reserves must still qualify immediately before the single completion roll, even if Standard would use fewer. Missing, Broken, incomplete or committed reserves pause work. Lawful pre-roll repair or replacement retains that entry's position and records its actual history. There is no post-roll substitution, purchase or choice of the best bodies. Transform the first resolved number of bodies; unused ones remain their actual mundane stock. Failed or Mishap transforms none, although a genuine mishap can damage stock. Returning unused bodies is not ingredient recovery.

The actual creator, station and safe storage must accommodate the work. Reserves can occupy separate lawful storage while each body is worked sequentially; they need not all fit on one bench. Source ingredients remain required for the whole printed batch, not once for each output. Their mass does not produce extra complete bodies or get added again to each output. Preserve physical identity, compatible containment, damage, losses and material age throughout.

## Training, access and work

All four trades are artisan creation using INT. Their primary credentials are Enchanter's tools, Runecarver's tools, Calligrapher's supplies and Tinker's tools respectively. Add one actually applicable PB, or replacing expertise, rather than adding numerical trade level. An untrained character can attempt an otherwise eligible level 1–4 recipe without tool PB and with disadvantage. Trained recipe eligibility is `max(4, trade level)`, with actual source/formula access. Recipes above level 4 still require the required formula. Possession of a finished item teaches neither its formula nor its trade.

Use the card's real station and auxiliary implements. A complete ordinary toolkit can supply the ordinary implements actually inside it; it does not also supply expensive tiered ingredients or a workstation. Source facilities and ordinary tools give no automatic bonus. An actually available compatible facility uses the established rental baseline of 1 gp per eight reserved hours. Paying that rate does not create capacity or permission.

These creation cards have `fieldCraftable=false`. A historical `timeMode=field` label does not waive that flag. An expedition worksite still needs the genuine fortified/research-site permission and fixed required facility. Other workshop, Haven and longer-term permissions retain their own requirements. Only eligible assistants within the card's limit can assist; they must work all required hours, do not shorten the order and receive no assistant crafting XP.

An actually eligible Forge Dwarf multiplies creation time by 0.75, rounding up once to a whole minute, and treats a Standard creation result as Exceptional. This changes neither access nor ingredients. It does not reduce repair, servicing, research, encoding, attunement, ordinary calendar waits or rest. Respect the two-active-work-Watch limit and actual rest restrictions; the same hours cannot also count as guard duty, travel, another job or beneficial rest.

The first stable phase is `min(15 minutes, adjusted total active creation time)` and is included in the printed total. Preregister later phases of at most eight active hours. Completed phases stabilize only the actual eligible incorporated inputs. Raw stock and a recoverable catalyst keep their own age and protection requirements. Interruption loses unfinished-phase progress, not costs, completed phases or physical history. Ineligible inputs pause the order; unfinished work has no active item power.

Use the existing crafting framework's first-success, repeat, qualified-failure and triviality rules. The card preserves its exact progression and capped trivial threshold. Using, repairing, servicing or studying an item does not award that recipe's creation XP. Formula research and ordinary formula copying keep their own reviewed procedures.

## Resolve the six creation outcomes

| Margin against the recipe DC | Outcome | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 10 or more | Masterwork | `floor(1.5Q)` outputs, or Q with the structural option; at most one eligible catalyst unit retained |
| 5–9 | Exceptional | `floor(1.25Q)` outputs, or Q with the structural option |
| 0–4 | Standard | Q outputs |
| −1 to −4 | Flawed | `floor(Q/2)` when Q≥2; see singleton rules below |
| −5 to −9 | Failed | No output; bounded ingredient recovery |
| −10 or worse, or natural 1 | Mishap | No output; consumed materials lost and the actual tradeskill mishap procedure applies |

A natural 20 adds no extra manufacturing-band shift. The Forge Dwarf adjustment changes Standard to Exceptional only. Choose the permitted yield or structural option once for the batch. It never improves charges, damage, DC, spell rank, service count, research potency, carrying capacity or other magical properties.

For a structural object, the Exceptional/Masterwork alternative retains Q outputs with P25/P50 workmanship. Use its actual ordinary B after legitimate size and grade, and only the strongest existing or newly selected percentage. Maximum HP is `B + max(1, floor(B×P/100))` for positive P; otherwise B. Apply one persistent flaw last: `max(1, floor(improved maximum/2))`. Retain the old damage deficit, `old maximum − old current`, and subtract it from the new maximum, clamped to [0, new maximum]. Standard does not heal. At B5, unflawed maxima are 5/6/7; flawed maxima are 2/3/3. At B1 they are 1/2/2 and 1/1/1.

A structural Q1 Flawed result creates one persistently flawed item, which can finish Broken if retained damage reaches its reduced maximum. Q≥2 Flawed only reduces yield and retains actual prior flaws; producing one output from that batch does not add a singleton flaw. No extra duration penalty is invented for these durable carriers. Repair does not erase the flaw or repeat the workmanship increase.

Automata are the explicit creature exception. Their printed creature AC and HP never increase from structural quality. Choosing the alternative simply retains Q with a quality label and no mechanical improvement. Q1 Flawed maps to Failed, including that failure's recovery procedure. Q≥2 keeps the reduced yield. A produced automaton starts at `max(1, floor(printed creature HP × latest chassis current HP / 5))`; a full chassis produces full printed HP. This is a creation conversion, not level scaling or a second structural track.

## Inputs, retained tools and failure returns

Keep every component occurrence separate, including repeated catalog IDs. Its consumed flag and role govern what happens. An expensive object labeled tool can be a consumed process input; it cannot also be the retained working implement. A price, core/essence title or material description does not make it a catalyst.

Masterwork may retain at most one actual unit total from an eligible consumed catalyst occurrence on that recipe, chosen after the check. It must have participated nondestructively until retention is resolved. The returned unit retains identity, age, condition and compatible vessel; it is not also a second embedded core in an output.

On Failed creation, for each eligible exact consumed non-catalyst input identity, recover at most one quarter of its fixed consumed-input quantity for this batch, capped by the eligible amount actually remaining from those same inputs, and rounded down once to that identity's legal source-unit precision. Do not take one quarter of the surviving remainder. Previously returned, forfeited or lost quantities and unrelated stock cannot fund this recovery. For each exact eligible identity in this batch, use `recovered_i = u_i × floor(min(Q_i / 4, R_i) / u_i)`. Q_i is its fixed consumed-input quantity; R_i is the eligible amount surviving from those same inputs; u_i is its legal source-unit precision. Group only genuinely identical eligible inputs within this same batch while retaining every physical occurrence. Catalysts and unrelated batches do not enter that recovery group. The expressly divisible bulk profiles are Enchanting Veilthread; Rune Carving Runic ink and Binding agent; Scribing Ink and Preservation treatment. They use 0.01 of their exact source unit only when actually supplied in that divisible form. Other stones, lenses, matrices, glyph pieces, parchment/cover/binding units, hardware packs, cells and consumed tools are indivisible unless their own genuine independent profile permits subdivision. No quarter-lens, value conversion, remainder banking or second salvage pass over failed waste.

Mishap or abandonment returns no consumed inputs. Nonconsumed tools and unused reserved bodies remain only as they actually survive. Existing gathering, preservation, finite-matter and exclusive-debris histories still apply. A public ingredient mention does not disclose a donor dossier, grant acquisition rights, supply missing metadata or make the required stock free.

## Structure, state and repair

The body catalogue gives complete physical bodies, primary members, dimensions, hands and capacity. Structural AC protects the item, not its bearer. Ordinary equipment targeting, actual damage compatibility and carried-item protection still apply. One aggregate HP track does not give each screw, page or limb a second independently targetable pool. A name or a flag does not establish intact structure.

Magic requires an actual complete positive-HP, accessible, unmelded, unsuppressed participating item. Required hands, legal bearing positions and line of effect remain mandatory. Suppression removes magical functions but leaves otherwise lawful ordinary physical functions. A suppressed automaton is inert; its paid operating time continues. A stowed or closed reference cannot help a check. Later suppression never reverses completed healing, damage or ordinary work.

Initialize each physical output's stated budget once at lawful completion, even if a structural Flawed output finishes Broken. Repair can expose its existing budget but never initialize it again. Separate outputs have separate budgets; copies, snapshots and changed display IDs are not new items. Record actual campaign time, phase/sequence, canonical activation/check identity, custody and expenditures. Invalid declarations spend nothing. Once a legal activation commits, misses, resistance, prevention, interruption or later loss of eligibility do not refund its costs.

Ordinary advanced restoration requires training in the recipe's primary credential, INT, its real compatible station and original required repair implements. Enchanted weapon/armor restoration also needs actual host-compatible fixtures/tools. This permission does not teach Smithing or manufacture a missing host. A broken target cannot serve as its own missing toolkit.

| Restoration | DC | Active work | Actual material reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Functional positive-HP body | 10 | `max(1 hour, recorded maximumHP/10 hours)` | 25% of admitted undamaged mundane body reference |
| Broken/wrecked recoverable original | Original recipe DC | `max(8 hours, recorded maximumHP/10 hours)` | 25% of undamaged finished per-unit reference |

Use Core task natural shifts and collapse to success/failure. Failure spends real time/materials; another attempt needs new work and stock. Success restores recorded maximum HP and otherwise lawful function only. It does not refill charges, service, spell uses, permissions or clocks. No generic assistant bonus, crafting XP, quality increase, catalyst return, flaw removal or Forge Dwarf reduction. Automaton repair time uses printed creature maximum HP.

The original creation record and magic-bearing primary members must survive. Missing/burnt inscription pages, destroyed plates, missing model-designated primary frames/actuators, terminal sacrifices and already-consumed debris cannot be recreated by paying a percentage. Compatible subordinate repairs need real stock and preserve the same item. Specific class exceptions remain limited to their exact eligibility and costs. A genuinely available reviewed repair service can provide its own complete quote instead of this self-repair route.

Finished per-unit reference is `original batch recommendedMarketValue / original Standard Q + actual admitted mundane body reference`. Do not divide by quality yield, regenerate historical labor, add reusable capital again or substitute legacy prices. Keep exact fractions until one final purchase-ceiling/sale-floor rounding to copper. Owned bodies need no repurchase but still must exist. Commissions apply to the actual agreed quote; no automatic buyer or quality-price multiplier is created. Spent, suppressed or Broken magical bodies remain outside generic mundane-only dismantling.

## Enchanting

Choose the card's permitted weapon, armor, wand, staff or charm body before work. Generic Enchantment cards explicitly list their permitted choices; that is one fixed host choice for the order. A weapon/suit must have its actual complete existing source profile, size, grade, value, mass, primary structure and damage history. Default examples are a Standard Medium longsword and chain shirt. Shields, ammunition bundles, natural attacks, creatures, unsupported special models and previously magical hosts are excluded. One permanent imprint per host; no transfer, replacement-imprint or recharge-by-reenchantment service.

Let B be the card's enhancement and C its maximum charges. The unsuppressed weapon gives +B through the existing item-accuracy comparison and +B ordinary flat weapon damage once per hit. Its ordinary B/P/S component is magical. An armor suit gives +B in equipment.defense-enhancement while its actual worn armor formula is selected. A Crude −1 grade contribution and EN +1 compete as eligible values: +1 wins; suppression reveals −1, not an invented zero. Independent eligible shield/class/spell rules retain their own predicates.

| Host | Charged activation |
|---|---|
| Actual weapon | **Charged Strike:** one Action and one charge before one actual legal weapon attack; hit adds Bd6 of the card's exact damage type to that same damage event. This is not the Attack Action and gives no Extra Attack. |
| Actual worn selected armor suit | **Typed Guard:** Reaction and one charge when a matching positive damage component remains after defenses but before HP and positive-damage-taken triggers; subtract Bd6, minimum zero. |
| Other permitted carrier | **Typed Pulse:** Action, one manipulating hand and one charge; one perceived creature or physically compatible object within 30 feet and line of effect; fixed attack `+(4+B)`, hit `2B d6` of the card's exact type. No ability, PB or enhancement added again. |

Attack natural 1/20 rules apply to the actual attack routes; critical attacks double dice, not flat damage. These activations are magical nonspells with no RP or spell cadence and cannot be Counterspelled. A committed failed/missed/prevented use stays spent. The source tag supplies only the printed damage type: no poison condition, flight, healing, creature command or other title-implied power. A Staff/Wand/Focus name supplies no independent class casting-focus permission. A carried charm must actually be grasped to fire; it has no passive wearing benefit.

EN charges use the fixed campaign 06:00 dawn, once per elapsed 86,400-second day. Record `(campaign identity, enchanting dawn, day index, physical item identity)`. At that event, a complete positive-HP unsuppressed item sets charges to C. Stowed or legitimately melded items receive this narrow internal-clock refill, not active use. An ineligible item records the missed event; later unsuppression/repair does not catch up. Local sunrises, travel and transfer do not create another dawn. No other trade inherits this refill.

## Rune Carving

Each ordinary rune is one separate plate, including a plate strapped to equipment. It does not merge HP with the host or create the host. The original inscribed plate is primary; captive feet/strap are subordinate. It is visible unless actually concealed through an available procedure.

For a ground rune, use an Action and one hand within 5 feet to place it on a real stable eligible unoccupied 5-foot square. It arms at the next regular-turn start, or after six elapsed seconds outside initiative. Its first real creature entry from outside can trigger it, including forced movement, falling or teleport arrival. Existing occupancy, touching an unrelated surface and repeating a movement description are not fresh entry. Resolve genuinely simultaneous entrants in a stable recorded order. The setter and allies are not generally exempt. A ground plate that is moved, rotated off its declared working face, wholly covered, Broken, incomplete or suppressed cannot trigger. Restore an invalidated ground trigger only through the actual one-Action manual arming procedure on its cleared eligible square, followed by the existing next-regular-start or six-second delay; merely uncovering, repairing or unsuppressing does not rearm it. A capable authorized setter can disarm their accessible plate with an Action; physically removing it uses its actual item interaction afterward. These ground-trigger rules do not replace the specific direct-touch Healing procedure or the separate attuned-Ward duration procedure.

A discharge spends one charge for that event, disarms the plate and observes one discharge per physical item per numbered round. A multi-charge plate needs a cleared eligible square, actual manual rearming and its delay before another discharge. One person may have at most three armed ground runes. Their printed charges are lifetime charges: dawn, HP repair, transfer and rest never refill them.

With safe access within five feet, one active minute permits an INT (Arcana) or INT check using Runecarver's tools against the original recipe creation DC, using normal Core task shifts collapsed to success/failure. Apply only the actual applicable proficiency contribution. This examination reveals supported operating type/trigger, not donor lore, and failure grants no repeat on unchanged evidence. Disarming an unknown accessible Rune requires actual Runecarver's-tools proficiency and tools, one active minute, and that source DC. Failure leaves it armed; it discharges only if an actual eligible entry/touch event occurs, not merely because the check failed. An unrelated tool proficiency is not a substitute.

**Binding:** on first entry, the card's STR save applies. Failure Restrains that creature for at most one minute; it repeats the save at each regular-turn end. It can spend an Action on STR (Athletics) or DEX (Acrobatics) against that DC through Core task binary resolution; an adjacent helper can use STR (Athletics). Moving the target completely outside the original trigger square/volume, or moving, covering, suppressing or Breaking the original plate, ends this particular Binding restraint. Restoring the plate does not resume that ended restraint, refund its charge or rearm its ground trigger. Other independent restraints remain separately governed. No Stun or removal of other independent restraints.

**Explosive:** the first entrant triggers one burst centered on the plate, radius 10 feet, affecting every creature with line of effect, including allies/setter. Card DEX save: printed full damage on failure, zero on success. No object damage, extra condition or additional discharge for another simultaneous entrant.

**Healing:** predeclare at most six known permitted allied identities. An eligible assigned creature entering or taking an Action to touch spends a charge for printed ordinary healing. An assigned willing or Incapacitated living ally can instead receive a helper's separate Action with direct plate contact within 5 feet. This explicit contact route does not sweep healing along a moving plate; an unarmed setup and the healing Action are separate. Dead creatures and destroyed crafted Constructs cannot be resurrected/reconstructed. Ordinary healing blockers/caps apply; a legal full-HP or prevented heal still spends the charge.

**Warding:** ten active minutes attunes one bearer to one plate; a bearer can have one such ward. Death ends attunement. Use one actual wrist, neck or chest position within the existing jewelry scheme; don/doff takes an Action. Before an attack roll targeting the bearer or a saving-throw roll by the bearer, spend one Reaction and charge. The printed B applies to that triggering event and later eligible events until the next regular-turn start. Outside initiative use one actual six-second boundary, retaining its history through mode changes. Extra turns do not advance regular-turn expiry or supply another Reaction. Removing the plate, melding, Broken or incomplete state, suppression or other lost eligibility suspends the remaining ward benefit while its original deadline runs. If eligibility returns before that deadline, only the still-unexpired original benefit can resume; there is no new activation, refunded charge or extended duration. This is distinct from an ended Binding restraint and an invalidated ground trigger.

Ward AC competes in equipment.defense-enhancement; ward saves compete in equipment.jewel-save-enhancement. A +2 ward and +1 same-family jewelry give +2, not +3. No universal strongest-only rule is created for unrelated class/spell effects. Unattuning, removal, meld, suppression or lost eligibility suspends the remaining benefit while its original deadline continues; restored eligibility can resume only that remaining duration.

The ordinary Runecarver kit's working optic is a 2 × 1 × 0.25-inch, 0.25-lb lens, HP5/AC13, reference 2 gp, included in the existing 25-gp/5-lb kit: stylus 0.25 lb, optic 0.25 lb, case/remaining implements 4.5 lb. Current working-tool wording for these public formulas is Resonance lens; historical Focusing crystal wording denotes this working implement. That never substitutes the ordinary optic for a tiered consumed ingredient. The historical consumed-name correspondence is limited to occurrences 399[1], 400[0], 401[1], 403[1], 407[0], 409[0], 413[1], 415[0]: current Resonance Lens and older Focusing Crystal must match the complete exact source identity, tier, form, quantity and lineage. All other consumed names stay unchanged.

## Scribing

Separate the actual carrier, authentic readable content and any magical payload. A printed title does not supply a spell, accurate map, known translation or concealed meaning. Book/Text bodies have finite page blocks, and maps/diagrams use a real sheet. The full original magic-bearing inscription/page block must remain in its one bound item for the whole-item benefit. Dividing pages does not divide a bonus or produce several scripts. Covers and supports are not another magical inscription.

Ordinary book consultation takes two manipulating hands unless a real stable support permits physically possible one-handed operation. A supported open script must stay within 5 feet and readable; it does not follow a moving caster. Spell somatic/material/focus requirements remain. If reading and components cannot both be performed, change the real arrangement rather than waive a hand. A closed bag gives no reading access.

**Encoding:** a new script is blank. Choose one authentic, complete operational spell/ritual at or below the card's rank capacity and record its exact version and fixed class placement. A trained Calligrapher works at the actual desk/kit using INT for `max(1, rank)` active hours, the same number of sp and 0.01 lb per hour of new compatible encoding supplies. DC is `10 + rank`, resolved as a Core binary task. Failure spends time/materials; no XP or Forge reduction. Successful encoding fixes the one payload. No overwrite, recharge or extra capacity from unused pages. Original source use counts are lifetime legal cast starts, not readings or automatic spell grants.

**Casting:** the narrow new script rule permits that payload to act as temporary repertoire for one activation only. The reader must already have the actual class/list placement, level/rank access, normal RP, components and every other casting qualification. It grants no off-list/noncaster access and no cheaper choice of another class placement. Use the spell's own casting time, range, effect, concentration, components and per-rest constraints. Commit one script use with the normal resources and record the actual named spell in its normal cadence. It is a spell for Counterspell. A committed interrupted/countered cast stays spent; a readied release does not count a second cast. Copying words never copies an activation budget.

**Research and navigation:** declare the actual lead and relevant content. Consult for at least one genuinely necessary minute, or throughout an independently authorized shorter task, with the complete unsuppressed item still participating at the check. Research can apply to the supported Arcana, History, Religion, Nature or Engineering question. A map must actually cover the place/route; a diagram must actually address the mechanism. These research/navigation bonuses apply only to their genuinely relevant recorded subject and actual eligible information task. They do not improve attacks, saves, spell DCs, spellcasting, unrelated checks, manufacture or repair. The item's own authoring, copying or encoding, and creation of a new bonus-granting reference from that same item, receive no self-production bonus. Genuine relevant formula research remains separately eligible without guaranteeing discovery. Use equipment.leather-task-enhancement once with the strongest eligible source; another person's item does not donate its bonus without their actual permitted participation.

**Map reroll:** after a related failed check but before consequences, its physical item may spend the printed reroll for the actual formal expedition. Reroll the original die selection with the same DC, modifiers, context and native result rules; accept the replacement even if worse. At most one Scribing reroll on that check. The budget belongs to the item, not each reader. A Watch, owner change, repair, brief Haven stop or a copied map does not reset it. Only genuine prior expedition closure and a newly authorized expedition create another entitlement. Independent non-Scribing rules keep their own explicit combination restrictions.

**Language Insight:** `languages:0` means zero Insight, not an implied language. Where a card genuinely offers Insight, require useful authentic parallel text and a language the reader actually understands. One active study hour grants the stated award once for that reader/target language/underlying evidence, with a maximum of six unspent points. Copying, photographing, rebuying or remaking the same evidence does not renew it; another genuine reader may earn their own nontransferable award. Overflow is not banked.

Spend three Insight plus eight active study hours on one preregistered supported passage of at most 250 words to obtain its supported literal meaning. This grants no fluency, proficiency, cipher key, absent meaning, magical casting permission or cosmological interpretation. A project cannot reuse the same paid points or overlapping hours; study grants no crafting XP. A reference formula grants its stated research benefit, not an unlimited supply of the material named in its title. A consumed case on a diagram recipe remains a consumed process input, not a free retained case for each output.

## Tinkering

**Trap restoration:** The original 3-lb trigger frame and original 2-lb working striker/discharge block are primary and must survive in recoverable form. The 2-lb captive spring/actuation member and separate subordinate contacts, supports, catches, grip covers, fittings and feet may be replaced with actual compatible stock through the applicable restoration procedure. Contacts do not replace the primary discharge block. Preserve the same trap identity, original primaries, 12 × 12 × 3-inch envelope, 8-lb empty mass and 3/2/2/1-lb allocation. A missing/nonfunctional spring prevents discharge until repair is complete. Positive-HP repair uses DC10, max(1 hour, recorded maximumHP/10 hours) and 2.50 gp material reference; HP0 uses the original recipe DC, max(8 hours, maximumHP/10 hours) and 25% of the existing finished-unit reference. Actual trained Tinker's tools, INT, station, implements, compatible stock and completed work remain necessary. No spring replacement refills charges, services the trap, resets arming/command/offense/custody history, removes flaws or reverses terminal dismantling. Original-actuator requirements for automaton chassis and other bodies are unchanged.

**Traps:** place one on an actual stable eligible unoccupied 5-foot square using both hands, Tinker's tools and one minute. Arm with an Action; arming completes at the next regular-turn start or after six elapsed seconds outside initiative. At most three armed crafted traps per setter. The first genuine creature entry, including allies/forced movement/fall/teleport arrival, can discharge it. Spend one charge and the setter's shared offensive allowance; card DEX save gives full printed damage on failure, zero on success. If the allowance is already spent, there is no discharge, charge spend or queued later damage. Setter incapacity/death alone does not disable its automatic eligibility.

After discharge it is unarmed. Movement disarms it. Manual rearming requires the real cleared-square setup and delay; one physical item can discharge at most once per numbered round. Remaining in place is not entry. No class Net Trap restraint or Hunting Trap profile is added. A capable authorized setter can safely disarm their accessible trap with one Action. This is distinct from the unfamiliar-trap procedure: trained thieves' tools or Tinker's tools, the appropriate DEX/INT method, one active minute and the printed trap save DC. Neither route grants free movement, rearming, servicing or replenishment. An unfamiliar disarm requires trained thieves' tools or Tinker's tools, a physically suitable DEX/INT method, one minute and the card's save DC as a Core binary task. Failure triggers only an actual qualifying physical disturbance/entry, not an invented automatic damaging result.

**Automata:** choose the actual Tiny/Small ground chassis before work. Use printed fixed creature AC/HP and the card's complete fixed magical melee attack, with ordinary attack natural results and no extra ability damage or condition rider. No level scaling or borrowed class-drone attack. Tiny has STR6, DEX12, CON10, INT3, WIS10, CHA1, walk20 feet and at most5 lb in its simple grippers. Small has STR12, DEX10, CON12, INT3, WIS10, CHA1, walk25 feet and at most20 lb. Both have ordinary senses/passive10, no PB or general humanoid item slots, poison damage immunity and Poisoned immunity only. Carrying still depends on real geometry, Load and available manipulators; it can make the attack/task impossible.

An automaton does not need food, breath or biological sleep; it still needs its actual operating allowance. At zero HP it is a destroyed wreck, with reconstruction possible only when original primaries genuinely survive. No death saves, Wounds or ordinary biological healing. Compatible positive-HP class repair and exact reconstruction exceptions are separate. Suppression makes it inert without changing its printed HP/AC or stopping elapsed clocks.

**Control:** a capable consenting, nonhostile INT6+ actor can register an unbound craft automaton with ten minutes and actual Tinker's tools; proficiency is not required for this binding. An existing registration requires the controller's actual release or a separately lawful process. Possession or sale alone is not a hostile rebind. A controller may have three active ordinary crafted automata; other actual stock is inert. Class Anacite and deployable slots keep their separate limits.

Across relevant class and crafted devices, one owner-wide Bonus Action command and one relevant offensive allowance are available per numbered round. On the controller's regular turn, communicate within 60 feet through a real audible/visible channel; the commanded subject executes after the whole controller turn. It may move and make one printed attack or one allowed task. No independent Reaction. A powered uncommanded automaton may only move defensively and Dodge. A committed failed command stays spent. Each subject also retains its own offensive-spend history, so changing controllers does not grant a second attack. Extra turns, another trade, another device, a trap trigger and class synchronization do not manufacture a fresh shared allowance.

Finite noncombat programming takes ten minutes: an actual recorded route, limited carriage, a known lever or visible finite sorting task. Start with an Action and the actual paid operating block. It grants no artisan/professional work, consumable/script activation or independent use of complex equipment. New facts or a blocked route do not create new capabilities.

**Service units:** the source encounter count is a finite operating budget. First actual operation of this automaton in a genuine encounter spends one service unit and opens that encounter's paid operating block, lasting at most ten elapsed minutes from that operation. Mere encounter start does not debit a stored or otherwise unoperated automaton. Ordinary idleness before a block starts spends nothing and grants no active behavior. Continuing operation beyond a block's expiry needs another paid block; a distinct genuine encounter has its own service requirement. Preserve TI-05's transition from an operating noncombat block into a genuine encounter, its no-refund/no-clock-pause rules and its physical-identity history. Noncombat operation spends one per ten-minute block. Entering combat closes the noncombat block and pays a new unit. Pauses, shutdown and suppression do not pause the clock. An item reaching zero remaining units inside an already paid block can finish that block; after expiry it is inert until serviced. No encounter splitting, controller transfer or rest refill.

**Utility devices:** the lead must personally use the probe, driver or gauge in genuinely necessary physically compatible work for at least one minute, or throughout a separately allowed shorter task. Declare one utility and spend one charge before the check. Apply the strongest eligible equipment.leather-task-enhancement once; a stronger competitor does not refund that charge. Actual ordinary tools remain required. A sensor only compares exposed reachable details, not hidden minds, enemies through walls or passwords.

A utility never assists the creation, repair, re-encoding, recharge or servicing of any bonus-granting advanced/gathering item, including another utility. Actual ordinary engineering, compatible tool work, exposed-mechanism investigation and genuine formula research remain eligible when its physical function applies. Complete positive-HP accessible unsuppressed participating state is required during work and at resolution.

**Servicing:** after ending active operation, a trained Tinker with actual tools, the complete positive-HP body and a genuinely safe surface spends one active hour and one real compatible maintenance pack. Pack reference is 10% of its admitted mundane body value; mass is 1% of body mass rounded up to 0.01 lb, minimum0.01 lb. INT tool DC10 uses Core binary task resolution. Success sets the printed charge/service maximum; failure spends the pack/time. No surplus, HP healing, XP, Forge reduction or new critical bonus. It does not reset prior rounds, paid blocks or physical history. Field servicing can qualify on that actual safe site even though field creation is false. Body repair alone never services it.

Use the actual acquired class card's action, cost, range, eligibility, cooldown, slot and duration; the statements here add no free feature.
