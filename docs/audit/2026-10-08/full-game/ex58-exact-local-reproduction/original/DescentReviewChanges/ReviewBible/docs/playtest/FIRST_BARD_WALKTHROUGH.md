# Your first Bard — a worked character and rules walkthrough

**Reviewed authored teaching draft.** Human teaching/balance, runtime and final systemic consistency remain open. Mira is a level-1 example built from the current character, ancestry, equipment, class and spell procedures. Use this beside the [new player guide](NEWCOMER_PLAYER_GUIDE.md) and [Bard cards](../mechanics/BARD_PLAYTEST_v0_1.md). The example introduces no new class abilities or setting lore.

Mira is a traveling musician who served as a battlefield medic. She helps companions stay alive, encourages their attacks, and carries more equipment than she can move with comfortably. Her strength is choosing the right intervention: her first-level RP pool is small.

## 1. Fill in the character sheet

Choose **Bard 1**, **Human, Nomadic variant**, and **Battlefield Medic**. Use the standard array, applying the base and variant increases once.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| DEX | 14 | +1 | 15 | +2 |
| CON | 13 | +2 | 15 | +2 |
| INT | 12 | +1 | 13 | +1 |
| WIS | 8 | +2 | 10 | +0 |
| CHA | 15 | +1 | 16 | +3 |

Record **10/10 HP**, **one unspent d8 Hit Die**, **PB +2**, **2/2 RP**, **0 Dissonance**, no previous ordinary spell, and no concentration. Mira is Medium. Her base walking Speed is 30 feet; her current carried load reduces it below.

Choose and record each training grant separately:

|---|---|---|
| Bard: three skills | Acrobatics, Investigation, Performance | +4, +3, +5 |
| Human: one skill | Perception | +2 |

| Battlefield Medic: tools | Healer's kit, herbalism kit | Training does not supply a second physical kit |

Bard gives light-armor and simple-weapon training. Its instrument weapon permission uses the existing improvised-weapon procedure. Mira's proficient saves are **DEX +4 and CHA +5**; her other saves are STR +0, CON +2, INT +1 and WIS +0. No choice here duplicates another proficiency or grants Expertise.

Human supplies speaking, reading and writing **Common and one chosen extra language: Dwarvish**. Nomadic Human supplies advantage on saves against extreme heat and cold, knowledge of north, and the scoped navigation advantage above. It grants no general elemental resistance or knowledge of an unknown route.

Keep the complete [ancestry/background procedures](../mechanics/ANCESTRY_BACKGROUND_PLAYTEST.md) beside these summaries. Battlefield Medic's **Trauma Response** uses a real healer-kit charge to stabilize a living dying creature, then grants 1 HP when recovery is permitted. It does not revive the dead or remove Wounds. The feature also identifies the nature and severity of injuries as printed. Record its starting relationships: **Liked with veterans of the conflict Mira served in; Neutral with those who fought on the opposing side**. The Director names the actual groups for the campaign; these relationship labels do not establish new factions or a numeric conversion.

## 2. Select four Base abilities and four ordinary spells

The Bard's level-1 schedule grants four Base selections, at least two active. This example chooses four actives:

| Selected card | Action and RP | First-level use |
|---|---|---|
| Verse of Courage | Action, 0 RP | Performance attack within 60 feet; hit deals 1d6 psychic. Hit or miss, one other eligible ally within 30 feet gains +1 damage on its next successful weapon attack before the end of Mira's next turn. |
| Chant of Renewal | Action, 2 RP; later pulses Bonus Action and 1 RP each | One eligible living ally within 15 feet heals 1d6 + 3. Concentration; at most three pulses including the first, and every later pulse must be paid. |
| Dissonant Note | Action, 1 RP; Recharge 2 | One eligible enemy within 60 feet makes WIS DC 13. Failure denies its Reaction until Mira's next regular-turn start. If it is concentrating, failure also requires CON DC 13 to keep that concentration. |
| Echo of Inspiration | Bonus Action, 1 RP; Recharge 2 | Store one eligible immediate performance's unmodified damage or healing dice. At the next regular-turn start, repeat half, rounded down with a positive minimum of 1, to one original eligible target within 30 feet. |

**Performance attack +5; performance DC 13; S = 1 song die and C = max(1, Charisma modifier) = 3.** C is the printed-effect Charisma value, not Mira's changing Crescendo stack count. Targets must satisfy the complete card, including perception, line of effect and hearing. Dissonant Note has no level-1 Stun. Echo copies the selected dice, not Charisma, encouragement, conditions or another Echo. The complete cards govern five-stack riders and exceptions.

Separately select the Bard's fallback starting repertoire: **two cantrips and two ranked spells**. These are operational catalog choices with Bard level-1 access; selecting them uses the ordinary spell allowance, not a Base-card choice.

| Known spell | Bard rank / base cost | What to remember |
|---|---|---|
| Mage Light | 0 / 0 RP | Action, V/S. One perceived unoccupied point within 30 feet; a fixed orb illuminates a 20-foot radius for one hour. Recasting replaces the old orb. It does not follow Mira. |
| Message | 0 / 0 RP | Action, V/S. One perceived creature within 120 feet hears a brief whispered message. No automatic reply, translation or communication through total cover. |
| Shield | 1 / 2 RP | Reaction, V/S/F. On its actual targeting trigger, protect Mira or one perceived willing creature within 30 feet with +5 AC until Mira's next regular-turn start. Use its exact Magic Missile exception. |
| Detect Magic | 1 / 2 RP | Action, V/S/F; concentration up to ten minutes. Perceive eligible active auras within 30 feet; a later Action can examine one aura's unprotected broad category. It does not identify every property or reveal through total cover. |

Use the [spell catalog](../../data/public/spell_playtests/catalog.json) and [casting procedure](../mechanics/SPELL_INTEGRATION_RULES.md) for the complete records. An actual instrument is Mira's ordinary Bard focus. A hand holding only that focus can supply F and S for the same cast; other objects and restrictions still matter. Voice alone suffices for an eligible Bard performance, but it does not replace a spell's S or F.

Mira's ordinary spell attack is +5, spell DC 13, and Dissonance Limit is **2 + PB 2 + CHA 3 = 7**. Her performances and spells share the same 2 RP. Two known ranked spells do not supply two separate pools.

## 4. Read a roll before choosing a result

For a relevant Perception task at DC 12, a roll of 11 gives **11 + 2 = 13: Success**. A natural 1 gives total 3, initially Failure, then worsens one degree to Critical Failure. Use the task's stated consequences; a degree does not invent injury or hidden information.

For Verse of Courage against AC 12, a natural 7 gives **7 + 5 = 12: a hit**. Beating AC by ten would not make a critical hit. For Dissonant Note's WIS DC 13, a save total 7 fails; the target loses its Reaction as printed. Its full Action remains available. These are three different roll procedures.

## 5. Work through two Bard turns

This teaching scene assumes a genuine hostile encounter, not rehearsal for resource gain. Mira begins with 10 HP, 2 RP, 0 Dissonance, 0 Crescendo, no last performance, no pending Echo and ready recharge counters. There is one eligible enemy with AC 12, 30 HP and no relevant defenses, within 25 feet; a hearing ally stands within 10 feet. Everyone has line of effect. Enemy statistics and rolls here are illustrative inputs, not a new bestiary creature or encounter-balance recommendation.

1. **First regular turn, stance then Action:** Mira chooses Mobile before acting. For this example she makes no movement or opportunity attack, so Mobile changes none of the listed AC, Speed, attack or damage values. It grants no free movement. Mira starts Verse of Courage. This first performance gains 1 Crescendo. Her roll 7 + 5 hits AC 12; the d6 shows 4. The enemy falls from 30 to 26 HP. The other ally receives the card's encouragement.
2. **Same turn, Bonus Action:** Mira pays 1 RP for Echo of Inspiration, recording Verse's raw die total 4 and the enemy's identity. RP becomes 1; Echo recharge becomes 2. Crescendo remains 1 because she already gained her one permitted stack this regular turn. Her last started performance is now Echo of Inspiration.
3. **Enemy turn:** An otherwise legal attack totals 15 against Mira's AC 13 and deals 3 resolved damage, with no temporary HP or Maiming. HP becomes 7. This neither crosses half her maximum nor drops her to zero, so it requires no Wound save. The damage resets Crescendo and the last-performance identity. The pending Echo remains; damage alone does not erase its record. Mira has only 1 RP and cannot pay Shield's 2 RP.
4. **Second regular-turn start:** Mira refreshes her Reaction and ticks Echo recharge from 2 to 1. Still conscious, audible and with the original enemy eligible within 30 feet, she resolves the Echo: floor(4 × 0.5) = **2 psychic damage**, leaving the enemy at 24 HP. Echo resolution spends no new Action or RP and gains no Crescendo. The pending package is gone.
5. **Second turn, stance then Action:** After the automatic start effects in step 4 resolve, Mira chooses Mobile again before acting. She again makes no movement or opportunity attack. Mira pays her last RP for Dissonant Note. RP becomes 0 and its recharge becomes 2. It is the first performance since the damage reset, so Crescendo becomes 1. The enemy's WIS save totals 7 against DC 13 and fails. Its Reaction is unavailable until Mira's next regular-turn start. Assume it is not concentrating; there is no concentration save. It is not Stunned and can still act normally within its other restrictions.

At this point Mira has **7 HP, 0 RP, 0 Dissonance, 1 Crescendo, last performance Dissonant Note, no pending Echo**, Echo recharge 1 and Dissonant Note recharge 2. These class techniques did not add ordinary spell Dissonance. They also did not qualify their turns for passive idle decay.

The encounter continues under its actual situation. On Mira's next regular turn, Echo will be ready after its tick, but she still cannot pay it with 0 RP. Dissonant Note's counter will be 1. She cannot start Chant of Renewal for free. Verse of Courage and an ordinary weapon action remain possible if their own requirements are met. Winning or ending the encounter clears Bard rhythm, not spent RP or current HP.

## 6. Compare three different interventions

These are separate decision examples with their stated starting states, not free resets in the preceding encounter.

**Healing with 2 RP.** At her regular-turn start in this separate genuine encounter, Mira has 2 RP, 0 Crescendo, no last performance and no concentration. After mandatory start processing, she chooses Mobile before her first performance of the turn. She and the target satisfy the Chant's actual voice, hearing, perception and line-of-effect requirements. The living ally is at 3/10 normal HP within 15 feet, has no Wounds or other healing modifiers/blockers, and has not received Chant of Renewal healing this round. Mira starts the Chant, pays 2 RP, starts concentration and rolls a 4: **4 + C 3 = 7 healing**, bringing the ally to 10 HP. Starting gains the one eligible Crescendo stack, not a five-stack rider. At 0 RP she cannot pay the next turn's 1-RP maintenance. Without a genuine intervening recovery, the Chant ends at that turn's end with no second pulse. The selected feature can offer three pulses; her current resources fund only one. This is no Wound treatment, free Echo or reset of the preceding encounter.

**Preventing a hit with 2 RP and an available Reaction.** In this separate fixture, Mira is in Mobile stance and has 2 RP, 0 Dissonance, no remembered ordinary spell and an available Reaction, with no condition barring the cast. Before the trigger, her flute is already held in a hand containing no other object; she can provide V and use that hand for S/F. This does not grant a free off-turn draw. When Mira becomes the legal target of an attack, she can commit Shield before that attack resolves. AC becomes 18. An attack total of 15 then misses. RP becomes 0, and a first ordinary rank-1 cast adds 1 Dissonance. Shield expires at her next regular-turn start. Casting it after HP has already been lost would be too late. A previously spent Reaction, an applicable Reaction prohibition, missing components or only 1 RP would prevent activation. Keep the spell's actual trigger; this example does not introduce an outside game's casting window.

**Emergency aid with 0 RP.** An adjacent companion is living and dying at zero HP after the complete damage/Wound procedure, with no recovery prohibition. Mira has an available hand, Action and accessible healer's kit. Spend one kit charge: ten becomes nine. The kit stabilizes the companion; Battlefield Medic's Trauma Response then grants 1 HP. The death-save counters clear, and zero-HP unconsciousness ends if it was the only cause. Prone and existing Wounds remain. No RP is spent. A Dead companion or a blocker to regaining HP requires its own rules; the feature does not bypass either.

## 7. Practice ordinary spell repetition

Start this separate sequence with no previous ordinary spell and 0 Dissonance. Each cast has a legal Action, target and V/S; the target of Message understands the actual language used. Take one entry per regular turn:

| Turn | Choice | Dissonance after the turn | Previous ordinary spell / repetition |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Mage Light at one legal point | 0 | Mage Light / no repeat |
| 2 | Message to the ally | 0 | Message / no repeat |
| 3 | Message again | 1 | Message / first repeat adds 1 |
| 4 | An ordinary nonmagical turn, with no spell or magical class ability | 0 | Still Message / repetition history survives |
| 5 | Message again | 2 | Message / second repeat adds 2 |

Each spell here costs 0 RP. None generates Crescendo. The quiet turn reduced Dissonance by one; it did not turn the next Message into a new first cast. Ordinary words spoken aloud are not another spell and supply no magical reply channel. The Mage Light orb stays at its original point until replaced or its one-hour duration ends.

## 8. Recover, then advance only when earned

After the party actually reaches a permitted recovery opportunity, a completed beneficial one-hour short rest allows Retune. For Mira, the recovery calculation is **round-half-up(2 ÷ 4) + 3 = 1 + 3 = 4**, capped by the 2-RP maximum. From 0 RP she returns to 2, and Dissonance clears. She gains no spare RP from the excess calculation. Track HP treatment and Hit Dice separately; quoting Retune is not healing. The ordinary limit is two beneficial short rests between beneficial long rests, with one beneficial long rest per 24 elapsed hours. Retune does not clear Mira's remembered ordinary-spell name or Refrain counter. In the §7 sequence, a short rest after the fifth turn clears Dissonance but leaves Message and its second-repeat history; another legal Message would be the third repeat and add 3 Dissonance. A beneficial completed long rest instead clears the remembered name and resets that counter, so the next legal Message begins a new sequence.

An announced milestone can later grant Bard 2. Using the fixed HP increment, maximum HP becomes **8 + 2 + 5 + 2 = 17**. If current HP was 7, it stays 7. Maximum RP becomes 3; a current pool of 0 remains 0. Add the new unspent d8 while retaining the status of the old die. The Bard-2 schedule offers two Base choices; Verse Mastery and Lingering Notes are legal examples. Advancing does not supply an Arch ability yet.

At **Bard 3** the schedule enters Virtuoso Arch. At **Bard 10** it selects Harmony, Virtuoso Hero or Discord. These are later stages of Mira's Bard class, with their own acquisition rows; they are not additional first-level characters or a reason to grant every card now.
