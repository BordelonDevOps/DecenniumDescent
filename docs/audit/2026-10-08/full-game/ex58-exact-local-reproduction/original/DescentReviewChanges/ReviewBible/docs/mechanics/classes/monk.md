## Class engine and setup

**Book setup (provisional engine resolves any explicit conflicts below):**

- Main Attribute: Dexterity
- Secondary Attributes: Wisdom, Constitution
- Hit Points: Medium (8 + Constitution modifier at 1st level, 1d8 per level thereafter)
- Armor Proficiency: None (benefits from Unarmored Flow)
- Weapon Proficiency: Simple weapons, monk weapons (kama, nunchaku, staff)
- Tool Proficiency: One artisan's tool of your choice

PROVISIONAL PLAYTEST CONTRACT. Dexterity is the Monk class ability. S = 1/2/3/4 at Monk levels 1/5/11/17. M = max(1, Dexterity modifier). Monk technique attack = d20 + normal character proficiency bonus + Dexterity modifier. Monk technique save DC = 8 + normal character proficiency bonus + Dexterity modifier.

Ki Chain is a Monk-only resource from 0 to 5. Begin each real encounter at 0 Ki and clear Ki when the encounter ends or after 1 minute without a real encounter. Ki cannot be generated by sparring with willing creatures, attacking objects, repeatedly dodging harmless attacks, healing between encounters or other staged actions. Ki is separate from Resonance Points and Dissonance.

Normal Ki generation is capped at 2 Ki during your own regular turn and 1 Ki total from reactions or defensive events between the end of one regular turn and the start of your next. Explicit spending has no per-turn cap other than available Ki. A feature cannot raise Ki above 5. Incapacitation or Stunned resets Ki to 0; ordinary damage does not.

All Monks automatically know Tiger, Tortoise and Crane Stances at level 1. Only one core stance can be active at a time. While unarmored and not using a shield, you may switch to one known core stance on your own turn without an Action, at most once per round. The stance remains until changed, incapacitated or the encounter ends. The optional Stance Shift feature improves when that same once-per-round switch may occur; it does not create a second switch.

Unarmored Flow is automatic class identity rather than a selectable card. Wearing armor or using a shield suppresses the mechanical benefits printed on Tiger, Tortoise and Crane Stances, Unarmored Speed, Perfect Balance and Tortoise Discipline features unless a card explicitly says otherwise. Ki Chain itself and Monk techniques remain usable unless their card requires being unarmored.

Tiger, Tortoise and Crane Ki generation obey the global Ki-gain ceilings. Tiger can generate Ki from critical unarmed strikes. Tortoise can generate Ki from specified defensive successes. Crane provides mobility and support but does not create free out-of-combat healing. A single event can satisfy more than one generator, but total gain still cannot exceed the appropriate own-turn or off-turn ceiling.

MINOR WEAVE INTERFACE. Cards tagged weave are magical Monk techniques. If a tagged technique would add at least 1 Dissonance under the broader Weave rules, after validating all ordinary costs but before resolution you may spend 1 additional Ki to prevent that 1 Dissonance. This prevention Ki is separate from the card's printed Ki and RP costs. It cannot prevent more than 1 Dissonance from one technique and cannot reduce Dissonance already present.

All printed RP costs use the character's existing Resonance Point pool where applicable; Monk cards create no second RP pool. Ki never converts to RP unless a card explicitly says so. Generic spell-slot mechanics do not apply. Monk techniques are not ordinary spells unless their card expressly says otherwise.

STUN GUARD. A creature can be Stunned by your Monk features at most once per encounter. Mark it after the first Monk-caused Stunned condition successfully applies. If another of your Monk features would Stun that marked creature later in the same encounter, the creature still makes the required save; on failure it instead loses its Reaction until the end of its next turn. This guard is tracked separately for each Monk.

Temporary maximum-HP reductions caused by Monk features do not stack on the same creature; use the largest reduction from your Monk features. Maximum HP cannot be reduced below 1. Unless a card specifies an earlier end, the reduction lasts until the creature completes a short or long rest. Restoring maximum HP does not restore current HP. These effects are temporary combat trauma, not unstated permanent injuries.

Only one concentration effect may be maintained at a time. Starting another concentration effect ends the previous one. Damage uses the current Core Systems concentration rule; any required half-damage rounding is down for this pilot. Round recharge ticks once at the start of the Monk's regular initiative turn, including while incapacitated; extra Actions and extra turns never create additional ticks.

All sources share a maximum of one additional full Action per creature per round. Additional Actions cannot grant another additional Action, Bonus Action or Reaction. A named ability can receive at most one positive-counter recharge reset or shortening per round. Only an ability whose current recharge counter is above 0 can be reset or shortened.

PROPOSED ACQUISITION. Monk 1 automatically gains Ki Flow, Unarmored Flow, all three core Stances and basic once-per-round stance switching, then chooses 4 Base cards, at least 2 Active. Monk 2 chooses 2 additional Base cards. Monk 3 enters Master and chooses 2 Arch cards, at least 1 Active. Levels 4 through 8 each grant one eligible unselected Base or Arch choice. Level 9 grants one eligible Arch choice and Martial Apex automatically.

Discipline Initiate is an optional Base selection. Choosing Tank does not grant five free Base abilities: it unlocks the four non-final Tortoise Discipline cards as additional Arch-selection options at their listed Monk levels. Final Form — Bastion of Breath is never a normal selection; at Monk 9 or the first later level when eligible it is gained automatically only if you chose Tank through Discipline Initiate and know at least three of Stone Shell Discipline, Redirecting Palm, Iron Shell Pulse and Echoing Guard. Tortoise Discipline remains compatible with any Hero path chosen later.

At Monk 10 choose Enlightened Master, Iron Fist Master or Way of Shadow and choose 2 cards from that Hero path. Levels 11, 13, 15, 17 and 19 each grant one eligible unselected Base/Arch card, including unlocked non-final Tortoise cards. Levels 12, 14, 16 and 18 each grant one Hero-path choice. At Monk 20 the chosen Hero path's Final is automatic and cannot be selected earlier. ASI/feat milestones remain separate.

All acquisition and scaling gates use Monk class level, not total character level. Normal character proficiency bonus still uses the game's ordinary total-character-level rule. A multiclass dip cannot unlock Master, Tortoise Final Form, Hero features or a level-20 Final.

Healing from Monk cards cannot exceed maximum HP, restore the dead or remove Wounds unless explicitly stated. Permanent HP recovery and Ki generation cannot be farmed outside encounters. Resurrection effects are explicitly limited-use. No Monk card can instantly kill a target merely because a descriptive source used language such as Death Touch; death continues to follow the game's ordinary damage and dying rules.

Unarmored Flow completion: while wearing no armor or shield, AC=10+DEX modifier+WIS modifier, or ordinary armorless AC if higher, never both. Unarmed strikes use DEX for attack/damage and 1d6 at 1, 1d8 at 5, 1d10 at 11, 1d12 at 17. This is a new numeric completion; no automatic Extra Attack beyond printed techniques.

Each technique tagged weave creates 1 D in this pilot, unless it explicitly says otherwise. Ki can prevent up to 1 D before resolution only if remaining Ki pays that additional cost. Full-Ki capstones may legally accept D. The Stun Guard shares the suite-wide once/character/target hard-denial mark; its later failed-save fallback of reaction loss is an explicit Monk exception, not a second Stun.

**Protected source interface:** Spend 1 Ki when using a minor Weave technique to prevent 1 Dissonance from that technique; Ki and Resonance remain separate pools.

## Worked encounter

1. Monk 5 example: Dexterity 16 gives M=3, PB=3 and S=2. The Monk is unarmored, starts a real encounter in Tiger Stance with Ki 0, knows Flowing Form, Flurry of Blows, Patient Defense and Focused Ki Burst, and begins with Dissonance 2 and 6 RP.
2. Turn 1: move at least 10 feet and use Flurry of Blows. Both unarmed strikes hit. Flurry grants 1 Ki from its first successful hit and Flowing Form grants 1 Ki for the move-plus-strike sequence, reaching the own-turn gain ceiling: Ki 0→2. A Tiger critical could not raise this turn above 2.
3. Turn 2: spend 1 Ki on Patient Defense, Ki 2→1. Before the next turn an enemy attacks and misses; Patient Defense grants 1 off-turn Ki, Ki 1→2. Even if another enemy also missed during that interval, the off-turn defensive gain ceiling prevents another Ki gain.
4. Turn 3: Flurry of Blows again. One strike critically hits in Tiger Stance. Tiger grants 1 Ki and Flowing Form grants 1 Ki, so Ki 2→4. Flurry cannot add a third point because the own-turn gain ceiling is 2.
5. Turn 4: use Focused Ki Burst, paying its printed 2 Ki and 2 RP, Ki 4→2 and RP 6→4. Because it is tagged weave and would add 1 Dissonance in this example, spend 1 additional Ki to prevent that Dissonance, Ki 2→1. The burst then resolves normally; the prevention payment does not alter RP.
6. Turn 5: switch from Tiger to Crane using the automatic once-per-round stance switch, then use Breath of Focus instead of attacking. Breath grants 1 Ki during a real encounter, Ki 1→2. No out-of-combat repetition could generate this Ki, and changing stance itself generates none.

## Source appendix and open balance questions

The complete original Monk book section, including lore, examples and alternate progression, is preserved in the [source evidence JSON](../../../data/playtest/class_sources/monk.json). The supplemental source tables are [preserved separately](../../../data/playtest/class_sources/supplements.json). Do not stack older level-table features on top of this draft.

- Does the 2-Ki own-turn and 1-Ki off-turn generation ceiling preserve a satisfying martial flow while preventing Flurry, Tiger criticals, Flowing Form and Tortoise reactions from filling the entire 0-5 chain immediately?
- Does the gated Tortoise Discipline provide a meaningful off-tank identity without becoming strictly better than ordinary Arch selections or forcing Tank-discipline Monks into a specific Hero path?
- Are healing, Stunned effects, temporary maximum-HP reduction and high-level multi-strike techniques sufficiently bounded across repeated encounters without erasing the tactical differences among Enlightened Master, Iron Fist Master and Way of Shadow?

A complete card specification is not balance approval. Record actual play results, particularly resource exhaustion, condition locks, survival and how often choices become unusable.
