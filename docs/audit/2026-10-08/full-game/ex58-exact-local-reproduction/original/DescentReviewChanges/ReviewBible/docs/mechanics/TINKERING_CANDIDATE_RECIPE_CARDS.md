**Effect and operation**

This fixed trap has 1 charge(s). Spend one minute with both hands and actual Tinker's tools to place it on a stable eligible unoccupied 5-foot square, then an Action to arm; it becomes armed at the next regular-turn start or six-second boundary. Its first actual creature entrant, including allies, consumes one charge and the setter's available shared offensive allowance: DC 11 DEX, 1d8 acid on failure, zero on success. It then disarms. No discharge, charge spend or queued attack when the setter's allowance is unavailable. Forced movement/fall/teleport arrival can be entry; remaining in place is not. Moving it disarms it. Max three armed crafted traps per setter; manual reset, cleared square, delay and one-item-per-round guard remain. Setter incapacity/death alone does not switch off an eligible automatic trap. A capable authorized setter can safely disarm their accessible trap with one Action. This is distinct from the unfamiliar-trap procedure: trained thieves' tools or Tinker's tools, the appropriate DEX/INT method, one active minute and the printed trap save DC. Neither route grants free movement, rearming, servicing or replenishment. TI-02 gives the remaining full disarm rules. TI-06's trained one-hour servicing may refill charges; HP repair/rest may not.

**Routed Dissonance**

Only the acquired TD-08 feature, once per genuine short rest, permits a Bonus Action to route up to two current Dissonance into one own active qualifying device within 30 feet. The routed disabled state prevents movement, attacks, auras, triggers and every other active device use. A routed trap cannot discharge, and a routed utility cannot provide its charged task benefit while disabled. Existing durations and paid service/winding clocks continue. Clearing the disable requires its existing specifically authorized completed removal procedure; it does not refill charges, service, RP or Refrain, erase expenditures, or grant a queued activation. An invalid blocked declaration spends nothing; a later disable does not refund a previously legal committed cost. Actual Field Repair, the existing ten-active-minute/1RP/Tinker's-tools procedure, the origin Tinker's next completed genuine short rest or completed qualifying ordinary repair may clear only the permitted functional state. A buyer's unrelated rest does not clear it.
