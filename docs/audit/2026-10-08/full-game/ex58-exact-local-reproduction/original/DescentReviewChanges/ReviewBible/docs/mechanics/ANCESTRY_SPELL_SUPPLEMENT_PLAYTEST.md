# Ancestry spell dependency supplement

**REVIEWED authored PLAYTEST manuscript.** Seven existing spell identities plus ten new ancestry-grant-only procedures. All four exact-review repairs are applied. The seven existing identities are approved for ordinary catalog promotion; the ten additions grant no ordinary class access. Runtime and balance remain open.

[Ancestry casting route](ANCESTRY_BACKGROUND_PLAYTEST.md) · [Original grant evidence](ANCESTRY_BACKGROUND_SOURCE_BINDINGS.md)

Existing identities keep their own class access and source school placement. The ten additions grant no ordinary class access. All school/rank choices for those additions are explicit new mechanics. No source record was overwritten.

## Shared resolution

No automatic level scaling, generic upcast or new action budget. Use the actual casting source's access, components, costs, shared concentration and Dissonance/Refrain, then the legal interruption window. Countering or later failure refunds no committed use/payment or displaced concentration. Spell attacks use d20 + PB + actual source casting modifier; saves use DC 8 + PB + that modifier. Actual defenses, critical rules, core conditions, ordinary life-state rules and source-specific callbacks retain their scopes. Total cover prevents targeting unless this card expressly allows otherwise. For ancestry casts use ANCESTRY_BACKGROUND_PLAYTEST.md; existing ordinary identities retain their actual class/school/rank access and RP price.

## Shape Water

Transmutation · rank 0 · existing-catalog

**activation:** Action

**rangeFeet:** 5

**targets:** One contiguous quantity of nonmagical liquid water fitting a 5-foot cube within range; unattended, caster-held or willingly presented/contained. No hostile worn/carried container.

**duration:** Instantaneous; ordinary gravity resumes after resolution.

**concentration:** False

**components:** V, S

**resolution:** No attack or save.

**effect:** Move the actual water up to 5 feet along an unobstructed path into available space, or reshape its surface harmlessly. Cannot create, freeze, purify, split an occupied body, lift a creature, deal damage, grant a swimming speed or maintain an unsupported shape. Actual potable water remains potable; no abstract Supply is created.

**scaling:** None.

**designNotes:** PRESERVED existing catalog/Core identity, source placement, action, range and components. Explicit operational additions are conference decisions.

## Gust

Transmutation · rank 0 · existing-catalog

**activation:** Action

**rangeFeet:** 30

**targets:** One visible Medium-or-smaller creature, one unattended object weighing at most 5 lb, or one harmless air effect in a 5-foot cube within range.

**duration:** Instantaneous.

**concentration:** False

**components:** V, S

**resolution:** Creature: STR save against spell DC; failure permits the push, success no movement. Object/harmless modes need no roll.

**effect:** Push the selected creature 5 feet directly away from the caster into legal space. No damage or Prone rider; normal destination hazards apply. Forced movement does not provoke Opportunity Attacks unless an exact rule says otherwise. Alternatively move the selected object 5 feet away along a clear path without attack damage; or briefly disperse ordinary smoke in the cube or extinguish one candle. Cannot end magical clouds, defeat weather, create ongoing breathable air or change a sound spell.

**scaling:** None.

**designNotes:** PRESERVED existing catalog/Core identity, source placement, action, range and components. Explicit operational additions are conference decisions.

**rangeFeet:** 30

**targets:** One stationary object image fitting a 5-foot cube, or one sound originating at a chosen point within range.

**duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute.

Physical interaction reveals an image to that observer; an informed observer perceives through it. A believable distraction uses ordinary checks. Illusion · rank 1 · existing-catalog

**activation:** Action

**rangeFeet:** 0

**targets:** Yourself and your worn/carried gear; Self.

**duration:** 1 hour; no concentration. Ends on expiry, legal dispelling or your Action dismissal.

**concentration:** False

**components:** V, S, F

A studied identity supplies no unknown mannerisms/passwords. Recasting replaces the prior disguise without stacking. No external one-foot height/anatomical arrangement rule is imported.

## Water Breathing

Transmutation · rank 3 · existing-catalog

**activation:** Action

**rangeFeet:** 60

**targets:** Up to ten willing creatures you can see within range.

**duration:** Instantaneous casting; NEW persistent respiration benefit lasts 24 elapsed hours.

**concentration:** False

**components:** V, S, F

**resolution:** No attack or save.

**effect:** Each selected creature retains normal respiration and can breathe water. A creature that does not breathe gains no useful respiration benefit. No swimming speed, depth/temperature immunity, freedom of movement or immunity to suffocation outside breathable air/water. Does not create water or remove poison. The benefit can be legally dispelled on an affected creature individually using original cast rank/school. Recasting refreshes only selected targets, never doubles benefits.

**scaling:** None.
