## Class engine and setup

Wisdom governs spells, form attacks and class DC/M. Hit Die d8; book armor, weapons, saves and skills remain. Retain your own ability scores, HP, saves, proficiency and personality in all forms; form traits replace only the stated anatomy, movement and attacks. No additional monster stat block or separate beast HP pool. One natural attack per Attack Action unless a form explicitly states otherwise, attack PB+WIS, printed dice+WIS damage. Missing natural attack is 1d6 bludgeoning; default walking Speed 30 ft, normal AC, Medium size. Hawk is Tiny, Bear/Earth Warden/hero forms may be Large when space permits. Equipment melds and cannot be used while melded; magical numeric bonuses persist only if compatible with form anatomy and never duplicate armor.

This class follows its explicit book progression instead of the ordinary 25-choice template. Base 1 grants Druidic, spellcasting, all five Base forms and 2 shifts/long rest. Choose one of seven Circles at 2; its level 2 features are a Base-stage exception. Enter the chosen Circle Arch path at 3, gain its 6 feature, then Circle Hero forms at 10 and its 14 feature. Arch Forms unlock 11 despite the historical word Arch; these are forms, not a second promotion. At 11 attune up to 2 Arch forms during long rest. Base forms remain available at higher levels. All features below are automatic at their gate for the chosen Circle; no generic extra class-card choices are added.

Preparation: two cantrips at 1, three at 4, four at 10, plus explicit Circle bonuses. Prepare class level+WIS modifier ranked Druid spells (minimum 1) each long rest, rank limited by Druid spell-rank access, not merely by enough RP. Use approved operational spell entries and Core V 2 RP table/costs in the shared configuration. Book rank-times-two cost and imported spell-slot terminology are preserved as superseded alternatives for this pilot. Circle always-prepared spells do not count toward preparation and use ordinary RP/Dissonance. Rituals must have ritual tag, be prepared and take printed casting time plus 10 minutes; waive base RP only, never consumed materials or other costs. ASI/feat milestones 4/8/12/16/19 belong to general advancement and must not be granted twice.

Shifting: Bonus Action, one long-rest shift use, 10 minutes, early reversion Bonus Action. Reaching 0 HP or incapacitation ends form; excess damage stays on your own HP. Shift uses follow detailed book table:2 at 1, 3 at 4, 4 at 7, 5 at 10, 6 at 13, 7 at 16, 8 at 19. Supplemental runtime alternative 2/3/4/5/6 at 1/4/8/12/16 remains preserved. At 20 changing form is unlimited but does not refill finite form benefits. Speech unavailable in Base forms, available in Arch/Hero. No spellcasting in form until Beast Spells 18 except an explicit form permission. Maintaining an existing spell concentration is allowed, still only one concentration. Changing form spends a use and ends old form features/summons; it does not reset once/rest abilities.

Transformation reserves prevent unlimited replacement HP: at long rest record Buffer Reserve=2 L+M and Form Surge Reserve=PB. A form giving temporary HP draws that many from Buffer Reserve, up to remaining amount, with normal nonstacking; never real healing. A form special described once/transformation also spends 1 Form Surge and cannot be refreshed by resuming the form. Form Surge refills only long rest. Forms cannot grant free casts, RP or shift uses. Auras affect at most 3 enemies and once per enemy per round; retaliation once/round total; ongoing riders refresh, never stack. Entering a legal Shapeshift clears the Refrain counter and remembered repeated-spell name, as the protected source contract requires. It does not reduce accumulated Dissonance. Natural attacks do not clear Refrain.

Circle of the Land chooses one terrain at 2. Always-prepared spell pairs at 3/5/7/9 are exactly the source table lines 2662–2669, reproduced in source appendix; their source names resolve to existing approved spell entries before use. They do not bypass rank gates. For this pilot Circle spells are permitted exceptions to ordinary list membership, not newly authored variants. Moon Circle Forms CR table is historical external-stat language: use only these bounded forms, not arbitrary CR beasts. Early Moon benefit is +S damage on first form hit each turn. No imported monster multiattack, spellcasting or legendary actions.

Circle summary: Land preserves lore and recovery; Moon improves shifting; Storm controls lightning and wind; Thorns works poison and roots; Winterfang works cold; Rotshaper uses bounded swarms; Ashen Grove works fire and regrowth. Land/Thorns duplicate Blight Elk and Tanglemaw names are kept as separate source identities. Source auto-failed hostile saves in Master of Decay/Ash become once/turn-2 on one save, not unavoidable control. Stronger supplemental flavor riders are represented explicitly below or retained as unadopted alternatives in source provenance.

**Protected source interface:** Entering Shapeshift clears the Refrain counter because the Druid changes the body sounding the Weave; beast-form casting remains limited by class features.

## Worked encounter

1. Druid 5 Moon, WIS 16 PB 3 S 2 M 3: RP 12, shifts 3, Buffer Reserve 13, Form Surges 3, HP 30/38. Prepare 8 ranked spells plus three cantrips.
2. Turn 1 Bonus Action Bear: shifts 3→2, draw 13 of requested 20 buffer, reserve 13→0, temp HP 13. Action claw attack PB+WIS=+6, damage 2d6+3+1 plus Moon first-hit 2. Shifting clears the Refrain counter and remembered repeated-spell name; accumulated Dissonance is unchanged.
3. Enemy deals 10 damage: temp HP 13→3, real HP 30 remains. No additional beast HP exists.
4. Turn 2 Moon healing Bonus Action spends 2 RP, RP 12→10, rolls 2d8=7, HP 30→37. It cannot repair the buffer reserve. Action claw as usual.
5. Turn 3 Bonus Action change Wolf spends another shift, shifts 2→1. Old Bear temporary HP ends. Wolf bite 1d8+3, target STR against DC 14 or Prone. No new buffer, RP or Form Surge generated.
6. At 10 this character gains Moon Hero forms; Arch forms still wait until 11. At 20 unlimited changes still cannot refill Buffer or Surge reserves.

<details><summary>Preserved source wording — book line2414</summary>

Spellcasting
Drawing on the essence of nature itself, you can cast spells to shape that essence to your will. See chapter 10 for the general rules of spellcasting and chapter 11 for the druid spell list.
Cantrips
At 1st level, you know two cantrips of your choice from the druid spell list. You learn additional druid cantrips of your choice at higher levels, as shown in the Cantrips Known column of the Druid table.
Preparing and Casting Spells
The Druid table shows how many Resonance Points you have to cast your druid spells. A spell's base RP cost is twice its Resonance Rank (Rank 0 cantrips cost 0), modified by explicit class features and current Dissonance. You regain expended RP according to the Resonance recovery rules.
You prepare the list of druid spells that are available for you to cast, choosing from the druid spell list. When you do so, choose a number of druid spells equal to your Wisdom modifier + your druid level (minimum of one spell). The spells must be of a level for which you have Resonance Points.
For example, if you are a 3rd-level druid, you have 7 Resonance Points. With a Wisdom of 16, your list of prepared spells can include six spells, and they can be of 1st or 2nd level in any combination. If you prepare the Rank 1 spell cure wounds, its base cost is 2 Resonance Points before explicit specialization or Dissonance modifiers. Casting the spell doesn't remove it from your list of prepared spells.
You can change your list of prepared spells when you finish a long rest. Preparing a new list of druid spells requires time spent in prayer and meditation: at least 1 minute per spell level for each spell on your list.
Spellcasting Ability
Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for your druid spells, since your magic draws upon your devotion and attunement to nature. You use your Wisdom whenever a spell refers to your spellcasting ability. In addition, you use your Wisdom modifier when setting the saving throw DC for a druid spell you cast and when making an attack roll with one.
Spell save DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier
Spell attack modifier = your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier
Ritual Casting
You can cast a druid spell as a ritual if that spell has the ritual tag and you have the spell prepared.

Spellcasting Focus
You can use a druidic focus as a spellcasting focus for your druid spells.

</details>

<details><summary>Preserved source wording — book line2656</summary>

Natural Recovery
Starting at 2nd level, you can regain some of your magical energy by sitting in meditation and communing with nature. During a short rest, you choose expended Resonance Points to recover. The Resonance Points can have a combined level that is equal to or less than half your druid level (rounded up), and none of the points can be used to cast spells of 6th level or higher. You can't use this feature again until you finish a long rest.
For example, when you are a 4th-level druid, you can recover up to 2 Resonance Points during a short rest.
Circle Spells
Your mystical connection to the land infuses you with the ability to cast certain spells. At 3rd, 5th, 7th, and 9th level you gain access to circle spells connected to the land where you became a druid. Choose that land, arctic, coast, desert, forest, grassland, mountain, swamp, or Murk, and consult the associated list of spells.
Once you gain access to a circle spell, you always have it prepared, and it doesn't count against the number of spells you can prepare each day. If you gain access to a spell that doesn't appear on the druid spell list, the spell is nonetheless a druid spell for you.

</details>
