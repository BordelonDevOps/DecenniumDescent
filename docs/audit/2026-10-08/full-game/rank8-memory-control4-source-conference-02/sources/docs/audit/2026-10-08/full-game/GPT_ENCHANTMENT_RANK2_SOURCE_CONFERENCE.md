### 2. Dire Charm

**Action; 30 ft; one creature perceived with LoE; V/S/F; WIS save; 1 minute; no concentration.**

On initial failure, establish two separately scoped components:

1. **Core Charmed toward the caster.**
2. **Loyal-servant compulsion.**

The servant compulsion claims the shared hard-control allowance once when successfully established. If the hard component is unavailable, Core Charmed may still apply if otherwise legal. Charmed immunity blocks Charmed only; broader mental/control immunity may block the servant component according to its actual text.

While the servant component remains, at the beginning of each of the target's regular turns after normal start-phase events, the caster may give **one brief audible order in a language the target understands**. No Action/BA is charged to the caster beyond ordinary brief communication, but the target must actually hear/understand that order when given.

An order may direct:

- ordinary movement;
- one ordinary Action;
- one ordinary interaction;
- an ordinary weapon/natural/unarmed attack;
- protection/Help/Dash/Disengage or comparable non-limited ordinary behavior.

It cannot compel:

- direct self-harm or a knowingly suicidal route;
- expenditure of RP, spell casts, consumables, limited-use class resources or other optional finite resources;
- permanent surrender/ownership transfer;
- disclosure of a protected/private secret;
- knowing violation of a binding oath/duty.

The caster may order attacks against other creatures. The target chooses legal route/method/details not explicitly fixed by the order. No extra Action, movement, Reaction, ammo or resource is created.

With no valid order, the target retains its normal agency subject to Core Charmed and other conditions.

At the **end of every affected regular turn, including the current regular turn if Dire Charm was established during it**, the target repeats the WIS save. Success ends the entire Dire Charm application. Extra turns do not grant repeat saves.

One active Dire Charm servant application per target across casters; a later legal failed-save application that successfully establishes its components replaces the older Dire Charm. Saved/blocked applications do not displace an existing valid one. There is no harm-ending clause.

**NEW:** no-concentration decision, Charmed + separate servant decomposition, per-turn order procedure, resource/order limits and current-turn repeat save.

### 3. Group Pacify

Each target saves independently.

On failure, while affected it cannot voluntarily **initiate a new combat-hostile act**:

- an attack;
- a damaging spell/effect;
- knowingly harmful control;
- knowingly harmful indirect action.

It may move, withdraw, defend itself through nonharmful choices, communicate, interact, heal, buff, use nonharmful control, or take other legal actions.

This **can halt an already ongoing fight prospectively**: it prohibits new hostile commitments after application. It never rewinds an attack/spell/effect already committed.

Each target's restriction ends early when **any creature commits an attack or knowingly harmful effect against that target or a creature it actually recognizes as its ally**. Commitment is sufficient even if the attack later misses or damage is prevented.

Mere insults, argument, proximity or unsupported threat are not provocation.

The affected set is fixed at casting. Leaving the original cone does not end the effect and entering later does not add a target.

This is a category restriction on harmful initiation, not blanket Action denial; the hard whole-Action guard is not consumed merely because a creature has few useful alternatives.

**NEW:** WIS saves, concentration minute, initial snapshot, broad hostile-act predicate and concrete provocation.

### 4. Illusive Commands

- “Drop the red key.”
- “Walk away from the doorway.”
- “Kneel beside the table.”

On failure, the target must carry out that instruction at the first legal opportunity during its next regular turn using only its existing movement/Action/interaction permissions.

The instruction may require:

- ordinary movement;
- one ordinary Action;
- one ordinary interaction;
- becoming/remain Prone where appropriate.

It cannot compel an attack, damaging/harmful act, spellcasting, finite-resource expenditure, self-harm, known serious hazard, secret disclosure, permanent ownership transfer or knowing binding-oath violation.

If the behavior needs only movement or a free/ordinary interaction, it does not automatically consume the hard-control allowance. If it commandeers the target's whole ordinary Action, that hard component must successfully claim the shared allowance when established; if blocked, no invented alternative Action occurs, though independently satisfiable non-hard portions of the same instruction may remain.

Drop releases an item without transferring ownership. Kneeling/Prone does not itself prohibit standing later unless the instruction's still-valid one-turn wording requires remaining kneeling through that turn. Walk-away movement uses ordinary movement and may provoke normal opportunity attacks.

One pending Illusive Commands record per target across casters. A later successfully established command replaces the prior one; saved/invalid/guard-blocked whole command does not erase an existing valid record.

Outside initiative, resolve one equivalent ordinary behavior opportunity within 6 seconds; no indefinite command.

The spell creates no illusion image merely because of its name.

**NEW:** WIS save, short-instruction interface, hearing/language, next-turn timing and action-versus-movement hard-control split.

### 5. Calm Emotions

**Action; choose a point perceived within 20 ft and LoE; V/S/F; NEW 20-ft-radius sphere; concentration up to 1 minute.**

The sphere is fixed. Total cover from its center excludes a creature from its effect.

When created, and when a creature first enters it during this application, determine eligibility:

- a willing creature may accept automatically;
- an unwilling creature makes a **NEW WIS save**.

Each creature makes at most one save/acceptance decision per application. A successful save makes it unaffected by this cast even if it leaves/re-enters.

While an affected creature remains in the sphere:

- ordinary strong emotional intensity—panic, rage, grief, euphoria, uncontrolled anger and comparable emotional escalation—is magically subdued;
- the **mechanical effects of the actual Frightened condition are suppressed**.

The Frightened record, duration, clocks and repeat saves continue. Leaving the sphere or ending Calm Emotions restores any still-existing Frightened effects without replaying one-time application events.

This spell does **not** generically suppress:

- Charmed;
- Incapacitated/Stunned;
- enchantments generally;
- Corrupted;
- Dissonance/Refrain/Strain;
- class Rage/Fury/Berserk/etc. mechanics unless that exact source expressly says an emotion-suppression effect disables it.

Ordinary beliefs, memories, allegiances, grievances and agency remain. Calmness does not impose indifference, prohibit attacks, end hostility, heal resources or repair an oath.

Entering/leaving changes only the currently suppressed emotional effects; the zone does not erase their underlying records.

**NEW:** fixed 20-ft-radius zone, WIS/willingness interface, concentration minute, entry procedure and explicit Frightened suppression.

No hearing or language is required after successful application; this is magical action control.

- direct self-harm/suicidal route;

- permanent surrender/ownership transfer;

### Numerical / human-balance flags

Separate from source fidelity:

- **Deep Mesmerize:** two-round Stunned for 3 RP is extremely strong even with a save, wake-on-damage and hard guard.
- **Dire Charm:** repeated servant Actions until an end-turn save succeeds can be encounter-defining; limiting finite-resource expenditure helps but needs human testing.
- **Group Pacify:** one-minute multi-target hostile-action suppression is materially stronger than rank-1 Pacify and Voice of Peace.
- **Illusive Commands:** flexible sentence-level compulsion can become significantly stronger than one-word Command depending on adjudication.
- **Calm Emotions:** a 20-ft-radius Frightened-suppression zone may trivialize some fear encounters; that is a balance issue, not a source contradiction.
- **Crown of Madness:** movement plus one Action every affected turn is strong, though repeat saves/concentration/hard guard constrain it.
- **Zone of Truth:** the rank-4 Divination placement is substantially more expensive than the mechanically unified rank-2 Enchantment mode. Preserve that redundancy/conflict for later balance/source cleanup rather than inventing a hidden rank-4 benefit.
