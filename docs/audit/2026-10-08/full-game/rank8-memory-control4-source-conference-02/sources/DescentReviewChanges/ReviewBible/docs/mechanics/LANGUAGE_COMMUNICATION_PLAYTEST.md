# Languages, literacy and communication

**Reviewed authored language/communication playtest v1.1.** This chapter implements the LA-01–16 source decisions for ordinary play. Rules marked **NEW** complete gaps in the prior sources. They are provisional game rules; no unfinished spell, feat or class option becomes available through this chapter. Use each acquired feature's actual card for its costs, targets, duration and access.

## Names, dialects and modes

Record spoken comprehension, speaking, reading, writing and script familiarity separately. Knowledge and current ability to use it are different: deafness may stop you hearing a language you know; it does not remove that knowledge.

**NEW permanent-grant convention:** a permanent ordinary grant saying “know,” “one language,” or a background language row grants spoken comprehension, speaking, reading and writing in the language's attested ordinary written form, unless expressly narrowed. Temporary understanding, basic ideas, fragmentary writing and magical communication give only their specific modes or meaning.

Orc and Orcish are one identity; Mycelic and Sporetongue are one. **NEW chosen interpretation of the book's option:** Imperial's Westreach, Eastern Sigil and Northern Vale use Common mechanically, while retaining their cultural/register labels. They do not consume extra choices or decode ciphers. “Mycelian” is an ancestry/legacy label, not an alias for Mycelic.

Trollish and Giant remain distinct. Deep Cant, Deep Speech, Murkish, Forgotten Script, Runic and Resonant Cant remain distinct. Shared letters, an ancient description or a similar name does not establish mutual comprehension or permission to select an otherwise restricted language.

**NEW Primordial policy:** general Primordial and Auran, Aquan, Ignan and Terran are independently known, related competencies. Knowing one does not automatically grant any of the others. Elemental-Touched chooses general Primordial **or** the dialect matching its already selected affinity: air/Auran, water/Aquan, fire/Ignan, earth/Terran. This fills one grant. Its Aquan is the same language an Amphibian knows. Other sources granting Primordial give general Primordial unless they expressly offer a dialect choice.

Gnomish and Orcish use Dwarvish script. Knowing its letters does not teach either language's grammar, meaning or cultural context. Where no named script is given, record a language-local orthography without inventing a lore name. **NEW written-competency completion:** Runic grants its attested written content; Forgotten Script must identify the actual admitted written tradition. Unspecified spoken forms remain unspecified. Recognizing or copying characters alone does not understand them.

No grant creates anatomy, a pheromone emitter, hearing, sight, hands, writing implements or a magical channel. Mycelic's description does not supply an invented biological ability. An ordinary flat ink page is not tactile-readable merely because its reader knows the language; an actual touch-reading spell exception has its own scope.

The published names The Voice and Resonant Cant retain their existing restrictions; this chapter grants neither ordinary enrollment nor an automatic class entitlement. Their meanings and deeper relationships remain deferred. No hidden-language list is created here.

## Learn a language

**NEW ordinary course:** choose one language/dialect and the modes sought. Identify the learner, the relevant script, legitimate access, and an actual qualified instructor or a complete usable curriculum. An instructor needs the modes taught and a mutually usable channel. Self-study needs authentic explanations, examples, exercises and a way to check their meaning. An unknown inscription, an isolated translation, money or an Insight balance is not a curriculum. Spoken practice needs actual production and correction; comprehension-only magic does not supply it.

| Module | Active work |
|---|---:|
| Spoken comprehension and production | 240 hours |
| Literacy after knowing the language's semantics | 80 hours |
| Script/orthography portion included in that literacy | 40 hours |
| Script familiarity alone, without semantics | 40 hours |

A complete new spoken-and-written language requires **320 active hours**, or **280** with genuine prior knowledge of that same script. Literacy for a semantically known language takes 80 hours, or 40 with that script already known. A written-only language uses 240 hours of written semantics plus the 80-hour literacy module and grants only its attested written modes. Related dialects give no automatic discount. An explicit grant bypasses study only for the modes it actually grants; valid starting knowledge incurs no retroactive fee.

Credit complete **15-active-minute phases**. Interruption loses the unfinished phase's prospective credit; previous credit survives, while real time, materials and fees remain spent. A shorter final phase is allowed only to finish the exact module after a real source-specific adjustment. Missing instruction, access or a usable channel yields no progress. Log real losses separately. Administrative phases do not break actual concentration or evade Oracle Tongues' actual save and ten-minute loss.

**NEW tuition reference:** 1 gp per eight actual instruction hours, including ordinary lesson consumables the provider actually supplies. A real quote or voluntary teacher may differ. Self-study has no invented tutor fee but needs actual materials. Pay for actual instruction time, including time an interruption made unproductive. Round each actual final purchase once upward to copper; do not round every phase separately. Prepaid time and refunds follow the actual agreement. A price does not conjure a teacher, rare text, permission or equipment.

Use actual time within eight-hour Watches and the two-active-work-Watch ceiling, with governing rest, Strain and access limits. The tutor is occupied too. A minute cannot also fund guarding, travel, beneficial rest, crafting, research, another course, gaining Insight or spending Insight without an explicit rule allowing that concurrency. No calendar-day shortcut, INT/PB discount or pending feat applies.

Completion is deterministic after genuine required work and authentic final practice, included in the last required phase. There is no random final fluency roll, added examination period or completion fee. Record the acquired modes and their source. Gain no XP, PB, Expertise, formula, spell, rank, faction permission or another language. Partial study can establish particular recorded words; it does not satisfy a requirement to know the language.

**Example:** an Orcish-literate learner studies Gnomish with a qualified teacher and usable lessons. Shared Dwarvish script reduces the course to 280 hours, reference tuition 35 gp. A 40-minute lesson interrupted during its third phase gives 30 credited minutes but consumes 40 actual minutes; one invoice at the reference rate costs 9 cp. An applicable Tongues failure later removing ten credited minutes remains a real loss.

## Understand, translate or decipher

Plain accessible speech or text in a known language needs no comprehension roll. That does not establish its truth, solve a code, authenticate it, reveal technical context or recover missing facts. Remembering a specific message understood through temporary magic preserves that information after the effect ends; general competence does not persist.

| Task | Procedure | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Copy visible characters | Actual access, tools, stock and time | A facsimile; no certified meaning or magical encoding |
| **NEW** ordinary prose translation | Source reading and destination writing; ten active minutes per 250 words, proportionately rounded up to a minute, minimum one | Plain noncoded prose in the destination language; no roll merely for translation |
| **NEW** uncertain recognition | One minute; normally INT/History, DC 10 familiar attested style, 15 supported related form, 20 damaged but diagnostic evidence | Supported identity or relationship; no translation |
| **NEW** Translate/Decipher project | One preregistered supported passage of at most 250 words; eight active hours; declared evidence, method, output and DC | Only the supported passage or cipher layer |

Routine obvious recognition needs no roll. A nondiagnostic specimen cannot reveal its identity through a high roll. Translation speed does not replace formula-copying, magical encoding or contract-authentication procedures.

For the eight-hour project, register the actual source meaning or finite supported meaning, relevant parallel/grammatical/key evidence and missing sections before work. Linguistic reconstruction uses INT/History: DC 15 supported related dialect, 20 unfamiliar but adequately evidenced language, 25 damaged yet recoverable correspondence. A separate mundane cipher uses INT/Investigation: DC 15 adequate known key, 20 constrained ordinary analysis, 25 difficult but genuinely solvable damaged/incomplete key. A plainly usable key may remove uncertainty; it does not provide a missing underlying language.

Declare one lead and one applicable PB contribution. **NEW project-specific assistance:** one qualified assistant contributes +1 after all eight hours of actually useful participation. This does not add assistants to other procedures. Apply the existing Core task degrees and natural shifts.

Success supplies only the registered supported result. Critical Success grants no second passage, fluency, extra meaning, code bypass or banked labor. Failure leaves notes and spent work, without a certified translation. Critical Failure has the same informational result plus only a genuinely predeclared, independently lawful hazard. No failure fabricates authoritative lore. An inaccessible or unsupported meaning makes the project unqualified before work; a destroyed gap stays a gap.

A fresh attempt requires genuinely new evidence or a concretely different supported method, and fresh required work. Changing readers, dates, copies or formatting alone does not create evidence or reset an unchanged failed attempt. Information genuinely discovered can support later work; past hours and rewards are never credited twice.

Formula research and copying retain their trade gates, DC, minimum research time and one-hour/1-sp copying rule. Scribing Insight uses its separate procedure below. A subject-limited background information feature is not general translation. Pending research/language feats give no current discount or bonus.

## Communicate in a scene

**NEW finite bound:** each creature's aggregate free ordinary speech/signing must fit at most **six seconds per numbered round**. Warnings, replies and extra turns share that allowance. Outside initiative use actual conversational time. Brief communication costs no additional Action, but cannot activate a feature, move you, grant a Reaction or interrupt an already resolved event. A spell's words follow that spell's casting procedure, without a new universal word cap.

Longer deliberate exchanges take an Action for each additional six-second segment on a turn with that Action available. Off-turn lengthy delivery requires a real applicable permission, such as a legitimately readied communication Action, or waits. Surprise keeps its printed brief-communication permission without granting an unavailable Action. Respect voice, hands, consciousness and all other actual restrictions.

An interpreter must first perceive and understand the incoming message, then produce a form the recipient can perceive and understand. A short source phrase and rendition may fit an interval; a long statement or multiple relays cannot arrive instantaneously. Record when meaning reaches each recipient. Late interpretation cannot retroactively qualify a resolved check or effect. A translator lends no fluency, bonus or mental link.

**NEW party signals:** ten active minutes of mutual demonstration/rehearsal establishes up to six recorded simple cues for the actual participants, such as halt, approach, retreat, danger, left and right. A one-hand cue requires that usable hand and a receiver who sees it; a two-hand cue requires both. Light, distance, cover and attention must support actual perception. Six cues do not teach a fluent sign language, arbitrary sentences or codebreaking. A formal sign language needs an actual source/course.

Player accessibility is free of fictional action costs: explaining your character's intent at the table is not the character speaking, signing, providing V or violating an oath. You need not perform a voice or gesture to declare its fictional use.

**Example:** in a six-second interval a three-second Aquan warning followed by a three-second Common rendition can reach the second listener at the end. A response cannot precede that receipt. A thirty-second explanation through two interpreters cannot qualify an attack that has already resolved. A Deafened ally who sees a rehearsed retreat cue receives that cue, without satisfying a feature requiring the ally to hear a performance.

## Hearing, silence and magic

| Restriction or permission | Actual consequence |
|---|---|
| Deafened | Cannot hear; language knowledge, reading, signs and otherwise eligible mental channels remain. It does not by itself prevent own speech or V. |
| Silenced | Prevents V, command words, Chants and audible coordination. **NEW explicit completion:** includes deliberate ordinary spoken messages. It does not itself forbid thought, reading, writing, signs or a separately valid mental effect. |
| Silence the Caster combat action | Prevents verbal-component Reactions through its printed window, with its actual concentration check. It does not suppress ordinary speech or legal Action-time V casting. |
| V component | Requires that caster's actual audible speech. An interpreter, sign, telepathic phrase, playback, mimicry or another speaker cannot supply it. |

S, F, material, hand and access requirements remain separate. An explicit class exception waives only its stated requirements. Language knowledge grants no casting access, RP, repertoire or formula. Track overlapping prohibitions separately with their own sources and expirations: an operation stays blocked while any applicable prohibition remains. Removing one effect does not remove another; identical restrictions do not add penalties or automatically extend durations.

Actor-specific verbal restrictions and a physical sound-suppression field differ. Neither automatically grants thunder immunity. Silence does not automatically end otherwise legal nonauditory concentration; a particular sustained audible performance may have stricter rules.

These language completions retain every card's existing status.

Druidic retains its actual hidden-message procedure: knowers notice/read the message; nonknowers can notice its presence at DC 15 without decoding it. **NEW**, permanent Druidic knowledge includes ordinary language modes. A plain sentence, hidden message and separate cipher remain different objects; translation magic still obeys its own exclusions. Form speech limits and actual Beast Spells/Archdruid exceptions are separate.

Oracle Tongues I grants receptive spoken-mortal comprehension, without speaking every language or universal literacy. Its interpretation bonus needs up to three actually hearing allies within 25 ft and a genuinely related check. **NEW “Lore” completion:** use one subject-appropriate existing knowledge skill, ordinarily INT/History for language/history; Arcana, Religion, Nature or Engineering applies only to its actual subject. No new proficiency, additional PB or Language Insight currency follows.

**NEW bounded meaning:** Tongues III's one question/answer, IV's meaningful exchange, V's one command and Heaven's Decree's selected instruction convey only the meaning needed by that specific lawful effect. Unknown answers remain unknown; NPC-save and player-refusal rules remain distinct. Dominion truth-restricting auras gain no translation bridge or compelled confession.

**NEW Echo Voice completion:** willing allies within its actual five-tile range can exchange directed intentional conceptual messages without a shared spoken language. Either participant may withhold or withdraw consent. This provides no thoughts, senses, broadcast, identity authentication, unknown script decoding, V or device protocol. Passive messages have no one-RC fee; the separate reroll retains its Reaction/one-RC cost and limits. No extra range, plane or line-of-effect exception is invented. Telepathy cannot start a speech-required boon while Silenced.

**NEW Knowledge Transfer completion:** the temporary language loan includes the donor's actual linguistic speaking/comprehension competence and only their actual read/write/script modes for that language. Keep the Action, 2 RP, touch, willing ally, one-hour duration and one/short-rest limit. Current physical suppression does not erase learned knowledge. On expiration remove the borrowed modes; preserve only particular information genuinely learned. It transfers no memories, secrets, permanent knowledge, curriculum or course-hour credit.

**NEW Hush Descends acoustic completion:** ordinary sound does not transmit within or through this named aura. This affects hearing across an affected sound path for both allegiances and prevents a V component that cannot be audible, absent an actual exception. Keep its express hostile prohibitions and every original rider/cost/duration. It does not apply the whole Silenced condition to everyone or grant thunder immunity/damage cancellation. This is a named completion, not a rule inferred from every feature titled “silence.”

Paladin Silent Smite checks speech since the **end** of the previous regular turn. Vow Unbroken checks since its **beginning**. Keep their separate windows and other riders. Intelligible speech, V and deliberate vocal commands count as stated; involuntary sounds do not. **NEW channel clarification:** writing, rehearsed signs, mental messages and player explanations are not vocal speech. Deliberately performing a speaking/V/command act still counts for a voluntary-silence history if an external field prevents reception, although it fails a V requirement when not audible.

Aura of Stillness expressly permits speech/V; Quiet Step quiets movement alone. Vow Absolute and Null Command Aura retain their selected V-only prohibitions. Hush, Silent Judgment and the relevant Death Touch/Null Command failures expressly restrict speech too. Wordless Ward keeps only its own no-V exception. Neither Crimson Agent Total Silence nor Monk Deathless Silence inherits the full Silenced condition from its name. Damage-only concentration defenses do not become defenses against every channel restriction; Balanced Elements does not extend silence.

Obey Aura requires the target actually know/comprehend the prohibition before a violation can be knowing. Saying it in an unknown language proves no such knowledge. Truth restrictions do not make a sincere mistaken belief a known lie, translate the claim, compel speaking or reveal objective truth. Preserve the exact oath conduct rules without inventing a ban on interpretation or accessible player communication.

## Scribing, runes and devices

The reviewed [advanced operating guide](ADVANCED_CRAFT_CANDIDATE_GUIDE.md), [Scribing cards](../../data/crafting/advanced_craft_recipe_candidate_public.json) and [Tinkering cards](../../data/crafting/advanced_craft_recipe_candidate_public.json) retain their authority. The two card links open the supplied complete public advanced-crafting collection; use the actual recipe ID within it. Reading words does not establish a functional magical payload, caster eligibility or device control.

A properly encoded script supplies temporary repertoire for its one activation only to an otherwise qualified real caster who can read/understand its actual functional instructions. Class/rank/school, components, hand/access, RP and casting time still apply. Comprehension magic can meet only the eligible ordinary-language part of this test; its exclusions remain. Do not add the reference-consultation minute as an extra script casting cost.

A reference's bonus needs actual relevant content, availability and genuine necessary lead consultation of at least one minute, or the whole genuinely shorter task, under its self-production exclusion. Copying/translation makes an actual information copy, without transferring the original physical item's magic, charges, bonus or fresh study award. A printed zero-language effect remains zero.

The public **recipe-500** procedure awards one Language Insight after one active hour with legible, authenticated, genuinely useful parallel text into a language the researcher understands. Insight belongs to one researcher and target language, with a maximum of six unspent. Points cannot transfer to another researcher. The same underlying evidence awards once per reader across copies, holders, editions and magical recreations. Reaching the cap creates no deferred credit. Insight here is a specific ledger, separate from the Insight skill, XP or fluency.

Spend **three points for that language plus eight active hours** on one preregistered, supported, accessible and legible passage of at most 250 words, translating into a language the researcher knows. The Director must possess or establish its real source meaning. Missing meaning/evidence makes it unqualified before spending. This procedure adds no decipher roll. It grants the supported literal passage, without fluency, a separate cipher solution, hidden meaning, formula or spell. No hour also funds a course, a new Insight award, ordinary deciphering or formula work. A completed translation can later become genuine evidence; its old hours are not credited again.

Runic knowledge does not confer Runecarver's-tools proficiency, glyph-function knowledge or arm/disarm permission. Use actual tools, hands, inspection/disarm procedures, DCs and triggers. A failed translation does not automatically activate a rune; reading does not recharge it. Ward attunement and charge/Reaction timing remain separate.

**TI-04 controlled automata, not all crafted devices.** A TI-04 automaton requires an actual capable consenting controller with INT 6+ and ten active minutes using actual Tinker's tools at its legitimate accessible interface to register that controller and one compatible simple task. No proficiency is granted or required merely to use a known functioning interface. A previously bound chassis requires actual release by its controller or a separately completed lawful control procedure; ownership does not hack unknown, locked or hostile equipment. A controller may have three active ordinary crafted automata; separately defined special profiles and class devices keep only their own actual limits and exceptions. Traps, manually operated utilities and operator-driven machinery do not acquire this binding or attached-phase protocol merely because they are crafted devices. Use their own arming, direct-operation, activation and servicing procedures.

**In initiative**, an eligible automaton command requires a Bonus Action on the controller's regular turn, the configured visible/audible channel within 60 feet, and the actual shared one-command/one-offensive-companion-or-device allowance per owner per numbered round. Execution follows that controller's whole turn in the attached phase. Failed execution spends committed allowances; transfer, rebinding and extra turns do not refund them. Other item families retain every shared budget that their own controlling procedures expressly apply; this scope correction waives none.

**Outside initiative**, programming or reprogramming one such automaton's actual finite, physically possible noncombat task takes ten active minutes. Do not charge a second programming period for the unchanged task validly registered during initial binding. Starting one already programmed, wholly noncombat task uses an Action, not the combat Bonus Action command. Actual appendages, access, capacity and paid power/service intervals remain necessary. No professional work, autonomous attack, indefinite production or service reset follows from a program or language lesson. Fluency, an interpreter or a telepathic ally supplies neither control authority nor undocumented hardware. Service, repair and controller transfer remain separate and preserve their real histories, costs, HP, charges and custody.

**TD-08 is a separate class-device permission, not limited by this automaton-command scope.** It retains once per short rest, a Bonus Action, and up to two current Dissonance into one own active qualifying device within 30 feet. The completed source's actually armed charged trap, powered automaton, utility in a live eligible task, or aligned operator-driven device can qualify on its own terms. Merely carrying an idle item does not. Keep the original routing disable, clearance, service and resource histories; no class feature is granted here.

**Example:** with two prior Insight, study one new qualifying correspondence for an hour to reach three. Spend three and eight more hours on a supported 200-word passage: nine actual hours, zero new fluency-course hours, and zero Insight remaining. Reading a second physical copy of that same correspondence adds no point.

## Keep a usable record

Record actual grant sources and choices; known modes; temporary effect start/end and current restrictions; course work, losses and payments; physical texts and their shared evidence identities; Insight gains/spends; and communication delivery/receipt order. Keep source spellings beside resolved identities. An “understood” checkbox alone is not proof of unknown or protected meaning.

An old save with only Common/Mycelian needs its actual regional choice reconciled while preserving its old record; never silently select Mycelic. Abbreviated Human/Undead arrays also need their real choices restored from evidence. This candidate does not migrate saves or change the companion runtime. Valid earlier choices remain valid without a retroactive course.
