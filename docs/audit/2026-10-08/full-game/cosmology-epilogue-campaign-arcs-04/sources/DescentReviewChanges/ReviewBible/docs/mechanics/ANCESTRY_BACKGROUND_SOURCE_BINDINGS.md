## Amphibian

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 293). Your Constitution score increases by 2.

**Size** (source line 299). Amphibians stand between 5 and 6 feet tall and average about 150 pounds. Your size is Medium.

**Speed** (source line 301). Your base walking speed is 30 feet. You also have a swimming speed of 30 feet.

**Amphibious** (source line 303). You can breathe both air and water.

**Moisture Dependency** (source line 305). You must immerse yourself in water for at least 1 hour every day or suffer one level of exhaustion. This exhaustion cannot be removed until you spend 1 hour immersed in water.

**Natural Camouflage** (source line 307). You have proficiency in the Stealth skill.

**Water Sense** (source line 309). You can sense the presence of water within 60 feet of you and can determine whether it is fresh or salt water.

**Languages** (source line 311). You can speak, read, and write Common and Aquan.


### River Amphibian

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 321). Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Water Manipulation** (source line 323). You know the shape water cantrip. When you reach 3rd level, you can cast the create or destroy water spell once per day. When you reach 5th level, you can also cast the water breathing spell once per day. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

**Underwater Combat Training** (source line 325). You have proficiency with spears, tridents, nets, and crossbows. These weapons don't suffer disadvantage on attack rolls made underwater.

**River Diplomat** (source line 327). You have advantage on Charisma checks when interacting with creatures that have a swimming speed.


### Poison Amphibian

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 333). Your Intelligence score increases by 1.

**Poison Resistance** (source line 335). You have resistance to poison damage and advantage on saving throws against being poisoned.

**Toxic Touch** (source line 337). Once per short rest, when you hit a creature with an unarmed strike, you can deal an additional 1d4 poison damage. At 6th level, this damage increases to 2d4, and at 12th level, it increases to 3d4.

**Toxin Knowledge** (source line 339). You have proficiency with poisoner's kits and have advantage on Intelligence (Nature) checks related to identifying poisons and venoms.


### Jungle Amphibian

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 345). Your Dexterity score increases by 1.

**Climber** (source line 347). You have a climbing speed of 30 feet and can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without making an ability check.

**Camouflage Expert** (source line 349). You can attempt to hide even when only lightly obscured by foliage, heavy rain, falling snow, mist, or other natural phenomena.

**Jungle Survival** (source line 351). You have advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks made in forest or jungle environments, and you can always find enough food and water for yourself and up to five other creatures when in such environments.


## Umbrin

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 383). Your Dexterity score increases by 2.

**Size** (source line 389). Umbrin are similar in size to humans, ranging from 5 to 5½ feet tall. Your size is Medium.

**Speed** (source line 391). Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

**Superior Darkvision** (source line 393). Accustomed to the depths of the Murk, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 120 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.

**Sunlight Sensitivity** (source line 395). You have disadvantage on attack rolls and on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight when you, the target of your attack, or whatever you are trying to perceive is in direct sunlight.

**Umbrin Magic** (source line 397). You know the dancing lights cantrip. When you reach 3rd level, you can cast the faerie fire spell once per day. When you reach 5th level, you can also cast the darkness spell once per day. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

**Keen Senses** (source line 399). You have proficiency in the Perception skill.

**Trance** (source line 401). Umbrin don't need to sleep. Instead, they meditate deeply, remaining semiconscious, for 4 hours a day. While meditating, they can dream after a fashion; such dreams are actually mental exercises that have become reflexive through years of practice. After resting in this way, they gain the same benefit that a human does from 8 hours of sleep.

**Languages** (source line 403). You can speak, read, and write Common, Elvish, and Murkish.


### Obsidian Highborn

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 413). Your Charisma score increases by 1.

**Enhanced Umbrin Magic** (source line 415). When you cast the faerie fire and darkness spells with your Umbrin Magic trait, you can cast each of them twice per day instead of once.

**Command Presence** (source line 417). You have proficiency in the Intimidation skill. If you already have this proficiency, you gain proficiency in one of the following skills of your choice: Deception, Insight, or Persuasion.

**Noble Training** (source line 419). You have proficiency with rapiers, shortswords, and hand crossbows.


### Shadow Blade

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 425). Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Shadow Step** (source line 427). Once per short rest, as a bonus action, you can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see that is in dim light or darkness. You have advantage on the first melee attack you make before the end of the turn.

**Poison Expert** (source line 429). You have proficiency with the poisoner's kit and have advantage on saving throws against poison.

**Assassin's Training** (source line 431). You have proficiency with the disguise kit and the thieves' tools.


### Cavern Mystic

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 437). Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Earth Communion** (source line 439). You can cast the detect magic spell, but only to detect magic affecting earth or stone. You can also cast the speak with plants spell, but only to speak with fungi. Once you cast either spell with this trait, you can't cast that spell with it again until you finish a long rest. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

**Stone Sense** (source line 441). You can sense the presence of unusual stonework within 60 feet, such as traps, hidden doors, and recent excavations. You can also determine your approximate depth underground.

**Murkish Lore** (source line 443). You have advantage on Intelligence (History) checks related to the Murk and on Intelligence (Nature) checks related to fungi, oozes, and subterranean creatures.


## Drakari

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 479). Your Strength score increases by 2, and your Charisma score increases by 1.

**Size** (source line 485). Drakari are taller and heavier than humans, standing well over 6 feet tall and averaging almost 250 pounds. Your size is Medium.

**Speed** (source line 487). Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

**Draconic Ancestry** (source line 489). You have draconic ancestry. Your breath weapon and damage resistance are determined by your Drakari variant.

**Breath Weapon** (source line 491). You can use your action to exhale destructive energy. Your draconic ancestry determines the size, shape, and damage type of the exhalation. When you use your breath weapon, each creature in the area of the exhalation must make a saving throw, the type of which is determined by your draconic ancestry. The DC for this saving throw equals 8 + your Constitution modifier + your proficiency bonus. A creature takes 2d6 damage on a failed save, and half as much damage on a successful one. The damage increases to 3d6 at 6th level, 4d6 at 11th level, and 5d6 at 16th level. After you use your breath weapon, you can't use it again until you complete a short or long rest.

**Damage Resistance** (source line 493). You have resistance to the damage type associated with your draconic ancestry.

**Languages** (source line 495). You can speak, read, and write Common and Draconic.


### Flame Drakari

**Draconic Ancestry** (source line 505). Your breath weapon is a 15-foot cone of fire. Creatures in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw. You have resistance to fire damage.

**Fire Affinity** (source line 507). You know the produce flame cantrip. Once you reach 3rd level, you can cast the burning hands spell once per day. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

**Heat Endurance** (source line 509). You are naturally adapted to hot climates, as described by the environmental rules in Chapter 5.


### Frost Drakari

**Draconic Ancestry** (source line 515). Your breath weapon is a 15-foot cone of cold. Creatures in the area must make a Constitution saving throw. You have resistance to cold damage.

**Ice Affinity** (source line 517). You know the ray of frost cantrip. Once you reach 3rd level, you can cast the ice knife spell once per day. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

**Cold Endurance** (source line 519). You are naturally adapted to cold climates, as described by the environmental rules in Chapter 5.


### Storm Drakari

**Draconic Ancestry** (source line 525). Your breath weapon is a 5-foot-wide, 30-foot line of lightning. Creatures in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw. You have resistance to lightning damage.

**Lightning Affinity** (source line 527). You know the shocking grasp cantrip. Once you reach 3rd level, you can cast the thunderwave spell once per day. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

**Speed Burst** (source line 529). As a bonus action, you can propel yourself with a surge of electrical energy. Your walking speed increases by 10 feet until the end of your turn. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.


### Stone Drakari

**Draconic Ancestry** (source line 535). Your breath weapon is a 5-foot-wide, 30-foot line of acid. Creatures in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw. You have resistance to acid damage.

**Earth Affinity** (source line 537). You know the mold earth cantrip. Once you reach 3rd level, you can cast the earth tremor spell once per day. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

**Stone Resilience** (source line 539). Your AC increases by 1 when you're not wearing armor.


### Gale Drakari

**Draconic Ancestry** (source line 545). Your breath weapon is a 15-foot cone of poisonous gas. Creatures in the area must make a Constitution saving throw. You have resistance to poison damage.

**Air Affinity** (source line 547). You know the gust cantrip. Once you reach 3rd level, you can cast the feather fall spell once per day. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

**Wind Step** (source line 549). When you take the Dash action, difficult terrain doesn't cost you extra movement for that turn.


### Wave Drakari

**Draconic Ancestry** (source line 555). Your breath weapon is a 5-foot-wide, 30-foot line of lightning. Creatures in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw. You have resistance to lightning damage.

**Water Affinity** (source line 557). You know the shape water cantrip. Once you reach 3rd level, you can cast the create or destroy water spell once per day. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

**Amphibious** (source line 559). You can breathe air and water.

**Swimmer** (source line 561). You have a swimming speed of 30 feet.


## Dwarf

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 601). Your Constitution score increases by 2.

**Size** (source line 607). Dwarves stand between 4 and 5 feet tall and average about 150 pounds. Your size is Medium.

**Speed** (source line 609). Your base walking speed is 25 feet. Your speed is not reduced by wearing heavy armor.

**Darkvision** (source line 611). Accustomed to life underground, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.

**Dwarven Resilience** (source line 613). You have advantage on saving throws against poison, and you have resistance against poison damage.

**Dwarven Combat Training** (source line 615). You have proficiency with the battleaxe, handaxe, light hammer, and warhammer.

**Tool Proficiency** (source line 617). You gain proficiency with the artisan's tools of your choice: smith's tools, brewer's supplies, or mason's tools.

**Stonecunning** (source line 619). Whenever you make an Intelligence (History) check related to the origin of stonework, you are considered proficient in the History skill and add double your proficiency bonus to the check, instead of your normal proficiency bonus.

**Languages** (source line 621). You can speak, read, and write Common and Dwarvish. Dwarvish is full of hard consonants and guttural sounds, and those characteristics spill over into whatever other language a dwarf might speak.


### Mountain Dwarf

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 631). Your Strength score increases by 2.

**Dwarven Armor Training** (source line 633). You have proficiency with light and medium armor.

**Mountain Born** (source line 635). You're acclimated to high altitude, including elevations above 20,000 feet. You're also naturally adapted to cold climates, as described by the environmental rules in Chapter 5.

**Mountaineer** (source line 637). You have advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks made to climb rocky terrain.


### Forge Dwarf

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 643). Your Intelligence score increases by 1.

**Artisan's Expertise** (source line 645). You gain proficiency with two additional types of artisan's tools of your choice.

**Fire Resistance** (source line 647). You have resistance to fire damage.

**Master Craftsman** (source line 649). When you use artisan's tools to create an item, the creation time is reduced by 25%, and the item is considered to be of exceptional quality.


### Deep Dwarf

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 655). Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Superior Darkvision** (source line 657). Your darkvision has a radius of 120 feet.

**Murk Knowledge** (source line 659). You have advantage on Intelligence (Nature) checks related to fungi, oozes, and subterranean creatures.

**Deep Resilience** (source line 661). You have advantage on saving throws against illusions and against being charmed or paralyzed.


## Elf

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 699). Your Dexterity score increases by 2.

**Size** (source line 705). Elves range from under 5 to over 6 feet tall and have slender builds. Your size is Medium.

**Speed** (source line 707). Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

**Darkvision** (source line 709). Accustomed to twilit forests and the night sky, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.

**Keen Senses** (source line 711). You have proficiency in Perception skill.

**Fey Ancestry** (source line 713). You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put you to sleep.

**Trance** (source line 715). Elves don't need to sleep. Instead, they meditate deeply, remaining semiconscious, for 4 hours a day. While meditating, you can dream after a fashion; such dreams are actually mental exercises that have become reflexive through years of practice. After resting in this way, you gain the same benefit that a human does from 8 hours of sleep.

**Languages** (source line 717). You can speak, read, and write Common and Elvish. Elvish is fluid, with subtle intonations and intricate grammar. Elven literature is rich and varied, and their songs and poems are famous among other races. Many bards learn their language so they can add Elvish ballads to their repertoires.


### High Elf

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 727). Your Intelligence score increases by 1.

**Elf Weapon Training** (source line 729). You have proficiency with the longsword, shortsword, shortbow, and longbow.

**Cantrip** (source line 731). You know one cantrip of your choice from the wizard spell list. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for it.

**Extra Language** (source line 733). You can speak, read, and write one extra language of your choice.


### Wood Elf

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 739). Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Elf Weapon Training** (source line 741). You have proficiency with the longsword, shortsword, shortbow, and longbow.

**Fleet of Foot** (source line 743). Your base walking speed increases to 35 feet.

**Mask of the Wild** (source line 745). You can attempt to hide even when you are only lightly obscured by foliage, heavy rain, falling snow, mist, and other natural phenomena.


### Twilight Elf

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 751). Your Charisma score increases by 1.

**Twilight Magic** (source line 753). You know the minor illusion cantrip. When you reach 3rd level, you can cast the disguise self spell once per day. When you reach 5th level, you can also cast the misty step spell once per day. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

**Planar Affinity** (source line 755). You have advantage on Intelligence checks related to the planes of existence, particularly the Verdant Reflection tradition of mortal planar cosmography.

**Shadow Step** (source line 757). When you are in dim light or darkness, as a bonus action you can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see that is also in dim light or darkness. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.


## Feykin

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 797). Your Charisma score increases by 2, and your Dexterity score increases by 1.

**Size** (source line 803). Feykin are about the same size as humans, ranging from 5 to 6 feet tall. Your size is Medium.

**Speed** (source line 805). Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

**Darkvision** (source line 807). Thanks to your fey heritage, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.

**Fey Ancestry** (source line 809). You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put you to sleep.

**Fey Magic** (source line 811). You know the druidcraft cantrip. When you reach 3rd level, you can cast the charm person spell once with this trait and regain the ability to do so when you finish a long rest. When you reach 5th level, you can cast the misty step spell once with this trait and regain the ability to do so when you finish a long rest. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

**Languages** (source line 813). You can speak, read, and write Common and Sylvan.


### Summer Fey

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 823). Your Constitution score increases by 1.

**Touch of Summer** (source line 825). You know the thorn whip cantrip. When you reach 3rd level, you can cast the goodberry spell once per day. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

**Green Thumb** (source line 827). You can cause a nonmagical plant to grow up to twice its normal size for 24 hours. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

**Vitality of Summer** (source line 829). When you roll a 1 on a healing die, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll.


### Winter Fey

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 835). Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Touch of Winter** (source line 837). You know the ray of frost cantrip. When you reach 3rd level, you can cast the ice knife spell once per day. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

**Frost Touch** (source line 839). As an action, you can touch one object no larger than a 1-foot cube and coat it in a thin layer of ice. If the object is being worn or carried by a creature other than you, you must make a successful melee attack to touch it. The ice melts after 1 hour or when exposed to fire. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

**Winter's Endurance** (source line 841). You have resistance to cold damage.


### Twilight Fey

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 847). Your Intelligence score increases by 1.

**Touch of Twilight** (source line 849). You know the minor illusion cantrip. When you reach 3rd level, you can cast the sleep spell once per day. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

**Twilight Step** (source line 851). As a bonus action, you can magically teleport up to 15 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

**Veil Sight** (source line 853). You can see invisible creatures and objects within 10 feet of you that are within line of sight.


## Gnome

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 895). Your Intelligence score increases by 2.

**Size** (source line 901). Gnomes are between 3 and 4 feet tall and average about 40 pounds. Your size is Small.

**Speed** (source line 903). Your base walking speed is 25 feet.

**Darkvision** (source line 905). Accustomed to life underground, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.

**Gnome Cunning** (source line 907). You have advantage on all Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws against magic.

**Natural Curiosity** (source line 909). You have proficiency in one of the following skills of your choice: Arcana, History, Investigation, or Nature.

**Languages** (source line 911). You can speak, read, and write Common and Gnomish. The Gnomish language, which uses the Dwarvish script, is renowned for its technical treatises and its catalogs of knowledge about the natural world.


### Tinker Gnome

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 921). Your Dexterity score increases by 1.

**Artificer's Lore** (source line 923). Whenever you make an Intelligence (History) check related to magic items, alchemical objects, or technological devices, you can add twice your proficiency bonus, instead of any proficiency bonus you normally apply.

**Tinker** (source line 925). You have proficiency with artisan's tools (tinker's tools). Using those tools, you can spend 1 hour and 10 gp worth of materials to construct a Tiny clockwork device (AC 5, 1 hp). The device ceases to function after 24 hours (unless you spend 1 hour repairing it to keep the device functioning), or when you use your action to dismantle it; at that time, you can reclaim the materials used to create it. You can have up to three such devices active at a time.
When you create a device, choose one of the following options:

**Clockwork Toy** (source line 929). This toy is a clockwork animal, monster, or person, such as a frog, mouse, bird, dragon, or soldier. When placed on the ground, the toy moves 5 feet across the ground on each of your turns in a random direction. It makes noises as appropriate to the creature it represents.

**Fire Starter** (source line 931). The device produces a miniature flame, which you can use to light a candle, torch, or campfire. Using the device requires your action.

**Music Box** (source line 933). When opened, this music box plays a single song at a moderate volume. The box stops playing when it reaches the song's end or when it is closed.

**Mechanical Sensor** (source line 935). The device can detect the presence of creatures within 30 feet. When a creature enters or leaves this range, the device makes a chirping noise audible within 10 feet.


### Arcanist Gnome

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 941). Your Charisma score increases by 1.

**Natural Illusionist** (source line 943). You know the minor illusion cantrip. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for it.

**Speak with Small Beasts** (source line 945). Through sounds and gestures, you can communicate simple ideas with Small or smaller beasts. Arcanist gnomes love animals and often keep squirrels, badgers, rabbits, moles, woodpeckers, and other creatures as beloved pets.

**Arcane Tinkering** (source line 947). When you cast a spell that deals damage, you can change the damage type to one of the following: acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.


### Prankster Gnome

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 953). Your Charisma score increases by 1.

**Minor Illusion** (source line 955). You know the minor illusion cantrip. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for it.

**Distracting Prank** (source line 957). As a bonus action, you can create a momentary distraction that causes a creature you can see within 30 feet to have disadvantage on the next Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation) check it makes before the end of your next turn. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

**Slippery Mind** (source line 959). You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.


## Goblin

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 999). Your Dexterity score increases by 2, and your Constitution score increases by 1.

**Size** (source line 1005). Goblins are between 3 and 4 feet tall and average about 40 pounds. Your size is Small.

**Speed** (source line 1007). Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

**Darkvision** (source line 1009). You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.

**Nimble Escape** (source line 1011). You can take the Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action on each of your turns.

**Fury of the Small** (source line 1013). When you damage a creature with an attack or a spell and the creature's size is larger than yours, you can cause the attack or spell to deal extra damage to the creature. The extra damage equals your level. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

**Languages** (source line 1015). You can speak, read, and write Common and Goblin. The Goblin language is filled with hard consonants and guttural sounds, making it distinctive and somewhat difficult for other races to pronounce correctly.


### Boom Goblin

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 1025). Your Intelligence score increases by 1.

**Demolition Expert** (source line 1027). You have proficiency with alchemist's supplies. When you make an Intelligence check related to alchemical substances, explosives, or incendiary devices, you can add twice your proficiency bonus, instead of any proficiency bonus you normally apply.

**Blast Resistance** (source line 1029). You have resistance to fire damage and advantage on Dexterity saving throws against effects that create explosions.

**Improvised Explosive** (source line 1031). As an action, you can create and throw a small explosive device. Choose a point within 30 feet. Each creature within 5 feet of that point must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier) or take 1d4 fire damage. This damage increases to 2d4 at 6th level, 3d4 at 11th level, and 4d4 at 16th level. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.


### Scrap Goblin

**Ability Score Increase** (source line 1037). Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Junk Collector** (source line 1039). You have proficiency with tinker's tools. When you make a Wisdom (Perception) check to find useful items in junk, trash, or ruins, you can add twice your proficiency bonus, instead of any proficiency bonus you normally apply.

**Scavenger's Luck** (source line 1041). When you make a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check or a Wisdom (Perception) check to find hidden objects, you can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to the check.

**Improvised Tool** (source line 1043). As an action, you can create a simple tool or weapon from available materials. You create one of the following items, which lasts until you use this trait again:
A simple weapon
A set of thieves' tools
A climber's kit
