ACTUAL WIZARD ENGINE EXCERPTS (unchanged current source):

/engine/2: SPELLBOOK. At Wizard 1 your spellbook contains six chosen rank-1 Wizard spells. Arcane School Specialization also adds one legal rank-1 bonus spell of your chosen school without replacing one of those six. For this provisional progression, add 2 legal Wizard spells to the book at each Wizard level 2-4, 3 at each level 5-8, 4 at each level 9-12, 5 at each level 13-16 and 6 at each level 17-20. Level-up additions cost no money or scribing time.

/engine/3: LEGAL RANKS. Maximum Wizard spell rank by Wizard class level is: rank 1 at levels 1-2, rank 2 at 3-4, rank 3 at 5-6, rank 4 at 7-8, rank 5 at 9-10, rank 6 at 11-12, rank 7 at 13-14, rank 8 at 15-16 and rank 9 at 17-20. Grand Tier at level 20 is a special casting permission, not a tenth ordinary rank. A Wizard may learn Wizard-list spells from any school up to the legal rank unless a spell itself has an additional prerequisite.

/engine/4: PREPARED SPELL MATRIX. After a long rest, prepare a number of matrices equal to Wizard level + Intelligence modifier, minimum 1, selecting only spells currently recorded in your spellbook and within your legal rank. You cast ordinary Wizard spells only from prepared matrices unless a feature explicitly overrides that restriction. Preparing the full daily matrix set normally requires 1 hour of spellbook study. Matrix Efficiency reduces this to 45 minutes.

/engine/5: DUPLICATE PREPARATION. You may prepare the same spell more than once, primarily to carry different Matrix Mastery configurations. Duplicate copies never create additional first-cast Dissonance exemptions. For Refrain and the protected strain rule, all prepared copies sharing the same underlying spell name belong to one matrix family regardless of modification.

/engine/6: MIDDAY SUBSTITUTION. Once between long rests, you may perform a 10-minute Spellbook Study ritual to replace one prepared matrix with another legal spell from your spellbook. The replacement cannot exceed your legal Wizard rank. Sigil Rewrite and Matrix Mastery may improve this process as explicitly stated.

/engine/8: SPELLBOOK LOSS. Losing the spellbook does not erase matrices currently prepared in memory; you may continue casting those prepared matrices until you next rebuild or change preparation. A blank replacement book can be seeded with your currently prepared spells at 1 hour and 25 gp per effective spell rank each. An unprepared spell you previously recorded can be reconstructed from memory with 8 hours, 100 gp per rank and an Intelligence (Arcana) check against DC 10 + spell rank. Failure consumes that attempt's time and half its materials; another attempt can be made after a long rest. Recovering the original book automatically restores access to its surviving entries.

/engine/12: Core Systems V 2 Resonance Points and spell-rank RP costs are authoritative for Wizard spellcasting. There are no historical spell slots in this pilot. Wizard cards tagged wizard-spell are always-prepared class matrices once acquired, do not consume a daily prepared-matrix position and use the same RP, concentration, Refrain and protected first-distinct-matrix rules as other Wizard spells. Their printed RP cost replaces any generic rank cost; never pay both.

/engine/13: RITUAL CASTING is automatic at Wizard 2. A Wizard spell in your spellbook with the Ritual tag can be cast from the book without being prepared by increasing its casting time by at least 10 minutes. A ritual cast costs 0 RP unless that ritual explicitly lists a consumed RP cost, cannot be used during an encounter and cannot generate or vent Dissonance through ordinary casting strain.

/engine/31: Cantrip completion: know 3 Wizard cantrips at 1, 4 at 4, 5 at 10, using the shared missing-repertoire fallback. Cantrips are known directly and do not consume prepared matrices. Always-prepared class matrices are additional cards, not extra arbitrary ordinary spells. Class spell ranks and the normalized activation field resolve incomplete source terminology.
