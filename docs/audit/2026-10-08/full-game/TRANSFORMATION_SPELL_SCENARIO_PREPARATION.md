# Transformation spell scenario preparation

**Test-design notes only.** The boundary conference is in progress; none of the expected outcomes below overrides its actual decisions.

Cover these adversarial situations when implementing the settled rules:

1. Harmonic Bulwark with ordinary base payment, legal discounted base, zero base plus paid conversion, surcharge, insufficient total RP, countering and a larger/equal existing shield. A free copy cannot mint credited RP.
2. A caster replaces their own concentration Mass Polymorph and the new target saves. Compare another caster trying unsuccessfully to replace one Mass link.
3. A low-rank transformation tries to replace a higher-rank active form; a new True Polymorph replaces permanent True Polymorph; concentration then ends during shaping. No hidden intermediate anatomy returns.
4. A Druid attempts to shift while transformed by each spell; a spell replaces a Druid with dependent swarms. Preserve finite reserves and original independent summons.
5. Ten distinct Mass targets, mixed successful/waived/failed saves, shared concentration ending, one replaced link, and an initially valid-looking set of mutually incompatible enlarged footprints.
6. A form attack uses caster statistics but belongs to its subject. Check subject natural/melee-qualified riders, caster damage/kill/resource procs and unresolved weapon-only proc gates separately.
7. An active temporary form is kept at1 by legal death prevention versus actually falling to0, being Incapacitated or dying. Do not resolve the packet twice or return a separate beast-HP pool.
8. True Polymorph during shaping versus permanence: target at0/dead/incapacitated, caster concentration loss, willing object-form rejection and the shared60-second repeat-save/stabilization boundary.
9. A creature becomes an object and later a creature again; an object-origin creature is recast through a humanoid profile, healed, resurrected, killed, soul-captured, sold or commanded by another owner. Immutable origin and value constraints must survive every chain.
10. Object transformations with absent durability, missing mass/value, carried or worn equipment, magic/attunement, structural terrain, corpse/remains, forbidden output and repeated value rebasing.
11. One caster tries to create a second permanent object companion while the first is suppressed, out of range, controlled by someone else, destroyed or dispelled. No second slot via temporary suppression.
12. An object-origin creature commands before its owner pays a BA, shares an offense round with a turret/swarm, loses command eligibility after issuance or reaches structural0HP. No independent full turn or manufactured corpse.
13. A humanoid profile equips external armor; a later no-hands profile changes that equipment's availability; a dropped/destroyed item must not reappear on dispel. Avoid duplicate melded/new armor calculations.
14. Shapechange switches on its casting turn, an extra turn, two grants of an extra Action, the next original regular turn, into an identical profile or into illegal space. No second cast/Refrain reset or new entry resources.
15. Damage-repeat saves after temporary-HP-only absorption, actual normal-HP damage, repeated packets in one numbered round and after original regular end. Each target owns its independent event/round history.
16. A permanent transformation suppresses under antimagic and later resumes. Ensure only one physical identity, unchanged damage history and no repeated entry benefits or cleared companion slot.

Additional interoperability questions to inspect in the boundary reply: whether anatomy profiles change creature-type tags or merely retain the original type; retention of innate physiology features versus expressly replaced anatomy; and the existing geometry procedure when mandatory reversion occurs in a space too small for natural anatomy. Do not silently guess those in code. Broader item, bestiary and hazard procedures remain separate domains in the whole-game ledger.
