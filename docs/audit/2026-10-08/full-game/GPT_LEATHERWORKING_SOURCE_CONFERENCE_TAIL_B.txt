LW-D07. Adopt complete ordered base reserves explicitly for Leatherworking

NEW cross-trade adoption: every output transforms one actual complete mundane base body in addition to every printed component.

The bodies above are fully constructed, ordinary stitched/riveted equipment before enchanting. Acquire them through actual ownership, purchase, commission or a separately supported mundane construction procedure. A blank’s baseline price is not permission to create it with an unprovided stock recipe or free check.

Before work, declare one model, fitted size and legal grade for the batch. Reserve:

maximum bases = floor(1.5 × source quantity)

Record a stable ordered list of physical identities, body profiles, actual material, current/maximum HP, persistent flaw/durability state and baseline values. Reserved containers must be empty. Bases must be nonmagical, complete and non-Broken; no existing recipe imprint from either trade is admitted through this generic route.

A damaged functional base is legal. A missing, committed-elsewhere or Broken reserve pauses the order before the completion roll. Repair or replace it at its existing list position, with no post-roll substitutions or reordering.

Transform the first resolved N bodies. A durability choice transforms the first Standard Q. Failed/Mishap transforms none. Untransformed bodies return in their actual condition and are not also credited under 25% ingredient recovery.

Use the latest lawful completion state, including intervening damage or repair, not the old reservation snapshot.

Physical conservation and quality

Finished empty weight is the actual base-body weight. Printed ingredients remain mandatory process/reinforcement inputs; their listed mass does not add a second body or a second saleable inventory asset. Solvent and process waste grant no automatic salvage identity.

For recipe-280, reserve six actual 5-lb backpacks. Standard transforms four; Masterwork yield transforms six. The 1.44 lb of source ingredients is paid once and does not supply those 30 lb of bodies.

NEW explicit durability adoption: use the reviewed Blacksmithing noncompounding physical layers:

B = ordinary body HP after actual size and applicable grade

P = strongest legally attached durability percentage: 0%, 25% or 50%

ImprovedMax = B + (P > 0 ? max(1, floor(B × P)) : 0)

Apply one persistent singleton-Flawed halving last, minimum 1. Preserve the completion-state damage deficit:

NewCurrent = max(0, FinalMax − (OldMax − OldCurrent)), capped at FinalMax.

Repeated flaws do not halve again. A stronger improvement does not remove the flaw. A badly damaged singleton can emerge Broken with a dormant imprint.

Quality never increases capacity, R, check bonus, AC enhancement, resistance or equipment grade.

All 201 source component occurrences are consumed, and none is a catalyst or tool. Therefore every one of these 44 recipes has zero eligible Masterwork catalyst returns. Independently required tools remain tools; do not relabel a consumed material to produce a return.

LW-D08. Work, repair and valuation
Work and incorporation

EXISTING: DEX; Leatherworker’s-tools credential; Tannery Bench; actual Awl and Cutting knife. Toolkit contents may satisfy auxiliary implements only where the actual kit contains them.

All fieldCraftable:false flags control. In particular, preserve the contradictory timeMode:"field" labels on 283, 285, 292 and 293 without allowing the portable field route.

Apply the framework’s actual formula/trade-level gates, assistant caps, Watch limits and active-work scheduling. No numeric trade level is added to the check.

NEW Leatherworking phase binding: after lawful creation-time adjustments, the first stable incorporation phase is:

min(8 active hours, final required active work time)

Later phases are at most eight active hours. Record incorporation and storage before work. Only actually incorporated components stabilize at the first phase’s completion; untreated or unincorporated lots retain their clocks. A replacement ingredient does not recover spent material or erase completed phases.

Forge Dwarf applies to this artisan creation work: ×0.75 time, rounded upward to a whole minute, and the reviewed Standard-to-Exceptional output choice. It does not shorten repair, training, preservation clocks or rest.

For recipe-319, preserve activeWorkHours:null, the one-year source text and laborValue:0. Use the existing framework’s explicitly labeled 1,920-active-hour overlay. An eligible Forge Dwarf requires 1,440 active hours = 86,400 minutes, ordinarily 180 eight-hour phases. This is not a guaranteed calendar completion or automatic multiseason access.

Use each recipe’s exact trivialAtSkillLevel, including the high-level records capped at 20. Do not regenerate those fields from the framework’s overgeneralized “skill level +5” description.

Repair and reconstruction

NEW Leatherworking repair binding:

Requirement	Functional item, HP≥1	Broken imprinted item, HP0
Credential	Actual Leatherworker’s-tools proficiency	Same
Equipment	Leatherworker’s tools, Tannery Bench, Awl, Cutting knife	Same
Check	DEX + applicable proficiency, DC 10	DEX + applicable proficiency, original recipe crafting DC
Active work	max(1 hour, recorded maximum HP/10 hours)	max(8 hours, recorded maximum HP/10 hours)
Materials	25% of mundane size/grade base reference	25% of undamaged finished per-item reference
Outcome	Binary; success restores recorded maximum	Binary; success restores recorded maximum and dormant imprint, subject to suppression

Failure consumes the repair materials and work. Neither procedure grants formula knowledge, requires the original recipe’s trade-level eligibility, rolls crafting quality, returns catalysts, awards crafting XP or receives Forge Dwarf’s creation reduction.

A recoverable original main body is required. For a Boots/Gloves pair, both original members must remain recoverable for this generic reconstruction; replacing a missing member needs a separate procedure. No duplicate pair is reconstructed from fragments assigned to another item.

Repair preserves grade, model, fit, identity, physical durability and flaw. It restores no lost contents. Core service pricing remains its separately available quoted route, not an additional automatic charge.

NEW explicit body-cost integration:

source unit reference = preserved Standard batch reference / source Q

finished unit reference = source unit reference + actual undamaged mundane base reference

Keep exact precision until the actual transaction. Purchased reserves are separate expenses; owned bodies need no repurchase. Source economy fields remain unchanged.
