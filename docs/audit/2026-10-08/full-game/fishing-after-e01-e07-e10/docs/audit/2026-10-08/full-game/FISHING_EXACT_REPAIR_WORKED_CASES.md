## FI-E01 — A Fishing result must belong to its declared check

A Rod attempt declares ordinary DC12 before commitment. Its separate Core result identifies the same canonical WIS/Fishing-tackle ability check, actor, attempt, selected creature, profile and DC. A trained angler with WIS+2 and PB+2 contributes `{WIS:2, Fishing:2}`. An untrained angler contributes `{WIS:2, Fishing:0}` and Core records the untrained disadvantage source. Expertise replaces that PB term with 4; adding another PB to make 6 fails qualification. These three cases are also exercised for Net and Trap. Trap uses its actual setup credential and permits no additional personal bonus terms.

Replacing the roll with an attack, DEX, Survival, harvesting, an automatic result or a non-roll still gives zero from the generic equipment selector, but the journal rejects the attempted catch even when its supplied degree says Success. Reusing its canonical roll ID for a different attempt also fails after the first attempt closes. A DC15 result cannot resolve the previously committed DC12 attempt merely by changing both the result and its Core record. Ordinary DC12/15/18 and a separately reviewed DC21 fixture retain their own recorded declarations. No dice engine is reimplemented here.

## FI-E07 — The response region ends at thirty feet

A receptive fish thirty feet from the actual bait/Lure point remains eligible. The same attempt can have a distinct forty-eight-foot angler-to-hook Rod path: the attraction and deployment distances measure different paths. At thirty-one feet the fish is excluded from selected attraction entirely, rather than retained at weight 1. A known-empty allocation cannot be committed as available stock; an actual exploratory allocation can record an empty result without drawing a replacement.

Within the region, three receptive physical candidates sorted A/B/C, with affinities matching/nonmatching/matching, have weights 2/1/2. Exactly five integer outcomes allocate A/A/B/C/C. More matching traits do not add weight. Each path names the actual response origin and the same opportunity. A different origin, candidate set, attempt, assembly or opportunity fails. A rejecting creature is never forced into receptive status. Replaying commitment or substituting another fish at the check cannot create a second response.

When the selected source is a Rig, response-origin validation uses its recorded dispenser point. This case tests point binding only; complete Rig load, mounting, handling and retrieval qualification remains FI-E05 work. An independently recorded ordinary visible Net response has no attraction weighting and does not enlarge an allocation that selected attraction.
