## Shared proposed contracts

### Status

UNADOPTED, UNSENT. These31 source feats remain pending whole-feat review.29 receive complete proposed operating procedures; Astral Projection and Planar Binding receive grant interfaces only, retaining their deferred spell/cosmology payloads. NEW supplements and interpretations are authoring proposals, not recovered source facts or ordinary catalog promotions.

### Acquisition

Use actual legal feat selections and permanent printed ability/total-character-level prerequisites. Spellcasting ability means an acquired personal route genuinely able to cast a spell, including a finite innate grant, not ownership of a scroll/device or an ally’s spell. A named spell/rank/school prerequisite requires actual acquired access to such a spell; exhausted daily uses or a temporary suppression do not erase ownership. No feat is repeatable under this proposal absent express source permission. Record each permanent choice and source; no free rest-time retargeting, extra class level or imported feature of the same name.

### Current authority

The authored-review index, not an older source preparation’s blocked flag, locates the current full spell procedure. Invisibility and Mage Hand now use the completed LRX standalone manuscript plus its shared rules. Find Familiar and Dispel Magic already have full reviewed cards. Familiar Bond’s +current-owner-character-level maximum-HP component was already reviewed; keep its capacity-only update and creation-HP rule. Astral Projection and Planar Binding remain pending payloads. Misty Step is a reviewed supplemental ancestry-only identity; a new feat route must be explicitly granted without adding ordinary class access.

### Feat casting route

NEW proposed common route for explicit feat spell grants: record feat identity, named spell, chosen source placement/school, granted rank, casting ability and each finite use separately. The feat route supplies its named rank access independently of class levels; it grants no ordinary class list, class level, RP pool, RC conversion, prepared position, spellbook or class technique. Spell attack=d20+total PB+actual chosen ability modifier, save DC=8+total PB+that modifier. A cantrip is repeatable only with its own actual actions/cadences. No generic cantrip scaling. NEW numerical payload-level completion: where an actual granted spell explicitly requires a casting class level for its intrinsic numeric effect, use total character level for this feat route only, never to unlock class features or additional spells. This numeric substitution needs joint approval, particularly for standard companion profiles; a class-cast version continues using its real class level.

### Casting costs and strain

NEW finite feat casts waive their normal rank RP price and price-only strain surcharge, spending their specified feat use at legal cast start. They retain actual components, targets, actions, concentration, interruption and independent costs. An explicit effect-level conversion/sacrifice cost still applies: Resonance Shield’s mandatory1RP is not a waived rank price, and a character without a real RP pool cannot pay it. No new pool is created. Every genuine feat cast uses shared character Dissonance/Refrain once: rank0 base0, rank1–5 base1, rank6–9 base2, actual same-name repetition and Limit=max(4,2+PB+max(0,casting modifier)). Rank1+ remains blocked at Limit+6; ordinary cantrip rules continue. Use the current no-RP/mixed-character recovery interface without granting resources. A committed interrupted/countered cast refunds neither charge nor materials and never restores displaced concentration. One actual casting source must be declared; learning through a feat alone grants no additional paid-RP casts. A separately acquired class route can cast under its own access/repertoire/costs and keeps genuinely shared named-spell/source-specific cadence limits.

### Focus and hands

NEW feat-only F completion: an actual source-legal class focus may serve its matching class-list feat grant; alternatively designate one actual nonvaluable hand-held token as a focus for that feat’s spells alone. This identifies an existing physical object, creates no item or universal focus, and never substitutes for priced/consumed materials. Actual V and S remain unless the feat expressly changes them. Occupied hands, anatomy, suppression, armor proficiency and source-specific costs still matter. No feat here silently grants the Battle Mage somatic exception to War Caster or adds unprinted Reaction spells.

### Spell modifiers

A modifier of spells you cast follows the actual casting source, school and placement. A creature summoned by a spell acts separately; its attacks are not automatically the caster’s spell damage. A spell-cast item route can qualify only if its actual rule says the user casts the spell; ordinary item activation, copied damage, a reflected packet or a similarly named class technique is not a new cast. A single shared area roll is one damage result applied through each actual target’s defenses; it does not multiply once-per-roll bonuses by the number of targets. Distinct flat feat bonuses may add under the proposed common feat rule, subject to already reviewed specific families.

### Damage order

NEW common proposed order for these modifiers: establish genuine spell damage components and critical dice; apply authorized rerolls to each actual die identity; apply Elemental Adept1-to-2 to final actual rolled faces of its chosen type; replace eligible native spell dice with maxima where Cantrip Mastery applies instead of rolling; add eligible flat/per-actually-rolled-die bonuses; compute each explicit percentage bonus from the same underlying pre-percentage eligible amount without compounding another percentage bonus; then resolve actual target defenses and the existing damage event. A die whose roll is replaced by maximization was not actually rolled for Evocation Focus. No bonus creates an otherwise prohibited attack, damage packet or target. Existing source-specific contrary ordering remains explicit, not silently rewritten.

### Supplemental scope

The proposed missing Spell Focus feat, two legacy-name initiate curricula, Ritual-tag overlay and Detect Thoughts feat-only payload are separately marked NEW in this proposal. None modifies the335-entry feat registry,848-entry ordinary spell catalog,17-class foundation, current public manifests or sealed lore. Their exact finite definitions must be accepted or replaced in the joint conference. A future source decision does not itself constitute exact candidate approval.
