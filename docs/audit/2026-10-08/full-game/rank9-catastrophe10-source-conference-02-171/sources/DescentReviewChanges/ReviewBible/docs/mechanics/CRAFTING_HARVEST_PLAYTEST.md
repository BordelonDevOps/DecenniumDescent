# Crafting, harvesting and preservation

**Status: REVIEWED authored operational PLAYTEST crafting/harvesting/preservation framework.** This supplement makes the shared procedure executable. It does not approve any of the 687 recipe effects, infer missing magic-item powers, approve balance, or change the companion runtime. An unreviewed recipe may be executed only as an isolated framework test of access, scheduling, ingredients, crafting check, quality, yield, trade XP, preservation or price-reference behavior. Its unfinished output effect grants no gameplay benefit, activation, statistic, condition, spell-like effect, charge, duration, resource change or market classification beyond fields already independently reviewed. Normal campaign use of the finished product requires that recipe's output/effect card to be separately completed and reviewed.

The mechanical source is Crafting Bible V4 (687 recipes, 696 material records, 16 trades); the public V5 book contains 433 formulas and 604 materials. Preserve all source IDs and visibility: 433 public, 39 restricted, 215 Director-only recipes. Public availability does not mean free ownership of a formula. Public dossiers must not expose the other 254 recipes or 92 restricted materials. Cosmology remains outside this review until after the authorized GitHub push is verified.

The [actual GPT review](../../../../docs/audit/2026-10-08/full-game/GPT_CRAFTING_ENGINE_EXACT_REVIEW.md) conditionally approved this framework; all eight required repairs are applied. Exact source text, hashes and paragraph/line bindings are in `docs/audit/2026-10-08/full-game/CRAFTING_ENGINE_SOURCE_EVIDENCE.json` at the game root. In this supplement, **Source** identifies recovered procedure; **Completion** identifies a new bounded interpretation. The original JSON, books and historical procedures remain unchanged.

## One work order, one batch

Before work, record the recipe ID and visibility, crafter and trade level, actual formula access, batch quantity, chosen ability, tools and station, component lots and substitutions, assistants, DC, expected active hours, work schedule, final product profile and market quotation. Record whether a product is an ordinary item, a magical item, a consumable or an alteration to an existing item. The name alone cannot decide that classification.

Check eligibility and reserve actual ingredients before beginning. Perform the required active work. Make one completion check for the entire batch; resolve quality, catalyst retention and failure recovery once. Debit consumed components, record returned components, add only actual finished units, and record trade XP. An interrupted batch is the same work order, with the same reserved ingredients. Neither interruptions nor moving to a new station create another roll, yield or first-craft award.

**Source:** quantity, difficulty, components, workRequirements and craftingCheck come from the exact recipe. **Completion:** recipe quantity is the number of finished units in one batch. Component quantities and costs are for that batch, not for each finished unit. Do not multiply components or recommended batch value by output quantity a second time. This interpretation applies to the new playtest projection; the ambiguous source `resultItem.value` is retained unchanged.

An unfinished output profile is a real dependency. The framework cannot invent a spell effect, charge recharge, activation action, target, range, save, duration, stacking rule, output weight or item price merely because a recipe has a `developed` label.

## Training and recipe access

**Source:** a character lacking the trade or the required tool proficiency has disadvantage and cannot attempt formulas above recipe skill level 4. Formulas above level 4 normally require a formula, schema, pattern or documented oral tradition. All 687 source formula flags agree with this threshold. Trade levels run from 1 to 20, separately from character level, class stage, PB, spell rank and material tier.

**Completion:** the primary-tool bindings in `CRAFTING_TRADE_REFERENCE.md` define the relevant training. A character who already has the listed proficiency may initialize that trade at level 1, with zero trade XP, without an extra proficiency grant. Merely owning the kit grants neither PB nor a trade level. Trade training and primary-tool proficiency acquired through this procedure are one credential, not two PB contributions. Existing explicit trade-level grants remain intact. Being proficient with unrelated tools, Arcana or Survival does not automatically give this credential.

An untrained crafter has effective trade level 0. Recipes of skill levels 1–4 can be attempted with the source disadvantage when otherwise physically possible. A trained crafter can attempt recipes up to `max(4, current trade level)`. Above level 4 the crafter must also possess or be given legitimate access to the exact formula. A trained level-1 novice does not lose the basic recipes available to an untrained character. Missing necessary physical tools or the station prevents the attempt; disadvantage does not manufacture either.

For a new credential during play, complete 80 active training hours with a willing instructor proficient in that primary tool and at least level 5 in that trade. Pay a default 10 gp tuition for the course, plus actual tool ownership or rental and living costs. Hours cannot overlap other work or rest. Completion grants that tool proficiency and trade level 1 with zero XP, once; repeated courses give no duplicate benefit. This is a new playtest training route, not a revision of class/ancestry/background grants. A named source's specific training route wins for its own grant.

Source rank bands (Novice, Apprentice, Journeyman, Expert, Master, Grandmaster) are descriptions of the catalog, not another hard tier gate. Forty-nine records sit outside the printed rank/tier bands. Use their exact skillLevel and explicit prerequisites rather than silently changing those recipes or denying them by a second band test. Public Tier I–V remains the public knowledge ceiling regardless of a character's high trade level. Restricted and Director recipes require actual in-world access and their separately reviewed cards; a rank title cannot reveal them.

## The crafting check

**Source:** `d20 + chosen ability modifier + proficiency contribution + tool bonus + facility bonus + assistant bonus`, against the recipe difficulty. Choose one of the recipe's printed ability options before rolling. **Completion:** do not add the numerical trade level to the check. PB comes from total character level, once, when qualified by actual primary-tool training; an explicit applicable expertise feature replaces that contribution with its stated multiplier. Training in both a skill and the tool cannot double it.

Ordinary functional tools and a suitable ordinary facility each give a bonus of +0. They permit the work. A specific reviewed item, facility or feature may give a stated bonus; similar descriptive words such as fine, superior or research station do not invent a crafting bonus. The expedition research station's +2 research/identification applies to those checks, not crafting. Same-named bonuses do not stack. At trade level 10, choose and record one specialty; its source +1 applies only to a work order within that specialty. No automatic free ingredient substitution follows from the word specialty.

Each qualified assistant gives +1, up to the recipe's `maximumAssistants` (0–3). They must possess the relevant primary-tool proficiency, be eligible to attempt that recipe, and work all of its required active hours alongside the lead. Formula access can be shared for this work order. Assistance does not reduce time, waive ingredients, award extra output or grant assistant trade XP. Assistants cannot assist multiple simultaneous orders or perform a separate Watch activity during those same hours. General team assistance cannot bypass a recipe's zero-assistant limit.

In a safe suitable workshop, a trained lead may use 10 on the die if the recipe is at least five skill levels below their current trade level. This replaces the die, is not a natural 10/20/1, and uses the same bonuses, costs and hours. It is unavailable when under threat or when actual disadvantage remains on the crafting check. Declare it before rolling; a failed roll cannot be replaced afterward.

## Active time and expedition scheduling

**Source recovered from public V5 P11–12 and P88:** an Opening is 100 days, or 2,400 elapsed hours. The V4 system's 240-hour references are stale. Crafting hours represent active work, not free downtime. The recipe records consistently convert a workday to 8 active hours, a workweek to 40, and a workmonth to 160. These are work units, not a promise that every calendar week/month has that many days.

**Completion:** use `activeWorkHours` when present. Five Director-only one-year recipes have null hours; for this framework only, one workyear is 12 workmonths = 1,920 active hours. Preserve their source null and mark the overlay as new; these formulas remain individually unreviewed. A time-mode name is a station/access requirement, not permission to reduce required hours. An eight-hour recipe labeled expedition still needs all eight hours.

Field work requires its actual portable tools and a suitable place. A recipe is field-capable only when its `fieldCraftable` flag permits it; the fact that its trade is generally field-capable is insufficient. Expedition-mode work needs a fortified camp or research station. Workshop work needs a proper workshop. Haven-mode work needs a named secure workshop and storage. Legacy/special work needs a protected long-term facility or an explicitly supported between-season arrangement. Shorter time from a feature does not erase the recipe's station/access requirement.

Schedule one eight-hour Watch at a time. A member assigned to crafting, gathering or preservation can perform compatible sequential tasks within that Watch, recording actual minutes. The unused minutes are not another full team activity. Travel, guarding, assisting, spellcasting, training and work cannot all claim the same minutes; only an explicit ability can authorize concurrent tasks. Each character may schedule at most two active Watches and must satisfy the separately reviewed rest rules; this is a ceiling, not an exemption from fatigue, interruption or danger. For this supplement, an active work Watch means time committed to crafting, harvesting, preservation, trade training, formula research or assisting those activities. The two-Watch ceiling limits this framework's work contribution; it does not by itself resolve whether another reviewed subsystem permits a distinct noncrafting activity in the remaining Watch. Rest, Strain, fatigue and expedition legality continue to come from their own governing rules. An eight-hour beneficial Long Rest cannot simultaneously be eight hours of crafting. Trance's sleep reduction does not shorten the required Long Rest or grant free working time.

Workday notation is normalized to active hours before a valid speed feature applies. Forge Dwarf Master Craftsman reduces eligible artisan-tool creation hours by 25%; use hours × 0.75, rounding upward only to the next whole minute. It does not shorten rest, travel, ingredient growth/spoilage, training, another person's work, harvesting, or a separately fixed natural-remedy/tinker racial procedure. It does not shorten a recipe's independently stated incubation or calendar wait. Other applicable time modifiers require their own actual text; no unprinted stacking multiplier is created here.

Long orders may be paused between active phases. A default phase is at most eight active hours. Completing a phase safely permits secure storage and later resumption without lost progress. Forced interruption inside a phase loses only unfinished progress in that phase; completed phases remain. No crafting roll occurs yet. Reserved perishables still age, and explicit physical destruction can lose actual inventory. There is no arbitrary full restart or repeated free quality roll. Before an uninterrupted-phase order begins, record its phase lengths and storage needs; absent a more specific formula, use this default.

At the season boundary, unfinished work continues only if its actual location, workers and storage remain legally and physically available under the existing campaign rules. The 2,400-hour Opening does not grant 2,400 labor hours per character. The Haven activity 'Brew a Batch' supplies a workday assignment (8 hours), not automatic completion of every recipe regardless of its own duration.

## Quality and finite output

These are **crafting outcome labels**, separate from equipment grade and Core artifact market condition. An Exceptional craft does not automatically become Exceptional equipment (+3 statistics), and a Masterwork craft does not create a new grade or a magic item. An item named Superior does not obtain equipment-grade bonuses unless its reviewed profile explicitly binds that grade.

Resolve this specific six-band crafting table instead of general expedition outcome shifts. A natural 1 is a Mishap even if the total is high. A natural 20 uses its numerical total with no extra band shift. A margin of −10 or lower is a Mishap.

| Total minus DC | Result | Procedure |
|---|---|---|
| 10 or more | Masterwork | Choose 150% batch yield, rounded down, or the improvement option below; also preserve one eligible catalyst unit. |
| 5 through 9 | Exceptional | Choose 125% batch yield, rounded down, or the improvement option below. |
| 0 through 4 | Standard | Listed number of ordinary finished units. |
| −1 through −4 | Flawed | If base batch quantity is at least 2, produce half quantity rounded down, with ordinary unit effects. If quantity is 1, make one item with a defined flaw below. |
| −5 through −9 | Failed | No product; recover 25% of consumed non-catalyst input quantity for each material identity, rounded down to that material's permitted unit. |
| −10 or less, or natural 1 | Mishap | No product; lose consumed inputs, return nonconsumed tools, and roll the mishap procedure. |

**Completion:** quality choices are declared after the check, once for the whole batch. Extra yield does not itself consume another batch of ingredients; it represents reduced processing waste. For physical outputs the card must specify lawful packaging and output mass; total output cannot exceed actual retained material plus explicitly supplied extra mundane carrier/packaging. A yield increase cannot create missing bottles, duplicate a unique named core, or duplicate the existing item being enchanted. Such an order uses its legal improvement option or retains Standard quantity and its quality label. Floor arithmetic may give no extra units; no fractional item or banked remainder is created.

The default improvement option increases explicitly defined maximum structural item HP by 25% (Exceptional) or 50% (Masterwork), rounded down, minimum +1. Current HP increases by the same amount for the new undamaged item. It never increases creature HP, AC, damage, save DC, spell rank, RP, charges or capacity. A recipe card may replace this with one precise property/charge option reviewed for that recipe. With neither known structural HP nor a reviewed improvement option, retain Standard output and the quality label only. Consumables do not gain reusable doses or longer durations from this generic option.

A quantity-1 Flawed durable item has maximum structural HP equal to half its ordinary maximum, rounded down, minimum 1, and begins at that reduced maximum. This reduced maximum is a crafting flaw, not ordinary structural damage. Ordinary equipment repair may restore current structural HP only up to that reduced maximum; it does not remove the flaw, increase the item's maximum, alter its equipment grade or convert it to Standard quality. Removing a crafting flaw requires an explicit reviewed rework procedure; absent one, the reduced maximum persists. A quantity-1 consumable whose useful effect has a positive finite duration instead has half that duration, rounded down to a whole round (six seconds), minimum one round. Instant healing/damage/RP restoration and otherwise incompatible results have no generic flaw profile: treat that Flawed result as Failed with the normal 25% non-catalyst recovery. Do not halve an undefined feature or invent a permanent debuff. The completed output card must state whether either default applies.

Masterwork preserves **one component unit total** bearing role catalyst and consumed=true, chosen after the check. It must be an actual catalyst used in this batch, not a named irreplaceable component whose exact formula requires destruction; an indivisible catalyst cannot be split. Return that unit to inventory unchanged rather than deducting it. With no eligible catalyst, nothing is returned. The ingredient's exact identity, tier and remaining shelf life persist; this is not a fresh item. Nonconsumed tools always remain unless a specific mishap physically damages one.

**Reviewed Forge Dwarf completion:** when eligible artisan-tool creation achieves Standard, treat its output as Exceptional for the yield/improvement choice. Already Exceptional/Masterwork remains so. Failed, Flawed and Mishap do not upgrade. This gives the ancestry's quality language a bounded crafting function; it grants no equipment grade, price multiplier, success guarantee or extra XP.

## Mishaps

The public source references a tradeskill mishap table but contains none. **Completion:** after losing consumed inputs, roll 1d6 on this shared table. The trade reference supplies the injury damage type, giving every trade an executable table. A Mishap still never destroys a formula or an irreplaceable tool unless the damage below actually reduces that tool to 0 HP. Cleanup/calibration time is extra active time after the failed batch, not part of its completion hours.

| d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | Spilled workspace: 1 active hour of cleanup before this station can be used again. |
| 2 | Miscalibration: the primary kit cannot support another crafting check until someone proficient with it spends 2 active hours recalibrating it. No extra ingredients are required. |
| 3 | Tool damage: the lead's primary kit takes 1 point of compatible physical structural damage, using its actual object defenses; if that kit cannot physically suffer it, use result 1 instead. |
| 4 | Minor accident: the lead takes 1d4 damage of the trade's listed injury type, resolved normally through defenses, with no extra condition. Assistants take none. |
| 5 | Fouled apparatus: the station requires cleanup labor equal to 1 active labor-hour × the number of workers who participated in the failed order. Any physically capable workers may perform that cleanup; multiple workers may contribute simultaneously. The station cannot support another crafting order until the total required cleanup labor is completed. |
| 6 | Lost notes: this order yields no failure-research XP; the lead must spend 1 active hour reconstructing its work log before beginning another order in this trade. The owned formula remains intact. |

No ordinary mishap automatically creates a curse, disease, Strain, cosmological revelation, persistent hazard or lost proficiency. A specific reviewed volatile component can supply a separately declared hazard before the order starts. Do not invent that hazard after the roll. The Director describes the accident according to the actual trade and ingredients.

## Trade XP and specialties

**Source:** first successful craft 4 XP; nontrivial repeat 2; difficult failed attempt with usable research 1; trivial mass production 0. Advancing from level L to L+1 costs 10+L trade XP. Maximum level 20. The source per-recipe 4-XP field is not an extra award on top of this system.

**Completion:** a successful craft means Standard, Exceptional or Masterwork. Award 4 for the first success of a recipe ID in that trade if not trivial; subsequent nontrivial successes award 2. A recipe is trivial when current trade level is at least `trivialAtSkillLevel` (all source thresholds are recipe skill level +5). Triviality wins even for a previously unmade recipe. Changing ingredients, quality, batch size, location or a source alias does not reset first-success history.

A Flawed/Failed/Mishap order may give 1 XP only when its recipe is at least the crafter's current trade level, it consumed actual inputs and hours, and the lead records a concrete usable research finding. This is once per recipe ID per trade level, not repeat failure farming; mishap 6 gives none. No assistant or uninvolved observer receives this work-order award. Untrained attempts record experience narratively but bank no numerical trade XP and grant no proficiency. Training initializes zero XP.

Spend XP immediately when a threshold is reached; carry excess toward the next level, repeat if enough, and stop at 20. At level 10 select one concrete specialty such as antidotes, metal shields or textile containers. Record its scope before the next check; it cannot be renamed for each work order. Its +1 applies once. Further specialties, retraining and unique substitutions require another explicit feature or agreed research result; the rank alone grants none.

## Material identity, consumption and substitution

Keep the exact recipe ID and component occurrence. Global material names alone are insufficient: 143 embedded names have different tier/price/weight variants. Record identity as the recipe component's name, tier, category, properties, unit weight, unit value and source occurrence. Quantity belongs to the inventory lot, not the identity. Preserve component IDs where supplied; none are silently merged or normalized to a cheaper same-name material. The 2-gp crafting Crystal Vial and 5-gp equipment Vial remain distinct records unless an explicit compatibility/substitution ruling is recorded.

An exact recipe component's stored numeric `tier` remains part of that component's identity and may be compared for that recipe's explicit substitution requirements even when the stored number exceeds 6. This does not extend the generic V4 material-tier ladder or generic harvesting entitlement beyond Tier VI. A Tier 7–10 stored component therefore receives no generic harvest DC, public availability, rarity right or substitution source merely from that number; it requires its actual recipe/source acquisition procedure or another separately reviewed rule.

**Source:** substitutions require the relevant properties and tier unless the recipe names an irreplaceable ingredient. **Completion:** before spending materials, a substitute must satisfy each replaced unit's tier (equal or higher), all required functional properties and role, compatible physical form, and at least its required amount. The Director records the mapping and its actual unit quantities/weights/value. Higher price alone is not compatibility; a higher-tier component does not increase the result tier, quantity or effect. A unique named core cannot be replaced under this general permission. Do not repeatedly change the substitution after observing the outcome.

Reserve consumed quantities at the start and deduct them once at resolution or deliberate abandonment. Abandonment after work starts loses consumed inputs with no product/recovery roll; actual nonconsumed tools and intact independently stored unused supplies remain. Ingredients left outside the reservation remain usable but cannot also fund another order. Return only the exact quantities authorized by Masterwork or ordinary failure, never both for one result. No recovery from a Mishap. Component `consumed=false` returns its full reserved quantity; role=tool is not itself a consumed flag.

Nineteen source component rows have role=tool and consumed=true. Treat these as incorporated parts in those exact recipes, not the separately required reusable toolkit; retain their flags until individual recipe review. A focusing lens incorporated into a device is not a reusable station lens. The work order must supply both if it needs both. Never 'repair' all role=tool rows to nonconsumed in bulk.

Ordinary failure recovery uses each identity's physical units: an indivisible organ, lens, bottle or crystal rounds down to whole units; explicitly divisible bulk reagent can be recorded to 0.01 source units. Recovery never creates money. For example, four ordinary indivisible non-catalyst vials return one; one irreplaceable catalyst returns zero. These recovered lots retain their age and identity. A failed batch cannot be salvaged again for another automatic 25%.

## Identify and harvest

**Source:** identify with the creature type's knowledge skill, then extract using a relevant gathering trade. The type mapping and size times/yields are in the trade reference. This procedure concerns actual remains and environmental nodes already present; it does not create a creature, corpse, mineral vein, legal entitlement or formula.

**Completion:** the Director records a finite harvest manifest before checks: actual accessible categories, unit identities/tier/weight/value, quantities or yield dice, damaged/unavailable parts, preservation deadlines, and any exceptional drop. Normally expose four to six plausible categories, but a creature with fewer real categories has fewer. These are choices within one finite yield pool, not four to six copies of the yield roll. A Tiny creature cannot produce a Large creature's organ or an unlimited number of crystals.

Use the exact component's explicit harvest DC when present; otherwise the highest desired material tier sets DC: I 12, II 13, III 15, IV 16, V 18, VI 19. These are the existing expedition tier DCs, not recipe crafting DCs. The generic table has no public Tier VII–X harvest entitlement. A specially restricted ingredient beyond this table needs a separately stated DC and access procedure before rolling.

Identification takes 1 minute of close examination. Use INT and the source type skill except Medicine uses WIS; PB requires actual relevant training, with existing task-scoped expertise replacing it. One lead and at most one trained assistant (+1) can participate. A success reveals the manifest's usable categories, hazards and preservation needs; failure identifies no hidden qualities. There is no invented false fact on failure. The party may still extract a plainly recognizable category such as a hide, using the declared DC with disadvantage while unidentified. Further identification attempts require new evidence, a different method or consultation, not repeated rolls by every party member on unchanged evidence. Identification does not itself extract anything.

The extraction lead selects a compatible gathering trade: Skinning for animal parts, Herbalism for plants, Mining for mineral deposits, Fishing for aquatic gathering, Salvaging for constructs and dismantled devices; a specific listed source may authorize another. Use that trade's ability and primary-tool rules. Recipe-level access gates do not apply to an unranked generic harvest; actual restricted material access still does. Up to one eligible assistant gives +1 and spends the same extraction time. Tools and access are required; no station is required for ordinary physical field removal beyond a stable accessible work surface and appropriate containers. A kit cannot contain an unsafe magical essence without a suitable actual vessel.

| Size | Full extraction time | Base total units |
|---|---|---|
| Tiny | 10 minutes | 1 |
| Small | 20 minutes | 1d2 |
| Medium | 30 minutes | 1d4 |
| Large | 1 hour | 2d4 |
| Huge | 2 hours | 4d4 |
| Gargantuan | 4 hours | 8d4 |

Roll base yield once for the remains when the manifest is made. It is the shared total of ordinary units across categories, constrained by actual anatomy and mass. An explicit bestiary drop uses its own finite chance/quantity, once, in addition only when the source calls it an exceptional separate drop. A global material's dropRate does not add another independent roll for every identical name in the material catalog. State which exceptional drops depend on a successful extraction before rolling.

Full extraction uses the listed time. For a partial harvest, select which manifest categories to attempt first. Time is full time × chosen categories / available categories, rounded up to whole minutes, minimum 1 minute. Allocation of the finite units across categories is fixed before selection, so selecting one category cannot redirect the entire pool into its most valuable part. Once partial harvesting begins, unselected ordinary categories are forfeited for this generic procedure; another worker cannot harvest the same remainder again. An explicit unique drop can retain its separately stated procedure.

Use the core four-degree check for extraction: total ≥DC+10 critical success, ≥DC success, >DC−10 failure, ≤DC−10 critical failure; natural 20 improves one degree and natural 1 worsens one. Success recovers the selected allocation. Critical success additionally preserves one otherwise damaged ordinary unit within the selected attempted allocation that the manifest already recorded as damaged but potentially recoverable. If the selected allocation contains no such unit, Critical Success grants no additional unit. It cannot recover an unselected or already-forfeited category, duplicate an exceptional drop or exceed the manifest's finite anatomy. Failure ruins one selected unit (highest-value selected unit, tied at player's choice), recovers no selected units, and ends that attempt. Critical failure ruins all selected ordinary units, recovers none, and invokes only a hazard explicitly declared in the manifest; absent one, apply the gathering trade's minor accident (1d4 damage) to the lead. No reroll or new generic attempt on the same exhausted selection; named exceptional-drop procedures remain as stated.

A hurried extraction takes half the listed/partial time, rounded up to whole minutes, minimum 1 minute, with disadvantage. This is a new reconciliation of the detailed size table with the older one-hour rushed Watch summary; it avoids making a hurried Tiny harvest slower than normal. Normal and hurried harvesting consume actual Watch time, not a second automatic eight-hour charge. Yield, limits and spoilage are unchanged. Resource Team membership grants no duplicate attempt or extra yield.

Harvested lots become actual cargo immediately, including containers and weight. Food, water or fuel gathered separately does not magically become the complete expedition Supply bundle. An ancestry's explicit forage or Natural Remedy grant follows its own finite procedure; this generic harvest does not refresh those uses. Humanoid and celestial remains keep the source's consent, lawful-authority or explicit-dark-path restriction; the procedure supplies no blanket permission and makes no cosmological conclusion.

## Preservation and spoilage

**Source:** blood/organs 1–4 hours, essence 30 minutes–2 hours unless contained, meat 8–24 hours, hides 12–24 hours before curing; dry bone/horn/teeth/mineral and ordinary construct parts can last indefinitely. Active cores are exceptions. **Completion:** the manifest fixes the interval before harvesting. Where no more specific interval is stated, use the conservative low end: organs/blood 1 hour, essence 30 minutes, meat 8 hours, hide 12 hours. The clock starts when the creature dies or the material leaves its sustaining environment, not when the player finally harvests it.

Track condition separately from material tier and rarity: Fresh → Degraded → Spoiled. At one unprotected interval the lot becomes Degraded: its unit value halves and it cannot meet a recipe calling for Fresh ingredients, but otherwise retains its exact tier/properties. At two unprotected intervals it is Spoiled, value zero and unusable as an ordinary recipe ingredient or edible food. A specific recipe may explicitly require spoiled material. No new poison, curse or disease is inferred. Dividing, trading, repackaging, recovering or reharvesting a lot cannot reset its elapsed age. The label quality here is preservation condition, never an equipment grade or crafting outcome.

Work on a perishable ingredient must begin while it is eligible; ordinary reservation does not suspend aging. An exact recipe card may say its first processing phase stabilizes the input; otherwise it must remain eligible through completion. A generic engine cannot promise a 160-hour potion made from an untreated one-hour organ will succeed. This makes the card's storage/processing details mandatory.

If a required reserved component ceases to satisfy its recipe requirement before completion and no completed recipe phase expressly stabilized it, pause the order before further active work. Before resuming, the lead may reserve a fresh legal component satisfying that same requirement, including an already-legal substitution. The replacement lot keeps its own identity and age. Completed active-work phases remain completed, but any component already physically consumed or irreversibly processed before becoming unusable remains spent. If no legal replacement is supplied, abandon the order under the normal abandonment rule; do not make a completion or quality roll.

The following new mundane preservation procedure supplies a usable default without inventing magical stasis. For each lot of at most 10 lb of eligible blood/organs, meat or hide, spend 30 active minutes, a suitable trade kit, a sealable appropriately sized container, and 1 gp/0.5 lb of preservative supplies (salt, clean packing and the appropriate nonmagical stabilizer). These supplies have no independent combat or magical effect. Alchemy handles specimens, Cooking handles food, and Skinning handles hide. Make one DC 10 check using the trade's normal ability/proficiency, no assistants or quality-yield table. Success pauses spoilage for 24 elapsed hours; failure uses the supplies and time but gives no extension. This treatment must finish before the lot becomes Spoiled. It does not restore Fresh condition or remove existing age.

On a successful treatment, freeze accumulated unprotected age and resume it after the 24 hours. A replacement treatment takes the same cost/time and extends protection from its completion to 24 hours later, never adding stacked days. Any actual container breach, contamination or incompatible heat ends protection; merely opening to portion with clean tools for less than a minute does not. Preserved supplies and container mass count as cargo until discarded or used. Processing into food is not automatically safe if the source explicitly says otherwise.

Mundane specimen jars alone contain fluids but do not pause decay. They do not stabilize magical essence or active cores. Those require a separately defined compatible containment method with its own lifetime, capacity and costs; without one, the printed/default clock continues. Dry durable materials remain indefinite only while kept dry and physically intact. No generic refrigeration, freezing or stasis benefit is inferred from a spell name; its actual reviewed effect must support the storage method.

## Salvage, research and sale

Do not confuse harvesting remains, manufacturing a Salvaging-tool recipe, and dismantling an existing item. A salvage tool's particular bonus requires its reviewed card. Ordinary failure recovery is already part of the batch check and cannot be claimed again as dismantling that failed batch.

**New basic dismantling procedure:** spend 1 active hour per item of at most 50 lb with actual Salvager's tools, a stable work surface, and applicable proficiency. Larger or magical/unique items require an explicit salvage profile first. Before work begins, record each recoverable ordinary component and the maximum quantity physically remaining in the item after known prior damage, loss or consumption. On Success, recover 25% of each recorded recoverable quantity, rounded down under that component's normal unit rule. Explicitly divisible bulk material may use the framework's 0.01-source-unit precision. Failure recovers none. No later appraisal may increase the predeclared recoverable cap. An unknown bill of materials gives no invented valuable component. Roll INT or WIS plus the one applicable PB contribution against DC 10. The item is irreversibly dismantled on either result. No general recovery of catalysts, expended ammunition/consumables, enchanting power, RP, souls, formulas or previously missing parts. No sale-value-to-free-material conversion. This check uses binary success; no natural-die degree bonus or extra recovery is introduced. If there are no known recoverable materials, ordinary scrap may be described but has no assigned recipe identity or automatic resale value.

**Source:** experiment with a stated goal, compatible components, station and research check; failed research can create notes. **Completion:** researching an existing available formula uses its `minimumResearchHours`, minimum 8 active hours, and a copy/sample or credible source sufficient for that knowledge. It uses the trade ability with proficiency against that recipe's DC, plus explicit applicable research bonuses. Success yields a usable transcription of the exact formula if the source actually contains enough information; failure yields notes, no formula, and consumes those research hours. No automatic ingredient consumption unless a destructive sample test is declared with actual quantities before research. Do not reroll unchanged evidence; a fresh attempt needs a new source/sample or eight additional hours of a concretely different method. Copying an already legible legitimately accessible formula requires 1 hour and 1 sp of writing supplies per recipe and no check, and grants no trade XP. These routes grant knowledge, not item effects or access through a faction's restrictions.

Designing a new recipe, a unique substitution, or reverse-engineering an unknown magical item yields a proposal for a new reviewed card. It cannot automatically invent a numerical power from a high roll. State the desired result, consumed test materials, facility, hours, DC and what information success can actually reveal before experimentation. This is an authoring boundary, not cosmology research or permission to expose Director lore.

**Batch pricing completion:** `recommendedMarketValue` is the recommendation for the Standard batch. A nominal unit reference value is batch value divided by Standard batch quantity; keep exact precision until the final transaction. Market recommendation is not a guaranteed buyer or profit. Actual quotes specify units, finished quality, condition, availability, legal claims and demand. Ordinary crafted mundane equipment uses the already reviewed mundane resale standing percentages. Actual artifacts use the Core artifact market procedure only when their reviewed profiles classify them as artifacts. The frameworks are not multiplied together indiscriminately. Crafting quality alone supplies no extra sale multiplier.

The source commission default is a 50% deposit on the agreed complete quote, plus delivery of all agreed irreplaceable monster parts before work. The quote explicitly states who supplies other ingredients, whether those costs are included, delivery schedule, acceptable quality and failure/replacement terms. There is no universal refund or loss of all funds inferred from a failed roll. Record these terms before accepting a commission in the fiction. Do not charge the same supplied ingredient twice or turn returned unused ingredients into a second payout. Use reviewed equipment rounding: final purchase cost up to a copper; final sale proceeds down to a copper, once.

A buyer commits to a finite quoted quantity and price before mass-production plans rely on that sale. Once it is filled, more production needs another actual buyer/quote; a reference price does not generate infinite demand. Existing Core/local market procedures can change later quotes. Generic crafting does not manufacture gold, create a guaranteed arbitrage floor, or silently apply the artifact market to every mundane crafted object.

The older expedition 0–3 Integrity scale does not replace reviewed structural HP for personal equipment or this chapter's kits. Use the equipment repair procedure for those actual items.
