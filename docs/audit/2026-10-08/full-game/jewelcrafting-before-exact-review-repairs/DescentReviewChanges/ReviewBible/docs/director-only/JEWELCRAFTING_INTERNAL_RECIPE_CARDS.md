## recipe-241 — Field Veiled Bracelet

**DRAFT; exact review pending.** public; Jewelcrafting level 2; tier 1; Common; crafting DC 11; Standard quantity 3.

**Preserved source effect:** While worn, grants +1 to one stealth-related check or save. It has 1 charge; expend one to gain advantage on such a roll. Charges return at dawn.

**Operational effect:** While complete, at positive structural HP, unsuppressed, unmelded, fitted and actually worn in one designated legal wrist-adornment position, the actual wearer gains +1 once on each independently qualifying Stealth-related ability check or saving throw under the exact predicates below. This passive benefit is not spent and remains with zero charges. This physical item has maximum 1 charges. A conscious, non-Incapacitated wearer may voluntarily spend one before an eligible roll commits to gain advantage on that event, with no separate Action, Bonus Action, Reaction, RP, speech or hand use. Declare before any die or replacement result; no retrospective reroll. At most one Jewelcrafting charge is spent per event, and ordinary advantage/disadvantage cancellation applies. The spending item must independently qualify but need not supply the strongest flat modifier. Ability-check modifiers share equipment.leather-task-enhancement with independently qualified Leatherworking/Tailoring; save modifiers share equipment.jewel-save-enhancement with other jewels. Use the strongest within the applicable family once. Charges initialize once at lawful creation and refill only at legitimate Jewelcrafting dawn events under the shared guide. Transfer, equipping, repair, suppression ending, new scenes and copied records never refill or reinitialize them. This grants no AC candidate, even zero; no resistance, attack modifier, proficiency, extra hand, focus permission or carrying relief.
