# Twelve rank-one Enchantment spells — source conference

All twelve Enchantment cantrips are now promoted with your three required repairs: Mental Tap's benefit starts immediately after positive actual damage is known; Soothe Animal's classification is an application-only gate; Beguiling Gaze allows ordinary momentary blinking while objectively preventing meaningful voluntary gaze breaks. Corresponding design explanations were conformed. Sixteen promotion checks and 42 pipeline checks / 89,040 access boundaries pass. Prior 198 operational cards, special concentration-class cards, frozen class rules and original source hashes are unchanged. Current catalog: 210 reviewed identities / 638 unfinished, including 203 unfinished with some actual class rank0–3. All848 identities /936 placements remain. No deletion or GitHub push; runtime, numerical balance and wider systems remain open.

Please reconcile the next twelve sources into complete draftable rules, preferably below17,000 characters. I will return exact authored cards for approval before promotion. These all have actual Enchantment rank1 / base2RP access: Bard, Mirrorsage, Wizard at class1; Crimson Agent, Paladin at2; Thief at3. All raw ranges30feet, components V/S/F (M/F mapped to F). All Action except Distract's Reaction. Preserve each exact source and explicitly label new targeting, saves, durations and restrictions NEW. Do not import an unrelated game's familiar spell defaults.

1. Mesmerize: CHA save; stunned AND unaware for1round. Preserve real Stunned, which inherits Incapacitated and ends actual concentration. Define unaware without silently applying Unconscious/Prone/adjacent criticals. Apply the combined source-character/target/encounter hard-denial cap; identify whether the whole stun/unaware bundle is prevented when that cap is unavailable and how narrower condition immunities interact. One-round hostile endpoint is first FUTURE target regular-turn END, excluding the entire currently underway turn; outside initiative six elapsed seconds. No biological/Intelligence gate or added concentration absent a specific decision.

2. Pacify: Enemy cannot attack or cast damaging spells for1round. Define NEW save and exact attack/damage-spell restriction. Source says enemy: use ordinary declared identification, not a free hidden-hostility oracle. Does the ban cover all attack rolls and spells with actual damage payloads across Actions/Bonus Actions/Reactions, while allowing other choices and movement? A control spell that does not itself deal damage need not be banned. Don't assume a whole-Action denial where the target retains meaningful other actions. Source prints no harm-ending clause, unlike Voice of Peace; do not copy one silently. Already committed effects should not rewind.

3. Sleep: Up to24HP worth of creatures fall Unconscious, expressly NO SAVE. Specify bounded target selection/area, allocation order, current versus maximum HP, temporary HP inclusion, zero-HP creatures and whether immune or hard-cap-blocked targets consume the budget. Avoid a free hidden-HP/immunity probe from preflight selection. Preserve fixed24 rather than imported rolled sleep dice. Need NEW finite duration, concentration decision and exact waking methods: positive damage packet versus actual HP loss, helper Action and reach, voluntary/no-cost awakening? External Unconscious is not stabilization/healing/death-save cure; zero-HP state persists. Active Undead/Construct are not automatically immune unless their own actual record says so. Existing Unconscious sources remain independent.

4. Confusing Flash: WIS save or randomly attacks OR flees. Preserve forced behavior and fair random branch selection, not a vague penalty. Need exact timing, random eligible victim selection, legal weapon/unarmed attack choice, range/path/flee direction, no victim or blocked path handling. Does it use the target's next ordinary Action/movement, or grant an immediate attack? Avoid free Action recursion and whole-turn commandeering beyond the chosen bounded command. Forced travel using the target's actual movement may provoke normally if it meets actual OA rules; distinguish that from positional push/pull. Define dangerous routes/self-harm, remaining Bonus Action/Reaction choices and hard-denial cap reservation/application. No caster-picked favorable branch after seeing the die.

5. Distract: Reaction, "Interrupt enemy action (no opportunity attacks this round)." Source could mean an actual pending Action interruption plus a separate OA restriction, not just the latter. Please choose explicitly and justify. Define exact visible/audible real trigger and whether the pending whole Action, one attack within Attack, Bonus Action or Reaction qualifies. If a cast Action qualifies, reconcile normal paid cast start / Counterspell LIFO without making Distract an unbounded duplicate counter source. What happens to committed costs/earlier completed attacks, and does this count as hard Action denial while ordinary Counterspell does not? NEW save? "This round" could mean current numbered round END; specify outside initiative and one-second-left cases rather than silently making it the next-turn round convention. Resolve independent OA rider if hard cancellation is blocked. No refunds or retroactive undo.

6. Voice of Peace: negotiate ceasefire; one enemy will not attack unless provoked. Need NEW save, duration/concentration, initial hearing/language and meaning of actual provocation (attack/harm, credible immediate threat, observed harm to allies versus insults/failed persuasion). Does it prevent only attacks or all knowingly harmful actions? Compare Pacify/Soothe without making permanent alliance, forced treaty terms, automatic successful Diplomacy or full Action loss. Say whether later hearing/language/range changes end it or only the initial gate matters. Harm-ending event before attack resolves is materially different from waiting for actual damage.

7. Command: forced one-word command for1round. NEW save, hearing/language, legal one-word scope and timing. Preserve actual compulsion rather than reducing it to Minor Suggestion's optional idea. Define representative commands (approach/flee/drop/halt/grovel), which existing Action/movement/interaction they consume and what unrelated permissions remain. No extra Action/resource reset. Impossible orders, direct self-harm, dangerous routes, compelled attack, secret disclosure, surrender of held object versus ownership/permanent dispossession, knowing oath violation need explicit limits grounded in the bounded command. Hard-denial cap and multiple competing commanders cannot grant multiple turns or silently bypass each other.

8. Hideous Laughter: WIS save, falls Prone AND Incapacitated by laughter. Need NEW duration, concentration, repeat save/harm ending. No imported INT>4 or Humanoid gate. Current Incapacitated alone does not set Speed0 or prohibit speech; Prone can crawl unless a specific rule says otherwise. Can they stand and still laugh? State explicitly instead of importing another version's cannot-stand clause. Hard denial prevents the Incapacitated rider but need not prevent independent immediate Prone, unless you decide the source creates one indivisible bundle. Narrow immunities likewise keep scope. Ending laughter does not stand the target up automatically or restore concentration.

9. Faction Charm: strong affinity toward your faction for1hour, no printed concentration. Preserve actual strength/length over Faction Sympathy (concentration1minute, +1Persuasion and genuine warmth), without mandatory obedience or assigning membership/standing/credentials. NEW save and exact social benefit. Is actual Charmed present or merely the named affinity? "Your faction" should use current actual affiliation/membership, not a logo or reputation-only proxy, checked after costs if hidden. Snapshot faction and harm-ending rules; faction leadership/member changes are not automatic retargeting. Avoid hidden affiliation diagnostics or unknown members' hostility ending it without recognition.

10. Alignment Suggestion: WIS save, concentration1minute, "Subtly push target toward your alignment." This source differs from Empathy's mere connection. Explicitly decide whether this is a useful temporary inclination toward an actual value orientation, or a temporary canonical axis shift. Don't silently dilute the source to a generic mood, but don't assume permanent identity change. If an axis overlay, define which axis/step, Neutral and missing records, actual gate interactions, and how expiry removes only the overlay without undoing independent Corruption/alignment changes. Alignment never alone compels specific conduct/PvP/oathbreaking. If inclination only, explain how its mechanical procedure realizes the printed push and differs from existing optional suggestion/social cantrips. Ward/Alignment tags do not create a ward. Actual caster orientation at application is a snapshot unless expressly otherwise.

11. Resonance Influence: Target's NEXT SPELL costs+1 or-1RP. Preserve real price change, not a Resonance task modifier. NEW willingness/save/mode choice, duration and one pending target record across casters without cheap banking. Determine order after actual rank price, printed mandatory extras (Resonance Shield+1), Dissonance surcharge and real class discounts; minimum0 without refunds/credit. +1 should address cantrips and -1 a zero-price cast explicitly. Only an actual spell cast (including class/item sources if truly cast using RP), not every RP technique; no fictitious RP obligation on charge-only effects absent decision. Consume at legal next cast commitment even if later countered; failed preflight including insufficient adjusted RP cannot consume it. Readied spell preparation, not later release. Self-target cannot retroactively change this cast's paid cost. Doesn't change rank/access/repertoire/Dissonance/Refrain or permit ordinary ranked casting while Fractured. Pricing helper currently lacks effect-state modifiers; authored rule does not certify its runtime.

12. Enthrall: rank1 "Captivate nearby creatures; they have disadvantage on Perception checks." Need exact area/targets/hearing, NEW save and duration/concentration. Preserve printed actual Perception disadvantage; no imported passive-5, automatic Hidden, Stun/Charm or Action loss. Does it affect every check or only non-caster subjects (the latter is explicit for reviewed Enthralling Tune but absent here)? Source rank2 Core349 separately says all creatures that can hear you/WIS negates, also raw30feet,3RP. No actual class has that rank2 placement. Preserve it as a blocked/unassigned variant; please explicitly decide its present status without merging it into rank1, granting an upgrade for extraRP or claiming broader variant closure. A future reviewed class-access/identity disposition remains possible.

Shared: select actual repertoire and class/rank, pay legal Action/components/RP, establish concentration, apply Dissonance/Refrain/beginning hooks, then counter LIFO and surviving effects. No refunds/old concentration restore. Initial hidden eligibility is evaluated after commitment, without diagnostic disclosure. V audible/S usable hand/F normal focus. Binary saves DC8+PB+casting modifier, no natural automatic pass/fail. No automatic upcasting/scaling. Hard whole-Action/turn denial has ONE combined source-character/target/encounter record across classes/spells/companions, not one per spell; actual boss immunities remain. Creature type alone is not mental immunity; Charmed-only immunity does not block every Enchantment. Ongoing source clocks/guards survive recasting, initiative reorder/extra turns, absence and plane changes; recasting never refreshes shared-use ledgers.

Please identify meaningful balance concerns explicitly while still returning exact draftable mechanics. Scope here is authored manuscript and source reconciliation; no numerical balance, runtime, entire-school or whole-game approval.

11: This overlay governs generic conditions in the combined candidate. A reviewed card overrides a generic clause only where it prints the specific exception. An unreviewed historical summary never grants an extra ability or bypasses a gate. The existing combined source-character/target/encounter hard-denial cap still applies to class/spell/companion applications.
12: 
13: Record each condition source, affected creature, applying DC, magnitude, start, duration and ending method. Same-name nonnumeric penalties apply once; independent sources continue until each ends. A generic cure naming a condition clears its source records unless a reviewed procedure specifies one-record scope. Bleeding Medicine and Burning DEX saves use the specific procedures below. Track Charmed, Frightened, Marked and Grappled relationships separately. Exhaustion, Intoxication, Unity and Wounds use their explicit tracks.
14: 
15: Immunity to a named condition prevents that condition, including an inherited component, but does not silently grant immunity to every parent condition that can cause it. For example, Prone immunity prevents the Prone component of Unconscious, not its other effects. Immunity to an inflicted condition never erases the underlying zero-HP or dead state. Gaining immunity suppresses applicable existing effects while immunity lasts; their clocks continue unless explicitly paused. Removing immunity does not reapply a one-time consequence or restart an old duration.

161: ## Charmed
162: 
163: **Preserved Core text:** Cannot willingly harm the charmer; charmer has advantage on social checks against it.
164: 
165: **Effect:** The creature cannot willingly take an action or make a choice it knows would harm the charmer: this includes attacks, harmful targeting, and knowingly placing the charmer in a harmful area or indirect effect. Unforeseeable or genuinely unknown collateral harm is not retroactively a willing violation. The charmer has advantage on social ability checks against it. Charmed itself never compels affection, obedience, beliefs, allegiance, affirmative protection or self-harm.
166: 
167: **Application:** Each application identifies the charmer/source.
168: 
169: **Duration and ending:** Use the applying effect's printed duration and repeat-save/ending procedure.

171: **Stacking:** A creature may be Charmed by more than one source; each source relation is tracked independently. Advantage does not stack.
172: 
173: **Immunities and specific exceptions:** Charm immunity prevents application. Forced actions from another effect are not made voluntary merely because the creature is Charmed.
