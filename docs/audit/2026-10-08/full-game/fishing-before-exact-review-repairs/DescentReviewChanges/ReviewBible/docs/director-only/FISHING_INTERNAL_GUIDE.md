## Start one attempt

Ordinary provision fishing uses one lead, one real site opportunity and one complete Rod, Net or Trap capture assembly. The prey must actually be present, receptive to the method, physically compatible and have an ordinary provision-catching profile. This does not defeat an actively resisting encounter creature, a character or a companion. Unsupported dangerous or larger catches require their own procedure.

Before setup, record the site and shared stock, opportunity, real candidate creatures, hazards, catch profile, DC, receiving arrangement, apparatus and tools, and the known recovery-category order. Reserve the actual opportunity and items. A lead may have only one unresolved Fishing deployment. Cancellation before commitment releases reservations but never time already spent. At first deployment/exposure, spend the opportunity, expose any bait, choose one attraction source and allocate at most one response atomically. After commitment, interruption, failure or abandonment refunds neither opportunity nor bait.

An ordinary attempt is five active minutes of assembly/deployment, fifty minutes of fishing, a check at minute 55, then five active retrieval minutes. No work block compresses into one combat Action: every six seconds credited as active work commits that worker's Action and required hands. Conditions, threats and incompatible work can prevent progress. Active fishing, setup and retrieval share the crafting framework's two-active-work-Watch ceiling; they cannot also be travel, guard duty, rest or crafting.

Use **d 20 + WIS + one applicable proficiency contribution + independently legal bonuses**. Expertise replaces PB. The declared ordinary DC is 12 for accessible conditions,15 for materially difficult current/cover/access, or 18 for severe but feasible conditions. A separately reviewed catch profile may supply its own DC. Recipe DC, tier, value and crafting time do not set catch parameters; impossible access has no routine check.

Use Core's four task degrees and natural-result shifts, then collapse Critical Success/Success to one interception and Failure/Critical Failure to no catch. There is no critical extra specimen, recovery unit or time discount. Critical Failure causes only the hazard assigned to that opportunity beforehand; absent one, invent no injury, Pressure, contamination or gear damage. Untrained ordinary fishing is WIS without tool PB and with disadvantage. Catching has no recipe-level/formula gate or crafting XP. Automatic results receive no check bonus and create no replacement roll; this candidate supplies no automatic catch.

One trained Rod/Net assistant grants+1 only after completing the five setup and fifty fishing minutes alongside the lead before the check. Assistance never supplies the lead's missing hands. Later retrieval help costs real time and cannot qualify an earlier roll. Trap setting has no generic assistant bonus.

## Bait, ordinary bait and storage

One finished Bait is a single-use 0.10 lb magical carrier dose and a 0.01 lb ordinary wrapper:0.11 lb packaged,2×1×1 in, body reference 2 cp (one for each). The dose has HP 1; wrapper separately HP 1/AC11. Count components or inclusive package mass once. Unwrapping is not damage or exposure by itself. No part grants player food credit.

At first deliberate exposure, consume the whole usable dose entitlement and make one receipt attached to that attempt's assembly. The receipt lasts 60 elapsed minutes from exposure, or until the attempt closes. Ordinary swallowing/dispersal does not cancel it; this is the explicit consumable exception to continuing-body eligibility. It creates no creature condition or concentration. Scope-specific suppression must actually reach the assembly's bait effect. Different assemblies cannot inherit residue.

Before exposure, a canceled still-eligible dose remains unused. After exposure, early retrieval, failure, escape, interruption, transfer or abandonment never refunds it. Retained residue has real mass but no fresh use. The same empty wrapper remains an ordinary object with no source-value recovery; reuse for a lawful new dose supplies no free dose. Rewrapping an unused dose preserves age. Destroyed/spoiled doses cannot expose.

A completed dry, properly wrapped dose reaches Spoiled directly after 24 accumulated unprotected hours from completion, losing its Fishing effect and ordinary finished-dose reference value. Opened but unexposed doses retain the same age. Only an actually compatible existing preservation method protects storage; no new stasis is granted. A receipt validly made before storage spoilage has its independent 60-minute life. No fractional dose works. Ordinary angling bait is a separate 0.10 lb/1 cp portion with its real prey compatibility and material spoilage. A consumed recipe ingredient marked bait is neither this portion nor a finished Bait dose.
