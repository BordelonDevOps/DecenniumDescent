# GPT — Divination cantrip exact manuscript review

One required wording repair remains. Everything else is approved as authored, including the Far Echo capture predicate, Protection-from-Scrying interfaces, exact-RP reading, weather accountability, and protected-information handling.

## 1. Foresight Flicker — approved

The card correctly preserves **self-only** despite raw 30-ft metadata.

Approved eligibility:

- attack roll targeting the caster;
- hostile opposed/task d20 directly against the caster;
- not a saving throw caused by the caster;
- consume before the roll;
- advantage/disadvantage cancels normally;
- one actual multi-subject roll is modified once for its entire resolution.

Its Divination classification does not make Protection from Scrying immunity to the roll modifier because Flicker is not obtaining new information about that protected attacker/target.

No repair.

## 2. Omen Whisper — one required narrowing repair

The **party-wide materially-equivalent-inquiry anti-fishing rule is approved**. Switching casters should not turn one cantrip into an unlimited interrogation engine.

However, this current sentence is broader than necessary:

> “Recasting about materially the same subject or action while circumstances remain materially unchanged…”

That can read as preventing genuinely different questions about the same object/location/action.

Replace that portion with:

> **Recasting a materially equivalent inquiry about the same subject or proposed action while the materially relevant circumstances remain unchanged gives the same omen or a restatement, without cumulative extra detail. This equivalence applies across casters: rephrasing the inquiry, passing it to another caster, or subdividing it solely to extract additional detail about the same issue does not create new information. A genuinely different material question about that subject, or a material change in circumstances, may produce a different omen.**

Keep the remainder.

This preserves the desired anti-fishing rule without making “I asked one question about this tomb” exhaust every distinct question about the tomb.

No daily cap is added.

## 3. Far Echo — approved, including the exact capture predicate

Confirm the candidate's bounded interpretation:

> speech must have been **produced inside the chosen 5-ft-radius sphere AND audible at its center** during the six elapsed seconds immediately before cast start.

That is a legitimate exact completion of “speech in location” and is more deterministic than allowing every distant voice merely audible somewhere within the region.

Also approved:

- own V component is outside the historical sample;
- original timing/language/approximate voice/relative volume preserved;
- overlapping speech remains overlapping;
- no translation/speaker authentication/truth determination;
- replay lasts no more than six seconds;
- Far Echo replay cannot recursively become Far Echo source material.

The Protection from Scrying interaction is also correct. A protection active when Far Echo attempts retrieval can withhold **new protected-subject information**. It does **not**:

- erase the historical speech event;
- destroy ordinary recordings;
- make witnesses forget;
- retroactively create silence;
- erase information already known.

No repair.

## 4. Soul Mark — approved

The card properly separates the **mark's existence** from the information it provides.

Thus a marked creature protected by Protection from Scrying can remain marked while supplying no directional information. If that information protection later ends while Soul Mark itself remains active, directional sensing resumes without refreshing the Soul Mark duration.

Approved:

- no save;
- living creature;
- same-plane;
- ≤5,280 ft;
- horizontal/vertical bearing but not distance;
- cover after application does not block;
- disguise/shapechange keeps creature continuity;
- death ends this application;
- no corpse/soul/resurrection tracking;
- one active mark per caster;
- independent marks from different casters.

A blocked/out-of-range/off-plane result remains nondiagnostic.

No repair.

## 5. Sense Alignment — approved

The actual information promise remains meaningful.

Approved output:

- actual Director-held L/N/C axis;
- actual Director-held G/N/E axis;
- hidden-but-unprotected values are revealed;
- protected/undefined axis = **Indeterminate**;
- protection of one axis does not suppress the other automatically.

For objects, the order is correctly:

1. apply actual information protection;
2. inspect actual alignment record if permitted;
3. only then use ordinary-object **Unaligned** when there genuinely is no alignment record.

A blocked read is therefore never converted into false evidence of “Unaligned.”

No repair.

## 6. Glimpse Future — approved

The two effects are correctly separated:

### Narrative glimpse
A qualitative six-second current-circumstances projection. Applicable Divination protection may suppress protected information within it.

### Mechanical initiative benefit
Advantage on the caster's **next actual new initiative roll within one minute**.

That mechanical benefit is not itself new information and therefore is not canceled merely because some glimpse information is protected.

Approved boundaries:

- no reroll of current encounter initiative;
- one pending benefit;
- consumption even when advantage already exists;
- no “declare another combat” resource refresh;
- no future dice transcript;
- no party-wide warning feature.

No repair.

## 7. Detect Faction — approved

The three-result procedure is correct:

- **Affiliated** — explicit actual affiliation/membership;
- **Not Affiliated** — explicit actual nonaffiliation;
- **Indeterminate** — no dispositive record or protected information.

Crucially, **absence of a field is not Not Affiliated**.

Hidden actual affiliation is legitimate Divination information unless specifically protected.

Object affiliation likewise requires a real institutional issuance/affiliation record; symbols, ownership, creator affiliation, or appearance do not create one.

No repair.

## 8. Resonance Sight — approved

The source promise is correctly preserved as actual RP information rather than weakened into “magic exists.”

Approved:

- self observer;
- same plane;
- 30 ft;
- LoE;
- ordinary visibility required;
- exact **current RP amount**;
- live updates during the one-round window;
- actual 0-RP pool distinguishable from a readable positive pool;
- undefined pool is not fabricated as zero.

A protected pool supplies no readable aura, but **no readable aura is nondiagnostic** because it can also mean no RP pool exists.

The colors are a private intuitive number encoding, not a new universal lore taxonomy.

No repair.

## 9. Read Surface Thoughts — approved

The normal WIS save remains mandatory unless a separate exact rule permits something else.

On failure, one immediate conscious surface thought only:

- phrase;
- image;
- emotion;
- sensory impression;
- simple idea.

Approved limitations:

- unknown verbal language stays untranslated;
- images/emotions remain nonverbal rather than magically converted into precise propositions;
- no memories;
- no truth compulsion;
- no guaranteed future intent;
- no automatic dreams;
- no species-based assumption of sentience/thought;
- no automatic notification beyond ordinary perception or an actual defense;
- successful save grants no future immunity.

A fresh cast can read a genuinely changed current thought after a fresh save and ordinary Refrain.

No repair.

## 10. Detect Weather — approved

The exact geographic completion is workable:

> **1-mile horizontal local outdoor region centered on the selected geographic point.**

An indoor/underground point can therefore legitimately refer to the outdoor weather above/around that same geographic location without pretending the spell's initial targeting pierced a wall.

Approved forecast contents include material local variation in:

- temperature;
- precipitation/timing;
- wind;
- weather visibility;
- significant weather hazards.

The forecast must remain accurate for the recorded existing weather trajectory. The Director cannot simply reroll unchanged weather later.

A later **actual new cause** may change conditions; that cause should exist in the fiction/game record even if players are not informed what caused the divergence.

Protection from Scrying does not make the ambient regional weather itself unavailable simply because a protected creature occupies the area, though protected information specifically about that creature remains protected.

No repair.

## 11. Sense Corruption — approved

This correctly remains a useful binary detector:

- Self;
- 30-ft radius;
- same plane;
- LoE;
- current canonical Corruption record/tag/state;
- **Yes/No** only.

Actual hidden but unprotected Corruption produces **Yes**.

A protected Corruption source may be excluded from the readable set. Therefore:

> **No = no permitted positive presence detected**

not proof that no masked Corruption exists.

Missing/undefined records do not become Corruption merely to answer the spell.

No direction/count/type/severity/numeric value/cure or other information is added.

No repair.
