### 1. Augury

**Action; Self; V/S/F; instantaneous.** State one **specific course of action you intend to begin within the next minute** and one concrete goal or interest against which its result should be judged. The goal may concern you, your party, or another specifically identified beneficiary.

The Director evaluates the most likely material consequences of beginning that course under **current circumstances during the following 30 minutes** and answers exactly:

- **Yes** — the course is more likely than not to materially advance the stated goal.
- **No** — it is more likely than not to materially harm, frustrate, or move away from that goal.
- **Unclear** — materially favorable and unfavorable outcomes are genuinely balanced/mixed, the course is too contingent to favor either result, or required information is undefined/protected.

“Good/bad” is therefore relative to the stated practical goal, **not moral alignment or Director preference**. A dangerous action that advances the stated objective can be Yes.

This predicts from current circumstances rather than fixing destiny. Later voluntary choices, new events, or die results can change the outcome. The Director should not use Unclear merely because uncertainty exists; if current evidence materially favors one side, answer Yes/No.

Materially equivalent course/goal inquiries under unchanged circumstances return the same answer across casters and rephrasing; genuinely different courses/goals or materially changed circumstances may yield different answers. No daily cap beyond normal casting costs/cadence.

**NEW:** Self informational mode, begin-within-1-minute requirement, 30-minute evaluation horizon, goal-relative Yes/No/Unclear procedure.

### 2. Divine Perception

**Action; 30 ft; one creature or object perceived with LoE; V/S/F; instantaneous.** No save.

Determine whether the target is currently subject to an **actual ongoing influence whose provenance is established as divine**. Report one or more actual categories when known:

- **Blessed**
- **Cursed**
- **Possessed**
- **Other Divine Influence**

If no qualifying divine influence exists and that fact can be established, report **No Divine Influence**.

The parenthetical states are categories, not automatic proof of divine provenance. Thus:

- an actual curse with explicitly divine source can report Cursed;
- a curse with established nondivine source does **not** qualify;
- a blessing/curse/possession with undefined provenance reports **Indeterminate** rather than being assumed divine or nondivine.

Established Director fiction can supply divine provenance even where no software tag yet exists. Conversely, Cleric spellcasting, religious symbols, Evil alignment, Corrupted, faction faith, or an Oracle intrinsic Curse do not automatically establish divine influence.

The spell reveals category/provenance status only. It does not identify a deity's secret identity, possession controller statistics/location, cure, exorcism method, command authority, theology, or hidden motive unless another actual record expressly supplies that as part of the detected influence.

**NEW:** creature/object target, category/provenance output and independent Indeterminate handling.

### 3. Clairvoyance

**Action; functional range 1 mile; V/S/F; 1 minute; no concentration.** Choose **Sight or Hearing** when casting; the mode cannot switch during this application.

Choose one fixed point on your plane within **5,280 ft** that you can uniquely designate through at least one of:

- direct perception;
- prior personal knowledge;
- a reliable map/description;
- a precise relative position from a known reference.

The source does not require the location to be previously visited, so a uniquely specified unseen point can qualify; “some hidden room in that building” does not.

No originating LoE is required to the remote point. It cannot be inside solid matter.

**Sight:** perceive from the fixed point in all directions using ordinary local lighting, visibility, cover and obscurity. This grants no darkvision, true sight, wall vision, or borrowed creature senses.

**Hearing:** hear from the fixed point under ordinary local sound rules.

Your normal local senses continue simultaneously. Blindness suppresses remote Sight; Deafened suppresses remote Hearing. Maintain same-plane/1-mile tether; leaving it suppresses the remote feed without ending/pausing duration, and returning restores current information only.

The point cannot move, follow, or search. It is a nonphysical ongoing magical effect with no HP. Actual effects that can detect/dispel such magic may affect it under their own permissions. It supplies no remote targeting, attacks, spellcasting origin, or LoE. Subject-specific Divination protection removes only the protected information from the feed when applicable.

**CONFLICT RULING:** explicit one-mile promise controls over raw 30-ft metadata.  
**NEW:** one-minute/no-concentration duration, fixed point, one-mode choice and designation rules.

### 4. Vision Trap

**Action; 30 ft; one creature perceived with LoE that you currently treat as an enemy from ordinary information; V/S/F; instantaneous damage plus pending rider.**

Target makes an **INT save**.

- **Failure:** take **2d6 psychic damage** and gain disadvantage on the **next attack roll** made before the end of its first future regular turn.
- **Success:** take `floor(2d6/2)` psychic damage and gain no disorientation rider.

Roll damage once.

The pending disadvantage is consumed on the first qualifying attack roll even if advantage cancels it or another effect already imposes disadvantage. If no attack occurs before the endpoint, it expires.

Damage and rider are separate: psychic resistance/immunity can reduce/prevent damage without automatically removing a rider earned from the failed save unless the applicable defense says otherwise.

One pending Vision Trap disorientation per target across all casters; a successful later application replaces that pending rider rather than stacking disadvantage, while its instantaneous damage still resolves normally.

This does **not** apply Dazed, Stunned, Incapacitated, Frightened, forced movement, command behavior, lasting false memory, or an arbitrary false message. Blindness or nonbiological creature type alone does not grant immunity; actual mental/illusion/psychic defenses apply only to their written scope. Protection from Scrying does not block this noninformational effect merely because the spell is Divination.

**NEW:** INT save, half-on-success damage, next-attack disadvantage and hostile-target interpretation.

### 5. Locate Person

**Action; Self; V/S/F; instantaneous bearing.** Designate one **specific individual person** whom you can identify unambiguously through personal acquaintance or a reliable identity description/name that resolves to one actual individual.

“Person” is an individual-identity predicate, not automatically Humanoid or biologically living. An active Undead or Construct may qualify when the campaign treats it as that continuing person. A corpse/object does not qualify merely because it belonged to a person.

If that individual currently exists as an eligible creature on your plane within **1 mile (5,280 ft)** and the information is not blocked, privately learn the direction from you to them, including meaningful vertical bearing.

You learn **direction only**, not distance, room, route, appearance, condition, companions, or precise coordinates. Ordinary walls/cover do not block the bearing.

If the designation is actually ambiguous, or the person is dead/destroyed, beyond one mile, on another plane, or protected, you receive **no direction** and cannot distinguish which reason applies. Shapechange/disguise retaining the same continuing identity does not defeat the designation.

This is an **instantaneous snapshot**, not a sustained tracker; target movement after resolution is not updated.

**CONFLICT RULING:** one-mile effect promise supersedes raw 30-ft range.  
**NEW:** person-identity predicate, identification requirement, instantaneous-bearing interpretation.

### 6. Faction Spy

**Action; V/S/F; 1 minute; no concentration.** Choose one exact canonical faction and **one specific headquarters belonging to that faction**.

No reputation, membership, Faction Seal permission, or special infrastructure is required.

The HQ must be specifically identifiable in established fiction. The caster need not know its street coordinates if they can uniquely identify it institutionally—for example “the Crimson Guild's established central headquarters in Haven.” The spell cannot discover an unknown/secret headquarters from only a faction name.

Functional reach is **any distance on the same plane**. No interplanar observation is granted.

Choose either:

- one **specific named room/common area** of that HQ the caster knows; or
- if none is specified, the HQ's established **primary common/operations area**. If the setting has not yet distinguished such an area, use the ordinary main interior space nearest the primary public/working entrance rather than a secret vault.

Create one fixed nonphysical observational point there.

For one minute, the caster receives **ordinary sight and hearing** from that point in addition to local senses. Sight uses ordinary local lighting/obscurity/walls; hearing uses ordinary sound conditions. Blindness suppresses remote sight and Deafened suppresses remote hearing. The point does not move/pan/search through rooms and cannot be placed inside solid matter.

Subject/area-specific Divination protections can remove protected information without blanking unrelated HQ activity. If the selected HQ does not exist, no longer belongs to that faction, or is otherwise not the identified HQ at resolution, the spell yields no feed. The point is fixed to the selected physical room/site for this casting; a later institutional relocation does not move it. An actually mobile structure carries its own room normally.

No attacks, casting, LoE, remote interaction, recording object, credentials, reputation change, or entry permission.

**NEW:** any-distance same-plane reach, fixed HQ viewpoint procedure, combined ordinary sight/hearing, no-concentration duration.

### 7. Alignment Prediction

**Action; 30 ft; one creature perceived with LoE; V/S/F; instantaneous.** No save.

Specify one **concrete imminent situation, offer, confrontation, or decision** that the creature is reasonably expected to encounter within the **next minute**.

The Director privately reports the creature's **single most likely voluntary response** under current circumstances, using its actual individual state—including its actual alignment where relevant—rather than generic alignment stereotypes.

Examples of valid output are behavioral forecasts such as “likely refuses,” “likely cooperates cautiously,” “likely attacks,” “likely attempts to bargain,” or another comparably specific response.

Alignment informs the forecast but **does not compel it**. Individual motives, relationships, current danger, knowledge, and other actual factors can outweigh an alignment tendency.

The spell does **not separately disclose the target's exact alignment axes**, full motives, thoughts, or future die results. If relevant facts are genuinely undefined/protected or multiple responses are materially coequal, report **Indeterminate**.

Future free choices or materially changed circumstances can invalidate the forecast without making the original answer false. Materially equivalent scenarios under unchanged circumstances return the same forecast rather than sampling alternative futures; genuinely different scenarios may be asked normally.

**NEW:** one-minute forecast horizon, specified-situation input, likely-response/Indeterminate output.

### 8. Resonance Tracking

**Action; initial search radius 30 ft; V/S/F; concentration up to 10 minutes.**

This spell tracks **residual provenance of an actual spell cast**, not the caster's later footsteps.

At cast resolution, select one qualifying spell-casting trace that:

- originated, resolved, or left an actual source-defined spatial effect/path within **30 ft and LoE** of you; and
- began no more than **3,600 elapsed seconds ago**.

If you can already distinguish a specific known casting event, select it. Otherwise the Director selects the **most recent qualifying unprotected cast trace** in the initial search area. Recasting under materially unchanged conditions does not rotate through older hidden traces merely to enumerate them.

A qualifying event is an actual **spell cast**. A class technique, magic-item activation, copied effect, or other magical event does not qualify unless its source explicitly performs/counts as a spell cast. A Counterspelled spell still had an actual cast start and can leave its **casting-origin trace**, but produces no resolved-effect path that never occurred.

For the duration, you privately perceive the selected casting's residual resonance when within **30 ft and LoE of a surviving trace segment**:

- its exact **casting-origin point**, when locally detectable;
- any actual source-defined spatial route/path the spell's magic genuinely traversed;
- actual resolved area/endpoint footprints that belong to that casting.

The spell does **not fabricate a connecting trail** where the original spell had no spatial traversal. Thus a self/instantaneous spell can still leave a useful origin trace, while a teleport/remote effect with no traversed path does not gain an invented line through intervening space.

Following the trace uses ordinary movement/navigation. At forks, the trace follows only the actual spell path; there is no additional route oracle. Ordinary visual concealment does not hide the magical residue, but solid total cover blocks sensing of a segment until you regain LoE.

If an actual spell path crossed a portal/planar discontinuity, the locally visible trace can lead to that transition point; this spell does not automatically continue sensing onto another plane.

Every residual component expires at the **original cast's timestamp +3,600 seconds**. Starting Resonance Tracking, recasting it, suppression, copies, or changing observers never refreshes that age. If all selected trace evidence ages out, the spell provides no further trail.

It reveals no spell name, rank, caster identity, caster's current location, RP, hidden statistics, or residue resource. A protected subject's identifying information remains protected even where an unprotected environmental trace is otherwise visible.

**CONFLICT RULING:** Instantaneous metadata cannot support “follow”; NEW concentration up to 10 minutes supplies the finite tracking procedure.  
**NEW:** trace-selection rule, one-hour residue age, casting-origin/path/endpoint model and no-fabricated-path boundary.
