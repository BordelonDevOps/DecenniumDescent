## What It Is

This Green Court agent embodies appetite, envy, and possession. Its apparent color is a jurisdictional mark, not decoration, and the seal it carries determines which promises, names, and boundaries it can turn into force.

## What Experienced Expeditions Notice

Wax with no heat source; footprints stopping at written boundaries; gifts arranged in court colors; a witness unable to describe what was promised but certain that it was fair. Heat appears in sealed metal before open flame.

## How It Fights

It opens with **Hellfire Claw** against the target most capable of controlling its movement. It uses **Soul Burn** to punish anyone who tries to stabilize that target. It repeatedly tests for careless speech and marks the first character who accepts a benefit or answers on behalf of another.

## How to Defeat It

**Prepare Before Contact**

- Prepare resistance or mitigation for fire damage.
- Appoint one speaker and forbid casual promises.

**Combat Priority**

Invalidate the court seal or brand before committing to damage.

**Exploit**

The defining weakness is **invalidated oath or court seal**. Treat this as an encounter solution rather than an automatic damage vulnerability. Successful Research, Recon, or Resource actions should make the exploit easier or safer.

**Withdraw When**

Withdraw or change the objective when its full multiattack reaches the support line; resources fall below half before the arena mechanic is solved. A failed retreat against this creature usually costs more than abandoning the immediate treasure.

## Habitat, Goals, and Lair

Court chambers use marked thresholds, braziers, oath circles, and audience galleries. Position often determines which creature is considered witness, petitioner, debtor, or trespasser.

The Pact Courts are fiendish legal cultures organized by the kind of obligation they weaponize. Their colors are not moral categories. They are schools of coercion, memory, appetite, secrecy, and authority.

Pact devils prefer enforceable victories. They may withdraw from a profitable fight to preserve precedent.
