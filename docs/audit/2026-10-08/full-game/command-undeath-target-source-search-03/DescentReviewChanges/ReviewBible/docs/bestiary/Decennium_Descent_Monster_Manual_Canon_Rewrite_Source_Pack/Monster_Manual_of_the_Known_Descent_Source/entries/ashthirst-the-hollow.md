## What It Is

Ashthirst the Hollow is the Gate Guardian for difference preserved against consuming heat. It is an arena-scale adversary whose body, Seal, and terrain operate as one examination. Reducing it to zero hit points is necessary, but an Expedition that ignores its Gate principle can still lose the passage, the reward, or members of the team.

## What Experienced Expeditions Notice

Architecture becomes ceremonial; lesser monsters stop crossing a boundary; previous Expeditions leave preparation caches rather than camps; repeated symbols appear in terrain and wounds. Different witnesses remember the same sound differently.

### Traits

**Gate-Bound.** The Guardian cannot be permanently displaced from its arena while the Seal remains active.

**Aerial Hunter.** It ignores ground difficult terrain and seeks angles that bypass front-line cover.

**Escalation Pulse.** **Hollow Wail** recharges on 5-6; visible signs appear one round before the first use.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The creature makes 2 attacks using the actions below.

**Ash Claw.** *Attack:* +9 to hit, one target. *Hit:* 3d8+7 necrotic damage (average 20).

**Cinder Lash.** *Attack:* +9 to hit, one target. *Hit:* 2d10+5 fire damage (average 16).

**Hollow Wail (Recharge 5-6).** Creatures in the affected area make a DC 18 Wisdom save, taking 6d8 psychic damage (average 27) on a failure and half on a success. The Director should describe the charge-up before resolving it.

## How It Fights

It opens with **Ash Claw** against the target most capable of controlling its movement. It uses **Cinder Lash** to punish anyone who tries to stabilize that target. When **Hollow Wail** recharges, it delays only if it can catch a more important cluster or interrupt a decisive ritual. It escalates by phase and uses the Seal Pulse to punish parties that solve damage but ignore the test.

## How to Defeat It

**Prepare Before Contact**

- Prepare resistance or mitigation for necrotic, fire damage.
- Carry ranged attacks, grounding tools, or vertical cover.
- Spread before **Hollow Wail** becomes available.

**Combat Priority**

Complete the arena objective while maintaining damage pressure.

**Exploit**

The defining weakness is **None identified**. Treat this as an encounter solution rather than an automatic damage vulnerability. Successful Research, Recon, or Resource actions should make the exploit easier or safer.

**Withdraw When**

Withdraw or change the objective when Hollow Wail catches three or more Expedition members; resources fall below half before the arena mechanic is solved. A failed retreat against this creature usually costs more than abandoning the immediate treasure.

## Habitat, Goals, and Lair

A Gate arena is part of the creature. Terrain, Seal Pulses, objectives, and failure conditions matter as much as hit points.

Gate Guardians are not random apex monsters. Each is a living examination tied to a Division of the dungeon, a Gate Seal, and a principle the Expedition must demonstrate under pressure.

Some Guardians speak, bargain, or test. None are safely reduced to ordinary patrons or quest-givers.
