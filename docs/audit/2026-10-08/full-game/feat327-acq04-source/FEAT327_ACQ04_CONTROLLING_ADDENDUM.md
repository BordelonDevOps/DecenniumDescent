## ACQ04-EC1 — minimal evidence record

Existing record fields may be referenced rather than copied. A `boundRef` resolves immutable bytes by `path`, `sha256` and exact `pointer` (or a precise text section for a textual record); existing versioned-record locators can be adapted losslessly. Resolving a hash/field proves identity of the cited content, not its truth, authority or fulfillment. Do not create self-referential/cyclic hashes.

### evidenceMode

All supporting records must remain in this evidence domain. A test fixture cannot be relabeled or mixed into a campaign acquisition.

### worldId

Stable world identity matching acquisition, actor, evidence and authority. The referenced world and its record-authority configuration must resolve; a string alone is not proof.

### characterId

Stable actor identity matching the acquisition snapshot and the subject of every supporting grant/state/event.

### acquisitionRef

Resolve an immutable acquisition/snapshot record with this character/world, exact feat ID, authoritative elapsed-time-plus-sequence order, permanent abilities/class levels/parent ownership as applicable, mandatory choices and a source-qualified independently payable opportunity. Eligibility checking does not spend that opportunity or create a feat grant.

### predicate

### credentialRefs

Resolve actual supporting grants, state/event records, source clauses and their subject identity/establishment or occurrence order. Branch-specific facts below must be evidenced by their content, not merely by attachment presence. Existing records may be reused; duplicate storage is not required.

### authorityRef

Resolve the existing world/character record authority and its actual scope to establish/admit the particular credential or event at the relevant time. No new witness, authentication service, religious adjudication or owner-confirmation gate is imposed. authorized:true is not a substitute for this binding.

### Results and chronology

Worship must qualify at the snapshot; exposure remains a past-event predicate. Later assertions cannot fabricate an earlier grant/event. Legitimate source corrections preserve predecessors and their authority rather than silently rebasing history. No additional minimum worship/exposure duration is created.

**CONDITIONAL_ONLY**: Literal predicate satisfied under explicitly conditional mock premises; never a campaign acquisition or positive production credential.

**UNESTABLISHED**: Required evidence missing, unbound, mismatched or insufficient; does not qualify, without asserting the event never happened.

**CONTRADICTED**: Resolved evidence affirmatively fails this predicate, e.g. actual Mage supplied as Sorcerer.

**ESTABLISHED_FOR_SNAPSHOT**: Only independently admitted campaign records can establish the external predicate for that snapshot. No such positive record is authenticated or supplied by this review; other acquisition gates remain separate.

All mock world, authority, deity/plane and imported-source references must remain explicitly conditional and opaque. Their predicate assertions exercise contract logic, not hidden-setting meanings. Unknown/missing source evidence is not silently converted into absence of a historical event or into success. An actual-world authority is the already-governed record authority; this contract neither defines host permissions nor adds a new consent/authentication service.
