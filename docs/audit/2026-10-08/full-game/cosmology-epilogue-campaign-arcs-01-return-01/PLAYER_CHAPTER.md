# Decennium Descent — EPI-15–17 Campaign Arcs
## Player Chapter — NEW Provisional Authored Playtest Reference

### Scope
These three adventures are optional continuations **after the epilogue**. This chapter does not choose the campaign's unresolved era, an AE year, a numbered Dungeon floor, or any unresolved cosmological identity. A Director must record the actual campaign placement before activating an arc.

All ordinary character, class, life/death, carrying, language, spell, relic, Null, inventory, rest, and clock rules continue to operate. Nothing in these arcs grants a new class feature, recipe, research qualification, planar authority, or automatic relic refresh unless the adventure explicitly says so.

## Shared table rules
- Time is continuous. Outside initiative, an ordinary Action occupies 6 elapsed seconds when exact timing matters.
- Pounds and **Load** are checked independently. Containers do not erase the weight/Load of contents.
- Physical stock is finite. Used, stolen, destroyed, abandoned, or transferred objects do not return because a session changes.
- Payments move actual coins from named pools. A claim can remain unpaid if the money is missing.
- Withdrawal is always a real option unless the current physical scene itself prevents it.
- Player consent boundaries remain in force. No real private belief, confession, or emotionally significant song description is required; neutral fictional labels are acceptable.

# EPI-15 — Rekindling Stars

## Mission
One of the new constellations above post-epilogue Yieldheart **flickers out nightly**. Three installed devices contain matching stray Null fragments that are shaping star energy into weaponized silence. The municipal offer is simple: recover the three serialed slivers, stabilize them at Lantern Court, and do so before the announced Equinox dawn.

At activation the Director announces the exact local Equinox dawn timestamp **E**. The mission begins at **E−72 hours**. This is a local deadline for this adventure, not a recovered universal astronomical date.

### Known sites
1. **North Observatory** — 0.5 mile from Lantern Court. A cracked three-ring lens bears the warning: **OPEN THE VENT BEFORE TURNING THE INNER RING.**
2. **River Bell House** — 0.5 mile from Lantern Court. The visible counterweight notice reads: **SUPPORT 3, RELEASE 1, THEN LIFT 2.**
3. **West Glass Garden** — 0.5 mile from Lantern Court. The cage controls are marked **SHADE / REFLECT / OPEN**, and the gardener's note says: **SHADE RELEASES THE LATCH.**

The North Observatory's 10-foot acoustic zone suppresses sound production and transmission. Audible verbal spell components cannot be produced inside it. It does **not** automatically cancel nonverbal magic or effects already running.

None of these three sites is a Residual Null exposure field. Merely standing near or carrying a recovered fragment does not start an hourly Null exposure save.

## Stabilizing a recovered fragment
Lantern Court has one fixed housing with sockets S1/S2/S3.

For each fragment:
1. Fit the actual sliver into its matching socket — **Action**.
2. Close the shutter — **Action**.
3. Turn the crank and maintain the stabilization for **10 uninterrupted elapsed minutes**.

The 10-minute interval is observation/stabilization, not Transition-eligible work. Interruption restarts that 10-minute interval but does not recreate or remove the installed fragment.

A fragment completing stabilization **exactly at E** counts as sealed before the deadline.

## Equinox consequences
- **3 sealed at E:** the constellation returns that night.
- **1–2 sealed at E:** it remains dim for **40 days**, then recovers naturally.
- **0 sealed at E:** it darkens for **one 400-day Aethel civil year**.
- Late recovery can still stop the remaining local machines and prevent further local injuries, but cannot retroactively change the already resolved Equinox result.

A recovered or powered-off site does not restart merely because its fragment was still unsealed at E.

## Payment
Lantern Court begins with exactly **150 gp** in the municipal bounty chest. Orra Sen pays **50 gp once per original serial** after the actual fragment is sealed and surrendered. A late sealed fragment can still earn its one payment.

Dera Venn separately begins with exactly **6 gp** for spindle scrap and offers **2 gp once** for each of the three original, disconnected, inactive 10-lb spindle serials if the physical body survives. Her purse and the bounty chest are separate funds.

# EPI-16 — Conclave of Untitled Songs

## Hook
Bards across Aethel are forgetting mundane melodies at random. The source is an **errant equation echoing from a Broken Ladder strand in the upper atmosphere**. This local errant equation is not the lost cosmic Equation.

The Conclave offers one expedition aboard its single sky-vessel, **Margin Kite**, to retrieve the strand.

## What melody loss does
Tracked bards lose only one recorded mundane melody at a scheduled event. They do not lose class cards, spell repertoire, languages, instrument proficiency, or unrelated memories.

A lost tune can be relearned with **10 uninterrupted active minutes** using an actual surviving score, recording, or knowledgeable willing teacher. That training is not halved by Transition.

The phenomenon ends for future losses when the strand is actually detached. Earlier lost tunes still need real relearning.

## Margin Kite — player-known operating limits
- Dry hull: **3,000 lb**, AC 15, 120 HP.
- Payload: **1,600 lb AND 160 Load**, checked independently.
- Maximum: **6 breathing occupants**; no-breath occupants still require legal footprint, mass, Load, and clearance.
- Powered speed: **10 miles/hour**.
- Fuel: **600 powered minutes** at departure.
- Cabin air: **12 elapsed hours** from activation.
- Six one-use breathhoods: **60 elapsed minutes each**.
- Four seal patchpads.
- Three 50-foot harness/line assemblies, 600-lb supported-load rating each.
- Three 2-lb clamps, two 1-lb insulated tongs, one 4-lb lined case with 8-lb capacity.
- One finite hull-repair lot and one finite canopy-repair lot.

Two distinct trained operators are required for powered tactical control: a pilot and engineer. One actor cannot fill both roles in the same round. Nima Voss and Pell Rusk are the Conclave's initial crew.

### Fuel and air are different clocks
Every powered second spends fuel. Fuel at zero removes powered lift.

The air cartridge runs continuously through engine-off mooring, empty-cabin time, airlock cycling, and breaches. Closing a door does not refill it.

## Upper-atmosphere warning
Above 10,000 feet, an unprotected **breathing** creature faces this local nonmagical inhalation hazard. At every completed **60 unprotected seconds**, make one **CON save DC 15**:
- failure: **3d6 cold** plus **1 Exhaustion** from thin-air fatigue;
- success: half cold, no new Exhaustion.

Actual no-breath traits and working cabin/airlock/hood protection prevent this specific inhalation event. Other real ancestry/class defenses retain only their printed or announced local interaction. A full uninterrupted protected minute clears only an incomplete exposure remainder; it does not heal damage or remove Exhaustion.

Before leaving protection, ask the Director how your exact ancestry/class effect interfaces with the two components. The complete reviewed local dispositions are in the Director chapter.

## Airlock
The vessel has one inner and one outer door. Each opening/closing is an Action. The normal outside cycle is:

**open inner → enter airlock → close inner → open outer → exit → close outer**

To return:

**open outer → enter → close outer → purge 60 seconds → open inner → enter cabin → close inner**

The airlock becomes protected only after a legal uninterrupted 60-second purge with both intact doors closed, a working hull seal, and running air cartridge.

## Route and baseline plan
Outbound:
- climb 30 miles vertically: **3 hours powered**;
- travel 20 horizontal miles: **2 hours powered**.

A fully powered return would consume the remaining five hours and leave no powered time for the worksite. The disclosed ordinary plan uses powered horizontal return followed by the gravity canopy.

With the stated sample manifest, no delay, no Transition benefit, two hoods for the outside worker, and powered idle while moored:
- complete voyage: **32,100 seconds = 8 h 55 m**;
- powered time: **482 m 12 s**;
- fuel remaining: **117 m 48 s**;
- cabin air remaining: **185 m = 3 h 5 m**.

A known weather current on the outbound horizontal leg can instead be avoided with a **30 powered-minute detour**. Taking it changes the sample to 9 h 25 m elapsed, 512 m 12 s powered, 87 m 48 s fuel remaining, and 2 h 35 m air remaining.

These are worked plans, not permission to ignore actual body, Load, damage, crew, or delay records.

## Strand site
The shared dial order is **3–1–4**. The operation has three phases:
1. **Alignment:** 20 elapsed minutes. Mooring and the three dial Actions must be completed inside the interval. A wrong order causes one 3d6 psychic pulse, WIS 15 half, and invalidates that alignment interval.
2. **Clamp work:** 10 active minutes using all three actual clamps.
3. **Release:** 30 active minutes using the actual insulated tongs and lined case.

After release, recover the three real clamps, unclip, cycle the airlock, secure the actual case, and unmoor.

### Transition
If a character actually has a lawful existing **Transition** benefit fixed at preflight to this exact Phase2+3 salvage work order, the same qualified worker's active Phase2 and Phase3 clocks become **5 minutes and 15 minutes**. Alignment, travel, study, Actions, door cycles, airlock purge, hood duration, and waits are not halved.

Under the same otherwise ordinary sample, that optional route takes **30,894 seconds = 8 h 34 m 54 s**, uses **462 m 06 s** powered time, leaves **137 m 54 s** fuel and **205 m 06 s** cabin air, and needs only one hood.

## Canopy return
A trained pilot uses one Action to deploy the fixed gravity canopy while hull HP remains above zero. The deployment itself consumes six powered seconds in the normal plan. After completion:
- first 300-foot descent step: +6 seconds;
- another 300-foot step every 6 seconds;
- pilot may set up to 30 feet lateral movement for the next step;
- repeated deployment does not reset the descent clock.

At the stated 30-mile height, 528 steps take **3,168 seconds = 52 m 48 s** after deployment.

## Wages and reward
Aren Hale controls two separate Conclave chests:
- **200 gp** mission pool: paid once for delivery of the one detached strand.
- **40 gp** wage pool: 10 gp each to Nima and Pell per actual sortie that takes off.

A canceled departure before takeoff earns no wage. Abort after takeoff still earns that sortie's shares. Two ordinary two-crew sorties exhaust the wage pool. An agreed advance moves the real coins aboard and therefore changes the manifest.

# EPI-17 — Devil's Signature

## The offer
A quartz contract appears in a public chamber of post-epilogue Crystalfall Citadel naming one willing player character as heir to three **NEW local CF-series Canian engine patents**. Veyr of the Second Margin is Mephistopheles' envoy for this Canian offer and a courier for the local patent office.

This does not prove ancestry, soul ownership, universal infernal authority, ownership of Cania, or control of the Frozen Engine.

If no player wants the named role, the offer may be staged to the neutral local claimant Lysa Mere instead. No player is required to accept.

## Concrete benefits of acceptance
An active claimant receives:
- the one reserved readable three-page CF plate set;
- recognition by this actual office as the named heir;
- first-priority booking for one 10-minute instructor-led proof-press service appointment per 24 elapsed hours while the term is active;
- permission to use the local proof press and its actually remaining finite office stock;
- retained technical knowledge of CF-1 isolation, CF-2 external cooling routing, and CF-3 keyed pressure-seal servicing.

Legitimate copies can be shared, but recipients do not inherit the annual obligation.

Ending the term ends title, appointment priority, and future office/bench permission. Legitimately retained paper and learned information remain.

## Starting the annual term
The term starts only when all three are complete:
1. the clauses are explained in a form the claimant actually understands;
2. the claimant signs or makes an understood witnessed mark;
3. the claimant transfers an actual **50 gp bond** into the real lockbox;
4. the claimant has actual access to the three readable plates.

Record that completion timestamp as **T0**. If any prerequisite is missing, the term has not begun.

## Annual contradiction record
Aethel's civil year is **400 elapsed 24-hour days**.

Record 1 opens at T0 and is due at T0+400 days. Record n opens at T0+(n−1)×400 days and is due at T0+n×400 days.

A qualifying annual statement identifies:
- two mutually incompatible fictional claims;
- one actually observed record supporting each;
- one concrete question that could reconcile them.

Honest uncertainty is allowed. A real-player secret or belief is never required.

A statement may be submitted after its record opens. Future unopened years cannot be prepaid.

### Delivery and review
Use the functioning panel slot or dictate to an actually available Veyr. Record the annual number, completion time, channel, contents, and custody.

Veyr reviews within 24 elapsed hours of actual receipt. A timely filing stays protected while legitimate review or notice is pending.

The first actual rejection notice for a record creates one **10-day correction interval**. Later rejections do not restart it. Early rejection never shortens the original annual due date.

If neither delivery channel can actually receive a filing at the deadline, a real timely documented attempt with the actual statement can protect that record. Each annual record needs its own statement/attempt.

## Ending participation
Before termination, the claimant is told whether the bond settlement is refund or forfeiture.

A refund requires:
- at least one accepted annual record; and
- every record whose **original due timestamp is at or before the exit time** to be accepted.

A record that has merely opened but is not yet due is not accelerated for exit.

If refund eligibility is not met, the claimant may remain active while completing the real record/review or may knowingly leave immediately and forfeit the original 50 gp bond.

Default ends the term and forfeits only that original bond. It creates no soul claim, curse, class change, forced Oath breach, or extra fee.

Actual true death also ends future obligations and applies the same bond-refund test. Being Downed, unconscious, or an Undead-ancestry character is not by itself true death.

## CF-pattern proof press
The public chamber contains one fixed proof press and finite stock:
- 6 numbered cold cells, 1 lb each;
- 2 replacement seals, 0.2 lb each;
- 1 hand tool, 2 lb;
- 1 fitted cracked teaching seal;
- 6 blank print sheets;
- ink for exactly 6 impressions;
- one separate reserved original three-page plate set for the actual claimant.

A cell is consumed only when an actual legal activation occurs. A dry reading, diagnosis, or routing demonstration does **not** spend a cell.

One successful three-page new copy set consumes three sheets and three ink impressions. At most two new complete sets can be printed from the starting press-paper/ink stock.

The plates teach local CF-pattern procedures; they are not universal engine compatibility.

## Refusal and the free-will scene
Refusing the patent term creates no annual obligation and no penalty. Veyr may then offer one separate 10-minute discussion about the **first newly printed complete three-plate copy set**.

He openly offers one **25 gp participation fee**, paid once for completing a choice and fictional reason. Declining the discussion is peaceful and earns no fee.

Two credible requests compete for the same scarce first set:

**Rella Tarn, public workshop archivist:** immediate attended public reading at her next eight-hour daytime shift. This maximizes access but accepts a greater risk of uncontrolled copying or theft.

**Garis Vell, guild safety examiner:** temporary custody for 24 elapsed hours to compare the plates against the real press and add a clearly attributed safety reading, then public access. This delays access but may improve warnings.

Choices:
- **ACCEPT** Veyr's recommendation: allocate the first set to Garis under that 24-hour promise.
- **REFUSE** his recommendation: allocate the first set to Rella for public reading.
- **REWRITE** the terms. The pre-agreed default compromise gives Rella custody at the next public reading while Garis receives the first 30 minutes there for shared inspection and may add an attributed note.

The ACCEPT discussion slip is **not** the patent contract. It creates no bond, title, or annual obligation.

If the physical copy set does not yet exist, allocation does not create it. The press must actually make it from remaining stock.
