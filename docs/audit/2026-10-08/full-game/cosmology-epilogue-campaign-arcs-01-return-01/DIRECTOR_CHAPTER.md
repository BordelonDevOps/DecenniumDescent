## Contract state machine
Aethel civil year = 34,560,000 s = 400 days.

For n>=1:
- openedAt = T0 + (n−1)×34,560,000.
- originalDueAt = T0 + n×34,560,000.

### Review and notice order
1. Apply prior life/availability facts at the timestamp.
2. Complete otherwise legal filings/corrections/notices finishing now.

Review may complete earlier than 24h, but never later under ordinary availability. A filing complete by its valid deadline remains timely while review/notice is pending.

`finalDeliveryDeadline = max(originalDueAt, firstActualRejectionNotice+10d, valid availability delivery deadlines)`.
