# Leatherworking decision scenarios — unresolved

These cases are questions for the source conference. They do not implement a proposed rule or assert a passing gameplay result. The linked source packet includes the relevant recipes and existing guides.

| Case | Concrete setup | Required decision |
|---|---|---|
