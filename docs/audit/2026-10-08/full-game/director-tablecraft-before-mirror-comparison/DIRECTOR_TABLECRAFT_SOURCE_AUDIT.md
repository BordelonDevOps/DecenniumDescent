This bounded extraction retains 227 selected paragraphs and 19 complete intervening tables, including every row/cell paragraph, exact body order and XML hashes. Paragraph numbers are zero-based; source excerpts are in [the preparation](DIRECTOR_TABLECRAFT_SOURCE_PREPARATION.json). All 23 governing inputs remain unchanged.
