# Combat proposal: counterexamples before implementation

I accept your bounded periodic-family disposition and am recording that scope only as REVIEWED / reference-VALIDATED. DD16 and the overall game remain open. Your combat proposal is preserved verbatim. Before I implement, these counterexamples need explicit decisions. Everything new below is proposed playtest integration, not a quotation from Core or V6.

1. **Movement expenditure, not merely distance.** Walk 10 ft in difficult terrain: spend 20 ft. Switching to Fly 30 and subtracting only distance 10 refunds 10 ft. Standing and squeezing have the same exploit. Propose one spent-movement-cost ledger across modes, with each mode's cap `(1 + legal Dash count this turn) * current Speed`, minus spent cost. Each legal Dash adds one Speed, not a once-only doubling. Confirm how Speed changes midturn work (spent cost never refunded), and whether Ready movement / Interpose / Press have separate movement allowances that still pay terrain costs. Speed 0 must prohibit their voluntary movement. Should an Interpose/Press grant 5 ft of movement allowance, or traverse one legal 5-ft step even when terrain costs more? Make this explicit.

2. **Pin and Silence the Caster need a real cost.** Once per turn plus no other maneuver is still an always-use free rider on an ordinary attack; that undermines paid class control. I propose each is declared before an attack with -2 to that attack roll, one attempt of each per regular turn, no other optional maneuver on that attack. This is a NEW accuracy tradeoff, not disadvantage (which could be erased by already having disadvantage). Is -2 adequate as an authored pilot, or would you instead replace/halve damage? Choose and explain. Pin multiple records must not accumulate -10s or create endless removal Actions; propose strongest/nonstacking state, each record own DC, one Action removes all ordinary Pins. Silence at zero post-mitigation damage still forces exactly one CON concentration save DC 10 if concentrating; if damage is positive use max(10, floor(damage/2)); no duplicate save for same hit. This is the one substantive benefit distinct from ordinary hit concentration.

3. **Start-turn death saves are missing from event order.** Closed HP rules require a death save at START of a regular turn, if alive/dying at 0. Where exactly do it, starting healing, start-turn damage, duration expiry and deferred Stable recovery occur? Choose deterministic ordering and test: dying at 0 with start-turn healing; Stable at 0 with start-turn damage; natural 20 then periodic damage; expired Decay with a due Stable-recovery deadline. Simultaneous beneficial/harmful effects need a fixed arbitration method rather than player-selected rescue order.

4. **Hide contradiction / task degrees.** You said passive Perception must be below Stealth, then said meet-DC succeeds. Use the closed Core four-degree task system including natural 1/20 degree shifts for Hide against each passive DC? Search against a recorded Stealth result is a static DC (meet succeeds subject to degree shifts), not a fresh opposed contest with status-quo ties? Please settle. Record natural die/total so one Hide roll may produce different degrees against different observers. Passive advantage/disadvantage: omit conversion unless sourced, or explicitly propose it. Search reveals location, not visual sight; does it end Hidden only for that observer? Cannot reuse a failed Hide to endlessly roll without changing circumstances/cost.

5. **Duration conversion currently ambiguous.** 'N future turns' may last N plus a fractional round; converting from last marker before application can end it prematurely. Give precise anchors for bare hostile target duration, self/ally buff/source aura, explicit next-turn endpoint, source death/removal, initiative reroll/reordering, and leaving/reentering initiative. Proposed solution: preserve a virtual original initiative clock until all extant marker-based effects finish (regular markers every six seconds); never reset or convert at a newly chosen slot. Application during target turn does not count its current end. Outside-initiative applications instead begin with an absolute N*6s deadline. We need one executable model, including initiative beginning before that deadline. Do existing explicit periodic profiles retain their own target clocks? Yes unless specifically superseded.

6. **Ready spell and pending limits.** All held spells require concentration, including ordinarily non-concentration spells (V6). At release, ongoing spell keeps concentration if required; otherwise hold concentration ends. Only Action-time casts may be Readied; not BA, Reaction or long rituals. Propose one pending Ready per creature: replacing loses earlier setup and held costs. Spell targets/area point selected at release, legal cast prerequisites/costs at preparation; recheck release legality. Counterspell opportunity occurs during actual cast at preparation, not a second casting at release. Confirm cast-trigger effects/Refrain/Dissonance occur once at preparation, no illegal banking of multiple held spells. Readied Attack gets ordinary attack count already agreed; a feature explicitly limited to own turn still does not work off-turn.

7. **Press / Interpose / Brace.** 'Once arm' conflicts with 'later hit replaces target': allow replacement only while unconsumed, never re-arm after use in the same regular-turn interval. Explicitly no attack required during extra turn to refresh it. Does Interpose allow staying still when already a legal adjacent protector, including Speed 0? NEW 'up to 5' differs from Core move 5: explain intended permission. Brace Hold Ground reducing falling can act like unearned hover; propose discrete push/pull displacement only, not gravity/fall markers or continuous travel, no damage mitigation. Intercept still needs legal weapon attack/reach. Rally selects one source-qualified Frightened record before DC, not all sources at once.

8. **Extra turns and stance budgets.** Let stance selection and replenishment occur only on regular own turn; extra turn preserves existing stance and spent Mobile/Focused allowance. Core says once on own turn; label this regular-only clarification NEW. Otherwise extra turns let Focused regain spell bonus or choose stance after it previously mattered. Extra full Action remains at most one per numbered round, independent of Reaction interval; paid movement/Action grants never manufacture additional grants.

9. **Omitted operational pieces.** Core Withdraw: Action, half Speed, no OA, +2 AC during move. Does this grant a separate half-Speed movement allowance or consume normal movement? Propose separate allowance paid by Action, normal terrain costs, no speed-0 movement, no lasting +2. Secure Cargo: Action stabilizes one Extraction Load class; needs exact meaning / inventory dependency rather than invented capacity. Ordinary grapple needs free hand? dropping grapple freely, one target per required hand, maintaining concentration compatible, reach break ends grapple? Add explicit NEW where V6 paragraph absent. Include TWF's Light/other-hand/BA/no positive ability-damage modifier and range-band legality from the provided V6. Climb/swim/jump/chase may require a later source pass; do not claim all DD21 done.

Please resolve these concretely, including examples/costs/endpoints. I will implement the settled candidate with a source-conflict table and bounded reference helpers/tests, then return the exact text/results for adversarial review. No whole combat-area closure yet.
