The remaining131 matches remain tracked. No original was changed.

Please apply the initial healing/death decisions to these exact sources and explicitly resolve the following boundaries before we implement the interface. Distinguish source rules from NEW integration choices; do not declare the whole healing catalog complete.

1. **Omitted zero-HP and timeline features.** Spiral-Touched BoonIII says instead regain one-quarter maximum and phase out. False Life BoonV says appear dead at0, then rise at next start with one-quarter maximum. Wound Eater BoonIII actually kills a living dying enemy. Fate-Bound BoonIV rerolls a failed death save. Resolve whether each is prevention, HP healing, delayed state recovery or direct death, including Decay, fatal Wounds, same-start death-save order, Stable status, voluntary activation and use consumption. Time Loop explicitly returns slain encounter participants to the initiative-start snapshot while preserving listed spent resources. Define whether it is temporal restoration rather than ordinary resurrection and what happens to Wounds acquired during the rewound interval, death tracks, soul bindings, body destruction and aura restrictions. Do not let a generic resurrection rule silently erase this explicit feature. If full temporal resolution requires its own package, give a precise provisional interface and list that dependency.

2. **Present-source death and healing gates.** Burning Blood rejects cold-tagged healing, not every magical heal. False Life's intrinsic Curse gives another creature's healing-spell caster an optional Insight check followed by a d2 on failure. What happens if that caster declines the optional check? A clear NEW disposition is needed so declining neither deletes the drawback nor invents automatic total failure. Non-spell healing remains unaffected. Unfinished Chorus blocks corpse raising in its actual area. Keep these exact gates separate from generic Corrupted, Decay, Crownshatter and ordinary resurrection denial.

3. **Bonuses conditional on actual HP.** Consecrated Ground, Aura of Faith, Radiance, Ember Mantle, Life-Linked and Ranger Healing Touch use restores/regains/actual healing wording. Aura of Mercy says receives HP healing; Throne's Grace adds per rolled die; Benevolent Spirits adds M but excludes triggered heals/copies. State which are pre-resolution modifiers and which require an initial positive actual gain. Example: base heal5, flat reduction5, eligible conditional+3: does it remain0 or become3? Preserve same-stat strongest-flat restrictions without accidentally erasing explicitly additional per-die or source-specific modifiers.

4. **Healing triggers and copies.** Distinguish numeric bonuses, a separate finite triggered heal, resource recovery and non-healing benefits. Radiance cure, Renewal Stamina, Sanctified Reprisal, Life Prophecy RC, Blood Tithe, paid Echo Leech, Blood Feast and finite reserve sources have different predicates. Meditative Healing says regain HP THEN1Ki; that is not textually conditional on actual HP unless source engine says so. Determine blocked/full-HP behavior and no-recursion boundaries. Preserved Echoes/copies explicitly exclude triggers; do not globally prevent unrelated first-generation paid heals from activating valid features.

5. **Overhealing and ceilings.** Cosmic Equilibrium converts healing beyond missing HP, while Overflowing Light also has excess handling in the initial packet. Is excess the legal post-modifier amount above the effective healing ceiling, or above actual maximum-HP missing amount? Reserve ceilings apply after final actual restoration and never pay for overhealing or prevented recovery.

7. **Finite pools and healing classification.** Paladin engine says Lay on Hands fully restored only by long rest, while Verdict Absolute expressly refills it once after a qualifying meaningful defeat. Preserve this source-specific exception. Base and Hero Lay on Hands have different allocation/action limits; no RP conversion. Distinguish paid/finite pulses from passive regeneration: paid Aura of Grace, Living Pulse, Renewal Totem, Blood Feast and Thief Exsanguinate versus automatic Bloodthick Node/Rapid Coagulation. Transfusion and Channel Life spend non-damage realHP first and cannot be refunded by an HP floor or prevented healing. Clarify whether a would-die replacement at0 can allow other parts of the original damage event to kill the creature again without a new event.
