## Complete bodies and exact ingredients

Every finished output is a **durable magical nonartifact item** made by integrating the exact recipe into one actual complete mundane base item. It is not fabricated from an assumed mass of tiny magical ingredients. The base is a supplemental input; it replaces none of the source ingredients, tools, properties, tiers, formula access or work.

Before work, declare one legal base model, one fitted size and one actual equipment grade for the whole batch. Every reserved base uses that model/size/grade. Record each physical base identity and its actual maximum/current structural HP. A damaged but functioning base with at least 1 HP may qualify. A Broken base does not. An item already bearing a magical Blacksmithing recipe imprint is not a generic input for another recipe; replacement and upgrading require a separately reviewed procedure.

Reserve **floor(1.5 × source quantity)** complete legal bases before beginning the order. Each must be a real available object and cannot also serve another simultaneous order. Having money or knowing a supplier does not substitute for a reserved body. No source ingredient mass, returned catalyst, quality result or name creates additional base items.

## Base custody, transformation and mass

The exact transformed base ceases to exist as a separate mundane inventory asset. Preserve its identity/history within the finished item. Standard transforms source quantity Q; Exceptional yield transforms floor(1.25Q); Masterwork yield transforms floor(1.5Q); Flawed Q≥2 transforms floor(Q/2); singleton Flawed transforms one body. A structural-improvement result transforms Standard quantity. Every unused reserved base remains an actual mundane object with its actual condition.

Failed and Mishap produce no recipe imprint. Reserved bases return as mundane items unless an independently resolved physical event actually damages one. Source ingredients still undergo the framework's exact recovery/loss. The supplemental base is not a printed recipe component and receives no extra 25% recovery credit. A recovery entry cannot duplicate the still-existing body.

Source tools, including nonconsumed Forge water, contribute zero finished structural mass. Fuel and solvent also contribute zero. Binder, substrate, material and catalyst occurrences remain required, but do not add their listed mass to the finished operational weight. The finished weight is the selected base's actual weight, including legitimate size/grade effects. Process mass becomes slag, trimmings or other waste without an automatic salvage or market identity. A legally retained Masterwork catalyst returns with its original identity and remaining age; it is not also incorporated into the finished body.

**Example — five axes:** recipe-45 has Q5 and maximum yield 7. Seven Medium Standard Battleaxes require seven actual 4-lb, 10-gp bases. Standard transforms five and returns two mundane axes. Masterwork yield transforms seven. The source four Brightsteel Ingots and putrid marrow are paid once as printed; their combined 4.18 lb does not supply seven axe bodies.

**Example — five shields:** recipe-53 has Q5 and maximum yield 7. Choosing Tower shield requires seven actual 15-lb, 50-gp Standard bases. Standard transforms five and returns two. The source's 5.43 lb of recipe inputs does not replace the 75 lb of transformed shield bodies.

**Ordered reserves:** before any active work, record every maximum-yield base in a stable ordered list, including model, size, grade, dominant load-bearing material, current/maximum HP and persistent durability modifiers. Transform the first N entries for the resolved quantity, or the first Standard Q for the durability option. Return all other actual bodies unchanged. No reordering, replacement, acquisition or selection of the best bodies after seeing the roll. If a base is missing, committed elsewhere or Broken before the completion check, pause the order and repair or replace it before rolling. A replacement occupies that exact list position with its own recorded condition. Damaged but non-Broken bases remain legal.

## Weapon benefits

An intact, unsuppressed weapon adds its printed recipe attack bonus only to an attack actually made with that physical weapon. Legal melee, off-hand and thrown attacks qualify. Carrying it gives no general attack bonus. Ordinary proficiency, ability, damage dice, reach, range, properties and action costs remain those of the selected model and the actual attack procedure.

The recipe damage bonus applies **once** to the attack's ordinary weapon-damage component on a hit. It still applies when the actual off-hand rule omits ordinary ability damage. It is not multiplied across damage types. If a reviewed effect changes that component's damage type, the flat bonus follows the actual resulting component. Flat bonuses never double on a critical. Legal attacks on objects qualify only where the weapon and actual targeting/damage rules permit them.

An unsuppressed Blacksmith weapon's ordinary bludgeoning, piercing or slashing damage counts as magical for defenses that distinguish magical damage. This does not change its damage type or confer spell rank, spellcasting, Counterspell eligibility, RP, concentration, attunement or an activation Action. Source elemental tags alone grant no elemental damage.

**New item-accuracy family:** the preserved V6 weapon-grade attack modifier and the recipe's attack bonus use the stronger value rather than adding. A Fine +1 grade weapon with recipe +2 uses +2 item accuracy. An Exceptional +3 grade weapon with recipe +1 uses +3 item accuracy and the recipe's +1 damage. Superior/Exceptional grade critical dice remain separate because they are not flat accuracy modifiers.

Other class, spell or item bonuses follow their actual reviewed combination rules. This family is not a universal strongest-bonus rule. No generic second recipe imprint can stack onto the body. Source names such as Superior, Masterwork or Legendary do not grant an equipment grade. The actual supplied mundane base retains its legal grade; a new mundane base without a different established grade is Standard.

Evaluate only real applicable modifiers. Crude weapon grade −1 and active recipe +1 use +1; suppressing the recipe leaves the actual −1. There is no phantom Standard +0 that cancels a remaining penalty. Broken items supply no normal weapon function to enhance. A separately legal improvised use does not recover the item's ordinary weapon or Blacksmith bonuses.

## Printed elemental riders

Only a card whose current `bonusDamage` actually contains dice receives this procedure. Its wielder may apply those exact dice/type when hitting a legal target with an attack actually made using the intact, unsuppressed physical weapon. Choose after the hit is known and before finalizing the attack's damage. Declining does not spend the budget.

The additional typed component belongs to the **same attack damage event**. It is not another attack, hit, action, target, save or proc. Its attack damage dice double normally on a critical; the flat recipe damage bonus does not. Apply actual defenses to each type within the same event and the ordinary rules for that event's consequences. Do not manufacture extra damage events to multiply Wounds or other thresholds.

The frequency identity is **physical item + actual turn instance**, with one use for that item during that turn. Two physical weapons each have their own budget, including different recipes or two copies. Transfer during the turn does not refresh an item's used budget. Extra Actions during the same turn do not refresh it. An actual extra turn is a new turn instance. A legal Reaction, opportunity attack or readied attack on another creature's turn uses the item's budget for that actual turn.

A legal attack on an ally may qualify, with the rider still optional; this grants no generic self-attack permission. A legal object hit may receive the rider, subject to actual object defenses and physical compatibility. Psychic immunity still prevents a psychic damage component.

Copied attacks or damage create no new rider trigger, refresh or second expenditure. A copy of an already-resolved event follows its own reviewed rule for the recorded damage. A companion gains no weapon permission or proficiency from the item. If an independently reviewed companion rule permits an attack using that actual weapon, its attack may use the item's remaining budget for the turn under the same procedure.

**Outside initiative:** a genuinely out-of-initiative lawful attack uses one budget per absolute six-second combat-action window. The first relevant combat-scale attack establishes the item's current window; additional lawful attacks within it do not create more budgets. A new window requires six complete elapsed seconds. This grants no attacks or acceleration. If the attempted attack begins an encounter, first establish initiative and resolve its actual legal entry/turn procedure; it receives no earlier outside budget.

Track `lastElementalRiderTriggerTime` across mode changes. If the item triggered outside initiative and initiative begins before six seconds have elapsed, its first actual turn before that deadline treats the rider as already spent. After that transition guard is satisfied, actual regular/extra-turn budgets apply normally. Ending initiative after an initiative trigger prevents another outside trigger until six seconds after that last trigger; normal outside windows then apply. Merely toggling bookkeeping never refreshes the item.

## Armor, shields and defensive enhancement

The printed recipe AC bonus is an **enhancement**, not a replacement for the entire base armor formula or shield bonus. Keep the selected suit's normal formula, full or capped DEX modifier, Strength requirement, Stealth rule, proficiency, fitting, weight and don/doff. Keep a shield's actual hands, Strength, proficiency, weight and don/doff requirements.

**New `equipment.defense-enhancement` family:** all active Blacksmith recipe AC bonuses and the applicable V6 worn-armor grade AC modifier use the strongest single value. They do not add together. The base shield's normal +1/+2/+3 is separate from this enhancement family.

- A suit's recipe enhancement qualifies when that suit supplies the worn-armor AC formula actually being used.
- A shield's recipe enhancement qualifies when it is the one usable shield contributing its ordinary shield bonus.
- One worn, intact and unsuppressed Helm and one Gauntlets pair can each supply a candidate enhancement.
- The actual worn suit's grade modifier qualifies under its normal armor rules.

Independent class, spell, ancestry and natural/unarmored rules retain their own wording. A suit's unused formula cannot contribute its recipe bonus as though it were an accessory. The new accessories do not themselves count as armor suits.

An ordinary Shield with recipe-53 contributes its base +2 and, if strongest, its recipe +1, for a total +3. If a higher eligible defensive enhancement is already present, the shield still supplies its base +2, while its lower enhancement adds nothing. Actual Tower shield requirements remain in force when that model is chosen; crafting does not waive them.

Resistance is a distinct printed benefit. An item's lower AC enhancement can be inactive while its printed resistance remains active under its own wearing requirements. Different resistance types coexist. Multiple resistance sources to the same type do not become immunity. No title, ingredient or metadata tag grants resistance absent the current effect.

Each lawfully donned, intact and unsuppressed shield supplies its printed resistance even if another shield supplies ordinary shield AC. Only the selected AC-contributing shield offers its recipe enhancement. Every donned shield still occupies its actual hand/forearm and retains its Strength, Stealth, proficiency and other drawbacks. A back-slung, carried, stored or dropped shield is not donned and supplies neither benefit. Same-type resistance still does not become immunity.

Shield bases default to Standard. An actual preexisting shield with an independently reviewed grade retains only the properties and valuation that its actual prior rule established. Suit-only grade AC, weight or HP rules are not imported onto shields. Blacksmithing creates no shield-grade entitlement.

The family considers actual modifiers only: a Crude suit's −1 and active recipe +1 contribute +1; suppressing the recipe leaves −1. No implicit +0 cancels it. At Broken, the item's normal positive armor/shield function ceases; the physical drawbacks remain rather than evaluating enhancement arithmetic for a function the item no longer supplies.

Only one benefit-bearing helm and one benefit-bearing pair can be worn in those respective slots. Additional copies are carried/stowed objects. These bodies have Standard equipment grade only; no separate Fine/Superior/Exceptional accessory-grade progression has been authored.

These are defensive accessories, not full light/medium/heavy armor. They require no armor proficiency and create no generic Strength minimum or Stealth penalty. ## Structural state and suppression

Every item separately records its base model, size, actual equipment grade, ordinary and actual maximum/current HP, source recipe imprint, crafting-quality modifier and source/base/finished values. These fields are not one generic quality tier. Structural AC and HP need their explicitly reviewed base bindings; magical durability is not inferred for unrelated magic items.

While magic is legally suppressed, keep the mundane base's ordinary usability, size, grade, mass, hand/slot use, armor formula, Strength minimum, Stealth, don/doff and physical drawbacks. Recipe accuracy, flat damage, elemental rider, AC enhancement, resistance and recipe-derived magical B/P/S classification are inactive. Suppression supplies no repair, new attack, catalyst, proc or budget refresh.

If a rider was already used in the current turn, suppressing and unsuppressing the item leaves it used. If unused, it may become available when legal suppression ends within that same turn. Suppression does not reveal any origin or cosmological explanation.

At 0 structural HP the whole item is **Broken**. Its mundane positive function and recipe benefits stop. The actual body, weight, occupied hand/slot, wearing drawbacks and don/doff remain. The imprint is dormant while a recoverable main body exists; it is not automatically erased. Ordinary rest restores neither HP nor function. A missing/destroyed main body cannot be recovered merely by naming its former imprint.

**Explicit structural binding for this family:** ordinary weapons HP5, Buckler/Shield HP10, Tower shield HP15, Medium armor HP15, Heavy armor HP20, Helm HP5 and Gauntlets pair HP5. Large doubles the applicable baseline before durability modifiers. The actual base's predeclared dominant load-bearing material supplies its reviewed structural AC: iron/steel 19, wood/leather 15, or another genuinely applicable reviewed material profile. Mixed bodies use the material actually carrying most structural load, recorded before work, never selected after an attack. An ingredient tag does not change it. Worn/held Sunder still uses the higher of that material AC and the wearer's actual AC under the equipment procedure.

Crafting durability increases and Flawed maximum reductions are physical workmanship records. Suppression changes no maximum/current HP, damage deficit, flaw, durability increase, material AC, size or mundane grade. Ending suppression restores only otherwise legal magical functions, without healing, removing a flaw or refreshing a budget.

## Craft quality and preexisting damage

Resolve the crafting framework's six outcomes, natural-1 Mishap and numeric margins. Natural 20 grants no extra band shift. Forge Dwarf's actual artisan-creation permission reduces time and treats an actual Standard result as Exceptional output; it does not upgrade Flawed, Failed, Mishap or an already higher result, grant equipment grade or add XP.

Choose quality yield or the structural option once for the whole batch after the check. The structural option keeps Standard quantity and raises an explicitly known ordinary maximum by 25% for Exceptional or 50% for Masterwork, rounded down, minimum +1. The eligible Masterwork catalyst return remains one actual consumed catalyst unit total, independent of yield versus durability. Repeated same-name/ID component occurrences still require their separate actual quantities.

Mundane nonmagical, non-Broken bases may already have a reviewed crafting durability increase or persistent singleton-Flawed maximum. Use one noncompounding calculation:

1. B is ordinary maximum HP after the actual model, size and equipment-grade HP rule, including Crude suit HP where applicable.
2. P is the strongest legally attached positive crafting durability percentage: 0, 25% or 50%. A new structural option replaces P only if stronger; it never multiplies an already improved maximum.
3. ImprovedMax = B + floor(B × P), with a minimum +1 increase when P>0.
4. If a persistent singleton-Flawed state exists, FinalMax = max(1, floor(ImprovedMax / 2)); otherwise FinalMax = ImprovedMax. A new singleton Flawed result establishes that one state, never a second halving.
5. Preserve DamageDeficit = OldMax − OldCurrent. NewCurrent = max(0, FinalMax − DamageDeficit), capped at FinalMax.

Standard preserves the existing modifiers and damage. A 3/5 weapon gaining +1 maximum becomes 4/6, not 6/6. A B20 base with +25% durability and a persistent flaw has max12. Replacing that increase with +50% produces max15; it still has the flaw and preserves its damage deficit. A badly damaged base may emerge Broken after a new Flawed result.

For Q≥2, Flawed changes output quantity to floor(Q/2), not unit potency or existing durability modifiers. For Q1 it establishes the one persistent flaw as above. Neither later durability improvement, suppression nor ordinary repair erases that flaw. No generic flaw-removal, recipe replacement or repeated-imprint upgrade is granted.

## Repair and reconstruction

For a functioning Blacksmith output with HP≥1, the imprint is intact. The reviewed structural repair uses actual Smith's tools, Forge and Anvil, and Tongs; choose STR or INT before the check and apply actual Smith's-tools proficiency once. DC10; active work max(1 hour, actual maximum structural HP / 10 hours); materials cost 25% of its mundane size/grade base reference. The recipe's magic/process value is not part of this functioning-item material price. Success restores current HP to the recorded maximum; failure spends the actual work and materials.

For a Broken output at HP0 with its recoverable main body, use the new reconstruction interface. The same actual tools/station and STR-or-INT choice apply. DC is the original recipe crafting DC. Active work is max(8 hours, actual maximum structural HP / 10 hours). Materials cost 25% of the undamaged **finished per-item reference**, including its source magic/process share and actual mundane base reference. Round an actual purchase under the ordinary currency procedure.

Reconstruction is a **binary repair task**, not the six-band crafting check. Success restores current HP to its existing maximum and restores the dormant imprint's usability, subject to any continuing suppression. Failure spends materials/time and leaves the item Broken. There is no yield, catalyst, quality roll, crafting XP or first-success award. Forge Dwarf's creation-time reduction does not shorten reconstruction.

Repair preserves model, size, grade, recipe identity, existing quality, any Flawed maximum and any recorded noncompounding HP increase. It cannot replace a missing main body, generate recipe ingredients, remove a flaw, stack a second imprint or upgrade the recipe. A genuinely missing body needs a new craft and new actual inputs. An available Core repair service retains its separately quoted source procedure and does not receive a second automatic self-repair charge.

Both repair procedures require actual **Smith's-tools proficiency**. Owning a kit or holding a trade level does not supply it. Neither procedure requires formula ownership or the original crafting recipe's trade-level eligibility: it restores the surviving imprinted object rather than creating another. Repairing a restricted item does not teach or grant its formula. Forge Dwarf's creation-only time reduction applies to neither functioning-item repair nor HP0 reconstruction. The generic binary repair outcome gives no crafting quality, XP, catalyst or extra result.

## Work phases, stations and source access

Use STR or INT with actual Smith's-tools proficiency where applicable, the exact recipe crafting DC and the framework's lawful tool/facility/assistant modifiers. Owning tools does not grant proficiency. Blacksmithing is an artisan creation trade. Keep the actual source work hours, formula and trade-level requirements, assistant cap and work/rest limits.

Compute lawful creation-time adjustments first. The first stable integration phase is **min(8 active hours, final actual required work time)**. It is included in total work. For an eligible Forge Dwarf, source 8 hours becomes 6 actual hours and a six-hour first phase; source 4 becomes 3 and a three-hour phase; source 80 becomes 60 with an eight-hour first phase. Later phases use the ordinary framework. Assistant hours follow the same actual order duration and do not independently shorten it.

At the first stable phase's completion, perishable/reactive recipe components already incorporated into that order become stable within its workpieces. Before then, their real preservation clocks continue. Unincorporated reserved components keep their own clocks. This is neither extractable preserved raw stock nor an early completion roll or second ingredient charge. Actual phase interruption rules still apply.

The station `Forge and Anvil` and the required Forge/Anvil tools can be satisfied by the same actual operational fixtures. They do not require two forges and two anvils. Actual Smith's tools and Tongs must also be available. Ordinary unquantified heat/fuel is part of operating that station; invent no extra quantified coal bill. A printed Coal component remains its separate required consumed input. Printed Forge water retains its exact component identity and consumed flag; it is not free generic water or structural mass.

All 43 source recipes have **fieldCraftable:false**. That flag controls even if a preserved source `timeMode` says field. The label does not permit portable field crafting or conjure a Forge. Expedition use requires a real eligible worksite supplying the actual Forge and Anvil. Preserve contradictory source labels rather than silently rewriting them. Optional Director guidance remains optional and adds no automatic ritual, extra ingredient or extra hours.

## Reference value and access

Preserve the source recommendedMarketValue as the **Standard source batch reference**, not a per-item price. For source quantity Q:

`source magic/process unit reference = source batch reference / Q`

`finished per-item reference = source magic/process unit reference + actual undamaged mundane size/grade base reference`

Keep exact rational values until the actual transaction rounding step. An output count N gives N finished items with their actual per-item references. Crafting quality alone gives no market multiplier. Buying supplemental bases is a separate real expense; owned bases need no repurchase. Neither changes original consumedMaterialValue, laborValue or source economy fields.

Recipe-42 has source batch value 92 gp and Q1. A Standard Medium Longsword base adds 15 gp, giving a 107-gp finished reference. Large sizing doubles the base to 30 gp, giving 122 gp; it does not double the 92-gp source field. Recipe-53 has 94 gp/Q5 = 18.8 gp source share. A Standard ordinary Shield adds 10 gp, giving 28.8 gp per finished unit before an actual quote.

Finished outputs are magical nonartifact equipment. Use a legitimate finite buyer and actual quote; the mundane standing resale table is not an automatic guaranteed magic-item buyback. Damage, flaws, scarcity, law, reputation and demand retain their actual quote consequences, with no newly invented universal adjustment. Magical status or high tier alone does not invoke artifact valuation. HP0 reconstruction uses the same undamaged per-item finished reference.

Recipe knowledge and public/Director visibility remain exactly as sourced. A identified item or repaired body does not silently grant a crafting formula. Material/monster names provide neither availability rights nor approved creature statistics. `wholeCreatureUse` metadata grants no donor powers or anatomy traits. Only current `mechanics.tag` is operative; secondary source tags and legacy effects remain preserved but supply no additional damage, resistance, healing, poison classification or regeneration.
