# Erase from Reality — NEW RIC5-ER-01 through RIC5-ER-04

Action;one actual perceived creature within120feet;V/S/F;rank7;base17RP;nonconcentration. A currently willing recipient may accept without resistance. NEW an unwilling recipient makes one CHA save against the actual caster's spell DC; success negates both components, failure admits them subject to their actual separate defenses. There is no creature-type/CR gate by name, no damage and no universal 'mind-affecting' immunity. This does not take the recipient's Actions, so mere untargetability does not spend the whole-Action denial guard.

## Untargetable is not absent or invulnerable
While operative the body cannot be newly selected as an individual direct creature target of an attack, spell, feature or item use, whether harmful, beneficial, self-targeted or targeted by an ally. An unresolved direct effect that explicitly revalidates a creature target at resolution fails against it under that effect's actual paid-target lifecycle; no refund or replacement target is supplied. An already launched physical object/projectile governed by collision is not erased or deflected by this clause: its actual world/collision source must supply its later consequences. No generic projectile engine is claimed. Previously established attached conditions, damage-over-time, controls, restraints, concentration dependencies and effects which operate without selecting a new target continue under their own sources. Actual areas, environmental collisions, support, Falling and mundane custody still affect the real body. You may target a legal point/area without selecting this creature; that area's later snapshot includes the body when its own predicates qualify.

The recipient remains in its real place with real gear, visible/audible when ordinary senses permit, and can act/move/attack other legal targets using real budgets. It is not Invisible, Incapacitated, immaterial, removed from a plane or immune to damage. It cannot use a new self-buff requiring direct self selection during the protection; ordinary existing personal passive state is not a fresh targeting action. An established grapple retains its source and actual reach; Erase does not teleport either body or free the grapple. A deliberate new direct grapple attempt is blocked. Mechanical owner/source IDs remain valid private bookkeeping, not controller targeting permission.

## Temporary memory fading — bounded selected meaning
NEW at establishment snapshot other actual minds within120feet of the recipient with real line of effect and actual perception of that recipient at that moment. The recipient's own mind is excluded. Only these observers receive a source-owned reversible mask on their preexisting episodic/recognition links identifying that recipient: their remembered name, prior recognition and target-associated recollection cannot be used as presently recalled knowledge while the mask is operative. No observer is informed that a spell selected them or given hidden roster/identity metadata. It does not erase language, skills, general facts, loyalties or other people's memories globally; it does not authenticate a disguised recipient. Opaque barriers/out-of-range/unperceiving observers are not included and later entrants are not enrolled.

This is a mask, not deleted history. Diaries, ownership, friendship/faction records, consents, issued orders, cast receipts, hard-control history, resource debts and true world facts remain. A temporarily unavailable remembered justification does not undo an already-lawful transaction or refund it. New sensory observation and new learning remain possible: an observer can see an unfamiliar body now and remember that new event, but cannot recover masked old recognition through private engine entity IDs. Actual memory-interference immunity/prevention can defeat this component for that observer without granting direct targetability through the recipient's independent protection. This selected scope is an explicit completion of 'memory fades temporarily', not global amnesia or a sixth information presentation.

## One source lifetime and ending
NEW both components have the same source deadline: the caster's next future regular START, excluding a START already underway when applied; outside initiative use application+6seconds and preserve the registered clock through initiative changes. Extra turns add no endpoint. Successful same-caster recast on the same recipient replaces only that caster's old source; other sources remain independently keyed and identical protections do not add strength. Each observer retains the mask belonging to each live source; ending one cannot bypass another.

Actual suppression affects only the expressions in its true scope. Suppressing the recipient protection permits otherwise legal new direct targeting while suppressed; a remotely unsuppressed observer mask need not vanish merely because the body entered a local field. Suppressing an observer's mask makes its preexisting available memories usable there; no interval is replayed on resumption. Expiry, genuine full source removal or recipient's actual death removes this source's protection and masks permanently. Zero HP alone is not actual death. Ending a mask removes only its interference from whatever memories really remain: an independent Disrupt Memory deletion or other real edit made during the interval remains deleted/edited, while new memories stay new. Never restore an old serialized full-mind snapshot over later experience. Gear/collision, elapsed costs, completed damage and other applications are not rolled back. No cosmetics are declared cosmological reality erasure.
