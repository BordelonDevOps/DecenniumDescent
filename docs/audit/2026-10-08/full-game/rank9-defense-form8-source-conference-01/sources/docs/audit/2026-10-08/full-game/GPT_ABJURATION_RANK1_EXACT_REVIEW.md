# Actual GPT exact manuscript review

Two small manuscript repairs remain. Everything else is ready for promotion.

## 1. Protective Sphere — approved as written

The Falling exception is the correct bounded completion.

Without it, the lower hemisphere necessarily becomes a magical floor capable of catching/suspending Tiny/Small creatures, which contradicts the settled **no floor/platform/support** rule. Treat unsupported gravity-driven Falling as distinct from bodily movement being actively carried, flown, jumped, pushed, pulled, or otherwise forced across the boundary.

So retain:

> unsupported ordinary Falling crosses the boundary without being arrested or creating support

This does **not** grant additional falling movement; it merely says the Sphere does not interrupt the ordinary Falling procedure. A thrown/propelled object is still a projectile while propelled; once its projectile motion is stopped, subsequent gravity is ordinary Falling.

No repair needed.

## 2. Dispel Resonance — approved

The authored card correctly settles the conflict.

- Eligibility uses the **original generating spell's recorded rank**.
- Rank 0 or 1 only.
- RP discounts/increases do not redefine spell rank.
- The dispeller's own rank/access does not redefine the target.
- “Tier 1” does not become ranks 1–4.
- A curse qualifies only where the curse itself is an otherwise eligible ongoing rank-0/1 spell effect.
- Ending it invokes its normal ending procedure.
- Prior damage, spent resources, deaths, triggered consequences, etc. are not rewound.

The perception qualification is also good: merely knowing “magic exists nearby” does not identify an individual legal dispel target.

No repair.

## 3. Binding Circle — one geometry repair

The mechanical result is approved, but the target currently allows:

> “solid ground or another immobile solid surface”

while the effect defines a circle with a boundary extending “10 feet vertically above its surface.”

A wall, ceiling, slanted roof, etc. makes that geometry ambiguous.

Use this narrower target:

> **One fixed point on solid ground or another approximately horizontal immobile solid surface that can contain the 5-foot-radius circle, within range and line of effect.**

Everything else remains exactly as written:

- 5-ft **radius**
- 10-ft vertical side boundary
- concentration 1 minute
- no save
- exact extraplanar record/tag only
- no Action denial
- no antimagic
- teleportation does not cross the physical boundary
- no magical cap/floor
- no initial intersection with affected creature

This is a geometry clarification, not a new power.

## 12. Faction Seal — approved, including the campaign permission gate

The explicit permission procedure is acceptable as the required **NEW playtest completion**.

There was no supplied universal source telling us which faction any arbitrary caster may legitimately invoke, and silently treating “knows the faction name” as authorization would be worse. The proposed procedure makes the dependency explicit:

> Director records eligible canonical factions from actual source permission or explicit campaign permission.

That does not fabricate legal authority because the permission comes from campaign state rather than the spell.

Keep the important distinction:

**permission to invoke the magical seal ≠ membership, reputation, credentials, legal authority, or institutional protection.**

The detection itself is correctly bounded:

- canonical `factionId`
- reputation −3 through −1 only
- missing = unknown
- binary visible flare
- hidden/invisible creatures can trigger
- no square/identity/disguise revelation
- no invisibility cancellation
- no standing modification

No repair.
