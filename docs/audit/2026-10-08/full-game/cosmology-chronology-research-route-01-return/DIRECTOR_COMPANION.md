## Chronology that can actually be used

The original Cosmology provides a long forward narrative sequence, but its printed chapter order is not a license to infer every missing dependency or assign AE dates. The mortal World source separately prints a 400-day year, a ten-year rotation, 87 AE as the First Cycle beginning, and 710 AE as the Current Cycle beginning.

Under the additional assumption that every Cycle begins on one uninterrupted ten-year phase measured from 87 AE, the difference is 623 years and leaves remainder 3. Nothing in the frozen evidence supplies the missing bridge.

**NEW PROVISIONAL DESIGN — current calendar treatment:** preserve both printed dates and use 710 AE as the prospective anchor for current ten-year scheduling. Do not extrapolate an uninterrupted phase backward to 87 AE. This changes no source date and leaves the historical offset honestly unresolved.

Primordial Cycles remain ages. They are not mortal ten-year Cycles. The 400-day year, Opening/Horn clocks, elastic Silver Prism Year, lunar/celestial movements, and primordial ages retain their own scopes unless a source explicitly bridges them.
