# Healing/death source candidate inventory

**NOT YET REVIEWED.** Exact objects preserved in HEALING_DEATH_SOURCE_CANDIDATES.json; inclusion is lexical, not operational promotion.

- `cleric engine[7]` — Healing cannot exceed maximum HP, does not remove Wounds, and does not restore a dead creature unless a card explicitly says it resurrects. No Cleric feature provides unlimited permanent HP or resource restoration outside encounters. Temporary HP does not stack with itself; use the higher value. Resurrection and corpse-based effects have explicit use limits and cannot generate repeatable corpses, XP, loot or Conviction.
- `cleric.base.healing-rite` — Healing Rite. Tags: rite, healing. One living ally regains S d8 + M HP. It cannot restore a dead creature or remove Wounds. Consecrated Ground and appropriate Invocation Path bonuses may modify this healing.
- `cleric.hero.saint.divine-rebirth` — Divine Rebirth. Tags: saint, resurrection. When one of your Cleric features returns an ally from death, you may use Divine Rebirth. That ally returns with half its maximum HP instead of the feature's normal returned HP and gains S + M temporary HP for 1 minute. It removes no Wounds and cannot affect a creature that was not actually dead.
- `cleric.hero.saint.unyielding-light` — Unyielding Light. Tags: saint, emergency, healing. When one damage event would leave every conscious living party member within 30 feet at 0 HP, resolve that damage, then before ordinary death-save progression each qualifying living creature at 0 HP regains floor(maximum HP/3), minimum 1. It cannot revive a creature killed outright and removes no Wounds. This one emergency trigger may resolve even if that event incapacitates you, provided you remain alive; it grants no other actions while incapacitated.
- `cleric.hero.saint.celestial-resurgence` — Final Miracle — Celestial Resurgence. Tags: saint, finale, resurrection, rite. Each legal target returns to life at 1 HP. The body must be sufficiently present to sustain life. This removes no Wounds, curses or other death consequences. Divine Rebirth may modify one returned ally if available. The Conviction expenditure can vent Dissonance once under the common rule.
- `cleric.hero.saint.mass-healing-light` — Mass Healing Light. Tags: saint, healing. Roll S d8 + M once. Each target regains that amount.
