## Corrected controlling rule

> **TI-trap primary and replaceable members:** The original 3-lb trigger frame and original 2-lb working striker/discharge block are primary constituents. Both must survive in recoverable form for magical reconstruction. The 2-lb captive spring/actuation member and its separate subordinate contacts are replaceable, as are compatible supports, catches, grip covers, fittings and feet. Replacement requires actual compatible stock and the applicable restoration procedure; payment alone does not supply a missing part. A replaceable contact is not permission to replace or recreate the original discharge block.
>
> Replacement preserves the same trap identity, its original primary constituents, the **12 × 12 × 3-inch envelope**, **8-lb empty mass**, and the **3/2/2/1-lb body allocation**. It grants no improved mechanism, additional effect or new magical body. A missing or nonfunctional spring prevents discharge until lawful restoration is completed.

This is the **single controlling TI-trap rule** wherever the prior descriptions disagree.
