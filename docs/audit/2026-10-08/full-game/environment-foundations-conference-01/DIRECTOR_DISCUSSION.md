# Environmental foundations — discussion draft

**NEW local proposals. Not submitted, agreed or active rules.** This draft supplies concrete procedures for actual GPT discussion. Source books and reviewed rules remain unchanged.

Read [the full proposal](PLAYER_DISCUSSION.md) before the examples. The original five climate references and all earlier draft files remain preserved. These are proposed outcomes, not completed gameplay tests.

## Decisions requiring actual conference

- Suffocation HP assignment, blocked recovery/death-save exceptions and companion life interfaces
- Baseline underwater V permission and six-second death-save/elapsed-time bridge
- Climate thresholds, stage progression and genuine recovery
- Climate ancestry benefit and Warrior ordinary-penalty interpretation
- Stoneback suppresses the level-1 component only, not a gained level
- Fire contact pacing, physical extinguishing amounts and finite smoke clearance
- Swim movement cost, sinking abstraction, current reactions and actual towing support
- Weather thresholds, Storm Magic adjustment/stacking and Eye of the Storm baseline
- All remaining HZ08–14 toxin/disease/corrosion/trap/fall/protection catalog work and existing unsent Director/bestiary/expedition conferences

## Authored situations

### EF-C01

Proposed result: One completed cold unit and one DC12 CON save; no second clock starts when initiative does.

### EF-C02

Proposed result: 1/2 + 1/2 = one unit; one save at the highest contributing DC15, then progress0.

### EF-C03

Proposed result: The short gap pauses progress; one DC12 save after the second exposed half-hour.

### EF-C04

Proposed result: The ten safe minutes clear unfinished progress; final progress1/2 and no new save yet. Existing cold levels remain.

### EF-C05

Proposed result: Cold1 grants DEX-check disadvantage; cold2 also −10 Speed; entering cold3 adds exactly one global Exhaustion. A fourth failure adds a second Exhaustion, not cold4.

### EF-C06

### EF-C07

Proposed result: Penalty suppression starts after completed dose; at expiry it returns. Cold level and partial exposure never reset.

### EF-C08

Proposed result: The dose has expired at that instant; cold1 disadvantage applies unless another actual protection remains. Do not add an Action before the deadline retroactively.

### EF-C09

### EF-C10

### EF-C13
