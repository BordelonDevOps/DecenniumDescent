Price/history/components still commit once at ordinary Ready preparation; Furnace qualification is tested at the actual damage checkpoint after legal release. Preparing in/releasing out loses this bonus; preparing out/releasing in can gain it. A countered/fully absorbed/suppressed payload deals no damage to amplify. A genuine Reaction spell can qualify without another Action or fabricated trigger. A multi-target shared raw roll remains one roll; each recipient's permitted packet uses the appropriate frozen event eligibility, without changing the dice when one recipient later moves or transfers damage.

Lesser Antimagic makes covered local support inoperative while its exact clocks continue; it does not merely inspect the remote field owner's location. Actual eligible Greater Dispel captures this rank9 whole field under its real immutable snapshot/check. Singularity can consume the generator when this field anchor is admitted, or the local enhancement manifestation at its own declared checkpoint. No emitted completed damage is undone. End only under RF9-CAST-LIFE-01; leaving, reentering or inspecting never grants a fresh buff clock, resource return or hidden magical scan.

## Resonance Singularity

KEEP rank9/class17 Druid, Mage or Wizard through Conjuration, Action/120ft/V/S/F/base26RP. NEW RS9-FIELD-01: the point has radius10ft operative influence and60-elapsed-second concentration, with RF9-GEOMETRY-01 clipping. It is indiscriminate: own, allied and hostile magic are included, at any actual rank, damaging or not. It does not physically attract creatures, draw matter across barriers or create a cosmological void.

NEW RS9-ALL-MAGIC-01: before local magical expression, absorb actual ordinary/class spells, expressly magical nonspell activations/riders, item-spell/charge/use outputs, existing creature-attached magical applications, ongoing fields and temporary spell-sustained creations. Intrinsic learned engines, RP/RC maxima, known spells, faith, souls, real ordinary anatomy, ordinary weapon matter, permanent converted matter and completed nonmagical consequences are not active magic to annihilate. Preserve explicit source immunity, unremovable curse engines, relic clauses and anti-resource-farm predicates; all magic is not permission to invent their opposite. A native source lacking a dispel rank is not automatically immune to this distinct new absorption operation, nor is it assigned rank0. The complete category map is operative, not a whitelist of the worked examples.

NEW RS9-LOCAL-01: qualifying local output is consumed before its own attack/save/payload RNG or damage root. Use actual source origin, recipient/area manifestation, destination or traveling effect path only where that source genuinely supplies one. If the true originating manifestation is inside, the newly emitted magical payload is absorbed before leaving; do not use the remote concentrating caster as the origin of an already-established outside emitter. An outside-origin nonprojectile with all actual manifestations outside is unaffected even if a drawn chord crosses this sphere. NEW locality clarification: an instantaneous area selection/line-of-effect center is not, merely by being a geometric center, a persistent generator or a traveling emitter. For an outside-caster instantaneous area with no actual source-defined propagation/emitter, resolve simultaneous recipient-local manifestations; a center inside this sphere does not by itself erase outside recipients. An actual persistent generator or expressly defined emitted/traveling source remains interceptable under its real procedure. A multi-target cast loses admitted local applications, not uncaptured outside ones. An indivisible creature buff is consumed as a whole application when its real affected body qualifies. If a field's discrete generator anchor is admitted, end that whole field; if its anchor stays outside, intercept only local emissions/expressions, retaining the outside parent and its original clock. New lawful later emissions can resume after the absorber ends; missed damage, healing, commands or duration never replay. Absorption is not Counterspell: a legally paid source that reached actual effect resolution completes that resolution even if every local output is consumed, with only its genuinely qualified completion hooks. An actual countered or pre-resolution-aborted cast receives no such completion. No refund, vent, counter success or extra use is invented merely by naming absorption.

For a temporary body whose existence is sustained by a removable creating spell, absorption of its actual creation/sustaining application invokes its actual ending/removal lifecycle, not a generic killing attack. Real carried objects drop locally; no corpse, loot, RP or kill trigger is fabricated from a body whose source forbids it. Full creation-source suppression and absorption-as-removal are distinct operations: do not substitute an AC5/AC6 pause rule for an actual end. A permanent ordinary corpse conversion or mundane created item persists if its creation is already complete; only a still-magical component/output is intercepted. Permanent enchanted items and persistent class passives retain item/engine identity, ownership and uses. Their local magical expression is unavailable inside, but native future expression can resume outside with the same history. A consumed discrete ongoing buff does not return merely on exit. No charge is spent merely by passive presence; an actually activated item spends its native charge at commitment normally.

NEW RS9-CONVERT-01: one original activation/creation/use has one global conversion entitlement across its recipients, pulses, descendants and all Singularity owners. The first eligible local absorption claims it, even if other uncaptured portions survive; this is explicitly a per-source allowance, NOT proportional damage conservation. No further partial interception pays again. Record the source's genuine ordinary baseRP actually paid after all prices, independently of rank, extras and protected principal. Wait until that source's actual resolution-triggered base refunds are settled. For paidBase>0, let U=max(0,paidBase-actualBaseRefunded); convert0 if U=0, otherwise max(1,floor(U/2)). For actually zero paid base or an unpriced magic source, convert1 intrinsic RP per original source-use, not per callback. Costs such as strain, optional feature extras, mandatory Dampening, Barrier X, Forge cycle principal, transfer value, item charge count, stored reserves, damage or nominal waived price are never added to this valuation. A refunded source's actual return is preserved; it is not reduced to finance Singularity. Existing delayed refund entitlements must be resolved/escrowed under their actual source before this bounded value settles; an unknown refund interface is a narrowly identified adapter prerequisite, not permission to speculate credit.

A stable continuous class/item enchantment has a stable source-establishment identity, so repeated entry, suppression or a new Singularity cannot farm it again. Real later independently paid/limited activations have their own use IDs. A free cantrip can lawfully generate1RP after absorption once per actual cast; actual Action, preparation, Refrain, concentration and other source limits still apply. This is an expressly new resource-generation choice, not an assertion of strict global RP conservation. Self/allied feeding is allowed unless that actual source prohibits conversion/refund/farming. A source expressly barred from resource generation produces0 conversion; absorption remains useful. Native transfer/draw/work/recovery postings do not mint fresh intrinsic conversion identities merely because a currency or counter changes. A Well draw or Conduit transfer retains its original generating source-use lineage; consume eligible underlying magic and consult that one original receipt, never the moved/reserved RP or an extra fallback1 per posting. The Conduit transfer itself still triggers no generation hook. The Well original source may be absorbed without resetting creation/receipt limits. Resonance Forge's explicit no-RP-from-dismantling/fixture-fuel protection yields0 for consuming that fixture, not a new harvesting exception. An actual interruption AFTER native transfer/draw/cycle commitment retains its spent principal/Action/use under that complete native rule; prevented preflight pays nothing. No spent principal becomes stored value to reclaim. An absorbed summon corpse/death record is not a new entitlement. No generic per-day cap or hidden rank ceiling is added.

An unknown payment record is not an unpriced source. A genuinely no-RP-interface source has explicit no-RP attribution; a source with missing required payment/refund evidence remains a pending conversion reconciliation, not guessed rank value or fallback1. Its lawful absorption can complete while its disputed credit remains ungranted until the actual record resolves, without redispatching its payload.

NEW RS9-RECIPIENT-01: credit the winning point's original caster into the same lawful personal RP pool that financed this point. There is no reserve, draw Action, pooled maximum, charge/RC conversion, loan or remote recipient selection. At final settlement use the actual current maximum and recovery permission; overflow or prohibited credit is lost. A dead/ended/missing recipient pool receives no credit. Ending the point after it has genuinely consumed an output does not re-create that output or cancel an already-earned pending conversion; settle the surviving receipt once to its original eligible owner. Mark conversion spent BEFORE pool mutation/callbacks; even a capped or prohibited0-credit receipt stays spent. No second claim after saving/loading. A Grand Reservoir or another actual source's own no-refund-combination restriction is not waived by calling a same-owner recovery conversion; disallowed combinations retain0 conversion rather than a new general refund exception. The separately accepted Soulburn intrinsic Reservoir output remains unchanged.

NEW RS9-ARBITRATE-01: apply RF9-ORDER-01. The point cannot absorb its own sustaining spell, conversion bookkeeping or source receipt. Different points do not share a blanket immunity: an already active older eligible point consumes a new intersecting point's formation before the new one establishes. Existing candidate competition uses oldest establishment then stable ID, once per local manifestation. No winner that already ended can act later in that operation queue. Local broad suppression has precedence; a suppressed point consumes nothing and banks no missed output. A legal source-specific termination operation directed at this point ignores only this target point's own absorption, just as the existing named antimagic termination bridge separates own protection; another operative point or different real suppression can still intercept it. This NEW exception prevents an automatically undispellable field without weakening its other absorption. Ordinary Counterspell acts only at actual cast time before formation. Expiry, concentration failure, actual eligible rank9 removal or generator absorption end the point once, with no refund, new pulse or re-credit.

## Resonance Nexus (Supreme)

KEEP rank9/class17 Druid, Mage or Wizard through Conjuration, Action/120ft/V/S/F/base26RP. Supreme Nexus is this identity's display alias, not a new spell. NEW NS9-FIELD-01: create the fixed radius30ft field with60-elapsed-second concentration. Its effects are indiscriminate: allies, enemies and the creator can qualify. It provides BOTH enhancement and half RP costs; ordinary Resonance Nexus supplies only its own independently scoped-2 comparison.

NEW NS9-PRICE-01: use RF9-PRICE-01. For actual spells, the real original caster body must intersect the operative field at commitment after any incompatible old concentration is removed. For actual magical nonspell activation/service fees, use the real payer/performer's body at that actual new payment; independently named protected principal stays whole. Familiar/projected release origins do not relocate the payer. No price is modified retrospectively, no pending future credit finances this transaction, and an otherwise absent ordinary RP-price interface is not manufactured. Actual Reaction casts qualify; ordinary Ready is priced at preparation only. A field cannot finance its own creation or its incompatible same-owner concentration replacement. A different owner's already-operative field can. Movement, absorption, suppression or ending after payment never reprices/refunds the committed source. An existing quote rechecks actual eligibility atomically at commitment; an unaffordable final price never partially spends Influence or another resource.

NEW NS9-ENHANCE-01: enhancement is distinct from price and is evaluated at the actual surviving native output stage, after absorption. For a new direct spell/activated technique/item-use output, use its actual acting body (or genuinely autonomous item's origin), not an invented beam or recipient proxy. For a continuing freestanding emitter, use its real current anchor; for a continuing creature/item-attached property, its actual affected body/item supplies the local expression. This permits local ongoing magic created earlier to benefit without treating a remote concentrating caster as the emitter. Select one eligible Nexus across duplicates, oldest establishment then stable ID; distinct instances retain their own clocks. A pure transferred/reflected already-scaled child never qualifies for another output multiplier.

Every eligible magical application gains+2 integrity against a genuinely source-defined opposed counter/dispel/neutralization CHECK: increase that opponent check's DC by2, not source rank. This does not turn an automatic source-permitted removal or suppression into a check, bypass a protected remover, create a saving throw, alter victim save DC/attack bonus or prevent source expiry/concentration failure. At cast-time counter checks, use the actual pending caster/declared manifestation under the same lawful field test; price remains separate. Existing active qualitative magic therefore has a concrete protective enhancement without inventing additional truth, language, recipes or minds. The Nexus does not confer integrity on itself; another actual Nexus can, once, without recursive numerical amplification.

In addition, apply one efficacy axis to each genuine native output, in the following exact priority: (a) numeric damage/healing/tempHP/prevention grant: multiply the native eligible amount by5/4 with floor, after real dice/flats and before defenses or source gain caps; typed damage combines with Furnace under RF9-PROVENANCE-01; (b) actual magical travel/displacement or granted Speed: multiply its maximum eligible distance/Speed by5/4, preserving actual current movement spent, support, occupied body, legal endpoints and transport prohibitions; (c) newly generated magical creature with a real finite source profile: multiply its established maximumHP by5/4, adding the difference to its lawful creationHP without healing an unrelated real donor, duplicating charges or granting actions; (d) an independently granted numeric magical statistic/bonus/penalty magnitude: multiply its nonnegative native magnitude by5/4 with floor, then use the source's stacking/condition restrictions; (e) qualitative sensing, communication, manipulation or utility with an actual positive native range/radius: multiply that one operating range by5/4 while locally eligible, without adding contents/identity or an otherwise missing perception channel; (f) a qualitative output with no earlier applicable axis but an actual finite ELAPSED manifestation lifetime may establish at5/4 of that ordinary lifetime, unless its source separately protects its deadline. Marker-based next-turn windows, source hard caps, held/capture deadlines and finite control-opportunity counts never turn into fractional extra turns. If no lawful scalable axis exists, integrity remains the enhancement. This priority is per native output, not a license to multiply every field of one profile or reapply to a derived child value.

Damage/healing/prevention and newly created finite grants retain the offered enhanced value under their own lifecycle after formation; no later entry offers a refill. Continuing stat/Speed/utility-range expressions recalculate from the unenhanced native baseline and actual spent budget while local and resume the same baseline on exit; they do not stack themselves. A previously existing finiteHP pool/body is not retroactively healed on entry. An actual-paid-base Barrier pool is first5×(actual retained base+fullX); only then the new independent enhancement multiplier may increase the offered prevention, with that bonus explicitly separate from paid-base credit. Same-name pool comparison, caps and exclusions still apply.

Protected numeric domains are not efficacy magnitudes: RP/RC generation or transfer, stored/reserved principal, RP/RC or charge-pool maxima/refill quantities, uses/charges, casting rank, targets/extra copies, Actions/Reactions/turns, cooldown/recharge, time of payment, field geometry/conversion multipliers, dependency budgets and source explicitly nonamplifiable results remain exact. These three resource fields never amplify their own or one another's radii, duration,1.5/1.25 coefficients,half-price operation or conversion ledger; their useful field benefits remain and they may receive the one integrity benefit from a DIFFERENT Nexus. The restriction prevents recursive meta-amplification, not a reclassification of ordinary combat/item/utility magic as ineligible. Conduit/Transfusion principal, Forge cycle5, the actual Reservoir/Soulburn combination and copied-feat caps are not rewritten. The owner-set Mass Suggestion24-hour maximum, actual capture/observation/entitlement deadlines, Sequester consent/wake boundary, regular-turn and death/Stable clocks, and Rank7 derivative resource or agency limits are protected contracts rather than efficacy ranges/durations. Such magic still gains the actual integrity benefit and any genuinely permitted separate output axis; no accepted Rank7 procedure or actual access is rewritten. Actual charge-only/item magic can gain enhancement without its charges being halved. Oracle RC remains RC with no new RP pool; actual removable magical outputs may enhance only within their own permitted source scope.

One Supreme half-price and one enhancement operation apply across duplicates. Ordinary Nexus's one-2 remains earlier, Dampening's spell-only+2 later, and Influence is final; field entry cannot repeatedly apply any of them. Furnace3/2 can multiply surviving eligible spell damage with Supreme5/4 once at the shared rational stage; source-specific class late riders retain their later stage. Suppression removes local enhancement and future pricing eligibility without erasing original deadlines, actual expenditures, consumed uses or already-established finite grants. Actual Greater Dispel or generator absorption ends the field through its actual source interface; no field becomes a source of crafting, hidden knowledge, unearned entitlement or a second resource refund.
