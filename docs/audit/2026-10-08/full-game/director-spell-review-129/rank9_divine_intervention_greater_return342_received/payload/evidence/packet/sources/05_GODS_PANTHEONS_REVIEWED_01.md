## Public faith wording example

Across Aethel, the Fifteenfold Pantheon gives everyday worship familiar names, stories and traditions. These are mortal interpretations of forces whose full nature remains uncertain, not a Director-level proof of cosmic genealogy. Scholars also distinguish the First Choir: seven source-supported named virtues associated with the Spiral — Korvelan, Vaerilune, Delvaran, Shaelara, Xalarex, Thalirion and Virellian. The Choir is not another name for the Fifteen. Calan'thir and Asmodeus are widely credited as the genuine joint builders of the Crucible. A character may sincerely follow a temple, local tradition or philosophy, and real rites or magic may answer that faith, without the character possessing the setting's hidden cosmology.

## Director identity boundary

Keep the owner-locked hierarchy as a distinction between cosmic structure, hidden machinery, custodians and mortal religion, not a new power-ranking or combat system. Exactly seven First Choir members remain separate from the seven named archon witnesses, seven Thrones, mortal aspirants, Fifteenfold worship masks, cultural gods, folk ascendants and class patron titles. Do not supply one-to-one equivalences that the sources do not establish. The prior creation/origin review and its unresolved causal and chronological dependencies remain controlling within their scope.

## Umbrin lore and interpretation

The Obsidian Burn, the exile of Umbrin forebears by Calan'thir and the First Choir, the Umbrin people of The Murk, their recorded geography, and the resulting obsidian-sheen tradition remain locked Umbrin lore. Their demonym and cult traditions invoke the primordial/theological principle called Umbra. Descriptions of 'Umbra's thread' or 'Lumina's glare' are the culture's and tradition's explanation of that history and do not independently establish two literal creator-beings. Preserve the historical/lore ruling while keeping Q5's Lumina/Umbra abstraction boundary.

## Infernal roster and public terms

The original cosmology contains a duplicated infernal roster: P56 associates Mephistopheles with both Maladomini and Cania. Later source passages repeatedly corroborate his Cania association, but no supplied authority repairs Maladomini's ruler or authorizes dual rule. Preserve the duplicate as source evidence. In bounded Director material, Mephistopheles may be described as associated with Cania while Maladomini's occupancy remains disputed. New player prose uses 'Nine Infernal Courts' and 'War of Chains and Teeth' and need not name a disputed Maladomini ruler. Do not import an outside roster or invent a replacement.
