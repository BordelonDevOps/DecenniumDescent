# Mass Undeath2 — proposed static procedures

Every NEW completion below is spell-local, unrated and unimplemented. Native originals remain unchanged.

## field-of-endless-dead

**authorship**: All MU213 profile, admission, scheduling, capacity and ending completions are explicit NEW spell-local proposals for these two identities only. They neither amend historical monsters nor confer class features or CR. Preserve every native card unchanged beside the procedure.

**casting**: Cleric/Druid/Wizard Necromancy rank9,minimumclass17,base26RP,Action120ft. Require actual acquired/prepared source, real components, Action and priced RP. Field V/S/F; Plague V/S/M-or-F, no invented costly reagent. Pay actual chronological cadence and concentration transitions once before counter window. Counter/late failure never refunds. Ready uses actual paid setup/held concentration/Reaction release/next-regular-start expiry; manifestation clock begins on successful release.

**corpseIdentity**: One physical body/death-event allocation per genuine currently dead creature body. Living0HP is not a corpse. Fragments sharing one identity cannot be counted twice; severed fragments must remain recorded and cannot grow into extra bodies. Explicitly protected/nonanimatable source remains protected. Raised/copied/illusory/summoned/spent-fuel remains do not generate a fresh legal corpse by renaming them. A separately legal resurrection/reconstruction and later real death uses its own exact contract; these spells grant neither.

**conversion**: NEW two full chassis in PROFILES. Skeleton requires actual articulated skeletal remains; zombie retains actual non-skeletal corpse material, including non-Humanoid anatomy. No age or Small/Medium/Humanoid-only restriction. Every actual corpse category has the same deterministic material-preserving mapping: skeletal -> skeleton; otherwise zombie. Source-recognized construct corpse can map; arbitrary object rubble is not a corpse. Missing limbs/senses limit actual actions, not admission as an additional corpse. No donor HP, CR, spells, resources, skills, powers, equipment, memories or identity doctrine inherited.

**material**: Body is exclusively consumed as animation fuel in the allocation ledger while its real matter persists physically. Assign animation ID distinct from original body ID; keep original death, anatomy, harvest and custody history. On ending record spent inert remains, never a fresh death/harvest/corpse entitlement. Existing external equipment stays actual owner/custodian/condition; no automatic wield proficiency, loot, tools, XP or new gear. No disintegration of independent matter merely because spell ends.

**control**: NEW permanent/temporary control is a one-way command relation, not shared sight, facts or soul ownership. One normal owner Bonus Action group command selects one of these actual cohorts and a finite order/member assignment. All selected members can each perform one legal Action in the attached phase. This expressly extends the single-body offensive allowance for that one cohort; it consumes the controller shared companion command/offense/support allowance for the numbered round and cannot combine with another owned-asset allowance. No additional owner turn or reset for multiple casts/controllers.

**commandLifecycle**: NEW commands use the operative magical source relation without a distance ceiling, including bodies outside Plague admission geometry; no new perception or remote-information feedback. Controller must be capable at issuance. Later incapacity or removal alone does not cancel an already issued order; actual source ending or body/target ineligibility does. Finite move routes are executed only through space the body can actually navigate with its own senses; attacks name a known target that the body must independently perceive and legally reach. Stable body-ID order immediately after the original creator regular turn; no separate body turn. Order expires at that phase end if impossible, suppressed, disabled, unaffordable or budget-starved. No bank/replay/retarget/refund. Later order replaces unexecuted earlier order; actual controller change cancels pending commands, not source ownership/stats/history. Existing permanent relation resumes only under the actual external control source ending rule, not an invented control override.

**default**: NEW without a valid order, capable members have only defensive movement up to actual remaining Speed and Dodge at the original attached phase; no autonomous attacks, Help, labor, casts, searches, item activation or reactions. Suppressed/disabled members cannot act. Original creator virtual regular markers continue through absence/incapacity/death under the bound scheduling contract; no duplicate phase or fresh budget from control change. Dead/incapacitated controller cannot issue orders; no automatic hostility or successor authority.

**actions**: NEW group order may assign Strike, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Search or simple physical carry/place/pull task. One Action per participating body; no multiattack, professional training, crafting/research credit or automated supply creation. Movement/standing uses actual terrain/pose, occupancy and carrying. Different members retain distinct target/roll/condition/HP histories. Each attack creates its own damage root with actual body as dealer, not automatically caster Null/status. Critical doubles dice only; no count-based merged damage shortcut.

**HP**: Apply only genuinely acquired source-qualified maximumHP modifiers that explicitly include this creation. Necromancy is not Conjuration; Wizard standard-minion scaling/cap and unrelated donor powers do not apply. Full new HP at first lawful animation; no rebind/refill of consumed bodies. Later maxHP rise adds capacity only; fall clamps per accepted ledger. At ordinary0 end only that animation and leave spent remains/cargo. Qualifying actual Null damage at0 gives Disabled/Incapacitated/Speed0 without destruction while parent persists. Actual eligible healing above0 clears Disabled, not costs/timers/history. Independent expiry or dispel still ends a disabled body.

**suppression**: NEW suppressing an individual animation/control manifestation makes that real body inert at its actual pose; keeps HP, source deadline, allocation and external cargo. No disappearance, free placement, healing or replacement. Inert body remains damageable under this chassis; real support/falling follows its own rules. Resume only a still-existing positiveHP source into legally available expression; no overlap/crushing or replay of missed commands. Unavailable physical clearance keeps that expression inert until actually clear. Suppression at formation prevents that payload under actual incoming/outgoing suppression scope, with paid costs retained.

**termination**: Each animated body is a distinct ongoing rank9 application of one generating cast. A targeted eligible dispel can end its captured application; Greater Dispel uses one generating-cast check for captured bodies, not global off-area ending. No unauthorized provider acquisition inferred. Source expiry/concentration ending for Plague ends all remaining applications; Field has no natural expiry. Every terminal callback is idempotent; real cargo remains at actual physical position/support. No future original body/charges/matter regeneration.

**privacy**: Public legal selection differs from paid hidden predicates. Plague returns no corpse-count/identity/inventory diagnostic to caster. Internal ledger can record hidden outcomes without player disclosure. No corpse becomes immune merely because it is hidden, nor eligible merely because it is named.

**admission**: NEW choose exactly eight distinct actual eligible bodies within120ft, each perceived with line of effect and complete physical allocation/anatomy. Choose skeleton only for actual skeletal remains; zombie for retained corpse tissue/material; a stated mixture is permitted. If fewer than8 are knowingly legal, no cast/no payment, not a reduced-effect alternative. At resolution atomically revalidate all8. A late invalid/protected/reserved/consumed body makes the paid Field payload fail entirely with no fallback or copied corpse.

**permanence**: NEW select one continuing animation-and-control application per body, with no natural deadline and no concentration. This reconciles permanent control with Until dispelled: neither a rest, elapsed minute, distance, owner absence nor owner death ends these applications. Actual eligible dispelling/ordinary destruction/source-specific ending does. Ending does not leave uncontrolled active undead; it leaves spent inert original matter.

**capacity**: NEW each successful cast adds a distinct eight-body cohort. No cap3, cap1, global population limit or replacement. Failed recast changes no prior cohort. Rebinding an existing animation does not create another body or HP pool. The paid group command still selects only one cohort per shared command allowance. No new remote voluntary dismissal is added; ending uses a real dispel or source-specific terminal.

**service**: Permanent service remains useful through repeated actual finite group orders. Source bodies can carry/hold/move actual objects with real anatomy and source task time; no rest/food/trade/research credit arises automatically. Action, load, injuries, material and costs persist across all elapsed opportunities.

Each eligible body is consumed once; other failures do not cancel admitted bodies or refund the single cast.

Expiry is processed before new events at equality. Independent caster death/incapacity/concentration failure ends parent by its actual rule. Leaving the original sphere never ends/re-admits a body. On expiry/dismissal by ending concentration/eligible whole-parent termination, each surviving or Null-disabled member becomes spent inert remains with unchanged real gear. No uncontrolled permanent population.

**capacity**: Every admitted body belongs to this cast cohort; no cap8 or screen/simulation cap. A new concentration cast ends old concentration at commitment, even if new payload later fails. Field nonconcentration cohorts remain independent. No donor-resource inheritance, new corpse fuel or administrative order replay.
