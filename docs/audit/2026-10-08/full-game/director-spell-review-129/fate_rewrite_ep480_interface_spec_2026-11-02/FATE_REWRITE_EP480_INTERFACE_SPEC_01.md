## Required interface evidence after its dependencies pass

The following are required bindings for a later, focused interface packet. No values or event facts are supplied here.

1. Guarantee source record: bind one actual source-complete guarantee history with legal creation/acquisition and cast permissions; actual paid cast and response/counter history; stable source IDs; the exact immutable obligation and original deadline; actual fulfillment before that deadline; and every causal descendant through the observed present. A successful conditional delivery alone does not satisfy TF192-B02.
2. Eternal Prophecy test branch provenance: if proposal 03 is separately adopted and its T1–T4 tests are constructed and Director-reviewed, bind the exact accepted TEST_ONLY branch, source hashes, paid cast receipt, obligation ID, terminal event, fulfillment event, versions, custody, and forward/inverse evidence. Do not promote those hypothetical facts to campaign facts or native-source qualification.
3. Compatible Fate Rewrite: bind one actual eligible event with timestamp within the procedure's 24-hour inclusive window, a real knowledge route and surviving anchor within 120 ft on the same plane with direct line of effect, one concrete lawful alternative, and a complete causal descendant history through the chosen present. Show a useful change while preserving the committed guarantee predicate and all fulfilled descendants.
4. Conflicting request: bind a later request that actually conflicts with the committed obligation at the exact procedure stage being tested. Identify the request, relevant obligation version, decision point, and whether any cost is due at that stage. The existing prophecy-creation-conflict case is only a precommit refusal with empty obligations and zero RP spent; it cannot stand in for this conflict branch.
5. Release-time checks: revalidate all affected versions, event age, knowledge, anchor, plane, line of effect, costs, body/soul eligibility when relevant, and exact current item custody at release. Preserve sunk costs; do not refund the already-paid Fate Rewrite or prophecy cast because history changes.
6. Global accounting: show one current history, one body per creature, one current custody per unique item, no duplicated rewards or objects, retained original memories for actual witnesses, and no free knowledge transfer to nonwitnesses. Include every affected outsider and descendant or keep the whole case held.
7. Reversibility: give a separate branch-start projection and proposal overlay; ordered forward and inverse operations with preconditions; deterministic state hashes; exact restoration to each branch's own start; and an idempotent retry check. Do not import a successful delivery, cost, or state from a sibling branch.
