# Rank7 Gate/Glyph3 — proposed operational procedures

Authority: 686 reviewed/applied; 162 pending; zero accepted-unapplied; 410 complete reviewed standalone procedures. This batch remains **accepted=false, applied=false**. Every NEW rule is spell-local proposed authorship.

## chain-gate

**KEEP**

Action; range 120 feet; V/S/F; rank 7/base 17 RP; concentration up to 60 seconds. Preserve exactly three linked portals appearing in rapid sequence and the Planar tag.

**NEW_topology**

Select C01-A: three fixed vertical apertures, each five feet wide and eight feet high, directed A→B→C→A. Front-face entry only; no reverse route, later link selection or six-aperture interpretation. All are opaque and transmit no sight, sound, spell/attack/sensor line, projectile, loose object, independent fluid or vehicle. Only creatures with their actually lawful carried goods/creatures can transit. No imported one-transit or 500-lb limit; actual capacity, fit and support remain mandatory.

**NEW_sites**

Select C02-A: A is a perceived supported local pose within 120 feet and caster line of effect on the actual current plane. B and C are exact personally known registered sites with genuine plane keys and portal poses. NEW remote selection has no geographic range ceiling, but require three pairwise distinct actual planes. Earned personal/site evidence and current legal poses must exist; rumor, Wheel labels, alignment, Heartcell theory or an invented plane/site cannot substitute. Missing endpoints are a whole-case hold/preflight rejection, not a license to populate the registry from schematic tests.

**NEW_establishment**

Validate all three complete aperture poses and required arrival spaces before payment and again at resolution. One cast, payment and counter window. Manifest A, then B, then C within one atomic resolution; no aperture is usable until all three complete. A hidden invalid endpoint defeats the entire establishment without refund or retargeting. One parent application and one active network per caster. New concentration ends an incompatible prior network at commitment even if the new cast fails. Deadline is successful network completion plus 60 seconds. Apertures have no physical HP or load-bearing body.

**NEW_transit**

Select C03-A: planar/extradimensional physical-gateway travel, NOT teleportation. Every edge crosses actual distinct planes. Test exact origin/destination blockers for every body in the whole group. Actual Dimensional Anchor blocks its matching endpoints; actual No Escape blocks its own voluntary extradimensional predicate, while teleport-only Verdict/Reign clauses do not automatically apply. No class feature or ward is acquired merely by citing it. Destination Planar Protection evaluates prospective actual origin before arrival; successful travel alone commits lastArrivedFromPlaneKey. No native-plane or soul fact follows.

**NEW_crossing_transaction**

Voluntary entry costs five feet of ordinary movement with real terrain/squeeze/crawl costs; approach remains ordinary movement. Independent lawful forced movement can cross only with five feet of actual remaining displacement and valid whole path/fit; it grants no extra force, consent immunity or action. Genuine squeezing permission is required, including height/anatomy. Revalidate the same complete body/cargo group. Preserve relative poses, rotate entry-forward to exit-front, and map lateral/vertical coordinates. Place the group immediately in front of the exit, using only the minimal front-normal offset needed for its entire volume and actual support. No adjacent safe-space search or alternative endpoint. A known illegal attempt spends no five-foot debit. A hidden/new failure after legal commitment spends the attempted movement/force cost but leaves every body/item at origin. Each later crossing requires a new real entry/cost; arrival never automatically loops through the cycle.

**NEW_lifecycle**

Select C06-A: local suppression of B disables A→B and B→C, leaving C→A only if both endpoints remain valid. Never reconnect A→C. No queued crossings; original deadline continues. Whole-parent removal/dispel, concentration loss/death/incapacity, dismissal, replacement or expiry closes all nodes. A legal already-resolving atomic crossing finishes before closure; no new crossing starts exactly at expiry. No split, ejection, recall or rewind of completed travel. Restored local expression keeps the original sites, graph and deadline.

## symbol

**KEEP**

Action; range 120 feet; V/S/M-or-F; rank 7/base 17 RP. Create a harmful magical glyph. Preserve actual reusable focus substitution with no unprinted expensive component.

**NEW_payload**

Select S01-A: one native payload only, a 10-foot-radius force burst. Roll one shared 6d8 force pool; each actual creature makes DEX against captured casting DC, full damage on failure and half rounded down on success. Include owner, allies, triggering entrant and initial occupants actually in the pulse. Require body-interior/clear-origin line of effect; actual half/three-quarters cover adds +2/+5 to DEX, total cover excludes. No Stun, direct kill, stored spell, external eight-symbol menu or borrowed Glyph of Warding mode.

**NEW_inscription_and_trigger**

Select S02-A and S03-A, with the full shared glyph rules: one stationary face at most one square foot, a 60-second nonconcentration armed application, future genuine proximity entry or failed disarm, and one consumed-before-payload discharge. Instantaneous is inscription metadata. No initial-occupant or HP-query trigger. Snapshot original class/DC/rank/owner at casting; roll the single damage pool at discharge and one real save per recipient.

**NEW_damage_boundary**

Create a separate damage root per recipient only when an actual unsuppressed payload resolves, with original glyph/cast/trigger/dealer provenance. Evaluate genuine dealer-owned Null at that root’s creation; owner labels alone grant no Veil. Apply actual defenses, one transfer, temporary HP, Instant Death, floors, Wounds, replacements, ordinary death and concentration in their accepted order. Never count parent and terminal transfer branches twice. Creator death preserves the finite glyph/source statistics but grants no new Action or dead-owner resource benefit. This procedure does not reopen the damage engine.

## symbol-of-death

**KEEP**

Action; range 120 feet; V/S/F; rank 7/base 17 RP. A rune kills nearby creatures with 60 HP or fewer. Killing is not a damage packet or an approximation that merely sets HP to zero.

**NEW_anchor_and_trigger**

Select D04-A: the full shared stationary-face, 10-foot-radius, 60-second nonconcentration, one-charge entry/disarm-trigger rules apply to this distinct identity. Range 120 is inscription range, not death radius. Initial occupancy does not trigger; owner/allies are not exempt from a real discharge.

**NEW_threshold**

Select D01-A: once per discharge, after the trigger packet and before replacements, snapshot the actual current normal HP of each actual recipient. Sixty qualifies, sixty-one does not. Exclude temporary HP and buffers; use the actual operative HP ledger, without combining hypothetical form/root HP pools. Living Dying0 and Stable0 qualify. Already Dead remains cannot be killed again. Active Undead, Constructs and finite bodies are not immune by name, but require their actual source-qualified kill/destruction endpoint; an incomplete profile is a whole-branch hold. Objects are not creatures. No threshold query is exposed before expenditure and no same-pulse rescan follows a replacement.

**NEW_direct_death**

Select D02-A and D03-A: add no new save. Each qualified recipient gets one direct nondamage would-die attempt with original caster, glyph, trigger and death-attempt ID. Genuine death immunity/exceptions and actually owned unspent would-die replacements apply once, then commit the real terminal lifecycle/remains/custody. No damage root, necrotic dice, temporary-HP absorption, Wound save, damage-resource trigger or damage-only Null/Undying Spellform substitution. Ordinary Death Ward does not cover this outright death.

**NEW_genuine_ritual**

An actual Cleric16 Cultist Ritual of Undeath living-ally mark must first be legally acquired and paid: Action, three Conviction, six RP, one use per long rest, actual 30-foot living-ally target. Its first would-die event instead leaves the target at one HP and temporarily both Humanoid and Undead until that target’s next original regular end. Preserve Wounds/resources. This is a replacement, not ordinary healing, and is not loaned to a Wizard or granted by this rune.

**NEW_successive_events**

A later independent rune snapshots the actual state after the earlier event, including spent replacements. Thus event one may leave a protected recipient at one HP, while event two may genuinely kill it. A consumed rune or duplicate callback never rescans its own survivor. An already-dead identity supplies no new death or body entitlement. Hunt finality/rare intervention applies only where its actual source/state is established; this spell invents neither a cosmology nor a resurrection permission/block. Suppression, dispel and expiry do not undo completed death.

NEW, only Symbol and Symbol of Death: inscribe one perceived coherent writable noncreature solid face of at most one square foot within 120 feet and line of effect. **GG141-GLYPH-TRIGGER**

NEW: one charge. At inscription record every body already intersecting the glyph’s 10-foot-radius sphere; initial occupancy does not trigger. The first subsequent genuine creature entry from outside to a body-interior intersection, with clear glyph-to-interior line of effect, triggers while armed and unsuppressed. Owner and allies are included. This is local physical proximity, not a visual identity, alignment, language or HP detector. Darkness/invisibility do not prevent mechanical proximity, nor does the sensor reveal an unseen body to observers. Resolve the actual movement/forced-movement/teleport-arrival packet first; evaluate ordinary movement in real segments no longer than five feet, without granting movement. Intervening solid total cover clips sensing. Removing cover, resuming suppression, changing HP, loading a scene or remaining inside is not entry. An actual failed disarm manipulation is the separate explicit trigger below.

**GG141-DISPATCH**

After the triggering packet, process eligible glyphs in creation-time then application-ID order. Revalidate live source, deadline, anchor and suppression at each dispatch. Consume that glyph’s charge and record DISCHARGED before resolving its payload, even if no recipient qualifies or an effect is prevented. Trigger receipts are unique; stale callbacks cannot discharge again. The payload is not another casting, Action, RP payment, Refrain event or Counterspell opportunity. Within one pulse freeze actual recipients/positions/line of effect and its threshold snapshot once. Resolve actual replacements before the next independent pulse. Damage, death and HP changes are not entry events and cannot recursively trigger a rune chain.

**GG141-DISARM**

NEW: an actor already lawfully within five feet who can perceive and physically reach the exposed face may spend an actual Action on an Intelligence (Arcana) task against captured glyph DC. Add only actual owned proficiency; use Core task degrees/natural shifts, with either upper degree succeeding. Success physically disrupts and ends that one inscription without a payload. Failure spends the Action and, if the rune is armed and unsuppressed, causes its one discharge. A failure under suppression causes no queued discharge. One attempt per actor per application; no free retry. Ordinary approach still triggers before reaching disarm range if it creates entry. Glyph Trace highlights only its actual accessible active face and grants no decoding or disarm. Covering a face is not disarming it. Destroying the marked face requires a genuine physical source/profile; no generic object-damage permission is added.

**GG141-END**

Glyph expiry is exactly creation plus 60 elapsed seconds and precedes optional use at equality. Local suppression keeps original deadline/charge but loses entry opportunities without a queue. Recipient manifestations obey actual local antimagic scope. Resume only for future genuine entries; do not replay missed events. Exact eligible dispel operations capture their real applications. Acquired Wizard Arch Dispel is rank 3, costs 3 RP and checks INT+PB against 10+the original rank, hence DC17 here; it is not assumed rank-5 Greater Dispel. Countered inscription creates nothing. Ending/suppression never undoes completed harm, death, movement, costs or stock history. Retain all inherited holds; no runtime, balance, cosmology or release approval follows.
