## What It Is

Ferry-Wraith of the Hush enforces Hush obligations. It prefers controlled panic to open slaughter, because a frightened survivor can sign, testify, carry debt, or lead the Broker to a more valuable target.

## What Experienced Expeditions Notice

Blank spaces in ledgers; bells wrapped in cloth; witnesses who repeat the same sentence word for word; quarantine marks that do not match local practice; doors that open only after someone states a name. Heat appears in sealed metal before open flame.

## How It Fights

It opens with **Hellfire Claw** against the target most capable of controlling its movement. It keeps a witness, hostage, or written object between itself and the Expedition and withdraws into jurisdiction rather than dying for pride.

## How to Defeat It

**Prepare Before Contact**

- Prepare resistance or mitigation for fire damage.
- Carry blank witness cards and refuse unexamined aid.

**Combat Priority**

Destroy or isolate the seal-bearing implement, then pressure the Broker away from witnesses.

**Exploit**

The defining weakness is **destroyed or invalidated contract seals**. Treat this as an encounter solution rather than an automatic damage vulnerability. Successful Research, Recon, or Resource actions should make the exploit easier or safer.

**Withdraw When**

Withdraw or change the objective when the creature reaches favorable terrain or isolates a target. A failed retreat against this creature usually costs more than abandoning the immediate treasure.

## Habitat, Goals, and Lair

Broker sites are legal terrain as much as physical terrain. Seals, witness circles, sample cabinets, toll chains, and written names can become hazards. Destroying the room may activate clauses rather than end them.

Hush-Brokers are fiendish intermediaries who convert fear, silence, quarantine, and contract language into enforceable power. They prefer an Expedition that survives indebted to them over one that dies free.

They bargain sincerely inside the exact wording they offer. Precision protects an Expedition better than moral outrage.
