# Bone Cage

Status: proposed authored procedure; independent exact acceptance/application remain separate.

Action; range 60 feet; V/S/F. KEEP creation of a bone cage to trap one creature. NEW select one perceived Large-or-smaller creature and a fixed supported cage position around its actual current body with line of effect. LM130-CAGE-01 — NEW floorless geometry explicitly replaces the old twelve-foot exterior-cube proposal: in local coordinates the interior is x/y[-5,5],z[0,10], with a12x12x11-foot exterior envelope x/y[-6,6],z[0,11]. There is NO floor or bottom face and no material below the supported floorz0. Four side walls and the roof touch the actual supporting surface without lifting the target. Its base must have actual support for a 100-pound structure plus whatever load it already actually bears, with no new bridge/floor spanning a void. The cage is an immovable anchored structure, not a portable vehicle. Before commitment its whole frame must fit without intersecting other creatures, real barriers or displacing the target; no free cutting, crushing, lift or insertion through a wall. The selected target makes DEX. Success causes this proposed cage to fail to form, without a free dodge movement or enclosure of an alternative creature. Failure forms it if placement remains legal. NEW replace Instantaneous with concentration up to sixty elapsed seconds. It has one tracked structure AC 15 and HP 30 across all faces, with vertical bars separated by three-inch gaps; no separate cheaper bar/hinge HP is invented. Bone-like conjured material is resource-ineligible. The captive has its actual movement inside, Actions, Bonus Actions, Reactions, speech, senses and component capability; trapped does not impose Restrained, Incapacitated, Paralyzed, Silence or hard-Action denial. Actual anatomy small enough to fit a three-inch gap may pass using ordinary movement; no whole-body passage is inferred solely from a Tiny label. The bars supply NEW half cover, +2 AC and DEX saves, only to a target whose actual attack/effect path crosses them under the ordinary cover rule. They do not block sight or all line of effect, prevent a fitting reach/weapon from passing, or create an automatic line to something otherwise hidden. Actual physical projectiles/objects must fit a gap. Spells retain their own targeting/geometry and only damage this structure if they really include objects. A reachable legal object attack from inside or outside uses the cage’s one AC/HP record; ordinary object type defenses apply, with NEW no additional cage resistance/immunity. At zero structure HP the entire cage ends, leaving a normal opening and actual bodies/cargo in place. NEW an adjacent creature can instead spend an Action on STR (Athletics) against the spell’s snapshotted DC; Core Success/Critical Success permanently bends open one chosen whole ten-foot face of this instance, while failure changes nothing and a later paid attempt is allowed. This work causes no damage to the captive and no damage-derived trigger. A legal teleport can leave; Speed 0 or a restraint immunity is not a general cage escape, and the cage adds no teleport ban. The empty cage remains until ending/replacement, never pursuing its former captive or re-closing an opened face. Concentration loss, expiry, qualifying removal or full creating-source suppression ends the structure permanently under the shared rule, with no renewable bones or later reformation around entrants.

LM130-CAGE-02 — NEW exact bars and fit. The cage has fixed local orientation chosen before commitment. Each x-facing wall occupies x[5,6] or[-6,-5]; vertical bars have y centers-5.875+0.5k for k0..23, width0.25ft in y and z[0,11]. The y-facing walls use the symmetric x-center list and y[5,6]/[-6,-5]. Corner posts occupy x/y end bands[5,6] or[-6,-5],z[0,11]. Roof strips occupy z[10,11], span x[-6,6] at the same y centers, width0.25, with the perpendicular symmetric cross strips. Open gaps are0.25ft=3in; overlap within the same frame is one structure, not duplicated HP/material. The100lb total is the authored structure mass, not a derived bone density. All perimeter-contact/support points must be on actual load-bearing surface; the cage supports no ground under its interior. Require the complete actual target and every retained gear/body part to fit the10ft interior and all actual frame parts to fit clear real space. Zero-thickness contact at a boundary is permitted; positive-volume overlap is not. A10x10x10 body only fits if its real extremities/gear fit and there is11ft roof clearance; a center/token test does not establish this.
Opening a side with the real Athletics Action removes its bars through that face's10x10 clear aperture, preserving corner/roof supports, one remaining HP track and a permanent opened-face key. A roof-face opening can be selected only with actual reach/work access; it does not give flight. No bottom opening or underfloor tunnel is created. Crossed-bar half cover is the selected local rule even when the actual effect's permitted narrow path uses a gap; independent total cover still blocks that path. Actual projectiles need their own physical gap fit, and object attacks need actual source object eligibility.
LM130-CAGE-03 — NEW source-specific suppression selection. Compute the union of real operative suppression volumes after independent total-cover clipping. The cage's own conjured frame does not self-shield the evaluation of its own suppression; this is only a Bone-Cage local source-expression order, not a global Antimagic amendment. Partial coverage removes only intersecting frame expression; HP, opened faces, original concentration and original60sec deadline persist. On resumption a formerly suppressed portion returns only if clear; an actually occupied portion stays absent/pending until clear, never cuts/pushes/crushes. No HP or material regenerates. If ALL still-present frame material is covered by that real union, full creating-source suppression ends the whole cage permanently, clears its owner's concentration and never resumes even if fields later end. Caster-only remote suppression does not reach a cage. In the supplied two-field fixture centers(+1,0,5.5) and(-1,0,5.5),radius10, every exterior point is within at least one sphere because (|x|-1)^2+y^2+(z-5.5)^2<=25+36+30.25=91.25<100. One sphere alone leaves real far corners outside. The accepted Antimagic same-family union rule is retained. Ending by HP0, concentration, full suppression, actual removal or60sec expiry releases bodies/gear where they really are, supplies no inventory bone, and cannot convert the cage into a bridge.
