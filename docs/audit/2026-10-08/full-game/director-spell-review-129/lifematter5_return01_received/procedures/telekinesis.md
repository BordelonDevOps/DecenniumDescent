# Telekinesis

Status: proposed authored procedure; independent exact acceptance/application remain separate.

KEEP Action,60feet,Instantaneous and moving objects OR creatures with the mind. NEW choose one perceived creature or one coherent nonliving object/actual contained cargo lot, Large or smaller and total moved weight at most1000lb including gear, with line of effect. Resolve one instantaneous displacement up to30feet along a declared continuous physically clear path whose every point remains within60feet and caster line of effect; horizontal,vertical and combinations are allowed, with one total30-foot path length. No maintained hand/beam/concentration, free later Action, suspended hover or extra worker remains. A willing creature may accept movement; an unwilling one makes STR vs spellDC, success negates. An unattended unanchored object has no save. A genuinely held loose object instead lets its resisting holder make STR; failure releases that object if physically releasable, success prevents it. Worn/fastened/embedded items, rooted structures, anchored mechanisms and solidly attached cargo cannot be torn free by this spell; target the whole otherwise eligible body/object or use a separately legal release first. There is no contested-check replacement or automatic dismembering attack. A legal declared path can end in air: once the instantaneous support ends, normal unsupported falling begins under the existing Fall Marker, with ordinary actual height and consequences; movement itself adds no attack roll, collision damage, Prone, Grappled or Restrained. Stop at the last legal position if a real blocker appears during resolution; the spell cannot phase through it or crush crowding bodies. Forced separation can end a genuine grapple when reach breaks, while an absolute legal anchor still prevents movement. Real moving-object/terrain hazards apply only on their own printed triggers, once with actual causal identity. Manipulation is gross translation/reorientation of that one object, including moving a freely hinged door if it fits and is not latched/locked; it is not lockpicking, disarming a mechanism, wielding a weapon attack, administering a potion, loading, crafting, providing spell components or supplying a missing hand/tool qualification. Real custody/location and load change once; it neither melds gear nor duplicates a body or inventory. Another actual paid cast may move something again with its own real Action/RP/Dissonance, but there is no stored movement or per-round free repeat from the first one. This explicitly preserves the supplied instantaneous model instead of importing a long-duration external Telekinesis spell.

LM130-TELEKINESIS-01 — NEW complete motion and holder arbitration. The30ft bound is the entire continuous path, not the endpoint diagonal. Every actual point of the complete body plus lawful gear/contained lot must remain in fitting space, within60ft and caster line of effect throughout. Record actual body/gear masses separately; no body-mass or container-content exclusion. A20ft horizontal segment followed by10ft vertical uses30, never sqrt(500). Move no other held creature as gear. All actual resisting holders of a loose object independently make STR; genuinely consenting holders may waive. Only if every resisting holder fails and every attachment releases legally does the single atomic move clear all old heldBy entries. A success/absolute fastening keeps custody and moves nothing; a failed holder is not prematurely disarmed while another holder prevents the move. Cast costs remain spent after a legally committed failure. No holder spends an Action merely to save.
LM130-TELEKINESIS-02 — NEW narrow hinged-door exception. Only an already freely rotating, unlocked/unlatched hinge may remain attached while its single rigid leaf rotates about its actual fixed axis. No translation of that hinge, tearing, unlocking or missing tool/attack Action is granted. The motion budget is the greatest arc length traced by any real material point, and all those swept points obey full fit/range/LoE; hinge displacement0 is not the motion length. The case's4ft-wide,0.25ft-thick leaf rotating90deg has maximum radius sqrt(16+1/64), arc(pi/2)*sqrt(16+1/64)<6.29ft, with an actual swept quarter-sector. A blocked sweep cannot use only clear endpoints as proof.
LM130-TELEKINESIS-03 — INHERITED Falling. The instantaneous support ends immediately at the actual endpoint. The10ft lift establishes a real Falling record at source-turn0.2, first fixed Fall Marker at that initiative slot's next-round END7. At7, descend10 to actual support; min(10,floor(10/20))d6=0dice, no damage and no damage-based Prone. This is one real fall and landing, not an omitted event or immediate descent. A real rescue can occur only through its own Action/Reaction, source/cost/range and original marker. Suppression/casting cannot create hover or a later free move from this spell.
