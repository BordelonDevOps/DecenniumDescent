## JC-D02. Resolve classification, the word “one,” and the eligible beneficiary

**NEW classification:** These outputs are durable magical nonartifact equipment. Their printed modifier is passive. Neither that modifier nor their charge use requires attunement, RP, concentration or spellcasting. Charge use receives its own finite activation procedure in JC-D07.

**NEW interpretation of “one”:** The printed modifier applies **once to an individual qualifying check or saving throw**, and remains available for later independently qualifying checks or saves while the item remains eligible. It is not consumed, is not limited to once per dawn, and does not require selecting one permanent skill when crafting or equipping.

This explicitly resolves the ambiguous source wording. It does **not** mean every roll qualifies: JC-D03 supplies the exhaustive domains, and JC-D04 limits combination.

The beneficiary is the **actual wearer making that check or save**. Ownership, an adjacent ally, a handler, a thief merely touching the item, or a separate person performing a task for the wearer receives nothing. Unlike the reviewed container benefits, Jewelcrafting has no operator-benefit route.

The item must be complete, at positive structural HP, unsuppressed, unmelded, genuinely fitted and actually worn in its designated legal benefit position. Evaluate those premises for the physical item at the roll’s commitment checkpoint.

Zero remaining charges does not disable the passive modifier. Concealing a legally worn jewel beneath compatible clothing does not disable it; these jewels do not inherit Tailoring’s visible-presentation or garment-coverage requirements.

These cards grant **no AC-enhancement candidate, including zero**, no resistance, immunity, attack bonus, proficiency, increased ability score, carrying relief or additional spell access. They cannot cancel a selected Crude suit’s −1 by supplying a phantom +0.

### Common exclusions and causal boundaries

**NEW:** Across all fourteen tags, exclude attack rolls, damage rolls, initiative, death saves, concentration checks/saves, crafting completion, formal formula research, harvesting/extraction, repair and combat-maneuver checks such as grapple, shove, trip or disarm.

Ordinary recognition or Medicine checks explicitly admitted above are not thereby converted into a crafting, harvesting or research procedure. A save against an imposed effect remains distinct from a combat-maneuver check.

A spell causing psychic damage can qualify for both Psychic and Arcane, but remains **one save**. A poison save covering both poison damage and Poisoned is also one save. Poisoned’s disadvantage on a later unrelated check does not make that later check poison-related.

For Arcane, distinguish the direct magical effect from a secondary event: a save expressly required by a spell can qualify; an ordinary Wound save following its damage does not qualify merely because the earlier damage was magical. It may independently qualify for Vitality.

**Unholy is a mechanical label here, not a moral or cosmological finding.** No new damage type is created. A donor’s name, “soul,” “spirit,” “sentient,” or an Oracle feature named Curse does not establish any predicate by itself.

## JC-D04. Extend the existing check family, but keep saving throws separate

**NEW cross-trade adoption:** Jewelcrafting flat modifiers on ability checks join **`equipment.leather-task-enhancement`**, alongside the already reviewed Leatherworking and Tailoring modifiers.

For one actual ability check, apply the **strongest independently qualifying modifier from these three trades once**. Do not add them together. This does not expand either older trade’s task domains.

**NEW save family:** Jewelcrafting flat save modifiers use **`equipment.jewel-save-enhancement`**. Apply the strongest independently qualifying Jewelcrafting value once to the save. This grants no save eligibility to Leatherworking or Tailoring.

For both comparisons, validate all equipment designations and physical compatibility **before filtering candidates for the particular roll**. A nonqualifying, suppressed or Broken item does not automatically vacate its physical or designated position. Each candidate needs its own eligibility evidence; a qualifying item cannot qualify an unrelated stronger item.

Class, ancestry, spell, tool and other reviewed benefits retain their own combination rules. This is not a universal strongest-bonus rule.

**NEW charge arbitration:** At most **one Jewelcrafting item may spend one charge on a given roll event**, even when several tags qualify. That item must independently qualify, but need not provide the winning flat modifier. A qualifying +1 jewel can supply advantage while a qualifying +2 jewel, Tailoring garment or Leatherworking item supplies the selected flat check modifier.

Use ordinary advantage/disadvantage cancellation. Several sources of advantage do not overcome one source of disadvantage.

## JC-D07. Define one before-dice charge checkpoint and atomic expenditure

**NEW activation:** A conscious, non-Incapacitated wearer may choose to spend one charge immediately before their qualifying roll is committed.

This choice costs **no separate Action, Bonus Action, Reaction, free interaction, RP, speech or hand manipulation**. It is an item-specific voluntary activation, not a spell. Reaction denial or Surprise alone does not prohibit it; an independently applicable activation prohibition still does.

Use this sequence:

1. Establish the real check/save, actor, governing source, legal equipment designations and each candidate’s tag eligibility.
2. Before any die or replacement result for that event is revealed, identify the one jewel supplying advantage.
3. Revalidate the item and its available charge at commitment. Debit one charge and register its advantage for that roll.
4. Resolve the roll and all ordinary modifier/cancellation rules.

For tabletop use, this is “declare, mark off the charge, then roll.” An implementation must prevent concurrent requests from spending the same available charge twice.

A declaration after seeing the first die, another advantage die, a replacement result, or success/failure is too late. It cannot become a retrospective reroll.

An ineligible declaration grants nothing and spends nothing. A declaration canceled before the roll commits releases any reservation without a debit. Once the roll commits and its dice/result procedure begins, the charge remains spent even if the result fails, is discarded, or a later effect makes the outcome irrelevant. Do not provide result-dependent refunds.

Spending while advantage already exists is **legal but potentially wasted**. Spending when advantage and disadvantage will cancel is also legal; the charge is still spent. It never grants a third die or “overcomes” disadvantage by supplying more advantage sources.

No charge can force a roll where the governing rule supplies automatic success/failure or no roll. Incapacitation does not cancel an otherwise eligible passive modifier, but it bars this optional activation. Unconscious automatic STR/DEX-save failure is not overcome.

Eligibility adjudication supplies no new knowledge of hidden DCs, causes, sources or private lore.

### Rerolls, copies and repeated saves

**NEW:** Record one roll-event identity for the whole check/save, including its advantage/disadvantage dice and any replacement procedure that explicitly belongs to that same event.

An authorized same-event reroll follows its own exact text. It does not open another Jewelcrafting expenditure window or produce another pair of dice automatically. Existing event modifiers remain only as that procedure permits.

Copying a die result does not copy the originating item’s flat modifier, advantage grant or charge transaction. Copying an entire resolved total does not permit adding the same modifier again.

A genuinely new later save against an ongoing effect is a new event: reevaluate current equipment and tag eligibility, and allow a new optional charge expenditure. The jewel itself creates no repeat saves.

## JC-D08. Establish persistent charge ledgers and one authoritative dawn series

**PRESERVED:** Each source supplies a per-item maximum of 1–6 charges and says charges return at dawn.

**NEW:** First lawful creation of an imprint initializes that physical item’s current charges to its exact source maximum **once**. Even an output emerging Broken receives that ledger, but cannot use it while Broken.

Track at least the physical item ID, recipe/imprint identity, maximum charges, current charges, creation event, charge transactions keyed to roll events, last processed dawn index, and condition/suppression history needed to resolve elapsed events. Preserve the ledger through every legitimate transfer, storage operation and reconstruction.

Removing, selling, stealing, equipping, copying a data record, changing owner, refitting, entering initiative or starting a new scene never initializes it again.

### Dawn convention

The supplied excerpts establish campaign elapsed time and ordered events, but **do not specify a dawn hour**.

**NEW Jewelcrafting-only recovery convention:** Use **06:00 on the recorded campaign clock, once per 24 elapsed campaign hours**, everywhere. Record one fixed campaign epoch for this series before play. This is a recovery convention, not an astronomical or cosmological assertion and not a change to other features’ independently defined dawn rules.

For fixed epoch \(D_0\):

\[
D_n=D_0+n\times86{,}400\text{ campaign seconds}
\]

Use an event identity such as `(campaignId, jewelcrafting-dawn, n)`. Local sunrise, travel, underground location, displayed clock changes, initiative and rest do not create additional events.

A legitimate refill sets current charges to maximum; it does not add maximum to the existing balance or bank unused charges.

Process each event at most once per physical item. Record an event as processed even when the item is ineligible to recharge. A missed dawn is not a pending refill.

When advancing across several days, resolve eligibility at the actual dawns using the intervening state history. Do not use the item’s current state to rewrite what happened at earlier dawns.

Use the reviewed event-ordering procedure for simultaneous events. When dawn coincides with a regular turn start, it occupies the non-action recharge stage after start-expiring effects and before the later start-stage effects. At other simultaneous checkpoints, retain stable registered event order. An item created at that timestamp initializes once and cannot replay a previously processed dawn.

Actual consecutive dawns can legitimately permit expenditure just before one and again after its refill. No rolling 24-hour cooldown is added. Conversely, clock relabeling or revisiting a location cannot replay the same dawn. **This does not enable Time Loop or authorize resource-history rollback.**
