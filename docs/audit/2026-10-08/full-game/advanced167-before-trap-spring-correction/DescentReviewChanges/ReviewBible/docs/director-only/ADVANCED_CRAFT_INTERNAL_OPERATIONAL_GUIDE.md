**Authored candidate appendix; exact operational approval pending.** Use with all167 internal cards. These complete procedure sections preserve the final joint source directions verbatim; numeric effects are instantiated on each card. ## AC-01 — Complete bodies and exact output identity

**Affected: all 167. KEEP the complete-body principle; NEW advanced body bindings below.** Each successful output transforms one separately reserved complete mundane body, not a fraction of a reusable source tool or an intact duplicate of a consumed core. Reserve `floor(1.5Q)` eligible bodies before work, where Q is original Standard quantity. A body is one whole selected model, not one charge, page, participant, automaton encounter or programmed task. Fixed models and actual alternate EN weapon/armor parent records must be chosen before work; one model/size per batch. The register lists every family and recipe membership.

One physical primary member cannot belong to two reserves, a retained working toolkit and an output, or two simultaneous orders. Printed inputs remain additional process requirements. Bodies carry their own actual mass, custody, grade and structural history. Consumed process matter becomes incorporated treatment or tracked process waste; its full mass is not copied into every output and it has no automatic second recipe/sale entitlement. Output bodies have the bound empty mass; actual separate contents remain additional mass. No undocumented finished ingredient or intact consumed catalyst is also present inside the result.

## AC-02 — Reservation, current damage and replacement

**Affected: all 167. KEEP ordered-reserve/current-state rules.** All maximum reserves must be complete, functional, uncommitted elsewhere and present immediately before the single completion roll, even if Standard would use fewer. Missing, Broken, incomplete or conflicting reserves pause work before the roll. Lawful repair or replacement keeps the original ordered position and records the replacement's own history. No purchase, substitution, reordering or selection after the roll.

Transform the first N entries for resolved yield, or first Q for an allowed structural option. Unused bodies remain their actual ordinary stock. Failed/Mishap transforms none; independently surviving bodies remain mundane except for actual mishap damage. Their return is not 25% ingredient recovery. The lead and station must have the actual capacity to work sequentially on the bodies; reserved stock may require separate storage rather than fitting simultaneously on a bench.

Ordinary EN weapon/armor bodies may retain a genuinely established mundane equipment grade, size and physical workmanship history, as in Blacksmithing. The newly specified fixed nonweapon bodies are Standard grade; a name such as Masterwork is not a grade. All reserves must be never-magically-altered; no spent or suppressed imprint qualifies as a blank. **NEW creature-chassis restriction:** the 18 automaton recipes and 686 use complete, unmodified, unflawed B5 mundane chassis, Tiny or Small as allowed; no prior creature or class device can be their blank.

## AC-03 — Six manufacturing bands; structural versus creature quality

**Affected: all 167; explicit creature exception applies to the 18 automata in TI-03 and recipe-686.** KEEP six-band creation, natural-1 Mishap, and no natural-20 band shift. For an actual structural object, the alternative keeps Q and attaches at most P25 or P50 to its actual ordinary B after lawful size/grade rules. Retain only the strongest lawful workmanship percentage. Improved maximum is `B + max(1,floor(B×P/100))` when P>0, otherwise B. Apply one persistent singleton flaw last: `max(1,floor(improvedMax/2))`. Validate old maximum from its history and retain `oldMax-oldCurrent` damage deficit; new current is the new maximum minus that deficit, clamped to [0,newMax]. Standard neither heals nor removes modifiers. At B5 maxima are 5/6/7 or flawed 2/3/3; at B1 they are 1/2/2 or flawed 1/1/1. EN armor uses its real B, not B5.

Expended Rune/script uses do not make their surviving body a disposable potion.

**NEW explicit creature-output decision:** source AC/HP for the 18 automata and 686 are creature statistics, not structural B. No structural option changes their HP, AC, Speed, attacks, service count or command capacity. The alternative simply retains Q with the quality label and no mechanical improvement; do not pretend an improvement occurred. A Q1 Flawed result maps to Failed under the unsupported-flaw fallback. For a produced creature, initial HP is `max(1,floor(sourceHP × latestChassisCurrent/5))`; full chassis produces full source HP, damaged chassis stays proportionally damaged. No quality choice increases charges, spell rank, attacks, damage, DCs, preparation scope, contract participants, storage, command targets, sunrise refills or creature HP. No later upgrade/reimprint/flaw-removal procedure is granted by a formula capable of calculating those numbers.
