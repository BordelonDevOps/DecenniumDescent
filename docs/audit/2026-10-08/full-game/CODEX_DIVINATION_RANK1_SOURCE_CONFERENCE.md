## CORE_RESOLUTION_PLAYTEST.md

24: 
25: Choose the roll type before rolling. Use the actual ability modifier, including negative values, and normal proficiency only when applicable. Existing advantage/disadvantage chooses the higher/lower of two d20s; multiple sources do not stack, and any advantage plus disadvantage cancels. Add other legal modifiers once. Fractions round down unless a rule specifies otherwise. One tile is 5 feet. Class L, S and effect M retain their definitions in the shared class rules.
26: 
27: **Attack:** roll d20 plus the appropriate attack modifier against AC. Meeting AC hits. Natural 1 always misses; natural 20 always hits and critically doubles damage dice, not flat modifiers. Beating AC by 10 is not a critical hit. An explicit broader critical range does not stack with another; use the broadest eligible range. In this overlay, a natural result below 20 in that range must still meet AC unless the feature expressly says it always hits.
28: 
29: **Task, skill or tool check against a fixed DC:** total at least DC+10 is Critical Success; at least DC is Success; at most DC-10 is Critical Failure; other totals below DC are Failure. Natural 20 then improves one degree and natural 1 worsens one, bounded at either end. A task printing only success/failure collapses the two upper degrees to success and two lower degrees to failure; no extra consequence is invented. A natural 20 can still fail an otherwise impossible check, and a natural 1 with sufficient modifiers can still succeed.
30: 
31: **Saving throw:** total at least DC succeeds; otherwise it fails. Natural 1 and 20 have no generic special outcome. A printed failure by 5 or 10 compares the final total with the DC; it does not add an unprinted critical-save system. Death saves are the specific exception below.
32: 
33: **Opposed check, new provisional tie rule:** compare final totals. Higher wins; an exact tie leaves the existing state unchanged, so the initiating creature fails to change it. Do not turn task natural shifts into hidden numeric modifiers. A maneuver explicitly printing degree outcomes must specify how those apply.

## REPUTATION.md

1: # Reputation — Canon Interaction Rules
2: 
3: **Status:** Playable canon
4: 
5: Reputation records how Aethel's institutions respond to the character. It is not a morality score and it is not a hidden multiplier on the laws of magic.
6: 
7: ## Scale
8: 
9: Faction standing is stored as `{ [factionId]: score }` on the character save, normally from **−3 (Hated)** through **+3 (Honored)**. Backgrounds and play can set or change starting standing.
10: 
11: ## What reputation can change
12: 

Existing class rerolls retain source timing: Oracle Spiral-Touched boon rerolls its own failed save and uses new result once/LR; Mirrorsage includes force-successful-save reroll and decoy Reaction attack-hit reroll. Bounded shared Core/combat/spell search found no universal every-die-only-ever-rerolled-once contract. Any NEW MarkOmen/FateCoin shared guard must be confined to these grants, not silently rewrite all class features.
