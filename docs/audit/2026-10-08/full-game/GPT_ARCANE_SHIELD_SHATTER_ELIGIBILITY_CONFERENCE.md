## General Arcane Shield Shatter predicate — SETTLED

The later phrase **“equivalent defensive protective effect” does not broaden Shatter into a generic defensive-buff dispel.**

Use this finite predicate:

> **An eligible shield effect is one individually perceptible ongoing magical effect generated by an actual rank-0 or rank-1 spell whose own source expressly identifies or describes that effect as a shield, barrier, ward, or an unambiguous equivalent magical protective layer, and which currently retains at least one operational defensive component protecting the designated creature.**

“Equivalent” is therefore a **source-language/sourced-function bridge**, not “anything that helps defense.”

A spell qualifies only when all applicable conditions hold:

- actual generating spell rank is 0 or 1;
- the ongoing effect itself is individually perceptible/identifiable;
- both the effect's legal targeting locus **and** the designated protected creature are perceived, within 30 ft, and in LoE;
- source identity/description satisfies shield/barrier/ward/equivalent-protective-layer;
- at least one actual defensive component still operationally exists;
- the selected effect is actually susceptible to ending.

All hidden predicates resolve **after paid commitment with no diagnostic**. Failure of any predicate means **no ending and no 1d8 force damage**.

### Arithmetic supersession versus suppression

**Arithmetic supersession does not remove eligibility.**

If Arcane Ward's +1 AC exists but a stronger +5 AC wins strongest-flat arbitration, the Arcane Ward still retains an otherwise-applicable defensive component and remains Shatter-eligible.

Likewise a pending save ward remains defensive even if another bonus would currently be numerically stronger.

By contrast, **actual complete suppression** means the effect is not presently eligible. If every defensive component of that source is genuinely suppressed rather than merely numerically superseded, it must wait until protection resumes.

This difference provides no diagnostic: targeting a secretly suppressed effect still pays the cast and simply fails.

A conditional defense need not have its triggering circumstance occurring **right now**. If its active record remains capable of supplying its source-defined protection when the condition arises, it is operational protection. Thus Alignment Shield does not need an opposed-alignment attacker currently swinging at the target.

## Resonance Shield and temp HP — exact ruling

**Spell-granted temp HP can be an ongoing Shatter-eligible defensive effect despite raw Core `Instantaneous` metadata when the reviewed operational spell creates a source-owned finite-lifetime grant.**

That does **not** mean every historical consequence of an instantaneous spell remains dispellable.

For Resonance Shield, eligibility exists only while the current temp-HP ledger still contains the **accepted Resonance Shield grant**:

- correct source/application identity;
- correct recipient/owner;
- remaining points > 0;
- grant has not expired, been depleted, or been replaced.

If a larger False Life, class shield pool, or another temp-HP grant replaced it under the existing temp-HP rules, the Resonance Shield grant is gone. Shatter cannot reach “underneath” to the discarded historical grant.

Successful Shatter administratively ends that current Resonance Shield grant and removes its remaining temp HP. That ending is:

- not damage;
- not paid absorption;
- not a hit;
- not a temp-HP depletion trigger unless the actual trigger expressly includes administrative removal.

**False Life remains excluded**, even while its current temp-HP grant exists, because it fails the source-qualified shield/barrier/ward predicate. This keeps Shatter a specialist rather than a general temp-HP remover. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}

## Save-only wards — exact ruling

**Yes: direct saving-throw protection qualifies when it comes from an expressly sourced Ward.**

Therefore:

- Corruption Ward → qualifies.
- Minor Elemental Ward → qualifies.
- Resistance → excluded.

The distinction is deliberate. Shatter is not “dispel any save buff”; it is “shatter an actual spell-sourced shield/barrier/ward protective layer.”

A pending save bonus/advantage is still operational protection even before its next qualifying save occurs.

If the save ward is consumed before Shatter resolves, it is no longer an ongoing eligible effect.

## Alignment Shield

The rank distinction matters:

- **Abjuration rank 1 application:** eligible.
- **Conjuration rank 3 application:** ineligible to this rank-1 Shatter because generating rank exceeds the 0–1 ceiling.

For a qualifying rank-1 application, absence of a currently attacking opposed-alignment creature does **not** make the shield dormant/ineligible. Its ongoing conditional defense remains present.

Shatter never reveals whether a given creature is opposed in alignment.

If the entire Alignment Shield effect is actually suppressed by another rule, rather than its +2 simply losing strongest-flat arbitration, it is ineligible while wholly suppressed.

## Protective Sphere — geometry and ownership

Approve the proposed model with this exact procedure.

Arcane Shield Shatter targets the **whole maintained sphere application**, not a custom piece/hole.

To target it:

1. choose **one perceived point on the actual sphere boundary** within 30 ft and LoE from the Shatter caster; this is the distributed effect's targeting locus;
2. designate **one perceived creature actually inside the sphere**, also within 30 ft and LoE from the Shatter caster.

That creature is considered currently protected by the sphere's defensive boundary even when no projectile is presently in flight.

A creature standing outside the sphere does **not** become the designated protected creature merely because the sphere could hypothetically lie between it and some future attacker.

The protected creature need not be the Sphere caster or original owner.

Successful Shatter ends the **entire selected Protective Sphere spell application**, invoking its normal concentration/source cleanup. It does not:

- punch a hole;
- remove one segment;
- move the center;
- leave the rest of the boundary intact.

If several independent Protective Sphere casts overlap, select exactly one perceptible application.

No owner/caster identification is automatically revealed merely from perceiving the sphere.
