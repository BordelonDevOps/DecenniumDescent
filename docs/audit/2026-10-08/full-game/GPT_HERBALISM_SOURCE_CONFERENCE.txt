## Herbalism source-conference disposition

**APPROVE the 45-formula Herbalism authoring direction subject to the finite rulings below.** These rulings are sufficient for Codex to author the exact cards; they do not require another generic Herbalism conference unless the exact manuscripts introduce materially different behavior.

This is **not** exact-card approval, numerical-balance approval, runtime approval, or whole-game completion. Source identities, visibility, ingredients, legacy text, and original effects remain preserved. Cosmology remains excluded.

1. **Shared consumable identity — APPROVED.** Treat all 45 finished Herbalism products as **magical, nonspell, single-use consumables**. They require no generic RP, RC, attunement, spell rank, concentration, focus, class, or tool proficiency to administer. Herbalism/tool training governs manufacture, not use. A family name, recipe name, tag, ingredient property, or legacy name grants no additional damage, condition, resistance, regeneration, sleep, toxicity, or crafting function.

   Explicitly preserve these boundaries:
   - **recipe-644 Dream-Bark Sleeping Draught:** only its printed `+1 to saves and checks related to psychic hazards for 1 hour`; **no sleep effect**.
   - **recipe-645 Living-Root Regeneration Poultice:** only `1d6+2` healing plus the approved topical-treatment rider; **no periodic regeneration**.
   - “Poison,” “Acid,” “Unholy,” etc. in names/tags do not harm the recipient unless the active effect expressly says so.
   - Legacy names/effects remain evidence only.

2. **Antidotes — APPROVE clearing all removable Poisoned applications.** For recipes **200, 220, 238, and 646**, use:

   > **Action; one available hand; one accessible dose. Drink it or feed it to one willing or Incapacitated active creature within 5 feet that can receive the dose. On resolution, end all currently present removable applications of the Poisoned condition on that recipient, then grant advantage on saving throws against poison for the recipe’s printed duration. Source-specific applications that expressly cannot be removed this way remain. Ending Poisoned does not itself end the poison, disease, toxin, curse, intoxication, injury, environmental exposure, or other underlying source that caused it. Such a source may apply Poisoned again only through its own actual continuing or future trigger.**

   The source `bonus: 1` / `bonus: 3` fields are **inactive abbreviated mechanics**, because the complete effect says **advantage**, not a numeric +1/+3.

   This is preferable to selecting one Poisoned record. The printed promise is “ends the poisoned condition”; because independent Poisoned records otherwise keep the creature Poisoned, removing only one could fail to deliver the stated condition-ending effect.

3. **Balms/compresses/salves/poultices — APPROVE with a bounded treatment rider.** Use the proposed one-minute application: one uninterrupted active minute, or **10 consecutive regular-turn Actions** in initiative; touch/access to compatible tissue and one available hand throughout; willing or Incapacitated recipient; dose spent at legal application start; interruption loses the dose and produces no healing/treatment result. No concentration.

   The printed healing resolves first as magical nonspell HP recovery. Then use this exact treatment rule:

   > **Local Treatment. After this dose’s healing and any healing-triggered condition endpoints finish resolving, choose at most one present local wound, burn, infection, or Bleeding source appropriate to actual topical treatment. If it is a Bleeding source with an existing Medicine-based removal DC, the applier may immediately make one ordinary Medicine check against that source DC; this dose supplies the local dressing/material for that attempt and requires no additional healer-kit charge. Success ends only that eligible Bleeding source. Do not make this extra check if this same dose’s healing already resolved an explicit removal/treatment attempt for that record.**
   >
   > **For another wound, burn, or infection, the application may satisfy one explicitly required topical dressing, cleaning, salve, or comparable local-treatment step in that source’s existing treatment procedure. It does not waive or shorten any other required time, check, Supply, kit charge, medicine, surgery, rest, or source-specific cure requirement. If the source has no mechanically defined topical-treatment interaction, the recipe provides no additional automatic condition removal beyond its healing.**

   Consequences:
   - It **does not remove a Core Wound** or bypass the 8-hour + medical kit + 1 Supply + Medicine procedure.
   - It does **not automatically remove Burning**; Burning retains its reviewed Action/immersion/save procedure unless the actual burning source has a compatible topical-treatment clause.
   - “Infection” is not universal disease removal.
   - No Injury, curse, Corruption, Poisoned, disease, Wound, or linked parent record disappears merely because the recipe says “treats.”
   - The local rider never duplicates a healing-triggered Bleed endpoint from the same dose.

4. **Hazard buffs — APPROVE the bounded interpretation, with one general eligibility sentence.** The key rule should be:

   > A “check or save related to a [tag] hazard” qualifies only when the roll’s immediate declared purpose is to **detect, identify, avoid, resist, escape, disable, safely traverse, or survive an already-present qualifying hazard**. It never applies to attack rolls, damage rolls, spellcasting checks, initiative, ordinary crafting, formula research, harvesting merely because an ingredient shares the tag, or a proactive task whose subject is merely thematically related.

   Apply the tag boundaries as follows:

   | Tag | Qualifying scope |
   |---|---|
   | Poison | Saves against actual poison/Poisoned effects; checks directly identifying, avoiding, safely handling, or disabling an actual poison hazard. |
   | Fire | Saves against an actual fire-damage/fire environmental hazard; checks directly avoiding/extinguishing/safely traversing a fire hazard. |
   | Cold | Saves against actual cold-damage or cold-exposure hazards; checks directly surviving/avoiding an actual cold hazard. |
   | Lightning | Saves against actual lightning-damage/electrical hazards; checks directly identifying/avoiding/disabling such a hazard. |
   | Acid | Saves against actual acid-damage/corrosive hazards; checks directly handling/avoiding/disabling such a hazard. |
   | Earth | Rockfall, cave-in, burial, unstable stone/ground, crushing geological movement, or another explicitly physical earth/stone hazard. Not every event occurring on earth/stone. |
   | Water | Currents, flooding, drowning, undertow, hazardous immersion, or another actual water hazard. |
   | Air | Hazardous wind, pressure, inhalation, air deprivation/loss, airborne environmental force. Not thunder merely because both are “air-themed.” |
   | Psychic | Actual psychic damage, mental intrusion, or an effect explicitly identified as a psychic hazard. Not every CHA/WIS/INT save or ordinary social influence. |
   | Unholy | Only a hazard explicitly mechanically labeled/tagged **Unholy**. Creature type, morality, religion, appearance, Fiend/Undead status, or origin does not infer it. |
   | Arcane | An explicitly magical/Arcane trap, ward, field, environmental phenomenon, or similar hazard. Not every hostile spell or spellcaster action. |
   | Strength | STR checks/saves whose immediate purpose is resisting/escaping an actual physical hazard, restraint, crushing force, forced movement, entrapment, etc. Not attacks, damage, carrying calculations, or every STR check/save. |
   | Stealth | Actual Stealth checks whose immediate purpose is avoiding detection by a present hostile observer, pursuer, guard, sensor, or hazard-detection system. No Deception, Initiative, attack, or automatic Hidden state. |
   | Vitality | CON checks/saves against fatigue, exposure, environmental endurance, or disease hazards. Explicitly not death saves, concentration saves, Wound/Injury saves, or an additional recovery roll. |

   Direct elemental matching can therefore qualify even when the hazard came from a spell; e.g. a fire-tag Herbalism buff can apply to the DEX save against an actual fire-damage effect. **Arcane** does not separately make every spell qualify.

   When multiple Herbalism hazard buffs qualify the same roll, use only the **highest numeric Herbalism bonus**. An independently legal source of advantage/disadvantage still uses the ordinary advantage/disadvantage rules and can coexist with that flat bonus.

5. **Worldroot Panacea — APPROVE the cure procedure, but change when the seasonal entitlement is spent.**

   Use:

   > **Application:** 10 uninterrupted active minutes, one available hand and continuous touch/access to one willing or Incapacitated compatible active creature. Spend the physical dose when legal application begins. Interruption destroys that dose and produces no benefit.
   >
   > **Resolution:** on successful completion, choose exactly one eligible category: one removable disease source; one removable poison/toxin source; one external removable curse source; or one legally removable Exhaustion level. End/reduce only that selected source/level and its explicitly dependent consequences. Then restore 10d6+20 ordinary magical nonspell HP under the normal healing rules. Cure and healing are independent; blocking HP recovery does not undo a legal cure.
   >
   > It cannot remove intrinsic ancestry/class traits, Oracle curses that are not ordinarily removable, Corruption, Wounds/Injuries, identity effects, or any source that expressly requires a different removal procedure. It does not revive Dead creatures.

   I would **not** spend the recipient's once-per-season entitlement at application start. The source says the creature may **benefit** once per season. If the ten-minute procedure is interrupted and produces no benefit, consuming the seasonal entitlement would exceed the printed restriction. The expensive physical dose is already lost.

   **Season clock:** retain an actual campaign season rather than equating it to an Opening.

   > Before the first Panacea benefit in a campaign, establish one stable `seasonLimitMode`. If the campaign already has explicit campaign-season IDs, use that `seasonID`; the entitlement refreshes only on an actual recorded campaign-season transition. Sessions, rests, travel, expeditions, contracts, stages and Openings do not create a new season unless the campaign chronology independently says they do. If no campaign-season structure exists, use a fixed fallback of **100 elapsed days from the prior successful Panacea resolution**. Once the fallback is selected, it remains that campaign's Panacea limit mode unless an owner-approved campaign chronology migration explicitly replaces it; players cannot switch modes to refresh the entitlement.

   Record `recipient + seasonID` in season mode, or `recipient + lastSuccessfulPanaceaTimestamp` in fallback mode. Death, resurrection, dose ownership, crafter, Long Rest, or a new Opening does not reset it.

6. **Packaging and stabilization — APPROVED, including recipe-201's carrier deficit.** All 45 have zero source vessel inputs, so every finished Herbalism dose requires supplementary packaging.

   Use the Alchemy-compatible physical profile:
   - one ordinary **Glass Vial**, 1 gp, 0.1 lb;
   - 0.1 lb finished Herbalism compound;
   - **0.2 lb gross** per completed dose;
   - AC 13, HP 1 normally;
   - one ordinary vial slot;
   - oral and topical products can use this same single-dose container;
   - after use, an intact oral/topical container becomes the previously reviewed ordinary spent 0.1-lb dose vial, not a recovered high-tier ingredient.

   Reserve enough empty vials **before the completion check for the maximum generic Masterwork yield**. If fewer units are actually produced, unused reserved vials remain ordinary inventory.

   Also reserve enough mundane carrier to physically fund the maximum possible finished compound mass after the worst legal one-unit catalyst retention. This carrier is additional to, and never substitutes for, exact recipe ingredients. For **recipe-201**, the listed consumed material supplies 0.46 lb while a Masterwork six-dose result requires 0.60 lb finished compound, so **0.14 lb clean carrier water** must be available/reserved before the check. Record its actual acquisition separately; do not alter immutable source component economics.

   The first **15 active minutes** are the Herbalism binding/stabilization phase, included inside the source work hours. A genuinely applicable time-reduction feature scales that phase proportionately with the whole order, rounding upward to a whole minute. Herbalism is **not** automatically eligible for Forge Dwarf Master Craftsman merely because crafting is occurring; that feature remains bounded to its actual artisan-tool scope.

   After the phase completes, the bound mixture follows the same work-order stabilization principle already accepted for Alchemy: no use as raw ingredients/another order, no generic stasis service, returned catalyst/failure lots keep their age, and abandonment follows framework loss rules.

   Completed sealed doses can use the proposed indefinite ordinary shelf life; once opened, magical potency expires after **1 elapsed hour**, and resealing/splitting does not refresh it. One dose has one recipient/use. This does not make raw ingredients Fresh or solve magical-essence containment before processing.

   **Nonconsumed source tools are an important exception:** all tiered `Mortar and pestle` rows marked `consumed:false` remain actual reusable components and are **not** packaging, carrier mass, or spent ingredient. Their preserved source tier/value remains source identity. Where physically appropriate, the actual mortar-and-pestle component can satisfy the recipe's corresponding auxiliary implement requirement under the reviewed no-duplicate-tool rule; it does not substitute for the Herbalism-kit credential or other missing station requirements.

7. **Quality — APPROVED with the proposed Herbalism-specific profiles.**
   - Base quantity ≥2: Flawed produces `floor(Q/2)` ordinary-strength doses.
   - Singleton fixed-duration **hazard buff**: halve only its printed duration.
   - Singleton fixed-duration **antidote**: keep the immediate Poisoned-removal effect, but halve only its advantage duration. Thus recipe-220's Flawed version still clears eligible Poisoned applications and grants advantage for 30 minutes.
   - Singleton **until-next-beneficial-Long-Rest** buff/antidote: no coherent halved duration exists; Flawed is Failed with framework recovery. This applies to recipes **238, 239, and 646**.
   - Singleton topical healing/treatment remedies: Flawed is Failed; do not invent half healing, half wound treatment, or a weakened cure.
   - **Worldroot Panacea:** Flawed is Failed; its ten-minute administration time is not its effect duration and therefore cannot be “halved” into a weaker Panacea.
   - Exceptional/Masterwork: use the source batch-yield option where mathematically productive, with reserved packaging/carrier, **or** keep Standard quantity and improve each produced container from HP 1 to HP 2. No potency, healing dice, hazard bonus, cure count, duration, condition breadth, seasonal entitlement, or charge increase.
   - No recipe title such as Tonic, Tea, Powder, Draught, Antidote, or Potion imports the separate 15-/30-day drug-dependence system without an actual reviewed substance profile.

### Source-specific exceptions to carry into the exact cards

I found no additional effect family requiring a new conference, but these must be explicit in the authored set:

- **200 / 220 / 238 / 646** are the only Antidote condition-clearing profiles in this packet. Their tags do not add Cold/Vitality/Unholy/Poison secondary powers.
- **647** is uniquely the ten-minute Panacea and uniquely uses the seasonal recipient cap.
- **644** has no sleep effect despite “Sleeping Draught.”
- **645** has no regeneration-over-time effect despite “Regeneration Poultice.”
- Every topical formula's “wound, burn, or infection” text uses the bounded Local Treatment rule above; **none automatically clears Wounds, Burning, disease, or infection records by name alone**.
- The tiered, nonconsumed **Mortar and pestle** components remain reusable where printed and must not be accidentally charged as consumed material or output packaging.
- Ingredient properties/legacy names never back-fill a recipe's active effect. In particular, unusual mappings such as lightning ingredients in non-lightning recipes or `shamanic bloodstone` in Earth/Lightning/Fire/Vitality contexts remain exactly what the recipe says, without inferred extra riders.
- Public/restricted/Director visibility remains source-owned; operationalizing an internal formula does not expose it publicly.

With those rulings, Codex can author the complete 45-card Herbalism candidate for the next **exact manuscript review**.
