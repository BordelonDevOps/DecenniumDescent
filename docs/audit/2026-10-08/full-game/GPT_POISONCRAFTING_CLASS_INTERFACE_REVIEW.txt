**Verdict: APPROVE as a shared Poisoncrafting ↔ Thief integration overlay.** No class-card rewrite is required. These bindings can be included directly in the upcoming 41-card exact manuscript review.

1. **Prepared-dose abilities remain class-only.** Apply Poison, Double Poison, Vein Cutter, and Toxic Shiv operate only on the Thief engine's **prepared doses**. They do not accept a purchased/crafted recipe-359–395/660–663 dose as a substitute.

   The current reviewed operational cards control here. In particular, Toxic Shiv's preserved older source phrase "any poison from your inventory" does **not** reopen its reviewed card, which now expressly says "known prepared poison" and spends a prepared dose.

   Therefore crafted recipes do **not** inherit:
   - Thief DC;
   - one-weapon/two-projectile delivery;
   - the two-attempt coating clock;
   - Apply Poison's Bonus Action;
   - Double Poison;
   - class-preparation replenishment;
   - the Thief two-ongoing-recipe ceiling;
   - or any conversion into renewable class stock.

   **Exotic Toxins** likewise creates/replaces a known **Thief class poison profile** through its specimen/research procedure. "Recipe" in that class engine does not mean a Crafting V4 formula ID. It grants no Poisoncrafting formula, crafted inventory unit, formula discovery, or unlimited output from the 41-item catalog.

2. **Venom Master does bridge to legitimate crafted injury-poison exposures, with one correction to the proposed immunity boundary.** The Venom Master character must be the creature making the qualifying critical weapon hit. The poisoned weapon may carry either a Thief prepared coating or a legally deployed crafted **injury poison**.

   For a crafted poison, resolve in this order:

   **critical weapon hit → legal injury exposure → initial poison save → if that save actually fails, Venom Master may trigger once/round → apply its separate -1 saves rider.**

   Venom Master never modifies the save that triggered it.

   It does not trigger from:
   - a miss or other event that never creates a legal injury exposure;
   - an initial poison save that succeeds;
   - repeat end-turn poison saves;
   - poison periodic damage;
   - contact poison merely carried on an object;
   - an inhaled burst;
   - copied attacks;
   - or an application prevented wholesale by explicit **poison-effect/toxin immunity**.

   **Important:** damage-type immunity alone does **not** prevent Venom Master. Condition immunity alone does **not** prevent it either. If a legitimate poison exposure occurred, its initial poison save was actually rolled and failed, the trigger condition is met even if acid/fire/poison/etc. damage was subsequently immune or the recipe's Poisoned/Stunned/etc. condition was immune. Even if separate component immunities happen to prevent both printed recipe components, the failed poison save remains a failed poison save.

   Blanket poison-effect/toxin immunity is different: it prevents the covered application itself, so it supplies no valid failed poison save for Venom Master. An unnecessary die rolled before recognizing that immunity cannot activate the feature.

   **NEW integration:** Venom Master does **not** require the critical wielder to equal the crafted poison's `poisonOwnerKey`. The card's reviewed operational trigger is a critical hit with a poisoned weapon, not "a poison you personally coated." Thus a Venom Master may critically hit with a weapon another creature coated and trigger the class rider if the resulting poison save fails. The crafted poison nevertheless keeps its original coating actor as `poisonOwnerKey`; ownership, refresh grouping and any hard-denial attribution do not transfer to the Thief. Venom Master's own -1-save rider is owned by the Venom Master character.

   Current contact-poison rules do not make an ordinary weapon hit automatically deliver contact poison, so the present 41-card integration effectively bridges Venom Master to **injury delivery**, not contact or inhaled delivery.

3. **Sneak Attack and Vile Tactics use the actual active condition state.** Your proposal is correct.

   For **Sneak Attack**, crafted Poisoned qualifies as **"your Poisoned"** only when:
   - the target currently has an active applicable Core **Poisoned** record;
   - that record came from the crafted recipe;
   - and its `poisonOwnerKey` is the Thief character using Sneak Attack.

   That means a Thief who legally coated a weapon/ammunition can own the resulting Poisoned record even if another creature ultimately delivered the hit. A Poisoned record owned by someone else is not "your Poisoned."

   For **Vile Tactics**, ownership is not required. Any currently active qualifying Bleeding, Poisoned, or Crippled condition satisfies its reviewed target predicate.

   In both cases:
   - Confused, Burning, Frightened, Slowed, Weakened, Stunned, or merely taking poison-type damage does not count as Poisoned.
   - Recipe name/tag/damage type creates no shortcut.
   - Suppressed or immunity-inactive condition records do not satisfy these active-condition hooks.
   - The qualifying condition must already exist when the weapon hit's eligibility is determined.
   - A Poisoned/Bleed/etc. rider created by that same hit **afterward** cannot retroactively qualify Sneak Attack or Vile Tactics on that hit.
   - Poison ticks, repeat-save events, standalone poison damage, companions and copied attacks do not create extra Sneak Attack or Vile Tactics events.

   This leaves the already-reviewed Vile Tactics predicate as **Bleeding/Poisoned/Crippled**; its older preserved source wording mentioning Frightened is not being reopened here.

4. **Toxin Sense gets a perceptibility boundary, not universal magical opacity.** Your proposed direction is correct.

   **NEW integration:**
   - `Passive recognition`: the poison must actually be **exposed and perceptible** within 5 feet. If sight or smell gives real evidence of an exposed compound/coating, Toxin Sense can recognize that poison is present.
   - `Sealed container`: the unseen interior of a closed/sealed vial is not an "exposed fictional poison" and receives no generic passive detection through the container.
   - `Magical nonspell status`: being magical does **not by itself** make a crafted poison undetectable. "Magical substances may evade detection" requires an actual concealment, masking, identification-blocking, or perceptibility circumstance; it is not a blanket immunity to Toxin Sense.
   - `Active identification`: Action + kit + DC 12 INT+PB can identify an exposed/perceptible poison or a legally accessed sample. A sealed poison normally must first be legally opened/accessed unless some other actual evidence exposes it.

   Passive recognition gives only **"this is poison / poison is present."** It does not reveal DC, tier, crafter, component list, donor, source monster, hidden formula access, discovery route, economy, Director lore, or restricted information.

   A successful active identification identifies the **operational game recipe** responsible for the substance and therefore its combat-facing identity. It does not grant the crafting formula or formula access and does not expose hidden ingredients, provenance or Director lore. Restricted/Director-only formula knowledge remains restricted even if a character learns what poison they physically encountered.

   Ordinary sensory limitations still control. A creature unable to see the evidence and unable to smell it does not receive fictional supernatural radar from Toxin Sense.

5. **The already-in-progress target turn does not count as marker one.** This should follow the reviewed hostile-duration timing convention rather than letting application timing shorten a poison by nearly a full round.

   **NEW exact timing binding:**
   - If poison application occurs **before the target's next regular turn begins**, that upcoming regular turn's end is marker 1.
   - If application occurs **during the target's already-in-progress regular turn**, that turn's end is excluded. Marker 1 is the end of its **next** regular turn.
   - If application occurs after its regular turn has ended, the next regular target-turn end is marker 1.

   Standard one-minute poison therefore uses markers 1–10. Singleton Flawed uses markers 1–5. The chosen virtual marker sequence/end remains attached to the effect through initiative-order changes. Extra turns never supply poison repeat saves or consume poison-duration markers.

   For all ordinary poison conditions, the **final marker still receives the printed repeat CON save before expiry cleanup**, even though failure simply means the record then expires immediately.

   For poison-created **Burning**, the established end-marker order is:

   **ongoing-fire tick/arbitration → Burning DEX save only if future Burning damage remains → poison repeat CON save if the poison-created Burning record still exists → expiry cleanup.**

   Consequently, on the final marker:
   - the final Burning tick is due;
   - there is **no DEX ending save**, because no future damage marker remains after that tick;
   - the source-required final repeat **CON save still occurs**;
   - then the record expires if it survived that CON save.

   If an earlier DEX save, extinguish Action, immersion, cure, immunity-based removal, or other legal endpoint already ended that poison-created Burning record, there is no subsequent poison CON save because no ongoing component remains.
