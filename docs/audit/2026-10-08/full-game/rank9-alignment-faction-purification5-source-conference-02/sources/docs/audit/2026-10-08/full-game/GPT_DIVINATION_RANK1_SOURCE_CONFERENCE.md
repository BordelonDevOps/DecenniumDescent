# GPT — rank-1 Divination source conference

These twelve are **SOURCE-RECONCILED / DRAFTABLE for exact authored cards**. All remain rank 1 / base 2 RP with only the supplied class-access rows.

### 1. True Sight (Lesser)

**Action; Self; V/S/F; 1 minute; no concentration.** For the duration, you can actually see creatures within **30 ft, same plane**, despite their Invisible or Hidden state and despite ordinary **non-solid visual obscurity** such as darkness, smoke, mist, foliage, or similar concealment.

This does **not** see through solid opaque barriers/total cover and does not override Blindness or another effect expressly preventing your sight. It does not reveal true forms, disguises, alignment, Ethereal creatures, illusion secrets, hidden objects, or creature statistics.

A creature revealed this way ceases to be Hidden **from you** because you have clear magical sight of it. Invisible's own unseen attack modifiers likewise cease for you under the existing Core exception; unrelated obstruction/cover still applies. Other creatures learn nothing automatically, though you may communicate normally.

Protection from Scrying can block this spell's **new Divination-derived perception** of its protected subject but does not make an otherwise ordinarily visible creature invisible.

**NEW:** Self beneficiary, 30-ft magical sight radius, non-solid-obscurity boundary.

### 2. Warning Ward

**Action; 30 ft; V/S/F; concentration up to 1 minute.** Place one faintly visible magical sigil on solid ground or another suitable immobile surface. It watches a **10-ft-radius, 10-ft-high area** centered on the mark.

A creature qualifies as an **enemy** when it has an actual current hostile relationship toward the caster under the encounter/campaign state at the instant it enters. This may include secretly hostile creatures if that hostility is an actual fictional fact. Do not infer hostility merely from species, alignment, faction, reputation, appearance, or suspicion. If current hostility is genuinely undefined, the ward does not invent it.

When a qualifying enemy **enters from outside**, the mark visibly glows for **1 round / 6 elapsed seconds**. Later qualifying entries refresh that glow but provide no additional information. Entry includes voluntary movement, forced movement, or teleportation that actually places the creature inside. A creature already inside when the ward is created does not trigger it until it leaves and re-enters.

The glow reports only **at least one qualifying entry occurred**. No identity, square, count, type, intent details, or statistics. Protection that prevents this Divination information can prevent that protected creature from supplying the triggering information. One Warning Ward per caster.

**NEW:** 10-ft-radius/height, actual-hostility predicate, entry-only trigger, one-round glow.

### 3. Scribe Destiny

**Action; 30 ft; one creature perceived with LoE; V/S/F; instantaneous.** Before the save, choose **greatest desire** or **greatest fear**. Target makes a WIS save.

On failure, privately learn one actual currently dominant deep desire or fear of the chosen category. This is more fundamental than a passing surface emotion. The result is communicated conceptually in a form the caster can understand rather than as a verbatim thought requiring shared language.

If multiple motives are genuinely coequal, the Director selects one consistently while that underlying state remains materially unchanged; repeated casts do not rotate through tied motives merely to mine additional psychology.

If the creature actually has no meaningful motive of the chosen category, report **None discernible**. If the fact is undefined or specifically protected, report **Indeterminate** rather than inventing psychology.

Undead/Construct or other creatures qualify whenever their actual state includes desire/fear. The Fear tag does not inflict Frightened, and immunity to Frightened alone is not immunity to this information effect. No compulsion, truth detection, memory search, automatic alert, or future-action guarantee.

**NEW:** caster chooses desire/fear, deep-motive boundary, tie handling, conceptual output.

### 4. Mark Omen

**Action; 30 ft; one creature perceived with LoE; V/S/F; 1 hour; no concentration.** No save. Apply one invisible, nonphysical omen mark.

Once before expiry, when **you** make an attack roll against the marked creature, after the complete initial attack roll is visible but **before hit/miss resolution**, you may consume the mark to reroll that attack. This requires no additional Action or Reaction.

Reroll the **entire attack-roll d20 procedure** using the same modifiers and the same advantage/disadvantage state. If advantage/disadvantage used two d20s, roll the complete replacement set again. The replacement result is mandatory and then resolves normally, including natural 1/20 and critical rules. Damage is not rerolled.

No new attack, ammunition, cost refund, spell cast, or duplicated on-cast/on-hit event is created merely by the reroll.

One active Mark Omen per caster; successful recast replaces the prior mark. Different casters may maintain independent marks. Death, destruction, or ending of a summoned creature's existence ends that caster's mark; ordinary shapechange retaining creature identity does not.

**NEW shared Mark Omen/Fate Coin guard:** one original roll event and all of its replacement lineage can receive **at most one reroll granted by either Mark Omen or Fate Coin**, across casters. This restriction applies only to these two spell-granted rerolls and does not rewrite Oracle, Mirrorsage, or other exact reroll features.

### 5. Clairaudience (Lesser)

**Action; V/S/F; 1 minute; no concentration.** Choose one **specific known location within 1 mile on the same plane**. You must have personally visited it or possess a reliable description/map sufficient to identify that particular location. No originating LoE is required to the remote location.

Create one fixed, nonphysical auditory sensing point there. For the duration, you hear sounds from that point according to the **ordinary sound conditions at that location**. Local walls, silence, soundproofing, distance from local speakers, etc. operate normally. The sensor does not move, pan, follow a creature, or search for a location.

You continue hearing your own surroundings normally at the same time. Being Deafened prevents you from receiving the remote audio while Deafened.

Maintain a **1-mile, same-plane tether** between caster and sensing point. Moving beyond 1 mile or changing planes suppresses the remote hearing without ending or pausing the duration; returning while time remains restores it.

No translation, authentication, recording, vision, remote targeting, casting through the sensor, line of effect, or physical sensor object. Relevant Divination protection can withhold new information concerning its protected subject while ambient unprotected sound remains available.

**CONFLICT RULING:** the printed promise of a “distant known location” requires a NEW operational **1-mile remote range** rather than limiting the spell to the raw 30-ft casting metadata.

### 6. Fate Coin

**Action; 30 ft; V/S/F; 1 hour; no concentration.** Conjure one noncurrency magical fate coin into your free hand or the free hand of a willing creature within range. It is Tiny/negligible weight, **AC 10, HP 1**. It may be handed or dropped normally. One active Fate Coin per caster; successful recasting replaces that caster's prior unspent coin.

The current holder may expend the coin as a **Reaction** after one eligible die has produced a visible result but before that result's mechanical consequences are applied. The holder must perceive the creature/object/event whose roll is resolving and it must be within **30 ft and LoE**. The roll may belong to the holder, an ally, or an enemy; no consent is required.

Choose **one actual die face** from that roll and reroll only that die:
- advantage/disadvantage: reroll one of its two d20s, then recompute the normal higher/lower selection;
- multi-die damage/healing/resource roll: reroll one die and recompute the total;
- ordinary single-die checks/saves/attacks: reroll that die.

The new face is mandatory. Secret Director rolls with no presently perceptible event are not eligible merely because a die exists behind the screen.

The coin is spent and vanishes when used. It cannot reverse an already-applied hit, HP loss, healing, RP transfer, cost, or completed effect. It is not currency, saleable material, or a crafting component. Handoff never resets its clock/use.

The **Mark Omen/Fate Coin shared reroll guard** above applies. A legal Fate Coin may reroll Resonance Well's initial `1d4` reserve roll **before that roll becomes the established reserve**; thereafter the Well's existing no-reroll/refill rules remain unchanged.

**NEW:** physical token, Reaction timing, 30-ft/LoE event range, one-die semantics and shared reroll-lineage guard.

### 7. Detect Secret Doors

**Action; Self; V/S/F; instantaneous.** Privately reveal the **precise locations and boundaries of concealed passage entrances within 30 ft on the same plane**.

Eligible features include actual secret doors, hidden trapdoors, movable concealed panels, concealed passage openings, and comparable intentionally hidden entrances. Ordinary walls, paneling, masonry, furniture, or visual concealment **do not block this specific detection**, because doing so would nullify the printed power. Specific anti-Divination protection still applies.

The spell reveals the concealed **entry**, not the entire passage network beyond the 30-ft area. A buried tunnel with no qualifying entrance within range is not mapped merely because part of the tunnel passes nearby.

If an illusion conceals an actual secret doorway, the doorway can be revealed; the spell does not thereby identify unrelated illusion properties.

No unlocking, opening, trap detection, trap disarming, construction history, route mapping, treasure information, or persistent visible public outline. It is an instantaneous private spatial revelation.

**NEW:** self-centered 30-ft detection and explicit ordinary-concealment bypass limited to hidden passage entrances.

### 8. Faction Insight

**Action; 30 ft; one creature perceived with LoE; V/S/F; instantaneous.** Choose one exact canonical `factionId` you know.

Privately learn the target's **actual current reputation/standing score** with that faction and, when the current canonical faction data defines a corresponding standing category, that category as well.

A Director-held hidden standing is legitimate spell information unless specifically protected. If no actual standing record is established, report **Indeterminate**, not 0.

Use the current playable reputation scale/record; do not migrate it to historical −5..+5 simply because historical material exists.

This reveals no membership, allegiance, intent, rank, credentials, authority, prices, exact social history, or other faction standings. No save, positive-standing caster gate, reputation change, or legal privilege.

**NEW:** exact known-faction selection, numeric-score/category output, missing-record Indeterminate.

### 9. Alignment Sense

**Action; 30 ft; one creature perceived with LoE; V/S/F; instantaneous.** No save.

Privately determine the creature's actual current canonical:
- Lawful / Neutral / Chaotic axis;
- Good / Neutral / Evil axis.

Director-hidden but unprotected values are revealed. Undefined or protected axes independently report **Indeterminate**. One protected axis does not automatically suppress the other.

Do not infer alignment from faction, reputation, ancestry, species, faith, appearance, conduct, oath, or stereotype. It reveals no intentions, personality, truthfulness, crimes, allegiance, or future conduct.

**SOURCE-FIDELITY NOTE:** this is intentionally largely redundant with rank-0 **Sense Alignment**, except that Alignment Sense targets creatures only and costs rank-1 resources. No undocumented multitargeting, protection bypass, longer duration, or stronger information is added to justify its price. Relative value is a future balance/source-cleanup question, not an authoring rewrite.

### 10. Resonance Map

**Action; Self; V/S/F; instantaneous.** Receive a private spatial snapshot of **active magical-energy concentrations within 30 ft and LoE on the same plane**.

Map the location and actual footprint/extent of:
- active spell effects;
- magical zones/fields;
- active magical auras;
- magical objects/effects that actually possess an active magical-energy record.

If an eligible source has an **actual comparable source-defined magnitude**, rank, or intensity value, the map may represent that concentration using that real value. Where no such comparable value exists, reveal presence/extent without inventing numerical power or ranking one effect against another.

A mere RP pool does **not** qualify by itself unless its exact source also creates an active magical aura/effect.

This can reveal the position/extent of an otherwise unseen magical effect. If that effect is attached to a Hidden/Invisible creature and the unprotected aura gives away its location, ordinary known-location consequences follow; the spell still does **not** grant sight of the creature itself.

Protection from Scrying or another exact Divination defense cannot be bypassed by treating a protected creature's attached magical information as an “independent aura.” Environmental magic genuinely independent of that creature remains separately detectable.

No spell name, owner, alignment, faction, charges, recipe, RP amount, harvest permission, permanent map item, or continuous tracking.

**NEW:** self-centered 30-ft spatial snapshot, footprint reporting, and source-defined-intensity-only rule.

### 11. Read Memories

**Action; 30 ft; one willing creature perceived with LoE; V/S/F; instantaneous compressed experience.** The creature must willingly consent **both at casting and resolution** to sharing one specific memory/topic.

The caster and target identify a specific memory or sufficiently narrow topic; the **target selects the actual remembered episode** that satisfies that consent. On resolution the caster experiences up to **1 continuous minute of that remembered event**, compressed into the spell's instantaneous information transfer.

The experience uses the creature's remembered sensory/subjective perspective. A mistaken, incomplete, altered, implanted, or biased memory remains what that creature remembers; it is not converted into objective historical truth.

Speech/language is experienced as remembered. The spell grants no independent language comprehension; unfamiliar language remains unfamiliar unless the caster already has another way to understand it.

If the willing creature has no such memory or withdraws consent before resolution, no memory is transferred and paid costs remain spent. Active Undead/Construct creatures may qualify if they actually have memories and can willingly consent.

No memory deletion/editing, forced reading, unconscious consent, permanent recording, skill/proficiency download, instant crafting/research progress, or objective verification. Protection from Scrying can still prevent the protected creature from supplying new Divination information; willingness alone does not override it.

**NEW:** specific-consent procedure and maximum one-minute continuous memory episode.

### 12. Corruption Insight

**Action; 30 ft; V/S/F; instantaneous.** Choose either:
- one creature you perceive within range and LoE; or
- one fixed **10-ft-radius sphere** centered on a perceived point within range and LoE.

Creature mode examines actual Corruption affecting that creature. Area mode examines actual **environmental Corruption belonging to the selected area itself**; it does not automatically inspect every creature/object merely standing inside.

Report the **type** and **proximate causal source** of one actual current qualifying Corruption record:
- type = its actual canonical corruption category/type/name where recorded;
- source = the immediate recorded cause responsible for that corruption, such as an actual spell, curse, artifact, creature effect, site, environmental source, etc.

Do not extrapolate from the proximate cause to an upstream conspirator, owner's location, hidden controller, or complete history.

If several corruption records qualify and the caster cannot already distinguish one to select, the Director chooses one deterministically; materially unchanged repeat casts return that record rather than cycling through hidden records. If the caster can identify a distinct known record, it may be selected on a later cast.

Each requested field can independently return **Indeterminate** if undefined or specifically protected. If no Corruption is actually present and no relevant protection prevents determining that fact, report **No Corruption**. A protected result must not be converted into false proof that Corruption is absent.

No numeric Corruption track, cure, save DC, statistics, exact actor location, alignment, poison/disease inference, suppression, harvesting right, or causal invention.

**NEW:** 10-ft-radius area mode, one-record procedure, deterministic multiple-record arbitration and proximate-source definition.
