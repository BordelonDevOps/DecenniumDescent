# Contraption Crafter: four temporary gadget procedures

269 checks pass across 38 recorded histories. All three current Gnome variants pay an actual Warrior feat opportunity; all four d4 results receive construction/expiry histories. Parts and the use are committed at the start, interruption refunds neither, and the next attempt uses 24 elapsed hours rather than midnight or rest. Acquisition supplies no parts, tools or general tool proficiency.

Lamp light, physical-contact bell, bounded clamp and held fan use their own Actions and limits. Handoffs retain the same identity and deadline; one damage destroys the HP1 object and spent parts provide no reclaim credit. Tinker Gnome clockwork, class devices, fuel and purchased supplies stay separate.

Physical access, supplies, work, turns and resolved object damage are conditional fixtures. Clamp release retains an ordinary-fall event for later resolution; full physics, virtual clock integration and campaign authentication are not claimed. Candidate source rules and approval flags are unchanged.
