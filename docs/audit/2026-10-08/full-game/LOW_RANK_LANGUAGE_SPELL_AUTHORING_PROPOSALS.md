## IL-02 — Silent Echo



**Existing source question:** Define a finite captured last word/phrase within10ft: actual hearing, maximum length, languages, source voice fidelity, projection location/audibility and instant playback. Resolve simultaneous speech and repeated casting. Mimicry creates neither translation, a compelled response nor automatic social or spell-component authentication.



**Preserved:** Core Action,10ft,V/S,rank0 and instantaneous rather than stored playback; Actual last word/phrase mimicry; each class access entry unchanged



**NEW proposed decisions:**



1. Capture eligibility looks back at most one minute before this cast starts, and takes the most recently completed intelligibly distinguishable spoken word or phrase actually heard within10ft. The caster need not understand its language.

2. A phrase is at most six seconds of contiguous actual sound; for a longer utterance use its last complete word or bounded phrase fitting that limit. A source without any actually distinguishable word/phrase is ineligible.

3. Replay once from one perceived unobstructed point within10ft at resolution. It follows the actual captured phonetic sound and voice quality, no louder than ordinary conversational speech. No rewriting, translation, missing syllables or visual speaker is supplied.

4. Playback occupies its actual recorded duration, up to six seconds, and leaves no stored charge, maintained field or later remote activation. This transient delivery is the NEW temporal completion of the instantaneous source, not a reusable recording item.

5. Tied simultaneous sources are chosen from the actually heard eligible utterances; older or unheard sources cannot be selected. The caster's new V is outside the already fixed capture window. A real eligible previous magical playback may itself be copied, without supplying additional fidelity or source authenticity.



**Proposed complete card:**



**id:** silent-echo

**activation:** Action

**rangeFeet:** 10

**targets:** One eligible actually heard recent word/phrase; one perceived unobstructed replay point within10ft

**components:** ['V', 'S']

**duration:** Instantaneous one replay, delivered over its actual utterance length (NEW cap six seconds); no stored use

**concentration:** False

**resolution:** Automatic reproduction of the eligible sound; no save or attack. No eligible captured utterance or legal replay point means no legal cast. Resolve mundane deception or mistaken attribution using the actual applicable social/perception procedure, without a spell-created success, DC or bonus.

**effect:** At cast start choose the most recently completed word/phrase actually heard within10ft during the previous minute. At resolution replay that sound once from the chosen point, with the original language/voice quality and at most ordinary conversation volume. Apply actual sound-path suppression and recipient hearing; hearing it is not understanding it.

**scaling:** None; no extra duration, volume, range, recipients or stored uses from rank/level.

**limitations:** No teacher, language competence, authentic identity, command authority or actual caster V is produced. Actual eligible sound may trigger a separate effect only if that effect's full predicate permits it. A heard recording cannot bypass actual controller authority, action timing or a source requiring a living speaker's act. Repeated casts each obey ordinary access/components and Refrain; hearing one replay does not refund or repeat a cast. If sound is suppressed at resolution, the legal cast still resolves/spends its history and yields no received sound. It cannot recover words the caster never heard.

**designNotes:** All capture age/selection, delivery point/volume/length and no-storage details are NEW proposals. Preserve the complete Core and catalog source; this is not yet available or approved.



**Authored boundary outcomes, not executed tests:**



- Two creatures utter different words at the same time within10ft; caster hears both clearly. Choose one of those tied latest utterances; replay one. No combination or arbitrary older phrase.

- Caster hears an Aquan warning but knows only Common. Can reproduce the actual eligible sound, but gains no Aquan understanding; recipients need their own comprehension.

- An eligible six-second echo is projected into an actual sound-suppression field. No sound reaches a recipient through the suppressed path; no stored retry or refund appears.

- A recording resembles a controller's voice. Resemblance supplies no controller authorization, current Bonus Action or spell component; only an independently lawful actual trigger can react.



## IL-26 — Silence



**Existing source question:** Preserve20ft-radius sphere and resolve Core20ft range versus origin/area and Instantaneous versus ongoing effect. Define duration/concentration, sound crossing boundaries, inside/outside hearing, moving creatures and verbal casting/commands. Distinguish the area from the Silenced condition; no assumed thunder immunity, vacuum or soundless physical event unless explicitly authorized.



**Preserved:** Core Action,20ft casting range,V/S/F and rank2/class-specific3RP base price; Printed20ft-radius sphere; no imported longer range or thunder immunity



**NEW proposed decisions:**



1. Place the center at one perceived point within20ft and actual line of effect; the20ft radius is separately preserved. The sphere is fixed in space and does not attach to a moving creature/object.

2. Concentration, up to one minute, replaces the inconsistent instantaneous operational duration for this persistent sphere; retain the original field as source history.

3. Ordinary sound does not transmit within or through any affected part of the sphere. Sound paths that do not pass through it remain subject to actual acoustics. This is a spatial acoustic field, not a creature-wide Silenced condition.

4. A creature whose actual speaking apparatus is inside cannot supply audible V or ordinary audible coordination there. A listener inside cannot receive ordinary sound. Crossing the boundary changes the applicable sound path immediately; it does not retroactively unsay or unhear an earlier message.

5. The field applies to either allegiance and requires no save. Ending concentration/expiration removes only this source. Other independent prohibitions remain.



**Proposed complete card:**



**id:** silence

**activation:** Action

**rangeFeet:** 20

**targets:** One perceived point with line of effect within20ft; fixed20ft-radius sphere

**components:** ['V', 'S', 'F']

**duration:** NEW concentration up to one minute; source Instantaneous preserved as conflict

**concentration:** True

**resolution:** No attack/save: create the fixed acoustic field at resolution. Ordinary target/area geometry and actual line-of-effect rules govern placement; no through-wall placement permission.

**effect:** Ordinary sound cannot be received along a path that traverses the sphere, including sound originating or ending within it. All creatures apply the actual hearing and audible-component consequences while their source/receiver/path is affected. Language knowledge persists. Nonvocal channels remain usable when independently lawful.

**scaling:** None; radius, casting range and duration do not scale.

**limitations:** No thunder/sonic damage immunity, vacuum, pressure injury, physical shield, thought suppression, universal deafness or class-feature acquisition. No spoken V can start this spell from within an already applicable sound-suppression field without an actual exception; the caster's own new sphere does not exist until resolution. The sphere can suppress the caster's later voice without automatically ending concentration maintained without audible performance. A narrower V-only prohibition or Silence the Caster is not this field. Overlapping spheres do not multiply penalties; track independent sources and endpoints. No movable-center update, standing command or extra Action is granted.

**designNotes:** Center targeting, fixed placement, duration/concentration and acoustic behavior are NEW proposed completions of the actual20ft range/radius source. Hush Descends remains a distinct class card; this spell inherits no class costs, hostile-only riders or Dread.

- Caster begins V outside all applicable fields, then the resolved sphere includes the caster. The completed cast is legal; later audible V fails while its sound path remains affected, but ordinary nonauditory concentration can continue.
