A paid Bonus Action on that regular turn directs at most one eligible asset. Do not grant an independent Reaction, an extra owner turn, a second command during an extra Action or a second offensive asset because spell/class asset caps differ. ## CL10-ORDER-01 — Command Undeath and released permanent bodies

**NEW Command-Undeath/Create-Undead-only schedule and agency completion**. Scope: No extension of DE4 domination to other sources.

Choose Q14 option B: an already independent bound creature keeps its actual pre-binding initiative position and regular opportunities. Do not migrate it to the binder's attached phase. An existing companion keeps its actual original owner's attached phase and all source-owner stats/slots. Binding, suppression, release, death of a controller and return from suppression do not create an immediate turn, refresh a used Action/Reaction, replay START/END hooks or reset a recharge. The exact actual owner or independent schedule must be bound before a current worked fixture can be claimed complete.

While Command Undeath is operative, its non-Charmed permanent restriction permits only defensive movement/Dodge without a valid command. On the binder's actual regular turn, a Bonus Action through the spell's 60-foot same-plane perceived/LoE command channel can queue one bounded order for one target's next actual future behavior opportunity. A turn already underway is not a future opportunity. The order expires at that single opportunity whether executed, suppressed, invalid, impossible or budget-starved, or at the binder's next regular START or issuance+6 actual seconds, whichever occurs first. The six-second cap is a NEW Command-Undeath-only absolute order expiry, not a new lifetime for the permanent relation. Only one pending order per binder-target exists; it cannot be banked through absence or suppression. Reaching a deadline that is also a possible execution point expires it first under the actual ordered phases. A successfully heard order does not create an ongoing remote channel: later plane separation can prevent new issue, not supply new knowledge or alter the already fixed instruction.

The issuing Bonus Action consumes the actual owner-wide command allowance of its issuance round. At execution the binder's owner-wide offensive allowance for that execution round must also be unused and is consumed once; a command that crosses a round boundary neither duplicates an offensive allowance nor refunds its prior command. For a source-owned companion, both binder's and original owner's structural allowance for that body/phase must be satisfied. Reuse an existing reservation only for the same body, same phase and same offensive opportunity; do not charge an unwilling owner a new Bonus Action or reserve another companion's attack. The absent original owner supplies no invented attached action phase. Resolve genuinely competing source-qualified directives under actual applicable ordering; this proposal does not grant authority to override another named source's explicit priority.

A valid instruction can use the body's actual movement and one ordinary Action, including a native inherent Multiattack only when the bound profile really has it. It does not force optional finite-resource powers, spellcasting, expended charges/recharges, new Bonus Actions/Reactions, secrets, prohibited oaths, item surrender or a suicide operation. Mandatory ordinary ammunition/material expenditure of an otherwise legal ordinary attack still needs the actual item; an instruction creates none. Type/obedience is not a source of private knowledge. Range and LoE for an executed attack are measured from the actual acting body, not the binder. No order changes donor or soul identity.

Suppressed Command control restores ordinary underlying agency at the same real scheduled opportunity; no instruction is performed while the control is suppressed and none replays later. When control resumes, restore the still-live relation and future defensive-only restriction without repeating its already-admitted hard-control save. Actual target death ends that relation; caster death alone does not, but a dead/absent/incapacitated binder cannot issue legal orders. The caster can release its own relation on its actual regular turn without an Action. It grants no automatic loyalty, hostility, faction membership or property ownership afterward.

For Create Undead only, losing/suppressing/replacing creator authority leaves the permanent actual body alive in its current animation state. On first independent agency, preserve that numbered round's spent opportunity and creation-round prohibition. Do not give an immediate action: place its next independent regular slot immediately after its former creator slot at that slot's next scheduled occurrence, using stable body-ID order for a group. If an old slot cannot recur because the owner has left the encounter, retain its captured virtual slot solely as a scheduling marker and assign an independent slot at the next actual encounter scheduling boundary; no retroactive turn is invented. Restoring authority retires that independent slot only prospectively into the creator's next genuinely available attached phase; at most one voluntary opportunity per round survives the transition. This is a NEW limited transition rule for these permanently created profiles, not a global controller/initiative rewrite.

Released bodies need actual NPC behavior/needs/context records before a autonomous-work/combat sequence can be claimed complete. Release is not a direction to attack, obey forever, donate HP, take a crew job or harvest another corpse. Two authority slots are not a world population cap. Repeated genuinely paid Create Undead casts with different eligible real corpses may accumulate independent bodies over time; no automatic service, material reward or free roster space follows.

Outside initiative, a released permanent body uses independent successive six-second opportunities beginning no earlier than its next unspent former cycle boundary; re-entering initiative maps that pending opportunity without replay or refresh. This defines timing only. It supplies no unreviewed NPC goal, ability, reaction budget, physiology or work permission.
