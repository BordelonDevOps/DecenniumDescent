## Principal source distinctions

**Soulburn economics are intentionally not hidden.** Successful undiscounted recovery is -7+3=-4RP and-20HP. Selecting Core5 would still yield-2RP. No imaginary base waiver is used to make the source profitable. Real acquired base-only waivers keep their own frequency, cadence and exclusions. A new rest cap is not justified by an unsupplied hypothetical exploit.

**Grave Gift chooses two genuinely NEW components.** It assigns effective maximumHP while retaining Wounds/Critical and, if blocked, independently stabilizes a still-Dying ally with one actual d4-hour deadline. Already-Stable keeps the old deadline. Current Chill Touch supplies a real broad HP-gain block; current Decay does not do so by name. Direct recovery is not a generic way to ignore every healing-spell gate. The full source-linked operation is in the proposed rule text.

**Suppression is not homogenized.** Golem/Wraith/Homunculus end; Knight temporarily loses expression but keeps identity/HP/deadline; Flood/Danse keep inert consumed physical bodies; permanent Create Undead retains flesh while control suppression restores underlying agency. Qualifying Null0 is separately bound, including an explicit limited extension for the new consumed animations. No AC4/AC5/AC6 living-ally healing predicate is imported.

**Wraith movement is whole-body.** A5ft body crossing a15ft slab from wholly before to wholly after translates20ft and costs40 at difficult rate2. The preserved15ft-contact/30cost/10remaining obligation can be developed through a10ft slab; it is not that larger full-slab crossing. There is no new1d10 rescue, distant last-open teleport, free cargo wall traversal or global Banishment conflict rule.

**Command's positive case is still conditional.** The26 complete frozen source-only bestiary records supply no exactCR2 Undead or exact Ghoul/Ghast/Wight/Mummy match, and none is promoted into an operative profile here. The four new created alternatives remain unrated. This is missing content, not permission to invent a target. The chosen command procedure nevertheless fixes initial admission, permanent relation, original schedule, shared budgets and order expiry.

**Permanent bodies are an explicit consequence.** Fresh eligible corpses and real repeated payments may create more than two independent animations over time. No creator loyalty, hostility, trained work, free donor stock, roster seat or global population cap follows. Actual retained needs and NPC context remain required before long-term autonomous-work/exposure cases can be called complete. Implementation/performance policy is a separate gate.

**Homunculus has a real source correction.** Wizard engine10 and the lifecycle/maxHP guide qualify actual Wizard-created creatures under the acquired Conjuration feature; Universal school alone is not an exclusion. The spell remains Universal. A qualifying Wizard11 creation can have20+11=31HP; a different caster does not acquire that bonus. A20lb/2Load parcel fits45lb/3Load but is Burdened, making walk10/fly20. Healing Rite and Mending do not grant generic Construct repair.
