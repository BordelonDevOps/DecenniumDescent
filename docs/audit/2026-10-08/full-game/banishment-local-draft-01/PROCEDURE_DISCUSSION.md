# Banishment — local authoring draft

UNSUBMITTED AND UNADOPTED. This draft deliberately marks unresolved return, visitor and transport-block interactions; it is not a selectable spell or approved cosmology.

## Source and authority

PRESERVED: Abjuration rank4 through each actual access row, base7 RP, Action, 60 feet, V/S/F, and temporarily sending one creature to another plane. Core labels duration Instantaneous and provides no concrete save, exile destination, timer or return method. NEW proposals below supply those missing procedures; none is recovered cosmology, an existing class ability, or approved playtest text. The old wait-for-push sequencing hold is satisfied. Banish Unholy remains a different Cleric technique and grants neither this spell nor its destination/return rules.

## Casting and target

Choose one actually perceived creature within60 feet and line of effect, including yourself. Use its current body, not an image, corpse or soul record. Actual creature-classified Undead and Constructs remain eligible unless an exact defense applies. Require actual class access/repertoire, V/S/F, Action, ordinary casting affordability, real target geometry and the ordinary cast/counter sequence. NEW concentration starts at legal cast commitment, displacing incompatible prior concentration; maximum exile is60 elapsed seconds from successful resolution. A saved-against, countered or otherwise failed cast keeps paid costs and does not restore prior concentration.

## Save, hard-control and replacement

NEW target CHA save against the actual casting-class spell DC, binary; an actually willing target may elect failure before rolling. Success produces no exile. Apply real defenses, including an actually acquired Oracle Fate-Bound Boon IV +2 against banishment; Charmed-only immunity does not block an effect imposing no Charmed condition. Treat establishment as one hard encounter-removal component under the shared source-character/target/encounter guard: if that source already spent its guard, no exile occurs and no invented soft effect replaces it. A single continuing application does not spend again each turn. Only one live Banishment application may attach to a creature across casters; an already attached creature is ineligible for another until the first ends, with no replacement, stacked cell or renewed timer. Known illegality prevents commitment; hidden eligibility is adjudicated after commitment without a diagnostic/refund.

## Explicit proposed destination

NEW bounded spell-space proposal requiring independent GPT cosmology/interface review: successful resolution creates one isolated temporary extraplanar cell distinct from the origin plane, then transports the same target into it. It is not identified with Bastion of Flame, Veiled Expanse, Radiant Currents, Boundless Void, any mortal Great Wheel label, an afterlife, a named god or an unresolved cosmic identity. It establishes no reusable route, recovered world or global plane-creation permission. The cell has a level supporting floor, ordinary light and a breathable environment compatible with the admitted creature; it itself inflicts no temperature/pressure/environmental damage. Existing disease, damage, life states and other hazards attached to the creature continue normally. Floor, walls, light and air are ephemeral spell boundaries, not harvestable stock. Cell dimensions are the target’s actual occupied bounding dimensions plus10feet along each axis, rounded up to5-foot increments, with its initial body centered legally; this supplies5feet of clearance on each side without a source-absent size/type cap. Geometry must be an actual established finite body record, not invented hidden anatomy.

## Agency and isolation

The target retains its existing condition-limited Actions, Bonus Actions, Reactions, movement and concentration. The banishment itself imposes neither Incapacitated nor stasis. Boundaries prevent mundane passage and ordinary line of sight/effect into another place; there is no native exit or attack path. Independently acquired exact cross-plane information/travel powers retain their actual permissions and costs; the spell creates no knowledge of another destination, receiving headquarters or portal. An actual legal escape leaving the cell ends this Banishment immediately without dragging the escaped identity back. A failed escape spends only its own source-defined costs. No other creature can be admitted through a new Banishment or by calling it cargo; independently legal interplanar entry still needs its actual route, known destination and fitting space, and does not receive a free return entitlement.

## Clocks, concentration and source ending

NEW this spell uses a fixed absolute deadline of resolution+60elapsed seconds. Traveler time and outside time advance1:1 for this cell only. Extra turns, initiative changes, suppression, travel, death, scene changes and companion control changes do not restart it; no rest/resource benefit is created. Ordinary personal effect durations, concentration checks, HP/Wounds, death saves, Stable recovery, summon expiry, Opening/Horn and Hunt histories continue on their existing clocks. Ending required concentration, actual dispel of this application, actual suppression of this application, or the absolute deadline initiates return and ends the isolation rather than suspending it for later resumption. A caster may end its own concentration through the normal permission. Mere distance or separation onto the cell’s plane does not end this spell. Independent condition/source rules still apply.

## Transport and real cargo

Transport one creature identity with its actually worn/carried noncreature gear within real carrying and dimensional limits. No second creature travels as gear, including a mount, carried passenger, swallowed separate creature or controlled companion. Before departure establish legal origin placement for any separate carried body that will remain; if that separation is physically impossible, no transport occurs. Do not cut, copy or move an external anchor/fixture merely by calling it inventory: a tethered assembly that cannot be transported intact with this one body prevents that proposed transaction unless another actual rule already permits separation. Known physical impossibility is preflight failure; hidden or intervening impossibility defeats the paid transport without refund. This is an explicit NEW atomic-transport limit to be reviewed, not a claim that mundane restraints universally block other teleports. Record every actual transported object, its custody and its physical location. Objects used/consumed/ended inside follow their own rules; no replacement appears on return.

## Return anchor and early-return proposal

NEW finite-return mechanism requiring explicit review: preserve the exact legal origin footprint/orientation and height as the return anchor; it does not move with the caster, an origin vehicle or a later map view. There is no force-wall reservation. Before an actual event would make that footprint illegal for the target’s then-current returning body, end this banishment and return it to the still-legal anchor first, then continue that event under ordinary rules. This includes another body/object entering the anchor, terrain/support changes, and a target body/gear transformation that would no longer fit the anchor or cell. The early return occurs at the event’s real resolution boundary, before the geometry changes, never rewinds a completed atomic damage/life event and never cancels committed costs. After return, the event can affect the target normally; ordinary movement may stop because a returned body now occupies its intended destination. This is a scoped NEW spell behavior, not a universal preemptive-trigger system or a source of remote information. A support-loss event returns the creature before loss and then ordinary Falling applies; a plane-destruction event does not grant immunity. No knowingly invalid origin anchor may be accepted at casting.

## Return transaction and exceptional collision

At ordinary early ending/deadline, place the same current target body at its recorded still-legal anchor once, with the gear actually still carried. Do not create a new body, refresh HP or move a creature that already escaped independently. If true death occurred, return the same surviving corpse; Hunt/return-from-death law is unchanged because moving a corpse is not resurrection. If an independent source actually removed the body, return no substitute. Existing noncreature objects left in the cell return once to the anchor’s floor in their recorded stable object-ID order, using actual nearest legal placements for loose objects without embedding or duplicating them; items already taken out are absent from that list. A separately arrived creature has no automatic return grant and prevents final collapse while physically present; that unresolved exceptional occupancy requires the exact entry source’s exit/return procedure. This last interaction is explicitly unresolved for review, not permission to strand or delete a body. If a supplied effect makes the anchor illegal without the proposed pre-resolution return window, do not improvise crushing, eviction, duplication or an indefinite denied turn: record the actual collision and resolve an explicit joint-review completion before this draft can be adopted.
