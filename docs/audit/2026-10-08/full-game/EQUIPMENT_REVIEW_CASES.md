# Equipment cases for the exact manuscript review

These are review questions and acceptance cases, not independently approved mechanics. Resolve them against the completed GPT equipment conference and the final authored guide. Keep the original sources unchanged.

1. A Dwarf with insufficient Strength wears heavy armor. Preserve the actual ancestry heavy-armor speed exception; it does not remove Load penalties or automatically excuse a tower shield's separate Strength rule.
2. A nonproficient caster has worn armor and a shield. Determine one armor surcharge, actual STR/DEX disadvantage, and concentration disadvantage without counting two copies of the same penalty. A charge-only ancestry cast must not acquire a phantom RP pool or conversion.
3. A holy symbol on a shield satisfies its F permission but does not free a hand for S. A held focus can share the same otherwise usable hand for S only under the final explicit rule.
4. Soldier Shield Trained permits its repeating crossbow/shield combination only when actually selected. Five shots consume five real loaded bolts; the sixth needs a legal reload. Tactical Reload changes the reload action cost, not ammunition ownership.
5. An ordinary heavy crossbow retains Loading even if the wielder has Extra Attack. Separate legal Actions/Reactions remain separate Loading windows.
6. An arrow fired, recovered after the encounter, and fired again has one current custody record. Recovery does not replay the first encounter's spent lot twice. Destroyed special ammunition is not recreated.
7. A net cannot target a formless creature or one larger than Large. Its one-attack restriction applies to its Action/BA/Reaction. Cutting it does not damage the restrained creature.
8. A Superior greatsword critical distinguishes doubled normal 2d6 weapon dice from its one additional quality die. Confirm the final source interpretation and keep noncritical attacks free of unprinted damage bonuses.
9. Forge Dwarf's source quality label, a recipe's Exceptional outcome, and a purchased Exceptional weapon do not silently become three stacked combat bonuses.
10. Source Dwarven plate remains AC 18 despite descriptive superior-protection wording. Elven weave supplies exactly its printed Stealth advantage.
11. Explorer's Pack has 59 pounds of printed gross equipment, counting the full waterskin once. The purchase supplies no extra pack-shell weight, carrier, magic storage or capacity waiver. Its container placement must be physically legal.
12. A potion belt carries six actual vials, with the belt's own empty weight. Putting an alchemist's bottle in storage does not turn one dose into three doses.
13. A waterskin recorded as five pounds full already includes its water. Pouring out one pint changes actual contents; it does not count the original gross weight and the transferred water twice.
14. A partly used healer's kit retains its actual charge count when transferred. A cloth or empty kit does not refill charges. Battlefield Medic's stabilization override applies only when its actual trigger occurs.
15. A Wildling's created basic healing potion is distinct from Field Storm Potion, despite the crafting recipe's preserved legacy healing text. Basic healing cannot remove a Wound or resurrect a dead target.
16. Alchemist's fire enters the existing ongoing-fire registry. Two applied flasks cannot create two simultaneous winning family pulses. Extinguishing and end-turn saves use the reviewed condition procedure.
17. Oil ignition, periodic ground fire and a direct alchemist's-fire application must state whether they share a family; do not allow duplicate ticks through ambiguous names.
18. Ordinary holy water checks actual Fiend/Undead type. The active Undead ancestry carries an actual Undead type; no inference from appearance or religious affiliation is needed.
19. An unwilling conscious creature cannot be bound with manacles or force-fed a potion through a free object interaction. Applying, escaping and destroying restraints use the stated actions and anatomy requirements.
20. A Sunder attempt attacks one legal object/component and never also deals free creature damage. A construct joint remains subject to the actual creature's rules. Unknown artifact durability is not assigned a mundane default.
21. A broken tool remains the original recoverable matter. Repair cannot duplicate it, restore consumed ammunition, regenerate an emptied vial, or multiply sale value by erasing its purchase provenance.
22. A Large character gets equipment fitting, mass and cost according to the reviewed rule, with no unprinted enlarged damage die. Powerful Build affects carrying only where its actual source says so.
23. The wealth route replaces both class and background physical packages and package money. A background's skills, qualitative standing and actual feature remain. An ancestry casting token remains scoped to ancestry.
24. A narrative background relic or journal preserves every actual source property without automatic combat power, free spells, universal focus access or unlimited resale value. Celestial Emissary's printed dim light must receive an exact bounded radius without cosmology adjudication.
25. Mundane resale uses the correct baseline and the actual buyer's qualitative standing. It does not combine an ordinary equipment markup, craft result grade and Core artifact valuation as three independent multipliers.
26. The public equipment guide references only public procedures. Restricted/Director recipe records are preserved in their own source boundary; source capture does not promote all recipes or certify full economy/transport readiness.

All quantitative procedures added in this pass remain authored playtest rules. These cases validate manuscript consistency, not human balance or companion execution.
