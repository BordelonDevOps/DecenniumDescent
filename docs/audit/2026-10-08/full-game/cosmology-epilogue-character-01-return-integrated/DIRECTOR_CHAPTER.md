# Epilogue Character Interfaces — Director Rules
**Status:** NEW PROVISIONAL AUTHORSHIP. Preserve source text separately. Campaign epoch and hidden identities remain unresolved.

Use the Player chapter as the public mechanical procedure. This chapter supplies adjudication, challenge answers, finite destination scenes, and anti-exploit boundaries.

## Home affiliation conduct
Warn before a foreseeable knowingly-contrary commitment. Evaluate only materially relevant deliberate choices in the 24 hours before the beneficial Long Rest. One or more aligned choices plus one or more contrary choices is **mixed conduct**: neither Inspiration nor Disquiet.

## Motif ledger and commitment
At adoption, assign a stable motifLedgerId to each genuinely separate Expedition. Split descendants keep the same ledger/history indefinitely. Merges link contributing histories using the latest refresh timestamp and lowest allowance; later splits do not unlink them. One registered member cannot refresh two linked pools in the same 24h.

Ordinary motif commitment occurs **only at successful 60-second completion after completion recheck**. Candidates who did not hear/remain willing/in range throughout are removed. If no legal effect remains, no commitment/no spend. Law is different: after legal Action preflight it commits during that Action immediately before denial/immunity/save. After commitment no refund for immunity, denial or no benefit.

**Shared hard-denial mark:** Law and mask reveal share one mark keyed source character + target + actual encounter. Legal activation pays cost first. Existing mark blocks the EPI control effect with no replacement rider. Set a new mark only when Law control or mask Stunned actually applies. Existing class features keep their own printed fallback. Initiative changes do not reset encounter identity.

## Transition and Balance
Transition fixes workOrder, task, materials, roles and up to four beneficiaries at preflight. Beneficiary must begin within 24h. Formula after exact source modifiers: for remaining minutes `r>=1`, `min(r,max(1,ceil(r/2)))`; positive sub-minute stays unchanged. Required simultaneous workers remain present/qualified for the actual common phase.

Examples: Wizard preparation60→45 by exact25% modifier→23 with Transition. Forge craft480→360→180. A ritual qualifies only if an exact rule supplies active casting-work time; a 10m base that Fervent Ritualist actually reduces to9m then becomes5m. Never infer work time from an effect duration.

Balance Wizard swap removes modifications from the removed matrix, makes any pair containing it inactive while history persists, and gives the replacement no modification until a later full preparation. Stability Reinforcement stops supplying its +2 once that matrix is removed; already-cast spell continues under its own rules. Druid Arch choices are Dire Wolf, Earth Warden (Dire Bear), Sky Serpent. Warrior Flesh Warden removal clamps current HP to reduced max; later capacity does not heal.

## Vows and masks
Every PC begins with one unfinished vow. Revision/retirement remains player-controlled. If a vow changes mid-project, disclose consequences: cancel and spoil blank, or finish a cosmetic dormant mask under original class/vow/deadline; later 1h rebind is available.

Only one operative mask. Completion of a second mask does **not** deactivate the first. If an operative mask already exists, switching designation occurs only at completion of a beneficial rest. Fulfilled/retired/superseded vow dormants its bound mask immediately. Reveal ledger belongs to wearer, not item/vow.

Finite blank reserve is keyed to adoption class. A completed mask weighs2lb. Repair requires an actually available suitable workshop and10gp of actually available mundane material; workshop description/price is not inventory.

## Conflict Die
Use the Player procedure verbatim. Exhaustion immunity at declaration makes the check ineligible. A known successful result that would grant immunity before the price also makes it ineligible. Unforeseen independent immunity after legal declaration follows its real rule without deferred punishment.

## Archon Petition symbolic acts
After Witness consultation and one uninterrupted hour of meditation, complete the chosen virtue's act:
- **Courage (10 minutes):** Name one feared but voluntary in-fiction task and take one concrete preparatory step toward it.
- **Balance (10 minutes):** Balance two unequal physical loads so neither support bears more than half the combined weight.
- **Law (10 minutes):** Write one rule for the current problem and one explicit exception condition.
- **Sorrow (10 minutes):** Memorialize a real in-fiction loss without inventing a new historical fact.
- **Pattern (10 minutes):** Arrange seven marked tiles into a repeatable documented sequence.
- **Contradiction (10 minutes):** State two apparently conflicting true propositions and the context in which both can be true.
- **Transition (10 minutes):** Disassemble and reassemble a simple nonmagical marker/tool into a different useful configuration.

Then roll the petition d12 under the Player procedure.

## Archon Petition shared Test procedure
One petitioner is the challenge actor. In round scenes the petitioner has one Action per 6-second round; helpers do not supply extra challenge turns. Actual external features work only when their exact target/timing permits them and real resources voluntarily spent remain spent. For challenge task checks, Critical Success/Success both resolve the listed success and Failure/Critical Failure both resolve the listed failure unless the scene expressly says otherwise. An action/check completing exactly at the deadline resolves before timeout. End immediately on success or failure; no same-petition restart. Projected props/hazards vanish and cause no real injury, but real character resources spent are not refunded unless their own rule refunds them.

### Courage — The Shaking Span
**Props:** Projected near platform 10x10 ft; far platform 10x10 ft; a 30-ft-long,5-ft-wide rope bridge connects them. Courier+sealed ledger begin at the near end of the bridge beside petitioner. Projected safety rope is coiled on near platform.

**Clock:** Three rounds/18 seconds. Resolve petitioner Action and any end-round courier movement first; at the end of round3, after that movement, release far anchor. Thus a third successful Brace on round3 reaches safety before release.


- **Coach:** Action, courier can hear within normal speech across bridge; CHA (Persuasion) DC15. Success immediately moves courier+ledger15 ft along bridge. Two successes by end round3 reach far platform.
- **Brace:** Action while petitioner can physically brace the line from near side; STR (Athletics) DC15. Success moves courier+ledger10 ft at end of that round. Three successes, including one on round3, reach far platform before anchor release.
- **Safety line:** Round1 Action DEX (Acrobatics) DC15 to rig projected rope. Success secures line; round2 Action escorts courier 15 ft; round3 Action escorts remaining15 ft with no further check. Failure leaves rope unrigged and another attempt may be made next round.

**Success:** Courier and ledger both on far platform before end of round3.

**Failure:** Otherwise projected freeze prevents injury and the Test boon fails. Projected props vanish; any real resource voluntarily spent on actual abilities remains spent.

**Cleanup:** All projected props vanish; real character resources actually spent remain spent unless their own rules say otherwise.

### Balance — The Twin Cistern
**Props:** Projected basins: capacity7 and5, both empty; reservoir contains exactly9 units; one3-unit cup; no other vessels. Gate reads 'Exactly six units in the basins; spill none.'

**Clock:** 20 elapsed minutes; each fill/pour is one Action/6 seconds outside initiative for bookkeeping.


- **Direct:** No check. Fill the3-unit cup and pour both full cupfuls into the7-unit basin (6/7), leaving the5-unit basin empty; or place one full cupful in each basin (3/7 and3/5). Each fill or pour is one Action/6s.
- **Study:** 10 minutes INT (Investigation) DC15. Success states a legal two-cup solution. Failure gives no false solution. Critical Failure has the same task failure plus one projected reset. A check completing exactly at20:00 resolves, but no scene time remains for the required physical pours.

**Success:** Exactly6 units in basins with no capacity exceeded/spill before timeout.

**Failure:** Every spill or stated Critical-Failure reset increments one common reset counter; third reset fails immediately. Timeout without completed legal pours fails.

**Cleanup:** Projected liquid/containers vanish.

### Law — The Broken Queue
**Props:** Projected claimants A/B/C; four numbered ration tokens. Start: A holds #1,#2; B holds #3; C holds none; #4 is in tray. Posted rule: 'One token per claimant. Give an unclaimed token to the earliest claimant still lacking one. If none lack one, leave extras in tray.' Queue order A,B,C.

**Clock:** 15 elapsed minutes. Token pickup/placement/reclaim is one Action each (6s outside initiative bookkeeping). An amendment proposal takes1 minute; each projected claimant answers immediately yes/no.


- **Apply rule:** No check: reclaim one of A's extras, give exactly one token to C, leave the remaining fourth token in tray. Final A/B/C each exactly one.
- **Voluntary correction:** CHA (Persuasion) DC15, up to two1-minute attempts, may get A to return an extra voluntarily; failure never forbids later lawful nonpunitive redistribution.
- **Audit:** 5 minutes INT (Investigation) DC10 identifies A's duplicate holding; does not decide policy.
- **Prospective amendment:** Before any redistribution, petitioner states one prospective amendment limited to queue order and which numbered single token each claimant receives; it cannot waive the one-token-per-claimant cap or require punitive forfeiture. Petitioner plus projected A/B/C must all explicitly agree during the1-minute proposal. Then apply amended rule transparently.

**Success:** Without a unanimously agreed prospective amendment: A/B/C each exactly one token and #4 remains tray. With a legal amendment, the one-token-per-claimant cap still holds and any fourth extra remains tray.

**Failure:** Empty/withheld claimant allocation, secret retroactive rule change, coercive punishment, exceeding one token per claimant, or timeout fails.

**Cleanup:** Tokens/claimants vanish.

### Sorrow — The Missing Name
**Props:** Projected stones Aven/Bria/Cale/blank; authenticated briefing: Daro carried the lamp home alive after the recorded event; the record does not identify which missing deceased belongs on the blank stone.

**Clock:** 10 elapsed minutes.


- **Preserve uncertainty:** Immediate explicit choice: leave blank or inscribe 'Unknown' and state that the evidence establishes Daro survived but does not identify the missing deceased.
- **Analyze:** 10 minutes WIS (Insight) or INT (History) DC15. Success confirms insufficiency; Failure still yields no fabricated identity. A check completing exactly at10:00 is followed by one immediate no-time answer before timeout.

**Success:** Refuse to invent unsupported dead identity while still honoring the loss.

**Failure:** Confidently inscribe Daro/another unsupported name, give no explicit preserve-uncertainty answer, or leave response unfinished at timeout.

**Cleanup:** Projection vanishes.

### Contradiction — The Mirror Ledger
**Props:** Door ledger states 'A never crossed the vault threshold.' Mirror ledger states 'A appeared inside at bell.' Wall note: 'The interior mirror projects the exterior witness line into the vault; projections are not entrants.' Logs are authenticated within projection.

**Clock:** 10 elapsed minutes.


- **Reconcile:** No check if player explains both ledgers can be true: A's image appeared inside while A did not enter.
- **Analyze:** 5 minutes INT (Investigation) DC15. Success points to wall note/projection mechanism. Failure leaves records unresolved. A second attempt is legal only if5 full minutes remain.

**Success:** State a context where both authenticated claims coexist without calling either false.

**Failure:** Unsupported fraud accusation, no stated reconciliation, or timeout fails. Two failed 5-minute analyses consume the full clock and therefore fail unless an immediate correct reconciliation was already stated at the deadline.

**Cleanup:** Projection vanishes.

### Transition — The Moving Steps
**Props:** Three projected 5x5-ft platforms A/B/C occupy Left/Center/Right tracks; each neighboring platform footprint is separated by a 5-ft open gap. Exit ledge is 5 ft beyond the Right platform edge. Crystal starts on A with petitioner; carrying it prevents Dash. At each round-end platforms rotate Left→Center→Right→Left.

**Clock:** Three rounds/18 seconds.


- **Ride:** Remain on A through end round1 (A→Center), remain through end round2 (A→Right), then on round3 use normal movement to step with crystal onto exit; no check.
- **Jump:** Use actual movement/jump rules. If the character's actual movement can clear the 5-ft horizontal gap, make DEX (Acrobatics) DC15 because the destination platform is moving. Success lands on the chosen adjacent platform with crystal; failure leaves the petitioner prone on the origin platform with that attempted movement spent. Speed0 cannot make the normal step/jump unless an actual ability says otherwise.
- **Read cycle:** Action INT (Engineering) DC15 identifies next round's rotation only; no movement. Failure consumes Action and gives no false direction.

**Success:** Crystal reaches exit ledge (a 5-ft step from Right platform) before end round3.

**Failure:** Otherwise projection resets harmlessly after round3; real resources voluntarily spent remain spent.

**Cleanup:** Crystal/platforms vanish; real expended resources stay expended.


## Concord Step
All four visitor alcoves below are enabled by this module as **NEW provisional adaptation scenes**. They are not recovered geography. Outbound requires an existing accessible hub physically on Aethel designated as a15-ft threshold. If no such hub is reachable, the granted sigil remains unspent but can still expire under its inter-session outbound window; disclose this before the petition roll.

**Destination area:** Each destination is a closed 20x20-ft visitor room plus a 5x10-ft vestibule behind one ordinary interior door. Six 5-ft arrival spaces are marked in the room. The vestibule's outer stone/metal door has no authored route beyond it and cannot be opened by this module. Light, breathable air, and temperate conditions persist after the 8h return deadline; expiry does not become lethal. Each room contains exactly six sealed 1-day mundane ration packets and six 1-quart water flasks total; once consumed they do not replenish. No beneficial-rest/Haven status is granted. Before outbound disclose that missing the return can strand travelers here and this module provides no onward/return route after flags expire; only a separately actually available approved travel effect or campaign rescue can leave.

**Outbound:** 10 uninterrupted minutes; petitioner must be living, conscious, willing and remain at origin. At start fix up to five total travelers including petitioner, all living/conscious/willing within10ft. Dead/unconscious creatures cannot be travelers; ordinary carried gear follows carrying capacity. Verify inter-session outbound window still open, origin hub accessible, chosen one of four enabled alcoves, traveler eligibility, legal destination spaces, and any stronger explicit travel denial. No commit if petitioner lacks a legal destination space. Destination has six marked spaces. Use them in fixed numbered order; occupied/illegal cells are skipped, then nearest legal unoccupied room space. Up to5 total travelers. If petitioner has no legal space, fail without consumption; if only some companions fit, disclose and omit companions in reverse declared order before commit. On completion recheck living/conscious/willing/present status and legal landing for each traveler. Move only valid committed travelers, consume sigil, record outboundAt and personal returnUnused=true only for actual outbound travelers. Ordinary carried gear within actual carrying capacity travels with them.

**Return:** Each outbound traveler has one personal returnUnused flag. Any subset with unused flags may attempt independently or together from destination alcove; petitioner need not accompany a companion return. A traveler can return at most once. 10 uninterrupted minutes from inside the destination room/vestibule. Preflight requires each participant living, conscious, willing, present, unused personal flag, and enough time to complete. Latest legal start is exactly outboundAt+7h50m. At completion recheck living/conscious/willing/present, unused flag, origin travel denial, and landing spaces. A companion who became invalid is omitted and stays with flag unchanged until expiry. If a solo returner becomes invalid, the return fails. Move valid participants to legal Aethel threshold cells, then consume only their flags. Interrupted channel spends elapsed time but no flag. Retry only if a full10m can still complete at or before expiry. A valid return completing exactly at outboundAt+8h resolves first; immediately afterward all remaining unused flags expire. Therefore start exactly at+7h50m is legal and +7h50m01s is refused. Session UI/opening advances no campaign time: opening the next scheduled session cancels only an uncommitted outbound activation, not already-issued personal return rights, which continue until their actual 8h deadline. At completion, if origin travel denial or no legal landing affects a traveler, that traveler remains and their flag stays unused only until expiry. Others may resolve independently.

The next scheduled session opening blocks only an uncommitted outbound activation. Already-issued return flags survive that UI/session boundary until their actual campaign-time expiry.
