# Skinning — candidate field guide

**Authored playtest candidate; exact review pending.** Use the [shared gathering rules](GATHERING_TOOL_CANDIDATE_GUIDE.md), body catalogue and exact card. Skinning uses the actual animal parts and procedures that a governing source permits. A name supplies no living-removal or regrowth procedure.

## Prepare the harvest

Identification remains the framework's one-minute creature-type knowledge check, with its own ability and assistance rule. Skinning item bonuses do not apply. Plainly recognizable unidentified categories can be selected with the existing disadvantage; do not invent hidden information or retry unchanged evidence.

Use an actual knife, bone saw, scraper or other physically complete compatible assembly, plus stable accessible support. No field rack is compulsory when a suitable surface suffices. A receiving kit cannot cut attached material; a scale remover cannot replace a needed bone saw. Check the body limits and all missing implements.

Roll the base once when the original manifest is made, capped by real anatomy and mass. Units are shared across categories. Skinning has no universal one-pound part: use the exact source unit or a predeclared real anatomical part or coherent usable patch with mass, dimensions and divisibility. A whole hide and its detached scales cannot both retain their original full mass. Preserve any real whole-hide requirement.

Choose categories before extraction. Partial time is ceil(full time × selected categories ÷ accessible categories), minimum one minute. Hurried work halves that applicable time, rounded up, with disadvantage, and must remain physically possible. Use the exact harvest DC if present; otherwise highest desired selected tier I–VI gives DC 12/13/15/16/18/19. Unknown tier needs an actual removal DC. There is no generic higher-tier permission. Use DEX or WIS/Skinning; one eligible trained assistant working the full task gives +1.

Knives support actual controlled cutting; scrapers support hide or compatible scale/chitin backing; scale removers support real lifting/removal of scales or plates. Tanning Tools support compatible hide/backing or scaled-sheet work. Preservation Kits support actual controlled receiving/separation, with necessary cutting tools supplied. An attempt-wide effect requires operation compatible with the whole selected allocation including reserve. A hide tool does not enhance an organ-only attempt.

Deploying or packing Tanning Tools takes one active minute each. Arranging or securing a Preservation Kit takes one Action with both hands each. Necessary setup may fit the stated task when the real workflow fits; packing afterward is additional. A short attempt may require an already deployed arrangement. Setup alone never satisfies the qualifying work minute.

## One donor, one ordinary claim

Before selection, record the actual donor, origin, life/death history, custody, original ordinary manifest, disjoint undamaged and damaged baseline, reserve, exceptional drops, unavailable parts, hazards and receivers. Damaged baseline is part of the original base pool. Reserve is real avoidable processing loss, not extra anatomy.

Starting extraction commits one generic ordinary claim and forfeits unselected ordinary categories, including their damaged and reserve units. Two workers must agree on one selection, lead and permitted assistant; they cannot separately harvest categories. Turning a carcass over, opening another cavity or discovering an ordinary organ later does not renew its claim. A separately governed exceptional procedure retains only its actual allowance.

Use Core degrees and natural shifts. Success recovers the eligible selected baseline. Critical Success also preserves at most one selected actual damaged baseline unit and up to the strongest eligible card allowance from real reserve. Public recipe-676 supplies one reserve unit on Critical Success: this is a **NEW completion** of an original data/prose omission. Its source effect stays preserved. Follow the prior recovery order, with neutral hidden-unit order and stable IDs for ties. Empty reserve gives no substitute or saved benefit.

Failure yields nothing and ruins the highest-value selected ordinary unit, including selected reserve; known equal-value ties allow choice. If unknown prices prevent that ranking, use the preregistered neutral order. Critical Failure ruins the selected ordinary allocation and applies the declared hazard, or 1d4 slashing to the lead when none was declared. Record a terminal no-check outcome if all selected material becomes unavailable or completion becomes unlawful. Surviving matter remains recorded after closure but has no renewed generic entitlement.

Fishing's original capture and first extra evaluation, including zero, remain intact. Where both routes have real reserve, the original record must divide it into disjoint Fishing F and Skinning S before the earliest affected capture or harvest. An old unpartitioned Fishing reserve belongs to F only; S is empty. Fishing's view remains F. Do not move unused F into S, strengthen an old award or infer S from desired output.

Eligible selected F already authorized by Fishing joins the ordinary allocation on a successful lawful extraction. Only independently evidenced original S can fund Skinning's critical reserve allowance. The one damaged-baseline benefit remains separate. Receipt records a physical unit once even if two records authorize it. Partial forfeiture and actual later loss still apply.

## Surface preparation and preservation

A card granting surface preparation can advance one eligible ordinary recovered hide, scale or chitin unit from Unprepared to Surface-prepared on Success or Critical Success. It must be no more than 10 lb and 4 ft² of actual treated surface; folding does not reduce surface area. Follow the predeclared target order. The operation completes ten minutes of compatible superficial removal of loose nonhazardous waste and orderly surface preparation inside extraction. It is not full fleshing, tanning, curing, sterilization, repair or manufacture. Organs, bone, meat and exceptional drops do not qualify.

A capable worker with compatible Skinner's tools, both hands and a stable surface can do the same feasible superficial work in ten active minutes without a roll or new chemical tax. Record waste and retained mass. No tier, value, Freshness, safe edibility, recipe progress or source identity changes. An already prepared or ineligible unit receives no substitute; there is no second target or banked step.

Keep the exact source's aging interval; otherwise use hide 12 hours, organs/blood 1 hour, meat 8 hours and essence 30 minutes from actual death/removal. Untreated organic scales/chitin use the NEW 12-hour hide default unless their source differs. One accumulated unprotected interval means Degraded; two means Spoiled. Degraded value halves and cannot meet Fresh requirements. Spoiled is unusable for ordinary ingredient/food use unless an exact recipe accepts it. Dry intact bone/horn/teeth retain their actual storage rule; a name does not establish a stable core.

Compatible hide/scales/chitin can receive a separate preservation treatment: one lot up to 10 lb, thirty active minutes, suitable kit, compatible sealable container, and 1 gp/0.5 lb actual preservative supplies. Use DEX or WIS/Skinning at DC 10. Only a genuinely participating Tanning Tools or Preservation Kit imprint can add its scoped check bonus. No assistant, extraction reserve or preparation award applies. Core shifts collapse to success/failure; untrained operation uses no PB and disadvantage.

Success pauses unprotected age for twenty-four elapsed hours from completion; it does not restore Freshness. Time before completion remains unprotected unless an earlier actual protection covers it. Treatment must finish before Spoiled. Failure spends supplies and time with no extension. A later treatment requires its own cost and work and expires twenty-four hours after that completion, without stacking duration. Breach, contamination or incompatible heat ends protection under the framework; its clean portioning exception of less than one minute remains. Suppression after a successful mundane treatment does not undo chemistry or packing.

Specimen and retained supplies both occupy capacity: a 10-lb hide plus 0.5-lb supplies exceeds the Preservation Kit's 10-lb contents limit. Use an actual suitable larger container or smaller lawful lot. Volume, shape and closure also matter. Recipe oils/salts are not free field supplies, and a name supplies neither containment nor decontamination.
