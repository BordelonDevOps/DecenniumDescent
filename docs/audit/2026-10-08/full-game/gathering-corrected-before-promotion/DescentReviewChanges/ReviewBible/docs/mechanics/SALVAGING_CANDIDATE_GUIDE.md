# Salvaging — candidate field guide

**Authored playtest candidate; exact review pending.** Use the [shared gathering rules](GATHERING_TOOL_CANDIDATE_GUIDE.md), body catalogue and exact card. Choose the governing route before work: mundane inventory dismantling, lawful construct-remains extraction and tool manufacture are separate procedures.

## Admit the target

Generic dismantling accepts one accessible, physically workable mundane nonunique item up to 50 lb. The trained lead needs actual Salvager's-tools proficiency and chooses INT or WIS. An untrained buyer cannot use a powerful tool to waive training. A Broken mundane item may qualify, but HP 0 does not establish zero matter or a full original component bill. A magical or suppressed-magical target, unique object, unsupported mechanism or structural installation needs its own separately completed profile.

Record current location, custody, origin, damage, manufacturing/repair history and actual component membership. Independently separate contents remain separate: safely remove them under their own permissions, and exclude them from the shell's bill. A magical object inside an ordinary container does not necessarily make the shell magical, but inaccessible, inseparable or unsafe contents prevent this generic route. The operation grants no safe handling of a hidden core or essence.

Before irreversible work, fix each recoverable exact identity's admitted Q0, physical units, permitted precision, condition, unavailable quantity, removal method and receivers. Identity includes relevant name, tier, properties, form, unit mass/value and source variant. Same-name parts with different profiles are not one pool. Purchase price, total weight, HP, repair cost or a hypothetical original recipe does not establish composition. Unknown composition yields no invented recipe stock or valuable scrap.

Choose a nonoverlapping representation for nested assemblies: the intact eligible subassembly or its constituents, never both. Loose tools, ammunition or supplies are not integrated components and transfer under their actual records. Spent fuel, solvent, ammunition, doses, charges, catalysts, RP, formulas and missing matter do not return. A heavy installation cannot evade its absent profile by inventing 50-lb chunks. A real preexisting separable subassembly needs actual lawful access and removal.

## Work and resolve once

Basic dismantling takes sixty active minutes with one trained lead: INT or WIS plus applicable lawful contributions, DC 10, binary meet-DC success and 25% recovery. Failure awards none. Natural 1 and 20 do not shift the result. The target is irreversibly dismantled either way; there is no harvesting critical yield, damaged-unit award or generic mishap.

Tool-assisted dismantling is declared before commitment and keeps the same trained lead, sixty minutes and DC 10 binary test. Register the actual compatible magical candidates. An eligible card adds its check bonus and may replace the 25% recovery ceiling. The public cards provide 25%, 35% or 45%, never an addition to the baseline. These cards grant no preparation benefit at any die result. If no candidate remains eligible, functional ordinary tools can still support the task with +0 recipe bonus and 25% recovery; do not replay or change routes after the result.

Allow five minutes admission/setup, fifty actual dismantling/separation and five sorting/receipt. Extra required station setup remains additional. There is no partial-category reduction, hurried half-time, creature-size time or assistant bonus. Other workers may handle actual lifting or cargo in their own time, but cannot substitute for the lead's work, hands or magic. The tools' receiving capacities limit real portions and supported loads; sequential handling must fit the necessary labor.

Reserve the target, pools, gear, receivers and time before work. First irreversible alteration commits the one claim and ends the integrated object's availability for use, sale or another order. Detached work-in-progress parts remain reserved until lawful resolution and receipt. Pausing preserves the claim, actual progress and continuing clocks. A replacement lead must be able to perform the complete required workflow without pretending irreversible removals can repeat.

## Calculate each identity

At resolution, Qi is the actual still-present eligible quantity, never more than its admitted Q0. Record every intervening reduction. Pi is 25% or the strongest eligible replacement percentage from a registered lead-operated candidate whose compatible work covered that identity's entire declared pool. Partial contact cannot enhance the rest. The check bonus comparison is once per task; percentage eligibility is per identity. Do not split or merge records after the die to improve rounding.

Recovery = u × floor((Qi × Pi/100) ÷ u). Use u = 1 for indivisible units; u = 0.01 source unit only for genuinely divisible bulk with that declared precision. Keep exact arithmetic until the floor. There is no minimum one, fraction of a lens, remainder bank, price conversion or compensating award when different percentages round equally.

| Recovery ceiling | One indivisible lens | Four indivisible metal parts | Three indivisible leather parts | Four source units of eligible divisible metal |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 25% | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1.00 |
| 35% | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1.40 |
| 45% | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1.80 |

Aggregate only distinct physical occurrences of the same exact identity within this work order; do not pool separate items or variants. Allocate output in the prior unit/portion order, skipping documented newly ineligible material. Actual collection may be lower because of a predeclared voluntary cap or genuine later loss, refusal or placement limit; record the disposition. “Up to” permits no arbitrary post-roll reduction. Calculation does not establish actual receipt, and a later loss does not reopen selection.

Completed success, failure, abandonment and terminal no-check outcomes close the claim even when every floor is zero. Track surviving unawarded residue separately from material that was physically lost. Neither unawarded residue nor forfeited matter gains a new generic claim from repair, a stronger tool, packaging, another owner or a new item ID.

Actually awarded components can fund lawful manufacture with real inputs, time and costs. Debit those components into the new product and retain lineage. A later lawful dismantling starts with matter actually present in that product, not the ancestor's original bill. Added material needs its own origin. A recovered intact subassembly cannot coexist in inventory with full credit for its constituent outputs. Repeated recovery without real new material remains bounded by successive percentage floors.

## Construct remains and storage

Where an actual source permits ordinary construct-remains harvesting, use the existing remains procedure: original finite manifest and once-rolled size yield, selected categories, partial forfeiture, Core degrees/natural shifts, exact or tier I–VI DC and one trained extraction assistant for +1. Tiny/Small/Medium/Large/Huge/Gargantuan use 10/20/30/60/120/240 minutes and 1/1d2/1d4/2d4/4d4/8d4 base units, capped by real matter. An otherwise permitted untrained extraction uses no tool PB and disadvantage.

Only a compatible Salvaging card's numerical check bonus applies on this route. Its percentage does not. Critical Success preserves at most one pre-recorded damaged ordinary unit. Failure and Critical Failure follow the harvesting framework, with a declared hazard or its 1d4 slashing fallback when none exists. Unknown-value failure selection uses the fixed neutral order. These rules do not migrate into inventory dismantling.

Previously harvested remains cannot become an inventory item for another percentage pass. Independently separate gear cannot also be counted as anatomy. A disabled, sleeping, controlled or bonded construct is not automatically a corpse or mundane target. Actual origin, life and source records govern. The tools do not extend Mining parcels, Fishing entitlements or Skinning reserve.

Keep actual removal/death times, unprotected age, condition and containment through all transfers. Dry intact ordinary parts can keep their own storage rule; volatile cores and essences need their actual special profile. A fitting box is not automatically safe containment. “Stabilizer,” “Purification agent,” “Separation matrix” and “Refiner” name source requirements; none grants an independent detoxification, storage or magical-target procedure.
