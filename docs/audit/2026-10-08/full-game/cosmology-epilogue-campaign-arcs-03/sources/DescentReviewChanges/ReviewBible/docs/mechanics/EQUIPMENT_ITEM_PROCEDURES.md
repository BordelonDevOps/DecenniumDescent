# Common equipment procedures

**REVIEWED authored operational PLAYTEST equipment/acquisition scope.** These new procedures complete source item names and prices under the [equipment guide](EQUIPMENT_ACQUISITION_PLAYTEST.md). They do not claim to reproduce missing Player-book effect text. Use the complete [source tables](EQUIPMENT_SOURCE_BINDINGS.md) for costs, weights and bundles.

Unless stated otherwise, using a consumable requires an Action, one available hand and access to the actual item. Spend one real unit. Drinking a potion or antitoxin, or feeding one to a willing or Incapacitated living creature within 5 feet, takes an Action. Inability to swallow blocks an oral dose. This gives no free way to dose an unwilling active creature. A potion, poison and healer's kit are not spells.

## Medicine and alchemy

| Item | Procedure |
|---|---|
| Basic Potion of Healing | Drink or feed to restore 2d4 + 2 ordinary HP, subject to actual healing blockers and caps. No Wound removal, resurrection, resource refill or stabilization of a Dead target. |
| Antitoxin | Drink or feed for advantage on saves against poison for one elapsed hour. It grants no immunity, automatic cure or advantage on unrelated CON saves. A new dose uses the later expiry without stacking advantage. |
| Healer's kit | A full 5-gp, 3-lb kit has ten consumable charges. Action and one charge automatically stabilize an adjacent living dying creature under the reviewed Core rule. Other medical procedures retain their own time, checks and charge costs. No universal HP healing. |
| Acid vial | Action: ranged improvised attack against one perceived creature or object within 20 feet, using DEX and PB only when actually eligible. Hit: 2d6 acid, with no ability damage. The vial is consumed hit or miss, with no splash or persistent acid pool. |
| Alchemist's fire | Use the same 20-foot Action attack as acid. A hit applies reviewed Burning at 1d4 fire, with no immediate damage. Its missing source DC uses the existing DC 10 fallback. Burning's family arbitration, regular-end pulses, saves and extinguishing procedures apply. Another flask does not create a second simultaneous winning fire-family pulse. |
| Holy water | Action: the same 20-foot ranged improvised attack, or a splash attack against one adjacent target. Hit: 2d6 radiant only if the target actually has Fiend or Undead type. The active Undead ancestry has that type. The flask is consumed even against an ineligible target. No inferred type from appearance or religious affiliation, and no extra exorcism. |
| Basic poison | Action: coat one piercing/slashing weapon or up to three compatible piercing/slashing ammunition units with one dose. For one elapsed minute, the first qualifying weapon hit, or first hit by each coated ammunition unit, forces DC 10 CON: failure takes 1d4 poison, success zero. That coating is then spent. No Poisoned condition. Coatings do not stack; expiry or washing ends them. |

Each coated ammunition unit keeps its own one-hit entitlement until used or expiry. Missing does not itself remove the coating, although firing still expends the ammunition normally. Recovery and physical integrity still apply.

A healer's kit transferred between owners keeps its actual remaining charges. Refilling a full ten-charge allotment costs 5 gp; an empty bag or reused cloth creates no charges. A healer's kit remains the source 3 lb while it exists in this candidate, regardless of remaining charges. Charge depletion changes its uses, not its recorded weight. Do not invent a separate empty-container weight or proportional mass until such a profile is expressly authored. Battlefield Medic, Shaman Orc and other reviewed medical exceptions retain only their actual triggers.

Wildling Natural Remedy produces this basic healing potion or antitoxin only through its reviewed beneficial-long-rest procedure with actual available components. It does not produce Crafting recipe-1 Field Storm Potion: that recipe's resistance effect and preserved legacy healing text remain distinct. Neither ordinary healing nor stabilization restores a Dead creature.

## Oil and light

One flask of oil fuels an appropriate lamp or lantern for six elapsed hours. Alternatively, spend an Action to spill it over one 5-foot square of level surface, or make the same 20-foot ranged improvised attack to coat one creature on a hit. Spilled oil does not ignite itself.

**Coated creature:** the coating expires 60 elapsed seconds after application. Before expiry, its first positive resolved fire-damage packet from another source consumes the coating after that packet is established. Then resolve one separate 5-fire-damage packet through normal defenses. A fully prevented zero packet does not trigger it. The coating then ends; it does not become a repeating Burning record. Track one mundane oil-coating record per creature across all users. A later successful coating spends another flask and replaces its deadline with application +60 elapsed seconds, without adding another pending 5-fire entitlement.

**Ground:** when legally ignited, the square burns for 12 elapsed seconds. It deals one 5-fire packet when a creature first enters the square during a turn, or at that creature's regular-turn start while it occupies the square, at most once to that creature during any one turn regardless of whose turn it is. Normal defenses apply. The clock does not reset when initiative begins or ends. No propagation through closed or carried containers is created by this rule. Wider environmental fire needs its own source.

| Light source | Fuel and duration | Illumination |
|---|---|---|
| Torch | One torch; 1 hour | Bright 20 feet, then dim another 20 feet |
| Candle | One candle; 1 hour | Bright 5 feet, then dim another 5 feet |
| Lamp | One pint oil; 6 hours | Bright 15 feet, then dim another 30 feet |
| Hooded lantern | One pint oil; 6 hours | Bright 30 feet, then dim another 30 feet; hooded, dim 5 feet only |
| Bullseye lantern | One pint oil; 6 hours | Bright 60-foot cone, then dim another 60 feet along the cone |

Spend an Action to light a prepared source using actual flame/tinderbox and fuel. Hooding or unhooding a lantern takes an Action. A source must be physically carried, mounted or placed; light ownership grants no extra hand. A torch used as an improvised melee weapon deals 1 fire damage with no ability damage and no automatic Burning. A candle gains no automatic combat-damage profile.

A tinderbox takes an Action to ignite exposed dry tinder or a prepared candle, torch or fueled lamp. Building an ordinary prepared fire takes one minute. It does not automatically ignite wet or incombustible material or create fuel.

## Traps and restraints

| Item | Procedure |
|---|---|
| Ball bearings | Action: spread one bag of 1,000 over a 10-foot-square level floor. A creature moving through normally makes DC 10 DEX or falls Prone, once per turn for this area. Moving at half speed avoids the roll. Scattering deals no damage. One minute recovers an accessible intact bag's contents once, without duplication. |
| Caltrops | Action: spread twenty over one 5-foot square. An entrant makes DC 15 DEX or takes 1 piercing, stops remaining movement that turn, and has walking Speed reduced by 10 feet until receiving at least 1 HP healing or spending an Action removing the caltrop. Moving at half speed avoids the save. Repeated applications from the same area do not stack reductions. This is its explicit physical control, not Root or Restrained. |
| Hunting trap | Action: set at a suitable anchor; the first entrant makes DC 13 DEX or takes 1d4 piercing and is tethered within 3 feet of the anchor. Action DC 13 STR frees self or an adjacent other; failure deals 1 piercing to the trapped creature. Reset with an Action before another use. Establish actual anatomy/weight compatibility before deployment. The tether does not add Restrained. |
| Manacles | Two Actions apply to a willing or Incapacitated creature with compatible anatomy within 5 feet, or follow an exact ability authorizing another binding procedure. Bind a selected pair of limbs: prevent the corresponding hand use, or hobble walking to 5 feet. Both effects require two actual sets. Action DC 20 DEX escapes, or Action DC 20 STR breaks; thieves' tools and Action DC 15 DEX unlock; a key unlocks with an Action. |
| Ordinary lock | Key and Action unlock, or actual thieves' tools and Action DC 15 DEX pick. Proficiency is not required to attempt with actual tools, but PB requires relevant training. Special locks state their own DC/time. |

Manacles create one restraint record per actual device, no unconsciousness or ownership. A free object interaction cannot bypass a resisting active creature. The above printed escape procedures take precedence over generic fallback restraints. Generic object destruction remains available only when the actual item is separately targetable and its durability applies; magical-only restraints do not acquire a cutting escape.

## Travel and practical tools

| Item | Procedure |
|---|---|
| Climber's kit | Actual harness, pitons and boot tips support a sound anchor. An Action secures the user. If the system stays intact and geometry permits, a fall stops within 25 feet of the anchor; this does not erase collision with an intervening surface or allow climbing past the rope without relocating the anchor. |
| Block and tackle | Ten minutes rigging at an actual sound anchor multiplies the user's ordinary pound lifting limit by four for a suspended load. It adds no carried Load capacity or flight. Movement still needs the actual rope, anchor, path and helpers. |
| Crowbar | Advantage on STR checks when its leverage physically applies; no universal STR bonus or extra attack damage. |
| Portable ram | +4 to a STR check to break a door. One actual helper can provide advantage. This grants no automatic object damage or Sunder attack bonus. |
| Spyglass | Distant things appear twice as large. No automatic Perception bonus, darkvision or penetration of obstruction. |
| Magnifying glass | Advantage on investigating tiny visible detail, not every Investigation check. |
| Merchant's scale | Compares real weights using actual standards. No automatic true-value or forgery appraisal. |
| Tent, bedroll, blanket and ordinary clothing | Actual shelter, bedding and weather coverage. No automatic safe rest, damage resistance or magical temperature protection. A two-person tent accommodates two ordinary compatible occupants, not two Large creatures without sufficient real room. |
| Other tools, books, ink, chalk, clothes and implements | Enable ordinary physically possible tasks using real quantities and the appropriate declared check. They grant no unprinted numeric bonus, ownership-based proficiency, infinite consumables, automatic trap discovery or universal lock access. |

A toolkit contains its ordinary durable implements, not every consumed ingredient or a complete workstation. Tool possession permits plausible attempted tasks; PB requires actual relevant training. Skill and tool proficiency on the same check do not add PB twice. Specific double-PB/expertise replaces the normal contribution. Recipe stations, heat sources and other separately required equipment remain separate.

Scribe's supplies use the existing Calligrapher's supplies item/training category. Herbalism kit and Alchemist's supplies are distinct. Ceremonial clothes use Clothes, costume: 5 gp, 4 lb. Celestial Emissary's particular dim-light reliquary uses the ordinary Holy Symbol Reliquary profile, 5 gp and 2 lb, plus dim light in a 5-foot radius while in darkness. It adds no bright light, sunlight, radiant damage or other power. This physical/light procedure makes no origin or cosmology decision.

## Examples

An alchemist's-fire hit applies a Burning record. At the creature's regular end, it offers its 1d4 fire through existing family arbitration; the normal ending save follows due ticks. Another ordinary attached fire may win instead. Spending the existing extinguish Action clears ordinary-extinguishable attached fires without an invented extra check.

A poisoned arrow that misses still physically exists wherever it lands. Its coating persists only until the original one-minute deadline and needs a legal physical recovery before another use. Recovering it does not refresh the poison or create a second arrow.

A full healer's kit with three charges spent has seven remaining. A transfer leaves seven. Battlefield Medic can change the result of its actual qualifying stabilization; using the kit repeatedly on an already Stable creature does not manufacture that trigger.

A party with five actual potions can put them into five vial slots on a potion belt if their containers fit. A six-slot belt does not create the sixth potion. The belt's own weight and all five filled containers still count.
