### LA-01 — Starting grants versus population descriptions



**KEEP exact grants; resolve descriptive-table conflicts. Candidate implementation; exact review pending.**



An ancestry's actual Languages trait grants the languages it names. A descriptive language-table population, culture, occupation or class association grants nothing by itself. Apply the actual base, one actual variant and the actual background; preserve all source text beside the resulting grant record.

Retain these base grants, with their printed speaking/reading/writing:

A Deep Dwarf retains Dwarvish/Common, without an additional Murkish grant. An Umbrin already has Murkish explicitly. A Storm Druid gains no Primordial merely from the population description.

The chapter's descriptive language groupings are not exclusive access ranks. Draconic occurs in its ordinary population table and as an Exotic example in the introduction. Preserve both labels and its single identity; an actual fixed grant remains valid under either description. LA-05 supplies finite choice eligibility instead of silently correcting the book.



**Worked boundary:** Mira's Battlefield Medic background supplies no language. ### LA-04 — Speaking, literacy and script knowledge



**KEEP explicit modes; NEW missing-mode completion. Candidate implementation; exact review pending.**



Record separately: receptive spoken comprehension; ability to formulate/speak that language; reading; writing; and familiarity with an identified script/orthography. Current hearing, sight, voice and manipulating capacity determine whether that knowledge can be used now; inability to use a channel does not erase the knowledge.

NEW default for a permanent ordinary language grant stated simply as “know,” “one language” or a background language row: it grants spoken comprehension, speaking, reading and writing in that language's ordinary attested written form, unless that source expressly narrows the modes. This completes the fifteen background fields and Imperial Human's shorthand consistently with the explicit base/High-Elf traits. It does not apply to a temporary “understand,” “communicate,” “basic ideas,” “fragmentary script” or research-progress effect. Those retain only their printed or separately completed modes.

Knowing a written language entails familiarity with the script actually used to read/write it. Gnomish and Orcish use the Dwarvish script as their source traits state. Recognizing those characters does not supply Dwarvish, Gnomish or Orcish vocabulary, grammar, fluency, cultural context or arbitrary codes. Other alphabet relationships are not inferred. Where no named script is supplied, use a language-local orthography identifier for bookkeeping; do not invent a lore name or assert an unrelated shared alphabet.

NEW script-only completion: selecting a specifically written/script-based competency provides reading/writing of its actually established conventional content, not a free oral mode. Runic's attested scope is script-based. Forgotten Script must be bound to the particular admitted written tradition, not “all forgotten writing.” Their spoken forms remain unspecified, not declared nonexistent. Knowing a script alone grants character recognition/copying, not an unknown language's meanings. A physical facsimile of writing is not an understood translation or a successfully encoded spell.

A “speak/read/write” knowledge grant does not generate missing vocal anatomy, sensory organs, a pheromone emitter, hands or a magical channel. Preserve the Mycelic description without authoring its biology here. Actual writing must be perceptible through a supported method: an ordinary flat ink inscription does not become tactile-readable by assertion. An explicit spell's touch-reading permission is a different source exception.

Temporary comprehension ends on its actual expiration/suppression conditions. A remembered specific message or a genuinely made translation remains ordinary acquired information; no general language or magical function persists merely because it was once understood.



**Worked boundary:** An Orcish-literate reader recognizes Dwarvish-script letters in Gnomish, but cannot translate the sentence without Gnomish competence, real evidence or a qualifying effect. A background's one named ordinary language includes literacy under this NEW completion. Comprehend Languages still grants neither speaking nor writing proficiency.

### LA-06 — Ordinary learning with finite work and costs



**NEW ordinary course; KEEP work and source-specific restrictions. Candidate implementation; exact review pending.**



No complete controlling ordinary fluency course was located in the supplied language evidence and current governing procedures. The bounded training search does not prove none exists in all historical project files. Adopt this NEW course for the candidate; these are game-authoring values, not claimed real-world acquisition rates.

A course names one actual language or dialect identity, the modes sought, its script where relevant, the learner, a qualified instructor or a complete usable curriculum, and legitimate access. An instructor must actually possess the modes being taught and a mutually usable instructional channel. Self-study requires sufficient authentic explanations, examples, exercises and a means of checking their meaning; a bare unknown inscription, an isolated translation, a price or a Language Insight balance is not a full curriculum. Spoken production requires actual means of practicing/receiving correction; a comprehension-only effect does not supply that ability.

NEW modules:
• Spoken comprehension and production of one language: 240 active hours.
• Literacy for a semantically known language in one written form: 80 active hours, comprising 40 of script/orthographic familiarity plus 40 of language-specific reading/writing practice.
• Script familiarity alone: 40 active hours, with no semantic fluency.
• A complete new ordinary spoken-and-literate language therefore requires 320 active hours. Prior genuine familiarity with its very same script satisfies only the forty-hour script module, reducing that complete course to 280 hours; it does not shorten the 240-hour language module.
• For an admitted written-only language, use 240 hours of its written-language semantics plus the same 80-hour orthographic/literacy module. It grants the attested written modes, not speech. Learning another dialect has no automatic related-language discount.

A source explicitly granting modes bypasses training only for those modes. Do not retroactively charge starting grants or newly impose training on existing lawful knowledge. Partial study can support specifically recorded learned words/cues as evidence; it does not satisfy a feature requiring knowledge of the language. No numerical language skill, PB increase or Expertise is created.

Credit completed fifteen-active-minute teaching/practice phases, with a final shorter phase only when needed to finish a module after a real source-specific loss/adjustment. Interruption loses the unfinished phase's prospective credit, not previously completed phases; real elapsed time, spent materials and fees remain. Resume from the same course ledger after actual prerequisites return. A missing teacher/channel, inaudible lesson, unavailable curriculum or contrary condition provides no progress. Administrative phase boundaries are not breaks in actual concentration. Apply an actual Oracle Tongues loss of ten minutes to the real credited progress; do not waive it by splitting the log.

NEW ordinary one-to-one instruction reference: 1 gp per eight actual instruction hours, including ordinary lesson consumables that the provider actually supplies. A named real quote or voluntary tutor can differ; neither quoted money nor ownership conjures the instructor, books, rare texts, copying rights or equipment. Self-study has no invented tutor fee, but requires actual curriculum/materials. Pay for actual instruction, including time that a real interruption or condition made unproductive; unused prepaid hours remain only under the actual agreement. Round the final purchase once upward to copper. No automatic refund, resale value or teacher availability is promised.

Training uses actual minutes within the existing eight-hour Watch and two-active-work-Watch ceiling, subject to the separately governing rest/Strain/access rules. No minute also funds guarding, travel, beneficial rest, crafting, another course, formula research, Insight gain or Insight expenditure unless an explicit actual rule allows concurrent work. Teaching also occupies the tutor's real work time. No calendar-day conversion, sleep shortcut, INT/PB discount, artisan creation benefit or pending feat applies.

Completion is deterministic when all required module hours, actual mode prerequisites and final authentic practice are recorded. Final practice occupies the last required phase; it adds no unbounded examination hours or separate completion fee. No last-minute random fluency check or extra failure fee is introduced. Complete precisely those modes; grant no trade XP, formula, spell, rank, faction permission or other language. Research, deciphering and temporary magical comprehension do not substitute for hours or modules.

Keep Quick Learner's pending thirty-day/once-per-category text in its feat queue; it is neither the origin of these hours nor an active course discount.



**Worked boundary:** An Orcish-literate learner already knows the Dwarvish script. Learning spoken and written Gnomish costs 240+40=280 active hours, reference tutor fee35gp, not zero because the letters look familiar. A learner starting with no matching script needs320hours/40gp at that reference. A 40-minute session interrupted in its third15-minute phase gives30minutes course credit but40minutes actual time; its single reference invoice is ceiling(40/60×100/8)=9cp. An applicable failed Tongues save later removes ten minutes of real progress; logging four fifteen-minute phases never pretends an uninterrupted session was broken.



### LA-07 — Understanding, translation, deciphering and research



**NEW bounded ordinary information procedures; KEEP specific routes. Candidate implementation; exact review pending.**



Ordinary comprehensible speech or plain text in a known language needs no check merely to understand it. Hearing/reading access, technical vocabulary, actual ambiguity, a separate code and the truth of a claim remain separate questions. Script recognition is not semantic understanding. Fluency does not itself identify magic, authenticate a speaker, detect a lie or supply lost facts.

NEW routine written translation: a capable reader of the source language who can write the destination language may translate accessible plain noncoded prose, using actual writing tools and stock, without a roll. Allow ten active minutes per250words, proportionately rounded up to a whole minute, minimum one. This is an ordinary-prose reference, not a new speed for copying/encoding formulas, magical payloads or authenticating contracts. A transcription in the same script can preserve characters without understanding them and receives no translation status.

NEW one-minute recognition task, only when uncertain and evidence-bearing: INT(History), DC10 for a familiar attested script/style, DC15 for a plausibly related attested dialect/script, DC20 for a damaged/obscured but still diagnostic specimen. Use the existing one applicable PB contribution and Core degrees. Success identifies only the supported identity/relationship; Critical Success may disambiguate supported evidence, not reveal a translation. If the specimen cannot support identification, there is no informational roll that invents it. Routine obvious recognition by a competent reader needs none.

NEW ordinary Translate/Decipher project: preregister one actual passage of at most250words, its real source meaning or finite supported meaning, the relevant bilingual/parallel/grammatical/key evidence, method, output language and potential missing sections. Spend eight active hours. For linguistic reconstruction use INT(History): DC15 for supported related-dialect reconstruction, DC20 for unfamiliar but adequately evidenced language, DC25 for damaged yet recoverable correspondence. For a separate mundane cipher, use INT(Investigation) with actual cipher evidence: DC15 for an adequate known key, DC20 for sufficiently constrained ordinary cipher analysis, DC25 for a difficult but genuinely solvable damaged/incomplete key. A plainly usable key may remove uncertainty entirely; no task manufactures the underlying language.

Declare the applicable DC and one lead ability/skill before work. Do not stack several proficiencies for several relevant disciplines. One qualified assistant may give +1 by performing all eight hours with a real useful method/channel; this NEW assistant applies only to this new ordinary project. It does not add assistants to courses, preservation, dismantling or a different source procedure.

Success yields only the preregistered supported passage/deciphered layer. Critical Success supplies no second passage, fluency, extra meaning, code bypass, permanent bonus or banked labor. Failure produces notes but no certified translation, consumes the work, and does not fabricate authoritative false lore; Critical Failure has the same informational result, plus only a genuinely predeclared independently lawful hazard, if any. Unsupported or inaccessible meaning makes a project unqualified, not a high-DC invitation to invent a solution. An illegible gap stays a gap.

A fresh check needs genuinely new evidence or eight additional hours of a concretely different supported method. Repeated rolling, a different reader holding the same unsupported page or reformatting the text supplies no new source meaning. Information found can become genuine evidence for a later project, but hours/rewards are not duplicated.

Keep formula research/copying at its own exact trade gates, DC, max(8, source minimumResearchHours), and one-hour/1sp copying rule. Keep Scribing Insight expenditure in LA-14 as a distinct supported-passage route without adding this new decipher check to it. Keep legitimate Seeker, Cult and Relic information features within their actual subject scopes; they do not create translation powers. Pending Polyglot/Diligent Researcher grant no modifier/time reduction yet.



**Worked boundary:** A reader can copy Dwarvish-script Gnomish characters without knowing Gnomish, but cannot call that a translation. Eight hours and a high History roll cannot recover a destroyed sentence for which no source meaning survives. Three genuine Scribing Insight plus its own eight hours can translate its supported passage without also completing eight course hours or gaining another Insight.



### LA-08 — Speech, signs and interpretation in real time



**KEEP brief communication; NEW finite scheduling and signals. Candidate implementation; exact review pending.**



Keep the normal six-second round and the rule that brief communication is not an additional Action. It is not free movement, a free feature activation, an extra Reaction or authority to interrupt a resolved event.

NEW finite scheduling: a creature's aggregate free ordinary speech/signing during one numbered round must fit at most six seconds of actual brief delivery. Count warnings/replies as part of that allowance, not a refresh on every interlocutor or extra turn. Outside initiative, use actual elapsed conversational time. This allowance never shortens another required action, permits overlapping incompatible vocal tasks or substitutes for the Action/Bonus Action/Reaction of an actual feature. A spell's own words are governed by its casting procedure, not a bonus ordinary utterance or a new universal word cap.

A longer deliberate exchange uses actual time and, in initiative, an Action for each additional six-second segment on a turn when that Action is available. Off-turn lengthy delivery needs an actual applicable permission such as a legitimately readied communication Action, or waits; a brief warning cannot stop someone else's completed attack. Apply silence, consciousness, speech, hands and other actual restrictions. Surprise alone retains its printed brief-communication permission; it does not grant the unavailable Action.

Interpretation requires the interpreter actually perceive and understand the incoming content and produce an outgoing form the recipient actually understands/perceives. A translator's fluency is not lent to the recipient. Receiving, translating and relaying must occur in that order. A short source phrase and equally brief rendition may fit the available interval; a long statement or multi-hop relay cannot be compressed into one instant. Record when the destination meaning arrives. After a check/effect has resolved, late interpretation does not retroactively qualify a hearing/understanding trigger. No default interpreter bonus or magical mental link is granted.

NEW ordinary party-signal procedure: ten active minutes of actual mutual demonstration/rehearsal may establish up to six fixed simple cues, recorded with meanings and participants. For example: halt, approach, retreat, danger, left and right. No new fluent sign language, arbitrary sentence encoding, universal cultural meaning or secret-codebreaking skill follows. A one-hand cue needs that usable hand and a receiver who can actually see its motion; a documented two-hand cue needs both. Distance, cover, light and attention must make the actual cue perceptible; no universal sight/hearing radius is invented. Complex formal sign languages require an actual source/course, not six cues renamed as fluency.

Accessible player communication at the table is not a fictional speech, gesture, V component or oath violation. Apply the character's actual declared action and capacity without demanding that the player perform the voice or signs. Fictional inability to speak does not prevent a player from privately explaining an intended action.



**Worked boundary:** Two allied interpreters cannot relay a thirty-second explanation through a chain before an attack already resolving. A Deafened character who sees the rehearsed retreat cue can understand that cue; it does not satisfy an ability requiring the character to hear the bard. Message's one-Action brief message retains its own existing bound, not a newly imported25-word limit.



### LA-09 — Hearing, silence and actual components



**KEEP native conditions; NEW explicit ordinary-channel completion. Candidate implementation; exact review pending.**



Deafened prevents hearing and hearing-dependent reception; it does not erase language knowledge, bar reading/signs/eligible telepathy, or by itself prevent speaking and supplying one's own V component. A hearing-dependent instruction, warning or performance cannot qualify merely because the creature knows the language.

Silenced prevents verbal components, command words, Chants and audible coordination as printed. NEW explicit ordinary-channel wording: this includes intentionally spoken ordinary messages; it does not prohibit thought or every nonvocal channel. A Silenced creature may read, write, sign or use an independently valid mental effect if its actual condition and physical state permit. Silenced is not automatically Deafened, sound deletion throughout an area, sonic-damage immunity or universal incapacity.

V requires the caster's actual audible speech. Ordinary translation, lipreading, recorded speech, another creature speaking, a signed message, a telepathic phrase or a voice-mimicking device does not provide that caster's V. S/F/material/access requirements remain their own procedures. A language grant never grants spell repertoire, components, a formula, RP or casting permission. An explicit actual class no-component or in-form exception retains only its exact scope.

Track each restricting effect separately with source, actual forbidden operations/recipients and duration. An operation is unavailable while any applicable prohibition remains. Removing one silence source does not remove another; identical prohibitions are not additive penalties or a longer stacked duration. A condition label alone does not rewrite a narrower effect.

Keep the combat action Silence the Caster narrower: it prevents verbal-component Reactions through its printed window, not ordinary speech or Action-time verbal casting, and uses its actual single concentration check. Keep generic concentration independent of silence unless sustaining that actual effect requires audible performance or another governing interruption applies. Bard audible performance and its rhythm rules have their express stricter behavior. Physical acoustic suppression and an actor-specific verbal prohibition are distinct records.



**Worked boundary:** A Deafened caster with usable voice/hands/focus can cast an otherwise legal V spell; a Silenced caster cannot supply V merely by telepathically saying its words. An enemy affected only by Null Command Aura may still speak ordinarily. A Silence the Caster hit does not suppress the target's next legal Action-time V spell.
