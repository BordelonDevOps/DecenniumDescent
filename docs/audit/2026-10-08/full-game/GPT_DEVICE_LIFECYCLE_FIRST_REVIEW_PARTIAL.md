# 1. Entity state model

Use mutually exclusive primary states:

`FUNCTIONAL`  
`DISABLED`  
`RETRACTED`  
`DESTROYED`  
`EXPIRED`  
`REPLACED`

plus optional `debrisAvailable`.

### Functional

Entity is deployed/active and can perform whatever its source permits.

### Disabled

Tinker protected-Dissonance state only:

- HP remains;
- slot remains occupied;
- duration continues;
- cannot move, attack, emit aura or trigger effects;
- repair or completed short rest may clear **disabled**, subject to source.

Disabled is not destroyed.

### Retracted

Applies to persistent Anacite Drone.

- drone still exists and retains HP/modules;
- not an active battlefield entity;
- cannot attack/intercept/receive commands requiring deployment;
- Drone ownership/cap still exists.

Retraction is neither destruction nor expiry.

### Destroyed

At 0 HP.

Anacite Drone explicitly persists as a 0-HP entity until a legal repair/rebuild.

Tinker deployables can leave an identifiable destroyed-object/debris record because Recycling, Salvage, Reaper and Snap Rebuild require one.

Totems at 0 lose their aura immediately. There is no supplied Totem repair/reactivation mechanic.

Standard companions are different: under the frozen rule they **disappear at 0 HP**.

### Expired

**NEW shared lifecycle integration:** expiration ends the active entity/effect and releases its capacity/slot without creating a destruction event.

Therefore expiry:

- triggers no Volatile Components;
- creates no salvage/refund entitlement;
- cannot be Snap Rebuilt where the source requires remaining original duration;
- does not reset or refund uses.

Timed Tinker debris created by an earlier actual destruction can persist as a debris record beyond the original timer where a salvage source needs it; the active deployable itself has already ended.

### Replaced

Capacity replacement is an administrative end, not destruction:

- release old capacity;
- end aura/effect;
- no hostile-destruction trigger;
- no salvage/refund;
- no explosion unless source explicitly says replacement causes one.

For ordinary visual/physical remnants not mechanically referenced by a source, **physical scenery persistence remains nonmechanical**. Do not manufacture blocking terrain or repair rights from an inert token.

# 2. Shared command budget

Apply the frozen companion rule as the outer ceiling:

> All companions belonging to **one character**, including multiclass companions, share one Bonus Action companion command and one damaging companion attack/action total per numbered round unless an expressly priced source says otherwise.

Tinker engine[1] is narrower, not looser:

> within a legal Tinker BA command on a regular turn, its drones/turrets/Scraplings cannot manufacture multiple damaging actions.

Thus one owner with:

- Drone,
- Sentry,
- Scrapling,
- Shaman elemental

does **not** get four companion attacks.

### Support versus damage

A support-only command:

- consumes the shared BA command;
- does **not** consume the damaging-action allowance unless it deals damage.

A damaging command consumes both the BA command and damaging-action allowance.

Commanded movement packaged into the same BA is not another action.

Default Drone follow/Dodge and standard-companion move/Dodge when uncommanded:

- consume neither damaging-action allowance;
- do not provide an attack.

### Reactions

Emergency Protocols, Protective Programming and Magnetic Wall use their printed **Reaction**, not the BA companion command.

They do not themselves consume the commanded damaging-action allowance.

Extra full Actions grant no BA. Extra turns do not reset the numbered-round companion command/damage budget or regular-turn allowances.

# 3. Gadget Sync

Track separately:

`companionCommandUsed`  
`companionDamageActionUsed`

### Earlier damaging command

If the owner already used the shared damaging companion action that round/turn, Gadget Sync cannot fire its turret shot.

### Earlier support command

If a BA support command occurred but did not consume the damaging-action allowance, Gadget Sync may still fire if:

- its trigger occurs;
- its 1RP is paid;
- all other source requirements hold.

The BA is already gone, but Gadget Sync itself is a triggered shot, not another BA.

### Gadget Sync first

Gadget Sync consumes the ordinary companion **damaging-action** allowance.

It does **not** retroactively spend the owner's BA.

A later BA may therefore command a legal **non-damaging support/module action**, but may not generate another device attack.

### Multi-target attacks

Rocket Tower's up-to-two-target shot is **one damaging activation**.

Advanced Turret Bay creates two objects, but a BA commands one turret, not both.

Shock/Burn/Concussive and other printed module denial/cadence limits remain unchanged.

# 4. HP and stat provenance

### Anacite Drone

`maxHP = max(1, 5 + actual INT modifier + Tinker level)`

**NEW integration:** because this is a persistent owned class engine rather than a short-lived created profile, recompute from the owner's current Tinker level and actual INT modifier when either permanently changes.

Capacity gain does not heal. Capacity decrease clamps current HP without damage under the reviewed max-HP rules.

No controller-transfer behavior is supplied; do not transfer this formula to another controller.

### Timed deployables

Engine base:

`maxHP = 2 × Tinker level`

`AC = 12 + PB`

**NEW creation-snapshot integration:** use owner level/PB at deployment for that particular short-lived object.

### Deployable Expertise

For an eligible HP-bearing deployable:

`+M maxHP and +M initial HP`  
`+1 AC`

Apply at creation/deployment.

It is a **source-specific compatible addition** to the engine's `2L` profile, not strongest-of-two.

It excludes drones.

A slot-using effect with no actual HP-bearing object does not magically acquire HP merely because it uses a deployable slot.

Magnetic Wall and ordinary engine deployables with object stats qualify.

### Totems

**NEW creation snapshot:**

`HP = 2 × Shaman level`  
`AC = 12 + PB`

at placement.

Later level/PB changes do not refill or retroactively reconstruct that Totem.

Elemental Harmony explicitly adds class level to max/current HP **on summon creation**, so that component is already source-snapshot-based.

All these objects have:

- no Wounds;
- no Stable state;
- no death saves.

Legally granted temp HP remains a separate buffer.

# 5. Repair/reconstruction

Keep these permissions separate.

### Field Repair

Functional damaged machine:

- Action +1RP;
- restores `S d8+M`;
- no destroyed revival;
- no duration/use/ammo reset.

Because the source **explicitly** says Field Repair clears protected disablement:

> it may target a protected-disabled full-HP device solely to clear `DISABLED`.

That use restores 0 HP but still costs Action+1RP.

### Destroyed Drone 10-minute repair

1RP +10 minutes, once/short rest → half max HP.

### Long-rest Drone rebuild

Destroyed Drone → full HP.

### Snap Rebuild

Destroyed device/Drone → half max, preserving:

- identity;
- original slot;
- original expiry;
- modules;
- consumed uses;
- destruction-trigger usage.

Expired device remains ineligible.

### Maximum 1 reconstruction

Do **not** import the living resurrection bridge.

Adopt a **NEW machine-reconstruction viability rule** only for sources that explicitly reconstruct a destroyed entity:

`restoredHP = max(1, floor(maxHP/2))`

This applies to:

- destroyed Drone 10-minute repair;
- Snap Rebuild.

It does **not** alter Repair Pulse on an already functional max1 device: `floor(1/2)=0` remains a valid zero repair result.

### Reconfiguration HP fraction

**NEW arithmetic integration:**

For a functional device changing profile:

`newHP = max(1, floor(oldHP × newMaxHP / oldMaxHP))`

capped at new maximum.

The min1 prevents rounding a functional object into destruction merely by reconfiguration.

This is a stat-state conversion:

- not damage;
- not healing;
- no destruction event;
- no use/ammo/trigger reset.

# 6. Capacity/replacement and Echo dependency

### Drones

Normal cap1.

At Tinker13 Dual Drone Protocols:

- two basic Drones, one module each; **or**
- one Drone with two distinct modules.

Never >2.

Changing long-rest configuration is not destruction and grants no salvage/death trigger.

**NEW:** an excess second Drone removed by switching to single-Drone mode becomes unavailable/retracted rather than “enemy destroyed.” No destruction entitlement is created.

### Deployables

Slots:

- Tinker6: 1
- Tinker13: 2
- Tinker17: 3

Destroyed, expired, sacrificed or otherwise ended deployables release their active slot.

Advanced Turret Bay:

- two available slots → two turrets for 4RP;
- exactly one available → one turret for 2RP;
- zero → cannot deploy.

### Totems

Capacity:

- 1 normally;
- 2 at Shaman13.

The source explicitly says a third replaces an existing Totem at capacity2.

**NEW generalization:** whenever new placement would exceed current Totem capacity, choose one existing Totem to replace.

This makes capacity1 coherent:

- placing a second ordinary Totem replaces the existing one.

Replacement ends it without refund/destruction trigger.

Echo Totem needs an additional free capacity slot. It cannot be created by silently replacing something during the Echo trigger.

### Parent/Echo lifecycle

The earlier pulse package deferred this. This packet now needs a rule.

**NEW source-specific dependency integration:**

Because Echo is “an echo anchor of that Totem,” has the **same remaining duration**, and shares its concentration/upkeep:

- if the **parent Totem effect** expires, is replaced, is destroyed, or otherwise ends, its Echo effect also ends and Echo capacity is released;
- this dependent Echo ending is **not destruction** and triggers no debris/resource/death effect;
- destroying/replacing only the Echo does **not** end the parent.

Concentration loss ends both through the shared concentration rule anyway.
