LW-D05. Bind finite check scopes without granting actions or training

All recipe check bonuses are passive modifiers to a qualifying check, not new actions or demands to roll.

NEW family: all Leatherworking skillBonus and container bonus modifiers compete in equipment.leather-task-enhancement. Apply the strongest qualifying value once per check, regardless of how many pieces could help.

This does not collapse unrelated reviewed class or tool benefits into that family. Applicable proficiency contributes once; expertise replaces its normal contribution. Advantage/disadvantage continues to use the existing rules.

Wearable task scopes

The wearer must perform the task with the complete, functional, unsuppressed item actually worn. The following list is exhaustive for this completion.

Family	Eligible tasks
Boots	STR Athletics checks to climb using actual footholds or perform a jump that genuinely requires a check; DEX Acrobatics checks to balance on a narrow, unstable or slippery support using the booted feet.
Gloves	STR Athletics checks to climb by handholds or maintain a rope grip; STR Athletics or DEX manual-handling checks to retain, lower or position noncreature cargo; DEX checks to fasten/unfasten ordinary nonlocking ties, straps or clasps, or free a physically jammed small object.
Leather Gear	STR Athletics checks for actual climbing; DEX Acrobatics checks for controlled bodily movement through a cramped or obstructed passage where a check is genuinely required.
Armor	The same bounded climbing and cramped/obstructed-passage tasks as Leather Gear.
Coat	The same bounded climbing and cramped/obstructed-passage tasks as Leather Gear; no temperature-endurance checks are added by this scope.

A normal movement allowance, ordinary jump, squeeze or retrieval does not acquire a check merely to use the bonus. The item supplies no new climb, jump, squeezing or movement procedure.

These bonuses do not apply to attacks, saves, initiative, concentration, spellcasting, crafting, research, harvesting, tool repair, Perception, general Stealth, social checks or combat maneuvers merely because somebody describes the task as physical. A Stealth tag is not a Stealth proficiency or bonus.

Container secure/retrieve tasks

The actual operator, including an ally, can receive the container bonus when physically using its normal accessible fittings to:

Secure: fasten or tension its ordinary closures/retainers, or retain its contents during a genuinely difficult cargo-handling operation.

Retrieve: operate a difficult ordinary unlocked closure or withdraw its actual contents through a jam, tangle or physical handling difficulty.

Use DEX for dexterous handling; STR Athletics when the actual task is forceful noncreature-cargo handling. DEX Sleight of Hand or a tool proficiency qualifies only when the declared task genuinely falls within that existing proficiency. Merely manipulating leather does not add Leatherworker’s-tools PB.

The container must be functional, unsuppressed and accessible, but its handling bonus may operate while it rests on a work surface. The direct-bearer slots constrain relief, not the ability to use a container’s accessible fittings. Melding blocks both.

A routine retrieval needs no check. The first ordinary interaction, second-interaction Action, explicit activation Action, Secure Cargo Action and free-hand requirements remain unchanged. Holding a container does not also provide a free manipulating hand.

Theft

The bonus grants no allegiance recognition or ownership lock.

It does not add to a thief’s concealment/distraction check, the wearer’s Perception check, a lock’s DC or a separate lockpicking check. Those are not the defined secure/retrieve handling tasks.

A thief who must separately free physically jammed contents may benefit on that actual handling check; the item does not judge entitlement. Likewise, an actual opposed physical retention/retrieval contest may qualify each actor’s relevant roll, while awareness and theft detection remain separate. Do not manufacture a second contest or grant automatic theft prevention.

LW-D06. Complete wearable bodies and cross-trade slots
Armor bodies

NEW finite whitelist: Armor recipes 289, 301 and 319 may use Leather, Studded leather or Hide. Exclude Padded; its cloth construction is not admitted by this Leatherworking completion.

Base	Preserved ordinary profile	Price / weight	Ordinary structural HP

Retain their actual proficiency, don/doff and other equipment rules. These supplied profiles have no STR minimum or printed Stealth disadvantage. Their ordinary leather-dominant structural AC is 15; a genuinely different previously reviewed material profile requires its own evidence.

New accessory bodies
Body	Definition	Price	Weight	Structural AC / HP	Benefit slot
Boots	One matched pair	2 gp	2 lb	15 / 5	One footwear pair
Gloves	One matched pair	1 gp	0.5 lb	15 / 5	One handwear pair

Each accessory takes one Action to don or doff, including the whole pair. Both matched members are required for pair benefits. Splitting a pair produces neither two complete outputs nor two independent magical benefits.

These accessories are not armor suits. They add no armor-proficiency requirement, STR minimum or generic Stealth penalty. A compatible vest or coat can be worn with ordinary clothing or armor; the underlying armor retains its restrictions.

Gloves and Blacksmithing Gauntlets occupy the same handwear-benefit slot. They cannot both operate by declaring the gloves an inner layer. Additional worn layers supply no second benefit and must remain physically compatible.

Gloves do not occupy the hands themselves, create free hands, grant unarmed damage or bypass normal component/focus requirements.

For these new wearable accessories, expressly adopt Large ×2 price, weight and ordinary HP, with unchanged magical potency. Small/Medium/Tiny retain listed numbers but require genuine fitting. Armor uses its already-reviewed size/grade rules. Container profiles instead follow LW-D02’s fixed capacities.

Only armor suits use the supplied V6 suit-grade progression. These new accessories and container bodies have Standard only; titles such as Superior and Masterwork create no other grade.
