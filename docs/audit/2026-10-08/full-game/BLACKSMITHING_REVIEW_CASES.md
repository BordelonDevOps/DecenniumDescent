# Blacksmithing exact-review cases

All 43 cards are drafts implementing the source and eight-interface conferences. These are tabletop rule traces and authored-projection checks, not live companion simulations or numerical balance approval.

1. **Real body reserve:** recipe-45 Q5 with Battleaxe requires seven actual bases before work. Standard transforms ordered entries 1–5; Masterwork yield transforms 1–7; Exceptional yield transforms 1–6; Flawed transforms 1–2; Failed/Mishap transforms none. Base references are 10 gp and 4 lb each at Standard Small/Medium/Tiny. The 4.18 lb of source ingredients never substitutes for bodies.

2. **Interrupted reserve:** reserved entry 2 becomes Broken before completion. Pause before rolling. Repair it or replace that exact entry with another eligible same-model/size/grade base; record its own real material/HP/modifiers. No reordering after the outcome to avoid a damaged output. Base bodies stay separate from source 25% failure-recovery arithmetic.

3. **Model restrictions:** recipe-628 uses Scale mail, not freely chosen Plate. Recipe-631 uses Breastplate. Generic Weapon admits Dart and Kama, but no arrows, bows, crossbows, natural weapons, whips or nunchaku. Specific Sword/Hammer/Axe/Mace/Spear families use their complete approved finite lists.

4. **Grade versus enhancement:** Fine weapon grade +1 with recipe +2 gives +2 item accuracy and recipe +2 damage. Exceptional grade +3 with recipe +1 gives +3 accuracy and +1 damage; its actual extra grade critical dice remain. Crude −1 with active recipe +1 uses +1; suppression leaves −1, never an imaginary +0.

5. **One damage event:** an ordinary weapon attack with a printed 2d4 rider adds those elemental dice to that same attack event, applying actual defenses by type. A critical doubles the rider dice to 4d4 as attack dice and doubles ordinary eligible weapon dice; it does not double the flat recipe bonus or the extra grade dice that are added once after doubling. No extra hit or Wound event is manufactured.

6. **Actual turn budgets:** physical weapons A and B each may spend their own elemental rider on a turn. Transferring A after its use does not reset A. Another Action on that same turn grants no new A budget. A genuine extra turn or a legal Reaction on a different creature's actual turn has a distinct turn budget. A damage copy does not proc A again.

7. **Outside windows:** a first out-of-initiative attack opens the item's six-second action window. Additional legal attacks in that window share its one optional rider use. An attack that starts combat goes directly through lawful initiative entry and claims no prior outside budget. Entry after a recent outside trigger and exit after a recent inside trigger retain the six-second transition protection and last-trigger timestamp.

8. **First-turn guard — submitted for exact precision:** the follow-up specifies the item's first actual turn before the deadline as already spent. The draft retains that wording rather than silently changing it to first attempted attack. Please explicitly confirm whether the first actual turn immediately after transition satisfies the guard even if that item makes no attack in that turn, or whether the guard attaches to the first actual turn in which use is attempted. Also confirm that outside windows stay anchored to the initially recorded window origin (elapsed-time window index), rather than a new attack roll restarting an early window. This is the one deliberately retained pending machine binding.

9. **One defense-enhancement family:** a Fine suit's +1 grade, a +1 Blacksmith helm and a +2 Blacksmith shield enhancement contribute one strongest +2 enhancement. The chosen shield's ordinary base AC remains separate. Independent class/spell bonuses use their actual rules. A weaker accessory enhancement may still supply its printed resistance.

10. **Two shields cost actual hands:** two legally donned shields can supply different printed resistances, but only the selected usable shield supplies ordinary shield AC and offers its recipe enhancement. Both retain actual occupation/STR/Stealth/nonproficiency effects. A shield slung on the back has neither wearing benefit. Two same-type resistances never become immunity.

11. **Accessories:** one gauntlet output is one matched pair at 4 lb, 10 gp, HP5 and material AC19. It takes one Action to don or doff the pair. It does not occupy the hands, grant extra free hands, supply unarmed damage or count as a suit of armor. One helm is 5 lb, 10 gp, HP5, AC19. Large doubles price/weight/ordinary HP for both, not AC or bonus magnitude.

12. **Persistent durability:** B20 with +25% and a flaw has max12. Starting at current9 gives deficit3. A Masterwork structural option replaces +25% with +50%, producing improved30, then flawed max15/current12. The flaw persists. Another Flawed outcome does not halve the already flawed maximum again. Repeating a weaker improvement cannot multiply durability.

13. **Badly damaged singleton:** a B5 mundane base at current1 has deficit4. A singleton Flawed outcome gives max2/current0, so the finished item emerges Broken. Its recoverable imprint is dormant. Repair cannot remove the flaw, and no refund or second output occurs.

14. **Suppression:** an improved or Flawed item keeps physical current/max HP, material AC, size, grade and deficits while suppressed. Only recipe combat functions/classification stop. Suppression/unsuppression does not reopen a spent turn budget or heal damage.

15. **Repair access and price:** recipe-42 on a Standard Medium Longsword has 92+15=107 gp finished reference. HP-positive repair materials use 25% of 15 gp = 3.75 gp; HP0 reconstruction uses 25% of 107 gp = 26.75 gp. Both require actual Smith's proficiency and Forge/Anvil/Tongs. The intact-body repair DC is 10; reconstruction uses the original crafting DC12. The repairer needs neither the original recipe trade level nor its formula, and learns no formula from repair. No creation-only Forge Dwarf reduction, crafting XP or catalyst applies.

16. **Rational batch value:** recipe-53 source 94 gp/Q5 gives 18.8 gp per unit plus actual base value. An ordinary Shield adds 10 gp for 28.8 gp. Large changes the base portion to 20 gp, not the source share. Owned bases cause no purchase; sold magic items need an actual quote rather than automatic mundane resale percentages.

17. **Forge Dwarf phase:** source 4 hours becomes 180 active minutes, all one stable first phase; source 80 becomes 3,600 active minutes with a 480-minute first phase. Required assistant participation follows actual modified work. Only incorporated components stabilize; loose reserves keep their own age. Field timeMode never overrides fieldCraftable:false.

18. **Repeated catalysts:** recipe-80 lists three consumed green dragon heartstone occurrences. Three actual units are needed. Masterwork may return at most one eligible unit total; source IDs/names do not merge the other two into a reusable item. Ingredient tags, donor lists, wholeCreatureUse and optional Director guidance grant no unstated powers or access.

No exact-review promotion is yet claimed. All original and prior reviewed files remain protected. Cosmology stays deferred until the authorized GitHub push succeeds and the remote commit is verified.
