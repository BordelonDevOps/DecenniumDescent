Please return finite authoring decisions for all eleven points, identifying newly authored rules as such. This is direction for drafting the complete cards, not exact manuscript or numerical balance approval.

1. **Identity, saves and repeated exposures.** All 41 current records explicitly say initial CON save or damage plus a condition for one minute, then repeat the save at end of each turn. Propose a magical nonspell consumable, actual printed damage/DC/type, binary save (failure applies both eligible components, success neither), and repeat saves end only that exposure's still-present ongoing condition rather than repeating initial damage. Actual damage and condition immunity operate independently. End-of-regular-target-turn saves, no extra-turn saves, one step per six elapsed seconds outside initiative, no duplicate event on transition. Confirm actual expiry versus final save order. Repeated real doses still consume real inventory and trigger their own initial saves/damage, but ongoing copies of the same recipe from the same source owner should refresh one record to the later legal expiry without increasing its penalty; different source records remain separate. Do not silently import the Thief class engine's maximum-two-ongoing-recipes limitation into ordinary crafted items. Resolve the intended limits and exact owner/record identity.

2. **Injury delivery (26).** Existing reviewed Basic Poison uses an Action, one dose, one piercing/slashing weapon OR up to three compatible ammunition units, one-minute coating; the first qualifying weapon hit or first hit by each ammo unit spends its coating, with source DC10/1d4 and no Poisoned. Please decide whether this general physical delivery model applies to the crafted recipes, including whether splitting onto three ammo creates three legitimate exposures per source dose. Explicitly define eligible weapon/natural weapon, access, one available hand/actual applicator, friendly coating and whether it needs actual positive post-defense piercing/slashing damage. If temporary HP absorbs that damage, does exposure occur? A miss/zero-damage hit, a critical, an immune target, a glancing hit, washing, underwater use, overwrite by another coating and recovered ammo must not create unbounded doses or refreshed coating clocks. Neither a recipe name nor carrying a poisoned weapon is an automatic exposure.

3. **Contact delivery (10).** The source gives no surface area, preparation time or persistence. Proposal for a complete bounded profile: Action with actual dose/tool access to coat one accessible small object or one contiguous five-foot-square surface patch; fixed one-hour coating life, first qualifying direct contact spends the entire dose whether the creature saves or is immune, no unlimited visitors. Actual impermeable gloves/cover prevent contact when they cover the contacted anatomy. Determine valid anatomy and whether a worn glove/held item touching a patch consumes the dose without exposing the wearer, or merely fails to trigger. Specify no generic touch attack/free forced contact; using a coated object as a weapon must use an actual legal delivery action. Can source-required Protective gloves/Applicator satisfy safe handling without an additional unprinted proficiency roll? Define removal/cleaning without laundering the dose into new active stock. These are fictional game mechanics, not real poison-handling instructions.

4. **Inhaled delivery (5).** Provide a finite ready-to-use release procedure. Proposal: an Action releases/throws one vial at a visible point with line of effect, using the already reviewed alchemical bomb 30/60-foot point targeting and long-range target-save advantage, a five-foot-radius instantaneous burst, and actual eligible breathing creatures present at resolution. No lingering cloud, entering-later exposure, windborne second area, attack roll, critical damage or chain reaction from a broken carried vial. Please choose exact range/area and whether the source supports this or another bounded profile. Resolve actual inability to breathe, pre-existing breath-holding, an available Reaction to avoid inhalation, total cover, underwater release and friend/self exposure. Do not infer persistent Miasma from recipe-663's name. One vial's exposure cannot repeat through walking in and out.

5. **Confused / Slowed / Weakened.** These are not generic Core conditions, and source-qualified class versions differ. Please author complete poison-specific profiles for the four instances of each, including actual penalties, duration/save endpoint, source-qualified identity and tags, stacking, specific immunities and cures. Existing Weakened examples are Mage/Wizard -1 attacks versus Warrior -1 weapon/natural-hit damage; existing Thief Slow halves Speed and carries eligibility.slowed-foe. Those examples establish no automatic poison profile. Pick a coherent interpretation of Confused rather than an undefined label or arbitrary Director behavior; if it denies a whole Action, resolve hard-denial bookkeeping. Do not alter all classes' existing definitions or import an unrelated game's condition wholesale.

6. **Frightened source (3).** Core Frightened needs a recorded fear source: disadvantage on attacks/checks while visible, cannot willingly move closer. An unattended contact toxin or an arrow does not always reveal a hostile actor. Please choose one concrete source rule that works for recipes 364/381/661 without revealing a hidden applicator or inventing hallucination mechanics. Options include the actual exposure object/point or another explicitly defined stable relationship; specify what happens if that object is carried, destroyed, embedded or no longer perceivable. The poison applies Frightened only, not mind-reading or automatic knowledge of its maker. Preserve ordinary fear immunity and source-specific cure endpoints.

7. **Burning (3).** Recipes 362/384/394 explicitly inflict Burning. Existing generic Core Burning is 1d4 fire at end of each regular target turn, in unified family.ongoing-fire arbitration, with ordinary Action/immersion extinguishing and per-record DEX ending save at applying DC (or10) after due tick. Its periodic damage is not the poison's initial source damage dice. Please resolve poison's printed end-turn CON ending save in addition to generic DEX, their order, suppressed-record cure eligibility, initial fire/condition immunity, one-minute expiry and ordinary extinguishing. One application must not generate both a duplicate custom fire and generic fire. Confirm the initial damage packet is separate from periodic family arbitration and a poison-effect cure can remove the remaining poison-created Burning record, while ordinary Poisoned-only removal cannot.

8. **Stunned, encounter ceiling and attribution.** Recipes 371/391 impose Stunned for the printed minute with repeat saves. Please include these crafted riders coherently, specifying when the once-per-encounter allowance is claimed, what happens to damage if condition application is immune/blocked/already spent, and how owner is attributed for coated weapons/ammo and unattended contact/inhaled traps. Crafter is not automatically attacker. Ownership transfer or a duplicate item record must not reset an already-established application. Distinguish one allowed application lasting until its real save/expiry from repeated new applications or repeated turn-by-turn cap charges.

9. **Immunities and curing classification.** Existing reviewed Thief interfaces distinguish poison damage immunity, Core Poisoned immunity and explicit immunity to poison effects/toxins. Propose all these crafted recipes as actual poison-effect applications regardless of elemental damage tag. Poison-damage immunity affects only actual poison damage; Poisoned immunity only that condition; explicit poison-effect immunity prevents all components of a covered application. Antitoxin/Cooking poison-save advantage should cover their initial and repeat poison CON saves even if damage is acid/psychic/etc, but not automatically generic Burning DEX saves. Herbalism antidotes end present removable Poisoned records, not all these distinct Confused/Burning/etc riders. Alchemy's explicit end-one-poison-effect cure and Panacea's actual eligible poison source could remove one full present removable application and its dependent symptoms; independent sources remain. Please approve/correct this classification, suppression and record removal without altering prior narrower cures.
