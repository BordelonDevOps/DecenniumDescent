### digitalGeometry

NEW scoped geometry for these25 cards: use actual world-space 3D occupied body volumes and Euclidean feet, not display sprites or the historical grid diagonal metric. Single-target range is the nearest actual caster-to-target body distance. A chosen area center must be a perceived point within the stated range from the actual caster body, with originating line of effect. A creature qualifies only if at least one actual body point intersects the closed specified volume and has line of effect from the relevant area origin/center. Tangency counts as intersection. Total cover prevents that application. Fix the whole recipient set and geometry after cast interruptions and before damage; destroying cover gives no second scan. Friendly targets and the caster are included whenever an ALL-creature shape actually includes them; ENEMIES means creatures the caster already ordinarily identifies as enemies at this snapshot, with no hidden-hostility or faction scan. No spell title creates an object-targeting permission. A card's own explicit origin exclusion, object mode, prism/cone shape or actual typed predicate takes precedence.

### saveAndDamage

A specified save uses8+PB+the real casting modifier under the actual placement/class and actual modifiers. Save outcomes are binary, not degrees; save damage never criticals. For each card use exactly its success branch, not a universal half-damage default. Unless a card expressly says per-target, use one shared raw pool for its area/chain and separate recipient damage packets, roots and actual defenses. Elemental Devastation explicitly rolls separately per enemy. Mixed typed parts of one actual hit stay one damage event for Wound/zero/lifecycle consequences; Meteor Spear alone uses its explicitly NEW DC25-MIXED-DEFENSE-02 apportionment at the one actually authorized source stage; this is not a general mixed-damage engine. Halve each specified raw typed part with floor only when that card says half, before applicable defenses. Apply ordinary real damage, temporary HP, Wounds, zero/death/Stable/Dying and source-specific callbacks once. No creature type supplies an unprinted blanket mental or damage immunity.

### rootsAndSuppression

At the actual permitted damage application create each recipient root under the accepted creation-time dealer/Null rules, even when the RNG pool is shared. Caster is the dealer for these direct spells; an arc origin is not a new dealer. Separate recipients and genuinely separate casts keep distinct roots. Apply current Lesser Antimagic, Globe and genuine defenses to actual payload locus/origin/rank. Globe blocks only its covered incoming rank0–5 application; rank6+ is outside that ceiling. An initially prevented rider does not establish or wait for later suppression ending. Established condition records retain identity, clocks and spent history through suppression; expired/ended records never replay. Completed instantaneous damage is not dispellable after the fact. No attack, roll, concentration, resource or root is manufactured by presentation or reentry.

### control

Stunned and Paralyzed attempt independent hard-denial components under the accepted one-source-character/target/encounter guard; a prior spent guard prevents only that new hard component with no unprinted fallback. A failed save can still resolve independent damage. Damage-type immunity does not prevent a separately legal condition, and condition immunity does not prevent damage. Establishment spends the hard guard once; no later recast, source rename, suppression or expiration refunds it. Prone, Blinded, Frightened and the explicit limited Reaction/movement losses use their actual narrower scope; they do not acquire hard-denial immunity by association.

### duration

Use each complete spell-specific endpoint, including its named regular-turn or actual attached-phase bridge. A generic default does not replace those original authored records. Preserve original typed/absolute clocks through reorder/absence/extra turns; initial prevention creates no deferred component. Any new companion/no-schedule bridge remains explicitly proposed and requires review. For the seven newly authored high-rank cards with no earlier record, the current01 one-target-round fallback is retained only where actually specified: original next future regular END or6sec outside/no independent schedule, without inventing creature turns. Prone follows its actual stand/cure ending.

### recordsAndCleanup

Record exact generating spell ID, school/rank, class/source owner, cast/event order, target, components, saved outcome, source DC, initial/root IDs, actual absolute or typed deadline, concentration link, spent guard and suppression state. Different paid casts keep independent records and do not add condition magnitude; deleting/ending one never removes another. A successfully established same-owner named condition can retain independent source records here; it cannot bypass a hard guard or trigger duplicate ticks. Death/ended identity, actual removal, duration or concentration ends only the covered application. Destroyed/ended creature identities are not silently recreated. Damage types and names add no Burning/Frozen, ice shell, soul, plane, alignment, faction or automatic environmental effect absent that card’s actual printed promise.
