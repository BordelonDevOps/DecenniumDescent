# Form and temporary-HP review preparation

Unreviewed questions, not adopted rules. Current preservation:124 class/form passages (including the complete Druid7-engine+59-card corpus),6 Bard cards in the separate Bard schema,7 feat objects,5 ordinary-spell candidates and2 frozen paragraphs. The142 candidate records are not an exhaustive semantic inventory; compatible V6/Core temporary-HP text and semantic supplements still need capture before conference. None of these candidates is complete merely because another component was previously reviewed.

## Buffer identity and lifetime

1. The frozen shared rule says choose the larger remaining amount, never stack. Define ties, whether an existing larger buffer can be retained without borrowing the incoming grant's duration, and whether an overwritten buffer is permanently discarded rather than waiting underneath. Distinguish a source's ongoing buff from its current temporary-HP grant.
2. Source-specific durations include one round, start/end of a turn, one minute, encounter end, while-form, while-device/zone and unspecified. Use actual source markers; do not import a universal rest expiry or permanent duration without the exact Core/V6 text.
3. An actual incoming grant that loses the comparison may still have paid RP, a once-rest use, Blood Gauge or Buffer Reserve. Decide which payments occur before arbitration and whether Buffer Reserve pays the offered amount, the new accepted amount or only increased capacity. “Draws that many” and “up to remaining” must be interpreted explicitly, without letting rejected grants become free future recharge.
4. A shield-linked permission such as Bloodshield's once-while-they-remain Reaction must point to the actual grant identity. Does replacement by another source terminate it even if the creature still has temporary HP? Do secondary AC/save riders have independent duration/use records? Soulbound and Protective Verse should not accidentally inherit another buffer's expiry.
5. Confirm the existing HP/death boundary: temporary HP is not normalHP restoration, stabilization, resurrection, Wound removal or payment for direct HP sacrifice. HP-healing prohibitions do not implicitly block a separate tempHP component. Retain exact target predicates, including living-only, after-revival and actual positive healing event qualifiers.

## Druid form transaction

6. Preserve the source's own HP/scores/saves/proficiency and no imported monster statblocks. Determine exactly how equipment, AC, senses, size, hands, speech, movement and natural attacks change for every59card form/feature, with no copied anatomy granting extra powers. Identify what is supplied versus a new bounded ruling.
7. Shifting spends actual long-rest shift use; forms last10minutes and end on0HP/incapacity. At20 unlimited form changes must never refill Buffer Reserve, Form Surge or per-rest effects. Preserve the adopted detailed shift-use table and historical alternate table separately. Form preparation/acquisition and Circle/Arch/Hero eligibility must remain explicit.
8. Buffer Reserve starts2L+M at completedLR; Form Surge startsPB. Define exactly when spending/entering/form-specific once-per-transformation uses commit and how interruption affects them. Ensure leaving/reentering a form cannot manufacture HP, surge or repeat riders. A valid form change clears Refrain name/count, never accumulated Dissonance; ordinary natural attacks do not clear Refrain.
9. Reaching0 or becoming incapacitated ends the form. Specify ordering with one incoming damage packet, tempHP absorption, Core Wound/InstantDeath and existing death-prevention effects. Do not create a separate form HP pool or “overflow” healing. Ending old-form features does not end independent paid spells/swarms, as the closed creature interface already establishes.
10. Native attacks and once-shift powers need one source-qualified action procedure each: printed dice/type/range/save, costs and current original class stats. Normal natural-attack classification for weapon-hit features must come from exact shared source rather than an imported system assumption. Broodmother/Carrion summons keep the reviewed dependency/command rules.

## Other class and catalog sources

11. Bard's separate structured cards include resourceCost, recharge, Crescendo rider and C=max1CHA. Keep those fields rather than normalizing them away. Residual notes are not new Chants; maintenance/Echo cannot retrigger Chorus Shield. Three-pulse Harmonic Resonance Ward tempHP needs a grant record per eligible pulse without stacking old grants.
12. Paladin redirection-to-tempHP, King's Smite/Noble Bearing replacement, Last King's Gift, and other damage/healing conversions must use the already-defined actual amount/event boundaries, single root transfer, once-round caps and no recursion. A new buffer does not retroactively absorb the event that triggered it unless the exact source says so.
13. Healing excess conversions retain one-point-one-conversion ownership and source-internal priority. Preserve Channel Life's packet-wide ban on excess-to-tempHP conversion; an unrelated provider cannot bypass it.
14. Feat sources are exact but some incomplete: Chef lacks amount/target count, Inspiring Leader lacks amount/target/frequency details, Soul Cage needs charge storage/eligibility/activation and malformed “disadvantage on healing” resolution. Do not import outside edition rules or add new named powers. Existing Blood Ritual, Empowered Summoning, Field Medic and Necrotic Affinity require source-qualified tempHP timing and no resource/kill loops. Their other mechanics remain distinct catalog work.

15. Ordinary spell candidates currently found are Resonance Shield, Heroism, Spare the Dying, False Life and Harmonic Bulwark. The first four have operational playtest drafts; Harmonic Bulwark remains blocked pending operational review. Preserve both original source objects and exact current overlays, including strain, costs, target/type and timing. Broader shield/transform semantics may need additional source discovery even without the literal phrase temporaryHP.

The final conference must classify each source role, cite the exact compatible Core/V6 support, and separate a bounded buffer/form implementation from human balance and full-game/runtime completion.
