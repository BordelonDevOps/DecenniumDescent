# Poisoncrafting final correction checks

These are worked readings of the five exact corrections approved in notebook turn `f8cd1339-42dc-413b-b3cb-b071aa52b12e`, reply `d151511f-1830-4828-8134-98c91c482444`. They supplement the earlier submitted cases; they are not a runtime simulation or human balance test. All 50 source/projection checks pass.

1. **Fear stays anchored.** Recipe-364 from owner A establishes Frightened at location X. A later legal exposure of the same recipe/owner/target at Y fails its initial save and legally refreshes the existing record. The expiry extends, but fear remains anchored at X. A successful later initial save changes neither. After the old record actually ends, a new failed exposure that establishes a new record at Z uses Z. Moving or destroying an applicator does not move the locus.

2. **Cleaning requires both time and work.** One designated cleaner acts at elapsed seconds 0, 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 42, 48 and 54 on ten consecutive regular turns. The tenth Action alone does not finish a large object/patch: maintain continuous access through second 60. No eleventh Action is needed. Interrupting at second 59 leaves the single original coating record and original expiry; there is no partially recovered dose or extended poison lifetime. A handheld object whose entire coated region is directly accessible from one space uses one Action instead. Protective barriers and actual bodily exposure still apply during cleaning.

3. **A required automatic failure is a failure.** A Thief with Venom Master makes a qualifying critical weapon hit that delivers a legal crafted injury poison. If an applicable rule makes the required initial CON save automatically fail, that failure can trigger the once-per-round rider after the poison save. No physical die is required, and the rider cannot affect that triggering save. Whole-poison-effect immunity prevents the application and supplies no qualifying save; rolling an unnecessary die anyway does not create a trigger. Damage immunity or condition immunity alone does not erase an otherwise legitimate failed save.

4. **Independent movement modifiers, once each.** A creature with unmodified walking Speed 40 and two active Poisoncraft Slowed records walks at 20, not 10. A distinct lawful effect that expressly halves Speed can additionally reduce it to 10. If a swimming mode is defined as half current effective walking Speed, swimming is then 5; applying Poisoncraft Slowed again to that derived 5 would count the same source twice. A separate fixed-base swimming mode instead receives each applicable modifier once from its own base. Movement-cost penalties remain costs, and Speed 0 remains 0.

5. **Actual delivery determines eligibility.** A creature's Undead or Construct type alone does not prevent an injury/contact exposure. Apply its actual anatomy, actual delivery conditions and explicitly printed damage, condition or poison-effect immunities. A nonbreathing creature fails inhaled delivery's ambient-gas breathing requirement. A Dead body receives no active creature-condition record. No living-only restriction is inferred.
