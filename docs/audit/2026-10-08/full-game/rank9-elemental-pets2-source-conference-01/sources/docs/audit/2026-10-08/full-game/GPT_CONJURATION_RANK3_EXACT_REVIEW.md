## 3. Burst of Flamewind — one required target-language repair

Damage/save/geometry are approved:

- source-faithful automatic 3d8 fire;
- STR save controls only 5-ft push;
- known but unseen locations can qualify;
- unknown locations do not;
- 15-ft cone with 15-ft far width;
- square-center inclusion including boundary;
- once per creature;
- no magical hostility detector;
- forced movement causes no ordinary OA/Prone/collision damage.

The problem is:

> “you may select some or all eligible creatures.”

The source says **all enemies in the cone**. Once a creature qualifies as an enemy under the caster's ordinary-information classification, the card should not separately permit selective exclusion.

Replace the target-selection portion with:

> **Before resolution, determine from ordinary information which creatures at known locations in the chosen cone the caster currently treats as enemies. Every such creature is affected. The spell does not magically identify enemies, reveal unknown locations, or establish objective allegiance.**

Remove:

> “you may select some or all eligible creatures.”

This still allows the intended subjective “enemy” classification without turning the spell into selective targeting among creatures the caster already classifies as enemies.

## 6. Resonance Conduit — one wording repair

The transfer procedure itself is approved.

In particular:

- deduct RP at transfer commitment;
- deducted RP becomes pending/unspendable;
- resolution rechecks active link, consent, same plane, ≤60 ft, and LoE;
- interruption/gate failure loses committed RP and Action;
- credit is capped against recipient's **actual maximum at resolution**;
- newly excess amount is lost;
- leaving transfer range suspends permission only;
- returning grants no resources/reset;
- participant may end whole link without an Action;
- no daily cap because RP is conserved rather than generated;
- copies cannot duplicate transfer;
- original 5-RP spell cost must already be paid.

One sentence is too broad:

> “New consent/recasting cannot reinstate an ended link.”

As written, that can be read to prohibit a completely new legal cast after the old link has ended.

Replace it with:

> **Renewed consent alone cannot revive an ended application. A later legal casting may create a new Resonance Conduit normally, paying all costs and establishing a new application and endpoint.**

That preserves the anti-revival rule without banning legitimate recasting.
