# Poisoncrafting — source preparation for the next GPT conference

No new rules or cards are approved by this document. The unchanged full source records, exact public book paragraphs and component occurrences are in [source preparation](POISONCRAFTING_SOURCE_PREPARATION.json). The next source conference must follow completion of the current Cooking exact review, using its actual verdict and updated aggregate counts.

There are **41 formulas: 25 public, three restricted and 13 Director-only**. Delivery is 26 injury, ten contact and five inhaled. Source riders comprise Poisoned (20), Confused (4), Burning (3), Blinded (1), Frightened (3), Slowed (4), Weakened (4) and Stunned (2). All source effects specify an initial CON save, damage and a one-minute condition with a repeated save at the end of each turn. Recipe-395 retains null active hours for its printed work year.

The conference needs concrete decisions on these game-rule interfaces:

1. **Delivery and exposure:** Exact Actions, hands, application area, targets, coating duration, contact consumption and inhaled release/area/duration. The reviewed equipment Basic Poison coats one piercing/slashing weapon or up to three ammunition units for one minute; do not silently transfer all details to these different crafted recipes. Define whether injury requires a hit, actual injury damage or a particular damage type, including damage absorbed by temporary HP. Contact and inhaled effects have no existing finite delivery profile in these records.
2. **Damage and repeated saves:** Source sentence structure needs explicit initial damage only versus repeated damage. Proposed questions should distinguish the condition's ending save from another exposure. Preserve exact source dice, DCs and damage types. Core saves are binary and do not acquire natural-1/20 degree shifts. Determine whether a save success ends all of that application or a specific rider only, and how simultaneous doses/records relate without stacking unprinted damage ticks.
3. **Source-qualified conditions:** Confused, Slowed and Weakened are not generic Core condition identities. Reviewed custom class versions do not automatically authorize poison versions. Supply bounded new poison definitions, tags, stacking/cure/immunity interfaces and source-qualified names. Frightened requires a concrete fear relationship even for an unattended trap. Poisoned and other Core conditions retain their ordinary definitions.
4. **Burning:** Recipes 362, 384 and 394 explicitly impose Burning. Bind the generic 1d4 end-of-regular-turn fire/arbitration/cure procedure or an explicitly reviewed replacement; do not mistake the poison's initial damage dice for ongoing fire dice. Resolve printed repeat CON saves alongside generic ending DEX saves, one-minute expiry, Action/immersion, suppression and poison-specific cures without duplicate records or ticks.
5. **Hard denial:** Two recipes impose Stunned; Confused could also deny Actions if authored that way. Determine the exact relationship with the existing source-character/target/encounter hard-denial ceiling, including crafter versus applicator ownership and unattended traps. No silent bypass by changing item owner or using a bought poison.
6. **Immunities and cures:** Existing reviewed boundaries distinguish poison damage immunity, Core Poisoned immunity and explicit immunity to poison effects/toxins. Determine the recipe-level application classification so acid/fire/psychic/necrotic poison effects interact coherently. Herbalism antidotes clear present removable Poisoned applications, not every condition or unrelated causal toxin; preserve that scope unless a new explicit integration is jointly approved.
7. **Physical mass and source vessels:** Some sources contain zero vessels, some too few for Standard/maximum yield, and some more than output quantity. Account for actual source vessels, incorporated mass, packaging, maximum-quality reservation and catalyst retention without returning consumed material twice. Fourteen Applicator rows are nonconsumed tools; recipe-661's Applicator is consumed and must retain that distinct source meaning. Names alone cannot turn every Applicator into a generic reusable tool or create multiple poison charges.
8. **Manufacture, storage and quality:** Set finished dose, sealed/opened/coated exposure clocks and first binding phase. Decide whether poisoner's kit is artisan for any actual ancestry feature from the reviewed tool rules, rather than inferring from trade name. Preserve all stations, work time, assistance, formula and access requirements. Quality may alter yield or eligible packaging and the appropriate Flawed rule, never unprinted DC/damage potency. Keep recipe-395's one-workyear source null while applying any agreed 1,920-hour operational overlay.
9. **Scope and source identity:** Recipe titles such as Armor Eater, Miasma or Legendary do not themselves corrode armor, generate persistent areas, or establish artifact status. Active tags come from current mechanics.tag; all legacy/source tags and economies remain preserved. Source-monster lists remain compatibility evidence, not inventory provenance or approval of the generated bestiary. Cosmology stays strictly post-push.

This preparation neither increments reviewed-recipe counts nor changes public availability. Numerical balance, harvest access, companion implementation and complete game-flow review remain separate unfinished gates.
