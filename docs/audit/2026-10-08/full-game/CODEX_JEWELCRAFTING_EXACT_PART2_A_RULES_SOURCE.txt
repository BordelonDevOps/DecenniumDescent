## JC-D07. Define one before-dice charge checkpoint and atomic expenditure

**NEW activation:** A conscious, non-Incapacitated wearer may choose to spend one charge immediately before their qualifying roll is committed.

This choice costs **no separate Action, Bonus Action, Reaction, free interaction, RP, speech or hand manipulation**. It is an item-specific voluntary activation, not a spell. Reaction denial or Surprise alone does not prohibit it; an independently applicable activation prohibition still does.

Use this sequence:

1. Establish the real check/save, actor, governing source, legal equipment designations and each candidate’s tag eligibility.
2. Before any die or replacement result for that event is revealed, identify the one jewel supplying advantage.
3. Revalidate the item and its available charge at commitment. Debit one charge and register its advantage for that roll.
4. Resolve the roll and all ordinary modifier/cancellation rules.

For tabletop use, this is “declare, mark off the charge, then roll.” An implementation must prevent concurrent requests from spending the same available charge twice.

A declaration after seeing the first die, another advantage die, a replacement result, or success/failure is too late. It cannot become a retrospective reroll.

An ineligible declaration grants nothing and spends nothing. A declaration canceled before the roll commits releases any reservation without a debit. Once the roll commits and its dice/result procedure begins, the charge remains spent even if the result fails, is discarded, or a later effect makes the outcome irrelevant. Do not provide result-dependent refunds.

Spending while advantage already exists is **legal but potentially wasted**. Spending when advantage and disadvantage will cancel is also legal; the charge is still spent. It never grants a third die or “overcomes” disadvantage by supplying more advantage sources.

No charge can force a roll where the governing rule supplies automatic success/failure or no roll. Incapacitation does not cancel an otherwise eligible passive modifier, but it bars this optional activation. Unconscious automatic STR/DEX-save failure is not overcome.

Eligibility adjudication supplies no new knowledge of hidden DCs, causes, sources or private lore.

### Rerolls, copies and repeated saves

**NEW:** Record one roll-event identity for the whole check/save, including its advantage/disadvantage dice and any replacement procedure that explicitly belongs to that same event.

An authorized same-event reroll follows its own exact text. It does not open another Jewelcrafting expenditure window or produce another pair of dice automatically. Existing event modifiers remain only as that procedure permits.

Copying a die result does not copy the originating item’s flat modifier, advantage grant or charge transaction. Copying an entire resolved total does not permit adding the same modifier again.

A genuinely new later save against an ongoing effect is a new event: reevaluate current equipment and tag eligibility, and allow a new optional charge expenditure. The jewel itself creates no repeat saves.

## What a jewel grants

Each complete, functional jewel is durable magical nonartifact equipment. It needs no attunement, RP, concentration or spellcasting. Its exact source tag, flat bonus and maximum charges govern; rarity, donor names, secondary tags and ingredient properties grant nothing else.

The passive modifier applies once to each independently qualifying ability check or saving throw made by the actual wearer. The source word “one” means one individual roll, not one permanent chosen skill or one use per day. The passive bonus remains available with zero charges. Ownership and consent do not lock the passive benefit, but the item grants no procedure for forcibly equipping someone. An ally, operator or owner who is not the actual wearer gains nothing.

At the roll commitment checkpoint, the item must be complete, at positive structural HP, unsuppressed, unmelded, actually fitted and worn in its designated legal position. Concealing it under compatible clothes is allowed. Tailoring's presentation visibility and coverage requirements do not apply to jewels. Incapacitation alone does not remove a passive modifier when a legitimate eligible roll still exists.

There is no armor enhancement candidate, including zero: a jewel cannot cancel a selected Crude suit's −1. No resistance, immunity, attack bonus, proficiency, ability increase, carrying relief or spell access is supplied.

## Spending a charge

A conscious, non-Incapacitated wearer may voluntarily spend one charge immediately before an eligible roll commits. It costs no separate Action, Bonus Action, Reaction, free interaction, RP, speech or hand manipulation. It is an item activation, not a spell. Reaction denial or Surprise alone does not bar it; an applicable independent activation prohibition does. Control instructions cannot bypass the reviewed prohibition on compelled finite-resource spending.

1. Establish the legitimate roll, actor, governing source, all equipment designations and item-specific eligibility.
2. Before any die or replacement result is revealed, select one independently qualifying jewel.
3. At commitment, revalidate eligibility and available charges; debit one charge and register advantage for this event atomically.
4. Resolve the roll with its ordinary modifiers and advantage/disadvantage rules.

At the table: declare, mark off the charge, then roll. Concurrent requests must not spend the same charge twice. A late declaration after any die, replacement result or success/failure grants nothing and spends nothing. An ineligible declaration does the same. Cancellation before commitment releases a reservation without debit. Once the dice/result procedure begins, the charge stays spent even if the result fails, is discarded or becomes irrelevant; there is no result-dependent refund.

Spending when advantage already exists, or when disadvantage will cancel it, is legal but may waste the charge. It grants no third die. No charge forces a roll in place of automatic success/failure or a no-roll procedure. Unconscious automatic STR/DEX-save failure is not overcome. Adjudication reveals no hidden DC, cause, source or private lore.

One roll-event identity includes advantage/disadvantage dice and any explicitly same-event replacement procedure. A permitted reroll follows its own text; it opens no second expenditure window and automatically grants no new pair of dice. Existing modifiers remain only as that procedure permits. A copied die does not copy the originating item's modifier, advantage or transaction; a copied resolved total cannot receive the same modifier again. A genuinely new later save reevaluates current equipment and may spend a new charge. The jewel creates no repeat save.

## Charge identity and the dawn clock

First lawful creation initializes each physical imprint's current charges to its exact source maximum once, even if the output emerges Broken and unusable. Track physical item ID, recipe/imprint, maximum/current charges, creation event, roll-keyed transactions, last processed dawn index and condition/suppression history needed to resolve elapsed events. Transfer, theft, sale, storage, fitting, equipping, scene changes, initiative and copied records never initialize it again. Reconstruction retains the ledger.

NEW Jewelcrafting-only convention: use 06:00 on one recorded campaign clock, once per 24 elapsed campaign hours everywhere. This recovery convention asserts no astronomy or cosmology and does not replace other features' independently defined dawn rules. Local sunrise, underground location, travel, rest and displayed clock changes create no extra event.

A legitimate refill sets charges to maximum; it never adds that maximum or banks unused charges. ## Recharge, suppression, meld and return

A complete positive-HP unsuppressed item refills at its legitimate dawn whether worn, stowed or melded, and regardless of its wearer's sleep or incapacity. Only an actually eligible worn item can supply a passive modifier; voluntary charge use additionally requires its capable wearer. Melded recharge is an explicitly permitted internal clock operation, not access or use through melded equipment. Whether suppression reaches a melded item depends on the suppression's actual scope.

Suppressed, Broken or incomplete items do not refill; mark that dawn processed. Existing maximum and current charges remain. Ending suppression, repairing or completing an assembly does not replay or catch up missed dawns. A destroyed or irrecoverably absent imprinted primary body cannot recharge or function. Repairing a newly created Broken item can expose its already-recorded initial charges; that is not a repair refill.

Count actual item pounds and canonical Load once, with no carrying relief. Meld preflight uses the governing full mass and Load ceilings; rescuers count the body and melded/external gear under the body guide. Melded jewels grant no modifier or activation. Compatible later anatomy does not demeld them. On return, existing external gear retains priority. A returned item resumes only in its legal available recorded position; otherwise use existing carried-storage/drop/pending-placement rules. A different free position grants no free redesignation. Broken or released items retain actual location and elevation, with no deletion, duplication or automatic relocation to the ground.

## What a jewel grants

Each complete, functional jewel is durable magical nonartifact equipment. It needs no attunement, RP, concentration or spellcasting. Its exact source tag, flat bonus and maximum charges govern; rarity, donor names, secondary tags and ingredient properties grant nothing else.

The passive modifier applies once to each independently qualifying ability check or saving throw made by the actual wearer. The source word “one” means one individual roll, not one permanent chosen skill or one use per day. The passive bonus remains available with zero charges. Ownership and consent do not lock the passive benefit, but the item grants no procedure for forcibly equipping someone. An ally, operator or owner who is not the actual wearer gains nothing.

At the roll commitment checkpoint, the item must be complete, at positive structural HP, unsuppressed, unmelded, actually fitted and worn in its designated legal position. Concealing it under compatible clothes is allowed. Tailoring's presentation visibility and coverage requirements do not apply to jewels. Incapacitation alone does not remove a passive modifier when a legitimate eligible roll still exists.

There is no armor enhancement candidate, including zero: a jewel cannot cancel a selected Crude suit's −1. No resistance, immunity, attack bonus, proficiency, ability increase, carrying relief or spell access is supplied.

## Spending a charge

A conscious, non-Incapacitated wearer may voluntarily spend one charge immediately before an eligible roll commits. It costs no separate Action, Bonus Action, Reaction, free interaction, RP, speech or hand manipulation. It is an item activation, not a spell. Reaction denial or Surprise alone does not bar it; an applicable independent activation prohibition does. Control instructions cannot bypass the reviewed prohibition on compelled finite-resource spending.

1. Establish the legitimate roll, actor, governing source, all equipment designations and item-specific eligibility.
2. Before any die or replacement result is revealed, select one independently qualifying jewel.
3. At commitment, revalidate eligibility and available charges; debit one charge and register advantage for this event atomically.
4. Resolve the roll with its ordinary modifiers and advantage/disadvantage rules.

At the table: declare, mark off the charge, then roll. Concurrent requests must not spend the same charge twice. A late declaration after any die, replacement result or success/failure grants nothing and spends nothing. An ineligible declaration does the same. Cancellation before commitment releases a reservation without debit. Once the dice/result procedure begins, the charge stays spent even if the result fails, is discarded or becomes irrelevant; there is no result-dependent refund.

Spending when advantage already exists, or when disadvantage will cancel it, is legal but may waste the charge. It grants no third die. No charge forces a roll in place of automatic success/failure or a no-roll procedure. Unconscious automatic STR/DEX-save failure is not overcome. Adjudication reveals no hidden DC, cause, source or private lore.

One roll-event identity includes advantage/disadvantage dice and any explicitly same-event replacement procedure. A permitted reroll follows its own text; it opens no second expenditure window and automatically grants no new pair of dice. Existing modifiers remain only as that procedure permits. A copied die does not copy the originating item's modifier, advantage or transaction; a copied resolved total cannot receive the same modifier again. A genuinely new later save reevaluates current equipment and may spend a new charge. The jewel creates no repeat save.
