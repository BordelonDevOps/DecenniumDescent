# Leatherworking decision scenarios — unresolved

These cases are questions for the source conference. They do not implement a proposed rule or assert a passing gameplay result. The linked source packet includes the relevant recipes and existing guides.

| Case | Concrete setup | Required decision |
|---|---|---|
| LW-01 | Public recipe-308 has armorBonus=1; its exact V5 effect mentions only +2 to a related check. | Choose and record explicit source precedence; retain both originals. |
| LW-02 | A wearer combines recipe-319 armorBonus=2, recipe-653 boots armorBonus=1, an existing Fine suit and a functioning Blacksmith accessory. | Resolve one legal armor formula and exact bonus families, after Blacksmithing's actual final review. |
| LW-03 | Recipe-295 promises 20 lb relief on a Pouch; an ordinary pouch holds 6 lb/0.2 cubic feet. | Define the actual finished body's physical capacity and usable relief without silently shrinking the promise. |
| LW-04 | A recipe-280 backpack contains 20 lb of ordinary contents. Empty mundane backpack weight is 5 lb; the source relief number is 10 lb. | Specify actual pounds, inventory Load, burden and support weight separately. |
| LW-05 | A recipe-290 pouch sits inside a recipe-297 backpack, with the same 8 lb of contents nested inside both. | Assign each actual item one mass record and prevent repeated relief on the same content; account for both empty bodies. |
| LW-06 | A recipe-297 backpack encloses an ordinary sack containing two full waterskins and a separately measured rock. | Apply inclusive 5-lb waterskin weights once, not plus duplicate water; decide which nested contents are eligible. |
| LW-07 | A character wears two magical backpacks, a belt and two pouches. One backpack is strapped outside the other. | Define legal benefit slots and actual custody; distinguish contents from externally attached gear. |
| LW-08 | A container holds an item with printed Load=3 and documented weight=1 lb. Another holds 50 coins. | Resolve whether relief acts on pounds, Load or both; do not automatically erase printed Load by converting it to weight. |
| LW-09 | A character hands a partially filled functioning container to an ally mid-turn. | Transfer actual contents once, apply the correct recipient/position requirements, and preserve capacity and condition. |
| LW-10 | Suppression or structural breakage turns off the relief on a container whose bearer is already at carrying capacity. | Recalculate overload and legal containment/placement without deleting, duplicating or teleporting inventory. |
| LW-11 | A voluntary Druid form change begins with containers that only fit the original burden allowance while their magic functions. | Determine preflight and fixed meld budgets under the current body rules; worn/held functions suspend in meld. |
| LW-12 | An ally carries that transformed character with the containers and all their contents melded. | Preserve body weight, casualty Load and full equipment records; identify any explicitly lawful relief once. |
| LW-13 | Gloves and boots could both be described as helping one climbing check; a tool and class feature also apply. | Define exact qualifying tasks and stacking without creating proficiency, extra Speed or multiple bonuses from vague descriptions. |
| LW-14 | A character retrieves an easy-to-reach item, then tries a second retrieval, then an ally retrieves from the same pack. | Preserve interaction/Action costs and hand/access needs; identify when a check exists and whose bonus applies. |
| LW-15 | A thief attempts to open someone else's container against its wearer. | Determine whether secure/retrieve bonuses apply to either actor's actual check and avoid automatic theft prevention. |
| LW-16 | Recipe-280 Q=4 consumes 1.44 lb of components; Masterwork yield could be six 5-lb backpack bodies. | Provide complete predeclared material/body supply and finite economic accounting; source components stay required. |
| LW-17 | A body reserved for an order is damaged, broken or unavailable before the completion check; the result is Flawed or Failed. | Specify reserve eligibility and return/consumption rules before any post-roll selection advantage. |
| LW-18 | A one-unit prior-flawed body gains a new structural quality improvement and is later repaired at zero HP. | Define noncompounding flaw/improvement layers, preservation of damage, recoverable-body requirement and actual repair credential. |
| LW-19 | Recipe-319 has null source active hours and one-year source time. A Forge Dwarf crafts it across interrupted Watches. | Retain the source null; label the 1,920-hour framework overlay, apply only lawful time reduction, and define incorporation/phases. |
| LW-20 | Recipe-653 boots enter acid; recipe-655 coat enters a freezing environment. | Boots have no source acid resistance. Coat's cold-damage resistance does not itself define environmental immunity. Armor-field conflicts remain separately recorded. |

Every resulting card must also preserve its original formula identity, visibility, required skill, tools/station, every consumed component occurrence, source quantity/value and lawful access. All 201 component occurrences are consumed and none is a catalyst, so there is no eligible source catalyst for Masterwork to return.
