# Herbalism review cases

These are manuscript walkthroughs for the exact GPT review, not executed runtime tests or evidence of numerical balance. The actual exact review approves these boundaries after the four recorded repairs.

| Case | Expected authored outcome |
|---|---|
| Two removable Poisoned applications plus one expressly irremovable application | Antidote removes the two eligible applications; the remaining source keeps Poisoned active. |
| Poisoned from an ongoing toxin with a new end-of-turn application trigger | Antidote ends the present condition. The underlying toxin continues and can apply a new record at its actual trigger. |
| Poisoned caused by level-4 intoxication | Clearing Poisoned does not clear intoxication points, Unconscious, its timer or the level-5 emergency. |
| Antidote source mechanics says bonus 3 | The complete source effect grants advantage; no +3 numeric bonus is added. |
| Recipe-220 singleton Flawed | Clears eligible Poisoned applications; advantage lasts 30 minutes. |
| Recipe-238/239/646 singleton Flawed | Until-Long-Rest duration is not divided; treat as Failed with framework input recovery. |
| A burning, wounded patient receives recipe-645 | Attempt 1d6+2 healing after the full minute. No automatic Wound, Injury or Burning removal; an actual compatible topical-treatment clause is required. No repeated regeneration. |
| Full HP, eligible local dressing step | No HP gain, but the independent legal local-care step can still resolve. It does not waive the rest of its procedure. |
| A healing-triggered Blood Curse save fails | That same dose cannot immediately retry that record through Local Treatment. |
| Two Bleeds, one without a magical-healing endpoint but with an actual Medicine DC | After healing endpoints, the applier can select the still-present untreated record for its one local Medicine attempt. |
| No actual Medicine removal DC on a descriptive injury | Do not invent a DC or a new mechanical cure. Dressing has only its explicitly defined interaction. |
| Dying patient with one death-save failure at application start | Dying continues during the minute; no immediate stabilization from starting a salve. Ordinary death may preclude completion. |
| Applier takes damage while keeping access and all required Actions | Damage alone does not interrupt and creates no concentration check. |
| Applier uses an extra Action or inserted turn | It does not replace a required regular-turn Action or shorten the full elapsed minute. |
| Recipient changes, touch is lost or one regular-turn Action is skipped | The attempt ends without benefit; paid dose remains spent. |
| Fire buff against a spell dealing fire damage | Its qualifying fire save may receive the bonus. Arcane does not automatically qualify merely because the source is a spell. |
| Arcane buff while casting a spell or researching a formula | No bonus. These are not eligible immediate hazard tasks. |
| Stealth buff with a present hostile sensor | Applies to the actual Stealth check to avoid its detection; grants no automatic Hidden state or knowledge of the sensor. |
| Vitality buff on a Wound, death or concentration save | No bonus; the named exclusions control. |
| Fire +2 and Vitality +1 both qualify one environmental CON save | Use +2, not +3. The independent advantage rule may coexist. |
| Unholy label inferred only from creature species or morality | Does not qualify. An actual mechanical Unholy hazard label is required. No ontology ruling follows. |
| Recipe-201 with only four empty vials and no carrier | Completion check cannot begin: reserve six vials and 0.14 lb actual additional clean carrier water first. |
| Recipe-201 produces Standard output after maximum reservation | Four doses use four vials and no supplemental water needed for compound mass. Unused two vials and reserved water remain actual ordinary stock. |
| Recipe-201 chooses Masterwork durability | Four doses, every vial max/current HP 2; not six reinforced doses. |
| Recipe with tiered nonconsumed mortar | It returns with actual condition and source identity. Its mass cannot fund compound, and it does not provide Herbalism proficiency. |
| Used topical container is sold or reused | It is the surviving ordinary 0.1-lb empty vial, no restored ingredient or magical dose. Actual market quote, or reuse without repurchase. |
| Singleton instantaneous topical healing gives Flawed | Failed, not half healing or a half treatment. |
| Open dose begins its otherwise legal continuous application just before one-hour expiry | The reviewed rule commits the entire dose at legal start and uses the application clock thereafter. This clarification is approved. |
| Panacea interrupted at nine minutes | Physical dose lost, temporary application lock released, seasonal entitlement remains available. |
| Simultaneous Panacea applications on one recipient | One temporary lock prevents a second application from starting. No parallel use of one entitlement. |
| Panacea removes an eligible Exhaustion level while HP healing is blocked | The cure is a successful benefit and spends the entitlement; its independent HP attempt can restore zero. |
| Panacea removes Exhaustion 4 to 3 | Recompute effective maximum without healing, then apply the ordinary 10d6+20 healing within that maximum and other limits. |
| No eligible cure source but missing HP | Reviewed rule skips the cure and attempts independent healing; positive actual HP spends the entitlement. |
| Every Panacea result and callback is blocked | Reviewed rule spends the dose but not the entitlement because no actual benefit occurred; explicitly approved in the exact review. |
| Panacea healing restores no HP but legally ends an eligible Bleed | Reviewed rule counts actual condition removal as benefit; mere unsuccessful save/check does not count. |
| Fallback Panacea campaign changes sessions, expeditions or Openings | The recipient's 100 elapsed-day cooldown remains. A second dose becomes eligible at its exact deadline. |
| Campaign has actual registered season IDs | Only the actual recorded season transition refreshes use, without inventing a new Opening or changing cosmology. |
| Corpse has Exhaustion 6 | Panacea cannot restore life. A separately living protected-return recipient follows its actual permitted Exhaustion removal rules. |

Original rules and source effects remain in immutable evidence. The exact-review reply and final manifest, control the accepted interpretations.
