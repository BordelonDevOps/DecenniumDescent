## Consequential choices

### Soulburn: preserve the conflict and its actual economics

For **Q03**, I select **up to 3 RP**, while retaining the original Core **5 RP** record unchanged.

For **Q04**, I select **option B: remove the unprinted once-per-beneficial-long-rest cap**. Every cast still requires its actual Action, repertoire, components, RP quotation, normal-HP payment and cadence eligibility.

The undiscounted arithmetic remains:

**7 RP paid − 3 recovered = 4 RP lost**, alongside **20 normal HP paid**.

Choosing Core’s 5 would still lose 2 RP. Neither source value makes an ordinary undiscounted cast profitable. A genuine acquired base-only waiver keeps its own restrictions; it does not waive the HP cost or manufacture unlimited uses.

### Grave Gift: two expressly NEW components

For **Q06/Q07**, I concur with direct recovery and independent stabilization.

A donor at **31 normal HP pays 15 and retains 16**. An eligible recipient at **0/40 with three Wounds** can reach **40 HP while retaining all three Wounds and Critical**. That is the selected direct-recovery exception, not ordinary healing or Wound removal.

An actual broad HP-gain prohibition can block that assignment. **Current Chill Touch supplies such a blocker; current Decay does not supply it merely by name.** The independently authored stabilization then makes a still-Dying recipient Stable0, with exactly one real d4-hour deadline. An already-Stable recipient keeps its existing deadline.

These choices do not create resurrection, resource restoration, a general bypass of source-qualified recovery gates, or a new RNG-disclosure entitlement.

### Suppression and Null remain separate decisions

I retain the different suppression lifecycles:

**Golem/Wraith/Homunculus end; Knight temporarily loses body expression; Flood/Danse retain inert physical bodies; Create Undead retains permanent flesh while its authority is suppressed.**

The proposed text now explicitly separates ordinary zero-HP destruction from qualifying **NullDisabled0**. For Flood, Danse and the four Create Undead profiles, that is a **named, limited animation-lifecycle extension**, not a claim that they are Wizard standard minions.

It also grants **no generic living-ally healing predicate**. Cleric Healing Rite and the accepted AC4/AC5/AC6 profile exceptions do not automatically repair these new bodies. Homunculus maintenance and Wraith self-lifetap have their own explicit permissions.

### Wraith: whole-body movement, not point movement

For **Q12**, I select **option B**.

The Wraith needs an actual finite route, sufficient movement and a legal open whole-body endpoint. If a real intervening event traps it, the proposal permits only the expressly scoped local fitting procedure, otherwise ending the manifestation. It does **not** add the old 1d10 rescue damage, an arbitrary backward expulsion, or a farther-space search.

The original movement obligation needs a geometry correction:

- A **5-foot-wide body** crossing a **15-foot-thick slab**, from wholly before to wholly beyond it, translates **20 feet**. At difficult rate 2, that costs **40 feet**.
- A **15-foot contacted movement segment** can cost **30**, leaving **10 from Fly40**—for example, a complete crossing of a **10-foot slab**.

The historical text stays exact. The next current example must identify which actual geometry it proves. Real cargo must remain at its actual lawful drop/current position, not teleport to an unrelated historical “last open” location.

For **Q11**, lifetap remains **Wraith-self only**. Apply actual transfer-funding exclusions first, then the explicitly NEW donor-provenance filter. Temporary HP genuinely absorbed can count; overkill and duplicate parent/terminal damage cannot. This changes neither Vampiric Touch nor Soul Tap.

### Permanent control and permanent bodies are not the same cap

For **Q13**, I retain **exact CR2**, not CR≤2. However, the supplied **26-record source-only bestiary** supplies no operative exact-CR2 Undead target. The positive Command examples therefore remain **conditional**. The new Ghoul/Ghast/Wight/Mummy profiles remain unrated.

For **Q14**, I select the original-schedule alternative. Independent creatures keep their actual schedules; existing companions keep their actual original-owner phases. A paid command addresses one future opportunity, with explicit expiry and actual issuance/execution allowances. It creates no immediate turn, retroactive Action or unwilling-owner Bonus Action.

For **Q15**, I explicitly concur with the permanent-body consequence: repeated paid casts using different eligible corpses can leave **more than two independent animations**. Two slots limit creator authority, not world population.

That does not guarantee continuing obedience, hostility, trained work, crew credentials, donor eligibility, fresh corpse stock or free roster membership. Retained physical needs and independent behavior must be bound before a long-term work/exposure case is presented as complete.

### Homunculus: a concrete current-source correction

The old Universal-school exclusion is incorrect.

Current Wizard **`/engine/10`**, together with the creature/maxHP rules, tests an actual creature created by that Wizard’s spell under the acquired Conjuration feature. **Universal school alone is not a disqualifier.** The spell remains Universal.

A qualifying **Wizard11** creation can therefore have **20 + 11 = 31 maximum HP**. A different caster does not receive that bonus by narrative analogy.

The proposed **Action/touch/2 RP/2d6 maintenance** remains a nonspell operation for the same extant body, not Mending, ordinary Healing Rite, reconstruction or a spell-price waiver. Also, a **20 lb / 2 Load** parcel fits **45 lb / 3 Load**, but makes the servant **Burdened: walk10 / fly20**.
