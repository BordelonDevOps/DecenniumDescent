# One controller, several companions — a worked command sequence

**Authored teaching draft; presentation review pending.** Read the [Tinker class cards](../mechanics/classes/tinker.md), [Tinkering operating procedures](../mechanics/ADVANCED_CRAFT_CANDIDATE_GUIDE.md#tinkering), [crafted recipe cards](../mechanics/TINKERING_CANDIDATE_RECIPE_CARDS.md) and [current communication/control procedure](../mechanics/LANGUAGE_COMMUNICATION_PLAYTEST.md#scribing-runes-and-devices) with this example. The reviewed numbered-round command limit and the later language/control clarification govern their respective interfaces.

This is a separate illustrative scene, not a full character build or a grant of starting equipment. **Ilan is a Tinker 1 with INT 14, PB +2, S = 1 and M = 2.** He has actually selected Field Repair, its recharge is ready, and he has 1 RP available. His ordinary Tinker's-tools proficiency and the physical tools are present. The party has legitimately acquired two complete Tiny Field Mighty Clockworks, recipe-596, called **Alpha** and **Beta**. Their source size was fixed before creation; their labels identify two actual bodies, not two names for one item.

## 1. Keep three separate records

| Companion | Actual source | Starting statistics | Operating budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ilan's Anacite | Automatic Tinker-1 class grant | AC **13 + INT 2 = 15**; HP **5 + INT 2 + class level 1 = 8**; flight 30 feet; bolt +4, range 60 feet, 1d6 piercing | Its class procedures; no recipe-596 service pool |
| Alpha | One completed Tiny recipe-596 | AC **12**, HP **12/12**; walk 20 feet; magical nonspell melee attack **+3**, reach 5 feet, **1d6 force** | **1/1 service unit**; no paid operating block yet |
| Beta | A different completed Tiny recipe-596 | Same printed statistics as Alpha | Its own **1/1 service unit**; no paid operating block yet |

The clockworks use their fixed **+3** attack, not Ilan's PB + INT. Their force damage adds no ability modifier or Shock rider. Each Tiny body weighs 8 lb, has simple grippers with a 5-lb maximum, ordinary senses/passive 10, and its own actual physical limitations. Neither flies nor receives the Anacite's modules. They are already on suitable ground in this scene; ownership does not make carrying or moving an inert body free.

The ordinary crafted-controller limit is three active crafted automata. Alpha and Beta occupy two of those positions. The Anacite has its separate class limit, but separate roster limits do **not** create separate command or offensive budgets.

## 2. Bind lawful controls before the encounter

Both clockworks begin unbound, accessible and lawfully available. Ilan spends **ten active minutes with actual Tinker's tools for Alpha**, then another **ten for Beta**. His INT 14 meets the INT 6 minimum; he is capable, consenting and nonhostile. Each binding records that actual controller and one physically possible simple task, together with a functioning configured visible command channel.

For this example, each stored noncombat task is to carry one particular 2-lb package along an already inspected, short unobstructed ground route to a marked tray. The package fits the actual grippers. Recording the task does not run it: **neither automaton has operated yet**, so both still have their one service unit. Ilan's tools proficiency is not an extra bonus to binding and supplies no additional unit, slot or command.

A machine previously registered to someone else would require that controller's actual release or another completed lawful control procedure. Buying, possessing or knowing the right language does not bypass that requirement. These crafted control records do not confer any of Ilan's class features on another character.

## 3. Choose one subject each numbered round

A genuine hostile encounter begins. The example enemy has **AC 12, 30 HP and no relevant defenses**; these are illustrative inputs, not a new bestiary entry or a balance recommendation. On each listed regular turn, Ilan has an available Bonus Action, his chosen subject is within 60 feet with the actual configured communication channel available, and the legal target is reachable through the subject's real movement/range. No terrain, condition or feature changes the listed rolls. Ilan chooses **Mobile** during each listed regular turn, after its automatic start events and before his first applicable action, movement or check. Record its ending at his next regular-turn start and retain that choice and its spent allowances across any extra turn. No opportunity attack or other stance-dependent numerical event occurs in this fixture; Mobile adds no bonus to his companions' attacks, repair or service.

**Each attack command below uses Ilan's one shared Bonus Action command and one relevant offensive allowance for that numbered round.** A pure support command spends the command allowance without automatically spending the offensive allowance; any later offensive activation still needs its own actual permission and payment. The subject acts after Ilan's **whole turn**. It does not attack between his Action and later movement. A second body or an extra turn does not give this owner another allowance.

The times below are the moments the attached companion phases actually resolve. They keep the paid service clocks visible.

| Numbered round / companion phase | Selected subject and actual result | Enemy HP | Resource record |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 / 10:00:00 | **Alpha:** move within its actual allowance, then roll 9 + 3 = 12, a hit. Its d6 shows 4: **4 force damage**. | 26 | Alpha's first actual operation spends its unit, **1 → 0**, opening this encounter's paid block for at most ten elapsed minutes. |
| 2 / 10:00:06 | **Anacite:** legal bolt, 8 + 4 = 12, a hit. Its d6 shows 3: **3 piercing damage**. The on-hit Shock d4 is 2, so no Shock save or rider occurs. | 23 | The class command spends Ilan's shared allowance this round. It spends no Alpha/Beta service unit. |
| 3 / 10:00:12 | **Beta:** legal melee attack, 8 + 3 = 11, a miss. | 23 | Beta's first operation spends its own unit, **1 → 0**, and opens its own paid block. The miss refunds neither the command nor that unit. |
| 4 / 10:00:18 | **Alpha:** after the repair described below, a legal melee attack rolls 10 + 3 = 13. Its d6 shows 2: **2 force damage**. | 21 | Alpha is still inside its actual paid block in the same encounter. It needs this round's command, but no second service payment for this attack. |

During round 1, Beta remains unoperated and receives no active behavior merely because initiative started. Once a crafted automaton has a paid block, its uncommanded options are only its permitted defensive movement and Dodge. Neither clockwork acquires an independent Reaction. The Anacite follows its own uncommanded Dodge/follow procedure. None gets an additional damaging action because another subject was selected.

Alpha's shown block can last **no later than 10:10:00**; Beta's can last no later than **10:10:12**. The record also retains the actual encounter and any event that closes a block earlier. A displayed service count of zero does not cancel an already paid block, but it cannot buy the next block. Shutdown, suppression, transfer and rest do not pause or refill those clocks.

## 4. Repair HP without buying more operation

Before round 4, Alpha has taken **5 already-resolved damage**, leaving it at **7/12 HP**. It is still complete, functional, positive-HP and friendly. On Ilan's round-4 turn, Alpha is within touch, with the required access and no repair blocker.

Ilan uses his **Action** and spends his **1 RP** on his selected **Field Repair**. With S = 1 and M = 2, a d8 result of 4 offers **4 + 2 = 6 HP**. Alpha has only 5 missing HP, so it returns to **12/12**, gaining 5. Field Repair's recharge counter becomes **2**. Ilan has **0 RP** remaining.

Alpha still has **zero unspent service units**, the same paid block, the same controller and the same physical identity. Repair has not serviced it. Ilan can then use the still-available Bonus Action for round 4's command above; Alpha executes only after his whole turn. Using the Action on repair does not supply a second command or an extra attack.

At zero HP, this crafted automaton would instead be a destroyed wreck. Field Repair cannot rebuild that wreck. Its reviewed reconstruction routes have separate costs, eligibility and surviving-primary requirements; it makes no death saves, gains no Wounds and receives no ordinary biological healing. It also does not inherit the Anacite's ten-minute, 1-RP restoration route.

## 5. Service one body when the opportunity is real

Later, after active operation has ended and the party actually has a safe one-hour servicing opportunity, Ilan services **Alpha only**. The complete positive-HP Tiny body, actual trained Tinker's tools, a safe surface and a real compatible maintenance pack are present. For this body, that pack has a **2.5-gp reference value and 0.08-lb mass**; actual acquisition remains an inventory and price record.

Spend the pack and **one active hour**, then make the INT tool check against DC 10. Ilan's natural 6 + INT 2 + PB 2 = **10: Success**. Alpha's service units become **1/1**. It still has 12/12 HP; servicing itself repairs no HP, grants no XP, restores no RP and resets no earlier round or paid-block history. Beta remains at **0 unspent units**. Servicing one body does not service another.

Had the check failed, the pack and hour would still be spent without a refill. Field-capable servicing on a genuinely suitable safe surface does not make recipe-596's original workshop creation field-capable.

## 6. Use the stored noncombat task in its own context

After that successful servicing, with initiative genuinely ended and the unchanged package route still legal, Ilan can start **Alpha's already registered task with an Action**. It does not require a second ten-minute programming period for the same unchanged task. First noncombat operation spends Alpha's one unit and opens one paid ten-minute noncombat block.

Changing that route into a new finite task would require the actual ten-minute reprogramming procedure. The task grants no autonomous attack, professional crafting, spell/script use, ram-crew entitlement or ability to solve an unknown obstacle. The two-pound package and its contents remain real inventory at their actual location.

If a genuine combat encounter begins while Alpha is working, its noncombat block closes and the combat operation requires a **new service unit**. In this example none remains. The unpaid portion of the old clock cannot substitute for that new payment; Alpha cannot operate in the new encounter until it can lawfully pay. The Anacite's separate class operation does not donate a service unit to it.

## Check these choices at the table

- **A second command in the same numbered round:** unavailable even if Ilan controls another automaton, chooses his Anacite or receives an extra turn. The owner-wide allowance remains spent.
- **A communication failure after legal commitment:** no refund of committed allowances. Language knowledge, an interpreter or telepathy alone does not install a receiver or bypass the actual channel.
- **A controller transfer:** preserve the subject's own offensive-spend history, service expenditure and clocks. The new controller gets no second attack from that same body in a round it already acted.
- **A trap or manual utility:** use that item's actual arming, entry-trigger or direct-work procedure and any shared budget it expressly uses. It does not acquire automaton binding or attached actions merely from the word device.
- **Another party member with no Tinker class:** legitimate crafted control can still be possible under its own requirements. It grants no Anacite, Field Repair, Dissonance-routing feature, class RP or proficiency.

Keep the controller's numbered-round allowances beside **each body's** controller, current HP, units, paid-block start/deadline, actual program and offensive history. Those separate records explain why three owned companions can have different states while sharing one owner's command limit.
