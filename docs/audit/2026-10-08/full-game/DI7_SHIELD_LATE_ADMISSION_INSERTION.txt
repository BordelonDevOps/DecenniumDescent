NEW — first-admission closure and replacement. For each actual spell occurrence and actual recipient, evaluate this ward family’s admission once, immediately before that recipient’s first actual resolution component at the timing specified above. This checkpoint belongs to the occurrence and recipient, not separately to each beam, damage root or ward application. At that checkpoint, the current eligible, live, unsuppressed ward with an available use automatically spends one use and binds that occurrence’s coverage to that exact ward application. If no ward can commit, record no admission and close that occurrence’s admission checkpoint for this recipient without spending a use. Later components of the same occurrence cannot create or reopen admission. Ending suppression, establishing a new ward, or successfully replacing a ward after that checkpoint does not admit the already-started occurrence. A successful replacement terminates the old application under the existing replacement rule and inherits neither its uses nor its admitted-occurrence coverage; the replacement spends no use on that already-started occurrence. Previously prevented damage remains prevented, but subsequent unresolved components receive no prevention from either the terminated application or its replacement. The replacement retains its own two uses and its own deadline of 60 elapsed seconds from establishment for subsequent qualifying occurrences, including genuinely separate source-scheduled occurrences of the same spell. Another actual recipient is evaluated at that recipient’s own first actual component, not when the spell first affected someone else. Existing retargeting, canceled/excluded-event and transfer rules remain unchanged. For a share-only recipient, its first positive pre-mitigation share supplies its first qualifying admission checkpoint; a zero share or post-defense branch explicitly skipping prevention supplies none. A qualifying direct component already resolved for that same recipient closes the checkpoint before any later share of that occurrence.
