CURRENT COMPLETE RELEVANT ENGINE PARAGRAPHS:
/engine/2: MUTATION SLOTS. At Warrior 1 you have one mutation slot; slot II unlocks at Warrior 5, slot III at Warrior 13 and slot IV at Warrior 20. During a completed short or long rest, equip one eligible mutation in each slot. Equipped mutations are dormant until activated unless their text explicitly says otherwise. You cannot equip the same named mutation twice.

/engine/3: ACTIVE MUTATIONS. Before Juggernaut Mutation Efficiency, only one equipped mutation can be active at a time. Mutation Efficiency raises the ordinary simultaneous-active limit to two. Transcendent, Apex Form and other explicit features can temporarily exceed that limit. Activating a mutation beyond the current limit ends one active mutation of your choice unless an explicit exception permits otherwise.

/engine/17: MUTATION TIERS. Basic mutations require Warrior 1. Advanced mutations require Warrior 6. Apex mutations require Warrior 15. You may equip any mutation whose level gate and other prerequisite you meet. Each tree permits any number of Basic mutations up to slot limits, at most two equipped Advanced mutations at once, and at most one equipped Apex mutation at once.

/engine/18: MUTATION FEATS. Mutation feats are not free mutation selections. They become eligible choices at an ASI/feat opportunity or other campaign feat grant once their listed level and mutation prerequisite are met. They do not consume mutation slots unless their own text says otherwise.

/engine/23: All class gates use Warrior class level, not total character level. Proficiency bonus follows the game's ordinary total-character-level rule. No other class level unlocks Warrior mutation tiers, serum capacity, Juggernaut, Hero paths, Apex mutations or Final Forms.
