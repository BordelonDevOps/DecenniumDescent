# Remaining condition/control work after Strain

The preserved `RIDER_SPEED_CURSE_MARK_CANDIDATES.json` contains119 lexical candidates. It is evidence for a focused source conference, not proof of exhaustive coverage. Full17-engine/1,223-card and operational-spell semantic mapping still remains.

Concrete source issues for the next packet:

- Mage Cold Focus, Elemental Bolt, Elemental Surge and Arch Elemental Mastery: avoid duplicate Focus/Bolt slow records from the same hit; determine +5 upgrade's treatment of Glacier Spike's half-Speed rider, strongest/nonstacking policy and ownership. Preserve Cascade Storm as a distinct unqualified next-turn/source-clock case unless expressly overridden.
- Roots/Speed0 are not automatically Restrained or Grappled. Bind source-qualified Speed0, flat slow and half-Speed records, their saves/endpoints and `eligibility.slowed-foe` hooks without granting every generic immunity to every source. Keep terrain/cargo/Prone/Exhaustion distinct.
- Generic physical Restrained escape remains a dependency in the condition guide. Exact-source methods win; decide an explicitly bounded fallback and which physical applications qualify, without inventing repeat saves for every restraint.
- Remaining marks include Cleric Balanced Scales/Divine Judgment, Dragoon Drake Mark, Wizard Binding Sigil/Gravebrand, Tinker Target Scanner and others. Names do not create shared Vengeance/Blood Mark rights. Ally buffs use reviewed source clocks; enemy marks whose benefit chiefly accrues to the owner need explicit clock classification where text only gives N rounds.
- Cleric Ritual of Undeath's Death Ward is a death-replacement feature, not just a Marked condition. Its interaction with HP prohibition and special death thresholds belongs in the healing/death-replacement pass; don't silently resolve it through the label.
- Curse/Diseased need actual inflicted-source metadata, immunity/cure exceptions and recognition hooks. Class Oracle Curse, quest/pact status, cursed equipment and effects merely naming a curse are not automatically the same inflicted condition.
- Wizard control cards retain player agency and shared hard-denial guard; loss of Reaction/Speed is a separate soft rider. Resolve source timing without granting another denied turn or inheriting blanket domination from a name.
- Mind Shock's explicit additional non-attack Reaction remains separate from ordinary Reaction. Per-dose reapplication/stacking and all other class resource grants still need the all-source semantic pass.

Warrior Weakened and Cleric Judged now use the unambiguous reviewed bare-two-round hostile target-end convention. Other original cards remain unchanged; all new decisions stay in the review layer.
