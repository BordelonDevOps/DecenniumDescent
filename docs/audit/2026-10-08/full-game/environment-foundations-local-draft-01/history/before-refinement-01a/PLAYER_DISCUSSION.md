# Environmental foundations — discussion draft

**NEW local proposals. Not submitted, agreed or active rules.** This draft supplies concrete procedures for actual GPT discussion. Source books and reviewed rules remain unchanged.

## EF-01 — One environment record and one elapsed timeline

Before exposure, record the hazard's real mapped volume, temperature or other trigger, whether it is ordinary or source-specific, start and ending conditions, check/DC, outcomes, physical counters and any scheduled changes. Give the characters perceptible warnings and available approaches. These defaults supply mundane recurring hazards; a specific reviewed hazard keeps its own printed procedure.

Use the existing elapsed timeline. Six seconds is the proposed environmental accounting step, not an extra turn, Action, rest, recharge or death save. Tactical Actions retain their actual turn scheduler; a round accounts for six seconds only once. Outside initiative, an ordinary discrete Action used in these new procedures consumes six seconds. Preserve all partly completed timers when initiative, watches, travel modes or scenes change.

For a due elapsed hazard event, apply already-resolved changes to anatomy, position, protection and the environment, then determine actual exposure. An effect with endpoint T is no longer active at T unless its specific rule says otherwise. A rescue or shelter change must have legally resolved before the hazard event to prevent it; no retroactive action is inserted. Resolve independently due events by earlier creation order if no existing rule sets precedence. Do not grant extra turns to break a tie.

The Director's Exposure pressure track remains a separate scene tool. Record which actual event raised it and what its announced complication changes. Do not charge the same cold save, fire packet or supply consumption twice merely because the pressure track also rose. A separate complication may create a new actual hazard, with its own warning and source record.

All damage uses existing defenses, temporary HP, Instant Death, Wound and zero-HP procedures. All new ability checks use Core task degrees; binary saves use existing save rules. A rule granting advantage or a numerical bonus does not create a roll where this chapter has none. A local draft cannot activate an unfinished spell, change source anatomy, create gear or certify an entire catalog.

## EF-02 — Breath, air loss and rescue

This is a NEW baseline for an actual creature whose current body needs to breathe. Record which medium that anatomy can breathe and whether its airway and that medium are currently available. Water breathing permits the actual stated water medium; it does not grant a swim Speed, speech, pressure protection or breathable air in a smoke-filled room. An explicitly nonbreathing body does not consume its deprivation timers. A mere creature name or damage immunity grants no exception.

After six continuous seconds of actual unblocked breathing, establish a full held-breath reserve of max(30, 60 × (1 + current CON modifier)) seconds. This is passive respiration, not an Action tax. At the first subsequent interruption, snapshot that full reserve and a grace allowance of 6 × max(1, current CON modifier) seconds. A creature already partway through deprivation keeps its existing unused reserve/grace instead of receiving a new snapshot. Stat changes and form changes alone do not refill either allowance.

While an actual breathing body cannot or deliberately does not breathe, spend elapsed time from reserve first, then grace. Brief access to breathing pauses depletion immediately, but only six continuous seconds of actual breathing refills both allowances. Shorter access does not reset consumed time. A nonbreathing form pauses rather than refills the same person's deprivation record and suppresses any suffocation prohibition/enforcement while that body has no need to breathe. It grants no HP or stabilization. Reversion resumes the remaining allowance if air is still unavailable; if both allowances are already exhausted, suffocation becomes active immediately. True death closes living respiration; genuine resurrection into a breathing body starts with zero reserve and its newly calculated grace until actual breathing restores the reserve. This resurrection rule is proposed, not a new soul or identity ruling.

A deliberate ordinary spoken sentence, audible verbal component or actual exhaled breath attack while holding breath spends all remaining reserve before the vocal/exhaled event resolves; unused grace remains. Ordinary nonverbal actions and taking damage do not automatically expel the reserve. NEW underwater speech limit: a body submerged in water cannot supply ordinary audible verbal components unless an exact capability permits underwater speech; water breathing alone does not. A source expressly supplying different communication retains that permission. This consequence requires explicit joint review because it affects spellcasting.

When both allowances reach zero without actual breathing, apply the NEW suffocating state. A living creature with positive HP is assigned 0 HP and processed through its actual zero-HP lifecycle. This is not damage: no damage resistance, temporary-HP absorption, ordinary damage floor, concentration-damage save, Wound roll, reflected damage or life-drain trigger arises from the assignment. A character/significant NPC becomes dying; a source-defined immediate-death or disappearance-at-zero creature follows that lifecycle and any actually eligible would-die replacement. Do not give companions character death saves.

While suffocating, a breathing creature cannot regain HP or become Stable through ordinary healing, Medicine, a kit, rest or three successful death saves. It continues its actual death-save cadence: at the third success clear the success count and retain any failures, remaining dying. Natural 20 while its HP gain is blocked also clears successes, retains failures and leaves it dying. These are explicit NEW exceptions proposed for the suffocating state, not the Decay natural-20 bridge. No extra automatic failure is added. An exact would-die replacement may still intercept actual death; honor its own broad HP-blocked/Stable-0 exception. Six seconds later, if deprivation continues, suffocation again assigns positive HP to 0 or ends replacement-created Stable without clearing existing failure counters. Repeat this deprivation enforcement each six seconds; it adds no damage packet or death-save roll.

Breathing becomes actually possible when anatomy, medium and airway permit it, even if the patient is unconscious. This immediately ends the suffocating prohibition and enforcement pulses. It grants no HP, stabilization, reversed death, removed Wound or recovered held item. At living 0 HP the patient still needs its ordinary recovery procedure. Dying outside initiative uses one death save every six seconds, first six seconds after entering that state; entering initiative retains the already-due interval rather than rolling again immediately solely for a new initiative position. This environmental/out-of-initiative bridge is NEW and must be reconciled with the ordinary scheduler in review.

A helper within five feet with an available hand may spend one Action to remove one accessible mundane airway obstruction or reposition a willing/incapacitated body so its airway reaches actual breathable medium, if the real geometry and carrying rules permit that movement. This does not teleport a submerged creature to the surface or cure a magical restraint, toxin, disease or damaged organ. If a legal ordinary move/door action already supplies air, do that action instead; no extra rescue roll is invented for an unobstructed solution. Spell and form rescues pay their actual costs and require actual access to components.

## EF-03 — Cold and heat exposure

This NEW mundane climate system supplies the missing rule; it is not recovered Chapter 5 text. Use actual temperature at the creature's occupied location. Cold: at or below 0 °C (32 °F), one exposure unit per 3,600 exposed seconds, CON DC 12. Severe cold: at or below −20 °C (−4 °F), one unit per 600 seconds, CON DC 15. Heat: at or above 40 °C (104 °F), one unit per 3,600 seconds, CON DC 12. Severe heat: at or above 50 °C (122 °F), one unit per 600 seconds, CON DC 15. The severe profile replaces the ordinary profile for that medium. These thresholds and numbers are explicit design proposals.

Whole-body immersion in water at or below 10 °C (50 °F), or a soaked body and clothing in air at or below 10 °C, uses the severe-cold profile. Wetness requires actual soaking, not a few drops. Immersion's water temperature governs this climate exposure instead of the ambient air; do not run simultaneous heat and cold clocks for that same physical medium. Drowning, currents, contact fire, magical damage and a specific altitude hazard are independent actual hazards, not additional unnamed climate levels.

Maintain separate cold and heat exposure progress and separate personal cold/heat levels 0–3. For each exposed segment add elapsedSeconds / currentProfileInterval to that climate's fractional progress. At a full unit subtract one and make its one save. Use the highest profile DC encountered during that unfinished unit, then initialize the next unit from the actual current profile. Protected or benign time pauses progress. Ten uninterrupted minutes genuinely free from that climate hazard clears its unfinished progress/DC memory; shorter gaps do not. A change of zone, dose, day, Watch, rest or initiative never resets progress by itself.

Failed cold save: increase cold level by one, maximum 3. Cold level 1 gives disadvantage on DEX ability checks. Cold level 2 also reduces every actual Speed by 10 feet, minimum 0. Entering level 3 adds one global Exhaustion level; each later failed cold save at level 3 adds another global Exhaustion level. Success does not reduce an existing level. Cold level 1 is not global Exhaustion 1, and there is no secret fourth cold level.

Failed heat save follows the same progression, but heat level 1 gives disadvantage on STR and CON ability checks. Heat level 2 gives the same −10-Speed profile. The cold and heat −10 profiles share one new climate-Speed group: use −10 once if both exist. They add to an independently applicable burden flat penalty before actual Speed multipliers. Entering heat level 3 and each later failed heat save at level 3 adds one global Exhaustion level. Existing Exhaustion effects and death at 6 remain unchanged. Saves are not ability checks, so level-1 penalties do not penalize these CON saves; global Exhaustion 3 can.

Actual warming/cooling recovery needs dry conditions, a temperature from 10 through 30 °C inclusive, and freedom from the corresponding exposure. Every continuous 30 minutes there removes one cold level and one heat level, down to 0. Interruption resets only this incomplete recovery interval; previously removed levels remain removed. Recovery may occur during an otherwise legal rest. It never removes already gained global Exhaustion, HP damage, Wounds or other conditions. Global Exhaustion still needs its actual reviewed recovery procedure, including food/water and a cause no longer actively imposing it.

A complete dry insulating outfit that actually covers the body and fits its anatomy prevents ordinary-cold progress; in severe cold it instead grants advantage on its exposure saves. Record the real garment identities, coverage, function and weight before use. Hide armor's descriptive pelts, an item name, one hat, melded clothing or an unrecorded free winter kit is insufficient by itself. Soaked insulation loses this benefit until replaced with actual dry garments or dried for 30 continuous minutes in dry 10–30 °C shelter with actual safe heat/air circulation. This recovery time does not consume nonexistent fuel. Prices and item masses are not invented here.

For heat, actual shelter or cooling changes the local temperature only when the fiction/source really establishes it. Shade alone supplies no arbitrary numerical temperature reduction. An actual maintained ventilated shade arrangement grants advantage against ordinary heat, not severe heat; it does not create water, cold resistance or breathable air. This proposal adds no extra generic water bill: keep existing actual daily requirements and any explicit source-specific increase. A Director introducing a different water requirement must declare it as that hazard's separate procedure before expenditure, not retroactively.

NEW resolution for the four source climate-adapted ancestry traits: Flame Drakari receives advantage on these mundane heat saves; Frost Drakari, Mountain Dwarf and Mountain Ogre receive advantage on these mundane cold saves. This replaces the unresolved reference with a defined benefit and requires actual joint approval. It does not rewrite their separate elemental damage or altitude clauses. Winterfang Druid already explicitly has cold-exposure advantage; do not grant a second die or an invented immunity. Nomadic Human's actual extreme-heat/cold advantage applies normally. Damage resistance alone does not suppress climate progress or Exhaustion.

NEW ordinary-penalty classification for Warrior environmental features: cold/heat level-1 check disadvantage and the climate −10 Speed profile are ordinary environmental penalties; ignoring those penalties suppresses them while the actual feature applies, not the underlying levels, elapsed exposure, future saves or the separate Exhaustion application. Actual advantage/resistance components retain their own scope. This interpretation must be discussed because the source's phrase is broad; it is not presented as a previously agreed Warrior ruling. An actual effect expressly granting immunity to this hazard prevents its new progress/outcomes in its real scope; an unrelated condition immunity does not.

## EF-04 — Stoneback Stout proposal and exact interfaces

Preserve the original Tier 2, 1 sp, four-hour source. NEW proposed interpretation: for four elapsed hours after one legally completed dose, suppress only the cold-level-1 DEX ability-check disadvantage described in EF-03. Suppression applies at cold levels 1, 2 and 3 because that level-1 penalty remains a component of each. Do not subtract a level, block a failed save, postpone level 3, suppress the level-2 Speed penalty, remove global Exhaustion or confer cold damage resistance. This is a deliberately explicit interpretation of 'ignore the first level', not a claim that the original supplied that meaning.

Drinking after cold level 1 already exists suppresses its check penalty immediately, without reducing progress/levels. At four-hour expiry the penalty applies again if cold level remains at least 1. Overlapping drinks do not multiply protection; their actual deadlines remain independent. Global Exhaustion 1 can still independently impose disadvantage on DEX checks. A new dose never grants a new first cold level. All proposed common dose/payment rules remain separately unadopted; this note does not promote CB-03 or alter the original 27-source consumable packet.

Field Tide Tea applies its actual +1 only to a real Water-hazard check/save. It creates no suffocation save and no extra breath seconds. Black-Dragon Shadow Cloak's actual +2 is for qualifying task checks, not the climate CON saving throws. Fey-Pollen Veilcloth grants no gas filter; Fiber-Husk Climbing Cord is a wearable girdle, not a load-bearing rescue rope. These current exact cards are frozen in the model to prevent using their names as permissions.

## EF-05 — Fire and smoke scenes

Use two NEW ordinary open-fire profiles when no more specific source applies. A prepared campfire occupies its actually fueled 5-foot square and deals 1d6 fire on first contact. An engulfing room fire occupies its declared burning volume and deals 3d6 fire on first contact. For either profile, one creature takes at most one contact packet from the same fire complex per six elapsed seconds: after damage, set nextEligibleAt = eventTime + 6; if still in contact then, apply the next packet; if not, wait for a later actual contact. Moving between connected burning squares or exiting/reentering does not create another complex or reset that deadline. Independent distinct fires retain their own source records. A campfire and an engulfing profile covering the same fuel/volume use only the engulfing profile.

The area fire has no saving throw and does not automatically apply Burning. Do not add the generic 1d4 Burning tick to these contact packets. A separate effect that explicitly inflicts Burning uses the reviewed attached-fire family, its regular-end cadence, own ending saves and existing Action/full-immersion extinguishing. A fire immunity prevents damage without necessarily ending attached Burning. Current ground oil retains its separate reviewed 5-fire/12-second/per-turn procedure; this new six-second contact cap does not rewrite it.

Record actual fuel, fire start and scheduled fuel exhaustion before the scene. These profiles do not generate fuel, infer propagation through walls, ignite carried inventories or permanently destroy gear by contact alone. A specific scene may schedule additional fueled squares with an actual cause and warning. Building a prepared fire, lighting equipment and consuming oil use their reviewed times and real items. Fire ends when its actual fuel expires or a physically sufficient extinguishing method covers it; damage immunity does not extinguish it.

For the NEW campfire profile, one Action using one real gallon of water extinguishes its one 5-foot fueled square. One Action using a suitable nonflammable blanket or three real pounds of loose earth also smothers that square if the actor can reach it safely. A blanket remains an actual wet/dirty item; water and deposited earth are expended from carried inventory. A hand cannot extinguish an engulfing room by fiat. Each engulfing 5-foot square needs four gallons and one full minute of continuous accessible dousing, or a specific actual counter. Concurrent helpers can supply water and access but do not divide that one-minute deadline; hazardous contact still occurs. These quantitative extinguishing rules are NEW, not general chemistry or recipe revisions.

An ordinary smoke scene has a mapped source and volume. Thin smoke is breathable and imposes disadvantage only on sight-dependent Perception checks through it. Dense choking smoke is heavily obscuring and unbreathable to an ordinary air-breather, using EF-02 rather than also inflicting an invented poison save. A veil, ordinary wet cloth or poison-damage resistance does not provide air. An actual respirator/source counter works only under its printed scope, capacity and duration. Toxic gases and diseases need their own full records; this smoke model does not identify them.

Finite example ventilation profile: a 20 × 20 × 10-foot room has one ordinary burning source and one operable five-foot vent. Opening the unobstructed vent is an Action. When the source is extinguished AND the vent is open, begin continuous clearance: after 60 seconds dense smoke becomes thin and breathable; after another 60 seconds it clears. Reignition or closing the vent interrupts clearance; reset the unfinished 60-second phase only, retaining the last achieved density. Continued burning at the next fixed 60-second emission marker raises density one step, at most dense. Set those emission markers from the original scene start, never from a player turn. Specific local smoke-dispersal effects retain their actual footprint/ending; only a later actual emission event can refill a cleared region.

## EF-06 — Swimming, currents and physical rescue

NEW baseline swimming: an actual body with suitable locomotor anatomy and an ordinary walking Speed may swim without a special swim Speed, paying one additional foot of movement cost per foot traveled. Use its current walking-mode cap and the same spent-movement ledger. An actual swim Speed uses its own cap without that extra cost. Terrain's strongest ordinary/difficult/severe cost still applies before adding the nonspecialist swim cost. Do not double-charge ordinary water as difficult terrain solely because the body lacks swim Speed. Explicit anatomy restrictions remain; no new hand or breathing capability is created.

Swimming has no default attack/save penalty, armor ban or automatic check. Actual burden, squeezing, conditions and specific underwater weapon/spell limits still govern. For depth support, a conscious creature able to move may spend movement equivalent to five feet of legal swimming to tread at its current depth until its next regular start. At least five actual feet of legal swimming during its regular turn also supplies this support. An actual swim Speed permits stationary depth support without that extra movement expenditure while conscious and movement-capable. Outside initiative apply the same support decision once per six seconds, without granting extra Actions.

NEW unsupported-body default: a body without an actual buoyant support, legal treading/stationary-swim support or a specific buoyancy feature sinks five feet per six-second water marker until reaching solid support. Being unconscious, movement-disabled or equipped with a swim Speed alone while unconscious does not establish active support. This is a game abstraction requiring review, not a density inference from body pounds. A real flotation device/boat may provide support under its capacity; it is not free because someone owns a rope. Reaching bottom ends sinking, not submersion or deprivation. Water entry does not cancel an already applicable falling/impact rule.

At scene creation fix water markers every six elapsed seconds. An ordinary current moves unanchored occupants ten feet along its mapped channel at each marker; a swift current moves them twenty feet. Move through each actual intervening space. Stop at blocking terrain; no invented impact damage, wall passage or opportunity attack is added. A supported swimmer can still drift unless anchored or successfully resisting; a water-breathing creature can drift. No adjacent current zone duplicates the same connected channel's marker. Resolve drift, then the same marker's sinking, then actual new location hazards.

Before that marker's drift, a movement-capable creature may spend its actual Reaction to hold position: STR (Athletics) task check DC 12 for ordinary current or 15 for swift current. Success/Critical Success prevents that marker's drift; Failure permits full drift; Critical Failure permits drift and removes active treading/stationary-swim support for that marker, so ordinary sinking follows unless an independent physical support remains. This is one check, not a save, and it does not permit movement above a cap. Extra turns do not move the current marker or grant an extra Reaction. Field Tide Tea and burden apply to the actual check within their scopes.

A legal, actually attached anchor/tether can stop drift at its real length and load limit. It must have real purchase and fit; no knot, tensile capacity or invulnerable attachment is inferred from an item name. This draft does not introduce generic rope structural statistics. Use an existing actual rope profile or specify a complete scene tether before exposure. A creature reaches only spaces permitted by the tether and real geometry; the rope does not automatically hold its airway above water. No automatic constriction, collision or rope-break damage is invented.

An adjacent helper with an available hand can spend one Action to secure a willing/incapacitated creature for a tow if its anatomy permits. Before moving, pass BOTH actual physical-weight and Load/casualty checks from the reviewed body rules, including all external and melded equipment. NEW towing then halves the rescuer's otherwise-current swim/walk-swimming Speed, after flat reductions. The shared movement ledger is retained. Keep the casualty in a legal adjacent space along the route; no path through solid or occupied spaces is granted. No automatic weight or Load reduction comes from water. If limits fail, use actual flotation, a capacity-qualified vehicle, gear removal or another legal rescue method.

A secured real line can support a NEW manual haul of five feet per Action when the actual puller's physical handling limits, attachment, line length and route permit it. No check is needed in a calm unobstructed situation. Under a stated resisting current or unstable footing, declare STR (Athletics) DC 12, or DC 15 for swift current, before the Action: Success moves five feet, Critical Success ten, Failure none, Critical Failure releases the puller's grip without automatically destroying the line. The elapsed hazard/current continues, so a failed check still costs time. The wearable Fiber-Husk Climbing Cord is explicitly ineligible. A feature-specific rescue overrides only what it actually states.

## EF-07 — Weather records and bounded weather scenes

Record an actual weather trajectory for the next 24 hours: temperature by location/time, precipitation, wind direction/speed, sight conditions and scheduled causes of change. This is ordinary local weather, not a new season, calendar, plane or Proof War era. The Director may choose a trajectory that fits the scene, but an accurate forecast must report that existing trajectory rather than a new roll on every question. Detect Weather retains its reviewed current-trajectory accuracy; only an actual later new cause changes the forecast, with that cause recorded.

NEW ordinary rain profiles: light rain has no universal modifier. Heavy rain gives disadvantage on sight- and hearing-dependent Perception checks in its actual outdoor volume. It does not automatically create difficult terrain, extinguish every flame or produce floodwater. A mapped muddy surface may separately be difficult terrain, and a source may declare actual accumulation or fuel exposure. Soaking requires ten continuous minutes of heavy rain without actual waterproof shelter/cover, or actual immersion; a brief gap pauses that counter, and ten uninterrupted dry minutes resets unfinished wetting. Existing soaking does not vanish from that reset.

NEW wind profiles: below 20 mph no generic mechanical wind penalty. At 20–39 mph, ordinary ranged weapon attacks through the wind and hearing-dependent Perception checks have disadvantage. At 40 mph or more, those penalties remain and voluntary travel directly against the wind is difficult terrain. Ranged spells are not weapon attacks; no generic spell-attack penalty is imported. Flight is not automatically ended merely by the wind category. A separately declared gust hazard uses the next paragraph.

Finite gale scene: an exposed 60 × 20-foot bridge has a fixed crosswind and one gust every 60 elapsed seconds from scene start. At each gust, an occupant without actual stable attachment makes STR save DC 15; failure moves it ten feet downwind and makes it Prone, success does neither. Use actual body footprints, obstacles, edge and falling rules; no generic collision damage is added. A hand on a railing counts only if it is an actual available-hand grip on a structurally sufficient railing and the body remains in reach. An ordinary rope of unknown capacity is not silently a certified anchor. This hazard has one save, no duplicated 'wind-category save'.

Natural fog is a declared volume of heavy obscurement; it does not supply cover, poison or unavailable air. A creature's known position is not sight. A fog-clearing effect changes only its actual footprint and duration; an independently continuing source may later produce new fog through its announced rule. No new generic spell-dispel power is created.

NEW numeric interpretation of Storm Magic's mild ordinary-weather adjustment: at most five mph up or down for existing wind in its actual 30-foot radius, and one rain category toward light/heavy or toward dry if it is already raining. It cannot initiate rain from dry conditions, create a new storm, or take an adjusted wind from below 40 mph to gale. The same feature's overlapping adjustments use the strongest single change rather than accumulating five-mph steps. Existing gale can be reduced by five mph; no infinite repeated reduction. These limits and overlapping-source policy require actual review rather than being treated as original class text. Actual concentration, residual duration, range and source restrictions remain.

Eye of the Storm supplies its existing ordinary-weather moderation inside its real sphere for its printed duration; for this NEW baseline its local wind is below 20 mph, precipitation does not wet occupants and temperature is 10–30 °C. It pauses relevant new exposure there but does not instantly remove prior levels, dry gear, refill breath or cancel magical hazards. An expiring sphere restores actual surrounding conditions without resetting progress. Master of Winter retains its actual bounded snow/sleet, difficult ground and sight restriction; this draft does not turn that card into the pending Control Weather spell or invent a universal cold temperature/damage output for it.
