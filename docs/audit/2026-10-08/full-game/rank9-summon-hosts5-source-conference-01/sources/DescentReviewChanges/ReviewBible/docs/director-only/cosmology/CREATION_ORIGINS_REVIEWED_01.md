## Public wording example

The accepted tradition credits Calan’thir and Asmodeus with jointly creating the Crucible as a proving ground and a monument of Order and Hell working together. Cultures dispute their shares of the credit. Adventurers enter a dungeon that has shaped Aethel’s fortunes, beliefs and conflicts over generations.

## Director account and unresolved provenance

Calan’thir and Asmodeus genuinely built the known Crucible around machinery they did not invent. The Silent Ones—also called the Old Ones—built the older Heartcell, including the hundred-floor resonance structure, ten Gate divisions, opening cycle and reward architecture. Owner-locked material also says its creators used loot as bait to invite discovery and release, while the Heartcell’s current operation generates the resonance and continuing difference that oppose Oblivion. The supplied sources do not yet establish why the Old Ones originally built a machine with these properties, how it became their prison, or the exact sequence by which the Divine Pact discovered, claimed, modified, activated and bound itself to it. Preserve those missing links as unresolved. The complete two-layer account is discoverable only through the recorded revelation ladder and is never ordinary world knowledge.

## How to read the original origin accounts

The surviving original books contain accounts of Great Stillness, contrary gestures, the Weave, Spiral, seals, the Sundering and the Pact. Their preservation does not make every passage authoritative. Use the registered domains and recorded owner decisions for the questions they actually settle; retain unresolved event relations rather than inventing a unified chronology.

## Peace and meaningful difference

Ordinary political peace, treaty, ceasefire or reconciliation is not the defined catastrophe. The threatened state is absolute ontological stillness: the loss of meaningful difference, possibility, motion and agency until existence no longer remains distinct. No measurable awakening threshold is established by this comparison; define one later only if mechanics actually require it.
