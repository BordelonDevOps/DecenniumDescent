# Mining — candidate field guide

**Authored playtest candidate; exact review pending.** Use the [shared gathering rules](GATHERING_TOOL_CANDIDATE_GUIDE.md), the body catalogue and the exact card.

## Choose the procedure

| Procedure | Work and check | Supported result |
|---|---|---|
| Prospect | 10 active minutes; WIS/Mining; one exposed accessible patch up to 100 ft²; DC 12 clear, 15 obscured or 18 severe but feasible signs | Real observable evidence and possible access in that patch |
| Assay | 1 active minute; WIS/Mining; one exposed mineral manifest or actual sample; exact source DC or highest assessed tier DC | Supported mineral categories, suitability and known handling requirements |
| Exposed-node extraction | 60 active minutes; STR or WIS/Mining, chosen before work; exact source DC or selected tier ladder | The selected finite mineral allocation |

Prospecting Tools can assist all three when their sampling, measurement or selection is necessary. Gem Cutters can assist compatible extraction and actual scoring/examination during assay. Mining Tools can assist actual screening/collection and sorting/examination during assay. Mining Picks and Ore Extractors assist their physical extraction roles only. Read each body's limits; a compatible role is necessary even when its printed bonus is large.

Prospecting and assay use Core task degrees and natural shifts. Critical results create no deposit, extra quantity, preparation or hidden detection. Information failure supplies no reliable new finding; prospecting Critical Failure adds only a hazard declared before work. Repeating unchanged evidence, renaming a patch or changing worker supplies no new roll. New exposure or a concretely different evidence-bearing method can support another attempt. A destructive assay needs an actual sample debit; nondestructive examination creates no inventory. Assay grants neither magical identification nor a guaranteed price, and does not replace required creature-type knowledge or specialist work.

Extraction admits one predeclared exposed, physically workable parcel up to 50 lb gross handled matter including gangue and 1 ft³. All selected baseline, damaged units and claimed reserve must fit the workload and receiving plan. Larger, structural, inaccessible, hazardous or incompatible work needs its own profile. The default gives five minutes setup, fifty for actual removal and five for sorting and receipt. It promises no excavation rate or tunnel.

Use a governing exact extraction profile when available. Otherwise the highest desired selected material sets the DC: I 12, II 13, III 15, IV 16, V 18, VI 19. Unknown tier needs an actual governing DC; this creates no higher-tier permission. Partial work takes ceil(60 × selected accessible categories ÷ all accessible categories), minimum one minute. This is the whole duration, including necessary setup. Hurry by halving that applicable duration and rounding up, with disadvantage, only if the actual work still fits. A separately authorized remains/removal profile keeps its own time. One trained assistant working the full information or extraction task can supply +1; the assistant's item magic does not transfer.

## Record and resolve the deposit

The Director records the actual parcel, exposure evidence, physical units, masses, categories, hazards and access before selection. Its finite ordinary yield comes from its real node profile, with an explicit source die rolled once if applicable. A deposit does not inherit creature-size dice. Use its exact material units. Where none exists, one ordinary mineral unit is 1 lb, with lesser baseline quantities retained to 0.01 lb. This accounting fallback invents neither tier, properties, price nor catalog ingredient identity. Additional-unit benefits require complete predeclared units; a 0.40-lb remainder is not one extra pound.

Keep disjoint undamaged baseline, damaged-but-potentially-recoverable baseline, ordinary recovery reserve and exceptional drops. Damaged and undamaged baseline are parts of the original base pool, not separate yield rolls. Blocked material is outside the accessible selection. No physical portion occupies two groups. Set baseline and reserve from the actual operation before equipment choice or the die result.

Select accessible categories, recovery preferences and a preparation-target order before commitment. Hidden units use a neutral preregistered order; stable unit IDs break remaining ties. Starting a partial attempt forfeits its unselected accessible categories and their reserve/damaged allocations. It does not forfeit previously blocked material outside that parcel. Zero accessible categories means no extraction attempt or division by zero.

On Success, recover only eligible selected undamaged baseline. On Critical Success, also preserve at most one actual selected damaged baseline unit and the card's strongest independently eligible critical-only reserve allowance. The public cards with that allowance offer one real reserve unit; absence of reserve gives zero, with no saved credit or exceptional-drop substitute. The source prose omitted this one-unit clause for recipe-347, recipe-349, recipe-350 and recipe-656; its critical-only trigger is a **NEW operational completion** beside their preserved printed text.

Failure recovers nothing and ruins the highest-value selected ordinary unit, including selected reserve in the failure allocation. Known equal-value ties allow the player to choose. If unknown values prevent identifying the highest, follow the fixed neutral order without inventing a price. Critical Failure ruins the selected ordinary allocation and applies the declared hazard; absent one, the lead takes 1d4 bludgeoning. If the whole selection becomes unavailable before resolution, record a terminal no-check outcome with actual losses and labor.

The claim closes once. Later processing of a recovered gem is not new extraction. A new attempt requires a genuinely unattempted disjoint parcel or documented newly accessible material, with cause, time and remaining membership. Another worker, Watch, tool or name cannot restore destroyed, extracted or forfeited units. Smelting, refining, commercial cutting and manufacture need their own actual procedures.

## Mineral preparation

A card with a preparation step may, on successful extraction including Critical Success, advance one eligible recovered ordinary mineral unit or real predeclared portion up to 1 lb from Rough to Dressed. Follow the prior order and skip newly ineligible units. The step completes ten minutes of feasible nonhazardous superficial dry dressing inside the extraction, removing loose surface gangue and preparing handling/inspection. Record actual removed waste and retained mass.

A capable worker can perform the same mundane operation in ten active minutes without a roll using compatible Miner's tools, two hands and a stable accessible surface. It is not a compulsory new tax on existing ingredients, transferable labor credit or recipe-time reduction. The operation repairs no fracture, restores no missing mass, neutralizes no hazard, pauses no age and changes no tier, rarity, grade, value, Freshness or ingredient identity. Already Dressed, exceptional, oversized indivisible or incompatible units receive no step and no substitute reward.
