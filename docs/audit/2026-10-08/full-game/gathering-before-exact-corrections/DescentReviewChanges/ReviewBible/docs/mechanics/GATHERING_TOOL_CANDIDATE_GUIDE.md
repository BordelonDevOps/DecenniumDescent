# Gathering tools — shared candidate rules

**Authored playtest candidate; exact review pending.** Use this guide with the Mining, Skinning or Salvaging candidate guide and the exact recipe card. These tools are not yet approved for normal campaign selection. The existing [crafting and harvesting framework](CRAFTING_HARVEST_PLAYTEST.md) supplies its unchanged general rules. Each card distinguishes the printed source effect from its operational completion.

## Set up one real task

Record the governing procedure, actual source object or deposit, lead, credential, chosen ability, tools, material selection, ordered recovery preferences, receiving containers, time, difficulty and hazards before work. The Director establishes the physical manifest and any hidden information. A recipe ingredient name establishes a requirement; it does not establish where that ingredient can be found, the anatomy of a donor, permission to harvest it, or its availability for sale.

Mining uses Miner's tools and Strength or Wisdom for extraction. Skinning uses Skinner's tools and Dexterity or Wisdom. Salvaging uses Salvager's tools and Intelligence or Wisdom. These are non-artisan credentials. Owning a tool supplies neither proficiency nor trade levels, formulas or free supporting equipment. Use the current lead's chosen ability and one applicable proficiency contribution; expertise replaces that contribution. Do not add trade level to the die. Artisan-only proficiency choices and the Forge Dwarf artisan creation benefits do not apply.

An ordinary permitted unranked extraction can be attempted untrained with no tool PB and disadvantage. Dismantling and restoration require the actual trade credential. Creation has the framework's separate untrained level-1–4 exception and formula requirements. A higher-level finished tool does not impose its creation level on an otherwise qualified user.

## Operate the tools

Before commitment, register at most one magical candidate per physical family. Family identities are trade-specific: a Skinning kit is not a Salvaging kit. The lead must operate each candidate in necessary compatible work for at least one active minute, or throughout a separately authorized task genuinely shorter than one minute. Setup, carrying it, touching it briefly or an assistant's operation does not qualify. Each credited six seconds of active work requires the worker's Action, necessary hands, access, support and workspace. Work intervals and hands cannot be counted twice.

At qualifying work and resolution, each candidate must be complete, positive-HP, accessible, unsuppressed, unmelded and participating. Check the current state of every registered candidate. A weaker eligible candidate can replace an ineligible stronger candidate under the relevant comparison; do not reroll. No new candidate enters after commitment, and later repairs or ended suppression do not revise a completed result. Ordinary indispensable implements remain necessary even when an enchanted candidate loses eligibility.

Compare each printed benefit within its own named family. Mining and Skinning compare their check bonus, extra recovery and preparation independently; qualifying operation must cover the selected ordinary allocation, including claimed reserve. Salvaging compares the check bonus once, then recovery eligibility for each entire declared component-identity pool. An assistant's equipment never lends its magical bonus to the lead. Other class, spell and equipment rules keep their actual predicates; these comparisons are not a universal stacking rule.

All finished bodies are durable magical nonartifact tools of Standard equipment grade, without attunement, charges, RP, concentration or a separate magical activation. Their structural AC protects the object, not its bearer. Names, tags and historical effects grant no further powers. The body catalogue gives fixed dimensions, weight, support and receiving limits. Contents count toward real capacity. Sequential handling is possible only when it fits the actual work; it supplies neither extra throughput nor a second roll. Positive HP cannot compensate for a missing required part.

## Preserve the history

Reserve actual matter, equipment and worker time. Cancellation before commitment releases only unspent reservations; already performed labor and damage remain. First extraction or irreversible dismantling work commits the single claim. A pause keeps that claim and its recorded progress unless an actual event undoes work. Decay, protection expiry and other elapsed effects continue. A replacement lead needs an accepted record and a genuinely feasible complete lead workflow on the same unresolved task; impossible repeated removals cannot satisfy it.

Success, failure, deliberate abandonment or a terminal no-check outcome closes the selected claim. Record actual surviving residue and losses. Changing owner, tool, label, container or trade never renews it. Newly exposed disjoint geological material can qualify under Mining's specific procedure; a carcass and dismantled residue have no corresponding renewal.

Removal, calculated entitlement and physical receipt are distinct facts. All recovered units need real access, fitting receivers and custody records. Preserve original identities or explicit descendant portions; never count both a parent and all its children at full quantity. An origin restriction, disappearance rule or no-resource condition survives later transfer and transformation. A real ordinary deceased companion may have lawful remains if its actual source permits them; that grants no living-removal, regrowth or summoned-resource exception.

Keep actual death/removal time, unprotected age, condition, contamination and containment. Completion, preparation, repacking, trade, manufacture and repair do not reset clocks. A genuine protection process changes only what its own rule allows. Corrected records retain their predecessor and supporting evidence. Dry intact ordinary mineral or bone storage does not authorize active-core or essence containment.

## Create an enchanted tool

Use the exact card's batch quantity Q, difficulty, components, tools, station, active hours, field flag, time mode, assistant limit, discovery and advancement. Public access to a formula does not give ownership or training. Required source component occurrences remain separate even when their names repeat. A tool marked consumed is spent; a retained tool is capital. Printed ingredients and exact required lots must be verified before work. Where an ingredient's detailed profile is not public, the Director verifies the actual stock against the preserved formula; its printed mention grants no acquisition right.

Reserve floor(1.5 × Q) actual complete eligible mundane bodies in a fixed order before work. Every reserved body needs positive HP, required members and its true history, including any existing flaw or structural improvement; it cannot have a prior magical alteration. It cannot simultaneously serve as a consumed ingredient, working toolkit or another order's reserve. Validate all reserved bodies, even those a lower result would leave unused. Repair or lawful replacement must occur before the roll, in the same recorded position. Transform the first bodies selected by the result; do not buy or select extra bodies after seeing it.

The first fifteen active minutes are included in printed hours. Only genuinely incorporated eligible inputs stabilize; other and returned stock keeps its own clocks and containment. Declare later phases of at most eight active hours. Interruption loses unfinished-phase progress while completed phases, spent material and elapsed history remain. Preserve the two-work-Watch limit and the framework's work/rest restrictions. Expedition-mode work needs the actual fortified camp or research station; a true field flag does not waive that requirement, and a false flag remains binding. Setup outside the stated work allowance costs real additional time.

Make one creation check using the framework. Each permitted assistant must meet its requirements and work all required hours; assistance gives neither a time reduction nor assistant XP. These tools do not improve their own creation, research, copying or repair. Ordinary tools and stations give +0 unless an independent reviewed rule grants a scoped modifier.

| Result | Finished bodies | Additional disposition |
|---|---:|---|
| Masterwork, margin at least +10 | floor(1.5Q), or Q with 50% structural improvement | Retain at most one real eligible catalyst unit in the entire batch, when the card lists one |
| Exceptional, margin +5 to +9 | floor(1.25Q), or Q with 25% structural improvement | Choose one option for the batch |
| Standard, margin 0 to +4 | Q | Keep existing lawful structural history |
| Flawed, margin −1 to −4 | floor(Q/2) when Q ≥ 2; one when Q = 1 | Only a singleton original Q adds one persistent flaw |

Natural 20 makes no additional creation-band shift. Yield increases use actual reserved bodies. A reduced multi-output batch adds no singleton flaw merely because one item results. A result changes neither check bonus, recovery ceiling, preparation, capacity, equipment grade nor source permissions. Failed recovery cannot return fractional indivisible tools, vessels or parts, and cannot convert price into matter. Skinning's actual oils, tanning solution and salts use source-unit precision of 0.01; other materials require their governing physical precision. No eligible catalyst means no Masterwork return. Returned stock keeps its identity, age and containment; workpiece-only protection ends when it leaves.

Crafting XP, first-craft awards, triviality and research follow the existing framework and exact source progression. Prospecting, harvesting, preparation, preservation, dismantling and repair gain no crafting award through a recipe's per-success field.

## Structural condition and restoration

Each output model has ordinary maximum HP B = 5. Use its strongest lawful structural percentage P, never the sum: floor(5 × (1 + P/100)). Apply one persistent flaw last by halving and flooring, minimum maximum HP 1. At P = 0/25/50, maxima are 5/6/7, or 2/3/3 with a flaw. Preserve prior damage deficit when maximum changes: new current HP = max(0, new maximum − (old maximum − old current HP)). Standard does not heal. A 1/5 singleton that becomes Flawed finishes 0/2 and Broken. One aggregate track is not 5 HP per constituent.

Functional repair requires the actual trained trade credential, chosen trade ability, functional required tools, suitable worksite and real compatible material. DC 10; time max(1 hour, recorded maximum HP ÷ 10); material reference 25% of the mundane body reference. Broken reconstruction uses the original recipe DC, max(8 hours, recorded maximum HP ÷ 10), and 25% of the finished-unit reference. All required original primary members and any additional irreplaceable members recorded at creation must survive. Only permitted subordinate replacements can use new compatible stock. The target cannot supply its own missing repair tools.

Use Core natural shifts and collapse the result to success or failure. Success restores only the recorded lawful maximum and function; failure spends time and material. Restoration grants no formula, creation-level gate, assistants, quality, XP, removed flaw or reopened claim. A terminally dismantled object is not a Broken repair candidate. A suppressed enchanted body remains magical.

With V = original recommended batch value and C = mundane body reference, finished-unit reference = V/Q + C. Broken reconstruction materials = 0.25 × (V/Q + C), with the whole sum inside parentheses. Keep exact fractions until the final actual transaction: purchase costs round up and sale proceeds down to copper. Owned bodies do not require repurchase. Reusable tools, actual stock, special handling, operating costs, labor commissions and willing buyers remain separate. There is no automatic quality resale or artifact multiplier.
