## Weapon benefits

An intact, unsuppressed weapon adds its printed recipe attack bonus only to an attack actually made with that physical weapon. Legal melee, off-hand and thrown attacks qualify. Carrying it gives no general attack bonus. Ordinary proficiency, ability, damage dice, reach, range, properties and action costs remain those of the selected model and the actual attack procedure.

The recipe damage bonus applies **once** to the attack's ordinary weapon-damage component on a hit. It still applies when the actual off-hand rule omits ordinary ability damage. It is not multiplied across damage types. If a reviewed effect changes that component's damage type, the flat bonus follows the actual resulting component. Flat bonuses never double on a critical. Legal attacks on objects qualify only where the weapon and actual targeting/damage rules permit them.

An unsuppressed Blacksmith weapon's ordinary bludgeoning, piercing or slashing damage counts as magical for defenses that distinguish magical damage. This does not change its damage type or confer spell rank, spellcasting, Counterspell eligibility, RP, concentration, attunement or an activation Action. Source elemental tags alone grant no elemental damage.

**New item-accuracy family:** the preserved V6 weapon-grade attack modifier and the recipe's attack bonus use the stronger value rather than adding. A Fine +1 grade weapon with recipe +2 uses +2 item accuracy. An Exceptional +3 grade weapon with recipe +1 uses +3 item accuracy and the recipe's +1 damage. Superior/Exceptional grade critical dice remain separate because they are not flat accuracy modifiers.

Other class, spell or item bonuses follow their actual reviewed combination rules. This family is not a universal strongest-bonus rule. No generic second recipe imprint can stack onto the body. Source names such as Superior, Masterwork or Legendary do not grant an equipment grade. The actual supplied mundane base retains its legal grade; a new mundane base without a different established grade is Standard.

Evaluate only real applicable modifiers. Crude weapon grade −1 and active recipe +1 use +1; suppressing the recipe leaves the actual −1. There is no phantom Standard +0 that cancels a remaining penalty. Broken items supply no normal weapon function to enhance. A separately legal improvised use does not recover the item's ordinary weapon or Blacksmith bonuses.

## Armor, shields and defensive enhancement

The printed recipe AC bonus is an **enhancement**, not a replacement for the entire base armor formula or shield bonus. Keep the selected suit's normal formula, full or capped DEX modifier, Strength requirement, Stealth rule, proficiency, fitting, weight and don/doff. Keep a shield's actual hands, Strength, proficiency, weight and don/doff requirements.

**New `equipment.defense-enhancement` family:** all active Blacksmith recipe AC bonuses and the applicable V6 worn-armor grade AC modifier use the strongest single value. They do not add together. The base shield's normal +1/+2/+3 is separate from this enhancement family.

- A suit's recipe enhancement qualifies when that suit supplies the worn-armor AC formula actually being used.
- A shield's recipe enhancement qualifies when it is the one usable shield contributing its ordinary shield bonus.
- One worn, intact and unsuppressed Helm and one Gauntlets pair can each supply a candidate enhancement.
- The actual worn suit's grade modifier qualifies under its normal armor rules.

Independent class, spell, ancestry and natural/unarmored rules retain their own wording. A suit's unused formula cannot contribute its recipe bonus as though it were an accessory. The new accessories do not themselves count as armor suits.

An ordinary Shield with recipe-53 contributes its base +2 and, if strongest, its recipe +1, for a total +3. If a higher eligible defensive enhancement is already present, the shield still supplies its base +2, while its lower enhancement adds nothing. Actual Tower shield requirements remain in force when that model is chosen; crafting does not waive them.

Resistance is a distinct printed benefit. An item's lower AC enhancement can be inactive while its printed resistance remains active under its own wearing requirements. Different resistance types coexist. Multiple resistance sources to the same type do not become immunity. No title, ingredient or metadata tag grants resistance absent the current effect.

Each lawfully donned, intact and unsuppressed shield supplies its printed resistance even if another shield supplies ordinary shield AC. Only the selected AC-contributing shield offers its recipe enhancement. Every donned shield still occupies its actual hand/forearm and retains its Strength, Stealth, proficiency and other drawbacks. A back-slung, carried, stored or dropped shield is not donned and supplies neither benefit. Same-type resistance still does not become immunity.

Shield bases default to Standard. An actual preexisting shield with an independently reviewed grade retains only the properties and valuation that its actual prior rule established. Suit-only grade AC, weight or HP rules are not imported onto shields. Blacksmithing creates no shield-grade entitlement.

The family considers actual modifiers only: a Crude suit's −1 and active recipe +1 contribute +1; suppressing the recipe leaves −1. No implicit +0 cancels it. At Broken, the item's normal positive armor/shield function ceases; the physical drawbacks remain rather than evaluating enhancement arithmetic for a function the item no longer supplies.

## Craft quality and preexisting damage

Resolve the crafting framework's six outcomes, natural-1 Mishap and numeric margins. Natural 20 grants no extra band shift. Mundane nonmagical, non-Broken bases may already have a reviewed crafting durability increase or persistent singleton-Flawed maximum. Use one noncompounding calculation:

1. B is ordinary maximum HP after the actual model, size and equipment-grade HP rule, including Crude suit HP where applicable.
2. P is the strongest legally attached positive crafting durability percentage: 0, 25% or 50%. A new structural option replaces P only if stronger; it never multiplies an already improved maximum.
3. ImprovedMax = B + floor(B × P), with a minimum +1 increase when P>0.
4. If a persistent singleton-Flawed state exists, FinalMax = max(1, floor(ImprovedMax / 2)); otherwise FinalMax = ImprovedMax. A new singleton Flawed result establishes that one state, never a second halving.
5. Preserve DamageDeficit = OldMax − OldCurrent. NewCurrent = max(0, FinalMax − DamageDeficit), capped at FinalMax.

Standard preserves the existing modifiers and damage. A 3/5 weapon gaining +1 maximum becomes 4/6, not 6/6. A B20 base with +25% durability and a persistent flaw has max12. Replacing that increase with +50% produces max15; it still has the flaw and preserves its damage deficit. A badly damaged base may emerge Broken after a new Flawed result.

For Q≥2, Flawed changes output quantity to floor(Q/2), not unit potency or existing durability modifiers. For Q1 it establishes the one persistent flaw as above. # Blacksmithing exact-review cases

All 43 cards are drafts implementing the source and eight-interface conferences. These are tabletop rule traces and authored-projection checks, not live companion simulations or numerical balance approval.

1. **Real body reserve:** recipe-45 Q5 with Battleaxe requires seven actual bases before work. Standard transforms ordered entries 1–5; Masterwork yield transforms 1–7; Exceptional yield transforms 1–6; Flawed transforms 1–2; Failed/Mishap transforms none. Base references are 10 gp and 4 lb each at Standard Small/Medium/Tiny. The 4.18 lb of source ingredients never substitutes for bodies.

2. **Interrupted reserve:** reserved entry 2 becomes Broken before completion. Pause before rolling. Repair it or replace that exact entry with another eligible same-model/size/grade base; record its own real material/HP/modifiers. No reordering after the outcome to avoid a damaged output. Base bodies stay separate from source 25% failure-recovery arithmetic.

3. **Model restrictions:** recipe-628 uses Scale mail, not freely chosen Plate. Recipe-631 uses Breastplate. Generic Weapon admits Dart and Kama, but no arrows, bows, crossbows, natural weapons, whips or nunchaku. Specific Sword/Hammer/Axe/Mace/Spear families use their complete approved finite lists.

4. **Grade versus enhancement:** Fine weapon grade +1 with recipe +2 gives +2 item accuracy and recipe +2 damage. Exceptional grade +3 with recipe +1 gives +3 accuracy and +1 damage; its actual extra grade critical dice remain. Crude −1 with active recipe +1 uses +1; suppression leaves −1, never an imaginary +0.

5. **One damage event:** an ordinary weapon attack with a printed 2d4 rider adds those elemental dice to that same attack event, applying actual defenses by type. A critical doubles the rider dice to 4d4 as attack dice and doubles ordinary eligible weapon dice; it does not double the flat recipe bonus or the extra grade dice that are added once after doubling. No extra hit or Wound event is manufactured.

6. **Actual turn budgets:** physical weapons A and B each may spend their own elemental rider on a turn. Transferring A after its use does not reset A. Another Action on that same turn grants no new A budget. A genuine extra turn or a legal Reaction on a different creature's actual turn has a distinct turn budget. A damage copy does not proc A again.

7. **Outside windows:** a first out-of-initiative attack opens the item's six-second action window. Additional legal attacks in that window share its one optional rider use. An attack that starts combat goes directly through lawful initiative entry and claims no prior outside budget. Entry after a recent outside trigger and exit after a recent inside trigger retain the six-second transition protection and last-trigger timestamp.

8. **First-turn guard — submitted for exact precision:** the follow-up specifies the item's first actual turn before the deadline as already spent. The draft retains that wording rather than silently changing it to first attempted attack. Please explicitly confirm whether the first actual turn immediately after transition satisfies the guard even if that item makes no attack in that turn, or whether the guard attaches to the first actual turn in which use is attempted. Also confirm that outside windows stay anchored to the initially recorded window origin (elapsed-time window index), rather than a new attack roll restarting an early window. This is the one deliberately retained pending machine binding.

9. **One defense-enhancement family:** a Fine suit's +1 grade, a +1 Blacksmith helm and a +2 Blacksmith shield enhancement contribute one strongest +2 enhancement. The chosen shield's ordinary base AC remains separate. Independent class/spell bonuses use their actual rules. A weaker accessory enhancement may still supply its printed resistance.

10. **Two shields cost actual hands:** two legally donned shields can supply different printed resistances, but only the selected usable shield supplies ordinary shield AC and offers its recipe enhancement. Both retain actual occupation/STR/Stealth/nonproficiency effects. A shield slung on the back has neither wearing benefit. Two same-type resistances never become immunity.

11. **Accessories:** one gauntlet output is one matched pair at 4 lb, 10 gp, HP5 and material AC19. It takes one Action to don or doff the pair. It does not occupy the hands, grant extra free hands, supply unarmed damage or count as a suit of armor. One helm is 5 lb, 10 gp, HP5, AC19. Large doubles price/weight/ordinary HP for both, not AC or bonus magnitude.

12. **Persistent durability:** B20 with +25% and a flaw has max12. Starting at current9 gives deficit3. A Masterwork structural option replaces +25% with +50%, producing improved30, then flawed max15/current12. The flaw persists. Another Flawed outcome does not halve the already flawed maximum again. Repeating a weaker improvement cannot multiply durability.

13. **Badly damaged singleton:** a B5 mundane base at current1 has deficit4. A singleton Flawed outcome gives max2/current0, so the finished item emerges Broken. Its recoverable imprint is dormant. Repair cannot remove the flaw, and no refund or second output occurs.

14. **Suppression:** an improved or Flawed item keeps physical current/max HP, material AC, size, grade and deficits while suppressed. Only recipe combat functions/classification stop. Suppression/unsuppression does not reopen a spent turn budget or heal damage.

15. **Repair access and price:** recipe-42 on a Standard Medium Longsword has 92+15=107 gp finished reference. HP-positive repair materials use 25% of 15 gp = 3.75 gp; HP0 reconstruction uses 25% of 107 gp = 26.75 gp. Both require actual Smith's proficiency and Forge/Anvil/Tongs. The intact-body repair DC is 10; reconstruction uses the original crafting DC12. The repairer needs neither the original recipe trade level nor its formula, and learns no formula from repair. No creation-only Forge Dwarf reduction, crafting XP or catalyst applies.

16. **Rational batch value:** recipe-53 source 94 gp/Q5 gives 18.8 gp per unit plus actual base value. An ordinary Shield adds 10 gp for 28.8 gp. Large changes the base portion to 20 gp, not the source share. Owned bases cause no purchase; sold magic items need an actual quote rather than automatic mundane resale percentages.

Required assistant participation follows actual modified work. Only incorporated components stabilize; loose reserves keep their own age. Field timeMode never overrides fieldCraftable:false.

18. **Repeated catalysts:** recipe-80 lists three consumed green dragon heartstone occurrences. Three actual units are needed. Masterwork may return at most one eligible unit total; source IDs/names do not merge the other two into a reusable item. Ingredient tags, donor lists, wholeCreatureUse and optional Director guidance grant no unstated powers or access.
