## 2. Research qualification

Research remains an **activity/project**, not a 21st skill. Matrix research preserves the Core requirement that one primary researcher personally possess high ranks in **Arcana, History, Religion, Nature, and Engineering**. For this candidate, “high ranks” means two separate predicates for each discipline:

1. current proficiency in the skill; and
2. an owned **Advanced Qualification mark** for that discipline.

Five bare proficiencies do not satisfy the gate.

A bounded discipline finding may be led before Full Matrix Lead. The lead needs the declared skill proficiency throughout every active interval of that finding, authenticated evidence, a preregistered discipline question, and a supported result. Two completed Equation-relevant findings in the same discipline from two different underlying evidence identities earn that discipline’s Advanced mark. Existing Expertise in that exact skill also satisfies the mark route. Marks grant no numerical bonus.

Full Matrix Lead exists only while the **same individual** owns all five marks and currently has all five proficiencies. Helpers cannot pool marks or proficiencies.

Knowledge Transfer transfers no marks or Expertise, but its actual card can supply one skill proficiency for one hour. Therefore a researcher who already owns all five marks can personally qualify during a lawful loan window if that loan supplies the only missing current proficiency. Qualification ends when the loan ends.

The four-hour Matrix Synthesis requirement is **240 recorded active work minutes**. It may pause. Every active interval records qualification, evidence, lead, assistants, start/stop and clock conflicts. Rest, travel, guard duty, recasting, recharge and other incompatible activity do not count. Four one-hour Knowledge Transfer windows can supply four synthesis hours only if every real activation, resource cost, short-rest recovery and elapsed-time requirement is paid. One elapsed minute cannot be credited twice.

This does **not** alter the existing eight-active-hour Translate/Decipher project or create a generic permission to subdivide it.

## 4. Four-hour synthesis and fixed-DC resolution

Before the first active synthesis minute, fix the exact question, proposed finding, evidence identities/categories, lead skill, legal assistance/modifiers, DC, and truthful four-degree outcomes. If a current rule prints a DC, use it. Otherwise this candidate uses the supported difficulty of the evidence/question; insufficient or nondiagnostic evidence has **no legal DC** that lets a high roll invent an answer.

Use the current Core fixed-DC task rule:

- total **>= DC+10**: Critical Success;
- total **>= DC**: Success;
- total **<= DC-10**: Critical Failure;
- otherwise below DC: Failure;
- then a **natural 20 improves one degree** and a **natural 1 worsens one degree**, bounded at the ends.

Ordinary saving throws remain binary meet-DC/fail; natural 1/20 do not generically shift saves.

Examples at DC15:

- roll 12 + 3 = 15 -> **Success**;
- natural 20 + 3 = 23 -> base Success, then natural 20 -> **Critical Success**;
- natural 1 + 14 = 15 -> base Success, then natural 1 -> **Failure**.

Success certifies only the preregistered supported finding. Critical Success adds only a preregistered constraint/corroboration actually supported by evidence. Failure records truthful progress. Critical Failure can add only a separately predeclared lawful hazard. No result creates a missing Equation fragment.

A retry needs a concrete state change: new authenticated evidence, repaired/recovered content, a genuinely different supported method, or another expressly relevant change. Rephrasing the same question, changing copies/readers/date, or taking a break does not reset it.

## 5. Functional understanding and Prediction Tests

## 6. Knowledge Taint (KT)

KT is a distinct qualitative ledger. All names, stages, DCs and symptoms here are **NEW PROVISIONAL DESIGN**.

- **KT0 Clear:** no verified dangerous operational knowledge.
- **KT1 Resonant:** first verified dangerous operational subprinciple.
- **KT2 Intrusive:** first supported Functional Model connecting multiple operational subprinciples.
- **KT3 Compromised:** first Demonstrated Operational Understanding.

Equivalent verified knowledge obtained by another lawful route receives the corresponding KT state. Execution Attempt, Enforcement and Outside Enforcement do not advance KT merely because they occurred.

### KT1 symptom

After each completed legal long rest, create at most one pending symptom: disadvantage on the first eligible INT/WIS Matrix-research task check before the next long rest. It is consumed by that check. Before consumption, **10 uninterrupted minutes of grounding** clears it. Grounding is deliberate quiet stabilization outside active Matrix research and outside qualifying exposure; it consumes real time and gives no free rest/synthesis/translation credit unless an exact rule expressly permits compatibility. No knowledge is lost.

### KT2 symptom

KT1 remains. In addition, after a failed Matrix Synthesis check the researcher cannot provide or receive generic research assistance for 10 uninterrupted minutes outside active synthesis/qualifying exposure. This has its own endpoint.

### KT3 symptom and episodes

Before one concrete Demonstrated Operational Understanding test or one concrete Hunt-capable operational-use commitment, make **one DC15 Wisdom save** before paying that operation’s costs. For a voluntary Hunt-capable act, present the Hunt warning first, then make this save, then resolve legal commitment/costs.

For this candidate, that save is mechanically an incoming **Corruption-effect resistance save**. Corruption Ward can grant its actual advantage while active.

On failure, apply Dazed **until the end of the creature’s next regular turn in initiative, or for 1 minute outside initiative**. Apply **actual Dazed only**: choose Action-mode or Movement-mode on the affected turn; no Bonus Action; Reactions and concentration are unaffected. There is no added inability to decide or unnamed mind-control effect. Dazed immunity blocks the symptom and leaves no substitute prohibition.

One save maximum per concrete episode. When Dazed ends, continuing the same unchanged operation does not reroll. A genuinely separate later operation/new target/function is a new episode. Arbitrary pauses cannot manufacture rerolls or suppress future distinct operations. The KT3 save is not itself a Hunt trigger.

## 7. Proximity/Voice Taint (PT)

PT is separate from KT. It is eligible only at **Descent level 5+** when an actual authored Old-One voice/proximity exposure source/scene qualifies. Deep location alone is insufficient.

One continuous qualifying source/contact period is one PT episode. Do not create repeated ticks by dividing turns/minutes or rereading the same nonqualifying page/record.

At first eligible exposure in an episode, make one Wisdom save: **DC12 at PT0, 13 at PT1, 14 at PT2, 15 at PT3**. For this candidate, the incoming influence is mechanically a **Corruption effect**, so active Corruption Ward can grant its authored advantage. Success prevents stage advance for that episode. Failure advances one stage, maximum PT3.

- **PT1 Whispered:** one next INT/WIS task check has disadvantage; it is consumed by that check or clears after 10 uninterrupted minutes outside qualifying exposure.
- **PT2 Pressed:** (a) one next-check disadvantage endpoint as above; and separately (b) no research assistance until 10 uninterrupted minutes outside qualifying exposure. Consuming (a) does not end (b).
- **PT3 Saturated:** actual Dazed until end of next regular turn, or 1 minute outside initiative; separately, no research assistance until 10 uninterrupted minutes outside qualifying exposure. PT3 does **not** silently add a repeat next-check disadvantage.

Mitigation never erases learned facts or PT history.

### PT recovery

After one full legal long rest completed wholly outside qualifying PT exposure, make **one DC12 Wisdom recovery save**. Exposure before the rest began does not disqualify the rest; qualifying exposure during it does. Success reduces current PT one stage; failure changes nothing. One completed rest grants at most one roll. This recovery save is not an incoming Corruption effect, so the provisional Corruption Ward classification does not grant advantage on it.

### Default voice/proximity encounter

At level 5+, character A begins PT1 and enters an authored qualifying chamber while Corruption Ward has 30 minutes remaining. Create one episode. PT1 means DC13 WIS; Corruption Ward grants advantage because the incoming effect is candidate-tagged Corruption. Suppose A’s better die plus WIS totals12: failure. A becomes PT2. Record two endpoints: next INT/WIS check disadvantage and no research assistance. Remaining in the same chamber creates no second save. After leaving and spending 10 uninterrupted minutes outside exposure, both still-active PT2 endpoints clear; PT2 history remains. After a later full legal long rest wholly outside exposure, A receives one DC12 PT recovery save.

Do not reveal unearned cosmological ownership or hunter identity.
