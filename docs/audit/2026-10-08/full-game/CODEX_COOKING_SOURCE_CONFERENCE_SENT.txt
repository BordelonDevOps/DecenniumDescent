# Cooking source conference — all 44 formulas

Herbalism's four exact-review repairs are applied and all 43 checks pass. Its 45 formulas are promoted. Recipe progress is 90 reviewed out of 687, with 597 remaining. No source was deleted or pushed. Cosmology stays deferred until the authorized GitHub push succeeds and its remote commit is verified.

I captured all 44 Cooking records and exact matching V5 effects for all 30 public entries. Two restricted and 12 Director-only entries remain internal. Please resolve the following operational gaps as finite provisional authoring decisions. I will then author all 44 cards for exact review. This conference does not itself approve those unwritten cards, numerical balance or runtime behavior.

1. **Consumption and one feast at a time.** Propose one actual serving per recipient, requiring ten uninterrupted active minutes. In initiative require 100 consecutive regular-turn Actions and at least 600 elapsed seconds, with no 101st Action. The recipient must have a compatible ingestion route and be willing, or Incapacitated with a helper and actual ability to swallow. Spend the serving at legal start; no magical benefit until completion. Interruption loses the portion but leaves the old feast unchanged. At completion the recipient may accept the entire new feast or forego its magic while retaining actual food consumption. Accepting atomically ends the old feast and its remaining linked temporary HP/riders, then offers new temporary HP against any independent grant and resolves the new rider. A rejected shield does not block an independent rider. No accepting RP while rejecting feast replacement. All are magical nonspell consumables with no generic RP/RC cost, attunement, focus, class or proficiency requirement to eat.

2. **Durations and rider scope.** For 81–120, 632, 634 and 635, source `mechanics.duration` supplies the parent feast and temporary-HP endpoint: next completed beneficial Long Rest. An explicit one-/four-hour rider in complete prose gets its own shorter deadline. Unspecified Athletics, CON, Stealth, Flora and first-charm/fear-save riders remain until the parent ends. Recipe-633 instead prints eight hours in its mechanics: propose that its whole feast, including temporary HP, lasts eight hours even through a Long Rest. Depleted/replaced temporary HP does not end independent riders, while accepting another feast does. The Psychic rider is consumed by the first actual qualifying save even if another advantage already applies. Unqualified CON-save bonuses include actual concentration/Wound CON saves; do not import Herbalism's narrower Vitality hazard rule. Athletics/Stealth bonuses apply their actual named skill. Settle whether +10 movement means walking only or all existing modes; propose walking only as a new explicit integration. Swimming speed follows effective walking speed dynamically, without water breathing or creating a walking mode. Prone advantage applies existing checks/saves against knockdown, not automatic immunity or standing up. Please correct any source conflict.

3. **Resonance recovery.** Recipes 88 and 103 each restore one expended RP, capped at an actual existing RP maximum. No RC, new pool, Dissonance or Refrain reset. The source prints no once-per-rest limit. Propose that every genuinely paid, completed ten-minute serving can restore one RP when the recipient accepts that new feast. Ingredients and food are finite costs; replaying a record or a partial serving cannot retrigger. Do not silently import Alchemy's source-specific Long Rest limit. If notebook evidence supplies another cap, identify it; any newly chosen cap needs an explicit provisional label.

4. **Serving mass, nutrition and packaging.** Source quantities mean servings, not whole parties. Some input totals are too small for a full meal: recipe-634 consumes only 0.39 lb. The packet includes all 44 mass calculations. We need a concrete finished-food weight, supplementary edible staple/carrier rule sufficient for maximum Masterwork yield, containers and finite nutrition. Reviewed equipment: one daily ration weighs 2 lb and provides food only; water weighs 1 lb per pint. Supply also includes water, fuel and camp consumables. Small/Medium characters use one Supply per day and Large characters two; the detailed food/water-to-Supply bill remains open. Propose one serving as one ordinary meal, worth one-third of a Small/Medium daily food requirement and half that fraction for Large. Please choose a precise ordinary-food conversion consistent with the 2-lb daily ration. Water, salt and inert components cannot create nutrition; recipe ingredients may count only when genuinely edible, and extra food must be paid. Never count the same ration twice or call one meal full Supply. Wet foods need an actual container. A reusable one-serving meal pot could be a new 0.5-lb, 2-sp wooden/ceramic item, but please settle its capacity, material AC and HP explicitly. Existing mess kits and returned recipe pots must not be charged twice. Reserve maximum-yield packaging and carrier before the check.

5. **Manufacture and shelf life.** Propose a first 15-active-minute cooking/binding phase included in source work, with actual heat and Cook's utensils. Perishables stay legal until the phase completes. The bound work order is stable only for that order, not a raw-ingredient preservation service. Cook's utensils are artisan tools, so actual Forge Dwarf scope can apply; month-long source work and facility gates remain. Finished meals need a food clock, not automatically Alchemy's indefinite bottle storage: propose 24 elapsed hours fresh, then Spoiled with no nutrition or magic. Existing mundane food preservation can pause age through its real cost/check. Opening never resets age. A legal ten-minute meal may begin before expiry and finish after it because the entire portion is already committed, as with Herbalism; no reusable partial serving. Successful cooking neutralizes unprinted incidental ingredient hazards, with recipe-633's explicit failed-neutralization exception. Names add no drug dependence.

6. **Quality and whole-creature success.** Generic completion uses natural 1 = Mishap and natural 20 = numeric result. Propose that the critical success in 632/634/635 means actual Masterwork margin at least 10, not every natural 20 or Forge Dwarf's Standard-to-Exceptional output improvement. That source-specific trait rider accompanies the normal quality choice and catalyst return; it is one trait per actual serving. For quantities at least two, Flawed halves quantity. For a singleton with an until-Long-Rest parent, propose Failed even if a secondary rider has a fixed shorter duration; otherwise the whole meal has no coherent half-duration profile. Recipe-633's eight-hour parent could normally halve, but its explicit failed-poison clause may override. Container durability improves the physical container only, not temporary HP, creature HP, charges or potency. Please settle these boundaries.

7. **Failed Serpent Skewers, recipe-633.** Source gives six temporary HP and eight-hour poison-save advantage, but says a failed craft remains poisonous. Propose that any below-DC completion, including Flawed, or a natural 1 produces one contaminated serving with no benefits. This specific failed product replaces generic 25% material recovery; all consumed inputs are spent once. Mishap also resolves its actual mishap. No keeping the meal and recovering its ingredients. Missing exposure profile needs new authored details: after a whole serving is eaten over ten minutes, DC 13 CON; failure causes Poisoned for one hour, success none. No unprinted damage, coating use or arbitrary subdivision into many poison doses. The cook knows neutralization failed; no false safety certification or guaranteed resale value. Standard or better makes the beneficial meal. Please approve or correct failure bands, exposure onset, DC/duration and material accounting.

8. **Traits from whole-creature feasts.** The source promises one temporary trait associated with the donor monster, using multiple parts of one creature or ecology. It needs an operational procedure. Generated bestiary JSON is unreliable here: Brown Bear is Tiny CR 0 with Goring Horn; Cave Bear is Medium with Goring Horn; Blink Dog is Gargantuan CR 20; Awakened Shrub is Huge Celestial CR 12. The canon manifest instead names the approved Monster Manual V2 source pack. Its Cave Bear is Large, walking 40 ft., with Mauling Claws and Territorial Morale; no Keen Smell. Its Sporefolk Adult has poison resistance, Poisoned immunity and Living Substrate. Exact approved passages follow. Do not import familiar external monster statistics or adjudicate origins.

   Propose fixing actual donor provenance before crafting: consumed parts must come from the recorded creature or shared ecology; a catalog's possible-source list is not proof of the actual stock. Predeclare one complete eligible borrowed profile before the roll, chosen from a printed walking/climbing/swimming speed, one damage resistance, bounded ordinary sensory capability, or another fully specified passive trait. Exclude extra Actions, copied stats/HP, spellcasting, regeneration, resource creation, legendary/lair powers and life/identity effects unless separately reviewed. Trait lasts one hour or until the parent feast ends, whichever comes first. Cave Bear walking 40 ft. and Sporefolk poison resistance are real options for properly sourced 632/635; no invented Keen Smell. Recipe-634 requires its actual donor, not just its compressed possible-source list. If no eligible documented trait exists, should we author a recipe-specific fallback or make valid donor/trait provenance a preflight requirement for the special rider? Please provide a complete rule that preserves the promised trait without permitting arbitrary monster abilities. Wider bestiary corrections remain open.

9. **Preservation and other exceptions.** Keep all 44 identities, current/legacy effects, ingredients, requirements and visibility. Active finished recipe tags come only from current `mechanics.tag`; 633 has none. Its actual poison/save effects remain explicit despite that lack of a tag. Food and container costs are separate from immutable source economics; printed values remain Standard batch references with actual finite buyer/supplier quotes. Identify any other concrete recipe exception. Return finite authoring decisions for this family without expanding into cosmology.
