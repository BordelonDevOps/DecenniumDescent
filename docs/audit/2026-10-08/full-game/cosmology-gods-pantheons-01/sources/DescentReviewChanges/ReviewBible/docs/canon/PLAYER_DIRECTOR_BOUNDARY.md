# Player / Director Information Boundary

Decennium Descent is designed around **layered truth**. Player lore must be complete enough to support roleplay without exposing the deepest mechanism of the setting.

## Safe Player-Facing Truth

Players may know that:

- the Crucible opens every ten years for 100 days;
- the Horn occurs halfway through the Opening;
- Calan'thir and Asmodeus are credited with establishing the Crucible;
- the dungeon has shaped economies, wars, religions, scholarship, and family fortunes;
- goblin financiers and other institutions are major participants in expedition finance;
- the ancient anti-goblin crusade is historically disputed;
- mortal religions disagree about the planes, gods, creation, and the afterlife;
- strange architecture and records sometimes appear older than accepted Crucible history.

## Director-Only Truth

Do not state as ordinary player knowledge that:

- the Silent Ones and Old Ones are the same entities;
- the Silent Ones created the primordial Heartcell structure;
- Calan'thir and Asmodeus found and modified an older machine;
- the dungeon's deeper purpose is resonance generation/preservation;
- treasure is deliberately used as behavioral bait by the system;
- the complete Matrix of Oblivion describes the survival chain of reality;
- Unity is false resonance;
- goblins possess heightened metaphysical susceptibility to Unity;
- Moloch exploits that susceptibility through the Coinfather pattern.

These truths should be discovered through architecture, contradictions, terminology, consequences, and increasingly explicit evidence—not handed out in a setting primer.

## Revelation Ladder

1. **Anomaly:** evidence predates accepted history.
2. **Vocabulary:** unfamiliar titles, roles, and system terms recur.
3. **Contradiction:** the Pact appears to govern something it did not originate.
4. **Mechanism:** the Heartcell and Matrix become legible.

A revelation should deepen the public myth rather than make earlier player investment feel foolish.
