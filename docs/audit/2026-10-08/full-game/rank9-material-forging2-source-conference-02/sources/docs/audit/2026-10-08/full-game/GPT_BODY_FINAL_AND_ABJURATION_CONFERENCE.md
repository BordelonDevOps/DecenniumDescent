Twelve bounded operational proposals

Repelling Pulse — Source preserved: Action, 5-foot range, V/S, instantaneous; push a small object or creature 5 feet away. NEW PROVISIONAL: “small creature” is operationalized as a Small-or-Tiny creature. Target one such creature you can perceive within 5 feet, or one discrete unattended/unsecured object no larger than Small, weighing no more than 10 lb, within 5 feet. A creature makes a Strength save against your spell DC; on failure it is pushed 5 feet directly away from you, on success it does not move. An eligible unattended object is pushed automatically. The destination follows ordinary forced-movement legality: stop before solid terrain or an occupied illegal space; do not push another creature onward, knock it Prone, disarm anyone, make a free attack, or add collision damage. If the legal destination removes support, ordinary Falling begins under the existing falling rules rather than Repelling Pulse dealing damage itself. A worn, held, attached, restrained, locked, secured, or otherwise externally supported object is not an eligible object target. Scaling: none.

Sense Magic — Source preserved: Action, 10 feet, V/S, one round; detect the presence of magic. NEW PROVISIONAL: target Self. Until the start of your next regular turn, you know only whether at least one active magical aura or ongoing magical effect exists within 10 feet and line of effect. Total cover blocks this sense. It gives no direction, count, distance, owner, school, spell name, rank, item property, creature identity, hidden curse, dormant enchantment, alignment, or statistics. Mundane invisibility/nonmagical creatures produce nothing. This deliberately remains weaker than Aura Sight and Detect Magic: it is a brief yes/no presence sense, not aura vision. No concentration, no save, no attack. Scaling: none.

Warding Gesture — Source preserved: Action, 30 feet, V/S/F through the reviewed M/F→class-focus interpretation, concentration up to 1 minute; advantage on the next save against magic. NEW PROVISIONAL: target one willing creature you can perceive within 30 feet, including yourself. The target gains advantage on its next saving throw caused by a spell or effect explicitly classified as magical, then the benefit is consumed whether that save succeeds or fails. If no qualifying save occurs before concentration ends or 1 minute passes, it expires. Multiple Warding Gestures do not create multiple stored saves; a later one may refresh the endpoint but never grants two uses. A qualifying save consumes the permission even if some other effect already supplied advantage. Nonmagical hazards and ordinary weapon effects do not qualify. Scaling: none.

Barrier Flicker — This source has an internal conflict: its effect is a “momentary shield against projectiles, +2 AC vs one ranged attack,” while Core metadata simultaneously says Reaction, concentration up to 1 minute, and Spell attack. The effect text and the reviewed Shield comparison support a one-attack reactive ward, not a sustained concentration spell or offensive spell attack.

 NEW PROVISIONAL operational resolution: Reaction when one willing creature you can perceive within 30 feet, including yourself, becomes the target of a projectile-based ranged attack roll, after the attack is declared but before the roll is made. Components V/S. The protected creature gains +2 AC against that triggering attack only, then the spell ends. No concentration. Nullify Element — Source preserved: Action, 30 feet, V/S, one round; resistance to one element. NEW PROVISIONAL: target one willing creature you can perceive within 30 feet, including yourself. Choose acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder when cast, matching the already-reviewed Elemental Resistance damage-type list. The target gains resistance to that damage type until the start of your next regular turn. No concentration. Resistance never stacks with resistance to the same type and never becomes immunity. A later different Nullify Element on the same target replaces the prior chosen type rather than granting several simultaneous cantrip resistances. Scaling: none. This intentionally remains much shorter than the reviewed rank-1 Elemental Resistance.

Aura Sight — Source preserved: Action, 30 feet, V/S, one round; see magical auras on objects or creatures. NEW PROVISIONAL: target Self. Until the start of your next regular turn, visible/perceivable creatures and objects within 30 feet that currently bear an active magical aura are outlined to you as magical. You must already be able to perceive the underlying creature or object; Aura Sight does not reveal invisible creatures, concealed objects, or entities behind total cover. It does not identify the aura's school, exact effect, rank, caster/owner, item abilities, curse, alignment, creature type, statistics, or motive. It does not detect free-standing magical areas/effects that are not attached to an object or creature; Detect Magic remains the broader sustained spell for that role. No concentration, save, or attack. Scaling: none.

Fortify Object — The source says temporarily strengthen a small object, while Core labels duration Instantaneous. Those cannot both be operative. NEW PROVISIONAL: Action, 30 feet, V/S; target one discrete Small-or-smaller object you can perceive that has an actual current durability/structural-HP record. If carried, worn, or held, its current custodian must be willing; the spell cannot be used as hostile equipment targeting. Duration: until the start of your next regular turn or until consumed. The first damage packet that would deal positive damage to the object before then is reduced by 2, minimum 0, after the object's ordinary defenses but before structural HP loss; then Fortify Object ends. It grants no HP, temporary HP, repair, healing, maximum-HP increase, AC increase, permanence, resistance, charge restoration, ammunition restoration, or immunity. Multiple Fortify Object effects on one object do not stack or create several reductions; a later cast refreshes the single pending reduction. No concentration. Scaling: none. Its temporary wording therefore overrides the unusable Instantaneous metadata for the operational draft while preserving that metadata in provenance.

Detect Corruption — Source preserved: Action, 10 feet, V/S, instantaneous; sense presence of corruption. NEW PROVISIONAL: target Self. On resolution, learn only whether at least one creature, object, area, or active effect within 10 feet and line of effect currently has an explicit canonical Corruption record/tag/state. Total cover blocks detection. The spell does not infer corruption from appearance, alignment, species, faction, damage type, disease, poison, spell school, Director suspicion, or narrative theme. It reveals no direction, number, severity, identity, source, exact corruption value, hidden consequence, cure, alignment, or statistics. It does not create a permanent detection state. No concentration, save, or attack. Scaling: none. This avoids turning an unfinished global taxonomy into omniscience.

Harmonic Protection — Source preserved: Action, 30 feet, V/S; create a tone granting +1 to the next saving throw. Instantaneous metadata does not define how long that future-roll entitlement persists. NEW PROVISIONAL: target one willing creature within 30 feet that can hear the tone when cast, including yourself if you can hear. The target gains +1 to its next saving throw made within 1 minute, then the benefit is consumed whether the save succeeds or fails. No concentration. Becoming deafened afterward does not erase an already granted benefit; inability to hear at casting makes the target ineligible. Across all casters, a creature can hold only one Harmonic Protection entitlement at a time; a later cast refreshes the endpoint, not the number of uses. The next saving throw consumes it even when a stronger flat save bonus means the +1 does not numerically change the roll. Apply the shared strongest-flat-bonus rule; Harmonic Protection never stacks additively with another flat bonus to the same roll. Scaling: none.

Purify Drop — Source preserved: Action, 30 feet, V/S, instantaneous; cleanse one drop of liquid of mundane impurities. NEW PROVISIONAL: target one physically discrete droplet of liquid you can perceive within 30 feet, no more than approximately 0.05 mL. The droplet must already be separated from a larger continuous liquid volume; pointing at the surface of a barrel, pool, bloodstream, vial, or tank cannot purify the whole container. Remove ordinary nonmagical dirt, sediment, harmless contamination, or unwanted mundane dissolved impurities from that one drop. It does not neutralize poison/toxin, disease/pathogens, Corruption, curses, magical contamination, alchemical reagents, medicines, drugs, active enchantments, or creature bodily conditions. Removed material does not become recoverable salt, metal, reagent, concentrate, or other resource. The spell creates no water and changes no other portion of a container. One Action purifies one drop; repeated casting therefore has exactly that measurable throughput and no industrial area propagation. No concentration, save, or attack. Scaling: none.

Repel Vermin — Source preserved: Action, 30 feet, V/S, 1 minute; small nonmagical insects and rodents avoid you. NEW PROVISIONAL: target Self, creating a behavioral avoidance field out to 30 feet for 1 minute, no concentration. Eligible “vermin” are ordinary natural nonmagical insects or rodents no larger than Small that are not summoned, transformed, trained companions, controlled creatures, magical creatures, parasites already inside a host, or statted encounter combatants. An eligible vermin creature within the field will not willingly move closer to you; if already nearby and it has ordinary discretionary movement, it prefers a safe unobstructed route that increases its distance. It is never forced to enter fire, traps, lethal terrain, sealed barriers, or abandon necessary shelter merely to flee, and if no safe route exists it may remain where it is. The spell does not force Dash, spend another creature's Action, impose Charmed/Frightened, prevent attacks by a combat creature, dismiss a summon, cure infestation/disease, expel internal parasites, or grant immunity to bites or contamination. Scaling: none.

Warding Mark — Source preserved: Action, 30 feet, V/S/F, concentration up to 1 minute; place a small mark that glows when specific creature types approach. NEW PROVISIONAL: place one visible magical mark on a fixed solid surface or ground point you can perceive within 30 feet. When cast, choose one literal trigger descriptor of either (a) one named species/template the caster actually knows, or (b) one exact canonical creature-type/tag that already exists on operational creature records. The spell never invents or infers an unstated taxonomy. For the duration, the mark glows while at least one creature matching that exact descriptor is within 10 feet of the mark with line of effect to it. A creature entering by teleportation counts once it actually occupies that area. Walls and closed barriers block the trigger. The glow reveals only that at least one matching creature is presently within the trigger area; it does not reveal which creature, exact square, identity, disguise, alignment, statistics, intent, hidden lore, or unrecorded creature type. If a creature has no matching explicit record, the mark does not guess. Recasting requires the normal concentration transition and ends the old mark. Because this is an ongoing rank-0 magical mark, the existing reviewed Dispel Minor may end it if otherwise legally targeted.

Detection-family separation

The four superficially overlapping information cantrips now have different jobs:

Spell	Operational information
Sense Magic	Brief yes/no: some active magic is within 10 ft.
Aura Sight	Brief visual overlay: these already-perceived creatures/objects bear active magical auras.
Detect Corruption	Instant yes/no: an explicit Corruption record is within 10 ft.
Warding Mark	One-minute fixed alarm: a creature matching one literal chosen species/tag is within 10 ft of this mark.

That keeps Detect Magic meaningfully stronger: it sustains broader aura perception at 30 feet for up to 10 minutes and allows a later Action to inspect one aura's broad school/category. 
Pasted text

Likewise, the defensive cantrips remain mechanically distinct. Arcane Ward is a proactive +1 AC through the next turn; Barrier Flicker is a Reaction +2 AC against one physical projectile attack; Warding Gesture stores advantage on one magical save but occupies concentration; Harmonic Protection stores a nonconcentration +1 on one general save; and Nullify Element is a one-round selected resistance. That gives each source identity a usable role without collapsing them into renamed copies.
