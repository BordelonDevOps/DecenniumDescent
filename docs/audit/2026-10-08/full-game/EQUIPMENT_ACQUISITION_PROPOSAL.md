## Hands, armor and proficiency

One legal AC formula at a time: light base+DEX; medium base+min(DEX,2), including negative DEX; heavy fixed base. One shield supplies its printed bonus only while equipped and usable. Printed Stealth advantage/disadvantage uses ordinary cancellation, including Elven weave. Dwarven plate is AC18, not an additional boost for descriptive 'superior protection'. Descriptive scale-mail slashing protection creates no resistance.

Core expressly replaces V6's prohibition on nonproficient-armored casting: a caster not proficient with worn armor pays +1 RP and has concentration disadvantage. Retain V6's noncasting disadvantage on STR/DEX checks, saves and attacks. PROPOSE: all three shield types require shield proficiency and trigger the same nonproficiency effects while used. RP surcharge applies once per spell regardless of the number of nonproficient pieces; it does not create a pool or an RP price on charge-only ancestry/Oracle casting. Concentration disadvantage still applies to those sources. This price interaction needs your explicit agreement.

PROPOSE missing Strength-column meaning: wearing a listed armor/shield below its STR minimum reduces Speed by 10 feet, once regardless of multiple deficient pieces, floor0. Do not make it a minimum to equip. Do not import a Dwarf exemption absent its exact trait. The reviewed Load procedure remains active; V6 optional encumbrance is an alternate and its penalties do not stack. Ordinary Load Burden and this distinct fitted-equipment penalty can coexist. Confirm that this new completion is preferable to removing the source STR column.

Don/doff retains light1min/1min, medium5min/1min, heavy10min/5min, any shield1Action/1Action. An assisting creature halves doff time only; minimum one actual Action where the source is an Action. No stacking multiple helpers.

Buckler is strapped to one forearm; PROPOSE it can coexist with carrying a small nonweapon object, but does not supply a free S hand, two-handed weapon support, loading hand, active focus manipulation or second weapon attack through that arm. An ordinary shield/tower occupies one hand. Tower supplies only its +3AC, STR13 and Stealth disadvantage, with no invented cover or extra mobility penalty. Soldier Shield Trained is the express exception permitting its actual repeating crossbow with a strapped shield; its reloading requirements and selected-feature eligibility remain intact.

PROPOSE held F and S can use the same hand only if otherwise free of a weapon/shield and manipulating that focus. An ordinary holy symbol may instead be visibly worn or borne on a shield for F, but S still requires its separate usable hand. A two-handed weapon needs both hands only during its attack; releasing one grip costs no action, regripping is part of the next legal use. You cannot do this through occupied hands. Class-card exceptions win.

## Three separate kinds of quality

Preserve V6 equipment grades: Crude armor−1AC/half structuralHP, Fine armor+1AC, Superior+2AC/80%weight, Exceptional+3AC/70%weight; crude weapon−1attack/natural1 causes independent5%break check, Fine+1attack, Superior+2attack/one extra base weapon die on critical, Exceptional+3attack/two extra base weapon dice on critical. No flat damage boost, no automatic magic tag. A critical doubles normal damage dice, then adds these extra quality dice once, not twice. Base weapon die means one die of the printed profile, so greatsword's extra die is d6, not2d6. Crude break check is d100≤5 after an actual natural1 attack; repairable broken object, not erased material.

V6 price multipliers Fine×3, Superior×10, Exceptional×25 apply to ordinary equipment baseline. New Crude price×0.5. Standard×1. Only one equipment grade applies. PROPOSE armor grade modifies worn suits, not shields, because the source does not spell out shield grade stacking; shield quality can affect structuralHP through an explicitly defined crafted output later. Unspecified Exceptional minor special property is no additional effect until an exact property is authored and approved. No free rolled enchantment.

Crafting result labels Standard/Exceptional/Masterwork govern that recipe's yield/charges/catalyst/result rules. They do not automatically change the V6 equipment grade. Forge Dwarf's 'exceptional quality' description remains no free+3AC/attack. A recipe must explicitly specify a resulting equipment grade before it gains one. Core artifact valuation quality×0.25/1/1.5/2.5/5/10 is a third field and applies in its market formula, not as extra ordinary retail markups or extra combat bonuses. Core's artifact valuation and V6 mundane retail rules are scoped, not accidentally multiplied together. Please challenge this separation if source evidence gives a stronger interpretation.
