# Alchemy recipe playtest

**Draft awaiting joint source and manuscript review.** These rules and the accompanying recipe cards are proposed completions of all 45 Alchemy formulas. They do not approve numerical balance, update the companion, or alter original recipes. The public cards contain 27 formulas; the other 18 remain in the separate Director file. Use the [reviewed crafting framework](CRAFTING_HARVEST_PLAYTEST.md) for training, formulas, work, checks, quality, recovery, XP and finite markets.

Recipe IDs, names, skill levels, difficulty, tier, rarity, quantities, work requirements, components, recommended batch values and legacy fields are preserved. A prefix such as Superior or Masterwork is part of a recipe name, not an equipment-grade or crafting-result grant. `rarity` and `tier` are separate fields. Current V4 effects and matching V5 public prose supply the active effect; conflicting abbreviated mechanics fields and older legacy effects remain comparative evidence, never extra powers. New details below are explicitly authored completions.

## Physical products and their manufacture

One output unit is one sealed single-use dose in a glass container, whether called potion, oil, essence, catalyst, extract, tincture, distillate, solvent or bomb. **New profile:** each contains 0.1 lb of finished compound in a 0.1-lb vial, total 0.2 lb; container AC 13, structural HP 1. It fits an ordinary vial slot. Mass includes its contents. It is a magical alchemical consumable, not a spell, weapon, armor grade or automatically an artifact. It requires no attunement, RP, RC, focus, class, school or proficiency to use. It grants no spell access. Carrying it grants no benefit.

Each finished dose needs an actual container. Recipe components with role vessel can supply that many output containers, each at their actual source tier and cost; all source consumed quantities are still expended as the printed process requires. Where final yield exceeds available vessel components, supply that many additional ordinary empty Glass Vials or equivalent 0.1-lb single-dose glass containers. Additional packaging costs and mass are separately recorded rather than silently editing source component totals or the source batch recommendation. Quality never creates glass. Extra prepared empty packaging is reserved but consumed only when actually used to package a finished unit; unneeded spare packaging remains in inventory. Original source vessel inputs follow their printed consumed flags even on a failed batch.

Each dose contains 0.1 lb of retained non-container compound. If the recipe's non-vessel consumed mass, after any actual retained catalyst, cannot supply the final compound mass, supply the exact deficit in ordinary clean water as carrier before packaging. This carrier has no independent magical effect; its mass counts and its ordinary acquisition costs are extra. All 45 current Alchemy recipes have enough source compound mass for their normal and maximum generic quality yields, including retention of the heaviest one-unit catalyst, but the explicit rule applies if a later authorized substitution changes that. The remainder becomes spent process waste, with no automatic item identity, sale value or second salvage award. Never treat total input mass as the output's weight or refund excess input vessels automatically.

**New processing phase:** the first 15 active minutes of each Alchemy order, included within its required work time, combine and stabilize its legal reserved ingredients in the actual closed working apparatus. A valid time reduction applies to this phase proportionately along with the whole order, rounded upward to whole minutes. Each required ingredient must remain legally usable until this phase finishes. If it becomes illegal earlier, use the framework's pause/replacement rule. A successfully completed phase suspends ordinary spoilage of the bound working mixture for that work order while its vessel remains sealed and intact. It does not create a reusable preservation service: the mixture cannot be removed as raw ingredients, sold as finished doses, used in another order, or harvested for fresh components. Abandonment expends the consumed inputs. Transferring custody does not create a second order.

Masterwork's one eligible surviving catalyst and ordinary Failed recovery remain the exact framework exceptions to consumed-input accounting. A returned lot retains its pre-processing condition and accumulated age, resuming its clock upon return; it is not restored to Fresh. A source that requires destruction of an irreplaceable part still cannot return it. Stabilization inside this process does not settle general essence containment, active cores, ingredient origins or cosmology. Before processing, each ingredient still needs its actually applicable acquisition and containment rules.

Completed sealed doses have no ordinary spoilage deadline while kept physically intact under ordinary storage conditions. **New opened-container limit:** once opened, use within one elapsed hour; resealing, splitting or transferring does not reset that deadline. Afterward its effect is lost. Every unit has one use: partial drinking/application cannot create fractional magical benefits or multiple recipients. Breaking/spilling destroys the dose without automatically damaging nearby creatures, producing an area, or triggering other carried bombs. The bomb Action below is the only generic detonation permission. Empty intact containers remain actual 0.1-lb vials after an oral dose, without a refund of recipe price; a bomb consumes/breaks its container.

## Use, effect identity and ending

Except bombs, these recipes use the already reviewed potion procedure: **Action**, one available hand and the actual accessible dose; drink it or feed it to one willing or Incapacitated active creature within 5 feet with compatible ingestion. An inability to swallow prevents oral delivery. Dead is ineligible; an active Undead or Construct creature is not excluded solely by its game type, but actual anatomy and explicit source restrictions still apply. There is no generic way to force a resisting active creature to drink. The source healing Oil still says Drink; names such as Oil and Catalyst do not create a free coating, attack, fuel or extra crafting role.

Verify an Action and a legal delivery before committing the dose. Once legal use starts, spend that unit and any recipient use entitlement immediately; interruption or later zero benefit grants no refund. A dose cannot be administered while simultaneously used as an ingredient or sold. Record its unique inventory unit, recipe ID, crafter, activating user, recipient, quality and start/expiry. That provenance does not grant caster level, spell rank or school.

These are magical nonspell effects. They do not trigger spellcasting Dissonance/Refrain, require concentration, qualify for Counterspell, or acquire a spell's rank through resemblance. An explicit effect that affects this actual class of magical item/effect can apply within its own scope; rank-limited spell dispelling does not treat a rankless item as rank 0. Ordinary anti-healing, damage/condition immunity, action restriction and actual source-specific recovery rules still apply.

For elapsed durations, start the clock when delivery resolves. Extra turns, rests, moving ownership and temporarily suppressed benefits do not reset it. Until-next-long-rest effects end at the recipient's next completed beneficial Long Rest, not merely when rest begins. Death ends the consumed dose's ongoing creature benefits; an item does not resurrect or resume those benefits afterward. Reversible suppression does not restore a spent dose, undo prior healing/damage or pause time.

Keep each dose's effect record with its own expiry. Identical resistances never stack. Apply only the strongest currently active Alchemy bonus to the same statistic; expired stronger records cannot borrow a later weaker record's deadline. Save advantage does not stack. New doses do not repair or replay an old record. Independent sources outside this supplement keep their actual stacking rules. Current HP recovery is an immediate event rather than an ongoing bonus; it can occur again from another legitimately consumed healing dose.

## The six Alchemy families

**Resistance:** gain resistance to exactly the printed damage type for the printed duration. Bludgeoning resistance is not limited to nonmagical attacks because the source gives no such limit; it does not extend to piercing/slashing. Poison-damage resistance does not grant Poisoned immunity, a poison save bonus or a cure. Use the reviewed damage defenses once per packet. Any immunity explicitly printed by a card replaces that card's resistance during its immunity interval; a later resistance interval starts only at its normal elapsed transition, not from early dispelling or ending.

**Resonance:** restore the exact printed amount of expended RP to the recipient's actual RP pool, capped at its actual maximum. No temporary excess RP, Dissonance/Refrain clearing, additional Action, spell access, RC conversion or other class resource follows. A recipient with no RP pool receives none. Track one recipient-wide use **per recipe ID per beneficial Long Rest**, regardless of manufacturer, item name alias, transfer or doses possessed. Commit this entitlement when legal administration starts, even if blocked or already at the RP cap; no delayed benefit or refund. Different recipe IDs keep their distinct printed entitlements. A later RP-pool grant during that same rest interval cannot erase a spent entitlement.

**Healing:** restore the printed dice and flat amount as ordinary magical, nonspell current-HP healing, subject to actual healing restrictions, caps, Wounds/Critical ceiling and actual paid/finite-heal callbacks. No generic Wound, Injury, curse, condition or resurrection removal. A still-dying recipient that actually gains positive HP follows the reviewed zero-HP procedure. A blocked heal supplies no invented stabilization. Full HP or prevented healing does not prevent an independent explicit cure on a card.

Where a card explicitly prints the higher-tier poison/disease rider, this proposed card completes it as: after the healing attempt, choose and end **one present removable poison effect or one present removable disease effect** on the recipient. The choice may include a suppressed but present eligible record, not an already ended effect or an immunity that prevented application. End only the selected source record and its explicitly dependent symptoms; other independent causes remain. It grants no future immunity and does not remove a source that expressly requires another cure. Do not remove intrinsic ancestry/class traits, Oracle curses, Corruption, Wounds or ordinary intoxication merely because their descriptions sound toxic. All three Alchemy recipes actually printing this rider are high-tier and receive it; no lower-tier recipe gains it from a name.

**Strength buffs:** recipe-11, recipe-12 and recipe-16 give their stated bonus to **Strength ability checks and melee damage rolls**, for their duration. The `attackBonus` source field is an abbreviated conflicting record, retained in evidence; it does not grant attack-roll accuracy. No Strength score, save, carrying-capacity, extra attack, ranged damage or spell-check bonus is added. The flat melee damage bonus applies once per actual melee damage roll, not per die or per damage type within that same hit; it is not doubled by a critical hit. No damage roll means no bonus packet. Spell/class damage qualifies only if it is actually a melee damage roll under that source.

**Veiled buffs:** give the printed Stealth-check bonus, without automatic Hidden status or erasing known location. The optional Invisible rider requires the recipe's **actual rarity field** to be Rare or higher. Thus the public Uncommon recipe-23 grants only its +3 Stealth/1-hour benefit; the separately gated Rare/Very Rare recipes can use the rider. Recipe tier and the word Masterwork do not satisfy the rarity condition.

For an eligible dose, its recipient may spend a **Bonus Action** during its active duration to become Invisible until the end of the recipient's next regular turn, or until that dose's parent duration ends, whichever comes first. This is one activation per actual dose record and is spent at legal activation start. No new Bonus Action is granted. Multiple live dose records do not stack Invisible or grant simultaneous activations; choose the one whose unused rider is being spent. A separately consumed new eligible dose has its own finite rider, but transfer/reopening or reactivation of the same dose cannot replenish it. Apply the reviewed Core Invisible observer/location rule; revealing location does not by itself remove visual invisibility, and invisibility does not grant sight through cover. This source adds no automatic ending on attack beyond the actual Invisible condition's observer consequences.

**Mental:** grant the printed advantage on saves against being Charmed or Frightened for the full card duration. Existing conditions remain unless a separate rule permits another save; no immunity or immediate cure. During that same duration, communicate telepathically with one willing creature at a time within the card's printed radius. **New communication completion:** both participants must know at least one language in common; initial contact requires perception and line of effect, and continuing exchange requires the range and line to remain legal. Select/reselect the partner on the recipient's own turn with ordinary brief communication, not a free Action, command power or extra turn. Partners need not have this potion to reply, but the link does not relay another telepathic endpoint or support unlimited group channels. It conveys voluntarily sent language, not thoughts, senses, location, truth or memories. Either endpoint can stop; incapacitation, unconsciousness, death, out-of-range or blocked line prevents exchange without extending the potion clock. Save advantage remains independently valid while its duration and subject eligibility hold.

**Bombs:** spend an Action and one accessible bomb to throw it to one point you can see with a clear physical path within 60 feet. It bursts immediately as a sphere of the card's radius; every creature in the area, including allies and the user if present, makes the printed DEX save. Roll the printed damage once, apply each save and defenses separately: failed save full damage, successful save half rounded down. No attack roll, attack critical, ability damage modifier, weapon proficiency, lingering zone or chain detonation is added. Objects do not receive this creature-only damage by default. An empty legal point is permitted; it wastes a real bomb and grants no invented hit/kill trigger.

**New 30/60 range completion:** within 30 feet use ordinary saves. Beyond 30 through 60 feet, affected creatures have advantage on this DEX save because the longer throw is less accurate; it neither doubles area nor invents scatter. Apply ordinary advantage/disadvantage cancellation. Beyond 60 feet the use is illegal. This is a save-based throw, not a ranged attack: adjacent-hostile attack disadvantage has no direct application. Other restrictions that actually prevent the Action or physical throw still apply. Evaluate area cover from the burst origin: half cover +2 DEX save, three-quarters +5, best only; total cover excludes that creature. A wall cannot be bypassed by choosing an inaccessible origin. No additional cover from the thrower is added to the same save.

## Quality and economics

Use the framework's one batch check and floor-rounded yield: Standard Q, Exceptional floor(1.25Q), Masterwork floor(1.5Q). Supply actual packaging for all produced units. These consumables have **no generic potency, duration, DC, healing, RP, invisibility-use, radius or charge improvement** from a high crafting outcome. The permitted non-yield durability option applies only to the physical container: maximum structural HP 1 becomes 2 for either Exceptional or Masterwork under the framework's minimum +1 rule; contents and effect are unchanged. A container with extra HP does not become an additional dose. The vial remains physically present with its actual HP after a legal oral use.

If the base batch quantity is at least 2, Flawed produces floor(Q/2) otherwise ordinary doses. For base quantity 1 with fixed elapsed ongoing duration, the card's Flawed result halves its primary duration (round down to a whole six-second round, minimum one round). A fixed sequential resistance-after-immunity phase is also halved. The optional Veiled rider still ends at the recipient's next regular-turn end and is capped by the shortened parent; its turn endpoint is not halved into an invented half-turn. Mental telepathy shares the shortened parent duration.

For base quantity 1 with an instantaneous healing/RP/bomb effect or an until-next-long-rest duration, there is no finite half-duration singleton profile. Resolve Flawed as Failed with ordinary 25% non-catalyst recovery. The existence of the glass container does not turn an otherwise ineligible instantaneous consumable into a Flawed durable item. Exceptional/Masterwork never upgrade the recipe's rarity, market class, tier or magical effect.

Preserve the source recommended **Standard batch** value and exact unit ratio. Additional packaging/carrier costs are actual costs, not automatic additions to recommended sale value. Proposed ordinary merchant treatment for these non-artifact Alchemy consumables uses the reviewed standing-based mundane resale percentages against that unit reference, only when an actual buyer makes a finite quote; it is a new extension of that merchant procedure, not a guaranteed market. Specific quotes take precedence. No automatic Core artifact rarity/quality multiplier applies merely because the contents are magical. A separately authored unique artifact version would need its own profile and valuation.

The cards retain source rarity even when the adjective in a name differs, along with expensive source component totals. This review makes their procedures executable; it does not silently rebalance a high-cost low-healing recipe. Those economic discrepancies belong in numerical playtest findings, with the original values retained for comparison.

## Worked boundaries for joint review

- Field Storm Potion's current lightning resistance is used; its retained legacy healing text grants nothing. Three Standard outputs require three actual containers; its five consumed source vials are still the printed process input.
- Field Mighty Catalyst helps a Strength check and a qualifying melee damage roll by +1. It does not add +1 to attack accuracy, a Strength save, or a ranged hit.
- Public recipe-23 is actually Uncommon, so it does not obtain Invisible solely from its Tier4/name. Public availability does not reveal the separate restricted alternatives.
- An RP dose given to a full recipient is spent and uses that recipe's one-per-rest entitlement; later expenditure of RP does not replay the earlier dose.
- A bomb at 40 feet gives its actual eligible victims save advantage; one behind half cover also adds +2 to the save. A creature behind total cover from the burst takes no damage from it.
- A singleton instant-effect recipe's Flawed result cannot be rescued by giving its bottle half HP. It follows Failed recovery. Four base outputs rolled Flawed instead give two ordinary completed doses.
- A long order's first completed processing phase stabilizes the bound mixture, not a stockpile of removable raw essence. Returned permitted catalyst/failure lots resume their original age.

The source bindings and individual entries are part of this same proposed review. Runtime inventory deduction, actual campaign balance and full game flow remain later gates. Cosmology is not reviewed here and remains after the authorized GitHub push and verified remote commit.
