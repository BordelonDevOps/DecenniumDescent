## FI-D01 — A lure is an item; a fishing attempt is a separate operation

**Resolves FI-01.**

**PRESERVED:** recipe-160 produces Q5 Lures through four active crafting hours and crafting DC10. Its printed +1 Fishing and extraYield1 remain unchanged. Those numbers do not establish five catches, a four-hour catch attempt or catch DC10. Fishing uses WIS and actual Fishing-tackle training. [S1, S2, S8: recipe-160]

**NEW:** one ordinary attended fishing attempt reserves a **60-minute work window**: five minutes assembling/deploying, fifty minutes fishing, and five minutes ordinary retrieval. Declare one lead, one capture method, one complete assembly, a site/opportunity, actual bait or lure, an intended receiving arrangement and the applicable DC before deployment. The catch check occurs after the fifty-minute fishing interval, at minute 55. Ordinary successful retrieval finishes at minute 60.

The default catch check is **d20 + WIS modifier + one applicable proficiency contribution + independently legal bonuses**. Use **DC12** for ordinary accessible conditions, **DC15** for materially difficult current/cover/access, or **DC18** for severe but still physically feasible conditions. The Director records the applicable category before commitment. A specific independently reviewed catch profile can instead supply its own DC. Recipe tier, price and crafting difficulty do not choose catch DC. Impossible access is not DC18.

Use Core's four-degree task resolution and natural-result shifts, but this catching profile collapses the two successful degrees to one successful interception and the two unsuccessful degrees to no catch. Critical Success creates no extra specimen, extra material unit or reduced time. Critical Failure triggers only a hazard expressly assigned to that opportunity before commitment; with none assigned, it adds no invented injury, Pressure, contamination or equipment damage. [S6]

An untrained user may attempt ordinary physically possible fishing with WIS, no tool PB and disadvantage. This disadvantage is a **NEW catching rule**, not an assertion that recipe access gates govern catching. An unranked catch attempt has no recipe-level/formula requirement and awards no crafting XP. An automatic result receives no check modifier and opens no replacement roll; only a separately governing procedure can grant a no-roll catch, and this candidate grants none. An applicable expertise feature replaces, rather than duplicates, PB. One trained assistant may give +1 on an attended Rod or Net attempt only by completing its five setup minutes and fifty active fishing minutes alongside the lead before the check. Subsequent retrieval help costs its real time but is not a future condition used to qualify an already rolled bonus. The unattended Trap exception is defined in FI-D05 and receives no generic assistant bonus.

Work-minute blocks cannot be compressed into a single combat Action. Each six-second interval credited as active work commits the worker's Action and required hands; actual threat, conditions and other activities can prevent that work. This grants no free casting, guard duty, extra Action or unattended Rod operation.

A basic catch candidate must be an actual locally available aquatic creature with a documented ordinary provision-catching profile, fitting the apparatus and responding to the actual method. This procedure does not defeat an actively resisting encounter creature, capture a character or companion, or bypass a creature's special defenses. Larger, dangerous or otherwise unsupported catches need their own reviewed procedure. Their proximity is not permission to improvise that procedure from an expensive item.

## FI-D02 — Finished Bait, ordinary bait and consumption

**Resolves FI-02.**

**NEW:** each Bait-family output is **one magical, nonartifact, single-use dose**, not durable tackle and not a rechargeable item. Its complete prepared body is **0.10 lb of actual mundane angling carrier plus a 0.01-lb wrapper**. The carrier reference is 1 cp and the wrapper 1 cp. The complete unexposed unit is therefore **0.11 lb and 2 cp before the source process share**, with a **2 × 1 × 1-inch packaged envelope**. Its listed HP1 belongs to the prepared dose; the ordinary wrapper separately has HP1/AC11. Routine unwrapping is not damage to the dose and is not exposure commitment by itself. Count dose plus wrapper separately or one inclusive packaged weight, never both. Neither its constituents nor its name grant player food credit.

At first deliberate exposure in a registered deployment, consume the entire dose's usable inventory entitlement once and create one **attempt-bound bait receipt**. That receipt carries the source bonus, extraYield and one affinity for that attempt only. It lasts **60 elapsed minutes from exposure**, without pausing for suppression, retrieval, travel, rest or melding, and ends earlier when its attempt is closed. Normal swallowing or dispersal during that attempt does not invalidate the receipt: this is the explicit consumable exception to a durable item's continuing-body requirement.

The receipt is attached to that one recorded capture assembly, not to its owner, wrapper or an attracted creature. It supplies no creature condition, spell, concentration requirement or separate activation Action. Actual bait placement is part of the five-minute deployment. Its magic is suppressed only when an applicable effect reaches that assembly's bait effect; do not infer suppression from an unrelated nearby object. A different assembly cannot inherit it by collecting residue.

Before exposure, cancellation leaves a still-eligible dose unused. After exposure, failure, early retrieval, escape, interruption, transfer or abandonment never refunds a usable dose. Remaining wet material is spent physical residue, not fresh Bait; its actual mass remains accounted for wherever retained. An emptied wrapper remains the same ordinary object until discarded, with no source-value recovery. An intact wrapper may package another lawfully produced dose, but supplies neither a dose nor a renewed use; rewrapping an unused dose preserves its age. A destroyed or spoiled unexposed dose cannot produce a receipt.

**NEW storage convention:** a completed, dry, properly wrapped dose has a **24-hour unprotected finished-bait age interval**, beginning at craft completion. At 24 accumulated unprotected hours it becomes Spoiled directly: no Fishing effect and no ordinary finished-dose reference value. An opened but unexposed dose keeps that same age and can be rewrapped without resetting it. No fractional dose has a Fishing effect. Existing preservation applies only where its actual method is compatible; this verdict grants no new magical stasis or automatic preservation treatment for finished Bait. Its 60-minute receipt clock is separate from the unused-dose storage clock; a receipt lawfully created before spoilage runs to its own endpoint.

Ordinary expendable angling bait is a separate **NEW 0.10-lb portion at a 1-cp reference**, subject to actual prey compatibility and its real material's spoilage. It has no magical bonus. A recipe's consumed `role=bait` component, an ordinary angling portion, and a finished Bait dose are three different records.

A genuinely separate combat use follows its independently authorized improvised-object procedure, without Fishing modifiers or yield. A net being used only as cargo wrapping is not conducting a fishing attempt.

## FI-D05 — Traps, the Chum Rig and unattended operation

**Resolves FI-05.**

Setting requires five active minutes with two manipulating hands, one actual compatible bait portion or finished Bait, and a real anchor/support able to hold the loaded apparatus against the actual current. The integral recovery cord gives at most a **20-foot actual deployment/recovery path**, not free placement through barriers. Record the setter's actual WIS modifier and applicable Fishing PB/expertise at completed setup. Those two values are the configuration snapshot for this one check; later ownership or a substitute retriever does not replace them. No stored personal aura, future buff or generic assistant bonus is admitted. Only registered, currently eligible deployed Fishing pieces and its valid bait receipt supply equipment modifiers at resolution.

This exception requires no continuing hands, consciousness or concentration after setup. It is not an autonomous creature or repeating hourly engine. The check resolves once even if the setter is elsewhere. Current physical access, prey availability, suppression and apparatus state still govern; an unavailable scheduled checkpoint must be resolved from its historical state before later events, not reconstructed from today's repaired trap.

No second catch can accumulate. Retrieval takes five active minutes with an actual capable retriever, both hands, a legal cord/path and sufficient handling capacity. An unattended trap provides no artificial air, water quality, food, survival duration or obedience. The catch's actual life requirements continue.

One person may have only **one unresolved Fishing deployment**, attended or unattended. A finished but unreconciled Trap continues to occupy that person's deployment allowance until physically retrieved, lawfully handed over with its existing record, or expressly abandoned. Abandonment cancels any not-yet-resolved Fishing check and closes future Fishing claims, but does not erase an existing trapped creature or move the apparatus. No new roll follows from a handover.

**NEW Rig profile:** the Rig is an accessory, not a capture method. Its usable interior is **5 × 3 × 3 inches**, with a **1-pint and 1-lb chum limit**, both required. It needs a real support and its integral 10-foot tether. Deploy it within **5 feet of one registered assembly's bait/capture point** and bind it to that attempt alone. It supplies no bonus to everyone fishing nearby.

Each Rig deployment needs **1 lb of actual compatible ordinary chum**, at a NEW 0.10-gp reference. One finished 0.10-lb Bait may replace exactly 0.10 lb of that chum when physically compatible; the remaining 0.90 lb is still required. Rig chum does not also fill a separate Trap bait holder or bait a Rod's hook: those physical locations need their own real portions, unless the Rod uses its admitted Lure alternative. Only one finished Bait receipt can contribute to the attempt. Opening dispersal commits the entire chum allotment once. Leftover residue has no reusable bait entitlement. The Rig has no initial free chum, no replenishment and no predator-summoning rule. Mounting the empty Rig and establishing its support/tether takes **five additional active minutes before the main deployment**. Load and open it during the main deployment, with actual hands and access; its bait exposure begins only then. Retrieving the Rig takes **five additional active minutes**, separately from retrieving the catch. Neither interval is free labor or another fishing attempt.

## FI-D08 — Finite sites, opportunities, concurrency and chronological ledgers

**Resolves FI-08.**

**NEW:** distinguish `siteId`, a shared `stockId`, actual `creatureId`, `opportunityId`, `attemptId`, physical equipment IDs and later material-unit IDs. Two banks or map labels accessing the same stock share its depletion record. An opportunity is permission for one finite attempt, not an extra animal.

Before productive attempts, the governing world records a finite site manifest: accessible region, actual available creature identities or finite stock members, currently available opportunity IDs, catch profiles/mass/geometry, response eligibility, hazards, permitted DCs, receiving access and known renewal events. Anonymous ordinary prey may have internal member IDs; those IDs do not create the prey. Missing availability is not infinite stock. Deliberately exploring a site whose contents are unknown to the angler can use a separately recorded finite opportunity, including a real empty result.

Reserve the opportunity and physical resources before setup. At first exposure/deployment commitment, atomically spend that opportunity, bind any bait receipt, and perform the one response allocation. Cancellation before that checkpoint releases reservations but not time already worked. Cancellation after it does not recover the opportunity or exposed bait.

At the allocation checkpoint, use authoritative campaign order and atomically reserve the selected available creature. Competing requests cannot both claim it. A failed attempt closes its opportunity and records the failed/escaped creature's actual state; it grants no automatic fresh opportunity on that same specimen.

**No default hourly, dawn, Watch, rest, scene, equipment-transfer or site-renaming refill exists.** More opportunities require a documented real change: actual previously unattempted stock becoming accessible, an independently governed arrival, or a materially new situation allowing another approach. Such a record carries its cause and time and never refreshes that creature's anatomy or extra-recovery history. Simply waiting or creating new IDs is insufficient.

Every transition uses the campaign's authoritative `(time, phase, sequence)` and the current ledger version. Reject earlier, replayed or conflicting transitions; process due deployed-trap and bait-expiry events from their actual histories first. Record allocations, spent opportunities, caught/escaped/released identities and terminal outcomes once. A copied die, reroll of the same event, report reload or replayed callback cannot mint another catch or debit. No check occurs without a legitimate unresolved opportunity.

Make the first **min(E, available reserve-unit count)** units in the predeclared reserve order eligible for that body's subsequent extraction. Actual subsequent loss, damage, contamination or decay can reduce recoverable inventory; the earlier manifest cannot restore it. No later recategorization or new manifest rerolls the body's base yield. Missing reserve means zero extra units, with the source E unchanged. No unused remainder is banked.

A sub-pound baseline portion remains its actual fractional quantity; it does not become a whole pound. An extra-recovery meat unit requires an additional whole 1-lb portion already present. This generic meat accounting profile is **NEW Tier I, with no elemental property and unknown market value**; its availability/edibility still requires actual donor evidence, and it is not automatically an exact catalog ingredient. Other named materials retain their exact existing source-unit weight and indivisibility; there is no universal one-pound organ.

**Synthetic arithmetic fixture, not a supplied species:** a fish with only 0.40 lb of recoverable edible flesh has at most that 0.40 lb, even with extraYield3. A body with a 1-lb baseline meat allocation and three additional real 1-lb reserve portions can authorize four pounds total, but only when all four pounds actually exist and subsequent extraction succeeds.

The generic harvest Critical Success may recover its separately prerecorded damaged baseline unit. It cannot claim a unit already in the Fishing reserve, duplicate the extra allowance or exceed the combined physical ceiling. No new category or unique drop is created by combining the two procedures.
