Casting-prerequisite snapshot bridge — FEAT327-ACQ-03. NEW acquisition completion. Apply this bridge only to an actual feat acquisition prerequisite or its expressly required acquisition selection, subject to a more specific acquisition rule in that feat’s final procedure. A casting prerequisite requires a source-qualified personal spellcasting entitlement already acquired by the character and still retained at the acquisition snapshot. Record the actual grant, exact spell identity and source version, casting route, relevant class/rank gates, and the repertoire or preparation evidence that makes that route available. Class-level capacity, an RP or RC pool, a spell merely appearing on a permitted list, ownership of a spellbook or scroll, item-enabled casting, an unacquired class feature, and the prospective benefit of the feat being acquired do not by themselves supply this entitlement.

Retained entitlement versus immediate casting legality. A retained personal grant does not lose its acquisition credential merely because its renewable charge is currently empty, RP is insufficient, components or a suitable casting hand/voice are unavailable, the character is unconscious, or an existing temporary prohibition or suppression prevents casting. Ignore those immediate-use impediments only when evaluating this casting credential. Do not refill a resource, restore components, lift a prohibition, perform a cast, or establish that the feat-selection transaction itself is otherwise lawful. Actual casting still requires every current action, component, body, target, range, payment, concentration, strain and source-specific condition. An expressly ongoing or currently-active prerequisite in a particular feat remains an ongoing or currently-active prerequisite.

Qualifying repertoire and preparation. For a known-spell route, require the exact spell actually learned or personally granted, with its applicable source gates satisfied. For an ordinary prepared route, require the spell actually prepared at the snapshot, an actually acquired always-prepared grant, or another already-established source exception that authorizes casting it without ordinary preparation. An unprepared spellbook entry and the ability to prepare or substitute it later are insufficient for that spell’s credential. A future study period, rest, substitution, level-up selection or other repertoire change cannot be anticipated. A ritual route qualifies when the character actually possesses its ritual-casting permission and the particular spell meets that permission’s exact book, tag, rank and other repertoire requirements; no ritual need already have been cast. Do not extend one spell’s ritual overlay to another spell. Loss of a required repertoire source follows the actual class procedure: Wizard’s surviving prepared matrices remain available under its spellbook-loss rule, while an unprepared lost-book entry is not made available by merely planning its reconstruction.

Generic and specific predicates. “Spellcasting ability,” “Actual personal spellcasting ability,” and “Ability to cast at least one spell” require at least one qualifying personal spell route under this bridge; a qualifying rank-0 spell is sufficient. “Ability to cast cantrips” requires a qualifying rank-0 route. A named-spell prerequisite requires the exact required spell identity and permitted source version, not a similarly named class technique or item effect. A school prerequisite requires an actual matching school on the qualifying route. A rank prerequisite uses the spell rank actually supplied by that route and the final normalized predicate’s existing exact-rank or minimum-rank comparison, not total character level, maximum possible class rank, available RP, damage, or a higher price paid for a lower-rank spell. Where one predicate combines identity, school, rank or route restrictions, one qualifying route must satisfy that complete predicate; do not combine different routes’ most favorable fields. Distinct alternatives retain their existing AND/OR structure.

Removal, replacement and expiry. Reevaluate the retained grant and repertoire for every new feat acquisition. An actually removed, expired or lawfully retrained-away grant cannot support a new acquisition unless another independently qualifying route exists. Replacing a prepared matrix removes that ordinary prepared route for later snapshots unless another valid route to the spell remains. Temporary suppression or renewable depletion is not removal. Exhausting a genuinely nonrenewable permission follows its actual source: if the permission terminates, it no longer supplies a retained route. Under FS-01, a previously acquired feat keeps its original acquisition snapshot; later prerequisite loss does not erase that historical acquisition or waive any of the feat’s actual ongoing operating conditions.

Unassigned schools and required school choices. Preserve each source spell’s actual school value, explicit null or absent field. An absent, null or unassigned school is not Universal, Enchantment, Evocation, a wildcard, or a school inferred from damage type or a grant’s name. A school-unassigned personal rank-0 grant can satisfy generic spellcasting and cantrip predicates, but cannot itself satisfy a school-specific predicate. Supplementary Spell Focus additionally requires its mandatory permanent selection of an actual school supported by at least one qualifying acquired spell route at that same snapshot. Satisfying its generic spellcasting prerequisite without a supported school choice is insufficient to complete its acquisition. Another genuine route may support that choice. Subsequent loss or suppression of the supporting route does not reselect the school or erase the original acquisition; later benefits still require actual eligible casts through that fixed school.

Authored-candidate evidence versus runtime selection. For authored-candidate qualification, use the exact complete controlling authored spell procedure and its actual source-access binding, supported by the applicable review and fulfillment chain. A preserved earlier blocked catalog snapshot does not override an explicitly reviewed, version-linked operational descendant merely because the original remains unchanged. A separate selectionEnabled:false or disabled runtime adapter does not by itself invalidate that authored evidence. Neither a favorable label nor the existence of a reviewed spell supplies the character’s personal grant or repertoire history. Genuinely unfinished, null, unsupported or operationally unavailable payloads remain unavailable as positive casting witnesses. Preserve original statuses and live selection flags; this acquisition bridge neither enables runtime selection nor promotes another card.

Snapshot and payment. Preserve the exact prerequisite text and record the qualifying grant, spell/source/mode, actual school-field state and rank, repertoire/preparation or ritual basis, applicable class gates, controlling authored-procedure reference, and acquisition time. Temporary use blocks may be recorded separately but are not treated as grant removal. Missing grant, preparation, source-version or required-school evidence leaves that predicate unestablished rather than assumed true. Check all other printed ability, level, ownership, credential and earned-opportunity requirements independently. The feat’s own prospective spell grant cannot bootstrap its prerequisite, and one paid opportunity cannot fund two acquisitions.

The ordinary preparation, substitution, book-loss, always-prepared and ritual distinctions retain the supplied Wizard engine. Their application as explicit feat-acquisition predicates is the NEW completion.
