1. **Foresight Flicker** — **Action; Self; V/S; 1 minute; no concentration.** The preserved 30-ft metadata does not grant an ally target; “against you” makes the beneficiary the caster. Until consumed, the **next eligible hostile d20 roll against you** has disadvantage. Eligible rolls are:
   - an attack roll targeting you; or
   - a hostile opposed/task check directly made against you as the opposing subject.

   A saving throw made against your spell/feature is not a roll “against you.” Damage rolls and other nond20 rolls do not qualify. Determine eligibility and consume the Flicker **before rolling**; you cannot see the result and save it for a later roll. If one d20 roll legally affects you and other creatures simultaneously, disadvantage modifies that one roll for its entire resolution; do not roll a separate version for others. Enemy/hostile status uses ordinary caster knowledge, not magical allegiance detection. Advantage/disadvantage cancels normally. One pending Flicker per caster; a successful recast replaces it. No initiative bonus, automatic miss, reroll, or retroactive change. **NEW:** self target, one-minute pending window, exact roll eligibility.

2. **Omen Whisper** — **Action; V/S; instantaneous.** Choose either:
   - one creature, object, or location you perceive within 30 ft; or
   - one specific course of action you are considering beginning within the next minute in the current situation.

   The Director provides **one brief, truthful, cryptic but usable phrase or sentence** concerning the most immediately relevant circumstance, danger, opportunity, or likely consequence connected to that subject/action. The whisper is privately perceived by the caster and is intelligible without establishing any deity, spirit, or speaking entity.

   It reads the supernatural omen available from **current circumstances**; it does not guarantee future choices, future dice, or events not yet caused. If later voluntary actions or new events alter circumstances, the omen can become obsolete without having been false. It does not reveal full stat blocks, exact hidden inventories, complete plots, or guarantee check success unless that specific fact is genuinely the bounded omen. Recasting the materially same question/subject while circumstances remain materially unchanged gives the same omen or a restatement, **not cumulative extra detail**. A materially changed situation may yield a new omen. No additional daily cap is invented beyond normal cantrip cadence/Refrain. **NEW:** bounded subject format and one-clue output.

3. **Far Echo** — **Action; 30 ft; V/S; replay lasts at most 6 seconds; no concentration.** Choose one perceived point within range and LoE. The spell retrieves speech sounds that were **actually audible at that point within a 5-ft radius during the 6 elapsed seconds immediately before this cast began**. The casting's own V component therefore does not overwrite the sampled window.

   The chosen point immediately replays those speech sounds once, preserving their original language, approximate voice, overlap, timing, and relative volume. Listeners hear the replay under ordinary hearing rules. Multiple speakers remain overlapping if they originally overlapped; the spell does not isolate, translate, identify, verify, or explain them. Speech blocked from that point at the historical moment by actual silence/sound-blocking effects was not recorded. No speech means silence.

   Far Echo's own magically generated replay is **not eligible source speech for another Far Echo**, preventing recursive echo amplification. It does not create a creature, reproduce spell components as a new cast, issue magical commands/oaths/consent, or reach another plane/time window. Director need only preserve the relevant recent fictional speech facts, not maintain a continuous audio simulation. **NEW:** 5-ft capture area and pre-cast six-second window.

4. **Soul Mark** — **Action; 30 ft; one creature you perceive with LoE; V/S; 1 minute; no concentration.** No save is added. Apply an invisible, nonphysical divinatory mark to that creature. While the marked creature is on the **same plane and within 1 mile (5,280 ft)**, you continuously know its direction from you, including meaningful vertical bearing, but **not distance, exact location, route, identity changes, surroundings, or condition**.

   After initial application, ordinary walls/cover do not block the directional sense. A specific anti-divination/masking effect can block it according to its own scope. If the creature is beyond 1 mile, on another plane, dead, or specifically protected, you receive **no direction** and the spell does not tell you which reason applies.

   Shapechange, disguise, or appearance change does not transfer/remove the mark because creature identity persists. **Death ends the mark**; it does not become corpse tracking, soul tracking, resurrection tracking, or a pointer to a new body. One Soul Mark per caster; successful recast replaces the prior mark. Multiple casters may independently mark the same creature. No visible tag, allegiance information, or transferable marker object is created. **NEW:** exact direction/range/death/replacement interface.

5. **Sense Alignment** — **Action; 30 ft; one perceived creature or object with LoE; V/S; instantaneous.** No save is added. Privately learn the target's **actual current canonical alignment record**:
   - Lawful / Neutral / Chaotic axis;
   - Good / Neutral / Evil axis.

   A recorded axis is reported even if Director-hidden from the player unless an actual specific protection blocks or alters that information. An undefined/unknown axis reports **Indeterminate**, not an inferred value.

   An object is **Unaligned** unless the object itself has an explicit canonical alignment record. Its creator, owner, faction, iconography, material, or reputation does not make the object aligned. If an aligned object has an actual record, report it normally. A protection affecting only one axis leaves the other independently reportable if allowed.

   This reveals alignment only: no personality, beliefs, intent, oath status, crimes, faction, soul state, or truthfulness. The historical Movement tag grants no movement. **NEW:** exact two-axis/Unaligned/Indeterminate output.

6. **Glimpse Future** — **Action; Self; V/S; instantaneous glimpse plus pending initiative benefit; no concentration.** The raw 30-ft metadata grants no ally targeting because the printed effect describes the caster seeing the future.

   On resolution, the Director gives the caster a **brief qualitative glimpse of the most likely events during the next 6 seconds if current circumstances continue**. This may reveal an imminent motion, hazard, approach, attack, collapse, or absence of meaningful immediate change. It is not a complete transcript, does not expose future die results, and does not force creatures to follow the glimpse after choices change.

   Separately, for **1 minute** after application, the caster has advantage on their **next initiative roll**. Consume this entitlement on that initiative roll even if another source already grants advantage. It cannot reroll initiative already rolled for a current encounter. If no new initiative roll occurs before the one-minute endpoint, it expires. One pending Glimpse Future initiative benefit per creature; successful recasting replaces it rather than stocking multiple future combats. No Action, round, condition, or resource is reset. **NEW:** one-minute initiative-entitlement window.

7. **Detect Faction** — **Action; 30 ft; one perceived creature or object with LoE; V/S; instantaneous.** When casting, choose **one exact canonical `factionId` you know**. Learn one of:
   - **Affiliated** — target has an explicit current affiliation/membership record with that faction;
   - **Not Affiliated** — target has an explicit record establishing it is not affiliated with that faction;
   - **Indeterminate** — the game's current records do not establish either result, or an actual protection blocks the answer.

   Actual hidden membership/affiliation is legitimate spell information and returns **Affiliated** unless specifically protected. Positive/negative reputation alone is not membership. Current loyalty, intent, credentials, rank, and legal authority are separate facts and are not revealed.

   For an object, only an explicit institutional-affiliation/issuance record qualifies. A logo, counterfeit emblem, creator's membership, present owner, or visual style alone does not establish affiliation. The spell never fabricates faction records for otherwise undefined NPCs/objects or guesses among ambiguous faction IDs. **NEW:** one-faction binary/Indeterminate test.

8. **Resonance Sight** — **Action; Self; V/S; 1 round; no concentration.** Until the caster's first future regular-start marker, or +6 seconds outside initiative, the caster privately perceives a colored resonance aura around **ordinarily visible creatures/objects within 30 ft and LoE that have an actual RP pool**.

   The aura lets the caster read the subject's **exact current RP amount**. Color is the spell's private visual encoding of that number rather than a universal setting-wide color taxonomy. An actual RP pool at 0 reads as a dim/colorless zero aura; a subject with **no defined RP pool** has no resonance aura. Current changes to RP update the aura during the duration.

   It reveals **current RP only**: not maximum RP, Dissonance, Refrain, charges, class identity, spell list, HP, recovery history, or other resources. It does not create RP or show transfer flows unless another rule independently changes current RP. The aura is perceptible only to the caster, emits no real light, and does not reveal Invisible/Hidden creatures or satisfy sight through concealment/cover. **NEW:** exact-current-RP interpretation of the colored aura.

9. **Read Surface Thoughts** — **Action; 30 ft; one creature you perceive with LoE; V/S; instantaneous; WIS save.** The target makes its normal Wisdom save. On success, you learn nothing. On failure, you receive **one immediate surface thought occupying its conscious attention at resolution**: a brief phrase, image, emotion, sensory impression, or simple idea.

   This is not memory search, interrogation, truth compulsion, motive detection, future-intent guarantee, control, or psychic damage. A verbal thought remains in the language in which the target internally frames it; if you do not understand that language, you receive its words/sounds but no automatic translation. Images/emotions/nonverbal impressions are perceived as such.

   A creature currently lacking a conscious surface thought—such as an actually unconscious creature—provides no thought to read even on a failed save. Ordinary sleep does not automatically provide a readable conscious thought; dreams require an exact source to count. The spell itself does **not automatically alert the target** to successful/failed reading; ordinary perception of the audible/somatic casting or an actual mental-defense feature may do so. A successful save grants no future immunity. Recasting can read whatever thought is actually current at that later resolution, subject to a new save and normal Refrain. **NEW:** exact one-thought semantic boundary.

10. **Detect Weather** — **Action; 30 ft; choose one perceived point with LoE; V/S; instantaneous.** Receive an **accurate 24-hour weather forecast for the local outdoor region within approximately 1 mile of that point on the current plane**. Indoors, the forecast concerns the outdoor/local weather affecting that location rather than the room climate.

   The Director clearly reports, where relevant:
   - temperature band/trend;
   - precipitation type and approximate timing;
   - wind direction/strength;
   - weather-caused visibility changes;
   - significant storm or environmental-weather hazards.

   This is an actual useful forecast, not merely “weather may change.” It accounts for current natural processes and already-existing magical weather expected to continue. If a later voluntary spell, supernatural event, catastrophe, or other genuinely new cause changes the weather after casting, actual conditions may diverge from the earlier forecast; the spell did not promise prediction of every future agency choice. A weatherless environment returns that fact.

   It does not control weather, grant travel protection, reveal encounters/loot, refill resources, or predict unrelated future events. **NEW:** 1-mile local forecast region and explicit forecast fields.

11. **Sense Corruption** — **Action; Self; V/S; instantaneous.** Learn privately whether **at least one** creature, object, area, or active effect within **30 ft and LoE** currently carries an explicit canonical Corruption record/tag/state.

   Output is strictly **Yes/No presence**. No direction, distance, count, location, identity, type, severity, numeric track, source, hidden consequence, cure, alignment, disease, poison, or statistics are supplied. Actual hidden Corruption is valid information and produces **Yes** unless a specific protection suppresses detection. Missing/undefined records are not treated as Corruption and do not create a new global Corruption value.

   Total cover blocks this sensing area, consistent with the reviewed corruption/magic presence interfaces. An actual masking effect applies only within its written scope and does not automatically tell the caster that masking occurred. The spell neither cures nor protects against Corruption and creates no attack/fear/harvest permission. **NEW:** self-centered 30-ft LoE binary interface.
