# Adjacent companion evidence checked during the conference

These observations are source evidence, not new rules. The 100-source conference remains pending.

## Wizard elite acquisition

The original extracted Player Rulebook at docs/reference/Player_Rulebook_v6_source_text.txt, lines5318–5325, says Necromancers may maintain up to three elite undead and lists Boneflayer, Dreadblade and Wraithbound with roles. That passage supplies no binding action, time, RP payment or replacement-after-death procedure.

The same text is preserved in DescentReviewChanges/ReviewBible/data/playtest/class_sources/wizard.json under bookLines. The current wizard.engine[21–23] adds the already-authored bounded combat profiles, but does not establish a complete binding procedure.

Do not copy the Mage Lich's explicit long-rest return or Soulbound Ritual into the Wizard simply because the three pet names match.

## Ranger bond

The original extracted Player Rulebook line3889 says: “Beast Companion: Tame one beast from the bestiary (CR ≤ your level, INT < 7, not corrupted) as a permanent companion that scales with your Wisdom and level.”

The surrounding original Ranger passage preserves the permanent bond and eligibility, but does not supply exact bonding action/time/RP steps. **The current playtest card already fills those steps:** Ritual;4RP;1 hour with a willing/tamed eligible beast; touch;1/long rest. This is not a missing operational acquisition procedure. Its persistent injury and new-bond-after-long-rest restrictions remain the supplied contract; they are not an automatic full heal on redeclaration.

The conference's conditional question about absent action/time/cost must not override these printed current card fields. Any remaining question concerns death/recovery and state conversion, not an invented replacement bonding price or duration.

## Mage contrast

Original lines2989–2998 distinguish Soulbound Ritual (“Revive a fallen undead pet with bonus stats”) and Lich elite capacity/profile names. The current Mage engines/card supply explicit return procedures. Matching names alone do not transfer those procedures across classes.

## Ordinary spell is separate and unfinished

The whole-book search also found Command Undeath at line9577: “Permanently bind up to 2 CR 2 undead to caster.”

Its exact public spell record has ID command-undeath and status blocked-pending-operational-review in DescentReviewChanges/ReviewBible/data/public/spell_playtests/catalog.json. It is also present in incomplete_queue.json. This has not been promoted, used as an elite acquisition loophole, or inserted into the current 100 class-source count. Its separate rank, CR, targeting/control and duration procedure belongs to ordinary-spell development.

The search was scoped to the preserved Player Rulebook text and associated class/spell extracts. These observations do not establish that every other historical file lacks additional evidence.
