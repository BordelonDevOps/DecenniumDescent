101: Use the character's current Strength score, not the modifier or an imported monster score. Tiny's Inventory capacity and casualty Load are new provisional additions. The pound limits come from V6; Small, Medium and Large Load values come from the Gameplay Loop. Larger unsupported transport sizes require their own completed rule before this table can handle them.
102: 
103: Keep fractional Loads without rounding. An item with a printed Load uses that value. If it has only a known weight, its **provisional Load is pounds divided by ten**. A Load-only item may use ten pounds per Load as a clearly labeled estimate for ordinary hauling; this is insufficient for effects requiring documented physical mass.
104: 
105: Count a container's own empty Load plus the contents' Loads, unless an explicitly inclusive Load already covers those exact contents. Handle pounds separately: sum the empty container weight and contents' weights, or use an explicitly inclusive gross weight for the contents it covers. An inclusive Load does not make a printed weight inclusive, or vice versa. Apply this rule through nested containers and meld budgets so every item counts exactly once in each quantity.
106: 
107: With positive carried Load at or above half capacity and no greater than capacity, you are **Burdened**: reduce Speed by 10 feet and apply disadvantage to checks to climb, swim or move silently. An empty inventory is not Burdened, including at zero capacity. Above capacity, that inventory cannot be carried routinely; drop or transfer something, or use a legal alternative such as dragging or a vehicle. These threshold and zero-Load details are provisional clarifications.
108: 
109: Apply actual physical limits when handling a known weight. Between physical carry capacity and twice that capacity, pushing or dragging uses the lower of otherwise-current Speed and 5 feet. A Speed of 0 stays 0; a Speed of 3 stays 3. Lifting does not independently permit movement: moving while supporting a weight must satisfy carrying, pushing, dragging or another exact rule. An anatomy restriction, such as Hawk's prohibition on carrying a Medium ally, still applies even if numbers would fit. Burden penalties do not create hands, harnesses, lift or access to melded gear.
110: 
111: Transporting a creature must pass both checks: the carrier can physically handle the body's effective weight and associated equipment, and can accommodate the casualty Load plus the equipment's Load. Petrified multiplies the body weight by ten, not its casualty Load. Removing gear can reduce equipment weight and Load; it cannot reduce the body's weight or erase its condition.
123: For a voluntary change, declare and preflight the full item disposition before spending costs. A successful hostile or forced change resolves its mandatory custody consequences without deleting items or refunding its costs. This same overflow procedure applies at the first forced transformation if the target began with excess cargo; only gear within both starting ceilings can become hidden.
124: 
125: When someone else carries the transformed character, count its casualty Load plus both its melded and external equipment Load. Count the effective physical body weight plus all that equipment for actual weight handling. Melding therefore provides no weightless cargo for another carrier.
