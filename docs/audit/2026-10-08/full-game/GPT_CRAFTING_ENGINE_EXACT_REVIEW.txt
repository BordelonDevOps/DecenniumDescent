## Verdict: CONDITIONALLY APPROVE with 8 finite repairs

The framework is structurally sound and the major source conflicts are resolved coherently. The **2,400-hour V5 Opening supersession, trade progression, six-band crafting resolution, batch-value interpretation, Forge Dwarf bridge, finite harvesting, preservation clocks, research, market separation, new tool bindings, and station-rental framework are all acceptable as provisional authored playtest rules.**

Apply the eight exact repairs below. They do not require another generic framework conference; after they are applied as written, this framework can be recorded as:

**REVIEWED — authored operational PLAYTEST crafting/harvesting/preservation framework; individual recipe effects, balance, runtime, full expedition/Haven integration and wider economy remain open.**

1. **Explicitly block unreviewed recipe effects from campaign execution.** The current opening permits a table to “test” an unfinished recipe, but it needs to be impossible to interpret that as permission to use its unreviewed benefit. Replace that sentence with:

> An unreviewed recipe may be executed only as an isolated framework test of access, scheduling, ingredients, crafting check, quality, yield, trade XP, preservation or price-reference behavior. Its unfinished output effect grants no gameplay benefit, activation, statistic, condition, spell-like effect, charge, duration, resource change or market classification beyond fields already independently reviewed. Normal campaign use of the finished product requires that recipe's output/effect card to be separately completed and reviewed.

This keeps all 433 public formulas visible without pretending their effects are operational.

2. **A Flawed durable item's reduced maximum structural HP must not disappear through ordinary repair.** As written, a Flawed singleton can be converted to a Standard-strength item through the inexpensive generic repair procedure. That makes the crafting failure band largely temporary and conflates damage repair with craftsmanship correction. Replace the durable Flawed paragraph with:

> A quantity-1 Flawed durable item has maximum structural HP equal to half its ordinary maximum, rounded down, minimum 1, and begins at that reduced maximum. This reduced maximum is a crafting flaw, not ordinary structural damage. Ordinary equipment repair may restore current structural HP only up to that reduced maximum; it does not remove the flaw, increase the item's maximum, alter its equipment grade or convert it to Standard quality. Removing a crafting flaw requires an explicit reviewed rework procedure; absent one, the reduced maximum persists.

The consumable half-duration rule is otherwise approved.

3. **Critical-success harvesting must not resurrect a forfeited unselected category.** Replace:

> Critical success additionally preserves one otherwise damaged ordinary unit already present in the manifest...

with:

> Critical success additionally preserves one otherwise damaged ordinary unit **within the selected attempted allocation** that the manifest already recorded as damaged but potentially recoverable. If the selected allocation contains no such unit, Critical Success grants no additional unit. It cannot recover an unselected or already-forfeited category, duplicate an exceptional drop or exceed the manifest's finite anatomy.

This preserves the partial-harvest forfeiture rule.

4. **Make basic dismantling recovery deterministic.** “Recover up to 25%” leaves an unnecessary Director-controlled yield range after a successful check. Replace that part of the dismantling procedure with:

> Before work begins, record each recoverable ordinary component and the maximum quantity physically remaining in the item after known prior damage, loss or consumption. On Success, recover **25% of each recorded recoverable quantity**, rounded down under that component's normal unit rule. Explicitly divisible bulk material may use the framework's 0.01-source-unit precision. Failure recovers none. No later appraisal may increase the predeclared recoverable cap.

No sale-value conversion, catalyst recovery, formula extraction or magical-energy salvage is added.

5. **Define what happens when a reserved perishable becomes illegal during a long order.** Add after the rule requiring perishables to remain eligible through completion:

> If a required reserved component ceases to satisfy its recipe requirement before completion and no completed recipe phase expressly stabilized it, pause the order before further active work. Before resuming, the lead may reserve a fresh legal component satisfying that same requirement, including an already-legal substitution. The replacement lot keeps its own identity and age. Completed active-work phases remain completed, but any component already physically consumed or irreversibly processed before becoming unusable remains spent. If no legal replacement is supplied, abandon the order under the normal abandonment rule; do not make a completion or quality roll.

This closes both the “rotten ingredient still crafts” and “spoilage forces a free quality roll” exploits.

6. **Repair Mishap 5 so an absent assistant cannot lock a station forever.** Replace that row with:

> **5 — Fouled apparatus:** The station requires cleanup labor equal to **1 active labor-hour × the number of workers who participated in the failed order**. Any physically capable workers may perform that cleanup; multiple workers may contribute simultaneously. The station cannot support another crafting order until the total required cleanup labor is completed.

Example: lead + two assistants = 3 labor-hours; three available workers can finish that in one elapsed hour.

7. **Separate stored component tiers above VI from the generic six-tier material/harvest ladder.** Add to Material identity/substitution:

> An exact recipe component's stored numeric `tier` remains part of that component's identity and may be compared for that recipe's explicit substitution requirements even when the stored number exceeds 6. This does **not** extend the generic V4 material-tier ladder or generic harvesting entitlement beyond Tier VI. A Tier 7–10 stored component therefore receives no generic harvest DC, public availability, rarity right or substitution source merely from that number; it requires its actual recipe/source acquisition procedure or another separately reviewed rule.

This is particularly important for the preserved Tier 7–10 consumed `role=tool` components.

8. **Scope the two-active-Watch ceiling to this work framework, not the unresolved entire expedition loop.** Add immediately after the two-Watch sentence:

> For this supplement, an **active work Watch** means time committed to crafting, harvesting, preservation, trade training, formula research or assisting those activities. The two-Watch ceiling limits this framework's work contribution; it does not by itself resolve whether another reviewed subsystem permits a distinct noncrafting activity in the remaining Watch. Rest, Strain, fatigue and expedition legality continue to come from their own governing rules.

That avoids accidentally closing DD-39/40/41 through the crafting chapter.

### Consequential decisions approved

**Time and scheduling:** V5's **100 days / 2,400 elapsed hours** supersedes the stale V4 240-hour sentence. The 8-hour workday, 40-hour workweek and 160-hour workmonth conversions are accepted as active-work units. The five source-null one-year Director recipes may use the explicit **1,920-active-hour overlay**, while retaining their source nulls and unreviewed status. No time unit becomes free downtime.

**Trade entry:** I approve the new credential bridge. Possessing the trade's designated primary-tool proficiency may initialize that trade at **level 1 / 0 XP**. Tool ownership alone does not. The separate 80-active-hour / 10-gp default training route may grant the primary-tool proficiency plus trade level 1. This is an explicit candidate completion rather than something claimed from V4.

The `max(4, trade level)` access rule is also acceptable: an otherwise legal trained novice can perform skill-level 1–4 work, while untrained characters retain source disadvantage and cannot exceed 4. Formula access above level 4 remains independently required.

The **49 rank-band conflicts** are correctly resolved by making each recipe's exact `skillLevel` and explicit requirements authoritative over the descriptive rank-band ranges. The rank bands remain useful advancement descriptions, not a second hidden gate.

**Crafting checks:** the crafting system's six quality bands are a specific procedure and therefore properly replace the generic four task degrees for the completion roll. **Natural 1 = Mishap regardless of total. Natural 20 uses the rolled numerical total and receives no automatic quality-band promotion.** PB is added once; trade level itself is not added. Ordinary tools/facilities provide +0 unless something explicitly supplies a bonus. Recipe `maximumAssistants` wins over generic assistance.

Take Ten is correctly limited to safe suitable workshops, trained leads, recipes five skill levels lower, declaration before rolling, and no remaining disadvantage.

**Batch/value interpretation:** I approve `recommendedMarketValue` as the **Standard batch reference**, not per-unit value. The nominal unit reference is the exact batch-value/output-quantity ratio and is not itself guaranteed retail stock or a divisible merchant quote. This interpretation is the better reconciliation of the recipe economy and avoids multiplying the source recommendation by quantity a second time.

**Crafting quality versus equipment/artifact quality:** the separation is correct. Exceptional/Masterwork crafting results do not produce V6 Exceptional equipment statistics and do not invoke Core artifact Quality. A recipe must expressly bridge to an equipment grade or artifact classification.

**Forge Dwarf:** approve the bounded integration. For an eligible artisan-tool item creation, the source 25% time reduction applies. A **Standard successful crafting outcome is treated as Exceptional for that batch's crafting-quality yield/improvement choice**. Exceptional/Masterwork remain themselves; Flawed/Failed/Mishap do not upgrade. This is an explicit mechanical meaning for the ancestry's retained “exceptional quality” wording, but grants **no equipment +3, price multiplier, magic property, automatic success or additional XP**.

**Masterwork catalyst retention:** approved. Exactly one eligible consumed catalyst unit can survive, retaining its identity, tier and age; no catalyst means no return. Indivisible/explicitly destructive irreplaceable requirements remain ineligible. This is separate from ordinary Failed recovery.

The preserved **19 `role=tool, consumed=true` rows** remain consumed components. Do not bulk-convert them into reusable toolkits.

**Mishaps:** with repair 6, the 1d6 table is a sufficient bounded completion of the missing public mishap table. It does not create open-ended curses, diseases, permanent debuffs, lost tradeskills or secret lore. Specific reviewed volatile ingredients can later add declared hazards.

**Material identity/substitution:** source occurrence + name + tier + category + properties + physical form + role remains the correct identity basis. Same-name components are not fungible merely because names or prices match. Equal/higher exact component tier plus all required properties/role/form is a valid generic substitution only where the recipe permits substitution. Unique named components remain outside that permission.

**Harvesting:** the finite Director-side manifest is approved. The size yield is one **shared finite pool**, not one full size-yield roll per exposed category. This is the necessary anti-duplication interpretation of “four to six component categories” plus the single size-based yield table. Manifest information remains hidden until legitimately identified.

The generic Tier I–VI DC mapping, type-skill identification, one-minute identification, one trained assistant, extraction-trade choice, category allocation before partial selection, partial time formula, forfeiture of unselected ordinary categories, half-time hurried attempt with disadvantage, and one-roll exceptional-drop treatment are approved.

A `dropRate` repeated through multiple material records does not create repeated loot rolls for the same physical drop.

**Preservation:** the conservative default intervals of **1 h blood/organs, 30 min essence, 8 h meat, 12 h hide** are acceptable explicit completions where a more specific source does not exist. Age begins at death/removal from the sustaining environment and remains attached to the physical lot through harvesting, division, failure recovery, sale and transfer.

Fresh → Degraded → Spoiled is an acceptable bounded interpretation of the source's undefined quality-step loss. Degraded halves current lot value but does not alter its intrinsic material tier/properties. Spoiled ordinary material is unusable absent an explicit recipe.

The mundane 30-minute / 1-gp / 0.5-lb preservative treatment per ≤10-lb lot, legal container, appropriate trade and DC 10 is approved. It pauses accumulated spoilage for 24 hours; it neither resets age nor restores Fresh. Jars alone do not create preservation, and the rule does not solve magical essence/active-core containment.

**Research/transcription:** approved. Research does not invent an effect. Copying a legitimately accessible readable formula for 1 hour + 1 sp grants formula knowledge only. Reverse engineering/designing new magic or unique substitutions produces a proposal requiring its own review, not automatic mechanics from a high roll.

**Trade XP:** approved. The per-recipe stored 4-XP field is not added again. Standard/Exceptional/Masterwork count as successful crafts. First nontrivial success = 4, later nontrivial = 2, defined useful failed research = 1 under the anti-farm restriction, trivial = 0. Excess XP carries through sequential advancement to level 20.

**Tools and stations:** the five new mundane kits are acceptable at the proposed prices/weights. Tailoring → Weaver's tools and Skinning → Skinner's tools are acceptable explicit bindings. None grants proficiency merely through ownership. Auxiliary implements physically incorporated into a kit may satisfy their listed requirement; consumed incorporated components cannot simultaneously remain reusable kit pieces.

The **1 gp / eight station-hours** ordinary Haven rental baseline, proportional with final copper rounding, is acceptable as a default quote. It supplies ordinary fixed fixtures and operating heat/fuel only, not ingredients, primary-kit proficiency, protected storage, labor, rare reagents, magic power, legal access or a numerical check bonus.

**Economy/commissions:** finite buyer quotes, batch recommendation rather than guaranteed sale price, mundane resale versus actual-artifact valuation separation, and the source 50% commission deposit are sound. Contract terms must state who supplies ingredients and what happens on failure; there is no generic refund rule.

With the eight repairs above, this is sufficiently executable for players to know **whether they can attempt a formula, what equipment/formula/training they need, how long it takes, what they roll, how quality/failure resolves, how finite materials are harvested/preserved, and how a batch enters inventory/economic negotiation**, while still correctly withholding every unreviewed recipe's unique output mechanics.

That is the correct boundary. It does **not** approve the 687 individual recipe effects, their numerical balance, runtime deduction, full Supply/Expedition/Haven flow, transport, artifact economy, or the complete game/final GitHub flow. Cosmology remains entirely deferred until after the authorized push succeeds and the remote commit is verified.
