# DE4 complete procedures and review context

Pending exact authored review for the video game. This complete manuscript preserves agreed procedure text; historical pending labels require the separate acceptance receipt to establish current authored status. Runtime implementation, human playtesting and balance remain open.

## Shared rules

### status

NEW complete current local DE4 proposal only; original shared proposal retained separately. No adopted rule or source is changed.

### casting

All fifteen retain Action and V/S/F. Use the actual casting class’s rank, school, minimum class level, independently acquired repertoire and base RP price. A legal cast commits costs, concentration transitions and ordinary Dissonance/Refrain before interruption. Failure, immunity, missing information and countering give no refund or restoration of old concentration. Use actual casting ability + PB for attacks and 8 + PB + that modifier for save DC; ordinary saves are binary. No generic upcast, additional Action or inferred class-card access.

### time

Unless a card states otherwise, a minute ends at the caster’s tenth future original regular-start marker, excluding a start underway, or after 60 elapsed seconds if begun outside initiative. Retain original virtual markers or absolute deadlines through reorder, extra turns, absence and travel. Suppression never pauses a clock, refunds spent history or replays an application. Later saved-against casts do not dispel an older valid application. Extra turns do not supply extra periodic pulses, order opportunities or repeat saves.

### information

Use actual established facts and each information protection’s real subject and generating-source scope. Hidden unprotected facts can be read; missing facts are not invented. An unavailable answer does not identify the hidden reason. Ordinary observed outcomes and inferences remain possible. No new faction roster, plane, moral axis, cosmic force, secret doctrine or guaranteed destiny is authored. Current accepted expedition/faction procedures now supply their actual records and social limits. This spell proposal grants no membership, standing, allied status, permission, roster or institution merely by requesting information.

### control

A successfully established hard whole-Action/turn component spends the shared source-character/target/encounter guard once. Its continued valid duration does not claim a new allowance each turn. Initial prevention leaves no banked component; later suppression retains the existing clock and spent history. A blocked component gets no unprinted fallback. Actual component immunities remain separate; creature type alone is no blanket mental immunity. Limited attack or movement restrictions do not become whole-Action denial merely because alternatives are unattractive.

### newControlBindings

NEW binding for Dominate Person and Confusion: use the reviewed Enchantment compelled-budget arbitration and shared six-second outside-initiative control cycle, preserving already spent budgets on entry to real initiative. A valid order/table instruction receives its priority timestamp when issued or generated at that opportunity; simultaneous instructions use application order then stable source ID. A losing instruction gets no second budget and does not erase an earlier mandate. Actual ordinary Attack Actions use native attacks unless the card expressly limits them to one. Only actual required ordinary ammunition can be compelled; no optional finite resource, spell/RP, recharge, charge, special expendable ammunition, permanent property surrender, protected secret, knowing binding-oath violation, direct self-harm or knowingly suicidal route can be forced. Bonus Actions, Reactions and extra Actions remain target-controlled. Hostile conduct uses ordinary initiative before execution, never a free virtual pre-initiative attack.

### newCompanionBinding

NEW for an actually eligible Humanoid under Dominate Person, or any eligible Confusion target, using an owner-attached companion chassis: use the actual original-owner regular attached phase, original profile and structural command/offense caps. No independent turn, dominator companion allowance or unwilling owner Bonus Action is created. A pending command reservation can be redirected only for this same companion in this same phase, under the reviewed Dominate Beast bridge; spent or another companion’s reservation cannot be stolen. A repeat WIS save occurs at the end of that real regular attached phase even when no instruction executed; this source-specific checkpoint adds no ordinary START/END, recharge or other condition tick. If no such phase is currently supplied, only an END-only six-second save schedule continues, with no orders/actions. On transitioning to actual phases, map the next unresolved save to the next regular phase end, preserve resolved saves and the original maximum, and never replay an opportunity. This extension is proposed explicitly; it is not already conferred by Dominate Beast.

## Alignment Forecast

Action; range 60 feet; V/S/F; instantaneous. NEW: select one acting creature you perceive with line of effect, state one concrete intended action beginning within the next minute and one identified beneficiary/goal. Evaluate the most likely material consequences of already established alignment-dependent records during the hour after that beginning. Answer privately with Favored, Opposed, Mixed, Unaffected or Indeterminate. Favored means those actual alignment-dependent interactions materially aid the stated goal; Opposed means they hinder it; Mixed means both materially apply without one predominating; Unaffected means established facts support no material alignment-dependent interaction.
