## Relative source sequence

### CH01 — First Choir formation and assault — contested timing

The original presents unnamed chords, invitation/formation and active seal repair before the direct scarlet-fire assault opening the Sundering. The owner says the First Choir arose in response to Asmodeus attacking Calanthir.

**Limit:** Owner intent is recorded; exact event/awakening timing remains unresolved. Do not restore the withdrawn no-agency-before-assault or one-to-one Harmony mapping.

### CH02 — Pillar realms mature after the Sundering

The Planes Ascendant opening explicitly follows the Sundering and describes four differentiating realms; the next chapter begins with all four contributing to the Weave.

**Limit:** This is relative narrative order, not a numbered-year or floor mapping.

### CH03 — Watch Convocation and aspirant gates

During the Lucent Interregnum, nascent divinity leads the Choir to open access to seven long-vacant Thrones. A gate appears at an unnamed Mirrorsage monastery library.

**Limit:** The Thrones are described as older than this opening. The monastery has no supplied numbered floor; current gate availability is not selected.

### CH04 — Candescent Loom and Weave refraction

After the Argent Gate shines, the narrative describes small acts leaving visible intention records, called the Candescent Loom.

**Limit:** A source episode does not automatically make every intention visible through current spells or provide a universal moral detection rule.

### CH05 — Pact sentience

Accumulated resonance gives the Pact a will distinct from a god or Choir chord in the narrative.

**Limit:** Do not use this development to identify the Heartcell builder or assign a calendar date.

### CH06 — Archon sites and living records

Within the Pact-sentience chapter, Valcendyr forms the Oath Archive in Crystal Bastion and Quorilith establishes Library of Shifting Sigils.

**Limit:** These are source-established events, with no chosen present-campaign access or Equation holdings.

### CH07 — Shardfall and Silver Prism Year

The Moment of First Scatter is called Shardfall; the ensuing Silver Prism Year has elastic length measured by transformed lives. Sixth Archive cartographers meet at Sigil Navigators Library.

**Limit:** Do not convert the Year into one standard 400-day year or treat Sixth Archive as Floor 6. The library alias remains unresolved.

### CH08 — Epoch of Chosen Threads

The Silver Prism Year closes; scholars call the transition the Epoch of Chosen Threads. Community story keys can manifest resource-bearing Dungeon enclaves in the source.

**Limit:** Epoch terminology supplies no fixed elapsed duration or automatically usable new portal mechanic.

### CH09 — Counter-expression and Unbound Academy

The Matrix broadcast prompts the Spiral Integral counter-expression. Quiet Wells later collapse and the Matrix equation fades; after the Sunrise of Chosen Threads a council builds the Unbound Academy.

**Limit:** The narrative subsequently develops a renewed threat; this episode is not proof that all Oblivion danger has permanently ended or that Academy access is guaranteed now.

### CH10 — Quiet Masquerade and Citadel of Many Echoes

Following the Masquerade confrontation, the Academy and Yieldheart guilds build the Citadel at Crystalfall Summit. Its galleries preserve songs, prototypes and restricted chronicles; later the Matrix develops deliberate calculus.

**Limit:** Do not merge the Citadel with a numbered dungeon floor or assume Selene’s foundation journal is freely accessible.

### CH11 — Matrix Horizon and imminent campaign

The Dawn Loom concert identifies the approaching Matrix Horizon. A later chapter places its arrival on the twentieth eve after the Citadel Vigil.

**Limit:** The passage gives a relative marker, not a bridge to AE dating. Do not equate the Vigil with the Citadel’s founding date without evidence.

### CH12 — High Chancel ascent

The fellowship ascends a ladder of living light toward the High Chancel to confront the Matrix edge.

**Limit:** The route is not a normal descent to Floor 97 or Floor 100 merely because the same saga discusses the Dungeon.

### CH13 — Silent Library and Final Ledger

Inside the Matrix, the Silent Library tries to reduce cognition to zero. Memory-rope and song stabilize its books; the Final Ledger is answered and blanks before the walls open onward.

**Limit:** Keep the encounter’s transformation. An untouched reusable library, a fixed floor or guaranteed access is not supplied.

### CH14 — Shard of Absolute Rest, victory and Charter

The black Shard of Absolute Rest splits; the Matrix curve scatters and Null breaches seal. The Charter of Infinite Yield follows. Ul nak’s dream recedes rather than vanishing; Stillness may try again in ages.

**Limit:** Do not identify this shattered shard with Q6’s focusing crystal, or convert receding dream into death of the survivor/Old God. No identity bridge is established.

### CH15 — Explicit post-Proof-War epilogue

Movement One explicitly places its scene after the Proof War. Later movements give the Spiral cosmograph and Choir chorus around the Charter.

**Limit:** The document’s later setting does not select the current campaign epoch. Map symbols and coils are not numbered dungeon floors.

### CH16 — Epilogue toolbox and express portability

Dungeon seeds are headed after Infinite Yield, and three arcs continue beyond the epilogue. P324 expressly permits tables and relics to be used independently of the saga or in multi-era campaigns.

**Limit:** Preserve both original placement and permission to adapt. Portability does not choose a floor, public truth, chronology or automatic mechanical replacement. Reuse the existing seventeen EPI groups.
