## 3. Magic Missile Storm — DRAFTABLE

**Action; 30 ft; V/S/F; no attack/save.**

Create exactly **three auto-hit missiles**.

Before resolution, allocate each missile to one of **one to three distinct perceived creatures within 30 ft and LoE**. Multiple missiles may be allocated to one creature.

Each missile:

- deals **1d4+1 force** — **NEW damage-type completion**;
- rolls its own `1d4`;
- is a **separate auto-hit and separate damage packet**.

Thus three missiles on one creature are three separate packets for:

- DR;
- temporary HP consumption;
- Wounds;
- concentration checks;
- actual once-per-hit/packet callbacks.

They are not one `3d4+3` packet.

Allocations lock before the first missile resolves. Death, Shield, immunity, etc. do not permit free retargeting.

### Shield compatibility — NEW SOURCE-QUALIFIED BRIDGE

The current ordinary Shield expressly protects against **“Magic Missile,”** yet the 848-ID ordinary spell catalog contains no separate `Magic Missile`; **Magic Missile Storm is the only ordinary missile-named spell**, while Mage Arcane Missile is a distinct class feature. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}

Therefore:

> **For the existing Shield spell's named Magic Missile exception only, Magic Missile Storm counts as Magic Missile. This is an interaction bridge, not a global alias or renaming of either identity.**

Consequences:

- when missile allocation designates a Shield-eligible willing creature, that counts as Shield's “targeted by Magic Missile” Reaction trigger;
- Shield may be cast after allocation and before that creature's first missile damage resolves;
- while Shield remains active, **every Magic Missile Storm missile allocated to that protected creature deals no damage**;
- the missiles remain expended/allocated and are not retargeted;
- their auto-hit event is not rewritten into a miss simply because Shield prevents their damage.

Mage **Arcane Missile remains separate** and is **not** covered by this bridge.

No physical projectile objects/recovery.

## 5. Arcane Shield Shatter — DRAFTABLE

This needs a deliberately narrow anti-shield interface.

**Action; 30 ft; V/S/F.**

Target:

> **one individually perceptible ongoing shield-spell effect within 30 ft and LoE that currently protects one creature you can perceive; designate that protected creature as the damage recipient.**

### NEW — “shield spell” criterion

An eligible effect must:

1. be an **ongoing effect generated by a spell**; and
2. be explicitly identified by its own source as a **shield, barrier, or ward**; and
3. presently provide direct defensive protection such as AC, damage prevention/reduction/absorption, attack interception, or a defensive barrier.

Therefore:

- ordinary **Shield** qualifies;
- explicitly named/authored magical shield/barrier/ward effects can qualify;
- generic healing does not;
- ordinary temp HP does not qualify merely because it feels “shield-like”;
- generic resistance buffs do not qualify unless their source explicitly identifies the effect as shield/barrier/ward;
- physical shields do not qualify;
- magic items, permanent enchantments, class engines and creature traits do not qualify.

An individually perceptible/identifiable effect is still required, matching the existing targeted-dispel boundary. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="3"}

### NEW — rank ceiling

Arcane Shield Shatter automatically affects only shield-spell effects generated by **actual spell rank 0 or 1**.

No check for higher ranks is invented. Higher-rank shields require another legitimate dispel mechanism.

This keeps the rank-1 specialist narrower than Dispel Magic (Lesser)'s reviewed rank-0–2 general dispel.

### Resolution

1. End the selected eligible shield effect using its ordinary ending/dependency procedure.
2. Then deal **1d8 force** to the designated creature it was protecting.

The source's literal **“arcane damage”** is therefore interpreted as **force for this spell only**.

> **NEW spell-local interpretation: Arcane Shield Shatter's “arcane damage” = force damage. This creates no global rule that “arcane” always means force.**

If the selected effect cannot legally be shattered, **no damage occurs**.

Ending rules:

- if the selected effect is the entire maintained spell application, ending it invokes its normal spell/concentration ending;
- if it is an independently separable shield component of a larger spell, only that component ends unless the parent source explicitly links sibling effects/concentration to it;
- no restoration/refund of resources.

**Balance flag:** specialist automatic anti-shield dispelling plus damage is strong even with rank≤1 and must be tested.

## 11. Witch Bolt — DRAFTABLE

The ongoing source effect overrides the raw Instantaneous placeholder.

**Action; 30 ft; one creature perceived with LoE; V/S/F; NEW ranged spell attack; concentration up to 1 minute.**

- miss → no tether and no sustained effect.

No repeat save.

### Maintenance

The initial cast gives **no free same-turn maintenance pulse**, even if the caster later gains another Action.

Beginning with the caster's **next future regular turn**, once on each caster regular turn the caster may spend its **ordinary Action** to maintain/pulse the tether.

If the target is presently:

- still the same continuing target;
- within 30 ft;
- perceived by the caster;
- within LoE;

the maintenance Action deals **1d12 lightning automatically**, with no new attack/save.

No additional RP is paid for maintenance. The original 2 RP bought the ongoing spell.

Extra turns do **not** create additional pulse opportunities.

If the target is temporarily unseen/out of range/out of LoE, the caster simply cannot pulse that opportunity. **The spell does not automatically end** for temporary ineligibility; concentration may continue and a later regular-turn pulse can resume if eligibility returns.

No Action spent → no damage that round, but concentration may remain.

### Outside initiative

Use six-second caster pulse opportunities corresponding to regular-turn cadence:

- first possible pulse at +6 seconds after the initial application;
- then each +6 seconds;
- each pulse still consumes one ordinary Action-length behavior opportunity;
- entering initiative maps the next unresolved pulse opportunity to the caster's next actual regular turn;
- already-used virtual Actions/pulses are not replayed;
- original 60-second maximum remains.

When continuing hostile pulses require actual combat sequencing, establish initiative rather than generating free pre-initiative attacks.

No automatic ten pulses from one Action.

**NEW:** attack, concentration minute, future-regular-turn Action maintenance, automatic subsequent pulse, no maintenance RP and clock bridge.

## 12. Zephyr Strike — DRAFTABLE

The printed **Action / 30-ft range** is preserved.

**Action; one willing creature you perceive within 30 ft and LoE, including yourself; V/S/F; concentration up to 1 minute.**

No attack is included in the casting Action.

While maintained, the recipient gains two effects:

### Movement rider

Its voluntary movement using its **ordinary existing Speed/movement allowance** does not provoke **opportunity attacks caused by that movement**.

This does not suppress:

- all Reactions;
- reactions with unrelated triggers;
- forced-movement rules;
- movement costs;
- difficult terrain.

No extra movement or +Speed is added.

### Pending strike

Once before the spell ends, when the recipient **hits with a weapon attack**, that attack deals an additional **1d8 force**.

- a miss does not consume it;
- only one hit receives the bonus, even within Extra Attack/Multiattack;
- the actual hit consumes the damage rider;
- the force die is part of that weapon-hit damage event and follows ordinary critical-dice handling;
- it supplies no free weapon attack or Attack Action.

After the bonus damage is consumed, the movement protection may continue until concentration/expiry.

One active Zephyr Strike application per recipient across casters; a later successful application replaces the older one rather than stacking multiple pending `1d8`s.

Initial 30-ft range is not an ongoing tether.

**NEW:** willing-recipient interface, concentration minute, one-hit pending rider and movement/OA scope.
