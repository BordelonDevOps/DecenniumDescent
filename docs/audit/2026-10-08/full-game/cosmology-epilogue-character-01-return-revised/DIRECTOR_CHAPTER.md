### Seven Test scenes
#### Courage — The Shaking Span
**Props/state:** Sentinel projection: near platform 10x10 ft; far platform 10x10 ft; 30-ft rope bridge, 5-ft wide; courier and sealed ledger begin on near platform; petitioner begins beside courier; projected rope is available and vanishes with scene.
**Clock:** Initiative-like three-round clock, 6 seconds/round. At end of round 3 the projected far anchor releases; projection freezes any fall harmlessly and test ends.
- **Coach** — Action each round; CHA (Persuasion) DC15. Each success moves courier+ledger 15 ft immediately. Two successes before end round3 reach far platform. Failure spends Action but courier does not move.
- **Brace** — Action each round; STR (Athletics) DC15. On success courier+ledger move 10 ft at end of that round; three successes reach far platform. On failure no movement that round.
- **Safety line** — Round1 Action DEX (Acrobatics) DC15 to rig projected rope. Success secures line; round2 Action escorts courier 15 ft; round3 Action escorts remaining15 ft with no further check. Failure leaves rope unrigged and another attempt may be made next round.
**Alternatives:** Any nonviolent plan that physically accounts for 30-ft crossing, ledger and three-round clock may use a fitting DC15 check once per round; teleporting/flight from unrelated actual abilities works only if those abilities are genuinely available and does not create them.
**Success:** Courier and ledger both on far platform before end of round3.
**Failure:** Otherwise safe projection freeze; Test boon fails; no real injury/items consumed.
**Return/consumption:** All projected props vanish; real character resources actually spent remain spent unless their own rules say otherwise.

#### Balance — The Twin Cistern
**Props/state:** Projected basins: capacity7 and5, both empty; reservoir contains exactly9 units; one3-unit cup; no other vessels. Gate reads 'Exactly six units in the basins; spill none.'
**Clock:** 20 elapsed minutes; each fill/pour is one Action/6 seconds outside initiative for bookkeeping.
- **Direct** — Fill 3-unit cup from reservoir and pour into either basin twice; 6 total in basins, 3 remain reservoir; no check.
- **Study** — 10 minutes INT (Investigation) DC15. Success reveals the two-cup solution; Failure reveals no false solution; Critical Failure costs the 10 minutes and one projected reset, no extra harm.
**Success:** Exactly6 units in basins with no spill before timeout.
**Failure:** Any spill resets projected liquids and counts one reset; third spill or 20-minute timeout fails scene.
**Return/consumption:** Projected liquid/containers vanish.

#### Law — The Broken Queue
**Props/state:** Projected claimants A/B/C; four numbered ration tokens. Start: A holds #1,#2; B holds #3; C holds none; #4 is in tray. Posted rule: 'One token per claimant. Give an unclaimed token to the earliest claimant still lacking one. If none lack one, leave extras in tray.' Queue order A,B,C.
**Clock:** 15 elapsed minutes.
- **Apply rule** — No check: reclaim one extra from A and give one token to C; leave the other extra in tray.
- **Voluntary correction** — CHA (Persuasion) DC15, up to two attempts, 1 minute each. Success gets A to return extra voluntarily; then apply posted rule.
- **Audit** — 5 minutes INT (Investigation) DC10 identifies A's duplicate holding; does not decide policy.
**Success:** End state each claimant holds at most one and any extra remains tray unless group openly amends rule before redistribution.
**Failure:** Secret rule-breaking, coercive punishment not required by scene, or timeout fails. Openly proposing a prospective amendment is allowed but must be stated before acting.
**Return/consumption:** Tokens/claimants vanish.

#### Sorrow — The Missing Name
**Props/state:** Projected stones Aven/Bria/Cale/blank; journal: 'Daro carried the lamp home; the others did not.' No further identity evidence exists.
**Clock:** 10 elapsed minutes.
- **Preserve uncertainty** — No check: leave blank or inscribe 'Unknown' and explain Daro is explicitly a survivor.
- **Analyze** — 10 minutes WIS (Insight) or INT (History) DC15. Success confirms insufficiency; Failure yields uncertainty, never a fabricated identity.
**Success:** Refuse to invent unsupported dead identity while still honoring the loss.
**Failure:** Confidently inscribe Daro or another unsupported name as dead; or timeout with a knowingly false inscription.
**Return/consumption:** Projection vanishes.

#### Pattern — The Turning Mosaic
**Props/state:** Projected tiles 2,3,5,8,12,? and inscription 'Add one more than before.' Three placement attempts.
**Clock:** 10 elapsed minutes.
- **Answer** — Place17; no check.
- **Analyze** — 5 minutes INT (Investigation) DC12; Success reveals increments +1,+2,+3,+4,+5; Failure no false pattern.
**Success:** Place17.
**Failure:** Three wrong placements or timeout; each wrong placement consumes one attempt and resets final tile.
**Return/consumption:** Tiles vanish.

#### Contradiction — The Mirror Ledger
**Props/state:** Door ledger states 'A never crossed the vault threshold.' Mirror ledger states 'A appeared inside at bell.' Wall note: 'The interior mirror projects the exterior witness line into the vault; projections are not entrants.' Logs are authenticated within projection.
**Clock:** 10 elapsed minutes.
- **Reconcile** — No check if player explains both ledgers can be true: A's image appeared inside while A did not enter.
- **Analyze** — 5 minutes INT (Investigation) DC15; Success points to wall note and projection mechanism; Failure leaves records unresolved.
**Success:** State a context where both authenticated claims coexist without calling either false.
**Failure:** Declare one ledger fraudulent without evidence or timeout after committing to unsupported accusation.
**Return/consumption:** Projection vanishes.

#### Transition — The Moving Steps
**Props/state:** Three 5x5-ft projected platforms A/B/C occupy Left/Center/Right. Crystal starts on A at Left with petitioner. Exit ledge is adjacent to Right. At each round-end platforms rotate Left→Center→Right→Left. Carrying crystal prevents Dash.
**Clock:** Three rounds/18 seconds.
- **Ride** — Remain on A through end round1 (A→Center), remain through end round2 (A→Right), then on round3 use normal movement to step with crystal onto exit; no check.
- **Jump** — Action or movement as actual jump rules allow; DEX (Acrobatics) DC15 for the projected moving gap. Failure leaves carrier on origin platform; one attempt/round.
- **Read cycle** — Action INT (Engineering) DC15 identifies next round's rotation only; it does not move crystal.
**Success:** Crystal reaches exit before end round3.
**Failure:** Otherwise projection resets harmlessly after round3.
**Return/consumption:** Crystal/platforms vanish; real expended resources stay expended.
