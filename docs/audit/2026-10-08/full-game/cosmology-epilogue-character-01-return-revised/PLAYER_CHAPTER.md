# Epilogue Character Module — Player Chapter

> **Status:** NEW PROVISIONAL AUTHORSHIP for EPI-01 through EPI-07. This is a playable candidate, not recovered canon and not runtime/human/balance/whole-game/publication approval.

## 1. Home affiliation, Inspiration and Disquiet

Choose one affiliation: Bastion of Flame, Veiled Expanse, Radiant Currents, or Boundless Void. This is a philosophical/spiritual affiliation only; it grants no planar origin, travel, resistance, language, or hidden lore.

At each beneficial Long Rest completed away from that chosen realm, review the preceding 24 elapsed hours. If qualifying conduct was only aligned, gain one Epilogue Inspiration die. If it was only knowingly contrary, gain Disquiet. If both occurred, or neither occurred, gain neither. A foreseeable contrary commitment should be warned before commitment.

**Epilogue Inspiration.** Two total slots maximum. Start with 0 ready and 0 spent-recoverable. Spend one ready d6 before an eligible ability check and add it to the total. Spending changes that slot to spent-recoverable. A normal gain restores one spent slot first; otherwise it creates one ready slot if fewer than two total slots exist. Courage motif restores only a spent-recoverable slot. A successful Archon Test sets both slots ready. Inspiration cannot be combined with Conflict Die on the same check.

**Disquiet.** One pending d6 maximum. Record `gainedAt`; it expires 24 elapsed hours later. On the next ability check before expiry subtract 1d6. That check cannot use Epilogue Inspiration or Conflict Die. Disquiet is not Corruption, KT/PT, Dissonance, Strain, or Exhaustion.

## 2. Seven Choir motifs

The Expedition begins this module with one finite Seven-Motif Folio containing the seven implemented contours. Copies preserve access but do not create more uses. Learning one motif takes 1 uninterrupted hour from the folio, a faithful copy, or the temporary onboarding Witness Maelin Quill. The learner must have an applicable Performance/instrument proficiency.

The Expedition has one shared Motif Resonance allowance. It starts at 1. It may refresh to 1 after a registered member completes a beneficial Long Rest only if at least 24 elapsed hours have passed since the ledger's last refresh. Party splitting, merging, renaming, or staggered rests do not reset the ledger.

Except Law, activation is 1 uninterrupted audible minute outside an encounter. Fix performer and willing listeners at commitment. Performer can listen if otherwise eligible. A legal activation spends the allowance at commitment before saves/effects. Resistance or immunity does not refund it. Invalid preflight with no legal target/effect does not spend it.
### Courage
Each legal listener may restore 1 spent-recoverable Epilogue Inspiration slot to ready, personal cap 2. A listener with no spent-recoverable slot gains nothing; Courage never creates a first never-earned die and restores no class/resource engine.

### Balance
Choose one willing listener and exactly one legal prepared-loadout family they actually possess. Druid spells: swap one currently prepared ordinary druid spell for another legal druid spell within rank/preparation rules. Druid 11+ Arch forms: swap one of the two currently attuned Arch forms for another unlocked Arch form; if the removed form is currently assumed, that form ends immediately under normal Shapeshift ending rules, with no refunded shift/reserve. Wizard: swap one prepared ordinary matrix for another legal spell already present in the Wizard's accessible spellbook; no-book means no Wizard swap. Existing Dissonance/Refrain histories persist. Any Matrix Mastery modification or linked-pair record tied to a removed matrix ends/pauses exactly as its source requires and is not cloned onto the replacement. Warrior: swap one equipped mutation for another owned/unlocked mutation satisfying actual tree/tier/slot gates; if the removed mutation is active, its effect ends immediately. Preserve serum doses, Strain, BT, HP, current/max-HP consequences, cooldowns and recovery reserves; do not refill or clamp upward. No pending spell is approved.

### Law
COMBAT EXCEPTION. As an Action, performer audibly presents the Law motif and chooses one revealed hostile creature within 30 feet that can hear it; also fix the protected-listener set as willing allied creatures within 30 feet that can hear the Action. Spend shared allowance before the target's binary Wisdom save vs Motif DC. On failure, for one round (until the same initiative marker next round; 6 elapsed seconds outside initiative, with no rebasing if initiative starts) the target cannot voluntarily attack or use hostile targeted abilities against protected listeners. It may speak, move, defend, Dodge, Disengage, refuse terms, or act nonhostile. The effect ends immediately when any protected listener directly attacks the target, whether that attack hits or misses; intentionally including the target in a hostile area effect also counts as a direct attack and breaks before resolving that attack/effect. Attacks by third parties outside the protected set and environmental damage do not break it. Save success/immunity gives no effect and no refund. This compels only a pause.

### Sorrow
Ordinary 1-minute performance grants each legal listener one pending Sorrow reroll for 24 elapsed hours. When that listener later fails one Wisdom saving throw, after seeing failure but before the failed-save consequence is committed, they may consume Sorrow to reroll that save once using the same save procedure/modifiers that applied to the original roll. The reroll replaces the original even if worse. It cannot rewind committed consequences, death, or an already-resolved save and cannot reroll the same save again through Sorrow.

### Pattern
Each listener gains one pending Pattern benefit for 24 elapsed hours: advantage on the next ability check whose primary task is study, research, deciphering, analysis or recall. Consumed when used. Duplicate Pattern benefits do not stack or extend.

### Contradiction
Each listener gains one pending Contradiction benefit for 24 elapsed hours: advantage on the next Wisdom (Insight) check whose declared purpose is discovering a creature's hidden motive or internal inconsistency. It is not truth detection. Consumed when used. Duplicates do not stack or extend.

### Transition
At completion choose one existing eligible workOrderId and up to four named participating workers who heard the motif. For each chosen worker, halve that worker's then-unperformed required ACTIVE work on that work order. Compute half of remaining active minutes, rounding fractional minutes up to the next whole minute; never increase remaining work and never reduce positive remaining work below 1 minute. Apply Transition once per underlying workOrderId. Eligible: mask shaping, 4-hour research/synthesis, 8-hour decipher, ordinary crafting active work, and fixed rituals/complex tasks whose exact rule measures active work. Ineligible: rests, travel, waits, observation windows, Opening/Horn clocks, spell/feature durations, Knowledge Transfer duration, training/level gates, and tasks missing prerequisites/materials. Existing exact time modifiers apply first to establish current required active work; Transition then halves the unperformed remainder. It creates no assistant qualification or shared clock.

**Transition timing.** Existing exact time changes establish the current required active work first. Transition halves the then-unperformed active work, rounded fractional minutes upward. It applies once per work-order identity, names at most four workers, and never shortens rests, travel, waits, observation windows, spell/feature duration, Knowledge Transfer duration, level/training gates, or missing prerequisites/materials.

## 3. Unfinished vow

SOURCE-PRESERVING MODULE DEFAULT: every player character begins this module with exactly one active unfinished fictional vow, recorded as one sentence describing an ideal action not yet fulfilled. For an existing campaign, create it at module adoption before using EPI-03/04. This is not optional within this adopted module, but later revision/retirement agency remains as below.

A character has at most one active unfinished vow. Fulfillment grants no automatic XP/resource/recovery. A vow may later be revised after 10 uninterrupted minutes and communication to an understanding ally/Sentinel, or retired. The old vow becomes superseded/retired. This never clears conditions or resource tracks.

Any mask bound to a fulfilled, retired or superseded vow becomes dormant. Rebinding that same physical mask to the current active vow takes 1 uninterrupted hour and does not reroll quality or refresh reveal.

## 4. Living and Cracked Masks

Each PC receives one initial class-reflective blank when this module is adopted. The Expedition also has exactly one keyed replacement blank per PC. Those reserves do not replenish automatically. A mask project uses 8 active hours of personal shaping, pausable only after completed 1-hour blocks unless Transition creates a shorter final remainder. Record project start before the first block; the project expires exactly 168 elapsed hours later if unfinished.

Before shaping begins, communicate the active vow to one willing understanding ally or Sentinel. Privately record 0–3 fictional withheld facts; final crafting DC is 10 + that number, fixed when the final 60 minutes of actual remaining shaping begin. Roll one check with the class mask key. Success/Critical Success = Living; Failure/Critical Failure = Cracked.

One mask may be operative per wearer. Don it as an Action. While worn, either Living or Cracked gives advantage on saves explicitly against fear effects and Null lullabies. Cracked also gives disadvantage on a social ability check when deception would materially impede the active vow.

New vows, rebinds, repairs, replacement masks and operative-mask swaps do not refresh it. As an Action choose one legal creature within 20 feet that can perceive the mask; spend charge before its binary Charisma save against 8 + PB + the fixed mask-key modifier. Failure = Stunned for exactly one round; same initiative marker next round in initiative, or 6 elapsed seconds outside initiative, with no rebasing if initiative starts/stops. Immunity/hard denial still applies and never refunds a spent legal activation.

Remove safely on your own turn as a Free Action while stating one truthful current emotional-state statement; outside your turn use a Reaction if available. If speech is unavailable, an understood signed/written statement takes an Action. Ordinary removal without the truth statement is still possible as an Action and creates no hidden curse.

## 5. Virtues and Conflict Die

Virtue/class pairings describe resonance, not alignment or class restrictions. At each session start choose one actual present class, name one real class strength, and choose one virtue in meaningful fictional tension with it. Once per session, before an eligible ability check, roll d20 and d12 together and add the d12. Never use on attacks or saves, and never combine with Epilogue Inspiration.

On a successful fixed-DC result, resolve the successful check, then gain 1 core Exhaustion. On opposed checks higher total wins; a tie leaves the state unchanged, so the initiating user fails and gains no Exhaustion. At Exhaustion 5, current disadvantage on ability checks applies before the d12 is added; success then raises Exhaustion to 6 and resolves the current lethal rule. A creature immune to Exhaustion at declaration cannot use Conflict Die.

## 6. Archon petitions

Eligible petitioners are Clerics, Monks, or characters with a pre-existing spiritual-mentor record. The finite onboarding Witness is Maelin Quill where actually accessible; no remote Witness is created.

Procedure: 10 uninterrupted minutes consultation; 1 uninterrupted hour meditation in a legal quiet space; 10-minute virtue symbolic act; then roll 1d12. Only the response roll starts the 7-day petition cooldown.

**1–4 Guidance:** advantage for 24 elapsed hours on the model's virtue-linked ability checks.

**5–8 Blessing:** gain the fixed temporary Base feature for that virtue until next module dawn. The card keeps its own envelope:
- **Courage — Combat Instinct**: Activation Passive; Cost 0; Target Self; Card duration Permanent; Frequency Always. Combat Instinct. Gain +1 on initiative rolls. You cannot be Surprised while conscious unless the opposing creature's Stealth check exceeds your passive Perception by 5 or more. This does not grant extra Actions or movement before combat. Temporary ownership ends at module dawn.
- **Balance — Natural Explorer**: Activation Passive; Cost 0; Target Self; Card duration While known; Frequency Continuous. Advantage on Survival checks in natural terrain; ignore nonmagical difficult terrain caused by vegetation, loose earth, mud or snow. Magical terrain, climbing and hazards still require their own rules. Temporary ownership ends at module dawn.
- **Law — Tactical Awareness**: Activation Passive; Cost 0; Target Self; Card duration During combat; Frequency Continuous. Gain+2 Perception and initiative. Does not reveal unseen creatures automatically or allow attacks without line of fire. Temporary ownership ends at module dawn.
- **Sorrow — Alchemical Knowledge**: Activation Passive; Cost 0; Target One potion/substance examined for 1 minute; Card duration Check only; Frequency At will. Gain advantage on INT(Arcana) or alchemist-tool checks to identify a substance's mundane ingredients and likely purpose. Identification does not guarantee safety or supply a recipe/materials; failure yields uncertainty, not false certainty. Temporary ownership ends at module dawn.
- **Pattern — Scholarly Mind**: Activation Passive; Cost 0; Target Self; Card duration Permanent; Frequency Always. Scholarly Mind. Tags: base, passive, knowledge. You have advantage on Intelligence checks whose primary purpose is recalling, researching or analyzing arcane theory, magical history, planar magical principles or constructed spell formulae. It does not grant advantage on spell attacks, Counterspell checks or general Investigation unrelated to arcane scholarship. Temporary ownership ends at module dawn.
- **Contradiction — Trap Intuition**: Activation Passive; Cost 0; Target Self; Card duration While searching for traps; Frequency Continuous. Advantage on Perception/Investigation to notice physical traps and their triggers. Does not reveal every trap automatically or defeat concealed magic. Temporary ownership ends at module dawn.
- **Transition — Unarmored Speed**: Activation Passive; Cost 0; Target Self; Card duration Permanent while unarmored; Frequency Always. Unarmored Speed. Tags: base, passive, mobility. While wearing no armor and using no shield, increase walking Speed by 5 feet and gain +1 on initiative rolls. At Monk 11 the Speed increase becomes 10 feet. This bonus does not multiply other Speed increases and is suppressed while armored. Temporary ownership ends at module dawn.

**9–11 Test:** use the virtue's finite Sentinel challenge. Success sets the same two-slot Epilogue Inspiration ledger to 2 ready dice. Failure creates VowReviewPending; petitions remain locked until the player revises/retires the vow or reaffirms it after 10 minutes. Reaffirmation gives Disquiet at that completion timestamp. There is no forced 24-hour rewrite.

**12 Revelation — Concord Step Sigil:** it is usable only after the granting session closes and before the next session begins, during actual campaign time. It provides one outbound step from the designated Aethel threshold to one enabled primary-realm Concord Alcove, then one personal return authorization per outbound traveler for 8 elapsed hours. It is not a spell, does not bypass Dungeon gates, and creates no rest/time.

## 7. Sentinel information encounters

Each authored Sentinel encounter can grant at most one information boon. Ask one earnest in-fiction question within that Sentinel's predefined knowledge. If out of scope it says it does not know; one follow-up within 1 minute must narrow the same subject, not switch topics. Then the encounter is spent.

Three Lamps example: exactly one plaque is true. First says “Second opens the door.” Second says “This lamp does not open the door.” Third says “First does not open the door.” First is uniquely correct: First-open gives false/true/false; Second-open and Third-open each give two true statements. Lighting a lamp costs one Action. A wrong lamp locks harmlessly for 1 minute before reset.
