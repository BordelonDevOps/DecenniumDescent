## Start one attempt

Ordinary provision fishing uses one lead, one real site opportunity and one complete Rod, Net or Trap capture assembly. The prey must actually be present, receptive to the method, physically compatible and have an ordinary provision-catching profile. This does not defeat an actively resisting encounter creature, a character or a companion. Unsupported dangerous or larger catches require their own procedure.

Before setup, record the site and shared stock, opportunity, real candidate creatures, hazards, catch profile, DC, receiving arrangement, apparatus and tools, and the known recovery-category order. Reserve the actual opportunity and items. A lead may have only one unresolved Fishing deployment. Cancellation before commitment releases reservations but never time already spent. At first deployment/exposure, spend the opportunity, expose any bait, choose one attraction source and allocate at most one response atomically. After commitment, interruption, failure or abandonment refunds neither opportunity nor bait.

An ordinary attempt is five active minutes of assembly/deployment, fifty minutes of fishing, a check at minute 55, then five active retrieval minutes. No work block compresses into one combat Action: every six seconds credited as active work commits that worker's Action and required hands. Conditions, threats and incompatible work can prevent progress. Active fishing, setup and retrieval share the crafting framework's two-active-work-Watch ceiling; they cannot also be travel, guard duty, rest or crafting.

Record setup start separately from first deployment/exposure during those five minutes. Dry setup at minute 0, first exposure at minute 5, the check at minute 55 and ordinary secure retrieval at minute 60 is valid; that dose expires at minute 65. Earlier actual exposure starts only the receipt clock earlier. It never restarts the attempt clock. Keep the governing pre-setup inventory reservation receipt and the distinct setup/fishing work records. A Trap needs its five actual setup minutes first and then fifty minutes continuously deployed; doing the only setup work at minutes 50–55 cannot qualify its minute-55 check.

Pre-exposure cancellation records actual worked time and any materials already spent or destroyed, releases only still-unspent reservations, and creates no replacement inventory. Rig mounting is a separate five minutes before main setup and remains worked time if the later setup is cancelled. After commitment, the opportunity and exposed bait remain spent. Order simultaneous expiry, capture and closure by the registered time/phase/sequence checkpoints; matching displayed timestamps do not extend an expired receipt.

Use **d 20 + WIS + one applicable proficiency contribution + independently legal bonuses**. Expertise replaces PB. The declared ordinary DC is 12 for accessible conditions,15 for materially difficult current/cover/access, or 18 for severe but feasible conditions. A separately reviewed catch profile may supply its own DC. Recipe DC, tier, value and crafting time do not set catch parameters; impossible access has no routine check.

Keep the declared catch profile and DC with the resolved Core check and the lead's actual Fishing credential. Each canonical roll belongs to only one attempt, including after closure. An attack, another ability or proficiency, a harvesting task, an automatic result or a non-roll cannot become a catch by assigning it Success or zero equipment bonus. The resolved proficiency term must be zero when untrained, PB when trained, or the replacing expertise contribution; record the untrained disadvantage source in Core. Trap configuration permits only its recorded WIS and that single proficiency term. Other independently lawful bonuses to an attended catch require their own evidence for that check.

Use Core's four task degrees and natural-result shifts, then collapse Critical Success/Success to one interception and Failure/Critical Failure to no catch. Untrained ordinary fishing is WIS without tool PB and with disadvantage. Catching has no recipe-level/formula gate or crafting XP. Automatic results receive no check bonus and create no replacement roll; this candidate supplies no automatic catch.

One trained Rod/Net assistant grants+1 only after completing the five setup and fifty fishing minutes alongside the lead before the check. Assistance never supplies the lead's missing hands. Later retrieval help costs real time and cannot qualify an earlier roll. Trap setting has no generic assistant bonus.
