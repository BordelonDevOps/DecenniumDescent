After suppression ends, only the same still-live ward application may resume prevention for an occurrence it already admitted before the gap, without spending another use. This includes an occurrence whose commitment spent that ward’s second use, subject to the existing termination rule when that occurrence finishes. Unused uses remain available for other qualifying occurrences at their own admission checkpoints; they do not permit late admission of an occurrence whose checkpoint has passed. Neither continued prevention nor a new commitment operates at or after that application’s expiry, or after its replacement or other termination.
