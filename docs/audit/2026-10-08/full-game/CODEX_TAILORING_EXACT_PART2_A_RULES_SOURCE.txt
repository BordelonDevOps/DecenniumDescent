The particular garment must physically contribute through the area or fittings it actually covers. This is not a general Survival, navigation, shelter-construction, crafting, or repair bonus.

**Exposure ability check.** An actual **CON ability check, not a saving throw**, to endure ordinary ambient exposure where the governing procedure permits assistance from suitable clothing and this garment’s real coverage contributes.
