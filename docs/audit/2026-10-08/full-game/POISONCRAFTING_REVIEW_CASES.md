# Poisoncrafting exact-review boundary traces

These are manuscript rule traces, not runtime execution or human balance evidence.

1. **Zero damage still hits:** a coated slashing weapon hits but armor prevention, damage immunity or temporary HP prevents current-HP loss. The hit still spends its injury coating and causes a legal poison exposure. A critical does not double the poison's separate save-based damage.
2. **Three ammunition entitlements:** one dose coats three present compatible arrows. Each can expose once on its first hit before the same original one-minute deadline. A miss leaves that arrow's entitlement only if it survives, is actually recovered, remains unwashed and has not expired. No fourth arrow or new inventory dose exists.
3. **Stable owner:** A coats B's weapon. B's hit applies poison owned by A. Possession does not change refresh grouping or Stunned allowance. A's active Poisoned can qualify A's later Sneak Attack; it is not B's Poisoned merely because B delivered the hit.
4. **Venom Master ownership:** B has Venom Master and critically hits with the weapon A coated. A valid failed initial injury-poison save can trigger B's class rider. The poison remains A's; the -1-save rider belongs to B. The original save is not penalized retroactively.
5. **Component immunity:** damage immunity, condition immunity, or both can block the recipe components without undoing an actually required failed initial save. Venom Master may still trigger. Explicit whole poison-effect immunity prevents the application and supplies no valid failed save; an unnecessary die cannot trigger the class feature.
6. **Contact barrier:** a held pole or intact impermeable glove touches the patch. The holder is not exposed and no dose transfers or is spent merely by that indirect contact. Actual later exposed bodily contact spends the whole charge, even if the target is immune. A legal cleaning procedure destroys the coating as inert waste.
7. **Inhaled exposure:** one vial bursts at a legal gaseous point. Each eligible breathing creature without total cover receives one initial save, allies included. Pre-existing legitimate breath-holding can prevent exposure; no new generic Reaction does. Long range grants initial-save advantage only; no creature entering afterward is exposed.
8. **Record refresh:** two real doses of the same recipe and owner each have separate initial save/damage histories. A second failed application refreshes one still-present ongoing record to its later legal endpoint, not two penalties or ticks. A second initial success leaves the first condition untouched.
9. **Stunned exception:** once A establishes Stunned on T, A's later dose of either Stunned recipe can deal its lawful initial damage but cannot establish or refresh Stunned again in that encounter. Initial success or condition immunity never spends the allowance. A different real owner has its own allowance.
10. **Target already taking its turn:** application during T's current regular turn excludes that turn's end. Marker one is the next regular turn end. Extra turns do not consume markers or add repeat saves. Standard uses ten; singleton Flawed five; transitions retain virtual timing rather than reset it.
11. **Burning final marker:** each eligible marker resolves family arbitration/tick, then DEX only with future scheduled fire remaining, then CON if the record remains. Final tick is due, final DEX omitted, final CON retained, then expiry. An earlier successful DEX or extinguish skips the now-absent record's CON.
12. **Poisoned-only removal:** Herbalism antidote can remove actual Poisoned, leaving no repeat-save record if that was the application's only ongoing part. It does not clear poison-created Burning, Confused or other differently named symptoms. An actual end-one-poison-effect cure can end the selected full present removable application.
13. **Suppression:** a present suppressed record retains clocks and legal ending saves/cures but fails active-condition hooks and contributes no suppressed fire tick. It does not replay initial damage on unsuppression. A physically spent suppressed dose is not reusable.
14. **Prepared versus crafted:** Apply Poison, Toxic Shiv, Double Poison and Vein Cutter cannot spend these crafted units in place of class-prepared doses. Exotic Toxins does not supply a crafting formula. Separate crafted inventory does not become renewable rest stock or acquire the class two-attempt coating/DC.
15. **Food/crafting independence:** source vessel units, returned tools and compound mass are distinct. Recipe-661's consumed Applicator contributes zero compound and is not returned. Recipe-368 reserves 0.20 lb and recipe-370 0.40 lb carrier for maximum output after the heaviest permitted catalyst return. Unused supplementary water/vials remain inventory; original required consumed quantities are not silently refunded.
16. **Source vessels and quality:** only min(source vessels, actual finished count) become filled vessels; excess successful-process source glassware is spent. Generic failed-input recovery remains its own rule. Maximum containers/carrier are reserved before rolling. First vial HP1→2 improvement adds +1 current HP, preserving damage deficit; repeated reuse does not compound it.
17. **Singleton Flawed:** initial DC/dice and deployment remain full; only the ongoing condition limit becomes five markers/30 seconds. No halved application Action, no shortened contact/injury deployment clock, no refunded dose if a condition is immune.
18. **Identification:** Toxin Sense needs actual exposed/perceptible evidence or a legally accessed sample. Magical status alone does not block it. Active identification names an operational recipe, not its crafting formula, hidden ingredients, donor/crafter, discovery permissions or Director lore.

Exact-review clarifications requested: retain an existing fear locus on same-record refresh; one-minute cleaning for an eligible object larger than a small object and its ten-Action/60-second timing; legitimate automatic initial CON failure versus an unnecessary immune-target die for Venom Master; and composition of poisoncraft Slowed with distinct independently lawful halving effects. These are not represented as already-approved source-conference choices.
