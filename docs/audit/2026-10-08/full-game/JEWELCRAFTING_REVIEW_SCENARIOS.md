# Jewelcrafting source/interface scenarios — unresolved

Internal preparation only. These questions grant no new rule and have not been sent to GPT. The 43 original formulas, including 26 public, one restricted and sixteen Director-only, are retained in [source evidence](JEWELCRAFTING_SOURCE_PREPARATION.json). Source versions match on effects, mechanics, components, work and economy; V2 alone omits visibility. The current reviewed crafting default improves structural HP only; source quality labels mentioning charges do not authorize charge improvement.

For the eventual conference, request a finite procedure and distinguish preserved source authority from each new completion. Preserve the 14 operative tags, every printed bonus and 1–6 charges. Resolve mechanical timing without entering the deferred cosmology review. Existing full rules and any completed Leatherworking/Tailoring decisions must accompany the request.
