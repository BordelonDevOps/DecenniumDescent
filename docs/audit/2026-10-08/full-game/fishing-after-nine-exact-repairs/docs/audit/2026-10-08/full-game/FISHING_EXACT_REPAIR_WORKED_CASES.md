Replacing the roll with an attack, DEX, Survival, harvesting, an automatic result or a non-roll still gives zero from the generic equipment selector, but the journal rejects the attempted catch even when its supplied degree says Success. Ordinary DC12/15/18 and a separately reviewed DC21 fixture retain their own recorded declarations. No dice engine is reimplemented here.
