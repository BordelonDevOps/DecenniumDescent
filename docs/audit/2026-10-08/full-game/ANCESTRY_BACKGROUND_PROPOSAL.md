## Authority, creation and clocks

1. Use the full Player book mechanical traits plus exactly one of that ancestry's printed variants. The builder is an abbreviated and sometimes conflicting alternative. Preserve it and all 45 racial-variants.json records as history; do not delete or grant their extra racialFeat/percentage powers. Human is every ability +1, not an optional +2/bonus feat; Feykin is CHA+2 DEX+1 before its variant; Mycelian is WIS+2 before variant; Troll is Medium; Ogre is Large. Drakari variants add no ASI. Apply base and variant ASIs once, including Mountain Dwarf STR+2 and Nomadic Human's CON+1 WIS+1 above human's all+1. NEW proposed ordinary creation ceiling 20 after ancestry increases; excess creates no alternate/banked points. Source L136 forbids increases above 20, but its immediate context is advancement, so this creation ceiling needs explicit agreement.
2. Ancestry 'level' and PB refer to total character level. The current reviewed class foundation owns proficiencies, duplicate choices and advancement. Fixed duplicated skill gives a choice from its printed substitute list, or any of the 20 basic skills if no list. Exhausted legal options give no extra proficiency, never block advancement, and create no feat/expertise/banked grant. Expertise-like 'twice PB instead' supplies 2PB only on its stated task, even if otherwise untrained, never PB+2PB. Same resistance does not stack; same advantage/disadvantage follows core. Natural armor is one base formula, not added onto worn armor; explicit flat AC+1 remains a bonus subject to normal stacking. Powerful Build affects carrying/push/drag/lift only; never occupancy, weapon dice, reach, grappling eligibility or creature size.
3. Per-short-rest traits refresh after a beneficial completed short OR long rest. Keep the reviewed limits of two beneficial short rests between long rests and one beneficial eight-hour long rest per 24 elapsed hours. Per-long-rest traits refresh only there. Preserve source 'per day' separately: NEW rolling 24-hour spent-use timestamps, not midnight or rest reset. A twice/day trait has two timestamps. Weekly attempts likewise max 1 in 168 elapsed hours. Four-hour Trance/inactivity satisfies that character's sleep substitute inside the 8 h Rest Watch; it does not create a 4 h full resource reset. Undead's eat/sleep/breath exemptions do not automatically waive expedition water, shelter or group supplies; drinking is not explicitly exempted in its mechanical paragraph.
4. Nonspell traits do not count as spellcasting just because magical or supernatural. Use their printed Action/BA/Reaction and recharge. Movement, concealment, sensing and environmental advantages retain exact scopes, not blanket immunity or automatic check success. A special Hide permission still requires a Hide attempt; it is not invisibility. Teleports require the printed visible unoccupied destination, fit actual size, and grant no route traversal or extra action. Printed dim/dark origin requirements apply only where present.

## Innate spell interface and dependency audit

Actual source grants must work even for a Warrior, Soldier or Oracle; they give no general Weave class access, RP pool, class rank access, prepared slots, class resource conversion or Revelation. Proposed NEW innate route: record source ancestry/variant, named spell, school/mode, rank, granting level, casting ability, finite use/recharge. Explicit grant supplies just that spell's access at the ancestry's total-level gate. Cantrips may be repeated; ranked casts spend their specified use at legal cast start, with no base RP payment and no optional generic upcast. Keep actual spell action/components/focus, legal target, concentration, interruption and source restrictions. No refund if countered. Components are not waived just because innate: a simple ancestry focus can be selected as starting personal gear for focus-only components, without waiving any specified consumed/costly material. This focus addition is NEW.

Proposed strain interface: all genuine innate casts update the same character-wide Dissonance/Refrain chronology. Use printed ancestry ability to compute Limit=max(4, 2+PB+max(0, modifier)); rank 0 baseD0/rank 1–5 baseD1/rank 6–9 baseD2 plus normal consecutive-name Refrain. An innate charge-only cast has no RP-price interface and no RP surcharge/Resonance Influence price alteration, but rank 1+ is still blocked at Limit+6. Cantrips remain available with ordinary Refrain. An RP-less caster clears Dissonance on a beneficial short rest without receiving RP; long rest clearsD and Refrain. Class hooks require their actual source eligibility; no automatic new engine/school ownership. Please explicitly accept or replace this interface, including Oracle's protected non-RP class engine.

Mode proposal for extant multi-school grants: Dancing Lights=Evocation, Detect Magic=Divination, Feather Fall=Illusion, Charm Person=Enchantment; all other named spells use their single source placement. These are NEW grant provenance, not a global school collapse. High Elf/Lichborn wizard-list choice means a specific catalog spell whose Wizard access rank is 0; choose at creation and retain it, use INT and that Wizard school. Merely sharing a name with a class card never acquires that class feature. Granting a spell does not manufacture an operational effect: the attached dependency table identifies 7 existing unfinished spells and 10 names with no actual ordinary catalog identity. These need the proposed complete cards below; preserve 936 original blocks/848IDs, and record additions separately.

## All background features and starting packages

Use every exact skill/tool/language/equipment row and feature in evidence, including startingReputation, not builder summaries. Creation chooses one background. Foundation packages-versus-wealth rule stays: wealth replaces both class/background physical gear/coins, not proficiencies, languages, features or standing. Please specifically confirm whether to keep this limit or standardize it to the 20-skill base with sourceflag.
