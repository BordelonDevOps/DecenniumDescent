Track total distance descended during this fall and cumulative distance during its current uncontrolled portion, both initially zero.\n\n**Timing:** If a fall begins during a regular initiative turn, set a fixed Fall Marker at the **end of that initiative slot in the following round**. Resolve the first Falling Step there, then one at the same marker each round. Reactions and extra turns use the enclosing regular initiative slot; an event between slots uses the most recently completed slot. Extra turns, extra Actions and initiative changes neither move nor multiply the marker. This gives every participant one normal turn opportunity before the first descent; inability to act still matters and no free rescue action is granted. A caster still needs legal targets within range when spending their Action.\n\nOutside initiative, when timing matters, establish an order before descent and use a virtual slot immediately before its first regular slot as the initiating slot. The first step occurs when that virtual slot next recurs, after everyone has had one normal turn opportunity. Forced movement that removes support starts a fall but adds no separate fall damage. Regaining genuine support, secure support from a hold, or flight ends Falling without resolving an uncompleted impact; teleporting safely onto support likewise ends it. A later loss of support begins a new fall with new trackers and marker.
