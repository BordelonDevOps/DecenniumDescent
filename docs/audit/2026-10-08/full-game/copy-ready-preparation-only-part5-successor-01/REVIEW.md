## Full operative policy and semantics

Read `POLICY_SELECTION.json#/completeOperativeWording` for the complete selected wording. It appears identically in all12 full operative mirrors. The separate rank/payment cross-reference supplies the thirteenth rule-field operation.

A new direct feat-copy cast or Ready preparation spends the applicable allowance in its actual commitment round. For Ready, pay the real Action/cost/components/permission, applicable feat allowance and actually owned/applicable Mirrorsage shared allowance atomically at preparation, with one history/strain transaction and one counter window. Existing direct commitments retain their cap. No class engine is invented merely because a route uses its casting ability.

A same-round or later-round release validates the same hold's durable paid preparation receipts, completed/perceived trigger, actual Reaction, maintained concentration, current target/source predicates and original deadlines. It consumes that hold once and the real Reaction. **It neither checks nor spends the release round's feat or Mirrorsage shared allowance.** A current-round spend by another lawful use remains untouched and is not alone a reason to block the old hold. There is no second RP/components/capture/history/counter transaction.

Precommit invalidity or decline does not spend a Reaction, but cannot revive an ended hold. Exact expiry wins. Actual lost concentration, abandonment and successful replacement end the hold without refund. Late prevention after release commitment does not refund Reaction or preparation. Earlier source/capture windows still govern: in particular the policy does not make every Eldritch Echo record survive into a later numbered round.

The allowance tradeoff is explicit. A genuinely legal competing use can happen in the same numbered round as a delayed release, in either order. That requires its own real source, permission, resources, Action and concentration legality. An ordinary Action after the owner's next START cannot be used to justify holding an expired Ready; the old hold must release before expiry or be lost. A pre-START competing Action remains a source-qualified illustrative prerequisite unless the actual engine supplies one. A concentration-breaking competing cast can end the old hold despite the absence of a current-round cap check. No Action, Reaction refresh, capture or concentration slot is granted.
