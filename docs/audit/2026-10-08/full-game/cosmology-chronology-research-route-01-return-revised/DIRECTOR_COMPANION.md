## CR-F01 — the operational relation

**NEW PROVISIONAL DESIGN — REL-PHASE-DIFFERENCE-01.** In the single paired-channel regulator family used by this route, a maintained nonzero separation between paired resonance indicators corresponds to continued active resonance. When the indicators converge to the same groove, the QUIET face brightens and the third witness mark begins to pale; the documented safe response is to abort before keeper-key seating.

This is deliberately a *local operational relation*, not the lost Equation. It is thematically supported by the supplied Matrix material: meaningful difference produces resonance, while the Matrix tends toward elimination of distinction. They cannot derive universal coefficients, identify the builders, identify an Old One, alter the Pact, bypass gates, or operate unrelated Matrix machinery.

The knowledge is dangerous because it supports intentional control of a real stillward transition. Provenance alone is not Knowledge Taint; supported operational knowledge is.
