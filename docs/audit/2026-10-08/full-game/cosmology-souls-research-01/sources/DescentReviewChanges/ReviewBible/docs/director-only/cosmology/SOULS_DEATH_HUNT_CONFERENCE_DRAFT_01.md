# Souls, forbidden research and the Hunt — conference draft

**Unsubmitted design discussion.** The proposed procedures below have not been adopted. They preserve original-source priority and the [latest owner decisions](HEARTCELL_OWNER_UPDATE_02.md). This is a concrete draft for GPT to assess after the planes review; it is not a completed numerical taint system or a playable Hunt subsystem.

## SH-01 — A soul is not a map coordinate

The original account places mortal soul formation near the prison and emphasizes agency. It later describes soul passage at the Argent Gate and a Gate of Gentle Departures. The Player appendix expressly calls its cosmography mortal belief. Those accounts do not yet give one exhaustive route for every dead identity.

**For discussion:** Retain each account with its time and authority. For an actual return, use the source’s willing identity, body/formation, location, clock and real blocker rules. Do not add a guessed afterlife destination or true-name prerequisite to True Resurrection. A soul’s transition is not automatically the cessation described in Matrix Tier Ten.

**Still required:** The campaign epoch, complete afterlife jurisdiction and the relationship between the Argent Gate and mortal Shadow Conclave claims remain for source-based discussion.

Evidence: MORTAL_ORIGIN, MORTAL_AGENCY, ARGENT_PASSAGE, GENTLE_DEPARTURES, MORTAL_TESTIMONY, SHADOW_CLAIMS, SHADOW_AGENTS, DEATH_CHANGE, CESSATION, TRUE_RETURN.

## SH-02 — Research records what was learned

The owner requires deciphering through Research across multiple Descent libraries. Core Matrix research requires the primary researcher’s own high ranks in five disciplines and cross-reference among drops, libraries, mechanisms and seasonal change. The secondary Instruments guide supplies clock examples, not the completed Equation puzzle.

**For discussion:** NEW record each discovery’s physical source, exact claim, translation evidence, researcher, prerequisites, elapsed work and demonstrated usable understanding. Keep a copied inscription distinct from understanding it. Duplicate copies of one claim do not become independent corroboration or new discoveries. Progress should reveal something usable in play as well as preserve its deeper interpretation for the Director.

**Still required:** Define high ranks, the Research skill/team interface, actual library sites, clue dependencies, time/cost/check rules and completion criteria with GPT. Do not select six universal ticks, five fragments, a single die roll or a fixed equation variant merely because an earlier example used one.

Evidence: OWNER_RESEARCH, RESEARCH_REQUIREMENTS, RESEARCH_CLOCKS, MULTIPLE_CLUES.

## SH-03 — Keep the two causes of taint visible

The owner gives two causes: functional knowledge of the Equation, and voices/proximity to the Old Ones at Descent level5 onward. No arithmetic combination, seven-stage scale or automatic mapping to Corrupted/Unity Exposure was chosen.

**For discussion:** NEW record the actual knowledge event or actual exposure event separately, with the affected identity and its source. Record any resulting condition or track change only under an explicitly defined rule. Knowledge is not required for proximity taint, and owning a manuscript alone does not prove functional knowledge. Neither record proves allegiance, guilt or a named Old One identity.

**Still required:** Progression, doses, thresholds, resistance, recovery, transfer and the meaning of falling remain open. An undefined interface is not permission to invent immunity or to impose an unsupported penalty.

Evidence: OWNER_RESEARCH, RECOVERY_TRACKS, UNITY_CONTEXT.

## SH-04 — Pact attention and Hunt initiation are separate decisions

Core forbidden magic can create evidence, attention or Pact pursuit even on a successful cast. The Matrix describes the Warden and automatons as guardians of the system. The owner’s final-death rule begins once a Hunt is initiated; the exact initiating condition and hunters are not named.

**For discussion:** NEW use a written Hunt initiation event: affected identity, actual triggering violation, enforcing authority, evidence, start time and the mechanical consequences communicated to the player. Preserve the distinction between investigation, suspicion and an active Hunt. Define what each pursuer actually knows and can do; do not grant all NPCs omniscience or equate every ordinary patrol with the Hunt.

**Still required:** Choose the exact violation threshold, recognition process, notice, pursuit roster, escalation and lawful ending. Original-source priority does not authorize inventing a universal automaton police force.

Evidence: FORBIDDEN_MAGIC, WARDEN_PURPOSE, WARDEN_SYSTEM, OWNER_HUNT, VISIBLE_BARGAINS.

## SH-05 — Resolve survival before deciding that death occurred

The current life interface distinguishes HP floors, healing a surviving zero-HP creature, would-die replacement, actual resurrection and companion reconstruction. Phoenix Rebirth targets a living ally; Ritual Ward replaces eligible pending death. Neither is an ordinary after-death resurrection.

**For discussion:** PROPOSED Hunt reading: retain each otherwise legal pre-death survival operation. Resolve its exact source timing and costs; if it prevents actual death, the character remains hunted and alive. Do not call Hunt status an unpreventable-death tag without an express rule. A later actual death still invokes the Hunt consequence. This proposal needs GPT agreement on the owner’s wording.

**Still required:** Confirm fatal Wound, Instant Death, third failure and lethal-track interactions separately through the existing ordered life interface. Do not generalize the narrow ordinary Death Ward into Ritual Ward’s broader replacement.

Evidence: OWNER_HUNT, LIFE_INTERFACE, WARD, RITUAL_WARD, PHOENIX, COMPANION, CAPABILITY_RESPECT.

## SH-06 — An actual hunted death is normally final

The owner states that a Hunt makes the character hardcore and death final, then allows very limited exceptions including extremely rare Divine/Abyssal intervention. Ordinary return abilities cannot provide a routine exception.

**For discussion:** PROPOSED apply the consequence to actual death during an active Hunt regardless of whether the final cause was a hunter, a fall or another hazard. Record the death identity and Hunt together. Ordinary Resurrection, True Resurrection and class revival do not bypass that consequence merely by meeting their usual body/window requirements. Transporting remains, releasing Soul Bind or changing appearance does not itself settle the Hunt. Any authorized exception must say exactly what it overrides.

**Still required:** Confirm the all-causes interpretation and the relationship between ending a Hunt before death, pardon after death and an extraordinary return. No absolute ban on Divine/Abyssal intervention is reinstated.

Evidence: OWNER_HUNT, RESURRECTION, TRUE_RETURN, SOUL_BIND, RETURN_CONSEQUENCES.

## SH-07 — An intervention needs its own written terms

The owner allows extremely rare Divine/Abyssal intervention without a chosen list, frequency, price, trigger or sponsor. A class feature named Divine Rebirth only modifies an already successful Cleric resurrection. Infernal resurrection clauses exist in the fiction but are not universal permission.

**For discussion:** NEW proposal: each exceptional intervention is an explicit campaign event with a named competent intervener, established reason, eligibility, timing, precise overridden barrier, body/identity result, willingness, price or obligation, retained consequences and limit on future use. State whether it ends the Hunt, permits one return while the Hunt continues, or changes a different consequence. Mere divine tags, spell rank, paid temple service or an ordinary signed contract do not create this event.

**Still required:** The actual exceptional routes and their scarcity need authorship and joint review. This record format does not approve an intervener, invent a price, force a player to accept, or make intervention a guaranteed rescue.

Evidence: OWNER_HUNT, DIVINE_REBIRTH, INFERNAL_CLAUSES, SOUL_CHARTER, VISIBLE_BARGAINS, TRUE_RETURN.

## SH-08 — Cleansing and diagnosis retain their real scope

Corruption Cleanse can remove an eligible ordinary Corrupted record without removing knowledge, a major/protected cause, Unity Exposure or an intrinsic curse. Corruption Ward helps existing qualifying saves but creates no new save. Detection and Insight use actual records and protection rules.

**For discussion:** Keep these benefits when their predicates are satisfied. NEW do not treat a cured symptom as automatic loss of functional understanding, cancellation of a recorded Hunt or destruction of a separate source. Conversely, do not declare all taint immune to cleansing by default. Define which records the taint system actually creates and which existing spell predicates can inspect, protect or remove them. Corruption Insight must answer honestly within its known/protected fields; secrecy alone is not a new protection.

**Still required:** Specify source tags, knowledge versus condition recovery and actual protections before applying detection or cure. Higher-rank Purge, Immunity and Purification remain pending; their stronger source wording must be reviewed rather than quietly reduced to lower Cleanse.

Evidence: CLEANSE, CORRUPTION_WARD, INSIGHT, PURGE, IMMUNITY, PURIFICATION, OWNER_RESEARCH, OWNER_HUNT.

## SH-09 — Returning a body does not erase history

Existing resurrection retains Wounds, Corruption, spent resources and legal/body claims. Corpse-local denial differs from an identity/soul blocker. The new Hunt rule creates an additional unresolved interface; it does not convert every legal claim into a soul prison.

**For discussion:** Use one continuing identity and one actual death/return event. Retain valid histories and resolve the actual source’s permitted body or formation mode. NEW require an explicit cause and procedure before treating a legal debt or contract as a return barrier. Keep the carrier of remains, owner of equipment, legal claimant and bound soul separate. Source-specific blocked activation versus later failed paid resolution retains its existing cost rule.

**Still required:** Complete any actual infernal clause or claim before using it to prevent return. Do not infer that all contracts survived refraction unchanged or that a former jurisdiction owns every present soul.

Evidence: RETURN_CONSEQUENCES, LIFE_INTERFACE, CORPSE_BLOCK, SOUL_BIND, TRUE_RETURN, INFERNAL_CLAUSES, SOUL_CHARTER.

## SH-10 — Copies and rewinds do not supply an unreviewed escape

Clone is still pending. Arcane Clone is an illusionary copy, with an unadopted procedure that excludes soul transfer. Full Time Loop remains unavailable; its bounded kernel separately restores life-state and conserves certain histories/memories.

**For discussion:** Keep those availability boundaries. NEW require an explicit temporal policy for functional knowledge, both taint causes, Hunt initiation, Pact enforcement and any exceptional intervention before a scene involving them is admitted to a future complete Time Loop. A generic claim that temporal restoration is not resurrection is insufficient to waive the owner’s Hunt consequence. A proposed temporal policy must also account for who remembers what.

**Still required:** The conference must recommend an exact policy rather than assert that everything rewinds or nothing does. This draft does not disable ordinary source effects already covered by their actual rules, complete Time Loop, or grant Clone a procedure.

Evidence: CLONE, ARCANE_CLONE, TEMPORAL, OWNER_RESEARCH, OWNER_HUNT.

## SH-11 — Reveal consequences without handing out the cosmology

The Player appendix distinguishes mortal belief from objective history. Current information spells report only their allowed facts; Soul Mark ends at death and does not track a detached soul. The Director guidance supports earned, distributed clues and intelligible bargain terms.

**For discussion:** NEW maintain a player record of perceived clues, understood claims and announced mechanical consequences, alongside the Director’s source/cause record. A player can know that a Hunt has made death normally final without being told every cosmic identity or the full Equation. Research should supply observable progress and actionable choices; hidden knowledge is not a reason to fabricate divination failure.

**Still required:** Define how the character learns about the danger, what signs precede the Hunt and what remains hidden. Keep public reliable knowledge and the Q6 ceiling intact.

Evidence: MORTAL_TESTIMONY, INSIGHT, SOUL_MARK, MULTIPLE_CLUES, VISIBLE_BARGAINS, OWNER_HUNT.

## SH-12 — Requirements for a playable chapter

The source comparison supports a coherent distinction among knowledge, taint, conditions, pursuit, death and extraordinary return. It does not yet supply a complete puzzle or all the procedures needed to run those consequences.

**For discussion:** Ask GPT to confirm or correct SH-01–11, propose finite solutions for the open interfaces and identify any original passage that supersedes this reading. Every new threshold, clock, cost, agency restriction or exception must be labeled authored design. Bring unresolved owner-level cosmology conflicts back as precise alternatives after exhausting the existing sources.

**Still required:** Completion requires the actual library/clue chain, research progression, taint rules, Hunt triggers/pursuers/ending, rare interventions, death/temporal interfaces, player warnings and worked campaign examples. This draft closes none of those domains by itself.

Evidence: OWNER_RESEARCH, OWNER_HUNT, RESEARCH_REQUIREMENTS, LIFE_INTERFACE.
