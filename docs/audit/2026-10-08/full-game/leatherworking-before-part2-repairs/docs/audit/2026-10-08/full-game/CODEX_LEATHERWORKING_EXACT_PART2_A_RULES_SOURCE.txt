### Defensive arbitration

**NEW cross-trade extension:** these Leatherworking enhancements join the already-reviewed **`equipment.defense-enhancement`** family. They do not create a second stacking family.

Evaluate the strongest **actual eligible** modifier among the applicable worn-suit grade, Blacksmithing enhancements and these Leatherworking enhancements.

A Boots, Gloves, Leather Gear or Coat enhancement qualifies while its complete item is functional, unsuppressed and actually worn in its designated benefit slot. ## LW-D03. Relief changes the direct bearer’s burden accounting, not physical mass

**EXISTING:** pounds, Inventory Load, casualty Load and Extraction Class are different quantities. Containers and contents remain distinct physical records.

**NEW interpretation of “without counting against normal encumbrance”:** eligible contents receive relief in the direct bearer’s ordinary pound-carrying comparison and in the pound-equivalent part of Inventory Load. Their real mass does not change.

For each eligible content record **i**, retain:

- `wᵢ`: its current known physical weight.
- `lᵢ`: its canonical Load before this Leatherworking relief.
- `rᵢ`: the pounds covered by this container’s remaining allowance.

Apply:

`0 ≤ rᵢ ≤ wᵢ`

`total covered pounds for the container ≤ R`

`Load relief for record i = min(lᵢ, rᵢ / 10)`

The direct bearer therefore uses:

`counted carrying pounds = actual carried pounds − total covered pounds`

`effective Inventory Load = canonical carried Load − total Load relief`

**Do not rewrite either physical weight or canonical Load with those derived results.**

This is an explicit exception for the direct bearer’s ordinary carrying burden. It does not increase Strength, push/drag/lift limits, casualty capacity, equipment anatomy or container strength. Supports, platforms and another creature carrying the bearer still confront the full physical mass.

### Allocation and contents

Allocate R in a stable recorded loading order. Within an inner container, count its empty body and then its contents in their recorded order. Repacking can change that order through actual handling; a player cannot reorder the accounting after a roll or resolved hazard.

Partial **accounting coverage** is allowed. A 12-lb rock may receive 10 lb of relief without being physically divided. Divisible lots retain their real quantities; allocation does not create additional lots or units.

The active container’s **own empty weight receives no relief from itself**. Ordinary inner bags, bottles and their contents may qualify, counting each record once. Explicitly inclusive gross weights must be expanded or counted inclusively, never both.

A record with **3 Load and 1 lb** can receive at most **0.1 Load** relief from that pound, leaving **2.9 Load**. Fifty ordinary coins weigh 1 lb under the supplied equipment convention and normally contribute 0.1 Load; that contribution can be fully relieved. An estimated `10 × Load` weight alone is insufficient to establish eligibility for pound relief; use a known weight or an expressly reviewed operational weight.

The contents must be actual noncreature cargo. This procedure grants no encumbrance exemption for a creature, casualty or creature’s body, including a Petrified creature. Harvested material lots remain ordinary eligible objects when their own source rules permit them.

### Nested containers and external attachments

**NEW nesting rule:** an enchanted container enclosed in another container is stowed and supplies no independent relief. The active outer container may cover the inner container’s empty body and eligible descendants once.

Consequently, there is no repeated inner-plus-outer credit on the same 8 lb. Retain one acyclic custody/containment tree and one physical record per item.

Ordinary inner bags do not block outer relief. External attachments do not receive it, except cargo occupying the Harness’s defined cradle or the Belt’s integrated compartments. Naming arbitrary lashed equipment “contents” does not expand that scope.

Transfer moves the container and its actual contents once. The former bearer loses its relief immediately; the recipient gains it only when their own slot, hold, fit and state requirements are satisfied. No depletion or refresh counter exists: recompute the continuous allowance from current contents and eligibility.

## LW-D04. Suppression, Broken containers and melding

### Suppression

Suppression disables recipe relief, check bonuses, AC enhancement and resistance. It changes no physical capacity, actual mass, structural HP, mundane grade or persistent workmanship.

Contents do not spill merely because relief stops. The body was required to support their unreduced physical weight from the outset.

Recalculate the bearer’s burden immediately. An over-capacity inventory remains in custody but cannot be moved through ordinary routine carrying until made legal by dropping, transferring or another permitted handling method. This is an **overloaded-custody state**, not invented damage, forced teleportation, automatic dropping of selected valuables or a universal Speed-0 condition.

### Structural breakage

**NEW container-failure default:** at 0 structural HP, the body loses its ordinary secure carrying/containment function as well as its recipe benefits.

Move each immediate contents record into the ordinary placement procedure at the container’s actual location. An intact inner bag moves as one physical subtree and retains its own contents; its contents do not also spill separately.

An independently recorded, intact restraint or supporting container can retain what it genuinely supports. An integral strap belonging to the Broken body does not supply an invented independent exception.

Place released contents at the same legal location, otherwise the nearest legal adjacent item position under the supplied placement rules. If none is legal, record pending placement. Pending items cannot simultaneously be retrieved from the former container.

Spilling itself deals no automatic damage. Actual falling, immersion, fire or another already-applicable hazard can resolve normally. The Broken container’s own body and mass remain, and it remains worn/held until removed where the existing rules require that.

Repairing the container does not retrieve or reload its former contents.

### Melding

**EXISTING:** wearing/holding-dependent benefits suspend in meld, and the original meld ceilings are ordinary pre-transformation Load and pound capacities.

**NEW explicit binding:** test proposed melded cargo against those ceilings using **full physical pounds and canonical Load, without Leatherworking relief**.

A voluntary transformation must preflight that result before paying. Excess equipment must be lawfully left external where the source permits and anatomy fits, or removed/dropped through the existing procedure. The relief cannot enlarge either historical meld ceiling.

For this interface, an unopened loaded container is handled as a containment subtree during capture and placement. Removing selected contents first requires actual prior handling. This is not permission to treat that subtree as one light object or to meld the bag while duplicating its contents externally.

For a mandatory transformation, resolve the transformation and existing overflow procedure without granting immunity through excess cargo. Use a pre-recorded disposition order; absent one, use stable physical-item-ID order for this bounded overflow decision, not a post-result tactical selection.

While melded, the container supplies no relief or handling benefit. Another creature transporting the transformed bearer counts the body’s full effective weight, its applicable casualty Load, and **all melded and external equipment records** as the body guide requires. The former bearer’s container relief does not pass through to the rescuer.

On return, apply the existing external-equipment priority. Relief resumes only for containers actually returned to legal worn/held relief positions. A returned container relegated to stowed inventory remains inactive. Recompute burden after legal equipment states are established, then resolve any remaining overload.

## Carrying relief and one physical inventory

Source `capacityPounds` means the recipe's relief allowance R. Physical capacity is a separate mundane body property and supports at least R before enchanting. Even when suppression stops relief, the container still has its physical space and support strength. A 30-lb Backpack can therefore have R10, R20 or R30 without changing its body.

There are five possible relief positions per direct bearer: one back-cargo (Backpack or compatible cargo harness), one quiver, one waist-cargo belt and two pouch positions. Actual anatomy, space and attachment compatibility can permit fewer. The container must occupy a recorded position and be properly worn or directly held by that same bearer. Holding needs the necessary actual hands. Touch, ownership, dragging, a vehicle or attachment to another container is insufficient. One Action dons/doffs one container. Designate its relief position during donning or a lawful take-hold operation. Switching competing already-carried occupants takes one Action to reposition/refit; the old benefit ends before the new one begins.

Track every physical item once in an acyclic custody tree, with a stable loading order. Keep known physical weight w, canonical Inventory Load l and derived covered pounds r separately. In the active container's eligible contents, allocate R in recorded order. For each record, 0 ≤ r ≤ w, and total r ≤ R. The direct bearer counts actual carried pounds minus covered pounds, and canonical carried Load minus the sum of min(l, r/10). Never replace physical weight or canonical Load with these derived results. An estimated 10×Load weight is not sufficient evidence for pound relief.

Partial accounting coverage is allowed: 10 lb of a 12-lb rock may be relieved without splitting the rock. A 1-lb item with printed 3 Load loses at most 0.1 Load and retains 2.9. Fifty ordinary coins weigh 1 lb/0.1 Load, so that ordinary contribution can be fully relieved. The active container's own empty body does not relieve itself. Actual repacking can change allocation order through real handling; no reordering after a roll or resolved hazard.

Ordinary inner bags/bottles and their eligible contents may receive relief, counting the empty body then descendants in recorded order. An inclusive gross record is either counted once as that inclusive record or expanded into disjoint body/contents records, never both. Creature bodies, casualties, living creatures and Petrified creatures are ineligible. Separately legal harvested material lots are ordinary objects. Record both weight and Load even where one capacity is not the immediate limiting factor.

An enclosed enchanted container is stowed and gives no independent relief. Its active outer container may cover the inner body and eligible descendants once. A Backpack strapped outside another is neither a second active Backpack nor interior cargo; arbitrary external lashings receive no relief. Actual cargo in a defined harness cradle or integrated belt pockets uses those real compartments. Ordinary inner bags do not block outer relief.

Transfer moves the same physical container subtree once. The old bearer loses relief immediately; the recipient receives it only when their own slot, hold, fit and state qualify. Recompute continuous relief from current contents; there is no charge, depletion or refresh counter. No carrying relief increases STR, push/drag/lift limits, casualty capacity, equipment anatomy or container strength. Supports and rescuers encounter full physical mass.

## Suppression, breakage, placement and melding

Suppression stops relief, check bonuses, AC enhancement and resistance. It changes no body mass, physical capacity, structural HP, mundane grade or persistent workmanship. It does not spill contents. Immediately recompute burden. Excess creates **overloaded custody**: inventory remains present, but cannot move through ordinary routine carrying until lawful dropping, transfer or another handling method makes it legal. This is not automatic damage, teleportation, deletion, forced selection of dropped valuables or a universal Speed-0 condition.

At 0 structural HP a Broken container loses both recipe benefits and ordinary secure containment. Release each immediate contents record to ordinary placement at the container's actual location. An intact inner bag moves once as its subtree; do not also spill its descendants separately. A separately recorded intact supporting container/restraint can retain what it actually supports. An integral strap belonging to the Broken body is not an independent restraint.

Use the same legal location, otherwise the nearest legal adjacent item position under the reviewed placement procedure; absent a legal position, record pending placement. Pending items cannot also be retrieved from the former container. Spilling itself deals no automatic damage; actual fall, fire, immersion or other hazards resolve normally. The Broken body's mass and worn/held state persist until actually removed. Repair does not retrieve or reload former contents. Worn/held structural attacks follow the reviewed equipment object defenses; the structural profile does not replace a creature's armor formula.

Before a voluntary transformation pays its cost, test proposed melded cargo using full pounds and canonical Load against the fixed ordinary pre-transformation ceilings, without any Leatherworking relief. Excess must be lawfully left external where permitted and anatomically compatible, or removed/dropped through the existing procedure. An unopened loaded container is handled as one containment subtree for capture/placement, but all of its physical pounds and canonical Load count. Removing selected contents first requires actual prior handling; neither duplicate contents nor a single artificially light object is created.

For a mandatory transformation, use the existing overflow procedure; excess gives no transformation immunity. Follow a pre-recorded disposition order, or stable physical-item-ID order if none exists, never a post-result tactical selection. Melded gear gives no relief or handling benefit. A rescuer carrying the transformed bearer counts the body's full effective weight, applicable casualty Load, and every melded/external equipment record under the body guide. Relief does not pass through the bearer to the rescuer.

On return, existing external equipment has priority. Only containers actually restored to legal worn/held relief positions reactivate. Returned stowed containers remain inactive. Recompute burden after legal equipment states are set, then resolve remaining overload.
