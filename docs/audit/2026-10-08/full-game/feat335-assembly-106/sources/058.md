## Acrobat (`acrobat`)

**Prerequisite:** Dexterity 13 or higher

Gain proficiency in Acrobatics if not already proficient immediately before acquiring this feat. Otherwise use twice PB for Acrobatics checks. This does not substitute Dexterity for an Athletics check, grant a climbing Speed, improve an actual jump distance or allow a reroll. Those benefits require their own actual sources.

### Worked examples

A DEX 16, PB +2 character without Acrobatics gains a +5 normal Dexterity (Acrobatics) modifier, not +7.

A DEX 16, PB +2 character already proficient in Acrobatics now uses +7. Already having another twice-PB Acrobatics source leaves +7, not +11.

Owning this feat does not satisfy a prerequisite asking for the differently named Acrobatic feat unless a future explicit identity decision says so.

## Acrobatic (`acrobatic`)

**Prerequisite:** Dexterity 13+

Gain Acrobatics proficiency; an existing proficiency supplies no extra grant under the proposed fixed-grant rule. On an actual Athletics check primarily resolving a jump, you may use your Dexterity modifier in place of Strength. NEW: choose the ability before rolling. It remains an Athletics check: apply actual Athletics proficiency or Expertise once, not Acrobatics proficiency merely because Dexterity is used. This substitution changes the check only. Calculate maximum jump distance, approach, movement spent, clearance and landing from the actual movement procedure and other owned features. No automatic success, standing-jump increase, climbing Speed or Acrobatic Mastery reroll is granted.

### Worked examples

DEX +3, STR +1, PB +2 and Athletics proficiency gives +5 on the chosen Dexterity (Athletics) jumping check. Acrobatics Expertise does not raise it to +7.

A legal check using Dexterity still cannot cross a gap beyond the character's actual jump reach or place them in an occupied landing space.

## Actor (`actor`)

**Prerequisite:** Charisma 13 or higher

Gain advantage on actual Charisma (Deception) or Charisma (Performance) checks whose declared purpose is passing yourself off as a different person. NEW: the identity can be invented or real, but the chosen presentation must be physically possible with your actual appearance, voice, clothing, languages, knowledge and any separately active disguise or magic. Familiarity with a real person's observed traits supplies evidence for imitating those traits; it does not create an automatic perfect voice copy. The Director states what scrutiny the check resolves before rolling. A forged identity does not defeat an independent magical identification effect unless that effect actually calls for the qualifying check. This feat grants no kit, prepared disguise, extra preparation time, memories or understanding of an unlearned language. Disguise Expert's separate flat bonus may coexist only under the final feat-stacking decision; advantage itself never stacks.

### Worked examples

An actor who speaks the requested language and has observed a guard's manner can gain advantage on the actual Deception check to pass as that guard. The feat does not reveal the guard's private password.

A magical scan that directly reports the subject's actual identity without a Deception/Performance contest is not defeated by rolling with advantage on an unrelated conversation.

## Animal Affinity (`animalAffinity`)

**Prerequisite:** Wisdom 13 or higher

Gain proficiency in Animal Handling if not already proficient immediately before acquiring this feat. Otherwise use twice PB for Animal Handling checks. Apply it to an actual Animal Handling task allowed by the creature and situation. It provides no telepathy, creature language, automatic attitude change, trained mount, companion, domination or guaranteed cooperation. A different social or knowledge skill does not gain this proficiency merely because an animal is present.

### Worked examples

A WIS 14, PB +3 character already trained in Animal Handling uses +8 on an eligible Wisdom (Animal Handling) check.

An untrained holder gains normal proficiency, then later gains another Animal Handling grant from a separate source: this feat remains its original proficiency branch. The new grant follows its own rule.

Even an excellent check does not create an absent beast or let an untrained mount use combat actions unavailable under its real control procedure.

## Appraise (`appraise`)

**Prerequisite:** Intelligence 13+

Spend ten active minutes closely inspecting one actual item with adequate sensory access and tools appropriate to safely examine it. NEW: make Intelligence (Investigation), DC10 for a plainly identifiable mundane object,15 for complex mundane construction,20 for an ordinary magical item, or25 for a unique unfamiliar construction; a specific item's explicit identification DC or concealment exception controls instead. Set the classification and actual information before rolling. On Success or Critical Success, learn its exact current reference value and complete discoverable operative properties, including magical properties and a curse unless that curse's specific source prevents this identification. Reference value means the recorded undamaged catalog price adjusted by the governing condition/valuation rule, or a Director-established local fair-market valuation when no catalog price exists, fixed before the roll. It is not a guaranteed purchase or sale offer. The property result states uses, limits, charges, activation and known operational risks from the actual item's complete record; it does not invent a missing profile, formula, historical truth or cosmology. A source explicitly resisting identification may withhold its protected part while other properties are identified. Safe appraisal is not activation or disarming; actual independent touch/proximity triggers remain. Failure spends time and yields no exact appraisal; another attempt needs changed evidence, access or method. This explicitly adopts the powerful source item-identification benefit. It differs from the reviewed Identify procedure; it does not amend Identify or make its protected disclosures public.
NEW settlement binding: a Haven Appraisal remains the full thirty-minute EX58 procedure with its own governing DC, actor, evidence and one check. This ten-minute direct examination may be performed as a genuine included part of that same appraisal, not as a free second valuation, skipped twenty minutes or concurrent second job. Standalone appraisal does not itself execute title/provenance, buyer negotiation, tax, sponsor, insurance or settlement. Reference price, actual agreed gross price and net receipts remain separate; apply FS-13 and the retained greater-of assay rule.

### Worked examples

A known item with reference value40gp can be appraised at40gp while a real buyer offers only20gp. The feat identifies the reference value; it does not compel that buyer to pay40gp.

An ordinary magical wand's complete operative profile can be learned on the proposed DC20 success. An explicitly identification-proof curse remains protected by its own source; its lore is not invented.

## Athletic Prodigy (`athleticProdigy`)

**Prerequisite:** Strength or Dexterity 15 or higher

The prerequisite is Strength 15 or Dexterity 15; either permanent score suffices. Evaluate Athletics and Acrobatics separately at acquisition. Grant proficiency in each untrained skill and twice-PB Expertise in each already proficient skill. Neither skill grant changes which ability the ordinary task uses. The feat gives no Speed, movement, jumping-distance increase or automatic success. Other Expertise in one of these skills is an overlapping grant of twice PB, not another multiplier.

### Worked examples

STR 15, DEX 12 meets the prerequisite. If Athletics is already trained but Acrobatics is not, acquire Athletics Expertise and ordinary Acrobatics proficiency.

With PB +3, STR +2 and DEX +1 in that mixed case, ordinary Athletics is +8 and ordinary Acrobatics is +4. An Acrobatics roll of 12 totals 16.

If both skills are already proficient, both gain twice PB independently. If both already have Expertise, neither becomes four times PB.

## Bruiser (`bruiser`)

**Prerequisite:** Strength 15+

Gain Intimidation proficiency. When making an actual Intimidation check, you may substitute Strength for Charisma. NEW: declare that choice before rolling and describe an actual physically intimidating presentation the target can perceive; false impossible strength or an absent threat does not become evidence from the feat. The check remains Intimidation and uses its applicable proficiency contribution once. Other legal intimidation methods may still use Charisma. This feat changes neither the ordinary social stakes nor the cost of violence, reputation, payment or consent. A successful check does not automatically impose Frightened, Charmed, obedience, a surrendered asset or a condition not included in the declared possible outcome. Commanding Presence remains a distinct source with its own reverse-substitution wording.

### Worked examples

STR +4, CHA -1 and PB +3 yields +7 for a qualifying Strength (Intimidation) check. Proficiency is not added again because an intimidating weapon is present.

A guard incapable of perceiving the display receives no automatic intimidation consequence. On an actual successful exchange, resolve only the possible concession stated before the roll.

## Cartographer (`cartographer`)

**Prerequisite:** Intelligence 13+

Create an accurate record of a route or place you actually observe, using real recording materials. NEW: record one minute of active map work for each distinct junction or approximately100feet of visible route, whichever requires more work; a scale overview from a clear vantage can be recorded in ten minutes. Ordinary mapping is separate work unless another feature, such as Dungeon Cartographer, permits concurrent normal-pace mapping. 'Never get lost' means you retain a reliable account of your own traversed path and current position relative to its still-valid recorded landmarks: you cannot accidentally choose a wrong already-known branch solely through a navigation failure. It does not reveal an unvisited area, locate an unknown destination, connect an unobserved teleportation gap or preserve a physically destroyed route. A changed or illusory landmark may require investigation; the feat preserves what was actually perceived rather than certifying it true. Gain advantage on actual Survival navigation checks, including where incomplete new information still makes the route uncertain. Others using your map receive its actual information, not your personal never-lost guarantee or advantage unless another feature grants it.

### Worked examples

On an unchanged route the holder already traversed, a failed navigation check cannot make them accidentally take the wrong recorded junction. A missing bridge still requires a real solution.

After an unobserved teleportation, the old map remains accurate where its terrain survives, but the feat supplies no invented connection between departure and arrival.

## Charismatic (`charismatic`)

**Prerequisite:** Charisma 13+

Evaluate Persuasion and Deception separately at acquisition: gain proficiency if untrained, or twice-PB Expertise if already proficient. These grants do not extend to Performance, Intimidation, Reputation or Insight. Persuasion and Deception remain distinct tasks with actual possible stakes, evidence, audience understanding and failure. The feat does not force agreement, make a lie objectively true, change standing or defeat magical truth restrictions by itself.

### Worked examples

A CHA 16, PB +2 holder already proficient in Persuasion but not Deception has +7 Persuasion and +5 Deception after acquiring this feat.

The same holder's Performance proficiency and modifier remain exactly what their other real sources provide; Charismatic grants neither.

Two actual feats each granting Persuasion Expertise still yield +7 in this example, not +11; the overlapping grant history remains recorded.

## Climber (`climber`)

**Prerequisite:** Strength 13 or higher

Gain a climbing Speed equal to your current legal walking Speed and advantage on actual Athletics checks made to climb. NEW: derive the speed from the current body and applicable speed penalties, not an unmodified ancestral baseline. Follow the shared spent-movement-cost ledger when switching modes, so movement already spent is never refunded. Climbing still needs support your actual hands, limbs, equipment and surface can provide; the feat does not add adhesion, free hands or a flying mode. A check occurs only when the route calls for one, and advantage does not eliminate its real failure consequence. Grip, slippery material, load, damage, support collapse, zero Speed and falls keep their ordinary rules. A second feature granting the same climb Speed does not add another walking Speed; use the actual applicable movement profile.

### Worked examples

Walking Speed20 gives climbing Speed20. With10movement-cost feet already spent, the current20-foot budget leaves10, subject to terrain costs on the climb.

Athletics advantage helps a real slick-wall climb, but a ceiling with no possible grip requires another supported method; owning Climber alone does not create one.

## Commanding Presence (`commanding_presence`)

**Prerequisite:** Charisma 15+

When an actual Intimidation task would use Strength, you may use Charisma instead; choose before rolling. Ordinary Charisma (Intimidation) already uses Charisma, so this clause gives no second modifier or bonus there. It does not grant proficiency. On actual Persuasion checks directed at a creature with an explicitly recorded character level lower than your total character level, gain advantage. NEW: compare true character levels, not CR, Hit Dice, faction standing, rank or apparent seniority. A creature with only CR and no character level is not qualified through a fabricated conversion; its Director can supply an actual level only if its real profile legitimately includes one. Check eligibility at the roll, without granting the speaker metagame disclosure of the hidden value. Equal or higher level gives no advantage from this clause. All actual social possibilities, agency and costs remain.

### Worked examples

A level8 speaker negotiating with an actual level5 NPC can gain advantage. An NPC listed only as CR5 is not silently treated as level5.

CHA +3 is used once on ordinary Intimidation. The feat does not make the total CHA +3 plus STR +2 or grant an extra +3.

## Courtly Manners (`courtly_manners`)

**Prerequisite:** Charisma 13+

Gain advantage on actual Charisma ability checks when directly interacting with someone who genuinely holds a noble social position recognized by the current community. Preserve the source's breadth: it is not restricted to etiquette checks or friendly nobles. A Charisma attack or saving throw is not an ability check. NEW audience procedure: spend fifteen active minutes preparing and submitting one credible request through an actually accessible official, host or gatekeeper. Gain advantage on a Persuasion check to arrange an audience with an important figure even when that intermediary is not noble. The audience must be possible under the figure's actual schedule, policy and willingness; announce the attainable appointment and any fee, introduction or condition before rolling. Success secures that opportunity on those terms, not immediate entry, a title, waived security or agreement to the later request. A repeated identical petition to the same gatekeeper yields no new roll without a changed circumstance. Required identity, dress and communication remain actual resources.

### Worked examples

A noble's hostile interrogation can still qualify for the feat's advantage on an actual Charisma check made in that interaction; nobility does not guarantee a friendly response.

A successful gatekeeper petition secures the announced appointment tomorrow with its stated fee. It does not place the character in a closed chamber immediately.

## Deceiver (`deceiver`)

**Prerequisite:** Charisma 13+

Gain Deception proficiency. NEW explicit source exception: you can deliberately speak a false assertion even while a magical effect would otherwise prohibit that assertion solely because it is a lie or require that assertion to be truthful. This is a narrow exception to truth-compulsion, including the lie-prohibition part of Zone of Truth, and must be approved against those exact spell records. You still make any actual save and remain subject to the effect's other clauses. It grants no automatic convincing success: resolve a real Deception check, opposed defense or other social procedure where required. It does not falsify memories, thoughts, directly revealed facts or independently recorded events; confer no blanket immunity to charm, compelled actions or orders; and does not let you avoid an order to speak or move when that obligation is separate from truthfulness. If another effect punishes actual lies, this feat permits the lie but does not negate its punishment. The caster receives only the information their source actually grants; the feat adds neither an automatic warning nor an undetectability guarantee.

### Worked examples

After failing a Zone of Truth save, the holder can attempt a deliberate verbal lie under this proposed exception. A suspicious listener may still defeat the Deception check.

A spell that directly reveals an actual memory is not changed by the holder speaking falsely. A separate penalty triggered by telling a lie still resolves.

## Diplomat (`diplomat`)

**Prerequisite:** Charisma 13 or higher

Evaluate Persuasion and Insight separately at acquisition: gain proficiency if untrained, or twice-PB Expertise if already proficient. Use the actual ability for each task, normally Charisma for Persuasion and Wisdom for Insight. Diplomacy still requires an eligible request and a perceivable, understandable interaction. Insight can reveal only what the actual successful task supports; it is neither automatic mind reading nor a guarantee that a suspected statement is false. This feat grants no extra audience, faction rank or negotiated discount.

### Worked examples

CHA 14, WIS 16 and PB +3 with existing Insight but no Persuasion produces +5 Persuasion and +9 Insight after acquisition.

A legal choice order acquiring Charismatic first can leave Persuasion proficient before a later Diplomat acquisition, so Diplomat grants Expertise there. Record both events; this is sequential ownership, not free retroactive rewriting.

An opposed Insight total of 16 against Deception 16 keeps the existing state under current Core ties; Expertise does not create an automatic win.

## Dungeon Delver (`dungeonDelver`)

**Prerequisite:** Wisdom 13 or higher

Gain advantage on actual Perception or Investigation checks whose purpose is detecting a secret door or a trap. Use Perception to notice accessible sensory clues and Investigation to examine and interpret the actual mechanism or evidence, as the task warrants; do not add both skill proficiencies or roll both automatically. NEW: the feat's advantage changes active rolls only. It does not add a passive+5 or create a passive Investigation procedure by itself. Actual light, senses, range, access and search time remain; a completely inaccessible or undetectable feature is not revealed by an uncalled-for roll. Success reveals the detection information defined by that task, not automatic disarming, a save bonus, damage resistance, magical identification or immunity to a trigger encountered while searching. Trap Sense and Observant are separate feats with their own actual passive grants.

### Worked examples

An actual Investigation search of a suspicious hinge gains advantage. The same check does not disarm a linked trap unless a separate procedure actually does that.

A passive Perception of15 remains15 from this feat alone; there is no invented+5 for advantage. Separately owned Observant would have its own modifier.

## Entertainer (`entertainer`)

**Prerequisite:** Charisma 13+

Gain Performance proficiency. NEW finite work procedure: arrange a real two-hour performance booking with an accessible venue, an actual audience and a willing payer, using the necessary real instrument, props or voice. The default mundane fee for this proposed booking is2sp, quoted and agreed before work; an actual negotiated contract may specify another amount or a performance standard. Complete the two active hours to earn the contractual payment from that payer, minus any actual venue costs. If the contract makes pay contingent on a check, state that DC and failure payment before accepting. During one fifteen-minute audience interaction within that booking, make Charisma (Performance) DC12 to engage attendees; on success one willing attendee offers an actual relevant local event, contact or rumor from their knowledge. Label hearsay and uncertainty; no attendee with relevant information means no invented rumor. The booking can coexist with that expressly allowed conversation, not another work action or rest. Repeating for the same unchanged audience gives no new information roll; a new booking needs real demand and payment. Record spent time, patron and money so no repeated-roll income is created.

### Worked examples

One completed2sp booking with5cp venue costs nets15cp. It consumes two actual hours and the payer's actual2sp, not an unlimited roll reward.

A successful performance for an audience that knows no relevant news creates no hidden fact. A later identical song to those same attendees does not reset their information.

## Forager (`forager`)

**Prerequisite:** Wisdom 13+

NEW finite interpretation of the source guarantee: in an accessible non-desert, non-arctic area that actually contains enough recoverable edible food and potable water, spend one active eight-hour forage Watch to gather the normal daily food and water requirement for yourself and up to six other ordinary Small/Medium people. This feat guarantees that result from qualifying available resources without a separate yield roll; it does not guarantee access through a hazard, remove contamination or defeat a resource-specific harvesting gate. Identify a real foraging area and deplete its recorded stocks. The actual expedition procedure supplies food/water units, body-size requirements, carry weight and Supply conversion; this feat creates no extra abstract Supply. Larger or unusual consumers use their actual greater requirement from the same finite quantity, not seven unlimited creature-day portions. Record one feat forage output per holder per24 elapsed hours, shared across all locations, with the window beginning on completion; a second feat owner can work separately only against remaining real stock and their actual time. Deserts and arctic regions use ordinary foraging. A stripped, sealed or foodless place cannot satisfy the prerequisite, and no water appears because food exists. Preserve, transport and consume the actual output normally.
NEW explicit quantity ceiling: the seven ordinary Small/Medium daily food-and-water portions are at most14lb edible food and56pints potable water in one completed480-active-minute Watch. Each pint of water is1lb for the existing cargo accounting. A Large consumer normally uses4lb/16pints from this same stock. These are food/water only, not seven complete Supply Units: no oil, sundries, storage or transport is awarded. If the full guarantee cannot be supported, before work declare an ordinary lawful forage attempt or a smaller supported quantity under this feat; its no-yield-roll benefit may recover only that predeclared real accessible amount. The one-completion/24-elapsed-hour and all actual acquisition/handling restrictions still apply.

### Worked examples

One lawful Watch recovers14lb food and56pints water for seven standard people from actual stock. Water adds56lb; the output is70lb before containers, and includes no oil or sundries.

A source has only4lb food and8pints safe accessible water. A predeclared smaller feat attempt cannot recover more than those amounts; a second worker or new container cannot recreate the depleted stock.

## Gossip (`gossip`)

**Prerequisite:** Charisma 13+

Identify an actual local information-gathering task, its contacts, topic, permitted method, normal active time and real costs before work. Spend half that actor's normal active time, rounded upward once to a whole minute. NEW: absent a more specific procedure, an ordinary round of local inquiries through accessible willing contacts takes two active hours, reduced here to one. The feat does not reduce an appointment's calendar wait, another person's work, mandatory travel, deciphering unknown language, a fixed magical duration or a research procedure that is not gathering local events, people or rumors. Use the ordinary information/social check and result without an extra numerical bonus. Faster acquisition does not make gossip true, complete, public or freely given. Keep actual bribes, admission, obligations and secrecy risks. No retry of unchanged evidence merely because the task was faster; record which contacts actually supplied which claims. Apply the final feat conference's time-reduction stacking rule rather than multiply overlapping discounts silently.

### Worked examples

A declared90-minute local inquiry becomes45active minutes. A separately scheduled meeting tomorrow still occurs tomorrow.

The holder learns a rumor more quickly, but a false rumor remains false and an unwilling contact does not become willing from the time reduction.

## Improved Athlete (`athlete_improved`)

**Prerequisite:** Athlete feat

Requires the actual Athlete feat, ID athlete. Gain climbing and swimming Speeds equal to current legal walking Speed; alternate sources granting those same modes do not add together. Use actual anatomy, support, water conditions, breathing and the spent-cost ledger; neither movement mode permits an impossible surface or cancels hazards. NEW reading of the source's unrestricted long-jump clause: a standing long jump can span up to the current Strength score in feet; a running long jump uses that same base and retains Athlete's separate increase equal to the nonnegative current Strength modifier. For this proposal, a running approach means at least10feet of actual immediately preceding movement on supporting ground. Both the approach and airborne distance spend movement normally. The feat changes horizontal distance, not vertical clearance, a high jump, flight or safe landing. A legal check can still be needed for a hazardous takeoff or landing, and no success creates additional distance. These proposed standing/running distinctions and the10-foot approach need comparison with the complete movement source review before adoption.

### Worked examples

STR16 and its+3 modifier permit a proposed16-foot standing jump or19-foot running jump. A10-foot approach plus19feet airborne spends29feet before any additional terrain costs.

Walking Speed0 gives climbing and swimming Speed0. A legal destination still cannot be reached by claiming the jump as free movement.

## Improved Climber (`climber_improved`)

**Prerequisite:** Climber feat

Requires the actual Climber feat, ID climber. NEW: ceiling and overhang climbing is possible with actual usable grips, anchors or equipment sufficient for the current body's support; the feat removes the angle-only bar but supplies no adhesion to a featureless unsupported surface. Pay the ordinary climbing movement budget and make any real required check, including Climber's advantage when applicable. If that climb check fails by a final numerical deficit of1–4, you avoid the fall caused by that failed check. Remain at the last legally supported position, spend the movement/action/time already committed, and gain no progress through the failed segment. A natural-1 degree downgrade with deficit below5 still receives this narrow fall protection. A failed result with total meeting the DC due solely to a natural downgrade also avoids that check's fall under this NEW clarification. Deficit5+ receives no protection. The feat does not stop an unrelated shove, lost anchor, collapsing support, already ongoing fall or deliberate release. A retry needs a legally available new attempt under the climb task, without refunded movement.

### Worked examples

DC18, total14 fails by4: remain at the last supported place without gaining the failed traverse, and keep the spent movement cost. Total13 fails by5 and may fall normally.

A rope anchor breaks after a successful climb. That is a separate loss of support, so the feat's failed-check protection does not suspend the climber in air.

## Keen Mind (`keen_mind`)

**Prerequisite:** Intelligence 13 or higher

Always know the direction designated as true north in the current region's established physical/map frame and the remaining elapsed hours to its actual next sunrise or sunset when that event exists. This requires no artificial navigation roll, but does not reveal passages, destinations or a safe route. NEW boundary: where no such direction/event is defined by actual setting evidence, record 'not defined here' or 'no scheduled event' rather than inventing a cosmological answer; confer on any real exception later. Accurately recall anything you actually perceived by sight or hearing within the past month, including sounds or marks you did not understand, without thereby translating them or learning concealed details. NEW: for this feat's memory window only, month means30elapsed24-hour days; this does not define the world's calendar. Recollection can reproduce the perceived sequence, but limited attention, obstructed senses and a deceptive illusion limit what was perceived. Explicit memory alteration follows its own effect. Numerical dawn-like recharge receipts are not evidence of sunrise. No soul, plane, alignment or celestial explanation is authored here.

### Worked examples

A rune seen clearly ten days ago can be recalled in its observed form, but an unread language remains untranslated. A hidden reverse side was never seen.

A place with no actual sunrise schedule receives no invented time-to-sunrise. An item that recharges at06:00 does not establish a sunrise at06:00.

## Leadership (`leadership`)

**Prerequisite:** Charisma 15+, level 6+

NEW finite recruitment/loyalty procedure: during a real recruitment effort, designate up to current PB actual willing prospective followers or hirelings. Spend one active hour interviewing and briefing them, agree on their actual jobs, pay, duration, risk, equipment and departure terms, and gain+2 on any legitimate Persuasion checks to secure those agreements. An automatic willing agreement needs no roll. The feat creates no candidate, free wage, stat block, level, binding, summoned unit or extra class companion. Keep at most PB current designated contracts receiving the feat's loyalty benefit; designate at agreement or at a new hour-long briefing, never retroactively during a morale event. While the employer honors the terms and the work remains within the agreed risk, a designated follower gains+2 on an actual loyalty/morale check whose purpose is deciding whether to abandon that agreement under pressure. This is not a bonus against every fear spell or a compulsion to stay; no roll is invented where the person's real decision is certain. Broken terms, unpaid wages or an unagreed suicidal demand remove this benefit until genuinely repaired and re-briefed. Actual transport, expedition caps, combat turns and command budgets continue to govern each recruited person. Dismissal ends designation without transferring any creature's property or earned wages.

### Worked examples

PB3 can designate three real hired people, each still requiring a valid contract and actual pay. The feat does not authorize a thirteenth expedition character against the owner's12-character cap.

A follower ordered into danger expressly excluded from their contract does not receive a loyalty check that forces compliance. The employer must negotiate a real change or accept refusal.

## Naturalist (`naturalist`)

**Prerequisite:** Wisdom 13 or higher

Evaluate Nature and Survival separately at acquisition: gain proficiency if untrained, or twice-PB Expertise if already proficient. Nature remains the relevant knowledge skill, normally Intelligence; Survival remains the actual navigation, tracking or resource-finding skill, normally Wisdom, when the task calls for it. The feat grants no free forage yield, hidden ecological fact, ingredient identity, wilderness immunity or new resource. It does not bypass the actual Supply, source-depletion, route, watch or creature-size procedures. No creature origin or cosmology is inferred from its name.

### Worked examples

INT 12, WIS 16, PB +2 with Nature trained and Survival untrained gives Nature +5 and Survival +5 after acquisition; equal totals arise from different ability/proficiency contributions.

Two sources of Survival Expertise use one twice-PB contribution. A tool credential that also fits the task does not add a second PB.

A successful knowledge check can identify a plant from available evidence. If an area is already depleted, the feat does not make another harvest exist.

## Negotiator (`negotiator`)

**Prerequisite:** Charisma 13+

Gain advantage on actual Persuasion checks during negotiations for real goods or services. NEW: before the check, establish the seller/buyer's available stock, normal quote, attainable concession, payment and conditions. A successful check secures that announced better quote or term; no universal percentage discount or guaranteed buyer is created. A concession can be a lower price, improved payment schedule, delivery, included fitting or a better actual sale offer, with its exact value set before rolling. The counterpart must have authority and willingness to consider it. Failed unchanged negotiations cannot be repeated by restating the same bargain or swapping speakers without a materially changed offer or evidence. Distinct standing, sponsor and service benefits use their own actual clauses; advantage does not stack. Different purchase and resale quotes remain separate, and this feat never establishes a same-price repurchase promise or circular money source. Record the final agreement and any taxes/fees through the actual settlement procedure.

### Worked examples

A merchant offers a possible reduction from50gp to45gp on success. A successful advantaged check secures45gp under the announced terms; it does not also apply another unprinted10% discount.

Buying for45gp does not force any buyer to repurchase for50gp. A later actual resale uses that buyer's own stock, demand and quote.

## Observant (`observant`)

**Prerequisite:** None

At acquisition choose Intelligence or Wisdom and increase that score by1, to the ordinary maximum20 unless a separately reviewed source explicitly permits more. Gain+5 to passive Perception and passive Investigation. NEW: passive Perception is10 plus the current Wisdom (Perception) modifier including applicable proficiency once; passive Investigation is10 plus the current Intelligence (Investigation) modifier including applicable proficiency once. Apply this feat's+5 once to each. Passive Investigation may resolve a declared sustained or routine examination of actually accessible physical evidence when the governing task permits a passive result; it is not a free constant scan for every hidden detail. Specific tasks requiring an active check or paid examination remain active. No natural-die shift or automatic passive±5 for advantage/disadvantage is added. Apply actual qualifying numerical penalties or bonuses in their normal scope. This feat gives no+5 to active rolls, attack detection through impossible senses or automatic truth about an illusion. The score increase can independently alter its own relevant ability modifiers.

### Worked examples

WIS16 and Perception proficiency at PB+2 gives passive Perception10+3+2+5=20. An active Perception roll gets the ordinary+5 modifier, not an additional feat+5.

INT15 increased to16 with Investigation proficiency at PB+3 gives passive Investigation10+3+3+5=21 when that passive task is allowed. A required lock examination still takes its stated time.

## Pathfinder (`pathfinder`)

**Prerequisite:** Wisdom 13+

While you actively lead one physically coordinated traveling party, the party may track at its actual normal travel pace and ignore ordinary difficult terrain's reduction of strategic travel rate. NEW: party members must remain on the led route and able to follow your current directions directly or through their actual communications chain; separated scouts and a different caravan receive no remote benefit. Use the normal rate of the actual slowest participating body/vehicle, with actual capacity, access and work/rest limits. A mount or wagon can benefit only on a route it can physically traverse; the feat supplies neither a road through an impassable gap nor a swim/fly/climb mode. Severe weather, visibility, moving currents, climbing tasks, hazards, loading and other independent delays still apply. This changes the difficult-terrain travel-rate factor and tracking activity, not tactical difficult-terrain movement costs, combat Speed, forced-march rules or resource consumption. If leadership/coordination ends, subsequent travel uses ordinary rates; already elapsed time is not recalculated.

### Worked examples

A coordinated party with a slower laden wagon uses that wagon's actual normal route rate while ignoring the ordinary rough-ground slowdown. The feat cannot make the wagon cross a path narrower than its body.

A scout who leaves the coordinated route loses the group benefit for their separate travel. During combat, ordinary difficult-terrain costs still apply.

## Ranger Training (`ranger_training`)

These are ordinary catalog-compatible items or usable supplies, not magic loot, a free recipe ingredient classification or converted gp-to-Supply. ## Survivalist (`survivalist`)

Consume half your actual normal food requirement and half your actual normal potable-water requirement under the governing body/environment schedule. NEW: choose the strongest applicable fractional requirement reduction instead of multiplying overlapping reductions. To complete the source's broad survival/help clause, NEW proposal: you have advantage on actual Survival checks to adapt a camp, clothing, route or shelter to an ordinary nonmagical environment and on Constitution saves caused solely by ordinary heat/cold exposure. This does not confer food/water reduction on them, make unsafe material edible, or permit survival in lava, vacuum, drowning, magical extremes or an environment with no physically possible protective method. Actual hazard frequency, severity, DC and finite protection still apply. These explicit environmental limits are proposed replacements for the source's undefined absolute phrase, not a claim that it already said them.
The canonical daily standard is2lb food and8pints water, so this feat requires1lb/4pints; a Large consumer requires2lb/8pints rather than its ordinary4lb/16pints. ### Worked examples

## Tracker (`tracker`)

**Prerequisite:** Wisdom 15+

The source expressly allows tracking over water and air. NEW proposed completion preserves a real exceptional benefit: from an actual known starting trail or witnessed passage, you can perceive faint physical-passage impressions of that same creature's continuous route within60feet of your position for up to24elapsed hours after passage, including its waterborne or airborne segments. This is a feat-local extraordinary trail sense, not free knowledge of every nearby creature. Spend one active minute examining a segment and make Wisdom (Survival) DC15, increased by5 when the trail is more than12hours old and by5 where multiple actual trails are heavily intermingled; an exact concealing source may expressly block or modify this sensing. ### Worked examples

At a witnessed flight departure ten hours ago, a nearby actual airborne trail can be followed on the proposed DC15 success. The tracker still needs a real route or flight capability to follow it vertically.

## Wilderness Walker (`wilderness_walker`)

**Prerequisite:** Survival 4+

Use the shared Skill-feat proposal's explicit acquisition bridge for Survival4+: actual Survival proficiency and permanent ordinary skill modifier at least+4, excluding temporary/situational bonuses. NEW broad completion of 'no movement penalty in terrain': when your own body moves through an actually traversable route, ignore extra movement-cost factors and Speed reductions whose sole stated cause is the terrain itself, including ordinary difficult/severe ground and magical terrain that only imposes those movement penalties. Crawling, squeezing, load, injury, creature conditions, lack of a movement mode, occupied-space restrictions, forced movement and an actual obstruction remain separate causes and still apply. A terrain hazard's attack, save, damage, falling risk or inflicted Grappled/Restrained condition is not itself waived; if its separate condition reduces Speed, that condition remains. In strategic travel remove only your own terrain-derived rate penalty, not every party member's; the group still uses its actual slowest participant. A mount, vehicle or carried companion does not inherit your feat. Mode switching retains spent movement costs and never refunds earlier expenditure. This deliberately proposes broad terrain-cost protection, including qualifying magical terrain, and needs exact spell/hazard comparisons rather than silently narrowing the source to woodland alone.

### Worked examples

Crawling and squeezing through difficult terrain normally costs4feet per foot under current Core. Removing the terrain surcharge leaves3feet per foot: base1, crawling1 and squeezing1.

A magical bog that only doubles terrain movement cost can be crossed at ordinary terrain cost. If its separate failed save inflicts Restrained, the resulting Speed0 remains until that condition ends.
