- Define dawn recharge using the already reviewed campaign time procedure, physical item identity, the actually recorded maximum and one legitimate recharge event. - Resolve active wearing slots and complete bodies for Bracelet, Earring, Crown, Amulet, Ring, Tiara, Necklace, Brooch, Gem and Pendant. - Define combination on one roll with another jewel and existing class/spell/equipment bonuses, including Leatherworking and Tailoring only after their actual decisions. A save can genuinely involve more than one tag; do not grant multiple independent roll events merely to use more charges.
- Resolve physical identity, required supplemental bodies or stock, reserved order, damaged inputs, latest completion HP, return/recovery, mass, ordinary value and finished-item reference. Source materials and tiny bills do not automatically fund unlimited complete jewelry bodies.

Any replacement charge/property option needs its own precise review; decide the permitted improvement, persistent flaw, maximum/current charge state, recharge and repair interaction.
