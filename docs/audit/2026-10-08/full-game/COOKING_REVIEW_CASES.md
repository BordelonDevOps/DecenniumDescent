# Cooking manuscript boundary cases

These are worked rule traces for exact manuscript review, not runtime tests or human balance evidence.

1. **Independent shield:** A creature has 12 remaining temporary HP from a spell and an old Cooking Athletics rider. It accepts recipe-81: the old rider ends, the spell's shield remains, the offered four temporary HP are discarded, and the acid resistance operates for its hour/parent deadline. No discarded shield comes back later.
2. **Same-feast shield:** The old feast still owns nine temporary HP. Accepting recipe-81 removes those nine first, then offers four. Choosing to retain nine also means declining all of the new meal's magic. Full consumption still provides food.
3. **Meal timing:** An extra Action cannot shorten 100 consecutive regular-turn Actions and 600 seconds. When the hundredth Action occurs before 600 seconds, uninterrupted hand/access/eligibility continue until 600; no 101st Action is spent.
4. **Interrupted meal:** Interruption at minute nine loses the serving and progress. It grants no RP, food credit or magic; the prior feast remains. The serving cannot be resumed or split into poison doses.
5. **RP:** Two genuine recipe-88 servings can each restore one expended RP, each after ten minutes and accepted replacement. A declined serving restores none. No missing RP or no existing pool gives zero. This creates no extra Cooking rest cap.
6. **Two clocks:** A one-hour resistance can expire while the parent feast/shield remains until Long Rest. A completed beneficial Long Rest ends an ordinary parent and all its remaining children. Recipe-633's entire eight-hour parent instead continues through rest until its fixed deadline or another valid endpoint.
7. **Charm/fear:** Recipe-95's first actual matching save spends its rider even with advantage already present or advantage canceled. A no-save charm effect is not prevented and spends no save rider.
8. **Dynamic swim:** Recipe-104 grants swim speed equal to current effective walking speed. Changes to walking speed update the grant; an independently higher swim speed remains available under ordinary rules. No breathing or immunity to currents follows.
9. **Exact food:** Three full 2/3-lb servings credit one Small/Medium food day; six credit one Large food day. Neither amount supplies water, fuel or a complete Supply Unit. Each meal's counted food leaves its input inventory exactly once.
10. **Catalyst reserve:** For every recipe, recompute the maximum food deficit after floor(1.5Q) yield and the largest legally returnable nutritious catalyst unit. Round only the supplementary reservation upward to 0.01 lb. Full source ingredients still apply. The actual selected catalyst cannot both return and supply meal mass.
11. **Capacity:** A Q=3 batch must arrange real food-safe capacity/ready immediate consumers for four possible Masterwork servings before the check. One one-serving crock cannot hold four simultaneous servings. Immediate consumption requires actual present recipients ready for ten-minute meals, not a promise to find storage afterward.
12. **Storage:** A serving begun legally just before its 24-hour unprotected shelf deadline may finish uninterrupted. Merely opening it does not stop the clock. Repackaging and ordinary reheating do not restore freshness. Spoiled food grants neither meal nutrition nor feast magic.
13. **Quality:** A Q=3 Flawed batch yields one normal serving; a singleton until-rest feast instead fails. Dedicated HP-2 crocks can improve to HP 3 at Standard yield, gaining +1 current HP to preserve the existing damage deficit (2/2 becomes 3/3; 1/2 becomes 2/3). Kitchen tools and unrelated mess kits cannot receive this permanent improvement. No repeated recooking compounds maximum HP.
14. **Serpent failure:** A below-DC recipe-633 check creates one contaminated serving and no material recovery. Natural 1 also applies the actual mishap. Full consumption gives food, leaves the prior feast in place, then calls for DC 13 CON; a failure applies Poisoned for one hour. An old applicable poison-save advantage can apply. No extra damage or weapon coating exists.
15. **Donor eligibility:** Before a whole-creature recipe starts, all monster-part donors and the concrete shared ecology/same creature record are verified. Cave Bear walking 40 is eligible from its approved profile; its Multiattack and an invented Keen Smell are not. Missing eligible provenance/profile prevents starting, rather than granting a fallback later.
16. **Masterwork trigger:** Natural 20 with only Exceptional numeric margin grants no donor trait. Actual margin +10 or more grants the predeclared trait for one hour/parent deadline in addition to the normal quality choice. The Forge Dwarf Standard-to-Exceptional bridge does not trigger it.
17. **Work year:** Recipe-120's source active hours remain null. Its operational work year is 1,920 active hours; eligible artisan reduction is applied to that overlay. Finished-food age starts only when actual work completes.

The exact GPT review approved these traces after five finite repairs. Supplemental food stays separate until the outcome, then incorporates only the actual finished-product deficit. Borrowed speed replaces the unmodified mode base with max(existing base, donor printed speed), then applies actual modifiers once. Crock improvement preserves damage deficit. Maximum output dispositions allow only one simultaneous immediate meal per actual recipient, with reserved-storage fallback if that arrangement fails. Recipe-120 labels its null source work hours separately from the 1,920-hour overlay.
