## Source/current-rule boundary

**SOURCE/CURRENT RULE.** The Matrix lead personally needs high ranks in Arcana, History, Religion, Nature and Engineering and must cross-reference rare drops, libraries, mechanisms and seasonal changes. The reviewed character foundation has 20 basic skills and no Research skill. Ordinary translation, deciphering, Language Insight, Oracle Tongues, Knowledge Transfer, death prevention, resurrection and corruption tools retain their actual reviewed/pending statuses and scopes. Soul Mirror is currently reviewed-authored as a one-shot reflection ward and supplies no soul custody.

**OWNER DIRECTION.** Research across multiple Descent libraries may yield functional understanding and knowledge taint. Level 5+ Old-One voice/proximity exposure is an independent taint cause. A Pact Hunt creates hardcore status; actual death while hunted is normally final, with extremely rare Divine/Abyssal exceptions. No fixed seven-stage model, six-tick model, universal DC or already-selected numeric combination follows.

## 1. Research and qualification

**NEW PROPOSED DESIGN.** Do **not** create a Research skill. Research is the activity wrapper; every roll uses an existing skill.

For Matrix work, the primary researcher must personally qualify in all five Core disciplines. Interpret “high ranks” for compatibility as follows:

1. Be proficient in Arcana, History, Religion, Nature and Engineering.
2. Earn an **Advanced Qualification mark** in each discipline. A mark is earned either through existing Expertise in that skill or by leading two authenticated Equation-relevant findings with that skill from two different underlying evidence identities.
3. The mark grants **no bonus** and never adds PB. It is only a gate.
4. Full Matrix Lead requires proficiency plus a mark in all five disciplines. Assistants do not replace a missing personal qualification.

Knowledge Transfer remains useful. Its actual one-hour proficiency is real during that hour and can satisfy ordinary proficiency-gated subwork wholly inside the window. It does not transfer Expertise, memories, secrets or Advanced Qualification marks, so it cannot by itself create a Full Matrix Lead.

## 2. Evidence and understanding states

**NEW PROPOSED DESIGN.** Track every source through:

`Possessed -> Facsimile -> Literal Translation -> Authenticated Meaning -> Functional Model -> Demonstrated Operational Understanding`

`Execution Attempt` and `Enforcement` are separate action records, not knowledge stages.

A physical copy has its own `physicalSourceId`; all copies of the same content share one `underlyingEvidenceId`. Copies improve redundancy/access but do not multiply independent corroboration.

### Functional Model gate

Require all of the following registered categories:

- evidence from **two distinct Descent libraries**;
- one mechanism observation;
- one seasonal/change observation;
- one rare drop or other physical clue.

This is an evidence-category gate, **not five recovered Equation fragments**. No missing manuscript mathematics is invented.

### Research block

**NEW PROPOSED DESIGN.** One Equation Synthesis Block is four active hours, one specific question and one named existing lead skill. Generic Expedition research assistance may apply (up to three trained meaningful assistants, +1 each) unless an exact language/crafting procedure supplies its own narrower rule. The block shares the same world clock with rest, travel, protection and guard duty.

Use the current Core task degrees:

- **Success:** one pre-authored supported finding.
- **Critical Success:** that finding plus one pre-authored corroboration or constraint from the same evidence set; never an extra formula.
- **Failure:** no certified finding, but give one truthful progress result chosen before the roll: eliminate one registered hypothesis, reveal one missing dependency, or identify one conflict needing another source.
- **Critical Failure:** the same truthful progress plus only an independently lawful, predeclared hazard.

Retry requires new evidence, a concretely different supported method, or resolution of the identified conflict.

## 3. Functional demonstration without real mathematics

**NEW PROPOSED DESIGN.** After a Functional Model, run three Prediction Tests:

1. **Mechanism prediction:** what will a registered mechanism do under a stated in-world condition?
2. **Cross-source prediction:** which of three interpretations is consistent with the authenticated records?
3. **Safety/abort prediction:** which condition means the procedure must stop or becomes unsafe?

The Director prepares three concrete outcomes from already authored evidence. The player chooses and explains using learned facts. If uncertainty remains, one appropriate existing-skill check resolves it. All three settled tests establish Demonstrated Operational Understanding. Failure identifies the unsupported premise and opens a research lead; it does not erase discoveries.

## 4. Language and borrowed competence

**SOURCE/CURRENT RULE.** Known-language plain prose uses the current translation speed. The eight-hour/250-word decipher project, its one qualified +1 assistant, one PB contribution, truthful failure behavior and Language Insight ledger remain unchanged. Oracle Tongues I keeps its actual receptive spoken-mortal and Lore/Insight limits.

**NEW PROPOSED DESIGN.** Temporary competence may complete work only while it is actually active. A one-hour Knowledge Transfer may therefore support a one-hour subtask, but not a four- or eight-hour job unless those jobs are split into independently legal blocks. Specific information understood during temporary competence remains retained under the current language rules; general competence expires.

## 5. Research route

**SOURCE/CURRENT RULE.** Cantor’s Archive is mapped to Floor 47. Wax Library is an original Dungeon chamber seed with no assigned floor or Equation holding. The Unbound Academy library, Citadel of Many Echoes and Silent Library are not silently relocated into the Dungeon. Library of Shifting Sigils and Sigil Navigators Library are not merged.

**NEW PROPOSED DESIGN.** Use route slots rather than inventing recovered fragments:

- **L1 — Cantor’s Archive, Floor 47:** established location; author a distinct research holding later if approved.
- **L2 — Wax Library or another explicitly authored Descent library:** Wax may be selected and placed later; its floor/holding remain open. If chronology/placement makes Wax unsuitable, fill L2 with a newly authored Descent library explicitly labelled new design.
- **L3 — optional unassigned library slot:** use only if pacing needs a third independent library.
- Nonlibrary evidence slots: rare drop/physical clue, mechanism observation, seasonal/change observation.

No slot contains a recovered Equation fragment until separately authored and approved. The post-Infinite-Yield frame remains context, while the original adaptation permission keeps earlier/multi-era use possible. Campaign epoch remains open.

## 6. Taint

**OWNER DIRECTION.** Knowledge taint and level-5+ voice/proximity taint are distinct.

**NEW PROPOSED DESIGN.** Keep two visible ledgers:

- **Knowledge Taint:** KT0 Clear -> KT1 Resonant -> KT2 Intrusive -> KT3 Compromised. First Functional Model advances to KT1; first Demonstrated Operational Understanding to KT2; first deliberate Execution Attempt or Enforcement to KT3. Copying or literal translation alone does not auto-advance KT.
- **Proximity/Voice Taint:** PT0 Clear -> PT1 Whispered -> PT2 Pressed -> PT3 Saturated. Beginning on Descent level 5, an actual Director-declared qualifying Old-One voice/proximity exposure scene can advance at most one PT stage. Merely standing on a deep floor without a qualifying exposure does not create a tick.

When one warning intensity is needed, use the worse stage; never add the tracks together or erase their separate causes. These are not Unity, ordinary Corrupted, Null Veil, a generic curse, or Pact attention by default. Existing cures/wards affect only what they actually name.

### Warning ladder

- Stage 1: sensory anomaly plus explicit acknowledgement that exposure changed the character.
- Stage 2: repeatable intrusion plus one actionable mitigation/research clue.
- Stage 3: explicit warning that further operational use can draw pursuit.

This gives informed choices without revealing actor identities or objective cosmology the characters have not earned.

## 7. Pact attention and Hunt

**NEW PROPOSED DESIGN.** Track attention (`None / Observed / Pursued`) separately from Hunt. Hunt begins only when a Hunt Trigger Record is created after at least one warning signal and one explicit trigger:

- deliberate Equation execution attempt;
- deliberate dissemination of demonstrated operational knowledge to a new party;
- Enforcement that changes or compels a protected Matrix/Pact function; or
- a specifically authored event expressly marked Hunt-capable.

Possession, copying, literal translation, a taint stage by itself, or mere level-5+ proximity does not automatically start Hunt. Once a trigger is confirmed, Hunt initiation is deterministic rather than a random taint roll.

## 8. Death and survival

**SOURCE/CURRENT RULE + NEW ORDERING.** Resolve the real survival rules first:

1. incoming effect;
2. legal prevention/reduction;
3. valid death-prevention ward/feature;
4. Wound/death procedure;
5. only if the identity is now actually Dead and Hunt is active, create **Hunted Death**.

A legal Death Ward/Ritual Ward is not cancelled merely because a Hunt exists. If it prevents death, there is no Hunted Death yet.

**NEW PROPOSED DESIGN.** Hunted Death blocks ordinary resurrection/return/copy escape. Moving the corpse, ending Soul Bind, cleansing ordinary Corrupted or changing planes does not erase it. Corpse retrieval, custody release and identity return remain separate operations.

## 9. Extremely rare intervention

**OWNER DIRECTION.** Divine/Abyssal intervention can very rarely override finality.

**NEW PROPOSED DESIGN.** Before an intervention resolves, write all seven fields:

1. Divine or Abyssal sponsor class (the exact identity may remain unrevealed);
2. demonstrated access/authority basis;
3. willing dead identity;
4. one-time offer;
5. irreversible price/obligation;
6. aftermath;
7. citation to the authored scene/source.

It cannot be purchased as ordinary gold/RP service or inferred from a spell/class name. The price must create at least one irreversible campaign consequence: permanent surrender of a major earned boon/resource, binding service/contract with enforceable consequence, or a persistent alteration/scar with a real drawback. Return keeps Hunt history, taint, Wounds, debts and custody history unless the intervention expressly changes one. Record an Intervention Scar and sponsor obligation permanently.
