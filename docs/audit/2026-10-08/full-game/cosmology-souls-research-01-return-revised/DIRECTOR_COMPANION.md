## Source/current-rule boundary

**SOURCE/CURRENT RULE.** The Matrix lead personally needs high ranks in Arcana, History, Religion, Nature and Engineering and must cross-reference rare drops, libraries, mechanisms and seasonal changes. The reviewed character foundation has 20 basic skills and no Research skill. Ordinary translation, deciphering, Language Insight, Oracle Tongues, Knowledge Transfer, death prevention, resurrection and corruption tools retain their actual reviewed/pending statuses and scopes. Soul Mirror is currently reviewed-authored as a one-shot reflection ward and supplies no soul custody.

**OWNER DIRECTION.** Research across multiple Descent libraries may yield functional understanding and knowledge taint. Level 5+ Old-One voice/proximity exposure is an independent taint cause. A Pact Hunt creates hardcore status; actual death while hunted is normally final, with extremely rare Divine/Abyssal exceptions. No fixed seven-stage model, six-tick model, universal DC or already-selected numeric combination follows.

## 1. Research and qualification

**NEW PROVISIONAL DESIGN.** Do **not** create a Research skill. Research is the activity wrapper; every roll uses an existing skill.

For Matrix work, the primary researcher must personally qualify in all five Core disciplines. Interpret “high ranks” for compatibility as follows:

1. Be proficient in Arcana, History, Religion, Nature and Engineering.
2. Earn an **Advanced Qualification mark** in each discipline. A mark is earned either through existing Expertise in that skill or by leading two authenticated Equation-relevant findings with that skill from two different underlying evidence identities.
3. The mark grants **no bonus** and never adds PB. It is only a gate.
4. Full Matrix Lead requires proficiency plus a mark in all five disciplines. Assistants do not replace a missing personal qualification.

Knowledge Transfer remains useful. Its actual one-hour proficiency is real during that hour and can satisfy ordinary proficiency-gated subwork wholly inside the window. It does not transfer Expertise, memories, secrets or Advanced Qualification marks, so it cannot by itself create a Full Matrix Lead.

## 2. Evidence and understanding states

**NEW PROVISIONAL DESIGN.** Track every source through:

`Possessed -> Facsimile -> Literal Translation -> Authenticated Meaning -> Functional Model -> Demonstrated Operational Understanding`

`Execution Attempt` and `Enforcement` are separate action records, not knowledge stages.

A physical copy has its own `physicalSourceId`; all copies of the same content share one `underlyingEvidenceId`. Copies improve redundancy/access but do not multiply independent corroboration.

### Functional Model gate

Require all of the following registered categories:

- evidence from **two distinct Descent libraries**;
- one mechanism observation;
- one seasonal/change observation;
- one rare drop or other physical clue.

This is an evidence-category gate, **not five recovered Equation fragments**. No missing manuscript mathematics is invented.

### Research block

**NEW PROVISIONAL DESIGN.** One Equation Synthesis Block is four active hours, one specific question and one named existing lead skill. Generic Expedition research assistance may apply (up to three trained meaningful assistants, +1 each) unless an exact language/crafting procedure supplies its own narrower rule. The block shares the same world clock with rest, travel, protection and guard duty.

Use the current Core task degrees:

- **Success:** one pre-authored supported finding.
- **Critical Success:** that finding plus one pre-authored corroboration or constraint from the same evidence set; never an extra formula.
- **Failure:** no certified finding, but give one truthful progress result chosen before the roll: eliminate one registered hypothesis, reveal one missing dependency, or identify one conflict needing another source.
- **Critical Failure:** the same truthful progress plus only an independently lawful, predeclared hazard.

Retry requires new evidence, a concretely different supported method, or resolution of the identified conflict.

## 3. Functional demonstration without real mathematics

**NEW PROVISIONAL DESIGN.** After a Functional Model, run three Prediction Tests:

1. **Mechanism prediction:** what will a registered mechanism do under a stated in-world condition?
2. **Cross-source prediction:** which of three interpretations is consistent with the authenticated records?
3. **Safety/abort prediction:** which condition means the procedure must stop or becomes unsafe?

The Director prepares three concrete outcomes from already authored evidence. The player chooses and explains using learned facts. If uncertainty remains, one appropriate existing-skill check resolves it. All three settled tests establish Demonstrated Operational Understanding. Failure identifies the unsupported premise and opens a research lead; it does not erase discoveries.

## 4. Language and borrowed competence

**SOURCE/CURRENT RULE.** Known-language plain prose uses the current translation speed. The eight-hour/250-word decipher project, its one qualified +1 assistant, one PB contribution, truthful failure behavior and Language Insight ledger remain unchanged. Oracle Tongues I keeps its actual receptive spoken-mortal and Lore/Insight limits.

**NEW PROVISIONAL DESIGN.** Temporary competence may complete work only while it is actually active. A one-hour Knowledge Transfer may therefore support a one-hour subtask, but not a four- or eight-hour job unless those jobs are split into independently legal blocks. Specific information understood during temporary competence remains retained under the current language rules; general competence expires.

## 5. Research route

**SOURCE/CURRENT RULE.** Cantor’s Archive is mapped to Floor 47. Wax Library is an original Dungeon chamber seed with no assigned floor or Equation holding. The Unbound Academy library, Citadel of Many Echoes and Silent Library are not silently relocated into the Dungeon. Library of Shifting Sigils and Sigil Navigators Library are not merged.

**NEW PROVISIONAL DESIGN.** Use route slots rather than inventing recovered fragments:

- **L1 — Cantor’s Archive, Floor 47:** established location; author a distinct research holding later if approved.
- **L2 — Wax Library or another explicitly authored Descent library:** Wax may be selected and placed later; its floor/holding remain open. If chronology/placement makes Wax unsuitable, fill L2 with a newly authored Descent library explicitly labelled new design.
- **L3 — optional unassigned library slot:** use only if pacing needs a third independent library.
- Nonlibrary evidence slots: rare drop/physical clue, mechanism observation, seasonal/change observation.

No slot contains a recovered Equation fragment until separately authored and approved. The post-Infinite-Yield frame remains context, while the original adaptation permission keeps earlier/multi-era use possible. Campaign epoch remains open.

## 6. Taint

**OWNER DIRECTION.** Knowledge taint and level-5+ voice/proximity taint are distinct.

**NEW PROVISIONAL DESIGN.** Keep two visible ledgers:

- **Knowledge Taint:** KT0 Clear -> KT1 Resonant -> KT2 Intrusive -> KT3 Compromised. First Functional Model advances to KT1; first Demonstrated Operational Understanding to KT2; first deliberate Execution Attempt or Enforcement to KT3. Copying or literal translation alone does not auto-advance KT.
- **Proximity/Voice Taint:** PT0 Clear -> PT1 Whispered -> PT2 Pressed -> PT3 Saturated. Beginning on Descent level 5, an actual Director-declared qualifying Old-One voice/proximity exposure scene can advance at most one PT stage. Merely standing on a deep floor without a qualifying exposure does not create a tick.

When one warning intensity is needed, use the worse stage; never add the tracks together or erase their separate causes. These are not Unity, ordinary Corrupted, Null Veil, a generic curse, or Pact attention by default. Existing cures/wards affect only what they actually name.

### Warning ladder

- Stage 1: sensory anomaly plus explicit acknowledgement that exposure changed the character.
- Stage 2: repeatable intrusion plus one actionable mitigation/research clue.
- Stage 3: explicit warning that further operational use can draw pursuit.

This gives informed choices without revealing actor identities or objective cosmology the characters have not earned.

## 7. Pact attention and Hunt

**NEW PROVISIONAL DESIGN.** Track attention (`None / Observed / Pursued`) separately from Hunt. Hunt begins only when a Hunt Trigger Record is created after at least one warning signal and one explicit trigger:

- deliberate Equation execution attempt;
- deliberate dissemination of demonstrated operational knowledge to a new party;
- Enforcement that changes or compels a protected Matrix/Pact function; or
- a specifically authored event expressly marked Hunt-capable.

Possession, copying, literal translation, a taint stage by itself, or mere level-5+ proximity does not automatically start Hunt. Once a trigger is confirmed, Hunt initiation is deterministic rather than a random taint roll.

## 8. Death and survival

**SOURCE/CURRENT RULE + NEW ORDERING.** Resolve the real survival rules first:

1. incoming effect;
2. legal prevention/reduction;
3. valid death-prevention ward/feature;
4. Wound/death procedure;
5. only if the identity is now actually Dead and Hunt is active, create **Hunted Death**.

A legal Death Ward/Ritual Ward is not cancelled merely because a Hunt exists. If it prevents death, there is no Hunted Death yet.

**NEW PROVISIONAL DESIGN.** Hunted Death blocks ordinary resurrection/return/copy escape. Moving the corpse, ending Soul Bind, cleansing ordinary Corrupted or changing planes does not erase it. Corpse retrieval, custody release and identity return remain separate operations.

## 9. Extremely rare intervention

**OWNER DIRECTION.** Divine/Abyssal intervention can very rarely override finality.

**NEW PROVISIONAL DESIGN.** Before an intervention resolves, write all seven fields:

1. Divine or Abyssal sponsor class (the exact identity may remain unrevealed);
2. demonstrated access/authority basis;
3. willing dead identity;
4. one-time offer;
5. irreversible price/obligation;
6. aftermath;
7. citation to the authored scene/source.

It cannot be purchased as ordinary gold/RP service or inferred from a spell/class name. The price must create at least one irreversible campaign consequence: permanent surrender of a major earned boon/resource, binding service/contract with enforceable consequence, or a persistent alteration/scar with a real drawback. Return keeps Hunt history, taint, Wounds, debts and custody history unless the intervention expressly changes one. Record an Intervention Scar and sponsor obligation permanently.

# SR-F01–SR-F07 revision conference — operating repair

**Status: NEW PROVISIONAL DESIGN.** This revision changes no frozen source fact, recovered holding, cosmological identity, campaign epoch, epilogue rule or pending-spell status. It replaces operating proposals from the first return where the owner identified a circular qualification path, an over-rigid evidence ladder, knowledge/action conflation, under-specified taint, a warning-order loophole, incomplete exceptional-return state, and an under-specified numerical research procedure.

## SR-F01 — Qualification without circularity

The Core still requires the primary Matrix researcher to personally possess “high ranks” across **Arcana, History, Religion, Nature and Engineering**. For this provisional interface, “high ranks” means two separate predicates per discipline: **current proficiency** and an **Advanced Qualification mark**. Five bare proficiencies are not enough.

A bounded **discipline finding** may be led before Full Matrix Lead. The lead needs the declared skill proficiency for every active interval that requires it, authenticated evidence, and a preregistered discipline question. Two completed supported findings in that same discipline from two different underlying evidence identities earn that discipline’s Advanced mark; existing Expertise also supplies that mark. The mark grants no PB, advantage or other bonus.

This removes the circularity: researchers earn marks through bounded findings, then later combine all five marks for Full Matrix Synthesis. Helpers never pool marks into a lead.

A Bard can legally select Arcana, History and Religion from the Bard class list, and a Director-agreed custom background can legally provide two skills, allowing Nature and Engineering. That establishes a possible five-proficiency character, not automatic “high ranks.”

**Knowledge Transfer correction.** It transfers no mark or Expertise, but its one-hour skill proficiency is real. Therefore a character who already owns all five marks but is currently missing one of the five proficiencies qualifies personally as Full Matrix Lead while a lawful Knowledge Transfer supplies that missing proficiency. The moment the loan expires, qualification ends.

The four-hour Synthesis Block is **240 recorded active work minutes** and can pause. Each interval records its exact start/stop, evidence, lead, assistants, five current proficiencies and five owned marks. Rest, travel, guard duty, recasting and recharge do not count as synthesis, and one elapsed minute cannot count twice. Four separate one-hour loans can cover four synthesis hours only if every activation and all real short-rest/resource/time requirements are actually paid. This pausable rule does **not** modify or subdivide the reviewed eight-active-hour Translate/Decipher project.

Examples: a History researcher can earn a mark by leading two distinct authenticated History findings without already owning that mark; two copies of one observation earn nothing extra; an expired Engineering loan pauses a synthesis at its recorded minute; a later legal loan can resume from that minute without counting the intervening rest.

## SR-F02 — Evidence custody is not epistemology

Replace the old Possessed → Facsimile → Literal Translation ladder with three independent registers:

1. **Access/provenance:** what was actually inspected/observed, when, where, underlying identity, custody/authentication basis and missing content.
2. **Physical copy:** optional facsimile/transcript/rubbing/image record. Copies keep the parent underlying identity and do not multiply corroboration.
3. **Epistemic record:** Uninterpreted, Literal Meaning, Authenticated Meaning, Supported Discipline Finding, Functional Model, Demonstrated Operational Understanding.

These are not mandatory sequential rungs. Plain accessible material in a language the character actually knows can be comprehended directly under the reviewed language rules. An observed mechanism can support a finding without first creating a copy. “Authenticated” means the evidence is supported as genuinely the claimed record/object/observation; it does not make every statement inside the source true.

One real object can yield several distinct authenticated observation receipts when the party genuinely observes different phenomena/conditions to answer different preregistered questions. A duplicate copy of one observation cannot fill several category receipts.

## SR-F03 — Knowledge taint follows knowledge

KT no longer advances because somebody merely **acts**. The provisional knowledge states are:

- **KT1 Resonant:** verified dangerous operational subprinciple.
- **KT2 Intrusive:** supported Functional Model connecting operational subprinciples.
- **KT3 Compromised:** Demonstrated Operational Understanding, meaning a supported bounded prediction/control demonstration.

Execution Attempt, Enforcement and Outside Enforcement stay in their own action records. Blind execution, accidental activation or rote use does not manufacture understanding. Conversely, equivalent dangerous operational knowledge lawfully acquired by another route still receives the corresponding KT state; skipping this worksheet is not taint immunity.

## SR-F04 — Default taint procedure

The owner supplied qualitative taint, not these numbers. The following DCs, durations and stage count remain **NEW PROVISIONAL DESIGN / UNTESTED**.

KT and PT are separate ledgers. For PT, only a real level-5+ Old-One voice/proximity scene can qualify. One continuous source/contact period is one exposure episode. Do not create extra rolls by splitting turns, minutes, pages or rereads.

On first eligible PT exposure in an episode, make one Wisdom save: **DC 12 at PT0, 13 at PT1, 14 at PT2, 15 at PT3**. Success prevents a PT advance from that episode. Failure advances one stage, maximum PT3, and applies the new-stage symptom once:

- **PT1 Whispered:** disadvantage on the next INT/WIS task check before recovery.
- **PT2 Pressed:** PT1 plus no research assistance while the symptom remains.
- **PT3 Saturated:** Dazed through the next regular turn (or 1 minute outside initiative), then the PT2 assistance restriction until grounding completes.

Leaving actual qualifying exposure for ten uninterrupted minutes ends the transient PT symptom. It does not erase PT history. After a full long rest with no qualifying exposure since the previous long rest, a **NEW PROVISIONAL DC 12 Wisdom save** can reduce PT one stage. KT does not automatically decay with rest because the dangerous knowledge is still known.

KT has bounded research symptoms: KT1 can impose disadvantage on the first research INT/WIS task after a long rest unless ten minutes of grounding occurs first; KT2 adds a ten-minute post-failure assistance lockout; KT3 requires a DC 15 Wisdom save before a voluntary Demonstrated Operational Understanding test or Hunt-capable operational-use commitment, with a failed save causing Dazed for one turn/minute before a new voluntary commitment. These symptoms never erase learned facts.

Use actual defenses and cures by **semantic scope**. A defense against Dazed can block the PT3 Dazed symptom without blocking the underlying taint. A rule that actually removes an applicable category of influence can affect the state according to its text even if it does not spell the internal labels “KT” or “PT.” Current Corruption Cleanse still only removes its authored eligible ordinary Corrupted record and does not alter KT/PT. Pending Purification remains pending.

**Complete default exposure example:** At Descent level 5+, the Director has a real authored qualifying voice/proximity source. A PT0 character begins continuous exposure, one episode ID is recorded, and they make DC 12 Wisdom once. On failure they become PT1 and take its one transient symptom. Remaining five more minutes does not reroll. An auditory-blocking defense only prevents exposure if the authored source actually requires hearing; it does not stop independently sufficient proximity. After leaving exposure and grounding ten minutes, the symptom ends but PT1 remains. The learned facts remain.

## SR-F05 — Hunt warning before commitment

Missing an earlier warning is no loophole. Immediately before a character first commits to a newly Hunt-capable voluntary action, give a mechanical warning: continuing is Hunt-capable; Hunt is hardcore status; if actual death later occurs while Hunt is active, Hunted Death normally blocks ordinary return. Do not disclose hidden cosmological ownership beyond earned evidence.

The warning itself does not start Hunt. If the player continues, record commitment and then the actual trigger. Initiation is recorded once. Later genuinely separate Hunt-capable acts can add trigger-history records, but one continuous act cannot tick repeatedly.

If an authored event is already resolving and no meaningful choice existed before an unavoidable consequence, do not retroactively fabricate consent. Apply the authored consequence. The provisional deliberate trigger is absent unless the source independently imposes Hunt. If a real choice window appears during resolution, give the warning before that choice closes. Lack of an old warning never grants permanent immunity: warn at the next legal commitment point.

Hunt ends only by an exact adopted release rule, an authored exceptional-intervention term, or another Director-authorized source event that actually says it ends/releases Hunt. No hunter roster or cosmic identity is inferred.

## SR-F06 — Death history and exceptional return

Keep exact healing/death timing. Resolve damage/effect, legal reduction, and every actual would-die replacement first. Only after those windows close is **Dead** committed. At that moment create an immutable deathEvent. If Hunt is active, also create immutable HuntedDeath history and the ordinary-return blocker.

History and current state are different records. An extremely rare valid Divine/Abyssal intervention can later change that same identity’s currentLifeState from Dead to Alive while the death/HuntedDeath history remains. It creates no spare identity, duplicate soul or second body entitlement.

Each exceptional offer needs a unique `interventionId` and a unique consumed resolution. Consuming it cannot authorize all future deaths. Its record must state sponsor class, demonstrated authority, willing identity, targeted death/HuntedDeath event, one-time terms, irreversible price/obligation, aftermath, and a complete return state: HP, Wounds, conditions/persistent afflictions, body/remains disposition, location, property custody, debts/contracts, Hunt release/retention, KT/PT, and other history.

Use an actually reviewed return procedure when the intervention expressly invokes it and makes its Hunted-Death override legal. If a valid exceptional return gives no HP/body procedure, the provisional default is **Alive at 1 HP**, clear death-save counters/Stable deadline, preserve Wounds and persistent states not expressly changed, and return at the usable body or expressly authored legal return location. This is only a default for an already-valid exceptional intervention; it does not promote or assume efficacy for a pending resurrection or Clone spell.

## SR-F07 — Four-hour synthesis numbers and retries

Before the first active synthesis minute, preregister: exact question, bounded finding, evidence receipt IDs/categories, lead skill, fixed DC and all four degree outcomes. Use a printed current DC when an exact procedure supplies one. Otherwise, for this new synthesis interface only, recommended bands are DC 10 direct/straightforward authenticated evidence, DC 15 reconciliation of multiple authenticated records, DC 20 damaged/indirect but genuinely sufficient evidence, and DC 25 only for an exceptionally difficult reconstruction the packet already shows is solvable. Nondiagnostic evidence has no DC that can invent the answer.

Then use the current Core fixed-DC task degrees. The Director can keep the answer secret but cannot retarget the question/DC/outcomes after seeing the roll. Success certifies only the preregistered supported finding. Critical Success may add only a preregistered constraint/corroboration that the evidence truly supports. Failure records truthful progress; Critical Failure can add only a predeclared independently lawful hazard. No result invents a missing Equation fragment.

A retry needs a real state change: new authenticated evidence, a genuinely different supported method/tool/language capability, repaired/recovered content, a resolved evidence conflict, or newly qualified competence. Rephrasing the same question or adding another copy of the same source is not a retry.

A single underlying object can support several discipline findings only if each has a distinct preregistered discipline question, distinct authenticated observation/meaning basis and independently supported result. One sentence cannot be farmed into five marks by changing the skill label.

**Worked fictional example:** Two authenticated Descent-library records, one authenticated observation of a fictional harmonic regulator, one seasonal observation and one rare residue are preregistered to answer: “Do these records support the limited proposition that the fictional regulator changes response when two observed conditions coincide?” Engineering, DC 15, because multiple authenticated records must be reconciled. Success records only that bounded relation. Critical Success may also record a preregistered seasonal constraint if supported. Failure records the unresolved conflict and the next needed observation. Nothing in the example is a recovered Equation fragment or source holding.
