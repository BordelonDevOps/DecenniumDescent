## DI7-01 — Mass Revelation

**Original question:** Preserve revealing HIDDEN TRAPS,ENEMIES,MAGIC AND LIES in a60FT CONE,Core Action/60ft/Instantaneous. Define exact origin/geometry,recipient(s),line of effect and information supplied in each of the four categories. A lie needs a bounded spoken/asserted event and distinction between deliberate deception,error and unknown truth; no unrestricted mind-reading or complete truth replacement. Enemy classification and hidden traps/magic need actual source records,not thematic inference. Decide interaction with concealment and information protections explicitly while retaining the broad reveal promise. The spell reveals rather than disarms,triggers,damages,dispels or compels honesty. Paid resolution and materially unchanged inquiries must preserve uncertainty without fabricating facts.

KEEP one instantaneous revelation in a60ft cone originating at the caster. NEW use the ordinary cone geometry and line of effect from that origin; choose direction at commitment. Every readable portion is examined once, without a creature-count cap. The caster alone receives the following snapshot; communicating it uses ordinary channels. Revelation neither paints targets for allies nor removes an actual concealment, wall or information defense.

Hidden traps: reveal the current physical site, occupied footprint, armed/dormant state and immediate trigger category of every actual trap in the readable cone. A mundane concealed plate qualifies; a dangerous creature is not automatically a trap. This reveals no automatic disarm solution, secret combination or future die outcome. Enemies: reveal the current occupied footprint and creature count of actual beings presently intending or executing a meaningful hostile act against the caster or a member of the caster's current party. This explicitly NEW intent predicate is not alignment, reputation, species or mere disagreement. It does not read the plan, memories, exact identity or statistics. Actual protection of intent/location can independently limit those fields. An unprotected invisible enemy's location can be learned without becoming visibly targetable for a sight-required spell; ordinary attack concealment remains.

Magic: reveal each actual current magical effect's local footprint, spell/nonspell category, generating school/rank when its source has those fields, and whether it is active or suppressed. An absent school is not Universal; a dormant enchanted item's relevant magic is distinguishable from an active spell but supplies no charges, attunement history or full property list. Overlapping sources remain distinct, not an invented combined spell. Lies: NEW consider factual assertions actually communicated by a creature currently in the cone during the preceding six elapsed seconds. For assertions the caster perceived and understood, identify whether the speaker knowingly asserted what they believed false. A sincere error is not a deliberate lie; uncertainty, irony and an assertion whose meaningful content was not understood yield no invented objective truth. A scripted sound without a current asserting mind supplies no fabricated belief. This is a bounded deception reading, not continuous honesty enforcement or a substitute correct answer.

No generic save is added to these information outputs. Any actual source defense prescribing a check or opposed procedure keeps that procedure and its real rank/ability requirements. No fact is extracted through a blocked field by repeated rewording; unchanged inquiries use the shared stable-information rule. The effect disarms nothing, ends no invisibility or spell, damages nothing and compels no confession. It leaves a remembered snapshot, with no tracking after the instant or promise that an enemy, trap or magical footprint remains where it was.

**New decisions:** NEW exact four-category information fields, hostile-intent predicate, private instantaneous snapshot and six-second understood-assertion window.

## DI7-03 — Premonition Shield

**Original question:** Preserve YOU OR ONE ALLY taking NO DAMAGE FROM THE NEXT2 ATTACKS OR SPELLS,Core Action/120ft/Instantaneous. Define finite ward lifetime/concentration,recipient selection,what qualifies as the next event and when each of two charges is consumed. Resolve misses,zero-damage hits,multiattack,multibeam spells,ongoing spell ticks,area spells and non-damage riders through actual source identities. State whether only damage is blocked and how damage-sharing/prevention ordering affects consumption; do not turn this into general effect immunity or unlimited reduction across a persistent spell. Exactly two events remain protected; recast/suppression/round changes do not recharge an old ward.

KEEP one willing perceived ally, including self, within120ft and line of effect, and exactly two protected attacks or spells. NEW resolve the Instantaneous source into a nonconcentration ward lasting at most one elapsed minute, with two charges and no recipient Reaction. One operative Premonition Shield family per recipient: a successful fresh cast replaces the prior ward, discarding unused old charges rather than adding them. Creation is not itself one of the recipient's protected subsequent spell events.

NEW event definition: each separate direct attack against the recipient is one event. A spell's immediate resolution that directly targets the recipient or actually includes its space in the resolved area is one event for that recipient, grouping that cast's simultaneous components/beams/attacks in that same immediate resolution. Thus a three-attack mundane Multiattack supplies three events, while a single multi-beam spell's immediate resolution supplies one; do not count both the spell and its constituent attacks. A later periodic spell occurrence is a fresh event at that occurrence, not unlimited protection from the entire spell identity. Resolve simultaneous event order under the ordinary stable source order.

Consume one charge before the qualifying attack roll or the spell's recipient resolution, even on a miss, successful save, zero damage or a nondamaging beneficial spell. An attack cancelled before it is made, a countered spell that never resolves, an area not actually including the recipient, or a dormant effect with no occurrence does not consume a charge. This explicit next-event rule prevents saving the ward for a favorable damage result. Suppression makes the ward unavailable without consuming charges for events during the gap; resumption retains only its original remaining charges and deadline.

For the covered event, prevent all eligible direct damage assigned to this recipient, never below zero, at the actual finite-prevention stage after recipient defenses and before post-defense sharing/tempHP. Pre-mitigation sharing such as Bloodbond/Veinlock has already assigned separate recipient shares; this ward covers only its recipient's own share. A post-defense transfer flagged to skip recipient prevention cannot be prevented again and does not become a new attack/spell event merely because it retains provenance. No prevented damage is transferred, stored, reflected or converted into healing. All actual would-die, concentration and Wound consequences then use the real remaining damage under ordinary rules.

The spell prevents damage only. A hit remains a hit; conditions, movement, lost concentration from a separate rule, item effects, direct HP assignments/sacrifices and death-state effects are not automatically prevented. Falling, mundane hazards and nonattack nonspell effects do not become eligible by association with a spell. On the second qualifying event finish its one protected resolution, then end the ward. Expiry, dispel or recipient destruction discards unused charges. Neither rest, transfer of a token nor another scene refreshes this application.

**New decisions:** NEW nonconcentration one-minute lifetime, exact event grouping/consumption including misses and nondamaging spells, recipient-stage prevention and same-family replacement.

### Authored examples

The second hits for24 eligible damage after defenses; charge2 prevents that24 before tempHP, while an independently applicable hit condition remains. A third sword attack is unprotected. The ward neither changes the miss/hit results nor grants a Reaction.

DI7-03-B: A single resolving spell hits the recipient with three immediate beams. The proposed grouping spends one charge and prevents their eligible damage in that immediate resolution. A later periodic occurrence of the same spell spends the second charge. A post-defense damage transfer that forbids another prevention step is not secretly blocked by this ward.

## DI7-04 — Fate Echo

**Original question:** Preserve copying ONE NON-DAMAGING SPELL CAST IN THE LAST3ROUNDS,Core Action/120ft/Instantaneous. Define the historical eligibility window,caster observation/knowledge,legal source spell rank/access and non-damaging classification including indirect summons or delayed harm. Compare Cast Echo,whose source permits any spell; preserve this narrower qualifier without merging identities. Decide new casting costs/components,Action and concentration,targets/range,save/DC and copied choices. Copying a spell is not replaying a past outcome,duplicating an already-spent item or learning a spell permanently. Prevent recursive copies of copies and revived one-shot effects through explicit source-event lineage,not by silently removing the printed copy capability.

KEEP copying one nondamaging spell cast in the last three rounds. NEW select one actual completed original spell-cast event that the caster directly perceived and identified when it occurred, no earlier than eighteen elapsed seconds before commitment; exactly eighteen seconds qualifies. Its original caster must have been within120ft and line of effect at that observed cast. Remembered event provenance, not the original caster's current presence or location, supplies the historical anchor. An item, nonspell technique, false image, countered attempt or merely reported event is not an eligible original cast.

NEW finite admission: the exact original spell mode must have a complete authorized operational procedure, generating rank0–7, and normal casting time of one Action or Bonus Action. The copied identity need not be on the echoer's ordinary class list: Fate Echo provides temporary access solely for this paid copy. It creates no class level, permanent knowledge, repertoire slot or later casting permission. Keep the original generating school, rank and version; do not union placements or choose a cheaper school. A source specifically prohibiting copying remains prohibited.

Nondamaging is a procedure-level restriction, not a lucky outcome. Reject a mode with any printed immediate, delayed, periodic or conditional damage, direct HP loss, lethal state assignment, damaging body/summon, damaging trap, damage rider or granted damaging attack. A damaging spell that missed is still damaging. A genuinely separate complete nondamaging mode can qualify on its own. Healing, protection, information and nondamaging control may qualify within their actual protections; ordinary consequences of later independent choices are not automatically the copied spell's damage. Copy/release/echo/contingency or further spell-access mechanisms are excluded, as are any events already descended from a copy, to prevent recursive permission and provenance loops. These are expressly NEW admission limits requiring agreement.

NEW one composite Action: commit Fate Echo's own final price/components/cast hooks first. On its successful resolution, immediately perform the selected copy within that Action, paying its ordinary rank-based price under the echoer's actual legal modifiers plus all independent RP, consumed materials, focus/tool and source costs. Validate ability to pay the full composite before commitment; reserved payment cannot be spent by another concurrent declaration. The copy is a new spell-cast event with its own real casting components, Dissonance/Refrain, concentration transition, cast triggers and Counterspell opportunity. No second Action is charged, but no independent next Action or second copy is granted. The echoer uses the same actual casting ability route that cast Fate Echo, their own PB and stats; no donor die, attack bonus, saves or prior outcome is replayed. Original rank scaling uses the fixed copied rank; printed caster-level scaling uses the echoer's actual granting class level, an explicit NEW choice.

Choose fresh legal targets/area and ordinary permitted modes within that exact copied procedure. Origin is the echoer and range is the copied spell's own range, not an added120ft. Original consent, body, environment, command, paid-information, material and source-specific use restrictions still apply. An expired or consumed original object/effect is not restored; a new legal creation pays its full real inputs and obeys its own capacity/economics. The same copied procedure shares the echoer's existing identity-based restrictions and cannot reset their spent source entitlement. Countering Fate Echo prevents the nested cast; countering the nested cast retains both actual payments and does not retry. New lasting effects belong to the echoer with original event provenance retained and their own fresh lawful deadlines. Cast Echo remains a different source with its own still-unadopted procedure.

**New decisions:** NEW observed eighteen-second event window, rank0–7 Action/BA admission, procedure-level nondamage test, explicit copy exclusions and separately paid nested cast within one composite Action.

### Authored examples
