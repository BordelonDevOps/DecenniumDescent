## 1a. Authorship (owner lock round 2, 2026-07-30)

> **Public:** Calan'thir and Asmodeus built the dungeon (Crucible / Calan'thir's Trial). Neutral ground, proving ground, controlled release for ambition, monument of Order+Hell cooperation. Cultures argue credit; virtually everyone accepts joint creation.
>
> **Secret:** Silent Ones/Old Ones built the actual machinery (Heartcell, 100-floor resonance, ten Gate divisions, opening cycle, reward architecture). Calan'thir & Asmodeus discovered/claimed/modified/activated/bound themselves to it — they did not invent the underlying machine. Treasure is bait. Expeditions serve the machine (combat resonance, death essence, gate victories, loot spreading, conditioning).
>
> **Formulation:** "The Silent Ones built the prison. Calan'thir and Asmodeus taught the world to call it a dungeon."

**The Heartcell** is the newly locked name for the machine core: the 100-floor resonance structure, its ten Gate divisions, the opening cycle, and the reward architecture. It is the Silent Ones'/Old Ones' original construction. Calan'thir and Asmodeus are genuine, public, joint builders of the **Crucible / Calan'thir's Trial** layered on top of it — not liars, not a myth invented by the Silent Ones to cover their tracks — but they found the Heartcell already there and discovered/claimed/modified/activated/bound themselves to it rather than inventing it. Loot is bait; the resonance the dungeon produces from combat, death, gate victories, loot circulation, and conditioning **is** the Heartcell's operation, not a separate mechanism running alongside it.

### Player revelation path (owner lock round 2)

The public myth (Calan'thir + Asmodeus + Crucible, joint Order+Hell monument) is **not a partial lie** — it is the genuinely *complete* explanation available to the world and to a party at campaign start. The Heartcell/Silent-Ones truth beneath it is **discoverable, never commonly known**:

1. Early dungeon architecture predates any written script or founding myth.
2. Discovery of **Custodians / Bearers / Signatories** — roles/offices tied to the machine, not to Calan'thir or Asmodeus by name.
3. Realization that **the Pact governs the dungeon but did not originate it**.
4. Direct contact with the **Heartcell's operating cycle** itself.

The world at large never climbs this ladder. **A sufficiently successful party might.**

## 3. Layer model (for writers)

```
[ GM-secret ]     Silent Ones built dungeon + loot → attract → discover → release
[ Pact layer ]    Keep them silent; war as dissonance; Matrix of Oblivion centralized
[ Public myth ]   Calan'thir / Asmodeus / Divine Pact; Crucible; resonance valve
[ Mortal belief ] Treasure, trial, divine war through mortals (Matrix § Economy)
```

Typical PCs and surface kingdoms operate at **mortal belief** and **public myth**. They **do not** know **Silent Ones** exist.
