# COSMOLOGY-PLANES-01 Director Companion

## Review result

**Verdict: PASS WITH EXPLICIT UNRESOLVED DEPENDENCIES.** This pass produces a usable source-linked plane reading without creating a master ontology that the sources do not supply. Original cosmology remains the first source for objective-history questions, modified only by explicit owner corrections. Appendix B remains mortal cosmography. The saved website and companion viewer remain additional authored presentations, not automatic replacements for either.

## 1. The original Planes Ascendant account

The original manuscript describes four primary dominions after the Sundering: **Bastion of Flame, Veiled Expanse, Radiant Currents, and Boundless Void**. It then describes exchanges among them through **Confluence Straits** and names four tributary realms at P71: **Molten Glass Tapestries, Luminous Dusk, Whispering Archive, and Calculated Pyre**.

Source: `The Cosmology of Decennium Descent.docx`, `word/document.xml`, P61-P71 (zero-based w:p in supplied extraction records). P68 explicitly says “With four primary dominions established”; P71 introduces the tributary realms.

This original account is not a license to identify every later Fire, Shadow, Dream, Astral, Fey, or Void label with one of those four. Shared imagery is insufficient.

## 2. Mortal teaching: the Wheel of Testimony

The current Player material contains **32 individually indexed plane labels** plus courts, factions, and campaign tensions. Q5 explicitly says the Great Wheel is **mortal folk religion/cosmography, not objective canon** (`docs/mechanics/OWNER_COSMOLOGY_LOCKS_2026-07-30.md`, line 81). The player/director boundary also states that mortal religions disagree about planes, gods, creation, and the afterlife.

The right reading is therefore layered rather than adversarial: mortals can sincerely teach a map that is useful in play without that teaching being a Director-level proof of universal topology. A mortal map also does not prove that a named location is fictitious.

### Example: conflicting maps
If a temple scholar calls the Plane of Fire one spoke of the Great Wheel while a recovered Crystalfall source speaks of Bastion of Flame, the Director should present both claims at their own authority. Unless an explicit source links them, do **not** tell the table they are definitely the same place or definitely different places.

## 3. Courts, dominions, and named authorities

The 76-claim source inventory is verified as an **inventory of claims**, not an inventory of 76 planes. It consists of 32 mortal plane labels; 16 court/member/site entries; 4 planar factions; 4 campaign tensions; 8 original realms; 5 standalone website labels; and 7 companion-viewer bodies.

The four Shadow Conclave entries are **members**. The four Astral Dominion entries are **sites**. Court titles are not promoted into objective cosmic seniority. **Mephistopheles belongs to Cania only; Maladomini's replacement remains unknown.**

## 4. Fire and Brogi

Keep four claims separate unless a future source connects them: original **Bastion of Flame**, mortal **Plane of Fire**, mortal **Eternal Flame** court, and Director **Anvil of Brogi**. Director P2299 explicitly allows a community to invoke Brogi “as a folk or historical figure rather than objective cosmology.” That sentence blocks using the dungeon echo as proof of an objective Fire-plane genealogy.

## 5. Dream, thought, Astral, Fey, and Void

Original **Radiant Currents** is a possibility/dream dominion and includes unpredictable time. Original **Boundless Void** is a questioning/thought dominion. Mortal **Astral Plane** and **Verdant Reflection** have their own teachings. Theme does not merge identity.

Likewise, **Boundless Void** is a place; **Void Sovereign** is an entity/title claim. Astral soul travel and Shadow soul collection are separate mortal claims. Soul/death jurisdiction is intentionally deferred. Do not use this chapter to decide it.

## 6. The saved cosmology website

The repository contains two additional presentations that must be preserved.

The standalone `webpage.html` names **Calan'thir – Axis of the Celestial Spiral** and four shells: **Infernal Spiral, Elemental Ring, Primordial Mandala, Celestial Arc** (lines 86-118). The visual radii/coordinates are implementation values, not approved astronomical measurements.

The companion viewer displays seven bodies: **Material Plane, Plane of Fire, Plane of Water, Plane of Air, Plane of Earth, Throne of Accord, Nessus**. Its twelve FirstSong section bodies match contiguous original DOCX text after whitespace normalization, but that code comparison is only a textual provenance check. The **World I-VI** placeholder remains a source gap; it is not recovered World chapter content.

### Example: using the viewer at the table
A Director may show the viewer as an authored cosmology presentation. If a player asks whether its orbit radius proves that Plane of Water is objectively farther from Aethel than Plane of Fire, the answer from this review is **no**. The code positions render the visualization; no source in this bounded review promotes them to measured cosmological distance.

## 7. Travel interfaces

Do not impose a blanket “no planar travel” rule. **Faction Portal** already has a real receiving-headquarters procedure that can target a known faction headquarters on the **same or another plane**, subject to its exact positive-reputation, receiving-point, payload, and ward rules.

Other reviewed effects keep their narrower scopes: **Mist Step** is local; **Arcane Portal** is a finite local one-transit doorway and its Planar tag does not make it cross-plane; **Blink Step** is a local post-attack Reaction teleport; **Planar Window** observes another plane but grants no physical travel/attack/casting origin; **Planar Ally (Minor)** summons but does not license traveler transit.

The following remain operationally pending: **Banishment, Plane Shift, Planar Summons (Lesser), Planar Summons (Greater), Astral Projection, Blink, Etherealness, Ethereal Disjunction, Teleport Object, Planar Gate, Teleportation Circle, Teleport**. Teleport Object and Teleportation Circle have complete supplied proposals, but those proposals remain **unadopted**. No spell approval occurs here.

### Example: attempted planar travel
A character with Faction Portal and the required positive reputation may use its exact cross-plane headquarters procedure if all card conditions are satisfied. A character merely pointing to a shop price for teleportation, a Planar tag, or a Plane Shift summary does not thereby possess a complete travel procedure.

## 8. Planar time, rests, and the Opening

Source descriptions can bend time: Radiant Currents says time folds unpredictably; mortal Astral teaching says distance and time have little meaning. Those statements are descriptive cosmology, not a complete clock engine. The owner lock fixes the Opening at **2400 hours / 100 days** and the Horn at **1200 hours / 50 days**.

This review therefore does not let planar prose silently stop the Opening clock, convert outside time, grant a rest, refresh resources, or extend spell durations.

**PROPOSAL — future planar-time interface:** any adopted procedure that changes time should state traveler elapsed time, outside elapsed time, duration handling, rest/resource handling, and Opening/Horn interaction. This is a proposal, not a newly adopted mechanic.

### Example: planar rest
If an Expedition spends what feels like eight hours in a dreamlike realm, the Director may not automatically award a long rest or freeze the Opening simply because the realm's prose says time behaves strangely. Use an explicit adopted rule when one exists; otherwise the operational conversion remains unresolved.

## 9. Still Time Spiral Map and campaign epoch

Original P270-P276 preserves a Still Time Spiral Map and a session-zero home-plane/rest rule. Keep both in the source inventory. They are not automatically current player-facing rules because the current revelation ladder deliberately limits common knowledge of hidden cosmic machinery. Establish the campaign epoch/disclosure state before handing out that map or applying its home-plane reward.

**PROPOSAL — presentation only:** if used before the epoch is settled, label the map as an in-world/source-era cosmograph rather than an omniscient current diagram.

## 10. Revelation handling and Q6 boundary

The planes review inherits, but does not expand, the Q6 later lock: reliable public knowledge ends at the failed Sixth Gate; regular Floor 97 has a distinct hidden chamber; Floor 100 confrontation **produces OR exposes** the crystal. The Old God/survivor/Ul'nak/Binding/Interstice identity remains unresolved.

When plane evidence would reveal more than the campaign's established knowledge ceiling, keep the Director truth and public claim separate. A recovered text can be true evidence without becoming universal public knowledge.

## 11. What this review does not decide

This pass does **not** decide operational soul/death jurisdiction, resurrection exceptions, extraction law, taxes, planar cargo/claims, full return behavior, or the identity merge prohibited by Q6. It does not approve any pending higher travel spell. It does not certify runtime implementation, balance, publication, or whole-game readiness.
