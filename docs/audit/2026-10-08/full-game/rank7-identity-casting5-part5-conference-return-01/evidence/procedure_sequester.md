## Admission and useful suspension
NEW this replaces the historical Instantaneous placeholder with a continuing, nonconcentration magical application. The caster and recipient choose a finite future absolute wake deadline at commitment; no universal numerical maximum is added to a source that prints none, but a missing, infinite or unknowable deadline is invalid. Agreeing to a very long finite deadline grants no infinite resources, inaccessible setting knowledge or safe campaign skip. At resolution admit the actual loss-of-agency component through the same caster-target-encounter whole-Action-denial gate. Willingness alone does not bypass that gate or an actual Incapacitated/suspension immunity. If the suspension cannot genuinely establish, no separate free invisibility remainder establishes from that paid attempt; no guard is consumed without genuine admission.

On admission the same body becomes suspended: actual Incapacitated, Speed0, no voluntary speech, Actions, Bonus Actions, Reactions or new orders. End its applicable existing concentration normally. Do not additionally assign Unconscious, automatic critical hits, Prone, death or Petrified merely by analogy. The body's existing posture, anatomy, HP, Wounds, gear, restraints, contact, collision and support remain real. Invisibility conceals its actual body and the real equipment worn/carried at admission while continuously in custody; dropped pieces become visible and later-picked-up cargo is not retroactively concealed. Ordinary sound/position knowledge does not become sight. True Seeing's accepted actual benefits see this Invisible body through darkness, not through real fog or walls. No unrestricted nondetection is granted.
