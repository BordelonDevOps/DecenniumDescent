## Amphibian
L293 Ability Score Increase. Your Constitution score increases by 2.
L299 Size. Amphibians stand between 5 and 6 feet tall and average about 150 pounds. Your size is Medium.
L301 Speed. Your base walking speed is 30 feet. You also have a swimming speed of 30 feet.
L303 Amphibious. You can breathe both air and water.
L305 Moisture Dependency. You must immerse yourself in water for at least 1 hour every day or suffer one level of exhaustion. This exhaustion cannot be removed until you spend 1 hour immersed in water.
L307 Natural Camouflage. You have proficiency in the Stealth skill.
L309 Water Sense. You can sense the presence of water within 60 feet of you and can determine whether it is fresh or salt water.
L311 Languages. You can speak, read, and write Common and Aquan.

### River Amphibian
L321 Ability Score Increase. Your Wisdom score increases by 1.
L323 Water Manipulation. You know the shape water cantrip. When you reach 3rd level, you can cast the create or destroy water spell once per day. When you reach 5th level, you can also cast the water breathing spell once per day. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for these spells.
L325 Underwater Combat Training. You have proficiency with spears, tridents, nets, and crossbows. These weapons don't suffer disadvantage on attack rolls made underwater.
L327 River Diplomat. You have advantage on Charisma checks when interacting with creatures that have a swimming speed.

### Poison Amphibian
L333 Ability Score Increase. Your Intelligence score increases by 1.
L335 Poison Resistance. You have resistance to poison damage and advantage on saving throws against being poisoned.
L337 Toxic Touch. Once per short rest, when you hit a creature with an unarmed strike, you can deal an additional 1d4 poison damage. At 6th level, this damage increases to 2d4, and at 12th level, it increases to 3d4.
L339 Toxin Knowledge. You have proficiency with poisoner's kits and have advantage on Intelligence (Nature) checks related to identifying poisons and venoms.

### Jungle Amphibian
L345 Ability Score Increase. Your Dexterity score increases by 1.
L347 Climber. You have a climbing speed of 30 feet and can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without making an ability check.
L349 Camouflage Expert. You can attempt to hide even when only lightly obscured by foliage, heavy rain, falling snow, mist, or other natural phenomena.
L351 Jungle Survival. You have advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks made in forest or jungle environments, and you can always find enough food and water for yourself and up to five other creatures when in such environments.

## Umbrin
L383 Ability Score Increase. Your Dexterity score increases by 2.
L389 Size. Umbrin are similar in size to humans, ranging from 5 to 5½ feet tall. Your size is Medium.
L391 Speed. Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
L393 Superior Darkvision. Accustomed to the depths of the Murk, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 120 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
L395 Sunlight Sensitivity. You have disadvantage on attack rolls and on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight when you, the target of your attack, or whatever you are trying to perceive is in direct sunlight.
L397 Umbrin Magic. You know the dancing lights cantrip. When you reach 3rd level, you can cast the faerie fire spell once per day. When you reach 5th level, you can also cast the darkness spell once per day. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells.
L399 Keen Senses. You have proficiency in the Perception skill.
L401 Trance. Umbrin don't need to sleep. Instead, they meditate deeply, remaining semiconscious, for 4 hours a day. While meditating, they can dream after a fashion; such dreams are actually mental exercises that have become reflexive through years of practice. After resting in this way, they gain the same benefit that a human does from 8 hours of sleep.
L403 Languages. You can speak, read, and write Common, Elvish, and Murkish.

### Obsidian Highborn
L413 Ability Score Increase. Your Charisma score increases by 1.
L415 Enhanced Umbrin Magic. When you cast the faerie fire and darkness spells with your Umbrin Magic trait, you can cast each of them twice per day instead of once.
L417 Command Presence. You have proficiency in the Intimidation skill. If you already have this proficiency, you gain proficiency in one of the following skills of your choice: Deception, Insight, or Persuasion.
L419 Noble Training. You have proficiency with rapiers, shortswords, and hand crossbows.

### Shadow Blade
L425 Ability Score Increase. Your Wisdom score increases by 1.
L427 Shadow Step. Once per short rest, as a bonus action, you can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see that is in dim light or darkness. You have advantage on the first melee attack you make before the end of the turn.
L429 Poison Expert. You have proficiency with the poisoner's kit and have advantage on saving throws against poison.
L431 Assassin's Training. You have proficiency with the disguise kit and the thieves' tools.

### Cavern Mystic
L437 Ability Score Increase. Your Wisdom score increases by 1.
L439 Earth Communion. You can cast the detect magic spell, but only to detect magic affecting earth or stone. You can also cast the speak with plants spell, but only to speak with fungi. Once you cast either spell with this trait, you can't cast that spell with it again until you finish a long rest. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for these spells.
L441 Stone Sense. You can sense the presence of unusual stonework within 60 feet, such as traps, hidden doors, and recent excavations. You can also determine your approximate depth underground.
L443 Murkish Lore. You have advantage on Intelligence (History) checks related to the Murk and on Intelligence (Nature) checks related to fungi, oozes, and subterranean creatures.

## Drakari
L479 Ability Score Increase. Your Strength score increases by 2, and your Charisma score increases by 1.
L485 Size. Drakari are taller and heavier than humans, standing well over 6 feet tall and averaging almost 250 pounds. Your size is Medium.
L487 Speed. Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
L489 Draconic Ancestry. You have draconic ancestry. Your breath weapon and damage resistance are determined by your Drakari variant.
L491 Breath Weapon. You can use your action to exhale destructive energy. Your draconic ancestry determines the size, shape, and damage type of the exhalation. When you use your breath weapon, each creature in the area of the exhalation must make a saving throw, the type of which is determined by your draconic ancestry. The DC for this saving throw equals 8 + your Constitution modifier + your proficiency bonus. A creature takes 2d6 damage on a failed save, and half as much damage on a successful one. The damage increases to 3d6 at 6th level, 4d6 at 11th level, and 5d6 at 16th level. After you use your breath weapon, you can't use it again until you complete a short or long rest.
L493 Damage Resistance. You have resistance to the damage type associated with your draconic ancestry.
L495 Languages. You can speak, read, and write Common and Draconic.

### Flame Drakari
L505 Draconic Ancestry. Your breath weapon is a 15-foot cone of fire. Creatures in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw. You have resistance to fire damage.
L507 Fire Affinity. You know the produce flame cantrip. Once you reach 3rd level, you can cast the burning hands spell once per day. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for these spells.
L509 Heat Endurance. You are naturally adapted to hot climates, as described by the environmental rules in Chapter 5.

L519 Cold Endurance. You are naturally adapted to cold climates, as described by the environmental rules in Chapter 5.

L527 Lightning Affinity. You know the shocking grasp cantrip. Once you reach 3rd level, you can cast the thunderwave spell once per day. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for these spells.
L529 Speed Burst. As a bonus action, you can propel yourself with a surge of electrical energy. Your walking speed increases by 10 feet until the end of your turn. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

### Stone Drakari

L549 Wind Step. When you take the Dash action, difficult terrain doesn't cost you extra movement for that turn.

## Dwarf
L601 Ability Score Increase. Your Constitution score increases by 2.
L607 Size. Dwarves stand between 4 and 5 feet tall and average about 150 pounds. Your size is Medium.
L609 Speed. Your base walking speed is 25 feet. Your speed is not reduced by wearing heavy armor.
L611 Darkvision. Accustomed to life underground, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
L613 Dwarven Resilience. You have advantage on saving throws against poison, and you have resistance against poison damage.
L615 Dwarven Combat Training. You have proficiency with the battleaxe, handaxe, light hammer, and warhammer.
L617 Tool Proficiency. You gain proficiency with the artisan's tools of your choice: smith's tools, brewer's supplies, or mason's tools.
L619 Stonecunning. Whenever you make an Intelligence (History) check related to the origin of stonework, you are considered proficient in the History skill and add double your proficiency bonus to the check, instead of your normal proficiency bonus.
L621 Languages. You can speak, read, and write Common and Dwarvish. Dwarvish is full of hard consonants and guttural sounds, and those characteristics spill over into whatever other language a dwarf might speak.

### Mountain Dwarf
L631 Ability Score Increase. Your Strength score increases by 2.
L633 Dwarven Armor Training. You have proficiency with light and medium armor.
L635 Mountain Born. You're acclimated to high altitude, including elevations above 20,000 feet. You're also naturally adapted to cold climates, as described by the environmental rules in Chapter 5.
L637 Mountaineer. You have advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks made to climb rocky terrain.

### Forge Dwarf
L643 Ability Score Increase. Your Intelligence score increases by 1.
L645 Artisan's Expertise. You gain proficiency with two additional types of artisan's tools of your choice.
L647 Fire Resistance. You have resistance to fire damage.
L649 Master Craftsman. When you use artisan's tools to create an item, the creation time is reduced by 25%, and the item is considered to be of exceptional quality.

### Deep Dwarf
L655 Ability Score Increase. Your Wisdom score increases by 1.
L657 Superior Darkvision. Your darkvision has a radius of 120 feet.
L659 Murk Knowledge. You have advantage on Intelligence (Nature) checks related to fungi, oozes, and subterranean creatures.
L661 Deep Resilience. You have advantage on saving throws against illusions and against being charmed or paralyzed.

## Elf
L699 Ability Score Increase. Your Dexterity score increases by 2.
L705 Size. Elves range from under 5 to over 6 feet tall and have slender builds. Your size is Medium.
L707 Speed. Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
L709 Darkvision. Accustomed to twilit forests and the night sky, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
L711 Keen Senses. You have proficiency in Perception skill.
L713 Fey Ancestry. You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put you to sleep.
L715 Trance. Elves don't need to sleep. Instead, they meditate deeply, remaining semiconscious, for 4 hours a day. While meditating, you can dream after a fashion; such dreams are actually mental exercises that have become reflexive through years of practice. After resting in this way, you gain the same benefit that a human does from 8 hours of sleep.
L717 Languages. You can speak, read, and write Common and Elvish. Elvish is fluid, with subtle intonations and intricate grammar. Elven literature is rich and varied, and their songs and poems are famous among other races. Many bards learn their language so they can add Elvish ballads to their repertoires.

## Feykin
L797 Ability Score Increase. Your Charisma score increases by 2, and your Dexterity score increases by 1.
L803 Size. Feykin are about the same size as humans, ranging from 5 to 6 feet tall. Your size is Medium.
L805 Speed. Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

L809 Fey Ancestry. You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put you to sleep.

## Gnome
L895 Ability Score Increase. Your Intelligence score increases by 2.
L901 Size. Gnomes are between 3 and 4 feet tall and average about 40 pounds. Your size is Small.
L903 Speed. Your base walking speed is 25 feet.
L905 Darkvision. Accustomed to life underground, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
L907 Gnome Cunning. You have advantage on all Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws against magic.
L909 Natural Curiosity. You have proficiency in one of the following skills of your choice: Arcana, History, Investigation, or Nature.
L911 Languages. You can speak, read, and write Common and Gnomish. The Gnomish language, which uses the Dwarvish script, is renowned for its technical treatises and its catalogs of knowledge about the natural world.

### Tinker Gnome
L921 Ability Score Increase. Your Dexterity score increases by 1.
L923 Artificer's Lore. Whenever you make an Intelligence (History) check related to magic items, alchemical objects, or technological devices, you can add twice your proficiency bonus, instead of any proficiency bonus you normally apply.
L925 Tinker. You have proficiency with artisan's tools (tinker's tools). Using those tools, you can spend 1 hour and 10 gp worth of materials to construct a Tiny clockwork device (AC 5, 1 hp). The device ceases to function after 24 hours (unless you spend 1 hour repairing it to keep the device functioning), or when you use your action to dismantle it; at that time, you can reclaim the materials used to create it. You can have up to three such devices active at a time.
When you create a device, choose one of the following options:
L929 Clockwork Toy. This toy is a clockwork animal, monster, or person, such as a frog, mouse, bird, dragon, or soldier. When placed on the ground, the toy moves 5 feet across the ground on each of your turns in a random direction. It makes noises as appropriate to the creature it represents.
L931 Fire Starter. The device produces a miniature flame, which you can use to light a candle, torch, or campfire. Using the device requires your action.
L933 Music Box. When opened, this music box plays a single song at a moderate volume. The box stops playing when it reaches the song's end or when it is closed.
L935 Mechanical Sensor. The device can detect the presence of creatures within 30 feet. When a creature enters or leaves this range, the device makes a chirping noise audible within 10 feet.
