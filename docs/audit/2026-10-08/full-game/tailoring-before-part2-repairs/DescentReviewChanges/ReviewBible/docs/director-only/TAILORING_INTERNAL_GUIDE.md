# Tailoring shared playtest rules

**Authored draft; exact review pending.** Internal: all 43 formulas, including restricted and Director-only records.

These operational additions implement the TA-D01–TA-D16 source conference in Calibration Review Part 2. Original effects, component records, discovery, economy, nulls, absent fields and source status remain preserved in the accompanying data. They do not establish runtime implementation, balance, material availability, whole-game approval or publication readiness.

## What an enchanted textile does

These are durable magical, nonartifact items with passive properties. They require no attunement, RP, concentration, spell activation or activation action. Read only the actual recipe effect and the operational scope: a name, tag, donor, legacy name or material does not add a power.

Tailoring offers **no AC enhancement candidate**, not even a zero that could cancel selected Crude armor. Cloth structural AC is a defense of the object, not a worn armor formula. Clothing adds no armor proficiency requirement, generic Strength minimum or generic Stealth penalty and waives none of the restrictions of actual armor.

A complete garment must have at least 1 structural HP, be unsuppressed and unmelded, actually fit, and be worn in its designated legal benefit position. Every such premise is evaluated when its benefit is used. Extra compatible layers may function as ordinary clothing but do not produce extra recipe benefits.

The printed damage resistance, if any, protects the eligible wearer independently of task bonuses and the chosen armor formula. Different legal types coexist; repeated resistance is not immunity. Resistance protects neither equipment nor cargo. Poison resistance does not cure or prevent Poisoned or unrelated nondamage effects. Bludgeoning includes magical bludgeoning, but not force or additional damage types, and does not cancel nondamage pressure, restraint or displacement.

All models have ordinary HP5, cloth-dominant structural AC11 and Standard equipment grade. Incidental recipe reinforcement does not change the dominant material. Fine clothing is a clothing model, not Fine armor grade. These prices are references, not stock or a new mundane construction recipe. An actual complete body must be bought, owned, borrowed with permission, commissioned or obtained under an independently supported construction procedure.

Garment-family recipes choose one complete outfit model for the entire batch. Record its actual constituent clothing before work. An outfit is one purchase unit and benefit-bearing item, not independent magical accessories. Splitting it among wearers divides neither benefits nor inclusive mass. Gloves need both original members of their matched pair.

Tiny, Small and Medium fitted garments use baseline values. The **separately authorized Tailoring Large variant doubles only garment body price, weight and ordinary HP**. ## Shared wearing positions

Use one head benefit position for Hat or Blacksmithing Helm; one handwear-pair position for Tailoring Gloves, Leatherworking Gloves or Blacksmithing Gauntlets; one body-clothing position for Garment or Robe; and one outerwear-benefit position for the selected outer garment or Leatherworking Coat. Veil has one face-accessory position and Cord one waist-adornment position. A private recipe's specific outerwear binding remains on its authorized card.

Cord's adornment position is not waist-cargo relief. Cord and a cargo Belt require actual compatible fitting. Veil plus Hat or Helm requires actual face opening, vision, closure and attachments. Compatible body clothing may accompany one actual armor suit, the Leatherworking torso vest and one outerwear layer without waiving armor or unarmored-feature restrictions.

A Robe or complete outfit takes one minute to don and one minute to doff. Other listed textile bodies take one Action each way. Designate the benefit with lawful equipping. Repositioning already-worn compatible competing layers takes one Action, ending the old benefit first; this does not bypass required removal of an obstructing item. Broken objects retain mass and their physical positions until lawfully removed. Gloves occupy handwear, not manipulating hands; they grant no free hand, weapon profile, unarmed bonus or spell-component exception.

## Task bonuses

**All qualifying Tailoring and Leatherworking task bonuses share equipment.leather-task-enhancement: apply the strongest qualifying value once per actual check.** This extension does not broaden any Leatherworking task scope, combine unrelated class/tool/spell bonuses or create checks. Declare the actual task, actor, ability, relevant proficiency, garment contribution and item state before rolling. Add proficiency once; expertise replaces the ordinary contribution.

The card lists its exhaustive domains. Presentation needs visible attire materially helping appearance, costume, ceremony or a role, and the wearer's CHA Persuasion/Deception/Performance check. Mere dressing during conversation, bargaining, intimidation or song is insufficient. A genuine DEX disguise-kit check arranging the wearer's own already-worn garment can qualify; a separate disguise-maker and observer cannot receive the wearer-only benefit.

Visual concealment requires actual useful coverage and independently legal DEX Stealth. Clothing creates neither cover nor hiding permission. Quiet-clothing motion concerns the wearer's own rustle, panels and snags; noisy armor, disadvantage, feet and speech retain their consequences. Discreet gloved handwork is genuine DEX Sleight of Hand for a physically compatible small object, not lock work or remote manipulation.

Weather arrangement is WIS Survival for one's own actual clothing coverage or DEX for one's ordinary ties and fastenings against ambient wind, rain, spray, grit, heat or cold. It does not assist general Survival, navigation, shelter building, crafting or repair. Exposure assists only an actual CON **ability check** under a separately governing procedure that permits clothing assistance; never a required save or an invented replacement. Actual covered body regions matter: a Hat is head coverage, and a thin Veil is not a protective outfit.

No Tailoring task bonus applies to attacks, saves, initiative, concentration, spells, crafting, research, harvesting, repair, Perception, Insight or general knowledge through relabeling. Hidden clothing cannot supply visible presentation. Tools, training, hands, access and actions remain required.

## Bag operation and carrying relief

Conceal-contents is the actual operator's DEX check, using Sleight of Hand only if genuinely applicable, to arrange actual noncreature cargo with existing folds, lining and other cargo against ordinary inspection. Organize-contents is INT for genuinely difficult sorting or packing of known accessible cargo, or DEX for difficult physical placement or separation. These are not Leatherworking secure/retrieve aliases. The accessible, complete, functional, unsuppressed Bag can assist an ally or thief at a table without a relief position. A melded or inaccessible Bag cannot assist. Nested access must respect outer closures.

These checks create no secret compartment, stored bonus duration, repeated inspection contest, identification, preservation, extra capacity or automatic retrieval. No bonus transfers to an observer's scrutiny or unrelated verbal deception, bodily concealment, lock DC or lockpicking. Reevaluate current checks against actual state; prior arrangements retain only consequences allowed by the governing detection procedure.

Physical capacities require both weight and volume fit plus opening, shape and real packing. Each Bag has one compartment, fitted strap and handle, ordinary flap and ties; no lock, airtight seal, preservation, extradimensional space or liquid containment. Fluids need an actual inner vessel. Use inner containers' occupied exterior geometry. Folding or compression requires its own valid recorded configuration.

The source capacityPounds is a **relief allowance R**, separate from physical capacity. For each eligible known-weight contents record i, allocate covered pounds r_i in stable loading order, with inner bodies before their descendants: 0 ≤ r_i ≤ actual weight_i and sum(r_i) ≤ R. Subtract those covered pounds from the direct bearer's carrying comparison and credit **min(canonical Load_i, r_i/10)** against that record's Inventory Load. Retain physical mass and canonical Load unchanged. A 1-lb/3-Load object therefore retains at least 2.9 Load. Fifty ordinary coins use 1 lb/0.1 Load. Use actual partly filled bottle body and contents or one legitimate inclusive gross record, never both. Never aggregate unrelated records to manufacture credit.

No relief applies to the active Bag's own empty body, unknown or estimated mass, creature bodies, casualties or Petrified creatures. Partial accounting coverage does not physically divide an object or increase Strength, push/drag/lift, support or casualty-handling capacity.

Both trades share at most five active positions: one back-cargo, one quiver, one waist-cargo and two pouch positions, with actual anatomy and space. Each Bag uses only its model's position. It must be properly worn or directly held by the bearer; actual holding hands remain required. Holding does not create a new position. Ownership, touch, dragging, vehicles or attachment to another container are insufficient.

An enclosed enchanted container is stowed and offers no independent relief. Its eligible body and descendants can receive an active outer container's allowance once. External lashings are not contents. Removing or transferring a container moves the same physical subtree, ends former relief and reevaluates the actual recipient's lawful equipping. There is no stored credit, depleted allowance or refresh.

## Suppression, Broken items and transformations

Suppression stops relief, task bonuses and resistance but preserves real capacity, custody, contents, physical mass, structural HP and workmanship. Recalculate burden immediately. Excess remains overloaded custody, not deletion, forced dropping, invented damage or a universal Speed-0 rule.

At HP0 a Bag loses ordinary secure containment and magical properties. Release each immediate contents record through the existing board placement procedure. An intact inner container moves once with its subtree; its descendants do not spill twice. Only an independently recorded intact support can retain what it actually supports; the Broken Bag's integral strap is not independent. Preserve actual elevation and resolve ordinary falls where needed.

Use the same legal location, otherwise nearest legal adjacent placement and the reviewed neutral tie rules. If none is legal, mark pending placement; pending contents cannot also be retrieved from the former Bag. Spilling causes no automatic damage and repair reloads nothing. The Broken body remains physical equipment.

Voluntary melding preflight uses full physical pounds and canonical Load against both fixed historical ceilings, without either trade's relief. Treat loaded containers as custody subtrees. Mandatory transformations still resolve under existing overflow and prerecorded disposition order or stable physical-ID fallback, not post-result tactical selection. While melded, these items provide no wearing, holding or access benefits. Later compatible anatomy does not demeld them.

Rescuers count actual body weight and actual size-based casualty Load plus all external and melded equipment. No relief passes through the carried creature. The reviewed Tiny example totals 155 lb and 3 Load from 140-lb/1.5-Load body plus 15-lb/1.5-Load equipment; actual handling permission is still needed. On final return, external equipment keeps priority. Only lawfully returned equipped items resume benefits; stowed returns stay inactive. Recalculate burden afterward.

## Creation, reusable tools and quality

Reserve floor(1.5 × source Q) actual complete nonmagical non-Broken bodies in a stable ordered physical-ID list before work, with one model, fitted size and grade. Bags must be empty. Record material, ordinary and modified maximum HP, current HP, existing durability, flaw and undamaged reference. No previous magical recipe imprint is admitted. Damaged functional bodies are legal. A missing, Broken, incomplete or otherwise committed reserve pauses completion before the roll; repair or replace it in the same position. Use latest lawful completion state, not an obsolete reservation snapshot.

Transform the first resolved N bodies, or the first Q for durability. No post-roll purchase, reordering or selection. Failed and Mishap transform none. Unused bodies remain their actual objects outside ingredient recovery. Consumed inputs are additional process materials paid once, not a second body or valuable waste entitlement. Finished empty mass equals actual selected body mass.

Retain every source reusable tool with exact quantity, tier, value, 1-lb weight, role, properties and nonconsumed flag. These are specialist mundane tool profiles, HP5, actual declared material defenses, with no implied crafting bonus or availability. The required Needle or Scissors can also perform ordinary Needles or Shears work when physically suited; measuring implements cannot substitute for cutting or sewing. Generic kit ownership is not possession of the expensive exact source profile. Document any legal substitute against every requirement.

One physical tool may serve sequential work but cannot satisfy overlapping active work or exclusive reservations twice. Restore actual access before resuming. Every outcome and abandonment releases the same tool's reservation, barring separately resolved damage/loss; never add another tool copy or 25% ingredient recovery. Inclusive kit accounting counts a documented tool once, without subtracting unknown constituent weight or inventing contents. Tailoring has no catalysts: Masterwork catalyst return is zero. Consumed Pattern material is distinct from unconsumed formula knowledge.

Use the framework's six bands, with natural-1 Mishap and no additional natural-20 band shift. Standard yields Q; Exceptional yield floor(1.25Q); Masterwork yield floor(1.5Q). Choose yield or durability once after rolling. Durability transforms Q bodies and supplies 25% or 50%. Flawed Q≥2 yields floor(Q/2) ordinary-effect items and no new singleton flaw; Q1 yields one item with one persistent flaw.

Let B be ordinary body HP after permitted size and P the strongest legally attached percentage, 0/25/50. ImprovedMax = B + max(1, floor(B×P/100)) for P>0, otherwise B. Apply one persistent flaw last: FinalMax = max(1, floor(ImprovedMax/2)). Otherwise FinalMax=ImprovedMax. NewCurrent = clamp(FinalMax − (OldMax − OldCurrent), 0, FinalMax). Preserve existing flaws and latest damage deficit. Weaker improvements do not compound or heal; repeated flaws do not halve again. A B5 body at 1/5 may emerge singleton-Flawed at 0/2 with dormant imprint.

## Work, repair and value

Use DEX with the reviewed Weaver's-tools credential, source Loom and Cutting Table, Tailor's tools, Needles and Shears. The station is separate. Framework untrained level-1–4 attempts retain disadvantage when physical requirements are met; higher eligibility and formula access remain required. Tool ownership supplies neither proficiency nor trade level; numerical trade level is not a check bonus.

All Tailoring fieldCraftable flags are false, even where the retained timeMode says field. Use the actual station at a permitted workshop/worksite. Preserve exact source assistant caps, discovery, access and trivialAtSkillLevel, including capped high-level thresholds.

After lawful time adjustments, the first stable incorporation phase is min(8 hours, final required active time), included in the total. Later phases are at most eight active hours. Record storage/incorporation before work. Only actually incorporated materials in completed stable phases gain stabilization. Untouched and replacement lots keep their own clocks and require actual processing/preservation. Replacing a lot does not erase completed work, return spent materials or retroactively stabilize it.

Eligible Forge Dwarf creation multiplies time by 0.75, rounded upward to a whole minute, and uses the reviewed Standard-to-Exceptional output choice. It does not reduce repair, training, rest, preservation clocks or independent calendar waits. Any source-null year uses a separately labeled 1,920-active-hour overlay; eligible Forge Dwarf uses 1,440 hours/86,400 minutes. Source null, literal year and zero labor remain unchanged. These are labor totals, not guaranteed calendar access. The framework governs unfinished phases, viability, assistants, Watch ceilings and rest.

Repair requires trained DEX + Weaver's-tools proficiency, actual Loom and Cutting Table, Needles and Shears. Functional HP≥1: DC10, max(1 hour, recorded maximum HP/10), materials 25% undamaged mundane body reference. Broken HP0 reconstruction: original recipe DC, max(8 hours, recorded maximum HP/10), materials 25% undamaged finished unit reference. Both are binary; failure consumes work/materials, success restores recorded maximum and dormant imprint usability subject to suppression.

Require the recoverable original main body, both original Gloves members and necessary original outfit constituents. Missing members cannot be replaced by this route; shared fragments cannot create duplicate outfits. Preserve identity, model, fit, size, grade, durability and flaw. Restore no contents. Original formula, original trade level and rare creation-tool rows are not generic reconstruction requirements; repair teaches no formula, grants no XP, rolls no quality, returns no catalysts and receives no Forge Dwarf reduction. Actual quoted Core repair services remain alternatives, not an automatic extra fee.

Finished unit reference = preserved Standard batch reference / source Q + actual undamaged mundane body reference. Never divide by final yield or add a reusable tool's whole purchase price to each output. Actual body purchases, reserves, tool purchases, rentals and commissions remain separate. Retain exact precision until a transaction, then apply copper rounding. Functional repair of a common-clothes body has 0.125-gp reference materials, purchased at 0.13 gp. Sale requires a finite actual buyer and quote, without automatic artifact multiplier, quality premium, tool reimbursement or mundane-table magic-item buyback.
