## GP-01 — the seven Choir identities

Owner Q5 says **First Choir stays exactly 7**. It does not itself enumerate the names.

The names and virtues are nevertheless well-supported current source identities:

- `The Cosmology of Decennium Descent.docx` P78 names Korvelan/Courage, Vaerilune/Balance, Delvaran/Law, Shaelara/Sorrow, Xalarex/Pattern, Thalirion/Contradiction and Virellian/Passage.
- P103 again depicts the same remaining six voices around Virellian using the same aspects.
- `docs/mechanics/APPENDIX_B_FOLK_RELIGION.md` explicitly maps those seven names/virtues as the First Choir while preserving Q5's hierarchy.
- Current Player Rulebook paragraph 9977 names the same seven to players as scholarly, non-secret-but-not-common Spiral knowledge.

**Authority label:** `source-supported current First Choir identity; exact count owner-locked; individual name/virtue list derived from bound current/original sources, not quoted as a verbatim owner enumeration`.

That label is sufficient for the bounded authored campaign candidate. A new owner question is unnecessary unless a later step proposes changing the names, virtues, membership or authority tier.

Asmodeus is not an eighth Choir member. P78's missing-voice language is a relationship/metaphor in that narrative, not a membership statement.

## GP-02 — Fifteenfold

The full current fifteen-name inventory is retained as mortal worship:

Solarius, Caelestra, Terralith, Aquaria, Vitalis, Nocturn, Tempestia, Infernus, Mortalis, Voidus, Chronos, Spatia, Arcanus, Naturae and Animus.

Q5 and the current Appendix-B framing make them **worship masks**. Their names, domains, rites, stories, cultural pantheons and folk ascendant traditions can be taught publicly and can have real social or magical consequences. That does not establish that each mask corresponds one-to-one with a Choir member, Old One, custodian or legacy World god.

## GP-03 — archons, Thrones and aspirants

`The Cosmology...` P104 creates a Watch Convocation of **seven Thrones**. The text says the Thrones cannot bestow power; they reflect the sitter's true intent.

P106 then separately names **seven archon witnesses/stewards**:
Myrriel, Valcendyr, Seraphon, Thalxior, Elarin, Quorilith and Shaenira. It expressly says they do not sit the Thrones.

P109–110 says Ko Thren, Velin Saal and Arak Emberchild did **not** ascend. P122 again states that the later sampled aspirants did not ascend. Serit's P116 bloom is recorded as the first Verdant Line / a *potential* pantheon, not as Choir membership. P124's “true Awakening” crowns no individual.

Later chapters continue Myrriel/Valcendyr/Seraphon/Thalxior/Elarin/Quorilith/Shaenira as archons, mentors, stewards and witnesses. Nothing supplied later converts them into First Choir members.

## GP-04 — infernal roster

P56 is genuinely inconsistent: it assigns Mephistopheles to Maladomini and then immediately to Cania.

Cania is repeatedly corroborated later, including P79, P120–121, and additional later frozen-engine/Cania material. No supplied owner/domain authority repairs Maladomini, supplies a replacement ruler, or authorizes dual rule.

**Bounded presentation:** player-facing prose can use *Nine Infernal Courts* and avoid naming a disputed Maladomini ruler. Director/candidate material may say **Mephistopheles is source-attested in Cania; P56's Maladomini occupancy is disputed**. Do not import another setting's roster.

No owner decision is required now. One becomes necessary only if an active roster, encounter or publication needs the canonical Maladomini occupant.

## GP-05 — public infernal terminology

Current public terms are **War of Chains and Teeth** and **Nine Infernal Courts**. Use those in new public prose.

Older `Blood War` / `Nine Hells` language remains preserved source terminology. The current Player Rulebook intentionally reports competing priestly interpretations of whether the infernal war protects or strains the Pact. Those are theological claims.

The public “absolute peace” rumor does not supersede the creation/origin ruling: ordinary treaty, ceasefire or reconciliation is not the established ontological catastrophe.

## GP-06 — Q7 legacy scope

Q7 retires the explicitly listed World-of-Aethel god names as **gate guardians or active-pantheon figures where they conflict with the live bestiary roster**:

Velkyrion, Zeharra, Kharix, Myressa, Elyria, Yshael, Sythrel, Threxian and Naegul.

Q7 simultaneously says the surrounding myth-history prose is **not retired**.

Therefore the Age of Dawns, Beastlands, Veil betrayal, songs, lineage claims and other surviving World material remain legacy historical/source evidence, but they do not create a second active pantheon or live gate roster. Draethon appears in a surviving song and is not individually listed by Q7; that omission does not promote him. He remains unpromoted legacy myth-history evidence.

World Sections I–VI remain missing and are not reconstructed.

## GP-07 — Umbrin / Umbra

The recorded Umbrin decision is preserved: the Obsidian Burn/exile, the Umbrin people, The Murk geography and the resulting obsidian-sheen tradition remain owner-recorded lore.

The same records use “primordial Umbra,” “Umbra's thread” and “Lumina's glare.” Q5, however, deliberately frames Lumina/Umbra as theological abstractions in the public cosmology.

These are compatible without changing the locked history:

> **Reviewed Umbrin wording:** The Obsidian Burn, the exile of Umbrin forebears by Calan'thir and the First Choir, the Umbrin people of The Murk, their recorded geography, and the resulting obsidian-sheen tradition remain locked Umbrin lore. Their demonym and cult traditions invoke the primordial/theological principle called Umbra. Descriptions of “Umbra's thread” or “Lumina's glare” are the culture's and tradition's explanation of that history and do not independently establish two literal creator-beings. Preserve the historical/lore ruling while keeping Q5's Lumina/Umbra abstraction boundary.

No owner question is required now.

## Reviewed summary text

### Public faith — ACCEPT WITH MINOR PRECISION

> Across Aethel, the Fifteenfold Pantheon gives everyday worship familiar names, stories and traditions. These are mortal interpretations of forces whose full nature remains uncertain, not a Director-level proof of cosmic genealogy. Scholars also distinguish the First Choir: seven source-supported named virtues associated with the Spiral — Korvelan, Vaerilune, Delvaran, Shaelara, Xalarex, Thalirion and Virellian. The Choir is not another name for the Fifteen. Calan'thir and Asmodeus are widely credited as the genuine joint builders of the Crucible. A character may sincerely follow a temple, local tradition or philosophy, and real rites or magic may answer that faith, without the character possessing the setting's hidden cosmology.

### Director identity boundary — ACCEPT

> Keep the owner-locked hierarchy as a distinction between cosmic structure, hidden machinery, custodians and mortal religion, not a new power-ranking or combat system. Exactly seven First Choir members remain separate from the seven named archon witnesses, seven Thrones, mortal aspirants, Fifteenfold worship masks, cultural gods, folk ascendants and class patron titles. Do not supply one-to-one equivalences that the sources do not establish. The prior creation/origin review and its unresolved causal and chronological dependencies remain controlling within their scope.

### Umbrin — CORRECT

> The Obsidian Burn, the exile of Umbrin forebears by Calan'thir and the First Choir, the Umbrin people of The Murk, their recorded geography, and the resulting obsidian-sheen tradition remain locked Umbrin lore. Their demonym and cult traditions invoke the primordial/theological principle called Umbra. Descriptions of “Umbra's thread” or “Lumina's glare” are the culture's and tradition's explanation of that history and do not independently establish two literal creator-beings. Preserve the historical/lore ruling while keeping Q5's Lumina/Umbra abstraction boundary.

### Infernal roster — CORRECT

> The original cosmology contains a duplicated infernal roster: P56 associates Mephistopheles with both Maladomini and Cania. Later source passages repeatedly corroborate his Cania association, but no supplied authority repairs Maladomini's ruler or authorizes dual rule. Preserve the duplicate as source evidence. In bounded Director material, Mephistopheles may be described as associated with Cania while Maladomini's occupancy remains disputed. New player prose uses “Nine Infernal Courts” and “War of Chains and Teeth” and need not name a disputed Maladomini ruler. Do not import an outside roster or invent a replacement.
