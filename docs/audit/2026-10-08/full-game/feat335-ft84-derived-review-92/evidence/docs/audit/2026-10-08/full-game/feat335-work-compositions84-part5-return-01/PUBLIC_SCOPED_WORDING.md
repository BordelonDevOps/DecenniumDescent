# FT84 — Proposed scoped work interactions

This public wording adds no private recipe or hidden source entitlement. All prerequisites, actual prices/stock, safe work and separate access restrictions remain. No selection or runtime flag is enabled.

## FT84-Q01

FT84-Q01 / FT84-WRITTEN-RITUAL-01 — scoped selection for Ritual Caster learning and Spell Scribe.
INTERPRETATION SELECTED: the actual Ritual Caster written-learning route is an authorized spell-copying procedure when the learner personally uses an already complete, legitimately accessible written version of the exact eligible ritual, can actually use its language/notation, and records the learned ritual in the learner's real usable ritual book. The written source must supply the complete instruction, not merely a title, unfinished notes or text whose missing content must be taught. This predicate is about how the real work is done; the presence of a tutor does not disqualify otherwise genuine written copying, and writing lecture notes does not convert teacher-led learning into copying.
For that written-copy work, an actually acquired Spell Scribe halves the ordinary eight-active-hours-per-rank learning requirement; take the strongest eligible time factor once, then apply Spell Scribe's final whole-minute upward rounding. A current rank-one ritual therefore requires 240 active minutes. Retain every completed 15-minute learning phase and its governing interruption/final-remainder rule. No reading, comprehension, recording, rank, tag, consent, facility, tool or source requirement disappears. Diligent Researcher, Craftsman, Quick Ritualist and Ritual Channeler do not reduce this learning transaction. No new starting ritual or physical book, Wizard spellbook entitlement, ordinary scroll-production permission, check, trade XP or automatic class acquisition is granted.
Teacher-led learning without the qualifying complete written source remains 480 active minutes per rank. A later lawful switch to written-copy work can affect only actually remaining unworked learning at a completed phase boundary; log the old and new basis and retain all actual time/materials. Do not retroactively relabel completed teaching or an interrupted uncredited segment as discounted copying. Only one route credits each actual minute. Wizard Spell Scribing keeps its own legal-found-Wizard-spell/spellbook predicate and its own bill; merely being a Wizard or having the same physical book does not substitute its class route for this feat route. When two reductions genuinely cover the same procedure and material category, take the strongest once, not their product.
NEW LIMITED BILL COMPLETION: before claiming a material reduction, record the real ten-gp-per-rank practice/writing bill as actual stock quantities and identified categories; it is not a new currency-only substitute for materials. Ordinary blank writing stock, ordinary nonrare ink and ordinary practice supplies are discountable only when their actual source/provider identifies them as such. Rare inks and unique or specifically required components remain full quantity; copying rights, instructor/facility services, reusable tools and the separate blank book are not made discountable by calling them consumables. There is no universal numeric split of the ten gp. A stated mixed bill must retain its undiscounted total and each actual lot's identity, availability and legal precision. Apply Spell Scribe's 3/4 mundane factor separately from time; preserve divisible quantities, ceil required indivisible units once, and ceil the final actual purchase once to copper. Known protected items remain unchanged.
An unitemized or ambiguous bill has no justified material discount. The otherwise legal learner may still use the qualifying half-time route and supply the complete genuine undiscounted materials/bill; missing classification does not ban copying or its independent time benefit. Missing actual required materials still prevents work. No guessed rarity classification, refund for supplies already used, duplication of the source, free rare ink or teaching-cost waiver follows. Real consumption, deterioration and fees are recorded as they occur; interruption loses only its actual phase credit and actual resources, never refills stock.

## FT84-Q02

FT84-Q02 / FT84-RESEARCH-PHASES-01 — two named research-process completions, not a universal research clock.
KEEP each actual more-specific project phase, completion check, loss, source access, material rule and fixed waiting period. Language training already uses completed fifteen-active-minute phases; Ritual Caster already names that study clock; advanced manufacture already has its included first min(15, adjusted total minutes) incorporation and declared later phases of at most eight hours. Those are distinct procedures. Diligent Researcher halves only authorized ordinary active research using actually relevant library/archive/facility access. It does not shorten teaching, language courses, copying, ritual learning/execution, item encoding, Scribing Insight gain or its specific spend route, repair or independently elapsed waits.
NEW NARROW COMPLETION applies only where no more-specific phase is supplied for (A) LA-07's ordinary preregistered Translate/Decipher project of at most 250 words, and (B) the Crafting/Harvest framework's research of an existing available formula. For these two named processes, establish the full baseline active requirement first: 480 minutes for A; 60*max(8, actual minimumResearchHours) for B. Then take the strongest actually applicable research-time factor once. Required time is tracked in active seconds, rounded upward only once if the final exact requirement contains a fractional second; this is local project precision, not a new global simulation tick.
For these processes alone, credit completed fifteen-active-minute research phases and one final shorter phase exactly equal to the remaining requirement. This final remainder can arise from the actual adjusted requirement or an actual authorized progress loss; do not pad it to fifteen minutes or truncate it. This is expressly NEW for A/B; matching the teaching quantum is a selected convention, not evidence that teaching governed research already. A phase boundary is bookkeeping, not an interruption of real continuous work. A real interruption or lost prerequisite loses only that phase's unfinished prospective credit; completed credit, actual elapsed work, paid access, used stock and genuine losses persist. No resource incorporation/stabilization occurs merely because a research phase completes. Work can stop safely at completed boundaries with secure real notes; a new session continues the same project and evidence identity.
Maintain separate ledgers for actual work, credited research, uncredited/lost work, stock, fees, fixed waits, participating actors, evidence/method and final check. No minute also funds another project, training, guarding or rest; at most two actual active work Watches remain. LA-07's one qualified assistant earns its +1 only by materially attending all adjusted required work, including the successful replacement of interrupted phases; pay actual agreed attendance/access, not a free assumed assistant. Recipe creation assistant caps do not automatically become formula-research permissions. A formula-research case with no independently supported assistant uses none. One declared lead check is made at real completion under its original skill/DC and task degrees. Success yields only the supported passage/formula; failure yields notes under that source. No intermediate phase roll, extra result, XP, material salvage or repeated unchanged-evidence roll is created. Retry prerequisites, concrete changed-method work and original information/public-private limits remain. Other research sources need their own actual clock; this completion does not assign them A/B's phases.

## FT84-Q03

FT84-Q03 / FT84-CLOSED-CREATION-01 — Craftsman applies to actual authorized creation, not names or all fixed-duration work.
INTERPRETATION SELECTED: personally leading an independently authorized closed feat process that makes/assembles a new usable physical body or prepared kit is eligible creation even when it is non-artisan, lacks a catalog recipe ID or prints a fixed base duration. Craftsman supplies only its half-active-time factor; it supplies no process, skill, formula, assistants, work opportunity, materials, use or output. Use the strongest genuinely applicable creation reduction once. An explicit source exclusion or distinct alteration/preparation/service category still controls. Keep actual physical minimum phases only where the source requires them; do not manufacture an advanced-recipe incorporation phase for every short feat process.
Contraption Crafter's ten minutes are the ordinary base for its real gadget construction. With actual personally leading Craftsman ownership the complete job is five uninterrupted active minutes, not ten with a final five-minute discount and not two-and-a-half from a second equivalent half. The whole adjusted job must be uninterrupted; phase logging, assistant handoff, pauses, rest or partially completed earlier attempts cannot satisfy it. Keep actual Gnome heritage, usable tinker's tools, the same five-gp suitable owned parts and the same once-per-24-elapsed-hours use committed at start. A new lawful construction still ends its old gadget at start. Interruption spends the committed parts/use and yields no working gadget, no completion roll, no refundable tail and no resumable partial product. Completion rolls the same one d4 openly, once; it grants no chosen profile, duplicate gadget, general proficiency, quality, recipe XP or parts recovery. The same one-hour gadget lifetime starts at actual completion, not at the undiscounted hypothetical finish. Transfer/deployment/activation/recharge rules are unchanged. The time benefit is prospective and cannot manufacture a mid-job feat acquisition opportunity; if a real change interrupts the required work, the ordinary failure consequence applies.
Trapsetter's independently authorized thirty-active-minute kit assembly is likewise eligible creation: fifteen active minutes with Craftsman. This includes only its actual prepared-kit assembly from the real source parts; it cannot manufacture an ordinary twenty-caltrop stock bag out of an arbitrary substitute or count buying/deploying one as new creation. Keep the one completion DC12 INT/Engineering or DEX/thieves-tools check, ordinary task degree/natural shifts collapsed to binary, actual parts, failure consumption and recoverable remaining metal/frame. No manufacturing quality/yield/XP or general recipe is added. NEW NARROW WORK-CREDIT COMPLETION for this otherwise unphased kit assembly: track actual active work in seconds against the adjusted required total; pauses do not by themselves erase already completed usable physical assembly work. A pause earns no credit, protection or rest entitlement. Preserve the actual partial body and committed stock under their ordinary custody, deterioration and loss rules; work that a genuine physical event destroys cannot remain credited. The unfinished kit has no function. Resume only the remaining actual work with the real usable/replacement parts; do not duplicate stock, credit or partial bodies. No completion roll occurs until the whole adjusted job is actually complete. Neither Contraption's uninterrupted-work/use forfeiture nor advanced manufacture's incorporation/stabilization is imported. A more-specific explicitly authorized phase/loss rule still controls.
Kit assembly does not spend or refresh Trapsetter's three source-specific quick-placement allowances, arm a trap, clear an old active slot, reset hard-denial history or reduce placement, arming, disarming, collection or trigger/Reaction costs. Those remain separately paid physical events.
Apply the same predicate to Bowyer's actual bounded mundane bow/arrow creation and Master Smithing's authorized weapon/armor creation: their actual creation work qualifies, with full bridge credentials, stock, DC, phases and waits. Combat Engineering's actual barricade creation also qualifies but its own half factor and Craftsman compare once (60 to30, not15). Master Smithing's expressly nondiscounted eight-hour temper and Combat Engineering's expressly fixed ten-minute kill-zone preparation remain unchanged. Separate encoding, repair, maintenance, harvesting, copying, ritual casting and rest remain outside Craftsman. Actual advanced recipe121 still keeps its own required included incorporation/phase/stock/assistant rules; the short feat processes do not rewrite them. Merely printing a fixed duration neither exempts genuine creation nor makes a noncreation activity eligible.
