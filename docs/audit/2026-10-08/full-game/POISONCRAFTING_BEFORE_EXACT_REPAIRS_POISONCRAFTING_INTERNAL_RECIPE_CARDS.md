## recipe-359 — Field Caustic Toxin

**Source:** public; Poisoncrafting level 2; tier 1; Common; crafting DC 8; 8 active hours; 2 dose(s). Standard batch reference 77 gp.

**Preserved effect:** Injury poison; DC 8 Constitution save or take 1d6 acid damage and become poisoned for 1 minute. Repeat the save at the end of each turn.

**Operational effect:** On a legitimate injury exposure, make a DC 8 CON save. Failure attempts 1d6 acid damage and Poisoned; success applies neither. The ongoing condition lasts at most ten future regular target-end markers (60 seconds outside initiative), with the printed repeat CON save ending that record only. Repeat saves never repeat the initial damage. Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks, not saving throws; ordinary Core Poisoned removal applies.

**Delivery:** one compatible piercing/slashing weapon or up to three compatible ammunition units present during application. One Action; one available hand; hit-gated one-minute coating, first weapon hit or one hit per up-to-three ammunition units.
