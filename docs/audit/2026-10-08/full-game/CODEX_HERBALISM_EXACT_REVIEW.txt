# Herbalism recipes — campaign-playtest procedures

## Authority and product identity

The current Crafting V4 recipe identities and V5 public effects supply the printed recipe, ingredients, trade level, DC, time, batch quantity, rarity and reference value. Exact source records and book paragraph bindings are preserved in the audit. Current complete effect prose controls abbreviated mechanics and inactive legacy effects. No recipe is approved merely because an old record says `developed`.

Every finished Herbalism product in this overlay is a magical, nonspell, single-use consumable. Administration requires no generic RP, RC, attunement, spell rank, focus, class or Herbalism proficiency. Manufacturing retains the actual Herbalism kit credential, formula, trade level and station requirements. These items grant no resource pool, Dissonance/Refrain reset or other spellcasting entitlement. Counterspell and spell-rank-only removal rules do not automatically apply. An effect expressly suppressing nonspell magic may apply within its actual scope. Suppression neither pauses elapsed time nor refunds a dose.

Names, tags, ingredient properties and legacy names supply no unprinted power. Dream-Bark Sleeping Draught does not induce sleep. Living-Root Regeneration Poultice gives its one healing application and local treatment, with no periodic regeneration. Poison, Acid, Fire or Unholy words in a product do not injure its recipient. Source tags remain available to rules that explicitly inspect such tags, without creating new resistance, damage or disease. A name containing Masterwork, Superior or Exalted is not an equipment grade or the result of this crafting check.

## Inventory, packaging and manufacture

Each dose consists of **0.1 lb finished compound in one actual 0.1-lb Glass vial**, for **0.2 lb gross** and one ordinary vial slot. Its container has AC 13 and 1 structural HP. This small single-dose container holds oral liquid or topical paste. Breaking/spilling destroys the usable dose; it creates no automatic attack, area, poison exposure or chain effect. One unit cannot be divided among recipients or converted into multiple reduced doses.

None of these source recipes contains a consumed vessel. Supplementary ordinary Glass vials use the reviewed equipment profile (`alchemical-containers.glass-vial`, reference 1 gp each). An intact owned vial may be reused without buying it again. Actual supplier quotes govern purchases. The source component and batch economics remain unchanged; additional packaging and carrier have separately recorded acquisition costs. A supplementary vial does not replace a tiered recipe ingredient or the Herbalism kit.

Before the completion check, reserve enough actual empty vials for **floor(1.5 × Standard batch quantity)**, even when intending to select durability. Also reserve enough actual clean carrier water to fund the maximum compound mass after the worst eligible one-unit catalyst retention. Carrier is supplementary bulk and cannot replace a listed ingredient's amount, tier, role, properties or form. Recipe-201 has 0.46 lb consumed ingredients but can produce six 0.1-lb doses: reserve **0.14 lb extra clean carrier water**. The cards record the calculation for every recipe. Consume only carrier actually needed to reach the chosen finished mass; unused reserved vials/carrier return to ordinary inventory. No post-roll purchase, borrowing or retrieval completes missing pre-check reservations. Without the required reservations, the completion check cannot begin.

Count reusable source tools separately. A `Mortar and pestle` row marked `consumed:false` is returned with its original identity, tier, value and actual condition; it is not a vessel or carrier mass. If physically suitable, that same implement may satisfy the corresponding auxiliary station-tool requirement. It does not supply the Herbalism-kit credential or any other missing station/tool.

The first **15 active minutes** of the recipe's existing work are its binding/stabilization phase; do not add 15 minutes to the source work time. Inputs must remain legal until that phase completes. An illegal perishable input pauses the work under the framework until replaced, or the order is abandoned. A feature genuinely applying to this trade scales the phase proportionately with total work, rounding upward to a whole minute. Herbalism is not an artisan-tool trade by default, so Forge Dwarf Master Craftsman does not apply merely because this is crafting.

After binding, the sealed mixture is stable only inside that one actual work order. It cannot be removed as fresh raw stock, sold as ingredients, transferred to another order or used as a generic preservation service. A legitimately returned catalyst or failure-recovered input retains its prior identity, condition and accumulated age; its ordinary clock resumes. Abandonment and failed material recovery use the shared framework. Excess process mass and spent residue are waste with no automatic component, food, salvage or resale identity.

An intact, sealed finished dose keeps potency indefinitely under ordinary storage. After first opening it has **one elapsed hour** of potency; resealing, transfer or splitting never resets that deadline. Administration must begin before it expires. At legal administration start, the entire dose is committed to that recipient; the begun continuous application uses its own uninterrupted completion rule rather than retaining a second reusable inventory dose. Merely opening it is not successful administration.

After oral or topical use, the surviving vial is an ordinary spent dose vial: 0.1 lb, AC 13, one-dose capacity and its actual remaining/max structural HP (normally 1, or 2 if durability was improved). It has no magical use, recovered ingredient tier/properties, or regenerated recipe value. Its ordinary empty-vial reference is 1 gp, subject to a real buyer's quote; it may be reused as supplementary packaging if intact. Old provenance is not usable high-tier crafting stock. The actual emptied object remains with its physical holder; the contents' application does not teleport the vial to the patient.

## Administration and uninterrupted application

An oral antidote or hazard buff takes **one Action and one available hand** to drink or feed to one willing or Incapacitated active creature within 5 feet. It needs a physically compatible ingestion route; inability to swallow blocks oral use. No forced dose to an unwilling active creature, no inhaled attack, coating or later tea-brewing Action is implied. “Active” here means not Dead; a still-dying creature can receive a compatible dose. Creature type alone does not prohibit a compatible route, and an actual source-specific healing restriction still controls.

A balm, compress, healing salve or poultice takes **one uninterrupted active minute**, with one available hand and continuing touch/access to compatible tissue. The recipient must be willing or Incapacitated. In initiative, the applier spends an Action on each of **10 consecutive regular turns**, and at least 60 elapsed seconds must pass. Extra Actions or inserted turns cannot shorten the interval. Track elapsed start time as well as the Action count; finish only when both requirements are met. One applier completes the whole application; workers cannot divide, parallelize or hand off those Actions. An active recipient may perform other legal activity if it keeps the necessary access; no new immobilization is imposed.

Choose and lock the actual recipient when beginning. Spend one dose at legal start. Before-start illegality prevents starting and spending; an interruption after start loses the paid dose with no result. Loss of required access, missing a required Action, applier incapacity or the recipient becoming ineligible interrupts. Taking damage alone is not an interruption and prompts no invented concentration check. The recipe grants no HP, condition removal or stabilization before completion; all ordinary dying clocks continue. A healer's kit may be needed first to keep a patient alive during the minute.

The applier is working and cannot claim that active time as rest. A recipient may receive care while resting only if the actual rest procedure permits it. A remedy does not pay the separate medical-kit, Supply or time costs of a different treatment project. Administering it is not a crafting check and grants no manufacturing XP.

## Antidotes

On resolution, **end all currently present removable applications of Poisoned**, then grant advantage on saving throws against poison for the card's printed duration. Source-specific applications that expressly cannot be removed this way remain. Ending Poisoned does not itself end its poison/toxin, disease, curse, intoxication, injury, environmental exposure or other underlying source. That cause may apply Poisoned again only through its own actual continuing or future trigger; do not invent an immediate reapplication just to negate the cure.

The source's abbreviated `bonus:1` or `bonus:3` is inactive here: complete effect prose promises advantage, not a numeric bonus. There is no damage resistance, immunity, general CON-save advantage, disease cure or intoxication emergency treatment. A target with no current Poisoned application can still receive the prospective save advantage.

## Healing and Local Treatment

At completion, attempt the printed dice and flat amount as ordinary magical, nonspell current-HP healing. Apply actual source blockers, modifiers, maximum and Critical ceilings, and eligible finite-healing callbacks. Positive HP recovery from dying follows the reviewed Core procedure; blocked healing does not invent stabilization. Dead creatures cannot be revived. These remedies do not automatically remove Wounds, Injuries, Burning, Poisoned, disease, Corruption or curses.

**Local Treatment:** After this dose's healing and all healing-triggered condition endpoints finish, choose at most one present local wound, burn, infection or Bleeding source appropriate to actual topical care and the remedy's ingredients. If the Bleeding source has an existing Medicine-based removal DC, the applier may immediately make one ordinary **WIS (Medicine)** check against that DC. The consumed remedy supplies dressing for this one attempt, with no additional healer's-kit charge. Actual Medicine proficiency applies once if possessed; none is newly granted or required. Use the source's check outcomes, or ordinary task success/failure with no invented extra critical benefit. Success ends only that eligible Bleeding source.

Do not make the extra Medicine check if this same dose's healing already resolved an explicit removal/treatment attempt for that record, including an unsuccessful source save. Ended records cannot be selected again. A different present eligible Bleeding record may be selected if this dose has not already attempted its healing endpoint. This gives no repeat attempt on one Blood Curse merely because the first save failed.

For another wound, burn or infection, the application may satisfy **one explicitly required topical dressing, cleaning, salve or comparable local step** in that source's existing treatment procedure. It does not waive or shorten any other required time, check, Supply, kit charge, medicine, surgery, rest or source-specific cure. If the source defines no topical-treatment interaction, the remedy has no additional automatic condition-removal effect. Treatment language alone cannot remove a Core Wound or its linked Injury; ordinary Wound care still takes its actual eight hours, medical kit, one Supply and Medicine check. Burning keeps its reviewed Action, immersion or save procedure unless its actual source permits this topical treatment.

The local-care component remains independent of actual HP gained; full HP or an HP-only blocker need not prevent a legal dressing. Broad suppression of the finished remedy's magic blocks its magical effects within that suppression's actual scope, without turning a spent dose into a reusable mundane treatment charge.

## Hazard bonuses

A check or save qualifies only when its immediate declared purpose is to **detect, identify, avoid, resist, escape, disable, safely traverse or survive an already-present qualifying hazard**. Establish that connection before rolling; the rule grants no secret identity, new skill entitlement or automatic detection. It never applies to attacks, damage, spellcasting checks, Initiative, ordinary crafting, formula research, or harvesting merely because the ingredient has a matching tag. A proactive task with only thematic similarity is insufficient.

| Tag | Actual qualifying hazard |
|---|---|
| Poison | Poison/Poisoned saves; checks identifying, avoiding, safely handling or disabling actual poison hazards. |
| Fire | Fire-damage and fire environmental saves; direct avoidance, extinguishing or safe traversal. |
| Cold | Cold damage or exposure; directly surviving or avoiding the cold hazard. |
| Lightning | Lightning damage or electricity; direct identification, avoidance or disabling. |
| Acid | Acid damage or corrosive hazards; direct handling, avoidance or disabling. |
| Earth | Rockfall, cave-in, burial, unstable ground, geological crushing or another physical earth/stone hazard. Merely standing on stone does not qualify. |
| Water | Currents, flooding, drowning, undertow or hazardous immersion. |
| Air | Hazardous wind, pressure, inhalation, air loss or airborne environmental force. Thunder is not inferred from theme. |
| Psychic | Psychic damage, actual mental intrusion or an explicitly psychic hazard; not all mental saves or social influence. |
| Unholy | Only an explicitly mechanically labeled Unholy hazard. Never inferred from species, morality, religion, appearance or origin. |
| Arcane | Explicit magical/Arcane trap, ward, field, environmental phenomenon or similar hazard; not every hostile spell/caster action. |
| Strength | STR checks/saves to resist or escape actual physical hazards, restraints, crushing, forced movement or entrapment. No carrying-capacity, attack or broad all-STR bonus. |
| Stealth | Stealth checks to avoid a present hostile observer, pursuer, guard, sensor or detection system. No Deception, Initiative or automatic Hidden state. |
| Vitality | CON checks/saves against fatigue, exposure, environmental endurance or disease. No death, concentration, Wound/Injury saves or extra recovery roll. |

A direct elemental match may qualify even if caused by a spell: Fire can affect the DEX save against actual fire damage. Arcane does not make all spells qualify. A mixed hazard may qualify several tags, but only the **highest numeric Herbalism hazard bonus** applies to any one roll. Each dose keeps its own expiry; a longer weaker dose cannot extend a stronger bonus. Independent legal advantage/disadvantage can coexist under the ordinary rules. No bonus creates a roll where the actual hazard has none.

## Duration, quality and markets

An ongoing benefit starts when administration resolves, not when crafting finishes or the vial opens. Use actual elapsed minutes/hours. A printed until-Long-Rest endpoint means the next completed beneficial Long Rest. Death ends ongoing benefits without resumption; it never refunds the dose or erases a persistent limited-use history. Suppression does not pause clocks. Repeated advantage sources never stack; independent applications have their own expiry and removal records. A buff applies to the actual recipient's qualifying rolls, without propagating to another creature it controls or summons.

For **Flawed** results, a Standard batch quantity of at least two produces floor(Q/2) ordinary-strength doses. A singleton fixed-duration hazard buff halves only its duration, rounded down to whole six-second rounds with a minimum of one round. A singleton fixed-duration antidote retains its immediate Poisoned removal but halves its save-advantage duration; recipe-220 therefore gives 30 minutes. A singleton instantaneous topical heal/treatment or a benefit lasting until the next Long Rest has no coherent half-duration profile: treat that result as **Failed** with ordinary framework input recovery. Application time is not effect duration.

Exceptional/Masterwork select floor(1.25Q)/floor(1.5Q) doses or retain Standard quantity with **every finished container at max/current HP 2**. Never combine bonus yield and container durability. The Masterwork eligible catalyst return remains independent. These options never improve HP dice, bonuses, save advantage, cure breadth, duration, use limits or charges. All maximum-yield packaging/carrier must have been reserved before the completion check.

Printed values are Standard **batch** references. Unit reference equals that value divided by Standard quantity; do not multiply the batch reference by its quantity again. Apply a real supplier/buyer quote and the reviewed ordinary market rounding/resale rules for nonartifact magical consumables; an item needs actual artifact status before using artifact-market multipliers.
