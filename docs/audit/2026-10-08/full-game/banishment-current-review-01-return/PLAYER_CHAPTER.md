# Banishment — Provisional Player Procedure

## Status
This is a **NEW provisional authored playtest procedure** for the ordinary rank-4 **Banishment** spell only. It does not alter the accepted AC4 fifteen-spell module, does not define a canonical plane or afterlife, and is not runtime, balance, human-playtest, whole-game or publication approval.

The original source identity is preserved: **Abjuration, rank 4, base 7 RP, Action, 60 feet, V/S/F; “Send a creature to another plane temporarily.”** The Core source also prints **Instantaneous** duration. That metadata remains part of the evidence. The temporary procedure below therefore adds an expressly **NEW concentration duration of up to 60 elapsed seconds** rather than pretending the source already supplied one.

## Casting Banishment
Choose **one creature you actually perceive within 60 feet and line of effect**. The target may be you. A corpse, image, soul record or unattended object is not a legal target.

Commit the Action, V/S/F, actual final spell price and ordinary casting/concentration requirements. Banishment begins concentration at legal cast commitment. Starting that concentration ends an incompatible prior concentration normally. Counterspell and other actual cast-time responses retain their own timing.

**NEW save:** the target makes a **Charisma save against your actual spell DC**. An actually willing creature may elect to fail before rolling. A successful save creates no exile. Costs already paid remain paid.

A failed save can establish exile only if the existing shared hard-control allowance for that source-character/target/encounter is available. If it is not, the spell creates no softer substitute. A creature can have only one live ordinary Banishment application.

### Voluntary restrictions
A failed save by an unwilling creature is forced planar displacement. A willing choice to fail is voluntary admission. Therefore actual effects such as Paladin **No Escape** or **Verdict Absolute**, which forbid voluntary teleport/extradimensional travel, can block the willing-election branch without granting blanket immunity against hostile failed-save Banishment.

## The temporary spell-cell
On successful establishment, the same target body and its legal **noncreature** worn/carried gear move to one temporary extraplanar spell-cell.

The cell:
- is **not identified with any named plane, afterlife, god, Heartcell state or soul destination**;
- begins with a level floor, ordinary light and nonharmful spell-local sustaining medium;
- starts large enough for the target’s actual body with 5 feet clearance on every side;
- remains finite but can expand outward when a legal transformation, visitor or creature-creation event needs more fitting space;
- never crushes, duplicates or resizes a creature;
- provides no harvestable air, water, material, resistance, immunity or biological trait;
- blocks mundane passage and ordinary line of sight/effect across its boundary.

You retain your actual movement, Actions, Bonus Actions, Reactions, concentration, HP, conditions, death saves and resources. Banishment itself does not Incapacitate, heal, rest or freeze you.

## Time
Successful establishment records a fixed deadline **60 elapsed seconds later**. All your other real clocks continue normally. Extra turns and initiative changes do not restart Banishment.

The spell ends early if its concentration ends, the application is actually ended/dispelled, Xi legally ends its ongoing target branch, or this Banishment application is suppressed. For this spell only, suppression closes the cell rather than pausing exile.

## What travels with the target
Banishment moves **one creature identity** plus its genuine noncreature worn/carried gear.

Another creature is not gear. A mount, passenger, swallowed separate creature or companion does not travel with the selected target. If such a body cannot be legally separated before transport, establishment fails rather than cutting or duplicating bodies.

Real external tethers and fixed structures do not become inventory. Items used, destroyed, transferred or consumed inside stay used, destroyed, transferred or consumed.

## Acting or escaping inside
The cell grants no native exit and no knowledge of another destination. An occupant may still use any independently legal cross-plane ability it actually has, paying its real Action/resources and meeting its own destination/knowledge/geometry requirements.

If the original target legally escapes the cell, its Banishment branch ends immediately and it is not pulled back later.

A different visitor may arrive only through an independently legal route that can actually reach this cell. A summon or created creature may appear only through its own legal source. Neither receives free knowledge, RP, actions or a reusable route.

## Ending and return
When Banishment ends, the cell performs one terminal closure:
1. return/eject the original target body or surviving corpse if it is still present;
2. eject any other real creature bodies still present in admission order;
3. eject surviving loose real objects in stable object-ID order;
4. close the cell.

For the primary target, return begins at the recorded world-space origin anchor. If occupied or physically unsuitable, use the nearest fitting legal space in deterministic 5-foot rings, up to 120 feet. Unsupported free space can be legal; normal Falling then begins.

Current **Dimensional Anchor** and other endpoint blockers are respected first. If endpoint-prevention effects alone would block every otherwise legal candidate through that 120-foot search, **BAN-CLOSE-01** ignores only those prevention predicates for this one terminal closure. This narrowly prevents a temporary Banishment from becoming indefinite; it is not a general Anchor bypass and gives no future transport privilege.

Death is not reversed. If a corpse remains, the same corpse returns. If a finite summon body has already ended, no substitute appears. Consumed gear does not reappear.

## Current interactions
- **Spell Shield:** ordinary Banishment is rank 4, so it is not eligible for the accepted rank-0–3 Spell Shield reflection.
- **Dimensional Anchor:** blocks Banishment establishment or ordinary escape when its real endpoint predicate applies. Closure first searches for an unblocked return; BAN-CLOSE-01 is only the terminal last resort described above.
- **Resonance Beacon:** requires same-plane traveler/beacon. A beacon outside the cell does not become same-plane.
- **Xi:** can end only the actual ongoing Banishment target branch using Xi’s own Action, range, perception, known-record, dual-consent and charge rules.
- **Oracle Fate-Bound Boon IV:** its actual +2 applies to saves against banishment.
- **Chain of Duty:** still requires same plane; the old origin anchor does not make an exiled ally same-plane.
- **Reign of Ashes:** Banishment is planar displacement here, not classified as teleportation for that feature.
- **Null-disabled finite summons:** remain their same Disabled body while their own source persists; Banishment neither heals nor refreshes them.

**Cleric Banish Unholy is a separate class technique** with its own WIS save, radiant damage/push and short banishment rider. It does not use this ordinary spell procedure.
