# Rank-one Evocation authoring clarification

The complete source conference is saved. All twelve remain unselected; catalog249 reviewed/599 unfinished. Please settle these finite drafting details before exact cards. Cosmology remains outside this work.

1. Magic Missile Storm: I propose numbering missiles1–3 and locking their targets and resolution order at cast commitment, then resolving in that order after the normal cast-counter window. Each is an independent auto-hit/packet with the source-prescribed defenses and hooks. For Shield, allocation is one named targeting opportunity per recipient from this cast, available after commitment and before that recipient's FIRST missile damage. Later missiles do not reopen that allocation trigger after it has passed. A recipient that dies/disappears/becomes an illegal creature target gets no free retarget; unresolved allocations are spent and follow normal target legality, not a new corpse attack. Confirm or repair. The bridge applies ONLY to the ordinary Shield spell's named exception; it grants no compatibility to Mage Arcane Missile or unrelated class features merely sharing names.

2. Arcane Shield Shatter: does BOTH the selected effect and designated protected creature need to be perceived, within30 feet and line of effect? I propose yes as an explicit new bound. Actual generating rank0–1, spell origin, protection status and immunity to dispel are resolved after paid commitment when hidden, with no free diagnostic; if any eligibility fails, neither ending nor damage occurs. 'Explicitly identified as shield/barrier/ward' is a source-qualified description/identity, not a substring match that accidentally admits a Ward-named class engine. A spell-created purely physical shield object is excluded; an ongoing magical defensive barrier with a protected creature can qualify. Select exactly one independent effect/component; its actual parent-ending callbacks govern siblings. Damage occurs only after successful ending and is one automatic1d8 force packet to the designated still-legal creature. Confirm or repair these interpretations. If a specifically protected shield cannot be ended, no damage fallback.

3. Ignition Bolt critical hit: the primary attack's1d10 is doubled on a critical hit, but the nearby DEX-half splash should use only the original shared1d10 base roll, not the extra critical die. Thus roll a base1d10 plus the critical attack-only1d10 separately when relevant. This preserves the source's splash magnitude without importing area criticals. Confirm. Does splash remain centered at the target's position at attack resolution even when the hit destroys it? I propose capture that resolved impact point once; no post-damage relocation or missing-object loss of the already-established splash. Other creatures need center line of effect, and the primary target never takes that splash twice.

4. Earth Tremor: I propose its patch lies on ONE contiguous physical supporting surface within10 feet of the selected center, with center line of effect. It does not reach through a floor/wall to another storey or automatically turn every stacked platform into terrain. The caster also saves if actually supported by the eligible patch. One-minute difficult terrain is a finite magical movement-cost effect; it neither creates permanent rubble/materials nor destroys the actual surface, and it ends normally at expiry/dispel. Initial damage has no later entry pulse. Each cast has its own patch/deadline, with overlap not increasing movement cost. Confirm or repair.

5. Ice Knife: confirm cold burst requires line of effect from the captured impact center to each creature, includes the caster/allies and initial target, and has no save. Capture the target's actual position at attack resolution BEFORE applying piercing damage, so piercing destruction/movement consequences do not relocate or erase the cold burst. If the original target was already illegal before attack resolution, no attack and no newly located burst occurs; this is not a hidden-target tracking oracle. Each target's cold packet remains separate from the primary piercing packet; only piercing attack dice can crit. Confirm or repair.

6. Thunderwave: is your 'up to10 feet' an optional chosen distance, or mandatory travel toward10 feet that stops early only when ordinary forced-movement legality prevents further travel? Source says pushes10; I propose the latter. Use direct radial movement away from the cone origin, actual support/falling rules, no new collision damage, and no caster self-push merely for being cone origin. A failed STR save supplies the push independently of whether thunder damage was positive. Confirm or repair.

7. Witch Bolt: confirm successful initial HIT establishes the tether even when lightning damage is reduced to0, subject to an actual defense that separately blocks the tether. Maintenance is an activation of the existing spell, NOT a new spell cast: no new RP/Dissonance/Refrain/cast-begin/Counterspell window, while actual damage/outcome hooks follow their real trigger wording. It uses one ordinary Action and at most one pulse on each future regular turn; no extra-turn or cast-turn pulses, and a separately granted extra Action does not create another pulse. All original duration markers remain, and expiry at the tenth future caster START / outside+60 precedes that turn's new voluntary pulse. Please specify the outside-to-initiative carry: if a virtual Action/pulse has already been used in the current six-second cycle, must the first real regular turn retain that used ordinary Action/pulse budget (as with prior controlled-creature cadence), or does it get its ordinary Action but no additional Witch Bolt pulse until the next unused opportunity? We need an explicit choice that conserves pulse history without silently taking unrelated Action permissions. When entering before any virtual pulse, can the next real turn pulse before original+6, while keeping original+60 cap? Also confirm no range/LoE/hearing tether between maintenance opportunities, but death/ended existence of the target ends a creature-only tether rather than preserving an active corpse channel.

8. Zephyr Strike: a successful initial hit with a weapon consumes the pending1d8 even when all damage is later prevented; no positive-HP-loss requirement. The rider adds to that one weapon-hit event and its actual critical dice, while movement protection persists independently. A replaced/ended cast removes only its own pending damage and movement protection and releases only its own concentration dependency; it cannot cancel a newer replacement. Ordinary range30 is an application gate only. Confirm these standard event/source bindings.

These questions do not reopen the agreed source dice/actions/ranges. Any added mechanics will be marked NEW in the exact manuscript. No cards are promoted until the later exact review.
