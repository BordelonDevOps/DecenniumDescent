## What It Is

Deep Dwarf Xarrorn is an intelligent floor-dweller with territorial, religious, or political motives. Directors should establish what it protects before combat, because defeating it may create a worse enemy or close a valuable route.

## What Experienced Expeditions Notice

Masonry repaired from the inside; water channels marked with votive cuts; discarded fungi sorted by toxicity; warning bolts deliberately embedded at eye level.

## How It Fights

It opens with **Weapon Strike** against the target most capable of controlling its movement. It uses **Off-hand Strike** to punish anyone who tries to stabilize that target. It uses prepared cover and retreats behind the next defensive layer when the first is lost.

## How to Defeat It

**Prepare Before Contact**

- Prepare resistance or mitigation for slashing, piercing damage.
- Map doors, bridges, and firing lanes before parley fails.

**Combat Priority**

Deny prepared terrain and command figures; accept surrender when the objective is secured.

**Exploit**

The defining weakness is **None identified**. Treat this as an encounter solution rather than an automatic damage vulnerability. Successful Research, Recon, or Resource actions should make the exploit easier or safer.

**Withdraw When**

Withdraw or change the objective when the creature reaches favorable terrain or isolates a target. A failed retreat against this creature usually costs more than abandoning the immediate treasure.

## Habitat, Goals, and Lair

Defended holds use layered doors, murder shafts, water traps, narrow bridges, and negotiated kill zones. Religious sites add chanting pools, toxin vents, and captive witnesses.

The deep folk are not one faction. They are cultures forced into the same trade roads, flooded caverns, and defensive wars. Their encounters should begin with motive before initiative.

Deep folk respond to ownership, passage rights, debts, clan names, and proof of common enemies.
