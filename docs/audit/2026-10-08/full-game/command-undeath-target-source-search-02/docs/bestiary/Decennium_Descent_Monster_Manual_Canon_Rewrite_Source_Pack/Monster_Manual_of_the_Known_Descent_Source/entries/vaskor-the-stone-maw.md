## What It Is

Vaskor, the Stone Maw is the Gate Guardian for possibility under pressure. It is an arena-scale adversary whose body, Seal, and terrain operate as one examination. Reducing it to zero hit points is necessary, but an Expedition that ignores its Gate principle can still lose the passage, the reward, or members of the team.

## What Experienced Expeditions Notice

Architecture becomes ceremonial; lesser monsters stop crossing a boundary; previous Expeditions leave preparation caches rather than camps; repeated symbols appear in terrain and wounds.

### Traits

**Gate-Bound.** The Guardian cannot be permanently displaced from its arena while the Seal remains active.

**Tremor Approach.** It can enter or leave the ground without provoking ordinary reactions unless its emergence point was prepared.

**Escalation Pulse.** **Seismic Roar** recharges on 5-6; visible signs appear one round before the first use.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The creature makes 2 attacks using the actions below.

**Stone Maw.** *Attack:* +7 to hit, one target. *Hit:* 2d10+7 bludgeoning damage (average 18).

**Crushing Jaw.** *Attack:* +7 to hit, one target. *Hit:* 2d8+5 bludgeoning damage (average 14).

**Seismic Roar (Recharge 5-6).** Creatures in the affected area make a DC 15 Strength save, taking 5d8 bludgeoning damage (average 22) on a failure and half on a success. The Director should describe the charge-up before resolving it.

## How It Fights

It opens with **Stone Maw** against the target most capable of controlling its movement. It uses **Crushing Jaw** to punish anyone who tries to stabilize that target. When **Seismic Roar** recharges, it delays only if it can catch a more important cluster or interrupt a decisive ritual. It escalates by phase and uses the Seal Pulse to punish parties that solve damage but ignore the test.

## How to Defeat It

**Prepare Before Contact**

- Prepare resistance or mitigation for bludgeoning damage.
- Mark tremors and protect the rear line from resurfacing attacks.
- Spread before **Seismic Roar** becomes available.

**Combat Priority**

Complete the arena objective while maintaining damage pressure.

**Exploit**

The defining weakness is **None identified**. Treat this as an encounter solution rather than an automatic damage vulnerability. Successful Research, Recon, or Resource actions should make the exploit easier or safer.

**Withdraw When**

Withdraw or change the objective when Seismic Roar catches three or more Expedition members. A failed retreat against this creature usually costs more than abandoning the immediate treasure.

## Habitat, Goals, and Lair

A Gate arena is part of the creature. Terrain, Seal Pulses, objectives, and failure conditions matter as much as hit points.

Gate Guardians are not random apex monsters. Each is a living examination tied to a Division of the dungeon, a Gate Seal, and a principle the Expedition must demonstrate under pressure.

Some Guardians speak, bargain, or test. None are safely reduced to ordinary patrons or quest-givers.
