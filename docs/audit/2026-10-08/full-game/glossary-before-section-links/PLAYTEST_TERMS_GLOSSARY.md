# Playtest terms — quick reference

**Editorial draft; joint presentation review pending.** These short definitions point to the current authored playtest rules. Use the linked chapter and the actual acquired card for exceptions, timing and costs. This glossary adds no rule or permission to use unfinished content.

## Character and card notation

| Term | Meaning | Full rule |
|---|---|---|
| Ability score and modifier | STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS and CHA are scores. Their modifiers are `floor((score − 10) / 2)`, including negative results. | [Character foundation](../mechanics/CHARACTER_CREATION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Character level | Total levels across the character's classes. Keep the chronological level record. | [Character foundation](../mechanics/CHARACTER_CREATION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Class level | Levels in the particular class supplying a card, spell access or engine. Check that class's own prerequisites. | [Character foundation](../mechanics/CHARACTER_CREATION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Base, Arch and Hero | Stages within a class. Follow its own acquisition schedule, including its explicit exceptions. | [Class library](../mechanics/CLASS_PLAYTEST_INDEX.md) |
| PB | Proficiency bonus from total character level. Apply it when actually trained or otherwise authorized; two relevant proficiencies do not automatically add it twice. | [Character foundation](../mechanics/CHARACTER_CREATION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| L on a class card | That card's class level. | [Shared class notation](../mechanics/CLASS_PLAYTEST_RULES.md) |
| S on a class card | The scaling value: 1, 2, 3 or 4 at that class's levels 1, 5, 11 or 17. | [Shared class notation](../mechanics/CLASS_PLAYTEST_RULES.md) |
| M on a class card | `max(1, the class ability modifier)` for effect amounts. Attacks and save DCs use the actual appropriate modifier, including negative values. | [Shared class notation](../mechanics/CLASS_PLAYTEST_RULES.md) |

Variables printed by another system keep that system's definitions. A trade level, material tier, spell rank and class stage are separate fields.

## Rolls and results

| Term | Meaning | Full rule |
|---|---|---|
| AC | Armor Class: the number an attack must meet. Use one applicable AC formula, then its legal modifiers. | [Character foundation](../mechanics/CHARACTER_CREATION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| DC | Difficulty Class: the threshold for the particular check or save. A printed failure margin compares the final total with that DC. | [Core resolution](../mechanics/CORE_RESOLUTION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Attack roll | Meets AC to hit. Natural 1 misses; natural 20 hits and critically doubles damage dice. Flat modifiers are unchanged. | [Core resolution](../mechanics/CORE_RESOLUTION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Task, skill or tool check | Uses the task procedure and its four outcome degrees. If the task provides only success/failure effects, use those effects without inventing extra critical consequences. | [Core resolution](../mechanics/CORE_RESOLUTION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Saving throw | Meets DC to succeed. Ordinary saves have no generic natural-1/20 effect; use any explicitly printed failure margins. Death saves have their own procedure. | [Core resolution](../mechanics/CORE_RESOLUTION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Opposed check | Compares final totals. An exact tie preserves the existing state. | [Core resolution](../mechanics/CORE_RESOLUTION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Advantage / disadvantage | Roll two d20s and use the higher/lower. Multiple sources do not stack; any advantage and disadvantage cancel. | [Core resolution](../mechanics/CORE_RESOLUTION_PLAYTEST.md) |

## Time and actions

| Term | Meaning | Full rule |
|---|---|---|
| Round | One numbered pass through initiative, representing approximately six seconds. Shared per-round limits use that numbered round. | [Combat procedure](../mechanics/COMBAT_ACTION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Tile | Five feet on the map. Apply the combat guide's actual geometry, terrain and movement-cost procedures. | [Core resolution](../mechanics/CORE_RESOLUTION_PLAYTEST.md), [combat movement](../mechanics/COMBAT_ACTION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Regular turn | A creature's normal initiative slot, including when it cannot act. An extra Action and an extra turn have their own limits. | [Combat procedure](../mechanics/COMBAT_ACTION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Action | The normal turn's main action allowance. A technique saying “make one weapon attack” uses its printed activation and attacks. | [Combat procedure](../mechanics/COMBAT_ACTION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Bonus Action | An allowance with uses granted by actual rules. Check both an available use and the card's cost. | [Combat procedure](../mechanics/COMBAT_ACTION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Reaction | A response to a legal trigger. The ordinary resource refreshes at the start of your regular turn; specific extra grants retain their own limits. | [Combat procedure](../mechanics/COMBAT_ACTION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Extra full Action | The shared limit is one per creature per numbered round. It cannot grant another extra Action, Bonus Action or Reaction. | [Combat procedure](../mechanics/COMBAT_ACTION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Recharge | A card's countdown to reuse. In combat, tick at regular own-turn starts; extra Actions/turns do not advance it. Outside combat, use its elapsed-time procedure. | [Shared class timing](../mechanics/CLASS_PLAYTEST_RULES.md) |
| Encounter | A genuine scene of uncertain opposition established by the Director. Resource resets require their actual conditions. | [Shared class timing](../mechanics/CLASS_PLAYTEST_RULES.md) |
| Concentration | A shared slot for effects that expressly require it, with only explicit exceptions. A duration or maintenance cost alone does not create a concentration requirement. | [Shared class rules](../mechanics/CLASS_PLAYTEST_RULES.md) |
| Beneficial rest | A completed rest satisfying its time, activity, availability and campaign requirements. Read each resource's actual recovery rule. | [Rest limits and Strain](../mechanics/STRAIN_PLAYTEST.md) |

## Magic and resources

| Term | Meaning | Full rule |
|---|---|---|
| RP | Resonance Points used by eligible casting classes and their actual features. Access, preparation and costs all remain required. | [Resonance reference](RESONANCE_CHEATSHEET.md) |
| RC | Oracle Revelation Charges, governed by the Oracle engine and its own costs, capacity and recovery. | [Revelation reference](RESONANCE_CHEATSHEET.md) |
| Ordinary spell rank | The rank of a spell for the chosen casting class. Its class-level gate, school and repertoire determine access. | [Spell integration](../mechanics/SPELL_INTEGRATION_RULES.md) |
| Dissonance | The current casting-strain amount used by the active ordinary-magic configuration. Check its pre-cast thresholds and actual class exceptions. | [Resonance reference](RESONANCE_CHEATSHEET.md) |
| Refrain | The remembered ordinary-spell repetition counter. Changing spells clears that counter while accumulated Dissonance follows its own rules. | [Resonance reference](RESONANCE_CHEATSHEET.md) |
| Class resource or gauge | A named pool such as Crescendo, Blood Gauge or a class store. Use that engine's grants, caps, costs and recovery. | [Class library](../mechanics/CLASS_PLAYTEST_INDEX.md) |

An ordinary spell, class feature, ancestry grant and crafted effect can share a name while keeping separate acquisition, costs and effects. Record the version actually used.

## Health and named states

| Term | Meaning | Full rule |
|---|---|---|
| HP | Current and maximum Hit Points are separate values. Living at zero HP and being Dead are different states with different procedures. | [Core resolution](../mechanics/CORE_RESOLUTION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Temporary HP | Absorbs damage before normal HP. It does not stack or restore ordinary HP; use the applicable temporary-HP rule. | [Core resolution](../mechanics/CORE_RESOLUTION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Hit Dice | Class-sized recovery dice tracked as available or spent. Their size, spending and recovery follow the character's actual class record. | [Character foundation](../mechanics/CHARACTER_CREATION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Wounds and Wounded | The lasting Wound count and its consequences. The Wounded condition refers to that same count. Ordinary HP healing does not automatically remove a Wound. | [Core resolution](../mechanics/CORE_RESOLUTION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Exhaustion | The six-level Core condition track, with its own consequences and recovery. | [Condition recovery](../mechanics/CONDITION_RECOVERY_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Warrior serum Strain | The Warrior's 0–3 serum/mutation track. Follow its actual gains, locks and recovery. | [Strain namespaces](../mechanics/STRAIN_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Expedition Strain | The 0–6 campaign track used by its specified survival and recovery sources, including Quickglass's crash. | [Strain namespaces](../mechanics/STRAIN_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Condition or named rider | An effect with a particular source, duration and ending. Core conditions and class-specific same-name riders retain their exact identities. | [Condition index](../mechanics/CONDITIONS_PLAYTEST.md), [source-specific control](../mechanics/CONTROL_STATES_PLAYTEST.md) |

For example, Core Staggered and the Monk/Warrior Staggered riders have different effects. Named readers and cures use their [reviewed source bindings](../mechanics/CONTROL_SOURCE_BINDINGS.md). A familiar word alone does not combine their penalties.

## Equipment, crafting and communication

| Term | Meaning | Full rule |
|---|---|---|
| Equipment grade | The equipment's grade and the specific statistics it changes. Apply its actual equipment procedure. | [Equipment](../mechanics/EQUIPMENT_ACQUISITION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Crafting quality | The crafting outcome and its permitted yield or improvement choice. It is separate from equipment grade and artifact market condition. | [Crafting framework](../mechanics/CRAFTING_HARVEST_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Trade level | Training progress in one trade, from 1–20. It is separate from character/class level and is not automatically added to the crafting check. | [Crafting framework](../mechanics/CRAFTING_HARVEST_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Batch | One recipe order and its stated output quantity. Source component quantities and costs are for that batch. | [Crafting framework](../mechanics/CRAFTING_HARVEST_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Tier or Gate requirement | Read the source's actual field and prerequisite. Material tiers, recipe access and spell ranks do not supply automatic conversions or extra permissions. | [Crafting framework](../mechanics/CRAFTING_HARVEST_PLAYTEST.md), [spell integration](../mechanics/SPELL_INTEGRATION_RULES.md) |
| Language modes | Speaking, understanding, reading and writing are recorded modes. A narrow grant keeps its actual scope. | [Language chapter](../mechanics/LANGUAGE_COMMUNICATION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Shared language | Use the actual communication procedure and modes required by an effect. A meaning-transfer ability does not automatically satisfy every shared-language requirement. | [Language chapter](../mechanics/LANGUAGE_COMMUNICATION_PLAYTEST.md) |
| Source of a grant | The actual ancestry, background, class, spell, feat or item that grants a capability. Keep its choices and restrictions with the character record. | [Character foundation](../mechanics/CHARACTER_CREATION_PLAYTEST.md), [language worksheet draft](CHARACTER_LANGUAGE_WORKSHEET.md) |
