## Concrete resolutions

### EPI-01
Resolved as an elective philosophical home affiliation using the four current primary-realm choices. The choice grants no literal origin, travel or resistance. A beneficial Long Rest away evaluates one preceding 24-hour conduct window. Aligned conduct grants one **Epilogue Inspiration d6** (cap 2); knowingly contrary conduct grants one bounded **Disquiet d6**; neutral/no relevant conduct grants neither.

No undefined hero-point/equivalent-resource engine is imported.

### EPI-02
Preserved all seven named source motif benefits while retaining the unresolved **nine-verses/seven-mechanical-benefits** accounting gap.

Motifs use finite scores, 1-hour learning, 1-minute live performance, 30-foot hearing, and one shared Expedition activation per qualifying Long Rest with a 24-hour refresh floor. Courage restores Epilogue Inspiration only. Balance is restricted to real prepared-loadout systems. Law, Sorrow, Pattern and Contradiction now have exact application timing. Transition is deliberately narrowed to a **25% active-work reduction** on a finite eligible project list instead of unrestricted source halving; this is a NEW mechanical adaptation required to preserve established rest/travel/observation/Opening clocks.

### EPI-03
Defined personal-vow states, fictional-only confession privacy, fulfillment, 10-minute revision/retirement and 1-hour mask rebind. Personal vows are explicitly separate from Paladin Sacred Oaths, Pacts, contracts and mind control.

### EPI-04
Completed a 17-class mask table, including the hybrid key-ability seams. Preserved the three explicit source materials and supplied 14 NEW class-significant reflective materials.

Mask shaping is a separate 8-active-hour personal ritual, not a new trade recipe. It uses a fixed 0–3 secret ledger and `DC 10 + secrets`, current four-degree check procedure collapsed to Living/Cracked, no critical quality tier, one wearer/vow identity, one operative mask, exact rest-based reveal charge, 20-foot reveal, `CHA save vs 8 + PB + fixed-key modifier`, current Stunned semantics, and explicit safe/ordinary/forced removal rules. No unsafe-removal curse or identity-loss consequence was invented.

### EPI-05
Preserved all seven virtue/class pairings, including Paladin and Mirrorsage appearing twice.

Conflict Die is once/session **d12** on ability checks only, declared before the roll. Opposition is an agreed fictional tension with one actual present-class strength, not a missing class-row test. Successful final outcome gives one level of **current core Exhaustion** after the check resolves. Expedition Strain and Warrior Strain remain separate. Exhaustion immunity makes this priced benefit unavailable rather than erasing its success cost.

“Next dawn” uses a recorded 24-hour administrative campaign-day boundary and is expressly not a new planar-time rule.
