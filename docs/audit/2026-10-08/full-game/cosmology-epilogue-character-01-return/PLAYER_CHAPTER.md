# Epilogue Character Module — Player Rules

**Status:** NEW provisional authorship. This is a playable candidate built from preserved epilogue source directions and current reviewed mechanics. It does not select the campaign epoch, reveal Director-only cosmology, approve EPI-08–17, or replace current class engines, rest rules, KT/PT/Hunt rules, conditions, death, spells, or crafting catalogs.

## 1. Home Affiliation, Inspiration and Disquiet

Choose one philosophical **home affiliation** when this module is adopted:

| Affiliation | Principle |
|---|---|
| Bastion of Flame | Disciplined passion tempered before it becomes destructive. |
| Veiled Expanse | Privacy and secrecy yield when honest motion and engagement are necessary. |
| Radiant Currents | Law responds to perspective and remains fluid enough to meet reality. |
| Boundless Void | Questions puncture complacency; uncertainty should be examined rather than hidden. |

This choice is **not** your ancestry or literal birthplace and grants no planar resistance, language, travel, access, or automatic knowledge.

When you complete a beneficial Long Rest **away** from your chosen affiliation, the previous 24 elapsed hours are evaluated. If a concrete choice materially aligned with the principle, gain **1 Epilogue Inspiration d6**, up to 2. If you knowingly acted contrary to it after fair notice, gain **Disquiet**. If no relevant choice occurred, gain neither.

### Epilogue Inspiration
- Capacity: **2d6**.
- Before rolling one ability check, spend one die and add it to the result.
- It does not apply to attack rolls or saving throws.
- Only one Epilogue Inspiration die can affect a check.
- It cannot be combined with a Conflict Die on the same check.
- Dice remain until spent; gains above the cap are lost.

### Disquiet
You can have one pending Disquiet d6. On your next ability check within 24 hours, subtract 1d6. Then Disquiet ends. A new Disquiet refreshes the 24-hour expiry instead of stacking. Disquiet is not Corruption, KT/PT, Dissonance, Strain, or Exhaustion.

## 2. Choir Motifs

The surviving source gives seven playable motifs. The historical source also mentions nine verses; this module does **not** invent missing verses or a missing eighth benefit.

To activate a motif:
- Learn that motif by **1 uninterrupted hour** of rehearsal from an authentic score or teacher.
- Be proficient with the instrument used, or with an applicable Performance skill.
- Perform for **1 uninterrupted minute**.
- Listeners must be within **30 feet** and hear the whole performance.
- The Expedition has **one shared Motif Resonance activation** after a beneficial Long Rest, and it cannot refresh more than once in 24 elapsed hours.
- Recordings do not activate motifs.
- Multiple performers do not multiply an effect.

### Courage
Each listener regains **1 spent Epilogue Inspiration d6**, up to the 2-die cap. It restores no class engine or item resource.

### Balance
One willing listener may perform one legal prepared-loadout substitution:
- **Druid:** one prepared spell for another legal druid spell.
- **Wizard:** one prepared ordinary matrix for another legal spell already in that Wizard's spellbook.
- **Warrior:** one equipped mutation for another owned/unlocked mutation.

No RP, spell use, serum, cooldown, charge, or expended resource is restored. If nobody has an eligible loadout, the shared motif allowance is not spent.

### Law
Choose one revealed hostile creature within 30 feet that heard the entire performance. It makes a Wisdom save against **8 + performer PB + performer CHA modifier**. On failure, until the start of the performer's next regular turn it cannot voluntarily make attacks or hostile targeted abilities against creatures that heard the motif. It may speak, move, defend, Dodge, Disengage, refuse terms, or take nonhostile actions. If a protected listener directly damages it, the effect ends immediately before that damage resolves. Law does not force truth, agreement, surrender, or consent.

### Sorrow
One listener who failed a Wisdom saving throw during the previous round may reroll that same save once if its consequence has not already become irreversible or fully committed. The reroll replaces the failed result even if worse. One save can benefit only once.

### Pattern
Each listener gains one pending benefit for 24 hours: advantage on the next ability check whose primary purpose is study, research, deciphering, analysis, or recall. It is consumed when used and does not stack.

### Contradiction
Each listener gains one pending benefit for 24 hours: advantage on the next Wisdom (Insight) check whose declared purpose is discovering a creature's hidden motive or internal inconsistency. This is not truth detection.

### Transition
Choose one eligible project. For up to four participating workers who heard the motif, reduce the project's **remaining active work by 25%**, rounded up to the nearest 10 minutes, minimum 10 minutes.

Eligible examples: mask shaping, a 4-hour research finding/synthesis, the 8-hour decipher project, ordinary crafting active work, or a fixed ritual explicitly measured in active work.

Transition does **not** shorten rests, travel, natural/seasonal waits, Opening/Horn clocks, spell/feature durations, Knowledge Transfer duration, observation windows, training/level requirements, or tasks lacking materials/access/qualification.

## 3. Your Unfinished Vow

Record one unfinished personal vow as a single sentence describing an ideal action not yet fulfilled.

This vow:
- is fictional character material;
- is not a Paladin Sacred Oath, Pact, legal contract, alignment rule, or mind control;
- never requires a player to disclose private real-world facts.

A mask confession can be spoken, signed, or written if an ally or Sentinel actually understands it.

### Fulfill, revise, retire
When the ideal action is truly completed in play, mark the vow **fulfilled**. This grants no automatic XP or resource refresh.

Outside an encounter, you may spend **10 uninterrupted minutes** to revise or retire your vow and communicate that choice to an understanding ally or Sentinel.

A mask tied to a fulfilled or retired vow becomes dormant. Rebinding that same mask to a new vow takes **1 uninterrupted hour**. Its Living/Cracked quality, wearer identity, key ability, and reveal-charge state do not reset.

## 4. Living and Cracked Masks

Mask-making is a separate personal vow ritual, not one of the reviewed trade recipes.

### The mask project
1. Communicate the active vow to one willing ally or Sentinel who understands it.
2. Commit one class-reflective blank.
3. Privately record **0–3 fictional vow secrets** still withheld from that confession.
4. Spend **8 active hours** shaping the mask.
5. Make one check using the fixed class key ability against **DC 10 + secret count**.

The 8 hours may be paused after completed 1-hour blocks. An interrupted partial block is lost. If the project remains incomplete for 7 elapsed days, the blank spoils and the project ends.

The final check uses the current four-degree fixed-DC procedure, but the result collapses to:
- Critical Success or Success → **Living Mask**
- Failure or Critical Failure → **Cracked Mask**

Critical results do not create better masks, more charges, or stronger DCs.

### Class mask table

| Class | Mask key | Reflective material |
|---|---:|---|
| Bard | CHA | silvered resonant glass scored with the character's own performance marks |
| Cleric | WIS | polished devotional mirror-metal bearing the cleric's chosen sacred symbol |
| Crimson Agent | INT | polished crimson hematite-glass with a fine capillary pattern |
| Dragoon | STR | mirror-polished lance-steel scale |
| Druid | WIS | reflective resin cast over shed bark or stone from a personally tended natural place |
| Mage | INT | smoked prism-glass that catches the mage's chosen elemental hue |
| Mirrorsage | INT | true mirror-silver backed by a hand-marked reflection grid |
| Monk | DEX | water-polished mirrorstone thin enough to wear as a face plate |
| Oracle | CHA | polished scrying glass marked with the Oracle's Mystery sign |
| Paladin | CHA | mirror-polished oathsteel carrying a nonbinding personal-vow inscription |
| Ranger | WIS | still water frozen beneath moonlight |
| Shaman | WIS | mica-backed spirit glass framed in polished bone or wood |
| Soldier | CON | mirror-polished shield steel cut from a retired defensive plate |
| Thief | DEX | polished shards of black glass |
| Tinker | INT | polished brass-and-glass lens plate with visible maker marks |
| Warrior | STR/DEX fixed | mirror-finished alchemical steel blank |
| Wizard | INT | liquid silver condensed from Arcane Crucible fumes |

A multiclass character chooses one actual class at project start. That class fixes the mask row for that vow-mask identity. Warrior chooses Strength or Dexterity at project start and cannot switch it later.

### Wearing a mask
Only the creator/wearer gains the mask's mechanics. One operative mask per wearer. Loaning, stealing, copying, or swapping physical masks cannot transfer function or refresh charges. Masks use no generic attunement slot.

If a form cannot physically wear/reveal the mask or the equipment melds, all mask mechanics are suppressed until it is wearable again.

While an operative Living or Cracked mask is worn:
- gain advantage on saving throws explicitly against fear effects and Null lullabies;
- a Cracked Mask also gives disadvantage on a social ability check involving deception when that deception would materially impede the active vow.

### Reveal
You have one reveal charge. It refreshes when you complete a beneficial Short Rest or beneficial Long Rest.

- **Action**
- One creature within **20 feet**
- You must perceive it; it must be able to perceive the revealed mask; line of effect required.
- Save DC = **8 + PB + current modifier of the fixed mask key ability**
- Target makes a **Charisma saving throw**.
- Failure: **Stunned until the end of its next regular turn**.
- Outside initiative: Stunned for 6 seconds.
- Success: no effect.
- Stun immunity and all current source-target hard-denial rules still apply.
- The charge is spent after a legal target is declared, regardless of success.

### Removing a mask
- On your turn: Free Action while stating one truthful current emotional-state statement.
- Outside your turn: Reaction, if available, while making that truthful statement.
- If speech is impossible but signing/writing is possible and understood: Action.
- You may always remove it as an ordinary Action without the truth procedure. There is **no invented curse, death, or identity-loss punishment**.
- Forced removal simply suppresses effects. It does not reset the charge or quality.

## 5. Virtues and the Conflict Die

The source's virtue/class pairings are:

| Virtue | Naturally resonant classes |
|---|---|
| Courage | Bard, Warrior, Paladin |
| Balance | Druid, Ranger, Dragoon |
| Law | Cleric, Soldier, Mage |
| Sorrow | Oracle, Shaman |
| Pattern | Wizard, Tinker, Mirrorsage |
| Contradiction | Thief, Crimson Agent |
| Transition | Monk, Mirrorsage, Paladin |

These pairings are descriptive. They do not restrict your class or define opposition automatically.

At the start of a session, if you want access to a Conflict Die:
1. Choose one current class you possess.
2. Name one real mechanical strength of that class.
3. Choose a virtue.
4. State a meaningful fictional tension between that virtue and the named strength.
5. You and the Director agree the tension is meaningful.

This does not alter alignment, belief, personality, loyalty, or Paladin Oath status.

### Conflict Die
Once per session, before rolling an ability check, you may spend your Conflict Die and roll **1d12** with the d20, adding the d12 to the check total.

- Ability checks only, including an opposed check if its normal procedure calls it an ability check.
- Not attacks or saving throws.
- Cannot combine with Epilogue Inspiration on the same check.
- For fixed DCs, calculate degree from the total, then apply the normal natural-20/natural-1 shift from the d20.
- On Failure/Critical Failure, the use is spent but you gain no Exhaustion.
- On Success/Critical Success, resolve the successful check, then gain **1 level of current core Exhaustion**.
- This does not also add Expedition Strain or Warrior Strain.
- A character immune to Exhaustion cannot declare a Conflict Die.

## 6. Archon Petitions

A **Cleric**, **Monk**, or character with an already established spiritual mentor record may petition.

A petition requires:
1. **10 minutes** consulting a qualified local Witness who records motive and eligibility.
2. **1 uninterrupted hour** of meditation in an actually quiet place.
3. **10 minutes** completing a symbolic act tied to the chosen virtue.
4. Roll **1d12**.

Once the d12 is rolled, that character cannot complete another petition for **7 elapsed days**. Interrupted work before the roll loses its time but does not start the cooldown.

### Response 1–4: Guidance
Gain advantage for **24 elapsed hours** on qualifying checks connected to the petition virtue. The Director confirms whether a declared check fits before the roll. Only one Guidance boon can be active at once.

### Response 5–8: Blessing
Gain the fixed temporary Base passive for the petition virtue until next module dawn:

| Virtue | Temporary feature |
|---|---|
| Courage | `warrior.base.combat-instinct` — Combat Instinct |
| Balance | `ranger.base.natural-explorer` — Natural Explorer |
| Law | `soldier.base.tactical-awareness` — Tactical Awareness |
| Sorrow | `shaman.base.alchemical-knowledge` — Alchemical Knowledge |
| Pattern | `wizard.base.scholarly-mind` — Scholarly Mind |
| Contradiction | `thief.base.trap-intuition` — Trap Intuition |
| Transition | `monk.base.unarmored-speed` — Unarmored Speed |

The grant bypasses only normal acquisition. It grants no class level, class engine, resource pool, spellcasting, proficiency, advancement credential, or equipment.

If you already have the exact feature, there is no additional mechanical effect and no reroll.

### Response 9–11: Test
A Sentinel projection presents the virtue's finite challenge. Success sets your existing Epilogue Inspiration pool to **2 dice**; it does not create a separate pool.

Failure creates **VowReviewPending**. Within 24 hours choose:
- revise your vow normally; or
- reaffirm it unchanged after 10 minutes of reflection and gain one Disquiet.

This never forces alignment, personality, belief, or a Paladin Oath change.

### Response 12: Revelation
Gain one **Concord Step Sigil**, a NEW provisional travel interface, not a spell.

At grant, select one Director-confirmed available destination among the module's fixed Concord thresholds. Before the next session begins, you may activate it once from an authorized threshold:
- 10 uninterrupted minutes;
- petitioner plus up to five willing creatures within 10 feet;
- ordinary carried gear only;
- no vehicles or unwilling creatures.

Outbound moves the group only to the marked arrival alcove and bypasses no barriers beyond it.

For 8 hours after outbound, the same travelers or a subset may spend 10 uninterrupted minutes at the destination alcove to return to the origin threshold. This return is part of the one authorization, not a recharge.

## 7. Sentinel Information Encounters

A Sentinel is a neutral observer, not an omniscient narrator.

Each Sentinel encounter has a unique encounter ID and can provide at most one information boon. Ask one concrete in-fiction question relevant to a real problem. If the Sentinel does not know, it says so rather than inventing an answer. You may get one immediate narrowing clarification on the same subject; then the encounter is spent.

Possible boons:
- a puzzle solution;
- a finite local map fragment;
- a contingent future vision.

A Sentinel boon cannot by inference grant an Equation fragment, resolve hidden identities, create a floor shortcut, guarantee the future, rewrite cosmology, grant a permanent class feature, or authorize travel.

Information learned by your Expedition does not automatically become ordinary public knowledge.
