## TA-D05. Define finite garment task domains and cross-trade arbitration

**NEW cross-trade extension:** all Tailoring garment and Bag task modifiers join the existing **`equipment.leather-task-enhancement`** arbitration family. Retain that existing identifier for compatibility.

For one actual check, apply the **strongest qualifying Leatherworking or Tailoring task modifier once**. This does not expand Leatherworking’s task scopes or collapse unrelated reviewed class, tool, or spell benefits into this family.

Declare the task, actor, ability, applicable proficiency, relevant garment contribution, and equipment state before resolving the check. Proficiency contributes once; applicable expertise replaces its normal contribution.

### Exhaustive task definitions

**Presentation.** The wearer’s CHA (Persuasion), CHA (Deception), or CHA (Performance) check where the actual visible garment materially supports the declared appearance, costume, ceremony, role portrayal, or attire-based presentation. An ordinary argument, bargain, threat, lie, or song does not qualify merely because the speaker is dressed.

This also permits a genuinely required DEX disguise-kit check by the wearer to arrange their **own already-worn** garment as part of a physical disguise. It supplies neither a disguise kit nor its proficiency, and is not permission to manufacture or enchant clothing.

**Visual concealment.** The wearer’s DEX (Stealth) check where the garment’s real coverage helps conceal the relevant part of their outline or appearance under otherwise legal hiding conditions. It supplies no cover, obscurity, invisibility, or permission to hide in plain view.

**Quiet clothing movement.** The wearer’s DEX (Stealth) check where controlling that garment’s rustle, loose panels, or snagging materially contributes to moving unnoticed. It does not remove armor noise, armor disadvantage, footsteps, speech, or other existing detection factors.

**Discreet handwork.** A genuinely required DEX (Sleight of Hand) check to palm or conceal an actual compatible small object using the wearer’s gloved hands. This grants no lockpicking bonus, remote manipulation, additional hand, or automatic theft.

**Weather arrangement.** A genuinely required WIS (Survival) check to arrange the wearer’s own clothing coverage, or DEX check to maintain its ordinary ties, fastenings, or position against ambient wind, rain, spray, blowing grit, heat, or cold. The particular garment must physically contribute through the area or fittings it actually covers. This is not a general Survival, navigation, shelter-construction, crafting, or repair bonus.

**Exposure ability check.** An actual **CON ability check, not a saving throw**, to endure ordinary ambient exposure where the governing procedure permits assistance from suitable clothing and this garment’s real coverage contributes. It creates no exposure procedure and no permission to substitute a check for a required save.

## TA-D06. Define Bag concealment and organization for the actual operator

A Tailoring Bag’s task bonus is **not Leatherworking’s secure/retrieve bonus under another name**.

**NEW finite scope:**

**Conceal contents:** DEX, with Sleight of Hand proficiency only when the existing task qualifies, to physically arrange actual noncreature contents within the accessible Bag so that an ordinary inspection does not readily reveal them. Use real folds, lining, and other actual cargo; no magical compartment is created.

**Organize contents:** an INT check for genuinely difficult sorting or packing arrangement of accessible, already-known cargo, or DEX for genuinely difficult physical placement and separation of that cargo. The actor must actually perform the organization, not merely discuss it remotely.

Success cannot override capacity, opening, shape, hazardous-material separation, or other physical requirements. It grants no instant retrieval, extra interaction, identification, inventory omniscience, preservation, or new cargo unit.

The bonus belongs to the **actual operator**, including an ally or thief, while using the complete functional unsuppressed accessible Bag. It can operate on a work surface without an active relief position. Melding blocks access and the benefit.

Nested stowage disables relief; it does not by itself forbid every operator check on an inner Bag whose fittings are genuinely accessible. Closed outer storage or inaccessible melded gear cannot be ignored.

The bonus does not improve the operator’s unrelated verbal deception, concealment of their whole body, an observer’s Perception or Investigation, a lock’s DC, or lockpicking. It grants no ownership recognition.

A prior resolved arrangement follows the governing concealment/detection procedure; this item adds no new stored bonus, duration, automatic contest, or repeated inspection roll. Current checks re-evaluate current eligibility, and physically disturbing the arrangement has its ordinary consequences.
