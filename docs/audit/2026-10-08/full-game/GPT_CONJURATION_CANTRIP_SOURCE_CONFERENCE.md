1. **Dust Gust** — **Action; 5 ft; V/S; instantaneous.** Choose one mode:
   - **Object:** one unattended, unsecured, Small-or-smaller object weighing ≤10 lb; push it up to 5 ft directly away from you.
   - **Dust/Sand:** up to 10 lb of existing loose nonmagical sand or dust contained within one 5-ft square; scatter that material up to 5 ft away from you.
   
   Stop before solid terrain/illegal occupancy. Worn, held, attached, restrained, locked, packed, structural, or otherwise secured material is ineligible; ordinary floor/table support alone is not security. Creates no sand/dust, excavation, blindness, damage, disarm, collision damage, or resource. Ordinary physical consequences such as exposed surface or Falling may follow, but the spell does not automatically erase a hazard/track merely because some dust moved. **NEW:** 10-lb ceiling, 5-ft displacement, two exact modes.

2. **Lodestone Pull** — **Action; 5 ft; V/S/F; instantaneous; no concentration.** Target one unattended, unsecured, discrete Small-or-smaller object ≤10 lb whose primary physical material is metal. Pull it up to 5 ft directly toward you, stopping before your occupied space, solid terrain, or illegal occupancy. Worn/held/attached/locked/anchored objects are ineligible; ordinary support is not. No disarm, inventory theft, levitation, repeated pull, or sustained tether. **CONFLICT RULING:** singular 5-ft pull controls; mismatched 1-minute concentration metadata supplies no repeatable effect and is not operational.

3. **Minor Elemental Ward** — **Action; 30 ft; one willing creature including self; V/S/F; concentration up to 1 minute.** Target gains **+1 to its next saving throw against an effect whose resolution can deal acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder damage**. Consume the benefit on that qualifying save regardless of success/failure or whether a stronger flat save bonus prevents the +1 from numerically applying. It creates no save where none exists and grants no damage/status immunity. One pending Ward per target across all casters; replacement ends the old application and releases only its old concentration dependency. **Elemental list is the five-type list**, not the eight-type Protection from Energy list.

4. **Flicker Flame** — **Action; 30 ft; V/S; 10 minutes; no concentration.** Create one harmless floating flame at a fixed unoccupied point you perceive within range. It sheds **dim light in a 10-ft radius** and may dance cosmetically within about 1 ft of that point but has no controlled tactical movement. One Flicker Flame per caster; recasting ends the prior one. It produces no heat, damage, ignition, fuel, physical object, sunlight trait, or tracking signal. Overlapping light follows ordinary illumination; it does not automatically dispel magical darkness, reveal Invisible creatures, or end Hidden independent of the normal sight rules. **NEW:** fixed point, 10-ft dim radius, one-light cap.

5. **Stone Skin Sliver** — **Action; 30 ft; one willing creature including self; V/S; concentration; 1 round.** Target gains **+1 AC until your first future regular-start marker**; outside initiative use +6 seconds and preserve that deadline on initiative entry. This is a magical flat AC bonus only: no stone body, armor, weight, resistance, hardness, or anatomy change. Strongest same-stat flat bonus applies. Separate casters may maintain independent nonstacking records with their own endpoints; no all-caster replacement rule is invented. **Concentration is retained from both effect summaries despite the Core duration field omitting the word.**

6. **Resonance Kindle** — **Action; Self; V/S; instantaneous.** On a legal cast, reserve the character’s single Kindle use **when the cast begins**. If Counterspelled, the use remains spent and no RP is recovered. If already at maximum when it resolves, the use is still spent and recovery is 0. It does not clear Dissonance/Refrain, restore another resource, target an ally, or create resource-copy/proxy loops.

The operational identity restores RP only from casting **Resonance Kindle itself**. Historical 30-ft range is preserved as alias evidence but grants no remote recipient.

7. **Pebble Swarm** — **Action; 5-ft cone; V/S; instantaneous.** Choose a 5-ft cone originating at your space; on a standard 5-ft grid this is **one adjacent 5-ft square** in the chosen direction. No ability modifier, cantrip scaling, attack roll, critical hit, automatic object damage, ammunition, or persistent stone stock. Conjured pebbles vanish after resolution. **NEW:** DEX-negates resolution and bounded 5-ft template.

8. **Elemental Touch** — **Action; Touch; V/S; instantaneous damage plus temporary cosmetic mark.** Target one creature, or one unattended/willingly presented discrete object within normal touch reach. Against an unwilling creature make a **melee spell attack**; willing creatures/unattended objects require no attack roll. Choose **acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder**. On a successful touch deal **1 flat damage** of that type and leave a visible harmless elemental mark for **1 minute, no concentration**.
   
   Flat 1 damage is not doubled by a critical hit; ordinary resistance/immunity applies. The mark appears on a successful touch even if damage becomes 0 and is **not** the generic Marked condition, a beacon, tracker, DoT, ignition, corrosion engine, parasite detector, or condition. For objects, apply damage only through an existing native damage track; invent no HP/Integrity conversion. One mark from the same caster on the same target replaces their prior Elemental Touch mark.

9. **Mist Veil** — **Action; 5 ft; V/S; concentration up to 1 minute.** Create one fixed **5-ft cube of magical mist** in legal space within range. The cube is **heavily obscured for ordinary vision**. It does not block line of effect, erase known location, automatically make occupants Hidden, or itself grant extra unseen modifiers beyond the current sight/Hide rules. One Mist Veil per caster; recasting replaces the old one. It creates no recoverable water/matter and no physical support. Ordinary wind/heat does not automatically end it because no source supplies such dispersal; an effect explicitly able to disperse magical mist may do so. **CONFLICT RULING:** finite concentration/1 minute supplies the required ongoing cloud procedure instead of unusable Instantaneous metadata.

10. **Phantom Key** — **Action; 30 ft; V/S; 1 minute; no concentration.** Choose one perceived **nonmagical key-operated mechanical lock** within range. Conjure one temporary key for that exact lock into your free hand. The key may be handed to another creature and, when physically inserted/used normally, **operates that lock as its correct mundane key without a lockpicking check** for the duration. One Phantom Key per caster; recasting ends the prior key.
   
   It does not remotely open anything, disable traps, reveal trap presence, bypass a jam/barricade, operate combination/keyless locks, or bypass magical protections such as Arcane Lock. If a mundane physical lock is also magically warded, Phantom Key can satisfy only the mundane mechanism; the magical ward remains. It cannot be sold, harvested for material, persist as a permanent key, or automatically provide a durable blueprint. Ordinary observers may draw normal conclusions from seeing the real lock/key operate. This deliberately retains a stronger “works” niche than Thief Lockpick Mastery; **balance remains open rather than rewriting the source into a check bonus.**
