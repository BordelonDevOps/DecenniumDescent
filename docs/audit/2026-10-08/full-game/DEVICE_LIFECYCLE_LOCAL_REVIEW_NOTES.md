# Local checks to reconcile with the device conference

These are unresolved source questions, not approved rules or new abilities.

- Snap Rebuild names a destroyed **device/drone**, not only deployables. It costs a Bonus Action,2RP and one encounter use; original expiry and slot remain. A persistent Anacite Drone needs an explicit representation of its absence of an ordinary deployment deadline. Reusing a released slot must not let reconstruction exceed capacity.
- Reconfiguration permits known **nonhealing** profiles and preserves HP fraction, slot, expiry and spent uses. A destroyed object's0HP must not become functional through reconfiguration. A low positive fraction can floor to0 when moving to a smaller maximum, requiring an explicit viability decision.
- Efficient Recycling occurs **after combat, one minute**, once per short rest; it consumes debris and refunds at most1RP actually spent on deployment. Repair costs and paid modules are not automatically deployment costs. The automatic Drone can have zero refundable deployment cost.
- Volatile Components costs0, occurs once per round, and needs an enemy-destroyed device plus one enemy within5ft. It expressly excludes deliberate sacrifices, copied objects, friendly damage and secondary explosions. It does not expressly consume debris.
- Scrap Salvage is an Action+1RP, once per encounter, using enemy-destroyed debris. Its Scrapling costs a deployable slot and cannot spawn a replacement. Scrap Reaper uses a Reaction+2RP and one short-rest use, then needs a later Bonus Action detonation command; its printed cost references the destroyed device/debris but its effect omits an explicit slot sentence. Resolve that omission from the shared engine.
- Source-qualified damage flags survive into destruction consequences. The earlier Emergency Protocols transfer explicitly prohibits retaliation; object death must not bypass that by relabeling Volatile Components as a destruction event. Magnetic Wall requires a new transfer binding and its own exact flag decision.
- Distinguish an intentional direct HP sacrifice from choosing to intercept genuine enemy damage. If a device dies while intercepting, retain the original attacker/packet provenance and applicable transfer flags when evaluating destruction or salvage.
- Shaman's four named elemental summons explicitly require concentration and last3 source turns. Elemental Harmony adds Shaman level to maximum/current HP **on creation** and one turn, capped4. Elemental Chorus is one shared4L-HP entity represented by four spirits, not four separate creature stat blocks or attacks.
- Totem Casting itself expressly says replacement ends a Totem without refund. This supports capacity replacement as an end event; it does not establish Echo-parent coupling or a destruction explosion.
- Cinderflame explodes on destruction, not dismissal. Its area can hit up to3 enemies and has its own save; the explosion creates no further death/resource triggers. This is distinct from Totem destruction.
- The persistent Drone's long-rest rebuild and timed deployment reconstruction need separate identity/epoch rules. Preserve anti-salvage and spent-use history through in-use repair and Snap Rebuild; do not assume that all source-defined new long-rest configurations either erase or preserve every lifetime entitlement without discussion.
