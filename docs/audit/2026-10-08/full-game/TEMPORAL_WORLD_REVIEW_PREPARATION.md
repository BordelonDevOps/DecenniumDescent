Broader Echo/copy/steal effects need a separate exact inventory. Their names do not automatically grant full Time Loop restoration, independent actions, RP refunds, monster profiles or copied triggered chains. Existing source-qualified numeric healing-copy limits remain in force until expressly reconciled.

The transformation candidate now separates immutable physical origin, current anatomy and source record. The temporal conference must expressly settle how a valid snapshot restores each without manufacturing hidden forms or dropping conserved state. Include:

- A permanent True Polymorph object creature whose original sponsor remains bound through another caster's recast or antimagic suppression.
- A creature-origin person temporarily expressed as an object, keeping their HP/Wounds/soul/agency but losing ordinary creature-target healing eligibility while object anatomy is expressed.

- Atomically restored original meld sets versus genuinely external/dropped/lost gear; conserved inventory cannot be re-created by replaying a closed transformation record.
- World-unique form/cast/grant IDs, per-target damage-repeat save round histories and canonical command budgets. A rewind is not an ordinary fresh cast or summon and must not accidentally use normal entry hooks to refill resources.

These are discussion dependencies, not a claim that Time Loop currently restores or excludes any newly named field. The exact final spell closure and subsequent soul-feat decisions must be read before adopting a temporal interpretation.
