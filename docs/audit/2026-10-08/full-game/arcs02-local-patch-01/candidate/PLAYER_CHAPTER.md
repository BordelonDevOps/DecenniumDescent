# EPI-15 — Rekindling Stars

## Mission
One of the new constellations above post-epilogue Yieldheart **flickers out nightly**. Three installed devices contain matching stray Null fragments that are shaping star energy into weaponized silence. The municipal offer is simple: recover the three serialed slivers, stabilize them at Lantern Court, and do so before the announced Equinox dawn.

At activation the Director announces the exact local Equinox dawn timestamp **E**. The mission begins at **E−72 hours**. This is a local deadline for this adventure, not a recovered universal astronomical date.

### Known sites
1. **North Observatory** — 0.5 mile from Lantern Court. A cracked three-ring lens bears the warning: **OPEN THE VENT BEFORE TURNING THE INNER RING.**
2. **River Bell House** — 0.5 mile from Lantern Court. The visible counterweight notice reads: **SUPPORT 3, RELEASE 1, THEN LIFT 2.**
3. **West Glass Garden** — 0.5 mile from Lantern Court. The cage controls are marked **SHADE / REFLECT / OPEN**, and the gardener's note says: **SHADE RELEASES THE LATCH.**

The North Observatory's 10-foot acoustic zone suppresses sound production and transmission. Audible verbal spell components cannot be produced inside it. It does **not** automatically cancel nonverbal magic or effects already running.

None of these three sites is a Residual Null exposure field. Merely standing near or carrying a recovered fragment does not start an hourly Null exposure save.

## Stabilizing recovered fragments

Fit each actual fragment to its matching socket, one Action per fragment. After all fragments intended for this cycle are installed, close the single shutter once with one Action. Then one worker actively turns the crank for 10 uninterrupted active minutes. The worker must remain able to perform that cranking work; interruption stops the active work and the full 10-minute crank interval must begin again, while already installed fragments remain installed. This is a fixed observation/stabilization interval, not crafting or casting, so Transition does not halve it. No check. At completion every actual fragment installed before the shutter was closed becomes inert sealed salvage at the same completion timestamp. From an empty bench, one fragment requires 612 seconds total (6 fit + 6 shutter + 600 crank), two require 618 seconds, and three require 624 seconds. No fragment can be added through the closed shutter during the running cycle.

Housing accepts 1–3 fragments. One completed crank cycle seals every fragment installed for that cycle. A later recovered fragment is a later addition: open/access the housing as physically required, fit that actual fragment with its own Action, close the shutter once, and perform a new full 10-minute active crank cycle. A prior completed cycle does not seal a later addition retroactively. Installed but unfinished fragments are not sealed at the deadline. Removing an inert sealed fragment is actual 10-minute tool work with the real wrench or other lawful tool access and does not reactivate that fragment or the site mechanism.

A fragment completing stabilization **exactly at E** counts as sealed before the deadline.

## Equinox consequences
- **3 sealed at E:** the constellation returns that night.
- **1–2 sealed at E:** it remains dim for **40 days**, then recovers naturally.
- **0 sealed at E:** it darkens for **one 400-day Aethel civil year**.
- Late recovery can still stop the remaining local machines and prevent further local injuries, but cannot retroactively change the already resolved Equinox result.

A recovered or powered-off site does not restart merely because its fragment was still unsealed at E.

## Payment

Dera Venn separately begins with exactly **6 gp** for spindle scrap and offers **2 gp once** for each of the three original, disconnected, inactive 10-lb spindle serials if the physical body survives. Her purse and the bounty chest are separate funds.

## Complete CF-pattern operating instructions

### CF-1 Isolation

diagram labels I (inlet), E (exhaust), V (vent), C (inspection cap), the pressure gauge and the cell socket. Close I, close E and open V, each an Action. If pressure was above zero, keep V open for sixty elapsed seconds; its calibrated gauge then reaches zero. Only then open C with an Action. A cold shell is not evidence of zero pressure. Closing V before the sixty seconds complete retains pressure and requires a new complete vent interval. There is no pressure refill without a real powered charge. At an already verified zero gauge, the sixty-second wait is unnecessary.

### CF-2 External Cooling Loop

diagram traces supply through port 2, the external return through port 1, then the bypass at port 3. At verified zero pressure, set those three indexed valves in order 2, 1, 3, one Action each. Labels mark the flow direction, not a universal code for every engine. During a sealed powered test, the blue return vane must turn continuously for sixty elapsed seconds. A stationary vane with pressure present identifies blocked or reversed routing, rather than proof that the machine lacks power. A leak instead prevents the pressure gauge holding at 1. These are distinguishable observations.

### CF-3 Pressure Seal

diagram shows the keyed 2-inch seat and the intact ring profile. At verified zero pressure with C open, two active minutes with the actual hand tool remove the old ring, inspect the seat, fit one actual compatible 0.2 lb replacement and tighten the retainer. Keep the old ring as scrap. Close C, close V and insert/activate one actual cold cell, each an Action. A sealed test must hold gauge 1 with the blue return vane moving for sixty elapsed seconds. A split ring cannot be repaired by tightening harder; a damaged seat needs separately sourced parts/work. Never install a ring under pressure.

### Appointments and diagnosis

A claimant with an active term may book one ten-minute instructor-led service appointment per actual 24 hours with an available Veyr. It teaches the three diagrams and services this press, with actual controls, parts and test time included. Reading the complete plates or doing later self-directed work has no artificial daily reset, but needs real access, time, tools, materials and any actual qualifications. Following the posted accessible steps succeeds without a roll. Investigating an unrecognized fault instead takes ten active minutes and INT (Engineering) DC 15; failure spends that time and reveals no false diagnosis. It does not consume a cold cell unless the actor actually starts a powered test. A teaching appointment is instruction, not Transition-eligible crafting; a separate ordinary qualified repair’s active tool-work uses existing work-order rules, and never halves Actions or mandatory elapsed pressure/flow observations.

### Cell activation and stored pressure

Each actual cell activation consumes that one cell’s use immediately, successful or not, supplies at most ten elapsed minutes of drive and can pressurize the sealed chamber only once. Record activatedAt and chargeSpent. Venting or a discharge empties that one charge; closing the vent does not recharge it. Its casing remains. After ten minutes the drive ends; any retained pressure must still be vented before opening. Loading a second cell is a new real activation, not a reset of the first. Activation requires the actual cap closed, vent closed and gauge verified at zero. An attempted activation without those prerequisites fails without consuming or resetting a cell; use ordinary Actions and the visible interlock. An intact seal raises gauge to 1. A cracked seal lets that one charge escape and the gauge reaches 0 by the failed sixty-second test. The running drive cannot refill pressure. A wrong route with an intact seal instead leaves gauge 1 with the return vane stopped. Both faults can coexist; the leaking gauge then reaches 0 and the operator must correct both before a passing test. Venting, discharge or pressure loss sets pressReady=false. Drive time does not recreate gauge1 after that one pressure charge is spent.

### Safe service

With the initial zero gauge, close/verify I and E, open/verify V and open C using their actual Actions; set 2→1→3; replace the cracked seal with two minutes of actual tool work; close C, close V, activate one real cell; observe sixty elapsed seconds. The ten-minute teaching appointment has enough time for these steps and explanation. If time is interrupted or prerequisites fail, record actual completed steps, remaining stock and pressure; incomplete teaching grants no fabricated completion record. Opening C, changing tested routing/valves, removing or changing the fitted seal, or otherwise breaking the tested configuration invalidates any existing pressReady state before service begins.

### Failure

A cracked seal or wrong route fails the sixty-second test: no ready indicator and the platen remains mechanically blocked. The actual activated cell is still spent; no paper or ink is used until a real print. Opening C or trying to replace a seal while gauge>0 releases that one stored charge: all creatures within 5 ft make DEX save DC 15, taking 3d6 force on failure or half on success, with ordinary defenses/life rules. Pressure then becomes 0 and that charge cannot blast again. The engraved warning makes this foreseeable. Destruction releases stored pressure once by the same rule, if any remains; inert wreckage does not repeat it.

### Printing

Printing requires pressReady=true at the moment of the print Action, the same live drive/current pressure charge that passed the current test, an actual blank sheet and at least one ink impression. One Action per sheet prints one selected engraved diagram, consuming one sheet and one ink impression. A three-plate set needs three actual sheets and three Actions. The starting stock supports at most two new complete sets in total. If readiness becomes invalid between print Actions, further printing is blocked until actual safe service, a new real cell activation and a new successful 60-second test restore pressReady; already printed sheets and already spent stock remain as they are. Failed tests do not delete paper.

### Using the learned procedures

The plates teach an actual safe isolation sequence, how to distinguish loss of pressure from failed coolant circulation, and a keyed-seal maintenance process. They can guide ordinary handling of a demonstrated compatible CF-pattern device. For another machine, first establish its real port mapping, pressure limits, materials, actual parts and operating procedure; a shared word such as engine does not establish compatibility. Apply the existing research/repair rules to genuinely new engineering. Nothing grants a free research qualification mark or promotes a pending crafting recipe.

### Current press readiness

NEW scoped local machine completion. Track pressReady as a current state, never as a historical achievement. pressReady=true only while all are simultaneously true: press structure is intact and supported; service cap C closed; vent V closed; inlet/exhaust and the 2→1→3 route remain in the tested correct configuration; functioning fitted seal is still intact; gauge=1 from the same unspent stored pressure charge created by the current actual cell activation; that same cell drive is still live; and a successful uninterrupted current 60-second test has completed after the last readiness-invalidating change.
