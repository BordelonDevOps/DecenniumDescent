### Airlock

#### Geometry

One10x10x12ft chamber, an inner10ft door to the main cabin and an outer10ft door to the gangplank. #### Interlock

Opening one door normally requires the other closed. The inner door additionally requires protected airlock status. Forcing a door open or destroying it defeats that physical interlock and creates its real breach; it does not magically preserve cabin protection.

#### Outside cycle

Open inner, move using actual legal movement into airlock, close inner, open outer, move to the gangplank/deck, then close outer. Actions and movement may share a normal6second interval only when the actor has those actual budgets and sufficient Speed. Main cabin stays protected while inner remains closed. Closing a door does not repair a hull breach or regenerate the cartridge.

#### Return cycle

Open outer, move physically into airlock, close outer, wait the actual60second purge, open inner and move into cabin, then close inner. Bodies must fit fully; no entry through occupied cells or solids. Actual no-breath/other protective abilities retain their printed scope. If the inner door is forced open while the chamber is unsafe, cabin protection is lost until both doors are repaired/closed, hull breaches patched and an actual60second purge completes. At a completed safe closure/purge or existing-source protection expiration use the actual timestamp.
