Exact completions needing attention:

- Augury's 30-minute horizon starts at the declared course beginning, itself within the next 60 seconds; materially neutral results are Unclear. This is a forecast, not a magical obligation to begin.
- Divine Perception reports each readable category once; multiple same-category influences do not disclose counts. It supplies category/provenance status, with no extra deity/controller identity details smuggled in merely because those are in a Director record.
- Clairvoyance preserves your precise-relative-position designation of unvisited points and ordinary sight in all directions from one fixed point. It adds one active point per caster, matching the bounded Clairaudience interface. Turning attention from that point is allowed; translating the point is not. No concentration is added.
- Vision Trap distinguishes a later failed save (new rider replaces old) from a later successful save (no new rider; does not erase an old one). Its end marker uses the first future target END strictly after application, preserving the shared end-phase-before-cleanup rule. Outside initiative the six-second limit uses the shared virtual/absolute cadence. Damage and rider remain independently affected by defenses.
- Locate Person is Self with one-mile informational reach, not a 30-foot tag. Its person predicate preserves active Undead/Construct continuing identities and excludes corpses/objects merely for their history.
- Faction Spy is Self with any-distance same-plane reach. It adds one active point per caster and a deterministic point in the center of the selected room's usable interior at ordinary eye height, nearest legal point in that same room if obstructed. Your primary operations/common-area fallback stays intact. A mobile structure carries the room; a later institutional move or ownership change does not retarget the point. Same-plane separation suppresses feeds without resetting time. These exact additions should not accidentally create secret-room selection or interplanar viewing.
- Alignment Prediction keeps a useful likely response without forcing agency, alignment or oathbreaks. It specifies the immediate response in a situation expected within one minute, rather than a whole downstream sequence.
- Resonance Tracking has a source-conference selection pitfall: its own currently resolving cast would otherwise always be the most recent nearby spell. I exclude only that current cast from its own selection, explicitly NEW. Older actual Tracking casts can leave origin traces, but observing a different trace never copies it, produces a path of the observer's movement or refreshes its age. Selection of equal-timestamp events uses real cast-start order, then fixed neutral event order. Unknown-event selection uses most recent readable event; selecting a known protected event gives no read rather than switching. Actual new path components of an ongoing selected spell become readable only after occurring, but all expire at original cast start +3,600 seconds, including at the exact endpoint. Age must therefore be strictly less than 3,600 at selection. Independent later planar travel can find only local genuine segments; this grants no remote interplanar feed. Please explicitly approve or repair these completions.
