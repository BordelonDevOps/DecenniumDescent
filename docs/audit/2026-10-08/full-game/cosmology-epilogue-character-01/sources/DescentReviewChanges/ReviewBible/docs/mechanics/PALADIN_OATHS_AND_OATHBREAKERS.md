# Paladin — all Sacred Oaths and Oathbreakers

**Provisional playtest rules.** Every Oath and named Oathbreaker in the preserved Sacred Oaths source is paired below. Original source wording and conduct clauses remain intact.

[Full Paladin rules and cards](classes/paladin.md) · [All Base classes](CLASS_PLAYTEST_INDEX.md) · [Preserved Oath source](../../data/playtest/class_sources/supplements.json) · [Card data and paired catalog](../../data/public/class_playtests/paladin.json)

Coverage: **10 Oaths, 10 named Oathbreaker paths, 120 path cards and 6 shared Oathbreaker cards.** Each Oath has a Smite, aura, five Revelation spells, Signature Power and Final Gift. Each corresponding Oathbreaker has its own Death Touch, inverted aura and Blasphemous Word.

## Progression and oathbreaking

**Paladin is the Base class:** levels 1–2 establish its core abilities. Choose one Oath at level 3 as the Arch specialization. At level 10 enter Hero mastery of that same Oath; Signature Power unlocks at 11 and Final Gift at 20. The names below are later Paladin paths, not additional Base classes.

Use the conduct clauses beneath each Oath when judging a breach. The Director identifies a relevant clause and a knowingly chosen serious breach. Disagreement, a failed roll, coercion or an unknown fact does not silently strip the character. Record a fall with the player, then choose the corresponding inversion or an agreed restoration scene.

An Oathbreaker replaces the normal Oath package: Death Touch uses the current Lay on Hands pool; the inverted aura, Blasphemous Word, shared Forbidden spells and Black Manifestation replace their normal counterparts. Preserve level and spent resources when switching. Normal and inverted packages do not stack. All level gates still apply. The full Paladin guide supplies the resource, timing, aura and restoration context.

## Oath of Mercy

Mercy is a shelter kept open in the worst hour: protection, healing, and the preservation of life before vengeance can make another wound.

**Preserved conduct clauses:**

- Never refuse a genuine plea for healing or protection when the oath can answer it
- Set the saving of life before the taking of it, even when anger makes the latter easier
- Offer defeated foes the mercy their surrender can bear, without abandoning those still in danger
- Do not raise lethal force against the innocent or defenseless; strength was given to stand between them and harm

Vengeance is judgment carried through the long road: fury disciplined into pursuit, execution held to a sworn account, and no refuge promised to the unrepentant.

**Preserved conduct clauses:**

- Do not abandon allies to feed a private vendetta, for judgment that leaves the faithful behind is only rage
