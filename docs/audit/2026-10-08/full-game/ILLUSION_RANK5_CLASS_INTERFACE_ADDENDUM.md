# Fifth-rank Illusion: Oracle comparison questions

Prepared source addendum, not sent and not operational approval. Read with the complete seven class records in [the preserved source packet](ILLUSION_RANK5_SOURCE_PREPARATION.json). Their current effects and retained original objects remain unchanged.

All seven textual matches for the ordinary spell `dream` occur in Oracle records. They are not evidence that the Oracle gains that ordinary spell. Mystery and Consumed refer to the chosen Mystery and its theme; Shared Dream is its own Revelation with its own movement, concealment and attack rider. Dream Prophecy Trigger requires an actual Dream Revelation, and Dream Revelation Mastery upgrades its named Revelations. An ordinary spell does not become a Revelation through its title.

The eventual source conference must nevertheless address the following real interface questions:

- `oracle.supplement.dream.threshold-collapse` refers to a hostile creature's first save to disbelieve “one of your Dream illusions.” Bind that scope to actual qualifying source features; do not make every spell with Dream in its name eligible by substring matching. Retain its distinction between a save to disbelieve and another save against damage or control.
- `oracle.supplement.dream.dream-revelation-mastery` refers to being deceived by a Dream illusion or suffering Dread from the Oracle. Establish the actual relation before reaction denial; an unrelated illusion or generic Frightened condition is not interchangeable with those records.
- `oracle.supplement.dream.dream-prophecy` has a broader final clause: “one illusion you create” during its stated interval. Compare this exact wording against the new ordinary Illusion spells. Identify which events are genuinely saves to disbelieve, whether the clause applies to them, and how a second save interacts with a shared cast, multiple targets, earlier disbelief and other reroll or second-save permissions. Do not silently narrow the broad clause to Revelations or extend it to every saving throw.
- A spell's misleading appearance or nightmare does not itself establish that a meaningful hostile failed a qualifying Revelation save for Dream Prophecy Trigger. Preserve one real triggering event, the stated cadence and the RC maximum.

The relevant full records are `oracle.supplement.shared.mystery`, `oracle.supplement.curse.consumed`, `oracle.supplement.dream.dream-prophecy-trigger`, `oracle.supplement.dream.shared-dream`, `oracle.supplement.dream.threshold-collapse`, `oracle.supplement.dream.dream-revelation-mastery` and `oracle.supplement.dream.dream-prophecy`. This comparison does not review or reinterpret their historical cosmological flavor. Cosmology remains after the authorized GitHub push and verified remote commit.
