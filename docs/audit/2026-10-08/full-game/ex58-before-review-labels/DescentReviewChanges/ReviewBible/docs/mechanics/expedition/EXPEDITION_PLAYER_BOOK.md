## 1. Charters, time and provisions

<a id="fl-01"></a>
### FL-01 — The rules used on an expedition

Use the current [Core resolution](../CORE_RESOLUTION_PLAYTEST.md), [combat and actions](../COMBAT_ACTION_PLAYTEST.md), [conditions](../CONDITIONS_PLAYTEST.md), [healing and death](../HEALING_DEATH_PLAYTEST.md), and [creature lifecycle](../CREATURE_LIFECYCLE_PLAYTEST.md) chapters. Their detailed procedures also apply during travel, camp work and recovery. An expedition assignment supplies neither another Action nor a second resource allowance.

A dying creature makes its death save at the start of its regular turn; an extra turn supplies no additional death save. Wounds use the current damage-event save and retain their linked injuries. A Critical Wound reduces Speed by 10 and applies its current healing ceiling. Use the actual harvest's size, time, partial-work and hurried-work rules. Older summaries of end-turn death saves, automatic critical-hit Wounds, reduced Hit-Dice healing per Wound, half Speed at Critical Wounds, or a generic one-hour hurried harvest do not replace those procedures.

An exact activity keeps its own assistance, interruption, output, training and recovery rules. Apply only the abilities, spells, recipes and items your character has actually acquired and can currently use, with their real costs, components, charges and timing. A name on a comparison list does not grant it.

<a id="fl-02"></a>
### FL-02 — Members, teams and offices

An Expedition has at most **12 member characters**, including recruited NPCs and hirelings assigned independent Expedition jobs. Small Expeditions can use fewer teams and undertake feasible solo tasks. The following sizes are recommendations, not entry requirements.

| Team | Suggested members | Work |
|---|---:|---|
| Combat | 4–6 | Threats, battles and immediate protection |
| Reconnaissance | 1–3 | Scouting and mapping |
| Research | 1–3 | Study and identification |
| Resource | 2–4 | Gathering, crafting and harvesting |
| Support | 1–3 | Camp, care, repair and transport |

Appoint the five source offices: **Expedition Leader**, **Quartermaster**, **Routekeeper**, **Surgeon**, and **Claimant of Record**. The Leader represents the company and records tied organizational decisions; the Quartermaster tracks provisions, Load, cargo priority, wages and losses; the Routekeeper maintains the map; the Surgeon directs Wound treatment and casualty transport; the Claimant of Record maintains provenance, contracts and ownership documents. One person can hold several offices. An office grants no skill, extra Action or power over another player's consent or another owner's property.

Before work, record each member's primary activity or Rest, location, intended result, lead and assistants, and actual start/end times. Short tasks may follow one another. A person cannot perform incompatible activities during the same seconds. For a generic expedition task, up to three trained assistants who provide meaningful help each add +1. Exact crafting, harvesting, preservation, social and companion procedures use their own assistance rules instead.

Porters, bearers, guards and drivers contracted as members occupy roster seats. A public ship or caravan's external crew stays on its own service record and is not additional party labor or automatic combat support. Animals and source-bound noncharacter companions/devices do not occupy character seats; an intelligent creature recruited as an autonomous expedition character does. Larger forces use distinct cooperating Expeditions with real communication, shared finite resources and the same world clock.

<a id="fl-03"></a>
### FL-03 — One clock, work and rest

The campaign uses one elapsed clock. A Watch is eight hours; a day contains Morning, Afternoon and Evening Watches. Combat rounds, rests, travel, treatment and work occupy their actual time within that clock. Completing a task does not add a second Watch. Entering Haven, changing teams or starting initiative does not reset elapsed time.

A generic Watch task needs **480 active minutes** before its completion check. Feasible unfinished work can continue in another Watch, without a proportional completion reward. Exact recipes, uninterrupted training, treatment, rest and spells keep their own interruption rules. Guarding, travelling, casting, directing, interpreting, assisting or working cannot also count as incompatible rest. Passive carriage qualifies for rest only when a genuinely suitable safe berth and the actual rest requirements are satisfied.

Plan at most two active work Watches and one Rest Watch per person each day. Evening is the default Rest slot; change it prospectively before that day's first assignment. Voluntary extra noncrafting travel or guard duty can replace Rest and incur its real costs and Rest Debt. This supplies no third crafting, training, research or harvesting Watch. Emergencies still consume time and interrupt the activity underway.

Missing a scheduled Rest creates **Rest Debt**. If an emergency interrupts Rest, the debt opens after that emergency ends; otherwise it opens when the missed Rest Watch ends. Complete a qualifying safe eight-hour Rest by the end of the second following canonical Watch. If the debt remains unpaid at that deadline, gain 1 Strain once for that due event and keep owing the Rest. Missing another scheduled Rest while indebted gives 1 Strain and resets the deadline. There is no automatic additional Strain every second or every Watch merely because the same debt remains.

A safe eight-hour Rest clears the debt even when you cannot receive another Long Rest's benefits. Keep the separate limits: one beneficial Long Rest per 24 elapsed hours, two beneficial Short Rests between beneficial Long Rests, the existing four-hour Morale Rest, and 24-hour Haven care. Trance reduces sleep, not the eight-hour Long-Rest requirement. Use the actual Strain and recovery chapter for what can be removed.

<a id="fl-04"></a>
### FL-04 — Supply and daily support

**Supply is a basket of real goods:** 2 lb of ration-equivalent edible food, 8 pints of potable water, 1 pint of compatible lamp oil in its flask, and 0.25 lb of ordinary camp sundries. The food and water cover one Small or Medium character's daily demand; Large characters require twice those quantities. This is game accounting. Two pounds of food equal three reviewed Cooking servings; a Large food day is six servings.

Track food and water actually consumed, by recipient and lot. Ordinary partial rations credit their edible mass. A fractional magical meal never gives a fractional benefit unless its own source permits that. The same serving cannot be spent in a recipe and remain available to eat.

Oil and sundries are spent when actually used. Do not burn spare oil or throw away sound stock to satisfy an abstract daily debit. The source lamp burns one pint in six hours. One actual light may serve several people within its real illumination, with its fuel spent once. A sufficient daylight or other safe lighting plan can use no oil; extra darkness, heating or work needs its real equipment and fuel.

At each canonical 24-hour day endpoint, settle each recipient's actual food, water and necessary declared logistics support. A recipient short of any requirement gains **1 expedition Strain total for that day's shortfall**. Missing food and water does not give two Strain. Retain partial consumption credit. The unsupported day blocks Strain removal; that block can clear on a later day only with a real sufficient provisioning plan available and maintained. Crossing a gate does not erase needs: actual Haven/home support pays needs outside the expedition.

Allocate shortages before the deadline. Joining, leaving or changing size/form divides demand by actual time and diet; it never refunds goods already consumed. A diet exception removes only its stated obligation. It gives no transferable provisions, fuel or camp labor. Unsupported nonmount Tiny, Huge or Gargantuan diets require their real profiles; mounts use their separate profiles.

If an exact procedure charges **one whole Supply**, reserve and commit the entire physical basket, or a substitute that procedure actually permits. Wound treatment keeps its one-Supply cost at the start, even if treatment fails or is interrupted. Food or water genuinely consumed by its named patient or healer can credit that person's daily need once. The committed basket does not also remain in inventory or feed another person.

The new ordinary camp-sundry unit costs 2 cp and weighs 0.25 lb. It contains expendable cloth, twine and cleaning material; it provides no medical-kit charge, reagent, repair stock or magical effect. At the Core low-tier water reference (Tiers I–III), 1 cp buys 8 pints of actual clean water. At Tiers IV–VI, that quantity costs 10 cp. Containers are separate.

Tents, tools, clothing, lighting apparatus and medical kits remain separate capital goods.

These are immediate ordinary meals without spell/recipe benefits. Packing requires a real container and preserves the existing 24-hour cooked-food clock; a meal does not become a preserved ration by being purchased.

Ordinary dry sealed rations expire 30 elapsed days after actual packing unless their source lot supplies a different interval. Retain known prior age. Clean potable water has no arbitrary expiry while its suitable sealed container stays clean; contamination needs an actual event. Raw meat, organs, essences and cooked meals keep their own clocks. Check weight, volume, shape, closure and weather independently. Replacing packaging does not renew age.

Filling one skin/flask or issuing one standard basket takes one active minute per worker, sequentially. A sealed prepacked handover instead uses actual item-access time. The new ordinary oil-flask profile divides its printed 1 lb into 0.25 lb empty flask and 0.75 lb oil per full pint. With half a pint remaining, the intact flask weighs 0.625 lb. Keep an actually defined particular container's own tare when different. An empty flask is still that object; it has no automatic resale value or fuel credit.

<a id="fl-06"></a>
### FL-06 — Foraging a finite place

A generic Forage attempt requires 480 active minutes, suitable gathering implements and access to a real opportunity. The lead makes WIS (Survival) against the route-tier DC; up to three trained assistants can provide meaningful +1 help unless an exact source changes that procedure.

The opportunity has an ordinary Sustenance allowance S from 0 to 4 and actual food, water and material quantities. Sustenance 0 gives no routine award. Success can recover up to S provision-equivalent units. Critical Success can recover up to **S + ceil(S/2)**, but the extra must come from an existing recorded reserve. A good roll cannot supply absent water, lamp oil, animals, herbs or industrial sundries.

Receive the actual gathered goods. A food-only site supplies food, not a complete basket. A complete Supply requires every component to exist and its real gathering/packing work to be completed. The recovered amount cannot exceed the site's real remaining allowance and matter.

Teams using the same opportunity share its allowance. Use one lead/check or genuinely disjoint portions allocated in advance, with no more total stock than the parent site contains. A new Watch, renamed site, new container or return visit does not replenish the place. Record gathered, ruined, inaccessible, forfeited and remaining quantities.

Failure gives nothing and ends that attempt; an unchanged immediate retry is unavailable. On Critical Failure, contaminate one actual selected ordinary provision unit only when a predeclared contamination hazard supports it; otherwise add 1 Pressure once for that cause. An actual class/background permission to gather during a beneficial Long Rest supplies only its named product and real components. It does not also complete generic Forage or create a second local pool.

<a id="fl-07"></a>
### FL-07 — Building and using a camp

Choose a legally accessible, stable 20 × 20 ft site with actual drainage, weather protection and space for the inhabitants and gear. A camp's name alone does not make it safe. An exposed bivouac needs no build task and has no certified storage; its real exposure remains.

One build task requires 480 active Support minutes and a STR or WIS check using an actually relevant tool/Survival proficiency against the tier DC. Up to three trained assistants can help. Success earns one build mark; Critical Success earns two; failure earns none. Reserve required materials before work and record their installation. Failure spends time and only goods actually damaged or used. Feasible unfinished physical progress survives a pause.

| Camp | Required work and new fixtures | Storage limit |
|---|---|---|
| Temporary Camp | 1 Support mark; real sleeping shelters; fitting pack, 20 lb/2 gp | 20 Load / 200 lb / 20 ft³ |
| Fortified Outpost | 3 total Support marks, including Temporary; additional timber/stone/fasteners, 300 lb/60 gp | 60 Load / 600 lb / 60 ft³ |
| Research Station | Fortified, plus 2 Research marks; desk/weatherproof record storage, 50 lb/25 gp | 60 Load / 600 lb / 60 ft³ |
| Forward Base | 8 total marks: 3 Fortified Support, 2 Research, 3 further Support; extra depot stock, 500 lb/100 gp; actual faction approval and a contracted functioning supply/evacuation line | 150 Load / 1,500 lb / 150 ft³ |

Each new storage limit replaces the previous limit. The Temporary fitting pack provides rope, pegs, ground covering and racks. Existing actual compatible fixtures may replace it by recorded quantities. Tents, bedrolls, tools, light and food are separate. The source two-person tent holds two Small/Medium sleeping places, or one Large place under this new rule.

An intact watched Fortified perimeter gives its warning benefit against attackers using that perimeter. It does not remove Surprise from betrayal, unseen infiltration, teleportation or an unwatched direction. A functioning Outpost gives the source +1 to feasible repair checks; it does not reduce hours or material costs or improve manufacture.

Research marks each require the completed Research work and an INT check using the actual relevant skill at the tier DC. A functioning Research Station gives +2 to legitimate research/identification checks. Libraries, instruments, evidence, active-core containment and exact recipe stations are still independently required. It supplies no universal knowledge or creation bonus.

A Forward Base's Haven link is the actual contracted service, with real carriers, crew, capacity, journey time, price and risk. It is not instant resupply, resurrection, teleportation or evacuation. None of these fixture kits is food, an independent recipe or an automatic salvage payout.

## 2. Routes, extraction and retreat

<a id="fl-08"></a>
### FL-08 — Pressure follows the expedition’s real exposure

Pressure is an integer from 0 to 6 for the actual group travelling or camping together on a route. Its source state names are:

| Pressure | State |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Secured; no routine complication roll |
| 1 | Watched |
| 2 | Unstable |
| 3 | Hostile |
| 4 | Hunted |
| 5 | Overrun |
| 6 | Immediate crisis |

At an active Watch's end, add +1 for each applicable category: open combat/explosion during that Watch; exceeding route Capacity; a missing/captured member or active pursuit; continuing after an agreed abort trigger; and actually using an exposed camp or one known to enemies. Several attacks are one open-combat increase, and several descriptions of the same cause do not multiply it. Different genuine categories can add together.

Attention cargo instead adds +1 for each actual lot's first exposed carriage, newly revealed concealment loss, or genuine re-exposure after concealment was restored. Continuous unchanged exposure does not add another point every Watch. Splitting containers, changing owners or re-entering a route does not renew the cause. Its exposed state can still affect an Extraction Test.

Qualifying Counter-Ambush, a genuine move onto a secured route, a full Watch lying low in a concealed camp, or a completed settlement ending pursuit can each earn −1. The group can receive at most two reduction credits per Watch, including reconnaissance and extraction-critical reductions. Conceal Trail cancels one actual pursuit increase; it does not remove all pursuit or grant an additional free −1. Apply real increases and allowed reductions once, then keep Pressure between the current route's Risk floor and 6. An actually Secured route permits a floor of 0.

Process routine complications once per active Watch. Safe inactive Rest alone produces no routine roll, though real raids and hazards can still happen. At Pressure 6, the due checkpoint produces one Severe complication.

When the group genuinely leaves its last exposed route for safe Haven during a Watch, settle any unprocessed exposure checkpoint at that boundary using causes accrued so far. Do not repeat it at the later Watch end or place a route patrol inside Haven. Safe arrival preserves history; it does not itself set Pressure to 0 or refill resources.

<a id="fl-09"></a>
### FL-09 — Complications have a declared cause and a finite response

At Pressure 0 there is no routine complication roll. At Pressure 6 resolve one automatic Severe complication. At Pressure 1–5 use the final lawfully selected natural d6 face n and its final modified total r:

1. If r is greater than Pressure, no complication occurs.
2. Otherwise, a final natural 1 gives Severe.
3. Otherwise, if r equals Pressure and an actually applied modifier or reroll produced that final equality, give Minor.
4. All other triggering results give Standard.

This rule grants no modifier or reroll. A natural 1 modified above Pressure does not trigger. Modified totals can be outside 1–6; do not clamp them. With no modifier or reroll, the full ordinary table is:

| Pressure | Natural 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | None | None | None | None | None | None |
| 1 | Severe | None | None | None | None | None |
| 2 | Severe | Standard | None | None | None | None |
| 3 | Severe | Standard | Standard | None | None | None |
| 4 | Severe | Standard | Standard | Standard | None | None |
| 5 | Severe | Standard | Standard | Standard | Standard | None |
| 6 | Automatic Severe; no die | — | — | — | — | — |

Before checking, establish compatible real stakes. A Minor complication is a 15-minute access/inspection delay or exposure of one actual concealed lot. Standard is a 60-minute obstruction/detour or one actual low-priority unsecured lot placed at risk. Severe is an actual encounter, forced evacuation, route closure, or severe cargo/casualty hazard with a declared resolution. No result invents an enemy, damage, curse or valuable debris.

A successful Patrol can reduce the next actual camp complication one tier—Severe to Standard, Standard to Minor, Minor to none—once, when its warning physically applies. It cannot undo an independent attack already resolved.

<a id="fl-10"></a>
### FL-10 — Route travel and mapping

Record the route's directed endpoints, finite Length in Progress, Risk, Mapping, Capacity in Load, actual Sustenance opportunities, clearance and permitted travel methods. Mapping is what you know about the passage. Route quality describes its actual physical/security condition; they are separate.

One travel segment represents **480 nominal minutes of route effort**. Its elapsed moving time is:

`480 minutes × route-quality factor × (3 ÷ current convoy pace in mph)`

| Route quality | Time factor |
|---|---:|
| Secured | 3/4 |
| Known | 1 |
| Contested | 5/4 |
| Collapsed | 3/2 |

The new ordinary reference is 3 mph for fit, unburdened walking Speed 30. Other lawful walking/mount paces use current Speed ÷ 10 mph; vessels use their specific travel profiles. The slowest necessary self-moving member or vehicle limits the convoy; passengers use their carrier. Apply actual environment, Load/Speed and the expedition-Strain-5 slow-travel rule once each where applicable. Strain 5 halves otherwise legal travel once after the other applicable restrictions. Repeated tactical Dash does not create free continuous overland speed.

At completion, the segment's starting Mapping gives 2 Progress for Mapped/Secured, 1 for Sketched, or 0 for Unknown until successful reconnaissance makes that actual traverse feasible, then 1. Unknown or severely obstructed passages need actual successful reconnaissance or a resolved forced march before ordinary advancement. Compatible moving reconnaissance/escort can share a traverse; stationary research or crafting does not move it.

A reconnaissance success can add one shared +1 Progress OR −1 Pressure; a critical can do both. All teams together get at most one such Progress bonus and one such reduction for that group/segment. Successful Map Route also earns one mapping mark, or two on a critical. After max(1, recorded Risk) marks, consume them to improve Mapping one step. New Mapping normally affects the next segment. Unknown travel made feasible by its own reconnaissance gets the permitted base 1, not a retroactive base 2. A Risk-0 Secured route has no further mapping level or reward.

If total convoy Load exceeds route Capacity, subtract 1 Progress once from the completed segment, minimum 0, after allowed base/bonus Progress. Its corresponding Pressure increase is also charged once. This route penalty never permits an individual carrier to exceed its own hard limit.

Progress refers to real traversable sections. You cannot cross a closed bridge, carry an oversized boat through a tunnel or relocate a stranded member by changing a number. A map purchase provides only its actual content and verified rating. Reverse travel can reuse observed geometry, without guaranteeing unchanged currents, slopes, guards or one-way access.

<a id="fl-11"></a>
### FL-11 — Forced march and interrupted travel

A forced march is the expedition procedure for attempting Unknown-route travel before mapping is complete. Declare one 480-nominal-minute attempt, with actual travel time adjusted by the route formula. Record participants, feasible transport and extra provisions before starting. The Routekeeper—or the actual permitted capable lead—uses the appropriate route skill, normally WIS (Survival), at **tier DC +6**. Use the generic trained-assistant limit unless an exact source overrides it.

| Task result | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Success or Critical Success | 1 Progress, +2 Pressure, and the actual extra support cost below |
| Failure | No Progress; one Standard complication |
| Critical Failure | No Progress; one Severe complication |

Reserve an additional complete provision basket for each active Small/Medium participant, or two for Large. A successful attempt consumes those extras once for its actual support; real nutrition consumption can credit daily needs once. There is no extra generic critical reward. An expected stock shortage blocks a voluntary attempt before commitment. A specific dietary exemption waives only its actual components, without generating transferable surplus.

On failure, return only genuinely unspent extras. Anything actually consumed or lost during the attempt remains spent. A roll creates no missing transport capacity, suitable route or safe geometry; revise, split or withdraw an infeasible group before starting.

Combat, blocked passages and rest pause unfinished travel and consume their actual minutes. Resume the same unresolved attempt if feasible. A resolved failure cannot be rerolled by renaming it; a materially different method/route requires a new real time/resource commitment. Scheduled Rest and Rest Debt continue. A character at expedition Strain 5 cannot lead this active check; lawful carrying and the actual slow-travel exception remain available.

<a id="fl-12"></a>
### FL-12 — Starting extraction and admitting cargo

Extraction begins with a genuine decision and movement toward a real Haven/surface destination. Inventory lots, people, casualties, vehicles and provisions. For each cargo lot record identity, owner/custodian, pounds, Load, Extraction Class, priority 1–5, condition, carrier and relevant tags. Before outcomes, the Quartermaster or Leader resolves priority ties; the lowest-priority appropriate lot is put at risk first.

Extraction Class is handling danger and attention, not mass, title or a promotion in rarity:

| Class | Source examples |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Ordinary trade goods |
| 1 | Common monster parts or durable salvage |
| 2 | Uncommon, fragile or regulated cargo |
| 3 | Rare artifacts, volatile substances or living captives |
| 4 | Very rare strategic cargo or dangerous curses |
| 5 | Epic cargo likely to draw sovereign/faction intervention |
| 6 | Legendary cargo requiring a dedicated extraction operation |

Keep actual Fragile, Volatile, Cursed, Living, Contraband, Perishable and Attention tags. A voluntary passenger is not automatically a Class-3 captive. People retain agency, care needs, legal status and actual consent/restraint facts. A classification grants no authority to bind or own someone.

At extraction's start add Pressure equal to **min(3, floor(highest carried Class ÷ 2))** once. Keep the episode's highest charged surcharge. Acquiring higher-Class cargo later adds only the positive difference; dropping or concealing it refunds nothing. Transferring allied cargo, changing transport or renaming the episode cannot renew or erase that history.

Secure, hidden and medically/chemically safe are different facts. [Secure Cargo](../BODY_EQUIPMENT_PLAYTEST.md) spends an Action on one feasible handling/protective operation. It supplies no free whole-convoy restraint, automatic success, capacity or lowered Class. A concealed volatile core still needs its real containment. Admit Class-6 cargo only with its declared dedicated handling and route operation.

<a id="fl-13"></a>
### FL-13 — Extraction checkpoints and checks

At route entry, record a finite ordered set of real checkpoint positions using the Mapping governing that traverse:

| Mapping | Checkpoints |
|---|---|
| Secured | Genuine route transition/exit |
| Mapped | Midpoint and exit for Length at least 6; otherwise exit |
| Sketched | Each positive multiple of 3 below Length, and exit |
| Unknown | Each positive multiple of 2 below Length, and exit |

Fractional midpoints stay exact. A later mapping improvement changes only future cadence and preserves all processed positions. Combine overlapping exit, midpoint, route-transition or multiple-based requests at the same real position/time into one test with all reasons. Changing the name of the same carriage is not a transport transition. A route exit and next entry at the same transfer point share one state test unless a distinct later operation intervenes.

The Quartermaster or a designated capable substitute makes the check using an actual ability modifier, one relevant Survival/vehicle/tool proficiency, and meaningful permitted assistants. Its DC is:

`tier DC + min(7, highest Class + exposed Attention + Burden + min(3, unsecured dangerous lots)) + pursuit − Secured`

Exposed Attention is +1 once if applicable; Burden is +1 once if the actual convoy is Burdened. Count each unsecured Volatile/Cursed/Living lot once even if it has several such tags. Active pursuit adds +2 outside the +7 cap; a Secured route subtracts 2 outside it. The tier DCs are I 12, II 13, III 15, IV 16, V 18 and VI 19. Use Core task degrees and natural shifts.

| Result | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Critical Success | Pass and choose −1 Pressure within the Watch reduction limit, or +1 Progress along an actually reachable compatible onward section |
| Success | Pass |
| Failure | Choose the declared eight-hour delay plus Standard complication, or the stated low-priority lot at risk |
| Critical Failure | Severe complication plus the predeclared two lots, one vehicle or one casualty at risk |

An ordinary check requires **one active minute** of actual convoy/route/receiver verification by the lead and assistants, with necessary hands, tools and access. It fits within travel only when that compatible moving workflow was declared; otherwise stop and add the minute. An exact longer procedure controls its own operation, with no extra generic minute added to already completed exact verification.

<a id="fl-14"></a>
### FL-14 — A failed extraction test creates a choice and a real problem

Before the test, the Director states the physical cause, real stakes, affected low-priority cargo/vehicle/person and feasible responses. On Failure, players choose an **eight-hour elapsed delay plus one Standard complication**, or one stated low-priority lot at risk. Critical Failure brings one Severe complication and the predeclared two lots OR one vehicle OR one casualty at risk. “At risk” does not mean automatic destruction, death or transferred ownership. Do not invent another lot or casualty when none exists.

The delay occupies 28,800 actual seconds. Record who must work, guard or handle the obstacle. The stalled convoy earns no Progress for that interval. Others may perform feasible independent activities, including genuinely safe uninterrupted rest, but the failed check does not certify safety. Food, water, protection, Rest Debt, Pressure and rival clocks continue at their real endpoints. This is eight hours once, not eight hours plus another Watch.

The default noncombat cargo response is a **60-active-minute securing/recovery task**, using a capable lead, necessary helpers/rigging and tier DC plus the affected lot's Class, capped at +6. A more specific real source controls instead. Success or Critical Success secures the reachable lot without extra loot, Progress or HP. Failure leaves the lot at its declared reachable stranded/abandoned position or applies its declared compatible hazard once. It does not universally destroy it.

If seconds matter, use actual combat movement, Secure Cargo, attacks, falls and rescue Actions instead of the hour abstraction. Voluntarily leaving a lot changes custody/location while preserving title/claims and insurance exclusions. Later retrieval is a new actual rescue against that position, not a replayed extraction award. Insurance does not replace a merely threatened item.

<a id="fl-15"></a>
### FL-15 — Retreat without restarting the encounter

Declare Retreat at the start of an actual round when a plausible escape route exists. Keep the same encounter, numbered rounds, positions, conditions, pursuit actors and spent resources.

Once per round, one withdrawing lead spends its Action on a fixed-DC escape task using its real method. Begin at route tier DC. A declared superior pursuit/obstruction adds +3, a severe version +6, or actual favorable preparation subtracts 3. Use the Core four task degrees and natural shifts. An exact source can provide a distinct opposed chase instead; do not make both checks for the same uncertainty.

| Lead result | Counter gained |
|---|---|
| Critical Success | 2 Escape |
| Success | 1 Escape |
| Failure | 1 Pursuit |
| Critical Failure | 2 Pursuit |

An independently achieved declared round objective, actual sacrificed cargo or blocking maneuver can supply 1 Escape instead of the ordinary lead success. It does not add another generic point. Generic Escape is capped at 1 per round, or 2 when the actual lead critical supplies 2. Losing a real member, route or vehicle can add 1 Pursuit when declared as a consequence; generic Pursuit is capped at 2 per round. Express paid features retain only their exact point permissions.

Each round includes one predeclared meaningful opposing threat: a legal attack, actual hazard or necessary sacrifice. It does not add a full enemy multiattack/nova to another chase punishment. Allies use their real Actions to Help, carry, treat, cast, command, move or create obstacles; they do not make extra lead checks. Combat Help keeps its actual advantage and expiry rules.

Resolve that round's costs, threat and check once, then compare totals. **Escape at least 3 with Pursuit below 3 succeeds. Pursuit at least 3 fails, including simultaneous 3/3.** Success returns to Expedition Mode with +1 Pressure, actual survivors/cargo and pursuit history. RP, recharge and encounter-use resources do not refresh. Failure gives the opponent its actual advantageous next position or capture opportunity; it does not automatically kill everyone or assign ownership.

<a id="fl-16"></a>
### FL-16 — Estimate encounters from actual participants

The Expedition roster is not the encounter's participant count. Track who is actually present, participating and able to contribute under their real action/command rules. Reinforcements enter at their actual times; they do not silently grant another unopposed wave.

Encounter budgets are provisional planning estimates, not promises of survival or measured balance. Establish objectives, terrain, exits, cargo, civilians, hazards, starting distance and surprise, then use the current combat rules. Cargo protection, escort and retreat require their own stated success conditions rather than only a kill count.

Boss Tenacity applies only to a declared source-qualified boss with its actual allowance. Size, cost or an encounter budget does not confer boss status. Use [current control/condition rules](../CONDITIONS_PLAYTEST.md), including exact source counters, replacement and duration. Fear, Reaction denial and ordinary Speed penalties are not interchangeable with full-turn denial.

<a id="fl-17"></a>
### FL-17 — Transport admission preserves every body and item

Before a move, validate all of these separately:

- The actual body, control permission and current movement/conditions.
- Physical route space, clearance and fit.
- Pounds, including supported living bodies and all their associated gear.
- Load, storage volume and real seats or cargo positions.
- Required crew, pullers, propulsion and their actual work.
- Legal custody and continuing item/effect clocks.

A container contributes its own empty body and each child once. A printed inclusive gross weight/Load covers only its stated contents. Keep pounds and Load distinct. A passenger's melded equipment still burdens an external carrier. Extraction Class changes the handling problem, never the carrier's capacity.

Use the [body/equipment procedure](../BODY_EQUIPMENT_PLAYTEST.md) for real mass, inventory Burden and casualties. With positive carried Load at or above half capacity, and no greater than capacity, the carrier is Burdened; empty inventory is not Burdened even at zero capacity. Hard physical and inventory limits still apply. Vehicles, mounts and their particular sources keep their own completed profiles.

Melding, petrification, changing vehicles, a sale, a service quote or renaming a record cannot restore missing cargo or spend another copy. A transport catalogue does not provide a living mount, available crew, actual route or unacquired feat.

<a id="fl-18"></a>
### FL-18 — Durability, old Integrity records and repair

Use actual structural HP for modeled equipment, fixtures and transport bodies. The old 0–3 Integrity track is not another damage pool, and its old Integrity-1 penalty does not automatically apply to a functioning positive-HP item. Keep actual grade, quality, flaws, constituents and custody. Living mounts use living HP and conditions, not structural repair.

At zero structural HP, an object is Broken under the [equipment rules](../EQUIPMENT_ACQUISITION_PLAYTEST.md). It still exists with mass, occupied space and physical drawbacks until actually removed or repaired. A missing wheel or other true primary constituent can make a vehicle incomplete even with positive aggregate HP. Inventing a cheap “pin” target does not disable a whole structure.

Ordinary self-repair requires a relevant trained toolkit and real compatible material worth **25% of the item's proper size/grade baseline**. Spend **max(1 hour, maximum structural HP ÷ 10 hours)** of active work and make the appropriate DC 10 tool task. Collapse Core degrees to success/failure: success restores maximum structural HP; failure spends time/materials and a new attempt needs fresh payment. A critical supplies no extra generic benefit. Repair at zero HP requires the recoverable main body.

An eight-hour Repair Watch is eight hours of available work, not eight completions. A 300-HP mundane vessel needs 30 active hours under this profile. Magical recipe bodies retain their exact functional/Broken bridge, prices, primary identities and persistent flaws. Repair does not recreate missing items, passengers, doses, ammunition, burnt pages, fuel, charges, class gauges or a terminal dismantled source.

## 3. Haven, settlement and advancement

<a id="fl-19"></a>
### FL-19 — Arriving at Haven

Use this order for a full return: **inspection/quarantine → provenance → appraisal → claims/liens → disposition → settlement → recovery → standing → advancement → next descent**.

A Tier-I return may use inspection → appraisal → sale → sponsor settlement → recovery only when every arriving lot qualifies and there is no disputed ownership, strategic cargo, lien, quarantine issue, contraband or faction hearing. The shorter route removes redundant administrative passes. It does not waive taxes, title, actual fees, care, unknown hazards or later standing/advancement records. A discovered disqualifier moves the same return to the full process, keeping work and charges already completed.

| Ordinary work | Active time and unit |
|---|---|
| Intake | 10 minutes per arriving Expedition, with receiving officer and representative |
| Additional flagged-lot inspection | 10 minutes per distinct homogeneous lot/assembly |
| Provenance, including an uncontested claim check | 30 minutes per title/evidence dossier |
| Appraisal | 30 minutes per item or previously constituted homogeneous lot |
| Ordinary disputed-claim hearing | 2 hours with actual parties, evidence and authorized adjudicator |
| Buyer negotiation/disposition | 30 minutes per coherent offered bill |
| Ledger/sponsor settlement | 30 minutes per Expedition bill |
| Standing notification | 10 minutes when needed |
| Advancement record | 10 minutes when needed |
| Next-charter planning | 60 minutes |

Real waiting for staff, evidence or authority adds elapsed time. Independently staffed desks can work on separate lots, but one representative cannot fully participate in incompatible simultaneous tasks. Handing over a completed document uses actual handling time rather than automatically repeating its full review. Exact specialist, identification, emergency or hearing procedures keep their own requirements.

Routine intake and uncontested bookkeeping need no roll. For uncertain appraisal, declare the appropriate INT skill—Arcana, History, Nature, Religion, Engineering or Investigation—or applicable craft tool and method. Add one proficiency where actually applicable; ordinary appraisal has no universal trained-only gate. Use the item's assessment DC, or its actual material-tier DC: I 12, II 13, III 15, IV 16, V 18, VI 19. One knowledgeable helper can provide ordinary Help under the social procedure; do not also add the generic expedition +3.

Success gives reliable conclusions supported by that method/evidence and a valuation in the correct domain. A critical adds the declared extra supported detail or access to a real alternative buyer. Failure leaves only established broad facts and unresolved value. A predeclared critical-failure stake can produce a fallible report, never change the object's real identity. Testing consumes a sample only when declared and actually debited. Rotating appraisers over unchanged evidence does not create fresh checks.

Identification, translation, title evidence, safe containment and a buyer's offer are distinct. Quarantine records an authorized institution's actual custody/location/access restriction; it neither destroys nor cleanses the item. Paid secure storage cannot substitute for an active core's specific containment.

<a id="fl-20"></a>
### FL-20 — Choose one valuation method

Before pricing, identify the actual offered object and quantity, condition, custody, lawful disposition and buyer. Choose one domain: ordinary equipment, reviewed crafted item, ordinary material/trade lot, true artifact, or an unsupported/unvalued object. A grand name, rarity label, high price, ingredient or magical condition does not choose the domain.

**Ordinary equipment.** Use its actual grade/size/fitting baseline and the reviewed buyer rules. For an undamaged mundane item to which that schedule applies, the buyer's actual Honored/Liked/Neutral/Disliked/Hated relationship gives 75/60/50/40/25% of baseline; refusal remains possible. Damaged goods need a real condition-specific quote. Gems, jewelry, art and trade goods keep their particular transaction rules. Do not apply an additional Loop multiplier to an already calculated equipment price. These percentages are not magical-item buyback.

**Reviewed crafted items.** Use their exact recipe reference and sale restrictions. For the reviewed durable supplemental-body families, use the actual source-batch/Standard-Q plus approved body reference once. Final quality yield, a legacy price, all reusable capital or artifact quality do not replace that reference. Charges, flaw, damage and permitted disposition affect the actual item's quote, without an automatic scrap floor. Retained catalysts and unused output reserves remain separate real stock.

**Ordinary nonartifact, nonequipment material/trade lots.** Where no overriding exact quote applies, use:

`Base × Provenance × Demand × Condition × Urgency`

Base is that lot's actual source reference. If genuinely absent, the Director can establish one finite homogeneous lot using these explicit new provisional references before the offer:

| Source band | Preserved lot range | New median reference |
|---|---:|---:|
| I | 5–15 gp | 10 gp |
| II | 15–50 gp | 30 gp |
| III | 51–148 gp | 100 gp |
| IV | 151–499 gp | 320 gp |
| V | 512–1,465 gp | 950 gp |

There is no general Tier-VI-or-higher median. These are finite lot values, not per-pound prices or values renewed by splitting a lot.

| Factor | Applicable values |
|---|---|
| Provenance | Undocumented 0.5; disputed 0.75; documented 1; actual Gate-Sealed 1.25 |
| Demand | Glut 0.75; normal 1; sought 1.25; strategic 1.5 |
| Condition | Damaged 0.5; worn 0.75; sound 1; actually exceptional 1.25 |
| Urgency | Forced 0.75; normal 1; real patient auction 1.10 |

Apply each factual factor once. An already applied Degraded half-value rule is not halved again as “damaged”; make the duplicate factor 1. Preparation with no price effect does not make material exceptional. A Gate Seal needs actual valid evidence. A patient auction needs its real appointment/buyer and waiting time. A disputed-provenance discount grants no right to sell another owner's property.

**True artifacts.** Use the actual artifact formula and profile, with separately stated Base Function Value and Gate Tier Multiplier or a genuine finite buyer quote. Missing inputs mean *unvalued*, not zero or infinite price. No ordinary sword or crafted Masterwork acquires artifact multipliers by being recovered from the dungeon. Record gross and each deduction separately. A negative net estimate exposes a possible liability; it is not a compulsory purchase, debt or negative payment to a seller.

<a id="fl-21"></a>
### FL-21 — One settlement ledger

Keep exact fractions of copper until each actual final payment. Use 1 sp = 10 cp, 1 gp = 100 cp and 1 pp = 1,000 cp. Where accepted, retain 1 Mithril Mark = 500 cp, Embermark = 2 sp, Soulshard = 1 gp and Tidesilver = 1 sp. Verdant Leaf and otherwise unvalued currency have no invented universal conversion. Names do not establish currency origins.

Round a final purchase, tax or fee payable **up** to copper once; round a final sale receipt **down** once. A coherent homogeneous sale bill is one receipt. Distinct real contracts can have separate final rounding; individual factors or components are not new transactions merely to change rounding. Displayed decimals never replace the exact value.

For each entry, record transaction ID, payer/payee, currency, goods/quantity or service, agreement/authority, exact quote, actual rounded payment, timestamp, status and related prior payment. Quoted, reserved, paid, owed, refunded and lawfully written off are different states. A price quote spends nothing. Apply deposits to their agreed bill. Insurance is an inflow, not sold cargo.

For the default Loop net-share sponsor agreement:

`SponsorBase = max(0, G − T − RQ)`

`SponsorShare = contracted percentage × SponsorBase, rounded once as payment`

`NetExpedition = G − T − RQ − SponsorShare`

G is actual sale receipts, T the direct Haven taxes on them, and RQ the episode's actual mandatory rescue/quarantine expenses. Count RQ once whether paid earlier or now. This sponsor base is before personal wages and discretionary purchases. A signed gross-share or priority-lien agreement instead uses its own declared terms.

True profit includes actual episode inflows less every distinct actual episode cost once: shares, wages, consumed/replaced supplies, operating fuel, repair, interest and other agreed costs. Show capital purchases and borrowed principal separately. Loans and principal repayment are financing, and selling existing personal capital is not earned cargo. Cash allocated to next-expedition reserves is a further allocation, not automatically the same as profit.

Report the charter's gross cargo target, net expedition target and true-profit/distributable-after-reserves target, stating whether the comparison is an appraisal or a realized sale. Appraised value does not pay wages. Keep insufficiently funded obligations owed; do not invent negative inventory, a compulsory new loan or unlimited sponsor funds. Allocations cannot exceed actual available money.

<a id="fl-22"></a>
### FL-22 — Claims, sponsors, taxes and insurance

Record entitlement and permitted disposition before spending or selling. The Core priority is actual authorized emergency survival use; existing liens/charter claims; wages, porter shares and rescue costs; Gate/sovereign taxes and authorized seizure; written party division; then sale, donation, study, crafting or retirement. A title dispute or incompatible orders needs a real hearing. Priority neither waives a due sale tax nor charges a bill twice.

Use the **single highest applicable** Loop levy on the same taxable disposition:

| Actual transaction category | Levy |
|---|---:|
| Registered routine goods | 3% |
| Licensed crafting materials/goods | 5% |
| Artifact auction | 6% |
| Strategic/regulated cargo | 8% |
| Authorized contraband-amnesty disposal | 12% |

Identify category and jurisdiction before the offer. Written pre-sale exemptions affect only their stated charges. The 12% bracket does not legalize unlawful trade. Ordinary purchases do not acquire this sale levy; separate tolls, hearings or seizures need their actual authority/contract.

The default Loop sponsor shares use the preceding net-share base and must be accepted before expenditure:

| Sponsor | Share | Source support, when actually provided |
|---|---:|---|
| Self | 0% | Own capital |
| Local guild | 20% | Supplies and ordinary licenses |
| Merchant/bank | 30% | Capital, transport, appraisal access |
| Noble/temple | 35% | Capital, political cover, specialists |
| Sovereign/great institution | 45% | Full logistical support and legal force within actual authority |

A contract must state its chosen rate, exact gross/net base, contribution as grant or loan, title/material exceptions, due date and payer. Support must really be provided. Never stack two alternative schedules for one obligation or impose them retroactively. Quoted rights cannot exceed real institutional authority or the table's Session Zero agreement.

For the standard Loop cargo policy, declare finite insured lots, insured value I, beneficiary, episode, carrier, provenance, covered hazards and exclusions, and pay the premium before loss. Charge the highest applicable premium once: ordinary 5%; dangerous/Volatile/Cursed/Living 10%; Attention/strategic 15%.

The deductible is **D = 20% of I once per policy episode**. The provenance rate r is 60% undocumented, 75% disputed or 90% documented/Gate-Sealed. For cumulative eligible covered loss L:

`TotalEntitlement = max(0, min(I, r × min(L, I) − D))`

`AdditionalPayment = max(0, TotalEntitlement − prior actual payouts)`

Apply the genuine policy terms and remaining insured value. This new partial-loss calculation matches the source whole-loss formula when L = I. A later claim does not restart the deductible or pay the full total again. The same already compensated interest cannot collect twice. Recovery of insured property requires its real return/subrogation adjustment.

Give an actual safe-access claim notice within 72 elapsed hours of return; an earlier authenticated notice counts. Declared inability to communicate postpones the deadline until communication is restored, without backdating coverage. Routine examination needs two active hours of qualified work plus real evidence-gathering time. It is not guaranteed payment in two hours.

Fraud, undeclared cargo, voluntary abandonment without a prior insured-sacrifice clause, and loss after knowingly ignoring an applicable abort condition are excluded. Insurance supplies money under terms, never new material, HP, souls or a restored person.

### FL-23 — Standing and earned history

The new ordinary policy, **standing-v1**, uses an integer from −3 to +3 for each resolved individual/institution relationship:

| Score | Description | Ordinary access guide |
|---:|---|---|
| −3 or −2 | Hated | An actual willing provider, safe intermediary or specific exception; no automatic attack |
| −1 | Disliked | The provider's ordinary public dealings under its real restrictions |
| 0 | Neutral | Eligibility to ask for public goods, lodging and contracts |
| +1 | Liked | Consideration for an offered introduction/license/buyer process |
| +2 or +3 | Honored | Consideration for offered bonded storage, credit, sensitive desks or senior audiences |

These are opportunities to seek actual services, not granted offices, ownership, credit balances, licenses, waivers or auction admission. Named existing entitlements keep their own terms. Hated with one institution does not ban unrelated public services. Keep +2 and +3 distinct even though both display Honored; absence, unresolved information and hidden data are not an explicit known Neutral 0.

Saved standing supplies **no automatic d20 modifier or magic discount**. It changes no RP, RC, spell rank, Dissonance, charges or alignment by itself. Reputation as a CHA skill is a separate applicable check: add proficiency once, not proficiency plus the relationship score. The actual known-buyer mundane sale fraction remains its specific equipment rule.

A significant public attributable ordinary event can propose ±1; a major public success/betrayal ±2. Within one subject/institution/scene, add the signed proposals, cap their sum at −2…+2, then apply it to that scene's opening score and clamp the result to −3…+3. Record every event, its contribution, actual applied change and overflow. Recompute from the opening score when opposite events occur; do not exploit the order of saturation. No banked overflow or extra award per witness/report.

A campaign-defining exception requires its real distinct event and expressly predeclared magnitude/cap exception. Other institutions respond only to information actually reaching them and their own stakes. NPC attitude, Expedition renown, legal status and named privileges remain separate.

For a genuinely fresh Cult Escapee, bind the full background to actual institutions: former cult Hated/−2, suspicious religious authority Disliked/−1, anti-cult group Liked/+1. This does not target every cult or religious order. Existing earned history is never overwritten by a fresh-start value.

### FL-24 — Haven work, services, learning and recovery

A Haven Turn is **24 elapsed hours**, not a token that buys any result. Queue real workers, providers, facilities, materials and intervals. The normal day has two eight-hour productive Watches and Rest. Waiting is elapsed time, not productive labor or automatically beneficial rest. A Haven provides no teacher, workshop, buyer, cure, safe berth, food or license merely by being named.

Exact recipes keep all their gates, phases, assistant limits and hours—including a 960-hour order. “Brew a Batch in one day” is an activity label, not permission to finish a longer recipe. Repair, service, charging and creation remain distinct.

| Learning procedure | Retained requirement |
|---|---|
| Ordinary trade training | 80 active hours with qualified instructor; 10 gp tuition; actual tools/living costs; grants only its stated proficiency/trade 1/XP 0 |
| Ordinary spoken language module | 240 hours |
| Ordinary literacy module | 80 hours, including 40 for script |
| Full language with new script | 320 hours total |
| Full language with already known script | 280 hours total |

Language learning credits actual 15-minute phases, genuine instruction and the 1 gp per eight instruction-hour reference. A shared script or translated passage does not teach the language or a recipe. Keep actual Scribing Insight earning and the three-point/eight-hour supported-passage consultation, specific to its language. There is no universal Insight exchange or ordinary instruction purchase of combat feats/proficiencies. Class, spell and feat choices keep their real gates and pending status.

Short Rest, safe eight-hour Rest/beneficial Long Rest, four consecutive hours of voluntary Morale recreation, and 24 consecutive hours of full Haven care are separate procedures. Debt-clearing Rest inside the reward limit does not refill resources again. Actual compatible patients may rest during care; active practitioners cannot count the same work as their rest. Wound treatment retains eight hours, kit, one Supply and its actual result degrees. A Haven label neither removes a Wound nor bypasses the three-Wound healing ceiling.

Dependence recovery keeps its reviewed seven consecutive no-dose project days, each with one actual treatment hour and one Supply. The designated practitioner uses the chosen Medicine, Religion or Alchemy method and the final substance DC, or fallback 14 where applicable. Failure needs a fresh seven-day project. A qualifying eight-hour daily care activity includes that day's treatment hour, grants one nonstockpiled advantage on the next recovery check, and prevents only an actually defined ordinary escalation. It does not cure dependence automatically or alter crashes, doses or tolerance. Exact interruption rules still govern.

| Core service reference | Tiers I–III | Tiers IV–VI |
|---|---:|---:|
| Clean water, one mapped person-day | 1 cp | 1 sp |
| Ordinary hot meal | 2 cp | 5 sp |
| Qualifying eight-hour lodging/berth, per person | 5 sp | 5 gp |
| Verified map update | 1 gp | 25 gp |
| Preservation, per begun 10 lb accepted bill | 5 sp | 10 gp |
| Ordinary repair service | 10% of applicable undamaged reference | 15% |
| Actual escort journey, per floor/person | 2 gp | 50 gp |

These are available-provider offers, not automatic stock or extra fees on an inclusive bill. Water and meal quantities follow part 1. Lodging supplies a real qualifying stay, not automatic rest rewards. A map update takes one active desk hour using actual relevant records; it adds their true facts, not secured terrain or firsthand mapping marks. An escort needs its actual team, journey, food and availability, not a free additional army.

Preservation charges once per begun 10 lb of the accepted nonempty bill. The provider supplies compatible real inputs and the exact method/lot limits: use 30 minutes per lot and the 24-hour treatment where that is the governing method. Payment cannot preserve an unsupported active core. Ordinary repair service takes the actual qualifications and at least max(1 hour, maximum HP/10 hours); special Broken magic bodies retain their specific requirements. An inclusive service price replaces the self-repair material bill.

### FL-25 — Coins, unlawful operations, labor and rescue

Track each coin's denomination, composition, count/weight, custody and actual mint acceptance. Declare the assay method and suspected defect before the roll; do not make a generic and specific test for the same uncertainty or switch to the easier one after failure.

| Generic bulk assay | DC |
|---|---:|
| Obvious defect | 10 |
| Mixed/clipped coin | 13 |
| Plated/adulterated coin | 16 |
| Expert false mint | 19 |

| Specific Core method/defect | DC | Preserved value range |
|---|---:|---|
| Trace shaving, 1–3% | 12 | 95–99% face |
| Light shaving, 4–8% | 14 | 85–95% face |
| Heavy shaving, 9–15% | 16 | 70–85% face |
| Ruined shaving, 16–30%, once weighed | 10 | 40–70% face |
| Washed/plated, without cutting or magic | 18 | 5–25% recoverable value |

One ordinary assay work unit handles up to 100 coins or 10 lb, whichever limit is reached first, in 30 active minutes. Keep materially different variants identified. Use INT (Investigation) or the relevant actual proficient tool once. An applicable functioning jeweler's-scale, touchstone, milled-edge or merchant-test-kit method can supply its source proficiency contribution to that assay, not a second proficiency bonus, general training or supernatural detection. Destructive testing spends the actual sample. Routine documented weighing needs no new roll.

Failure leaves uncertainty. Only a declared false-assessment stake can give a fallible critical-failure report; no die changes metal. New evidence or a genuinely different test can justify more work and another roll. Changing the appraiser alone cannot.

KEEP ordinary assay fees at 1% of the face value of the actual accepted assay bill, minimum 1 gp for that real service, and remint fees at 2% of the recovered legitimate metal value accepted for actual reminting. A combined commissioned assay bill receives its minimum once, not per coin, variant or thirty-minute stage; services not incurred create no fee, and an already-owned private testing toolkit creates no fictional desk fee to its owner. KEEP the Loop greater-of rule for a holder lawfully established to be knowing, not a 10% cap. NEW explicit total-payment and fee allocation: let F be the confirmed false lot's face value, A the actual assay fee, R the actual remint fee, and S=A+R for the same assessed lot/bill; the total monetary liability is P=max(0.10×F,S), allocated to service fees S and an additional criminal fine of P−S=max(0,0.10×F−S). Do not charge P plus those fees or levy another 10% fine on top. Compute on exact amounts under FL-21/EC-10's existing final-transaction copper rounding; allocating the total between fees and fine does not itself create extra transactions. Credit actual prior payments and fees already withheld from returned legitimate metal once against the same liability, leaving only its unpaid balance. Confirmed counterfeit coin or plated false currency is seized in full only through actual lawful authority; seizure is neither a fee nor a payment credit. Genuine coins and legitimate metal remain separately identified and are returned with actual fee deductions accounted for once. An innocent holder with documented provenance pays no criminal fine; absent a lawful knowing-holder finding, this rule imposes no criminal fine. Suspicion, ancestry, a failed die or missing documentation alone does not establish knowledge. Assessment, collection and custody require actual authorized actors; an unpaid amount remains an obligation, not automatic borrowing or remote confiscation. Cutting/reminting takes the provider's actual work and links consumed/replaced coin identities to real metal/output quantities without duplication.

Unlawful trade still needs real contacts, agreed terms, actual cargo movement, any real concealment/document act and actual payment. Resolve only stages that exist as separate uncertainties. A deal does not create forged authority, a new skill or a free Scribing recipe.

Underways Heat is 0–6 for the actual operation/institution pursuit history. A triggering act adds its highest applicable category once: petty illicit 1, restricted 2, strategic theft/smuggling 3, trafficking/forbidden 4. At each completed 24-hour Haven interval with an active operation, roll once; d6 at or below Heat triggers the declared enforcement complication. Heat 0 has none. Faction reporting does not add a second roll.

An actual accepted bribe of 10% of the agreed lot value reduces Heat by 1, or 25% by 2, spending the payment once. An active operation's Heat cannot fall below 1; no bribe is paid at 0. Refusal or unsuccessful negotiation grants no reduction. Renaming/repacking an operation does not reset evidence, Heat or unresolved legal consequences. Enforcement requires actual actors, authority and means, not remote automatic seizure.

People remain actors with factual and asserted legal status recorded separately: Free, Contracted, Debt-Bound, Penal, Enslaved, Rescued/Liberated or Disputed. A label creates no ownership, restraint or immunity. Use actual body weight, equipment and reviewed casualty Load: Tiny 1.5, Small 3, Medium 6, Large 12; larger unsupported bodies require their profile. Petrification multiplies body pounds by 10, not casualty Load. All melded and external passenger equipment burdens an outside carrier. Voluntary passengers use their actual seats/custody facts, not captive ownership.

Ordinary lawful wages are 1 gp/day unskilled, 3 gp skilled or 10 gp specialist; dangerous expedition work doubles them unless the named service or real contract supplies its own rate. Do not charge both. A wage day buys at most two eight-hour work Watches and a protected Rest opportunity. Extra risky/emergency duty requires actual agreement and its costs/Rest Debt. A lawful debt bond records principal, credited wages and agreed simple interest no greater than 10% per literal Opening; no hidden compounding or automatic debt. Coercion, kidnapping, fraud or unlawful confinement cannot demand an invented buyout.

An actual trafficking investigation uses an Evidence Clock 0–4. A concrete accessible lead takes one active hour and the appropriate Investigation/relevant-skill task at declared DC/stakes. Success adds 1, Critical Success 2, capped at 4; repeated evidence adds nothing. At 4, seek a real authorized warrant/hearing or plan a genuine rescue/raid. The clock does not conjure police, prove a false report or resolve combat. Guards, restraints and escape routes are actual scene facts. Consent-based adult services remain distinct from trafficking, with the table's boundaries controlling.

### FL-26 — Advancement and the next descent

Choose milestone or optional XP advancement before awards. Do not grant both for the same achievement. The milestone default grants an earned stated level when its actual objective is confirmed, not simply after a Watch, appraisal or return. Follow the real class, ASI, Arch and Hero schedule and pending acquisition constraints. A higher maximum does not heal or refill a pool; retain spent resources and grant only the actually earned new unspent Hit Die.

For optional XP, freeze each eligible recipient's actual gap to their next level at the beginning of the distinct award scene:

| Achievement | Percentage of that frozen gap |
|---|---:|
| Routine | 5% |
| Standard | 10% |
| Hard | 15% |
| Severe | 25% |
| Guardian victory | 40% |
| Actual profitable extraction meeting its declared threshold | 10% |
| First qualifying route/text/faction discovery | 5–15%, chosen before resolution |

Sum that scene's eligible predeclared percentages, cap at 60% of the frozen gap, then round the scene award once to the nearest 10 XP, halves upward. If rounding exceeds the exact 60% ceiling, limit the award to floor(0.60 × gap ÷ 10) × 10. Do not recalculate earlier percentages against a larger new gap after a mid-scene level. At a level cap or without a supplied next threshold, invent no next gap or XP award.

All materially contributing members can qualify, including real reconnaissance, research, transport and support contributions away from the fight. Roster presence, unrelated waiting, repeated reports or reused evidence are not automatic contributions. Name the actual contribution, unique achievement and eligible recipient. Notifications, sale, claim or auction records of the same achievement do not award it again. Crafting-domain XP stays its own ledger. A valuable failed objective can have a predeclared reward; do not invent one after failure.

Close the episode at its actual time with outcomes and the declared success metric, each member's location, routes/camps, Pressure, pursuit/Heat, provisions and containers, HP/Wounds/injuries/Strain/Rest Debt/resources, companions/devices/corpses, cargo title/custody and terminal harvest/salvage claims. Include quotes, actual payments, liabilities, insurance, attributable standing/renown, pending faction actions and earned advancement.

Then accept the next charter prospectively, with real access, budget, reserves and abort conditions. A new descent does not erase unresolved disputes, replenish stock, reset clocks or replay an old claim. It can proceed only when its actual conditions allow it.

## 4. Social scenes and faction work

<a id="so-01"></a>
### SO-01 — Ask for a concrete outcome

State what your character wants and how they try to obtain it. You can describe the approach in ordinary words; you do not need to perform a voice, accent or persuasive speech.

Before a consequential check, establish this scene frame:

| Field | What to establish |
|---|---|
| Want | The specific change each side wants before the scene ends |
| Leverage | The offer, evidence, threat or withholding each side can actually use |
| Limit | What the decision-maker cannot or will not concede under current conditions |
| Clock | The real deadline and time needed for this exchange |
| Witness | Who can perceive the exchange or later receive a report |

Identify the people involved, the resource or information requested, its owner/controller, and who can authorize the concession. A person may possess a key without permission to sell the building. A clerk may permit supervised viewing without permission to give away the record.

The Director states the obtainable success and its terms, failure, any optional costly alternative, any available extra on Critical Success, and any extra consequence on Critical Failure. Agree the approach, ability/skill, resolution method, difficulty, duration and costs before committing the work and rolling. The Director can preserve secrets while explaining an observable obstacle or that this request cannot currently succeed. A promised ordinary Success cannot secretly require Critical Success or acquire a new price after the die.

If willingness, authority and prerequisites already settle a routine request, record the agreement and carry it out without a check. If the requested outcome is impossible or presently unavailable, no check can create it. You may seek a different feasible outcome. Roll when a meaningful, possible uncertainty remains.

Close the approach when its agreement, refusal or interruption is recorded. Later performance can still be pending. A new request follows the changed-circumstances rules below.

<a id="so-02"></a>
### SO-02 — Resolve the declared uncertainty

Choose the procedure before seeing any dice. A social concession or bounded inference normally uses a task against a fixed DC. Two actors making genuinely opposed attempts can use opposed totals. An explicit feature using a saving throw or passive Insight keeps its own procedure.

These are new ordinary references where no more specific source supplies the DC:

| DC | Negotiable concession |
|---:|---|
| 10 | Plausible, modest and within the person's authority |
| 13 | The person has a substantial reservation |
| 16 | Difficult, but genuinely negotiable |
| 19 | Exceptional leverage for an actually possible outcome |

Do not add a general route-tier or standing modifier to these DCs. Use only adjustments an applicable rule actually grants.

For a fixed task, compare the total with the DC: at least DC+10 is Critical Success; at least DC is Success; at most DC−10 is Critical Failure; the remaining totals below DC are Failure. Then a natural 20 improves one degree and a natural 1 worsens one, bounded at the ends. An impossible action does not acquire a roll or a fabricated failure margin.

| Final degree | Result |
|---|---|
| Critical Success | The promised success on its stated terms, plus only the declared available extra |
| Success | The promised success on its stated terms |
| Failure | The declared refusal/failure; a costly alternative exists only if offered beforehand and feasible |
| Critical Failure | That failure plus only the declared actual extra consequence; a costly alternative exists only if expressly offered for this branch |

An optional costly agreement is separate from the failed check. Accept its stated terms if willing and able, or decline and keep the unspent payment along with the refusal. Accepting needs no new roll merely to enact a feasible offer. Its performance still takes time, and the original roll remains failed. No outcome degree automatically supplies obedience, truth, standing, consent or resources.

For opposed checks, compare the two declared final totals: higher wins; a tie leaves the existing situation unchanged. Task degree shifts for natural 1/20 do not apply, and there is no general opposed critical reward. Ordinary saves remain binary under Core. Apply an operative source's reroll only in its exact window and with its exact replacement rule.

<a id="so-03"></a>
### SO-03 — Choose a skill for the approach

| Skill | What the approach can resolve |
|---|---|
| CHA (Persuasion) | A genuinely negotiable decision on stated terms |
| CHA (Deception) | Belief in a particular communicated claim |
| CHA (Intimidation) | A decision influenced by a credible perceived risk |
| CHA (Performance) | The declared audience response to an actual performance |
| CHA (Reputation) | An appeal to credentials or standing actually known or credibly demonstrated to the recipient |
| WIS (Insight) | A supported inference about observed motive, hesitation or leverage |
| INT (Investigation), History or an appropriate tool/skill | Its actual evidence or knowledge task, such as examining a document |

Add one applicable proficiency contribution, or expertise replacing it where granted. Several persuasive proficiencies do not add several bonuses. Neither a numeric trade level nor the saved standing score adds another bonus unless a specific operative rule says so.

Deception can change a belief without changing history. Insight cannot read an inaccessible document, detect every lie, read minds or force a player to believe a diagnosis. A sound answer can be that the observed evidence cannot distinguish two motives. Translation establishes received meaning, not truth.

A particular lie confronted by active scrutiny can use CHA (Deception) opposed by WIS (Insight). A bounded inference without an active opponent normally uses a fixed DC. Physical forgery examination uses its declared Investigation/tool procedure. A credible threat does not automatically require a saving throw or impose Frightened. A genuine competing social strategy can be an opposed attempt when declared that way beforehand.

Useful true information may settle a routine request without rolling. A successful Performance grants its stated audience response, not a license, access to property or magical crowd control. A Reputation appeal must have evidence the recipient can actually receive. The result does not automatically change permanent standing.

<a id="so-04"></a>
### SO-04 — Use standing as a relationship

This chapter uses the new ordinary standing scale taught in the Haven module:

| Score | Band |
|---:|---|
| −3 or −2 | Hated |
| −1 | Disliked |
| 0 | Neutral |
| +1 | Liked |
| +2 or +3 | Honored |

Standing can influence whether an institution will consider a request and what terms are plausible. It does not itself create permission, credit, an appointment, available stock or authority. It adds no automatic bonus, penalty, advantage or disadvantage to a social check under this procedure.

CHA (Reputation) remains a separate skill: add its one eligible proficiency/expertise contribution to an appropriate appeal. A known score of +2 does not add another +2. Specific acquired item, class and background modifiers retain their actual conditions and stacking rules. Reviewed equipment sale fractions still use the buyer's known relationship where that particular schedule applies; they are not a universal price modifier.

When bringing forward an older character, preserve the original score and its version. A companion record marked +2 and an older Loop record marked +2 did not use the same labels. Unknown versions need interpretation before use; absence is not a recorded neutral score. Keep genuinely earned access, titles and obligations even when deriving a new display.

<a id="so-05"></a>
### SO-05 — Keep alignment-dependent questions separate

Ordinary social checks in this candidate receive no automatic alignment-derived advantage or disadvantage. Shared goals, stated values, promises and trustworthy conduct can provide genuine leverage in the scene; they do not establish a cosmic classification or add a numerical modifier.

An acquired oath or class feature still follows its exact stated restrictions. If a particular use depends on a deep meaning not yet defined, record that specific unresolved requirement. It does not prevent ordinary conversation, trade, travel or a standing record.

The older alignment-modifier wording is preserved for the later cosmology review. This chapter does not establish its compatibility chart or activate that general modifier.

<a id="so-06"></a>
### SO-06 — Keep one clock and a working channel

Everyone participates on the same fictional clock. A ten-minute negotiation involving four people normally advances ten minutes, provided their actual exchanges fit that time. A person's contribution can occupy that interval without adding another ten minutes to the world.

Declare these new ordinary time references before work:

| Exchange | Actual time |
|---|---|
| Short request and answer | 1 minute |
| Negotiated concession or evidence presentation | 10 minutes |
| Formal complex negotiation/hearing | Its scheduled time; an ordinary disputed Haven hearing is 2 hours |

Simple routine consent can be immediate within real brief communication. These references do not replace a printed Action, casting time, five-exchange limit or other specific procedure. Add time for actual extra exchanges or delays, not for how quickly a player speaks at the table.

In initiative, each creature has at most six seconds of aggregate free ordinary speech/signs per numbered round. Replies, warnings and extra turns share it. Each additional six-second segment requires an available Action on a legal turn or an actual off-turn permission. Communication does not create an interruption, Reaction or additional Action. Use the fulfilled language rules for ten-minute rehearsal of up to six simple cues and each cue's actual hands, sight and distance.

An interpreter first receives a comprehensible message, then conveys it through a channel the next person can receive. A three-second warning followed by a three-second rendition arrives at the second listener at six seconds. A ten-minute original speech plus its rendition needs time for both; relays do not happen instantly. Late information cannot backdate a decision, modifier, consent or saving-throw requirement.

An interpreter does not supply a caster's verbal component, new fluency, a mental link, the meaning of an unknown proper name or proof the message is true. Use each participant's actual language, mode and script knowledge. A closed soundproof compartment cannot receive an oral instruction through a high check.

Deafened does not itself prevent speaking or verbal components. Silenced prevents ordinary deliberate speech and actual verbal components, Chants and command words, while separately legal signs, thoughts or mental effects retain their own rules. An effect ending does not remove another overlapping prohibition. Form-specific limits and exact Oracle/Paladin exceptions remain in force.

Player explanations and accessibility accommodations cost no fictional Action and produce no fictional speech or component.

<a id="so-07"></a>
### SO-07 — Help with the work; retry when something changes

In initiative, Help uses its actual Action and expires at the helper's next regular start. The contribution must be possible with the helper's real position, channel, tools and information. Social play does not extend the expiry to a convenient later check. The attack-assistance branch still requires a foe within 5 feet and benefits the helped ally's first attack before that deadline.

Outside initiative, this new procedure grants advantage on one check when a helper participates usefully throughout the relevant work interval through its resolution. The helper needs the actual language, access, knowledge or tools their contribution requires. There is no new universal proficiency requirement where ordinary Help has none. Mere presence, payment or ownership of a better tool is not Help.

A helper's time is occupied. They cannot also guard a different room, rest, travel elsewhere or fully help a simultaneous separate negotiation. If an exact long task defines its own trained assistants and numeric cap, use that procedure without adding this general Help benefit. Multiple sources of advantage do not stack; any advantage and any disadvantage cancel to a normal roll under Core.

Resolve one committed approach to the same uncertainty once. Rotating speakers, repeating the sentence or renaming the scene does not grant more attempts. A new offer, authentic new evidence, an audience with different real authority, a changed deadline or a meaningfully different method can create a new uncertainty. Identify that change and declare the new stakes, time and costs before rolling.

Earlier refusal, spent time, payments, consumed evidence and other consequences remain. A source-granted reroll follows its exact cost, timing and new-result rule; it is not permission for unlimited retries.

<a id="so-08"></a>
### SO-08 — Apply the exact magical effect

Charmed is a relationship to an identified charmer. It prevents choices the affected creature knows would willingly harm that charmer and gives that charmer advantage on social ability checks against it. It does not compel affection, obedience, belief, allegiance, surrender, protection, self-harm, consent, ownership or truthful speech. Unexpected collateral harm is not retroactively a willing violation.

Frightened gives disadvantage on checks and attacks while the creature can see its fear source. Its restriction against willingly moving closer to that source continues while the condition lasts, even when the visibility-based disadvantage no longer applies. Forced movement is not willing approach. Successful ordinary Intimidation grants only its declared concession and does not automatically apply this condition.

Use the complete procedure of an effect the character has legitimately acquired and can currently use. Pay its activation and resources, check range and target eligibility, apply its actual save, resistance/immunity, concentration, duration, awareness and ending rules. A social check cannot replace a spell save or recreate a resisted payload. An immunity to charm or a named control effect does not by itself prevent ordinary persuasion.

No general language requirement is added where an effect has none; printed hearing, sight and component requirements still apply. A feature that offers refusal or backlash keeps that choice. A title or a familiar spell name does not grant an unavailable effect or merge ordinary, class, innate and recipe versions.

Knowledge features reveal only information supported by their exact source and subject. Unknown remains possible. They do not establish ownership, invent a buyer or route, supply an absent cure or resolve deferred cosmology.

<a id="so-09"></a>
### SO-09 — Record agreements and respect table choices

Record an agreement's actual parties and authority, property or service, deliverable, consideration, time, communication, witnesses and remedies. A clerk's promise of supervised access is not a transfer of the employer's ledger. A guard's key does not authorize selling the absent owner's property. Later breach is a new event; it does not change the earlier die result.

The Session Zero agreement governs player consent and table boundaries. A skill check cannot assign a player's belief, require acting, override agreed PvP limits or grant their willingness to an effect that requires it. A player can pause, skip or request an accessible way to participate without explaining personal circumstances or receiving a character penalty. Apply any explicit magical control procedure with its own actual agency and refusal limits.

Accepting a costly alternative requires the real payer's choice and funds. Failure alone does not deduct the payment. An unavailable object, absent person or forged receipt cannot provide missing performance.

Consent to an audience or carriage does not imply consent to spellcasting, surgery, restraint, property transfer, dangerous labor or disclosure of protected history. Establish each relevant permission when that operation needs it. A later agreement cannot retroactively supply a missing action prerequisite or turn counterfeit provenance into genuine evidence.

<a id="so-10"></a>
### SO-10 — Let consequences reach the people who react

Standing changes follow significant attributable acts and a real way for the institution to learn about them. Record what happened, who acted, witnesses or documents, what each recipient actually learned, and when. A privately rescued person's relationship may change before any institution hears the story.

Public means exposed or documented to a relevant audience; it does not mean every faction instantly knows. One deed reported five times remains one underlying cause for that institution. Different institutions can react differently when their interests and information actually differ.

Separate established facts from reports and beliefs. An institution can act on a lie it believes; that belief does not rewrite the historical event. A later correction creates a new evidence/response record, not silent erasure or an automatic equal-and-opposite score change.

For ordinary standing, start from the subject's score at the opening of that institution/scene record. Sum the signed proposed changes from distinct eligible events, limit that sum to −2 through +2, then apply the −3 through +3 scale. Keep proposed change, applied change and any overflow. Overflow is historical information, not future credit. Recompute from the scene opening as additional events arrive; do not saturate each event separately and lose an offsetting change.

A successful social check does not automatically award standing, renown or a license. A genuinely performed, attributable public outcome can support the declared event. Existing titles, access, obligations and privileges remain separate records with their own terms. Delayed notice cannot backdate permission to an earlier purchase.

<a id="so-11"></a>
### SO-11 — Faction activity takes real work

Other groups act on the campaign clock using the people, resources and information they actually possess. Their work can continue while your expedition is away.

After a genuine expedition return, the Director reviews up to three watched factions and one eligible action for each. A return closes a real episode with an objective and outcome, including abandonment or extraction. Repeatedly crossing the boundary during the same return does not create more activity. A split party's arrivals stay linked to their actual episode.

The new regular review also occurs at each completed campaign 24-hour boundary. A return at exactly the same time and that scheduled review form one checkpoint. A checkpoint records and reports work; it does not itself advance the world by 24 hours or give free labor, goods, recruits or success.

| Action | Actual ordinary work when no more specific procedure applies |
|---|---|
| Recruit | 8 active staff hours seeking/interviewing one available candidate |
| Denounce | 1 active hour preparing/delivering one attributable statement to an actual audience |
| Buy | 1 active hour arranging/executing an available bill with real goods, funds and authority |
| Raid | Actual preparation, route, encounter and resolution |
| Research | The particular research process; a generic original-evidence question requires 8 active hours and its declared knowledge task |
| Fortify | Actual construction under the camp procedure |
| Bargain | 1 active hour of concrete negotiation |
| Conceal | 8 active hours working on finite evidence, an asset or a route |
| Wait | The actual interval and status, with no automatic money, healing or knowledge |

Specific procedures keep their actual duration and costs, even when shorter. Do not add a second free faction outcome to them. An ongoing project keeps its identity and accumulated work. A return cannot replace it with an instantly completed action.

When real work finishes between checkpoints, its consequences occur then. A later report acknowledges them once. People who witness the result can react immediately without waiting for the administrative review. News still needs a real messenger, rumor, changed offer, guard presence or other observable event to reach you.

<a id="so-12"></a>
### SO-12 — Rival objectives leave a history

Rival groups have bounded goals, strengths, weaknesses and information. They travel real routes, need support, and can offer useful cooperation as well as opposition. Their successes can change the situation without erasing your expedition's choices.

The Director tracks four kinds of objective clock:

| Clock | Length fixed when opened | Qualifying progress |
|---|---:|---|
| Depth | 4–8 | Actual travel or support that materially advances its named route objective |
| Claim | 4 | Genuinely obtained relevant proof, witnesses or legal support |
| Grudge | 4 | Attributable humiliation, loss, betrayal or public accusation actually learned by the rival |
| Collapse | 4–6 | Actual casualty, debt, internal conflict or exposure materially advancing the named crisis |

One distinct qualifying event advances that clock by one segment. Several labels for the same deed or repeated reports do not add segments. A knowledge-dependent trigger qualifies when the rival first credibly receives it, not when an unknown event happens elsewhere. One event can affect different clocks only through independently real consequences.

The Director declares the finite completion consequence and its prerequisites when opening the clock. Reaching its threshold resolves it once only when those prerequisites exist. Otherwise it remains at its threshold with the specific missing requirement recorded. A clock does not teleport travelers, authenticate a false witness, create title or grant an extra attack.

An objective already actually completed needs no meaningless extra ticks. A resolved objective keeps its event history; a new clock needs a distinct objective or crisis. A genuine reversal is another recorded event, not erasure or an automatic refund. Players learn only the rival identity, events and consequences their characters can actually know.

## 5. Mounts, vehicles and transport

<a id="tr-01"></a>
### TR-01 — Choose an actual mount

Use the complete [transport catalogue](TRANSPORT_CATALOGUE_CANDIDATE.md) for Camel, Donkey or mule, Elephant, Draft horse, Riding horse, War horse, Mastiff, Pony and Warhound. Each is a separate profile. “Donkey or mule” means one chosen individual.

The printed price, walking Speed and carrying pounds are retained. The additional body size, mass, abilities, defenses, HP, Hit Dice, attack, training, nutrition, draw rating and Load cap are explicit new provisional game values. A carrying rating does not determine Strength or make a template a record of the particular animal being sold.

Use the profile's natural AC, which already includes its Dexterity, and its fixed maximum HP. Record the individual's current HP, remaining Hit Dice, Wounds, conditions, injuries and training. Saves use its ability modifier without an added save proficiency unless an actual source grants it. Ordinary senses do not grant darkvision or automatic detection. These profiles supply no class levels, RP, spellcasting, special immunity or class features.

An independent animal's listed attack costs its Action and makes one attack using that profile's bonus, reach and damage. Core attack criticals apply. It has no automatic charge damage, knockdown or Multiattack, and the rider receives no extra attack. A controlled ridden mount instead uses the restricted Action choices below.

Large mounts occupy a 10-foot square, Medium mounts a 5-foot square, and the new Huge Elephant a 15-foot square. A saddle admits one physically compatible rider at least one size smaller. A Medium character cannot ride a Medium Pony merely because their weight is low. Count the rider, all equipment, tack, containers and contents against both the mount's pounds and Load limits. Its separate harness draw rating is not extra back capacity.

A positive carried burden at or above half either profile limit applies the one Burdened penalty: Speed −10 feet and disadvantage to climbing, swimming or moving silently. Do not apply it twice because both measures qualify. Routine carriage must fit both full limits and the actual body/seat geometry.

A registered ordinary expedition mount is, by this new default, a significant creature using Core Wounds, dying and death saves. Establish that status before the encounter. An actual Ranger Beast bond instead follows its own HP-only, immediate-death lifecycle. No repair, stable fee, new template or replacement purchase revives a dead individual.

<a id="tr-02"></a>
### TR-02 — Control and training

A physically compatible, trained and willing mount can be controlled. An intelligent or untrained independent mount keeps its own agency. Ownership, restraint and payment do not establish willingness or training.

A conscious, nonincapacitated seated rider can direct intended movement and choose the controlled mount's one allowed Action: **Dash, Disengage or Dodge**. Use an actual trained audible or tactile channel. An audible cue shares the language rules' speech allowance. Reins require an available hand; a trained seat/leg cue works only if that individual knows it and the current seat permits it. Ordinary riding direction adds no separate Bonus Action. It does not issue a class companion command, authorize an attack or transfer rider bonuses to the animal.

A controlled mount with its own regular turn counts toward the controller's existing limit of two commanded independent-turn combat-capable subordinates. The controller's own character is separate. A source-bound companion with only an attached phase retains its own command and offensive budgets; calling it a mount does not give it a full turn. Additional pack animals can be led outside combat through real handling, but do not give a third controlled combat turn. There is no automatic animal swarm.

An independent NPC acting as a party character counts toward the twelve-character roster. A merely ridden animal does not occupy a member seat, although its actions, cargo, provisions and history still matter. Independent animals are directed by their real agency, not treated as free player attack orders.

Recheck control when the mount acts. If the rider is no longer seated or able to cue it, the channel fails, or the mount is unable or unwilling, an unexecuted riding cue cannot compel it. If able, it defensively Dodges and stays still or moves only to an immediately safer available space, chosen through its actual agency. Its existing turn and spent budgets do not reset. An executed cue is not undone. A legitimate trained noncombat lead/follow cue can continue only while its actual conditions and handler remain available.

**New ordinary training course.** A genuinely tame, willing animal can learn one named riding or harness function through 40 active hours, at most four credited training hours per animal per day. The instructor needs Animal Handling proficiency, a suitable safe space, fitting tack and a usable conditioning method. Credit complete 15-minute phases. An interruption loses unfinished prospective phase credit; actual time, feed and fees remain spent. The full-course tuition reference is 5 gp, unless an actual voluntary or paid agreement differs.

Completion requires the real course and demonstrated safe routine behavior. It grants only the practiced function, without an obedience spell, rider proficiency or Ranger bond. A documented already-trained animal needs no repeat course. Wildness or unwillingness cannot be cured by depositing hours. A war-named purchase includes combat habituation only if its actual contract and behavior establish it. Feed, water, tack and stabling are separate unless genuinely included.

<a id="tr-03"></a>
### TR-03 — One complete mount turn

At initiative registration, a controlled mount shares its rider's initiative value and takes one complete regular turn immediately **after** the rider's complete regular turn. Their movement and Actions do not interleave. You cannot move the mount twenty feet, insert a free rider attack segment, then finish the mount's movement.

You may Ready a permitted Action through the normal procedure, paying your Action and later Reaction, with its actual trigger, components and duration. This creates no free attack. An independent mount keeps its own initiative and choices.

The mount has its own regular-start/end markers, movement, normal Action and applicable Reaction budget. Its controlled Actions remain Dash, Disengage and Dodge. Control grants no offensive Reaction or opportunity attack; a specific defensive Reaction works only if actually available. It makes its own death saves when its lifecycle requires them. A rider's extra turn does not create another mount turn, start, recharge or condition tick.

Each physical actor receives at most one scheduled regular turn per numbered round. Mounting, dismounting or changing controllers retains its spent budgets and original allocated slot for that round. A new valid controlled relation takes its new rider-linked position beginning next round. This round, use the existing unspent slot if any; never replay a taken one.

A mount entering mid-round uses a legal not-yet-passed insertion under the combat-entry rules, or starts next round. It does not replay the new rider's earlier slot. Changing to independent behavior also grants no second turn. Separate rider and mount effects still expire at their own stated markers even when initiative values match.

<a id="tr-04"></a>
### TR-04 — Mounting, dismounting and losing the seat

An ordinary mount or voluntary dismount costs half your current walking Speed, rounded down in feet, from your remaining regular movement. A positive Speed whose half is below one instead costs one foot. Speed 0 does not permit it. You may perform at most one mount **or** dismount operation per regular turn; an extra turn supplies no additional operation through this rule. The cost is movement, not a Bonus Action or free object interaction.

Start within five feet of the accessible seat at a reachable height, with a stable approach, one usable supporting hand and a physically fitting destination. A high Elephant saddle needs actual steps, help or a legal route to reach it; an exotic saddle does not lift you. If Prone, first stand using the real cost or use another valid assisted procedure. Mounting does not remove Prone.

To dismount, choose a visible, free adjacent space within five feet of the relevant body boundary at a reachable level. A wall, another creature, an inadequate gap or an unpaid fall is not a legal destination. Insufficient remaining movement or an inaccessible seat prevents the ordinary operation. A specific acquired feature overrides only its actual clause. The animal's remaining movement and Actions stay unchanged.

When one resolved source event moves a seated rider's mount against its will **or** knocks it Prone, the rider makes one DC 10 Dexterity saving throw. If that same event does both, make one save. Distinct events can each trigger a save. This is a binary Core save with no generic natural 1/20 degree shifts.

On failure, leave the seat and land Prone in the first legal adjacent space from your recorded dismount preference, or the Director's neutral accessible ordering. Resolve actual height and falling under the falling rules; unseating adds no fixed damage by itself. On success, retain the seat only if support remains physically possible.

A Prone mount does not automatically make its successful rider Prone, but its awkward body and blocked movement still matter. A rider independently made Prone gains that condition without another seat save. Voluntary ordinary travel alone causes no save. If an exact effect ejects you regardless of a save, use that effect and the actual landing. Impossible support or landing resolves through the real fall/entrapment situation, never placement inside a wall.

<a id="tr-05"></a>
### TR-05 — Position, areas and opportunity attacks

Record the rider's actual saddle point or fitting five-foot subspace on the mount before movement. Attacks, reach, line of effect and area exposure use that position, not every edge of an Elephant's footprint. Moving to another accessible seat position takes your own real movement, path and support.

Rider and mount remain two creatures with separate AC, HP, conditions and resources. An area can affect both if it actually includes each; resolve separate saves and damage histories. Riding grants no automatic cover, shared HP or immunity. Actual barriers and body geometry can supply cover under the normal rules.

When the mount's own voluntary movement provokes a legal opportunity attack, the reactor may choose the mount **or** rider. The chosen target must actually be visible and within legal reach/line at the departure edge. Spend one available Reaction for one permitted ordinary attack before completing that departure step. One provoking move is not an attack on each creature, and a distant rider's seat does not become reachable merely because the mount is adjacent.

The mount's Disengage prevents the opportunity provoked by its movement, including this rider-or-mount choice. The rider's own Disengage protects the rider's own voluntary movement; it does not give the mount Disengage. Your own movement around the seat or while dismounting uses your own opportunity-attack rules.

Forced displacement or teleportation does not provoke a generic movement opportunity attack, although an actual seat-save trigger or other specific effect may apply. An attack against the mount does not automatically damage or unseat its rider; its actual consequence must trigger that result.

Vehicle carriage does not use this living-mount exception. Passive displacement of vehicle occupants gives no generic opportunity attack without a specific source. Moving pullers can provoke for themselves, without granting an attack through a wall against a distant passenger.

<a id="tr-06"></a>
### TR-06 — Movement belongs to the actor who performs it

Riding changes your position, but it does not automatically spend your movement, perform your leap, count as your flight or charge, deal your weapon damage or activate a class feature. Track who moves under their own power, who is carried, the path, forced or teleport displacement, the budget spent, and whose turn it is. A rule asking whether someone is within a distance still uses actual positions.

Cavalry and Mounted Combatant remain separate pending feats. Ordinary riding grants neither their attack advantages nor Cavalry's +10 mount Speed. Their different prerequisites and attack scopes are not merged or assumed to stack.

Dragoon Momentum keeps its actual engine: genuine self-powered movement of at least 15 feet toward a tactical destination on the Dragoon's regular turn supplies its one movement gain; a qualifying personal weapon-damage event supplies the other default gain. All gains and refunds remain capped at two per round. Being carried, forced movement, teleportation, movement in place, allies, summons or another actor's hit does not qualify. A mount's leap is not the Dragoon's paid leap technique.

**New narrow transport interpretation:** the Dragoon's separate requirement to move at least twenty feet on its turn before a Weave cast counts actual voluntary self-propelled path, including a legitimately paid source leap. It excludes carriage, forced movement and teleportation. Keep the actual before-cast window and the Dissonance reduction of one, minimum zero. It grants no RP discount or additional Momentum.

Other class, oath, ancestry, spell and companion movement rules retain their exact acquired effects. This interpretation does not rewrite every distance condition or prohibit an actual feature expressly benefiting passive carriage.

### TR-07 — Tack, food, water and boarding

Use the catalogue's actual tack profiles and fitting times. These new empty masses are separate from the old blank source weights:

| Tack | Empty mass |
|---|---:|
| Bit and bridle | 2 lb |
| Exotic saddle | 36 lb |
| Military saddle | 24 lb |
| Pack saddle | 15 lb |
| Riding saddle | 18 lb |

These ordinary tack bodies have AC 15 and 5 HP. Saddlebags contain two actual cells of 20 lb and 0.5 cubic feet each, for 40 lb and one cubic foot total. Fit, balance, closure and shape still matter; they add no carrying capacity to the animal. A pack saddle is a load frame, not a rider seat. Military or exotic names grant no feat, seat-save advantage or fall immunity.

Fit equipment to the named animal's current anatomy. A new draw harness costs 5 gp and weighs 8 lb per compatible Medium/Large puller; the Huge version is 20 gp and 32 lb. Each has AC 15, 5 HP and five-minute setup. A bridle is not a complete draw harness. An actual purchase already including the fitting harness need not buy it again.

**Barding.** Choose an actual reviewed ordinary armor model. Its price is four times that baseline, replacing rather than compounding the general Large price multiplier. New mass multipliers are ×2 Medium, ×4 Large and ×8 Huge; armor HP multipliers are ×1, ×2 and ×4 respectively. Use that armor's real AC formula, Dexterity cap, Strength requirement and Stealth penalty. Choose between eligible armor and natural-AC formulas; do not add them together. Different grades or magic require their own real records.

New armor habituation takes eight active hours for one named armor category, at most four credited animal-training hours/day, in completed fifteen-minute phases. It requires a tame, willing, trained mount, fitting armor, a competent Animal Handling instructor and actual suitable work. Ordinary full-course tuition reference is 1 gp or the agreed rate. It grants only safe ordinary wearing competence, not rider proficiency or immunity to armor restrictions. Previously documented training needs no repeat course. Donning/doffing takes the actual armor time and a capable helper; changing form does not resize armor.

**Provisions.** The printed 5 cp daily feed reference buys one actual species-compatible animal-day quantity from the catalogue: Camel 12 lb, Donkey/mule 10, Elephant 60, Draft horse 16, Riding horse 12, War horse 16, Mastiff 2, Pony 8, Warhound 3. This is an explicit provisional price/quantity choice. Actual compatible stock is required. Water is separate: respectively 32, 24, 128, 40, 32, 40, 8, 16 and 12 pints/day, with real containers and weight. These replace the human Supply basket for those animals; add no human oil ration or free desert-fasting trait. Unmet daily food/water causes at most one ordinary expedition Strain for that day, not a duplicate for each missing component.

**Stabling.** Five sp/day buys one actual fitting safe stall or pen for 24 hours, ordinary supervision/cleaning and ten active caretaker minutes per animal-day. Food and water are separate unless the real quote includes their quantities. A small stall cannot accommodate an Elephant because the posted price was paid. Real suitable rest can qualify normally; payment does not heal, cure, refill Hit Dice or grant a second Long Rest. Emergencies and treatment require their actual additional work.

### TR-08 — Drawn vehicles and the two sleds

### RP-A05 — Bind relationships to real institutions

Give each actual campaign institution a stable identity and record its public name, scope/jurisdiction, authorized agents, relationship to the character, source references, visibility and actual resources/obligations. Link it to source categories only where the relationship is established.

Similar category names are not automatic aliases. Religious orders, mainstream faith and good temples can refer to different groups; so can arcane guilds, arcane institutions and academic factions. A political split or merger is a new event with real relationships, not a command to sum scores or keep the highest.

An unknown saved key can later be bound to a real local institution, or remain unsupported, without being discarded. New local institutions can use this ordinary record once their actual facts are supplied. A name alone does not establish world history, allies, enemies, doctrine or special powers.

Public information includes only identities and relationships the audience may know. Private membership, backers and dossiers remain private. Literal faith, planar, prophecy and resonance references create no inferred spell, RC, RP or cosmological effect.

### RP-A06 — Record signed events without losing history

A standing event records its unique identity, original scene, subject, actual institution, cause and received information, witnesses/evidence or rumor, source policy/version, signed proposed change, scene-opening score/version, chronological position, actual result, overflow, time and permitted audience. Titles, offices, licenses, debts, NPC attitudes and renown remain linked separate records.

For ordinary events, propose −2, −1, +1 or +2 according to the actual declared consequence. Within one institution/subject/scene:

1. Add the signed proposals from distinct eligible causes.
2. Limit that sum to −2 through +2.
3. Add it to the **original scene-opening score**.
4. Limit the resulting score to −3 through +3.

Keep both scene-limit overflow and scale saturation as history. Neither is credit for a future scene. When another event in the same scene arrives, recompute from the opening score and all its recorded proposals; do not add the cumulative total to the latest result again.

For example, opening +2 with a +2 proposal ends at +3: applied change +1, scale overflow +1. A later independent −2 proposal in that same scene makes the signed sum zero, returning the result to +2. Applying each saturated step in isolation would incorrectly yield +1.

A genuinely predeclared campaign-defining exception changes only its specified scene cap/magnitude, not the scale, raw source values or prior history. Record it before resolution rather than using the exception after an unwanted result.

Duplicate event IDs, duplicate claims for the same cause/institution/scene, stale versions, invented policies and numerical updates to unresolved values do not apply. A correction appends a replacement or compensating interpretation identifying the original contribution and replay basis; it does not delete the historical deed or an unrelated relationship.

Correcting an institution's belief after a false report is a real later information/response event. Correcting an erroneous initialization is a different operation. Both retain what was previously recorded.
