## recipe-160 — Refined Grave Lure

**DRAFT; exact review pending.** public; Fishing level 4; tier 2; Common; crafting DC 10; Standard quantity 5.

**Preserved source effect:** Grants +1 to Fishing checks. On a successful catch, gain 1 additional unit of usable meat or material; tagged bait preferentially attracts unholy-aligned creatures.

**Operational effect:** On one eligible ordinary provision catch check, this actual participating Lure offers +1 in equipment.leather-task-enhancement; choose the strongest independently eligible modifier once. It offers 1 in equipment.fishing-extra-recovery, separately compared at secure capture among sources that qualified at the check and still qualify. The first secure capture records one recovery evaluation for that physical creature, including zero; later recapture cannot raise it. Authorize only available disjoint real reserve units for later lawful harvesting, never immediate meat or extra creatures. Its one selectable attraction affinity is Unholy; choose one source before one finite, weighted response allocation within 30 feet of connected water path. The complete item must have positive structural HP, remain unsuppressed and unmelded, and actually participate in the registered method. It is reusable, but supplies no bait/chum or new opportunity.
