# Twelve Enchantment cantrips — actual source conference

Conversation: Calibration Review

These twelve are **SOURCE-RECONCILED / DRAFTABLE for exact authored cards**. All retain only the supplied rank-0 access. No tags independently add conditions, movement, healing, wards, or control.

1. **False Comfort** — **Action; 30 ft; one creature perceived with LoE; V/S; instantaneous sensation plus NEW bounded distraction.** Target makes a **NEW WIS save**. On failure it experiences a convincing subjective surge of relief/recovery, such as warmth, easing pain, or the impression that healing has begun, but receives **no HP, temp HP, stabilization, healing event, Wound recovery, condition removal, or healing trigger**. It has disadvantage on its **next Perception check** before the end of its first future regular turn; outside initiative the rider expires after 6 seconds. Consume the rider on that check even if another source already supplies disadvantage. The sensation does not alter the creature's actual knowledge that it is wounded when wounds, damage, HP-dependent rules, or other evidence make that apparent, and never forces it to behave as though mechanically healed. One pending distraction per target across casters; later failed-save application replaces it, while a successful later cast does not erase an existing rider. No Action denial or biological/type immunity. **NEW:** WIS save, one-Perception-check rider and one-round endpoint.

2. **Glimmering Eyes** — **Action; Self; V/S; 1 minute; no concentration.** Choose **Persuasion or Deception when casting**. For the duration, gain the printed **+2** to every actual check using that chosen skill. It is a flat same-stat bonus and therefore follows the existing strongest-applicable-flat-bonus rule. One active Glimmering Eyes per caster; a successful recast replaces the prior application and its chosen skill rather than letting the caster maintain both. No eye contact is required and the visual theme creates no gaze mechanic. Normal communication, context, target attitudes and task legality still apply. No compelled assent, truth detection, authority, reputation change, friendship, or automatic success. **CONFLICT RULING:** Self beneficiary replaces raw 30-ft metadata. **NEW:** skill chosen at application and one-application replacement.

3. **Disrupt Memory** — **Action; 30 ft; one creature perceived with LoE; V/S; instantaneous; NEW WIS save.** On failure, the target permanently loses its own episodic recall of the **six elapsed seconds immediately preceding spell resolution**, and every actual Concentration application that creature is personally maintaining ends through the normal Concentration-ending procedure. If an exact feature legally allows several simultaneous Concentration records, all records maintained by that creature end; unrelated sustained effects that are not Concentration do not. The spell erases recollection, **not history**: movement, attacks, damage, healing, costs, RP, Dissonance/Refrain, consumed uses, conditions, deaths, physical evidence, other observers' memories and completed outcomes remain exactly as they occurred. The target may subsequently learn facts about that interval from witnesses, records or evidence, creating new knowledge without restoring the lost firsthand memory. Each cast affects only its own six-second window; overlapping casts may overlap but do not recursively reach older memories. No lost Action, rewound turn, erased casting cost, or automatic later immunity. **NEW:** WIS save, exact six-second resolution-relative window and all-current-Concentration scope.

4. **Enthralling Tune** — **Action; 30 ft; one creature that can hear you; V/S; NEW one-round rider.** Target makes a **NEW WIS save**. On failure its attention is magically drawn toward the tone/caster until the end of its first future regular turn; outside initiative, 6 seconds. During that window it has disadvantage on **Perception checks concerning subjects other than the caster/tone**. This does not end Concentration, despite the source phrase “loses focus,” because Disrupt Memory expressly owns that stronger mechanic. It requires hearing but not an instrument. The target may move, attack, cast, react and make choices normally; no Dazed, Stunned, forced movement, Action denial or compelled approach. The tone does not automatically reveal an unseen caster beyond ordinary hearing rules. Overlapping applications do not stack; a later failed save refreshes/replaces the endpoint. **NEW:** WIS save, hearing gate and one-round attention/Perception procedure.

5. **Mental Tap** — **Action; 30 ft; one creature perceived with LoE; V/S; instantaneous damage plus NEW pending benefit.** Target makes a **NEW INT save**. Failure deals **1d4 psychic damage**; success deals none. After all actual defenses and HP handling, determine the existing `actualDamageDealt` measure: temp HP actually absorbed plus ordinary HP actually lost, excluding overkill and later reassignment. Only if `actualDamageDealt > 0` does the caster gain **+1 to their next actual saving throw** within 1 minute. In initiative the benefit expires at the caster's tenth future regular-start marker; outside initiative at +60 seconds. Consume it on the next actual saving-throw event even if a stronger flat bonus supersedes +1 or an automatic-save rule leaves no die for +1 to alter; if no saving-throw event occurs, it is not consumed. One pending Mental Tap benefit per caster; a later qualifying damage event replaces it with a fresh endpoint, while a cast dealing zero actual damage does not erase an existing benefit. No RP, Action, or resource recovery. **NEW:** INT save, actualDamageDealt trigger and one-minute next-save entitlement.

6. **Friends** — **Action; Self keyed to one creature perceived within 30 ft and LoE; V/S; NEW 1 minute; no concentration.** After costs commit, determine whether the designated creature is actually **non-hostile toward the caster**. If not, no benefit applies and the spell does not disclose hostility as a separate diagnostic. If eligible, for 1 minute the caster has **advantage on actual Charisma checks directly interacting with that creature**. This is not limited to Persuasion/Deception because the source says Charisma checks, but it grants no advantage on attacks, saves, spellcasting or unrelated checks. If the creature becomes actually hostile toward the caster during the duration, that application ends and does not resume merely because its attitude later improves. No saving throw is added because the source grants a caster-side social benefit rather than imposing Charmed or compulsory conduct; written broader mental/Enchantment defenses can still apply to their actual scope. One active Friends target per caster; successful recast replaces it. No Charmed condition, unwilling-to-harm restriction, post-expiry hostility, automatic notification, obedience, or belief alteration. **NEW:** one-minute duration, post-payment non-hostile predicate and per-caster target cap.

7. **Soothe Animal** — **Action; 30 ft; one creature with an actual Beast classification or explicit current source-authorized animal record/tag; V/S; 1 minute; no concentration.** A profile name, appearance, ancestry guess or transformation that does not expressly change the relevant record does not qualify; an exact transformation that truly changes it does. Target makes a **NEW WIS save**. On failure it becomes genuinely calm: while the effect lasts it does not voluntarily initiate attacks or knowingly harmful actions. It can move, flee, defend itself through nonharmful choices, interact, perceive and otherwise act normally. The effect ends immediately when another creature commits an attack or knowingly harmful effect against it, or when it actually takes damage; this prevents attackers from exploiting the calm while denying retaliation. It does **not** remove the Frightened condition or any other named condition unless another rule says so; ordinary fear/agitation and this magical calm can coexist with actual condition mechanics. No domination, commands, loyalty, taming, ownership, Action denial, or permanent attitude change. Later failed applications refresh rather than stack. **NEW:** eligibility bridge, WIS save, exact pacification restriction and harm-ending rule.

8. **Faction Sympathy** — **Action; 30 ft; one creature perceived with LoE; V/S/F; concentration up to 1 minute.** Choose one exact canonical faction with which the caster has an **actual current affiliation/membership record**. Positive reputation alone is not enough. Target makes a **NEW WIS save**. On failure it feels genuine temporary warmth and favorable disposition toward that faction as an institution. While active, the caster gains **+1 to Persuasion checks made directly to that target about the chosen faction, its interests, or cooperation with it**, subject to normal task legality and strongest-flat-bonus rules. The feeling does not create membership, reputation, credentials, legal authority, institutional standing, obedience, loyalty or Charmed. It ends early if the caster, or a creature the target actually recognizes as acting on behalf of that faction, attacks or knowingly subjects the target to a harmful effect. Concentration replacement handles recasting normally. Movement/Ward tags grant nothing. **NEW:** actual-affiliation faction predicate, WIS save, +1 faction-related Persuasion interface and hostility ending.

9. **Alignment Empathy** — **Action; 30 ft; one creature perceived with LoE; V/S; NEW 1 minute; no concentration.** Target makes a **NEW WIS save**. On failure it briefly experiences a genuine sense of personal resonance or kinship with the caster's **actual current alignment orientation**, without learning the caster's canonical axes. For the duration, the caster gains **+1 to their next Persuasion check against that creature**, consumed on that check even if a stronger flat bonus supersedes it. If the caster has no actual current alignment record, no rider is created and the spell does not separately diagnose that absence. The target's own alignment need not match. No alignment changes, alignment disclosure, alignment-gated qualification, behavior compulsion, oath consequence, PvP permission, movement or canonical attitude rewrite. One pending application per caster/target pair; successful replacement owns the endpoint. **NEW:** WIS save, one-minute subjective connection and one-check +1 rider.

10. **Resonance Whisper** — **Action; 30 ft; one creature perceived with LoE; V/S; NEW 1 minute; no concentration.** Choose **Guide** or **Disturb** at casting. Guide requires a willing target and automatically grants **+1** to its next qualifying source-authorized Resonance/Weave task. Disturb may target an unwilling creature, which makes a **NEW WIS save**; on failure its next qualifying task instead receives **−1**. Qualification is exactly the Resonance Mote bridge: a real source-authorized task/ability/tool check explicitly concerned with manipulating, diagnosing, stabilizing, calibrating or directly interacting with Resonance/the Weave, or expressly named a Resonance check. It creates no new check, DC or subsystem and does not affect spell attacks, saving throws, Concentration saves, Counterspell, ordinary casting, RP cost, Dissonance/Refrain or legacy Burn/fizzle. Consume on the next qualifying check regardless of outcome. Use the caster's one-minute source clock. One pending Resonance Whisper per target across casters; a successful new application replaces the old mode/benefit. A failed Disturb save creates no replacement and does not erase an existing rider. “Subtly” does not conceal audible V, visible S or identify the caster as hidden. **NEW:** Guide/Disturb ±1 modes, willingness/WIS interface and one-minute pending window.

11. **Minor Suggestion** — **Action; 30 ft; one creature perceived with LoE that can understand a language in which you can formulate the idea; V/S; NEW WIS save and 1-minute salience window.** State one simple, single-purpose **harmless idea** before the save. On failure the idea appears in the target's conscious mind as a plausible, salient option for 1 minute; it is not permanent belief and does not overwrite memory. The target retains complete agency and may reject it. During the window, the caster has advantage on **one Persuasion check made directly to encourage that exact planted idea**, after which that mechanical benefit is consumed; the thought itself may remain until expiry.

“Harmless” is evaluated from actual circumstances after costs commit, not merely the caster's wording. An idea is ineligible if carrying it out would directly require self-harm, harming another creature, entering an actual meaningful hazard, surrendering/discarding materially valuable property, revealing a protected/private secret, committing a hostile act, or knowingly violating a binding oath/duty. If actual circumstances make the proposal non-harmless, no idea is planted and the spell does not reveal the hidden reason. No compelled Action, movement, obedience, Charmed condition, confession, dispossession, eight-hour command, truth verification, or permanent belief. Ordinary observation of V/S can still reveal that magic was cast. **NEW:** language gate, WIS save, one-minute salience and one-check Persuasion support.

12. **Beguiling Gaze** — **Action; 30 ft; one creature with which you can establish mutual visual gaze or an actual analogous directed visual sense, within LoE; V/S; 1 round.** Target makes a **NEW WIS save**. On failure the gaze locks until the end of the target's first future regular turn, excluding any current turn already in progress; outside initiative it lasts 6 seconds. While locked, the target cannot **voluntarily avert its primary gaze from the caster or voluntarily close/cover its visual organs solely to break the gaze**. For any separate rule that asks whether it is looking at or meeting the caster's gaze, treat it as doing so while this lock remains.

This creates **no universal facing system**. The target may still move, attack, cast, interact, perceive other things with ordinary peripheral/other senses, and choose targets normally. The spell itself grants no gaze attack and imposes no generic attack/Perception disadvantage. It does not compel movement to preserve range or sight. The lock ends early if caster and target exceed 30 ft, LoE/visual contact is actually broken, either participant becomes unable to maintain the relevant sight, or the caster voluntarily ends the gaze. A creature lacking eyes is not categorically immune if its actual anatomy/sense supplies a genuine gaze analogue; otherwise no mutual gaze can be established. One active Beguiling Gaze per caster; a later failed-save application replaces the prior lock. **NEW:** WIS save, mutual-gaze predicate, maintained-contact endings and explicit no-facing-engine boundary.
