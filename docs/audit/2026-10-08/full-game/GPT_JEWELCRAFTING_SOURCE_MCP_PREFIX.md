## JC-D02. Resolve classification, the word “one,” and the eligible beneficiary

**NEW classification:** These outputs are durable magical nonartifact equipment. Their printed modifier is passive. Neither that modifier nor their charge use requires attunement, RP, concentration or spellcasting. Charge use receives its own finite activation procedure in JC-D07.

**NEW interpretation of “one”:** The printed modifier applies **once to an individual qualifying check or saving throw**, and remains available for later independently qualifying checks or saves while the item remains eligible. It is not consumed, is not limited to once per dawn, and does not require selecting one permanent skill when crafting or equipping.

This explicitly resolves the ambiguous source wording. It does **not** mean every roll qualifies: JC-D03 supplies the exhaustive domains, and JC-D04 limits combination.

The beneficiary is the **actual wearer making that check or save**. Ownership, an adjacent ally, a handler, a thief merely touching the item, or a separate person performing a task for the wearer receives nothing. Unlike the reviewed container benefits, Jewelcrafting has no operator-benefit route.

The item must be complete, at positive structural HP, unsuppressed, unmelded, genuinely fitted and actually worn in its designated legal benefit position. Evaluate those premises for the physical item at the roll’s commitment checkpoint.

Zero remaining charges does not disable the passive modifier. Concealing a legally worn jewel beneath compatible clothing does not disable it; these jewels do not inherit Tailoring’s visible-presentation or garment-coverage requirements.

These cards grant **no AC-enhancement candidate, including zero**, no resistance, immunity, attack bonus, proficiency, increased ability score, carrying relief or additional spell access. They cannot cancel a selected Crude suit’s −1 by supplying a phantom +0.

### Common exclusions and causal boundaries

**NEW:** Across all fourteen tags, exclude attack rolls, damage rolls, initiative, death saves, concentration checks/saves, crafting completion, formal formula research, harvesting/extraction, repair and combat-maneuver checks such as grapple, shove, trip or disarm.

Ordinary recognition or Medicine checks explicitly admitted above are not thereby converted into a crafting, harvesting or research procedure. A save against an imposed effect remains distinct from a combat-maneuver check.

A spell causing psychic damage can qualify for both Psychic and Arcane, but remains **one save**. A poison save covering both poison damage and Poisoned is also one save. Poisoned’s disadvantage on a later unrelated check does not make that later check poison-related.

For Arcane, distinguish the direct magical effect from a secondary event: a save expressly required by a spell can qualify; an ordinary Wound save following its damage does not qualify merely because the earlier damage was magical. It may independently qualify for Vitality.

**Unholy is a mechanical label here, not a moral or cosmological finding.** No new damage type is created. A donor’s name, “soul,” “spirit,” “sentient,” or an Oracle feature named Curse does not establish any predicate by itself.

## JC-D04. Extend the existing check family, but keep saving throws separate

**NEW cross-trade adoption:** Jewelcrafting flat modifiers on ability checks join **`equipment.leather-task-enhancement`**, alongside the already reviewed Leatherworking and Tailoring modifiers.

For one actual ability check, apply the **strongest independently qualifying modifier from these three trades once**. Do not add them together. This does not expand either older trade’s task domains.

**NEW save family:** Jewelcrafting flat save modifiers use **`equipment.jewel-save-enhancement`**. Apply the strongest independently qualifying Jewelcrafting value once to the save. This grants no save eligibility to Leatherworking or Tailoring.

For both comparisons, validate all equipment designations and physical compatibility **before filtering candidates for the particular roll**. A nonqualifying, suppressed or Broken item does not automatically vacate its physical or designated position. Each candidate needs its own eligibility evidence; a qualifying item cannot qualify an unrelated stronger item.

Class, ancestry, spell, tool and other reviewed benefits retain their own combination rules. This is not a universal strongest-bonus rule.

**NEW charge arbitration:** At most **one Jewelcrafting item may spend one charge on a given roll event**, even when several tags qualify. That item must independently qualify, but need not provide the winning flat modifier. A qualifying +1 jewel can supply advantage while a qualifying +2 jewel, Tailoring garment or Leatherworking item supplies the selected flat check modifier.

Use ordinary advantage/disadvantage cancellation. Several sources of advantage do not overcome one source of disadvantage.

Thus a wearer has at most **seven active Jewelcrafting items**, and anatomy or physical conflicts can permit fewer. Extra fingers, arms, ears, piercings or necklaces do not create additional positions.

Rings and Bracelets do not replace the existing handwear-pair position. They may coexist with Gloves/Gauntlets only when actual finger and cuff clearance, closures and movement permit. They supply no free manipulating hand or component exception.

Brooch/Gem pinning requires an actual compatible supporting garment or other already-supported wearable arrangement. Its host remains a separate item with separate mass, condition and benefits. Host suppression alone does not remove physical support. Loss of actual support can end the jewel’s worn state without destroying the jewel.

Every jewel takes **one Action to don and one Action to doff**. Designation accompanies lawful equipping. Switching between already-worn, physically compatible competing items takes one Action to reposition/refit, ending the old designation first. This does not bypass removal of an obstructing Helm, Gauntlet or other item, and is not a body-resizing procedure.

A held, pocketed or undesignated extra jewel provides neither modifier nor charge use. Its charges remain its own. Lawfully swapping to another real charged item is allowed, but requires actual equipping; this creates neither a pooled reserve nor a refill of the first item.
