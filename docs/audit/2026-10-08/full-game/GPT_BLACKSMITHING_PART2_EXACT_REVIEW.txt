## 2. BS-01 — Replace the unresolved rider timing with an exact state procedure

**Decision: the first actual host turn satisfies the entry guard even when the item makes no attack during that turn. Do not defer the guard until an attempted use.**

“Host turn” means the actual turn during which an attack would resolve, regardless of whose turn it is. It does **not** mean the wielder’s next own turn. At midturn entry, an already-running actual host turn counts.

Outside windows use a **recorded origin and an elapsed-time window index**. They do not restart on another attack, a miss, a declined rider, suppression, transfer or an initiative-mode change.

The following is the exact replacement for the shared guide’s outside-initiative and transition paragraphs.

:::writing{variant="document" id="84726" title="Blacksmithing elemental-rider timing — exact playtest clarification"}
### Outside initiative and transitions — NEW exact clarification

Use the existing game timeline, measured in elapsed game seconds. Do not use real-world table time or advance the game clock by six seconds merely because another creature takes a turn.

**Outside-window origin.** For each physical item with a printed elemental rider, record `outsideWindowOriginTime` when its first relevant lawful, non-copied weapon attack is resolved genuinely outside initiative. Establish this origin whether that attack hits or misses and whether its rider is used or declined. An attack routed into an encounter before resolution does not establish an outside origin.

Once established, preserve that origin for the same physical item. At game time `t`, its outside-window index is:

`floor((t − outsideWindowOriginTime) / 6)`

Window `k` includes its starting boundary and excludes its ending boundary:

`[outsideWindowOriginTime + 6k, outsideWindowOriginTime + 6(k + 1))`

The item can use its rider at most once in each such window. Skipped windows supply no accumulated uses. Another attack, ownership transfer, suppression, breakage, repair, melding or initiative-mode change does not reanchor the windows or clear a spent window.

This is a fixed-window allowance, not an ordinary rolling six-second cooldown measured from every outside trigger. Separate transition restrictions below still apply. The window procedure grants no attack, action, acceleration or permission to strike a target.

**Entering initiative after an outside trigger.** When initiative begins governing the item, examine its most recent actual rider trigger. If that trigger occurred outside initiative at time `L`, and entry occurs before `L + 6`, establish a transition guard associated with that specific trigger event. Its assignment deadline is `L + 6`.

If an actual turn is already running at entry, immediately assign the guard to that host turn. Otherwise assign it when the first genuine regular or extra turn begins before the deadline. Mark the item’s rider budget for that actual turn as spent immediately, whether or not anyone attacks with, holds, transfers or attempts to activate the item during that turn.

The guard does not wait for an attack, hit, attempted activation, the wielder’s own turn or an eligible wielder. Assignment itself does not constitute a rider trigger and does not change `lastElementalRiderTriggerTime`.

Once assigned, that turn’s budget remains spent for the entire actual turn, including any portion occurring after the assignment deadline. The guard does not carry forward to another turn merely because the item was unused. Subsequent genuine turns have their normal independent budgets, even if they occur before the former assignment deadline.

If no actual turn has received the guard when the deadline is reached, the unassigned guard expires. A turn first becoming applicable exactly at or after the deadline is not blocked by that expired guard.

**Actual turn identity.** Use the identity of the actual host turn, not merely the acting creature, a numbered round, or the most recently observed turn. Reactions and readied attacks use their host turn. A genuine extra turn has its own identity. Resuming an interrupted parent turn resumes that parent’s existing budget; it does not reopen it.

Virtual duration markers, empty initiative slots and bookkeeping-only mode changes are not additional actual turns. While initiative governs but no actual turn is running, an otherwise lawful attack receives no elemental-rider allowance from this procedure. It cannot borrow an outside window. Its ordinary weapon effects still follow their own rules.

**Leaving initiative.** If the item’s most recent actual rider trigger occurred in initiative at time `L`, another outside rider use is prohibited before `L + 6`. At or after that deadline, the relevant anchored outside window must also be unspent. Ending initiative does not itself establish a new origin or provide a rider use.

Preserve the item’s outside-window record, actual-turn expenditure records needed for continuing or resumable turns, and transition-guard history across mode changes. A bookkeeping toggle must not manufacture another turn identity, erase expenditure, or recreate an already assigned guard as a fresh resource. Associate a guard with its originating trigger event so repeated entry does not move or duplicate that guard.

**Actual expenditure.** On a qualifying hit, the attacking creature chooses whether to use the rider before finalizing damage. An accepted legal use spends the applicable item budget and records the actual trigger time, trigger context and trigger-event identity before damage mitigation. Immunity or mitigation reducing that component to zero does not refund the use.

A miss, declined rider, ineligible attack, or suppressed/unavailable rider does not spend an actual rider use. None resets an established window or removes a transition guard. Suppression ending can make an otherwise unspent, unblocked budget usable; it cannot reopen a spent or guarded budget.
:::

## 3. BS-02 — Remove the unconditional promise of an “intact” output

The first sentence of the operational paragraphs repeatedly says the base **“becomes an intact magical nonartifact output.”** That is too broad.

The shared durability procedure correctly permits a damaged functional output and, after a singleton Flawed result, a **Broken output at 0 HP**. The individual operational paragraphs must not promise the opposite.

Make these precise changes throughout the 43 operational paragraphs, including inline paragraphs and dictionary-derived paragraphs:

**Output description:** describe receipt of the recipe imprint by each actually transformed reserved base. Do not promise full HP or positive HP merely because an output was produced. Preserve the matched-pair wording for Gauntlets.

**Functional definition:** define a functional Blacksmith item as one with **current structural HP of at least 1**, subject to its actual physical-use requirements. “Functional” does not mean undamaged or at maximum HP.

**Damage snapshot:** use the base’s latest lawful maximum/current HP **immediately before applying the completion outcome**, including damage, repair or a legal replacement recorded during the work order. The original reservation snapshot is historical evidence, not a frozen HP value that can restore subsequent damage.

Add the following governing paragraph and make the card openings consistent with it:

:::writing{variant="document" id="39158" title="Blacksmithing output state — exact playtest wording"}
### Output state and completion snapshot

Each unit actually produced is its transformed reserved base, retaining that physical item’s identity. Receiving the recipe imprint does not itself repair damage or guarantee a functional output.

Immediately before applying the crafting outcome, use each transformed base’s latest lawful structural state to calculate its damage deficit and resulting maximum/current HP. Include any intervening damage, completed repair or legal replacement recorded for that reserved position. Do not substitute the earlier reservation snapshot for the item’s current state.

A functional output has at least 1 current structural HP; it may still be damaged. A singleton Flawed outcome can instead produce a Broken output at 0 HP with a dormant imprint in its recoverable main body. Apply the shared Broken and reconstruction rules. Do not add another output, refund ingredients, erase damage or award an unprinted repair.
:::

This is a consistency repair to the authored text and its state specification. **Do not alter the preserved source-effect paragraphs or change the durability arithmetic.**

## 4. BS-03 — Put the correct defensive eligibility directly into each defensive card

The shared guide and shared machine profile correctly distinguish suit, shield and accessory eligibility. The card-level phrase **“While legally equipped, offer +X”** is less precise and can be misread when the operational paragraph is consumed separately.

Replace that generic eligibility clause in the **16 defensive cards** with the appropriate explicit predicate:

| Item category | Exact eligibility to offer its recipe AC enhancement |
|---|---|
| Armor suit | The item is functional, unsuppressed and actually worn, **and its worn-armor AC formula is the one being used**. |
| Shield | The item is functional, unsuppressed and actually donned, **and it is the selected usable shield supplying the ordinary shield AC contribution**. |
| Helm or Gauntlets | The item is functional, unsuppressed and actually worn in its one legal accessory slot. A Gauntlets unit remains one matched pair. |

Keep resistance eligibility separate. A functional, unsuppressed, lawfully donned shield supplies its printed resistance even when another shield supplies shield AC. A suit’s printed “while worn” resistance does not acquire an additional selected-formula requirement merely because its AC enhancement has that requirement.

For every wearing-dependent benefit, **melded equipment does not count as actually worn or donned for active use**. Its recorded former slot is not an active equipped state. This follows Part A’s body/equipment rules; it grants no new transformation behavior.

Keep all existing enhancement values, resistance types, slots, proficiency consequences, hand occupation and don/doff costs unchanged. The shared guide already supplies these distinctions. This repair makes each card’s operational text agree with those governing predicates.
