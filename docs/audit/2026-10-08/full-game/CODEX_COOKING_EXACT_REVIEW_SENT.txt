# Cooking recipes — campaign-playtest procedures

**Draft awaiting exact manuscript review.** The source conference settles the authoring direction; these completed cards still need exact GPT review. Use the [public cards](COOKING_PUBLIC_RECIPE_CARDS.md), [crafting framework](CRAFTING_HARVEST_PLAYTEST.md), [equipment guide](EQUIPMENT_ACQUISITION_PLAYTEST.md) and [temporary-HP rules](FORM_TEMP_HP_PLAYTEST.md). Original Crafting V4 records and exact V5 public effects remain preserved. Current complete effects control abbreviated or legacy effects. Source `developed` labels are not approval.

## What one serving does

Each source quantity is a number of **individual servings**, not a number of whole-party feasts. A serving has its own inventory identity, recipe, actual ingredients, crafter, quality, completion time, accumulated food age and any predeclared donor-trait record. It is a magical, nonspell consumable. Eating it requires no generic RP, RC, focus, attunement, concentration, class or Cooking proficiency. It creates no spell access, resource pool, Dissonance or Refrain change. Spell-only countering/removal rules do not automatically apply; actual nonspell-magic suppression applies only within its scope.

The recipe's current `mechanics.tag`, if present, is its only operational recipe tag. Keep the original source tags, secondaryTag, ingredients and legacy text separately as evidence. Ingredient properties, names and donor lists do not add finished-food powers or tags. A title such as Feast, Wine Pie, Masterwork or Legendary grants no extra grade, drug dependence, alcohol effect, attack, transformation, artifact status or ability.

## Eating, feeding and interruption

One designated eater or feeder spends **ten uninterrupted active minutes** consuming one full serving with one actual recipient. The eater needs an available hand and a compatible ingestion route; an Incapacitated recipient needs an actual helper with an available hand, access within 5 feet and compatible swallowing. An unwilling active creature cannot be force-fed by this procedure. Actual anatomy and source-specific restrictions control; creature type alone creates no new exclusion. A Dead body is ineligible.

In initiative, spend an Action on each of **100 consecutive regular turns** of the designated eater/feeder and allow at least **600 elapsed seconds** from the start. Extra Actions and inserted turns do not accelerate either requirement. Resolve at the first instant both requirements are satisfied; no 101st Action is needed. If the hundredth Action precedes the elapsed deadline, keep the meal continuously in progress until that deadline, maintaining the required hand, access and recipient eligibility. No handoff preserves progress. Damage alone creates no interruption or concentration check. Loss of required access, a missed required Action, eater/feeder incapacity or recipient ineligibility interrupts.

Spend the whole serving at legal start. Starting before the completed-food shelf deadline commits the serving to this one uninterrupted meal even if the deadline would occur mid-meal. Opening, tasting or separating food does not commit a reusable magical dose. No magic or full-serving nutrition credit occurs before completion. An interrupted portion is lost and cannot be resumed, divided into doses, fed to another creature or returned to inventory. Dying and other independent clocks continue; this meal grants no early stabilization or healing. The active eater/feeder cannot count those minutes as simultaneous active work or required rest. Rest eligibility is determined by its actual procedure.

## Accepting and replacing a feast

After full consumption, the recipient's controller chooses **accept all new feast magic** or **decline all of it**. This is the benefit selection, not an extra Action. Declining retains the old feast but the consumed food still gives its ordinary nutrition credit. No RP, temporary HP, trait or other rider can be taken from a declined feast.

Acceptance is one transaction:

1. End the recipient's prior Cooking-feast record and every remaining rider belonging to it.
2. Remove remaining temporary HP only if the active grant actually belongs to that old feast. Do not remove an unrelated grant, and do not restore any grant discarded earlier.
3. Establish the new feast, then offer its printed temporary HP under the ordinary single-grant rule: a larger offer replaces the existing grant; an equal or smaller offer is discarded. Compare against actual remaining points, retaining an equal grant's original identity/deadline.
4. Resolve the new feast's independent riders. Keeping a stronger independent temporary-HP grant does not suppress them. A blocked/zero rider does not undo acceptance or recover the meal.

Later depletion or replacement of a feast's temporary HP does not end its independent riders. No old feat/meal/shield becomes available underneath a newer record. A second accepted meal may replace a stronger feast with a weaker one; the recipient makes that choice with the actual outcome known. A contaminated failed Serpent Skewers serving creates no successful feast record and cannot replace the old one.

Temporary HP is not current-HP healing and cannot stabilize, revive, reset death saves, pay sacrificial HP or remove Wounds. A living dying recipient can receive the grant only if the entire legal consumption finishes before death. Actual damage, defenses and shield expiry retain the reviewed Core procedure.

## Parent and rider clocks

Unless a card explicitly supplies a different parent duration, its feast and remaining feast temporary HP end at the recipient's next **completed beneficial Long Rest**. Shorter one-/four-hour riders use their own elapsed deadline and also end if the parent feast ends/replaces earlier. The parent can remain after a shorter rider expires; depletion of its temporary HP does not reactivate the rider.

Successful **Neutralized Serpent Skewers (recipe-633)** is the explicit exception: the whole feast, its six temporary HP and poison-save advantage last **eight elapsed hours**. A Long Rest does not independently end that feast. All other existing removal, replacement and death endpoints still apply.

Start clocks at accepted meal resolution, not cooking completion or the first bite. Death ends ongoing feast magic and does not restore the serving, used rider or past RP expenditure. Suppression does not pause clocks or supply a later replay of an instantaneous recovery; restoration of a still-existing ongoing benefit follows the suppression source. Record each meal only once.

## Exact rider families

| Rider | Operation |
|---|---|
| Damage resistance | Resistance only to the card's explicit damage type. No related-type protection, immunity or condition cure. Use the normal defense order once per packet. |
| Poison / poison-and-disease save advantage | Advantage only on actual matching saves. No cure, Poisoned immunity, disease immunity, damage resistance or broad CON advantage. |
| Athletics / Stealth bonus | Modify actual checks using that named skill, not all STR/DEX rolls, attacks, Initiative or passive automatic success. |
| Constitution-save bonus | Apply to any actual CON save, including concentration or Wound/Injury procedures when they call for CON. Do not convert death saves or other rolls into CON saves. |
| Walking +10 feet | Increase walking speed only. Other modes change only if their own rule derives them from walking speed. Existing zero-Speed, halving and other modifiers still control; no missing walking mode is created. |
| Swimming speed | Gain a swimming speed equal to current effective walking speed, recalculated while active. Preserve a higher independently granted swimming speed under normal same-mode rules. No new walking mode, water breathing, underwater sight, special current resistance or traversal exception. |
| Knockdown advantage | Advantage on an actual check/save whose direct consequence includes becoming Prone. No immunity to automatic Prone, free save, standing benefit or broad STR/DEX advantage. |
| First charm/fear save | Gain advantage on the first actual qualifying charm-or-fear save before the parent ends. That resolution spends the rider even if another advantage already applies or disadvantage cancels it. A source requiring no save spends nothing. No cure or immunity. |
| RP recovery | On accepted resolution restore one expended RP, capped at a real existing RP maximum. No RP pool or no missing RP gives zero. No RC conversion, Dissonance/Refrain reset, extra Action or delayed recovery. |

Only one Cooking feast is active, so its own numeric bonuses do not stack through repeated servings. Independently legal class, spell, item or other modifiers retain their actual stacking rules; a meal does not supply a new universal bonus-stacking exception. Advantages/resistances do not multiply. A bonus applies to the actual recipient's roll, not another creature it commands or summons.

**No new RP cap:** Recipes 88 and 103 have no source once-per-rest limit. Every separate genuine serving can restore its printed one RP after full ten-minute consumption and accepted feast replacement. Its real ingredients, food, time and inventory use remain costs. A partial, declined, interrupted, replayed or duplicated record cannot restore RP. Alchemy's different source-specific recovery limits do not transfer to Cooking.

## Food mass and the food ledger

One finished serving contains exactly **2/3 lb edible food**, excluding its container. Full consumption supplies **1/3 of a Small/Medium daily food requirement** or **1/6 of a Large daily food requirement**. Three ordinary servings match the reviewed 2-lb daily ration; six provide twice that food for Large. This is an explicitly authored game accounting convention, not a physiological claim. Other size/anatomy profiles keep their actual dietary requirements rather than gaining invented daily demand from this table. A creature that does not require food can still receive legally applicable meal magic through a compatible consumption route, but unused nutrition does not transfer to others.

This is **food credit only**. It grants no water, fuel, camp consumables or complete Supply Unit. Record food actually consumed separately from the still-open expedition food/water-to-Supply conversion. Food counted as a recipe input or supplement cannot also remain an uneaten ration or pay another meal/Supply entry. Food credit is still real when feast magic is declined, its shield loses comparison or an independent magical rider is blocked. Mere tasting or incomplete consumption grants no fractionated recipe benefit under this overlay.

Each card identifies its credited nutritious input mass. This draft credits these ordinary food inputs after actual recipe preparation: **Meat, Milk, Fruit, Vegetables, Flour, Butter, Honey, Sugar, Eggs, food-grade cooking Oil, and the named Bear, Fire, Poisonous, Quetzalcoatlus, Sea and Vulture meat inputs**. The last group is treated as meat under the successful recipe's neutralization rule. Source Oil here must be actual food-grade cooking oil, not lamp fuel.

All other inputs remain required real ingredients but receive **zero credited nutritive bulk in these cards**: water, salt, wine, spices/herbs used as seasonings, bones, sinew, blood samples, marrow, bark, living root, pollen, petals, eyes, trophies, ichor, goo and bloodstones. This conservative accounting does not pronounce every such material forever inedible; a later exact edible profile may qualify it. Source names, magical properties and possible-donor lists alone cannot create nutrition. No numerical tier or monetary value substitutes for food mass.

For Standard output without catalyst retention, supplementary ordinary ration-equivalent food required is:

`ceil to 0.01 lb(max(0, 2 × Standard servings / 3 − credited source food mass))`.

Before any completion check, reserve enough for **maximum Masterwork yield floor(1.5Q)** after the **worst legal one-unit nutritive catalyst return**. The cards show that conservative maximum. A non-nutritive catalyst does not reduce the nutritious mass. A nutritive catalyst that is returned cannot simultaneously feed the meal. Selecting a different catalyst or lower yield never increases the maximum requirement. Food is supplementary and replaces none of the recipe's exact component amounts, tiers, properties, roles or forms.

Use the reviewed ration reference of **5 sp per 2 lb = 25 cp/lb**. Apply actual supplier quotes and ordinary transaction rounding once; owned real rations need not be repurchased. Preserve the immutable source economy and separately record extra food/storage acquisition. A small rounded surplus remains process waste when actually incorporated. Consume only the supplemental food needed for the resolved yield and actual catalyst choice; unused reserved stock returns to ordinary inventory. Reserved capacity/food cannot be pledged to simultaneous work orders. A generic failed craft with no finished food does not consume a still-unincorporated reserved supplement, but all actually incorporated inputs follow the framework's failure rules. Source ingredients do not become refundable merely because the supplement was not needed.

Excess legal process mass is waste, with no automatic extra ration, bonus serving, salvage goods, resale value or Supply. Added ordinary water may serve the actual process but gives no nutritional offset. Source work tools are not food. All 44 current source recipes have no vessel or nonconsumed component rows; their required kitchen implements remain actual separate reusable tools.

## Containers and capacity

**New baseline: Lidded Meal Crock.** Mundane glazed ceramic; reference price **2 sp**; **0.5 lb empty**; **one quart** capacity; **AC 13, structural HP 2**. It holds at most one of these finished servings. It grants no numeric bonus or preservation. One full baseline crock weighs **7/6 lb**, not 0.5 lb plus a second already-counted gross figure.

Reserve actual food-safe capacity for the maximum possible Masterwork servings before rolling. An existing mess kit, bowl or pot/pan can hold food to its actual capacity without being bought twice. A source-required kitchen pot may hold food after cooking; it cannot simultaneously provide unlimited cooking/storage capacity. Wet/loose food needs an actual container. A dry solid may use an actual food-safe wrapper with an established capacity/weight profile; absent such a profile, use the crock baseline rather than free packaging.

The source-conference permission for immediate consumption applies only when actual compatible recipients and serving/access arrangements are present and ready to begin their ten-minute meals at completion. It is not future storage capacity or free magical ingestion. Any remainder needing to be stored still requires reserved capacity. Do not promise the same one-serving vessel to several simultaneous completed servings. A larger actual vessel can hold a stated number of servings if its real food-safe capacity permits, with the actual vessel mass counted once. The one-quart crock baseline alone supplies no unreviewed generic capacity for other equipment.

The surviving emptied crock or other actual vessel remains an ordinary object with its real remaining/max structural HP, mass and capacity. Reuse creates no food, ingredient, magical serving or recovered source value. The holder retains the physical object; eating does not teleport it. A broken container loses its containment function and needs actual alternative capacity or repair. Spilled/lost portions cannot become new complete servings by recombining empty containers.

## Cooking, stabilization and storage

The first **15 active minutes** are the cooking/binding phase within the existing recipe work. Maintain actual heat, Cook's utensils and the printed station, knife, pot/pan and other requirements. Perishable ingredients must remain legal until binding completes. Pauses, replacement of illegal inputs and abandonment follow the shared framework. A genuinely eligible time-reduction feature scales this phase with the whole work order, rounding the phase up to a whole minute without adding it to the total.

Cook's utensils are artisan tools. Forge Dwarf's actual artisan time and Standard-to-Exceptional quality bridge may apply within their reviewed scopes. They do not waive ingredient freshness, formula/trade level, station/facility gates, calendar waiting, sleep/rest or storage. The one-workyear recipe uses the existing **1,920 active hours** overlay while preserving the source's null active-hours field; the eligible 25% time reduction operates on those work hours, not on the immutable source record.

Bound mixture is stabilized only inside its actual work order. It cannot be extracted as fresh stock, used in another order or sold as a generic raw-ingredient preservation service. Returned catalysts and legitimate recovered inputs keep their source identity, condition and accumulated age. No source-specific magical containment or lawful-harvest requirement is waived.

Successful finished meals start a **24-hour unprotected cooked-food age clock at craft completion**. At that deadline they become Spoiled directly, without an intermediate Degraded stage: no Cooking magic, meal nutrition credit or ordinary finished-meal market value. Existing mundane food preservation may pause age using its actual time, costs, container, checks and maximum lot size. Opening or repackaging never resets age. Spoiled food cannot be made Fresh by resealing or ordinary reheating. Consumption legally begun before expiry follows its committed-meal rule above.

Successful preparation neutralizes only unprinted incidental ingestion hazards of the recipe's ingredients **inside that finished serving**. It does not establish general poison immunity, purify unrelated stock, authorize harvesting, or change the ingredient's raw properties. The explicit failed-skewer profile below overrides that default. Contamination newly imposed by another actual effect uses its own rules and is not erased by the fact that cooking succeeded earlier.

## Quality and failure

Use the reviewed six-band crafting check. A natural 1 is Mishap regardless of total; a natural 20 has no independent success shift. Generic Flawed batches with Q at least two yield floor(Q/2) ordinary servings. A singleton feast lasting until Long Rest has no coherent halved-duration profile: Flawed is Failed even if one secondary rider lasts a fixed hour. Do not halve its ten-minute eating time, invent half temporary HP or keep a free instantaneous RP rider. Recipe-633 has its own failure override.

Exceptional/Masterwork choose floor(1.25Q)/floor(1.5Q) servings or Standard quantity with improved **dedicated mundane packaging containers**. Never combine yield and durability. A baseline dedicated Meal Crock improves HP 2 to HP 3 under either grade using the framework's minimum +1; its maximum cannot be repeatedly compounded by recooking. An existing crock already at that approved maximum receives no additional maximum. Ordinary current damage still needs the actual applicable repair/creation procedure; food does not freely repair unrelated objects.

A reusable kitchen/station pot, tool, unrelated mess kit or magical container cannot gain a permanent durability improvement just by holding a meal. If the actual containers are not eligible dedicated mundane packaging, that option is unavailable; an unproductive generic yield remains its literal floored quantity, with no invented substitute potency benefit. Masterwork's one eligible catalyst return remains independent. Quality never increases creature HP, temporary HP, RP, bonuses, resistance, duration, borrowed-trait strength, extra charges or unlimited food.

## Whole-creature Masterwork traits

For a recipe expressly promising a whole-creature/ecology trait, complete all provenance and profile checks **before beginning the work order**, not after seeing its result. All monster-part lots must either come from the same recorded donor or have actual donors connected to the same concrete ecology by the campaign's approved source/harvest record. Being found in the same dungeon or appearing on a compressed possible-source list is not enough by itself.

Select one actual donor that supplied at least one consumed monster component. Predeclare one exact eligible trait from that donor's approved mechanical profile and pin its source/version and complete operating values to the work order. No eligible documented trait means this recipe cannot begin under this overlay; there is no invented fallback or post-roll selection. This requirement concerns access to a valid source, not permission to import old generated `trusted` stat blocks. Later bestiary review may supply more approved donors without changing existing completed meals' records.

The generic eligible set is limited to:

- A printed walking, climbing or swimming speed. Its borrowed profile gives that mode a base/floor of the donor's printed value rather than adding that value; a missing climb/swim mode can be gained. No fly, burrow, water breathing, special traversal or absent walking mode follows. Apply the recipient's actual speed modifiers and hard restrictions once; the floor cannot override zero Speed or a halving rule.
- One printed damage resistance, with ordinary defense stacking and no immunity.
- A bounded ordinary sensory trait explicitly printed in that donor's profile, such as darkvision at its printed range or a named ordinary-sense Perception benefit. It grants only those stated senses/conditions. No blindsight, truesight, tremorsense or telepathy is included.

Other passive traits require their own exact review. Attacks, Multiattack, Action/Bonus Action/Reaction powers, save DCs, ability scores, HP, spellcasting, resources, regeneration, healing, temporary-HP generation, damage/condition immunity, legendary/lair powers and identity/lifecycle effects are excluded.

An **actual Masterwork margin of at least +10** gives each completed serving that predeclared trait in addition to the ordinary quality choice and catalyst return. Natural 20 alone does not qualify; natural 1 never does; Forge Dwarf's Standard-to-Exceptional output bridge does not. The trait lasts one elapsed hour from accepted feast resolution or until that parent ends/replaces, whichever comes first. Rejected feast magic grants no trait. Temporary-HP depletion does not end it.

Approved source examples are deliberately narrow. Cave Bear supplies walking 40 feet; its Mauling Claws/Multiattack and behavioral Territorial Morale are not transferable. Its approved entry has no Keen Smell. Sporefolk Adult supplies walking 40 feet or poison resistance; its Poisoned immunity and Living Substrate action-origin power are excluded. Sporefolk resistance for Living-Root Fey Dumplings still requires an actual Sporefolk component and documented shared-ecology connection to the other donor. These examples do not approve the unrelated generated bestiary catalog.

## Recipe-633 contaminated failure

Standard, Exceptional and Masterwork produce the normal beneficial Neutralized Serpent Skewers feast. **Any total below DC**, including Flawed or Failed, produces one contaminated serving. Mishap, including natural 1, also produces one contaminated serving and the actual normal mishap. All consumed inputs are spent once; **no 25% material recovery** applies. No ingredient can remain in that serving and also return to inventory.

The contaminated serving offers no temporary HP, resistance, save advantage, RP or other feast magic and creates no new feast record. An interrupted/partial meal creates no divisible poison dose. There is no weapon coating, extractable poison, guaranteed resale value or safety certification. The cook knows neutralization failed and records the contaminated identity, not the successful item name alone. Specific lawful sales/deception procedures, if relevant to play, remain their own systems rather than becoming a guaranteed poison market.

WORKED RULE TRACES

# Cooking manuscript boundary cases

These are worked rule traces for exact manuscript review, not runtime tests or human balance evidence.

1. **Independent shield:** A creature has 12 remaining temporary HP from a spell and an old Cooking Athletics rider. It accepts recipe-81: the old rider ends, the spell's shield remains, the offered four temporary HP are discarded, and the acid resistance operates for its hour/parent deadline. No discarded shield comes back later.
2. **Same-feast shield:** The old feast still owns nine temporary HP. Accepting recipe-81 removes those nine first, then offers four. Choosing to retain nine also means declining all of the new meal's magic. Full consumption still provides food.
3. **Meal timing:** An extra Action cannot shorten 100 consecutive regular-turn Actions and 600 seconds. When the hundredth Action occurs before 600 seconds, uninterrupted hand/access/eligibility continue until 600; no 101st Action is spent.
4. **Interrupted meal:** Interruption at minute nine loses the serving and progress. It grants no RP, food credit or magic; the prior feast remains. The serving cannot be resumed or split into poison doses.
5. **RP:** Two genuine recipe-88 servings can each restore one expended RP, each after ten minutes and accepted replacement. A declined serving restores none. No missing RP or no existing pool gives zero. This creates no extra Cooking rest cap.
6. **Two clocks:** A one-hour resistance can expire while the parent feast/shield remains until Long Rest. A completed beneficial Long Rest ends an ordinary parent and all its remaining children. Recipe-633's entire eight-hour parent instead continues through rest until its fixed deadline or another valid endpoint.
7. **Charm/fear:** Recipe-95's first actual matching save spends its rider even with advantage already present or advantage canceled. A no-save charm effect is not prevented and spends no save rider.
8. **Dynamic swim:** Recipe-104 grants swim speed equal to current effective walking speed. Changes to walking speed update the grant; an independently higher swim speed remains available under ordinary rules. No breathing or immunity to currents follows.
