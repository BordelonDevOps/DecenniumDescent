# EPI-15 — Rekindling Stars
One post-epilogue constellation flickers out nightly. Three finite installed fragments are interfering with it. The Director announces Equinox dawn **E** when this arc activates; activation is at **E−72h**.

### The three public sites
1. **North Observatory:** warning **OPEN THE VENT BEFORE TURNING THE INNER RING.** A 10-ft acoustic zone blocks sound production/transmission, including audible verbal components, but is not antimagic and is not a Residual Null field.
2. **River Bell House:** notice **SUPPORT 3, RELEASE 1, THEN LIFT 2.**
3. **West Glass Garden:** controls **SHADE / REFLECT / OPEN**; gardener note **SHADE RELEASES THE LATCH.**

Recovered fragments do not create portable Null exposure fields.

## Lantern Court containment
The fixed bench has sockets S1/S2/S3, one shutter and one crank. Choose the fragments actually present for this cycle.
1. Fit each actual fragment: **one Action each**.
2. Close the single shutter: **one Action once**.
3. One worker turns the crank for **10 uninterrupted active minutes**. This is continuous active work; interruption means the full 10-minute crank interval must restart, though already installed fragments remain installed.
4. At completion, **every fragment installed for that cycle seals at the same timestamp**.

From an empty bench: one fragment = **612 s**, two = **618 s**, three = **624 s**. A fragment recovered later needs a new cycle. Removing a sealed fragment is real **10-minute tool work** and does not reactivate it or its former machine. Transition does not halve this stabilization.

At E: three sealed restores the constellation that night; one or two sealed means 40 days dim; zero means one 400-day Aethel civil year dark. Later local recovery can still stop hazards but cannot retroactively change the E result. A physically stopped/recovered site does not restart merely because its fragment was unsealed.

**Finite payment:** municipal chest 150 gp, 50 gp once per original sealed/surrendered serial. Separate scrap purse 6 gp, 2 gp once per surviving disconnected inactive original spindle serial.

# EPI-16 — Conclave of Untitled Songs
Bards across Aethel are forgetting recorded mundane melodies. The local cause is an **errant equation echo from a Broken Ladder strand in the upper atmosphere**, not the lost cosmic Equation. The Conclave offers its one vessel, **Margin Kite**, for one retrieval mission.

## Investigation and melody loss
At Conclave Hall, players may either compare the three affected written scores for **20 active minutes, INT (Investigation) DC15**, or hear all three witnesses explain their records for **30 minutes with no roll**. Failure gives no false bearing and leaves the 30-minute route available. The fixed opening losses are: Aren Hale—**Copper Dawn**; Bessa Thorn—**Quiet Ferry**; Caro Vale—**Three Knocks**. Their written scores remain.

After the opening setup, each tracked bard has one eligible mundane-melody event at the first recorded local sunset and every 24 elapsed hours while the strand remains attached. Class cards, spells, language and unrelated memory are untouched. Ten uninterrupted active minutes with a surviving score/recording/teacher relearns one tune. Releasing the strand ends future loss events; old losses still require relearning.

## Margin Kite: start conditions and legal frames
The vessel-local berth/interior frame is x0–20,y0–30. At launch it is physically rotated into x0–30,y0–20 using **xLaunch = 30 − yVessel, yLaunch = xVessel, zLaunch = zVessel**. This is a quarter-turn plus translation, not a reflection. Crew, stations, doors, airlock and fittings rotate with the hull.

Before a normal departure all **four** conditions must be true: (1) intended occupants/cargo are legally aboard within payload/footprint limits; (2) airlock cycle/doors are in a safe departure state; (3) trained pilot and engineer are at their transformed stations; (4) fuel/air/hull/support prerequisites are actually serviceable.

Published operating limits: dry hull 3,000 lb, AC15, 120HP; payload **1,600 lb AND 160 Load** independently; at most six breathing occupants; speed10mph; initial fuel600 powered minutes; cabin cartridge720 elapsed minutes; six one-use 60-minute hoods; four patchpads; three 50-ft/600-lb harness lines; three clamps, two insulated tongs, one lined case; finite hull/canopy repair lots.

## Activation, takeoff, fuel, air and cancellation
The ordinary baseline sets **t0 at actual fuel and cabin-air activation at the start of takeoff**. Takeoff is a separate coordinated pilot+engineer **6-second event** and gives no free travel. Outbound travel begins only after it completes.

Declining **before activation** consumes no fuel or air. Once activated, actual clocks run even if takeoff is delayed or canceled. Wage entitlement begins only when takeoff actually completes. Earlier cartridge activation or preflight engine running adds its real cost; cancellation never refunds it.

Fuel runs only while powered; zero fuel removes powered lift. Cabin air runs continuously after cartridge activation, including engine-off mooring, opening/breach, empty cabin and airlock cycling. A dock refill canister contains 300 powered minutes; safe refill takes 10 active minutes and transfers only what fits. One replacement air cartridge exists at dock; safe unoccupied replacement takes 30 minutes.

## Airlock
Each inner/outer door is AC15/HP20 and each open/close is an Action. Normally one door cannot open unless the other is closed; the inner door also requires a protected airlock. Forcing/destroying a door defeats that interlock and creates a real breach.

Outside cycle: open inner → enter → close inner → open outer → exit → close outer. Return: open outer → enter → close outer → **60 uninterrupted seconds purge** → open inner → enter cabin → close inner. Opening during an unfinished purge restarts that 60 seconds. A safe purge requires both intact doors closed, working hull seal and running cartridge. Protection completion resolves before a same-instant new exposure event; prior due exposure is not erased. The outer door also has a cabin service control.

## Upper atmosphere
Above10,000ft a breathing creature without actual protection tracks unprotected exposure. Every completed60s calls for one CON15 save: failure3d6 cold +1 Exhaustion; success half cold and no new Exhaustion. Actual no-breath permission, protected cabin/airlock or active compatible hood prevents this specific inhalation event. Other ancestry/class defenses apply only according to their actual printed/local interaction; ask the Director for the source-bound AIR-01–14 table.

# EPI-17 — Devil's Signature
A public quartz offer names one willing claimant as heir to three NEW local CF-series Canian engine patents. Veyr of the Second Margin is Mephistopheles’ envoy for this offer. Nothing proves ancestry, soul ownership or universal infernal authority.

## Offer and term start
The term starts only after all four conditions complete: (1) all clauses are actually understood; (2) signature/understood witnessed mark; (3) actual50gp bond transferred to the lockbox; (4) actual access to the three readable plates. T0 is that completion timestamp.

Benefits while active: reserved readable three-plate set, office recognition, priority service appointment, permission to use this local press/remaining stock, and retained CF-1/CF-2/CF-3 technical information. Legitimate copies may be shared; recipients inherit no annual obligation.

Annual record n opens at T0+(n−1)×400days and is originally due T0+n×400days. It must state two incompatible fictional claims, one actual observed record for each and one reconciling question. No real-player secret/belief is required. Delivery uses the actual panel slot or an available Veyr; timely filings remain protected through real review/notice. First actual rejection notice creates one10-day correction window; later rejections do not reset it. Genuine channel outage requires a real timely documented attempt with the actual statement.

Termination requires an informed refund/forfeit settlement. Refund requires at least one accepted record and every record whose original due time has arrived to be accepted. Otherwise the claimant can remain active or knowingly terminate and forfeit the original50gp bond. Termination/default/true death ends future records/title/bench permission but does not take soul, memory, class abilities or legitimately retained information. Already recovered/returned bond money is credited; no duplicate50gp appears.

## Proof press and finite stock
One fixed press: AC15/HP60; six cold cells, two replacement seals, one hand tool, fitted cracked teaching seal, six blank sheets, exactly six ink impressions, plus the separate reserved original plate set. A cell is spent only by a legal activation. The running drive does **not** recharge the one pressure charge.

### Safe isolation and service
For any positive pressure: close inlet I and exhaust E, open vent V and maintain venting until the gauge has been at zero for **60 uninterrupted elapsed seconds**; closing V early restarts that interval. Only then open service cap C. A cap opened at gauge>0 releases the one stored charge once: creatures within5ft DEX15,3d6 force, half success; pressure then0. Destruction releases retained pressure once by the same rule.

Initial teaching sequence uses Actions to set/verify valves, opens C at safe zero, sets route2→1→3, performs2 active minutes seal replacement, closes C and V, legally activates one real cell, then observes a current60s test. Gauge1 + stopped vane means route fault; gauge falling0 means leak; both may coexist. Interrupted states, spent cells and changed pressure remain actual.

### Current readiness — NEW local completion
Printing is permitted only while **pressReady=true now**. That requires: intact supported press; C closed; V closed; correct tested route/valves; functioning intact seal; gauge1 from the **same unspent pressure charge**; that same cell drive live; and a successful current uninterrupted60-second test completed after the last readiness-invalidating change. Actual blank sheet and ink are also required.

Venting/discharge, C opening, seal removal/loss/damage, route/relevant-valve change, gauge0, drive expiry, or loss of structural support makes pressReady=false. Repairing the cause is not enough by itself: a new legal cell activation and new60s test are required. Cells, pressure, seals, paper, ink and prior prints never replenish from that reset.

Each print is one Action, one real sheet and one ink impression. A three-page set needs three Actions/sheets/impressions. Starting stock permits at most two new complete sets.
