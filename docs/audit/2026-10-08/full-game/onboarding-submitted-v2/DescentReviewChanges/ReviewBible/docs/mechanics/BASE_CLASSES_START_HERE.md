# Start with a Base class

There are **17 Base classes**. Arch and Hero describe later progression within a class family. A new character does not receive all three stages at once.

[Complete class library](CLASS_PLAYTEST_INDEX.md) · [Shared test rules](CLASS_PLAYTEST_RULES.md) · [Legal builds](CLASS_PLAYTEST_BUILD_EXAMPLES.md) · [Session log](CLASS_PLAYTEST_SESSION_LOG.md)

These are provisional playtest packets. The approved books and original descriptions remain preserved. Use the packet and shared rules together; do not add conflicting older level-table grants on top.

## Build levels 1 and 2 first

For Bard, Cleric, Crimson Agent, Dragoon, Mage, Mirrorsage, Monk, Ranger, Shaman, Soldier, Thief, Tinker, Warrior and Wizard:

1. Read the class engine and starting proficiencies in its packet. Record the level-1 resource maximum and automatic grants.
2. At class level 1, choose four eligible Base cards, including at least two active options. Automatic cards do not consume these choices.
3. At class level 2, choose two more eligible Base cards. Follow any expressly stated class-specific engine or spell milestone.
4. Keep the chosen cards and their costs in front of you. A feature selection is not a skill point that can be banked or transferred.

Druid, Oracle and Paladin use their own starting schedules:

| Class | At level 1 | At level 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Druid | Druidic, spellcasting, five Base forms and two shifts per long rest | Choose one Circle and gain only its level-2 features |
| Oracle | Choose one Mystery and one Curse; gain its starting identity, Prophecy and shared Oracle features; maximum 3 RC | Curse Manifestation, Oracular Trance and Oracle’s Burden |
| Paladin | Fixed martial/divine starting abilities and the Lay on Hands pool | Righteous Challenge, Bolstering Presence, one Fighting Style and spell access |

## Arch and Hero come afterwards

Ordinary class families enter **Arch at class level 3**, then choose **one Hero path at class level 10**. Follow the guide’s exact card gates and acquisition table through level 20. A Hero card never becomes available just because another class raised your total character level.

Druid chooses its Circle at 2, gains Circle Hero forms at 10, and attunes the historically named Arch forms at 11. Oracle develops its chosen Mystery through Revelation tiers and receives only its own Curse’s later Boons. Paladin chooses one Oath at 3 and masters that same Oath later; an Oathbreaker inversion replaces its Oath package.

The [build examples](CLASS_PLAYTEST_BUILD_EXAMPLES.md) show complete legal progressions. Use them to learn the acquisition rules, then make different legal choices for your character. Actual play still needs to establish numerical balance.
