# Relics, Dungeon Seeds, and Residual Null
## Optional NEW provisional EPI-08–14 module

This chapter is an authored playtest interface for the preserved epilogue material. It does not establish that Infinite Yield has occurred in the active campaign. The twelve Dungeon Seeds retain the original heading **“Dungeon Seeds after Infinite Yield.”** If a Director deliberately uses one outside that temporal premise, it is alternate staging rather than a chronology ruling.

Nothing here changes the current EPI-01–07 module, ordinary spell status, class progression, KT/PT, Hunt, or reviewed recipe catalog except the narrowly identified Null-purge interfaces below.

## Relic Bond

The Shard Pendant of Xi and Concord Spear Fragment require a **Relic Bond**. Spend 1 uninterrupted active hour with the actual item. This is not a rest and requires no check. The item’s eligibility still applies. Transfer, or separation from the item for 10 minutes or more, ends that bond. This does not create a general magic-item attunement cap.

### Shard Pendant of Xi

A 0.2-lb pendant worn at the neck. Any class can bond it.

**Motive-Certainty Refusal.** Magical information cannot report your actual motive, fundamental desire, or intended reason as an absolutely certain fact. This does not create blanket divination immunity. Emotion, surface thought, alignment, observed conduct, probability, and other outputs still use their own rules.

**Declare Contradiction.** Once the pendant has a ready charge, spend an Action to select one ongoing spell-effect record on one creature you perceive within 60 feet. The actual caster and the affected target must both knowingly agree to end that effect. If both consent, the charge is spent and that one effect branch ends. Other branches remain. An area/zone with no individual target record is not eligible. A failed eligibility/consent attempt spends the Action but not the charge. No RP, slot, action, use, or prior damage is refunded.

The charge refreshes only when the bonded bearer completes a beneficial Long Rest while possessing the pendant. Transfer or rebonding does not refresh it.

### Concord Spear Fragment

A functional 3-lb spear built around the surviving fragment: 1d6 piercing, versatile 1d8, thrown 20/60. For its bonded user it is also **Finesse** and **Heavy**. A Small wielder therefore has disadvantage on its attacks under the actual Heavy rule. Finesse permits the same chosen Strength or Dexterity modifier for attack and damage. Paladin or Soldier class level 1+ can bond it, but bonding does not grant spear proficiency.

**Hesitation.** Before one spear attack on your turn, spend a Bonus Action. That attack gains radiant damage equal to half your **character level**, rounded down. The charge applies only to that one same-turn spear attack. A miss consumes it; not attacking by turn end loses it. The radiant amount is flat and is not multiplied by a critical hit. A thrown spear does not return automatically.

### Mirror of Potential Silence

A 1-lb hand mirror. It requires no bond.

Spend an Action touching the mirror and state, sign, or write one **fictional-character fear**. The mirror shows a brief possibility in which that fear is untrue. You gain advantage on your next Wisdom saving throw made within 24 hours. Only one pending benefit can exist at once.

Resolve the save completely, including normal advantage/disadvantage and any legal rerolls. If its **final** outcome succeeds, the benefit ends and the mirror remains intact. If the final outcome fails, the benefit ends and the mirror shatters. If the mirror is lost or destroyed before the save, the pending benefit ends.

A shattered mirror has one finite reformation opportunity in this module: recover its pieces and the three silver reflection tabs found with it, then complete the two-hour Sentinel repair scene. The same mirror returns; this does not reset unrelated Sentinel or EPI-07 histories. If it shatters a second time, this module provides no further repair.

No player is required to state a real-world fear.

### Glove of Null Ink

A 0.5-lb glove worn on one compatible free hand. It is an already-created relic; this module provides no Matrix-dust harvesting or crafting recipe.

Spend an Action to write up to 25 words or equivalent simple symbols on a visible plane within 10 feet, no larger than 10×10 feet. The glyphs last 10 minutes. Only one inscription per glove can exist; a new inscription erases the old. You can erase your own inscription early with an Action within 10 feet.

A creature can derive the underlying text only if, at the moment it observes the glyphs, it sincerely advocates a peaceful but passionless solution to the immediate dispute addressed by the writing. The glove does **not** tell anyone which creatures qualify. An eligible reader still needs the written language or a real translation effect. Blindness, visual obstruction, and darkness still matter.

An eligible reader may remember or transcribe the understood text normally. An ineligible observer can copy the shapes, but the mundane copy does not later become a magical decoder. This is not mind reading, alignment detection, loyalty detection, compulsion, or KT/PT protection.

## Dungeon Seeds

These rooms are finite. Re-entering does not refill relics, creatures, ingredients, books, or one-use rewards. Their exact campaign placement is chosen only when an actual undocumented-floor site is available.

### 1. Memory Forge
A 40-ft square chamber with four metal childhood-toy forms and an east exit. The toys visibly warn before violence. They can be comforted through character-fiction interaction, repaired, fought, or avoided by withdrawing west. Nothing in the room requires real-player childhood disclosure.

### 2. Gallery of Unpainted Murals
A long hall of blank murals. Touching a panel shows a plausible **character** ambition, not prophecy. The exit responds to an acknowledged or rejected possibility. A neutral statement is always allowed; real-player ambitions are never required.

### 3. Echoless Vault
Sound does not carry. Floor vibration attracts a Silence Wraith. Slow careful movement, stillness, a discoverable floor pattern, ordinary legal mobility abilities, or combat can resolve the room. The west entrance remains a retreat route.

### 4. Tessellation Garden
Geometric flowers telegraph sideways gravity. Perception/Investigation can reveal safer paths. A failed traversal uses a catch-net rather than an untelegraphed fatal drop. Actual ropes, climbing, flight, teleportation, and forms work only if you genuinely possess them.

### 5. Negative Feast Hall
A projected steward offers a finite banquet governed by a restraint rule. No creature is forced to eat. The room can be solved by fair serving, careful study, or simply withdrawn from.

### 6. Ladder Debris Shaft
Four broken-light climbing segments rise 60 feet. Use actual Athletics or Acrobatics; fear can be steadied with a Wisdom save. There is no new “Courage skill.” A fixed safety cable prevents a single failed check from becoming an arbitrary fatal fall.

### 7. Quiet Well Remnant
Mask imagery surrounds a faint Null glow. Interaction is optional. Current Living/Cracked mask state can produce the chamber’s finite boon/bane response. The room also contains a local Witness able to serve as the consultation step of the existing EPI-06 petition procedure; that does not guarantee a particular Archon response.

### 8. Wax Library Seed — Silent Cell
This is **not** the reviewed Floor-68 Melted Annex and cannot replace or restore its holdings. Six harmless local wax strips melt if read aloud. Silent reading/copying preserves them. A separate mechanical fallback can open the exit if too many strips are lost. No Equation research credit is created.

### 9. Twin Wind Passages
Two projected local possibilities appear. The party chooses which corridor becomes physical. No second party, inventory, HP state, class engine, resource pool, or alternate historical timeline is created.

### 10. Crimson Accord Arena
Two projections reenact a devil/demon duel. You may influence one side, fight, or attempt an accord. The result changes this performance only; it does not rewrite historical events or grant general planar standing.

### 11. Shardpool Confluence
Four facedown one-use plane cards are drawn **without replacement**. Each successful activation swaps a willing group of up to six creatures into one bounded 20×20-ft landing cell for exactly 10 minutes, then returns them. The four destinations are finite cells associated with Crystal Bastion, Veiled Expanse, Radiant Currents, and Boundless Void. They contain no onward portal and grant no general plane-travel permission.

If a group cannot physically fit at the destination, the card is not consumed. Return tries the marked origin, then nearest legal adjacent placement, and finally the fixed emergency return pad if obstruction persists through the defined second attempt.

### 12. Omega Theatre
The theatre deals only in fictional-character vows. A participant may state one vow fact, use the neutral line **“My character admits the vow is unresolved,”** or decline. Nobody must disclose personal information. A declined scene has a noncombat terminal route. An Echo Doppelganger, if created, copies appearance only—not class cards, resources, inventory, XP, spells, memories, or identity.

## Residual Null Fields

A Null field has an explicit boundary and identity. Track exposure separately for each genuine field.

- Accumulate actual time physically inside.
- Each completed 60 minutes requires a **Wisdom save DC 15**.
- Partial minutes pause on exit and resume on re-entry to the same field.
- A beneficial Long Rest completed wholly outside that field clears only its partial exposure accumulator.
- If already Veiled, later failed exposure saves do not stack additional Veils.

### Null Veil

Until purged:

**Passive Insight:** your final passive Insight is 5 while the Veil is active and unsuppressed.

**Nonlethal damage:** when you create a damage root while the Veil is unsuppressed, mark that root as Null-nonlethal. Ordinary attacks, saves, mitigation, resistance and temporary HP still resolve. The damage may reduce a creature to 0 HP, but that packet does not cause Instant Death, a Wound/Maiming save, death-save failures, or ordinary-monster death. A living creature newly reduced to 0 becomes Unconscious and Stable. An already-0 living recipient does not gain death failures from that packet. Existing Wounds, injuries, previous death failures, conditions and Dead state are not erased.

A nonliving creature reduced to 0 by such a packet becomes **Disabled**: Incapacitated, Speed 0, and unable to function until an actual repair/healing rule that applies to it restores function. Noncreature objects still take normal damage. Direct HP payments and other non-damage lethal costs are not damage and are unaffected.

Damage transfer/redirection keeps the original packet’s Null-nonlethal flag. A genuinely new reflected/retaliatory damage packet evaluates the reflector’s own Veil state. Independent companions deal their own damage; your Veil does not spread to them.

Delayed or ongoing damage keeps the state recorded when its root was created. Later suppression does not rewrite that old packet.

### Shrug Off Empathy

While Veiled and able to take a Bonus Action, make a **Charisma save DC 15**.

- **Success:** suppress the Veil until the start of your next regular turn; outside initiative, 6 seconds. During suppression your passive Insight and damage conversion return to normal. Exposure time continues.
- **Failure:** you become **Incapacitated** until the end of your next regular turn; outside initiative, 1 minute. Current Incapacitated rules apply, including concentration ending. When it ends, the Veil remains and you may later try again.

### Purging Null Veil

Three exact routes are available.

**Mask confession — NEW narrow EPI-04 extension.** While an operative mask is physically worn, spend an Action to communicate one truthful current fictional emotional-state statement tied to its bound vow to one willing creature or Sentinel who understands it. Spoken, signed, or written communication can qualify under current language rules. If understood, the Veil is purged. The mask stays worn and reveal-charge/safe-removal rules do not change.

**Archon blessing — NEW narrow EPI-06 interpretation.** Complete the current petition procedure. If the response is the actual **5–8 Temporary Blessing** result, receiving that blessing also purges one Null Veil from the petitioner. Guidance, Test, Revelation, old blessing history, or merely possessing a temporary blessing do not purge.

**Bittersap antidote.** Consume one actual dose described below. Continuing exposure remains; a later hourly save can impose Veil again.

None of these procedures cures Corrupted, Dissonance, either Strain, Exhaustion, KT/PT, Hunt, Wounds, poison, disease, or another state merely because it purges Null Veil.

## Null Flora and Bittersap Antidote

The flora stock in this module exists only in the finite Veiled Expanse landing cell of Shardpool Confluence.

**Bittersap Root:** six nodes. Identify 10 min, Nature DC14. Harvest one node in 20 min with a knife/herbalism kit, Survival DC14. Critical Success yields two 0.1-lb parts; Success one; failure consumes the node for zero.

**Tranquil Thorn:** four sprigs. Identify 10 min, Nature DC14. Harvest 10 min with gloves/tongs, DEX (Herbalism kit) DC14. It has no default combat/cure effect here.

**Echo Lily:** four blooms. Identify 10 min, Nature DC14. Harvest 10 min with herbalism kit, DEX (Herbalism kit) DC14. It is the second antidote ingredient.

**Black Hush Moss:** four patches. Identify 10 min, Nature DC14. Harvest 10 min with scraper/herbalism kit, DEX (Herbalism kit) DC14. It has no Null-cure effect; it can serve as mundane black dye.

Harvest stock does not replenish in this module. Freshness and storage are tracked as printed in the Director procedure.

### Antidote batch

Requires:
- 2 Bittersap parts
- 1 Echo Lily part
- 1 pint actual Bastion flame water
- heatproof pot, stirring tool, heat source
- Herbalism kit or Alchemist’s supplies

The Crystal Bastion Shardpool cell contains six sealed one-pint flame-water flasks total. Home-plane affiliation and other travel systems do not generate them.

Commit the ingredients when boiling begins. Work 30 uninterrupted active minutes, then make **INT (Herbalism kit) or INT (Alchemist’s supplies) DC15**.

- Critical Success: 1 dose, shelf life 30 days.
- Success: 1 dose, shelf life 7 days.
- Failure: no dose; ingredients consumed; clean pot 10 min.
- Critical Failure: no dose; ingredients consumed; pot requires 1 hour cleaning.

Use a dose with an Action and one free hand. You may drink it or feed it to a willing/Incapacitated living creature within 5 feet if it can swallow. It purges Null Veil only.

This special procedure does **not** add a reviewed trade recipe to the existing catalog.
