## Refined Tide Enchanted Armor — recipe-121

**Player candidate; Enchanting level 4; DC 8; exact review pending.**

Use the [complete operating guide](ADVANCED_CRAFT_CANDIDATE_GUIDE.md) and [body catalogue](ADVANCED_CRAFT_CANDIDATE_BODY_CATALOGUE.md).

**Effect and operation**

Each finished body has enhancement +1, maximum 1 charge(s), and cold as its sole charge damage type. An admitted actual weapon adds +1 to its eligible item accuracy comparison and +1 ordinary flat weapon damage once per hit; its unsuppressed ordinary B/P/S damage is magical. Charged Strike costs one Action and one charge before its single actual weapon attack; a hit adds 1d6 cold to that attack's damage event. It is not the Attack Action and grants no Extra Attack; an ordinary critical doubles dice, never flat bonuses. An admitted armor suit supplies +1 in equipment.defense-enhancement only while its worn armor formula is selected. Typed Guard spends a Reaction and charge on a positive resolved cold damage component, after defenses but before HP/positive-damage triggers, reducing it by 1d6 to a minimum of zero. Every other admitted carrier uses Typed Pulse: Action, one charge, one manipulating hand, one perceived creature or physically compatible object within 30 feet and line of effect; fixed +5 nonspell attack, hit 2d6 cold, no ability/PB/flat enhancement added again. Only the profile belonging to the registered body is available. These activations spend no RP, do not enter spell cadence and cannot be Counterspelled. Charges refill to maximum only at an eligible actual campaign 06:00 dawn under EN-06; suppressed/missing/zero-HP items miss that event, with no catch-up or local-sunrise/transfer reset. Titles supply no unprinted flight, command, healing or class focus. EN-01–10 and AC-07 supply the full use and comparison procedures.
