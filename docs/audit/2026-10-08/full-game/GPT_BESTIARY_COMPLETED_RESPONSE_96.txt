## 2. What is operationally authored

Every printed profile has its complete native record beside the proposed body geometry, action procedures, trait procedures, knowledge boundary, retreat behavior and finite harvest handling.

**Printed HP, AC, CR, type, ability values, attack modifiers, dice and stated defenses remain unchanged.** Missing mechanics are labelled **new scoped authorship**, rather than represented as recovered source text.

### Actions, damage and Multiattack

All **162 actions** have explicit operational treatment. The **42 Multiattacks** specify their permitted constituent attacks, one actual Action commitment, ordered subattacks, independent rolls and damage events, and the consequences of a miss or changed target eligibility.

Multiattack does not manufacture extra Actions, Reactions, resource refreshes or unlisted recharge attacks. Printed attack bonuses control; they are not “corrected” by recomputing an assumed player-character proficiency formula.

The damage-average comparison confirms the supplied observation: all **119 printed averages** agree with ties-to-even rounding of their exact dice means. **That remains a display-pattern observation.** Actual damage uses the declared dice and applicable modifiers, not the printed average.

The encounter states also include the existing Wound handling. Qualifying packets receive their actual check; a resulting effective-maximum clamp is recorded separately and does not become another damage event.

### Geometry and formation identity

Missing reach, range, area shape and body-space details receive explicit profile-local completions. Source-defined geometry remains controlling. A natural feature’s name does not automatically grant flight, swimming, burrowing, incorporeality or a new movement speed.

For the selected aggregate/formation profiles, the proposal uses **one printed mechanical actor with finite positioned members**:

- The formation has one printed HP account, Action budget, Reaction budget and finite remains history.
- Member positions matter for actual reach, line of effect, local coverage and targeting.
- Splitting visible members does not create more stat blocks, commands, XP opportunities or harvest inventories.
- A qualifying area application against that unit resolves its shared recipient damage once, rather than multiplying the packet by every intersected member.

This is **new formation authorship**, not a global ruling that all similarly named creatures are swarms or aliases.

### Pressure Wave receives its missing procedure

The Pact Automaton’s Pressure Wave retains **DC16** and **3d10 thunder**. The new completion is a **30-foot cone, STR save**, with:

**Failure:** full damage, a bounded ten-foot push along the defined direction and actual Prone.  
**Success:** half damage, no new push or Prone.

Its independent worked example rolls **16 damage**. The failed recipient takes 16 and receives the admitted movement/posture consequences; the successful recipient takes 8.

The separate printed **Containment Pulse** keeps its own Strength-save procedure. Pressure Wave does not silently replace it or inherit a different DC.

### Traits, recharge tells and counterplay

All **136 printed traits** have complete selected procedures, with their source names retained. They are organized into operational families without replacing the individual rules.

Recharge uses each actual printed threshold and a defined spent/ready history. Telegraphs communicate observable readiness or preparation; **they do not grant a free Reaction, reveal hidden recharge dice or create an interruption ability**.

The creature dossiers’ noncombat counterplay—territory, seals, anchors, credentials, sensory cues and emotional behavior—is preserved through explicit evidence and action requirements. A prose weakness does not automatically become an unprinted damage vulnerability.

Regeneration, on-death effects, phase/visibility behavior, fear, grappling, conditional attack modifiers, resistance and other families retain distinct triggers and ending rules. No generic foreign-game trait package is imported.
