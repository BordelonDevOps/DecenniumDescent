1. **Dispel Magic (Lesser)** — **Action; 30 ft; V/S/F; instantaneous.** Target one individually perceptible ongoing magical effect generated by an actual **rank-0, rank-1, or rank-2 spell** within range/LoE. Automatic; no save/check. End only that selected effect and invoke its ordinary ending procedure/dependencies. Eligibility uses the original generating cast’s recorded rank/class placement, never RP cost, discount, caster level, or dispeller access. “Tier 2” is operationally reconciled to **spell rank ≤2**, not a larger character tier. No global purge, permanent enchantment/item/class-engine/trait removal, retroactive damage/resource repair, or unknown-rank guessing. A curse qualifies only if it is itself an otherwise eligible ongoing rank≤2 spell effect and is not specifically protected.

2. **Protection from Energy** — **Action; 30 ft; one willing creature including self; V/S/F; 1 minute; no concentration.** Choose **acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder, force, necrotic, or radiant**. Target gains **immunity to damage of that chosen type**. **NEW bounded energy list:** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing, poison, and psychic are not “energy” for this spell. One Protection from Energy may be active on a target across all casters; later application replaces the old type/application. Immunity prevents only the chosen damage, not conditions/riders merely associated with that source unless they independently require positive damage. It does not rewind damage already resolved. It does not apply to a post-defense transferred branch explicitly marked to skip a second mitigation/prevention stage. No physical-weapon immunity merely because an attack is magical.

3. **Barrier of Faith** — **Action; 10 ft; V/S/F; concentration up to 1 minute.** Choose a **10-ft-long, 10-ft-high, negligible-thickness vertical wall** resting on solid ground or another suitable approximately horizontal immobile surface; the entire wall must fit in legal space within range. Choose one exact current source-authorized creature **type/tag** at casting. Creatures matching that record cannot physically cross the wall in either direction. No taxonomy inference from appearance/name/faction/lore. Boundary cannot initially intersect a matching creature; they may begin on either side. A matching creature cannot bypass it by being carried. Objects, attacks, spells, gases, sound, and nonmatching creatures cross normally. Teleportation/planar relocation does not physically cross the wall and is not blocked. The wall has no HP, floor, platform, support, cover, or collision damage. Unsupported Falling is not arrested; if its path would otherwise require the wall to act as support, ordinary Falling continues on the same legal side rather than creating a perch.

4. **Corruption Cleanse** — **Action; 30 ft; V/S/F; instantaneous.** Choose either:
   - one willing creature you perceive; or
   - one fixed **10-ft-radius area** you perceive.

   **Creature mode:** remove one ordinary **Corrupted** condition record that has no explicit special protection. The reviewed Corrupted condition counts as one playable “minor corruption effect” because its own source expressly allows explicit cleansing.  
   **Area mode:** remove one environmental Corruption effect in the chosen area only if its actual source expressly classifies it as **minor** and permits cleansing; otherwise the spell does not invent “minor” status.

   No save. It grants no immunity against reapplication. It does not reduce **Unity Exposure or any numeric Corruption/Exposure track**, restore prior alignment, reverse a historical Corrupted alignment shift, heal Wounds/HP/death, remove an Oracle intrinsic Curse/Burden, or terminate a major/protected ongoing source merely because one symptom resembles corruption. Later broader Purge/Purification spells retain their separate scope.

6. **Counterspell** — ordinary catalog spell: **Reaction; 60 ft; V/S/F; instantaneous; rank 3 / 5 RP.** Trigger when a creature you can perceive within range and LoE **begins casting a spell**, before that spell resolves. Make a binary spellcasting-ability check using the ability for the class placement through which you cast Counterspell, adding PB, against:

   **DC = 10 + 2 × triggering spell rank**  
   rank 0 counts as 0.

   Meet/exceed = success. This is **not** a spell attack, saving throw, or four-degree task check. On success the triggering spell is negated after its costs were committed; on failure it continues. You need not know its rank: the Director may calculate the DC secretly, and attempting Counterspell grants no free spell/rank identification.

   **Cast-interruption timing settlement:** when any interruptible spell begins, its caster first commits RP/material or other costs, components, any concentration transition, Dissonance/Refrain, and source hooks whose exact trigger is beginning/casting the spell. Then open the Counterspell window. If the spell survives, resolve its effect and later resolution-dependent hooks. Negation refunds nothing and does not restore concentration that ended when casting began.

   Counterspell is itself a spell and can be Counterspelled by another creature with a legal Reaction. Resolve nested counters **last-in, first-out**; if the countering Counterspell is negated, the earlier spell/counter resumes without being cast again. Each nested spell updates its own Refrain/Dissonance once when it starts; resuming a parent does not rewrite that later history.

   A creature may attempt Counterspell at most once against a particular cast. If one attempt fails, another eligible creature may react; once the spell is negated, later attempts have no trigger. A readied spell’s Counterspell window occurs at **preparation**, when it is actually cast, never again on release. Countering a spell is not shared whole-Action hard denial.

   Existing Wizard/Mage **class cards remain separate acquisitions** with their own costs, ranks, recharge, stats, and other text.

9. **Barrier Field** — **Action; 15 ft; V/S/F; concentration up to 1 minute.** Choose a fixed point on suitable horizontal ground/surface within range; create a **faintly visible 15-ft-radius hemisphere**, height 15 ft, with no floor. Creatures and ordinary nonattack movement may cross freely.

   The hemispheric boundary blocks **both directions**:
   - attack rolls whose attack/effect path crosses it; and
   - hostile effects that directly deal damage across it, including magical save-based or automatic damaging effects.

   A damaging area originating on one side does not propagate its damaging portion through the boundary to the other side. Melee reach through the boundary is blocked. This prevents a free one-way bunker: occupants cannot attack or project damaging magic outward through it either.

   It does **not** block nondamaging magic/control merely because magical, ordinary sound/light/gas, teleportation, creature movement, or an effect entirely originating and resolving on the same side. No HP, object identity, cover item, entrapment, floor/platform, or physical support is created. Unsupported Falling crosses normally. Creation causes no displacement or damage.

   Classification follows the **actual generating source/class placement**, not theme or guessed purpose. A class sense is blocked only insofar as its exact source identifies it as Divination/Scrying; its underlying class engine is not deleted or disabled globally.

12. **Resonance Anchor** — **Action; 60 ft; V/S/F; concentration up to 1 minute.** Target one ongoing magical effect with a discrete spatial origin/area that you created **or whose current source owner/controller explicitly permits you to anchor**. Effects permanently attached to/following a creature or carried object are ineligible. A fixed zone is eligible; a source-authorized movable/recenterable zone may be anchored, but loses that movement/recentering permission while anchored.

   While active:
   - the target effect’s origin/area is fixed in place;
   - ordinary dispelling effects, including Dispel Minor/Resonance/Lesser/Magic where otherwise eligible, cannot end the anchored effect.

   Resonance Anchor **itself remains normally dispellable** according to its own rank/source. It cannot target itself or another Resonance Anchor. This prevents mutual indestructible loops.

   Anchor does not extend the target’s duration, replace its concentration, change uses/costs, revive an ended effect, restore expended pulses, freeze ordinary expiry, or retroactively protect a cast from Counterspell. The target can still end through its own duration, concentration, destruction/source condition, explicit special removal, or an effect that expressly bypasses anchoring. If target ends, Anchor ends because nothing remains anchored.

   Because Anchor itself requires concentration, **you cannot anchor an effect whose continued existence currently depends on your own existing concentration**: beginning Anchor ends that concentration before resolution, so the proposed target has ended and is no longer legal. You may anchor another creature’s concentration effect with permission; that creature must continue maintaining it.

Creatures already inside when created remain and need no save until they leave and try to re-enter.
