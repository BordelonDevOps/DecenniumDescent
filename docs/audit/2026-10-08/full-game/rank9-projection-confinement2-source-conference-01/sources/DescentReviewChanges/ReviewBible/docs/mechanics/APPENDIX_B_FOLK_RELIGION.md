# Faiths and Cosmologies of Aethel — Appendix B Canon Note

**Status:** Applied player-facing canon as of 2026-07-31.

The Player Rulebook now treats Appendix B as **mortal theology and cosmography**, not an omniscient description of the universe. Its faiths are real social institutions and sincere beliefs; their explanations of creation and the planes are incomplete interpretations of deeper forces.

## Locked presentation

- Title: **Faiths and Cosmologies of Aethel**.
- The **Fifteenfold Pantheon** remains usable as the dominant mortal religious framework.
- The Fifteen are **worship masks**: names, symbols, stories, and ritual personalities mortals use to approach forces they cannot fully perceive.
- **Lumina** and **Umbra** are theological abstractions in this mortal system, not proof of two objective creator-beings.
- The **Wheel of Testimony** is a mortal cosmographic diagram built from reports, doctrine, planar travel, and scholarly inference. It is useful without being guaranteed complete or literally correct.
- Planar names such as the Verdant Reflection or other traditional scholarly terms are presented as mortal names for observed realms, not as omniscient map labels.
- The First Choir remains **exactly seven** and is not folded into the Fifteenfold Pantheon.

## Canon hierarchy

| Layer | Status | Player access |
|---|---|---|
| Spiral | Deep cosmic structure | Known incompletely |
| Old Ones / Silent Ones / Ul'nak | Director truth | Secret |
| Heartcell | Director truth | Secret; discoverable through play |
| Calan'thir & Asmodeus / the Divine Pact | Public and substantially true | Commonly known |
| First Choir (7) | Cosmic truth filtered through mortal scholarship | Scholarly knowledge |
| Fifteenfold Pantheon | Mortal worship framework | Common knowledge |
| Cultural gods / ascended figures | Local mortal religion | Regional knowledge |

## Public-versus-secret rule

Learning the Heartcell or Silent Ones truth does **not** retroactively make ordinary believers foolish or their institutions meaningless. A priest can be wrong about cosmic architecture while still channeling real power, preserving a community, and acting on genuine revelation. The setting should retain that ambiguity.

## Director use

Do not use Appendix B as a shortcut for revealing the hidden cosmology. Its purpose is to make Aethel feel inhabited by people who have spent millennia interpreting partial evidence. When a deeper truth is discovered, it should **add a layer** beneath the public religion rather than reducing every faith to a lie.
