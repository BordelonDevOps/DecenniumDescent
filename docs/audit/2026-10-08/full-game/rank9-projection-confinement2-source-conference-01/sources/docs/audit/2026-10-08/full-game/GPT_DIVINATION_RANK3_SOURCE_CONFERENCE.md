### 1. Eyes of the Divine

**Action; 60 ft; one creature you perceive with LoE; V/S/F; instantaneous.** No save.

Privately perceive the creature's current appearance **with disguise layers identified and visually stripped away for you only**:

- mundane costume, cosmetics, mask, prosthetic disguise or similar deliberate impersonation → perceive the undisguised current appearance beneath it;
- magical visual disguise/illusion → perceive the appearance beneath that effect;
- actual shapechange, polymorph, innate alternate form or comparable physical transformation → perceive that a real transformation is present and, if the creature has an actual readable source-recorded underlying/base form, see that form as a private overlay.

The spell does not revert or physically expose the target.

If an underlying/base form is undefined or protected, report that the current form is transformed/disguised while the protected/undefined underlying appearance remains **Indeterminate**.

Ordinary clothes or appearance choices are not automatically “disguises”; the spell identifies presentation actually functioning as concealment/impersonation.

Initial targeting still requires actual perception: Invisible/Hidden status, darkness, Blindness, smoke and ordinary sight rules can prevent target selection. It does not inherit True Sight (Lesser)'s ability to find unseen creatures. Solid total cover blocks targeting.

No legal name, ancestry, class, history, statistics, possession controller, alignment, motive, or prior form chronology. Protection from Scrying and specific masking apply to the information they actually protect.

**NEW:** one-creature instantaneous inspection and exact disguise/physical-transformation layering.

### 2. Future Echo

**Action; 60 ft; V/S/F; pending until next numbered round.** Cast only while an encounter initiative order exists. Outside initiative, it resolves with no scheduling effect.

At resolution choose **yourself and/or any willing allied creatures** within 60 ft and LoE that currently possess regular initiative slots, then record their desired relative order.

At the boundary before the **next numbered round**:

1. Remove any selected creature that no longer has a regular encounter slot.
2. Gather the regular slots that would otherwise belong to the remaining selected creatures.
3. Sort those slots earliest→latest.
4. Assign the selected creatures to those slots in the recorded order.

Enemy and unselected slots never move. Thus the spell **permutes selected allies among their existing slot positions** rather than manufacturing arbitrary new positions between enemies.

Each affected creature receives exactly one ordinary regular turn in that round. That turn has its normal movement/Action/BA permissions and one normal regular-start Reaction refresh. Future Echo itself creates no extra turn, Action, Reaction, recharge, death save, pulse, fall step, recovery, or other start/end event.

Existing effects retain their already-recorded **virtual regular markers** despite this temporary reordering. New effects created during the affected round use the then-current actual schedule.

After that one round finishes, the encounter's prior baseline initiative assignment resumes for the following round unless another exact initiative rule changes it. Restoration creates neither a duplicate nor a missing turn.

A creature absent/dead/removed when the affected round starts simply does not receive a Future Echo reassignment; the spell does not create a replacement participant or move another creature into its abandoned baseline slot.

One pending Future Echo schedule per caster; a later successful cast by that caster before execution replaces their earlier schedule. Across different casters, resolve pending schedules in application order at the round boundary: an earlier schedule claims its creatures first; a later schedule skips creatures already reassigned by an earlier Future Echo but may reorder its remaining participants among their own remaining slots.

**NEW:** willing participant set, existing-slot permutation, next-round boundary algorithm, restoration and competing-cast arbitration.

### 3. Reveal Weakness

**Action; 60 ft; one creature perceived with LoE; V/S/F; up to 1 minute or until consumed; no concentration.** No initial save.

The target's **next actual saving throw of any kind** is made with disadvantage. Any roll explicitly classified by its source as a saving throw qualifies; ordinary checks, attack rolls and opposed checks do not.

Consume the effect **before that saving throw is rolled**, even if:

- another source already imposes disadvantage; or
- advantage cancels the disadvantage.

It does not force a save to occur, modify its DC, convert success to failure after resolution, or create critical-save rules.

One pending Reveal Weakness per target across all casters. A successful later application replaces the prior pending record and owns a new endpoint; it does not stock multiple disadvantaged saves. If no saving throw occurs before expiry, it ends unused.

Protection from Scrying does not block this noninformational roll modifier merely because its generating school is Divination.

**NEW:** one-minute pending window, all-saves eligibility and all-caster replacement.

### 4. Planar Window

**Action; local placement within 60 ft; V/S/F; concentration up to 1 minute.**

Choose:

1. one **specific known plane** other than your current plane; and
2. one **specific identifiable location on that plane** that you have personally visited or can uniquely identify from a reliable map, description or established source.

No universal “corresponding coordinates” between planes are invented.

Create one fixed, visible, nonphysical **5-ft-wide × 5-ft-high planar viewing window** at a legal point/surface you perceive within 60 ft and LoE. The remote viewpoint is fixed at the chosen planar location.

The window displays ordinary **vision only** from the remote point in all directions. Everyone who can ordinarily see the local window may see the unprotected remote scene. Local Blindness prevents viewing it. Remote lighting, darkness, smoke, terrain and solid barriers work normally. The spell grants no sound, remote LoE, attacks, casting, targeting, passage or physical interaction.

Additionally, the caster privately detects within **30 ft and remote LoE of the remote viewpoint**:

- beings with an actual established native/origin relationship to that plane; and
- ongoing effects/phenomena with an actual established native planar origin.

For each readable qualifying presence, learn its **location and whether it is a native being or native effect**. This detection can locate an unprotected Hidden/Invisible native being without granting visual sight of its body. Undefined native-plane provenance reports **Indeterminate** rather than native/nonnative.

A visiting creature is not native merely because it currently occupies that plane. A spell cast there by a traveler is not automatically a native planar effect. Established setting fiction may supply native provenance even without a software field.

Specific information/planar protections can filter their actual subjects or remote region. One Planar Window per caster; successful recast replaces it.

**NEW:** known remote-location requirement, 5×5 public visual window, one-minute concentration, 30-ft native-detection radius and native-provenance interface.

### 5. Speak with Dead

Both preserved placements use the **same corpse procedure**, but the generating school remains the actual school of the class used:

- Cleric/Mirrorsage/Ranger/Shaman/Wizard/Paladin → **Divination**
- Crimson Agent/Druid/Thief → **Necromancy**

Do not union those class lists into either mode.

**Action; 60 ft; one corpse or substantial remains perceived with LoE; V/S/F; 1 minute; no concentration.**

Eligible remains must belong to **one currently dead creature identity** and contain enough actual physical remnant to establish which deceased creature is being addressed. No intact tongue, mouth or biological flesh is required. Bones or equivalent substantial creature remains may qualify. A stray blood trace, generic ash with no identity, ordinary broken device, or object that was never a creature does not.

An active Undead/Construct continuing as a creature is not its own corpse. Old remains of an identity that is currently alive again are ineligible. Multiple fragments of one deceased creature remain the same deceased identity, not independent memory copies.

This spell animates **no soul and no creature**. It invokes a **retained memory echo associated with the deceased identity/remains**. It neither resurrects nor summons the free soul.

For the duration, the caster may ask up to **five questions**. In initiative, asking one question and receiving its answer uses one Action. Outside initiative, each exchange takes roughly one six-second interval.

The echo answers **audibly and truthfully according to what the deceased remembered/believed before death**, using a language the deceased actually knew and would reasonably use. No translation is granted. It may answer “I don't know,” “I don't remember,” or equivalent.

One question is one materially unified inquiry. Distinct independent subquestions count separately; wording several unrelated requests in one sentence does not bypass the five-question limit.

The echo knows no post-death events, does not gain omniscience, and cannot supply skills/proficiencies as downloads. It has no independent hostility/cooperation choice that permits deliberate lying; the spell promises answers, but those answers can faithfully reproduce the deceased's mistaken beliefs.

A new paid casting may ask another five questions; no unprinted daily immunity is added.

Actual corpse/soul/memory protections apply. Soul Bind or another resurrection blocker does not automatically erase retained memories unless its own rule says it blocks communication/information. A protection that specifically blocks Divination information can affect a **Divination-placement** cast if its protected subject remains within scope; it does not automatically block the same identity when cast through the **Necromancy placement** merely because the spell ID is shared. Necromancy-specific corpse/soul protections retain their own scope.

If the remains are destroyed so no qualifying physical remnant exists, further casting is impossible. Existing answers/knowledge are not erased.

**NEW:** one-minute interview, retained-memory-echo model, remains eligibility, truthful-perspective answers, Action/question cadence and five-question parsing.

### 6. Faction Divination

**Action; Self; V/S/F; instantaneous.** Choose one **exact known canonical `factionId`**. No physical faction representative, positive reputation, membership, Seal permission, geographic range or planar anchor is required.

Privately learn **one current major institutional objective** of that faction.

A qualifying objective is an actual established goal being pursued or prioritized by the faction as an institution or its recognized governing authority—not a random member's private wish, the Director's preferred plot, or a reputation inference.

Output is one concrete goal such as:

- “secure control of the eastern trade route,”
- “recover the stolen archive,”
- “remove Governor X from office.”

It does not automatically reveal operational plans, member lists, exact locations, timetables, secret agents, resources, or every subordinate objective.

If several objectives are genuinely coequal, the Director records one stable priority selection while that institutional state remains materially unchanged. Repeated equivalent casts do not rotate through hidden coequal objectives.

If the faction genuinely has no current major objective, report **No Current Major Objective**. If the objective is undefined or the relevant institutional information is specifically protected, report **Indeterminate**.

Protection of one leader's personal information does not automatically erase an independently established faction-level objective. Conversely, an objective existing only as that protected person's private plan is not transformed into institutional fact.

A subfaction must have its own actual canonical identity to be selected separately; the spell cannot invent subdivisions to mine additional objectives.

**CONFLICT RULING:** abstract institutional information uses Self informational casting rather than raw 60-ft physical targeting.  
**NEW:** exact-faction input, institutional-objective predicate, stable coequal handling and explicit None/Indeterminate outputs.

### 7. Alignment Revelation

**Action; 60 ft; one creature perceived with LoE; V/S/F; instantaneous; CHA save.**

On success, no revelation occurs.

On failure, magic publicly manifests the creature's currently readable canonical alignment as **two self-luminous symbolic sigils** immediately around it:

- Lawful / Neutral / Chaotic
- Good / Neutral / Evil

The sigils exist only as part of the spell's instantaneous resolution—long enough to be ordinarily perceived by creatures that can see the target—and then vanish.

They emit no meaningful surrounding illumination. Blindness, solid visual obstruction or inability to see the target prevents an observer from seeing the revelation. No language/hearing is required.

Each actual unprotected axis displays its true current value. An undefined or specifically protected axis displays an **Indeterminate** sigil. A masking effect may alter the result only where its exact rules authorize doing so.

The failed save causes the **magic** to reveal the value; it does not compel the creature to speak, confess, believe anything or verify a lie.

No alignment change, behavioral compulsion, oathbreaking, PvP authorization, crime/motive information, or future-action guarantee. No repeated-use immunity or daily cap is added.

**NEW:** public visible two-sigil revelation and observer interface.  
The overlap with lower-rank private alignment readers remains a balance/source-design issue; no secret ward-piercing power is invented to justify rank 3.

### 8. Resonance Sight (Greater)

**Action; Self; V/S/F; instantaneous private snapshot.**

Detect **every currently existing active magical effect with any readable portion within 60 ft, same plane and LoE**. Ordinary non-solid visual concealment, Invisible/Hidden status and lack of mundane visibility do **not** prevent detection of the magical effect itself. Solid total cover blocks the unreadable portion unless an exact source says otherwise.

For every qualifying effect, privately learn:

1. its readable location/footprint within range;
2. its actual effect/spell/property identity **when that identity is explicitly recorded and unprotected**; otherwise its established broad magical category;
3. its **proximate generating source** when recorded and readable.

“Source” means the immediate provenance that created/sustains that effect, such as:

- the specific spell cast and its originating caster identity;
- the item producing an active property;
- the feature/creature producing an aura;
- the site/zone/ritual generating an environmental effect.

If that source is now outside 60 ft, the spell may still report its **recorded identity/provenance**, because the qualifying effect is local; it does **not** reveal the source's current remote location, condition, inventory or upstream controller.

Undefined/protected effect identity and source fields independently report **Indeterminate** rather than suppressing other readable fields.

Active intrinsic magic qualifies only when an actual source records a presently active magical effect/aura. Merely being a magical creature does not automatically create another effect. A mere RP pool does not qualify.

A permanent magical item qualifies only through a currently active magical property/effect; dormant historical enchantment with no active effect is not automatically exposed.

A copied effect is reported as that actual copied effect and its proximate copying source where recorded; the spell does not recursively reconstruct every ancestor unless provenance explicitly contains that information.

An ongoing effect that is **suppressed but still exists as an ongoing magical record** remains detectable as an existing suppressed effect if that state is readable. An effect actually ended/removed is not. One-hour extinguished spell residue belongs to Resonance Tracking, not this spell.

Detecting an attached aura may reveal a Hidden/Invisible creature's location through the aura without granting sight of the creature itself. Protection from Scrying or another exact information defense can prevent attached-magic information about its protected subject; independent environmental magic remains separately readable.

No dispel, control, RP/charge extraction, spell copying, recipe/statblock download, remote attack/cast, or permanent map.

**NEW:** Self 60-ft snapshot, effect identity + proximate provenance fields, concealment-versus-solid-cover treatment and active/suppressed/dormant boundaries.
