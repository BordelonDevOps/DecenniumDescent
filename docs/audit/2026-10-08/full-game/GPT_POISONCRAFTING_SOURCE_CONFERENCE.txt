Direction is sufficient to author the full 41-card Poisoncrafting candidate. I would use the following finite rulings. Anything marked NEW is provisional operational authoring, not recovered canon and not exact-card approval.

Identity, initial saves, repeat saves, refresh, and source ownership — APPROVE with an exact record model. All 41 finished products are magical nonspell poison-effect consumables. They create no generic RP/RC cost, attunement, focus, Dissonance/Refrain interaction, spell rank, Counterspell interaction, or class entitlement. On a legitimate exposure, resolve the printed CON save once. Success means neither initial component applies. Failure attempts both printed components: the printed damage packet and the printed condition. Damage immunity/resistance and condition immunity are then applied independently; immunity to one does not cancel the other. Repeat saves never repeat the initial damage. They exist only while that exposure still has an ongoing condition record. NEW: ordinary one-minute poison records expire at the 10th future regular target-end marker after successful condition application; a Flawed 30-second record uses the fifth. Each regular target end before expiry receives its printed repeat CON save; extra turns do not. On the final marker, the repeat save resolves before expiry cleanup. Outside initiative, use one step per complete six elapsed seconds, with the tenth at 60 seconds or fifth at 30; initiative transitions never duplicate a step. A successful repeat save ends only that exposure's remaining condition record. If another legal method already ended its only ongoing condition, there is no later repeat save. NEW owner binding: every deployed dose receives a stable poisonOwnerKey, distinct from crafter identity. Injury: the creature that performs the coating Action. Contact: the creature that coats/arms the object or surface. Inhaled: the creature that releases the dose, or, for an armed unattended mechanism, the creature that committed the dose to that mechanism. If no responsible character exists, use an immutable deployment/source-entity key. Transfer of the item does not change that key. Each actual exposure still gets a unique exposureId for damage, inventory spending, and audit history. The ongoing stack identity is effectively recipeId + poisonOwnerKey + targetId. A later real dose of the same recipe by the same owner against the same target gets its own initial save/damage, but if its condition legally applies while that prior record remains present, refresh that one record to the later legal expiry rather than increasing magnitude. Different recipes or different owner keys remain independent source records. No ordinary crafted-poison rule inherits the Thief engine's two-recipe occupancy ceiling. Stunned is the explicit exception discussed in point 8: the hard-denial ceiling blocks later Stunned refreshes from an already-spent owner.

Injury delivery — adopt the reviewed Basic Poison physical model for all 26 injury formulas. NEW family binding: one finished dose takes one Action, one available hand, actual access to the dose, and access to either one compatible piercing/slashing weapon or up to three compatible piercing/slashing ammunition units. All three ammunition units must be present during that Action. One dose therefore can create up to three finite ammunition exposure entitlements; it does not create three inventory doses. One external natural-weapon profile on the applier or a willing creature can qualify if it actually deals piercing or slashing damage and can physically retain a coating; claws, talons or horns are reasonable, while a mouth/bite is not compatible by default absent a specific safe-use rule. An unwilling creature's held weapon or anatomy cannot simply be coated without separately gaining lawful control/access. The coating lasts one elapsed minute from application. A coated weapon spends its coating on its first qualifying hit. Each coated ammunition unit spends its own coating on its first hit. Preserve the Basic Poison precedent that this is hit-gated, not positive-damage-gated: a legal hit still exposes even if the weapon's piercing/slashing damage is later reduced to 0, prevented by immunity, or fully absorbed by temporary HP. Temporary HP therefore does not prevent exposure. A miss does not expose. A rules-defined hit that produces 0 weapon damage still exposes. A critical hit produces one poison exposure and never doubles the poison's separate save-based damage dice. A poison-immune target still spends the coating when legitimately hit. Coatings never stack on the same weapon/ammo unit: a new coating replaces the old one and loses the old entitlement. Thorough washing/full immersion of an exposed coating ends it; ordinary incidental dampness need not. A missed ammunition unit can retain its original coating only if it remains physically intact, is legally recovered, was not washed, and the original one-minute coating deadline has not expired. Recovery never refreshes the deadline. Once an ammunition unit hits, its coating is spent even if the save succeeds or all poison effects are immune. Merely carrying a coated weapon, touching its safe grip, or standing near one is not exposure.

Contact delivery — use one finite surface charge, not an unlimited hazard. NEW: deploying one contact dose takes one Action, one available hand and actual access to the finished dose. It coats either one accessible object no larger than a 5-foot cube or one contiguous 5-foot-by-5-foot surface patch. The coating lasts one elapsed hour from deployment unless spent, washed/cleaned, destroyed with its carrier, or superseded on the same coated region. The first qualifying direct bodily contact spends the entire dose whether the target succeeds, is condition-immune, damage-immune or explicitly poison-effect immune. Qualifying contact means the coated surface reaches exposed bodily tissue/anatomy without an actual intervening object or poison-blocking barrier. A separate held object touching the patch does not expose its holder, does not consume the coating and does not transfer a new usable poison dose. Protective gloves or another actual impermeable/poison-blocking barrier prevent qualifying contact while covering the contacted anatomy. A coated glove or other coated object can itself expose a different creature if its coated exterior later makes direct qualifying contact with that creature. No generic touch attack, grapple rider or forced-contact Action is created. Using a coated object as a weapon still requires an independently legal attack/action, and a weapon hit is not automatically a contact-poison exposure merely because the weapon touched armor; injury poisons have their separate model. Legal coating using the actual Protective gloves or Applicator needs no invented Poisoncrafting/proficiency check and causes no automatic self-exposure. Actual direct unprotected contact, if it occurs, uses the same trigger as anyone else's. NEW cleaning: one Action plus actual washing/wiping access can remove the coating from one small object; cleaning an entire 5-foot patch requires one uninterrupted active minute. Cleaning destroys the dose into inert waste. Runoff, cloth, rinse water and cleaned gloves do not become additional active poison stock. Beginning the coating Action before the finished dose's one-hour opened deadline commits that dose; after deployment, the one-hour contact-coating clock controls instead.

Inhaled delivery — approve a destructive, instantaneous 5-foot burst. For the five inhaled recipes, use the already-reviewed point-targeting pattern as a NEW Poisoncrafting binding: Action, one available hand, visible point with line of effect, 30-foot normal range / 60-foot long range, no attack roll, one 5-foot-radius instantaneous burst. At 31–60 feet, each otherwise eligible creature has advantage on that release's initial poison CON save from the long-range dispersal rule; repeat saves later are normal. The vial is destroyed when released and returns no spent container. Every eligible creature in the burst, including allies or the releaser, receives at most one exposure from that vial. Total cover from the burst point prevents exposure. There is no lingering cloud, entry-later exposure, wind drift, second area, critical, chain reaction or repeated walking-in/out trigger. A creature is eligible only if it actually breathes the ambient gaseous medium at resolution. A creature that does not breathe, or that already had a legitimate breath-holding state established before release, is not exposed. There is no generic Reaction to start holding one's breath after seeing the release; an explicit feature that actually grants such a Reaction can work on its own terms. The defined burst requires a nonliquid atmosphere. An underwater point is therefore not a legal inhaled release point unless it is an actual air/gas pocket; breaking a vial underwater loses it without creating this gaseous exposure. Recipe-663's word Miasma adds no persistence.

Confused / Slowed / Weakened — create three explicit poison-qualified profiles. These do not replace or rewrite class-specific definitions. NEW poisoncraft.confused: while present, the creature has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks other than Initiative and cannot take Reactions. It retains movement, Action and Bonus Action normally; it chooses its own targets and actions; there is no random behavior, compelled movement, friendly-fire rule or whole-Action denial. Thus this profile does not consume the hard whole-Action-denial allowance. Multiple poisoncraft Confused records do not multiply disadvantage or remove additional resources; their source records and repeat-save endpoints remain independent. Tag it eligibility.confused only as source-qualified classification. NEW poisoncraft.slowed: halve each existing movement speed while active, preserving Speed 0, with no teleport denial. Multiple poisoncraft Slowed records do not quarter speed. It carries eligibility.slowed-foe; that does not make it the Thief class record. NEW poisoncraft.weakened: weapon and natural-weapon hit damage is reduced by 1, minimum 0. It does not reduce spell damage, save damage, attack rolls, ability checks or saving throws. Identical -1 weapon/natural-hit penalties do not add together; use the strongest applicable identical flat reduction. It carries eligibility.weakened, but remains distinct from Mage/Wizard/Warrior source-qualified records. All three use the recipe's one-minute/Flawed-duration limit and printed end-turn CON save. Explicit immunity to poison effects/toxins blocks them; an effect explicitly immune to Confused, Slowed or Weakened can block the corresponding profile if its actual scope includes it. Do not infer such immunity from some other class condition. Their ordinary removal routes are their repeat save, duration expiry, or an actual cure covering the poison application/profile. No new universal generic cure is created.

Frightened recipes 364/381/661 — use a fixed exposure locus, not the hidden applicator. NEW: when the failed initial save establishes Frightened, record the exact spatial point at which the poison exposure occurred as that application's fear locus. It is fixed for that application and does not follow the coated object, projectile, weapon, carrier, applicator or crafter afterward. Destroying, moving, dropping or carrying the original object therefore does not erase or move the fear source. For Core Frightened, treat that locus as "visible" only when the affected creature currently has ordinary visual line of sight to that location; Blindness, total visual obscuration or total cover can make the visibility-dependent disadvantage inactive. The prohibition on willingly moving closer is measured against the same fixed locus for as long as the Frightened record remains. This abstraction reveals neither who applied the poison nor any hidden creature and creates no hallucination, mind-reading or false image. Fear immunity blocks application normally. The poison repeat CON save and other actual fear-removal effects can end the record.

Burning recipes 362/384/394 — integrate the source CON save with the already-reviewed Burning procedure, without double fire. A failed initial poison save attempts the printed initial fire-damage packet and one ordinary poison-created Burning record independently. Fire resistance/immunity changes the initial fire packet but does not by itself block Burning. Burning immunity blocks the condition but not the initial packet. Explicit poison-effect immunity blocks the entire application. The Burning record's ongoing damage is the already-reviewed 1d4 fire through family.ongoing-fire; the recipe's 1d6/4d6/8d6 initial damage is separate and never becomes its periodic magnitude. One application creates one Burning record only. At each regular target end, resolve in this order: due ongoing-fire arbitration/tick; then, if the poison-created Burning record is still present and has future duration remaining, its normal DEX ending save against that recipe's applying DC; then, if it still remains, the recipe's repeat CON save; then ordinary end-marker expiry cleanup. A successful DEX save, extinguish Action, full immersion or successful repeat CON save ends that poison-created Burning record, so later steps requiring its presence are skipped. At the final duration marker, the ordinary due tick and permitted ending saves resolve before expiry cleanup. Present but suppressed/condition-immune records retain legal extinguishing and ending saves under the reviewed cure-presence rule; suppressed records do not contribute damage to arbitration. A same-owner/same-recipe legal re-exposure can refresh the same still-present Burning record to the later expiry but never creates an extra same-group 1d4 tick; its new initial fire damage still resolves normally. A cure that ends only Core Poisoned does nothing to Burning. A cure that removes the underlying poison application may remove the remaining poison-created Burning record.

Stunned recipes 371/391 — bind crafted items into the existing hard-denial ceiling. NEW item integration: Stunned from these poisons uses the same combined once-per-sourceKey / target / encounter hard-denial allowance already used by classes, spells and companions. Use the stable poisonOwnerKey from point 1 as the source key when it is a character. If no character owner exists, use the immutable deployment/source-entity key as the equivalent item source key; duplicating a database/item record, changing possession or relabeling the item cannot change that key. The crafter is not automatically the source. For injury poison, the coating actor is the poison owner for all weapon/ammo entitlements created by that deployment. For contact poison, the coating/arming actor is the owner. For directly released inhaled poison, the releaser is the owner; an unattended mechanism uses its arming/deployment owner. Claim the hard-denial allowance only when a failed initial poison save would actually establish a Stunned record and Stunned is not blocked by immunity or another denial rule. Initial-save success claims nothing. Stunned immunity claims nothing. Explicit poison-effect immunity claims nothing. If the allowance is already spent, a failed initial save still resolves its printed damage component normally, but the Stunned component fails to establish. If another source already has the target Stunned, a different unused source key may still establish its own record and spend its own allowance; the mechanical Stunned effect itself does not stack. Once an owner establishes one Stunned application against that target in the encounter, later recipe-371/391 doses from that owner cannot establish or refresh Stunned even after the first record ends. This hard-denial rule therefore overrides the ordinary same-recipe refresh rule. Repeat end-turn saves on the one allowed application are maintenance of that existing record, not new applications and never consume additional hard-denial allowances.

Poison-effect classification, immunities, suppression and cures — approve the proposed separation. Every one of these 41 is a poison-effect application regardless of whether its damage type is poison, acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, psychic, radiant, thunder, bludgeoning or necrotic. Poison-damage immunity affects only actual poison-type damage. Core Poisoned immunity blocks only Poisoned. Blinded, Frightened, Stunned and Burning use their actual condition immunities. The three new source-qualified profiles use their explicit profile/poison-effect immunities. Explicit immunity to poison effects/toxins blocks the covered application as a whole: no initial damage, no condition and no repeat-save record, although the physically spent dose remains spent. Antitoxin and Cooking effects granting advantage on saves against poison apply to both the initial and repeat CON saves of these poison-effect applications even when the actual damage type is something else. They do not automatically apply to Burning's separate generic DEX extinguishing save. Herbalism antidotes retain their narrow scope: they end present removable Poisoned records only. They do not erase Confused, Slowed, Weakened, Blinded, Frightened, Stunned or Burning merely because those came from poison. If Poisoned was an application's sole remaining ongoing component, removing it leaves no ongoing effect and therefore no further repeat saves; the old source can remain only as history, not as a mechanism to reapply Poisoned. An actual Alchemy cure explicitly ending one poison effect, or Panacea selecting one eligible removable poison source, can remove one whole present application record and all dependent ongoing symptoms created by that application. Independent poison applications remain. Suppression: an already-established magical nonspell poison record can be suppressed by a legal effect covering it; its clocks and repeat/end saves continue, missed initial damage is never replayed, and removal procedures may still end the present record. If the finished poison itself is suppressed at exposure resolution, no delayed damage/condition is banked for later unsuppression; the deployed dose is simply spent without active effect unless the suppressing source says otherwise.

Manufacture, vessels, tools and mass — approve with exact vessel allocation. Poisoncrafting remains Intelligence + Poisoner's kit at a Poisoner's Bench with Sealed glassware and Protective gloves. Poisoner's kit remains non-artisan, so Forge Dwarf artisan time/quality bridging does not apply merely because this is crafting. NEW family manufacture binding: the first 15 active minutes are the binding/stabilization phase inside the listed source work time. Legal ingredients and required station/tools must remain available through that phase. Bound work-order material is stable only inside that order and is not generic ingredient preservation. Recipe-395 preserves source craftingTime:"1 year" and activeWorkHours:null; the operational work-year overlay is 1,920 active hours, recorded separately rather than rewriting source data. Finished sealed doses use the reviewed poison/alchemical physical profile: 0.1 lb compound + one 0.1-lb Glass vial = 0.2 lb gross, AC 13, HP 1, indefinitely stable while sealed under ordinary conditions, then a one-hour opened-inventory clock. Injury/contact deployment begun before that deadline commits the dose and replaces the inventory-open clock with its own coating clock. Inhaled release is immediate and destructive. Injury/contact use leaves an intact surviving container as an ordinary spent 0.1-lb vial, AC 13, actual remaining/max HP, one-dose capacity, 1-gp reference, no magical use, and no retained high-tier ingredient identity/properties/value. Inhaled release destroys its output vial and returns nothing. Source vessel accounting: consumed Glass-vial component units can satisfy output-container count on a successful craft. Assign at most min(source vessel units, resolved finished quantity) of them as actual filled output vessels; any additional source vessel units are consumed process glassware, not automatically returned as expensive empty stock. Supplementary ordinary 1-gp vials cover the rest. For pre-check capacity, reserve enough total source/supplementary vials for floor(1.5Q) maximum yield. A source vessel's high tier/value remains source provenance/economics; after its filled output is used it becomes only the ordinary spent-vial object above. Generic Failed/Mishap recovery still follows the existing framework; this "surplus process glassware is spent" rule applies to successful manufacture and does not silently abolish an otherwise legal failure-recovery rule. Exclude all vessel and tool mass from finished-compound calculations. Therefore recipe-661's consumed 1-lb Applicator is genuinely consumed process equipment, contributes zero compound mass, and is not automatically returned. Every consumed:false Applicator returns unchanged with its original identity, condition, tier and value. The proposed mass method is correct: compare maximum required compound mass (0.1 lb × maximum yield) against non-tool/non-vessel consumed mass after the worst eligible catalyst return; reserve ordinary clean water only for an actual deficit. On the supplied audit that produces the nonzero maximum deficits already identified for recipe-368: 0.20 lb and recipe-370: 0.40 lb; the others require none under the stated arithmetic. Reserve carrier before the check, then incorporate only the amount actually required by the resolved yield/catalyst choice; unused reserved water remains inventory. It never replaces source ingredients.

Quality and final source boundaries — approve reduced-duration singleton Flawed rather than forcing Failed. Q≥2 Flawed batches produce floor(Q/2) otherwise ordinary doses. NEW singleton Flawed profile: a singleton still produces one usable dose with the full printed save DC, delivery procedure and initial damage dice, but its maximum ongoing condition duration becomes 30 seconds / five future regular target-end markers instead of one minute. Repeat saves are otherwise unchanged. This is the coherent "one reduced-duration result" supplied by the source quality framework. If the ongoing condition is immune/blocked, the dose remains consumed after its normal exposure and does not acquire a second benefit, refund or reuse. For Burning this means at most five ordinary Burn-family target-end opportunities subject to saves/extinguishing/arbitration. For Stunned it means at most five markers but still requires and consumes the same hard-denial allowance when established. Do not halve the injury one-minute coating lifetime, contact one-hour deployment lifetime, sealed/opened inventory lifetime, inhaled range/area, save DC, initial damage, exposure count, or application action. Exceptional/Masterwork can change finite yield or dedicated output-vial durability only. The durability option raises a baseline output vial from max HP 1 to max HP 2 once and increases current HP by the same +1, preserving any existing damage deficit; it is not a repair and cannot compound through later reuse. Catalyst return remains independent. Preserve every original source identity, visibility, economy, legacy field and source tags[], but only current mechanics.tag is operational. This matters for multi-tag sources such as 389–395: their extra top-level tags do not silently create secondary mechanical tags. Additional source-specific boundaries: recipe-662 remains an injury poison despite recipeFamily:"Paste" and gains no armor/object corrosion from “Armor Eater”; recipe-663's wholeCreatureUse:true creates no donor-trait borrowing because its actual effect prints none, and “Miasma” creates no persistent cloud; legacy names containing “Ingested Poison” do not create an ingested delivery route because every current operative effect in this 41-card family is injury/contact/inhaled as explicitly printed; recipe-395's legacy “Cosmic” name creates no cosmology rule; and the deep-tier/Legendary directorGuidance paragraphs remain preserved optional Director guidance, not automatically activated extra catalysts, downtime, Resonance rituals, public lore or mandatory gates unless separately reviewed and chosen.
