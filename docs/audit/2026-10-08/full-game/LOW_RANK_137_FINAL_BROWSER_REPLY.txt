Governing decisions

The complete wording is in LR-G01–20 and the individual LR-IL / UN / TR / NE / EV3 / R3 entries. The following are the principal decisions and explicit source conflicts.

1. Preserve actual access, costs and distinct permissions

KEEP every actual ordinary class-access row: school, rank, minimum class level, base RP and additional RP. Enough RP does not confer access, preparation, repertoire or a higher-rank variant.

An already completed, explicitly named ordinary-spell access exception—such as the actual Druid Circle always-prepared table—retains its own acquisition, rank and preparation requirements once its matching ordinary payload passes exact review. It does not rewrite the ordinary classAccess object.

Ordinary spell permissions remain distinct from same-named class techniques, ancestry grants, crafted-item uses and Oracle Revelations. Their costs, components, action windows and resource histories do not merge.

KEEP the existing casting, Counterspell, Dissonance, Refrain, concentration, damage, healing, condition and equipment procedures. New defaults apply only where the selected spell lacked an operating procedure.

4. Give copying and delayed casting real action and resource histories

NEW — LR-G13 and UN-05/14/17/21:

Cantrip Echo is one composite Bonus Action casting of the eligible payload. It uses the payload’s ordinary spell ID for its actual casting history, rather than inserting a wrapper name to reset Refrain. It makes a fresh legal attack/save where required; it does not replay a saved successful result.

Quick Cast spends its own Bonus Action to prepare a conversion on the caster’s next regular turn. It does not supply two Bonus Actions in the preparation turn. The later payload consumes that turn’s real Bonus Action and pays its actual costs.

Delay Spell has separate arming and payload commitments. Holding the already committed payload uses concentration for no more than six seconds. Targets and selections are locked; release revalidates current range, line and eligibility. Release is not a second casting or a new Counterspell window.

Echo Cast is separately paid arming followed by a real Action replay on the next regular turn. Only base RP is waived.

These wrappers cannot recursively take one another or an already replayed payload as replay fuel. Existing acquired class exceptions retain their own actual predicates; they are not automatically absorbed into this wrapper family.

5. Keep different invisibility and control endings different

Ordinary Invisibility, IL-22, retains its printed action-ending clause. The NEW precise boundary is before commitment of an Action, Bonus Action or Reaction. It does not automatically end merely from movement or another event that is not such an action.

That rule is not imposed on Cloak of Shades, Mislead, Shadow Dive, Dreamer’s Veil or other distinct class sources. Each keeps its own specified ending.

Consequently, ordinary IL-22 alone does not satisfy Unseen Hand’s requirement to take an Action while Invisible: this spell has already ended at the relevant commitment. An independently acquired invisibility source that actually persists through that Action can still qualify under the class card and its shared budget.

Haste, R3-12, receives one explicitly named exception. It retains its printed extra full Action, rather than an imported restricted action menu, while respecting the existing one-extra-full-Action-per-creature-per-numbered-round limit.

Its ending Stun is retained as a NEW informed, voluntarily accepted buff-ending cost, not another hostile hard-control claim. Actual expiry, concentration loss, dispel, replacement or dismissal triggers it; temporary suppression alone does not. Actual Stunned immunity still applies. This exception does not extend to other hostile denial effects.

6. Reflection redirects one effect; it does not create another cast

NEW — LR-G14, Spell Mirror and Soul Mirror:

The first eligible resolving ordinary spell with the protected creature as its sole direct creature recipient can be intercepted, whether beneficial or hostile. A Touch spell can qualify through this explicit magical return when the original caster is otherwise a legal recipient.

Area, multitarget, self-only, object/anchor, summoning, ongoing-tick and already redirected effects do not qualify.

The original caster remains the spell owner and supplies its statistics. The ward spends before the returned effect resolves. An illegal or blocked return spends the ward but leaves the original recipient; it does not cancel the spell for free. A causal root can be redirected only once by these two spells. A second mirror neither redirects nor consumes itself on that already redirected root.

7. Preserve finite damage, corpses, material and created bodies

Every ongoing damage spell now has an explicit number of checkpoints, ending procedure and suppression treatment. Suppressed scheduled ticks are spent, not banked for later. Extra turns do not manufacture more regular-turn ticks.

Soul Tap uses one d6 for the damage and both transfer ceilings. Its NEW transfer funding requires actual underlying normal-HP loss to a genuinely hostile living nonconstruct opponent. HP recovery is bounded by that actual loss and ordinary healing limits. RP transfer is separately bounded by the same roll, the donor’s actual available RP and the caster’s actual missing RP after payment. RC is not RP.

These new funding choices do not retrospectively rewrite the nonidentical completed Lifedraw, Vampiric Touch or feat procedures.

Pact of Bone’s “+2 necrotic resist” becomes a NEW flat reduction of two, not another resistance percentage. Its new reduction stage is expressly placed after the save amount and before passive resistance/vulnerability.

Corpse questions, corpse preservation, animation and ordinary harvesting retain distinct histories. Gentle Repose does not restart a death timestamp or a question/admission deadline. New corpse-fueled assets do not leave a fresh resource pool when their terminal history says the original matter was consumed.

Shadow Blade, Phantom Steed, Corpse Puppet, Summon Bone Archer, Summon Dreadknight and Animate Dead receive their own finite body, defense, movement, attack or noncombat-task, capacity, command and ending profiles. No unsupplied monster stat block, equipment, ammunition, corpse loot or autonomous production is imported.

Material transformations retain actual mass lineage, damage, ownership and old acquisition claims. Enlarge/Reduce does not compound repeated magical snapshots or refill HP. Stone Shape supplies bounded elementary shapes from actual stone, not a free fine mechanism, repaired structure or authored crafting result.

8. Keep literal short ranges and every printed damage expression

Several source choices are unusual but are not corrected to another game’s familiar version:

Fireball: its actual 20-foot casting range remains distinct from its 20-foot radius.
Minute Meteors: six committed centers remain within the literal 5-foot casting range. The NEW operation resolves six instantaneous, separately rolled 2d6 bursts, not a stored remote launcher.
Absorb Elements: the source Action becomes a finite prepared defensive ward, not an imported Reaction.
Primal Savagery: the literal 30-foot range is retained through a NEW momentary spell-local claw extension for one spell attack, not permanent natural-weapon reach.

The complete rows provide target admission, saves, damage-on-success, overlap, aftermath and termination. Descriptive tags such as Ward, Healing or Damage do not independently add an effect absent from the actual clause.
