# Banishment — Repair 03 Provisional Player Procedure

## Scope and source identity
This is a **NEW provisional authored playtest procedure** for the ordinary rank-4 **Banishment** spell only. It does not alter the accepted AC4 fifteen-spell module, choose a canonical plane, settle soul/origin identity, or merge with Cleric Banish Unholy.

Preserved source identity: **Abjuration; rank 4; base 7 RP; Action; 60 feet; V/S/F; “Send a creature to another plane temporarily.”** The Core source also preserves **Instantaneous** metadata. Because that source does not itself provide a runnable temporary duration, this candidate keeps that metadata as evidence and adds an explicitly NEW concentration limit of **60 elapsed seconds**.

## Casting and save
Choose one creature you actually perceive within 60 feet and line of effect. A corpse, image, soul record or unattended object is not a creature target.

Before the save, record the target's **admission intent**:
- **ACCEPT** — the creature is voluntarily accepting this extradimensional displacement. It may elect failure or roll; if it rolls and fails, the admission is still voluntary.
- **RESIST** — a failed save produces forced displacement.

Commit the Action, V/S/F, actual final RP price and concentration normally. A willing target may elect to fail before rolling. Otherwise the target makes the NEW Banishment **Charisma save** against the actual spell DC. Actual save features apply, including Fate-Bound's exact +2 against banishment.

A failed save can establish exile only if the existing shared hard-control allowance for that source-character/target/encounter is available. Only one live ordinary Banishment application may attach to one creature.

## Exact Paladin distinction
For this spell only, **Banishment is planar/extradimensional displacement and is not teleportation**.

- **No Escape** forbids voluntary teleportation, invisibility **or extradimensional travel**. It therefore blocks ACCEPT, whether the target elects failure or rolls and fails. It does not block a genuinely unwilling RESIST target that fails.
- **Verdict Absolute** forbids voluntary teleportation or invisibility only. It does **not** block this non-teleport Banishment.
- **Reign of Ashes** prevents benefit from Dash or teleportation. It does **not** block this non-teleport Banishment.

This classification is Banishment-only and does not redefine other planar travel.

## The temporary cell
On successful establishment, the same target identity and its legal noncreature worn/carried gear move to a generated extraplanar spell-cell. The cell is not identified with any named plane, afterlife, deity, Heartcell state or soul destination.

Cell-local x is east, y north, z up; floor is z=0. The primary starts centered at local (0,0).

Initial horizontal extent is the current footprint plus 5 feet clearance on every side, rounded upward to the next 5 feet. Height is current occupied body height plus 5 feet overhead, rounded upward to the next 5 feet. A Medium 5x5 body of occupied height 6 feet therefore starts in **x/y -7.5..+7.5, z 0..15**.

The cell floor is ephemeral support. It supplies ordinary light and a nonharmful sustaining medium, but no harvestable air/water/material, resistance, immunity or biology.

### Geometry inside
A voluntary size/form change must already fit the current cell before payment under its actual body/source rules. Banishment cannot expand first to legalize the cast.

A **mandatory** death, source expiry, summon ending, actual-0-HP ending, Polymorph reversion or other mandatory body event always occurs. Banishment never cancels or delays it. If the resulting body needs more room, the cell expands **after** that mandatory event.

## Visitors and created creatures
A different creature can enter only through an independently legal source that can truly identify/reach this cell and target a fitting footprint already inside the current opaque boundary.

An occupant may summon/create a creature only into a fitting unoccupied footprint it can currently perceive/target **before** casting. No pre-cast expansion supplies missing space or line of effect. After a body legally establishes, the cell may expand only for ordinary clearance.

Every visitor/summon keeps its own real Action/RP/frequency/concentration/command/deadline rules.

## Time and agency
Successful establishment records an absolute deadline **60 elapsed seconds later**. Existing HP, Wounds, conditions, death saves, concentration, summon timers and other real clocks continue normally. Banishment itself adds no Incapacitated, healing, rest or stasis.

The application ends early when its concentration ends, when a legal Xi interaction ends the actual target branch, when the primary legally escapes, or when this Banishment application is actually ended/suppressed under its current rule.

## Legal escape
An occupant may use an independently legal travel source, paying its real costs and meeting its own destination, perception, geometry and blocker rules.

If the **primary** legally escapes, its Banishment application ends immediately. The spell then settles every remaining secondary body/object and destroys the cell in that same resolution sequence. The escaped primary is never pulled back later.

## Closure and physical placement
When Banishment ends, every remaining real identity is mandatorily returned to the **same snapshotted origin plane**. The primary uses its fixed world origin anchor; secondary bodies use deterministic anchors based on their in-cell offsets. The cell then ends.

A current **Dimensional Anchor** or other true endpoint blocker is respected for ordinary establishment/escape. At terminal Banishment closure only, a NEW narrow exception ignores **only** such transport-prevention if necessary to complete the mandatory return. It does not ignore walls, occupied space, anatomy, death, missing bodies or item existence and grants no later bypass.

After the plane return, physical fitting uses the existing body rule:
1. normal current-anatomy space at the same anchor;
2. a legal squeeze;
3. nearest legal adjacent placement in the same unobstructed region, using minimum displacement, greatest overlap, then fixed neutral board order.

It never searches farther afield, passes through a wall/closed barrier, crushes another body or invents damage.

### Placement conflict
If none of those inherited physical placements fits, **Banishment still ends** and the cell is destroyed. The same identity is on the origin plane in the existing physical **placement conflict** at its fixed closure anchor.

**NEW BAN-CONFLICT-04** supplies only the missing Banishment-origin spatial reference; it does not rewrite the global body rule. The fixed closure anchor remains the creature's logical location for measuring range and evaluating an effect's actual source predicates while no legal occupied footprint exists. It grants no movement, adjacency, occupancy, cover, squeezing, wall passage, overlap, flanking or support-contact benefit.

Perception and line of effect use the real current terrain from that anchor. A wall or solid collapse can therefore block sight/LoE normally. Actions and Reactions are **not automatically forbidden**: evaluate the actual activation, components, target, range, perception, path and geometry requirements of the attempted effect. Self-targeting or other effects that do not require a legal occupied footprint may still be legal. Ordinary movement cannot start from a nonexistent legal footprint/path, but an independently legal relocation effect can resolve the conflict if its own rules can use the anchor as the source location and establish a legal destination.

All HP, Wounds, conditions, concentration, death/body rules and real clocks continue. Already-attached effects remain attached unless their own source ends them. When the obstruction is physically removed or changed, rerun the inherited same-anchor → squeeze → nearest-adjacent procedure from the same anchor and current anatomy; that normalization spends no movement and grants no movement rider.

Real terrain/support at the anchor still matters to source predicates, but ordinary Falling begins only once a legal unsupported footprint is actually established.

Loose surviving items use the existing item-placement rule. If no legal item drop exists, the item remains real with placement pending.

## Current interfaces
- **Spell Shield:** Banishment is rank 4, outside accepted rank-0–3 Spell Shield eligibility.
- **Dimensional Anchor:** applies normally at establishment and voluntary escape; only the narrow terminal return exception above can ignore its prevention.
- **Resonance Beacon:** keeps its actual same-plane requirement.
- **Xi:** can end only the actual ongoing target branch through Xi's own Action/perception/known-record/dual-consent/charge rules.
- **Chain of Duty:** keeps its actual same-plane requirement.
- **Finite summons:** actual source ending/0-HP/Null Disabled rules remain real; Banishment never recreates an ended body.

**Cleric Banish Unholy remains a separate class technique.**
