## 6. Fate Coin — **one required timing repair**

Everything substantive is approved:

- 0.01 lb;
- AC10 / HP1;
- noncurrency conjured object;
- usable hand required;
- current holder spends Reaction;
- one active coin **per caster**, not per holder;
- can affect holder/ally/enemy rolls;
- holder must perceive the generating creature/object or actual local event within 30 ft + LoE;
- no secret-roll revelation;
- rerolls one die face only;
- multi-die pool remains the same roll;
- advantage/disadvantage reselects normally;
- shared rolls remain shared;
- Well's initial d4 may be rerolled before reserve establishment;
- mandatory new face;
- no retroactive reversal/refund;
- Mark/Fate lineage rule as above.

The only problem is this Reaction timing:

> “after one eligible die face is visible”

Because the card now explicitly defines an entire multi-die pool/set as **one original roll event**, that wording could allow Fate Coin to interrupt halfway through rolling a pool.

Replace that phrase in `resolution` with:

> **A current holder with a usable hand may flip and expend it as a Reaction after the complete original roll event has been rolled and its die faces are available under ordinary table information rules, but before that roll's mechanical consequences are applied.**

Then retain:

> **Choose one physical die from that completed original roll and reroll only it.**

This means, for example, you see all 4d6 before choosing which one d6 to reroll; you do not stop after die #1 and manipulate an incomplete event.

That matches the shared event/lineage architecture and does not weaken “any one die result.”
