## 2. Dominate Beast

The Beast predicate is **application-only**. Appearance/name does not establish it; an actual transformation changing the classification can. A later type change on the same continuing creature does not itself end established control unless its source expressly says so.

Initial INT success: no control.  
Failure: establish a separate **non-Charmed action-control component** and claim the shared hard-control allowance once. If blocked, no control/fallback.

### Orders

At each future regular target START, after ordinary start processing, the caster may consciously issue a **mental order** directing:

- ordinary movement; and
- one ordinary Action.

No hearing/language or continued 60-ft/LoE tether is required.

The caster receives **no remote sight, location, memory, target-list or legality oracle**. Orders can rely only on information the caster actually possesses. If the caster cannot consciously formulate an order, none is issued and the Beast retains ordinary agency that turn.

An ordered Attack Action uses its native ordinary attack sequence, including Extra Attack/native ordinary Multiattack. Ordinary required ammunition is spent; no compelled:

- RP/spellcasting;
- consumables/charges;
- recharge/per-rest resources;
- optional finite-resource riders;
- special limited ammunition;
- direct self-harm/suicidal routes;
- secret disclosure;
- permanent property surrender;
- binding-oath violation.

BA, Reactions and separately granted extra Actions remain target-controlled.

### Saves

### Shared-control integration

- **Compelled Budget Arbitration**;

- the reviewed **outside-initiative six-second control cadence** for START order opportunities.

Because there is no repeat save, virtual ENDs do not generate one.

If a controlled Beast is also a companion, its bond, ownership relation, companion slot, upkeep and command system remain. A competing owner command or other compulsion contests only the same actual Action/movement/inter­action budget under arbitration; nothing automatically deletes the companion framework.

No harm-ending clause.

**NEW:** non-Charmed control architecture, application-only Beast predicate, mental per-turn orders, no repeat saves and shared cadence binding.

## 4. Mental Chains

**Action; range 10 ft; one creature perceived with LoE; V/S/F; NEW INT save; concentration up to 1 minute.**

On failure, record the target's **fixed physical position at resolution** as the anchor point.

While active, the target cannot voluntarily move or voluntarily accept transport that would place its representative position **more than 10 ft from that anchor**.

This is a **boundary restriction**, not Root, Restrained, Speed 0 or a Speed reduction.

Within the 10-ft radius it may move normally, including tangential movement.

### Teleport/transport

The target cannot voluntarily:

- self-teleport beyond the boundary;
- willingly use a portal/teleport effect that would place it beyond the boundary;
- consent to voluntary transport beyond it.

This is not a universal planar/teleport anchor against hostile involuntary effects.

### Forced displacement

Forced movement, involuntary teleport or other hostile displacement can move the target beyond 10 ft.

If involuntarily outside the boundary:

- no snapback occurs;
- it may voluntarily move **toward** the anchor;
- it may move tangentially without increasing its current distance;
- it may not voluntarily increase its distance farther.

If involuntarily moved to another plane, Mental Chains does not block that transport and has no cross-planar distance measurement while separated; concentration/clock continue. If it later returns to the anchor's plane before expiry, the boundary restriction resumes against the original anchor.

No repeat save and no continuing caster-range tether.

The restriction does not itself invoke the hard whole-Action guard because it does not remove an Action/turn.

**NEW:** INT save, concentration minute, fixed anchor geometry and voluntary/involuntary transport distinction.

## 6. Suggestion

- ordinary labor/work;

- delivering or retrieving an ordinary item;
- speaking/meeting someone;

Player/target controller retains legal route/method/detail choices consistent with completing the suggested task.

No repeat save.

One active Suggestion per target across casters; later successfully established Suggestion replaces the older one. Saved/invalid/guard-blocked new attempts do not erase a valid older application.

## 8. Slow

Keep the two source modes **mechanically distinct**. They share an identity/name but not activation or rider package. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="3"}

# A. Slow — Enchantment mode

**Action; 60 ft; choose up to 6 perceived creatures with LoE; V/S/F; NEW WIS saves; concentration up to 1 minute.**

Each target independently saves.

Failure applies:

- Speed becomes **half its otherwise applicable Speed**;
- `−2 AC`;
- `−2 on Dexterity ability checks and Dexterity saving throws`;
- **at most one Action total on each turn**.

### “−2 DEX” conflict ruling — NEW

Do **not** change the underlying Dexterity score/modifier.

It is exactly:

> **−2 to Dexterity ability checks and Dexterity saving throws.**

It does **not** independently alter:

- initiative;
- Dexterity-based attacks;
- damage;
- spell DC;
- other derived values.

AC already has its own explicit `−2`, so no double-derived AC penalty.

### “one action only” — NEW

The creature may take at most **one Action total during a turn**.

This:

- retains its normal Action;
- suppresses any second/additional full Action during that same turn;
- does not remove Bonus Action;
- does not remove Reaction;
- does not limit inherent attacks inside one legal Attack Action;
- does not convert Action into Action-or-BA.

Because the creature still retains its normal Action, this is an **Action ceiling**, not whole-Action denial, and does **not** claim the shared hard-control allowance.

At every affected regular-turn END, including the current turn if applied during it, repeat the WIS save; success ends this mode's application for that target.

Outside initiative, use six-second virtual ENDs only for these repeat saves while the absolute 60-second deadline remains.

### Reaction interruption behavior

The trigger is **not** a Bonus Action or Reaction. Therefore the newly imposed no-Reactions rider never retroactively cancels the Reaction that triggered Slow, because such a trigger is not authorized.

### Reaction denial
