## 3. Locked hierarchy (verbatim order, with role notes)

| # | Layer | Role |
|---|-------|------|
| 1 | **Spiral** | The real cosmic order-principle (Calan'thir's domain); the true "top" of the cosmology. |
| 2 | **Old Ones / Ul'nak** | = the Silent Ones (confirmed same entity, [`OWNER_COSMOLOGY_LOCKS_2026-07-30.md`](./OWNER_COSMOLOGY_LOCKS_2026-07-30.md) Q1). "Ul'nak" is the First Song's name for this force/hush (`cosmology-lore.json` Ch. V: the Choir "parleys with Ul'nak/the hush"). GM-secret. |
| 3 | **Heartcell** | The Silent Ones'/Old Ones' machine — 100-floor resonance structure, ten Gate divisions, opening cycle, reward architecture ([`OWNER_COSMOLOGY_LOCKS_2026-07-30.md`](./OWNER_COSMOLOGY_LOCKS_2026-07-30.md) Q3). GM-secret. |
| 4 | **Calan'thir & Asmodeus (custodians)** | Public, real, joint builders of the Crucible atop the Heartcell — "custodians" of the machine, not its inventors. Public-facing layer. |
| 5 | **First Choir (7)** | Korvelan, Vaerilune, Delvaran, Shaelara, Xalarex, Thalirion, Virellian — named archon-virtues from the real cosmology. Exactly seven; never expand or fold into the Fifteenfold Pantheon. |
| 6 | **Fifteenfold Pantheon (mortal)** | Appendix B's Solarius/Caelestra/etc. — mortal worship-masks over deeper truths mortals cannot perceive directly. This is where the *folk religion* layer begins. |
| 7 | **Cultural gods / ascended** | Appendix B's Cultural Pantheons (Thalassian, Nordheim, Zephyrian, Oceanic) and Ascended Beings (Herolux the Brave, Mystara the Archmage, etc.) — the most localized, most mortal-invented tier. |

**Rungs 1–4 are cosmic truth** (rungs 2–3 GM-secret, rung 4 public-and-true). **Rungs 5–7 are the domain of this rewrite** — rung 5 (First Choir) is also cosmic truth but is the *point of contact* mortal religion dimly perceives and mythologizes into rungs 6–7.

## 4. Rename + disclaimer (draft opening, for eventual rulebook patch)

**New title:** *Appendix B: Faiths and Cosmologies of Aethel* (was: "The Lore of Aethel")

**Draft disclaimer paragraph** (to precede the existing Appendix B prose once patched in):

> *The beliefs recorded in this appendix are mortal theology, not objective cosmic history. Priests, scholars, and cults across Aethel have spent millennia reasoning about forces they cannot directly perceive — the true architecture of the Spiral, the silence of the Old Ones, and the machinery beneath the Great Descent are known to few, if any, mortals. What follows is what the faithful believe: a creation myth, a pantheon of masks, and a diagram of the planes, sincerely held and widely taught, and useful for understanding how ordinary people in Aethel think about the divine. It should not be read as a statement of what is actually true in the setting.*

**This paragraph is a draft starting point only** — see §6 for why the underlying file is not patched yet.

| Layer | Do players/PCs know it? | Basis |
|-------|--------------------------|-------|
| **Spiral** | Know it exists as a cosmic force/setting concept; do not know its full mechanics | Standard cosmology exposure, not secret-locked |
| **Old Ones / Ul'nak (= Silent Ones)** | **No.** GM-secret. Not in newcomer guides, not commonly known even to scholars | [`SILENT_ONES_CANON.md`](./SILENT_ONES_CANON.md) — "no one knows about the Silent Ones" |
| **Heartcell** | **No.** GM-secret, discoverable only via the clue ladder (architecture predating scripts → Custodians/Bearers/Signatories → Pact-governs-but-didn't-originate → Heartcell operating cycle) | [`OWNER_COSMOLOGY_LOCKS_2026-07-30.md`](./OWNER_COSMOLOGY_LOCKS_2026-07-30.md) Q3 player-knowledge model |
| **Calan'thir & Asmodeus as custodians of the Crucible** | **Yes — this is the complete public myth.** Virtually everyone accepts the joint-creation story; cultures argue over credit, not over whether it happened | Q3: "virtually everyone accepts joint creation" |
| **First Choir (7, named)** | Known at a scholarly/cosmology level as part of the wider Spiral/Pact story; not secret, but not everyday common knowledge for a random villager either | `cosmology-lore.json` Ch. V — named in-world, not flagged secret |
| **Fifteenfold Pantheon (mortal)** | **Yes — this is what ordinary people worship day-to-day.** Widely known, widely taught, treated by most mortals as literally true | Appendix B content, now reframed as folk religion per this lock |
| **Cultural gods / ascended** | **Yes** — local/regional worship, same as any real-world folk pantheon; varies by culture and continent | Appendix B content |

**Do not** let a party's discovery of the Heartcell/Silent-Ones truth (rungs 2–3) retroactively "correct" NPCs' Fifteenfold Pantheon worship (rung 6) — folk religion keeps functioning normally in the world even after a party learns the truth; the world at large never learns it (per Q3 player-knowledge model).
