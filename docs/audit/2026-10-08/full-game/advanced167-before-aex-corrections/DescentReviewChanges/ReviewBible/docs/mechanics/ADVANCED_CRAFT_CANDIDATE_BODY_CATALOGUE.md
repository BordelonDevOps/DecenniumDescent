## Actual stations and implements

One active creator needs a stable clear surface at least24 × 18 inches and appropriately sized actual jigs for the selected complete weapon, suit or machine. A Resonance Circle needs one clear5-foot-square work bay and the genuine ordinary lens/stylus apparatus. A Slab supports the whole plate; a Scribe’s Desk permits real open-page preparation; a Tinker’s Workshop safely supports/fixtures each current assembly. These are actual admission requirements, not free stations or new automatic construction charges. Separate reserved storage can hold other bodies while work proceeds sequentially. Actual load can require stronger support. A portable sheet on a ledge does not satisfy the fixed facility flag.

Keep each actual complete primary kit’s original price/mass. An included ordinary implement may satisfy a source-listed working requirement but is not a second physical tool, consumed tiered ingredient or complete output body. The player guide gives the narrow Runecarver working-lens correspondence. Actual light, safe access, hands, material preservation and hazardous containment remain necessary.
