# Core resolution and injury playtest rules

This is the active provisional integration for rolls, damage, zero HP, Wounds and injuries in the developing campaign candidate. It resolves specific conflicts between approved Core V2, compatible Player Rulebook V6 rules and the retained Gameplay Loop. Original books and alternate procedures remain unchanged. Full conditions, equipment, hazards and recovery integration remain separate work.

Use with [class rules](CLASS_PLAYTEST_RULES.md), [spell rules](SPELL_INTEGRATION_RULES.md) and the [complete coverage ledger](GAME_DEVELOPMENT_COVERAGE.md). The [source conference](../../../../docs/audit/2026-10-08/full-game/CODEX_CORE_RESOLUTION_CONFERENCE.json), [V6 findings](../../../../docs/audit/2026-10-08/full-game/CODEX_CORE_SOURCE_FOLLOWUP.md) and [GPT proposal](../../../../docs/audit/2026-10-08/full-game/GPT_CORE_STATE_AND_INJURY_PROPOSAL.md) preserve the reasoning.

This overlay states the selected interpretation or newly authored rule wherever those sources leave a gap. An explicit exception on an operative, reviewed class/spell/item card applies only within its printed scope; a historical alternate or a same-named feature is not an extra grant. This candidate does not promote an entire historical branch.

## Rolls

Choose the roll type before rolling. Use the actual ability modifier, including negative values, and normal proficiency only when applicable. Existing advantage/disadvantage chooses the higher/lower of two d20s; multiple sources do not stack, and any advantage plus disadvantage cancels. Add other legal modifiers once. Fractions round down unless a rule specifies otherwise. One tile is 5 feet. Class L, S and effect M retain their definitions in the shared class rules.

**Attack:** roll d20 plus the appropriate attack modifier against AC. Meeting AC hits. Natural 1 always misses; natural 20 always hits and critically doubles damage dice, not flat modifiers. Beating AC by 10 is not a critical hit. An explicit broader critical range does not stack with another; use the broadest eligible range. In this overlay, a natural result below 20 in that range must still meet AC unless the feature expressly says it always hits.

**Task, skill or tool check against a fixed DC:** total at least DC+10 is Critical Success; at least DC is Success; at most DC-10 is Critical Failure; other totals below DC are Failure. Natural 20 then improves one degree and natural 1 worsens one, bounded at either end. A task printing only success/failure collapses the two upper degrees to success and two lower degrees to failure; no extra consequence is invented. A natural 20 can still fail an otherwise impossible check, and a natural 1 with sufficient modifiers can still succeed.

**Saving throw:** total at least DC succeeds; otherwise it fails. Natural 1 and 20 have no generic special outcome. A printed failure by 5 or 10 compares the final total with the DC; it does not add an unprinted critical-save system. Death saves are the specific exception below.

**Opposed check, new provisional tie rule:** compare final totals. Higher wins; an exact tie leaves the existing state unchanged, so the initiating creature fails to change it. Do not turn task natural shifts into hidden numeric modifiers. A maneuver explicitly printing degree outcomes must specify how those apply.

## Resolve a damage event

Resolve each genuinely separate hit or damage event once. Do not split one packet to multiply Wound saves or combine separate hits merely to cross a threshold. Feature-specific prevention that acts before damage or before reaching zero resolves at its printed timing first.

1. Resolve attack/save, damage and applicable defenses. The remaining nonnegative amount is **resolved damage**.
2. Record HP, Wounds and zero-HP state before the event. Remove temporary HP first. **Post-temp damage = resolved damage minus temporary HP absorbed.** Keep this amount even when it exceeds current HP; floor the resulting current HP at zero.
3. Check V6 Instant Death. If post-temp damage minus HP before the hit is at least maximum HP, the creature dies. For a creature already at zero, this is post-temp damage at least maximum HP. Stop: there is no Wound save, death-save failure or successful knockout from that event.
4. Otherwise, a living creature makes one Wound save if it dropped from positive HP to zero, if **2 × post-temp damage is at least maximum HP**, or if the effect actually inflicts Maiming. Overlapping triggers still cause only one save. The post-temp threshold and one-save rule are new provisional interpretations. Maiming is an independent trigger even when temporary HP absorbs all ordinary damage; immunity to Maiming itself still applies.
5. Make a Constitution save against **max(10, floor(post-temp damage / 2))**. Use pre-event Wounds for the -1-per-Wound penalty. Maiming with zero post-temp damage uses DC 10. A Wound gained now cannot penalize the roll that created it.
6. A success adds no Wound. A failure that began with zero, one or two Wounds adds exactly one; failure by 5 or more also gives that Wound one Injury. A failure that began with three Wounds kills the creature instead: no fourth Wound and no Injury roll. Stop processing later living-target consequences if dead.
7. If the creature was already at zero before this event and post-temp damage is positive, end Stable. A creature using death saves adds one failure, or two for a critical hit; three failures kill it. An ordinary creature that does not use death saves dies instead, without starting death counters. A hit from positive HP to zero does not also cause a same-packet death failure.
8. If this event newly reduces positive HP to zero, a valid melee knockout makes the surviving creature Stable. Otherwise a character/significant NPC using death saves begins dying; an ordinary monster dies. A previously Stable creature whose temporary HP absorbed all damage remains Stable unless an independent Maiming result killed it. The Director identifies significant NPCs consistently. Knockout cannot override Instant Death or a fatal Wound save.

Core's Maiming and Wound trigger rules remain subject to an actual feature's eligibility. The reference helper receives already legal events; it does not grant every attack Maiming or a damage-prevention exception.

## Zero HP and recovery

A living character at zero HP is Unconscious. Core's condition already includes Incapacitated, unawareness, dropping held items and falling Prone; an adjacent hit is critical as specified there. Healing does not silently remove other independent causes of Unconscious or Prone.

While dying, make one death save at the **start of each regular initiative turn**. Roll d20 without modifiers unless a feature specifically modifies death saves. Natural 20 restores 1 HP; natural 1 adds two failures. Other results of 10 or more add one success; 9 or less add one failure. Three successes make the creature Stable; three failures make it Dead. Extra turns and Actions add no extra death saves. Stable or Dead creatures do not roll them.

Regaining positive HP or becoming Stable resets both counters. Temporary HP does not raise normal HP, end Unconscious or reset counters. Fully absorbed damage does not end Stable or add a death failure in this provisional interpretation. Independent Maiming can still cause its Wound save.

**Stabilize:** an Action and DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check, or an Action using one healer's-kit use automatically, stabilizes a living dying creature at zero. It remains Unconscious and makes no death saves. Roll 1d4 once when it becomes Stable and record its absolute recovery time that many hours later. If still Stable then, it regains 1 HP. Damage reaching normal HP cancels that deadline and resumes dying with the appropriate failures. Rest and Stable recovery use the same elapsed clock, so four hours of recovery inside an eight-hour Watch do not cost twelve hours. A later rest does not separately replay the Stable healing event.

**Dead:** ordinary healing and stabilization do not restore life. Resurrection must satisfy its own willing-soul, body, time, cost and target rules. It does not automatically remove Wounds, Corruption, debts, legal death or claims upon the body. Corpses cannot supply repeated resource triggers.

## Wounds and Critical state

Each Wound imposes **-1 Constitution saves** and reduces the normal maximum number of Hit Dice recovered per long rest by one, to a minimum of zero. It does not reduce each healing die. The broader recovery rules own the normal recovery maximum and rest legality.

At three Wounds the creature is **Critically Wounded**: Speed is reduced by 10 feet, to minimum zero, and the next failed Wound save is fatal. The failure that first raises two Wounds to three is not that later failure. Core's named **Wounded** condition (paragraph 782, preserved in [the 28-condition source register](../../data/playtest/core_condition_sources.json)) explicitly summarizes these same Wounds; it does not add duplicate penalties. Its conflicting V6 predecessor is inactive in this overlay. This individual source comparison does not complete the remaining condition audit.

**New provisional healing ceiling:** while Critical, healing cannot increase HP above **max(1, maximum HP - 1)**. Maximum HP itself does not change. Gaining the third Wound does not immediately remove existing HP; a later attempted heal also cannot reduce HP. The minimum of one permits recovery when maximum HP is one and is an explicit exception to “cannot regain full HP.” Removing enough Wounds to leave Critical removes its Speed penalty and ceiling, but does not itself heal HP.

This is an explicit Gameplay-derived integration. ## Encounter and action resources

Establish terrain, light, hazards, objectives, exits and cargo; determine awareness; roll `d20 + DEX modifier + legal bonuses`. Highest first. Players choose player/player ties; the Director resolves ties involving NPCs. Keep the resulting ordered slots for the encounter. A round represents approximately six seconds. A **regular turn** is a creature's normal initiative slot even if it cannot act. An extra turn is distinct.

A turn normally permits movement, one Action, a Bonus Action when an actual rule grants a use, and one ordinary free object interaction. A second ordinary interaction or an Action-time object activation uses an Action. Brief communication is not an extra Action. Conditions and specific features may restrict these permissions.

The shared limit is one **extra full Action per creature per numbered round**. Such an Action cannot grant another extra Action, Bonus Action or Reaction. This cap is independent of Reaction timing. Extra turns do not by themselves refresh Reactions, recharge, periodic ticks, recovery ticks or the regular-turn allowances defined here.

**Reaction interpretation.** Begin an encounter with one available Reaction unless surprised or otherwise prohibited. Refresh at each regular own-turn start; never at the numbered round boundary or an extra turn. A late-initiative creature can therefore react before its first turn, refresh, and react again in the same numbered round. A legal trigger, available resource and all feature prerequisites are still required.

**Explicit additional Reactions.** A reviewed card can grant a separate limited use without refreshing the ordinary resource. Warrior Mind Shock supplies its stated once-per-duration non-attack Reaction; spending one resource does not consume/reset the other. Surprise, Incapacitated and Focused still restrict both. It grants no Counterspell knowledge or waived costs. Extra turns refresh neither. Per-dose reapplication/stacking and other specific grants remain part of the class semantic pass.

**Surprise — V6 plus NEW Bonus Action clarification.** On a surprised creature's first regular turn it cannot voluntarily move or take an Action or Bonus Action. It cannot react until that turn ends. Its slot, durations, passive effects, concentration, periodic effects and recharge still occur. Surprise alone is not Incapacitated or Unconscious. Brief communication and an otherwise legal simple free interaction remain possible.

**Entry — NEW.** Standard end-round reinforcements roll/receive initiative and first act next round. An explicit midround entrant acts this round only if its complete ordered slot is still upcoming; entry during that exact slot counts as already passed. It has its initial Reaction unless prohibited. A summon-specific timing rule overrides this generic entry procedure.

After the final slot, resolve Core's end-round hazards, reinforcements, morale, objectives and boss phases. Conclude the scene with surrender, capture, pursuit, harvesting access and noise consequences under their own rules; winning initiative does not resolve those automatically.

## Time and event ordering — NEW

For a duration created in initiative, record the relevant original regular start/end marker. Existing effects keep that **virtual marker**, repeating every six seconds if the creature leaves or changes initiative. Reordering, rerolling, extra turns, death/removal or leaving/reentering initiative never restart an existing duration. New effects use the current schedule. Source death still ends concentration, a source-dependent aura/tether or an effect explicitly ending on death.

- A bare hostile target-bound **N rounds** ends at the Nth future regular target-end marker. An in-progress target turn's current end does not count.
- A bare self/source buff, ally-targeted buff or source-controlled aura **N rounds** ends at the Nth future source-start marker. Explicit target-turn, pulse or absolute timing overrides this default. Activation during source turn 1 for three rounds ends at source turn 4's start.
- **Next turn** means the next future regular turn of the named creature, not the current turn's still-upcoming end. An unqualified next turn refers to source/user under the existing shared rule. Explicit “this turn” can include the current turn.
- An outside-initiative bare N-round effect instead gets an absolute application-time + N×6 seconds deadline. Entering initiative preserves that deadline.
- Explicit periodic profiles retain their registered clocks and tick counts. Never create a second timer merely because a generic duration rule also exists.

The [exact GPT review](../../../../docs/audit/2026-10-08/full-game/GPT_COMBAT_EXACT_FINDINGS.md) confirms ally-buff source clocks and excluding the current turn for explicit next-turn endpoints. The helper excludes a specific current-end marker only if that marker would otherwise be selected: application during a turn at time1 with end3 excludes3 and selects9; application at/after3 also selects9, without erroneously skipping again to15.

At a regular turn's **start**, resolve in this order:

1. Expire start-ending effects and apply immediate state changes from those expirations.
2. Refresh Reaction; tick regular-start recharge/non-action clocks.
3. Resolve already-due absolute state recovery if now legal, including a previously blocked Stable recovery deadline.
4. If still alive, dying and at 0 HP with death saves, make that turn's death save.
5. Resolve other start effects and periodic events in application order.
6. Begin voluntary turn decisions.

At **end**, resolve triggered effects, due damage, saves and maintenance before end-ending effects expire; then the slot finishes. No extra turn creates another regular marker. The caller must not reuse a resolved marker.

For simultaneous effects, earlier application timestamp precedes later. For simultaneous components of one source, its printed internal order controls; otherwise use stable application sequence, with source/mechanical ID as a final tie-break. Record that sequence when creating the effects. Beneficial/harmful labels do not change priority. Periodic family arbitration happens once per family/marker before its damage, retaining the family registry's consumed loser ticks.

Examples: dying at 0 rolls its death save before ordinary start-turn healing; a fatal death save is not undone by ordinary healing. Stable at 0 skips the save, then start-turn damage can break Stable. A natural 20 can restore 1 HP before a due Bleed drops it to 0 as a new drop event. If Decay expires while a Stable recovery deadline is already due, the old deadline resolves once without rerolling recovery time.

For ordinary nonspell actions, feature/ammunition costs are paid on release unless text charges setup. ## Movement and geometry

Track **spent movement cost**, not distance, for ordinary turn movement. Mode cap = `(1 + number of legal Dash actions this turn) × current Speed for that mode`; remaining = max(0, cap − spent cost). Switching modes never refunds cost. Recalculate cap on Speed changes, preserving spent cost. Stand from Prone costs floor(half current Speed) and requires nonzero Speed and enough budget. Dropping Prone costs no movement.

For each foot traveled, normal/difficult/severe terrain costs 1/2/3; use strongest terrain only. Add +1 for crawling and +1 for squeezing. Crawl in difficult =3:1; crawl+squeeze in difficult =4:1. A nonhostile occupied space counts as difficult terrain. Enter a hostile creature's space only with ≥two-size difference or an express feature; never willingly end in an occupied space. Squeeze through a space one size smaller, with own attack/DEX-save disadvantage and attacks-against advantage.

Walk10 through difficult spends20; switch to Fly30 leaves10. Stand with Speed30 spends15; switching to Fly30 leaves15. Two legal Dashes at Speed30 give cap90; prior expenditure still counts. Interpose/Press grant5 budget: they cannot enter a difficult 5-ft tile costing10. Their separate budgets never refill ordinary movement. Ready movement uses Speed at release; Withdraw uses half current Speed. All voluntary movement requires nonzero relevant Speed.

## Cover, sight, Hide and Search

Keep **visibility**, **known location**, and **line of effect** separate. Seeing through a barrier does not itself permit attacking through it. Best cover only: half +2 AC/DEX saves; three-quarters +5; total prevents direct targeting through that obstruction. Never add +2 and +5. For areas, evaluate line/cover from the area's origin to each target; total cover there excludes a creature unless the effect explicitly travels around/through the obstruction. The origin point itself must be legally targetable.

Within normal ranged range, no range penalty; beyond normal through long, disadvantage; beyond long, illegal. Ranged attacks also have disadvantage when within5ft of a non-Incapacitated hostile that can see the attacker, even if the attacker cannot see that hostile. All advantage/disadvantage follows the single cancellation rule.

NEW **Hide**: permitted only against observers unable to see you clearly or where a feature permits. Roll DEX Stealth once, record natural die and total. Against each observer's passive DC `10 + WIS Perception modifier including applicable proficiency`, use the Core four-degree task system. Meet DC succeeds before natural1/20 degree adjustment; Success/Critical Success establishes Hidden for that observer. No unsourced passive ±5 adjustment for advantage/disadvantage. Actual Search rolls use their normal modifiers.

Hidden means that observer cannot see you and does not know your location. Clear sight or learning your position ends Hidden for that observer. Moving between hiding places can retain Hidden only if no clear sight/revealing event occurs. A failed Hide cannot simply be rerolled unchanged; a materially new hiding position, concealment, distraction, sight-breaking event or express reroll feature is required. Hidden is not a generic immunity or a free stealth roll for every move.

**Search** Action: WIS Perception for sensory detection; INT Investigation for appropriate traces/deduction; Core Arcana/Engineering where relevant. Directly locating a Hidden creature defaults to Perception against its recorded final Stealth total as a static DC, using task degrees/natural shifts. Success reveals location only to the searcher and ends Hidden for that observer, without automatically granting sight. Communicate normally to inform others. Search can be repeated on later turns by paying the Action.

Attacks against unseen targets have disadvantage; unseen attackers have advantage, subject to the **Core Invisible exception**: once its position is known, Invisible's own attack modifiers end for that observer and cannot immediately return solely because it remains Invisible. Independent darkness, smoke, blindness or hiding can still supply modifiers.

Resolve an attack using pre-attack information; afterward reveal location to observers able to perceive its evidence. If an unseen target's location is unknown, do not directly choose it for a creature-target effect. A weapon/projectile can instead attack a suspected space: wrong space automatically misses; correct space uses unseen rules. A “creature you can see” requirement is never met by position knowledge alone. Area effects can target legal points under their own rules.

Existing explicit trade-level grants remain intact. Being proficient with unrelated tools, Arcana or Survival does not automatically give this credential.

An untrained crafter has effective trade level 0. Recipes of skill levels 1–4 can be attempted with the source disadvantage when otherwise physically possible. A trained crafter can attempt recipes up to `max(4, current trade level)`. Above level 4 the crafter must also possess or be given legitimate access to the exact formula. A trained level-1 novice does not lose the basic recipes available to an untrained character. Missing necessary physical tools or the station prevents the attempt; disadvantage does not manufacture either.

For a new credential during play, complete 80 active training hours with a willing instructor proficient in that primary tool and at least level 5 in that trade. Pay a default 10 gp tuition for the course, plus actual tool ownership or rental and living costs. Hours cannot overlap other work or rest. Completion grants that tool proficiency and trade level 1 with zero XP, once; repeated courses give no duplicate benefit. This is a new playtest training route, not a revision of class/ancestry/background grants. A named source's specific training route wins for its own grant.

Source rank bands (Novice, Apprentice, Journeyman, Expert, Master, Grandmaster) are descriptions of the catalog, not another hard tier gate. Forty-nine records sit outside the printed rank/tier bands. Use their exact skillLevel and explicit prerequisites rather than silently changing those recipes or denying them by a second band test. Public Tier I–V remains the public knowledge ceiling regardless of a character's high trade level. Restricted and Director recipes require actual in-world access and their separately reviewed cards; a rank title cannot reveal them.

## The crafting check

**Source:** `d20 + chosen ability modifier + proficiency contribution + tool bonus + facility bonus + assistant bonus`, against the recipe difficulty. Choose one of the recipe's printed ability options before rolling. **Completion:** do not add the numerical trade level to the check. PB comes from total character level, once, when qualified by actual primary-tool training; an explicit applicable expertise feature replaces that contribution with its stated multiplier. Training in both a skill and the tool cannot double it.

Ordinary functional tools and a suitable ordinary facility each give a bonus of +0. They permit the work. A specific reviewed item, facility or feature may give a stated bonus; similar descriptive words such as fine, superior or research station do not invent a crafting bonus. The expedition research station's +2 research/identification applies to those checks, not crafting. Same-named bonuses do not stack. At trade level 10, choose and record one specialty; its source +1 applies only to a work order within that specialty. No automatic free ingredient substitution follows from the word specialty.

Each qualified assistant gives +1, up to the recipe's `maximumAssistants` (0–3). They must possess the relevant primary-tool proficiency, be eligible to attempt that recipe, and work all of its required active hours alongside the lead. Formula access can be shared for this work order. Assistance does not reduce time, waive ingredients, award extra output or grant assistant trade XP. Assistants cannot assist multiple simultaneous orders or perform a separate Watch activity during those same hours. General team assistance cannot bypass a recipe's zero-assistant limit.

In a safe suitable workshop, a trained lead may use 10 on the die if the recipe is at least five skill levels below their current trade level. This replaces the die, is not a natural 10/20/1, and uses the same bonuses, costs and hours. It is unavailable when under threat or when actual disadvantage remains on the crafting check. Declare it before rolling; a failed roll cannot be replaced afterward.

## Active time and expedition scheduling

**Source recovered from public V5 P11–12 and P88:** an Opening is 100 days, or 2,400 elapsed hours. The V4 system's 240-hour references are stale. Crafting hours represent active work, not free downtime. The recipe records consistently convert a workday to 8 active hours, a workweek to 40, and a workmonth to 160. These are work units, not a promise that every calendar week/month has that many days.

**Completion:** use `activeWorkHours` when present. Five Director-only one-year recipes have null hours; for this framework only, one workyear is 12 workmonths = 1,920 active hours. Preserve their source null and mark the overlay as new; these formulas remain individually unreviewed. A time-mode name is a station/access requirement, not permission to reduce required hours. An eight-hour recipe labeled expedition still needs all eight hours.

Field work requires its actual portable tools and a suitable place. A recipe is field-capable only when its `fieldCraftable` flag permits it; the fact that its trade is generally field-capable is insufficient. Expedition-mode work needs a fortified camp or research station. Workshop work needs a proper workshop. Haven-mode work needs a named secure workshop and storage. Legacy/special work needs a protected long-term facility or an explicitly supported between-season arrangement. Shorter time from a feature does not erase the recipe's station/access requirement.

Schedule one eight-hour Watch at a time. A member assigned to crafting, gathering or preservation can perform compatible sequential tasks within that Watch, recording actual minutes. The unused minutes are not another full team activity. Travel, guarding, assisting, spellcasting, training and work cannot all claim the same minutes; only an explicit ability can authorize concurrent tasks. Each character may schedule at most two active Watches and must satisfy the separately reviewed rest rules; this is a ceiling, not an exemption from fatigue, interruption or danger. For this supplement, an active work Watch means time committed to crafting, harvesting, preservation, trade training, formula research or assisting those activities. The two-Watch ceiling limits this framework's work contribution; it does not by itself resolve whether another reviewed subsystem permits a distinct noncrafting activity in the remaining Watch. Rest, Strain, fatigue and expedition legality continue to come from their own governing rules. An eight-hour beneficial Long Rest cannot simultaneously be eight hours of crafting. Trance's sleep reduction does not shorten the required Long Rest or grant free working time.

Workday notation is normalized to active hours before a valid speed feature applies. Forge Dwarf Master Craftsman reduces eligible artisan-tool creation hours by 25%; use hours × 0.75, rounding upward only to the next whole minute. It does not shorten rest, travel, ingredient growth/spoilage, training, another person's work, harvesting, or a separately fixed natural-remedy/tinker racial procedure. It does not shorten a recipe's independently stated incubation or calendar wait. Other applicable time modifiers require their own actual text; no unprinted stacking multiplier is created here.

Long orders may be paused between active phases. A default phase is at most eight active hours. Completing a phase safely permits secure storage and later resumption without lost progress. Forced interruption inside a phase loses only unfinished progress in that phase; completed phases remain. No crafting roll occurs yet. Reserved perishables still age, and explicit physical destruction can lose actual inventory. There is no arbitrary full restart or repeated free quality roll. Before an uninterrupted-phase order begins, record its phase lengths and storage needs; absent a more specific formula, use this default.

At the season boundary, unfinished work continues only if its actual location, workers and storage remain legally and physically available under the existing campaign rules. The 2,400-hour Opening does not grant 2,400 labor hours per character. The Haven activity 'Brew a Batch' supplies a workday assignment (8 hours), not automatic completion of every recipe regardless of its own duration.

These are **crafting outcome labels**, separate from equipment grade and Core artifact market condition. An Exceptional craft does not automatically become Exceptional equipment (+3 statistics), and a Masterwork craft does not create a new grade or a magic item. An item named Superior does not obtain equipment-grade bonuses unless its reviewed profile explicitly binds that grade.

Resolve this specific six-band crafting table instead of general expedition outcome shifts. A natural 1 is a Mishap even if the total is high. A natural 20 uses its numerical total with no extra band shift. A margin of −10 or lower is a Mishap.

| 5 through 9 | Exceptional | Choose 125% batch yield, rounded down, or the improvement option below. |
| 0 through 4 | Standard | Listed number of ordinary finished units. |
| −1 through −4 | Flawed | If base batch quantity is at least 2, produce half quantity rounded down, with ordinary unit effects. If quantity is 1, make one item with a defined flaw below. |

**Completion:** quality choices are declared after the check, once for the whole batch. Extra yield does not itself consume another batch of ingredients; it represents reduced processing waste. For physical outputs the card must specify lawful packaging and output mass; total output cannot exceed actual retained material plus explicitly supplied extra mundane carrier/packaging. A yield increase cannot create missing bottles, duplicate a unique named core, or duplicate the existing item being enchanted. Such an order uses its legal improvement option or retains Standard quantity and its quality label. Floor arithmetic may give no extra units; no fractional item or banked remainder is created.

The default improvement option increases explicitly defined maximum structural item HP by 25% (Exceptional) or 50% (Masterwork), rounded down, minimum +1. Current HP increases by the same amount for the new undamaged item. It never increases creature HP, AC, damage, save DC, spell rank, RP, charges or capacity. A recipe card may replace this with one precise property/charge option reviewed for that recipe. With neither known structural HP nor a reviewed improvement option, retain Standard output and the quality label only. Consumables do not gain reusable doses or longer durations from this generic option.

A quantity-1 Flawed durable item has maximum structural HP equal to half its ordinary maximum, rounded down, minimum 1, and begins at that reduced maximum. This reduced maximum is a crafting flaw, not ordinary structural damage. Ordinary equipment repair may restore current structural HP only up to that reduced maximum; it does not remove the flaw, increase the item's maximum, alter its equipment grade or convert it to Standard quality. Removing a crafting flaw requires an explicit reviewed rework procedure; absent one, the reduced maximum persists. A quantity-1 consumable whose useful effect has a positive finite duration instead has half that duration, rounded down to a whole round (six seconds), minimum one round. Instant healing/damage/RP restoration and otherwise incompatible results have no generic flaw profile: treat that Flawed result as Failed with the normal 25% non-catalyst recovery. Do not halve an undefined feature or invent a permanent debuff. The completed output card must state whether either default applies.

Masterwork preserves **one component unit total** bearing role catalyst and consumed=true, chosen after the check. It must be an actual catalyst used in this batch, not a named irreplaceable component whose exact formula requires destruction; an indivisible catalyst cannot be split. Return that unit to inventory unchanged rather than deducting it. With no eligible catalyst, nothing is returned. The ingredient's exact identity, tier and remaining shelf life persist; this is not a fresh item. Nonconsumed tools always remain unless a specific mishap physically damages one.

**Reviewed Forge Dwarf completion:** when eligible artisan-tool creation achieves Standard, treat its output as Exceptional for the yield/improvement choice. Already Exceptional/Masterwork remains so. Failed, Flawed and Mishap do not upgrade. This gives the ancestry's quality language a bounded crafting function; it grants no equipment grade, price multiplier, success guarantee or extra XP.

## Trade XP and specialties

**Source:** first successful craft 4 XP; nontrivial repeat 2; difficult failed attempt with usable research 1; trivial mass production 0. Advancing from level L to L+1 costs 10+L trade XP. Maximum level 20. The source per-recipe 4-XP field is not an extra award on top of this system.

**Completion:** a successful craft means Standard, Exceptional or Masterwork. Award 4 for the first success of a recipe ID in that trade if not trivial; subsequent nontrivial successes award 2. A recipe is trivial when current trade level is at least `trivialAtSkillLevel` (all source thresholds are recipe skill level +5). Triviality wins even for a previously unmade recipe. Changing ingredients, quality, batch size, location or a source alias does not reset first-success history.

A Flawed/Failed/Mishap order may give 1 XP only when its recipe is at least the crafter's current trade level, it consumed actual inputs and hours, and the lead records a concrete usable research finding. This is once per recipe ID per trade level, not repeat failure farming; mishap 6 gives none. No assistant or uninvolved observer receives this work-order award. Untrained attempts record experience narratively but bank no numerical trade XP and grant no proficiency. Training initializes zero XP.

Spend XP immediately when a threshold is reached; carry excess toward the next level, repeat if enough, and stop at 20. At level 10 select one concrete specialty such as antidotes, metal shields or textile containers. Record its scope before the next check; it cannot be renamed for each work order. Its +1 applies once. Further specialties, retraining and unique substitutions require another explicit feature or agreed research result; the rank alone grants none.

Keep the exact recipe ID and component occurrence. Global material names alone are insufficient: 143 embedded names have different tier/price/weight variants. Record identity as the recipe component's name, tier, category, properties, unit weight, unit value and source occurrence. Quantity belongs to the inventory lot, not the identity. Preserve component IDs where supplied; none are silently merged or normalized to a cheaper same-name material. The 2-gp crafting Crystal Vial and 5-gp equipment Vial remain distinct records unless an explicit compatibility/substitution ruling is recorded.

This does not extend the generic V4 material-tier ladder or generic harvesting entitlement beyond Tier VI. A Tier 7–10 stored component therefore receives no generic harvest DC, public availability, rarity right or substitution source merely from that number; it requires its actual recipe/source acquisition procedure or another separately reviewed rule.

**Source:** substitutions require the relevant properties and tier unless the recipe names an irreplaceable ingredient. **Completion:** before spending materials, a substitute must satisfy each replaced unit's tier (equal or higher), all required functional properties and role, compatible physical form, and at least its required amount. ## Identify and harvest

**Source:** identify with the creature type's knowledge skill, then extract using a relevant gathering trade. The type mapping and size times/yields are in the trade reference. This procedure concerns actual remains and environmental nodes already present; it does not create a creature, corpse, mineral vein, legal entitlement or formula.

**Completion:** the Director records a finite harvest manifest before checks: actual accessible categories, unit identities/tier/weight/value, quantities or yield dice, damaged/unavailable parts, preservation deadlines, and any exceptional drop. Normally expose four to six plausible categories, but a creature with fewer real categories has fewer. These are choices within one finite yield pool, not four to six copies of the yield roll. A Tiny creature cannot produce a Large creature's organ or an unlimited number of crystals.

Use the exact component's explicit harvest DC when present; otherwise the highest desired material tier sets DC: I 12, II 13, III 15, IV 16, V 18, VI 19. These are the existing expedition tier DCs, not recipe crafting DCs. The generic table has no public Tier VII–X harvest entitlement. A specially restricted ingredient beyond this table needs a separately stated DC and access procedure before rolling.

Identification takes 1 minute of close examination. Use INT and the source type skill except Medicine uses WIS; PB requires actual relevant training, with existing task-scoped expertise replacing it. One lead and at most one trained assistant (+1) can participate. A success reveals the manifest's usable categories, hazards and preservation needs; failure identifies no hidden qualities. There is no invented false fact on failure. The party may still extract a plainly recognizable category such as a hide, using the declared DC with disadvantage while unidentified. Further identification attempts require new evidence, a different method or consultation, not repeated rolls by every party member on unchanged evidence. Identification does not itself extract anything.

The extraction lead selects a compatible gathering trade: Skinning for animal parts, Herbalism for plants, Mining for mineral deposits, Fishing for aquatic gathering, Salvaging for constructs and dismantled devices; a specific listed source may authorize another. Use that trade's ability and primary-tool rules. Recipe-level access gates do not apply to an unranked generic harvest; actual restricted material access still does. Up to one eligible assistant gives +1 and spends the same extraction time. Tools and access are required; no station is required for ordinary physical field removal beyond a stable accessible work surface and appropriate containers. A kit cannot contain an unsafe magical essence without a suitable actual vessel.

| Tiny | 10 minutes | 1 |

A global material's dropRate does not add another independent roll for every identical name in the material catalog. State which exceptional drops depend on a successful extraction before rolling.

Full extraction uses the listed time. For a partial harvest, select which manifest categories to attempt first. Time is full time × chosen categories / available categories, rounded up to whole minutes, minimum 1 minute. Allocation of the finite units across categories is fixed before selection, so selecting one category cannot redirect the entire pool into its most valuable part. Once partial harvesting begins, unselected ordinary categories are forfeited for this generic procedure; another worker cannot harvest the same remainder again. Failure ruins one selected unit (highest-value selected unit, tied at player's choice), recovers no selected units, and ends that attempt. No reroll or new generic attempt on the same exhausted selection; named exceptional-drop procedures remain as stated.

A hurried extraction takes half the listed/partial time, rounded up to whole minutes, minimum 1 minute, with disadvantage. This is a new reconciliation of the detailed size table with the older one-hour rushed Watch summary; it avoids making a hurried Tiny harvest slower than normal. Normal and hurried harvesting consume actual Watch time, not a second automatic eight-hour charge. Yield, limits and spoilage are unchanged. Resource Team membership grants no duplicate attempt or extra yield.

Harvested lots become actual cargo immediately, including containers and weight. Food, water or fuel gathered separately does not magically become the complete expedition Supply bundle. An ancestry's explicit forage or Natural Remedy grant follows its own finite procedure; this generic harvest does not refresh those uses. Humanoid and celestial remains keep the source's consent, lawful-authority or explicit-dark-path restriction; the procedure supplies no blanket permission and makes no cosmological conclusion.

## Preservation and spoilage

**Source:** blood/organs 1–4 hours, essence 30 minutes–2 hours unless contained, meat 8–24 hours, hides 12–24 hours before curing; dry bone/horn/teeth/mineral and ordinary construct parts can last indefinitely. Active cores are exceptions. **Completion:** the manifest fixes the interval before harvesting. Where no more specific interval is stated, use the conservative low end: organs/blood 1 hour, essence 30 minutes, meat 8 hours, hide 12 hours. The clock starts when the creature dies or the material leaves its sustaining environment, not when the player finally harvests it.

Track condition separately from material tier and rarity: Fresh → Degraded → Spoiled. At one unprotected interval the lot becomes Degraded: its unit value halves and it cannot meet a recipe calling for Fresh ingredients, but otherwise retains its exact tier/properties. At two unprotected intervals it is Spoiled, value zero and unusable as an ordinary recipe ingredient or edible food. A specific recipe may explicitly require spoiled material. No new poison, curse or disease is inferred. Dividing, trading, repackaging, recovering or reharvesting a lot cannot reset its elapsed age. The label quality here is preservation condition, never an equipment grade or crafting outcome.

Work on a perishable ingredient must begin while it is eligible; ordinary reservation does not suspend aging. An exact recipe card may say its first processing phase stabilizes the input; otherwise it must remain eligible through completion. A generic engine cannot promise a 160-hour potion made from an untreated one-hour organ will succeed. This makes the card's storage/processing details mandatory.

If a required reserved component ceases to satisfy its recipe requirement before completion and no completed recipe phase expressly stabilized it, pause the order before further active work. Before resuming, the lead may reserve a fresh legal component satisfying that same requirement, including an already-legal substitution. The replacement lot keeps its own identity and age. Completed active-work phases remain completed, but any component already physically consumed or irreversibly processed before becoming unusable remains spent. If no legal replacement is supplied, abandon the order under the normal abandonment rule; do not make a completion or quality roll.

The following new mundane preservation procedure supplies a usable default without inventing magical stasis. For each lot of at most 10 lb of eligible blood/organs, meat or hide, spend 30 active minutes, a suitable trade kit, a sealable appropriately sized container, and 1 gp/0.5 lb of preservative supplies (salt, clean packing and the appropriate nonmagical stabilizer). These supplies have no independent combat or magical effect. Alchemy handles specimens, Cooking handles food, and Skinning handles hide. Make one DC 10 check using the trade's normal ability/proficiency, no assistants or quality-yield table. Success pauses spoilage for 24 elapsed hours; failure uses the supplies and time but gives no extension. This treatment must finish before the lot becomes Spoiled. It does not restore Fresh condition or remove existing age.

On a successful treatment, freeze accumulated unprotected age and resume it after the 24 hours. A replacement treatment takes the same cost/time and extends protection from its completion to 24 hours later, never adding stacked days. Any actual container breach, contamination or incompatible heat ends protection; merely opening to portion with clean tools for less than a minute does not. Preserved supplies and container mass count as cargo until discarded or used. Processing into food is not automatically safe if the source explicitly says otherwise.

Mundane specimen jars alone contain fluids but do not pause decay. They do not stabilize magical essence or active cores. Those require a separately defined compatible containment method with its own lifetime, capacity and costs; without one, the printed/default clock continues. Dry durable materials remain indefinite only while kept dry and physically intact. No generic refrigeration, freezing or stasis benefit is inferred from a spell name; its actual reviewed effect must support the storage method.

No general recovery of catalysts, expended ammunition/consumables, enchanting power, RP, souls, formulas or previously missing parts. No sale-value-to-free-material conversion.
