## What It Is

Spiral Breach Brood-Sage is a product or cultivator of Spiral instability. Its anatomy should not be read as a stable species description; parts may change function when the breach, room geometry, or observer changes.

## What Experienced Expeditions Notice

Repeated angles that do not close; echoes arriving before the sound; footprints on ceilings without disturbed dust; crystal growth turning toward empty air; headaches synchronized among several people. Different witnesses remember the same sound differently.

## How It Fights

It opens with **Warped Tentacle** against the target most capable of controlling its movement. It uses **Psychic Pulse** to punish anyone who tries to stabilize that target. It changes angle, route, or target whenever the Expedition repeats a successful pattern.

## How to Defeat It

**Prepare Before Contact**

- Prepare resistance or mitigation for bludgeoning, psychic damage.
- Mark routes with physical cord and damp vibration.

**Combat Priority**

Stabilize or destroy the breach anchor before the room rewrites the fight.

**Exploit**

The defining weakness is **stabilized geometry and vibration damping**. Treat this as an encounter solution rather than an automatic damage vulnerability. Successful Research, Recon, or Resource actions should make the exploit easier or safer.

**Withdraw When**

Withdraw or change the objective when its full multiattack reaches the support line; resources fall below half before the arena mechanic is solved. A failed retreat against this creature usually costs more than abandoning the immediate treasure.

## Habitat, Goals, and Lair

Breach lairs are shaped by impossible shortcuts, thin walls, sudden gravity changes, and resonant surfaces. A straight retreat may return the party to the fight.

Spiral creatures arise where geometry, memory, and matter cease agreeing. Some are born animals altered by repeated exposure; others are organized broods that cultivate breaches and use them as roads, nurseries, or weapons.

Most breach fauna cannot bargain. Brood-Sages and certain Fracture-Kin can exchange information, but their concept of identity may not match the speaker’s.
