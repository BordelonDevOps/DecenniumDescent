## What It Is

This Grief-Bound is need that consumes provisions, warmth, and eventually identity. It hunts emotional repetition rather than flesh alone, and it grows more coherent whenever the Expedition reenacts the behavior that created it.

## What Experienced Expeditions Notice

A memory repeated by people who were not present; food spoiling only after it is counted; camp chairs facing an empty place; wounds aching in response to an unrelated argument.

## How It Fights

It opens with **Hunger Maw** against the target most capable of controlling its movement. It uses **Tail Sweep** to punish anyone who tries to stabilize that target. It attacks the character whose behavior most closely matches its defining loss.

## How to Defeat It

**Prepare Before Contact**

- Prepare resistance or mitigation for necrotic, bludgeoning damage.
- Agree on verbal and physical contact signals before entry.

**Combat Priority**

Identify the repeated emotion and stop feeding it before striking the exposed core.

**Exploit**

The defining weakness is **breaking the emotional loop**. Treat this as an encounter solution rather than an automatic damage vulnerability. Successful Research, Recon, or Resource actions should make the exploit easier or safer.

**Withdraw When**

Withdraw or change the objective when its full multiattack reaches the support line. A failed retreat against this creature usually costs more than abandoning the immediate treasure.

## Habitat, Goals, and Lair

Their territory accumulates symbolic objects. Destroying every object can strengthen the entity by confirming its loss; identifying the central memory is safer.

Grief-Bound are emotional deaths that found enough matter or Resonance to keep moving. They are not ordinary ghosts. Each is a self-reinforcing need that hunts situations capable of repeating the moment that made it.

Some can be quieted, redirected, or released if the original loss is understood. False promises often produce a stronger return.
