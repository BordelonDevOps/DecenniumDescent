## What It Is

The Bloom That Hungers is the Gate Guardian for choice under deprivation. It is an arena-scale adversary whose body, Seal, and terrain operate as one examination. Reducing it to zero hit points is necessary, but an Expedition that ignores its Gate principle can still lose the passage, the reward, or members of the team.

## What Experienced Expeditions Notice

Architecture becomes ceremonial; lesser monsters stop crossing a boundary; previous Expeditions leave preparation caches rather than camps; repeated symbols appear in terrain and wounds. Nearby insects die before larger animals show symptoms.

### Traits

**Gate-Bound.** The Guardian cannot be permanently displaced from its arena while the Seal remains active.

**Tremor Approach.** It can enter or leave the ground without provoking ordinary reactions unless its emergence point was prepared.

**Escalation Pulse.** **Spore Burst** recharges on 5-6; visible signs appear one round before the first use.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The creature makes 2 attacks using the actions below.

**Vine Maw.** *Attack:* +8 to hit, one target. *Hit:* 2d10+9 piercing damage (average 20).

**Root Constrict.** *Attack:* +8 to hit, one target. *Hit:* 2d8+6 bludgeoning damage (average 15).

**Spore Burst (Recharge 5-6).** Creatures in the affected area make a DC 16 Constitution save, taking 4d8 poison damage (average 18) on a failure and half on a success. The Director should describe the charge-up before resolving it.

## How It Fights

It opens with **Vine Maw** against the target most capable of controlling its movement. It uses **Root Constrict** to punish anyone who tries to stabilize that target. When **Spore Burst** recharges, it delays only if it can catch a more important cluster or interrupt a decisive ritual. It escalates by phase and uses the Seal Pulse to punish parties that solve damage but ignore the test.

## How to Defeat It

**Prepare Before Contact**

- Prepare resistance or mitigation for piercing, bludgeoning damage.
- Mark tremors and protect the rear line from resurfacing attacks.
- Spread before **Spore Burst** becomes available.

**Combat Priority**

Complete the arena objective while maintaining damage pressure.

**Exploit**

The defining weakness is **None identified**. Treat this as an encounter solution rather than an automatic damage vulnerability. Successful Research, Recon, or Resource actions should make the exploit easier or safer.

**Withdraw When**

Withdraw or change the objective when Spore Burst catches three or more Expedition members. A failed retreat against this creature usually costs more than abandoning the immediate treasure.

## Habitat, Goals, and Lair

A Gate arena is part of the creature. Terrain, Seal Pulses, objectives, and failure conditions matter as much as hit points.

Gate Guardians are not random apex monsters. Each is a living examination tied to a Division of the dungeon, a Gate Seal, and a principle the Expedition must demonstrate under pressure.

Some Guardians speak, bargain, or test. None are safely reduced to ordinary patrons or quest-givers.
