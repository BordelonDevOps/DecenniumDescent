## What It Is

The Summer expression embodies heat, abundance, and exhaustion. It is drawn to places where the natural cycle has been accelerated, delayed, commercialized, or magically denied.

## What Experienced Expeditions Notice

Plants acting one season ahead; insects emerging at the wrong temperature; frost preserving fresh blood; fruit ripening only when watched. Different witnesses remember the same sound differently.

## How It Fights

It opens with **Fey Whisper** against the target most capable of controlling its movement. It uses **Mislead Strike** to punish anyone who tries to stabilize that target. It cycles terrain toward its season and avoids neutral ground.

## How to Defeat It

**Prepare Before Contact**

- Prepare resistance or mitigation for psychic damage.
- Identify the seasonal anchor before damaging the body.

**Combat Priority**

Move the fight to a neutral boundary and break the seasonal anchor.

**Exploit**

The defining weakness is **destruction of the seasonal anchor**. Treat this as an encounter solution rather than an automatic damage vulnerability. Successful Research, Recon, or Resource actions should make the exploit easier or safer.

**Withdraw When**

Withdraw or change the objective when its full multiattack reaches the support line. A failed retreat against this creature usually costs more than abandoning the immediate treasure.

## Habitat, Goals, and Lair

A Season-Bound site contains overlapping climates and zones of accelerated growth, heat, decay, or preservation. Crossing the wrong boundary can empower the creature.

Season-Bound are fey embodiments of transition rather than weather alone. Each appears when a place is forced to change too quickly or prevented from changing at all.

They can be reasoned with through cycles, exchanges, and timely sacrifice. Demanding permanence is offensive to most of them.
