## What It Is

Order-Drone Pair is a distributed containment intelligence. Its bodies are dangerous individually, but the real weapon is the geometry they maintain between one another and the terrain.

## What Experienced Expeditions Notice

Evenly spaced scoring on stone; dust cleared in repeated arcs; metallic clicks answering one another around corners; corpses moved out of pathways rather than looted. Loose metal turns to face the same direction.

## How It Fights

It opens with **Iron Slam** against the target most capable of controlling its movement. It uses **Grinding Gear** to punish anyone who tries to stabilize that target. It uses every action to preserve formation and converts broken lines into immediate repositioning.

## How to Defeat It

**Prepare Before Contact**

- Prepare resistance or mitigation for bludgeoning damage.
- Bring forced movement and terrain creation.

**Combat Priority**

Split the formation and destroy the coordinating body.

**Exploit**

The defining weakness is **forced movement that breaks formation**. Treat this as an encounter solution rather than an automatic damage vulnerability. Successful Research, Recon, or Resource actions should make the exploit easier or safer.

**Withdraw When**

Withdraw or change the objective when its full multiattack reaches the support line; resources fall below half before the arena mechanic is solved. A failed retreat against this creature usually costs more than abandoning the immediate treasure.

## Habitat, Goals, and Lair

Drone zones favor grids, choke points, rotating doors, magnetic floors, and lines that improve formation attacks. Their positions are part of the stat block.

Order-Drones are collective constructs designed to solve movement, trespass, and containment problems through formation. A “pair,” “tetrad,” or “quintet” is one tactical intelligence distributed across several bodies.

Correct credentials, maintenance codes, or a higher-priority emergency may redirect them. Emotional appeals do nothing unless the automaton was built to interpret them.
