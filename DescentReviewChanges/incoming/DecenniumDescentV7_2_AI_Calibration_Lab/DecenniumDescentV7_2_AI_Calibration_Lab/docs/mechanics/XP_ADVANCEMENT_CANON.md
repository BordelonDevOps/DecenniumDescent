# XP and Advancement — Public Canon

**Status:** Canonical public procedure  
**Version:** 1.1  
**Effective:** 2026-08-06

## Default method: milestones

Milestone advancement is the default for Opening campaigns. Characters gain a level when the Expedition completes a meaningful campaign milestone, such as:

- Securing a major route or permanent camp.
- Completing a defining contract, rescue, survey, or recovery.
- Resolving a Gate-scale objective supported by public knowledge.
- Changing a Haven institution or major faction relationship.
- Surviving a defining extraction whose consequences reshape the company.

Every materially participating Expedition member advances together unless a character was absent for the entire milestone. Combat body count never determines eligibility by itself.

## Optional method: detailed XP

A table may use detailed XP instead of milestones. Do not combine detailed XP awards with milestone levels during the same advancement period. Use the percentage-of-next-level-gap awards in `GAMEPLAY_LOOP_CANON.md`.
