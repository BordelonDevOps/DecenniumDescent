# Decennium Descent Gameplay Loop — Canon Rules

**Status:** Canonical gameplay procedure  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Effective:** 2026-08-06  
**Authority:** `CANON_MANIFEST.json`

## The complete loop

A Decennium Descent campaign repeats the following playable sequence:

1. **Choose an objective.** Select a destination, contract, rescue, hunt, survey, recovery, or Gate attempt.
2. **Finance the descent.** Self-fund or accept sponsors, loans, insurance, and obligations.
3. **Build the Expedition.** Organize up to twelve characters across Combat, Reconnaissance, Research, Resource, and Support teams.
4. **Load the convoy.** Purchase supplies, tools, transport, preservation equipment, and reserve capacity for recovered cargo.
5. **Enter the dungeon.** Begin the Opening clock and select a route.
6. **Resolve Watches.** Assign every character an activity for each eight-hour Watch, make team checks, advance the route, consume supplies, and test Pressure.
7. **Resolve encounters.** Fight, negotiate, evade, research, harvest, rescue, or retreat.
8. **Manage attrition.** Treat Wounds, repair equipment, establish camps, replace supplies, and decide whether the objective still justifies the risk.
9. **Choose cargo.** Appraise, preserve, secure, and prioritize what the Expedition can carry.
10. **Extract.** Reverse or change the route while cargo, casualties, pursuit, and the Horn increase risk.
11. **Resolve the Haven.** Submit to inspection, settle claims, appraise and sell cargo, pay shares and debts, recover, craft, research, and change faction standing.
12. **Advance and prepare.** Award XP, improve the Expedition, record discoveries, and select the next objective.
13. **Close the Opening.** Resolve the season's economic and political consequences and create a Cycle Record for the next decennial Opening.

## 1. Modes of play

### Encounter Mode

Use rounds and turns when seconds matter. A round is approximately six seconds.

Each creature normally receives on its turn:

- Movement up to Speed.
- One Action.
- One Bonus Action, but only when a feature permits it.
- One free object interaction.
- One Reaction before the start of its next turn.

No character can gain more than one additional full Action in the same round. Effects granting additional Actions do not stack; the character chooses one.

### Expedition Mode

Use Expedition Watches when hours matter. One Watch is eight hours. Every twenty-four-hour day contains three Watches: Morning, Afternoon, and Evening.

Every member must be assigned to one activity during every Watch. A character may Rest instead of joining a team.

### Haven and Downtime Mode

Use days or weeks for recovery, crafting, research, business, training, politics, and preparation. One Haven Turn is normally one day unless the activity states otherwise.

## 2. Core resolution

Roll:

`d20 + ability modifier + proficiency, when trained + circumstance modifiers`

### Difficulty Classes

| Circumstance | DC |
|---|---:|
| Routine under pressure | 10 |
| Tier I standard | 12 |
| Tier II standard | 13 |
| Tier III standard | 15 |
| Tier IV standard | 16 |
| Tier V standard | 18 |
| Tier VI documented limit | 19 |

Use +3 DC for a hard version of the task and +6 DC for a severe version. Use -3 DC when preparation or superior tools make the task favorable.

### Degrees of outcome

- **Critical Success:** Total is at least DC + 10.
- **Success:** Total meets or exceeds the DC.
- **Failure:** Total is below the DC.
- **Critical Failure:** Total is at least 10 below the DC.

A natural 20 improves the result by one degree. A natural 1 worsens the result by one degree. It never improves beyond Critical Success or worsens beyond Critical Failure.

### Advantage and disadvantage

Roll two d20s and use the higher result for advantage or the lower result for disadvantage. Multiple sources do not stack. If both apply, they cancel.

### Assistance

A trained assistant who can meaningfully participate grants +1. Up to three assistants may contribute, for a maximum +3. An assistant exposed to the same danger suffers consequences on a Critical Failure.

## 3. Expedition organization

An Expedition contains no more than twelve characters. Larger forces are multiple Expeditions coordinating.

| Team | Normal size | Primary purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Combat | 4–6 | Fight, breach, escort, and hold routes |
| Reconnaissance | 1–3 | Scout, map, trail, conceal, and warn |
| Research | 1–3 | Identify, translate, analyze, and reconstruct |
| Resource | 2–4 | Harvest, forage, mine, preserve, and craft |
| Support | 1–3 | Camp, heal, repair, transport, and administer |

Every Expedition designates:

- **Expedition Leader:** Breaks tied decisions and represents the company.
- **Quartermaster:** Tracks Supply, Load, cargo priority, wages, and losses.
- **Routekeeper:** Maintains the map and route state.
- **Surgeon:** Directs Wound treatment and casualty transport.
- **Claimant of Record:** Maintains provenance, contracts, and ownership documents.

One character may hold more than one office, but no office changes class abilities.

## 4. Expedition objectives and abort conditions

Before entry, record:

- Primary objective.
- Up to two secondary objectives.
- Desired extraction value.
- Maximum acceptable casualties.
- Minimum reserve Supply.
- Latest voluntary return hour.
- Conditions that cause immediate withdrawal.

When an abort condition occurs, each officer may argue to continue, but the Expedition Leader must record the decision. Continuing after an agreed abort condition increases Pressure by 1 until the Expedition begins extracting.

A declared withdrawal is a **successful withdrawal** when the Expedition reaches the Haven or surface without violating the charter's recorded casualty, reserve Supply, latest-return-hour, or other withdrawal limits. Record successful withdrawal separately from primary-objective success and profitability. An Expedition can survive correctly while failing its contract, or complete its contract while losing money.

## 5. Opening clock and Watches

The Opening lasts 2,400 hours. At hour 1,200, the Horn announces the point of no return. Expeditions still inside must withdraw or accept the risk of being sealed below for ten years.

A Watch advances the Opening clock by eight hours. A normal day contains:

- Two active Watches.
- One Rest Watch for each character.

A character assigned to work during their scheduled Rest Watch gains **Rest Debt**. An attempted Rest Watch interrupted before completion also creates Rest Debt after the emergency scene ends.

Record a two-Watch countdown. The character must complete one full Rest Watch before the end of the second following Watch. At the end of that second Watch, an unpaid debt causes 1 Strain and remains owed until a full Rest Watch is completed. A character can owe only one Rest Debt at a time; missing another scheduled Rest while already indebted immediately causes 1 Strain and resets the countdown to two Watches. Completing the owed Rest clears the debt. A character cannot remove Strain until completing a safe Rest Watch.

### Short Rest

A Short Rest takes one hour. A character can benefit from no more than two Short Rests between Long Rests.

- Spend Hit Dice as permitted by class rules.
- Recover one-quarter of maximum RP, rounded down, minimum 1 for a character with an RP pool.
- An Oracle regains 1 Revelation Charge, up to maximum.
- End effects that specify a Short Rest.

### Long Rest

A Long Rest occupies one full Watch and can benefit a character only once per twenty-four hours.

- Restore HP according to the Player Rulebook.
- Restore RP and Revelation Charges.
- Recover half expended Hit Dice, rounded up.
- Remove 1 Strain in a secure camp or Haven.
- Wounds require treatment; a Long Rest alone does not remove them.

## 6. Route procedure

A route is written with five ratings:

- **Length:** Number of Progress required.
- **Risk:** Starting Pressure from 1–5.
- **Mapping:** Unknown, Sketched, Mapped, or Secured.
- **Capacity:** Maximum convoy Load that can pass without penalty.
- **Sustenance:** Natural Supply available from successful Forage, rated 0–4. A route at Sustenance 0 cannot support routine Forage.

### Progress per active Watch

- Secured or Mapped route: 2 Progress.
- Sketched route: 1 Progress.
- Unknown or severely obstructed route: 0 Progress until Reconnaissance succeeds, then 1 Progress.

A successful Reconnaissance activity may add +1 Progress or reduce Pressure by 1. A Critical Success can do both.

**Forced March on an Unknown Route:** When the Expedition cannot or will not wait for Map Route, the Routekeeper may attempt a Severe route check. Success gains 1 Progress, increases Pressure by 2, and consumes one additional Supply per active character. Failure gains no Progress and causes a Standard complication. Critical Failure gains no Progress and causes a Severe complication. Forced March prevents a procedural deadlock, but Reconnaissance remains safer and more efficient.

Load above route Capacity reduces Progress by 1, minimum 0, and increases Pressure by 1.

## 7. Pressure and complications

Pressure ranges from 0–6.

| Pressure | State |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Secured; no routine complication roll |
| 1 | Watched |
| 2 | Unstable |
| 3 | Hostile |
| 4 | Hunted |
| 5 | Overrun |
| 6 | Immediate crisis |

At the end of each active Watch, roll 1d6. If the roll is equal to or lower than Pressure, resolve a complication.

Increase Pressure by 1 for each applicable condition:

- Open combat or an explosion occurred during the Watch.
- The convoy exceeds route Capacity.
- An Attention-tagged cargo lot is first carried exposed into an active Watch, is revealed by a mishap, or loses its concealment. Continuous controlled carriage does not add Pressure again every Watch.
- A member is missing, captured, or actively pursued.
- The Expedition continued after an abort condition.
- The camp is exposed or its location is known to enemies.

Reduce Pressure by 1 for each applicable condition, maximum 2 reductions per Watch:

- Reconnaissance succeeds at Counter-Ambush or Conceal Trail.
- The Expedition changes to a secured route.
- The party spends a Watch lying low in a concealed camp.
- A negotiated settlement ends pursuit.

Pressure cannot be reduced below the route's base Risk until the route is Secured.

### Complication severity

- Roll exceeds Pressure by exactly 0 because of a modifier or reroll: Minor.
- Natural roll 2–6 that triggers: Standard.
- Natural 1: Severe.
- Pressure 6: Severe complication automatically; do not roll.

## 8. Team activities

Each team chooses one activity per Watch. The listed ability or skill is a default, not the only possible approach.

### Combat Team

- **Advance:** Secure Progress through an occupied route. Athletics, Survival, or attack-based challenge.
- **Breach:** Remove a defended obstacle. Athletics, tools, spellcasting, or siege procedure.
- **Escort:** Protect Resource or Support personnel. On success, assistants avoid the first consequence suffered that Watch.
- **Patrol:** Prevent incursions near camp. On success, reduce the severity of the next camp complication by one degree.

### Reconnaissance Team

- **Scout Ahead:** Reveal the next site, hazard, or encounter before commitment.
- **Map Route:** Improve Mapping one step after accumulated successes equal to route Risk.
- **Counter-Ambush:** Reduce Pressure by 1 on success.
- **Conceal Trail:** Cancel one pursuit-based Pressure increase.

### Research Team

- **Identify:** Determine an item's function, risk, provenance, and Extraction Class.
- **Translate:** Accumulate progress on a text, code, or inscription.
- **Analyze Threat:** Learn one reliable resistance, vulnerability, behavior, or retreat trigger.
- **Experiment:** Attempt a new formula, ritual, or interaction with explicit mishap stakes.

### Resource Team

- **Harvest:** Recover creature or environmental materials.
- **Forage:** Produce Supply up to the route's Sustenance rating. Success yields Supply equal to Sustenance; Critical Success yields Sustenance plus half that amount, rounded up; Failure yields none; Critical Failure contaminates one Supply or increases Pressure by 1. Forage cannot produce more Supply than the route can sustainably provide during that Watch.
- **Mine or Salvage:** Recover bulk materials and mechanical components.
- **Preserve:** Prevent spoilage and reduce Fragile or Living cargo penalties.

### Support Team

- **Establish Camp:** Create or improve a camp.
- **Treat:** Stabilize casualties, treat Wounds, and control disease or withdrawal.
- **Repair:** Restore equipment Integrity and transport capacity.
- **Transport:** Move, secure, redistribute, or hide cargo and casualties.

### Team check procedure

1. Select a lead character.
2. Choose the approach and relevant skill.
3. Add up to three trained assistants.
4. Resolve against the route or tier DC.
5. Apply the activity's outcome and any complication.

A Critical Success normally grants the Success benefit plus one of: +1 Progress, -1 Pressure, one extra unit of output, or improved information. A Critical Failure normally costs time, Supply, Integrity, position, or secrecy in addition to failing.

## 9. Supply and attrition

### Supply Units

One Supply Unit sustains one Medium character for one day and includes food, water, fuel, and ordinary camp consumables. Small characters still consume one unit; Large characters consume two.

At the end of each twenty-four hours, consume Supply equal to the Expedition's daily requirement.

If the Expedition cannot pay the full requirement, every unsupported character gains 1 Strain and cannot remove Strain that day.

### Strain

Strain represents exhaustion, sleep debt, hunger, exposure, and sustained magical stress.

| Strain | Effect |
|---:|---|
| 0 | No penalty |
| 1 | -1 initiative and expedition checks |
| 2 | Speed -10 ft.; -1 attacks and saves |
| 3 | Disadvantage on expedition checks; cannot Dash twice consecutively |
| 4 | Maximum HP reduced by 25% |
| 5 | Incapacitated except for slow travel and Rest |
| 6 | Death or permanent collapse, as appropriate to the cause |

A secure Long Rest removes 1 Strain. Haven care removes all ordinary Strain after one full day. Specific conditions may require treatment.

### Equipment Integrity

Important equipment, vehicles, and transport have Integrity 0–3.

- 3: Sound.
- 2: Worn; no penalty.
- 1: Damaged; checks using it suffer -2.
- 0: Broken; unusable until repaired.

A Repair activity restores 1 Integrity on Success and 2 on Critical Success if materials are available.

## 10. Load and cargo

### Load Units

One Load is approximately ten pounds of ordinary cargo or one awkward bundle. Armor, weapons, packs, and carried treasure count unless an item states otherwise.

A character's Carry Limit is equal to Strength score, modified by size:

- Small: -2 Load.
- Medium: no adjustment.
- Large: double Carry Limit.

At more than half Carry Limit, the character is **Burdened**: Speed -10 ft. and disadvantage on checks to climb, swim, or move silently.

A character cannot voluntarily travel while above Carry Limit.

### Extraction Class

Extraction Class measures handling danger, attention, and logistical complexity, not only weight.

| Class | Example |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Ordinary trade goods |
| 1 | Common monster parts or durable salvage |
| 2 | Uncommon, fragile, or regulated cargo |
| 3 | Rare artifact, volatile substance, or living captive |
| 4 | Very rare strategic cargo or dangerous curse |
| 5 | Epic cargo likely to attract sovereign or faction intervention |
| 6 | Legendary documented-limit cargo requiring a dedicated operation |

Add one handling complication tag when applicable: Fragile, Volatile, Cursed, Living, Contraband, Perishable, or Attention.

### Cargo priority

Before extraction, assign each cargo lot a priority from 1–5. When forced to abandon cargo, the lowest priority is abandoned first. Ties are decided by the Quartermaster or Expedition Leader.

### Casualties and captives

A Medium unconscious or restrained creature occupies 6 Load. A Small creature occupies 3 Load. A Large creature occupies 12 Load. A suitable stretcher or cart may reduce the personnel required but not the Load.

## 11. Camps

| Camp | Establishment | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Exposed bivouac | No activity | Rest possible; Pressure +1; no Wound recovery |
| Temporary Camp | 1 Support Success | Safe storage equal to 20 Load; no exposure Pressure increase |
| Fortified Outpost | 3 accumulated Support Successes and materials | Storage 60 Load; attackers lose surprise; repairs gain +1 |
| Research Station | Fortified Outpost plus 2 Research Successes | Research and identification gain +2; preserves records and samples |
| Forward Base | 8 total accumulated successes, supply line, and faction approval | Storage 150 Load; counts as a Haven link for resupply and casualty evacuation |

A camp's location can be compromised. A Severe complication may reduce its rating by one step or force evacuation.

## 12. Encounter loop

At the start of an encounter:

1. Establish objective and stakes.
2. Establish terrain, exits, cargo, civilians, and hazards.
3. Determine surprise and starting distance.
4. Roll initiative.
5. Run turns until the objective is resolved, one side withdraws, or the scene changes mode.

### Combat turn

1. Resolve start-of-turn effects.
2. Move and take Action/Bonus Action in any order permitted.
3. Resolve end-of-turn saves and durations.
4. Pass initiative.

### Universal actions

In addition to class actions, characters can Attack, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Ready, Search, Use an Object, Guard, Withdraw, Secure Cargo, Disarm, Feint, Interpose, Overwatch, Pin, Press, Sunder, Trip, Silence the Caster, or Break Formation as defined in the Core Systems Bible.

### Action-economy limits

- **Shared Additional Action Slot:** A creature can receive no more than one additional full Action in a round, regardless of source. Effects granting a full Action do not stack; the creature chooses which one fills the slot. A specifically granted extra attack, movement, Bonus Action, or Reaction does not fill this slot unless the feature explicitly calls it an Action. An Action granted by a feature cannot retrigger that same feature.
- A creature can take only one Reaction before its next turn unless a feature explicitly replaces, rather than adds to, that limit.
- A character can maintain only one Stance unless a feature explicitly permits two.
- **Independent actor cap:** A controller can command no more than two independent combat-capable actors with their own turns. Summons, autonomous deployables, independent reflections, companions, drones, and similar entities all count toward the same cap. Passive devices and minions that do not take full turns do not count. Additional minions act as one swarm, cohort, or unit stat block.
- An actor without a command defends itself and takes no complex action.
- **Dual Cast:** When a feature permits Dual Cast, it is one Action that resolves two Tier 1–2 spells with a casting time of one Action. Pay both RP costs. Only one of the two spells may require concentration. Dual Cast does not grant a second Action.
- **Traumatic Casting:** A spell forced through the Weave by Traumatic Casting cannot refund, reset, or retrigger Traumatic Casting. The direct magical trauma caused by that use cannot be prevented or reduced by Traumatic Casting or by an effect created by the spell being forced through.
- Cooldown resets cannot reset the same ability more than once per round.
- A creature cannot lose more than one full turn to the same control source without a new save or escalation cost.

### Retreat

A side may declare Retreat at the start of a round. Retreat succeeds when the withdrawing side accumulates three Escape successes before suffering three Pursuit successes.

Each round, one lead character makes an appropriate check. Allies can assist, create obstacles, carry casualties, or sacrifice cargo. The default round presents the Pursuit check and one meaningful threat, attack, hazard, or sacrifice—not the pursuer's complete nova or full multiattack routine. A failed check, Severe complication, or fictionally trapped position may justify greater danger.

The withdrawing side can gain at most one Escape success in a round from either the lead check or winning the round, unless a feature explicitly grants another. A Critical Success on the lead check may still grant two Escape successes. Losing a member, route, or vehicle can grant one Pursuit success.

On a successful Retreat, return to Expedition Mode with Pressure +1. On failure, the opposing side chooses the next encounter position or captures abandoned characters or cargo consistent with the fiction.

## 13. Downed, Wounds, and death

At 0 HP, a character is Downed, Prone, and begins death saves at the end of each turn.

- 10 or higher: one success.
- 9 or lower: one failure.
- Natural 20: regain 1 HP.
- Natural 1: two failures.
- Three successes: Stable.
- Three failures: Dead.

Damage while Downed causes one failure, or two if the damage is a critical hit. Healing ends Downed.

### Wounds

Gain one Wound when any of the following occurs:

- Dropped to 0 HP by a critical hit.
- Suffered two death-save failures before stabilization.
- Revived after death or magical soul separation.
- Suffered a Severe hazard or injury result that specifies a Wound.

Each Wound imposes -1 Constitution saves and reduces Hit Dice healing by 1 per die, minimum 1. At three Wounds, the character is Critical: Speed is halved and Strain cannot be removed until at least one Wound is treated.

Treating a Wound requires a medical kit, one Supply, eight hours, and Medicine against the tier DC. Critical Success removes two Wounds; Success removes one; Failure consumes the Supply; Critical Failure also inflicts 1 Strain.

## 14. Encounter construction

Assign every enemy a creature level. Challenge Rating may be used as creature level until a dedicated level is written.

### Relative Threat Points

| Creature relative to party level | Threat Points |
|---:|---:|
| -4 or lower | 5 |
| -3 | 10 |
| -2 | 15 |
| -1 | 25 |
| Equal | 40 |
| +1 | 60 |
| +2 | 90 |
| +3 | 135 |
| +4 or higher | 200 |

### Four-character encounter budgets

| Difficulty | Budget | Expected effect |
|---|---:|---|
| Routine | 50 | Low resource cost; failure unlikely |
| Standard | 90 | Meaningful cost; normal expedition opposition |
| Hard | 130 | Serious resource use; casualties possible |
| Severe | 180 | Defeat possible without preparation |
| Catastrophic | 240 | Intended only as a Gate, climax, or avoidable disaster |

Use the following explicit multipliers; do not compound the 20% adjustment exponentially:

| Party size | Budget multiplier |
|---:|---:|
| 3 | ×0.8 |
| 4 | ×1.0 |
| 5 | ×1.2 |
| 6 | ×1.4 |

### Difficulty accounting

The encounter's **final listed difficulty** includes creature Threat Points and all objective, hostile-terrain, surprise, and active-hazard adjustments. Apply each adjustment once. A worksheet that already labels an encounter Hard because it protects cargo does not step it to Severe merely because the cargo objective is written again.

- Ordinary surprise is one step and normally changes position, advantage, or reactions.
- A full unopposed enemy round is two steps and should require failed reconnaissance, a Severe complication, or equivalent fiction.
- Defeat-or-drive-off encounters use creature budget as the main measure.
- Cargo and hazard encounters add one step unless the final label already includes it.
- Escort encounters require an explicit progress, survival, or movement clock; kill win rate alone does not measure them.
- Retreat uses the three-Escape-before-three-Pursuit clock; kill-budget win rate does not determine its difficulty.

### Calibration targets

| Final label | Intended party success rate | Intended use |
|---|---:|---|
| Routine | 95–100% | Low resource cost; failure exceptional |
| Standard | 70–90% | Meaningful cost; normal opposition |
| Hard | 40–65% | Serious cost; casualties plausible |
| Severe | 15–35% | Defeat likely without preparation or advantage |
| Catastrophic | 0–10% | Gate, climax, or avoidable disaster |

The numeric budgets above remain a provisional chassis. V7.1 simulations showed Level 1 Standard encounters too lethal and Levels 5–16 Standard encounters too soft. Until a holdout calibration replaces the table, Level 1–2 Standard encounters should avoid pairs of above-level creatures, Levels 5–16 should use meaningful objectives and phases rather than arbitrary HP inflation, and Levels 17–20 require party-composition testing. Do not apply global class buffs or nerfs from proxy simulations.

### Boss structure

A boss encounter should include:

- A visible objective beyond reducing HP.
- At least one telegraphed ability.
- At least two phases or a meaningful escalation.
- Terrain or objects players can exploit.
- A retreat or failure state.
- Tenacity against repeated control.

**Tenacity:** The first time each round a boss would become Stunned, Paralyzed, or lose its full turn, it instead becomes Staggered until the end of its next turn. A Gate Guardian may have two Tenacity uses per round.

## 15. Harvesting and salvage

After an encounter, the Resource Team may Harvest or Salvage during the current or next Watch.

- A hurried attempt takes one hour and suffers disadvantage.
- A normal attempt consumes one activity assignment.
- Failure damages or contaminates one recoverable component.
- Critical Failure may expose the team to poison, disease, curse, or Pressure.

Harvested material becomes cargo immediately and must be preserved if Perishable.

## 16. Extraction

Extraction begins when the Expedition formally turns toward the Haven or surface.

Before movement:

1. Inventory all cargo, casualties, captives, vehicles, and Supply.
2. Assign cargo priority.
3. Select a route and abort alternatives.
4. Determine convoy Load and route Capacity.
5. Increase Pressure by the highest Extraction Class carried, divided by two and rounded down, maximum +3.

### Extraction Test

Make Extraction Tests according to Mapping and always after a Severe complication:

| Mapping | Test cadence |
|---|---|
| Secured | At the route transition only |
| Mapped | At the midpoint and exit when Length is 6 or more; otherwise at exit |
| Sketched | Every 3 Progress and at exit |
| Unknown | Every 2 Progress and at exit |

A route transition is entry into a different named route, floor band, controlled territory, transport method, or chokepoint—not every ordinary room or Watch. Do not repeat a test at the same Progress unless a new Severe complication changes the convoy state.

Resolve each Extraction Test as follows:

`d20 + Quartermaster's Survival, vehicle, or relevant tool modifier + assistants`

DC equals the tier DC plus:

- The highest Extraction Class carried: +0 through +6.
- +1 once if any Attention cargo remains exposed.
- +1 if the convoy is Burdened.
- +1 for each Volatile, Cursed, or Living cargo lot not properly secured, maximum +3.
- +2 if actively pursued.
- -2 on a Secured route.

The total cargo-and-handling increase from Extraction Class, Attention, Burden, and unsecured dangerous lots is capped at +7 before pursuit and route modifiers.

Outcomes:

- **Critical Success:** No loss; reduce Pressure by 1 or gain +1 Progress.
- **Success:** Cargo and convoy pass safely.
- **Failure:** Lose eight hours and resolve a Standard complication, or place one low-priority cargo lot at risk.
- **Critical Failure:** Resolve a Severe complication and place two low-priority cargo lots, one vehicle, or one casualty at risk.

The Director must state what is at risk before the players choose a response. On a Failure, the players choose between the stated time-and-complication cost and the stated low-priority cargo risk.

## 17. Haven resolution

Arrival at the Haven follows this order:

**Tier I fast settlement:** When there is no disputed ownership, strategic cargo, active lien, quarantine crisis, contraband case, or faction hearing, resolve only Inspection → Appraisal → Sale → Sponsor Settlement → Recovery. Escalate to the full procedure whenever a skipped step becomes materially relevant.

1. **Quarantine and inspection.** Identify disease, curse, contraband, and dangerous captives.
2. **Provenance filing.** Record who found, financed, carried, and legally claims each major item.
3. **Appraisal.** Determine function, authenticity, rarity, and base value.
4. **Claims and liens.** Resolve sponsors, creditors, guild rights, sovereign seizure, and competing claimants.
5. **Disposition.** Sell, auction, craft, research, store, ransom, liberate, return, or smuggle cargo.
6. **Settlement.** Pay wages, shares, taxes, debts, insurance, and replacement costs.
7. **Recovery.** Treat Wounds, remove Strain, repair gear, and replace personnel.
8. **Standing.** Adjust faction reputation and Expedition renown.
9. **Advancement.** Award XP and unlock training, property, specialists, and routes.
10. **Next descent.** Set the next objective and reserve.

### Appraisal

Use Intelligence with Arcana, History, Nature, Religion, or the relevant craft proficiency against tier DC.

- Critical Success: exact function, risks, provenance, and best current buyer.
- Success: reliable function, rarity, and base value.
- Failure: broad category and uncertain value.
- Critical Failure: false assumption; Director records the error and reveals it when materially relevant.

### Sale value

`Final Value = Base Value × Provenance × Demand × Condition × Urgency`

Use these multipliers:

- Provenance: undocumented 0.5; disputed 0.75; documented 1.0; Gate-Sealed 1.25.
- Demand: glutted 0.75; normal 1.0; sought 1.25; strategic 1.5.
- Condition: damaged 0.5; worn 0.75; sound 1.0; exceptional 1.25.
- Urgency: forced sale 0.75; normal 1.0; patient auction 1.1.

Round to the nearest gold piece. Taxes, shares, and liens are paid after value is determined.

### Haven tax schedule

| Cargo or transaction | Tax |
|---|---:|
| Registered routine goods | 3% |
| Licensed crafting materials or crafted goods | 5% |
| Artifacts and auctioned relics | 6% |
| Strategic or regulated cargo | 8% |
| Authorized contraband amnesty, surrender, or disposal | 12% |

Apply one category, normally the highest applicable rate. A legal exemption, faction privilege, or negotiated charter must be written before the sale.

### Coin assay and reminting

Iron Ledger assay houses use Investigation, relevant tools, or mercantile proficiency. Standard DCs are 10 for obvious shaving, 13 for professional clipping or mixed weight, 16 for plated or sophisticated counterfeit coins, and 19 for a masterwork false die or mint. Assay costs 1% of the lot's face value, minimum 1 gp. Reminting costs 2% of recovered metal value. The holder receives the actual legitimate metal value minus fees.

Confirmed counterfeit coin or plated false currency is seized in full. Genuine coins or legitimate metal in the same documented lot are returned after fees. An innocent holder with documented provenance pays no criminal fine. A knowing holder pays the greater of 10% of the false lot's face value or the assay and remint fees.

### Public commodity index

These are common Haven baselines before demand, condition, provenance, and urgency multipliers:

| Documented source band | Typical lot range | Median reference |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tier I | 5–15 gp | 10 gp |
| Tier II | 15–50 gp | 30 gp |
| Tier III | 51–148 gp | 100 gp |
| Tier IV | 151–499 gp | 320 gp |
| Tier V | 512–1,465 gp | 950 gp |

The values describe individual documented material lots, not finished artifacts. Directors should alter Demand rather than rewriting Base Value for ordinary market fluctuation.

### Underways Heat

Illegal work in the Underways creates Heat from 0–6. Petty illicit trade adds 1 Heat, restricted cargo adds 2, strategic theft or smuggling adds 3, and trafficking or forbidden cargo adds 4. At the end of each Haven Turn involving the operation, roll 1d6; a result equal to or below Heat creates an enforcement complication. A bribe equal to 10% of the lot's value reduces Heat by 1; 25% reduces it by 2. A bribe cannot be paid at Heat 0. The floor of Heat 1 applies only when the operation had Heat 1 or higher before the bribe and remains active. Capture normally causes seizure and a faction-standing loss in addition to legal consequences.

### Labor, bondage, rescue, and trafficking

Track each person's legal and factual status separately: **Free, Contracted, Debt-Bound, Penal, Enslaved, Rescued,** or **Disputed**. Do not assign universal prices by race or ancestry. Legal wages are normally 1 gp per day for unskilled work, 3 gp for skilled work, and 10 gp for a specialist; dangerous expedition work doubles the rate. A lawful debt bond records principal plus no more than 10% interest per Opening, minus credited wages. Coercion, kidnapping, fraud, or unlawful confinement voids any buyout requirement.

Investigating trafficking uses an Evidence Clock from 0–4. Success adds 1 Evidence and Critical Success adds 2. At 4 Evidence, the investigators can trigger a warrant, raid, public hearing, rescue operation, or faction confrontation. Consensual adult sex work is not trafficking and is resolved through patronage, licensing, safety, information, and reputation procedures.

### Low Lantern and Draughtworks activities

During one Haven Turn a character may:

- **Seek Patronage or Information:** Pay an appropriate lifestyle cost and make a social check; Success gains a contact, lead, or audience, while Critical Failure creates rumor, blackmail, or unwanted attention.
- **Secure a License:** Pay the district fee and satisfy the relevant guild or health check to operate legally.
- **Brew a Batch:** Use the crafting formula, ingredients, facility, and one day of work; illegal substances also create Underways Heat.
- **Treat Dependence or Withdrawal:** Spend one day under qualified care to gain advantage on the next recovery check and prevent one ordinary withdrawal escalation.
- **Protect Workers:** Establish guards, safe transport, debt review, medical access, or an evidence operation; Success reduces exploitation risk or adds Evidence against a trafficker.

### Expedition cargo insurance

Insurance must be purchased and the cargo declared before loss.

| Cargo category | Premium |
|---|---:|
| Ordinary | 5% of insured value |
| Dangerous, volatile, cursed, or living | 10% |
| Attention or strategic | 15% |

The deductible is 20% of insured value. Maximum payout rates are 60% for undocumented provenance, 75% for disputed provenance, and 90% for documented or Gate-Sealed provenance.

`Payout = insured value × provenance payout rate - deductible`

Payout cannot exceed insured value. Covered losses include a recorded extraction complication, theft, transport failure, or named insured hazard. Fraud, undeclared cargo, voluntary abandonment outside an insured-sacrifice clause, and loss after knowingly ignoring an abort condition are excluded.

### Sponsor shares

Default agreements:

| Sponsor | Up-front support | Share of net expedition proceeds |
|---|---|---:|
| Self-funded | None beyond own capital | 0% |
| Local guild | Supplies and ordinary licenses | 20% |
| Merchant or bank | Capital, transport, appraisal access | 30% |
| Noble or temple | Capital, political cover, specialists | 35% |
| Sovereign or great institution | Full logistical support and legal force | 45% |

Specific contracts may differ. Net proceeds means sale income after direct Haven taxes and mandatory rescue or quarantine costs, but before personal wages and discretionary purchases.

Every charter records three different thresholds:

- **Gross cargo target:** Appraised sale value before taxes and shares.
- **Net expedition target:** Value after mandatory Haven costs and sponsor share.
- **True profit target:** Value remaining after wages, repairs, replacement equipment, debt service, and required reserves.

The charter designates one as the primary success metric. Never call an expedition profitable without stating which threshold is being used.

## 18. Reputation and renown

Use a standing score from -5 to +5 with each faction.

| Score | Standing |
|---:|---|
| -5 | Kill-on-sight or total proscription |
| -4 | Hunted |
| -3 | Hostile |
| -2 | Distrusted |
| -1 | Unwelcome |
| 0 | Unknown or neutral |
| +1 | Recognized |
| +2 | Favored |
| +3 | Trusted |
| +4 | Champion |
| +5 | Institutional authority |

Standing also controls routine institutional access:

| Score | Default access |
|---:|---|
| -5 | Proscribed; detention or attack expected |
| -4 | Hunted; covert contacts only |
| -3 | Hostile; enemy or black-market intermediaries only |
| -2 | Necessities at surcharge; no licenses, credit, or restricted auctions |
| -1 | Routine public market only |
| 0 | Ordinary markets, lodging, and contracts |
| +1 | Ordinary licenses and verified-buyer access |
| +2 | Restricted-material desks and selected auctions |
| +3 | Institutional credit, bonded storage, and sensitive contracts |
| +4 | Strategic auctions, command audiences, and major exemptions |
| +5 | Contract-issuing and institutional authority |

A normal success affecting a faction changes standing by 1. A major public success or betrayal changes it by 2. Standing cannot change by more than 2 from one scene without a campaign-defining event.

Expedition renown uses the established cultural scale:

- Survive Tier I: Proven adventurer.
- Reach Tier II: Established professional.
- Reach the Haven profitably: Career-defining success.
- Operate through Tier III: Elite expedition member.
- Return from Tier IV: Famous or feared.
- Return from Tier V: Living legend.
- Face the Sixth Gate: Historical figure.
- Pass the Sixth Gate: No verified public record.

## 19. Advancement rewards

**Milestone advancement is the default for Opening campaigns.** Award a level when the Expedition completes a meaningful campaign milestone such as establishing a secured route, completing a major contract, resolving a Gate-scale objective, changing Haven institutions, or surviving a defining extraction.

The detailed XP procedure below is optional for tables that want granular progression. Do not combine milestone levels and detailed XP awards during the same advancement period.

When using detailed XP, award it to every Expedition member who materially contributed during the relevant Watch, not only characters who entered combat.

Use the gap between the character's current level and next level as the basis:

| Achievement | XP per character |
|---|---:|
| Routine encounter or activity | 5% of level gap |
| Standard encounter or objective | 10% |
| Hard encounter or major discovery | 15% |
| Severe encounter or expedition objective | 25% |
| Gate Guardian victory | 40% |
| Profitable extraction meeting the declared threshold | 10% |
| First verified route, major text, or faction breakthrough | 5–15% |

Round to the nearest 10 XP. Do not award more than 60% of a level gap from one scene. Combat, research, resource, support, and reconnaissance achievements are valued by difficulty and consequence, not by body count.

Non-XP rewards include routes, licenses, property, faction authority, specialists, formulas, claims, camps, and institutional changes.

## 20. Seasonal and decennial play

The dungeon opens **once in each ten-year Cycle**. One Opening may contain many expeditions, contracts, stages, and campaign seasons, but those are not additional Openings.

At the end of an Opening, create two linked records:

- **Public Cycle Record:** Routes, Haven history, artifacts, factions, economics, recognized achievements, and the accepted depth record that characters and institutions can know.
- **Director-Sealed Consequence Record:** Hidden causes, unknown transformations, unrevealed enemies, and consequences that must not appear in player-facing files or summaries.

Record:

- Routes discovered, secured, lost, or deliberately concealed.
- Haven districts strengthened, damaged, captured, founded, or recovering.
- Major artifacts extracted or retained below.
- Faction standings and debts.
- Surviving specialists and successors.
- Unresolved captives, disappearances, oaths, and rivalries.
- Economic shocks caused by recovered materials.
- Publicly accepted depth record.
- Director-sealed consequences in the separate record.

### Consequence magnitude

Classify each Geography, Ecology, Economy, Politics, or Resonance change as:

- **Minor:** Localized; one route, district, faction cell, or commodity.
- **Standard:** Regional; several routes, an entire district, a major faction, or a persistent market.
- **Severe:** Campaign-defining; multiple districts, international economics, public doctrine, or the structure of future Openings.

### Haven district condition

Each major district has one state: **Intact, Strained, Damaged, Captured,** or **Recovering**. The state changes access, prices, available services, hazards, and faction control. Recovery requires a project, time, and resources rather than resetting automatically between Openings.

### Map persistence

Secured trunk-route principles and major landmarks persist as institutional knowledge. Unsecured spurs, chambers, resource nodes, and local hazards reset or transform unless a specific Cycle consequence preserves them. A map grants an informed starting hypothesis, not perfect future geometry.

### Heirs and successor characters

A legitimate successor may inherit documented titles, property, recognized reputation, trainers, contacts, and extracted artifacts according to law and play. They do not inherit exact unsecured chambers, resource nodes, personal class resources, or unrevealed Director knowledge.

### What persists into the next Opening

- Character and institutional knowledge.
- Provenance records and artifacts already extracted.
- Haven civic core and major permanent institutions unless destroyed in play.
- Faction relationships, debts, bloodlines, titles, and property.
- Secured route principles, but not every chamber or resource node.
- Consequences of Gate victories and major failures.

### What replenishes or changes

- Creature populations.
- Resource nodes and ordinary treasure.
- Unsecured routes, chambers, hazards, and local settlements.
- Market demand and scarcity.
- Faction leadership and expedition competition.
- The exact expression of the dungeon's tests.

Before the next Opening, the Director chooses or rolls one change in each category: Geography, Ecology, Economy, Politics, and Resonance. Each change must connect to the previous Cycle Record.

## 21. Table closeout checklist

At the end of every session record:

- Opening hour and Watch.
- Expedition location and route Progress.
- Pressure.
- Supply and transport Integrity.
- Every member's team, Strain, Wounds, HP, RP/RC, and status.
- Cargo lots, Load, Extraction Class, condition, and priority.
- Active objectives and abort conditions.
- Faction standing changes.
- New facts, maps, formulas, and unresolved questions.
- Whether the next session begins in Encounter, Expedition, or Haven Mode.
