# Decennium Descent — Level 1 Quickstart

**The loop:** Objective → Finance → Roster → Load → Watches → Encounter → Cargo → Extraction → Haven → Advancement.

## Your turn

- Move up to Speed.
- Take one Action.
- Take one Bonus Action only when a feature grants it.
- Use one free object interaction.
- Take one Reaction before your next turn.
- A creature can use only one additional full Action per round, regardless of source.

## The roll

Roll `d20 + ability modifier + proficiency when trained + circumstance modifiers`.

- Critical Success: total is DC +10 or higher.
- Success: total meets DC.
- Failure: total is below DC.
- Critical Failure: total is DC -10 or lower.
- Natural 20 improves one degree; natural 1 worsens one degree.

## One eight-hour Watch

1. Assign every expedition member to Combat, Recon, Research, Resource, Support, or Rest.
2. Consume one-third of daily Supply.
3. Resolve team activities.
4. Add route Progress.
5. Apply Pressure changes.
6. Roll 1d6; a result at or below Pressure causes a complication.
7. Update cargo, Strain, Wounds, Integrity, and Rest Debt.
8. Advance the Opening clock by eight hours.

**Rest Debt:** If a scheduled Rest Watch is lost or interrupted, record `Due in 2 Watches`. Complete a full Rest Watch before the end of the second following Watch. Missing the deadline causes 1 Strain; the debt remains.

## Pressure and retreat

Pressure runs 0–6. Pressure 6 causes an automatic Severe complication.

Retreat is a race: gain three Escape successes before three Pursuit successes. The side normally gains at most one Escape success per round from either the lead check or winning the round. A Critical Success may grant two.

## Cargo and extraction

- Record each lot's Load, Extraction Class 0–6, priority 1–5, value, and tags.
- Lowest-priority cargo is risked or abandoned first.
- Declaring extraction increases Pressure by `floor(highest Extraction Class ÷ 2)`, maximum +3.
- Extraction Test DC includes +0–+6 for highest Extraction Class, +1 exposed Attention cargo, Burden, unsecured dangerous cargo, pursuit, and route modifiers. Cargo-and-handling modifiers cap at +7.
- On Failure, the Director states the risk; players choose the time/complication cost or the identified low-priority cargo risk.

## Tier I preparation defaults

- Six-person company.
- Eight days of Supply.
- Reserve 40% of convoy Load for recovered cargo and casualties.
- Abort when two members are Critical, less than two days of Supply remain, transport reaches Integrity 0, or the latest return hour arrives.

## Tier I Haven fast settlement

Use **Inspection → Appraisal → Sale → Sponsor Settlement → Recovery** when there is no disputed claim, strategic cargo, active lien, quarantine crisis, contraband case, or faction hearing.

Track three separate outcomes:

- **Gross cargo:** value before taxes and shares.
- **Net expedition proceeds:** after mandatory Haven costs and sponsor share.
- **True profit:** after wages, repairs, replacements, debts, and reserve.
