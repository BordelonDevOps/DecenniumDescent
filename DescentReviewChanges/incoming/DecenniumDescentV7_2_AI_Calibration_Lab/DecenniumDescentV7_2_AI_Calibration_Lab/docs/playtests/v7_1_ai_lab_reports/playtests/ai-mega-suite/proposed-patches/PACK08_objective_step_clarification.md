# Proposed patch — Pack 08: clarify objective difficulty step (DRAFT)

**Status:** proposed only — RULES_HALT clarification, not a number change  
**Evidence:** Pack 08 RULES_HALT on CARGO/ESCORT/HAZARD/AMBUSH/RETREAT; ambush 0% all bands; escort band collapse

## What the books say

Gameplay Loop Canon: objectives, hostile terrain, surprise, and active hazards each increase effective difficulty by **one step** unless already included in creature levels.

## Ambiguity

When a scenario matrix (or Director prep card) labels an encounter “Hard” **and** includes cargo/escort/hazard/ambush, is the creature budget:

- (A) Hard creatures + objective mechanics, or  
- (B) Standard creatures stepped to Hard because of the objective, or  
- (C) Hard creatures with the step already “included”?

## Proposed clarification (wording only)

> If an encounter worksheet lists a difficulty **and** separately lists an objective/terrain/surprise/hazard modifier, apply **one** difficulty step for those modifiers only when they were **not** used to choose the listed difficulty. Do not double-step.

## Follow-up calibration (after wording lock)

- Ambush kill needs either mandatory step-up **or** a separate ambush budget table (0% success across labels in Pack 08 stress model).
- Escort/retreat need objective success metrics distinct from kill WR.
