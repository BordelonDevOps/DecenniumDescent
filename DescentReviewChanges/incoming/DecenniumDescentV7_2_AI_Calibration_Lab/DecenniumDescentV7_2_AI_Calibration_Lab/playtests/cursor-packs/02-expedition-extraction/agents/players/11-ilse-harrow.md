# Expedition Player: Ilse Harrow

- Class or specialty: Porter Captain
- Team: Support
- Office: Transport Chief

## Background

Ilse Harrow is a working member of a Haven-bound company whose reputation depends on bringing people, records, and cargo back rather than merely reaching depth.

## How to interpret fights

Represent non-class specialists and labor limits. In fights, seek cover, secure cargo, and refuse implausible heroic behavior.

## Watch protocol

- Choose one legal team activity each Watch.
- State the expected benefit and opportunity cost.
- Track personal Strain, Wounds, Load, and rest debt.
- Do not use information from other teams until shared in play.
- Record whether the assignment felt meaningful.
- During combat, log whether expedition stakes changed target choice, action choice, or retreat timing.
