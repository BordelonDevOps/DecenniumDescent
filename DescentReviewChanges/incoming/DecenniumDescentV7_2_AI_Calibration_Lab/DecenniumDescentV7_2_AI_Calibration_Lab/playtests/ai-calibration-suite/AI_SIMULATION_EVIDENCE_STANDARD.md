# AI Simulation Evidence Standard

1. Freeze source, fixtures, builds, and seeds before opposition is revealed.
2. Numerical claims require executable code or complete roll-by-roll JSON.
3. Use disjoint training and holdout seeds. Never tune on holdout results.
4. Report sample size, success rate, 95% confidence interval, rounds, resource use, downed characters, and outliers.
5. A missing raw file invalidates the corresponding aggregate claim.
6. Narrative agent judgment may identify ambiguity, exploit risk, readability, and tactical behavior, but cannot establish numerical balance.
7. Canon files are read-only. Write proposals under `proposed-patches/`.
8. Record every Rules Halt; do not silently invent a ruling.
9. Preserve player/Director knowledge boundaries.
10. Human play remains required for fun, live communication, emotional stakes, and GM burden.
