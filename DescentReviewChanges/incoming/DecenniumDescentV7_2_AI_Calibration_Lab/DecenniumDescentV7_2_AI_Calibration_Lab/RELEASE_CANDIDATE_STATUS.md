## Evidence ruling

AI simulations may establish arithmetic, invariant, coverage, and repeated-outcome evidence when the fixtures and rolls are preserved. AI narration alone cannot prove balance, usability, or fun. Human GM and player sessions remain required after the mathematical chassis passes.
