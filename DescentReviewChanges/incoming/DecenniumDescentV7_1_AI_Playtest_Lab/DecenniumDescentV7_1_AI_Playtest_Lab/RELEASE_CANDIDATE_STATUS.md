**Status:** Rules-complete release candidate; empirical playtesting pending.

- Final encounter-budget calibration.
- Long-run extraction profitability and Haven market stability.
- Multi-Cycle persistence balance.

## V7.1 evidence ruling

Cursor simulations were used to find ambiguities and exploit surfaces. They are not accepted as proof of numerical balance or human usability. Structural corrections are promoted; numerical rebalance awaits dice-by-dice and human table evidence.
