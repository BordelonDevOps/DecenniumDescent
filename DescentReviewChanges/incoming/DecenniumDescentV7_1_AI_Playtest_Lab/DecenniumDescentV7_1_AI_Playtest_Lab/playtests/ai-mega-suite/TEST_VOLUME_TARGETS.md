# Test Volume Targets

These are minimum targets, not claims that the runs have already occurred.

| Domain | Minimum volume |
|---|---:|
| Deterministic combat scenarios | 12 scenarios × 100 seeds |
| Encounter-budget calibration | 6 levels × 5 budgets × 4 party sizes × 100 seeds |
| Class-persona combat sessions | 17 classes × 4 personas × 6 levels |
| Feat/spell/ability interaction scan | all records statically; top 2,000 scored combinations reviewed |
| Multiclass/action exploit tests | 500 generated legal combinations plus targeted known risks |
| Expedition attrition | 10,000 seeded expeditions |
| Cargo extraction | 20,000 seeded route/cargo combinations |
| Haven economy | 50,000 transaction and charter settlements |
| Seasonal persistence | 1,000 three-Cycle campaigns |
| Boss/control tests | 5,000 seeded boss encounters |
| Rules fuzz/property checks | 100,000 generated state transitions |
| Readability/onboarding | all eight AI personas, then human follow-up |
| Regression | all accepted baselines after every proposed patch |

The scripts should do high-volume arithmetic. Agents should investigate anomalous clusters and full gameplay decisions rather than narrating every one of tens of thousands of runs.
