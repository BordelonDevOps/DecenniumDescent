# Lumen of the Shattered Thread — The Sixth Gate Disaster Record

**Family:** Gate Guardian  
**Knowledge status:** Historical Failure Record  
**Publication classification:** Public history with restricted tactical annex  
**Dungeon range:** T6, Floors 51-60  
**Primary source:** The Sixth Gate Disaster Archive  
**Reliability:** High that the encounter occurred; fragmentary on exact mechanics  
**Encounter role:** Unconquered Gate Guardian

## What the World Knows

The most famous recognized expedition in Aethel reached the Sixth Gate and confronted the entity its surviving records call **Lumen of the Shattered Thread**. The expedition did not pass. Its failure defines the accepted limit of mortal descent.

The public record confirms a Gate arena, severe radiant and displacing effects, the collapse of formation discipline, and a final retreat that returned only fragments of the original company. No complete victory condition survives. Different copies disagree about how many people reached the arena and whether the retreat was voluntary.

## Signs Recorded Before Contact

- Reflections continued moving after the expedition stopped.
- Tether lines returned tied in unfamiliar knots.
- Written orders lost conjunctions, changing cooperation into mutually exclusive commands.
- Light sources illuminated different routes for different observers.
- The final camp contained preparation caches rather than sleeping spaces.

## What Failed

| Attempt | Recorded Result |
|---|---|
| Concentrated frontal assault | The formation was divided and support could no longer verify targets. |
| Independent flanking teams | Teams returned with incompatible memories of the arena. |
| Repeated radiant protection | Reduced casualties but did not solve the Gate principle. |
| Forced retreat after the first phase | Preserved a fraction of the expedition but abandoned wounded and records. |
| Destroying visible anchors | Some anchors reappeared in different positions. |

## What May Help

These are professional hypotheses, not proven solutions:

- Redundant command methods that do not depend on one voice, map, reflection, or light source.
- Physical tethers marked by touch as well as sight.
- Prearranged authority for retreat so contradictory orders cannot split the company.
- A Research team dedicated to the Gate principle rather than damage output.
- A willingness to abandon the attempt before the support line loses shared reality.

## How to Defeat It

**No verified method exists.** This manual does not provide a complete stat block, exact save DCs, or a guaranteed counter because the world of Aethel does not possess them. Reaching the Sixth Gate is a historical achievement. Passing it has no confirmed public record.

## Director Boundary

No public combat block exists. The Director should build on this incomplete record and allow characters to improve it only through play, recovered evidence, and direct observation.
