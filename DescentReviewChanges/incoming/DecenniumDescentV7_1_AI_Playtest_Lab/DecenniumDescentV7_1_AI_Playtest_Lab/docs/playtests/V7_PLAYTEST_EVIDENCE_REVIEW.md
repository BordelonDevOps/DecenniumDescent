# V7 Playtest Evidence Review

**Reviewed:** 2026-08-06  
**Source:** `DD_V7_Playtest_Reports.zip`  
**Decision:** Promote supported procedural clarifications; defer global numerical rebalance.

## Evidence classification

The supplied reports are useful synthetic simulations created by Cursor agents. They are not human table sessions, and the combat pack explicitly estimates DPR from class identity and level breakpoints instead of recording complete dice-by-dice turns. The reports therefore support findings about ambiguity, missing procedures, exploit surfaces, and data conflicts. They do not establish real win rates, real turn speed, player comprehension, or final encounter calibration.

## Promoted findings

- One shared Additional Action Slot across all full-Action grants.
- Independent actor categories unified across summons, deployables, reflections, companions, drones, and undead cohorts.
- Dual Cast defined as one Action rather than two turns or two Actions.
- Traumatic Casting cannot refund, reset, protect, or retrigger itself.
- Retreat defaults to one meaningful threat per pursuit round.
- Routes gain Sustenance and bounded Forage output.
- Rest Debt receives an explicit two-Watch/day-boundary deadline.
- Attention cargo adds Pressure on exposure, not automatically every Watch.
- Extraction Test cadence is defined by Mapping.
- Charters separate gross value, net expedition proceeds, and true profit.
- Haven taxes, coin assay, reminting, commodity baselines, Underways Heat, labor, trafficking evidence, and district downtime receive runnable procedures.
- Reputation is normalized to -5 through +5 in rules and application data.
- Cycle Records split public and Director-sealed information; consequence magnitude, district condition, map persistence, and heir inheritance are defined.
- Milestone advancement is the default; detailed XP is optional.
- Public validation now scans public mechanics documentation, not only runtime source.

## Deferred findings

The following are hypotheses, not promoted balance changes:

- Raising Standard encounter HP or damage by 15–25%.
- Increasing Catastrophic level-20 budgets or boss output.
- Automatic counter-economy penalties at a wealth-index threshold.
- Exact smuggling, trafficking, or market-shock rates generated by assumed agent models.
- Claims about onboarding time or human turn speed; Pack 05 was not included in the returned report archive.

## Required next evidence

1. Run Pack 05 with human or closely observed reader sessions.
2. Run Pack 06 with fixed character sheets, fixed enemy stat blocks, visible random seeds, and every die roll recorded.
3. Compare observed encounter length, resource expenditure, knockouts, action-loop attempts, and lookup burden against the mathematical chassis.
4. Promote numerical changes only when repeated observed sessions support them.
