# World and Dungeon Loop

**Status:** Canonical campaign procedure  
**Primary rules:** [`GAMEPLAY_LOOP_CANON.md`](./GAMEPLAY_LOOP_CANON.md)

## Campaign cycle

```text
Surface preparation
  → Expedition organization
  → Dungeon Watches
  → Encounters, research, harvesting, and camps
  → Cargo selection
  → Extraction
  → Haven resolution
  → Further descent or surface return
  → End-of-Opening consequences
  → Ten-year Cycle Record
  → Next Opening
```

## Public depth knowledge

- Tiers I–III are established professional knowledge.
- The Haven at approximately Tier 2.5 is the center of ordinary expedition life.
- Tier IV is a dark frontier of contradictory evidence.
- Tier V is legendary and fragmentary.
- The most famous recognized expedition failed at the Sixth Gate.
- The public companion does not confirm geography or successful passage beyond that limit.

## Decennial persistence

At the end of the Opening, record routes, Haven changes, artifacts, debts, specialists, rivalries, economic shocks, and the accepted depth record. The next Opening changes geography, ecology, economy, politics, and Resonance according to those consequences.
