# Session and Expedition Loop

**Status:** Canonical companion workflow  
**Primary rules:** [`GAMEPLAY_LOOP_CANON.md`](./GAMEPLAY_LOOP_CANON.md)

## Two linked table surfaces

### SESSION tab

Use the Session tab for Encounter Mode:

- Load player characters and foes.
- Roll and manage initiative.
- Track HP, RP, Revelation Charges, forms, conditions, and combat notes.
- Advance combat rounds and turns.
- Resolve Short and Long Rests when the table returns to Expedition Mode.

### EXPEDITION tab

Use the Expedition tab for the complete campaign loop:

- Organize up to twelve members.
- Assign Combat, Reconnaissance, Research, Resource, Support, or Rest activities.
- Track three eight-hour Watches per day.
- Track the 2,400-hour Opening and 1,200-hour Horn.
- Track route Progress, Mapping, Capacity, Pressure, Supply, Strain, Wounds, camps, and treasury.
- Record cargo Load, Extraction Class, priority, value, and handling tags.
- Declare extraction and enter the Haven resolution sequence.

## At-table procedure

1. In EXPEDITION, record the objective and abort conditions.
2. Assign every member for the next Watch.
3. Resolve team checks at the table.
4. Press **Resolve 8-hour Watch** to advance time, route state, Supply, and Pressure.
5. When a complication becomes an encounter, open SESSION and run it in rounds.
6. Return to EXPEDITION after combat, negotiation, escape, or hazard resolution.
7. Add recovered cargo and casualties to the manifest.
8. Press **Declare Extraction** when the company turns back.
9. Resolve route transitions and cargo risk.
10. Press **Enter Haven Resolution** after arrival and complete quarantine, claims, appraisal, settlement, recovery, advancement, and preparation.
