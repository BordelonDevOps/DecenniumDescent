Decennium Descent Design Doctrine
Status: Owner-locked canonical design rule Effective: 2026-08-05
**Decennium Descent should have 5e's readability and speed, Pathfinder 2e's tactical clarity and balance discipline, and its own expedition, extraction, Haven, and seasonal systems.**
Order of priority
When two design goals conflict, resolve the conflict in this order:
1. Preserve Decennium Descent's identity: expedition organization, extraction risk, the Haven economy, the decennial Opening, and seasonal consequences.
2. Preserve tactical clarity and mathematical balance.
3. Simplify wording, timing, and table procedure wherever simplification does not damage the first two priorities.
Practical tests
A rule is ready only when all of the following are true:
A new player can explain the rule after one reading.
The action cost, trigger, target, duration, and outcome are explicit.
The rule can be resolved at the table without hidden arithmetic.
It does not create an unlimited action, summon, control, healing, or resource loop.
It contributes to combat, exploration, extraction, Haven play, or seasonal consequences.
A Director can adjudicate an edge case from the core resolution rules rather than inventing a new subsystem.
System vocabulary
Action: The character's primary act on a combat turn.
Bonus Action: A fast ability used only when a rule grants it.
Reaction: One response between the start of one turn and the start of the next.
Movement: Travel up to Speed during a combat turn.
Expedition Watch: One eight-hour block of the Opening.
Activity: A team assignment resolved during an Expedition Watch.
Pressure: The current likelihood of a dungeon complication.
Load: Physical carrying burden.
Extraction Class: The handling danger and attention created by cargo.
Haven Phase: Appraisal, claims, sale, recovery, research, and preparation between delves.
Cycle Record: The persistent consequences carried into the next Opening.
