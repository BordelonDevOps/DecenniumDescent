### ready

Legal release spends a Reaction, rechecks target/origin/perception/range/line of effect, chooses the target then and begins the effect duration at release. ### geometry

NEW for these six only: measure Euclidean world-space feet, including vertical separation; use actual occupied body volumes for membership and collision, not a grid-center shortcut. A legal origin needs actual caster perception and unobstructed caster-to-origin line of effect. Subsequent recipient expression needs the specified origin-to-body route; solid barriers clip expression. Every potentially affected extant creature is checked, including allies, caster and hidden bodies where the spell says all creatures. Hidden bodies are not disclosed to players merely by being in the adjudication ledger. Missing physical body/roof/support facts hold that branch instead of supplying an invented map.


### damage

Each listed die remains an individual recorded RNG draw, owned by the referee before disclosure. Roll a shared area total only where that procedure says so; distinct attacks retain separate attack and damage draws. Apply the exact source-specific save, cover, typed immunity/resistance/vulnerability and reduction once, floor where required, then temporary HP and HP. Preserve actual HP loss, Wounds, injury, death, concentration and downstream custody consequences from the bound Core procedure; temporary HP does not erase a concentration trigger based on resolved damage. Lightning and thunder remain different types. Damage defense never silently cancels a separately admitted movement, temporal or containment component. No object, ignition, structure failure, weather or elemental condition is granted without express text below.


### nullAndSuppression

NEW mapping only for these spells: each eligible scheduled area opportunity creates one independent recipient root, actual dealer = original caster, actual caster Veil snapshotted at pulse commitment; pure-transfer descendants inherit that root. Recipient defenses snapshot at that recipient resolution, after earlier completed events; a complete committed area group preserves its membership/root snapshot but does not overwrite live HP or create a second source. Frozen has no damage root. Thunder creates a separate attack root at each authorized strike commitment. A suppressed/lost opportunity creates no damage root. Apply exact Null root/recipient consequences without cancelling independent movement or confinement. Actual antimagic distinguishes suppression at the fixed source origin from local recipient expression. No arbitrary field or line-crossing immunity is fabricated. Suppression never pauses a source deadline, queues missed opportunities or restores spent class history.


### ending

Preserve actual identities and custody. Concentration loss, qualifying removal, cancellation and original exclusive expiry end only future source work; damage, movement, death, fallen equipment and physical aftermath already resolved remain. Do not heal, rewind, move a body back, restore an action or recycle a root on cleanup. Apply source-specific committed-group behavior below. Exact dispel must be actually acquired with its own rank, cost, range and check; Wizard Arch Dispel is rank3/3RP/60ft and check INT+PB against10+target rank, not Greater Dispel. Suppression is not removal.

### admission

NEW choose one fixed perceived point within100ft. Radius100ft sphere around that point, including above/below it, affects every creature whose actual body intersects the sphere with point-to-body line of effect. No self-origin restriction or ally exemption is invented. No visual omniscience is granted.


### clock

NEW choose recurring 15d10 force plus pull: concentration up to60 elapsed seconds from manifestation t0. Exactly ten scheduled opportunities t0+6k for k=0..9, exclusive expiry t0+60. Entry, reentry, numbered-round boundaries, extra turns, absence and resumption grant none. Each due opportunity is either resolved or marked lost with its actual reason; source termination cancels every future remaining opportunity explicitly. One live Cosmic per owner; a new established Cosmic replaces that owner’s old field. Different owners remain independent paid sources, with no imported numbered-round family cap.


### resolution

At each unsuppressed opportunity snapshot actual recipient membership, origin barriers and caster root flag. Roll one shared15d10 force. Each recipient STR save against recorded caster DC: failure full, success floorhalf. After its damage and ordinary consequences, a failed-save still-extant creature attempts ten feet of straight three-dimensional forced displacement toward the center, stopping at the center or last legal whole-body position before solid/occupied volume or an actual anchoring rule. Damage resistance/immunity does not cancel that pull. Anchoring cancels only movement within its real scope, not damage. A corpse created by the damage is no longer an eligible creature for this pull. Stable/Dying living bodies remain eligible. No crushing/impact damage from a blocked pull, no forced stacking or overshoot, no ordinary opportunity attack.


### continuity

Resolve recipient records in stable creature-ID order; recompute whole-body collision against positions resulting from prior recipients. A committed pulse finishes its saved member list even if pulse damage ends caster concentration; after its final consequences cancel all later pulses. A true interrupt before commitment prevents the entire pulse. Falling begins immediately on actual support loss; the existing fixed Fall Marker/impact rules apply, with a separately recorded environmental root and no automatic inheritance of the spell’s Null flag. No flight, Hover, safe landing or destruction of support is implied.
