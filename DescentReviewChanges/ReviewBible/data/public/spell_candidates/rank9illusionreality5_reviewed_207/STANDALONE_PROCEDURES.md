Binary saves have no natural-die shifts. A personally known-false depiction or operative True Seeing blocks conviction without rolling. Warning alone is not conclusive. This test is involuntary even for allies, with no willing auto-fail or repeated same-instance trial. For self, object, point or no-creature-target modes, select one other conscious perceived witness within120ft as the conviction anchor; the caster cannot supply belief in its own known fabrication. The witness perceives the depiction and tests once. This NEW anchor interpretation permits real utility without assigning a mind to an object; no witness means no effect after an otherwise legal committed cast. Hidden knowledge supplies no prepayment diagnostic.

RIR5-Q03 — Conviction admits the child’s actual typed damage, healing, condition, movement or utility under its bound procedure. Damage and ordinary healing already resolved are real and never reversed on disbelief. Future ongoing benefits require their original admitted recipient’s conviction; object/area utility requires its chosen anchor. No substituted psychic type, shadow percentage or blanket immunity is introduced. A child’s summoned body, item, resource restoration, information or travel requires that exact current complete source and a finite source-specific adapter; missing adapter facts hold that mode, not its family. Conditional source/campaign facts remain held. Independent physical work already done remains. NEW the wrapper cannot itself mint campaign knowledge, souls, materials or resource permissions absent from the child.

RIR5-Q04 — NEW composite: spend the outer Action/base26RP and V/S/F, cadence and concentration at commitment; surviving outer resolution supplies exactly one embedded native Action-time ordinary child cast without another Action. The child pays its complete native RP, HP, materials, real limited uses and components at its own later commitment, rechecking current surcharge, resources and geometry. It gets a separate counter window and source hooks. No reservation is payment; an unaffordable child fails without refunding the outer. Rank/class adapter uses the chosen real class row, but outer caster INT, PB and actual Wizard class level for explicitly scalable operands; source-specific prerequisites still need actual acquisition. A foreign engine/pool/charge is never fabricated. NEW Reaction, Bonus Action, long-cast and nested casting/copy modes are not operationally admitted by this first version; retain them as named coverage holds, not a claim the native any-spell wording forbids them. A prepared native child uses its own first-family exemption; an unprepared imitated mode has no first-prepared exemption. The two cast events chronologically update cadence separately.

RIR5-Q05 — The outer120ft admits the creature/witness; the child retains its native range and geometry measured from the actual caster, without extra120ft. Both predicates must hold at child resolution. NEW outer and child concentration are one composite dependency occupying one normal concentration slot: replacing/breaking it ends both, with the child’s own earlier deadline/conditions also controlling. Outer maximum is60elapsed seconds from outer manifestation; child duration starts at its own manifestation and cannot exceed that maximum while ongoing. Native completed instantaneous consequences remain. No additional concentration slot or unpaid tether. A native child that needs simultaneous independent concentration beyond this one dependency remains held.

RIR5-Q06 — Live personally witnessed conclusive contradiction, actual applicable True Seeing, or successful Action INT(Investigation) against outer DC identifies the depiction for that observer. Use Core task degrees/natural shifts, collapsed success, and no repeat failed examination without new evidence/method. Only that recipient’s ongoing belief-dependent child ends; an anchor’s loss ends the anchored child with its actual cargo/support cleanup. Other observers retain their own histories. No damage/heal rollback, material refund or hidden identity/stat disclosure. Identification is permanent for this instance; later blindness, suppression of True Seeing or recrossing cannot manufacture renewed belief. A genuinely new paid outer cast is distinct but known repeated deception still supplies actual knowledge.

RIR5-Q07 — NEW lineage is outer→one native child→that child’s ordinary noncasting consequences. No recursively imitated wrapper, cast-grant, copied-feature release, or descendant imitation is admitted in this version; missing broader branches remain explicit holds. One active outer per caster; successful replacement ends the old one, while commitment displaces prior concentration even if countered. Generic Ready pays/holds only the outer at preparation; on legal Reaction release it resolves and then commits the child as the expressly new composite event, with its own costs/counter window. Child may not itself be held again. Outer suppression pauses current child operations, not clocks; source-native ending or retained-body exceptions govern. No replay, missed opportunity bank, reacquired dropped cargo or refunded stock. Damage dealers remain the actual caster and each child’s real source-specific roots; copying a name does not grant Null. COPY-READY-ALLOWANCE-02 remains limited to its seven accepted profiles.

Justification: Q01/Q04/Q07 expressly permit named NEW exclusions with useful positive examples. The finite Action-time paid composite preserves outer/child costs and provider constraints; Fireball and Mage Hand demonstrate real damage and useful physical work. No universal copy, foreign pool or free reaction is implied.
