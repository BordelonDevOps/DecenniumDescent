# Disintegrate

Status: proposed authored procedure; independent exact acceptance/application remain separate.

KEEP both Evocation and Transmutation source placements, actual class-specific access, Action, 60 feet, V/S/F, instantaneous 10d6+40 force and disintegration when reduced to zero HP. NEW choose one perceived creature or independently targetable exposed object with line of effect and a real current/max durability track. A willing or unwilling creature makes DEX; success negates all damage and disintegration, failure receives 10d6+40 force. An eligible immobile object has no creature save; an actually mobile/specially protected object uses its actual applicable profile. Hostile worn/carried possessions cannot be independently selected without a genuine separate permission. This is one selected body/object, not a ray tunneling through a room, automatic wall-of-force removal or deletion of all items in an inventory. An object exceeding its actual damage scale receives only a legal independently profiled exposed section, bounded by a five-foot cube; it is never an automatic whole-building HP proxy.

NEW the disintegration clause belongs to the actual selected target's retained damage branch and requires that this spell's positive normal/structural HP damage take that same target from positive HP to zero after all legitimate defenses, pure prevention, sharing, temporary HP and same-packet survival replacements. Temporary-HP depletion alone is insufficient. The spell does not add a second form pool: Druid, Polymorph and creature-origin object anatomy retain their one real HP ledger. A valid positive-HP survival floor prevents the qualifying zero state. For this new ordering, Disintegrate's conditional disintegration is evaluated after Death Ward and other independently eligible crossing prevention; it is not classified as an outright-death exclusion before that very crossing exists. Core Instant Death and another independently established outright-death source keep their own ordering and exclusions. Same-packet would-die replacement that truly leaves this identity alive above zero also prevents final disintegration. An attempted later healing or resurrection is not such a prior replacement.

After those replacements, a qualifying still-living creature reaching this local checkpoint at zero is disintegrated and dies immediately, with no new ordinary dying/death-save interval. This is an explicit NEW direct body-destruction consequence of the source threshold, not a rule that all zero-HP creatures die. A creature already at zero before this packet does not undergo a new positive-to-zero crossing; resolve its ordinary damage/death consequences without inventing this threshold. A protector receiving transferred damage is not automatically disintegrated: the transfer retains force damage and ordinary life consequences, but the original-target disintegration rider does not travel. If the original target retained no positive normal HP loss or stayed above zero, destruction does not occur even if a protector died. Retargeting an actual attack is irrelevant to this save-based spell unless an actual source can retarget this spell itself before its target snapshot.

NEW qualifying destruction converts the selected body's remaining material into mundane inert dust at its actual location; record the identity as dead with its usable corpse material consumed. It creates no harvestable organ, functioning core, magical ingredient or intact significant remains. Dust retains only provenance and its actual mundane mass; it cannot be counted again as the former corpse, restored item, valuable component or full material lot. This does not assert destruction of a soul or establish cosmology. A later resurrection must actually supply missing-body reconstruction, willingness, death-window and all genuine identity restrictions: the reviewed ordinary Resurrection's sufficient-body gate fails, while reviewed True Resurrection may qualify under its own complete rules. A same-body form reversion, dispel or generic snapshot supplies no free regrowth or reconstruction of disintegrated physical stock. This spell does not override an independently sourced temporal-restoration or actual missing-body-reconstruction provider: apply that provider's own exact contract, costs, identity, equipment, topology and clock limits. In particular HEALING_DEATH_PLAYTEST.md explicitly preserves Spiral-TouchedV Time Loop's conceptual undoing of post-snapshot death/body destruction, while its full equipment/environment topology, body-part duplication and clock-rebasing interface remains held. This is no positive operational Time Loop fixture, feat reopening, temporal closure or cosmology approval.

Carried, worn, attached-but-distinct and melded possessions are separate identities and are not erased merely because their bearer dies. Resolve their actual release/body-equipment rules at the remaining location, retaining damage, charges, custody and unique-item restrictions. Native body matter is destroyed with that body; an actual independent prosthetic/device/item is not silently reclassified as native anatomy. An actually disintegrated object loses only that selected object's/section's material and eligible functioning identity; its container contents survive as distinct objects where legally released. An object-origin transformed creature retains its structural-life interface: destruction supplies no organic death saves, soul, corpse or new resurrection right. Creature-origin object anatomy retains its actual character identity and HP history; changing presentation cannot create a replacement body or a second threshold. Ordinary future retrieval, repair or recreation cannot claim unavailable original material. No delayed ongoing source remains to dispel, and no arbitrary immunity or override is inferred from the target's name.
