# Contagion

Status: proposed authored procedure; independent exact acceptance/application remain separate.

KEEP Action,one perceived creature60feet and instantaneous infection with a disease. The examined Core spell entry/condition guidance supplies no named complete disease roster; NEW use exactly one chosen fictional profile below, never an imported external Contagion list. Target must be a living creature of a type that is not Construct or Undead, with no actual disease immunity; Beast/Plant bodies can qualify unless their actual source says otherwise. No contact, wound, poison or real-world pathogen is required. The target makes CON vs spellDC; success prevents infection, failure establishes one disease record immediately, with symptoms beginning60elapsed seconds later. The disease is an instantaneous magical cause with an ordinary persistent disease result, not a continuing rank-five spell or curse: counter the initial cast, but later Dispel/suppression alone does not remove it. Disease immunity acquired later suppresses symptoms without curing; Petrified uses its actual suspension rule. Original incubation/recovery/expiry clocks keep running during suspension. Choose Grey Ague (disadvantage on STR ability checks and STR-based attack rolls), Glass Chills (halve each ordinary Speed; same-stat half-Speed penalties use their actual strongest-family arbitration rather than repeated halving), or Moth Haze (disadvantage on INT and WIS ability checks, not saves, attacks or passive scores by an invented extra rule). All are fictional disease profiles, not Poisoned, Exhaustion, ability-score loss, HP damage or mind control. Symptoms do not escalate beyond that one profile. At each24elapsed-hour anniversary from infection make one CON recovery save against the original DC, unaffected by that disease's own check penalties. Three cumulative successes end it; failures do not erase prior successes. It expires unconditionally after seven elapsed days, with expiry first if coincident with a final save. No Long Rest or calendar midnight grants an extra save. NEW thirty uninterrupted minutes of actual Medicine care with ordinary usable medical supplies allows the caregiver one WIS/Medicine task check against the original DC: Success/Critical Success grants advantage on the next scheduled recovery save, and no additional immediate recovery roll. Only one such unspent care benefit exists per disease record and another unchanged attempt cannot stack it; supplies/credentials come from actual inventory, with no newly invented consumed item fee. A permitted actual disease cure ends the selected record and its dependent symptoms, including during suppression. Poisoned-only cures, ordinary healing and Remove Curse do not cure it by name. One Contagion disease per creature across all casters: later failed casts while it remains add no new profile, onset, duration, care benefit or reset successes; other independently sourced diseases remain distinct. No contact spread or epidemic is granted. Death ends this spell-caused disease record, without a corpse transmissibility feature, new harvest trait or immunity promised to later resurrection.

Ordinary Antimagic cannot suppress this completed disease result. A temporary independent immunity's loss resumes only still-live, already-onset symptoms; no renewed infection or clock.
