## What It Is

Giant Constrictor Snake is a dungeon-adapted predator or hazard. Its behavior remains rooted in food, territory, nesting, and escape, which gives a prepared Expedition more options than a direct fight.

## What Experienced Expeditions Notice

Scratches at the creature’s true height; prey dragged away rather than consumed in place; mineral dust in droppings; repeated avoidance of a seemingly safe route. Prey trails stop abruptly where the animal begins carrying rather than feeding.

## How It Fights

It opens with **Bite** against the target most capable of controlling its movement. It uses **Constrict** to punish anyone who tries to stabilize that target. It attacks from concealment, protects its escape path, and stops pursuing once territory or food is secured.

## How to Defeat It

**Prepare Before Contact**

- Prepare resistance or mitigation for piercing, bludgeoning damage.
- Carry bait, scent control, and a noncombat retreat route.

**Combat Priority**

Control its escape and feeding choices rather than cornering it.

**Exploit**

The defining weakness is **None identified**. Treat this as an encounter solution rather than an automatic damage vulnerability. Successful Research, Recon, or Resource actions should make the exploit easier or safer.

**Withdraw When**

Withdraw or change the objective when the creature reaches favorable terrain or isolates a target. A failed retreat against this creature usually costs more than abandoning the immediate treasure.

## Habitat, Goals, and Lair
