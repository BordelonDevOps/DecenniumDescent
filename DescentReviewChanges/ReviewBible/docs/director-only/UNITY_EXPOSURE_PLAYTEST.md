# Unity Exposure — Director integration boundary

**Provisional review layer, 8 October 2026.** Use the reviewed public 0–6 track and Anchor Project in [condition recovery](../mechanics/CONDITION_RECOVERY_PLAYTEST.md). Its numerical table is newly authored. The bounded source search and notebook conference did not recover an owner-approved numerical threshold table.

The track is not a revelation ladder. Raising a score does not identify a faction, institution, individual, location, metaphysical cause or hidden endgame truth. A player can use the public mechanical record without knowing the Director explanation. Keep private scene context, historical evidence and true identities in Director materials.

Use established Director sources for why a particular hazard or encounter creates Exposure. The condition itself grants no ability to inflict it. Every new application needs a reviewed trigger, target, magnitude, resistance/save if any, frequency and prevention/removal rule. Qualitative susceptibility in lore does not by itself provide a numerical ancestry penalty; any mechanical application needs separate explicit review.

At a threshold, describe only information the characters could perceive. Invite the affected player to choose how identity pressure appears in their portrayal. Apply the printed current-row penalty, never an additional secret penalty. Neither score 6 nor a failed Anchor Project automatically transfers character control, erases a factual memory, changes loyalties or deletes the character. Actual transformation, retirement, death or other campaign outcomes need their own reviewed rule and scene context.

Examples:

- At score 2, a character distinguishing two similar witnesses takes the stated −1 if the Director identifies that check's scope before the roll. This does not prove either witness is an impostor.
- At score 5, mental ability checks have disadvantage. Do not also add the score-4 −1; the table uses its current row only.
- At score 6, the Anchor Project's WIS save also has disadvantage. A positive Exposure gain capped at 6 still interrupts the project; a prevented gain does not.

This closes the public/private mechanics boundary for the generic condition. It does not replace the Director cosmology, revelation ladder, individual exposure sources, endgame rules or their remaining source audits.
