**Still to settle:** Confer on the interface. A divine name, class tag, spell rank or infernal contract does not by itself establish the owner’s extraordinary exception; this is a proposed implementation boundary, not a newly approved mechanic.

## SD-06 — Prevented death versus return after death

Phoenix Rebirth heals a living target; Ritual Ward replaces eligible death before finalization; Soulbound Ritual reconstructs specific companions.

**Still to settle:** Ask GPT to distinguish what the owner’s “if they die” wording supports from any new Hunt-specific restrictions. Do not disable all survival effects or treat a prevented death as automatically final without a source.
