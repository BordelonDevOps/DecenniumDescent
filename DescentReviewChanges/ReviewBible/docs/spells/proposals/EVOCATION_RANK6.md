## Shared procedures

### casting

Keep each actual rank-six access row, minimum class level, school and base price of 13 RP. All eight retain Action, 60-foot source range and V/S/F. Disintegrate retains both Evocation and Transmutation placements and its actual class-specific school. Actual repertoire/preparation, components, legal anatomy, class casting ability, PB, discounts/surcharges, Dissonance/Refrain and concentration transitions govern. Commit legal costs and cast-begin hooks before Counterspell; failed saves, invalidated targets and counters refund nothing or restore former concentration. Saves use DC 8 + actual PB + casting modifier; spell attacks use d20 + PB + casting modifier. Binary spell saves do not gain task-check degrees. No generic scaling or upcast.

### geometryAndDamage

Fix the actual recipient/geometry snapshot after the cast interrupt window and before the first damage packet. Recheck declared individual targets; an unavailable target is not replaced after outcomes. An area can include unperceived creatures when its own rules say all creatures, but individually chosen or hostile-only targets require actual perception. Center line of effect and real occupied volumes apply. Destroying cover does not add a second scan. Roll one shared raw amount per stated simultaneous area, then each recipient resolves its own save, defenses, eligible prevention, one sharing operation, temporary HP, real HP and life consequences. Save-halving rounds down before defenses. Transferred damage is not a new hit/failed save or an automatic carrier of original-target riders. DamageDealt is terminal temporary HP absorbed plus actual normal HP lost, never overkill, an unsplit parent counted twice, or later structural removal.

### timeAndHistory

One round means the explicitly named future original regular marker, not an extra turn. Preserve that original or virtual marker when initiative, owner presence or life state changes. Outside-initiative one-round effects use six elapsed seconds. A minute maximum is 60 elapsed seconds. Suppression removes eligible consequences while the original clock and actual spent histories continue; missed opportunities are not queued. Expiry wins at its exact deadline. Completed damage, real lost matter and consumed costs do not reverse when magic ends.

### bodyAndMaterial

There is one physical identity and one current/max HP track across Druid and spell forms. No second form-HP pool or overflow pool is introduced. Actual items, carried loads, corpse identity, material lots, damage, contamination and consumption remain recorded. An apparent body snapshot cannot recreate destroyed stock. Created machinery, altered terrain and real ice receive only their explicitly proposed profiles; spell names do not grant all Tinker features, permanent salvage, arbitrary structural damage or crafting quality.

### temporalBoundary

Absolute Zero proposes a finite local suspension policy. The reviewed Time Loop conservation and exact-source/clock interfaces are comparison constraints, not an implemented generic stasis adapter. Missing device/stasis/copy/parent adapters remain missing; this proposal does not release full Time Loop or certify application execution.

### workedBaseline

Unless a case says otherwise, use an eligible Wizard 11 with INT modifier +4, PB +4, maximum/current RP 40, Dissonance 0, limit 10 and no discount/surcharge. A new rank-six cast costs 13 RP, leaving 27, adds the actual first-cast cadence increment, and uses DC 16 or spell attack +8. Mage 11 instead has maximum 32 RP; do not borrow Wizard capacity. Source-specific defenses and chronology still apply.

### scope

All source records remain complete and unchanged. These are authored playtest proposals, not applications of external-game rules. No cosmology, soul ontology, planar topology, automatic publication, human balance approval or source deletion is supplied.

## EV6-01 — Solar Flare

**Original question:** Preserve BLINDING ALL ENEMIES IN60ft and8d6 FIRE,Core Action/60ft/Instantaneous. Define center,finite geometry,actual enemy selection/perception,line of effect and initial target snapshot. Supply save/outcomes for damage and Blinded independently,required visual perception if any and a finite blindness endpoint/removal rule. Flashbang does not automatically add Deafened,Stunned,Prone,explosive knockback or an enduring light source. Compare current Blinded and information/cover rules: immunity to fire,sightless senses,blocked vision or harmless flash perception must affect only components actually dependent on them. No recurring damage or new targets after the initial snapshot,including those exposed when the same blast destroys cover.

KEEP Action, 60 feet, V/S/F, instantaneous 8d6 fire and blinding all enemies in the area. NEW center a 60-foot-radius sphere on the caster; this operationalizes the source range as its radial reach, with no remote center. Include every other creature in the sphere that the caster actually perceives and currently regards as an enemy, with caster-origin line of effect. The caster cannot selectively omit a perceived enemy after seeing saves. This is the caster's contemporaneous hostile selection, not a detector of secret allegiance, morality or hidden bodies. Allies, objects, buildings and worn/carried gear are not damage targets of this hostile-creature-only profile.

Each recipient makes one CON save. Roll 8d6 fire once. NEW success takes half, rounded down, and no blindness. Failure takes full damage and, after that packet, attempts Blinded on the same still-eligible creature if it could actually visually perceive this flash at the original snapshot. Sightless anatomy, an already fully opaque eye covering or an actual optical barrier prevents that visual rider, not fire damage. A real defense against Blinded prevents only that condition. Fire immunity can reduce damage to zero without preventing visually delivered blindness. Seeing the flash through ordinary glass does not defeat a glass barrier that independently blocks the spell's line of effect; both predicates must hold. A creature cannot claim a retroactive eye closure after learning its save; any genuine previously established closed-eye state carries its ordinary sight restrictions.

NEW this source-owned Blinded record lasts until the end of the target's next future regular turn, excluding a turn already underway when applied; outside initiative it lasts six elapsed seconds. It is nonconcentration and can end through actual condition cure, eligible removal/dispelling, death or its deadline. Suppression suspends the rider without resetting its clock. Multiple sources retain their actual deadlines while the same condition has no multiplied penalty. A later real cast may create its own finite blindness record but cannot replay the earlier damage. Ordinary Blinded consequences apply; unaffected nonvisual senses keep their actual scope. No whole-Action denial allowance is spent solely by Blinded. The flash grants no continuing light source, sunlight trait, Deafened, Stunned, Prone, ignition, blast movement or environmental fire. Subsequent entrants receive nothing.

**New decisions:** NEW self-centered geometry, subjective all-perceived-enemies selection, CON half/negates-rider, visual dependence and a finite one-target-turn blindness endpoint; source fire amount and hostile scope retained.

### Authored examples

EV6-01-A: Three perceived enemies are 20, 40 and 60 feet from the caster with line of effect. The shared roll is 29 fire. The first fails CON and takes 29 before defenses, plus the visual Blinded rider. The second succeeds and takes floor(29/2) = 14 with no rider. The third fails but is fire-immune: it takes 0 fire and can still be Blinded if its sight perceived the flash. The one cast costs 13 RP, leaving the baseline Wizard 27.

EV6-01-B: An enemy concealed behind total effect-blocking cover is absent from the initial area snapshot even if the blast is bright. Another enemy is perceived through a genuine nonvisual sense but has no functioning visual sense: it makes the damage save and receives no visual blindness. The spell does not reveal a hidden hostile, ignite the cover or expand its target list after another event removes that cover.

## EV6-02 — Absolute Zero

**Original question:** Preserve ONE CREATURE frozen IN TIME,10d6 COLD and CON SAVE NEGATING ALL,Core Action/60ft/Instantaneous. Resolve the time-freezing clause explicitly rather than silently replacing it by generic cold damage or whole-body Petrified. Supply any finite control/stasis duration,concentration,scope and ending as NEW where absent,while retaining complete successful-save negation. If temporal suspension is adopted, bind recipient actions,Reactions,movement,incoming damage,ongoing effects,ordinary clocks and source-history restoration to the reviewed temporal procedures and their stated incomplete integration limits. Suppression,resumption,extra turns and death cannot duplicate ticks,refund costs,erase actual history or release an unbounded stored-damage burst. No temperature-based instant death,cosmological time travel or ice inventory follows from the title.

KEEP Action, 60 feet, V/S/F, one creature, 10d6 cold and CON success negating ALL effects. NEW select one perceived creature with line of effect, regardless of willingness; there is no voluntary save waiver. Success deals no damage and establishes no suspension. Failure first resolves 10d6 cold using the ordinary packet, survival and body rules. If the same target remains a living or otherwise active creature and the caster's combined source-character/target/encounter whole-Action denial allowance is available, attempt the following new temporal suspension. The guard is spent only when that suspension is actually established. A relevant complete protection against temporal suspension or whole-Action denial prevents it; cold immunity alone does not. An unavailable guard leaves failed-save cold damage intact, with no soft fallback or delayed claim.

NEW the control is genuine finite local suspension, not the Core Frozen or Petrified condition. It requires the caster's concentration from cast commitment and lasts until the caster's next future original regular START, or six elapsed seconds outside initiative, whichever applicable original clock was established. If no suspension is established, this cast leaves no ongoing concentration burden. The suspended creature is unaware, cannot speak, act, take Bonus Actions/Reactions, make voluntary choices or move, and its body and actually carried equipment are held at their current location and pose. Voluntary and forced relocation, including ordinary teleportation, cannot move that held body; a source expressly overcoming temporal suspension retains its stated exception. No ordinary STR/DEX failure or attack-advantage rule is inferred from Incapacitated, because this is its own action-denial state, not a newly applied Incapacitated condition.

During operative suspension, the held body cannot receive new damage, healing, temporary HP, conditions or physical alterations. Attempts otherwise legal to target it spend their actual costs but have no bodily payload and store nothing. Existing periodic bodily outputs due during the interval are skipped, not banked. Death saves are likewise not rolled while suspended and create no success or failure. The body's age and immediate respiration/metabolic activity pause for this finite interval; it gains no food, water, rest credit or completed work. World time, existing absolute deadlines, source/recipient regular markers, spell expiry, actual rest calendars and resource-use history continue. The marker still occurs even when its body cannot act; this is not a new initiative slot. Existing concentration held by the target remains unless its own actual deadline, unpaid upkeep or independent ending ends it; suspension alone does not inflict ordinary Incapacitated or restore ended concentration. A required voluntary upkeep that cannot be paid fails by its original rule.

The suspension itself remains a reachable ongoing rank-six magical effect for specifically legal dispel/suppression/removal, even though the protected body cannot take a new bodily payload. A protection that only stops fire or poison does not defeat this state. The caster may release concentration normally. On expiry, ending or suppression, bodily interaction resumes at the current real location with no backlog, fresh turn, restored spent Action, resource reset or returned missed tick. Only still-unspent permissions in an actual current turn may be used. If suppression ends before the original deadline, the same still-live suspension resumes around the surviving creature at its then-current position, without snapping it back or spending the guard again. It cannot resume on a dead/removed identity. Simultaneous suspensions do not add duration or borrow another instance's clock; a body already protected from a new bodily payload cannot acquire a new suspension through it. After support ends, genuine unsupported height uses the ordinary Fall Marker. Gear remains the same real inventory, inaccessible while held; no detachable ice, time travel, stored-damage burst, reset disease duration or implemented Time Loop restoration is created.

**New decisions:** NEW concentration, finite source-start endpoint, explicit local suspension rather than generic paralysis, complete body/clock/output policy, hard-control admission and suppression bridge. Full temporal runtime integration remains absent.

### Authored examples

EV6-02-A: The baseline Wizard commits 13 RP and concentration. A target fails CON, takes 34 cold before defenses, survives, and has no spent hard-control claim from this caster. Suspension establishes and spends that one claim until the caster's next future regular start. A later sword strike and an already scheduled poison tick during the operative interval cause no bodily damage and are not queued. The target's external poison deadline still advances; ending suspension grants no free turn or replayed tick.

EV6-02-B: A CON success negates both cold and suspension despite the committed RP. On a separate failed-save attempt against a target whose hard allowance from this caster is already spent, the 10d6 cold still resolves but suspension does not. If an established suspension is suppressed halfway through its original six-second window, real interaction resumes during suppression; resumption cannot create a new six seconds, refund a Reaction, restore lost concentration or protect a body that died during the gap.

## EV6-03 — Arcane Mortar

**Original question:** Preserve a REPEATING ARCANE CANNON that FIRES3ROUNDS AT5d6 FORCE,Core Action/60ft/concentration up to one minute. Resolve three ammunition shots versus three combat rounds explicitly; do not silently multiply either count by the minute. Provide a complete finite device or other stated effect profile: size,placement,HP/AC if real,owner,attack/save,range,geometry,targeting,command cost,initiative and exact cadence/total ammunition. Reconcile devices with summon allowances without granting both profiles' benefits. Define first shot,unavailable/skipped opportunities,suppression,destruction,movement,concentration loss and end-of-fire disposition. No extra turns,autonomous hidden-enemy targeting,free ammunition or salvageable cannon/components on expiration unless expressly NEW and conserved.

KEEP Action, placement range 60 feet, V/S/F, concentration up to one minute, a summoned repeating arcane cannon and three rounds at 5d6 force. NEW interpret rounds as three ammunition shots total, with at most one actual shot per owner numbered round, not three shots each round for a minute. Create one magical machine object on a perceived unoccupied supporting surface within range and line of effect. Its whole Medium, five-foot occupied space must fit. NEW profile: AC 16, maximum/current HP 30, mass 100 lb, no movement of its own, poison/psychic damage immunity, no organic Wounds, Stable state or death saves. It has no creature type, independent senses, initiative, speech, hands or autonomous enemy selection. Dexterity saves fail automatically as an immobile object; for genuinely applicable STR/CON object saves use +3, and other saves require an effect that actually targets an object. It does not acquire an organic condition merely from a creature-only spell.

One mortar per caster is allowed. A genuine replacement ends the former mortar administratively, without destruction rewards; a rejected cast replaces nothing before ordinary legal cast commitment. This source has its own one-object capacity and occupies no Tinker deployment slot or Wizard creature-summon slot. It nevertheless shares the owner's existing single Bonus Action command and single damaging companion/device activation per numbered round. These limits apply across all actual owned companions and devices. A separate minion, extra Action, extra turn, reconstruction or movement cannot provide a second mortar shot that round.

NEW while conscious and able to command, the owner spends that shared Bonus Action on their own regular turn, selects one perceived creature or independently legal exposed object, and declares one shot. The mortar must be operative, have ammunition, be within 60 feet of the owner with command line of effect, and have a clear firing line to the selected target within 60 feet of the mortar. Hostile worn/carried gear is not independently targeted without an actual applicable permission. Resolve the commanded shot in the single attached companion phase after the owner's whole turn. The cast itself grants no shot, but a remaining legal Bonus Action on that same regular turn can command its first one. If the command/shot predicates fail before firing, the committed command is lost; no ammunition or damaging activation is spent when no shot begins, and no queued retargeting occurs. A later genuine command may try again while time/ammunition remain.

When the legal shot begins, spend one ammunition unit and the shared damaging activation, then make one ranged spell attack using the owner's current actual casting modifier and PB from this spell's acquired class. Hit deals 5d6 force; a natural critical doubles only those attack dice. A miss spends the shot. This is a narrow shot along its firing line, not a blast radius or arcing attack through roofs. No attack rider, subclass missile feature or creature-summon bonus applies solely because the object is a cannon; require the real source predicate. After its third resolved shot, it ends. Losing concentration, its 60-second deadline, dismissal or replacement also ends it.

The machine can be damaged or moved by a genuinely legal object-handling/forced-motion source. A moved cannon fires only while on a stable supporting surface and with legal placement; carrying it provides no mobile firing exception. Suppression makes it an inert but still physical object while its clock continues; the same HP, ammunition and spent history resume if still valid. Genuine functional-machine repair may restore positive HP under the repair source's requirements, but cannot refill shots, extend duration or reconstruct the vanished cannon. At zero HP it is destroyed and the conjured body vanishes, supplying no usable original material for Snap, harvest or salvage. Destruction and administrative ending remain distinct events for any independently qualified trigger, but Tinker-owner/class-deployment predicates are not waived. Its conjured metal/force components never become saleable equipment, permanent scrap or an ammunition supply.

**New decisions:** NEW three total shots, complete magical-machine profile and slot, explicit shared command/activation bridge, attached phase, shot range, current-owner attack, repair/material and suppression lifecycle.

### Authored examples

EV6-03-A: A Wizard 11 pays 13 RP, places the 30-HP/AC-16 mortar and uses their still-available shared Bonus Action to command one shot. In the attached phase the +8 attack hits for 18 force, spending one of three shots. There are two shots left. A second companion cannot also attack in that numbered round. In a later round a missed shot still reduces ammunition from two to one; the minute duration does not replenish it.

EV6-03-B: The caster commands a target, then an intervening effect suppresses the cannon before the attached phase. That command remains spent, but no shot began, so the ammunition remains three and the damaging activation was not consumed by this cannon. Suppression ending within the original minute allows a later new command, not a delayed free shot. If damage instead destroys the machine, it vanishes; a repair feature requiring original debris cannot reconstruct it or claim 100 lb of scrap.
