## Shared proposals

### status

UNADOPTED AND UNSENT. Eight complete operating proposals and sixteen authored examples await joint discussion and exact review. All unsupported completions are explicit NEW choices; no catalog, class access, runtime, balance or cosmology approval changes.

### casting

KEEP rank5/base10RP, actual classAccess, acquisition level and genuine repertoire. All eight use Action. All use V/S/F except Flame Typhoon keeps its V/S/M/F unpriced material-or-legal-focus alternative. Ordinary actual hands, voice, components, perception/line of effect, commitment, Counterspell window, Dissonance/Refrain, concentration displacement and source-qualified class hooks apply. A legal cast spends actual resources and cast-begin consequences before countering; failure does not refund or restore displaced concentration. No generic upcast, replacement repertoire, arbitrary additional targets or automatic level scaling. Spell save DC is8+PB+actual casting modifier. Saves are binary; unrelated actual task checks retain Core degrees and natural shifts.

### damage

Roll each stated area damage type once per simultaneous pulse, then apply each recipient’s own save and defenses. A half-save floors each typed amount separately before typed mitigation. Multiple typed components are one simultaneous damage event for that original recipient, with source/root/type-preserving sharing and actual terminal branch accounting; they do not create duplicate concentration/Wound/hit callbacks. Different recipients retain distinct events. Damage zero does not inherently negate a separately stated condition or movement component. Save spells have no attack critical. No source here gains object, equipment or structure damage unless expressly stated; no flavor grants reusable fire, ice, ammunition, harvested material or an unnamed condition.

### geometryAndSelection

NEW explicit finite geometry is specified below. Use genuine occupied bodies, three-dimensional distance, actual cover and line of effect from the stated origin/center. Snapshot each event’s eligible recipients and geometry before resolving it; cover destroyed by another simultaneous result does not add recipients. A named creature target requires actual perception at commitment and resolution. Ordinary indiscriminate areas can affect unperceived bodies actually inside them. For the three source clauses explicitly naming enemies, NEW interpretation: use all creatures currently perceived by the caster and affirmatively treated as hostile from the caster’s available information, within the geometry at that pulse. No arbitrary numerical cap or post-roll omission is permitted. This is intent/information-based selection, not detection of actual allegiance: a deceiving enemy believed friendly may be missed, and a mistakenly designated innocent may be affected. Caster-origin enemy effects exclude the caster. This narrower perception requirement is expressly proposed for joint review, not silently added to the original summaries.

### ongoingClocks

NEW minutes below are absolute60 elapsed seconds from resolution. Expiry precedes a use exactly at the deadline. Record original owner, class, source/cast ID, rank, snapshotted DC, duration and spent opportunities. Suppression preserves those facts and skips suppressed events without catch-up damage; it never rewinds costs, restores HP, adds a cast, recreates ended concentration or resets frequency history. Fixed periodic field pulses occur at creation and +6,+12,...,+54 seconds while otherwise eligible. A family once-per-target-per-numbered-round limit spans all casts/owners of that named field; outside initiative use fixed six-second world intervals and carry spent history across initiative transitions. Extra turns, entry/reentry and resuming a field do not create pulses. Single-target round riders use their specifically recorded original future regular-turn markers, preserved across initiative changes, or application+6N seconds outside initiative. Already-underway target turns are excluded from future-turn counts.

### ongoingOwnership

NEW one active field of each field identity per caster; a successful new field replaces that caster’s old field only. Existing concentration is still displaced at commitment. Once a creation pulse has committed, finish its snapshotted recipients even if its own consequences end concentration; future opportunities then end. Target-attached riders retain their exact separately stated duplication and ending rules. A magical rider ends on actual removal, expiry or recipient death/ended creature identity; a later resurrection or replacement body identity cannot inherit an ended rider. Zero HP without death remains a creature unless the actual source says otherwise. No casting-location tether is added after an attached effect or stationary field is established.

### examplePremises

All sixteen examples are authored synthetic expectations, not runtime executions. Unless stated otherwise, the caster is a single-class Mage9 with genuine spell access/repertoire, PB4, actual INT modifier+4, maximum/current RP26, Dissonance0, a different preceding spell, all real components and an available Action. The legal unmodified rank5 cast pays10RP, leaving16, gains1Dissonance and uses DC16 with Dissonance limit10. The Enchantment-mode example instead uses Wizard9, maximum/currentRP32, otherwise the same relevant modifiers and legal school row, leaving22RP. Neither resource maximum is increased for the example. A new concentration spell ends the caster’s former incompatible concentration under the existing rule.

## EV5-01 — Synaptic Static

**Original placements, unchanged**

- Enchantment rank 5: 20-ft sphere, 8d6 psychic damage and -1d6 to attacks and checks.

- Evocation rank 5: 8d6 psychic damage in 20-ft radius, -1d6 to rolls.

**Proposed procedure**

Action; casting-point range20feet; V/S/F. KEEP both source placements and8d6 psychic. NEW school-bound modes preserve rather than flatten their differences: the actual Enchantment class-access row creates a twenty-foot-diameter sphere (radius10), and penalizes attacks and ability checks; the actual Evocation row creates the explicitly stated twenty-foot-radius sphere and penalizes d20 attacks, ability checks and saving throws. Choose a perceived center within20feet with caster line of effect. Affect every creature in the applicable sphere with center line of effect, including caster/allies when geometrically present; no INT threshold, language or mind-reading qualification is added. Each creature makes one INT save. NEW success halves psychic damage and prevents this cast’s penalty; failure takes full psychic damage and independently attempts the source-owned penalty. Psychic defenses apply only to damage; a mind-effect immunity prevents the penalty only if its actual text covers this effect, not merely because its title sounds mental. Damage resolves before attachment; a dead recipient receives no rider. NEW penalty lifetime: no concentration, ending at that recipient’s third future original regular-turn END after application (outside initiative18seconds). At each of those ENDs the recipient may make an INT save to end this rider early; the current penalty applies to that save only in the Evocation mode. At the third marker expire before any redundant repeat save. No damage repeats. The Evocation phrase rolls is NEW bounded here to the three d20 resolution categories, including an actual initiative ability check and death saving throw; damage/healing dice, random tables, recharge rolls, DCs and static scores are not reduced. Enchantment never penalizes saves. For each eligible actual resolution, roll one fresh1d6 and subtract it from the final selected d20 total before determining success, margin and any normal natural-die shift. Advantage/disadvantage selects the d20 normally; replacing that d20 within the same resolution keeps the same penalty die. A genuinely new roll resolution gets a new penalty die. NEW only one Synaptic Static penalty die applies to a given roll across casters/modes; select the earliest established eligible unsuppressed rider for that roll, with stable cast-ID tie-breaking. Other riders retain their own original clocks/repeat saves and can become relevant after that one ends. An actual Bane penalty can coexist because it is a different source effect; apply each once only in its own categories. A later Synaptic Static cast resolves its own damage and may establish its own finite rider, but does not reset older endpoints. Actual suppression suspends only eligible components and keeps the endpoints; no missed save or penalty is replayed. This soft roll penalty consumes no whole-Action hard-control allowance and supplies no Charmed, Frightened, confusion, incapacitation or knowledge of thoughts.

**Explicit NEW choices:** NEW two school-bound geometries, INT full/half plus failed-save rider, three-target-round nonconcentration duration with repeat INT saves, explicit d20-category interpretation of broader Evocation wording, one family penalty die with stable duplicate arbitration and distinct Bane coexistence.

**Authored examples**

- EV5-01-A: Wizard9 uses its Enchantment placement, paying32→22RP. A foe15feet from the chosen center is outside radius10; a foe5feet away fails INT and takes a shared8d6 result29. Its next attack rolls13+5−4=14 using a fresh penalty d6=4, but its subsequent saving throw has no Enchantment penalty. The third future target END is the absolute last endpoint even if every earlier escape save fails.

- EV5-01-B: Mage9 uses the Evocation placement and legally pays26→16RP. A perceived or unperceived creature15feet from the center is in the indiscriminate radius20 area. It fails INT and later rolls a save with selected d20=14, modifier+2, Synaptic die3 and an independently active Bane die2: total11. A permitted replacement d20=18 changes that same save total to15, keeping both penalty dice; another Synaptic source does not add a second d6.

## EV5-02 — Flame Typhoon

**Original placements, unchanged**

- Evocation rank 5: 60-ft radius whirlwind, 6d10 fire, pulls enemies inward.

**Proposed procedure**

Action; casting-point range60feet; V/S/M/F; KEEP concentration up to one minute, sixty-foot radius,6d10 fire and inward pull of enemies. NEW choose a perceived point on a real supporting surface with caster line of effect. Create a fixed vertical cylinder radius60 and height30feet above that center, clipped by actual center line of effect; no relocation or following is granted. The field can extend beyond caster range after its legal center is chosen. At creation and each scheduled six-second pulse before expiry, affect all currently perceived designated hostile creatures whose occupied bodies intersect that cylinder and have center line of effect under the shared selection rule. No other creature or object receives its damage/pull. Each eligible creature makes one STR save against original DC; roll shared6d10 fire. NEW success takes half fire and no pull. Failure takes full fire and independently attempts ten feet of horizontal forced movement directly toward the cylinder’s vertical central axis, preserving elevation until real support/falling rules change it. Stop on reaching the axis or the last legal position before an obstacle, occupied destination or actual forced-movement defense. An actual anchoring rule applies; no additional size immunity is invented. An anchored creature may take damage even when the pull cannot move it. Do not pull through a wall, drag across the center, crush bodies together or add impact damage. A flying body can be pulled horizontally but is not given flight or hover; loss of real support has its ordinary consequences. Damage immunity does not cancel failed-save movement. NEW each target can receive at most one Flame Typhoon pulse’s save/damage/pull per numbered round across every owner/cast; a suppressed or otherwise ineligible field supplies no exposure. Entry, leaving/reentry, teleport arrival, extra turns or field resumption creates no additional pulse. The next genuine eligible scheduled pulse reevaluates perception, hostility intent, geometry and cover without revealing anyone’s secret affiliation. The spell itself produces no damage to structures, oxygen removal, obscurement, difficult terrain, Burning, fuel, wind-driven cargo or harvestable flames. Concentration ending, qualifying removal or expiry ends future pulses; real positions and damage already caused remain.

**Explicit NEW choices:** NEW fixed supported cylinder height30, perceived hostile selection, STR full/half with failed-save mandatory horizontal10foot pull, periodic six-second pulses and cross-owner family cap; source radius60 and6d10 remain intact.

**Authored examples**

- EV5-02-A: The center is50feet from the legal Mage. Two perceived hostile creatures are within its60foot radius and30foot height. Shared6d10 is31. One succeeds and takes15 before defenses with no pull; the other fails, resists fire to15, and is still pulled10feet toward the axis along a legal path. No creature is pulled past the central axis.

That travel creates no new pulse, and another caster’s pulse in the same round cannot bypass the family cap. At the next scheduled eligible pulse it fails STR, but a real wall stops the inward path after5feet. No collision damage or second attempt occurs; fire still uses its own defenses.

## EV5-03 — Frozen Tomb

**Original placements, unchanged**

- Evocation rank 5: Target frozen solid, CON save or paralyzed 2 rounds + 5d8 cold.

**Proposed procedure**

Action; range60feet; V/S/F. KEEP one target, CON save,5d8 cold and failed-save Paralyzed for two rounds. NEW target one perceived creature with line of effect; objects are ineligible. One CON save governs both components. Success negates damage and Paralyzed; failure deals5d8 cold and, after damage, attempts actual Paralyzed on the same continuing eligible creature. Cold resistance/immunity and Paralyzed/control defenses are independent; a cold-immune failed-save target can be paralyzed unless an actual relevant defense prevents it. Admit the rider only if the existing combined source-character/target/encounter whole-Action denial allowance is available, and spend that allowance only on actual establishment. If the allowance is unavailable or an immunity prevents the rider, the failed-save cold damage still resolves with no invented soft replacement or refund. NEW no concentration; immediate damage is instantaneous and the separately recorded magical Paralyzed rider ends at the target’s second future original regular-turn END after application, excluding an entire already-underway target turn; outside initiative it ends after12elapsed seconds. No repeat save is added. Paralyzed uses the actual reviewed condition and its real incapacitation, movement, attack and concentration consequences; merely calling the creature frozen solid does not add Core Frozen, an ice object, a container, suffocation, weight change or preservation. Actual condition cure, qualifying source removal, expiry or death ends this source-owned rider. Suppression suspends its consequences for the suppressed interval while its original endpoint runs; ending suppression never restores concentration already lost or refunds spent hard-control history. Same-source repeated hard control cannot refresh this rider through the spent allowance; a distinct eligible owner retains its own allowance and duration. No damage ticks, conjured ice, shattering multiplier or generic fire-thaw method is granted.

**Explicit NEW choices:** NEW creature-only target, successful CON negates both, two future target-END duration without concentration or repeat saves, source-qualified Paralyzed and existing hard-control admission; frozen flavor grants no second condition or prison.

**Authored examples**

- EV5-03-A: A cold-immune creature fails the legal Mage’s DC16 CON save. Its5d8 cold event is reduced to0, but it has no Paralyzed immunity and the source’s hard-control allowance is unused. No Frozen, Speed0 substitute, new denial budget or recovered10RP is supplied.
