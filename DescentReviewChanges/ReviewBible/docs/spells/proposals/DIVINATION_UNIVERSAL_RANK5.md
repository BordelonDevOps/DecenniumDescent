One bank per recipient across casters; a successful new paid application replaces the entire prior bank with exactly3and its new hour, never adds3to unused charges. The bank cannot be handed off,stolen as a physical coin,sold,refilled by rest/suppression or copied into another balance. Death,eligible removal,expiry or spending the last charge ends it. Suppression blocks spending while preserving remaining charges and original expiry.

**Explicit NEW choices:** NEW willing60-foot recipient,three-charge one-hour bank,real Reaction spending,ordinary Success semantics and bounded third fixed-DC task mode. Hit negation is preserved even for critical hits; no fate-reroll or completed-outcome rollback is substituted.

## DI5-04 — Woven Threads

**Original placements, unchanged**

- Divination rank 5: Alter a creature's recent memory (last 1 minute, WIS save resists).

**Proposed procedure**

KEEP Action,60feet,one perceived creature,Divination,WIS resistance and alteration of the LAST ONE MINUTE. NEW at commitment select one real elapsed interval wholly within the60seconds immediately before resolution, and choose Erase or Replace. No access to unknown original memories is granted. Erase removes episodic recall of that interval or one clearly described event within it, without deleting general skills,identity,language,class knowledge or older memories. Replace supplies one coherent first-person sensory narrative of at most60ordinary spoken words, covering no more than that same interval, using only ideas the caster actually knows. It may depict events that did not occur, but cannot implant a mechanically usable formula,ability,repertoire,trained proficiency or factual omniscience. No hearing/shared language is required for simple sensory content; semantic content beyond the recipient's actual concepts/languages is not translated into new knowledge. The target makes WIS; success leaves memory unchanged, failure applies the chosen edit if the target has an actual episodic-memory capacity and the selected experience exists. Lack of an experience,an actual memory protection or an ineligible target gives no readout or substitute earlier interval. An unconscious period with no experienced memory is not a blank training slot. The60second window refers to actual event time, not the date claimed inside a memory; editing a present recollection does not permit rewriting the much older underlying event or recursively extending the window. Keep the original source-event/memory provenance in the Director record. NEW the alteration is an instantaneous lasting change of accessible recollection, not an ongoing spell effect; ordinary Dispel/suppression afterward does not roll it back. No fixed expiry or concentration is added. It changes neither actual world events,physical evidence,other people's memories,previous consent/contracts,spent resources,injuries,conditions,existing commands nor action/cast history. Unlike reviewed Disrupt Memory, this source does not independently end concentration. The affected creature remains free to interpret the edited recollection,question it and choose behavior; it is not Charmed,compelled loyalty or an authority to decide a player's future actions. NEW recovery: a genuinely applicable effect expressly restoring altered memories can restore the preserved original episode. Alternatively, after encountering specific credible independent evidence materially contradicting the alteration or proving the erased event, the recipient can spend10uninterrupted minutes considering it and make WIS/Insight vs original spellDC. Success/Critical Success identifies the alteration and restores the actual originally experienced episode; failure supplies no false certainty, and another attempt needs materially new evidence or method. This supplies no general perfect recollection of facts never perceived. Mere retelling of the same unsupported assertion is not infinite unchanged retries. If the application left a memory gap, ordinary noticing of that gap is possible but does not itself identify the caster. No automatic announcement of a successful hidden edit is added; witnessing actual casting remains ordinary evidence. For overlapping edits, maintain one history per original event interval: a later successful edit changes the currently accessible layer only within its actual new60second window, preserving true underlying history and every earlier provenance. Recovery of that event removes present Woven Threads alterations over the recovered portion and restores the real original, not an adversary's already fabricated older layer. Different untouched portions stay as recorded. Recasts create no extra minutes,charges or hidden diagnostic, and actual death/revival alone does not recreate unedited memory or delete this retained identity history.

**Explicit NEW choices:** NEW finite Erase/Replace modes and60-word narrative,real-event-time window,instantaneous persistent recollection with protected original history,ten-minute evidence-based recovery and precise overlapping-edit restoration. No independent concentration ending or retroactive consent.
