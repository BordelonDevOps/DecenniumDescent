## Shared proposed rules

### status

UNADOPTED AND UNSENT. Eight complete proposed mechanical procedures and sixteen authored examples. Every unsupported completion is explicitly NEW. No source card, ordinary spell review status, class access, runtime, balance or cosmology approval changes.

### casting

All eight retain Action and V/S/F. Their actual four ordinary class placements are rank 4 with base 7 RP, but repertoire and minimum class level remain independently required. Use the actual class engine, hand/focus/voice requirements, current RP and Dissonance surcharge, paid costs, cast-begin cadence and interrupt window. No legal observable target/placement means no commitment; hidden ineligibility after commitment is no refund or free diagnosis. A legal concentration cast ends incompatible old concentration at commitment even if countered. Spell attacks use d20 + PB + actual casting modifier; saves use DC 8 + PB + actual casting modifier. Saves are binary with no natural-die degree shift. No generic upcast, scaling, free Action or newly acquired source is supplied.

### geometry

NEW precise defaults for this group: declare fixed dimensions, orientation, origin and perceived placement before resolution. Every creature whose occupied space intersects the stated area and has the stated area-origin line of effect is eligible, including allies/caster unless the particular origin rule excludes them. A placement needs caster line of effect to its chosen point. Total barriers clip the affected region rather than being silently traversed. Snapshot each simultaneous area event before damage, including its actual cover and recipients; destroyed cover does not create another wave from that event. Future genuine pulses take new snapshots. No general selective ally exclusion, water/metal chain or structural damage follows from a damage type.

### damage

KEEP the reviewed pipeline. Roll each specified raw area expression once for that simultaneous event, not separately per recipient. Apply each recipient’s own save, typed defenses and actual prevention/sharing independently. A half-save floors each typed component before its own defenses. A mixed-type simultaneous hit remains one damage event with typed subamounts, one root for that original recipient and no artificial extra concentration/Wound triggers; genuinely later or separately triggered packets stay separate. Attack criticals double only the actual attack dice; a save or environmental packet cannot critically hit. Zero damage does not by itself erase an independent condition or future rider. Apply only source-qualified class hooks at their real hit/damage/cast stage and frequency.

### newClocks

NEW for this group: a stated one-minute field ends at an absolute deadline sixty elapsed seconds after completed resolution. At that deadline expire it before a same-time field pulse, voluntary use or entry; hence an immediate event plus future six-second pulses at +6 through +54 has ten opportunities, not eleven. Preserve that deadline through initiative entry, exit, reorder, extra turns, absence and suppression. A pulse schedule is source-owned elapsed time and does not require a creature turn, invent companion START/END events or grant Actions. A saved or fully prevented eligible damage exposure still spends its applicable exposure allowance. A suppressed scheduled pulse is lost, never queued. A removed, expired or countered field produces no later pulse.

### newRoundExposure

NEW spell-specific anti-duplication: where a card says family once per round, keep an actual target/spell-identity/category ledger across all owners, cast IDs and equivalent bodies. Use the current numbered combat round; outside initiative use the scene’s continuing six-second cycle established before the first relevant exposure. Crossing timing modes maps the still-open cycle to the current round and preserves its already spent entries until that cycle/round ends; do not reset at a cast, reentry, resurrection of a record ID, suppression or initiative transition. Simultaneous competing eligible exposures use original application order then stable source ID. Distinct spells and explicitly distinct categories do not silently merge. This is not a universal rewrite of other spells’ periodic rules.

### newEndBridge

NEW only for Wall of Fire’s nearby END check: use an actual creature regular-turn END, including a current turn, subject to its family round cap. An owner-attached companion instead uses its real original-owner regular attached-phase end. If no real turn/phase exists, use an END-only checkpoint every complete six seconds without granting actions, ordinary START/END benefits or recharge. On transition map the next unresolved checkpoint to the next actual regular END/attached-phase END, preserving completed checks, round history and the original absolute deadline. Extra turns add none.

### newGroundFireStartBridge

NEW for Inferno Wave’s physical ground-fire hazard only: a real creature regular START checks actual ground contact. For an owner-attached companion, use the beginning of its original owner’s regular attached phase after that phase’s required processing, without creating ordinary START benefits. With no real turn/phase, use one contact check per completed six seconds, beginning six seconds after ignition. Map the next unresolved check on transition, retaining prior exposures and every actual patch endpoint. Extra turns add no scheduled contact checks. Independently actual entry can qualify under the same target/family/round limit. These environmental packets are not fresh spells, casts or spell attacks; apply only actual source hooks that cover their real cause.

### persistence

Each field/rider carries actual owner, originating class/rank, cast ID, snapshotted save DC, geometry/recipient, event history and expiry. A dispel or suppression must qualify against that exact source. Suppression does not restart it, refund costs, repeat creation or restore lost concentration. New concentration obeys the existing one-concentration rule, not an invented per-spell allowance. Objects, corpses, ended summons and replacements are not interchangeable recipients. Creature-only damage grants no collateral attack on every carried item. Fire/cold/acid imagery alone supplies no harvestable substance, light level, condition, cooking/purification permission or magical repair.

## Decisions for joint review

- Inferno Wave retains real ground ignition but adds a finite small-fire profile. Confirm its sixty-second maximum, no automatic spread, contact timing, real-fuel prerequisites and separation from creature Burning without replacing a larger independently established environmental fire.

- Orb of Annihilation adds a narrow legal-object mode and explicit object-save bridge. The name grants no disintegration or artifact deletion; object profiles remain actual use prerequisites.

- Freezing Rain and Storm Sphere replace contradictory Instantaneous metadata with explicit concentration fields. Their source-owned six-second pulses, initial exposure and cross-cast family caps need exact review, including timing-mode transitions.

- Freezing Rain’s slippery ground adds difficult-terrain cost plus a bounded movement-triggered DEX/Prone check. This terrain is not an elemental Speed-control component for Balanced Elements.

- Voltaic Detonation proposes separate DEX and CON saves, a ten-foot sphere and the existing hard-denial guard. Its one-turn endpoint and attached-companion mapping are explicit; lightning immunity does not automatically block Stunned.

- Ice Storm keeps both full 4d6 components in one typed event and adds a finite nonconcentration magical terrain residual. Shared rolls and one event per recipient must remain distinct from separately typed defenses.

- Wall of Fire preserves both printed 5d8 clauses as two possible real packets per round, with separate crossing/nearby family caps, two-sided heat, a DEX save each time and no opaque/physical wall. This potential throughput needs balance discussion, not an assertion of balance.

- Vitriolic Sphere preserves the delayed 5d4 on both initial save outcomes using the same full/half factor. Compare explicitly with Acid Arrow’s different attack and delayed-damage procedure rather than importing an external familiar version.

- Storm Sphere’s lightning needs the caster’s actual Bonus Action, one per numbered round, with a ranged spell attack and center-based cover. Its ordinary spell is separate from same-named class techniques and companion commands.

- All eight keep original source dice, class ranks and repertoire gates. Existing lower-rank unusual ranges and approved procedures remain unchanged; these proposed choices await actual GPT discussion and separate exact review.

## EV4-01 — Inferno Wave

**Original placements, unchanged**

- Evocation rank 4: 60-ft line, 6d6 fire and ignites ground.

**Proposed procedure**

Action; V/S/F; instantaneous wave, no concentration. KEEP 60-foot line and 6d6 fire. NEW: a straight line from the caster, five feet wide and five feet high, in a declared direction; being its origin alone does not include the caster. Stop at a total solid obstruction. Snapshot all eligible creatures, each making DEX for full 6d6 fire on failure or half on success, with one shared raw roll. The wave itself has no general object-damage packet. KEEP its distinct ground-ignition clause: after the snapshot, ignite genuinely exposed combustible ground/floor material intersecting this footprint, including dry surface vegetation or an actually burnable wooden surface. Bare stone, water, saturated ground and unsupported air supply no fuel. NEW finite small-fire completion: divide actual ignited footprint into at most twelve five-foot patches, each clipped to the line and its available fuel. Before applying a patch, record the real combustible substrate, the finite amount that actually ignites, its physical burn endpoint no later than sixty seconds after ignition, and actual extinguishing method. If available fuel burns out sooner, use that earlier endpoint; no invented fuel mass, structural HP loss or total floor collapse follows from the spell. Each patch is an ordinary physical flame, not a continuing spell or Core Burning applied to a creature. It remains until fuel/endpoint or real extinguishing, independently of caster concentration, dispel or suppression of other magic. NEW hazard: a creature in contact with its burning surface takes 1d6 fire without a save on first actual entry or its regular START there, at most once per Inferno-ground-fire family/target/round across all patches and owners. A creature eligible for the initial wave counts as exposed to this family for that same round even if its save/defenses prevent damage; it takes no second immediate ground packet. No creation pulse, airborne heat damage or extra-turn START is added. A wholly suppressed original cast creates neither wave nor ignition; suppression after actual ignition cannot unburn material. Real dousing or complete smothering can end a contacted patch; one adjacent creature may spend an Action using actual sufficient water, sand or a usable nonburning cover to extinguish one patch, with no roll and no fabricated equipment. A source effect such as Tidal Wave can extinguish the ordinary flames it genuinely contacts. NEW conservative fire boundary: these patches do not automatically spread or persist past their recorded endpoint. Continued/spreading environmental fire requires a separately established ordinary hazard and remaining real fuel, never a fresh spell packet or free manufactured stock. Reignition is possible only for actual remaining unburned material and never resets a current round exposure.

**Explicit NEW choices:** NEW five-by-five-foot cross-section, DEX half, bounded actual-fuel ground patches, sixty-second maximum small-fire endpoints, 1d6 contact hazard and explicit extinguishing. Ground ignition is retained; Burning, structural destruction and fuel creation are not inferred.

**Authored examples**

- EV4-01-A: The line rolls 23 fire. A failed creature takes 23 before defenses; a successful one takes 11. Both were initial eligible recipients and cannot take a second ground-fire packet in that same round. Dry grass in the first two five-foot patches actually ignites; the stone next to it does not.

- EV4-01-B: A recorded patch has real fuel lasting 18 seconds. A new creature entering at second 7 takes the shared current entry event’s 1d6, rolled 4; leaving and entering another Inferno patch in the same round adds nothing. Actual dousing at second 10 ends that patch; no future START packet occurs and no Burning cure is needed.

## EV4-02 — Orb of Annihilation

**Original placements, unchanged**

- Evocation rank 4: Single target, 8d8 force; DEX save halves.

**Proposed procedure**

Action; range 60 feet; V/S/F; instantaneous, no concentration. KEEP exactly one target, 8d8 force and DEX half. NEW eligibility: one perceived creature or independently legal object with line of effect. A direct object must be unattended, held by you or deliberately offered by a willing holder; this grants no hostile worn/carried inventory targeting. Choose the actual object body, not an arbitrary structural collection. Resolve creature DEX normally. NEW object bridge: if an actual object profile explicitly supplies its own DEX save, use it; otherwise an ordinary inanimate object cannot evade and fails this save without inventing an ability score, proficiency or a bearer’s substitute save. Use actual object durability and material defenses; absent a needed object profile, resolve its legitimate statistics before using it as a mechanical target rather than assigning free destruction. A legally committed cast whose target is no longer eligible at resolution fizzles with no retarget, splash or last-location impact. Roll 8d8 once: failure takes full force, success half floored, then actual defenses. This is a saving-throw damage event, not a spell attack. The title supplies no disintegration, unhealable HP loss, automatic killing, object erasure, antimagic, persistent orb, exceptional artifact permission or special zero-HP threshold. Apply the ordinary creature/object zero-HP outcome once. Destruction of cover or a recipient does not pass damage through to a second target. Separate real paid casts are separate instantaneous events, with no stored orb or ongoing rider.

**Explicit NEW choices:** NEW explicit single creature/legal-object eligibility, a source-profile-or-automatic-failure object DEX interface, and no title-derived annihilation mechanics. Printed 8d8 and DEX half remain.

**Authored examples**

- EV4-02-A: A creature succeeds against raw force 37 and takes floor(37/2)=18 before force resistance, which then gives 9. No attack critical or later orb is created.

- EV4-02-B: An unattended ordinary chest has a real HP/material profile but no DEX-save profile, so it fails and receives the one 37-force event under those defenses. Destroying it does not also strike the creature behind it or erase an artifact inside.

## EV4-03 — Freezing Rain

**Original placements, unchanged**

- Evocation rank 4: 30-ft radius, 4d6 cold/round, slippery terrain.

**Proposed procedure**

Action; V/S/F. KEEP the casting-point range of 30 feet separately from the 30-foot radius and the 4d6 cold per round. NEW replace the contradictory Instantaneous duration with concentration up to sixty elapsed seconds. Choose a perceived point within range with line of effect; create a fixed vertical cylinder thirty feet in radius and twenty feet high, centered vertically on that point. Its volume and affected surfaces are clipped by actual solid cover from the center; rain does not cross a roof or sealed floor by name alone. All legal creatures in that volume make CON at creation, then at each source six-second pulse before expiry. Roll shared 4d6 cold for each pulse: full on failure, half on success. NEW family cap: initial and periodic cold together affect a target at most once per Freezing Rain identity per round across owners/casts. Each pulse takes a fresh geometry/cover snapshot. Entry, forced movement, reentry, extra turns or suppression ending adds no cold tick; entering can expose a creature at the next genuine pulse. Damage is independent of the slippery ground. NEW slippery surface: actual exposed walkable surfaces inside the active cylinder cost double ground movement, using ordinary difficult-terrain arbitration without multiplying an existing double cost. At the first voluntary ground-movement expenditure on a covered surface in a round, after the first up-to-five-feet segment, make DEX against the first encountered active rain’s recorded DC; failure applies Prone if the creature can receive it. This slip check is family once/target/round, shared across casts, walking and attempts to stand then move. Already Prone creatures spend the same opportunity but receive no additional condition; ordinary crawling and standing costs remain. Standing alone does not trigger it, and forced movement, falling, flight and teleportation do not themselves spend ground Speed or force a slip save. A failed creature still chooses how to use remaining legal movement/actions. The rain supplies no Frozen, restrained body, attack penalty or magical Speed reduction; terrain therefore is not a Balanced Elements slow. Suppression removes only that source’s active cold/terrain effects while original clocks and spent history continue. At ending, all its magical rain and slipperiness vanish, with no residual ice, drinking water, repair material or harvestable stock. Existing mundane weather and unrelated terrain remain.

**Explicit NEW choices:** NEW concentration sixty seconds instead of source Instantaneous; twenty-foot cylinder height; CON half at creation and six-second pulses; cross-cast damage and slip caps; difficult ground plus a bounded DEX/Prone movement procedure. No automatic Frozen or physical water supply.

**Authored examples**

- EV4-03-A: A field completes at t=0 and expires at t=60. Its source pulse opportunities are 0,6,12,...,54, ten total. A creature entering at t=5 first faces cold at t=6, not an entry packet; raw 15 with a successful CON save becomes 7 before defenses. If the t=12 pulse is suppressed, it never replays at t=13.

- EV4-03-B: A walker spends 10 feet of movement for its first five-foot slippery segment, then fails DEX and becomes Prone. It can use the ordinary standing/crawling procedure with remaining budgets, but standing and walking again that round cannot force a second slip check. A flying creature crossing above the actual surface can still be in the cold volume, but does not pay ground terrain cost.
