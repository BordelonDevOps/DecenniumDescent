### casting

KEEP each actual rank-five class/school/repertoire gate, base10RP, Action and V/S/F. Actual casting-point ranges: Lesser Antimagic10feet, Greater Dispel30feet, Massive Elemental Blast20feet, all others60feet. No generic upcast or acquired spell access. Determine real components, hand/voice/focus, actual available Action, RP, Dissonance surcharge and exact source costs at preflight. Commit a legal cast and its original concentration transition before countering, actual outcomes and hidden eligibility; costs and cadence are not refunded. DC=8+actual PB+actual casting modifier, spell attack=PB+actual casting modifier. A cast reduced to0RP is still its actual rank and has its normal Action, Dissonance/Refrain, components and other costs.

### geometryAndChoice

Choose actual targets, center, orientation, profile, faction, damage type and conversion amount before outcomes. Named targets/centers require caster perception and line of effect; areas require center line of effect to affected portions. A hostile-selection spell includes every currently perceived creature affirmatively regarded as an enemy from available information, without detecting hidden allegiance or allowing post-save omission. Indiscriminate areas include allies and other casters. Record one finite resolution snapshot; ending a barrier does not retroactively enlarge it. Invisible does not itself mean without a physical barrier, and suppressed does not mean ended.

### clocks

NEW minute effects use60actual elapsed seconds, including in initiative, with expiry first at the exact deadline. An hour is3600seconds and24hours86400. Preserve original regular-turn/round markers for explicit pulses and exclusions; extra turns do not supply them. Outside initiative use actual six-second virtual cycles only for those per-round opportunities, carrying spent history into combat. Suppression preserves all clocks, costs, source IDs, spent grants, capacities and causal history; skip unavailable pulses without banking. Source-specific ending and resume procedures remain authoritative. Later genuinely paid casts have new application IDs; stale cleanup cannot end a replacement.

### sourceScopes

Spell-origin means an actual original spell cast, retaining its class-qualified generating rank and causal provenance through a continuing rider or explicitly permitted copy. A name, magical-looking technique, RP payment, type or lore adjective alone is not proof. Item-cast spells remain spells; charge-only prices do not become RP prices. A class engine, innate nonmagical trait, ordinary item, actual permanent change of matter or completed healing/movement is not an ongoing spell merely because magic once caused it. An explicitly magical nonspell component has its own source-defined suppression/dispel permissions; do not erase the whole class/identity. Exact source exceptions and protected components are checked individually. Copies do not acquire new rank, components, independent charges, resource credit or extra companion budgets.

### companionPhase

Created companions use the reviewed original-owner attached phase after that owner's whole turn. One global BA command and one offensive companion Action per original owner/numbered round apply across classes, devices and summons. NEW these two profiles receive a simple mental command only while perceived by their conscious owner within60feet and line of effect; no shared sight, unknown language, remote report or hidden hostility detector. A command selects one body, ordinary movement and one printed attack or physically possible basic Action. Without a command, capable bodies can only move defensively and Dodge. No independent turn, Reaction, BA, spellcasting, class feature, tools, labor authority, summon power or resources are granted. An attack/control Action spends the global offense; support still requires the command. The Greater Fire Pet's expressly printed passive aura is the narrow additional exception stated on its card, not another offensive Action or permission for other companions to attack. Original owner supplies current spell attack and PB saves; new controller does not donate another set of statistics/caps. Printed HP is base before genuinely source-qualified creating-spell modifiers; later maximum growth does not heal.

### summonEndings

These are finite created bodies, not transported named individuals or ordinary resource-bearing corpses. At0HP, actual ending/removal, expiry, concentration loss or owner's voluntary dismissal during their own turn without an Action, disappear with intrinsic manifested gear. Real externally supplied items remain the same inventory and drop at the actual last position using legal local placement; no corpse, free weapon, fuel, donor trait, XP-farm resource or refreshed item is produced. Suppression makes the created body unavailable and drops real carried gear while keeping its currentHP, source clock and last location. Resume only there if legal, otherwise nearest legal adjacent same-region placement or a pending conflict; no remote respawn/new HP. Copies retain owner-wide caps and resource exclusions. No plane, soul, afterlife or cosmological origin is assigned.

### damage

Use current typed defenses, pure prevention, damage transfer, tempHP, HP floors, actual Wounds/death/concentration and source callbacks. A shared damage roll creates separate actual recipient packets. Half-save damage rounds down before an independent resistance half. Positive resolved damage and actualDamageDealt are distinct: actualDamageDealt=temp absorbed+ordinary HP lost, excluding overkill, state assignment and duplicate parent/child branches. This packet creates no reaction refund, RP regeneration, healing or harvesting from blocked/absorbed damage.

### pricing

NEW Nexus is a distinct field-price stage after ordinary rank cost, mandatory RP extras, Dissonance and genuine source-qualified class/item discounts with their own floors, but before reviewed final Resonance Influence Ease/Burden. Nexus subtracts exactly2from the legally discountable RP-priced portion, minimum0, once per real cast. Separately protected conversion/transfer principal remains actually paid and undiscounted when its exact source expressly requires that; identify it before arithmetic, never hide an ordinary mandatory surcharge in that bucket. The new Barrier adopts such protected X by explicit proposal. This bridge must be reviewed with the existing Harmonic Bulwark X rule and final Influence wording; it is a NEW clarification for protected conversion principal, not silent alteration of the reviewed Resonance Shield mandatory1RP, which remains normally discountable by an eligible final price effect. No discount credits recovered RP or reduces an HP/material/charge cost, Dissonance gain, rank or later nonspell transfer.

## AB5-01 — Antimagic Field (Lesser)

**Original placements, unchanged**

- Abjuration rank 5: 10-ft sphere suppresses magic effects and spells.

**Proposed procedure**

KEEP Action,range10feet and a10-foot sphere suppressing magical effects/spells. NEW10feet is RADIUS, centered on one perceived fixed point within10feet; it does not follow a creature. Concentration up to60seconds explicitly replaces Instantaneous. Its volume is limited by actual solid total-cover barriers from the center; it does not burrow through a closed wall. It has no physical boundary, damage, pressure, air removal or movement restriction. No rank ceiling is proposed: actual eligible spells of any rank and explicitly magical components are suppressed within its covered volume, subject only to genuine source-specific immunity. An artifact, divine title or class name receives no invented exception; an actual written protected-source exception keeps its scope. NEW each instance of this same Lesser Antimagic field is exempt from suppression by this same family, so overlapping fields form the union of their active volumes rather than an oscillating mutual shutdown. This exemption permits maintaining an existing field, not creating new magic from within another already-active field. Independent effects that specifically suppress this field apply by their own rules. Concentration maintenance itself remains possible inside; field creation does not cancel remote already-established effects merely because their concentrating caster entered. It suppresses the actual local expression/affected component, not a distant source solely through caster ownership. A creature-attached magical benefit/condition or discrete magical item is wholly suppressed while any of that body/item's actual occupied volume intersects the covered sphere; a large freestanding area/barrier is suppressed only in its actual intersection. A merely overlapping target space does not suppress unrelated creatures elsewhere. Created summons use their source's suppression placement/history rules; real animated bodies become inert only if their actual animation source says so. Magic items retain their mundane body/profile and inventory while their actual magical enhancements/actions are unavailable; intrinsic mundane functions do not vanish. If no ordinary fallback profile exists, do not fabricate one as an extra weapon/armor entitlement. Nonmagical class abilities, learned facts, RP maxima/records, physical injuries, completed ordinary material creation/transmutation, finished teleportation, restoredHP and consumed items are not rolled back. A continuing spell curse is suppressed only in its magical components; a persistent mundane disease or intrinsic nonmagical trait is not automatically cured. A present magical tempHP/buffer grant is unavailable while suppressed but retains its remaining balance and original ending; no damage then absorbed from it, no later refund of HP loss. The ordinary single-grant/strongest-buffer records still apply to another actual accepted grant, with no second hidden usable bank. Suppressing a changed body uses the reviewed body/root/meld/placement rules; it does not clone gear or close-and-recapture its meld set. A legal spell cast may still be committed inside if its ordinary nonmagical casting permissions/components/resources exist: pay full applicable costs and cadence, but suppress its payload before application. Suppressed focus enhancements do not destroy a mundane casting focus. An instantaneous component originating inside, or whose required actual source-to-target/initial-origin path crosses the covered volume, cannot resume that same component beyond the field. A caster outside cannot choose an area center beyond it to bypass an intercepted originating path. Once a persistent source was legally established outside, its later actual origin and local intersection govern: suppress only intersecting portions without retroactively countering the whole old cast. No free list of what failed/which ranks or secret curses is supplied. Leaving/ending suppression restores only still-live permitted expressions, originalHP/buff balances and clocks; missed damage/healing/orders/saves that were unavailable are not queued. Any source that still permits an ending save while suppressed retains it. Ending the field does not refill charges, renew expiries, refund prices or recall dropped gear.

**Explicit NEW choices:** NEW fixed10-foot-radius concentration minute, actual-source local suppression without rank cap, same-family self/overlap exemption, explicit cast/cross-boundary pricing and body/item/buffer history rules. Instantaneous consequences stay real.

**Authored examples**

- AB5-01-A: A caster inside the active sphere commits an otherwise legal rank-five spell, spending10RP and its ordinary cadence. Its payload is suppressed and receives no later delayed resolution on leaving. An already-established remote buff outside the sphere is not canceled merely because its concentrating caster entered it.

- AB5-01-B: A body-attached magical buffer has12remaining when the bearer enters. It cannot absorb a new8damage packet inside; leaving within its original lifetime restores the same12remaining, not20and not an8HP refund. Ordinary stone already created by an instantaneous material spell remains real, and two Lesser Antimagic fields do not switch each other on and off indefinitely.

**Proposed procedure**

KEEP Action,casting-point range30feet,30-foot RADIUS and ending multiple magical effects. NEW choose one perceived center within30feet. At resolution capture every ongoing independently addressable spell-effect application with some actual affected body, discrete effect/anchor or freestanding area portion inside the30-foot sphere and center line of effect, allies included. No numerical cap and no selective omission after hidden results; individual effect perception is not required for this area purge, a NEW distinction from the reviewed single-target lower dispels. Hidden actual source data determines eligibility without identifying magic to the caster. Exclude instantaneous completed consequences, innate nonmagical traits, class engines as a whole, permanent magic-item enchantments/body, and expressly nondispellable effects. A nonspell magical component is eligible only when its own actual source explicitly supplies a dispel interface/rank or DC; no guessed rank0 is assigned. Unknown actual rank without a separate supplied dispel interface is not guessed into eligibility. Same-cast multi-recipient records are individually captured: ending a buff on one recipient does not automatically remove unselected remote recipients or the caster's concentration unless the source's real parent dependency requires it. ## AB5-04 — Spell Resistance

**Original placements, unchanged**

- Abjuration rank 5: Target gains advantage on saves against spells for 1 hour.

**Proposed procedure**

KEEP Action,one target60feet,one HOUR and advantage on saves against spells. NEW target is one willing perceived creature, including self, with caster line of effect at resolution; no concentration is added.
