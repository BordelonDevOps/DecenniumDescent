## Shared procedures

### casting

All five retain Action, 60 feet and base13RP. Divination access remains Cleric/Wizard11 and Mirrorsage/Shaman16; Chrono Lock additionally retains Bard16 and Mage11 Universal access. Use the actual acquired class grant, current repertoire, casting ability, components and cadence. V/S/F applies except Eye of Judgement V/S/M-or-F, with its unpriced focus alternative and no invented consumed component. DC=8+actual PB+actual source ability. Pay legal costs, concentration transition and cast-start hooks before Counterspell/hidden resolution. Countering, a save or a blocked reading refunds nothing. No automatic upcast, additional source access or borrowed Oracle RP is supplied.

### identityAndTime

One application retains its exact caster, recipient, school/rank, components, admission history and original endpoint. The two explicit one-round procedures below use target first future regular END, excluding an in-progress target turn; this is an explicit unified choice also for willing recipients. Preserve that virtual marker through absence/initiative changes, with end events before expiry cleanup. Outside initiative use application+6seconds and retain that deadline on entry. Extra turns never advance these clocks. Elapsed maxima start at resolution; suppression never resets them or banks missed events. No temporal rewind or full Time Loop implementation is claimed.

### information

Actual established hidden but unprotected facts are legitimate information within each spell's stated scope. Missing facts are not invented. Apply real information defenses to the actual subject, field, generating school/rank and event. A field-specific block does not erase unrelated readable information. Indeterminate does not identify the hidden cause; actual defenses that specify a different non-actionable result retain it. Equivalent inquiries under materially unchanged facts share a stable answer across wording/casters; materially different relevant facts or questions can support a new paid inquiry. No extra RP, Reaction, command, consent, credential or future choice follows from learning an answer.

### sourcesAndLimits

Reviewed ordinary control, life, capacity, body/equipment, resource and EX58 work/rest rules remain governing. Suppression is not deletion, payment reversal, restoration, ordinary rest or a new effect-creation event. Shared hard-control history remains source-character/target/encounter, not spell-name-specific. Source-defined immunity can prevent a component; a generic spell title supplies no immunity or classification. Actual faction identity/standing remains distinct from membership and authority. Comparison proposals are full retained drafts, not approvals.

### examples

Unless stated otherwise, eligible Wizard11 has INT+4, PB+4, DC16, spell attack+8, RP40/40, Dissonance0 and limit10. A fresh rank-six cast costs13 and leaves27 before any other actual event. Mage11 has RP maximum32. Authored examples are table adjudications, not executed spell-runtime or human-balance tests.

No service authority, creature origin, portal, soul, pantheon or cosmological interpretation is supplied before the authorized push and verified remote commit.

## DI6-02 — Chrono Lock

**Original question:** Preserve BOTH placements: Divination freezes ONE creature in time for1ROUND with NO SAVE; Universal locks time for a target with NO ACTIONS OR DAMAGE for1ROUND. Both Core entries retain Action/60ft/1round. Resolve school-specific differences explicitly rather than deleting no-save or no-damage clauses. Define actual target,precise ending event,concentration,Actions/Bonus Actions/Reactions/movement,ongoing effects and incoming damage while frozen. Bind temporal stasis to existing source/target/encounter hard-control admission and temporal control/parent/kernel limits,including already-active clocks,extra turns,suppression,resumption,death and objects held by the creature. No refund of paid costs,duplicate ticks,past-event erasure or unlimited deferred damage. Any NEW target exceptions or shared rule changes require explicit joint review; an incomplete temporal runtime is not proof of spell approval.

KEEP both actual placements: Divination freezes exactly one creature in time for one round with no save; Universal grants no Actions or damage for one round. NEW harmonize them into the following full local suspension, with no save in either mode and damage protection in both. This expressly extends the Divination wording with the Universal clause; the original two entries remain distinct evidence and the selected class still determines the generating school. This is not a selectable bargain between a vulnerable and an invulnerable mode. One perceived living or otherwise active creature within60feet/line of effect, including self; an inanimate corpse or detached object is not a creature target. NEW no concentration, lasting to the target's first future regular END, or the original outside-initiative six-second endpoint. It is not ordinary Frozen, Petrified, Stunned or Incapacitated.

Apply the shared source-character/target/encounter hard-denial admission even when the recipient is willing, an explicit NEW conservation choice. Actual immunity to temporal suspension or equivalent whole-Action denial prevents admission. No save is silently introduced to replace the source's express no-save clause. A used allowance or actual protection produces no suspension or protective fallback and no delayed attempt; legal committed casting costs remain spent. Spend the allowance only on actual establishment. Neither ending nor suppression refunds that history. A target already under operative full bodily suspension cannot receive another bodily application through it; a genuinely suppressed old suspension may allow a different source's legal attempt, without extending either original endpoint.

While operative, the target is unaware and cannot speak, act, take Bonus Actions/Reactions, make voluntary choices or move. Hold its actual body and actually worn/carried inanimate gear at the current place and pose. Voluntary/forced relocation, ordinary teleportation and gravity cannot move that held body; an actual explicit source overcoming temporal suspension retains only its printed exception. Carried or mounted other creatures are not frozen by association: ordinary custody/support and separation rules determine their legal positions, and the target cannot carry them away because it cannot move. Gear cannot be detached, used or looted through the held body. Do not infer automatic STR/DEX failure, attack advantage, adjacent criticals or prone from a different condition's name.

The suspended body and its held gear receive no new damage, healing, temporaryHP, condition, directHP loss or physical alteration. Otherwise legal attempts pay their real costs and resolve no such payload; incoming damage is prevented, never accumulated for release. No resource can be paid through the body's unavailable voluntary action. Existing periodic bodily outputs due while held are skipped, not banked; death saves create neither successes nor failures. Age, immediate respiration and metabolism pause for the interval, but no food, water, completed work or rest credit is supplied. World elapsed time, actual regular markers, rest/shortage calendars, recharge history and existing effect deadlines continue. A due lethal world-track transition is recorded under its real source; suspension does not erase or defer that source's explicitly world-timed death rule. An effect that ends while held stays ended.

Existing concentration is not ended solely by this distinct non-Incapacitated state. Its ordinary expiry, actual unpaid voluntary upkeep, dependent source ending or genuine independent concentration-ending rule still applies. Already independent external spell fields, completed projectiles and other bodies keep their own legal behavior and do not become frozen. NEW body-emitted class/spell aura output and voluntary command/control operation are unavailable while the source body is held; the underlying source clock continues, and missed outputs/orders do not replay. A pending paid activation requiring further personal action cannot complete that requirement; a fully committed independent effect may still resolve under its own actual dependency rules. No cost is refunded.

The Chrono Lock application itself remains reachable for actual eligible rank-six dispel, suppression or specific removal, despite the body's payload protection. NEW the originating caster may dismiss its own application without an Action during its actual turn only while able to act; a self-locked caster cannot use dismissal while suspended. Ending or suppression restores ordinary interaction at the current real location, with no backlog, free turn, fresh movement or restored Action. Only actual unspent permissions of a still-current turn remain. When suppression ceases before the original deadline, the same live application resumes around its surviving eligible creature at its then-current position; it cannot snap back to its old position or restart the round. Death/removal of that creature ends its application. Unsupported height after suspension uses the real Fall Marker; physical gear, broken support and real elapsed consequences remain. This finite state supplies no world rewind, snapshot restoration or implementation approval for the temporal kernel.

**New decisions:** NEW shared no-save/no-damage suspension for both schools, nonconcentration target-next-end clock, willing-recipient hard-control admission, full body/gear boundary and explicit suppression/return procedure.

Success negates both revelation and suppression. Failure establishes the independently scoped reading and class-feature suppression below until the target's first future regular END, or the original six-second outside endpoint; no concentration and no repeat save. Classless creatures can be targeted, but no class features are manufactured for them.

READING: on the initial failed save, privately show the caster the actual currently acquired class-feature identities and their complete operative mechanical descriptions, including applicable Base/Arch/Hero/Mystery/Oath selections and genuine passive/active modes.
