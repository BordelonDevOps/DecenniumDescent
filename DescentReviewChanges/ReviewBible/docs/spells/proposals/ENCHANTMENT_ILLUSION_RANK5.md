## Shared proposals

### status

UNADOPTED AND UNSENT. Fourteen complete local proposals and twenty-eight authored examples await joint discussion and exact review. NEW marks an authoring choice, not recovered source wording. No ordinary catalog, class access, public selection, runtime, human balance or cosmology approval changes.

### casting

KEEP each actual rank-five/base10RP class-access row, minimum class level, repertoire and casting ability. All use V/S/F, Action and60-foot range except Mental Bomb keeps Reaction and20-foot casting-point range. Actual components/hands/voice/focus, legal commitment, RP/strain surcharge, Dissonance/Refrain and the Counterspell window apply. A legal attempted cast pays before hidden eligibility and countering; counter/failure refunds nothing and restores no displaced concentration. Only the explicitly stated spells require concentration. Save DC=8+actual total PB+actual source casting modifier; Investigation uses an actual Core task check against that DC, not a save. No generic upcast, automatic cantrip/rank scaling, substitute class levels, missing RP pool or new access is granted.

### snapshots

Choose all visible/perceived legal named targets, ordered lists, modes, centers, commands and appearances at commitment before hidden results. Recheck actual geometry and eligibility at resolution. Actual creature identity, current body/type/CR/HP, line of effect, cover and sensory gates control. A named-target spell requires perception and caster line of effect unless an exact exception is stated; ordinary area snapshots include unperceived bodies inside center line of effect. Hidden ineligibility spends an otherwise legal cast without furnishing a diagnostic. Enemy selection here means currently perceived creatures the caster affirmatively regards as hostile from available information, not a detector of true affiliation. Specific indiscriminate areas include allies. No flavor supplies real objects, extra bodies, terrain, damage, blindfolds or private memories.

### clocks

NEW minute-long proposals use a recorded absolute60-second deadline from resolution, including in initiative; expiry takes precedence at that deadline. Longer hour/day durations use actual elapsed campaign seconds, never a calendar label or a rest counter. Preserve original regular START/END identities through initiative transitions; extra turns do not add orders, saves or allowances. Suppression preserves costs, source identity, original expiry and spent history, skips ineligible execution without catch-up, and cannot revive an ended application. Scheduled repeat saves remain available during suppression when the procedure provides them; they are not additional casts. No ongoing range tether is added to established target-attached spells unless expressly stated. An ended/dead target creature ends its attached magical applications; reaching0HP without death alone does not.

### control

KEEP actual condition components and the source-character/target/encounter shared hard-control allowance. Successful establishment of whole-Action/turn control or Paralyzed/Unconscious claims it once; immunity/prevention or prior history prevents that hard component without banking it. Charmed-only immunity does not block a distinct non-Charmed effect; actual broader defenses retain their scope. Compelled ordinary movement can provoke ordinary opportunity attacks and spends real remaining movement. No missing Action, extra turn, Bonus Action, Reaction, owner command budget or new movement mode is created. Use reviewed earliest-specific-instruction priority, then stable application/ID ties; compatible demands may share only the same genuine execution. Costs, ownership, actual absolute prohibitions and limited resources remain real.

### controlCadence

NEW bind Dominate Monster and Geas to the reviewed shared six-second control cycle outside initiative and the reviewed Dominated Companion Execution bridge. One target has one existing virtual behavior budget across all controls. Establish ordinary initiative before a not-yet-committed hostile action; the current virtual cycle maps to the first actual regular turn with spent ordinary behavior/order opportunities carried forward, while normal actual regular-start resource/death-save processing still occurs. Companion orders use only an existing original-owner regular attached phase and its actual structural command/offense limits, never an unwilling owner Bonus Action or independent turn. An affected current-cycle END can grant the specifically allowed repeat save; no retroactive START order. One-shot Phantom Swarm does not gain repeating commands from this shared bridge.

### damage

Resolve each simultaneous area damage roll once, then each recipient save and typed defenses separately. Half-save damage rounds down before later resistance rounding. Actual resolved damage, temporary HP, HP floors, concentration, Wounds, life states and terminal causal branches use the reviewed rules. A zero numeric HP loss does not necessarily mean a zero pre-temp-HP damage packet. A separately stated condition/compulsion is not removed by psychic resistance/immunity alone. Save damage has no attack critical; no copied/replayed sound or image is a spell cast, material lot, companion attack or resource-generating event.

### disguises

NEW shared Mass Mirage/Seeming shell: each recipient keeps its real body, size category, footprint, anatomy, reach, carrying, equipment, HP and abilities. Apparent clothing/possessions must fit the actual body and same mechanical size category; no functional limb, actual concealment from nonsight senses, cover, line-of-effect barrier or credential is created. No imported one-foot height restriction. Real worn/carried objects have an appearance overlay only while possessed; leaving possession ends that object's overlay, with no delayed disguise attached to the dropped object. Later real form changes retain the chosen appearance only where it fits; unsupported portions cease until the body again supports them, with no forced reversion or expiry reset. True sight and actual illusion-ignoring senses apply within their real scopes. Direct physical contradiction reveals the shell to that observer, and an observer can spend an Action on INT/Investigation vs original DC to recognize it; success makes this cast's shell transparent to that observer, not everyone. A failed unchanged inspection supplies no new evidence for repeated retries. Recognition grants neither magic removal nor secret true identity/lore. Appearance alone does not guarantee a social outcome.

Unless stated otherwise, use a Wizard9 with genuine repertoire and the exact school row, PB4, INT modifier+4, max/currentRP32, Dissonance0, a different preceding spell, actual V/S/F and available required Action or Reaction. ## EN5-01 — Dominate Monster

**Original placements, unchanged**

- Enchantment rank 5: Control any creature of CR 10 or less (WIS save each turn).

**Proposed procedure**

Action; one perceived creature within60feet and line of effect; V/S/F; concentration up to60seconds. KEEP any creature with actual documented CR at most10 and WIS save each turn. NEW require a real numerical CR in the current operative creature/form profile, not character level, expedition tier, name, apparent size or an invented PC-to-CR conversion. A creature without such a CR is ineligible; a real form change before resolution uses the operative profile then. Resolve hidden CR after paid commitment. An eligible creature makes initial WIS; success gives no control. Failure attempts a non-Charmed hard action-control application under the shared guard; a blocked component gives no fallback. Eligibility is application-only; later same-creature type/CR changes alone do not end established control. At each future regular target START after mandatory start processing, a conscious capable caster may issue one mental order without another Action/BA, directing that opportunity's ordinary movement and one ordinary Action. No language/hearing is required and no remote senses or legality diagnostics are supplied. No valid new order means ordinary agency; an order is not automatically repeated. At each actual affected regular END, including an already-underway current turn, the target repeats WIS; success ends this application. Extra turns add neither order nor save. The target/controller chooses legal unspecified details. Ordinary Attack uses its real native ordinary attack sequence and normal ammunition, but no compelled spell/RP, charged/recharge/per-rest ability, special expendable ammunition, consumable magic or optional rider. No direct self-harm, knowingly suicidal route, private secret disclosure, permanent property surrender or knowing binding-oath/duty violation. BA, Reaction and separately granted extra Actions remain the target's own. Use current companion execution for an attached body and ordinary earliest-instruction priority for conflicting compulsions; changing controller never creates another owner cap or free attack. Independent applications retain separate casters/deadlines/END saves and shared history; a successful save clears only its own application. Caster incapacity normally ends concentration; target death, actual removal or expiry ends the application. Ordinary damage adds no unprinted repeat save.

**Explicit NEW choices:** NEW current documented-CR eligibility with no level substitute, initial and regular-END WIS saves, Dominate-Beast-style non-Charmed command channel, bounded ordinary budgets, explicit shared virtual/attached phase bridge and no automatic standing order.

**Authored examples**

## EN5-02 — Mass Sleep

**Original placements, unchanged**

- Enchantment rank 5: 60 HP worth of creatures fall asleep for 1 minute.

**Proposed procedure**

Action; range60feet; V/S/F; no concentration; independent applications lasting at most60seconds. KEEP fixed60HP total, not dice. NEW use reviewed Sleep's ordered-list allocation at this spell's real range: declare any finite ordered list of distinct creatures personally perceived within60feet and caster line of effect before hidden HP/defense results; no sphere or arbitrary target cap. At resolution snapshot eligible recipients and their current ordinary HP, ignoring temporary HP. Each costs max(1,currentHP). Start with60; in declared order, if a cost exceeds the remaining amount, stop without attempting it or any later entry. Otherwise spend that cost first, then attempt this cast's actual Unconscious component with its genuine immunities and hard-control history. An immune, already-protected or guard-blocked attempt still consumes its cost; no reallocation or hidden-budget report follows. No save. Successful applications impose actual Unconscious consequences; another cause of Unconscious,0HP or Stable is separate and not healed/stabilized/reset. An already-asleep target can acquire an independent source only if actually admitted; its existing source/guard history is not reset. No creature-type immunity is invented. End each Mass Sleep application on its deadline, applicable removal, a positive post-defense/pre-temp-HP damage packet received by the target, or another creature's Action physically rousing it within reach. NEW that rousing Action ends all Mass Sleep applications on that recipient, but not Sleep or other independently named causes; each source retains its own explicit removal scope. Damage ends every Mass Sleep application that sees the qualifying packet, after the triggering hit/critical outcome is fixed; an HP floor or tempHP can prevent HP loss without preventing waking. Fully prevented0damage does not wake. Suppression resumes a still-existing source without replaying drop/Prone events or extending its clock. No remaining HP budget is banked, damaged away or transferred; spell-induced unconsciousness gives no automatic safe rest credit.

**Explicit NEW choices:** NEW finite ordered perceived-target list, current-HP/minimum1 budget and stop-on-unaffordable rule borrowed explicitly from reviewed Sleep; same-family rousing scope, fixed minute and no-save application.

**Authored examples**

- EN5-02-A: The declared list has currentHP20,25,20. The budget becomes40 then15, so the third20HP entry stops allocation; a fourth lowHP target is not reached. If the first creature is sleep-immune, its20cost is still spent. No rolled dice or post-result rearrangement supplies another target.

A sleeping creature with5tempHP receives a post-defense3damage packet: Mass Sleep ends even when no ordinaryHP is lost. A same-family rousing Action does not also remove an independently existing reviewed Sleep source.

## EN5-03 — Group Illusion

**Original placements, unchanged**

- Enchantment rank 5: Create illusionary party of allies; target enemy misdirected.

**Proposed procedure**

Action; one perceived designated hostile creature within60feet and line of effect; V/S/F; concentration up to60seconds. NEW this is a target-local mental audiovisual illusion, not a public party of creatures. The target makes WIS; success establishes nothing. Failure creates four chosen apparent allies of the caster, each fitting a10-foot cube, at distinct apparent positions within a fixed20-foot-radius sphere centered on the target's initial position. Only that recipient perceives their appearances and ordinary speech/noise; the caster designs them without gaining the recipient's thoughts or senses. Actual broader mental/illusion defenses apply; no Charmed or creature-type condition gate is added. Images have no bodies, occupied squares, attacks, HP, cover, touch, carrying or mechanical flanking. NEW finite misdirection: while the recipient can see at least one still-believed image within20feet of itself, its attack rolls have disadvantage from distraction. This is an explicit spell modifier, not automatic redirection, a forced victim choice or loss of its Action. It still chooses genuine actions and perceived targets; merely being within an image's apparent reach creates no OA. Images initially make only simple plausible idle movements/brief phrases chosen at casting. During the caster's own regular turn, an Action can move any/all images up to30apparent feet each within the original sphere and give each one brief phrase from the caster's actual knowledge; no later control without that cost, no actual movement mode or semantic command power. A target without the relevant sight cannot suffer the visual distraction; apparent sounds are capped at ordinary speech and confer no unknown language. An Action INT/Investigation vs DC recognizes the whole party and ends this target's application on success. Direct physical contradiction, such as actual passage through/touch of an image, also reveals the common illusion and ends it without a roll; a remote miss or an area effect not physically testing it is not automatically proof. Actual senses expressly bypassing visual/mental illusions prevent the relevant false perception. Leaving the original sphere or losing sight removes current distraction but does not reset expiry or move the images. One active Group Illusion application per recipient across casters; only a successfully established replacement ends the prior application. Recognition/death/removal/concentration loss/expiry ends it. No hard-control allowance is claimed for this soft distraction.

**Explicit NEW choices:** NEW one-target mental illusion, four finite images, WIS admission, actual attack-disadvantage meaning of misdirection, paid image control, whole-party Investigation/physical recognition and one-family replacement.

**Authored examples**

- EN5-03-A: After a failed WIS save, the target sees four apparent hostile-to-it allies in the original sphere. It attacks a real opponent with disadvantage while seeing a nearby image; neither the image nor caster receives a free attack or physical flanking benefit. The target can instead spend its Action investigating.

- EN5-03-B: The target walks through an image along a legal route and recognizes the entire false party. The application ends, but concentration costs are not refunded. An observer other than the affected creature never saw a physical party and cannot use its supposed bodies as cover or cargo helpers.
