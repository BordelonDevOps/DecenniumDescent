## Shared proposals

### status

UNADOPTED AND UNSENT. Sixteen complete local procedures and thirty-two authored examples await actual GPT discussion and exact review. NEW denotes an explicit design proposal. Sources, catalog access, reviewed status, runtime and cosmology remain unchanged.

### casting

KEEP actual rank-five school access, minimum class levels and base10RP. All sixteen keep Action and range60feet except Mass Decay keeps30feet. All keep V/S/F except Danse Macabre keeps V/S/M/F and the reviewed ordinary material/focus alternative; no unprinted consumed material price is imported. Actual source casting ability, repertoire, components, permissions, RP/strain surcharges, Dissonance/Refrain and countering apply. DC=8+actual PB+actual casting modifier; spell attack=actual PB+actual casting modifier. A legal commitment pays actual costs before hidden target checks and countering, without refunds. Known illegal targets/costs are rejected at preflight. Only explicitly proposed concentration requirements apply; new concentration ends the prior one at its actual commitment, even if the new cast fails. No automatic upcasting, class feature or outside statblock is acquired.

### geometry

Named targets and chosen centers require actual perception and caster line of effect. Areas also require center-to-recipient line of effect; an area alone does not require seeing every indiscriminate recipient. A designated enemy is a perceived creature the caster regards as hostile from current information, not a hidden alignment detector. Choose ordered lists, modes, limbs, shapes and paths before resolution and hidden results. Recheck eligibility/geometry at resolution; hidden failure creates no diagnostic. Actual movement and body volumes cannot pass through walls or other solid/occupied spaces. Forced movement spends no movement budget and causes no ordinary opportunity attack; actual loss of support uses the reviewed Fall Marker, not a second damage event invented by this packet.

### clocks

A proposed minute is60 elapsed seconds, hour3600 and day86400. Expiry wins at its exact deadline. Suppression never resets expiry, used payments, per-round history or recovery history; skip suppressed opportunities without storing them. Genuine extra turns do not advance regular START/END counters or grant another explicitly regular-turn pulse. For the three-round spell endpoint, record the next three future original-source regular END markers, excluding a turn already underway; outside initiative use18elapsed seconds. Preserve that original schedule through death, initiative changes and transition out of combat, with six-second virtual cycles when the source lacks an active turn. This is a NEW finite clock bridge, not permission to halt a spell by removing its caster from initiative.

### damageAndHealing

Use reviewed typed defenses, minimum-HP prevention, temporary HP, Wounds, Instant Death, dying/Stable, actual death and eligible immediate survival responses. Resistance halves the matching component with normal rounding. actualDamageDealt=tempHP absorbed+actual ordinary HP lost, excluding overkill, state-assignment loss and double-counted parent/child transfer packets. Count each eligible terminal damage branch once. Healing based on this number remains ordinary healing unless a procedure explicitly identifies direct state recovery. Broad cannot-regain-HP applies to living direct state recovery; ordinary healing multipliers and Critical ordinary-healing ceilings do not automatically apply to it. HP costs use normal HP only, are not damage and cannot be paid with tempHP, redirected, reduced, prevented or refunded by a failed benefit. No spell here revives an actually dead identity.

### companions

All commanded bodies of one owner share the reviewed one Bonus Action command and one offensive companion Action per numbered round, including other class companions/devices. Issue within60feet with actual owner perception and line of effect. NEW these spell-created/animated undead understand only their owner's simple mental command channel; it grants neither a remote sensory feed nor free speech/knowledge. A BA selects exactly one body and one ordinary Move plus its single profiled Action or ordinary permitted basic Action, executed only in the owner's existing attached phase after the whole owner turn. Every other capable body has only defensive Move and Dodge in that phase. No independent initiative, Reaction, BA, multiattack, spellcasting, Help engine, tool proficiency, resource pool or summon power exists. Committed orders retain reviewed validity rules. Damage/control consumes the shared offensive allowance; support still needs its paid command. Original source owner supplies the current spell attack and PB-based saves; changing controller grants no second cap or new source stats. Each independent application keeps its actual duration and original ownership. Legal ordinary companion healing cannot reconstruct a destroyed body or refill absent resources.

### bodyClaims

One physical identity has one state: living, corpse, consumed or removed. Animate only actual eligible non-summoned, non-copied, non-illusory, not-previously-raised remains; fragments do not become extra bodies. Legal resurrection already completed during immediate death responses marks that same body living before later claims. A reservation by Auto-Raise is not exclusive ownership; still resolve its actual later checkpoint and capacity, consuming no second body. These spells do not grant Wizard Corpse Harvest, Auto-Raise, Infernal Pact or Mage assets. Spell-specific body caps are separate from Wizard's three standard-minion slots and Mage asset slots, but all retain the shared command/offense budget. NEW spent animated remains retain their physical identity and actual inventory, with consumed status: no second raising/harvest fuel and no refreshed recipe donor qualities. An actual resurrection source must meet its reviewed reconstruction requirements for a consumed body; this packet grants none. Created summons leave no corpse/loot. Source-qualified maximum-HP modifiers require genuine access and actual spell provenance; a Wizard spell creation can qualify for its actual Conjuration modifier, never a generic borrowed Wizard level. Printed HP here is base HP before such genuinely applicable modifiers.

### zombieProfile

NEW shared Flood/Danse zombie animation profile: actual Small or Medium eligible body, type Undead; baseAC10, baseHP20, walk20feet only with its original functional walking supports; STR12, DEX6, CON14, INT3, WIS6, CHA3. All six saving throws use original owner's current PB as their total modifier; other checks use the listed ability modifier with no proficiency. Ordinary sight/hearing only, no darkvision, languages, private memory or remote senses. It makes one melee attack at5feet using original owner's current spell-attack modifier, dealing1d6 bludgeoning with its actual available arm/body strike, no ability damage bonus. Native shape/size/weight and missing anatomy remain real: no hand attack without a surviving striking limb; missing legs give no new crawl/flight/swim mode. Physical wounds/injuries and harvest losses persist and restrict the surviving anatomy; the new animation HP pool does not repair them. Existing usable worn armor can replace the baseAC under its own rules without creating proficiency, a new item or a second AC formula. Supplied equipment stays real, encumbers normally, and does not add weapon attacks outside this profile. No resistances, immunities, Undead Fortitude, regeneration, death saves, loot reward, donor substrate or other zombie powers are imported. This is an HP-only animation destroyed at0; destruction or expiry leaves that same consumed inert body and its real surviving gear. It neither restores nor duplicates the former living identity.

### materialLedger

NEW permanent material conversions below are explicit finite transmutation exceptions: output occupies the same selected eligible volume, while its mass becomes that of its new named mundane material. They do not claim both mass and volume conservation. Record each actual source region/lot, volume, old and new composition/mass, contamination, claims, damage and later splits/removals. No original stock remains separately available. No new source-tier, precious mineral, monster property, finished item quality, magical reagent role or trade XP follows from renaming matter. Separating ordinary output does not copy the original region; consumed/sold quantities are gone. Existing tools, actual work, access, Load, transport, finite buyers, provenance and recipe requirements still apply. Transformation is not Fabricate, mining work, an appraisal or a guaranteed sale. A permanent instantaneous result is ordinary matter: later Dispel/suppression does not revert it. Real later physical melting, erosion and use change that actual stock once. Source contamination/embedded foreign matter is not purified by changing its host material. No cosmological origin or universal matter-creation rule follows.

## NE5-03 — Terror Chain

**Original placements, unchanged**

- Necromancy rank 5: One target feared for 3 rounds; jumps to next if failed.

**Proposed procedure**

KEEP Action,60feet,one target feared for three rounds and a jump on failure. NEW before casting declare an ordered list of one to three distinct perceived creatures, every one within60feet and caster line of effect and each later creature within30feet/line of effect of its predecessor. No affiliation restriction is added. At resolution try the first legal entry with WIS; success ends propagation and imposes nothing. A failed save attempts actual Frightened anchored to the original caster. Only successful admission of that fear allows the one immediate jump to the next declared entry, which repeats the same WIS procedure; maximum two jumps/three recipients. An immune/ineligible/blocked entry or broken link ends propagation without skipping it. Being already Frightened by another source is not itself immunity; admit an independent relationship if otherwise legal. A repeated target cannot appear later, and no post-result selection, hidden creature search or cycling occurs.
