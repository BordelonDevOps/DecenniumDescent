# Rank-four Divination and Enchantment: operating proposals

## Shared proposed rules

### status

UNADOPTED AND UNSENT. Fifteen proposed mechanical procedures with complete originals and current comparisons. No public spell, class access, review count, source book or cosmology is changed. All added procedures are explicit NEW proposals, not approval or runtime evidence.

### casting

All fifteen retain Action and V/S/F. Use the actual casting class’s rank, school, minimum class level, independently acquired repertoire and base RP price. A legal cast commits costs, concentration transitions and ordinary Dissonance/Refrain before interruption. Failure, immunity, missing information and countering give no refund or restoration of old concentration. Use actual casting ability + PB for attacks and 8 + PB + that modifier for save DC; ordinary saves are binary. No generic upcast, additional Action or inferred class-card access.

### time

Unless a card states otherwise, a minute ends at the caster’s tenth future original regular-start marker, excluding a start underway, or after 60 elapsed seconds if begun outside initiative. Retain original virtual markers or absolute deadlines through reorder, extra turns, absence and travel. Suppression never pauses a clock, refunds spent history or replays an application. Later saved-against casts do not dispel an older valid application. Extra turns do not supply extra periodic pulses, order opportunities or repeat saves.

### information

Use actual established facts and each information protection’s real subject and generating-source scope. Hidden unprotected facts can be read; missing facts are not invented. An unavailable answer does not identify the hidden reason. Ordinary observed outcomes and inferences remain possible. No new faction roster, plane, moral axis, cosmic force, secret doctrine or guaranteed destiny is authored. The pending expedition reputation verdict is not presumed.

### control

A successfully established hard whole-Action/turn component spends the shared source-character/target/encounter guard once. Its continued valid duration does not claim a new allowance each turn. Initial prevention leaves no banked component; later suppression retains the existing clock and spent history. A blocked component gets no unprinted fallback. Actual component immunities remain separate; creature type alone is no blanket mental immunity. Limited attack or movement restrictions do not become whole-Action denial merely because alternatives are unattractive.

### newControlBindings

NEW binding for Dominate Person and Confusion: use the reviewed Enchantment compelled-budget arbitration and shared six-second outside-initiative control cycle, preserving already spent budgets on entry to real initiative. A valid order/table instruction receives its priority timestamp when issued or generated at that opportunity; simultaneous instructions use application order then stable source ID. A losing instruction gets no second budget and does not erase an earlier mandate. Actual ordinary Attack Actions use native attacks unless the card expressly limits them to one. Only actual required ordinary ammunition can be compelled; no optional finite resource, spell/RP, recharge, charge, special expendable ammunition, permanent property surrender, protected secret, knowing binding-oath violation, direct self-harm or knowingly suicidal route can be forced. Bonus Actions, Reactions and extra Actions remain target-controlled. Hostile conduct uses ordinary initiative before execution, never a free virtual pre-initiative attack.

### newCompanionBinding

NEW for an actually eligible Humanoid under Dominate Person, or any eligible Confusion target, using an owner-attached companion chassis: use the actual original-owner regular attached phase, original profile and structural command/offense caps. No independent turn, dominator companion allowance or unwilling owner Bonus Action is created. A pending command reservation can be redirected only for this same companion in this same phase, under the reviewed Dominate Beast bridge; spent or another companion’s reservation cannot be stolen. A repeat WIS save occurs at the end of that real regular attached phase even when no instruction executed; this source-specific checkpoint adds no ordinary START/END, recharge or other condition tick. If no such phase is currently supplied, only an END-only six-second save schedule continues, with no orders/actions. On transitioning to actual phases, map the next unresolved save to the next regular phase end, preserve resolved saves and the original maximum, and never replay an opportunity. This extension is proposed explicitly; it is not already conferred by Dominate Beast.

### newEndSaveBinding

NEW: Overwhelming Grief, Charm Monster, Erase Emotion and Phantasmal Killer use affected regular-END saves, including an affected current turn, without extra-turn saves. Outside initiative their first END-only save is at application +6 seconds and then each six seconds. On entering initiative map the next unresolved save to the next actual regular END, retain completed saves and the original absolute maximum, and do not add an immediate transition save. For an owner-attached target, use its actual regular attached-phase end with the same END-only fallback/transition above, granting no actions. Dominate Person and Confusion instead use their shared behavior cycle and companion binding.

### newCompanionPulseBinding

NEW for Illusion Phantasmal Killer on an owner-attached companion: its periodic packet uses the beginning of the actual original-owner regular attached phase, after that phase’s required processing, under the same once-per-numbered-round exposure. It creates no independent START or other start-trigger event. If no actual phase exists, use only the six-second damage schedule and original deadline, granting no actions. Transition to a real phase maps the next unresolved pulse opportunity without replaying an initial or completed pulse. Its separately scheduled repeat save still follows the END-only bridge.

## Required joint decisions

- Fate Reversal proposes extending the currently two-spell Mark Omen/Fate Coin guard. Adoption would require an explicit shared-rule amendment, not an implied change from this index.

- Seal Vision deliberately protects against noninformational Divination too, while Protection from Scrying remains information-only. Its self-protection exemption requires joint review.

- Resonance Sight is currently a reviewed cantrip with exact RP aura information. Resonance Divination preserves its source rank and 60-foot snapshot but has a substantial overlap; do not describe the lower spell as merely qualitative.

- Dominate Person full control remains bounded by current agency and resource rules. The proposed companion repeat-save bridge and Confusion table are new authoring, not existing approved behavior.

- Mass Pacify uses the narrower actual-attack category and per-target attacked release, unlike Group Pacify’s broader harm/provocation. Its breadth and rank value need explicit discussion.

- Confusion keeps Enchantment and Illusion target/range modes separate. The concentration requirement and Illusion duration/save are added explicitly.

- Phantasmal Killer keeps Enchantment initial damage separate from Illusion recurring damage. Fear immunity does not remove independently established damage; current fear geometry and information limits remain.

- Erase Emotion targets actual rage-state/Frightened-generating components, including parts of mixed abilities. No source is classified by a suggestive name alone.

## DE4-01 — Fate Reversal

**Original placements, unchanged**

- Divination rank 4: Force a reroll of one attack, save, or check anywhere on battlefield.

**Proposed procedure**

Action; V/S/F. NEW: establish one personal pending permission for one minute without concentration; a successful recast replaces your prior unused permission. Core’s 60-foot range applies to the later roll’s actual generating creature/object or local event, which you must perceive with line of effect. The battlefield phrase grants no unlimited reach. After the complete initial attack, save or check d20 result is available under ordinary information rules, but before its consequences, spend your Reaction and this permission to replace the entire d20 procedure using its original modifiers and advantage/disadvantage state. A two-d20 procedure receives two new dice and normal higher/lower selection; a single-d20 procedure receives one. Use the new result even if worse. Process natural results only from the final roll under its actual attack/task/save rules. No damage, healing or resource die is covered. NEW: this joins Mark Omen and Fate Coin’s shared one-reroll guard for one original roll event and its entire replacement lineage, across all casters and recasts. Reserve that guard at legal commitment; competing declarations use initiative order or the preselected neutral out-of-initiative order. An already barred declaration spends neither permission nor Reaction. Exact class rerolls retain their own timing but never reset this three-spell guard. Suppression blocks use while the original timer continues. Replaced dice generate no duplicate cast, payment, hit hook or completed consequence; interruption after commitment refunds nothing. No secret roll is revealed and no finished consequence is reversed.

**Explicit NEW choices:** NEW: Action establishes a minute-long personal permission; a later Reaction works within 60 feet; the Mark Omen/Fate Coin guard expressly expands to three spells if adopted.

**Authored examples**

- An attack made with advantage rolls 4 and 17. A legal Fate Reversal replaces both dice, rather than choosing only the 17, and uses the new selected face even if worse.

- If Fate Coin already used the shared allowance on that roll’s lineage, Fate Reversal cannot spend another Reaction or permission to reroll it.

## DE4-02 — Seal Vision

**Original placements, unchanged**

- Divination rank 4: Block divination spells from affecting an area or creature.

**Proposed procedure**

Action; range 60 feet; V/S/F. NEW: choose one willing perceived creature with line of effect, including yourself, OR a fixed 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a perceived point with line of effect. Concentration up to one minute. The creature mode follows its actual body without a continuing range tether. In area mode, protection applies to creatures and effects while their relevant footprint is inside with line of effect to the center. Block effects of actual Divination-school spells on those protected subjects, using the generating cast’s real school placement. This deliberately includes noninformational Divination buffs, marks and reroll permissions, unlike Protection from Scrying’s information-only scope. Suppress an existing affected component without deleting it; prevent a new component from establishing. Leaving or suppression removes this ward’s protection while surviving old components retain their original deadlines. A Divination observation point inside supplies no feed while protected; an outside point cannot obtain protected information about subjects inside but can still read independent permitted surroundings. Queries about a protected creature return the querying spell’s neutral unavailable result, without a reason or roster. A Divination reroll cannot alter a protected roll-generator’s event. Ordinary senses, independent records, remembered facts and non-Divination powers remain governed by their actual rules. NEW self-consistency exception: Seal Vision’s own protective records are exempt from suppression by any Seal Vision, so overlapping seals do not disable each other. Other seals add no magnitude and each retains its source and clock. No blindness, physical concealment, spellcasting ban or general antimagic follows.

**Explicit NEW choices:** NEW: willing-creature or fixed-area mode; concentration minute; full Divination-effect suppression including noninformational components; explicit self-protection exemption.

**Authored examples**

- An existing Soul Mark remains recorded but gives no protected bearing. After the seal ends it can resume only if its original deadline and identity still permit.

- A protected creature’s ordinary visible body remains visible. An unclassified class sense is not blocked merely because its description resembles divination.

## DE4-03 — Aspect of Insight

**Original placements, unchanged**

- Divination rank 4: Gain advantage on all mental saves and +2 INT/WIS for 1 minute.

**Proposed procedure**

Action; range 60 feet; V/S/F; one minute without concentration. NEW: one willing perceived creature with line of effect, including yourself, gains advantage on INT, WIS and CHA saves, plus +2 to each of its INT and WIS ability scores. This is a score increase, not +2 to every derived modifier. Each score increase is capped at 20; a preexisting score above 20 is not reduced and gets no increase from this spell. Apply the strongest competing flat bonus to the same score, not additive duplicate casts. Recompute actual ability modifiers and only statistics that genuinely depend on their current values: checks, saves, spell attacks/DCs, applicable limits and explicitly dynamic class resource maxima. Existing fixed or snapshotted values keep their source timing. Temporary increases do not grant class levels, repertoire, prepared spells, trained skills, permanent prerequisites or fresh uses. Keep current HP/resources unchanged when maxima rise; clamp down if a maximum falls, without damage, healing or refund hooks. Ordinary concentration remains CON-based unless an actual source says otherwise. The mental-save advantage does not create extra saves or modify unmodified death saves. Ending, suppression or replacement recomputes the same effective values without resetting any pool, preparation, acquisition or clock.

**Explicit NEW choices:** NEW: willing recipient; mental means INT/WIS/CHA; +2 means actual INT/WIS scores capped at 20, with precise derived-value and resource handling.

**Authored examples**

- INT 17 becomes 19: its modifier changes from +3 to +4. A dependent spell DC rises by 1, not 2, while current RP stays unchanged.

- INT 22 remains 22. Two Aspects do not make it 26 or reduce it to the cap.

## DE4-04 — Locate Creature

**Original placements, unchanged**

- Divination rank 4: Sense direction to a specific creature within 5 miles.

**Proposed procedure**
