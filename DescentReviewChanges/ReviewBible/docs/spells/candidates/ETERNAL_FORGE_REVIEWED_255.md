## spellLocalNEW.creation

NEW: create a new complete physical body; do not transform or consume an ordinary inventory base. A recipe card supplies an output specification only. No recipe work, formula ownership, tool proficiency, ingredients or crafting time are silently imposed. One Action produces the useful authorized item, at maximum standard body HP and its source-defined initial charges once. No Exceptional/Masterwork output or quality upgrade unless separately authorized by a complete profile.

## spellLocalNEW.permanence

NEW Forge-local reconciliation: the created body is permanent ordinary matter; a separately identified rank-9 ongoing Forge application maintains this creation’s magical imprint. No natural expiry, concentration, caster-survival tether or ongoing range limit. Until dispelled applies to that explicit application. This does not make ordinary permanent item enchantments eligible for Greater Dispel. On successful capture/termination of this Forge application, only its imprint ends; the actual damaged mundane body, custody and independent contents remain. No consumed stock returns and no spent effect or damage is undone.

## spellLocalNEW.dispel

The Forge application is a discrete anchor on the created body. Greater Dispel captures that ongoing spell application under its exact 30-foot-center/30-foot-radius snapshot and generating rank 9, one actual spellcasting-ability+PB check against DC28 per generating cast. A class Dispel has its distinct cost/range/DC. No acquired source is inferred from a comparison card. This explicit local spell dependency is not an amendment to all magic-item dispelling.

## spellLocalNEW.suppression

The mundane body remains; intersecting suppression disables the Forge imprint and item magical functions while applicable. NEW: this item-bound application is indivisible when its body intersects the operative field. Charges and damage history persist; clocks continue. A dawn missed while suppressed does not recharge later. Ending suppression restores only a still-live imprint and currently lawful functionality.

## spellLocalNEW.bodyLifecycle

Broken at zero structural HP disables positive mundane/magical functions under the item source; the root survives on the recoverable original main body. Actual destruction or full consumption ends the imprint and tombstones the root. Ordinary repair follows source tools, proficiency, station, material costs, work duration and checks; no recreation of a missing main body or charge reset. Losing a detached required part makes the item incomplete; it does not create another imprinted item. Salvage and partial consumption preserve exact ordinary material lineage; no detached component duplicates magic. Whole-body recipe consumption ends this imprint before the new recipe outcome. Independent socketed/contained objects survive all root endings.

Require an actual available finite buyer, agreed quote and inventory/currency settlement. No new item values or guaranteed market are authored. Recast always creates a new ID; never refill or modify the old body by pretending it is newly created.
