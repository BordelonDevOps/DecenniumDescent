## woven-threads

WOVEN THREADS — selected complete proposed procedure.
KEEP: Divination rank5, base10RP, Action,60feet,V/S/F, one perceived creature, WIS resistance, alteration of the genuinely last minute and Instantaneous duration. Retain all six actual class routes and their exact level gates. MC3-CAST-120, MC3-READY-120, MC3-MEMORY-120 and MC3-RECOVERY-120 apply only within their written scopes.
NEW temporal declaration: choose Erase or Replace and either FIXED absolute [a,b), 0<b-a<=60, or RELATIVE offsets [-u,-v),0<=v<u<=60. A direct cast anchors relative offsets at its legal commitment; a readied cast fixes those offsets at preparation but anchors them at its legal release. Explicit absolute timestamps never move. Freeze the resulting interval and payload before saves, private eligibility or interruption outcomes; no post-result retargeting. At the initial direct declaration or Ready preparation, the fixed interval must be within that then-eligible preceding minute; a knowingly impossible fixed plan is rejected before preparation/direct payment. A lawful fixed plan that subsequently ages is not rebound at release: its stated temporal payload check occurs at resolution after an otherwise legal release has spent its Reaction. The controller may decline release under ordinary Ready, but aging supplies no refund or replacement interval. A RELATIVE plan is instantiated at direct commitment or Ready release before private results, then remains fixed. At effect resolution t, revalidate against [t-60,t). Erase applies only the still-valid intersection and the actual experienced/event-selected portions inside it; a partly aged interval can therefore be partly erased with the same full payment. A wholly aged/empty intersection does nothing. Replace requires its ENTIRE fixed time interval still to lie inside the resolution window; partial aging makes that replacement fail wholly, because automatically trimming or retiming its narrative would change the fixed content. This asymmetry is explicit NEW scope, not a generic casting rule. A relative plan is anchored once, never slid again after a delay. Both temporal failures are paid when lawful commitment already occurred, give no new interval/target and no hidden diagnostic. Temporal validation is distinct from the recipient's private experience capacity.
Erase may select the whole interval or one clearly described event/facet predicate using at most60 ordinary whitespace-delimited words; an empty event description means the whole interval. Replace supplies one coherent first-person sensory narrative of at most60 such words covering no more than that fixed interval. Ordinary linguistic words count; identifiers/code/concatenation cannot bypass the content limit. The caster must actually know the described concepts, not the unknown original memory. It may assert false events using known concepts, but supplies no proficiency, formula, language, repertoire, genuine consent or factual omniscience. No hearing/shared language is needed for simple sensory content; semantic content the target cannot actually understand is not translated or taught. Replace changes only actual personally experienced eligible portions; absent experience/unconsciousness cannot become a training slot or an invented original. Preserve the entire declared narrative and record unsupported semantic facets privately rather than substituting a different story.
At resolution require actual creature, current perception/range/line of effect and memory capacity/protection eligibility. Roll one native WIS save if there is an otherwise operative mental application; success leaves recall unchanged. Failure applies the selected permissible change once. A hidden capacity/protection failure or nonexistent experience provides no readout, fallback event, prior timestamp or control effect. Actual suppression at the target prevents the local mental application before its payload; no save/overlay is invented for a fully suppressed output.
On successful application write a private source/owner/recipient/originalDC/root/event ledger and change accessible recollection instantly. There is no ongoing concentration, fixed expiry, repeated save or later dispellable field. Completed external events, physical records, resources, injuries, skills and independent commands persist. No caster success notification or forced future decision is added. Preserve all earlier memory provenance under later edits. Shared recovery can subsequently remove this family's supported portions against the highest relevant originalDC without restoring an independent Disrupt Memory loss. Ended or recovered history remains stored; death/revival, ordinary rest and loading are not replay/reset permissions.

## haze-of-forgetting

HAZE OF FORGETTING — selected complete proposed procedure.
KEEP: Enchantment rank6, base13RP, Action,V/S/F,30feet, INT resistance, all targets forgetting the last minute and Instantaneous duration. Retain actual Bard/Crimson Agent/Mirrorsage/Wizard routes and levels. MC3-CAST-120, MC3-READY-120, MC3-MEMORY-120 and MC3-RECOVERY-120 apply.
NEW geometry: at effect resolution freeze one point at the center of the caster's actual current occupied anatomical body-volume bounds, recorded in that body's real pose; do not extend the area from its nearest surface or an external focus. This is a Haze-only point convention, not a universal origin rule. Snapshot every extant creature whose actual occupied body interior intersects the closed30-foot-radius sphere at a point reachable by a clear center-to-that-interior line of effect. The caster's own body is excluded solely as the emanation's self-occluder, and the caster is included; this is not permission to ignore independent walls or other genuine blockers. Tangency with no body-interior witness is insufficient. Same-position logical aliases of one creature are one recipient. Actual allies, hidden/unnoticed creatures and the caster are not selectively omitted; no individual perception or sight/hearing/language of the spell is required. No privileged hidden-roster result is presented. Other actual defenses retain their scopes.
Use the SINGLE resolution-relative interval [t-60,t) for this snapshot. This is not a Woven FIXED-selector cast. At ordinary Ready, payment and interruption remain preparation events, but the actual center, recipient snapshot and intrinsic interval arise at release/resolution. No ongoing fog, visual obscuration, future entrant application, replacement narrative, delayed pulse or range tether is created.
Each eligible local recipient independently makes INT against the actual casting-source DC. Success leaves recall unchanged. Failure removes accessible firsthand episodic recall only of personally experienced portions in that actual minute; do not fill unobserved or unconscious periods or substitute older events. Snapshot the independent applications; forgetting one's preparation does not refund or retroactively cancel the already committed application to the remaining recipients. Death or another genuine intervening ineligibility uses its actual scope; no additional recipient is substituted. Actual antimagic suppresses the affected local output before that mental payload, not already completed edits elsewhere.
The alteration is an instantaneous lasting recollection change, with no concentration or automatic ending save/deadline. It independently ends no concentration. Actual completed class/repertoire learning, general language/identity, skill use, money, physical traces, actions and resources remain. Newly heard passwords can be inaccessible through the affected firsthand episode while older independent knowledge and external notes remain available. A current thought of an old event is not authority to erase that old event.
Write the private original experience/source/DC ledger before hiding recall. Apply/replay each source-recipient transaction once, preserving all intersecting older restrictions. Shared ten-minute recovery is explicitly NEW for only Haze and Woven: restore only actually evidenced portions against the highest unrecovered relevant originalDC, including masked layers; no free whole-episode truth, no actor/location/source diagnosis and no restoration of independent Disrupt Memory. Outside portions remain. No ordinary dispel, suppression after completion, rest or revival undoes the lasting outcome. The target retains agency and may learn new facts or question its recollection without being compelled to tell or believe a supplied story.

No direct placement inside a living body or sealed/inaccessible container.
Choose a crude stock lot or one complete existing wholly plant-derived mundane finished profile. This source-only production exception replaces that item's ordinary raw-material input and production work, but grants no new recipe, station qualification, rare catalyst, magic effect, quality/grade bonus, extra yield or tradeXP. Choose and freeze real composition/quantity/shape/pattern before hidden results. On ordinary Ready the material plan is prepared and the real placement chosen at legal release. Revalidate the full output and its local suppression at resolution. If any essential part of this one indivisible materialization is suppressed or fails fit/support/eligibility, create NO partial stock, with no refund after paid commitment. No free unsupported falling attack or forced excavation is supplied. A successful transaction creates one new root lot exactly once; duplicate callbacks grant nothing.
Existing actual object profiles win. Actual cloth/paper/rope use their reviewed AC11, not the draft's generic AC10; actual wood uses AC15. Retain specific item structuralHP (e.g. weapon5; targeted rope segment5; letter1) and any source-specific defense/repair. This is one stock body's budget, not HP per gram or automatic armor/building capability. Declare its real physical damage compatibility before an attack. Ordinary object poison/psychic immunity and Broken-state rules remain. No finite liquid-damage behavior is fabricated from a sword hit.
Partition exactly by MC3-STOCK-120's capacity-aware largest-remainder algorithm. Actual cut history, leaf IDs, exact masses, damage and value provenance remain. ZeroHP/max-zero fragments remain material without functioning item benefits. An actual repair source can restore only what its source authorizes: ordinary Mending can repair a qualifying minor connection but does not supply combat durability, missing/consumed material or a destroyed mechanism. Existing mundane self-repair needs actual recoverable item, trained tools/materials, source price, work and task. The source-only crude-stock fallback supplies no automatic priceable generic repair; an applicable actual rule is still needed. Bundling/rejoining is not HP redistribution or free manufacture.
Completed output is ordinary lasting physical matter. Later Greater Dispel or antimagic cannot delete it, food already consumed or ordinary derivatives. Actual casting suppression/countering still prevents formation at its real checkpoint; actual separately magical later additions remain their own effects. No continuous aura, source concentration or one-object-at-a-time limit is added. Every subsequent paid legal cast can form a separate bounded lot, with actual RP/strain/actions/repertoire; no unlimited discounts or guaranteed sale are implied. Qualified ordinary edible matter supplies only its genuine food mass/preparation-grade benefits under EX58/Cooking. It creates no water, packaging, meal boon, preserved-ration classification or full Supply by assertion. Age/freshness begins at creation for genuinely new stock and follows its actual profile; no unsourced shelf-life is invented. A later recipe consumes it only where its exact material identity/grade truly matches. A buyer, actual quote, provenance, fees and scarcity remain necessary; reference10gp is not cash.
