# Starting equipment and packs

**REVIEWED authored operational PLAYTEST equipment/acquisition scope.** Use with the [equipment guide](EQUIPMENT_ACQUISITION_PLAYTEST.md) and [item procedures](EQUIPMENT_ITEM_PROCEDURES.md).

Choose the actual class and background packages, or the reviewed starting-wealth route. Wealth replaces both physical packages and all package money, without removing background training, standing or features. Fixed packages grant no proficiency or unselected feature. Record actual choices, quantities, fitted size, custody, ammunition and container placement. Check pounds and Load after every source is included.

The Druid package is preserved Player source; its choices add their actual weights to the fixed subtotal. The Tinker package completes the source example’s undefined basic supplies with Explorer’s Pack. Other kits are new conference decisions. Warrior’s two tridents replace the proposal’s unproficient handaxes. Staff means quarterstaff. Ammunition counts are individual units even when the source sells bundles.

Soldier’s repeating crossbow magazine begins empty until actually loaded. Shield and crossbow may be used together only with the actual Shield Trained permission. Wizard’s book contains only its actual reviewed class spell entitlement. A selected Druid simple weapon supplies no missing proficiency or unlisted ammunition.

Fixed Large-sized armor/shields/weapons are included without an added creation payment, at the guide’s doubled mass and later baseline value. A kit may exceed capacity or the Burdened threshold; legally store, transfer or carry it using actual resources. Do not silently delete items, create a porter or waive encumbrance.

## Background equipment

Every original equipment field is retained below. Existing named items keep their ordinary equipment function without granting training, spells, recipes, attunement or hidden properties. Record actual choices and finite quantities for descriptions such as “several”; a character sheet cannot leave carried quantities infinite or blank. Documents and oddities preserve their narrative information.

### Aethel City Guard Veteran

**Preserved source package:** A piece of your old uniform (e.g., a dented helmet or worn insignia), a map of Aethel's main districts, a set of common clothes, manacles, and a belt pouch containing 10 gp.

**Physical mapping:** Uniform insignia uses small narrative token unless an actual larger item is chosen; map uses small document; common clothes, manacles, pouch and coins use named catalog items.

### Rift Scarred Survivor

**Preserved source package:** A strange trinket from the other side (e.g., a shard of obsidian that hums faintly, a feather that never gets dirty), a tattered map of a place that doesn't exist on Aethel, a set of traveler's clothes, an amulet to ward off bad luck, and 5 gp.

**Physical mapping:** Trinket and luck amulet use small narrative tokens; map uses small document; traveler clothes and coins retain their named profiles.

### Disgraced Noble Scion

**Preserved source package:** A set of fine clothes (perhaps slightly worn), a signet ring bearing a tarnished or altered family crest, a letter detailing your disgrace (or a forged letter proclaiming your innocence), and a purse containing 25 gp (perhaps the last of your inheritance).

**Physical mapping:** Fine clothes and signet ring retain normal profiles; letter uses small document; purse uses pouch. Source worn appearance alone does not invent mechanical damage.

### Wildling Herbalist

**Preserved source package:** An herbalism kit, a staff carved with natural symbols, a collection of dried herbs and rare flowers, a set of common clothes made from animal hides, and a belt pouch containing 5 gp.

**Physical mapping:** Herbalism kit; carved staff uses quarterstaff; one finite dried-herb/flower bundle; common clothes, pouch and coins.

### Guild Artisan Dropout

**Preserved source package:** A set of artisan's tools you managed to keep, a sample of your best work (now perhaps used for mundane tasks), a letter of resignation (or expulsion) from your guild, a set of traveler's clothes, and a belt pouch containing 15 gp.

**Physical mapping:** Select one actual artisan-tool item. Retain best-work sample description and choose its actual comparable profile, with no inferred hidden power. Letter, traveler clothes, pouch and coins use normal profiles.

### Seeker of the Lost Pantheon

**Preserved source package:** A rubbing from an ancient tablet, a book of fragmented myths, a holy symbol of a forgotten deity (perhaps self-made), a set of common clothes, incense, and 10 gp.

**Physical mapping:** Rubbing uses small document; myth book uses Book; select an ordinary holy-symbol profile; common clothes and coins. New agreed quantity completion: one incense block; no unlimited incense.

### Marked Fugitive

**Preserved source package:** Broken manacle, false identity papers, hidden mark or scar, 10 gp in mixed coin

**Physical mapping:** Broken manacle is a 3-lb unusable fragment; false papers use small documents; mark/scar is a body description, not extra carried inventory; mixed coins use actual denominations totaling10gp.

### Expedition Cartographer

**Physical mapping:** Cartographer tools; new agreed quantity completion: three blank parchment sheets and three partially completed map documents; compass, traveler clothes, pouch and coins use normal profiles.

### Arcane Academy Dropout

**Preserved source package:** A spellbook or arcane focus (perhaps damaged or modified), a letter of expulsion or resignation, a set of common clothes, a student's robe, and a pouch containing 10 gp.

**Physical mapping:** Choose Spellbook OR one actual Arcane Focus. It retains normal equipment function but no free spells. Letter, common clothes, student robe=Robes, pouch and coins.

### Cult Escapee

### Elemental Touched

### Celestial Emissary

**Preserved source package:** A holy symbol or celestial token, a set of ceremonial clothes, a small reliquary containing a minor celestial blessing (emits dim light in darkness), a journal of visions or instructions, and a pouch containing 15 gp.

**Physical mapping:** Choose actual holy symbol OR narrative celestial token; ceremonial clothes=Clothes costume; additional dim-light reliquary uses its exact source-specific profile; journal, pouch and coins.

The Dimensional Refugee’s unusual object remains a possession with an actual recorded physical profile, not an automatically identified artifact. Celestial Emissary’s particular reliquary has only its source-specific dim-light procedure. No origin, cross-plane or cosmology ruling is made here.
