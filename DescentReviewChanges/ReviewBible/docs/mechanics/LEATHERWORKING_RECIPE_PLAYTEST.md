## Use a card

Choose a legally accessible formula and check the reviewed [crafting framework](../mechanics/CRAFTING_HARVEST_PLAYTEST.md). Reserve its actual components and the complete mundane bodies below. Record one body model, fitted size and legal equipment grade for the batch; every reserve uses them. Perform active work, resolve one completion check, then select the allowed quality outcome once for the batch. Equip each actual finished item under the rules below. A title such as Superior or Masterwork does not determine equipment grade.

Finished outputs are durable magical nonartifact equipment. Their passive recipe properties require no attunement, RP, concentration or spell activation. The imprint does not repair the body: functional means at least 1 current structural HP and actual complete physical usability. Suppression or melding suspends the appropriate benefits even at positive HP.

## Defensive enhancement and resistance

Positive source `mechanics.armorBonus` fields supply the separately labeled LW-D01 AC overlay even where the original effect paragraph omitted it. Each affected card prints the preserved effect and the operational AC clause independently. A zero source armor bonus supplies no candidate: it does not create a phantom +0 that cancels a Crude suit's −1.

All eligible Leatherworking enhancements join the existing `equipment.defense-enhancement` family. Use the strongest actual eligible value among the selected worn suit's grade, Blacksmithing enhancements and Leatherworking enhancements. A suit's recipe enhancement needs a complete functional unsuppressed actually worn suit supplying the armor formula in use. An accessory needs the complete functional unsuppressed item actually worn in its one legal benefit slot. Carried, stowed or melded items do not qualify. Only one suit supplies the formula; layering another suit cannot add its grade. The ordinary AC bonus from one selected usable shield remains outside this family. Class, spell, ancestry and unarmored rules retain their own requirements.

Printed resistance is separate from enhancement arbitration. It requires the complete functional unsuppressed actually worn item. Suit resistance does not require selecting that suit's AC formula, although wearing it may violate another formula's unarmored requirements. Resistance protects the wearer, not automatically the item or contents. Repeated same-type resistance never becomes immunity. A title promising acidproofing grants no acid resistance without the actual printed resistance field. A cold-damage resistance grants no environmental immunity or automatic success in cold weather.

## Carrying relief and one physical inventory

Source `capacityPounds` means the recipe's relief allowance R. Physical capacity is a separate mundane body property and supports at least R before enchanting. Even when suppression stops relief, the container still has its physical space and support strength. A 30-lb Backpack can therefore have R10, R20 or R30 without changing its body.

There are five possible relief positions per direct bearer: one back-cargo (Backpack or compatible cargo harness), one quiver, one waist-cargo belt and two pouch positions. Actual anatomy, space and attachment compatibility can permit fewer. The container must occupy a recorded position and be properly worn or directly held by that same bearer. Holding needs the necessary actual hands. Touch, ownership, dragging, a vehicle or attachment to another container is insufficient. One Action dons/doffs one container. Designate its relief position during donning or a lawful take-hold operation. Switching competing already-carried occupants takes one Action to reposition/refit; the old benefit ends before the new one begins.

Track every physical item once in an acyclic custody tree, with a stable loading order. Keep known physical weight w, canonical Inventory Load l and derived covered pounds r separately. In the active container's eligible contents, allocate R in recorded order. For each record, 0 ≤ r ≤ w, and total r ≤ R. The direct bearer counts actual carried pounds minus covered pounds, and canonical carried Load minus the sum of min(l, r/10). Never replace physical weight or canonical Load with these derived results. An estimated 10×Load weight is not sufficient evidence for pound relief.

Partial accounting coverage is allowed: 10 lb of a 12-lb rock may be relieved without splitting the rock. A 1-lb item with printed 3 Load loses at most 0.1 Load and retains 2.9. Fifty ordinary coins weigh 1 lb/0.1 Load, so that ordinary contribution can be fully relieved. The active container's own empty body does not relieve itself. Actual repacking can change allocation order through real handling; no reordering after a roll or resolved hazard.

Ordinary inner bags/bottles and their eligible contents may receive relief, counting the empty body then descendants in recorded order. An inclusive gross record is either counted once as that inclusive record or expanded into disjoint body/contents records, never both. Creature bodies, casualties, living creatures and Petrified creatures are ineligible. Separately legal harvested material lots are ordinary objects. Record both weight and Load even where one capacity is not the immediate limiting factor.

An enclosed enchanted container is stowed and gives no independent relief. Its active outer container may cover the inner body and eligible descendants once. A Backpack strapped outside another is neither a second active Backpack nor interior cargo; arbitrary external lashings receive no relief. Actual cargo in a defined harness cradle or integrated belt pockets uses those real compartments. Ordinary inner bags do not block outer relief.

Transfer moves the same physical container subtree once. The old bearer loses relief immediately; the recipient receives it only when their own slot, hold, fit and state qualify. Recompute continuous relief from current contents; there is no charge, depletion or refresh counter. No carrying relief increases STR, push/drag/lift limits, casualty capacity, equipment anatomy or container strength. Supports and rescuers encounter full physical mass.

## Suppression, breakage, placement and melding

Suppression stops relief, check bonuses, AC enhancement and resistance. It changes no body mass, physical capacity, structural HP, mundane grade or persistent workmanship. It does not spill contents. Immediately recompute burden. Excess creates **overloaded custody**: inventory remains present, but cannot move through ordinary routine carrying until lawful dropping, transfer or another handling method makes it legal. This is not automatic damage, teleportation, deletion, forced selection of dropped valuables or a universal Speed-0 condition.

At 0 structural HP a Broken container loses both recipe benefits and ordinary secure containment. Release each immediate contents record to ordinary placement at the container's actual location. An intact inner bag moves once as its subtree; do not also spill its descendants separately. A separately recorded intact supporting container/restraint can retain what it actually supports. An integral strap belonging to the Broken body is not an independent restraint.

Use the same legal location, otherwise the nearest legal adjacent item position under the reviewed placement procedure; absent a legal position, record pending placement. Pending items cannot also be retrieved from the former container. Spilling itself deals no automatic damage; actual fall, fire, immersion or other hazards resolve normally. The Broken body's mass and worn/held state persist until actually removed. Repair does not retrieve or reload former contents. Worn/held structural attacks follow the reviewed equipment object defenses; the structural profile does not replace a creature's armor formula.

Before a voluntary transformation pays its cost, test proposed melded cargo using full pounds and canonical Load against the fixed ordinary pre-transformation ceilings, without any Leatherworking relief. Excess must be lawfully left external where permitted and anatomically compatible, or removed/dropped through the existing procedure. An unopened loaded container is handled as one containment subtree for capture/placement, but all of its physical pounds and canonical Load count. Removing selected contents first requires actual prior handling; neither duplicate contents nor a single artificially light object is created.

For a mandatory transformation, use the existing overflow procedure; excess gives no transformation immunity. Follow a pre-recorded disposition order, or stable physical-item-ID order if none exists, never a post-result tactical selection. Melded gear gives no relief or handling benefit. A rescuer carrying the transformed bearer counts the body's full effective weight, applicable casualty Load, and every melded/external equipment record under the body guide. Relief does not pass through the bearer to the rescuer.

On return, existing external equipment has priority. Only containers actually restored to legal worn/held relief positions reactivate. Returned stowed containers remain inactive. Recompute burden after legal equipment states are set, then resolve remaining overload.

Reserve floor(1.5×Q) complete mundane bases before any work, recording stable order, physical identity, model, size, grade, actual material, current/maximum HP, persistent flaw/durability and baseline value. Containers must be empty. Nonmagical complete functional damaged bodies may qualify. Broken, incomplete, committed-elsewhere or already-imprinted bodies do not. This route adds no second recipe imprint from either trade. A missing/Broken/unavailable reserve pauses completion before the roll; repair or replace it at the same list position. No post-roll substitution, purchase or reordering.

Transform the first resolved N bodies; a durability choice transforms the first Standard Q. Failed/Mishap transforms none. Return unused actual bodies in their actual condition, separately from 25% ingredient recovery. Use the latest lawful completion HP, including intervening damage, repair and permitted replacement; the reservation snapshot does not freeze condition. Transformation preserves physical identity.

The finished empty mass equals the actual base body mass. Every source component remains mandatory process/reinforcement input. Its listed mass does not supply a second body or saleable inventory asset; solvent/waste grants no automatic salvage. All Leatherworking source component occurrences are consumed, with no catalyst/tool roles. Thus Masterwork returns zero eligible catalyst units. Required tools outside that bill remain tools; do not relabel a consumed component to manufacture a return.

Use DEX and the actual Leatherworker's-tools credential under the framework; Tannery Bench, Awl and Cutting knife are required. A kit satisfies an auxiliary tool only where its actual contents include it. All fieldCraftable flags are false, including contradictory preserved field time-mode labels; no portable field route is supplied. Apply actual formula/trade eligibility, assistant caps, station/access modes, Watches, rest and active scheduling. Never add numerical trade level to the check.

After lawful creation-time adjustments, the first stable incorporation phase is min(8 active hours, final required active time); later phases are at most eight hours. Record storage and incorporation before work. Only actually incorporated material stabilizes on completion of the first phase. Other reserved lots retain their age and spoilage clocks. Replacement ingredients neither recover spent materials nor erase completed phases. Forced interruption loses unfinished phase progress under the framework; completed phases persist only where lawful storage/access persists.

Forge Dwarf applies ×0.75 to this artisan creation time, rounded up to a whole minute, and treats an actual Standard output as Exceptional for its output choice. It does not upgrade Flawed/Failed/Mishap or shorten repair, training, preservation clocks or rest. Cards preserve exact source hours, nulls, labor values and trivialAtSkillLevel thresholds. ## Repair, reconstruction and value

Both repair procedures require actual Leatherworker's-tools proficiency, DEX plus the one applicable proficiency contribution, Leatherworker's tools, Tannery Bench, Awl and Cutting knife. They are binary tasks:

| Existing condition | DC | Active work | Materials | Success |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Functional, current HP≥1 | 10 | max(1 hour, recorded maximum HP/10 hours) | 25% actual undamaged mundane size/grade base reference | Restore recorded maximum HP |
| Broken, current HP0, recoverable imprinted main body | Original recipe crafting DC | max(8 hours, recorded maximum HP/10 hours) | 25% undamaged finished per-item reference | Restore recorded maximum and dormant imprint, subject to suppression |

Failure consumes materials and work. Neither procedure needs or teaches the original formula or trade-level eligibility, rolls quality, returns a catalyst, gives crafting XP or gets Forge Dwarf's creation reduction. The original main body must be recoverable. For a pair, both original members must survive; replacing a missing member needs a separate procedure. Fragments assigned to one item cannot reconstruct a duplicate. Preserve model, grade, fit, identity, physical durability and flaw. No lost contents return. A separately available quoted Core repair service is an alternative route, not an additional automatic charge.

Source process unit reference = preserved Standard batch reference / source Q. Finished unit reference = that share + actual undamaged mundane size/grade base reference. Keep exact precision until the transaction. Purchased reserves are separate expenses; owned bodies need no repurchase. Preserve all original economy fields. Actual finite buyers and quotes control; magical classification and crafting quality invoke neither artifact multipliers nor automatic mundane standing buyback.
