# Decennium Descent — Canon Core

## Public Premise

Every ten years, the Crucible opens for **2,400 hours / 100 days**. The Horn sounds at **1,200 hours / day 50**, marking the point of no return. Adventurers descend through 100 floors seeking wealth, knowledge, prestige, and power.

The accepted public tradition credits **Calan'thir and Asmodeus** with creating the Crucible through a divine pact. That tradition is not a deliberate lie: they genuinely shaped, governed, and gave the known dungeon its present role.

## Director Truth — current owner corrections

The **Silent Ones are the Old Ones**. The owner’s current account says many attacked, were imprisoned, and then devoured one another until one primal one remained. Aethel gradually appeared around the prison after their confinement. The exact identity of that survivor in relation to Ul’nak, the Floor-97 Old God and the Interstice remains unresolved.

The **Pact manifested the prison**. The owner describes the Heartcell as a desperate attempt to find something that would silence the Old Ones: its changing rooms provide chaotic noises that keep them lulled. Calan’thir and Asmodeus have **not tied their essences**; the Pact itself became sentient.

## Why the Dungeon Exists

Reality remains alive only while meaningful difference continues to produce possibility, choice, motion, history, memory, identity, and will. The Heartcell harvests and preserves the resonance created by consequential mortal action. This operating description does not settle the original builder or replace the owner’s containment purpose above. The Matrix distinguishes the dangerous ontological stillness from ordinary political peace, treaties and reconciliation.

Treasure is therefore not the Heartcell's ultimate purpose. **Treasure is the invitation.**

Recovered wealth drives expeditions, contracts, rivalries, research, patronage, war, ambition, sacrifice, and future Openings. The surface world is part of the machine because the consequences of the dungeon continue producing choices long after an expedition leaves it.

## The Matrix

The constructive chain is:

**Possibility → Choice → Difference → Conflict → Motion → Time → History → Memory → Identity → Will → Life**

Oblivion attacks the chain in reverse by eliminating distinction and agency until no meaningful state remains.

## Guardrails

- Do not reveal the full Heartcell mechanism in ordinary player-facing text.
- Do not reduce the setting to “conflict is good.” Conflict has cost; the cosmological problem is the erasure of all meaningful alternatives.
- Calan'thir and Asmodeus are not retroactively frauds. Their role is real but not primordial.
- The Old Ones' logic must remain intelligible: eliminating difference can eliminate suffering, but also eliminates personhood, will, history, and life.
