## What a jewel grants

Each complete, functional jewel is durable magical nonartifact equipment. It needs no attunement, RP, concentration or spellcasting. Its exact source tag, flat bonus and maximum charges govern; rarity, donor names, secondary tags and ingredient properties grant nothing else.

The passive modifier applies once to each independently qualifying ability check or saving throw made by the actual wearer. The source word “one” means one individual roll, not one permanent chosen skill or one use per day. The passive bonus remains available with zero charges. Ownership and consent do not lock the passive benefit, but the item grants no procedure for forcibly equipping someone. An ally, operator or owner who is not the actual wearer gains nothing.

At the roll commitment checkpoint, the item must be complete, at positive structural HP, unsuppressed, unmelded, actually fitted and worn in its designated legal position. Concealing it under compatible clothes is allowed. Tailoring's presentation visibility and coverage requirements do not apply to jewels. Incapacitation alone does not remove a passive modifier when a legitimate eligible roll still exists.

There is no armor enhancement candidate, including zero: a jewel cannot cancel a selected Crude suit's −1. No resistance, immunity, attack bonus, proficiency, ability increase, carrying relief or spell access is supplied.

## Exact check and save domains

These are exhaustive causal predicates for already legitimate checks and saves. Check eligibility needs both the listed task and ability/proficiency combination. Save eligibility needs the actual applying effect or hazard. Scenery, proximity, an ingredient's donor, and descriptive relabeling do not qualify a roll. No check, tool, permission, action, movement, knowledge or access is created; proficiency still applies only under its governing procedure.

Across every tag, exclude attacks, damage rolls, initiative, death saves, concentration checks/saves, crafting completion, formal formula research, harvesting/extraction, repair and combat-maneuver checks such as grapple, shove, trip or disarm. An eligible ordinary recognition or Medicine task does not thereby become a crafting, research or harvesting procedure. A save against an imposed effect remains distinct from a maneuver check.

A save involving multiple eligible tags remains one event. A poison save covering damage and Poisoned is one save; later independent poison-ending saves may qualify again, while Poisoned disadvantage on an unrelated check does not make that check poison-related. Arcane covers the direct spell or expressly magical effect; a later ordinary Wound save does not qualify merely because the preceding damage was magical, although Vitality may independently qualify.

Unholy is a bounded mechanical label. It is not a moral or cosmological finding or a new damage type. “Soul,” “spirit,” “sentient,” a donor name, or an Oracle feature named Curse does not establish a predicate. Existing source identities and setting language remain preserved for the separate post-push cosmology review.

## Combining eligible benefits

On an ability check, Jewelcrafting modifiers join Leatherworking and Tailoring in `equipment.leather-task-enhancement`: use the strongest independently qualifying value once. Each older trade retains its own actual task domains and actor rules. On a saving throw, use the strongest independently qualifying Jewelcrafting value once in `equipment.jewel-save-enhancement`; neither older trade gains save eligibility.

Validate all equipment designations and physical compatibility before filtering candidates for the roll. A nonqualifying, suppressed or Broken item does not automatically vacate a position. Each item needs its own premises; one qualifying weaker item cannot make an unrelated stronger one qualify. Other class, ancestry, spell and tool benefits retain their own stacking rules.

At most one Jewelcrafting item may spend one charge on a roll event. It must qualify independently but need not supply the winning flat bonus. Thus a qualifying +1 jewel can supply advantage while another qualifying +2 item supplies the selected modifier. Several advantages do not overcome one disadvantage; apply ordinary cancellation.

Every participating item's evidence must describe this same actual roll. Match the roll's identity, kind, actor, ability, proficiency and whether a roll occurs; an automatic result grants no check bonus. Each trade may describe its qualifying contribution differently, but evidence from an earlier or different check cannot qualify an item now.

## Spending a charge

A conscious, non-Incapacitated wearer may voluntarily spend one charge immediately before an eligible roll commits. It costs no separate Action, Bonus Action, Reaction, free interaction, RP, speech or hand manipulation. It is an item activation, not a spell. Reaction denial or Surprise alone does not bar it; an applicable independent activation prohibition does. Control instructions cannot bypass the reviewed prohibition on compelled finite-resource spending.

1. Establish the legitimate roll, actor, governing source, all equipment designations and item-specific eligibility.
2. Before any die or replacement result is revealed, select one independently qualifying jewel.
3. At commitment, revalidate eligibility and available charges; debit one charge and register advantage for this event atomically.
4. Resolve the roll with its ordinary modifiers and advantage/disadvantage rules.

At the table: declare, mark off the charge, then roll. Concurrent requests must not spend the same charge twice. A late declaration after any die, replacement result or success/failure grants nothing and spends nothing. An ineligible declaration does the same. Cancellation before commitment releases a reservation without debit. Once the dice/result procedure begins, the charge stays spent even if the result fails, is discarded or becomes irrelevant; there is no result-dependent refund.

Spending when advantage already exists, or when disadvantage will cancel it, is legal but may waste the charge. It grants no third die. No charge forces a roll in place of automatic success/failure or a no-roll procedure. Unconscious automatic STR/DEX-save failure is not overcome. Adjudication reveals no hidden DC, cause, source or private lore.

One roll-event identity includes advantage/disadvantage dice and any explicitly same-event replacement procedure. A permitted reroll follows its own text; it opens no second expenditure window and automatically grants no new pair of dice. Existing modifiers remain only as that procedure permits. A copied die does not copy the originating item's modifier, advantage or transaction; a copied resolved total cannot receive the same modifier again. A genuinely new later save reevaluates current equipment and may spend a new charge. The jewel creates no repeat save.

## Charge identity and the dawn clock

First lawful creation initializes each physical imprint's current charges to its exact source maximum once, even if the output emerges Broken and unusable. Track physical item ID, recipe/imprint, maximum/current charges, creation event, roll-keyed transactions, last processed dawn index and condition/suppression history needed to resolve elapsed events. Transfer, theft, sale, storage, fitting, equipping, scene changes, initiative and copied records never initialize it again. Reconstruction retains the ledger.

NEW Jewelcrafting-only convention: use 06:00 on one recorded campaign clock, once per 24 elapsed campaign hours everywhere. Record a fixed campaign epoch D0; dawn n is D0 + n × 86,400 campaign seconds, identified by (campaignId, jewelcrafting-dawn, n). This recovery convention asserts no astronomy or cosmology and does not replace other features' independently defined dawn rules. Local sunrise, underground location, travel, rest and displayed clock changes create no extra event.

A legitimate refill sets charges to maximum; it never adds that maximum or banks unused charges. Process each dawn at most once per physical item and mark it processed even when recharge is ineligible. Missed dawns are not pending refills. Across multiple days, resolve the actual state at each dawn using intervening history, not the item's current state projected backward. Coincident regular-turn dawns use the non-action recharge stage after start-expiring effects and before later start effects; otherwise retain stable registered event order. Creation at that timestamp initializes once and cannot replay an already processed event.

Spending immediately before a real dawn and again after refill is legal; no rolling 24-hour cooldown is added. Relabeling clocks or revisiting locations cannot replay events. This does not enable Time Loop or resource-history rollback.

Resolve creation, dawns and expenditures in the campaign's actual event order, including their order at a shared timestamp. Before spending, resolve every preceding dawn from the item's actual state at that event. Retain each item's last processed event position and reject earlier or replayed transitions; a late dawn cannot overwrite a later expenditure. Use one authoritative resource history. Copies record that history and do not create another usable balance. This ordering grants no additional action or roll opportunity.

## Recharge, suppression, meld and return

A complete positive-HP unsuppressed item refills at its legitimate dawn whether worn, stowed or melded, and regardless of its wearer's sleep or incapacity. Only an actually eligible worn item can supply a passive modifier; voluntary charge use additionally requires its capable wearer. Melded recharge is an explicitly permitted internal clock operation, not access or use through melded equipment. Whether suppression reaches a melded item depends on the suppression's actual scope.

Suppressed, Broken or incomplete items do not refill; mark that dawn processed. Existing maximum and current charges remain. Ending suppression, repairing or completing an assembly does not replay or catch up missed dawns. A destroyed or irrecoverably absent imprinted primary body cannot recharge or function. Repairing a newly created Broken item can expose its already-recorded initial charges; that is not a repair refill.

Count actual item pounds and canonical Load once, with no carrying relief. Meld preflight uses the governing full mass and Load ceilings; rescuers count the body and melded/external gear under the body guide. Melded jewels grant no modifier or activation. Compatible later anatomy does not demeld them. On return, existing external gear retains priority. A returned item resumes only in its legal available recorded position; otherwise use existing carried-storage/drop/pending-placement rules. A different free position grants no free redesignation. Broken or released items retain actual location and elevation, with no deletion, duplication or automatic relocation to the ground.

## Bodies, components and quality

Before work reserve floor(1.5 × source Q) complete mundane bodies in stable physical-ID order, declaring one admitted model, fitted size and Standard grade. Record ordinary and legally modified maximum HP, current HP, durability percentage, persistent flaw, material, required original constituents, identity and undamaged body reference. Every reserve must be nonmagical, complete, functional, actually available and exclusively reserved; no prior magical imprint or alteration is admitted. Damaged functional bodies are allowed.

At completion use each body's latest lawful state. A missing, Broken, incomplete or committed-elsewhere reserve pauses before the roll; lawful repair/replacement retains its list position. Transform the first resolved N, or first Q for durability; never select, reorder, purchase or substitute after rolling. Failed/Mishap transform none. Unused bodies remain actual mundane objects, separate from ingredient recovery. Finished body mass is its admitted mass. Recipe components are additional process inputs paid once per batch; they create no extra body or automatic saleable waste.

Preserve every component occurrence, quantity, role, tier and flag. Masterwork may return at most one eligible actual catalyst unit total for the batch, whether yield or durability is selected. Choose after the check; record nondestructive participation separately from final consumption. Do not destroy it early and invent a replacement. Retain its identity, condition, age, quantity and shelf life, and actual lawful preservation/containment throughout participation. Workpiece stabilization does not automatically stabilize a recoverable catalyst. Failed recovery excludes catalysts; Mishap and abandonment return none. Names, category, donor identity or drop quantity cannot relabel materials as catalysts. A Forge Dwarf Standard-to-Exceptional upgrade is not Masterwork.

Use the six-band crafting procedure, including natural-1 Mishap and no extra natural-20 shift. Standard yields Q; Exceptional yield floor(1.25Q); Masterwork yield floor(1.5Q). Flawed yields floor(Q/2) when Q≥2, retaining ordinary effects and any existing flaws; Q=1 yields one item with one persistent structural flaw. Reduced multi-output yield reaching one does not add a singleton flaw.

Choose yield or structural durability once for the whole batch after rolling. Durability transforms Q bodies and attaches 25% Exceptional or 50% Masterwork, retaining only the strongest legally attached percentage P. With ordinary size-adjusted HP B: improved maximum = B + max(1, floor(B×P/100)) if P>0, otherwise B. Apply a persistent flaw once last: max(1, floor(improved maximum/2)). New current HP = clamp(final maximum − (old maximum − old current), 0, final maximum). Preserve latest damage deficit; weaker improvements do not compound and repeated flaws do not halve again.

At ordinary HP5 the 0%/25%/50% maxima are 5/6/7, or 2/3/3 when flawed. A body at 1/5 can emerge singleton-Flawed at 0/2. Quality never changes tag, flat bonus, maximum or initial charges, dawn schedule, grade or use permission. No charge-improvement option is authorized. A later structural improvement needs an independently authorized route; these equations do not authorize re-enchanting a jewel. Ordinary repair preserves durability and flaws.

## Work, implements and preservation

Creation chooses DEX or INT before work; Jeweler's-tools training is the applicable credential. Actual Jeweler's toolkit, Jeweler's Bench, Fine files and Magnifying lens remain necessary. The framework permits otherwise eligible untrained attempts at recipe levels 1–4 with disadvantage; trained attempts use the actual trade/formula gates and one applicable proficiency contribution. The supplied toolkit costs 25 gp and weighs 2 lb; ownership grants neither training nor a numerical bonus. The bench is a separate station requirement. NEW ordinary Fine-files set: 1 gp, 0.1 lb, HP5, steel-dominant structural AC19; it supplies only that physical implement. The supplied 100-gp Magnifying glass can satisfy the lens when suitable and available; its null source weight and 0.02-lb operational estimate remain distinct.

A documented implement inside an inclusive toolkit can satisfy compatible overlapping requirements without duplicate purchase or mass. Generic kit ownership does not establish undocumented contents. One physical implement cannot satisfy overlapping active work or exclusive reservations twice. All fieldCraftable flags remain false even where source timeMode says field. Require the actual station at a lawful workshop or otherwise permitted worksite.

Keep each card's exact hours, assistant cap, formula/discovery gates and trivialAtSkillLevel; no null-hour overlay or regenerated level+5 threshold applies. After lawful time adjustments, the first stable incorporation phase is min(8 active hours, total required active time), included in that total. Later phases are at most eight active hours. Declare incorporation and storage before work. Only inputs actually incorporated in a completed stable phase stabilize. Untouched, replacement and recoverable catalyst lots retain their clocks unless another exact procedure preserves them. Replacement restores neither spent inputs nor completed phases.

Apply actual training and formula eligibility, assistants, interruption, Watch ceilings, rest and long-term access. Trade level is never added to the check. Eligible Forge Dwarf creation uses ×0.75 time rounded up to a whole minute and the reviewed Standard-to-Exceptional choice. It shortens neither repair, training, preservation clocks, rest nor independent calendar waits.

## Trained repair and original identity

Both routes require actual Jeweler's-tools proficiency, the actual toolkit, bench, Fine files and Magnifying lens. Choose DEX or INT for the attempt. Compatible overlapping implements can satisfy several requirements; a quotation cannot supply a missing kit or station.

| State of recoverable original assembly | DC | Active work | Materials |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Functional, current HP≥1 | 10 | max(1 hour, recorded maximum HP/10 hours) | 25% undamaged mundane body reference |
| Broken, HP0 | Original recipe crafting DC | max(8 hours, recorded maximum HP/10 hours) | 25% undamaged finished unit reference |

Use Core tool-check resolution collapsed to success/failure, with no quality roll or extra critical repair benefit. Success restores recorded maximum structural HP and otherwise-eligible usability. Failure consumes work and materials.

Require the recoverable original primary body and every original stone/primary setting prerecorded as required constituents of that imprint. Mounted Gem requires both its original crystal and original primary mounting. Ordinary subordinate links, pins and closure pieces may be replaced while repairing that original assembly; missing primary bodies/settings or required original stones may not. No imprint transplant, duplicate recovery from shared fragments or invented second Earring is allowed.

Neither route requires or teaches the original formula or original trade level, grants crafting XP, returns catalysts, rolls quality, removes a flaw or receives Forge Dwarf creation reduction. Preserve identity, model, size, grade, durability, flaw, maximum/current charges and dawn history. A genuine dawn during repair uses the actual item state at that instant, never the later repaired state. Existing Core quoted repair services remain alternatives where applicable, not an automatic extra fee or missing-body exception.

## Reference values and transactions

Process unit reference = preserved Standard batch value/source Q. Finished unit reference = process unit reference + actual admitted undamaged body reference. Divide by source Q, never final yield. Large doubles only the body's contribution. Do not add materials again, subtract a returned catalyst from the preserved reference, or buy a toolkit for every output. Retain exact precision until a real transaction. Purchases round up to copper and sale proceeds down, once at the final transaction.

Owned reserves require no repurchase. Body/tool acquisition, rentals and commissions are separately recorded. The source's 50% deposit applies to the actual agreed complete quote without charging supplied inputs twice. Charges, damage and flaws can affect an actual buyer's assessment but create no automatic discount, guaranteed full-price sale, melt value, artifact multiplier or mundane buyback. Repair references remain undamaged references, not a depleted/damaged sale quote.
