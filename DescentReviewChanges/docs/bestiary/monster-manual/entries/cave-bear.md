## Overview

Dungeon-twisted cave bears — Yieldheart's brute answer to noisy galleries. Toolkit pairs one bear with Spiral Worms and an Umbrin Arachnid scout.

## Appearance

[DRAFT expansion] Shoulders dusted in luminous grit; claws packed with mineral crust. Eyes reflect hearth-crystal blue. Hibernates in echo-shadows until vibration wakes it.

## Ecology & Behavior

Beast pressure and travel threat. Morale: scavengers flee if the bear drops and the party holds light. Not evil — hungry and territorial.

## Stat Block

**Armor** 13; **Hit Points** 68; **Speed** 40 ft..

### Actions
- **Goring Horn** — +5 to hit — 1d10+2 piercing (avg 7.5)

*Source combat:* `band-trusted` · draft=`False`

## Tactics

Close and lock a frontliner; ignore fleeing targets if food (rations, carcasses) is thrown — optional lever. Worms punish casters while bear tanks.

## Lair & Terrain

Echoing Gallery dens; side-caves off Calcified Road.
