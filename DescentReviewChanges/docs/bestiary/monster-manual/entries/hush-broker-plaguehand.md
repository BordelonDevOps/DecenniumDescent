## At a Glance (Required)

Hush-Broker Plaguehand is a mid-court hush agent: disease-as-contract, quarantine as leverage. It wants signatures more than kills -- a sick Expedition still owes. Meet it at haven clinics, caravan cordons, and "helpful" quarantine tents.

### Habitat & floors
[DRAFT expansion] Travels broker roads between Emberrest, Crystalfront clinics, and Choir pilgrim camps. Not a wilderness predator; it follows crowds and Opening traffic.

### Social structure / numbers
Works with a retainer (often a lesser Hush-Broker or hired humanoid). Higher courts may recall it mid-deal.

### Behavior & goals
Offers cures that create debt; spreads rumors of plague to raise prices.

### Signs & omens
Fresh lime-wash; missing fingers on statues; clinic ledgers with blank name columns.
