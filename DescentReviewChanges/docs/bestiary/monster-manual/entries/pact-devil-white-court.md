## At a Glance (Required)

White Court Pact Devils adjudicate pale bargains: clarity, cold equity, contracts that look merciful until the clause turns. Mid-band Expeditions meet them as observers and haven lawyers -- foreshadowing deeper Pactbound Arena culture without requiring T7 floors.

## Ecology (Required for Full Entry; Optional for Field Brief)

### Habitat & floors
[DRAFT expansion] Prefer bright ash, white chalk circles, and rooms where heat bleaches color. Bastion of Flame sheet zone may be sandbox; stage in Anvil white-ash courts or Radiance pale plazas.

### Social structure / numbers
Solo adjudicator with optional scribe. Defers to higher Pact ranks in Arena tiers.

### Behavior & goals
Witness, interpret, enforce. Rarely starts violence; finishes it.

### Signs & omens
White ash handprints; twin copies of the same writ; temperature drop when names are spoken.
