## At a Glance (Required)

Season-Bound Autumn is a fold-adjacent fey whose presence turns corridors into harvest-thin places: leaves that should not exist, bargains priced in hours of Opening time. It wants a clean trade more than a corpse -- and it remembers who breaks terms. Expeditions meet it as politics and attrition, not a pure damage check.

## Ecology (Required for Full Entry; Optional for Field Brief)

### Habitat & floors
[DRAFT expansion] Autumn-fold pressure appears wherever Spiral seasons bleed into stone -- mid-band corridors that smell of wet leaf-rot despite forge heat or choir dust. Sheet zone *Veiled Iron Spiral* is deep-campaign geography; Directors staging Tranche B should prefer T3-T5 side-folds and note the deep zone as a migration omen, not default spawn.

### Social structure / numbers
Usually solitary or in a bargaining pair. Rare courts of three seasons meeting is a campaign beat, not a random encounter.

### Behavior & goals
Seeks fair-seeming exchanges: maps for names, rations for silence, a debt for a door. Violence is what happens when the ledger cannot close.

### Signs & omens
Unseasonal leaf litter; clocks that lose minutes; Recon reports of "someone already waiting" at junctions.

## Role vs Expedition (Required)

- **Combat:** Psychic cuts that punish clustered Support; prefer one exposed face.
- **Recon:** Detect bargain traps and false doors before the trade starts.
- **Research:** Record season-fold grammar; do not accept verbal contracts without writ.
- **Resource:** Sparkling petal / leaf / blossom drops -- preserve dry, away from forge heat.
- **Support:** Counter charm-pressure and keep Opening-clock discipline during talks.
