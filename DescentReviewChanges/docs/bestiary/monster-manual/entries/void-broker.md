## Overview

Large market-makers of absence — they buy what Expeditions abandon and sell routes that skip fair fights. Chaotic Evil as predatory dealmaking in a closed biome.

## Appearance

[DRAFT expansion] A tall rift wearing a merchant's coat of woven shadow-reed. Tentacles count coins that aren't there. Voice like a drowned auctioneer.

## Ecology & Behavior

Competes with Blue Court Pact Devils for clients. Prefers Expeditions deep in contamination debt. Research teams want behavior notes; Recon wants routes that avoid them.

## Tactics

**Warped Tentacle** to snatch gear; **Psychic Pulse** to scramble formation. Offers mid-fight bargains ("your rope for your friend"). Leaving gear can end hostilities — Maw's Tribute lesson recycled in marsh form.

## Lair & Terrain

Phantom Mire auction shallows; fog markets that vanish at Sporemist Gate approach.
