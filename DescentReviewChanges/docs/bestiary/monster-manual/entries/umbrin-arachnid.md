## Overview

Murk-bred hunting beasts associated with Umbrin deep routes — scouts and pack animals of shadow caverns. Product demonym is **Umbrin** (never Drow).

## Appearance

[DRAFT expansion] Obsidian-sheen chitin, too many jointed legs, silk that drinks lantern light instead of gleaming. Eyes like cooled slag. Move with Quiet-Step economy — silence is etiquette, not stealth theater.

## Ecology & Behavior

Used by Umbrin houses as pathfinders through Yieldheart back-ways. Wild specimens claim crystal ledges. Transactional: an Umbrin handler may call one off for coin, favor, or shared enemy — never as redemption. Surface folk treat them as proof the Murk is near.

**Armor** 13; **Hit Points** 52; **Speed** 40 ft..

## Tactics

**Barbed Lash** on isolated Recon. Prefer vertical approaches and silk trip-lines. Retreats toward Murk-marked tunnels rather than dying for pride.

## Lair & Terrain

Ceiling nests above Echoing Gallery side-shafts; silk deadens sound — ironic in a noise-hungry tier.
