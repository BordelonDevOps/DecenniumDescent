## Overview

Season-Bound Fey locked to a single climate face — Winter manifests as hush-frost in a biome that should only steam. Bargains cost more than steel.

## Appearance

[DRAFT expansion] A child-tall figure of rime and reed-ice, antlered with icicle branching. Footprints melt into cold black water. Nearby spores crystallize midair.

## Ecology & Behavior

Unaligned sheet = seasonal compulsion over personality. Appears when Expeditions burn too much heat or purify spores with fire: Winter arrives to rebalance. Offers warmth for memories of summer — do not treat lightly.
