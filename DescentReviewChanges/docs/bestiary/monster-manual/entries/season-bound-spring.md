## At a Glance (Required)

Season-Bound Spring wears growth as a weapon: vines of soft light, offers of renewal that cost something permanent. Huge on the sheet -- stage mass as overlapping blossoms and body-doubles rather than a single giant if Size feels wrong. It wants the Expedition to *plant* a debt.

## Ecology (Required for Full Entry; Optional for Field Brief)

### Habitat & floors
[DRAFT expansion] Prefers damp folds, drip-pools, and places where forge steam meets cold air. Sheet *Duskshard Pools* is Tier 8 geography; for mid-band, use Anvil condensate gardens or Isles mist-bridges as staging.

### Social structure / numbers
Often alone; may "borrow" a Season-Bound Autumn for a paired negotiation.

### Behavior & goals
Offers healing, guidance, or a shortcut. Payment is a name, a memory, or a future favor timed to the Horn.

### Signs & omens
Green shoots through slag; perfume in sulfur air; Recon coughs pollen that glows.
