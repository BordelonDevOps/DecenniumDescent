## Overview

[DRAFT expansion] Ancient constructs bound to Yieldheart's public procedure: verify claim, tax noise, turn back the unprepared. Floors 1–3 only — beyond the Warded Gate they do not venture.

## Appearance

[DRAFT expansion] Bronze and crystal automata with hearth-lantern eyes. Joints tick like patient clocks. Friendly at Crystalfront so long as coin and paperwork hold; indifferent past that.

## Ecology & Behavior

Fauna role in toolkit: Pact automata as procedure enforcers. They retreat from paperwork compliance. Noise clock: shout → +1 reinforcement in ~2 rounds. Core pressure: diminishing supervision teaches that safety was always conditional.
