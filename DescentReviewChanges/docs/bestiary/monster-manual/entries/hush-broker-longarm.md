## At a Glance (Required)

Longarm is reach and retrieval: the broker who collects across floors. It wants the debtor, not the debate. Expeditions meet it when a prior hush deal comes due mid-descent.

## Player-facing rumor (Optional)

## Ecology (Required for Full Entry; Optional for Field Brief)

### Habitat & floors
[DRAFT expansion] Travels Spiral-adjacent shortcuts and haven walls. Sheet Spiral Nexus may be metaphorical -- stage as "wherever the debt can be seen from."

### Social structure / numbers
Pairs with Scriptor or Plaguehand; sometimes leads a small retrieval crew.

### Behavior & goals
Serve notice, then take collateral (gear, maps, a person).

### Signs & omens
Scratches at impossible heights; unpaid tabs settled overnight; empty bedrolls.
