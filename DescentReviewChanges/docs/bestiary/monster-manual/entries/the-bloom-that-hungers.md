## Overview

The Bloom That Hungers is Bloomdeep's closed-biome answer to ten floors of everything that never left: a carnivorous plant-mass grown on expedition heat. It does not so much hunt as grow toward the nearest warm body on Cycle schedule.

## Appearance

A wet cathedral of root, blossom, and sap-light. Vine-maws open like wounds in the mist; spores bead on armor as sticky frost. The smell is sweet rot under rain — wrong for any surface swamp.

## Ecology & Behavior

Root-graspers at the Overgrown Reliquary are outriders, not a separate species — killing them feeds biomass and information toward the Sporemist Gate. Reedcaller cultures read spore weather as language; Expeditions that burn blindly make enemies of medicine and predator alike.

## Seal Staging

F11 Reedcaller Circle teaches sound-betrayal. F12–13 Spore-Veil Falls / Phantom Mire soften parties. Sporemist Gate wants three Heartroot Blossoms burned clean — impure blossoms change the confrontation (predator vs regulator question).

## Tactics

**Vine Maw** every round as baseline pressure. **Root Constrict** on isolated skirmishers. **Spore Burst** the moment the party spreads to kite. Poor Int, better Perception/Stealth fictionally — once blood (or sap) is in the water, it rarely loses the trail.

## Lair & Terrain

Closed wet chamber; ambient spore density punishes stillness. Gate backlash can start the fight before the Bloom is visible.
