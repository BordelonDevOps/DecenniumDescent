## Overview

Heavy Deep Dwarf war-caste — expedition-breakers when claim wars escalate past negotiation. Sheet size Large reflects siege armor and shield-wall bulk more than giant ancestry.

## Appearance

[DRAFT expansion] Tower shields fused with Automaton breastplates; war-picks etched with hearth-runes. March cadence matches Vaskor's distant grind — intentional intimidation.
