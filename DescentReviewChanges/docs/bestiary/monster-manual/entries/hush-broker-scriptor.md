## At a Glance (Required)

The Scriptor writes the hush into stone and paper. Ink that dries into binding. It wants the Expedition's true names on a line that outlives the Opening. Combat is what happens when someone tries to leave with an unsigned copy.

## Player-facing rumor (Optional)

[DRAFT expansion] Survivors speak in half-true nicknames and haven gossip; Directors keep Silent Ones / Heartcell / Level 97 chamber lore out of PC rumors.

## Ecology (Required for Full Entry; Optional for Field Brief)

### Habitat & floors
Cave scriptoria, haven back rooms, and seal-approach camps where people suddenly need "just one form."

### Social structure / numbers
Often attended by a Chainmaw or hired guard. Defers to Court Broker ranks when present.

### Behavior & goals
Clarify, redact, and trap. Prefers witnesses.

### Signs & omens
Fresh parchment smell; identical signatures in different hands; quills that write without dipping.

## Role vs Expedition (Required)

- **Combat:** Protect the writ; punish the character reaching for documents.
- **Recon:** Find the real contract vs the decoy stack.
- **Research:** Translate hush-clauses; flag Horn-timed penalties.
- **Resource:** Ink and vellum reagents; do not burn unread.
- **Support:** Keep the party's legal speaker from improvising oaths.
