## Overview

Blue Court Pact Devils broker damp contracts — moisture, contamination, and mutual dependence made into clauses. Chaos-Realm lineage that feeds the Pact's required dissonance through bargained discord.

## Appearance

[DRAFT expansion] Ink-blue lacquered plates over a clerk's posture; fingers stained with wet parchment that never dries. Smile shows too many notarizing teeth.

## Ecology & Behavior

Unaligned on sheet as professional detachment. Offers safe passage through living routes for oaths that bind later tiers (foreshadow Pactbound Arena). Reedcaller cultures hate them; some still sign.
