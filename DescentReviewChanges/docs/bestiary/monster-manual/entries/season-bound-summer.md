## At a Glance (Required)

Season-Bound Summer is the loud season: heat-mirage manners, bright bargains, and psychic cuts that feel like midday glare. It wants motion -- parties that stall become fuel. Mid-band Directors use it to punish overlong camps between Anvil and Choirs.

## Ecology (Required for Full Entry; Optional for Field Brief)

### Habitat & floors
[DRAFT expansion] Thrives where heat and open sightlines meet -- Anvil overlooks, Radiance bridges, cave mouths that catch forge glow. Sheet Size Huge / Type Fey: stage as a heat-haze giant or a court of overlapping summer-selves.

### Social structure / numbers
Solitary stalker of slow Expeditions; may herd lesser beasts ahead as "festival."

### Behavior & goals
Demands dance, race, or duel before passage. Refusal is taken as insult.

### Signs & omens
Sweat in cold rooms; shadows shorten; distant festival drums with no source.
