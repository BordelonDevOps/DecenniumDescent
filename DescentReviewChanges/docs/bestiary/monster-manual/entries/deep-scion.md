## Overview

Deep Scions are pressure-adapted humanoids of flooded seams and cistern roads — and, crucially, the **bestiary template** the T1 encounter toolkit uses to reskin **Pact Automaton Sentinels** (no Automaton row exists yet).

## Appearance

[DRAFT expansion] Salt-white eyes, webbed joints, skin like wet limestone. When reskinned as Automatons: bronze plaques, procedure-script scrolling behind glass, indifferent posture.

## Ecology & Behavior

Native scions claim water rights in Stonewake cisterns. As Automaton reskins (F1–3): procedure enforcers who stand down if parties show registered claim / follow posted rules. Teaching encounter — not every foe fights to the death.

**Weapon Strike**; **Pressure Wave** to break clusters. Automaton mode: one unit stands down on compliance; shouting adds reinforcement clock.
