## Overview

Deep Dwarves are Murk-adjacent stoneworkers and claim-holders whose abandoned forges litter Floor 3's Stoneworks. Living enclaves still negotiate salvage law with Automatons and Expeditions.

## Appearance

[DRAFT expansion] Broad frames, slag-scarred beards, crystal-lens goggles fogged from hearth-crystal light. Armor scavenged from Automaton plate and dwarven heirloom mail.

## Ecology & Behavior

Politics matter — slaughter has reputational cost. They respect posted claim markers and hate echo-vandals who crack cisterns. May hire as Support smiths or oppose parties that loot ancestor tools without tribute.
