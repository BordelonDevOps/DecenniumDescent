## At a Glance (Required)

An Order-Drone Quintet is five lawful purpose-bodies moving as one schedule. It wants the corridor to remain *correct*. Expeditions that rearrange stones, camp off-grid, or carry chaotic Spiral trophies get scanned and corrected.

## Player-facing rumor (Optional)

[DRAFT expansion] Survivors speak in half-true nicknames and haven gossip; Directors keep Silent Ones / Heartcell / Level 97 chamber lore out of PC rumors.

## Ecology (Required for Full Entry; Optional for Field Brief)

### Habitat & floors
[DRAFT expansion] Anvil maintenance tunnels, Radiance bridge clocks, Choir metronome halls. Sheet mountains may be approach ridges.

### Social structure / numbers
Always five functional nodes. Destroying one degrades pattern; does not create a solo drone narrative unless Director reframes.

### Behavior & goals
Measure, correct, quarantine anomalous objects.

### Signs & omens
Perfect footprints; tools stacked by size; humming in five-note chords.

## Role vs Expedition (Required)

- **Combat:** Construct attrition; focus fire one node to break formation.
- **Recon:** Predict patrol clocks; move in the gaps.
- **Research:** Decode order-chords vs Choir sacred music.
