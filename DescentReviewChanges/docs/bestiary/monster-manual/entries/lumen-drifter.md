## At a Glance (Required)

Lumen Drifters are soft lantern-minds that herd lost parties toward "helpful" light. They want company in the glow. Mid-band: false haven lights on Radiance and Choir approaches. Not the Gate Guardian *Lumen of the Shattered Thread*.
