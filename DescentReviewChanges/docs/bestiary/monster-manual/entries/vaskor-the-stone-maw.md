## Overview

Vaskor is the Tier 1 Gate Guardian — seal-intelligence of burden and passage at the end of Yieldheart Caverns. It is less a hunting animal than a held breath the size of a cavern: crystalline stone that learned to close its mouth around intruders until Automaton patrols simply stopped past Floor 3.

## Appearance

A colossal maw of fused limestone and luminous mineral veins. When still, it reads as architecture — a chamber with too many teeth. When it moves, the floor itself peels open. Dust tastes of ore; every footfall returns as a soft grind of millstone jaws.

## Ecology & Behavior

Vaskor does not patrol. Expeditions that chip Luminous Veins, shout in the Echoing Gallery, or refuse Recognition at the seal teach it where weight gathers. Automatons treat its threshold as a procedure boundary, not a moral one. Research teams map burden plates; Resource teams want tremor cores; Combat teams learn that clustering is an invitation.

## Seal Staging

Telegraph across F1–9: Spire murmurs, echo discipline, Abandoned Stoneworks of a dwarven try that stopped. **Warded Gate (F3)** ends Automaton patrol — it is *not* Vaskor's seal. **Vaskor's Seal (F10)** opens with Recognition questions before initiative; contradictions activate abandoned gear as bait.

## Tactics

Open with burrow-and-surface to prove the floor is hostile. Alternate **Stone Maw** (multi) and **Crushing Jaw**. Save **Seismic Roar** to punish planted Support. Vaskor does not bargain or taunt — middling Int, low Cha — it keeps closing until nothing fits.

## Lair & Terrain

Living rock veined with Crystalfront minerals. Burrow 20 ft.; thin stone = resurface points. Burden plates count as hazard-actors only if they act each round.
