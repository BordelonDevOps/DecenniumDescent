## Overview

[DRAFT expansion] Mineral feeder swarmlets that lick luminous veins. Encounter toolkit lists them as **Crystal Shardling** while bestiary still stores the pre-publication shell name **Ochre Jelly** — MM uses the Aethel display name.

## Appearance

[DRAFT expansion] Translucent amoeboid blobs webbed with glowing ore flecks. Sound like grinding sugar when they move. Harmless until a party chips the cavern's backbone.

## Ecology & Behavior

Attracted to harvesting Luminous Veins (F1–2). Spire of Whispers optional 1–2 scavengers if ore is chipped. Resource vs Recon tension: glow-shards vs waking feeders.

## Tactics

Cling to boots and rope; ignore heavily armored brutes to dissolve soft packs. Flee bright hearth-crystal if not feeding.

## Lair & Terrain

Vein seams, Weight Garden pressure crystals, Stonewake burrow walls.
