## Overview

A hush-touched ferryman remnant — small fiendish echo that collects silence where expedition noise breaks. Ties thematically to **Hush Beneath the Song** patronage without being a Silent Ones reveal.

## Appearance

[DRAFT expansion] A hooded silhouette of ash-smoke and wet stone, poling an invisible keel through crystal pools that should not be deep enough for boats. Voice arrives half a second late.

## Ecology & Behavior

Appears where parties argue over claims or shout tribute demands. Offers "safe crossing" for a price paid in quiet — abandoned words, muted horns, surrendered signal whistles. Lawful Evil as contract, not cartoon malice.

## Stat Block

**Armor** 10; **Hit Points** 9; **Speed** 40 ft..

### Actions
- **Hellfire Claw** — +3 to hit — 1d4 fire (avg 2.5)

*Source combat:* `t1-5-trusted` · draft=`False`

## Tactics

**Hellfire Claw** as breach of contract punishment. Prefers one target who broke a hush bargain. Flees if drowned in continuous noise (choir, bells) — its medium is absence.

## Lair & Terrain

Still pools under Spire of Whispers; fogged cisterns near Stonewake.

## Treasure & Harvest
- **tier1:** Gelatinous Sample (Tier 1) (rate 0.45, value 10)

[DRAFT expansion] Ferry-ash is a Shadow-mystery ritual dust; selling it openly angers Choir-orthodox NPCs.

## Variants

Stronger hush-brokers appear in deeper tiers (Hush-Broker family) — do not promote this CR 0 shell into Marquis lore.
