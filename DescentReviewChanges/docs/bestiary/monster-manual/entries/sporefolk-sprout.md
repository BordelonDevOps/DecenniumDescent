## Overview

Juvenile Mycelian network members — swarm skirmishers of Reedcaller ambushes. Bloomdeep cultures that speak with spore weather.

## Appearance

[DRAFT expansion] Knee-high caps and fibrous limbs dusted in glowing pollen. Eyes are spore-slits. When afraid, they release a cough of signaling mist.

## Ecology & Behavior

Not Bloom clones. Medicine vs predator identification is a Recon/Research job. Morale: sprouts break if their toad-brute flees into mire (toolkit `t2-reed-ambush`).
