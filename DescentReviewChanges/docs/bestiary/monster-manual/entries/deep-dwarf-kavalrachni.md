## Overview

Deep Dwarf riders bonded to cavern arthropods — rapid-response claim enforcers who police vertical terrain Expeditions forget exists.

## Appearance

[DRAFT expansion] Lance and hooked stirrups; mounts are reinforced Umbrin-adjacent arachnids or crystal-crabs, armored at the joints with dwarven rivets.

## Ecology & Behavior

Deployed when salvage disputes go vertical — ceiling roads, chimney shafts, Echoing Gallery balconies. Neutral true: contract first, blood second.
