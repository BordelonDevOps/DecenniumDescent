## Overview

Small aberration clerks of forgotten expedition logs — they catalog what Bloomdeep digests. Spiral-thin difference curdling into wrong geometry, but bureaucratic.

## Appearance

[DRAFT expansion] A floating mantle of pale membranes inked with drowning handwriting. Hands of stylus-bone. Speaks in quotations from dead journals.

## Ecology & Behavior

Drawn to Overgrown Reliquary sarcophagi and abandoned maps. Will trade a true route for a fresh memory page (literal). Unaligned curiosity is still lethal if you refuse cataloguing.

## Stat Block

**Armor** 12; **Hit Points** 31; **Speed** 40 ft..
