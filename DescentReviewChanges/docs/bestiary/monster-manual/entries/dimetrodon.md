## Overview

Sail-backed saurian beasts adapted to crystal heat — sunning on luminous veins the way surface kin use daylight.

## Appearance

[DRAFT expansion] Low-slung reptile with a vein-fed dorsal sail that fluoresces faintly. Teeth like broken chisels. Neutral Good on sheet reads as "trainable / not spiteful," not fairy-tale kindness.

## Ecology & Behavior

Basks on Luminous Veins; aggressive if sail-shade is stolen (parties harvesting glow). Deep Dwarf handlers sometimes leash trained individuals as camp alarms.
